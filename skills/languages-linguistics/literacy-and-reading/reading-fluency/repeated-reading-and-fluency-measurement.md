# Repeated reading and fluency measurement

**Goal:** help a reader (child or adult) read connected text accurately, at a
conversational pace and with natural expression, so attention is freed for
understanding.

## 1. What fluency is
Three parts: **accuracy** (words read correctly), **rate/automaticity** (without
laboured sounding-out), **prosody** (phrasing, stress and intonation that match the
meaning). Fluency is a bridge to comprehension, not a goal in itself — fast,
expressionless reading isn't the target.

## 2. Measure first (1 minute)
1. Choose an unseen passage at the reader's expected level.
2. Reader reads aloud for **60 seconds**; you follow on a copy.
3. Mark errors: substitutions, omissions, words you had to supply after ~3 seconds.
   Self-corrections and repetitions are **not** errors.
4. **WCPM = words read − errors.** Accuracy % = correct ÷ words read.
5. Rate prosody with a simple rubric (e.g. a 4-point scale: word-by-word → phrased
   with expression) — the NAEP oral reading fluency scale is a common one.
- Compare to published norms (Hasbrouck & Tindal tables give US grade-level WCPM
  percentiles). *Norms are for English and for children; for adults or other
  languages, track the individual's own progress instead.*

## 3. Diagnose before you drill
- **Accuracy below ~90–95%** on grade/goal-level text → the problem is **decoding**
  or vocabulary, not fluency. Repeated reading of a too-hard text just rehearses
  errors. Work on phonics/word recognition and use easier text.
- **Accurate but slow and choppy** → fluency practice is the right tool.
- **Fast but flat or ignoring punctuation** → prosody and comprehension work.

## 4. Choosing practice text
- **Instructional level:** roughly 90–95% accuracy on first read (ranges vary by source).
- Short passages (100–250 words), interesting to the reader, with natural language;
  poems, scripts and speeches work well for prosody.

## 5. Core routines (15–20 minutes)
- **Model:** you read the passage aloud with good expression.
- **Echo reading:** you read a sentence, reader echoes it.
- **Choral reading:** read together.
- **Repeated reading:** reader reads the same passage 3–4 times; time each read;
  graph WCPM. Seeing the line go up is powerful motivation.
- **Paired reading:** reader and partner read together; reader taps to go solo;
  partner rejoins on an error.
- **Phrase-cued text:** mark phrase boundaries with slashes to teach chunking.
- **Readers' theatre:** rehearse a script for a performance — a real reason to reread.

## 6. Error correction
- Wait 3 seconds before helping (lets the reader self-correct).
- Point to the word; prompt decoding ("Look at the letters"), not guessing from pictures.
- After the passage, practise missed words in isolation, then reread the sentence.

## 7. Tracking progress
- Weekly 1-minute probe on an **unseen** passage at the same level (practised text
  overstates gains).
- Graph WCPM and accuracy; move to harder text when the reader hits the target rate
  with good prosody on unseen text.

## Verify
- [ ] Baseline WCPM, accuracy and prosody recorded on unseen text.
- [ ] Accuracy checked before choosing fluency vs decoding work.
- [ ] Practice text is at instructional level (~90–95% first-read accuracy).
- [ ] Repeated reading timed and graphed.
- [ ] Progress measured on unseen passages, not the practised ones.
