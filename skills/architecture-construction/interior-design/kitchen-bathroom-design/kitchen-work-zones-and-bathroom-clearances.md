# Kitchen work zones and bathroom clearances

**Goal:** a kitchen that's efficient for the people who cook in it and a bathroom
that's comfortable and serviceable — planned with numbers before choosing doors and tiles.

## Kitchens
### Workflow
- Classic **work triangle** (sink–hob–fridge): each leg ~1.2–2.7 m, total ~4–8 m, no
  through-traffic across it (NKBA-style guidance, in imperial: legs 4–9 ft, total ≤ 26 ft).
- Modern practice thinks in **zones**: consumables (fridge, pantry), storage
  (crockery), cleaning (sink, dishwasher, bin), prep (main worktop run), cooking (hob,
  oven). Sequence them in the order of work: fridge → sink → prep → hob → serve.

### Key dimensions (rules of thumb; check local codes and the client's height)
- **Aisle** between runs/island: ~1000–1200 mm (one cook); ~1200–1400 mm for two cooks
  or with an appliance door open opposite.
- **Worktop height** typically ~900–920 mm finished; tune to the main cook (roughly
  elbow height minus ~100–150 mm).
- **Landing space:** ~300–400 mm each side of the hob; ~400 mm+ beside the fridge
  handle side; ~600 mm+ beside the sink; main prep run ~900 mm+ continuous.
- Wall units ~500–600 mm above the worktop; cooker hood height per maker (often
  ~650–750 mm over hob).
- Dishwasher next to the sink; bin under or beside it.
- Corners: blind corners waste space — use carousel/LeMans or design them out.
- **Fillers and scribes** at walls (~25–50 mm) so doors and drawers clear handles
  and adjacent walls.

### Services and appliances
- Integrated appliances: use the maker's **niche/installation drawings** (ventilation
  gaps, plinth vents for fridges, oven housing dims).
- Extraction: ducted to outside where possible, short duct runs, sized per hood
  (check local regs for minimum extraction rates). Gas hobs: clearances to
  combustibles and windows per regs.
- Sockets above worktop at sensible spacing; isolators for appliances accessible.

## Bathrooms
### Clearances (rules of thumb; ADA/Part M have their own for accessible rooms)
- **WC:** aim for ~400 mm from the pan centreline to a side wall or fixture (US
  codes commonly set 15 in / ~380 mm as the minimum), with ~600 mm+ clear in front.
- **Basin:** ~700 mm wide activity space in front; vanity height ~850–900 mm.
- **Shower:** 900 × 900 mm minimum, 1000 × 1200 mm+ comfortable; walk-in screens need
  enough length (~1200 mm+) to keep spray contained.
- Door shouldn't swing into a fixture or the WC zone.

### Wet areas
- **Falls** to the drain in wet rooms (commonly ~1:40–1:80; tile makers and
  waterproofing systems give values); a tanking/waterproof membrane system tested as
  a whole.
- **Electrical zones** around bath and shower (e.g. BS 7671 zones 0/1/2 in the UK; IP
  ratings for fittings in each).
- **Ventilation:** extract fan sized per regs (e.g. 15 l/s for a UK bathroom in
  Approved Document F — verify edition), with overrun timer or humidistat.
- Access to traps, valves and concealed cisterns via panels.

## Gotchas
- Island too close to the run — dishwasher door blocks the aisle.
- Hob in a corner with no landing on one side.
- Fridge door hinged against a wall, unable to open fully for drawers.
- Wet room without proper falls → standing water at the door.

## Verify
- [ ] Zones in work order; triangle legs and aisles in range.
- [ ] Landing space each side of hob, sink and fridge.
- [ ] Appliance niche drawings checked; ventilation gaps provided.
- [ ] Bathroom fixture clearances and door swing checked on plan.
- [ ] Falls, waterproofing system, electrical zones and extract specified.
