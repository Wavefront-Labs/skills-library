# Layered planting plan: species selection and densities

**Goal:** planting that survives the site, looks intentional in every season, covers
the ground within ~2–3 years, and can be maintained by the people who'll actually
look after it.

## 1. Conditions first ("right plant, right place")
From the site analysis, map planting zones by: sun/shade (hours), soil texture and pH,
moisture (wet, moist, free-draining, dry), exposure (wind, salt, pollution), hardiness
zone, and space above and below ground (utilities, foundations, overhead lines).
Shortlist only species tolerant of each zone. Native and regionally adapted species
first where ecology is a goal; check invasive species lists for your region.

## 2. Compose in layers
Think like a plant community, not a catalogue:
1. **Canopy trees** — the long-term structure: size at maturity, crown shape, root
   habit, clearance over paths (~2.4 m+) and roads (often more; check highways rules).
2. **Understorey / small trees and large shrubs** — screening and mid-height mass.
3. **Shrub layer** — structure and evergreen winter backbone (often ~30–40% evergreen
   in temperate schemes as a starting rule, adjusted to character).
4. **Herbaceous / grasses** — seasonal colour and movement, often as a **matrix**
   (a repeated base mix) with scattered emphasis plants.
5. **Groundcover and bulbs** — close the soil, suppress weeds, extend the season.
Repetition gives coherence; too many species in small quantities looks chaotic.

## 3. Seasonal structure
Tabulate each species by month for flower, foliage, fruit and winter form. Make sure
every season has something — and that winter isn't just mud (seedheads, bark, evergreens).

## 4. Densities and stock sizes
Typical planting densities (adjust to species vigour and budget):
- Groundcover / small perennials: ~7–11 per m² (9 cm or 1 L pots).
- Larger perennials and grasses: ~5–7 per m² (2–3 L).
- Small shrubs: ~3–5 per m²; medium shrubs ~1–3 per m² (2–10 L).
- Hedges: ~3–5 per linear metre in a single or staggered double row (whips/feathered).
- Trees: specify by **girth** (e.g. 12–14 cm, 16–18 cm at 1 m height) or height for
  multistem, with root type (bare root, root-balled, container) and clear stem.
Denser planting covers sooner but costs more. State plants/m² and pot size per
species in the schedule; quantity = area × density.

## 5. Establishment and maintenance
- Soil preparation (decompaction, amendment based on soil test), mulch (~50–75 mm,
  kept off stems), watering regime for 1–3 growing seasons, formative pruning,
  weeding, replacement of failures (defects liability period).
- Write a **maintenance plan** that matches the client's actual capability; meadow
  and matrix schemes need different (not necessarily less) skill than lawn and shrubs.
- For planting execution see
  `agriculture-environment.landscape-grounds-keeping.landscape-installation.planting-container-trees-and-shrubs`.

## Gotchas
- Species chosen for looks in a catalogue photo, wrong for the soil.
- Trees under power lines or over drains with no root strategy.
- Too many species, each in ones and twos.
- Densities so low it's a weed farm for three years.

## Verify
- [ ] Every species matches the zone's light, soil, moisture and exposure.
- [ ] Layers present; evergreen/winter structure considered.
- [ ] Seasonal interest table has no empty season.
- [ ] Densities and stock sizes stated; quantities = area × density.
- [ ] Establishment and maintenance plan written for the actual maintainer.
