# Inspecting hold points and signing off work before cover-up

**Goal:** nothing gets concealed (poured over, lined, backfilled, roofed) until someone
competent has checked it against the drawings and specification and recorded that check.

## 1. The inspection and test plan (ITP)
For each work package, list in sequence:
- **Activity** (e.g., footing excavation, reinforcement, pour, strip).
- **Criteria** — the drawing, spec clause or standard, and the tolerance.
- **Check method** — measure, visual, test (slump, pressure, compaction).
- **Point type:**
  - **Hold point:** work *must not proceed* until signed released by the nominated person
    (engineer, inspector, client rep).
  - **Witness point:** the nominated person must be notified and may attend; work
    proceeds if they don't.
  - **Record/review:** checked by the contractor and filed.
- **Responsible/verifier and record** (checklist, photo, test certificate).
Get the subcontractor to sign their own ITP; you verify.

## 2. Typical hold points
- Foundations: excavation base before blinding/pour.
- Reinforcement before pour (bar size, spacing, cover, laps, chairs, cast-ins, cleanliness).
- Formwork/falsework before pour (by the temporary works coordinator).
- Under-slab drainage and plumbing pressure tests before pour.
- Framing, services rough-in, insulation and fire stopping **before lining**.
- Waterproofing (flood test) before tiling/screed.
- Roof underlay and flashings before covering where possible.
- Local authority mandatory inspections (they are hold points whether you list them or not).

## 3. Running an inspection
1. Take the current drawings, spec and the ITP checklist. Know the tolerances.
2. Check systematically by grid/area so nothing is missed — tick each line.
3. Measure: tape, level/laser, cover meter, string line. Don't eyeball cover or
   spacing.
4. Photograph wide (location) and close (detail with a tape in shot).
5. **Non-conforming work:** issue a clear defect note — location, requirement, what you
   found, required action. Serious or systemic issues → non-conformance report (NCR) with
   root cause and corrective action.
6. Re-inspect after fix; release the hold point in writing only when compliant.

## 4. Clerk-of-works mindset
- Independent: you check against the documents, not against what the trade says is
  normal.
- Early and often: a defect found before the next trade starts costs a fraction of one found
  after.
- Consistent: same standard every time, or trades learn which inspector to wait for.

## 5. Handover and sign-off
- Pre-handover walk with the punch list (see the Procore skill for logging), trades
  close items, you verify.
- Collect: test certificates, commissioning records, warranties, as-builts, O&M manuals,
  compliance certificates. Missing paperwork can hold up practical completion as much as
  physical defects.

## Gotchas
- "We poured because the engineer was late" → no record, possible break-out.
- Verbal releases → no evidence later.
- Inspecting from the doorway → missed defects in the far corner.
- Defect notes without location or required outcome → arguments.

## Verify
- [ ] Every work package has an ITP with hold points identified and owners.
- [ ] No hold point released without a signed record and photos.
- [ ] Defect notes specify location, requirement, finding and action.
- [ ] Re-inspection recorded before close-out.
- [ ] Handover document set complete.
