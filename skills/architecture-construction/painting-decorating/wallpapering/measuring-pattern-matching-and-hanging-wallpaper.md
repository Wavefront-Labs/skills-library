# Measuring, pattern matching and hanging wallpaper

**Goal:** plumb drops, invisible seams, pattern matched round the room, and the
mismatch hidden in the least-seen corner.

## 1. Read the label
- **Type:** traditional paper (paste-the-paper, soak/book), **non-woven** (usually
  paste-the-wall, dry-strippable), vinyl (heavy, often needs heavy-duty or
  fungicidal adhesive), specialty (grasscloth, fabric, murals — follow maker exactly).
- **Pattern repeat** (cm/in) and **match type**: free (no match), straight (pattern
  lines up across at the same height), **half-drop/offset** (alternate drops shift by
  half the repeat).
- **Batch number:** all rolls for a room from one batch — colours vary between batches.

## 2. Calculate rolls
1. Number of drops = room perimeter (excluding large openings) ÷ roll width, rounded up.
2. Length per drop = wall height + one pattern repeat + ~100 mm trim allowance.
3. Drops per roll = roll length ÷ drop length, **rounded down**.
4. Rolls = drops ÷ drops per roll, rounded up; add a spare roll (and more for large
   repeats or many cuts). Example: 10.05 m roll, 2.4 m wall, 64 cm repeat → drop ~3.14 m →
   3 drops per roll.

## 3. Prepare the wall
- Sound, smooth, clean, dry; fill and sand. Prime/seal porous or painted surfaces as
  the adhesive maker says (size or a wallcovering primer) so you can slide the paper
  and strip it later.
- **Lining paper** on poor walls or for high-end finishes: hung horizontally
  (cross-lined) so its seams don't coincide with the top paper's.

## 4. Plan the start
- Where will the final mismatch fall? Usually the corner behind the door or above it.
  Start next to it and work away in both directions, or start on a focal wall
  centred (a drop centred on a chimney breast or centred over a seam, whichever looks
  better).
- Mark a **plumb line** (laser or plumb bob) one roll width minus ~10 mm from the
  corner. Never trust corners or door frames to be plumb.
- Decide pattern position at the ceiling: a full motif just below the ceiling line
  looks best.

## 5. Paste and hang
- **Paste-the-paper:** paste evenly to edges, **book** (fold pasted faces together) and
  leave for the stated soak time — the same time for every drop, or widths differ and
  seams won't meet.
- **Paste-the-wall:** roll adhesive on the wall a little wider than the drop, hang dry
  paper from the roll or cut lengths.
1. Hang the first drop to the plumb line with ~50 mm excess top and bottom.
2. Smooth from the centre outward with a brush or plastic smoother; push out air.
3. Crease at ceiling and skirting, trim with a sharp blade (snap off a segment every
   cut — a blunt blade tears wet paper) against a broad knife.
4. Next drop: slide to **butt** the seam tight (don't overlap normal papers), matching
   pattern. Roll seams gently with a seam roller (not on embossed papers).
5. Wipe paste off the face and ceiling/skirting immediately with clean water as
   allowed for the paper.

## 6. Corners and obstacles
- **Internal corners:** cut the drop to wrap ~10–15 mm round the corner, then hang the
  offcut on a **new plumb line** on the next wall, overlapping the wrap (vinyl overlaps
  need vinyl-to-vinyl adhesive or a double cut).
- **External corners:** wrap ~25 mm round, re-plumb next drop.
- Switches/sockets: isolate power, hang over, cut diagonal star, trim to the plate
  (loosen faceplates if the rules allow working that way; isolate first).

## Gotchas
- Mixed batches → stripes of colour.
- Inconsistent soaking → seams that gap.
- Starting from a crooked corner → drops drift out of plumb around the room.
- Paste left on the face drying to a shine.

## Verify
- [ ] Rolls calculated with the repeat; one batch number.
- [ ] Every new wall started from a fresh plumb line.
- [ ] Seams butted, matched, no gaps or overlaps (unless intentional).
- [ ] Clean trims at ceiling and skirting; no paste on the face.
- [ ] Mismatch placed in the least-visible spot.
