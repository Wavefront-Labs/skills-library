# Planning an asbestos abatement job — get it out before the building comes down

**Goal:** make sure every asbestos-containing material (ACM) affected by demolition is
found, removed by the right category of contractor under the right controls, cleared
and disposed of legally — **before** strip-out or structural demolition starts.

## 1. Survey and register
- Commission the destructive pre-demolition survey: UK **refurbishment and demolition
  (R&D) survey**; US **thorough inspection** under the EPA NESHAP by an accredited
  inspector; Australia a **hazardous materials / pre-demolition asbestos survey**.
  A management survey is not enough — it doesn't open up the fabric.
- The report should list each ACM: location, material type (sprayed coating, pipe
  lagging, insulating board, cement sheet, floor tiles, textured coating, gaskets),
  extent, condition and sample result.
- Note **areas not accessed** (e.g. live plant rooms, voids). They must be treated as
  presumed ACM and surveyed when they become accessible.

## 2. Classify the work
Each jurisdiction separates higher-risk from lower-risk work, and the split decides who
may do it and what notification is needed:
- **UK:** licensed work (e.g. most sprayed coatings, lagging, most insulating board)
  needs an HSE-licensed contractor and **14-day notification** to the enforcing
  authority (as commonly cited — check current HSE guidance); notifiable non-licensed
  work and non-licensed work have lighter rules but still need trained workers,
  controls and records.
- **US:** OSHA Class I–IV work categories, plus EPA NESHAP notification (commonly
  10 working days before demolition/renovation for regulated amounts — check current
  rules and the state agency).
- **Australia:** Class A / Class B licences under state WHS regulations.
Rule of thumb: friable, high-fibre-release materials are the licensed/top-class
category. When unsure, treat it as the higher class.

## 3. Write the plan of work
The removal contractor's plan should cover, per area:
- **Enclosure design:** sealed polythene enclosure, airlocks and baglock, viewing
  panels, **negative pressure units (HEPA-filtered)** sized for the enclosure volume.
- **Pre-start checks:** visual inspection and **smoke test** of the enclosure, NPU
  pressure check, documented before any disturbance.
- **Removal technique** that minimises fibre release: controlled wetting or injection,
  removing whole boards/tiles instead of breaking them, shadow vacuuming with
  H-class/HEPA vacuums.
- **Decontamination:** a hygiene/decon unit (dirty-shower-clean) linked to the
  enclosure; transit routes defined.
- **RPE and PPE:** face-fit-tested respirators of the protection factor the assessment
  requires, disposable coveralls.
- **Waste:** double-bagged and labelled, sealed skips, licensed carrier, consignment
  or manifest notes to a permitted disposal site.
- **Emergency procedures:** enclosure breach, NPU failure, fire, injured worker inside.

## 4. Air monitoring and clearance
- **Background** sampling before work; **leak/reassurance** sampling outside the
  enclosure during work; **personal sampling** of workers' exposure.
- At completion, an **independent analyst** (not employed by the removal contractor)
  carries out clearance. UK practice is the **four-stage clearance**: completion check,
  thorough visual inspection, air test inside the enclosure, final assessment — then a
  certificate of reoccupation. Equivalent clearance inspections apply elsewhere.
- Only after clearance does the enclosure come down and demolition proceed.

## Gotchas
- **Hidden ACMs** emerge during demolition (behind panels, under floors, in ducts).
  The demolition plan needs a **stop-work and re-survey** rule for suspect materials.
- The removal contractor shouldn't be the one who clears its own work.
- Lead paint, PCBs in sealants, mercury in switches/lamps and contaminated plant may
  need their own removal plans alongside asbestos.
- Weather and power: an NPU on a temporary supply needs a backup plan.

## Verify
- [ ] Pre-demolition (R&D or equivalent) survey covers all affected areas; gaps listed.
- [ ] Each ACM classified; licensed/class-appropriate contractor appointed; notifications sent in time.
- [ ] Plan of work with enclosure, NPU, decon, RPE, waste and emergency procedures.
- [ ] Smoke test and air monitoring records exist.
- [ ] Independent clearance certificate issued for every area before demolition.
- [ ] Waste consignment/manifest records retained.
