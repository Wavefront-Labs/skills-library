# Setting out and installing a suspended grid ceiling

**Goal:** a dead-level grid at the specified height, with equal border tiles on opposite
sides, services fitting cleanly, and a ceiling that stays put.

## 1. Before you start
- Ceiling height from the reflected ceiling plan (RCP), and the **plenum depth** —
  check ducts, sprinkler mains and cable trays leave room for the grid and fittings.
- Coordinate: lights, diffusers, sprinkler heads, smoke detectors, access panels.
  Sprinkler heads usually want to land **centred in tiles** — agree the grid with the
  fire contractor before they drop heads (or they'll ask you to move the grid).
- Confirm whether a seismic or fire-rated ceiling is required (changes wire spacing,
  perimeter details and bracing).

## 2. Level line
- Laser level (or water level) to shoot the ceiling height around the room; mark every
  ~1 m. Snap lines.
- Fix **wall angle** to the line. Fixings at ~400–600 mm (check spec); corners mitred or
  overlapped.

## 3. Balanced layout
Work out borders on both axes so edge tiles are equal and ideally **more than half a tile**:
- Room 5,300 mm, 600 mm grid: 5,300 / 600 = 8.83 → 8 full tiles = 4,800, leftover
  500 → two borders of 250 mm (less than half). Try 7 full tiles: 4,200, leftover
  1,100 → two borders of 550 mm. Better.
- Snap the first main-tee line and first cross-tee line from these borders, and
  check the room for square (3-4-5) — rooms rarely are; split the error.

## 4. Hangers and mains
- Hanger wires (typically at ~1.2 m along mains, and within ~150–200 mm of the main's
  end; ~1.2 m between rows of mains — **confirm with the grid system's spec/code**)
  fixed to structure, not to ducts, pipes or other trades' hangers.
- Pre-bend wires at the level line, or use adjustable hangers.
- Hang **main tees** (main runners), cut so the slots align to the layout: the first
  cross-tee slot must land on your cross-tee line, so trim the main from the wall end.
- Level mains with a string or laser; twist wires three full turns.

## 5. Cross tees and tiles
- Click in cross tees (1,200 then 600), check diagonals of a few modules for square.
- Cut border tees and rest them on the wall angle.
- Install tiles last, after services and trades above are finished — handle with clean
  gloves. Cut border tiles face up with a sharp knife; for tegular (rebated) edges, cut
  the rebate to match.
- Light fittings and diffusers heavier than the grid's rating need their own
  independent hangers (often required for luminaires by code).

## 6. Bulkheads and access
- Bulkheads/drop-downs framed in steel and board, tied to structure.
- Access panels where valves, dampers and junction boxes need service.

## Gotchas
- Hanging wires off ductwork → sag and cracking when ducts move.
- Forgetting the room isn't square → tapering border tiles on one wall.
- Tiles installed early get damaged by other trades.
- Sprinkler heads not centred → tearing out the grid later.

## Verify
- [ ] Laser across the finished grid: level within the spec (often ~±3 mm over room).
- [ ] Opposite border tiles equal and ≥ half a tile.
- [ ] Every wire fixed to structure; spacing per system/seismic spec.
- [ ] Heavy fittings independently supported; sprinkler heads centred.
- [ ] Access panels at all service points.
