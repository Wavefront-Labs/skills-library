# Planning and running a first date

**Goal:** spend an enjoyable hour or two finding out whether you'd both like a second
date — not to impress, perform or decide the whole future.

## 1. Plan: short, public, easy to extend or end
- **Length:** aim for 60–90 minutes with a natural endpoint. Coffee, a drink, a walk
  with a stop for food, a market, a gallery. If it's going well, you can extend
  ("want to grab dinner?"); if not, it ends without awkwardness.
- **Conversation-friendly:** avoid cinemas and loud venues on a first date — you can't
  talk.
- **Public and accessible** for both of you; meet there rather than picking them up.
- **Confirm the day before or the morning of** with a short message: place, time.
- Have a backup if it's weather-dependent.

## 2. Arriving
- Be on time; if you'll be late, message *before* the start time.
- Greet warmly; follow their cue on hug vs. handshake vs. wave.
- Put your phone away and on silent. Checking it is the most common complaint about
  first dates.

## 3. Conversation: balanced, curious, light-to-medium
- **Roughly 50/50 airtime.** If you notice you've talked for a few minutes straight,
  ask them something.
- **Open questions that invite stories:** "What got you into that?", "What's been the
  best part of your week?", "Where would you go if you had a free month?"
- **Follow up** on their answers instead of jumping to your next question; that's what
  makes someone feel listened to.
- **Share too** — answering their questions with real detail is half the job.
- **Usually leave for later:** exes in detail, heavy trauma, finances, intense
  politics, interrogation-style "where do you see yourself in five years". Do mention
  real dealbreakers (wanting or not wanting children) if they come up naturally — no
  need to hide them.
- **Nerves are normal.** Saying "I'm a bit nervous, I don't do this often" is usually
  endearing and lowers the tension for both of you.

## 4. Paying
Norms vary by culture, age and community, so there is no single correct rule. Reliable
approaches:
- **Whoever invited offers to pay** for a modest first date, and gracefully accepts if
  the other insists on splitting.
- Or **offer to split** plainly: "Shall we split it?" Either way, settle it without a
  tug of war.
- **Gotcha:** never treat paying as creating an obligation of any kind. If one person
  pays, the other owes nothing beyond thanks.

## 5. Ending the date
- If you'd like to see them again, say so simply: "I've had a great time — I'd love
  to do this again."
- If not, a warm close without false promises: "It was really nice meeting you,
  thanks for coming out." Don't say "let's do it again" unless you mean it.
- Physical affection at the end (a hug, a kiss) is optional and should follow clear
  mutual cues; asking is fine ("Can I give you a hug?").
- Make sure they're happy with how they're getting home.

## 6. The follow-up
- **Within about a day**, send a short message whichever way it went:
  - Keen: "Thanks for tonight, I had a lot of fun. Free next week?"
  - Not keen: "Thanks for meeting up. I enjoyed chatting but didn't feel a romantic
    spark — wishing you the best." (see relationships-social.dating-and-courtship.ending-courtship.ending-early-dating-kindly-without-ghosting)
- Waiting days "to seem cool" mostly reads as disinterest.

## Gotchas
- **Drink pacing:** stay well within your limit; you're making a judgement about a
  stranger and need to get home safely.
- **Tell a friend** where you are, especially for app dates.
- **One date is a small sample.** Mild chemistry on date one can grow; a mismatch in
  basic values or respect usually doesn't.
- If anything makes you uncomfortable, you can leave at any point — a polite
  "I'm going to head off" is enough.

## Verify
- [ ] The plan had a natural end point within ~90 minutes and allowed conversation.
- [ ] You confirmed the details in advance and arrived on time.
- [ ] Talk time felt roughly balanced and you followed up on their answers.
- [ ] Paying was settled without pressure or implied obligation.
- [ ] You sent a clear follow-up within a day.
