# Running the development and stoping cycle

**Goal:** keep an underground heading or stope moving through its cycle safely and on
plan, and recognise where each mining method punishes shortcuts.

> Generalised from hard-rock and coal practice; cycle times, support
> standards and re-entry rules are set by each mine's ground control management plan
> and local regulation.

## 1. The development cycle (one heading)
1. **Re-entry:** wait for the post-blast ventilation period, test the atmosphere,
   check the heading from under supported ground only.
2. **Wash down and scale:** water the face and backs to expose cracks and settle
   dust; bar down or mechanically scale loose rock, working from supported ground
   outward.
3. **Bog (muck out):** LHD removes broken rock to a stockpile or truck. Remote or
   tele-remote bogging is used once the operator would otherwise pass under
   unsupported ground — **no person under unsupported back, ever** (US: 30 CFR 57.3200 for metal and nonmetal and 30 CFR 75.202(b) for coal restrict work and travel under unsupported ground).
4. **Support:** install bolts and mesh (and shotcrete if specified) to the standard
   for that ground type, to within the specified distance of the face.
5. **Survey/mark-up:** surveyor or senior miner marks centreline, grade and the drill
   pattern so the heading stays on design.
6. **Drill:** face jumbo drills the round per pattern (cut holes, easers, perimeter).
   Check for bootlegs and misfired holes before collaring anything.
7. **Charge and fire** at the scheduled firing time from a central blast system.

Handover notes at shift change carry the state of every heading: where in the cycle,
what support is in, ground observations, misfires, water, equipment faults. An
incomplete handover is how someone ends up drilling into a socket or bogging an
unsupported face.

## 2. Method notes and their traps
- **Room-and-pillar (coal, flat-lying ore):** cut rooms leaving pillars to carry the
  roof. Trap: robbing or thinning pillars to chase tonnes, and cutting rooms wider
  than the support design allows. Pillar dimensions are an engineering decision.
- **Longwall:** a shearer cuts a long face under powered roof supports that advance
  behind it; the roof caves into the goaf. Traps: face alignment drifting, supports
  not set to pressure, gas from the goaf at the tailgate. See
  energy-mining-utilities.mineral-mining.continuous-miner-longwall.operating-a-longwall-face-and-continuous-miner.
- **Sublevel / long-hole open stoping:** drill rings or fans from sublevels, blast to a
  slot, draw ore remotely from the drawpoint. Traps: overbreak and dilution from poor
  drilling accuracy, people entering an open stope brow (never), and inaccurate
  void surveys before filling.
- **Cut-and-fill:** mine a slice, fill it (rock, hydraulic or paste fill), mine the next
  slice on top. Traps: mining before fill has cured to strength, and fill fences or
  bulkheads failing under hydraulic pressure.
- **Block and sublevel caving:** undercut and let the ore cave and flow to drawpoints.
  Traps: uneven draw causing hang-ups and early dilution, and **mud rush / wet muck**
  — follow drawpoint classification rules and never stand in the line of fire of a
  hung-up drawpoint.

## 3. Daily measures worth tracking
Advance per round (metres pulled vs drilled; a low ratio points at drilling or charging problems), tonnes bogged vs expected, overbreak from scan surveys, and
support installed vs required. These are rules of thumb; site KPIs differ.

## Gotchas
- Fire times are fixed; a cycle step that overruns waits for the next window.
- "Just a quick look" under unsupported ground is the most common serious-injury
  pattern in development.
- Water inflow, new joints or drummy ground mean stop and call the geotech, not push on.

## Verify
- [ ] Re-entry gas check and ventilation confirmed before entering.
- [ ] Scaling done and support to standard and to face before anyone works there.
- [ ] Remote bogging used wherever the LHD goes past supported ground.
- [ ] No drilling started until the face was checked for misfires and sockets.
- [ ] Handover recorded each heading's cycle state and hazards.
