# Scaling, bolting and reading ground

**Goal:** turn freshly exposed rock into a place people can work, and notice early when
installed support is no longer winning.

> Support types and patterns differ by mine and are specified in the
> ground control management plan / support standard. This describes the operator's
> side; design is in engineering-technology.mining-geological-engineering.rock-mechanics.rock-mass-classification-and-support.

## 1. Scaling (barring down)
- Always work **from supported ground toward unsupported**, with the bar (or the
  mechanical scaler) between you and the rock. Stand so falling rock and the bar's
  kickback go away from you; keep a clear escape route behind.
- **Sound** the rock by tapping: a ringing note is generally sound; a dull "drummy"
  note means a loose slab or separated layer. Bar it down if you can; if it won't come
  down and is large, it needs support or the area is barricaded.
- Wash down first; water reveals cracks and fines that dust hides.
- Re-scale ("check scale") at every re-entry, after blasting nearby, and whenever
  conditions change. Rock that was sound yesterday can loosen overnight.

## 2. Installing support
- US rules: ground conditions that create a hazard to persons are taken down or supported before other work or travel is permitted in the affected area, and until corrected the area is posted with a warning against entry and, if left unattended, barricaded (30 CFR 57.3200, underground metal and nonmetal). In underground coal, roof, face and ribs where persons work or travel must be supported or otherwise controlled, and no person works or travels under unsupported roof except as the rules allow (30 CFR 75.202).
- **Order and distance:** support to the specified distance from the face before
  anyone works forward; don't install the next row from under unsupported back.
- **Friction bolts (split sets):** hole diameter is critical — too large and the bolt
  has little holding power; too small and it won't drive. Check bit gauge.
- **Resin bolts:** use the right resin cartridge length and set times (fast end at the
  back); spin for the specified time, hold, then tension. Cold resin cures slowly —
  check the maker's temperature chart.
- **Cable bolts:** grout to full length; a grout bleed tube that never returns grout
  means voids.
- **Mesh:** overlap sheets per standard and pin them with the bolt plates, tight to the
  rock. Bagging mesh full of loose rock is a load warning, not just a mess.
- **Shotcrete:** spray onto washed, scaled rock at the specified thickness; check with
  depth pins or probe holes. Respect re-entry time under fresh shotcrete — it has little
  strength for hours.
- Record what was installed where; quality-control pull tests on a sample of bolts
  confirm the installation, not the product brochure.

## 3. Reading the ground
Warning signs to report and act on:
- New cracks, especially opening ones, in the back, ribs or shotcrete.
- Spalling, "popping" or slabbing noises — in stressed or deep ground this can precede
  a rockburst.
- Bolt plates deforming, bolt tails pulling in, mesh bagging.
- Water appearing where it was dry, or changed colour/flow.
- Squeezing or floor heave; convergence readings accelerating.
Instruments (extensometers, telltales, convergence pins, seismic systems) show the
trend — read them against previous readings. **A change in rate matters more than
the absolute number.**

## 4. When it goes wrong
If ground is actively moving: withdraw everyone, barricade, report, and let the
geotechnical engineer decide. Do not send someone in to "have a look".

## Gotchas
- Scaling with the bar above head height in a low back sends rock down the bar.
- A bolt that drills easily through broken ground may still be holding nothing.
- Fatigue: long scaling sessions get sloppy; rotate.

## Verify
- [ ] Area sounded and scaled from supported ground before work.
- [ ] Support type, spacing and distance to face match the standard.
- [ ] Bolt and resin installation checked (hole diameter, spin/hold, plate tight).
- [ ] Shotcrete thickness checked and cure time respected.
- [ ] Monitoring readings compared with previous and any acceleration reported.
