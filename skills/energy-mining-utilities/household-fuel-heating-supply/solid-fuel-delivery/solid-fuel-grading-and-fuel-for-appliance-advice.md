# Solid fuel grading and fuel-for-appliance advice

**Goal:** sell and deliver the solid fuel that suits the customer's appliance and local
law, in the weight paid for, so they get heat without smoke, damage or a fine.

## 1. Know the fuels
- **House coal (bituminous):** lights easily, smoky; banned or restricted for sale in many
  places (e.g. England restricted retail sale of traditional house coal from 2021–23).
- **Anthracite:** hard, high-carbon, low smoke, slow to light, very hot; needs an appliance
  designed for it (good underfire air, deep firebed). Sized: grains, beans, peas,
  "singles/doubles/trebles" (names and sizes vary by country/merchant).
- **Manufactured smokeless fuels** (ovoids/briquettes): consistent, low smoke; some are
  for closed appliances only (open fires can't burn them well and they may damage grates).
- **Wood pellets:** for pellet stoves/boilers only — quality class matters (ENplus A1 for
  domestic). Low-grade pellets (high ash, fines) clog burners.
- **Wood/heat logs (compressed sawdust):** dry, dense, for stoves; burn hot — don't overload.

## 2. Match fuel to appliance
Ask: appliance type (open fire, multi-fuel stove, wood-only stove, boiler/range, pellet
boiler), make/model and what the manual approves.
- **Wood-only stoves** often have no grate/ashpan for coal — coal there overheats and
  damages them.
- **Multi-fuel stoves:** smokeless fuels or anthracite per manual.
- **Ranges/boilers with back boilers:** may need a specific size/grade; check.
- **Smoke control areas** (UK and many cities): only authorised fuels or exempt
  appliances may be burned — check the customer's address status; selling unauthorised
  fuel for use there may be an offence.

## 3. Weights and bags
- Common bag weights (UK) 20/25 kg and 50 kg; US/elsewhere often 40 lb/50 lb bags or bulk
  by tonne/ton. Bags should be sealed and weights legally verifiable — a check-weigher on the
  bagging line, with periodic checks recorded.
- Bulk delivery: weighbridge ticket where required by law.
- Label: fuel name, grade, net weight, merchant, any authorisation mark.

## 4. Delivering
- Manual handling: 50 kg bags are heavy — sack trucks, two-person carries or 25 kg bags
  where possible; rest the bag on the shoulder correctly, keep the back straight.
- Coal bunkers: empty bags fully, don't leave bags on paths; check the bunker isn't
  damaged or full of water.
- Pellets in bulk: blown into stores by a tanker — limit blowing pressure per store
  specification, earth/bond, and ensure the boiler is **switched off** before filling
  (blowing into a store with a running auger risks dust and backfire). Pellet stores can
  accumulate **carbon monoxide** — ventilate before entering, never enter a closed pellet
  store alone.
- Count bags off with the customer or leave a delivery note.

## 5. Safety advice worth giving
Sweep chimneys at least annually (twice for coal is often advised), a working CO alarm
in the room, ash bins metal with lids and ashes cold before disposal, store fuel away
from the appliance.

## Gotchas
- Smokeless ≠ authorised everywhere; check the authorisation list for your area.
- Damp pellets swell and jam augers — keep bags dry.
- Anthracite "won't stay lit" calls usually mean an appliance/air problem, not the fuel.

## Verify
- [ ] Fuel suits appliance per manual and the address's smoke-control status.
- [ ] Bag/bulk weights verified and labelled.
- [ ] Safe handling; pellet store filled with boiler off and ventilated.
- [ ] Customer told about chimney sweeping and CO alarm.

*Uncertain:* legal fuel restrictions change; check current national and local rules.
