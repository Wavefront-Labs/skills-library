# Johnstone status transactions

Keith Johnstone, *Impro* (1979), ch. "Status": status is something you **do**, not
something you **are**. Every line and gesture raises or lowers your status
relative to others. Scenes come alive when status shifts.

## Physical and vocal markers (Johnstone's classroom observations)
**High status**: still head when speaking, sustained eye contact (or
looking away calmly), slow and complete movements, taking space, full sentences
without "er", touching your own face rarely, comfortable with silence.
**Low status**: head moves while speaking, brief eye contact then away,
fidgeting, touching face/hair, taking up little space, adding "er"/"sorry",
quick nervous movements, toes turned in.

## Exercises
1. **Status walk**: walk the room at status 1-10 as the teacher calls numbers.
2. **Head still / head moving**: same line, still head vs moving head — feel the
   difference.
3. **Status cards**: actors draw a number (1-10) secretly and play a party scene;
   audience lines them up in order.
4. **Status see-saw**: in a two-person scene, as one goes up the other goes down;
   then swap during the scene.
5. **Master-servant**: the servant must keep the master's status high even
   while mocking him behind his back; the master can demand anything.
6. **Lowering the other / raising the other**: you can play low by raising
   your partner, or high by lowering them.

## Using status in scenes
- Choose a status relationship at the start, then look for the moment it
  **reverses** (the servant learns a secret; the boss is caught).
- Status of space: some characters treat the room as theirs.
- Status can be played against social rank (a low-status king, a
  high-status beggar) for comedy.

## Gotchas
- Equating high status with aggression; the highest status is often calm.
- Static status: no transactions, no scene.
- Playing status at the audience rather than at the partner.

## Verify
- [ ] Observers can rank players' status from behaviour alone.
- [ ] Each scene has at least one status shift.
- [ ] You can raise or lower status with the same line.
