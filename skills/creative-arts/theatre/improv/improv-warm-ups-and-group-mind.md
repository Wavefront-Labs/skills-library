# Improv warm-ups and group mind

**Group mind** (Del Close's term): the ensemble acting as a single attentive
organism — listening, supporting and building on one another.

## Warm-up sequence (10-20 minutes before class/show)
1. **Physical**: shake-out countdown (shake each limb 8-7-6...-1), stretches;
   get breath and blood moving.
2. **Focus/energy circle**:
   - **Zip Zap Zop**: pass "zip", "zap", "zop" across the circle with a clap and
     eye contact; speed up.
   - **Pass the clap**: pass a clap round; then pairs clap simultaneously.
3. **Word association**: one word each round the circle, fast; then pattern
   association (each word connects to the previous two).
4. **Listening**: "Last word, first word" — your sentence starts with your
   partner's last word.
5. **Character/emotion**: walk the room shifting emotion or character on
   call.
6. **Agreement**: "Yes, let's!" — someone proposes an action, all say "yes,
   let's!" and do it enthusiastically.
7. **Team ritual** before going on: a huddle and agreed phrase (e.g. "I've got
   your back").

## Group-mind behaviours in performance
- **Backline**: everyone not in the scene stands at the back, attentive,
  ready to walk on, edit, or provide sound/support.
- **Walk-ons**: brief entries that heighten the game, then exit.
- **Support moves**: becoming objects, environment or sound effects.
- **Editing generously**: end scenes at their peak to help, not to grab focus.
- **Callbacks** show everyone was listening.

## Gotchas
- Warm-ups that are long and exhausting before a show.
- Backline chatting or checking phones.
- Edits that cut scenes off before they find their game.

## Verify
- [ ] Warm-up covers body, focus, listening and agreement.
- [ ] Backline attentive throughout scenes.
- [ ] Callbacks/walk-ons show shared attention.
