# Performing the Harold

Developed by Del Close (with Charna Halpern; described in Halpern, Close and
Johnson's *Truth in Comedy*, 1994) and codified in the UCB manual. Usually
20-30 minutes, 6-8 players.

## Structure
1. **Suggestion** from the audience (a word or idea).
2. **Opening** — the ensemble explores the suggestion to generate themes and
   ideas (see openings below).
3. **First beat**: three separate two-person scenes (1A, 2A, 3A), each with its
   own base reality and game, inspired by the opening.
4. **Group game** (first): an ensemble scene or pattern game.
5. **Second beat**: 1B, 2B, 3B — revisit each scene, heightening its game
   (often time-jumped or with the game applied to new characters).
6. **Group game** (second).
7. **Third beat**: 1C, 2C, 3C — scenes revisit and **connect**: characters or
   games from different scenes collide, themes tie together. Often quicker
   scenes ending on a big unified moment.

## Openings
- **Invocation** (Close): "It is..." → "You are..." → "Thou art..." → "I am...",
  addressing the suggestion as an object, then a god.
- **Monologue opening**: players give true personal stories from the
  suggestion (see Armando).
- **Organic/movement**: the group creates sounds and movements, evolving
  images.
- **Cocktail party**: groups of conversations on the suggestion, focus passes.
- **Pattern game / word association**.
The opening should generate **ideas and themes**, not finished scenes.

## Edits
- **Sweep edit**: a player runs across the front of the stage to end a scene.
- **Tag-out**: a player taps someone out to jump within the same scene's game.
- Edit on a laugh or when the game has been fully played.

## Gotchas
- Opening that's too long or inward; keep it 3-5 minutes and generative.
- Second beat that repeats rather than heightens.
- Forced connections in the third beat; let them arise from shared themes.

## Verify
- [ ] Opening produced at least 3 usable ideas.
- [ ] Each scene thread has A/B/C beats with a clear game.
- [ ] At least one organic connection in the third beat.
