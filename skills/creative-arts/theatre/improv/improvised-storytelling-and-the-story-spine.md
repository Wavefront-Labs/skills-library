# Improvised storytelling and the story spine

## The story spine (Kenn Adams, *How to Improvise a Full-Length Play*)
Fill in the blanks:
1. Once upon a time...
2. Every day...
3. But one day...
4. Because of that...
5. Because of that... (repeat)
6. Until finally...
7. And ever since then...
(Optionally "And the moral of the story is...")
"Every day" = the routine/platform; "But one day" = the tilt; "Because of
that" = causal chain; "Until finally" = climax; "Ever since" = new routine.

## Johnstone's narrative tools (*Impro*, *Impro for Storytellers*)
- **Platform**: the stable routine/relationship.
- **Tilt**: something that disturbs the platform and changes the
  relationship.
- **Reincorporation**: bring back earlier elements (objects, characters,
  lines) later — audiences feel the story is "planned".
- **Circles of expectation**: the audience expects certain things to follow
  from a set-up; satisfy some, surprise within others.
- **Avoid cancelling**: e.g. a dragon appears and then "it was only a dream" —
  cancels the story.
- **Avoid wandering**: introducing new elements instead of developing existing
  ones.
- **Spontaneity / overcoming blocking**: Johnstone noted most "blocks" come
  from the improviser's fear; drills like "Yes, let's!" and "word-at-a-time"
  train acceptance. **Overaccepting** — accepting everything with
  delight — is the antidote.

## Drills
- **Story spine round**: group says one spine line each, in order.
- **Word-at-a-time story**: pairs speak one word each, building sentences.
- **"What comes next?"**: storyteller acts out a story, asking the audience/
  partner "what comes next?"; they say "yes" or "no".
- **Reincorporation challenge**: tell a story; at the end, every introduced item
  must come back.

## Gotchas
- Too many "because of that" steps without escalation.
- Introducing new characters late.
- Ending without "ever since then" — the change must stick.

## Verify
- [ ] Story has clear platform, tilt, causal chain, climax, new normal.
- [ ] At least two early elements reincorporated.
- [ ] No cancelled events.
