# Coaching an improv team

## Practice plan (2-3 hour session)
1. **Warm-up** (15 min) — see
   `creative-arts.theatre.improv.improv-warm-ups-and-group-mind`.
2. **Focus exercise** (30 min) targeting one skill: initiations, listening,
   game finding, edits, character.
3. **Scenes with notes** (60 min) — two-person scenes, short, with immediate
   notes.
4. **Form run** (30-45 min) — run the team's form (Harold, Armando) as in a show.
5. **Notes and goals** (10 min).

## Spolin theatre games
Viola Spolin (*Improvisation for the Theater*, 1963) built games with a **focus**
(point of concentration), and coached via **side-coaching** during play.
- Examples: **Mirror** (follow the leader until there's no leader), **Where
  game** (players build a location with objects, entering one at a time),
  **Gibberish** (communicate without real words), **Give and take** (focus passes
  between groups).
- Side-coaching phrases are short and in the moment: "Share your voice!",
  "See the space!", "Follow the follower!", "Give it over!"
- Evaluation questions after: "Did they keep the focus?" — not "was it
  funny?".

## Giving scene notes
- Name what worked first, then one key note (two at most).
- Notes about the principle, not the bit: "You asked three questions; make a
  statement" rather than "the dentist line wasn't funny".
- Rerun the start of the scene with the note to lock it in.

## Diagnosing team problems
| Symptom | Likely cause | Practice |
|---|---|---|
| Scenes wander | No game identified | First-unusual-thing drills |
| Slow starts | Weak initiations | Three-line scenes |
| Chaotic group games | No pattern established | Pattern circles |
| Long scenes | Fear of editing | Edit-on-laugh drills |
| Same players dominate | Low group mind | Give-and-take games |

## Gotchas
- Too many notes at once.
- Coaching your own preferences as rules.
- Never running the full form before a show.

## Verify
- [ ] Each practice targets one named skill.
- [ ] Notes limited and principle-based.
- [ ] Full form run before each show.
