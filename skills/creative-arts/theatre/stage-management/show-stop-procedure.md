# Show-stop procedure

Agree the procedure with the venue (technical manager, duty/house manager) before
the first performance and keep the text in the prompt book.

## Reasons to stop
- Injury or medical emergency onstage, in the wings or in the audience that
  can't be handled discreetly.
- Technical failure making continuation unsafe (automation, flying, power,
  fire curtain).
- Fire alarm/evacuation (follow venue fire procedure).
- Major disruption in the auditorium.
Who decides: usually the **DSM/SM** calling, in consultation with the duty manager
for front-of-house issues. Anyone may call a stop for imminent danger.

## Stop sequence (typical)
1. On headset: "**Stand by for show stop**" (if time) then "**Show stop, show
   stop**" — clear and calm.
2. LX: hold current state or go to a pre-programmed **show-stop state** (stage
   working light/house lights to half or full).
3. Sound: fade music/effects; prepare the announcement mic.
4. Stop automation and flying immediately.
5. **Announcement** from the SM desk (pre-written): "Ladies and gentlemen, due to
   [technical reasons / a medical situation], we have had to pause the
   performance. Please remain in your seats; we will update you shortly."
6. Actors leave the stage or hold position as agreed; crew attends to the issue.
7. Inform front of house via comms/radio; duty manager manages audience.

## Restart
- Decide a **restart point** (usually the start of the scene or a clean line
  before the stop) and tell cast and operators.
- Reset the cue states (LX/sound jump to the right cues; automation reset).
- Announcement: "We will now resume the performance from..."
- House lights out, standby, go.

## Evacuation
If the fire alarm/evacuation is called, follow the venue's emergency plan:
announcement script, house lights up, doors open, staff to exits, cast and
crew to their muster point. Don't restart until the fire service/duty manager clears.

## After
- **Incident report** and show report entries: time, cause, actions, restart
  time, injuries.
- Debrief with the technical team.

## Gotchas
- No pre-written announcement — improvised text panics audiences.
- No LX show-stop state programmed.
- Forgetting to stop automation/flying.

## Verify
- [ ] Show-stop text and procedure in the book.
- [ ] LX show-stop state and announcement mic tested.
- [ ] Restart method agreed with all departments.
