# Smoke and haze on stage

## Types
- **Haze** (thin, long-lasting, reveals light beams): oil-based or water/glycol-
  based fluids; runs through the show at low output.
- **Smoke/fog** (thick, short bursts): glycol-based fluid heated.
- **Low fog/low smoke**: cooled fog that hugs the floor (chilled glycol machines,
  dry ice, liquid CO2 or LN2 systems).

## Safety
1. **Use theatrical fluids from the machine's manufacturer** only; follow the
   safety data sheet.
2. **Exposure**: ESTA/ANSI E1.5 (theatrical fog fluid) and guidance on exposure
   limits for glycols and mineral oil — keep concentrations modest; ventilate
   between shows; asthma and voice concerns for performers (consult them).
3. **Dry ice / CO2 / LN2**: asphyxiation risk in pits, traps and low areas
   (gas sinks); cold burns; ventilation and monitoring; gloves for handling.
4. **Slip hazard**: oil-based haze and glycol residue on floors; clean floors.
5. **Visibility**: dense smoke can obscure exit signs and edges; never fill
   escape routes; ensure performers can see edges/marks.

## Fire detection
- Haze/smoke triggers detectors. Only the **venue** isolates or changes detector
  modes, following its fire risk assessment (often switching to heat detection
  zones under supervision with a fire watch). Never cover or disable detectors
  yourself.
- Re-enable at the end of the show; record in the log.

## Operation
- Machines placed where noise doesn't intrude; DMX-controlled from the LX desk
  for repeatable levels; pre-show haze timing agreed (start ~30 min before).
- Cue sheets: output %, fan speed, duration.
- Audience notice ("This production uses haze and smoke effects") at FOH and
  on the website.

## Gotchas
- Different fluid in a new venue changing density and residue.
- Air conditioning clearing haze mid-show; coordinate with venue.
- Haze drifting into FOH and setting off detectors there.

## Verify
- [ ] Fluids, SDS and exposure considerations documented in risk assessment.
- [ ] Venue detector procedure agreed in writing.
- [ ] Audience notice displayed.
