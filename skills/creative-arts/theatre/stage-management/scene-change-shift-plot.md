# Scene-change shift plot

The **shift plot** (UK also "running plot" or "scene change plot") is the master
list of who does what in every change. Each crew member and ASM gets a **track**
derived from it.

## Shift plot format (per change)
| Change | Cue / trigger | Item | From | To | Who | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1→2 | LX 34 (blackout) | Armchair | DSL spike 4 | SL wings | ASM 1 | Glow tape on spike |
| 1→2 | LX 34 | Rug | C | Fly bar 12 | Fly op | Cue from DSM "Fly 3 GO" |
| 1→2 | LX 34 | Table | SR wings | C spike 7 | Crew 2 + 3 | Lift together on "lift" |
Plus estimated time for the change and lighting state (blackout/transition/
seen change).

## Procedure
1. From the ground plans and the designer's storyboard, list every item for each
   scene (preset vs moved).
2. Assign movers to balance load and routes (no crossing paths in the dark;
   heavy items by two people).
3. Write **individual tracks**: for each person, a chronological list of all
   their actions in the show (props handoffs, costume help, shifts, cues on
   cue lights).
4. Rehearse changes in tech: walk them in working light, then in show light,
   then with sound/timing. Time each and record.
5. **Spike marks**: coloured tape (one colour per scene) at furniture corners;
   **glow tape** on spikes and edges for blackouts; keep spike numbers in the
   plot.
6. Update after every tech session; issue revised tracks.

## Running
- Crew on headset or cue lights for GO.
- After the change, each person confirms clear ("SL clear") if the DSM requires.
- Note problems in the show report.

## Gotchas
- Two people assigned to the same item, or nobody.
- Spike tape colours reused across scenes.
- Changes timed only in working light — slower in blackout.

## Verify
- [ ] Every moved item appears once per change with a named owner.
- [ ] Individual tracks issued and current.
- [ ] Each change timed in show conditions.
