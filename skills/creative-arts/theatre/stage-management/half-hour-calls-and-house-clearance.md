# Half-hour calls and house clearance

## UK convention
- **"The half"** is called **35 minutes before curtain up** — i.e. 30 minutes
  before the "beginners" call, which is 5 minutes before the start.
- Calls over the show relay/tannoy: "Ladies and gentlemen of the *Company*,
  this is your half-hour call. Half an hour, please." Then **quarter** (20 min
  before curtain), **five** (10 min before), **beginners** (5 min before:
  "Act One beginners, please: Mr X, Ms Y... to the stage").
- Actors must be **signed in** by the half (sign-in sheet at stage door); the
  SM chases anyone missing at the half and alerts understudies.

## US convention
- **Half hour** is 30 minutes before curtain, then "15 minutes", "5 minutes",
  "places". AEA rules require actors to sign in by half hour.

## Clearance with front of house
1. Before opening the house: SM confirms stage is ready (preset checked, safety
   checks done, stage clear) and gives **house clearance** to the duty/house
   manager: "You have clearance to open the house."
2. Near the start: the **duty manager gives the SM front-of-house clearance**
   when the audience is seated (often a few minutes late for latecomers).
3. SM decides to start ("House to half... house out... go").
4. Late seating policy agreed (latecomers held until a suitable moment).

## Interval
- Interval calls: interval length announced, then calls "Act Two beginners" at
  agreed time; FOH bells/announcements to return audience (e.g. 5, 3, 1 minutes
  in UK).
- Interval reset of stage and props per the reset list.
- FOH clearance again before starting Act Two.

## End of show
- Curtain call cues; house lights; "Thank you everyone" call; post-show
  security check (props, weapons locked, practicals off).
- Show report written and sent.

## Gotchas
- Calling the half late; time-of-calls must be consistent.
- Starting before FOH clearance — audience still entering.
- Not knowing who's missing until beginners.

## Verify
- [ ] Calls given at the agreed times each show.
- [ ] Sign-in checked at the half; absences actioned.
- [ ] Clearance exchanged with FOH before opening house and before start.
