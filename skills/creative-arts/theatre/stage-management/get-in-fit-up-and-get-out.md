# Get-in, fit-up and get-out

UK "get-in/fit-up/get-out" = US "load-in/hang/strike/load-out". Usually led by the
production manager or touring technical manager with the SM team.

## Get-in and fit-up order of work (typical)
1. **Rigging and overhead first**: LX bars, flown scenery, speakers — while the
   stage floor is clear. Hard hats in the zone below if required by the venue.
2. **Floor**: dance floor/stage floor laid, taped.
3. **Set** built from upstage to downstage (so access stays clear).
4. **Masking** hung and trimmed.
5. **LX focus** (needs a dark, quiet stage), **sound** system check and tuning.
6. Props, wardrobe, dressing rooms set up in parallel.
7. Technical checks (automation, flying, effects), then re-tech for touring.

## Truck pack
- The truck is loaded **in reverse order** of the get-in at the next venue:
  first needed = last loaded. Keep a truck-pack diagram and photo each layer.
- Heavy items low and forward; secure with straps; label cases by department
  and destination (SL, SR, wardrobe).

## Safety during work
- Work-at-height controls (scaffold towers, MEWPs, harnesses where needed).
- Flying system locked off when loading/unloading bars (weights balanced).
- Clear walkways; working lights on; no performers on stage during fit-up.
- Venue rules: induction, PPE (safety boots), hours.

## Get-out (strike)
1. Show ends → safety announcement; crew briefed.
2. Personal and fragile items (props, costumes) packed first by SM/wardrobe.
3. Floor-level set struck, then masking, LX, flown items, finally floor.
4. Truck packed per diagram.
5. Venue left **as found**: house rig restored to their plan (or as agreed),
   tape removed, rubbish cleared, sign-off with the venue technical manager.

## Gotchas
- Fly bars unloaded without counterweights removed — runaway bar danger.
- Get-outs rushed at night with fatigue — schedule enough crew.
- Missing items discovered at the next venue; checklists per case.

## Verify
- [ ] Schedule with order of work and crew calls.
- [ ] Truck-pack diagram and case labels.
- [ ] Venue sign-off at get-out.
