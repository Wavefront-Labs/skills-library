# Naked flame and pyrotechnics on stage

## Prefer alternatives
LED candles, flicker lamps, video fire, fabric/air "flame" effects, and sound
effects cover most needs with no fire risk. Use real flame only when it truly
matters.

## Approvals
- **UK**: the venue's premises licence often requires notifying or seeking
  consent from the licensing authority/fire authority for naked flame and pyro;
  follow ABTT guidance. Storage and use of pyrotechnic articles is regulated
  (Explosives Regulations 2014, Pyrotechnic Articles (Safety) Regulations).
- **US**: NFPA 160 (flame effects before an audience) and NFPA 1126
  (pyrotechnics before a proximate audience); local fire marshal permits; licensed
  pyrotechnic operators.
- Apply early (weeks), with a risk assessment and demonstration if asked.

## Naked flame controls (candles, lighters, cigarettes, lanterns)
- Flame-retardant treatment of costumes, scenery and drapes near flame (see
  `creative-arts.theatre.scenic-design.flame-retardancy-compliance`).
- **Fire watch**: a trained crew member in the wings with a suitable
  extinguisher (CO2 or water mist/water by type; fire blanket) whenever a flame
  is onstage; line of sight to the flame.
- Candles in stable, heavy holders; short wicks; never left unattended;
  extinguished and checked offstage by the ASM; snuffers.
- Matches/lighters signed out and in; spent matches into a sand bucket.
- Keep flames away from hair (lacquer), wigs, chiffon, paper props.

## Pyrotechnics
- Only competent, trained operators (UK: trained/qualified pyro operator; US:
  licensed). Devices from reputable theatrical suppliers, stored in approved
  boxes, logged.
- Firing system with key switch; operator has **clear sight** of the effect area
  before firing; safety distances per manufacturer.
- Pre-show checks and test-fire procedures; misfire procedure (wait time before
  approach).
- Audience notice of loud bangs/flashes; smoke detectors managed by the venue
  (never disabled without the venue's fire procedure).

## Records
Risk assessment, approvals, operator competence, device inventory, show-by-show
firing log.

## Gotchas
- Unapproved candles introduced in previews.
- Wrong extinguisher type for electrical/liquid fires.
- Haze plus flame triggering fire alarms.

## Verify
- [ ] Approvals in writing before first use.
- [ ] Fire watch and extinguisher at every flame moment.
- [ ] Pyro operator competent; firing log kept.
