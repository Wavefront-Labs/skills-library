# Venue advance and tech rider

## The tour pack / tech rider (what you send)
- Show overview: running time, interval, company size, contact list.
- **Stage requirements**: minimum playing area (W x D), height, wing space,
  masking, floor (sprung? dance floor laid by company?), rake, traps.
- Set: dimensions of largest pieces, flown items (weights, bars needed),
  ground plan and section in CAD/PDF.
- **Lighting**: plan and channel list, what the company brings vs venue rig,
  dimmer/DMX needs, console, haze (fluid type).
- **Sound**: system requirements, playback, radio mics, comms.
- Power requirements (e.g. 63A three-phase for touring rig).
- **Get-in schedule** and **crew calls** needed (numbers per session, skills —
  flys, LX, sound).
- Dressing rooms, wardrobe facilities (washers/dryers, steamers).
- Special effects: pyro, naked flame, smoke — needs local authority/fire approvals.

## The advance (what you check, 4-8 weeks before)
1. Get the venue **technical specification** and plans; compare against your rider
   point by point. Flag mismatches (smaller stage → set adjustments; no flying →
   alternative).
2. Agree **schedule**: get-in time, fit-up, focus, tech/re-tech, half, show,
   get-out; load-in access (dock height, door size, parking).
3. Confirm **crew** numbers and hours, and who pays overtime.
4. Front-of-house: show start time, running time, interval bars, access
   performances.
5. Accommodation and travel for the company; digs lists.

## Company welfare (company manager)
- **Per diems** / touring allowances under the applicable agreement (UK
  Equity/UK Theatre touring allowance; US AEA per diem) — check current rates.
- Travel days, rest days, sickness cover, wellbeing contact.
- Local information pack: doctor, pharmacy, laundry, stage door details.

## Gotchas
- Assuming a venue's spec is current — call the technical manager.
- Forgetting the get-out time affects the next venue's get-in.
- Noise curfews limiting get-in hours.

## Verify
- [ ] Rider vs spec comparison done with all mismatches resolved.
- [ ] Schedule and crew calls confirmed in writing.
- [ ] Accommodation, travel and per diems arranged.
