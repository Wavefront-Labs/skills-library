# Consumable and food props

Consumables are props used up each performance: food, drink, letters torn,
plates smashed, blood, cigarettes, paper. They need a per-show budget and a daily
prep routine.

## Food and drink
1. **Ask each actor** about allergies, intolerances, dietary needs (vegan, halal,
   kosher), and preferences — confidentially.
2. Choose foods that are **easy to eat and speak through**: soft, small,
   non-crumbly, not sticky (banana, soft bread, sponge cake, tinned peaches,
   jelly). Avoid nuts entirely if anyone in the company has a nut allergy.
3. **Hygiene**: prepare in a clean area, with hand washing and clean utensils;
   store chilled where needed; label with prep date; discard leftovers after the
   show. Follow food hygiene rules (UK: Food Standards Agency guidance).
4. **Alcohol substitutes**: water with food colouring (white wine), diluted
   cola or tea (whisky/brandy), flat ginger ale or apple juice (champagne/beer),
   non-alcoholic beer, blackcurrant cordial (red wine). Sugar-free options for
   repeated swallowing.
5. Portion exactly the amount needed; pre-cut and plate on the props table.

## Smoking
- Real tobacco smoking on stage is banned in many places (e.g. England's smoke-
  free legislation applies in workplaces/enclosed public places); use **herbal
  cigarettes** where allowed, **e-cigarettes/vapour props**, or unlit
  cigarettes. Check venue and fire officer rules.

## Breakables and paper
- **Breakaway** glass/sugar glass or resin; plates scored or plaster-cast to
  shatter safely; clean-up plan in the next scene change.
- Letters/newspapers: printed per show (keep a master file); torn items
  replaced nightly.

## Blood
Washable stage blood (products suited to costume fabrics); blood bags/
capsules; wardrobe agrees products and laundry.

## Budget and stock
Calculate cost per show x number of performances + previews + spares; keep a
stock list; reorder before running out.

## Gotchas
- Dry foods (crackers) making actors cough.
- Hidden allergens in "safe" foods (check labels every purchase).
- Running out of a consumable on a Saturday matinee.

## Verify
- [ ] Allergy and dietary info collected and respected.
- [ ] Hygiene and disposal routine written.
- [ ] Per-show consumables budget and stock list.
