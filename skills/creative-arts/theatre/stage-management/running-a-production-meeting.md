# Running a production meeting

Production meetings bring together director, designers, production manager (PM),
stage management, heads of department and sometimes marketing/FOH. Usually weekly
during rehearsals, chaired by the PM (sometimes the SM), minuted by the SM team.

## Before
- Fix a regular slot (e.g. same weekday lunchtime, 60 minutes), in or near the
  rehearsal room; video link for remote designers.
- Circulate an **agenda** 24 hours ahead: open action items from last minutes,
  department items, schedule (tech, fit-up, previews), budget, H&S.

## Agenda (typical order)
1. Apologies and minutes of last meeting (quick check of actions).
2. Director's update (rehearsal progress, changes).
3. Department by department: Set, Props, Costume, Wigs/Make-up, Lighting, Sound,
   Video, Automation/Flying, Stage Management.
4. Production schedule: fit-up, tech, dress, previews.
5. Budget (PM).
6. Health and safety/risk assessments.
7. Marketing/press/FOH (photo calls, programme deadlines).
8. AOB; date of next meeting.

## Minutes
- Record **decisions** and **actions** (what, who, by when), not full
  discussion.
- Action log format: `# | Item | Owner | Due | Status`.
- Circulate within 24 hours; carry open actions to the next agenda.

## Chairing tips
- Keep to time; take detailed technical problem-solving offline ("PM and LX to
  meet after").
- Make sure quieter departments are asked.
- Surface conflicts early (e.g. costume quick change vs set change timing).

## Gotchas
- Meetings becoming design debates — agree the decision path.
- Minutes that don't name owners/deadlines.
- Not inviting venue staff for get-in planning.

## Verify
- [ ] Agenda circulated 24h before.
- [ ] Minutes with action log sent within 24h.
- [ ] Open actions tracked to closure.
