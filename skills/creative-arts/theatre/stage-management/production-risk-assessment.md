# Production risk assessment and incident reporting

## Risk assessment
HSE five steps: (1) identify hazards, (2) decide who might be harmed and how,
(3) evaluate risks and decide on controls, (4) record findings and implement,
(5) review and update.

**Areas to cover**:
- Set: heights (platforms, stairs, edges), handrails, traps, revolves,
  automation, rakes, scene changes in the dark.
- Flying: loads, hanging points, crossing below flown pieces.
- Electrical: practicals on stage, water near electrics, cable runs.
- Effects: haze, smoke, flame, pyro, water, strobe (photosensitive warning).
- Performance: fights, falls, lifts, intimacy, quick changes, child performers.
- Costume: footwear, visibility in masks/wigs, trailing fabric.
- Environment: temperature, noise exposure, working hours, lone working.
- Audience: thrust/immersive proximity, effects reaching seats, aisle use.

**Format**: Hazard | Who | Risk before controls | Controls | Residual risk |
Owner | Review date. Rating = likelihood x severity (e.g. 1-5 each).
Specialist sections (fights, pyro) attached from the relevant experts. The
production manager typically owns the overall RA; the SM contributes and
maintains it in the book.

## Briefing
Share relevant parts with the company: a safety briefing on day one and on
moving into the theatre (stage walk, hazards, emergency procedure).

## First aid and incidents
- Know the trained first aiders and location of kits/AED for each venue.
- **Report every accident and near miss**: accident book/incident form (who,
  what, where, when, injury, action taken, witnesses).
- UK **RIDDOR**: certain injuries (e.g. fractures, injuries causing over 7 days
  incapacity), dangerous occurrences and diseases must be reported to the HSE
  by the responsible person; check thresholds.
- Review the RA after any incident.

## Gotchas
- RA written once and never updated after rehearsal changes.
- Near misses not reported — they predict accidents.
- Generic copy-paste RAs not matching the actual show.

## Verify
- [ ] RA covers all listed areas and is signed off by the PM.
- [ ] Company safety briefing given in rehearsal room and on stage.
- [ ] Incident forms and first aid information in the book.
