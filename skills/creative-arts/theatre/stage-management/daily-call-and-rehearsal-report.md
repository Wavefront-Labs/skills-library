# Daily call and rehearsal report

## The daily call (rehearsal call)
Issued every day for the next day, typically by early evening (agree a deadline
with the director, e.g. by 6pm).
**Contents**: date, venue/room, times per session, scenes/pages, who's called
when, specialist calls (fight, music, dialect, costume fittings), notes ("bring
rehearsal shoes").
- Build from the director's plan and the character-scene chart.
- Include costume fittings and press calls from other departments.
- Post on the callboard, email/group message to all.

## Breaks and hours
- Track start, breaks and end times against the applicable agreement (e.g. UK
  Equity/UK Theatre or ITC; US AEA contract): maximum daily/weekly hours, break
  frequency (commonly a short break every 80-90 minutes or a 5-minute break
  per hour, depending on contract), lunch length, rest periods between
  days, overtime triggers. Check the current agreement — rules differ by contract.
- Call breaks on time; log them in the report.

## The rehearsal report
Sent at the end of each rehearsal day to the creative team, production manager and
heads of department.
**Sections**:
1. Header: production, date, report number, times (start, breaks, finish),
   who was called, absences/lateness.
2. What was rehearsed.
3. **Department notes**: Set, Props, Costume, Wigs/Make-up, Lighting, Sound,
   Video, Production/Management, Front of House, Marketing — "No notes, thank
   you" where empty so everyone knows they were considered.
4. Health and safety notes, injuries.
5. Schedule for the next day (summary).
- Notes are specific: "Props: Hamlet needs a skull that can be held in one hand
  and dropped; please advise options".
- Neutral language; never criticise people in reports.

## Gotchas
- Late calls — actors can't plan.
- Unanswered notes: follow up next day and in production meetings.
- Recording private matters (health, personal) in widely circulated reports.

## Verify
- [ ] Call issued by agreed time every day.
- [ ] Breaks logged and within agreement limits.
- [ ] Report sent daily with every department listed.
