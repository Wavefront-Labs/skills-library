# Cue-to-cue, dress rehearsals and previews

## Technical rehearsal (tech)
- **Who runs it**: the stage manager runs the room; the director sets
  priorities. Lighting/sound/video designers work at production desks in the
  stalls.
- **Cue-to-cue**: rehearse only the moments with cues, skipping dialogue between
  ("jump to the line before LX 45"). The SM calls "hold" to stop; resume from
  an agreed line.
- Director's job in tech:
  1. Protect safety time (fights, flying, automation, quick changes) first.
  2. Make decisions quickly; give designers time to fix, not watch.
  3. Keep actors informed (they'll stand around a lot); release them when
     possible.
  4. Note acting issues but save most for later.
- Plan the tech schedule with the PM/SM: e.g. 2-3 days for a mid-scale play;
  musicals and large shows a week or more.

## Dress rehearsals
- Full runs with costume, make-up and all cues, without stopping unless
  unsafe.
- Notes session after each: creative team first (tech fixes), then company.
- Invited dress (friends/staff) tests audience response.

## Previews
- Paid performances before press night. Changes happen during the day; the show
  that evening must be safe and rehearsed.
- Each day: notes from last night, afternoon rehearsal (fix specific
  moments, re-tech changed cues), show.
- Watch audience behaviour (laughs, restlessness, confusion) — not your own
  wishes.
- Cuts and changes need SM book updates and cue rewrites; deadline them.

## Freezing and press night
- **Freeze** the show (typically one or two performances before press): no more
  changes. Further rehearsal maintains, not changes.
- Press night: brief, calm notes; thank the company and crew.
- After opening: the SM/resident director maintains; you return for brush-up
  notes per contract.

## Gotchas
- Re-directing scenes in tech — wastes everyone's paid time.
- Changing too much in the last previews: actors need runs of the final version.
- Not freezing: press night becomes a first run.

## Verify
- [ ] Tech priorities agreed with SM before tech.
- [ ] Every change in previews is rehearsed before that night's show.
- [ ] Freeze date announced and kept.
