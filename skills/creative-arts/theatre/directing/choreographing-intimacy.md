# Choreographing intimacy

Intimate moments are choreographed and repeatable, like a fight. Nothing is
improvised in performance.

## Procedure
1. **Story first**: what does the moment say about the relationship (first
   kiss, power, comfort, violence)? Write it as beats.
2. **Boundaries in place** (see
   `creative-arts.theatre.directing.consent-and-boundary-practice-for-intimacy`).
3. **Build with technical language**, not emotional: "your right hand to her
   left shoulder blade, count 1-2-3, slow", "lips closed, cheek-to-cheek,
   hold 4". Vocabulary includes:
   - **Levels of intensity** (e.g. 1-10, agreed meaning).
   - **Tempo** and **counts**; **breath** cues to show arousal without
     contact.
   - **Placement** of hands and contact points; **stage kiss** types (closed lips,
     off-centre, masked by angle).
   - **Sightline tricks**: angle so the audience infers more than happens;
     a thumb masking lips.
4. **Start slow**, mark at half intensity, build to performance level only once
   the sequence is secure.
5. **Notate**: the intimacy director and SM write the sequence in the prompt
   book (beats, counts, contact points). This is the record for maintenance.
6. **Fix it** — no changes without the intimacy director and re-consent.

## Simulated sex (where in scope)
- Barriers and modesty garments always.
- Rhythmic movement choreographed to counts or music; contact points limited to
  agreed zones.
- Short duration often reads more strongly than long.

## In the run
- **Intimacy call** before each performance (like a fight call): walk the
  sequence at half speed with a check-in.
- An **intimacy captain** (cast member) or SM leads calls and reports drift.
- Cover/understudy put-ins: full process from boundaries up.

## Gotchas
- Actors "making it more real" over a run → drift; SM notes and resets.
- Kissing with tongues or open mouths without explicit agreement.
- Lighting changes revealing what angles were hiding — check in tech.

## Verify
- [ ] Every intimate moment has beats, counts and contact points written down.
- [ ] Sequence repeatable at performance level without improvisation.
- [ ] Intimacy calls scheduled for every show.
