# Staging scene transitions and ensemble moments

Long blackout scene changes kill momentum. Treat each transition as a scene with
its own story beat.

## Transitions procedure
1. **List every transition** with what changes: furniture, props, costume,
   actors on/off, location/time jump. The SM's shift plot and the designer's
   storyboard are the source.
2. **Set a target time** for each (often 10-30 seconds); add them up — a
   play with 20 transitions at 45 s loses 15 minutes.
3. **Decide the style**: in-character (actors move furniture as part of the
   world), stylised (choreographed ensemble), crew in blacks under low light,
   automation, or a flown/revolve change.
4. **Assign tasks** — who carries what, from where to where, in what order.
   Coordinate with the SM: the shift plot must say exactly who sets each item.
5. **Choreograph** to sound: the sound designer can underscore with a fixed
   length; set moves to musical counts.
6. **Light**: agree with the LD whether the change is seen ("transition state")
   or covered (blackout, followed by restore). Blackouts need glow tape and are
   slower.
7. **Overlap** where possible: the next scene's first line starts while the
   last pieces settle.
8. **Rehearse transitions separately** in tech, then in sequence; time each.

## Crowd and ensemble scenes
- Give every ensemble member a character, objective and track ("you're the
  baker's apprentice, you want to see the hanging, you push forward on cue").
- Break the crowd into small groups with leaders; stage group by group.
- Control focus: freeze or reduce the crowd's activity when principals speak.
- Build reaction moments (gasps, cheers) on clear cues.

## Gotchas
- Actors carrying heavy or awkward pieces in the dark — safety first: glow
  tape, clear routes, training from the SM.
- Transitions designed but not rehearsed until tech — add them to the rehearsal
  schedule.
- Crowd members all reacting the same way at the same time — vary.

## Verify
- [ ] Transition list with target times and owners for every item.
- [ ] Each transition timed in tech and within target.
- [ ] Ensemble members can state their character and track.
