# Running auditions and callbacks

## Room and schedule
- Slots: 10-15 min first round (20-30 min for callbacks). Build a 15 min buffer
  every hour; lunch.
- Room: actor's space with clear floor, a chair, water; panel behind a table
  with good light on the actor; a **reader** (not the director) for scenes.
- Outside: a greeter with sign-in, sides available ahead of time, content
  warnings already in the breakdown.
- Panel: director, casting director, MD/choreographer for musicals; one person
  leads talking.

## In the audition (first round)
1. Greet warmly, introduce the panel, ask a quick human question.
2. Let them do the prepared piece without interruption.
3. **Redirect** at least once: change an objective or circumstance ("this time
   she already knows"). You're testing collaboration, not just talent.
4. Thank them; tell them when they'll hear.
5. Write notes immediately, in a consistent format: role, strengths, range,
   redirect response, availability, a 1-5 score. Never write anything you
   wouldn't want read.

## Callbacks and chemistry reads
- Pair candidates for key relationships (lovers, siblings, rivals) and swap
  pairings. Look for listening, contrast and believable history.
- Check physical pairings (height for lifts, fights), vocal blend in musicals.
- For roles with intimacy or violence, describe the content and how it will be
  staged (with intimacy/fight director); do not ask for it in the audition.

## Decisions
- Cast for the ensemble, not individual best performances.
- Keep a first choice and a second for each role; clear offers in order.
- Offers go via agents (with pay, dates, billing); give a deadline to respond.
- **Everyone who auditioned in person hears back**, ideally with a short
  personal note for callbacks.

## Gotchas
- Running late all day — protects nobody; keep time.
- Asking about age, pregnancy, health or nationality — discriminatory and in many
  jurisdictions unlawful.
- Casting from the most impressive monologue rather than the redirect.

## Verify
- [ ] Schedule with buffers, reader booked, sides sent in advance.
- [ ] Notes recorded for every candidate.
- [ ] Chemistry reads done for key pairs.
- [ ] All candidates informed of outcome.
