# Directing children and young performers

## Legal and safeguarding (check your jurisdiction)
- **UK**: children up to the end of compulsory school age (16) need a **child
  performance licence** from their local authority for most professional
  performances (Children and Young Persons Act 1963; Children (Performances and
  Activities) (England) Regulations 2014). Rules cap hours, performances per
  week, breaks and latest finish times; licensed **chaperones** (one per up to
  12 children) are required.
- **US**: rules vary by state (e.g. New York and California child-performer
  permits, Coogan accounts in some states).
- Productions rotate **teams** of children (often 2-4 teams) to stay within
  limits.
- Safeguarding policy: DBS/background checks for adults, no adult alone with a
  child, separate dressing rooms, a designated safeguarding lead, photos only
  with consent.

## Rehearsal craft
1. **Short sessions** with clear goals; children's focus fades fast. Plan
   breaks and snack times.
2. **Games-based warm-ups** (focus, listening, energy) to start every session.
3. **Concrete instructions**: "walk to the tree, look at the house, count three,
   then say your line" beats "feel scared".
4. **Imaginative circumstances** framed for age: "you've just seen a dragon in
   the garden."
5. **Side-coaching** during runs ("louder, look at mum") works better than long
   notes after.
6. **Praise specifically**, correct briefly.
7. **Repetition with variety** — keep runs fresh by giving a new focus each time.
8. Teach basic stage craft explicitly: open up, project, hold for laughs,
   what to do if something goes wrong.

## Working with chaperones and parents
- Chaperones manage the children offstage; the SM coordinates calls with them.
- Parents usually not in the rehearsal room.
- Communicate schedules early; teams need equal rehearsal.

## Gotchas
- Over-rehearsing to exhaustion; outside the licence hours is unlawful.
- Giving adult psychological notes.
- One team rehearsed much more than the other.

## Verify
- [ ] Licences/permits approved before first rehearsal.
- [ ] Chaperone ratios met at all times.
- [ ] Rehearsal plan respects hour limits per team.
