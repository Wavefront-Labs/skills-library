# Staging for thrust and in-the-round

In thrust (audience on three sides) and in-the-round (four sides / circular)
someone always sees an actor's back. The aim is that **no section is shut out for
long**, not that everyone sees every face all the time.

## Orientation
- Label positions as a **clock face** (in the round) — 12 o'clock at the
  control box or a fixed entrance — or as compass points. Thrust: DSC is the
  tip, use "corners" and "vom" for vomitory entrances.
- Tape out the full playing area and seating edges in the rehearsal room; place
  chairs to represent each section.

## Principles
1. **Diagonals over straight lines.** Two actors talking on a diagonal across the
   space are visible to most sections. Avoid lining up on a straight line that
   one section looks straight down.
2. **Keep it moving** — rotate focus with motivated moves every so often; long
   static two-handers in the centre exclude two sections.
3. **Use the edges and corners** — an actor at the edge facing in gives their face
   to the opposite side and others see the listener.
4. **The listener matters**: arrange so a section that can't see the speaker
   sees the listener's reaction.
5. **Open up big moments** to the corners in thrust; in the round place key
   speeches near an aisle or vom with the actor turning slowly.
6. **Furniture low** (no high backs); place upstage-equivalent pieces at the
   theatre's least important sightline (often vom areas).
7. **Entrances via voms** are powerful but can be slow; time them.

## Traverse (audience on two sides)
Actors work along the long axis; diagonals and the ends give focus; avoid
facing one bank for long.

## Checking
Sit in each section during runs (or have the AD/SM do so) and note "dead"
stretches with timings.

## Gotchas
- Directing from one seat (usually the "centre") — the classic mistake.
- Lighting that only works from front; involve the LD early.
- Props tables and quick-change spots in voms blocking entrances.

## Verify
- [ ] Rehearsal room shows all audience sections.
- [ ] Each section notes no dead stretch longer than about a minute at key scenes.
- [ ] Major moments open to multiple sections.
