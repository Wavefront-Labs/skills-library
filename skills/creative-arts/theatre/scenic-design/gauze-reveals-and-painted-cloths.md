# Gauze reveals and painted cloths

## Gauze (US: scrim)
- **Sharkstooth gauze/scrim**: rectangular weave, most opaque when front-lit —
  best for painted gauzes and reveals.
- **Bobbinet**: hexagonal open weave, very transparent — softens images,
  for ghostly or hazy effects.
- **Theatrical gauze/cotton gauze (UK)** varieties; black gauze to soften upstage
  views.

**The reveal**:
1. Light the gauze from the **front at a steep angle** (from above or side, so
   light doesn't pass through to the scene behind) — the gauze looks solid,
   showing the painted image.
2. Keep the area behind **dark**.
3. Cross-fade: front light on gauze out, light behind up — the gauze becomes
   transparent, revealing the scene. Then fly the gauze out if needed.
4. Avoid light spilling onto the gauze from behind; black backing for
   clean effects.

## Painted cloths (backcloths, drops)
- Size the cloth to sightlines and masking (see sightline skill); allow extra
  for **turn-backs** and bottom pocket.
- Seams: horizontal seams are less visible than vertical on many fabrics;
  seamless widths (muslin/cloth up to ~10 m wide) avoid them.
- **Paint elevation**: a scale painting (often 1:25 or 1/2" = 1'-0") showing
  the exact colours, techniques and textures for the scenic artist, with a grid
  for scaling up and colour swatches.
- Translucent cloths (painted with dyes) can be backlit for day/night changes.

## Cyclorama
- Plain cloth or hard cyc for sky/light effects; lit with cyc floods/LED
  battens top and bottom; a **groundrow** masks bottom units.
- Hang it taut; creases show. Stretch with bottom pipe; keep away from actors'
  traffic.

## Gotchas
- Gauze in tracking front light from FOH — reads as a haze across the scene.
- Wrinkles in gauze — steam out; store rolled not folded.
- Paint elevation colours shifting under stage lighting — test with the LD.

## Verify
- [ ] Gauze type chosen for the effect; reveal tested in tech light.
- [ ] Cloth sizes cover sightlines with turn-backs.
- [ ] Paint elevations issued with grid and swatches.
