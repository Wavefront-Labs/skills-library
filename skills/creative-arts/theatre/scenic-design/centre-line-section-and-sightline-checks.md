# Centre-line section and sightline checks

## Get accurate venue data
Venue plan and section (CAD where possible): stage depth, proscenium width and
height, setting line, fly bar positions, seating rows with seat heights (eye
height), balcony fronts. Survey the venue if drawings are doubtful.

## Horizontal sightlines (plan)
1. Mark the **extreme side seats** of the front row (and boxes) on the plan
   — the "worst seats".
2. Draw lines from each extreme seat's eye position through the opposite
   proscenium edge (or the edge of masking legs) into the stage.
3. Anything beyond those lines is **visible offstage** from the opposite side —
   mask it (legs, wing flats) or keep it clear. Inside the lines is the visible
   acting area; check that key action is visible from both extremes.

## Vertical sightlines (section)
1. Draw the **centre-line section**: a vertical slice through the centre of the
   stage and auditorium, at the same scale as the ground plan (1:25 or 1:50 UK;
   1/2" or 1/4" = 1'-0" US).
2. Plot **eye heights** for the front row (low, seeing up into the flies) and
   the back row of the highest balcony (high, seeing down onto the floor and over
   borders). Typical seated eye height ~1.1-1.2 m above the seat-floor level.
3. Lines from the **front-row eye** under each border show the **trim height**
   needed to hide lighting bars and flown pieces.
4. Lines from the **top balcony eye** show how far upstage the floor is seen
   (and whether the back wall/cyc base is visible), and whether high scenery
   blocks the view of upstage action.
5. Adjust border trims, set heights, cyc position, and platform heights until
   both extremes see what they need.

## Output
- Section drawing showing set, borders, lighting bars and sightline lines.
- Trim height schedule for borders and flown items.
- Notes to LX (bar trims) and production manager.

## Gotchas
- Using a "typical" venue section — every theatre differs.
- Forgetting boxes and side balconies.
- Ignoring raked stages or audience risers changing eye heights.

## Verify
- [ ] Extreme seats plotted in plan and section.
- [ ] No unintended visibility of offstage/fly areas.
- [ ] Trim heights confirmed with production and lighting.
