# Flame-retardancy compliance for scenery

## Standards
- **UK**: venues and licensing authorities generally require scenery and drapes
  to meet **BS 5867-2** (fabrics for curtains and drapes, Type B for theatres)
  or equivalent; timber and boards to achieve suitable surface spread-of-flame
  classes; ABTT guidance covers testing and treatment.
- **US**: **NFPA 701** (flame propagation test for textiles and films); local
  fire codes (IFC/NFPA 101) require compliance for scenery and drapery.
- EU: EN 13501-1 classifications.

## Materials
- **Inherently flame-retardant (IFR)** fabrics (e.g. FR polyester, Trevira CS):
  retain properties through washing; preferred for drapes and long runs.
- **Treated (durably or non-durably) FR fabrics**: cotton velour, serge, muslin
  treated with flame-retardant chemicals; non-durable treatments wash out —
  re-treat after cleaning or wetting, and periodically.
- Timber/MDF/ply: use FR-rated boards or apply FR paints/varnishes/impregnation.
- Plastics and foams: many are highly flammable — specify FR grades; check
  smoke and toxicity.

## Process
1. Specify FR requirements in the design notes and to suppliers.
2. Obtain **certificates** for every material (supplier test certificates
   referencing the standard), and treatment certificates for applied FR.
3. **Field test** (where accepted by the authority): a small sample held in a
   flame per the standard's field test procedure (e.g. a match/lighter test of
   specified duration — follow the local authority's method) — record date,
   material, result.
4. Keep a **flame file**: certificates, treatment records, test logs; present to
   the venue/fire officer on request.
5. Re-treat after washing, wetting, or per the treatment's life.

## Gotchas
- Found or borrowed items (antique furniture, curtains) with no FR status —
  treat or replace.
- Paint and glaze layers changing fire performance.
- Ignoring props and costumes near naked flame (see naked flame skill).

- [ ] Certificate or treatment record for every scenic material.
- [ ] Field tests logged where required.
- [ ] Flame file available to the venue.
