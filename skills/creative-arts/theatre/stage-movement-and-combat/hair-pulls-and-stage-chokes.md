# Hair pulls, chokes and kicks

Close-contact illusions rely on **victim control**: the victim generates the
movement and the attacker provides only the shape.

## Hair pull (victim-controlled)
1. Attacker places a **flat hand on top of the victim's head**, fingers curled
   loosely as if gripping — no hair actually held, or only a light hold of the
   hair close to the scalp per technique.
2. Victim places their own hand over or around the attacker's wrist (often
   looks like trying to free themselves) and **pulls the attacker's hand down
   onto their own head**, controlling direction and pressure.
3. Victim moves (bending, stumbling) and the attacker follows; the victim leads.
4. Vocal reaction and facial tension sell the pain.

## Stage choke (front)
1. Attacker's hands go around the neck with **thumbs and fingers not
   pressing** — hands rest on the collarbones/shoulders or around the back of
   the neck; **no pressure on the throat or windpipe, ever**.
2. The attacker's arms can look tense (elbows out, shoulders lifted) while the
   hands stay soft.
3. Victim grabs the attacker's wrists/forearms and controls; the victim
   pushes *against* the attacker to create the struggle.
4. Victim's breathing is acted (gasps on inhale, strained voice); they should
   always be able to speak/breathe normally.
5. Agree a **tap-out** signal for immediate release.

## Kicks
- **Kicks to the body of a fallen partner**: usually **land on the floor** in
  front of the body, or pass by out of distance, with the victim
  reacting; or a contact kick with a soft instep to the abdomen with very
  controlled energy only when taught.
- **Stomps**: foot comes down beside the head/hand on the floor, making the
  knap; the victim reacts.
- Standing kicks: aimed out of distance with masking like punches.

## Safety rules
- No real pressure on neck, joints or hair roots.
- Long hair, wigs and hairpins: check with wardrobe/wigs; pins can hurt.
- Release signals rehearsed and respected.

## Gotchas
- Attackers "helping" realism by squeezing — the classic injury.
- Victim losing control over a long run as both get comfortable.
- Choke illusions under wigs/microphones (mic packs at the neck/hairline).

## Verify
- [ ] Victim controls every hair pull and choke.
- [ ] No pressure on throat confirmed at each fight call.
- [ ] Kicks land on the floor or pass out of distance unless specifically taught.
