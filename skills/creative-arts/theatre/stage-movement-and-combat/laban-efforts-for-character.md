# Laban efforts for character

Rudolf Laban, *The Mastery of Movement* (1950). Actor training uses his **effort**
theory: the inner attitude to movement described by four factors.

## The four motion factors
| Factor | Indulging | Fighting/condensing |
|---|---|---|
| **Weight** | light | strong |
| **Time** | sustained | sudden (quick) |
| **Space** | indirect (flexible) | direct |
| **Flow** | free | bound |

## The eight basic effort actions (weight + time + space)
| Effort | Weight | Time | Space |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Punch / thrust** | strong | sudden | direct |
| **Press** | strong | sustained | direct |
| **Slash** | strong | sudden | indirect |
| **Wring** | strong | sustained | indirect |
| **Dab** | light | sudden | direct |
| **Glide** | light | sustained | direct |
| **Flick** | light | sudden | indirect |
| **Float** | light | sustained | indirect |

## Training sequence
1. Explore each factor alone (move with only strong vs light weight; etc.).
2. Explore each of the eight efforts with the whole body, then hands only,
   then voice (speak a line as "punch", then "float").
3. **Transitions**: move between neighbouring efforts by changing one factor
   (press → wring by changing space).
4. **Kinesphere**: the sphere of space around the body reachable without stepping.
   Explore near, middle and far reach and the planes (door/vertical, table/
   horizontal, wheel/sagittal).

## Building character
1. Assign a **dominant effort** (e.g. a bureaucrat: dab/press; a dreamy
   poet: float; a bully: punch/slash).
2. A **secondary effort** under pressure (the bureaucrat slashes when cornered).
3. Apply to walk, gesture, and speech rhythm.
4. **Score scenes**: mark effort changes on the script as beats change.

## Laban movement analysis (observation)
Watch people (cafés, stations) and note effort qualities, kinesphere use and
flow — a vocabulary for notes ("more bound flow in the entrance").

## Gotchas
- Turning efforts into caricature; reduce once embodied.
- Ignoring flow (free/bound), which carries emotional tension.
- Using the efforts only physically — they also shape vocal delivery.

## Verify
- [ ] You can perform all eight efforts and name their factors.
- [ ] Character has dominant and secondary efforts.
- [ ] Script scored with effort changes at beat shifts.
