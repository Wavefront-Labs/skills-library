# Neutral mask and the seven levels of tension

From Jacques Lecoq, *The Moving Body* (*Le Corps poétique*, 1997) and the Lecoq
school pedagogy.

## Mask etiquette
- Masks are put on and taken off **with the back to the audience**/group.
- Handle by the edges, not the face; store face up.
- Look at the mask before wearing; never talk in a neutral mask.
- The body, not the face, now speaks; movements must be clear and economical.

## The neutral mask
A mask with balanced, expressionless features (Lecoq's leather masks by
Amleto Sartori). Purpose: strip the actor's personal habits to find an
economical, open, curious state — "a being without past".
**Exercises**:
1. **Waking up**: the mask lies down, wakes, and discovers the world as if for
   the first time.
2. **The fundamental journey**: the mask comes out of the sea, crosses a beach,
   climbs through a forest, a mountain, sees a vast plain; each terrain
   discovered with full presence.
3. **Elements and materials**: becoming water, fire, air, earth; paper,
   oil, metal — identifying with their dynamics.
4. **Farewell**: saying goodbye to someone on a departing boat.
Notes focus on economy: no unnecessary gesture, eyes leading the head, the head
leading the body.

## Larval and expressive masks
After neutral: **larval masks** (simple, unfinished shapes from Basel
carnival) and **expressive/character masks** — each mask demands a body,
rhythm and attitude; actors find them by looking in a mirror and following
the mask's impulse.

## Seven levels of tension (Lecoq, developed by many teachers)
1. **Exhausted / catatonic** — no tension, collapsing.
2. **Laid-back / Californian** — relaxed, cool.
3. **Neutral / economic** — present, nothing unnecessary.
4. **Alert / curious** — readiness, reactive.
5. **Suspense / passionate** — tension in the body, about to act.
6. **Passionate / melodramatic** — full emotion, operatic.
7. **Tragic / petrified** — total tension, can barely move.
Exercise: walk the room at each level; play a scene at different levels;
comedy often comes from mismatched levels between characters.

## Gotchas
- Talking or "face acting" behind the mask.
- Neutral mask played as blank boredom — it's alert and open.
- Rushing past neutral to character masks.

## Verify
- [ ] Mask etiquette observed every time.
- [ ] Neutral journey performed with economy and curiosity.
- [ ] Actors can move cleanly between the seven levels on call.
