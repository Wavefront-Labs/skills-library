# Period movement and etiquette

Period movement gives actors the physical codes of a social world: how people stood,
walked, greeted, sat and showed status. Clothes shaped bodies — rehearse in
approximations of the costume.

## Process
1. **Research** the era and class: paintings, conduct books (e.g. 18th-century
   courtesy manuals), dance manuals, costume history (V&A, Met Costume Institute).
2. **Rehearsal costume** early: corset, long rehearsal skirt, heeled shoes, tail
   coat, sword hanger, hats — wardrobe provides.
3. Teach **deportment** first (spine, head carriage), then greetings, then
   handling accessories, then social space and touch.

## Common elements
- **Bows** (men, broadly): from a simple head inclination to a full bow with
  one foot back, weight shifting back, torso inclined, hat swept (17th-18th
  century). Depth signals status.
- **Curtsey**: knees bend with one foot behind, back upright, head inclined;
  depth and duration by rank (deep court curtsey vs bob). Rise smoothly.
- **Corset deportment**: breathe into the upper ribs and back; sitting means
  perching on the front of the chair with a straight back; bending from hips,
  not waist.
- **Skirts**: hoops and trains — kick the train behind when turning; manage
  stairs by lifting the front of the skirt; walk with gliding steps.
- **Fans** (18th-19th c.): open/close gestures, fluttering, hiding the face;
  "fan language" is largely a later invention — use it as character business,
  not code.
- **Hats, canes, gloves, swords**: removal of hats indoors and to superiors;
  cane carriage; sword hangers affect sitting and turning.
- **Social space**: distance between sexes and ranks; who sits first; who
  offers an arm.

## Adapting for the production
- Decide with the director how strict the period should be; modern audiences
  need clarity over accuracy.
- Keep it consistent across the cast.

## Gotchas
- Learning period movement in tracksuits, then struggling in tech.
- Mixing centuries' etiquette.
- Fussy business that clutters scenes.

## Verify
- [ ] Rehearsal costumes used from early rehearsals.
- [ ] Bows/curtseys and greetings agreed and consistent.
- [ ] Actors can sit, stand and turn in costume cleanly.
