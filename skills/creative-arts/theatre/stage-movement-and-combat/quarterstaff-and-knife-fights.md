# Quarterstaff and knife fights

Both are SAFD skills-proficiency weapons and appear in BASSC syllabi. Learn with a
certified fight director.

## Quarterstaff
- **Staff**: hardwood (often ash or rattan), roughly 6-7 ft (1.8-2.1 m),
  checked for cracks and splinters; ends may be rounded.
- **Grips**: quarter grip (hands dividing the staff into thirds/quarters),
  middle grip; hands slide for reach.
- **Blocks**: staff held horizontal or vertical, struck **between the hands**
  (never near the fingers); classic blocks for head (high horizontal), flanks
  (vertical), legs (low vertical/angled).
- **Strikes**: swings to head, flank, leg — aimed to the **block, not the
  body**; contact is staff-on-staff. Thrusts with the end off-line or out of
  distance.
- Space: large clearance radius — set and audience proximity matter; check
  overhead (borders, lights).
- Hand protection: avoid knuckles on the striking surface; some productions
  use gloves.

## Knife
- **Prop types**: rubber/foam knives (closest work), blunt aluminium or wooden
  knives (for gleam at distance), **retractable** blades (blade slides into the
  handle on contact — check spring and slide reliability before every show;
  they can jam).
- **Never** use a real sharp knife on stage — including in "cutting food"
  moments near a fight.
- Techniques: slashes passing in front of the body out of distance; thrusts
  to the body using **masked stops** (the blade stops short, masked by the
  victim's body), or retractable contact with flat palm pressure; grabbing
  the knife hand/wrist for struggles.
- **Blood effects**: blood bags, blood knives (reservoir) — coordinate with
  wardrobe (washable blood, costume duplicates).

## Safety
- Weapons signed out/in by props/ASM; locked away offstage.
- Pre-show weapon check every performance; spare duplicates.
- Fight call before each show.

## Gotchas
- Retractable knives failing — always choreograph so a failure still misses.
- Staff hitting set or lights in a new venue.
- Knife props looking fake under bright light — fight director balances
  realism and safety.

## Verify
- [ ] Staffs and knives inspected and logged each show.
- [ ] Staff contact only staff-on-staff between hands.
- [ ] Knife thrusts masked or retractable-tested every show.
