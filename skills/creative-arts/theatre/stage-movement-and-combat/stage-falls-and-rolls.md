# Stage falls and rolls

Learn with a qualified fight/movement teacher on mats before the stage floor.

## Principles
- **Get low first**: the fall's height is reduced before impact (bend knees, sink).
- **Spread the contact**: roll through soft, muscular parts of the body in
  sequence (calf, thigh, buttock, back) — never land on joints (knees, elbows,
  tailbone, wrists) or the head.
- **Protect the head**: chin tucked (backward falls) or head turned (forward
  falls).
- **Exhale** on contact; stay relaxed.
- The **sound and speed** sell the fall; the body touches down slowly.

## Backward fall (sit-down fall)
1. Step back with one foot, bend both knees deeply, lowering the hips.
2. Sit onto the buttock beside the heel of the back foot.
3. Roll back along the spine with chin tucked; arms may slap the floor
   (break-fall) for sound and to dissipate force, or stay soft.
4. Head never hits the floor.

## Forward fall
1. Avoid landing on the knees: step forward into a lunge, drop
   low, place hands (fingers forward, elbows soft) and lower chest/side to the
   floor, turning the head to one side.
2. A sliding version transfers weight from hands to forearms to chest/hip.

## Side fall / collapse
- **Collapse (faint)**: knees bend, then hip, then side of the body, then
  shoulder, in a spiral — like a puppet whose strings are released, with
  control.

## Shoulder roll
- From a crouch, one arm curved in front, roll diagonally across the back from
  one shoulder to the opposite hip, head tucked to the side; come up on the
  feet. Used for tumbling falls and recovery.

## Stair falls
Highly specialised: choreographed **step by step** using controlled slides on the
sides of the body, with padding and specific stair design (rounded nosings,
non-slip). Fight director and set designer coordinate; consider whether to
fake it with masking.

## Gotchas
- Practising on hard floors without mats.
- Costumes (corsets, heels, armour) changing mechanics — rehearse in costume
  early, add hidden padding.
- Raked stages and slippery surfaces.

## Verify
- [ ] No joint or head contact in any fall.
- [ ] Falls rehearsed in costume and on the actual floor.
- [ ] Padding and stage surface agreed with wardrobe and set.
