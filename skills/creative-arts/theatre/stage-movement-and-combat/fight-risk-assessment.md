# Fight risk assessment

In the UK, employers must assess risks (Management of Health and Safety at Work
Regulations 1999); fights are among the highest-risk elements of a show. The
fight director typically drafts the fight section with the production manager
and SM. US practice follows similar logic under OSHA general duty and company
policy.

## Structure (per fight sequence)
| Column | Example |
|---|---|
| Sequence / page | Act 2 Sc 3, "the tavern brawl", p.54 |
| Hazard | Unarmed punches near face; fall onto table; broken bottle prop |
| Who might be harmed | Actors A, B; ASM in wings; front row |
| Existing controls | Out-of-distance strikes; rubber bottle (breakaway sugar-glass alternative considered and rejected); padded table edge; fight call before every show |
| Risk rating | Likelihood x Severity (e.g. 2 x 3 = 6, medium) |
| Further action | Knee pads under costume; fight captain notes; non-slip finish on platform |
| Owner / date | Fight director / SM, date |

## Hazards to consider
- **Techniques**: strikes, falls, lifts, chokes, sword work, firearms.
- **Weapons**: condition, blunted edges/points, blade breakage, maintenance
  schedule, storage, sign-out (armourer/props).
- **Environment**: floor surface, rake, set edges, lighting changes (blackouts,
  strobe, haze reducing visibility), stairs, audience proximity.
- **Costume**: skirts, heels, capes, masks, wigs limiting vision; padding.
- **People**: fatigue in long runs, illness, understudies, child performers.
- **Time**: tech schedules squeezing fight rehearsal.

## Control measures (typical)
- Choreography by a qualified fight director; written notation in the prompt
  book.
- Daily **fight call** led by the fight captain.
- Weapons checked before each show; damaged items withdrawn.
- No changes without the fight director.
- First aid plan and incident reporting.

## Review
Re-assess when choreography, cast, venue, costume or set changes; after any
incident or near miss; and at intervals through a long run.

## Gotchas
- Generic assessments copied from another show.
- Not including crew (scene changes near fights).
- Forgetting the audience in immersive/thrust staging.

## Verify
- [ ] Every sequence listed with hazards and controls.
- [ ] Weapons inspection regime documented.
- [ ] Assessment signed and reviewed after changes.
