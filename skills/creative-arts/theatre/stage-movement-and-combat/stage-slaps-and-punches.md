# Stage slaps and punches

Learn from a certified fight teacher; practise slow, with eye contact. Unarmed
syllabi (BASSC, SAFD) include these as core techniques.

## The non-contact slap (crossing slap)
1. Partners at arm's length plus; eye contact.
2. Attacker's hand travels on a path that **passes in front of** the victim's
   face (a few inches clear), at the angle where the audience's sightline is
   blocked by the victim's head/shoulder or the attacker's body.
3. **Knap**: the sound is made by someone not seen — commonly the victim claps
   their hands at chest level hidden from the audience, or the attacker slaps
   their own body/hand. Timing must be exact.
4. **Reaction**: the victim's head turns *with* the passing hand (not
   before), driven from the torso, plus a vocal reaction. The reaction sells
   the hit.
5. Follow-through: attacker's hand continues, not stopping at the face.

## Contact slap (when used)
Some productions use a **light contact slap** to the fleshy cheek only (never
ear, nose, jaw), with a relaxed flat hand and no follow-through push — only when
taught and supervised by a fight director, with the victim's consent, and checked
in every fight call. Many fight directors avoid it.

## Punches (non-contact)
- **Jab/straight punch to face**: fired upstage-downstage on a line that the
  audience sees masked by the victim's head; stops short (out of distance).
  Shared knap.
- **Roundhouse (hook)**: an arc passing in front of the face; victim's head
  turns with it.
- **Uppercut**: passes in front of the chin, victim's head snaps back from the
  torso.
- **Stomach punch**: a **contact** punch with pulled energy to the
  abdomen in some syllabi (open fist, light contact, victim tenses and
  reacts), or non-contact with masking — fight director's choice.
- The attacker's fist should be relaxed until the "impact" point, then retract.

## Safety rules
- Never aim *at* the face; aim at a target point beside it.
- Rings, watches, fake nails removed or taped.
- Re-check distance in every fight call.

## Gotchas
- Knaps late or early — audiences notice instantly.
- Victim reacting before the strike arrives ("anticipation").
- Masking that works from centre but not side seats.

## Verify
- [ ] Each strike has an off-body path and masking checked from the house.
- [ ] Knap ownership and timing agreed.
- [ ] Reactions start from the torso, in time with the strike.
