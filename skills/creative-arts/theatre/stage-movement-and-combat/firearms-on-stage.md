# Firearms on stage

Firearms are high-risk props regulated by law. **Always use a qualified armourer
or the company's designated responsible person** and follow your jurisdiction's
law (e.g. UK Firearms Acts and police guidance on theatrical use; US state and
local law).

## Choose the least dangerous option that tells the story
1. **Non-firing replica / rubber gun** + **sound effect** (QLab or offstage
   cap/blank under control) — safest; most theatres default to this.
2. **Cap-firing replica** — flash and small bang, close-range risk minimal but
   not zero.
3. **Blank-firing gun** — sound and flash; **blanks are dangerous** (hot gas,
   wadding, burning particles) at close range — injuries and deaths have occurred.
4. Real deactivated guns — props only, no firing; legal status varies.

## Handling rules
- Armourer issues and collects guns for every rehearsal and show; logs
  them; locked storage at all times.
- Treat every gun as loaded; check it in front of the actor.
- **Never point at anyone**, even replicas. Aim **off-line** — upstage of or
  beside the target, angled away from audience and cast — using masking to
  sell the aim.
- Minimum distances for blanks (set by the armourer/manufacturer) from any
  person; never fire at close range or into a contact.
- No one touches a gun except the actor and armourer; no "playing" offstage.

## Sound and audience
- Hearing protection for actors (molded earplugs) and warn audiences of gunshots
  (signage/front-of-house).
- Loud blanks in small venues can exceed safe noise levels — consider sound
  effects instead.
- Fire safety: flash near costumes, haze, scenery; fire officer approval may be
  required.

## Documentation
- Specific risk assessment per firearm moment (see
  `creative-arts.theatre.stage-movement-and-combat.fight-risk-assessment`).
- Licences/permissions, storage record, issue log.
- Police notification when firearms (even replicas) are transported or used in
  public-facing spaces, where required.

## Gotchas
- Using a gun "borrowed from a friend".
- Firing at an actor because "it's only a blank".
- Forgetting the replica rules when moving through public streets on tour.

## Verify
- [ ] Armourer/responsible person named; issue log in place.
- [ ] All aims off-line and checked.
- [ ] Audience warnings and hearing protection arranged.
