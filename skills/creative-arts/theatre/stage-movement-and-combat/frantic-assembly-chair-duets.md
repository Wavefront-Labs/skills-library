# Frantic Assembly chair duets

Frantic Assembly (Scott Graham and Steven Hoggett; *The Frantic Assembly Book of
Devising Theatre*) use **building blocks**: simple tasks that untrained performers
can do, which generate sophisticated movement material.

## Chair duets
1. Two chairs side by side, two performers seated, facing front.
2. Performer A creates a sequence of **simple touches and movements** involving
   both of them (e.g. A's hand on B's knee; B removes it; A rests head on B's
   shoulder; B leans away; A places B's hand on their own face). Aim for 6-10
   moves.
3. B accepts each move passively at first; then they learn it as a fixed
   sequence together.
4. **Repeat** until fluid and exact.
5. **Edit and manipulate**: change speed (very fast, very slow), reverse
   the order, swap roles, add pauses, perform with eye contact vs never looking.
6. **Add context/text**: perform while delivering a scene of dialogue that seems
   unrelated (e.g. a mundane conversation); the movement reveals the subtext
   of the relationship.
7. Show back; discuss what relationship the audience reads.

## Related building blocks
- **Round/By/Through**: performers pass around, by and through each other in
  close proximity with simple rules, creating flowing duets.
- **Hymn Hands**: one partner places their hands on the other's body in a
  sequence; the receiver responds; leads to tender or tense duets.
- **Lifting basics**: counterbalance and safe lifts with teacher guidance (see
  `creative-arts.theatre.stage-movement-and-combat.partner-lifts-for-actors`).

## Safety and consent
- Touch-based work requires a check-in on boundaries (see consent practice).
- Warm up properly; avoid sudden weight on necks and lower backs.

## Gotchas
- Over-complicating tasks — simplicity is the engine.
- Playing emotions instead of executing the task precisely.
- Not recording sequences; they're easy to lose.

## Verify
- [ ] Sequence of 6-10 moves fixed and repeatable.
- [ ] At least three manipulations tried (speed, order, roles).
- [ ] Material tested under text for subtext.
