# Wig, make-up and breaking-down design

## Wigs and hair
- Decide per character: own hair styled, wig, hairpiece, facial hair.
- **Lace-front wigs** (hand-knotted hair on fine lace) read naturally at close
  range; hard-front wigs for stylised work.
- **Wig and hair plot**: character, scene, wig/style, changes, facial hair,
  quick-change notes.
- Actors need **wig fittings** (head measurements, wig prep: pin curls/wig
  cap) and time for the wig team in the half.
- Facial hair: laid (hand-applied hair) or knotted pieces; sweat and
  microphones affect adhesion.

## Make-up
- **Make-up chart** per character: face diagram with products/colours, ageing,
  scars, blood, special effects.
- Stage make-up is about clarity at distance and under light; in small venues
  keep it natural.
- Skin tones: ensure foundations and products match every performer; many
  performers bring their own base — budget accordingly.
- Prosthetics (latex, silicone, gelatine) need allergy tests and application
  time.

## Breaking down (distressing)
Making costumes look worn, aged, dirty or damaged.
Techniques: sanding and rasping edges/elbows/knees, cheese-grater for fraying,
dye and fabric paint washes (darken seams, sweat stains, grime), spray (dirt
patterns), wax/oils for grease, fuller's earth or powders (temporary), cutting
and burning (with care — flame-retardant implications).
- Reference real wear patterns: where hands touch, where bodies sweat, where
  kneeling scuffs.
- Break down for the distance: subtle in studios, bolder in big houses.

## Costumes for combat and dance
- **Combat**: room to move, padding (knees, hips), no restrictive skirts at
  speed, safe footwear with grip, duplicates for blood. Consult the fight
  director.
- **Dance**: stretch fabrics, gussets, secure closures, dance shoes; costume
  movement must be tested in a dance rehearsal; avoid sharp trims.

## Gotchas
- Wigs not budgeted for the actor's maintenance in long runs.
- Breaking down "evenly" — real wear is uneven.
- Heavy period costumes in fights without testing.
