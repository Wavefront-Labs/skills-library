# Running costume fittings

## Preparation
- Schedule through the SM (fittings appear on the daily call); respect
  rehearsal hours rules for fittings.
- Have measurements on file (taken at contract/first rehearsal).
- Room: private, warm, mirror, chair, good light; a robe; pins, chalk,
  tape measure, fitting notes sheet; the rendering on display.
- Attendees: designer, cutter/maker or supervisor, the actor; minimal others.

## Fitting stages
1. **Mock-up/toile fitting** (built garments): a calico/muslin version to check
   pattern and proportions.
2. **First fitting** in real fabric, basted.
3. **Final fitting**: finished garments, accessories, shoes.
For bought/hired costumes: one or two fittings to select and alter.

## During the fitting
- Put foundations and shoes on first.
- Check silhouette against the rendering; hem lengths; sleeve and collar.
- **Movement test**: sit, kneel, raise arms, fight/dance moves, stairs; quick
  change trial for closures.
- Ask the actor about comfort and practical needs (pockets, mic pack placement
  with sound department).
- **Photograph** front, side, back (with consent; consistent background).
- Keep conversation respectful about bodies; no comments on weight/shape;
  offer the actor a say where practical.

## Fitting notes
Per garment: changes needed (take in 2 cm at side seams, shorten hem 5 cm, add
gusset), who's responsible, due date. Circulate to the shop the same day.

## Dress parade
Before tech, actors wear each costume onstage under stage light, in order, for the
director, designer and LD to see together; note changes. Saves tech time.

## Gotchas
- Fittings booked without the SM/director knowing.
- Forgetting shoes — changes posture, hems and movement.
- Mic pack placement left to tech.

## Verify
- [ ] Every principal costume fitted at least once in final form.
- [ ] Fitting notes and photos filed per actor.
- [ ] Dress parade held before tech.
