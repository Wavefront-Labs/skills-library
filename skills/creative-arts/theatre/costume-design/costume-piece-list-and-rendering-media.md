# Costume piece list and rendering media

## Piece list
After renderings are approved, list **every item** per character per look —
the document the costume supervisor builds, buys and budgets from.
| Character | Look # / scene | Item | Build / Buy / Hire / Pull | Notes (fabric, colour, quick change, practical needs) | Status |
Include: underwear and foundations (corsets, petticoats, padding), garments,
outerwear, shoes, hosiery, hats, gloves, jewellery, bags, glasses, wigs/
facial hair (with wigs dept), personal props carried in pockets.
- Mark **duplicates** (blood, water, distress effects, understudies).
- Flag **quick-change** pieces (velcro/magnet closures, pre-rigged layers).
- Track status: sourced, in build, fitted, finished.
Combine with the costume plot (who wears what when) in the costume bible.

## Rendering media
Renderings communicate silhouette, colour, fabric and character — not fashion
illustration for its own sake.
- **Croquis/figure templates** matched to the actor's proportions once cast
  (or a neutral figure before casting).
- **Watercolour**: luminous, quick washes; good for fluid fabrics; build
  light to dark.
- **Gouache**: opaque, flat matte colour — strong for saturated, graphic
  designs; corrections easy.
- **Markers** (alcohol-based): fast, consistent tones; layer for shadows; add
  coloured pencil for texture.
- **Coloured pencil/pastel**: texture, soft fabrics.
- **Digital** (Procreate, Photoshop, Clip Studio): layers for variations,
  easy colour changes, fast revisions; print at good resolution for the shop.
- **Collage**: fabric swatches or printed textures on the rendering.
Attach **fabric swatches** and notes on fabric weight/drape; add back views and
detail sketches where construction isn't obvious.

## Gotchas
- Beautiful renderings that hide construction (no back view).
- Piece lists missing underwear/foundations — budget blow-out late.
- Digital colours that print differently from screen — calibrate or proof.

## Verify
- [ ] Every look has a complete piece list with sourcing decisions.
- [ ] Renderings show front/back and swatches.
- [ ] Quick-change and duplicate items flagged.
