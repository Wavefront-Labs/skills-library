# Consonant and articulation drills

Consonants carry intelligibility; vowels carry emotion and volume. In large
houses, energised consonants matter more than volume. Draws on Edith Skinner
(*Speak with Distinction*) drill tradition and contemporary voice practice.

## Warm-up the articulators (3-5 minutes)
- **Lips**: blow through relaxed lips (lip trill/"motorboat"), stretch wide
  smile to tight pout x10, "puh-buh-muh" lip pops.
- **Tongue**: tongue stretches out and down, side to side, circles around the
  teeth inside the lips; "la-la-la" tip flicks; "ka-ga-nga" back of tongue.
- **Jaw**: massage, gentle release drops (jaw loose, not forced).

## Consonant drills
1. **Plosives** (p b t d k g): crisp release, no extra breath puff:
   "pa ta ka, ba da ga" in rhythm, increasing speed; then "pata kata pata kata".
2. **Final consonants** — the most commonly dropped: "hot dog", "act fact
   pact", "asked tasks", "worlds", "fifths sixths". Over-energise in practice.
3. **Fricatives** (f v th s z sh): sustained then short; check lisping on s.
4. **Consonant clusters**: "strengths", "twelfths", "she sells sea shells".
5. **Linking**: "next stop" — release both t's or link deliberately; know
   which choice suits the character/accent.
6. **Tongue twisters progression**: slow and exact → medium → fast without
   losing precision. E.g. "Red leather, yellow leather"; "The lips, the teeth,
   the tip of the tongue"; "Unique New York".

## Applying to text
- Pick the **operative words** and give their consonants full energy.
- Practise the speech whispered (forces consonants to carry), then with full
  voice keeping the precision.
- Speak to the back wall while a partner sits there and flags any unclear words.

## Gotchas
- Over-articulation that sounds mannered in naturalistic plays — calibrate
  to venue and style.
- Extra breathy "h" on plosives (aspiration overdone) creating mic popping.
- Jaw tension to "force" clarity — the tongue and lips do the work.

## Verify
- [ ] Final consonants audible from the back of the room.
- [ ] Twisters at speed with no slurring.
- [ ] Partner test: no flagged unclear words in a 1-minute speech.
