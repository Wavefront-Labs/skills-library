# Received Pronunciation and General American

Standard reference accents used in actor training and classical/period work.
Modern usage: "RP" now often taught as a contemporary "Standard Southern
British" rather than old upper-class RP; General American (GenAm) is a broad
North American accent without strong regional features.

## Key differences (with Wells lexical sets)
| Feature | RP (contemporary) | General American |
|---|---|---|
| Rhoticity (r after vowels: *car*, *NURSE*) | non-rhotic: [kɑː], [nɜːs] | rhotic: [kɑɹ], [nɝs] |
| BATH (*bath, dance, can't*) | [ɑː] | [æ] (same as TRAP) |
| LOT (*hot, stop*) | rounded [ɒ] | unrounded [ɑ] |
| THOUGHT (*caught*) | [ɔː] | [ɔ] or merged with LOT [ɑ] |
| GOAT (*go*) | [əʊ] | [oʊ] |
| T between vowels (*better*) | [t] | flapped [ɾ] |
| STRUT | [ʌ] (more open/central) | [ʌ] |
| Yod after t/d/n (*tune, news*) | [tjuːn] / [tʃuːn] | dropped: [tun], [nuz] |
| Linking/intrusive r (*law and order*) | common in RP | n/a (rhotic) |
Also: RP *-ary* words (*secretary*) often reduced [ˈsekɹətɹi]; GenAm
[ˈsɛkɹəˌtɛɹi] with secondary stress.

## Oral posture
- RP: relatively forward tongue, less jaw opening on short vowels, crisp t.
- GenAm: tongue more retracted/bunched for r-colouring, more relaxed jaw.

## Drill procedure
1. Learn the table; record yourself on a lexical-set word list (TRAP BATH LOT
   THOUGHT GOAT NURSE START NORTH etc.).
2. Practise minimal contrasts: "bath / trap", "cot / caught", "father / bother".
3. Transcribe your script's key words in IPA
   (`creative-arts.theatre.voice-and-speech.ipa-transcription-for-actors`).
4. Drill sentences dense in target features: "Pass the bottle of water to Laura
   after the party" (BATH, LOT, t-flap/RP t, NURSE/START).
5. Run text with a coach or reference recordings (IDEA archive).

## Gotchas
- Old-fashioned "cut-glass" RP when contemporary is wanted (or vice versa).
- Hyper-rhotic GenAm (over-strong r).
- Inconsistency under emotional pressure — drill until automatic.

## Verify
- [ ] Lexical-set list recorded in both accents and checked.
- [ ] Script key words transcribed.
- [ ] Consistency maintained in a full emotional run.
