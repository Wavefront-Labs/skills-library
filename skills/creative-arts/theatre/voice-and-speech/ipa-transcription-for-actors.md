# IPA transcription for actors

The **International Phonetic Alphabet** (IPA, International Phonetic Association)
gives one symbol per sound. Actors use it to record pronunciations,
accents and dialects precisely, independent of spelling.

## Basics
- Write transcriptions in **slashes** for phonemic (broad) /ˈθiətə/ and
  **square brackets** for phonetic (narrow) [ˈθɪəʔə] detail.
- **Stress**: ˈ before the primary stressed syllable, ˌ before secondary.
- **Length**: ː (e.g. /ɑː/).

## Consonants to know (English)
θ (*th*ink), ð (*th*is), ʃ (*sh*ip), ʒ (mea*s*ure), tʃ (*ch*ur*ch*), dʒ (*j*u*dg*e),
ŋ (si*ng*), j (*y*es), ɹ (English r), ɾ (flap, as in US *butter*), ʔ (glottal stop, as
in some London *bu'er*), ɫ (dark l, as in *full*), ʍ (voiceless *wh* in some accents).

## Vowels
Learn the **vowel chart** (tongue height: close–open; position: front–back; lip
rounding). Key English vowels: i ɪ e ɛ æ a ɑ ɒ ɔ o ʊ u ʌ ə ɜ, and diphthongs eɪ
aɪ ɔɪ əʊ/oʊ aʊ ɪə eə ʊə. Use J.C. Wells' **lexical sets** (FLEECE, KIT, DRESS,
TRAP, BATH, LOT, THOUGHT, GOAT, GOOSE, FOOT, STRUT, NURSE...) to compare accents
(see `creative-arts.theatre.voice-and-speech.dialect-breakdown-with-lexical-sets`).

## Useful diacritics
̃ nasalised [ã]; ʰ aspirated [tʰ]; ̚ unreleased [t̚]; ̥ voiceless [n̥]; ̩ syllabic
[n̩]; ʷ labialised; ˞ r-coloured [ɚ].

## Procedure for script work
1. Mark words with accent-specific sounds or unfamiliar names.
2. Transcribe each in IPA above the word (broad first).
3. Add narrow detail where the accent differs from your own (e.g. [ʔ] for t).
4. Check against recordings (IDEA archive, native speakers) and with the
   dialect coach.
5. Drill from the transcription, not the spelling.

## Tools
- IPA keyboards: ipa.typeit.org; Mac/Windows IPA input; the IPA chart with sounds
  at the IPA website.
- Dictionaries with IPA: Longman Pronunciation Dictionary (Wells), Cambridge
  English Pronouncing Dictionary.

## Gotchas
- Mixing phonemic and phonetic notation inconsistently.
- Transcribing from spelling assumptions instead of listening.
- Treating /r/ symbols loosely: ɹ vs r (trill) vs ɾ matter.

## Verify
- [ ] Every accent-critical word in your script transcribed.
- [ ] Transcriptions checked against a recording.
- [ ] You can read the transcription back accurately without the spelling.
