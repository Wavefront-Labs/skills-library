# Chekhov qualities, imaginary centre and imaginary body

From *To the Actor* (chapters on the body, imagination and characterisation,
atmosphere). Three tools to change body and inner life from imagination.

## 1. Four qualities of movement (elements)
Move about the room in each; then speak a line in each.
- **Moulding** (earth): space is like clay; every movement shapes resistance.
  Weighted, sculptural, determined.
- **Floating** (water): carried and supported, legato, continuous.
- **Flying** (air): light, lifting, flight-like, easy.
- **Radiating** (fire/light): sending energy out beyond the body's edges;
  presence that fills the space even in stillness.
Use: assign a quality to a character, a scene, or a single speech.

## 2. Imaginary centre
Imagine the body's centre (the source of movement) placed somewhere specific:
- Centre in the **head** → thinker, nervous energy.
- In the **chest** (heart) → the "ideal" open centre.
- In the **belly/pelvis** → earthy, sensual, heavy.
- Outside the body (1 m in front of the nose, behind the back) → strange,
  comic or grotesque.
Also give it qualities (a hot hard ball, a soft warm cloud). Walk, sit, pick
up objects led from that centre.

## 3. Imaginary body
Imagine a different body occupying your space (taller, fatter, older, a
stiff spine, long fingers). "Step into" it slowly, let it change how you move
and speak, then step out. Combine with the centre.

## 4. Atmospheres
An atmosphere is the feeling of a place or event that everyone shares (a
funeral, a fairground, the tension before a storm). Imagine it filling the
air; let it influence tempo and gesture. Characters can **resist** or **yield**
to the atmosphere — the conflict between personal feeling and atmosphere is
dramatic.

## Gotchas
- Caricature: these are starting points; let them become 10-20% visible in
  realism.
- Changing centre every rehearsal: commit to one long enough to discover it.
- Atmosphere played as mood-acting by individuals rather than a shared air.

## Verify
- [ ] Character has a chosen centre, imaginary body and dominant quality.
- [ ] You can step in and out of the imaginary body at will.
- [ ] Each scene has a named atmosphere and your relation to it (yield/resist).
