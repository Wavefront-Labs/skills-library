# Meisner independent activity and knock at the door

## Choosing the activity
The activity is a real task done in the room. It must be:
1. **Physically difficult** — so it takes full attention (gluing a shattered
   plate back together, threading 20 needles, sorting tiny beads by colour, writing
   a precise calligraphy letter). Not easy busywork.
2. **Done for a specific, urgent reason** (imaginary): "I must repair my
   grandmother's plate before she comes home at 6 because I broke it and it's the
   only thing left of her mother." The reason must matter to *you* personally.
3. **Doable truthfully now** with real objects — no mime.
4. **Completion uncertain** in the time available.

## Playing it (with repetition)
- Actor A (inside) works on the activity. Actor B enters. They pick up the
  repetition based on what they observe. A keeps working; the activity is
  real, not a prop.
- A's attention divides between activity and partner — the struggle between
  them creates real behaviour. Don't stop the activity to "act".

## Knock at the door
1. A is in the room on the activity. B, outside, has a specific reason to knock
   (an imaginary circumstance, e.g. "I've come to tell her I got the job").
2. **The knock itself carries meaning**: urgent, timid, angry. A responds to the
   knock — the *way* it was knocked — before opening.
3. B enters, and repetition starts from the first observation; the reason for
   coming and the activity collide. Later stages add relationship (e.g. siblings)
   and three-way variations.
4. End when the teacher calls it; discussion: what happened, what was real.

## Gotchas
- Activities that are too easy (folding laundry) allow the actor to watch
  themselves.
- Reasons that are melodramatic but not personally felt — the urgency must be
  believed; make it specific, not big.
- Entering actor "planning" what to say: let the knock and the first sight lead.
- Dropping the activity to play the scene: the task must stay real.

## Verify
- [ ] Activity is difficult, specific, urgent and uses real objects.
- [ ] Reason is written in one sentence with a deadline.
- [ ] The knock was answered with a response to its quality.
- [ ] Both actors could describe what actually happened between them.
