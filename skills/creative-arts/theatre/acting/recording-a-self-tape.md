# Recording a self-tape

**Rule 1: follow the casting brief exactly** (slate requirements, file names,
length, upload method, deadline). The defaults below apply when the brief is
silent.

## Setup
- **Camera**: phone is fine. Landscape (horizontal), lens at eye level, on a
  tripod. 1080p is standard; don't use beauty filters or portrait mode.
- **Framing**: mid-shot / medium close-up — top of head near top of frame, cut
  around mid-chest. Wider only if action requires.
- **Background**: plain, neutral (grey, blue, off-white), no clutter. Stand ~1 m
  in front to avoid shadows.
- **Light**: soft frontal light — face a window or use a ring/softbox at 45°
  slightly above eye level; avoid overhead-only light and backlight.
- **Sound**: small furnished room > echoey hall. Lavalier or the phone close
  to you; reader quieter than you (reader stands *by the camera*, further back).
- **Reader**: a live person beside the lens reading off-camera lines flatly but
  responsively. Eyeline: just off the lens on the reader's side; don't look into
  the lens unless directed.

## Recording
1. **Slate** (often separate clip): name, agent, height, location/availability as
   requested; full-length shot if asked. Friendly, 10 seconds.
2. Scene: learned or near-learned; sides can be off-camera for a quick turnaround
   but never read down for long.
3. **Do 3-4 takes**, varying one choice; pick the best. Many casters want **one
   take per scene** only.
4. Trim dead air at start/end; no edits within a take.

## Delivery
- File name per brief, e.g. `Firstname_Lastname_Role_Scene1.mp4`.
- Use the platform specified (Spotlight, WeTransfer, Vimeo private link,
  Casting Networks). Check the link works in a private browser window.
- Meet the deadline; early is fine.

## Gotchas
- Reader louder than you (phone mic nearest the reader).
- Looking at the lens by mistake (reads as talking to the audience).
- Over-producing: costumes, props, music — usually unwanted.
- Portrait orientation unless requested.

## Verify
- [ ] Watched back with headphones: clear voice, reader quieter.
- [ ] Face evenly lit, eyes visible, plain background.
- [ ] File names, length and slate match the brief.
- [ ] Link tested logged-out before sending.
