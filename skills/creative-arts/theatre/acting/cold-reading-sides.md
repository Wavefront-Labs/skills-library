# Cold reading sides

Sides are the pages of the scene used in an audition. "Cold" means little or
no prep time — from a few minutes to a day.

## The 5-minute analysis
1. **Read the whole side twice**, including the other characters' lines.
2. Answer quickly: Who am I talking to? What do I want from them? What's the
   event (what changes)? Where am I and what just happened?
3. **Mark 2-3 beat changes** with a slash and a one-word action above each.
4. Find the **last line**: where do you end up?
5. Note any key word you might stumble on and say it aloud once.

## Technique in the room
- **Hold the pages in one hand** at chest level, not covering your face.
- **Thumb-track**: slide your thumb down the margin to mark your place so you can
  look up and find it again instantly.
- **Look down, take the line, look up, deliver**: grab a phrase with your eyes,
  then say it to the reader. Never speak into the page for more than a short
  phrase.
- Listen to the reader's line fully before looking down for yours (the reaction
  is part of the audition).
- Commit to choices; a strong, specific, wrong choice beats a vague safe one.
- At the end, hold the moment, then drop out of character.

## Redirects
Casting often gives a note ("try it as if you don't trust him"). That tests
whether you can take direction. Change the **objective or circumstance**, not just
volume or speed. Ask one clarifying question if needed, then go.

## Gotchas
- Trying to memorise in 5 minutes — use the time for choices instead.
- Acting with the paper (gesturing with pages).
- Skipping the other characters' lines in prep; you'll miss your cues.
- Apologising for mistakes mid-read: keep going.

## Verify
- [ ] Objective, partner, event and ending identified in under 5 minutes.
- [ ] More than half the read delivered eyes-up.
- [ ] A rehearsed second reading ready for a redirect.
