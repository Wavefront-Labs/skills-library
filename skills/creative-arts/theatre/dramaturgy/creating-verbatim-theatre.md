# Creating verbatim theatre

Verbatim theatre uses the exact words of real people, from interviews or
public records (e.g. *London Road*, Alecky Blythe/Adam Cork; the Tricycle tribunal
plays; *The Laramie Project*, Tectonic Theater Project).

## 1. Ethics and consent
- Written **release forms**: interviewee agrees to recording, to words being
  performed, edited, and published; options for anonymity (name change,
  location change).
- Explain the project honestly; tell them they may withdraw before a stated date.
- Duty of care for vulnerable interviewees and sensitive subjects; legal review
  for defamation and contempt (live court cases).
- Public records (inquiry transcripts, court records) — check reproduction
  rights and restrictions.

## 2. Interviewing
- Record high-quality audio (lav mic, quiet room); note setting and body
  language.
- Open questions; let silences sit; follow the story.
- Interview many more people than you'll use.

## 3. Transcription
Transcribe **exactly**: hesitations (um, er), repetitions, false starts,
overlaps, pauses (mark duration). The idiosyncrasies are the material.
Time-code the transcript to the audio.

## 4. Editing
- Select and order extracts to build a dramatic structure (see
  `creative-arts.theatre.dramaturgy.analysing-a-play-with-aristotle-and-freytag`).
- You may **cut and juxtapose**, but must not alter words or create a false
  impression of what someone meant. Keep an edit log mapping each line to source
  and timecode.
- Composite characters must be declared in the programme.

## 5. Performance approaches
- **Recorded delivery** (Blythe's method): actors hear the edited audio
  through earpieces during performance and reproduce it exactly, including
  rhythm and hesitation.
- Learned verbatim: actors learn from audio, not just text.

## Gotchas
- "Tidying up" speech destroys the form's authenticity.
- Editing to fit an agenda — legal and ethical risk.
- Promising interviewees approval of the script without being able to deliver.

## Verify
- [ ] Signed releases for every voice used.
- [ ] Exact transcripts time-coded to audio.
- [ ] Edit log maps lines to sources.
- [ ] Legal review done where real allegations appear.
