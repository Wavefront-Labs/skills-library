# Licensing performance rights

## 1. Is it in copyright?
- **UK/EU**: author's life + 70 years (from end of year of death). Translations
  and adaptations have their own copyright.
- **US**: works published in or before 1929 are public domain as of 2025
  (date rolls forward each 1 January); later works depend on publication,
  registration and renewal — check, don't assume.
- Public domain play in a *copyright translation or edition* still needs rights
  for that version.

## 2. Find the rights holder
- **Licensing houses**: Concord Theatricals (Samuel French, Tams-Witmark, R&H),
  Music Theatre International (MTI), Theatrical Rights Worldwide (TRW),
  Broadway Licensing/Dramatists Play Service, Nick Hern Books (UK amateur),
  Josef Weinberger (UK).
- New or unpublished plays: the playwright's agent.

## 3. Apply
Provide: venue, capacity, dates and number of performances, ticket prices,
amateur/professional/educational status, territory. Apply **before** announcing
or selling tickets — many houses won't license if the area is restricted (e.g.
a professional tour nearby).

## 4. Fees and terms
- Plays: per-performance fee or percentage of box office (professional deals
  often a royalty % of gross).
- Musicals ("grand rights"): fees based on capacity/ticket price, plus rental of
  scores, scripts and sometimes orchestrations/tracks; deposits; return
  deadlines.
- Credit requirements (billing size, author's name), programme wording.
- **No changes** to text, lyrics, music, gender of characters or setting without
  written permission. Cuts generally need permission too.
- Recording/streaming is usually prohibited unless separately licensed.

## 5. Keep records
Licence contract, invoices, credit wording, return of materials, box-office
reports where % based.

## Gotchas
- "It's for charity/school" — still needs a licence.
- Using music in a straight play (pre-show songs) — separate music licensing.
- Advertising before the licence is granted.

## Verify
- [ ] Copyright status checked for the specific text/version.
- [ ] Licence signed before announcement.
- [ ] Credits and no-change terms followed.
