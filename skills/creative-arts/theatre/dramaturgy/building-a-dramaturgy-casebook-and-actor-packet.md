# Dramaturgy casebook and actor packet

The **casebook** (also "protocol") is the dramaturg's full research file for the
director and designers. The **actor packet** is a short, curated subset for the
company on day one.

## Casebook contents
1. **The author**: biography relevant to the play, other works, the play's
   composition and first production.
2. **Context**: historical, social, political, geographic background of the
   play's world *and* the time it was written.
3. **Production history**: notable productions, how they staged it, critical
   reception, images. Especially useful for avoiding (or quoting) famous
   choices.
4. **Text**: editions and variants (quartos/folio, revised drafts),
   translations compared, cuts others made.
5. **Critical perspectives**: key scholarship, summarised.
6. **Visual and sound research**: period images, maps, music.
7. **Glossary and lexicon**: every unfamiliar word, name, place, allusion,
   foreign phrase — with definition, pronunciation (IPA or plain respelling)
   and the page/line where it appears.
8. **Questions** for the director: points of interpretation the research raises.
All sources cited (author, title, date, URL/archive).

## Actor packet (10-20 pages max)
- One-page synopsis and character list with relationships.
- A timeline of events in the play's world.
- The glossary in script order (actors use this most).
- 1-2 pages of essential context, plus images.
- Pronunciation guide for names.
Deliver before or on day one; offer the full casebook on request.

## Format tips
- Shared drive with sections; a printed copy in the rehearsal room.
- Glossary as a table: `Page | Line | Word | Meaning | Pronunciation`.
- Update during rehearsal as questions come up (keep a "rehearsal questions"
  log with answers).

## Gotchas
- Dumping 200 pages on actors — curate.
- Uncited internet claims; verify against reputable sources.
- Research that pushes an interpretation the director hasn't chosen —
  present options.

## Verify
- [ ] Every glossary item has meaning, pronunciation and location.
- [ ] Actor packet under 20 pages.
- [ ] All sources cited.
