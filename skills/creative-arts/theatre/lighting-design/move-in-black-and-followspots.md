# Move-in-black and followspots

## Moving lights: move in black (MIB)
- Moving lights should **reposition (pan/tilt, colour, gobo, zoom) while dark**
  so the audience never sees them swing or change.
- **Mark cues**: on Eos, "Mark" (`[Mark]` in a cue or Auto Mark) pre-sets
  non-intensity parameters in a preceding cue where the fixture is at zero; the
  console then fades up already in position. Check mark timings (they must
  complete before the fade-up).
- Check in tech that no "live moves" happen unintentionally (watch from the
  house after every cue edit).

## Fixture selection
- **Profile/spot** (shutters, gobos, iris) for sharp specials and texture;
  **wash** (soft-edged, zoom) for washes; **beam** (narrow) for rock/aerial
  effects; **hybrid** units.
- Consider: output (lumens) for throw distance, noise level (fans in quiet
  plays), colour system (CMY vs LED emitters), weight/power, DMX footprint.
- **Gobos**: steel (opaque patterns, leaves/windows), glass (detail, colour),
  rotating gobos for movement; break-up gobos add texture to washes.

## Followspots
1. **Spot plan**: number of operators and positions (FOH, bridges).
2. **Followspot cue sheet** per operator: cue number, pick-up (who, where,
   entrance), size (head-and-shoulders, full body, iris), colour (frame number),
   intensity, fade times, fade out/blackout.
3. **Calling**: a spot caller (often the LD's assistant or DSM) calls on a
   dedicated channel: "Spot 1, standby pickup Christine stage left entrance, head
   and shoulders, frame 2 ... GO."
4. Operators practise pick-ups in tech with performers; mark positions with
   tape on the stage or numbers on their spot.
5. **Remote followspot systems** (e.g. tracking systems driving moving lights)
   reduce operator positions — set up and calibrate with the vendor.

## Gotchas
- Mark cues with zero time causing visible flicks.
- Moving-light fan noise in quiet scenes — choose quieter units or reduce fan
  modes.
- Followspots picking up late after a blackout entrance — glow tape marks and
  cue placement before the entrance.

## Verify
- [ ] No live moves visible in any cue.
- [ ] Spot cue sheets complete for each operator.
- [ ] Pick-ups rehearsed in tech.
