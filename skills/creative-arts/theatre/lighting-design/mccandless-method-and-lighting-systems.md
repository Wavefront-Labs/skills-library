# The McCandless method and lighting systems

## McCandless (A Method of Lighting the Stage, 1932)
- Divide the acting space into **areas** (commonly 2.4-3.6 m / 8-12 ft circles,
  overlapping) — e.g. 3 across x 2 or 3 deep on a proscenium stage.
- Light each area with **two front-diagonal sources**, each about **45° from the
  vertical** and **45° to each side** of the actor in plan (90° apart).
- Colour one side **warm** and the other **cool**: the balance shifts mood and
  suggests a motivated source (sun from one side) while giving facial modelling.
- Add **blending/toning** washes to smooth and colour overall.

## Systems thinking (contemporary practice)
Instead of (or as well as) areas, design **systems** — sets of fixtures from one
angle and colour covering the whole stage evenly:
- **Front light** (from FOH): visibility; flat if too frontal; at ~45° elevation.
- **High side light**: sculpts the body; key in dance.
- **Low side / shins**: legs and bodies (dance).
- **Backlight**: separation from background, halo; colour often saturated.
- **Top light/downlight**: isolation, drama.
- **Specials**: single-purpose (a chair, a window shaft).
- **Washes**: colour fields (e.g. a blue wash from above for night).
Each system is circuited/channelled by area so you can isolate parts of the
stage.

## Procedure
1. Divide the set into areas from the ground plan; mark actor positions and
   key moments.
2. Decide systems from the concept (naturalistic interior → strong motivated
   side/front from a window direction; abstract → side and back systems).
3. Calculate positions and beam sizes per area (beam angle and throw distance;
   overlap edges by ~1/3 for evenness).
4. Assign channels in a logical layout (e.g. system 1 = 1-9 areas, system 2 =
   11-19).
5. Draw the plot (see `creative-arts.theatre.lighting-design.usitt-lighting-graphics-symbols`)
   and paperwork.

## Gotchas
- Front light only → flat, two-dimensional.
- Gaps between areas ("dark holes") — check overlap at head height (~1.7 m).
- Too many systems for the dimmer/channel count or rig budget.

## Verify
- [ ] Areas cover the playing space with overlap at head height.
- [ ] Each system covers all relevant areas evenly.
- [ ] Channel layout logical by system and area.
