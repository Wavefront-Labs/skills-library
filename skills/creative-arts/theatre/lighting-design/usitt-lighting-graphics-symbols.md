# USITT lighting graphics (RP-1)

The USITT Recommended Practice for Theatrical Lighting Design Graphics (RP-1)
standardises how light plots and sections are drawn in North America; UK plots
follow similar conventions (often with manufacturer symbol libraries).

## Instrument symbols
- Each fixture type has a **symbol drawn to scale in plan**, its shape
  suggesting the unit (ellipsoidal/profile with barrel and lens, Fresnel,
  PAR, cyc light, moving light, LED wash).
- The symbol points in the **direction of focus** where possible (or a generic
  orientation with focus in paperwork).
- Different beam angles of the same family are distinguished by a label or
  symbol variant (e.g. Source Four 19°, 26°, 36°, 50°).

## Label layout around each unit (common RP-1 practice)
- **Unit number** inside or next to the symbol (numbered along each position,
  usually stage left to stage right, or from the centre out).
- **Channel number** in a circle/ellipse near the front of the unit.
- **Colour** (filter number) near the front/lens end.
- **Gobo**, **accessory**, **dimmer/address** and **circuit** near the unit,
  per the plot's legend.
- Keep the same relative positions for every unit; the legend illustrates them.

## Positions
- Each hanging position drawn as a line (pipe/bar) and **named**: e.g. FOH
  positions (Box Boom SL, 1st Cove), onstage electrics (1st Electric, 2nd
  Electric / UK LX1, LX2), booms, ladders, floor.
- Trim height and distance from the plaster/setting line noted per position.

## Sheet content
- **Legend/key** of all instrument types with symbols and their labels.
- **Title block**: production, venue, designer, assistant, date, scale (1/2"
  = 1'-0" or 1:25/1:50), revision.
- Set ground plan ghosted (light grey) under the plot, centre line and plaster/
  setting line.
- Notes: general notes about colour, gobos, special instructions.

## Tools
Vectorworks Spotlight (Lighting Device objects with label legends and data
linked to Lightwright), AutoCAD with manufacturer blocks, Capture for
visualisation.

## Gotchas
- Inconsistent label positions — electricians misread channels.
- Symbol not to scale — clashes on crowded bars.
- No legend — undecipherable for touring crews.

## Verify
- [ ] Every unit has unit number, channel, colour and type identifiable.
- [ ] Legend covers all symbols and label positions.
- [ ] Positions named with trims.
