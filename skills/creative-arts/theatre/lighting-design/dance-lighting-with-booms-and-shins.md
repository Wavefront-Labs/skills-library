# Dance lighting with booms and shins

Dance lighting prioritises **the body in space**: side light sculpts muscles and
movement; front light is used sparingly.

## The boom system
- A **boom** (vertical pipe on a base) in each wing (between legs), each side.
- Typical units per boom, bottom to top:
  - **Shins** (shin-busters): ~0.3-0.6 m (1-2 ft) above floor — light legs and
    feet, give lift; dancers pass close, so units must be safe and guarded.
  - **Mids**: ~1.2-1.5 m (4-5 ft) — torso.
  - **Heads**: ~2-2.5 m (7-8 ft) — head and shoulders, across the stage.
- Shutter **off the floor** (cut the beam just above stage level) so pools on
  the floor don't show; shutter off the opposite leg/masking.
- Each boom position focused across the stage to the opposite side; together
  they create "lanes" across each wing opening.
- **High side**/pipe ends from overhead bars add top-side angle.

## Colour
- Side light colours (often pale tints or saturated choices per piece), with a
  different colour per side or per height for options; LED or scrollers add
  flexibility.
- Backlight saturated for mood; front light minimal and neutral (or none).

## Other elements
- **Backlight** and **downlight** for separation and isolation.
- **Specials**: pools for solos, gobo textures on the floor (dance floors show
  patterns well), cyc washes.
- Dance floors (vinyl marley) — black absorbs light, grey/white reflects;
  choose with designer.

## Working with choreographers
- Learn the piece from video/rehearsal: entrances (dancers come out of the
  wings → lanes), spacing, key moments.
- Plot cues to music counts.
- Check boom units don't obstruct wing entrances; tape and pad boom bases.

## Gotchas
- Shins too bright — light spills into the audience's eyes from opposite wing.
- Booms blocking traffic or quick changes in the wings.
- Shutters not cut off the floor — distracting floor pools.

## Verify
- [ ] Each wing has shin/mid/head coverage both sides.
- [ ] Beams shuttered off floor and opposite masking.
- [ ] Booms safe (weighted bases, padding, glow tape).
