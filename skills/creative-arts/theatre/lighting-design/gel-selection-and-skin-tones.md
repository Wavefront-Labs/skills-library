# Gel selection and skin tones

## Swatch books
Lee Filters (numbered, e.g. L201 full CTB, L204 full CTO) and Rosco (Roscolux "R"
and Supergel numbers, e.g. R02 Bastard Amber, R60 No Color Blue) publish swatch
books showing each filter's **transmission %** and **spectral curve**.
- **Transmission** tells you how much light you lose (dark blues transmit
  under 10%; pale tints 70-90%).
- **Spectral curve** shows which wavelengths pass — two filters that look
  similar can render skin and fabrics very differently.

## Choosing
1. Start from the concept: time of day, temperature, motivated sources.
2. **Front light/key on faces**: pale tints (no colour or very light warm/cool:
   e.g. Lee 103 Straw, Lee 202 half CTB, Rosco R02 Bastard Amber, R51 Surprise
   Pink, R60 No Color Blue) — saturated colours on faces look ill.
3. **Side and back**: more saturated colour possible.
4. **Warm/cool pairs** (McCandless) — e.g. a pale amber vs a pale steel blue.
5. Test on real skin and costume swatches under the actual fixture (tungsten
   at full and dimmed — dimming tungsten warms the light, shifting the gel).

## Skin tones
- Light should flatter a **range of skin tones** in the cast: very saturated
  ambers or blues can grey darker skin or wash out lighter skin.
- Pinks, lavenders and pale warm tints (e.g. Lee 162 Bastard Amber, Rosco
  R33 No Color Pink, Lee 136 Pale Lavender) are common "skin-friendly" choices;
  test with each performer.
- Increase front/side level or add a tint rather than relying on saturated
  key colours; check with the costume designer.

## Correction and diffusion
- **CTO** (colour temperature orange) warms; **CTB** (blue) cools — full,
  1/2, 1/4, 1/8 strengths; used to match LED and tungsten or daylight.
- **Diffusion** (Lee 250, Rosco R119 etc.) softens edges and spreads beams;
  "silk" (directional) diffusion stretches beams.

## Gotchas
- Choosing from the swatch book by eye under fluorescent office light.
- Forgetting dimmed tungsten shifts warm.
- Deep blues fading quickly under heat — replace regularly (or use LED).

## Verify
- [ ] Gel choices tested on skin/costume under the fixture.
- [ ] Faces lit with tints that suit all performers.
- [ ] Colour call (gel order) lists filter number, size and quantity.
