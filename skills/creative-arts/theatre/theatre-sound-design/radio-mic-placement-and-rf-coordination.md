# Radio mic placement and RF coordination

## Mic placement
- **Hairline/forehead** placement (capsule just at or in front of the hairline,
  centre of forehead) is standard for musicals: consistent distance to the
  mouth, out of the way of breath, hidden in hair/wigs.
- **Ear-hook/cheek** booms for higher gain-before-feedback (rock shows) — more
  visible.
- **Chest/lapel** placement in plays where hair doesn't allow — tonal change when
  the head turns.
- Run cable through wig netting or hair, secured with clips/tape/elastic;
  transmitter pack in a mic belt (small of the back or thigh), cable strain-
  relieved.
- Coordinate with wigs and costume: pack location, cable routes, hats.
- **Sweat protection**: condoms/finger cots or moisture barriers on packs,
  windscreens/covers on capsules; dry and clean after each show.
- **Hygiene**: sanitise mics and packs between actors (alcohol wipes suitable for
  the materials); no sharing without cleaning.

## RF coordination
1. **Licensing**: UK — Ofcom PMSE licences (shared channel 38 or coordinated
   assignments); US — FCC rules (post-600 MHz auction bands, licensed/unlicensed
   operation); check local rules.
2. Scan the venue's RF environment (receivers' scan or a spectrum analyser).
3. Use coordination software (Shure Wireless Workbench, Sennheiser WSM) to
   compute **intermodulation-free** frequency sets for all transmitters
   (including IEMs and comms).
4. Antenna distribution: directional antennas (paddles/helicals) positioned with
   line-of-sight to the stage; low-loss cable; antenna combiners for IEMs.
5. Keep a frequency plot; avoid TV channels and venue systems.

## Daily mic check
- Fresh or fully charged batteries per show (log changes); check each mic:
  RF signal, battery, audio (actor speaks/sings a line) via talkback.
- Spares ready: spare packs/elements; a mic swap procedure mid-show with the
  mic No.2 / A2 (the backstage sound person).

## Gotchas
- Batteries half-used from rehearsal.
- Mics taped under a costume collar rubbing (rustle).
- Unlicensed frequencies or IEMs intermodulating with mics.

## Verify
- [ ] Coordinated, licensed frequency plan.
- [ ] Mic plot per actor with pack/cable positions agreed with wigs/costume.
- [ ] Daily check log with battery changes.
