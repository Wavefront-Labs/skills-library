# Script sound breakdown and underscore

Approaches from Kaye and LeBrecht (*Sound and Music for the Theatre*) and Gareth Fry
(*Sound Design for the Stage*).

## 1. Script breakdown
Go through the script and list every sound by page:
| Page | Cue idea | Type | Source/where | Notes |
Types: **spot effects** (doorbell, gunshot, phone — synchronised to action),
**atmospheres** (rain, city, birdsong — continuous beds), **music** (preshow,
transitions, underscore, songs), **reinforcement** (voice mics), **practical**
(onstage radios). Mark motivated sounds (the script says so) and opportunities
(transitions, mood).

## 2. Concept
Discuss with the director: realism vs abstraction, period, where sound comes
from (speaker positions = perceived sources), silence as a tool. Write a short
concept and reference playlist.

## 3. Underscore
- Underscore sits under dialogue to shape mood; keep it **out of the speech
  frequency range** where possible (avoid busy mids around 1-4 kHz under words),
  low level, sparse textures, few melodic hooks.
- Compose/arrange to scene length with **loopable** sections and **stings**;
  build stems so you can rebalance in tech.
- Place entries and exits on story beats (not mid-line); fade under
  dialogue rather than stopping abruptly.
- Agree with the director where *not* to use underscore.

## 4. Preshow and interval
- Preshow music sets world and period; a playlist timed to the house opening
  (~30 min), ending with a clear transition into the show's first cue.
- Clear licensing for any commercial recordings (see music licensing).

## 5. Effects
- **Field recording**: record real sources (doors in the venue, specific
  locations) with a portable recorder (e.g. Zoom/Sound Devices) at 48 kHz/24-bit;
  record multiple takes and room tone.
- Libraries (Pro Sound Effects, BBC Sound Effects archive licensing terms vary).
- Edit/layer in a DAW; consider how it will sound from the speaker position in
  the room.

## Gotchas
- Underscore masking dialogue — test with actors in the space.
- Effects that sound right on headphones but wrong in the theatre.
- Unlicensed music.

## Verify
- [ ] Breakdown lists every sound with type and page.
- [ ] Underscore tested under dialogue in the space.
- [ ] All music and effects licensed or original.
