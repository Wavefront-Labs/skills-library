# Healthy belting and mix voice

Study with a qualified voice teacher; terminology varies (Estill Voice Training,
LoVetri Somatic Voicework, CCM pedagogy).

## Belt
Belt is a loud, bright, speech-like sound carried higher than speech, with a
"chest-dominant" (thyroarytenoid-dominant) quality and a high closed quotient.
**Healthy set-up** (common elements):
1. **Speech-based**: start from a calling voice ("Hey!", "Yeah!"), not a
   shout.
2. **Twang** (Estill: aryepiglottic sphincter narrowing): a bright, focused
   ring that increases loudness efficiently.
3. **Retracted false folds** (silent-laugh feeling) — avoid constriction.
4. **Raised larynx and tongue position** suitable for the style; jaw free.
5. **Vowel modification**: open, speech-like vowels ("eh", "ah" leaning towards
   speech), shorter durations on the highest notes.
6. **Breath**: steady, not over-pressurised; support from the torso.
Range: typical female belts commonly go to around C5-D5 (higher in contemporary
pop/rock styles); male belts to around A4-C5 and beyond in rock — but voices vary.

## Mix
A blend between chest (TA-dominant) and head (CT-dominant) qualities: speech-like
and bright but with less weight than full belt — allows easier access higher
and smooth registration.
**Exercises**: sirens on "ng" through the passaggio; "nay-nay" bright
twangy patterns up a fifth; speak-sing lines on pitch then sustain; gradually add
or remove weight (Estill: thick vs thin fold mass; thyroid tilt vs cricoid tilt).

## Estill figures (selected)
Estill Voice Training teaches control of structures (true folds onset/body-cover,
false folds, thyroid and cricoid cartilages, larynx height, velum, tongue,
aryepiglottic sphincter, jaw, lips, head/neck and torso anchoring) combined
into qualities: **Speech, Falsetto, Sob, Twang, Opera, Belt**.

## Warning signs (stop and get help)
Pain, persistent hoarseness, loss of top notes, needing to push harder each show,
"two-note" sounds — see a laryngologist and voice teacher (see
`creative-arts.theatre.voice-and-speech.vocal-hygiene-and-injury-warning-signs`).

## Gotchas
- Pulling chest voice up without twang or retraction (yelling).
- Imitating cast recordings mixed with studio effects.
- Belting cold.

## Verify
- [ ] Belt sound produced without throat pain or constriction.
- [ ] Smooth mix through the passaggio on sirens.
- [ ] Teacher has checked your set-up.
