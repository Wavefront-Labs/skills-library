# Swing book and covering tracks

A **swing** covers several ensemble **tracks** (each ensemble member's full show
path) and goes on when someone is off, often at short notice. Understudies/covers
cover principal roles while performing their own track.

## The swing book
For each track you cover, a section (paper binder or tablet app) with:
1. **Track sheet**: every entrance/exit with side and wing, numbers, scene
   changes the track moves, props handled, quick changes (where, dresser).
2. **Spacing charts**: stage maps per number with your position at key counts
   (numbers on the stage floor — spacing numbers — and offsets), formations
   and "traffic" (who passes whom, where).
3. **Choreography notes** per number (counts, track-specific variations).
4. **Vocal part**: which harmony line (e.g. Tenor 2, Alto 1) per number, solos/
   lines, mic notes.
5. **Costume and wig** notes.
6. **Partnering**: who you dance/lift with and safety moments.
Colour-code by track; diagrams beat prose.

## Learning
- Watch from the wings and the house; observe each track repeatedly.
- **Harmony learning**: get rehearsal tracks (part-predominant recordings)
  or learn with the MD/assistant MD; sing each line against the others; note
  where tracks switch parts.
- Run tracks in your head with music daily; physically in understudy/swing
  rehearsals.
- The **dance captain** maintains choreography standards, teaches swings,
  runs put-ins, and gives notes; ask them early.

## Going on
- Put-in rehearsal if time; otherwise talk-through with dance captain/SM.
- Check props, costume, mic; brief partners on any differences.
- Afterward: note problems for the dance captain.

## Gotchas
- Learning steps but not traffic — collisions are the real danger.
- Not knowing which part to sing when tracks share harmonies.
- Out-of-date book after choreography changes.

## Verify
- [ ] Book section for every covered track with spacing, traffic, vocals, props.
- [ ] Vocal parts learned for each track.
- [ ] Dance captain has checked each track at least once.
