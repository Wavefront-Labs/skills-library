# Writing an exhibition press release

## Structure (one page, 400-600 words)
1. **Header**: "For immediate release" or embargo date; venue logo.
2. **Headline**: artist name + exhibition title + venue. Subhead: one line on what it is.
3. **Key facts block**: dates (opening reception date and time), address, opening hours, admission.
4. **Lead paragraph**: who, what, where, when, why it matters now, in plain language.
5. **The work**: 2-3 paragraphs from the project statement, rewritten for a general reader; process and context.
6. **Quote** from the artist and/or curator (a sentence that a journalist can lift).
7. **Events**: talks, book signing, tours.
8. **Bio** (100 words).
9. **Notes to editors**: press images available, interviews available, image list with captions.
10. **Press contact**: name, email, phone.

## Images
- 5-10 high-resolution JPEGs (300 ppi at A4, sRGB), plus web versions, in a download link (not attached).
- Caption format: `Jane Doe, Tideline #12, 2025. Archival pigment print. Courtesy the artist and Gallery X.`
- State usage terms: "for editorial use in connection with the exhibition only".

## Timing
- Long-lead (monthlies): 3 months ahead. Weeklies and online listings: 3-4 weeks. Reminder a week before opening.

## Verify
- [ ] Dates, address and contact are correct and repeated nowhere inconsistently; every image has a caption with credit.
