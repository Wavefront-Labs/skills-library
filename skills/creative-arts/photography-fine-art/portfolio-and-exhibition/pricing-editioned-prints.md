# Pricing editioned prints

## Find the level
- Compare with artists at a similar career stage (exhibition history, collections, press), same medium, size and region: gallery websites, fair price lists, Artsy. Your price must be defensible to a collector who checks.
- Emerging artists' editioned prints commonly start in the low hundreds to low thousands per print depending on size and market; price on comparables, not on costs alone.
- Floor: cost of production (print, framing, mat) x 2.5-3, plus your time. If comparables are below your floor, change size/format.

## Structure
- **Size tiers**: e.g. 30x40 cm edition of 7, 60x80 cm edition of 5, 100x130 cm edition of 3; price rises faster than area for large prints (more scarce, higher production cost).
- **Edition stepping**: raise prices as an edition sells (e.g. prints 1-3 at base, 4-5 +20%, last print +50% or held). Announce the stepping in the price list.
- **Artist's proofs** (usually 1-2 per size) priced at the top step or withheld.
- Unframed vs framed: framed price = unframed + framing cost at retail; state which on the list.

## Consistency
- **One retail price everywhere**: your studio, website, gallery, fair. Undercutting your gallery ends the relationship. Galleries typically take 40-50% commission, so retail must already include that.
- Never lower published prices; reduce size or offer a smaller edition instead. Discounts (10% to museums, repeat collectors) at the gallery's discretion and agreed in the consignment agreement.
- Price list: title, year, medium, sizes, edition, price, framed/unframed, currency, tax, date.

## Verify
- [ ] Prices match on every channel, each step and AP is documented, and the gallery split still covers production cost at the lowest step.
