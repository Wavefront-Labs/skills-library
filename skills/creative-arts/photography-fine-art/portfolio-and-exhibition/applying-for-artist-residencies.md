# Applying for artist residencies

## Types
- **Funded/fellowship** (stipend, travel, accommodation): most competitive.
- **Free** (accommodation and studio provided, no stipend).
- **Fee-based** (artist pays): judge value carefully; some are well run, others are paid holidays.
- Photography-specific facilities: darkroom, large-format scanners, printers, alternative process labs; or location/research-based residencies (archives, landscapes, institutions).
- Directories: Alliance of Artists Communities, Res Artis, TransArtists, CaFE listings.

## Fit
- Why this place: the location, community, archive or facility must matter for the project. A generic application loses.
- Check facilities lists precisely (enlarger formats, chemistry provided, printer sizes), length (2 weeks-12 months), family/accessibility policies, and expectations (open studio, talk, workshop, donation of a work).

## Application
1. **Proposal**: what you will do there and why there, outcomes, 300-500 words.
2. **Work samples**: 10-20 images closely related to the proposal.
3. **CV, statement, references** as required.
4. Budget your own costs: travel, materials, lost income.

## During
- Plan a realistic schedule; leave time to respond to the place.
- Document process; make contacts with local artists and institutions; deliver the open studio or talk well.

## After
- Thank-you and a short report with images; credit the residency in CV and future exhibitions.

## Verify
- [ ] Proposal names specific resources at the residency, the cost/benefit is calculated, and samples match the proposed work.
