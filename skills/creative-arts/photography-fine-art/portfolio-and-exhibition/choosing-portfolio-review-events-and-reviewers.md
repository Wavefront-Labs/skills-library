# Choosing portfolio review events and reviewers

## Know your goal first
- Exhibition (galleries, museums, festivals), publication (book publishers, magazines), representation (gallery), feedback for an unfinished project, or network building. Choose reviewers accordingly.

## Events
- **FotoFest International Portfolio Review** (Houston, biennial; large, international).
- **Review Santa Fe** (CENTER, juried selection; the application itself is a competition).
- **Photolucida** (Portland; reviewer list published, sessions chosen by preference).
- **PhotoNOLA**, **Medium Festival**, **Les Rencontres d'Arles** portfolio reviews, **LensCulture** online reviews, **SPE** conference reviews, plus regional review days.
- Online reviews are cheaper and good for early feedback; in-person events build relationships.

## Evaluating an event
- Published reviewer list (names, institutions), number of sessions per photographer (often 8-20 x 20 min), cost (registration, travel, hotel), the selection process, and alumni outcomes.
- Budget realistically: a major review with travel often costs USD 1,500-3,500.

## Selecting reviewers
1. Research each: their programme, recent exhibitions/books, stated interests in the event bio.
2. Score fit 1-3; rank. Include a mix: 50% strong-fit decision makers, 30% people for critical feedback, 20% stretch contacts.
3. Avoid reviewers whose programme never shows your kind of work (a commercial gallery of large colour landscapes for a small archival-process project).
4. Submit preferences early; some events allocate on registration order.

## Verify
- [ ] Every chosen reviewer has a written one-line reason tied to your goal and a recent example from their programme.
