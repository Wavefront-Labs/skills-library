# Art fairs and print fairs

## Types
- **Gallery fairs** (Paris Photo, AIPAD The Photography Show, Photo London, Unseen): exhibitors are galleries; artists participate through representation. Some have emerging or artist sections.
- **Artist-run and affordable fairs** (Affordable Art Fair, print fairs, open studios): direct artist booths.
- **Photobook/print fairs** (Offprint, Polycopies, Miss Read, NY Art Book Fair): tables for books, zines and small prints.

## Decide with numbers
- Costs: booth fee, walls/lighting extras, travel, shipping, framing, staff, card fees, insurance. Break-even sales = total costs / average net per sale.
- Look at the fair's visitor profile and past exhibitors' price levels.

## Booth
- Hang fewer works than you think: 6-12 framed works on a 3x3 m booth; one strong "signature" piece visible from the aisle.
- Consistent framing; title labels with price or a price list available on request (follow fair rules).
- Browser bin of unframed matted prints in sleeves for smaller sales; books and postcards for entry-level buyers.
- Lighting: check the booth's spots and bring adapters/extra lamps if allowed.

## Sales admin
- Card reader, invoice book or app, edition ledger on hand (never sell an edition number twice), packing materials, delivery options and certificates of authenticity.
- Collect contacts (with consent) for a mailing list.

## Follow-up
- Thank buyers within a week; ship on time; send serious enquirers a follow-up with images and prices.

## Verify
- [ ] Booth plan, inventory list with edition numbers, price list and payment setup ready before load-in; post-fair tally against break-even.
