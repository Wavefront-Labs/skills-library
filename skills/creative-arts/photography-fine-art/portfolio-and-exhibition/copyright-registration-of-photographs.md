# Copyright registration of photographs (US)

Copyright exists from creation, but in the US registration is required before suing for infringement of US works, and statutory damages and attorney's fees are available only if registered **before the infringement** or within **three months of first publication** (17 U.S.C. 412). Other countries generally have no registration system; keep dated evidence instead.

## Group options (Circular 42; eCO online system)
- **GRPPH: Group registration of published photographs**: up to 750 photos by the same photographer, all published within the **same calendar year**. The application gives each photo's title and publication date.
- **GRUPH: Group registration of unpublished photographs**: up to 750 unpublished photos by the same photographer.
- A single photograph: Standard Application.
- Fee per application (check current fee schedule at copyright.gov).

## Steps (eCO)
1. Choose Register a group of published/unpublished photographs.
2. Claimant, author (must be the same individual for group options; works made for hire excluded).
3. Titles: a list of file names/titles; for GRPPH also the publication date for each (via the provided spreadsheet template).
4. Upload deposit copies: JPEGs (the Office accepts lower-resolution files; sufficient to identify the image).
5. Pay; keep the case number. Certificate arrives months later; the effective date is the date the Office received the complete application.

## Workflow
- Unpublished register quarterly (before posting online); published register within three months of publication.
- Lightroom: a smart collection per quarter "Not yet registered"; add keyword `registered-2026-Q3` and the case number in a metadata field.
- Online posting may count as publication depending on circumstances; when unsure, register as unpublished before posting.

## Verify
- [ ] Every exhibited, published or licensed image has a registration case number recorded in its metadata or inventory.
