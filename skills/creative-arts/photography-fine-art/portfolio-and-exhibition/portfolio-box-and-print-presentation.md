# Portfolio box and print presentation

## Box
- Archival clamshell or drop-front box (museum box) in a size that fits the prints with 1-2 cm margin: common 11x14 in, 13x19 in (A3+), 16x20 in. Lightweight enough to carry on a plane.
- Black or grey cloth; your name discreetly inside the lid or a label on the spine.

## Prints
- **15-20 prints** of one project (a second project at most as a separate small set).
- Same paper and size throughout; image size consistent, generous white border (prints are handled without mats in reviews).
- Prints must be at the quality of an exhibition print: the review is judged on the object.
- Unmounted prints on heavy paper, or prints dry-mounted to thin board for handling. Avoid sleeves that glare under event lights; if sleeved, use matte polyester or none at all.

## Order and handling
- In sequence order; the first and last are the strongest arguments.
- Interleave with tissue only if prints are delicate; tissue slows a 20-minute review.
- Pencil on the back: title, date, your name, sequence number.
- Bring cotton gloves only for delicate prints; most reviewers handle prints by the edges with clean hands.

## In the session
- Hand over the stack; let the reviewer look first, then talk. Bring a tablet as backup for other projects, not as the main presentation.
- Include a short project statement and leave-behinds in the box lid.

## Verify
- [ ] Every print is clean, flat, consistently sized and labelled on the back, and the box opens to the first image the right way up.
