# Writing an artist bio

A bio is about **you** (third person, factual); a statement is about **the work**.

## Content order
1. Name, discipline and where you live and work.
2. One sentence on what your work is about (from the statement).
3. Selected achievements: 2-4 most significant exhibitions, books, awards, collections.
4. Education or training (optional for 50-word versions).
5. Optional: a human detail related to the work (background, profession before photography).

## Lengths
- **50 words**: catalogue entries, social, open call forms.
- **100 words**: press releases, festival programmes.
- **250 words**: website, grant applications.

## Example (50 words)
"Jane Doe is a photographer based in Glasgow. Her large-format work examines how coastal communities adapt to erosion. She has exhibited at Street Level Photoworks and Fotofestiwal Lodz, and her book *Tideline* (2025) won the XYZ Award. Her work is held in the collection of the National Galleries of Scotland."

## Style
- Third person, present tense, name used once then "she/he/they".
- Specific venue names, not "numerous international exhibitions".
- Keep consistent with CV facts; update when a major item changes.
- Provide a headshot and an image credit line alongside the bio for press.

## Verify
- [ ] All three lengths exist, facts match the CV, and none uses unverifiable superlatives.
