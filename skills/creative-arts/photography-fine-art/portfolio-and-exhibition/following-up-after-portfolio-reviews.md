# Following up after portfolio reviews

## During the event
- After each session write: reviewer, date, their main comments, any requests ("send me the full set", "apply to our open call in March"), named contacts they suggested, and your follow-up action.

## Within one week
- Short personal email to each reviewer: thank them, refer to one specific thing they said, attach 3-5 small JPEGs (1500 px, sRGB) or a link to the project page, and deliver exactly what they asked for (PDF, dummy, print price list).
- Subject line: `Review Santa Fe 2026 - Name - Project Title`.
- No large attachments; total under 5 MB.

## Track
- Spreadsheet or CRM: name, institution, event, date, feedback summary, interest level (1-3), next action, next contact date.
- Act on the suggestions you agree with and tell the reviewer later what changed.

## Long term
- Update interested reviewers when something real happens: a new body of work completed, a book, a major exhibition. About 1-2 times a year; avoid newsletters to individual reviewers unless they opted in.
- Re-meet at future reviews to show development: relationships often take 2-3 meetings before an offer.

## Etiquette
- Never pressure a reviewer about decisions; accept non-replies.
- Reviewers' personal contact details are not for mailing lists without consent.

## Verify
- [ ] Every reviewer has a sent follow-up within seven days and a next-contact date in the tracker.
