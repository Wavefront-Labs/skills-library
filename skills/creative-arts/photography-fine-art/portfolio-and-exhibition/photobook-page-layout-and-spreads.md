# Photobook page layout and spreads

## Document setup (InDesign)
- New Document: Facing Pages on, trim size (e.g. 240 x 300 mm), page count in signature multiples.
- **Bleed 3 mm** (0.125 in) on outer edges (or as the printer specifies); Slug optional for notes.
- Margins: inner (gutter) larger than outer for perfect-bound books; a consistent master grid for image boxes.

## Image placement patterns
- One image per spread on the **recto** (right), blank verso: quiet, classic.
- Facing pairs (one per page) for dialogue.
- Full-bleed for immersion, sparingly; check trimming tolerance (+/- 1.5 mm).
- Across-the-gutter spreads only with lay-flat or Swiss binding, and never with a face or key element in the gutter.
- Consistent image positions across pages build calm; variation creates rhythm.

## Typography
- One typeface family, two sizes; text on its own pages, not over images.
- Captions: in the back (plate list with page numbers) or small on page. Titles, statement, colophon (printer, paper, typeface, edition size, ISBN).

## Image files
- Convert to the printer's specified output (usually CMYK via their ICC profile such as FOGRA39/FOGRA51 or GRACoL, or RGB with their conversion). 300 ppi at placed size; effective ppi visible in InDesign Links panel.
- Black-and-white: discuss duotone/tritone or 4-colour B&W with the printer.

## Preflight and export
- Preflight panel: no missing links, no RGB in CMYK workflow (if required), no overset text.
- Export PDF/X-1a or PDF/X-4 as the printer requests, with bleed and crop marks.

## Verify
- [ ] Preflight green, every placed image at >= 300 effective ppi, and full-bleed images extend into the bleed on all trim edges.
