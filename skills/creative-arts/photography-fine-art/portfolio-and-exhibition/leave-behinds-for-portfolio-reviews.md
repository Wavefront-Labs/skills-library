# Leave-behinds for portfolio reviews

A leave-behind reminds a reviewer of your work weeks later when they sort what they collected. It must be visual first and flat enough to file.

## Formats
- **Postcard or card set**: 1-5 images, A6/5x7 in, heavy matte stock, contact on the back.
- **Folded mini-zine** or **promo booklet** (8-16 pages): a short sequence plus statement. Most memorable; costs more.
- **Single print card** with a key image and short statement on the reverse.
- Avoid USB drives and loose sheets; reviewers rarely use them.

## Content
- Your name, project title, one key image (the one you want them to remember), 2-3 line statement, website, email, Instagram handle, and the review event/date (helps them recall the session).
- Match print quality to your work: soft colour and poor blacks undercut the prints they just saw.

## Quantity
- One per reviewer plus 20-30% extra for people met in passing; a smaller card for informal contacts.

## Use
- Hand over at the end of the session, after asking whether they would like one; some reviewers prefer a follow-up email only.
- Note the reviewer's name and one line of their feedback on your session sheet immediately after the meeting.

## Verify
- [ ] A stranger holding only the leave-behind can identify the project, the look of the work and how to contact you.
