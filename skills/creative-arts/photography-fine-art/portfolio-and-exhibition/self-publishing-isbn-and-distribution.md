# Self-publishing, ISBN and distribution

## Budget and edition
- Typical photobook runs: 300-1,000 copies. Get 3+ quotes on identical specs (size, pages, paper, binding, colour, run) at two run lengths; unit cost drops sharply with run length but unsold stock is the main risk.
- Budget lines: design, separations/proofs, print, shipping/freight, ISBN, launch, postage, distributor/shop discounts, storage.

## ISBN
- Buy from your national agency (Bowker/myidentifiers in the US, Nielsen in the UK, others via isbn-international.org). One ISBN per format/edition (special edition with print = separate ISBN).
- Register title metadata with the agency; generate an **EAN-13 barcode** with price add-on if desired, placed on the back cover.
- ISSN does not apply to books.

## Legal deposit
- Many countries require free copies to national libraries (UK: British Library within a month of publication plus requests from other legal deposit libraries; US: Library of Congress mandatory deposit within three months of publication for works published in the US under 17 U.S.C. 407).

## Pricing
- Retail price at roughly 3-5 x unit cost; shops take 35-45%, distributors 55-65% of retail.

## Distribution
- Direct sales (website, Bandcamp-style shops, fairs) give best margin.
- Specialist photobook shops (consignment or wholesale), photobook fairs (Offprint, Polycopies, NY Art Book Fair, Miss Read), distributors for wider reach.
- Special editions with a signed print fund the run.

## Fulfilment
- Packaging: rigid mailers with corner protection; tracking for international; calculate postage before setting price.

- [ ] ISBN metadata registered and matching the colophon, legal deposit copies sent, and price covers unit cost after trade discount.
