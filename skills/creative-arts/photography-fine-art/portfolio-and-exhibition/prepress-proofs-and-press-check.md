# Prepress, proofs and press check

## Prepress
- Agree specs: paper (and its ICC/characterisation, e.g. FOGRA51 coated, FOGRA52 uncoated), screening (AM 175 lpi or FM/stochastic for fine detail), ink (CMYK, extra blacks/greys for B&W tritone), varnish.
- Printer's prepress team often does separations from your RGB files: provide RGB with embedded profile plus reference prints, or separate yourself with their profile.

## Proofs
- **Digital contract proof** (inkjet proof to ISO 12647-7 with a control strip/Fogra media wedge): simulates press colour. Check the control strip verifies.
- **Wet proof / press proof**: printed on the real press and paper; best for B&W books and tricky papers.
- Mark proofs with clear instructions: "shadows open +5%", "less magenta in skin", circled areas. Reference the image number.

## Press check (on site)
1. Bring signed proofs, reference prints and a loupe; judge only in the printer's D50 viewing booth.
2. Check the first sheet off each signature against the proof under D50: overall density, colour balance, shadow detail, highlight dots, registration (sharpness of fine detail), hickeys (spots), ink set-off.
3. Adjust: ink keys per zone change density across the sheet in columns; a change for one image affects others in the same column.
4. Sign the approved sheet (OK sheet); press runs to match it.
5. Check mid-run sheets periodically on a long run.

## Gotchas
- Uncoated paper darkens and dulls images (dot gain); lighten files accordingly.
- Varnish and lamination shift appearance; proof with them if possible.

## Verify
- [ ] Signed OK sheets for every signature kept with notes, and a running sheet compared at end of run.
