# Editing a series down

Editing (choosing) is separate from sequencing (ordering) but they inform each other. Target sizes: portfolio review 15-20 prints; gallery wall 10-25; book 40-80; open call 10-20.

## Passes
1. **Generous first pass** from contact sheets/Grid view: everything that could belong to the project, ignoring quality ranking. Typically 10-20% of frames.
2. **Duplicates out**: one frame per situation unless the repetition is the point (typology).
3. **Against the statement**: does each image carry the project's idea? An image that is beautiful but off-topic is a different project. Keep a parking collection, not a delete.
4. **Weak link test**: remove any image that you need to explain. The series is judged by its weakest image.
5. **Range check**: vary scale (wide, medium, detail), density (busy/quiet), light, and subject type so the series has rhythm.

## Tools
- Small work prints (4x6 or 5x7) on a table or wall (see the work-print wall skill); physical edits beat screens for seeing the group.
- Lightroom: collection `Edit v1`, `Edit v2`; rate inside the collection; never delete from the master.

## Outside eyes
- Show a tight edit to 2-3 trusted viewers separately; note which images each mentions unprompted, and which they skip. Images nobody mentions are candidates to cut.
- Ask "what is this about?" before explaining; their answer tests the edit.

## Killing darlings
- Favourite images tied to the experience of making them (hard hike, rare moment) often fail to serve the series. Cut and see if anyone misses them.

## Document
- Keep dated edit versions (`Edit 2026-10 v3`) to retrace decisions; record final list with titles.

## Verify
- [ ] Every image survives the "would the series be weaker without it?" test; the edit fits the target count.
