# Hanging hardware and security fixings

## On the frame
- **D-rings** (two, screwed into the frame back about 1/4-1/3 down from the top): hang on two hooks or screws for a level, stable picture. Avoid hanging wire for public shows: frames tilt and can be lifted off.
- **Mirror plates** (keyhole or flat): screw into frame back, project beyond the edge; screw through into the wall. Paint them the wall colour. Standard for galleries with security needs.
- **Security mirror plates/T-screws**: one keyhole plate with a special screw locked by a key (T-bar or tamper-proof) at the base of the frame.
- **Z-clips / French cleats**: for heavy or frameless (face-mounted, metal) works; a matching cleat on the wall.
- Frameless works (dibond, acrylic face mounts) arrive with a subframe; confirm hanging method with the mounting lab.

## On the wall
| Wall | Fixing |
|---|---|
| Plasterboard (drywall) | Self-drilling plasterboard anchors or hollow-wall anchors; for heavy works find studs |
| Timber stud / ply-backed gallery walls | Wood screws directly |
| Masonry, brick, concrete | Drill with masonry bit, wall plug, screw |
| No drilling allowed | Picture rail systems (cable + hook), rated per hook load |

## Install procedure
1. Measure from frame top to D-ring/hanger point (drop). Mark wall: centre height + half frame height - drop.
2. Level across the two marks with a laser level; drill and fix.
3. Hang, check level, add bumpers (clear felt pads) at bottom corners so the frame sits flat and doesn't mark the wall.
4. Fit security screws last; log key location.

## Gotchas
- Rated loads of anchors drop with repeated hanging; use new holes or fill and re-drill.
- Never fix through glazing clips or into the image package.

## Verify
- [ ] Each work level, tight to the wall, cannot be lifted off the hooks, and fixing type matches the wall material.
