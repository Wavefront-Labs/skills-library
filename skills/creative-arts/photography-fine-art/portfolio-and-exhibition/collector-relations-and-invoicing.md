# Collector relations and invoicing

## Invoice (each sale)
- Your name/studio, address, tax ID/VAT number if registered, invoice number and date.
- Buyer name and address (needed for provenance).
- Work: title, year, medium, size, edition number (e.g. 3/7), framed/unframed, price, discount, shipping, sales tax/VAT, total, currency.
- Payment terms and methods; "title passes on receipt of full payment"; note that copyright stays with the artist.
- Keep sales tax/VAT rules for your jurisdiction: artists may need to charge sales tax on direct sales; art often has special VAT rates or schemes in the UK/EU. Check with an accountant.

## With the print
- Certificate of authenticity (matching edition number), signed print, care instructions (light levels, no direct sun, framing materials).
- Professional packing and tracked, insured shipping.

## Records (provenance)
- Inventory record per work: buyer, date, price, edition number, invoice number. This is the provenance chain and protects edition integrity.

## Relationship
- Thank-you note after delivery. Updates when a significant event happens (museum acquisition, book, solo show), 1-3 times a year.
- Offer collectors first view of new work or prints from a new edition before public release; invitations to openings.
- Respect privacy: never publish a collector's name without permission.
- Through a gallery: the gallery owns the collector relationship; do not contact gallery clients directly to sell around them.

## Verify
- [ ] Each sale has an invoice, COA and inventory entry with the same edition number, and the collector is on the contact list with consent recorded.
