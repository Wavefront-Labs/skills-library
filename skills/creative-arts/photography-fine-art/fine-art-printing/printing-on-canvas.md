# Printing on canvas

## Media
Inkjet canvases (Breathing Color Lyve, Hahnemuhle Daguerre/Monet Canvas, Epson Exhibition Canvas) in matte or
satin/gloss; poly-cotton or cotton. Use the manufacturer's ICC profile and media settings; increase platen gap.

## File preparation for gallery wrap
- Add extra image around the edges for the wrap depth: stretcher depth 38 mm (1.5 in) + 10-15 mm to staple.
- **Mirrored edges** (reflect the outer strip) or solid colour edges if you don't want the image to wrap.
- Size at the printer's native resolution (Epson 360/720 ppi, Canon 300/600).

## Coating
1. Let the print dry 12-24 h (outgassing).
2. Apply varnish (Breathing Color Timeless/Glamour 2, Hahnemuhle Protective Spray) with a foam roller or HVLP
   sprayer in thin even coats; choose matte/satin/gloss finish.
3. Coating protects from abrasion, UV and moisture and prevents cracking when stretching.

## Stretching
1. Stretcher bars square (measure diagonals).
2. Lay canvas face down, stretcher on top; staple centres of opposing sides first, work outward alternating.
3. Fold corners neatly (hospital fold); keybars/wedges for tension later.
4. Back with dust cover; add hanging wire.

## Gotchas
- Uncoated canvas cracks along the fold, especially in dark areas.
- Canvas inkjet doesn't show fine detail like paper - suits painterly images.

## Verify
- [ ] Coating even (no streaks), no cracks at edges, canvas tight and square.
