# Window mats and hinge mounting

## Materials
100% cotton museum board (4-ply or 8-ply), PAT-passed; Japanese tissue (kozo); wheat starch paste; Mylar/
polyester photo corners; bone folder; mat cutter.

## Window mat
1. Mat package: window mat hinged to a backing board along the top edge with linen tape (book fold).
2. Window size: overlap the print by about 3-6 mm per side if covering the edges, or 10-25 mm reveal around
   a print to show the paper edge/signature ("float" in the window).
3. Proportions: equal sides or bottom margin slightly larger (weighting) to correct optical centre.
4. Cut bevel from the back with a mat cutter; overcut-free corners.

## Hinging (reversible)
- **T-hinge** (hidden): tissue tab pasted to the print verso top edge, crossed by a second strip pasted to the
  backing - print hangs freely, can expand.
- **V-hinge / pendant hinges** for floated prints (hinge folded behind).
- Use thin wheat starch paste, small amount; dry under weight with blotter and polyester to prevent cockle.
- Hinge only at the top so the print can move.

## Photo corners
Polyester corners on the backing hold the print without adhesive - quickest fully reversible method when the
window covers the corners.

## Gotchas
- Never use self-adhesive tape, rubber cement or dry-mount tissue on valuable/original prints when reversibility matters.
- Inkjet prints scuff - handle with gloves; let outgas before matting.

## Verify
- [ ] Print held only by reversible hinges/corners; mat package square and clean.
