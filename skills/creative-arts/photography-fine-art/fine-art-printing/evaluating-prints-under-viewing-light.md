# Evaluating prints under viewing light

## Light
- **ISO 3664** P1 (critical comparison): D50, about 2000 lux; P2 (practical appraisal): D50, about 500 lux.
- Viewing booths (GTI, Just Normlicht) offer dimmable D50 with high colour rendering.
- Budget: high-CRI (95+) 5000 K LED lamps (e.g. Solux for warmer gallery match); dimmable is essential.
- Also check prints under the **light they will be displayed in** (gallery halogen/LED at 3000 K) - final judgement.

## Dry-down
Inkjet prints change for hours after printing: blacks deepen, colours shift slightly (especially matte papers).
Wait at least 15-30 min for a first look; for critical decisions and profiling, 24 h.

## Procedure
1. Set booth brightness so paper white looks similar to the display's soft-proof paper white.
2. Hold print at normal viewing angle (avoid gloss reflection - family of angles).
3. Compare highlight detail, shadow separation, neutrality of greys, skin, saturated areas.
4. Note corrections on the back of the proof (e.g. "+1/3 shadows, -5 magenta").
5. Edit the proof copy, reprint small test strips (crop key areas) rather than full prints.

## Gotchas
- Daylight from windows varies all day; don't evaluate by the window.
- OBA-containing papers look bluer under UV-rich light than under gallery LED.

- [ ] Evaluation done after dry-down under known light; notes recorded.
