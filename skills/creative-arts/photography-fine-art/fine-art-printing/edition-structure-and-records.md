# Edition structure and records

## Decisions (before the first sale)
1. **Limited vs open**: fine-art photography is usually limited.
2. **Edition size** per image: small (3-10) for high-end gallery work; larger (25-50) for more affordable prints.
3. **Sizes**: one edition across all sizes (e.g. 7 total) or separate editions per size (5 at 50x60, 3 at 100x125) -
   state it clearly; per-size editions are common but collectors increasingly prefer total-count transparency.
4. **Artist proofs (AP)**: usually up to about 10-20% of edition size (1-2 APs for an edition of 7-10). APs are
   often sold last at a premium.
5. Printer's proofs, exhibition copies: decide and count them.
6. Price ladder: price steps up as the edition sells (see pricing).

## Ledger
Spreadsheet/database row per physical print: image ID, title, edition number (3/7 or AP 1/2), size, paper,
printer, print date, signature date, location (studio/gallery/consigned), sale date, buyer, price, certificate
number, notes (damaged/destroyed). Never reuse a number of a destroyed print.

## Legal
Some US states (California, New York, etc.) have multiples disclosure laws requiring edition info for sales; check yours.

## Verify
- [ ] Edition structure written before first sale; every print accounted for in the ledger.
