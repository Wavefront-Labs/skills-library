# Chromogenic and metal prints

## Chromogenic (C-type) prints
- Laser or LED exposure (Durst Lambda, Oce LightJet, Chromira) onto colour photographic paper (Fujifilm Crystal
  Archive, Kodak Endura), processed in RA-4.
- Continuous tone (no dots), smooth gradients, glossy/lustre/matte and Fuji Flex/DP II high-gloss surfaces.
- Common for face-mounted acrylic (Diasec) work.
- Permanence: good in dark storage; display life generally lower than pigment on cotton - check WIR data.
- Files: lab profile, 8-bit or 16-bit TIFF at the printer's native resolution (e.g. 200/400 ppi).

## Metal prints (dye sublimation on coated aluminium)
- Image printed on transfer paper with dye-sub inks, then heat-pressed into a polymer-coated aluminium panel
  (ChromaLuxe). Finishes: high gloss, satin, matte; white or clear (silver metallic) base.
- Very durable, waterproof, vivid; frameless with float mounts.
- Highlights look metallic on clear base - choose white base for neutral whites.

## Choosing
| Need | Choice |
|---|---|
| Traditional photographic paper look, mounting under acrylic | C-type |
| Maximum archival life on cotton paper | pigment inkjet |
| Durable, vivid, frameless decor / public spaces | metal |

## Care
Metal and acrylic: microfibre cloth, no paper towels; acrylic scratches easily. C-type: handle with gloves.
