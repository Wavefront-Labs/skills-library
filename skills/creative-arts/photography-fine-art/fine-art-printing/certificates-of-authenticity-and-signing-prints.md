# Certificates of authenticity and signing prints

## Signing
- **Where**: lower margin right (signature), lower margin left (edition number "3/10" or "AP 1/2"),
  centre (title) - on the white border below the image. Or on the **verso** (back) if the image will be
  floated/borderless or face-mounted.
- **With what**: soft graphite pencil (2B-4B) on matte/cotton papers - archival and conventional.
  On glossy/baryta, pencil doesn't take well: use an archival pigment pen (Sakura Pigma Micron, Faber-Castell
  Pitt) and let dry; test on a margin scrap first.
- Avoid ballpoint (embossing, ink migration), felt-tip dye markers.
- Sign after the print has fully dried; use a clean hard surface (not soft board that embosses).

## Certificate of authenticity (COA)
Contents: artist name, title, year of capture and year of print, edition number and size, medium and paper
("archival pigment print on Hahnemuhle Photo Rag Baryta"), image and paper size, printer (artist or lab),
signature and date, unique certificate number matching the ledger; optionally a thumbnail and hologram/
NFC sticker with matching ID on the print verso.

Print COAs on archival paper; keep a copy in records.

## Gotchas
- A COA doesn't replace a signed print; keep both consistent.
- Mistakes on the print: don't erase on fine papers - better to annotate on the verso or reprint.

## Verify
- [ ] Signature, number and COA match the ledger entry.
