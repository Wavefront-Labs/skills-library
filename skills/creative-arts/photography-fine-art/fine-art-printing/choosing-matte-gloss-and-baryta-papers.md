# Choosing matte, gloss and baryta papers

## Families
| Family | Black ink | Dmax (approx) | Character |
|---|---|---|---|
| Matte cotton rag (Photo Rag, Canson Rag Photographique) | Matte Black (MK) | 1.5-1.7 | soft, tactile, smaller gamut, no gloss problems |
| Smooth/textured matte (watercolour style) | MK | 1.4-1.6 | painterly texture |
| Lustre/semigloss RC (Epson Premium Luster) | Photo Black (PK) | 2.2-2.4 | proofing, commercial |
| **Baryta / fibre gloss** (Canson Baryta Photographique, Hahnemuhle Photo Rag Baryta, Ilford Gold Fibre) | PK | 2.2-2.5 | silver-print look, deep blacks, archival base |
| Glossy RC | PK | 2.4+ | maximum gamut, reflections |

## Black ink switching
Epson printers switch between MK and PK (costs ink and time on some models; Canon PRO models use separate channels).
Using MK on glossy smears; using PK on matte gives weak, greyish blacks.

## Optical brightening agents (OBAs)
- OBAs fluoresce under UV, making paper look whiter/bluer. They lose effect with time and light, so paper
  white yellows relative to the original look.
- OBA-free papers (Hahnemuhle Photo Rag, Canson Platine, many "natural white") are preferred for longevity.
- Measure with M1/M2 mode when profiling OBA papers.

## Choosing
1. Image character: deep shadows and silver-print look -> baryta; quiet tones and texture -> matte.
2. Display: glossy surfaces reflect gallery lights; matte forgives.
3. Permanence: cotton/alpha-cellulose, OBA-free for archival claims.
4. Make the same image on 3 candidate papers and compare under display light.
