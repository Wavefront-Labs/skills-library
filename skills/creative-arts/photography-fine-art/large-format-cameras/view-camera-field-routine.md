# View camera field routine

## Setup sequence
1. Find the viewpoint first with a viewing card/frame or phone at the equivalent focal length; then set the tripod.
2. Tripod: legs spread wide, centre column (if any) down; head level.
3. Mount camera, open bellows, zero all movements, level the bed and rear standard.
4. Fit lens, open shutter (press-focus), open aperture, dark cloth on.
5. Compose: rough focus, then front rise/fall/shift for framing, then tilt/swing for focus plane.
6. Check corners for vignetting at taking aperture.
7. Meter (spot meter), decide placement and development, calculate bellows/reciprocity, set aperture and speed.
8. Close and cock shutter, insert holder, **remove dark cloth from back** if it might push the holder,
   pull dark slide, wait for vibration to settle, fire with cable, reinsert slide reversed, remove holder.

## Exposure record (one per sheet)
Holder no., date/time, location, lens, movements, meter readings (shadow/highlight), placement, aperture,
time, filter, development (N, N-1...), notes. Mark the holder.

## Wind and stability
- Shield the camera with your body/a pack; hang weight from the tripod; low camera height.
- Fold back/short bellows; avoid extended rails; wait for lulls; time exposures between gusts.
- Larger cameras catch wind like sails - an 8x10 bellows needs very short exposures or calm.

## Verify
- [ ] Holder record complete for every sheet; no wind blur at 100% scan.
