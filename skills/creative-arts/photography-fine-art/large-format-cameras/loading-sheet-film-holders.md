# Loading sheet film holders

## Notch codes
Each film has a unique notch pattern on one short edge. With the notches at the **top right edge**
(held portrait, notches upper right), the **emulsion faces you**. Manufacturers publish notch code charts
for identifying film type in the dark.

## Procedure (total darkness - changing tent or darkroom)
1. Prepare: holders cleaned, dark slides in. Practise in daylight with a scrap sheet first.
2. Pull the dark slide almost out, open the flap.
3. Find notch top-right, emulsion toward you; slide the sheet under the guide rails on both sides
   (feel that both edges are under the rails - common error is one edge over the rail).
4. Close the flap and push the dark slide fully in with the **light/white band out = unexposed**
   (the common convention); after exposure reinsert it reversed, **black band out = exposed**.
5. Label holder numbers and keep a record.

## Dust control
- Vacuum or blow holders before loading; wipe with an anti-static cloth; keep holders in zip bags.
- Load in a clean environment (humid bathroom reduces dust); avoid fleece clothing.
- Tap holders before inserting into the camera to dislodge dust.

## Light leaks
- Check dark slides and light traps (felt) - streaks along the edge suggest a worn light trap.
- Never pull the dark slide in direct sun without the dark cloth over the back.

## Verify
- [ ] Sheets in rails, consistent slide convention, holder record kept, no dust spots in negatives.
