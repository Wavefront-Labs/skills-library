# Developing sheet film in trays and tanks

## Tray shuffling (total darkness)
1. Trays slightly larger than the film (5x7 trays for 4x5); pre-soak sheets one by one in water so they don't stick.
2. Up to about 6 sheets: transfer the stack into developer one at a time, then **shuffle**: pull the bottom sheet
   out and place it on top, gently, continuously; rotate the stack orientation every cycle to even out.
3. Handle by edges; keep emulsion up; short fingernails (scratches!).
4. Same procedure in stop, fix; then wash.
- Pros: very even, any number of sheets. Cons: total darkness for minutes; scratch risk.

## Small tanks
- **SP-445 / Stearman**: 4 sheets of 4x5 in a daylight tank with small volume; inversion agitation; check
  for edge surge.
- **Mod54** insert for Paterson tanks: 6 sheets, use enough chemistry and gentle agitation.
- **Taco method**: roll sheets in hair-elastic "tacos" in a roll-film tank; risk of uneven edges.

## Rotary (Jobo Expert drums)
Continuous rotation, low volume, very even, temperature controlled; common for colour sheet film.
Reduce development time 10-15% vs inversion (more agitation).

## Gotchas
- Sheets sticking together in trays = undeveloped patches; keep the stack loose.
- Uneven edges (surge marks) in tanks = over-vigorous agitation.

## Verify
- [ ] Even skies without edge density; no scratches; times logged per method.
