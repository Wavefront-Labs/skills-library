# Using roll film backs on view cameras

## Types
- **Graflok-mounted** backs (Horseman, Linhof Rapid Rollex, Mamiya RB adapter): remove the ground glass
  or use a back with Graflok slider rails; secure with Graflok sliders.
- **Slide-in** holders (Horseman 6x12, Calumet C2N, Linhof Technorama style): slide under the ground glass
  like a sheet film holder - fast.
- Formats: 6x7, 6x9, 6x12 (panoramic); 6x17 needs dedicated back/large ground glass.

## Procedure
1. Compose on the ground glass with a framing mask (pencil marks or a cut mask) for the roll format.
   Format is smaller than 4x5 - effectively a longer lens (6x9: 150 mm on 4x5 framing becomes tighter).
2. Advance film to frame 1 per the back's counter; insert; remove dark slide.
3. Use movements as usual (image circle needs are smaller).
4. Expose; reinsert dark slide before removing the back.

## Pros/cons
- Cheaper per frame, many frames per roll, easier processing, but **one development per roll** (Zone System
  per roll) and possible flatness issues: advance just before exposure; film left in the gate for hours can bow out of focus.

## Gotchas
- Light leaks at worn Graflok seals; check with test rolls.
- Slide-in backs can push the ground glass back; ensure it reseats.

## Verify
- [ ] Framing masks match frames; even spacing; no leaks.
