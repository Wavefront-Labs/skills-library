# Large format lens coverage and format equivalence

## Image circle
The diameter of usable sharp image at a stated aperture (usually f/22) and focus distance (infinity).
- Film diagonal: 4x5 about 153 mm, 5x7 about 210 mm, 8x10 about 325 mm.
- Spare circle beyond the diagonal = room for movements.
- Example: Schneider Apo-Symmar 150 mm about 214 mm circle; on 4x5 that allows about 30 mm rise.
  Super-Angulon 90 mm f/8: about 216 mm - large movements on 4x5.

## Choosing
1. Decide format and needed movements (architecture: big rise; landscape: small tilt).
2. Check manufacturer's image circle at f/22.
3. Close focusing enlarges the circle (lens farther from film).

## Equivalence (horizontal angle of view approx, matched by diagonal)
| 35 mm | 4x5 | 8x10 |
|---|---|---|
| 24 mm | 75 mm | 150 mm |
| 28 mm | 90 mm | 180 mm |
| 35 mm | 110 mm | 210 mm |
| 50 mm | 150 mm | 300 mm |
| 85 mm | 270 mm | 480 mm |
4x5 factor about 3.0x vs 35 mm (by diagonal; aspect ratios differ - 4:5 vs 2:3).

## Lens types
Plasmat (Symmar, Sironar, Nikkor-W): general. Wide-angle (Super-Angulon, Grandagon): need recessed boards.
Tele designs (Nikkor-T): shorter bellows draw. Process lenses (Apo-Ronar) for close-up.

## Verify
- [ ] Lens circle covers diagonal plus planned movements at taking aperture.
