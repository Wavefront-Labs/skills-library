# Lens boards and leaf shutters

## Lens boards
- Each camera system has its own board size (Linhof Technika 96 mm, Sinar 140 mm, Wista/Toyo field 99 mm/110 mm).
- Hole size matches the shutter: Copal 0 = 34.6 mm, Copal 1 = 41.6 mm, Copal 3 = 65 mm.
- Mount: remove rear lens cell, place shutter through board, tighten retaining ring with a spanner wrench (not pliers).
- Wide-angle lenses on field cameras often need **recessed boards** for focus at infinity and movements.

## Shutter operation
1. Set speed (B, T, 1 s - 1/500 depending on size), set aperture.
2. Open for focusing with the press-focus lever (or T setting).
3. Close, **cock** the shutter, insert holder, pull dark slide, fire with cable release.
4. On many Copal 0/1 shutters the 1/500 setting engages an extra spring: set 1/500 **before** cocking,
   not after (check the shutter's instructions).

## Testing speeds
- Shutter testers (photodiode-based apps/devices) measure actual times. Old shutters often run slow
  at slow speeds and fast at 1/250-1/500.
- Record the measured offset and correct exposure (e.g. "1/125 marked = 1/90 actual").
- Sluggish slow speeds or sticking blades = service (CLA) needed.

## Gotchas
- Never oil blades; oil migrates and causes sticking.
- Loose retaining ring lets the lens rotate - light leaks and off-axis shutter.

## Verify
- [ ] Lens firm in board, shutter speeds measured and noted on a card in the case.
