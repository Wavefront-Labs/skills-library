# Crop, aspect ratios and overlays

Crop Overlay tool: `R`.

## Aspect
- Lock icon keeps the current ratio. Aspect menu: As Shot, Original, 1x1, 4x5/8x10, 8.5x11, 5x7, 2x3/4x6, 16x9, 16x10, plus **Enter Custom** (e.g. 11x14 = 1:1.273, 3x1 panoramas).
- `X` swaps portrait and landscape orientation of the crop.
- Match the crop to the final paper or frame ratio early for a series (A-paper is 1:1.414, close to but not the same as 2x3).

## Straighten
- Angle slider, or the Straighten tool (level icon): drag along a horizon. Or hold Ctrl/Cmd in crop mode and draw a line.
- Double-click the Angle tool to Auto straighten.

## Overlays
- `O` cycles: Thirds, Grid, Diagonal, Triangle, Golden Ratio, Golden Spiral, Aspect Ratios (shows several ratios at once).
- `Shift+O` flips spiral/triangle orientation.
- Tools > Crop Guide Overlay > Choose Overlays to Cycle trims the list.

## Craft
- Crop for the edges first: check every frame edge for intrusions and cut-off limbs before thinking about thirds.
- Consistent ratio across a sequence or book; different ratios jumble a wall.
- Note pixel dimensions remaining (Info overlay `I`) against print size needs (see print resolution skill).

## Gotchas
- Constrain to Image (in Crop panel) prevents white corners after Upright/rotate.
- Crops are non-destructive; virtual copies hold alternate crops.

## Verify
- [ ] Info overlay shows enough pixels for the intended print size at 180-300 ppi; edges clean.
