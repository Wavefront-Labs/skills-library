# Custom white balance in camera

## Typical colour temperatures
Candle 1900 K, tungsten 2800-3200 K, sunrise/sunset 3000-4000 K, daylight 5200-5600 K,
flash ~5500-6000 K, overcast 6500-7500 K, open shade 7500-10000 K.

## Methods
1. **Kelvin** setting: dial a value; fast, repeatable, no target needed.
2. **Custom/preset (PRE)**: shoot or measure a neutral target filling the centre of frame under the
   scene light; the camera computes WB. Most accurate under odd sources (LED, sodium mix).
3. **Raw + grey card frame**: shoot a frame with a grey card, click it with the WB eyedropper in
   Lightroom, sync to the series.

## Procedure (custom)
1. Put a neutral grey or white target (WhiBal, ColorChecker grey) in the subject's light, angled
   toward the camera.
2. Fill the metering circle, expose so it is mid-to-light (not clipped).
3. Menu: Custom WB / PRE, select that frame or press measure.
4. Shoot a confirm frame; compare against the target.

## When not to neutralise
Golden hour, candlelight, blue hour: correct WB removes the mood. Use daylight preset or a
partial correction (e.g. 3800 K under tungsten for a warm-but-not-orange result).

## Gotchas
- Auto WB drifts between frames of the same scene - kills series consistency.
- White paper often contains optical brighteners -> wrong (blue-ish) reference. Use a real target.

## Verify
- [ ] Neutral target reads R=G=B within 2-3 points in the raw converter.
