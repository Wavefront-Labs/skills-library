# Balancing flash with ambient light

## Two exposures in one frame
- **Ambient** exposure: shutter speed, aperture, ISO.
- **Flash** exposure: power, distance, aperture, ISO - **not** shutter speed (below sync).
So **shutter speed controls ambient only**. That is the lever.

## Procedure
1. Meter ambient. Decide the ambient level relative to the subject: e.g. background 1 stop under.
2. Set aperture and ISO, choose shutter to give that ambient level.
3. Add flash to light the subject correctly at that aperture (manual power or TTL with FEC).
4. To brighten background: slower shutter (drag the shutter) - flash unchanged.
5. To darken background: faster shutter up to sync, or HSS/ND beyond.

## Fill flash
Subject in backlight: ambient for background, flash at -1 to -2 EV (FEC) to open shadows without
a "flashed" look.

## Motion with dragged shutter
Slow shutter with flash: sharp flash image plus ambient blur. **Rear-curtain sync** puts the trail
behind a moving subject.

## Colour
Gel the flash to match ambient: CTO 1/2 or full for tungsten, Plus Green for fluorescent; then set
WB for the ambient.

## Verify
- [ ] Background and subject brightness relationship as planned; no colour clash between flash and ambient.
