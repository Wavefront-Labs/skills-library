# Intentional camera movement (ICM)

Moving the camera during exposure to turn a scene into streaks and colour fields. The
craft is choosing a subject with strong structure and repeating a controlled motion.

## Settings
- Shutter 1/15 to 1 s; start at 1/4-1/2 s.
- Low ISO, small aperture (f/16-f/22), and a 2-6 stop ND in daylight.
- Manual focus at the subject distance; stabilisation off (or set to the panning mode).
- Shoot raw; continuous drive.

## Movements and subjects
| Subject | Motion |
|---|---|
| Vertical tree trunks | Smooth vertical pan along the trunks, start before releasing |
| Horizon / sea | Horizontal sweep, keeping the horizon level |
| Flowers, lights | Rotate around the lens axis, or zoom during exposure (zoom burst) |
| Architecture | Short jerk at end of exposure for a sharp ghost plus streak |

## Making it repeatable
1. Start moving before pressing the shutter and continue after it closes.
2. Change one variable at a time (speed of move, shutter time, direction) and shoot 20+ frames.
3. Edit hard - keep frames where a recognisable structure survives; pure blur rarely holds.
4. Mixing a short flash (rear curtain) adds a sharp element on top of the streaks.

## Gotchas
- Bright skies blow out at long times; meter for the sky, add ND.
- Tripod-based panning heads give straighter streaks than handheld if that is the look.

## Verify
- [ ] Series shows a deliberate, consistent motion vocabulary rather than accidental shake.
