# Urban night photography

## Exposure
- Highlights (signs, lamps) clip easily; meter or bracket for them and accept clipped bare bulbs.
- Blue hour (20-40 min after sunset) balances sky and lights; full night needs blending.
- Traffic trails: 10-30 s at f/8-f/11, ISO 100; time it as cars pass in the right lane.

## Colour
- Old sodium vapour = deep orange, nearly monochromatic (WB cannot fix it fully).
- Modern LEDs are 3000-5000 K, often greenish. Pick WB for the dominant source, or embrace the mix.

## Flare and ghosting
- Remove UV/protective filters (reflections between filter and lens create green ghost dots).
- Use a lens hood; shade with a hand.
- f/11-16 gives starbursts from point lights; blade count sets the number of rays.

## Handheld option
Modern stabilisation allows 1/4-1 s handheld; or ISO 3200-6400 at 1/60 for street at night.

## Gotchas
- Security/tripod restrictions in private plazas; carry a small tripod or clamp.
- Condensation on gear when entering warm buildings.

## Verify
- [ ] Light sources not hiding detail you need; no ghost dots; WB intentional.
