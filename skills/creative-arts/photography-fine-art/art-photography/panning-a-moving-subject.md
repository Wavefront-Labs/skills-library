# Panning a moving subject

## Shutter speeds (starting points)
| Subject | Speed |
|---|---|
| Car / motorbike at speed | 1/60-1/125 |
| Cyclist | 1/30-1/60 |
| Runner, horse | 1/15-1/30 |
| Walking person | 1/8-1/15 |
Slower = more streak, lower keeper rate.

## Camera setup
- Shutter priority (or manual), continuous AF (AI Servo/AF-C), single point or zone over
  the subject, continuous high drive.
- Stabiliser in **panning mode** (Canon Mode 2, Nikon Sport/Normal with pan detection,
  Sony auto-detects) so only the vertical axis is corrected.
- ND in bright light to reach slow speeds.

## Technique
1. Pre-focus on the spot the subject will pass; stand square to it, feet apart.
2. Pick up the subject early, track it smoothly by rotating from the hips.
3. Release as it reaches you, **keep following through** after the shutter closes.
4. Shoot bursts; expect 1 in 10 sharp at slow speeds.
5. Choose a background with colour and contrast (crowds, foliage) - plain walls give no streak.

## Gotchas
- Parts moving in other directions (legs, wheels) will blur even when the body is sharp; that is part of the look.
- Monopod with a loose head improves vertical stability.

## Verify
- [ ] The subject's face/main body is sharp at 100%, background streaks parallel to motion.
