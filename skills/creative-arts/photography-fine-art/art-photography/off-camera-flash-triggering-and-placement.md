# Off-camera flash: triggering and placement

## Triggering
- **Radio** (Godox X, Profoto Air, Elinchrom Skyport, Canon/Nikon RT): reliable, no line of sight.
- Optical/infrared master-slave: needs line of sight, unreliable outdoors.
- Match transmitter and receiver: same **channel**, set each light to a **group** (A, B, C), set
  ID if others are using the same system.

## TTL vs manual
- TTL: quick, adapts to distance changes (events, moving subjects); varies frame to frame.
- Manual: repeatable; use for still life, portraits, and art work. Set power in fractions (1/1 to 1/128).

## One-light placement procedure
1. Set ambient exposure first: shutter at or below sync speed, aperture for DoF, ISO base.
2. Put the light 45 deg to the side and 45 deg up (loop/Rembrandt) at 1-2 m with a modifier.
3. Start flash at 1/8 manual; adjust power in 1/3 stops until subject exposure is right.
4. Adjust light-to-subject distance for falloff on the background.
5. Lock settings and shoot; if the subject moves forward/back, re-test.

## Gotchas
- Batteries cool down: recycle time grows; overheat protection kicks in after rapid full power.
- Receivers on stands in the sun can lose line; radio solves this.
- Mixing brands needs compatible protocols.

## Verify
- [ ] Every frame fires (check flash-ready on the transmitter); exposure consistent across the set.
