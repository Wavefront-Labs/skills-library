# Infrared photography

Foliage reflects near-infrared strongly (**Wood effect**: white leaves), blue sky goes black,
skin becomes pale and smooth.

## Capture options
- **Film**: Rollei Infrared 400 / Ilford SFX 200 with an R72 (720 nm) filter; load and unload in
  subdued light; meter tests needed (often ISO 6-25 effective with filter).
- **Converted digital**: hot mirror replaced with an IR-pass filter (590 super colour, 665, 720
  standard, 850 deep B&W). Normal handholding and AF.
- **Unconverted digital + R72**: possible on some bodies with very long exposures (seconds in sun).

## Focus
IR focuses behind visible light. Use the lens's IR index mark (red dot) on older lenses, or live view
AF on converted bodies (calibrated for the filter). Stop down to f/8-f/11.

## Processing (digital)
1. Custom WB off foliage (often requires a DCP profile for very red files).
2. False colour: Photoshop Channel Mixer - Red output 0/0/100, Blue output 100/0/0 (swap R and B).
3. For B&W, convert and contrast up.

## Gotchas
- **Hotspots**: many lenses show a bright central spot in IR; check lens hotspot lists.
- Bright sun midday is best (the opposite of normal landscape light).

## Verify
- [ ] No central hotspot; foliage bright, sky dark; focus sharp at distance.
