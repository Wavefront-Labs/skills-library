# Long exposure with ND filters

## Kit
Tripod, remote release or intervalometer, ND filters (3, 6, 10, 15 stop), eyepiece cover.

## Procedure
1. Compose and focus **without** the filter. Switch lens to manual focus so it does not hunt.
2. Meter and take a correct exposure without filter, e.g. f/11, 1/30 s, ISO 100.
3. Add the stops: 1/30 x 2^10 (10-stop) = 34 s. Apps (PhotoPills, Lee Stopper) do this.
4. Close the viewfinder eyepiece; light entering it fogs DSLR long exposures.
5. Use Bulb with a remote for > 30 s. Long-exposure noise reduction on doubles total time
   (dark frame) - useful for hot-pixel removal, costs time; alternative is a manual dark frame.
6. Check the histogram; long exposures often need +1/3-2/3 extra as light changes.

## Choosing the time
| Effect | Time |
|---|---|
| Wave texture with motion | 1/4-1 s |
| Misty water | 2-15 s |
| Glass-flat water, streaked clouds | 1-4 min |

## Gotchas
- **Colour cast**: many strong NDs go warm/magenta or blue; shoot raw and set WB from a
  reference frame or a grey card shot through the filter.
- **Light leaks**: square filters in holders leak at the gasket; use foam gaskets or shade.
- Strong NDs plus wide lenses on polarizers vignette; stack carefully.
- Infrared contamination with cheap NDs causes reddish blacks; buy IR-corrected ones.
- Wind shakes tripods: hang a bag, shield the camera, avoid extending the centre column.

## Verify
- [ ] Static elements (rocks) are sharp at 100%; no cast or leak streaks; histogram centred where intended.
