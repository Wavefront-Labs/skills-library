# Reading weather for photography

## Key forecast variables
| Want | Look for |
|---|---|
| Colourful sunset/sunrise | high or mid cloud (cirrus/altocumulus, 30-70%), clear horizon toward the sun |
| Dull sunset | low cloud covering the horizon toward the sun |
| Valley fog / mist | clear calm night, temperature dropping to dew point, wind < 5 km/h, high humidity, damp ground |
| Dramatic storm light | clearing showers, sun breaking under a cloud deck |
| Long-exposure cloud streaks | moderate wind at cloud level, broken cloud |

## Tools
- Windy (cloud layers by altitude: low/medium/high; dew point; wind at altitude).
- SunsetWx / Alpenglow-type sunset quality forecasts (treat as probability).
- Satellite and radar loops for timing.

## Procedure
1. Check 48-72 h ahead for candidate days; re-check the evening before and 3 h before.
2. For sunsets, look west along the sun azimuth: is the horizon clear for 100-200 km?
3. For fog: temperature at dawn vs dew point; fog in valleys means shoot from a higher viewpoint.
4. Have backup locations for each wind direction.

## Gotchas
- Forecast low-cloud data is the least reliable; satellite truth beats the model.
- Mountains create their own weather; local webcams help.
