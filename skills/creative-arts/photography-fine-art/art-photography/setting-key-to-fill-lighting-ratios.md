# Key-to-fill lighting ratios

The **lighting ratio** compares light on the lit side (key + fill) to the shadow side (fill only).

| Difference measured | Ratio | Character |
|---|---|---|
| 1 stop | 2:1 | open, high-key, commercial |
| 1.5 stops | 3:1 | standard portrait (PPA classic) |
| 2 stops | 4:1 | dramatic, modelled |
| 3 stops | 8:1 | low-key, theatrical |

## Measuring
1. Incident meter at the subject's face, dome pointed at the key light with fill off -> key reading.
2. Dome pointed at camera from the shadow side with key off/shaded -> fill reading.
3. Difference in stops gives the ratio (each stop doubles).
Alternatively use test frames and compare cheek values in the histogram/RGB readout.

## Setting
- Raise/lower fill power or move a reflector closer/farther.
- Fill near the camera axis avoids second shadows.
- For print, remember paper compresses contrast less than screens show; check final output.

## Gotchas
- A strong ambient level adds to fill; measure with all lights on in the real environment.
- Silver reflector at 50 cm can make 1:1 accidentally.

## Verify
- [ ] Recorded ratio for the setup; repeatable across a session.
