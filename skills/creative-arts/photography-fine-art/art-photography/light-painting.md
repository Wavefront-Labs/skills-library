# Light painting

## Two kinds
- **Painting with light**: lighting a subject (tree, car, ruin) with a hand-held torch during a long exposure.
- **Light drawing**: the light source itself visible as lines/shapes in frame.

## Base settings
Manual exposure, ISO 100-400, f/8-f/11, Bulb or 15-60 s, manual focus (focus with a torch on the subject first),
WB to match the torch (or deliberately mismatch).

## Procedure (painting a subject)
1. Shoot an ambient frame for the sky (blue hour or stars).
2. Expose with the torch moving continuously, oblique to the subject (side light reveals texture);
   never point it at the camera; wear dark clothing and keep moving so you do not record.
3. Brightness depends on dwell time and distance: test passes, adjust speed.
4. Do separate exposures per area/side; blend in Photoshop with Lighten mode or masks.

## Tools
High-CRI LED torch with dimmer, coloured gels, LED wand (Pixelstick-type), fibre-optic brushes.

## Gotchas
- Uneven streaky patches: keep the beam moving steadily; diffuse the torch.
- Fire hazards with steel wool spinning - do not use near dry vegetation; eye protection.

## Verify
- [ ] Even, intentional light on the subject; no ghost of you in the frame.
