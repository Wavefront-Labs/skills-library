# Intimate landscapes and details

Coined from Eliot Porter's 1979 Met exhibition: landscape without the grand view - a stream bank,
a tangle of branches, frosted leaves.

## Procedure
1. Choose **soft, even light** (overcast, shade) so colour and texture read without hot spots.
2. Use a 70-200 mm or macro lens to isolate; exclude sky and horizon.
3. Look for **pattern + interruption**: repeated forms with one variation.
4. Keep the sensor parallel to flat subjects (forest floor, rock face) for even sharpness; otherwise stack.
5. Work slowly: one small area can yield many frames as you refine edges.
6. A polarizer removes glare from wet leaves, saturating colour.

## Gotchas
- Wind ruins long exposures on leaves; raise ISO or wait for lulls.
- Without a clear structure, details become wallpaper - ask what holds the frame together.
