# Loading reels and diagnosing tank faults

## Plastic (Paterson/AP) ratcheting reels
1. Reel completely dry (damp reels jam). Clip film corners rounded with scissors.
2. In darkness, feed the film under the entry guides past the ball bearings, then twist the two halves
   back and forth to ratchet in.
3. For 120: peel the backing paper away and feed film only; cut the tape at the end.

## Steel reels (Hewes)
1. Clip film end into the centre spring/hook, bow the film slightly between thumb and finger, rotate the
   reel so film lays into the spiral from the centre out.
2. Feel the spacing; no touching layers.
- Steel reels need less chemistry and dry instantly; harder to learn.

## Faults
| Symptom | Cause |
|---|---|
| Crescent marks (half-moons) | film kinked while loading |
| Undeveloped blotches, film stuck | two layers touching (overlap) |
| Small clear dots | air bells - tap tank firmly after pouring developer |
| Dense streaks from sprocket holes | over-agitation (surge) or stand bromide drag |
| Uneven density at the top of the reel | insufficient developer volume |
| Fogged edges | light leak in tank lid or changing bag |

## Practise
Sacrifice a roll: load it in daylight with eyes closed, then in a bag, until it is automatic.

## Verify
- [ ] No kinks, overlaps or air bells on the last 5 rolls.
