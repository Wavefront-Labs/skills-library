# Dodging, burning and printing maps

## Tools
- **Dodgers**: card shapes on thin wire (black, bent wire disappears when moving); your hands.
- **Burning cards**: matt black card with a hole; a card with different hole sizes/shapes; a card
  with a straight edge for skies.
- A **foot switch** frees both hands.

## Technique
1. Base exposure and grade from test strips; make a straight print.
2. Analyse it under good light: where do the eyes go? Which areas are too light/dark?
3. **Dodge** during the base exposure to lighten (hold back light), **burn** after to darken.
4. Keep tools moving and at a height where the shadow edge is soft; higher = softer.
5. Express each move in **stops/fractions** of base (e.g. sky +1 stop, face dodge -1/3 stop).
6. Burn edges/corners slightly (edge burning) to hold the eye in the frame.

## Print map
Sketch the image on paper or on a work print; mark areas, grade, times/stops, order of operations.
Store with the negative sleeve. Example entries:
- Base: 16 s grade 2.5, f/8 at 11x14.
- Dodge left face -1/3 stop (during base).
- Burn sky +1 stop grade 1, card edge from top, move in arc.

## Gotchas
- Halos around dodged areas = tool too still or too low.
- Burned skies with high grade block clouds - burn skies at a softer grade.

## Verify
- [ ] Second print from the map matches the first; map stored with the negative.
