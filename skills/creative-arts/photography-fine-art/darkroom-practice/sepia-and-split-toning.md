# Sepia and split toning

## Sepia (bleach and redevelop)
1. Fully fixed and washed print (residual fixer causes stains).
2. **Bleach** (potassium ferricyanide + potassium bromide): image fades to pale yellow. Shadows bleach
   last. Rinse thoroughly until all yellow stain leaves highlights.
3. **Toner/redeveloper**:
   - Sodium sulfide (traditional, strong rotten-egg smell) - classic brown.
   - **Thiourea** (variable sepia, e.g. Fotospeed ST20): tone colour set by the activator (sodium
     hydroxide) amount - more activator = darker brown, less = yellower.
4. Wash; the sulfide image is very permanent.

## Split toning
Partial bleach then tone gives two colours:
1. Bleach only highlights and light mid-tones (short bleach, watch it), rinse, sepia redevelop -> warm highlights.
2. Then tone in selenium -> shadows go cool purple-brown/black.
Alternatively selenium first (protects shadows), then bleach and sepia the unprotected highlights.

## Safety
Sulfide and thiourea release toxic/irritant compounds; thiourea is a suspected carcinogen - gloves,
ventilation; keep away from film and paper storage (sulfide fogs materials).

## Gotchas
- Bleached prints look lighter after toning; print slightly darker for sepia.
- Iron in water or residual fixer = uneven blotches.

## Verify
- [ ] Highlights and shadows show the intended colour split, with no blotches or stains.
