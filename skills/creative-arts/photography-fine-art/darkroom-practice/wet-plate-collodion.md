# Wet plate collodion

## Kit
Salted collodion (e.g. Bostick & Sullivan), 9% silver nitrate bath (silver tank), iron sulfate developer,
fixer (sodium thiosulfate, or potassium cyanide - highly toxic, avoid), varnish (sandarac/lavender), plates
(trophy aluminium for tintypes, glass for ambrotypes), dark box, large-format or plate camera.

## Procedure (must finish while the plate is wet, about 10 min)
1. Clean plate (glass: whiting/alcohol; aluminium: peel film, wipe).
2. **Pour collodion**: hold level, pour a pool, tilt to each corner, drain from the last corner; wait until it
   sets ("tacky", about 10-20 s).
3. **Sensitise**: lower into the silver bath for about 3-4 min in the dark (red safelight OK).
4. Load wet in the plate holder, expose: ISO around 0.5-1 - several seconds in bright shade.
5. **Develop** in dark box: pour iron developer over the plate in one sweep, 10-20 s; stop with water.
6. **Fix** in sodium thiosulfate until the creamy iodide clears (image appears positive on black backing).
7. Wash, dry, warm the plate and **varnish**.

## Safety
Ether and alcohol in collodion are highly flammable - no flames, ventilate. Silver nitrate burns skin/eyes.
Cyanide fixer can kill; prefer thiosulfate.

## Gotchas
- Oyster-shell, comets, fog from dirty plates or exhausted/contaminated silver bath (maintain bath pH, sun it).

## Verify
- [ ] Clean plate with full tonal range, varnished.
