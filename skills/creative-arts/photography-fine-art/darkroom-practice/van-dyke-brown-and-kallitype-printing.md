# Van Dyke brown and kallitype printing

Both use light-sensitive ferric salts that reduce silver nitrate to a silver image.

## Van Dyke brown
- Sensitizer (James, per 100 ml): about 9 g ferric ammonium citrate, 1.5 g tartaric acid, 3.8 g silver nitrate,
  dissolved as three solutions then combined; age a day; brown bottle.
1. Coat sized cotton paper in subdued light; dry.
2. Contact print in UV until shadows are reversed/bronzed; the image prints out visibly.
3. Develop in running water (image changes from yellow-brown).
4. **Fix in 3% sodium thiosulfate** (plain hypo) 2-5 min - image darkens.
5. Hypo clear, wash 30 min. Optional gold or selenium toning for permanence.

## Kallitype
- Sensitizer: ferric oxalate (about 20%) + silver nitrate (about 10%), mixed on coating.
- Expose (image faint), then **develop** - developer controls colour:
  sodium acetate (neutral-brown), borax (black-brown), Rochelle salt (sepia), sodium citrate.
- Clear in EDTA/citric acid baths, **fix in 2-5% sodium thiosulfate** with a little ammonia, wash.
- Tone in gold, platinum or palladium for permanence and colour.

## Safety
Silver nitrate stains skin and is corrosive to eyes - gloves and goggles. Oxalates are toxic.

## Gotchas
- Insufficient fixing = fading; over-fixing bleaches highlights. Follow times.
- Metal (staples, steel trays) contamination causes spots; glass/plastic only.

## Verify
- [ ] Image fixed and washed; dense, even blacks with clean highlights.
