# Gum bichromate printing

A pigment process: gum arabic mixed with pigment and a dichromate hardens where exposed to UV;
unexposed gum washes off in water. Built in multiple layers (and colours).

## Materials
- Gum arabic solution (14 deg Baume, with preservative).
- Tube watercolour pigment (lightfast, e.g. lamp black, PB15).
- Saturated potassium dichromate (about 13%) or ammonium dichromate (faster).
- Paper: heavy cotton watercolour paper, **pre-shrunk** in hot water and **sized** (gelatin hardened with
  glyoxal/formalin or acrylic gesso) so pigment does not stain.

## Procedure
1. Mix equal parts gum and dichromate plus pigment (e.g. 1 ml gum : 1 ml dichromate : 0.25-0.5 g pigment).
2. Brush coat thinly and evenly; smooth with a dry hake brush; dry in the dark.
3. Contact print with a negative in register (punch registration); UV 2-10 min.
4. Develop face down in still water 10-60 min (or longer); let pigment float off; assist with a soft brush.
5. Dry; recoat and print again for more density or another colour (CMYK separations for colour).

## Safety
Dichromates are toxic, carcinogenic and environmentally harmful: gloves, no dust, don't drain; dispose as hazardous.

## Gotchas
- Thick coats flake ("frilling"); staining = insufficient sizing; register shifts = paper not pre-shrunk.

## Verify
- [ ] Layers in register, highlights clean, pigment adhered after final wash.
