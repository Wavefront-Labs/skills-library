# Hand coating and sizing paper for alternative processes

## Paper choice
- 100% cotton, acid-free, **unbuffered** for iron processes (cyanotype, Pt/Pd) - buffering degrades them.
- Good choices: Arches Platine, Revere Platinum, Hahnemuhle Platinum Rag, Bergger COT 320, Stonehenge (test).
- Test each paper: some cause speckling or poor Dmax.

## Sizing (when paper absorbs too much or for gum/salt)
- Gelatin size: 2-3% gelatin in warm water, brush or float, dry; harden with glyoxal (for gum).
- Arrowroot/starch size for salt prints (matte, sharp).
- Pre-shrink paper (soak in hot water, dry) before sizing for multi-layer gum.

## Coating
- **Glass rod / Puddle Pusher**: uses least solution, very even; needs a smooth paper.
- **Hake or synthetic brush** (no metal ferrule contact with silver/iron solutions): more forgiving on
  textured papers; uses more solution; brush up-down then side-to-side.
- Count drops (e.g. 24 drops per 8x10 for Pt/Pd), coat quickly, avoid overworking (abrades surface).
- Humidity 50-60% helps even coating and Dmax; dry flat in the dark, then gentle cool air.

## Gotchas
- Coated paper keeps poorly for most processes (salt, Van Dyke) - coat close to printing.
- Fingerprints and contamination show as spots; gloves.

## Verify
- [ ] Even coating edge to edge (no streaks or pooling) seen after exposure and processing.
