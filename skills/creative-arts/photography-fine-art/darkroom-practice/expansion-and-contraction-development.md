# Expansion and contraction development (N+ and N-)

## Concept
Development mainly affects higher densities (highlights); shadows (toe) change little. So:
- **Expansion (N+)**: longer development raises highlights in a **flat** scene - a Zone VII value
  prints as VIII.
- **Contraction (N-)**: shorter development lowers highlights in a **contrasty** scene - a Zone IX value
  prints as VIII.

## Starting time changes (derive yours by testing)
- N+1 about +40% of N; N+2 about +100%.
- N-1 about -20-25%; N-2 about -35-40% (contraction beyond N-1 needs dilute/compensating developers).

## Procedure
1. Meter and place shadows as usual; read highlight fall.
2. Choose N, N+x or N-x; mark the holder/roll.
3. For contraction, **give extra exposure** (about +1/2 stop for N-1, +1 for N-2): reduced development
   also lowers shadow density.
4. Develop accordingly; print on normal grade.

## Limits
- N+2 increases grain and fog; N+3 needs intensification or harder paper.
- N-2 risks muddy mid-tones; use compensating developers (two-bath, dilute) instead.
- Variable-contrast paper gives +/- 1-2 grades of further control - Zone System workers use this
  rather than extreme development.

## Verify
- [ ] Prints from expanded/contracted negatives need about grade 2 and hold intended zones.
