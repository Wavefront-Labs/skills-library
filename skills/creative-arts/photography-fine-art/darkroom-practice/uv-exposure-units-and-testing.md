# UV exposure units and testing

## Light sources
| Source | Notes |
|---|---|
| Sun | free, very variable; use a step tablet each time or a UV integrator |
| BL fluorescent tubes (350-370 nm) | even, cheap, consistent once warmed; 5-10 cm above the frame |
| UV LED arrays (365/395 nm) | compact, no warm-up; 395 nm is less effective for some processes; check evenness |
| Metal halide / mercury vapour | powerful point source, sharper with point-light printing; heat, cost |
| Platemaker with integrator | commercial units count light units, not time - repeatable |

## Contact frames
Split-back frames let you inspect printing-out processes (salt, Van Dyke) without losing register. A vacuum
frame gives best contact for large negatives.

## Testing procedure
1. Check evenness: expose a sheet of sensitized paper with no negative for a short time - look for
   hot spots or falloff at edges.
2. Make a step tablet print (Stouffer 21-step) at a guessed time; find the step where maximum black first
   occurs = standard printing time.
3. Keep source-to-frame distance fixed; warm up tubes 5 min.
4. Log time, humidity, chemistry batch.

## Safety
UV damages eyes and skin: enclose the unit, never look at UV LEDs/tubes, wear UV glasses.

## Verify
- [ ] Evenness test uniform; standard printing time recorded per process.
