# Residual hypo and residual silver tests

## Residual silver (inadequate fixing) - ST-1
- Solution: 2% sodium sulfide stock, diluted 1:9 for use (Kodak ST-1). Smells of rotten eggs; ventilate.
1. Blot a white border area of a wet, washed print.
2. Place a drop of working solution, wait 2-3 min, blot.
3. Any yellow/brown stain beyond a barely visible cream = residual silver -> fix was inadequate or fixer
   exhausted. Refix in fresh fixer and rewash.

## Residual hypo (inadequate washing) - HT-2
- Solution (Kodak HT-2): 750 ml water, 125 ml 28% acetic acid, 7.5 g silver nitrate, water to 1 litre;
  store in brown bottle in the dark. Silver nitrate stains skin black - gloves.
1. Place a drop on a wet border area, leave about 2-4 min, rinse off with water (or salt water).
2. Compare the stain with a reference scale (Kodak Hypo Estimator); a faint cream stain is acceptable,
   a brown/yellow stain means more washing needed.

## Procedure use
Run both tests on a sample print each time you change washing method or paper; and periodically.

## Gotchas
- Test on border areas (they stain); trim them off afterwards if needed.
- Stale HT-2 gives false readings - make fresh at intervals.

## Verify
- [ ] Both tests pass on a sample of each archival batch; results logged.
