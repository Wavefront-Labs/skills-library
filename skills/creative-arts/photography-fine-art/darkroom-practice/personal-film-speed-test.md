# Personal film speed test

## Target
Exposure at which **Zone I** (4 stops under the meter reading) gives a density of about **0.10 above
film base plus fog (FB+F)**.

## Procedure
1. Evenly lit, textureless mid-tone card filling the frame; camera defocused to infinity; tripod.
2. Meter the card (that reading = Zone V). Close down **4 stops** for Zone I.
3. Shoot a series at Zone I with the meter set to EIs at 1/3-stop steps around box speed (e.g. 200, 250,
   320, 400, 500). Include one unexposed frame (FB+F) and one Zone V frame for reference.
4. Develop with your normal time.
5. Measure densities with a densitometer (or compare visually against a step tablet).
6. The EI whose Zone I frame reads FB+F + 0.10 is your personal speed. Commonly 1/2 to 1 stop slower than box.

## Without a densitometer
Make a contact print at the minimum time for maximum black through clear film base; the Zone I frame
should be just distinguishable from pure black.

## Gotchas
- Shutter and meter calibration errors are built into the result - that is the point: it's a system test.
- Change the developer or meter and you must retest.

## Verify
- [ ] EI written on the film's datasheet card; future negatives have printable shadow detail.
