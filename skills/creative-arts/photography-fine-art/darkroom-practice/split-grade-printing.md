# Split-grade printing

Variable-contrast (VC) paper has two emulsions: a soft (green-sensitive) and a hard (blue-sensitive).
Exposing separately through the softest and hardest filters lets you control highlights and shadows independently.

## Procedure
1. **Soft exposure test**: grade 00 (or yellow max on a colour head). Make a test strip; choose the
   time where **highlights** show the desired texture. Ignore shadows (they will be grey).
2. **Hard exposure test**: on a new sheet, give the chosen soft exposure overall, then a test strip at
   **grade 5** (magenta max). Choose the step where **shadows** reach the desired depth.
3. Print: soft time + hard time. Raising soft time darkens highlights mostly; raising hard time
   deepens shadows mostly.
4. **Local control**: dodge/burn with one grade only - burn a sky with grade 00 to add tone without
   blocking clouds; burn a shadow corner with grade 5.

## Gotchas
- Mid-tones get exposure from both - adjust in small steps (1/6 stop).
- Ilford Multigrade filter 00 and 5 need different exposure factors than mid grades (an Ilford MG 500
  head compensates automatically).
- Fogging safelight affects soft emulsion more (amber safelights are fine within limits).

## Verify
- [ ] Recorded soft and hard times; highlights and shadows each correct; dodges noted per grade.
