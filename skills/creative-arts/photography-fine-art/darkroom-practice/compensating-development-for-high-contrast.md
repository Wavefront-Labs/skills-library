# Compensating development for high contrast

Compensating developers exhaust locally in highlights while shadows continue, compressing a long
scene brightness range onto a printable negative.

## Options
| Method | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Two-bath (Divided D-23 / Stoeckler)** | Bath A (metol + sulfite) soaks in, little development; Bath B (borax) activates; development stops when absorbed developer exhausts in highlights. Times about 3-5 min each, temperature tolerant. |
| **Dilute developers** (Rodinal 1:100, HC-110 1:63+) with reduced agitation | compensating and acutance; risk of streaks |
| **Pyro (PMK, Pyrocat-HD)** | staining developer; stain acts as a proportional mask in highlights; excellent for contrasty scenes and alt-process negatives; toxic, gloves |
| **Water-bath development** | alternate developer and plain water baths; classic but less controllable |

## Procedure (two-bath)
1. Expose with shadows placed on Zone III or IV (generous).
2. Bath A 3-5 min with gentle agitation; pour back (reusable).
3. Bath B 3 min, minimal agitation; pour away or reuse limited times.
4. Stop, fix, wash as usual.

## Gotchas
- Low local contrast in mid-tones can look flat; print on a harder grade.
- Pyro stain needs enlarger/paper response consideration (stain is filtering for VC papers).

## Verify
- [ ] Scene with 8-10 stop range prints with texture in shadows and highlights on grade 2-3.
