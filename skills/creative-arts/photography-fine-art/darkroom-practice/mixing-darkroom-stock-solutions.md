# Mixing darkroom stock solutions

## Dilution notation
- **1+4** (or 1:4 in Ilford/Kodak usage) = 1 part concentrate + 4 parts water = 5 parts total.
  For 500 ml at 1+4: 100 ml concentrate + 400 ml water.
- Some authors use 1:4 to mean 1 in 4 total - check the source's convention.

## Powder procedure (e.g. D-76/ID-11)
1. Start with about 3/4 of final volume of water at the specified temperature (D-76: 52 deg C).
2. Add powder slowly while stirring; for multi-part kits add parts in order, each dissolved before the next.
3. Top up with cold water to final volume.
4. Let it rest/cool to room temperature (D-76 benefits from 24 h rest).
5. Store in full, airtight brown bottles (collapsible or with marbles to exclude air); label with name,
   date, dilution.

## Formula mixing (from Anchell)
Weigh with a 0.1 g scale; dissolve in the listed order (preservative sodium sulfite often first except for
metol which goes in before sulfite); distilled water for consistency.

## Safety
- Gloves, eye protection, dust mask for powders; mix in ventilated area.
- Never mix acids into small water volumes violently; add acid to water.
- Label everything; keep away from food containers.

## Gotchas
- Air (half-empty bottles) oxidises developer fast - discoloured developer is suspect.
- Hard tap water can precipitate; use distilled for stock.

## Verify
- [ ] Bottles labelled with contents, dilution and date; developer tested with a film clip if old.
