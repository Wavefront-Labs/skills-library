# Stand and semi-stand development

Highly dilute developer with little or no agitation. Developer exhausts in dense (highlight) areas while
continuing in thin (shadow) areas -> **compensation** (tamed highlights) and **edge effects** (acutance).

## Typical recipe
- Rodinal/Adonal 1:100, 20 deg C, 60 min.
- Agitate gently 30-60 s at start, tap the tank to remove bubbles.
- **Semi-stand**: one gentle agitation (2-3 inversions) at the halfway point (30 min) reduces streaking.
- Stop, fix and wash normally.
- Some workers use HC-110 dilution H or 1:100, or Pyro (PMK) semi-stand.

## When to use
- High-contrast scenes; mixed-EI rolls (forgiving); sheet film with sharp edges wanted.

## Risks
- **Bromide drag / streaks**: by-products slide down the film from sprocket holes or dense areas -
  streaks or uneven density. Semi-stand and enough developer volume help; vertical tanks need
  extra volume above the reel.
- **Uneven development**, mottle in skies.
- Shadow speed may be lower than claimed; test.

## Gotchas
- Use at least the minimum concentrate (e.g. >= 5 ml Rodinal per roll; 1:100 in a 500 ml tank = 5 ml).
- Temperature drifts over an hour - stand the tank in a water bath.

## Verify
- [ ] Even skies without streaks; highlights printable on normal grade.
