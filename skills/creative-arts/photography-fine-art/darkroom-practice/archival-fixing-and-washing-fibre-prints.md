# Archival fixing and washing of fibre prints

## Two-bath fixing
1. Fixer bath 1: does most of the work (absorbs most silver).
2. Fixer bath 2: fresh, completes fixing with low silver content.
3. Time in each: per datasheet (e.g. half the total time each).
4. When bath 1 reaches capacity, discard it, move bath 2 to position 1, mix a new bath 2.
   Keep a log of prints per bath.

Ilford's shortest archival sequence (Ilford Rapid Fixer at film strength 1+4): fix about 1 minute,
wash 5 min, Washaid (hypo-clear) 10 min, final wash 5 min - check the current Ilford datasheet; shorter fixing
keeps thiosulfate from soaking deep into the fibre base.

## Washing
- **Hypo clearing agent** (Kodak Hypo Clearing Agent, Ilford Washaid) shortens washing.
- Archival vertical slot washer: separate prints, continuous flow; 30-60 min without hypo-clear, about
  10-20 min with (per sequence used).
- Tray washing: fresh water changes, shuffle prints; less reliable.
- Water at 20-24 deg C washes faster; very cold water slows thiosulfate removal.

## Selenium in the sequence
Toning in selenium (diluted with hypo clearing agent) after the first wash is common; then wash fully.

## Gotchas
- Overfixing (long times in strong fixer) increases washing time and can bleach highlights.
- Air bubbles or prints stuck together = uneven wash; separate them.

## Verify
- [ ] Residual silver (ST-1) and hypo (HT-2) tests pass on a test print per batch.
