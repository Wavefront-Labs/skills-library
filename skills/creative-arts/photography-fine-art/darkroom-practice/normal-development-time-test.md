# Normal development time test

## Target
With your personal EI, a **Zone VIII** exposure should give roughly:
- about **1.25-1.35** above FB+F for a diffusion/cold-light enlarger,
- about **1.15-1.25** for a condenser enlarger (more contrast from Callier effect).
These are starting values from Zone System literature; adjust to print well on grade 2 paper.

## Procedure
1. At your EI, meter the evenly lit card and **open 3 stops** for Zone VIII. Expose several frames/sheets.
2. Also expose Zone I and Zone V frames for reference.
3. Develop at a series of times (e.g. datasheet time -15%, 0, +15%, +30%) - one film/sheet per time, or cut the roll.
4. Measure Zone VIII density for each.
5. Plot density against time; read the time giving the target = **N**.
6. Practical check: print the negative of a real scene on grade 2; highlights should hold texture.

## Derived
- N+1: time where Zone VII exposure reaches the Zone VIII target (often about +40% of N).
- N-1: time where Zone IX exposure reaches the Zone VIII target (often about -20-30%).

## Gotchas
- Agitation, temperature, water and dilution must be identical to normal practice.
- Roll film can't be split by development easily; cut test rolls in the dark into sections.

## Verify
- [ ] N, N+1, N-1 times recorded for film/developer/EI combination.
