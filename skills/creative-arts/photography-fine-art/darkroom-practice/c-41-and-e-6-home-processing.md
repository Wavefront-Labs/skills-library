# C-41 and E-6 home processing

## C-41 (colour negative)
- Standard developer temperature **37.8 deg C (100 F), tolerance +/- 0.15 F (about 0.08 deg C) per Kodak**; developer 3:15.
- Home kits (CineStill Cs41, Tetenal/Fuji kits): developer 3:30 at 39 deg C typical; blix 6-8 min; wash; stabiliser
  (formaldehyde-free versions in modern kits). Times increase with reuse (follow kit chart).
- Lower-temp options (e.g. 24-30 deg C with extended time) exist; colour crossover risk higher.

## E-6 (slide)
- Kodak 6-bath process: first developer **6 min at 38 deg C** (most critical: controls density/speed),
  wash, reversal, colour developer, pre-bleach, bleach, fix, final rinse.
- 3-bath kits (Fuji/Tetenal-type): first dev, colour dev, blix. First developer temperature +/- 0.3 deg C.

## Temperature control
- Water bath with **sous-vide circulator** or a Jobo rotary processor.
- Pre-heat tank with water at temperature for 1-2 min.
- Use a calibrated digital thermometer (check against a lab thermometer).

## Agitation
Rotary (Jobo) or inversion per kit. Consistency matters more than method.

## Faults
| Symptom | Cause |
|---|---|
| Colour crossover / casts | temperature drift, exhausted developer |
| Dense (dark) slides | first developer too short/cold |
| Milky negatives | incomplete blix |
| Pink/magenta base on old negs | missing stabiliser/final rinse (kit dependent) |

## Verify
- [ ] Logged temperature trace; base colour and density consistent roll to roll.
