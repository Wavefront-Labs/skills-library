# Zone placement and reading highlight fall

## Zones (print values)
0 pure black, I first density, II first texture, **III textured shadow**, IV dark mid, **V mid-grey (meter)**,
VI light skin, VII light textured, **VIII highlight texture**, IX white without texture, X paper white.

## Procedure
1. Spot-meter the **darkest area where you need texture** (e.g. dark tree bark) -> meter says Zone V.
2. **Place** it on Zone III: close down 2 stops from that reading. This sets exposure.
3. Spot-meter the **important highlight** (white wall). Count stops from the shadow reading:
   if the highlight reads 5 stops brighter than the shadow, it **falls** on Zone VIII (III + 5).
4. Decide development:
   - falls on VIII -> **N** (normal);
   - falls on IX -> **N-1**; X -> N-2;
   - falls on VII -> **N+1**; VI -> N+2.
5. Record placement and development on the exposure record / holder.

## Gotchas
- Using a personal EI is essential; placement assumes calibrated speed.
- A spot meter with 1 deg angle; aim at an area larger than the spot.
- Specular highlights fall above X and are allowed to block.

## Verify
- [ ] Exposure record shows shadow placement, highlight fall and chosen N.
