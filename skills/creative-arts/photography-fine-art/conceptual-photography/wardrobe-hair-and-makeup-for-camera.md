# Wardrobe, hair and makeup for camera

## Wardrobe
- Choose colours from the image palette; avoid logos, fine stripes/checks (moire on digital), and
  pure white/black that clip or crush unless intended.
- Matte fabrics photograph better than shiny; satin creates hot spots under hard light.
- Fit: clip and pin at the back (A-clamps, safety pins) out of camera view; steam out creases.
- Period: check silhouette, hem length, shoes; viewers notice shoes and glasses.

## Hair and makeup
- Makeup for strobes: matte foundation matched to neck, powder to reduce shine, avoid SPF with
  titanium/zinc (white flashback).
- Hair: control flyaways if not intended (spray, wax stick); stylised staging may want natural messiness.
- Check under the shooting light, not mirror light.

## Continuity (for series or multiple takes/composites)
Photograph wardrobe and hair from front, side and back after styling; note jewellery, sleeves, buttons;
reset between takes.

## Gotchas
- New garments look new - wash, wear in or age them for lived-in characters.
- Strong makeup reads stronger in print than on set.

## Verify
- [ ] Continuity photos taken; wardrobe matches palette; no shine or moire.
