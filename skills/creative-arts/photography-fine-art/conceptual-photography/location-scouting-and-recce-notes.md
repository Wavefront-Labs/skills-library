# Location scouting and recce notes

## Capture on the recce
1. Photograph from each candidate camera position with the planned focal length (or phone zoom
   equivalent), plus 360 deg panoramas.
2. Record GPS pin, address, contact/owner.
3. Sun path: use PhotoPills AR to see where the sun will be at the shoot time; note shadows from buildings.
4. Measure room dimensions and ceiling height (lights and backdrops need space).
5. Note power sockets, circuits/amperage, parking, loading access, toilets, noise (traffic, schools),
   phone signal.
6. Permissions: who signs off; fees; insurance requirements; public land permits (city film offices).

## Recce sheet
Single page per location: photos, sketch plan with camera positions, sun/shadow note, power, access,
contact, permissions status, risks.

## Gotchas
- Scout at the same time of day and weekday as the planned shoot.
- Seasons change foliage and sun height; re-scout if months pass.

## Verify
- [ ] Recce sheet complete; permission confirmed in writing.
