# Dressing a real location

## Procedure
1. **Photograph the location before touching anything** - wide and detail shots for restoration.
2. Clear: remove modern clutter, logos, personal items; bag and label by room/spot.
3. Dress: place props from the prop list to the sketch; build from background to foreground.
4. Check through the lens constantly; dressing only matters where the camera sees.
5. Add life: slight disorder, used objects, light sources on (lamps, TV glow).
6. Protect: floor runners, felt pads under furniture, low-tack tape only, no nails without permission.
7. Restore: reverse with the photos; owner walk-through and sign-off.

## Gotchas
- Mirrors, windows and glossy surfaces reveal crew and lights; check reflections.
- Removing items can damage paint/wallpaper - photograph and handle with care.
- Insurance: confirm your liability cover for location damage.

## Verify
- [ ] Before/after photos; owner sign-off; frame free of unintended objects.
