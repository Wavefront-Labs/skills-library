# Sketching the frame before the shoot

## Procedure
1. Draw 4-6 small thumbnails in the final aspect ratio; vary camera height, distance and where
   figures sit. Choose one.
2. Add a **plan view** (bird's-eye) of the set: camera position, lens angle of view lines, figures,
   furniture, lights with modifiers and direction.
3. Note lens and approximate distance (angle of view for a given focal length tells you how wide the
   set must be - e.g. 35 mm FF at 5 m sees about 5 m wide).
4. Add a **lighting diagram** (lightingdiagrams.com Online Lighting Diagram Creator or hand drawn)
   with key, fill, practicals.
5. On the scout, photograph the location from the planned spot with the planned lens to check.

## Gotchas
- Sketches without a lens decision lead to building sets too small or too large.
- Keep the sketch flexible: the set may offer better angles.

## Verify
- [ ] Thumbnail, plan with camera/lens/lights, and a scout frame all agree.
