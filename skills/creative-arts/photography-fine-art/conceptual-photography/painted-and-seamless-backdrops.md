# Painted and seamless backdrops

## Types
- **Seamless paper** (2.72 m / 1.35 m rolls): clean colour; tears and creases show - pull fresh paper.
- **Vinyl**: wipeable, but shiny - keep lights out of its family of angles.
- **Hand-painted canvas/muslin** (Oliphant-style mottled): painterly portrait look; expensive; roll, don't fold.

## Painting a canvas backdrop
1. Primed cotton canvas or muslin stretched on the floor or a frame.
2. Base coat in mid tone (acrylic/latex house paint, matte).
3. Sponge or rag 2-3 related tones, wet-in-wet, blending large soft shapes; lighter in the middle.
4. Test photograph at shooting distance and aperture - mottles blur at f/2.8 and 2 m from subject.

## Lighting
- Separate the subject 1.5-3 m from the backdrop to avoid shadows and to control tone.
- Use a gridded background light for a gradient/halo; for pure white, light 1-1.5 stops over subject.
- Use flags to keep subject light off the backdrop for darker tone.

## Gotchas
- Seams and footprints on paper show at floor level; tape down, use shoe covers.
- Colour spill from a coloured backdrop onto the subject's edges.

## Verify
- [ ] Backdrop tone/gradient as planned; no creases, shadows or spill on the subject.
