# Colour gels for mood

## Common schemes
- **Warm key / cool fill or background** (orange-teal): separation and cinematic depth.
- **Single colour wash** (red/magenta) for intensity; skin loses detail - use with a white/neutral key.
- **Two complementary rim lights** on either side of the subject.
- **Coloured background** gel on a white wall while the subject stays neutral.

## Procedure
1. Light the subject with a neutral key first.
2. Add coloured lights for background/rim/fill; set WB for the neutral key (fixed Kelvin).
3. Control saturation: gel density, distance (brighter = more desaturated in camera), and how much
   white light mixes in.
4. Dense gels (deep blue, primary red) cost 2-5 stops; plan power.
5. Expose so coloured areas do not clip a single channel (watch the RGB histogram).

## Gotchas
- Gels on hot tungsten/old strobes burn; leave space or use heat-resistant gels.
- RGB LED panels mimic gels but have narrow spectra - skin can look odd.
- Channel clipping in red/blue produces flat blobs - underexpose coloured areas slightly.

## Verify
- [ ] Colour contrast is intentional; skin in the neutral light holds detail; no channel clipping.
