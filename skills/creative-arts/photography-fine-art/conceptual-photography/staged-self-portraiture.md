# Staged self-portraiture

## Setup
1. Camera on a tripod, composition locked using a **stand-in** (light stand, chair, mannequin) at your
   planned position.
2. Focus on the stand-in in manual focus; mark the floor (tape X) where your face/eyes must be.
3. Stop down a little (f/5.6-f/8) for some focus margin.
4. Trigger: camera app (Canon Camera Connect, SnapBridge, Imaging Edge) with live view on a phone/tablet
   - lets you see framing and expression; or intervalometer (shoot every 3-5 s for 2 min); or remote.
5. Eye/face AF tracking with the app remote can focus automatically once you are in frame.

## Working method
- Shoot many takes; review in batches; adjust one thing at a time (pose, light, wardrobe).
- A mirror next to the camera helps check pose (or tethered screen facing you).
- Sherman's method: costume, makeup and set built for the character; you are the actor, not yourself.

## Gotchas
- Phone remote lag means late expressions; use intervalometer bursts.
- Your own shadow on the backdrop when walking to the mark - plan lights around the route.

## Verify
- [ ] Face sharp at the mark; selected frame reads as character/idea, not as a test.
