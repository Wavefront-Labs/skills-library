# Shot list and call sheet

## Shot list (per setup)
Setup number, description/sketch reference, location area, lens/camera position, lights, talent,
props/wardrobe, estimated time, priority (must/nice). Order to minimise lighting resets and to follow
the light (exterior sunset setups at the right time).

## Call sheet (send the evening before)
- Project title, date, producer contact, emergency contact.
- **Call times** per person (crew earlier than talent).
- Location address, map pin, parking, access instructions, nearest hospital.
- Weather forecast, sunrise/sunset times.
- Schedule by setup with times.
- Crew roles: photographer, assistant(s), digital tech, stylist, hair/makeup, producer.
- Talent list, wardrobe notes.
- Safety notes: haze, ladders, road, water, children present.

## Procedure
1. Draft shot list from sketches; estimate times generously (+30%).
2. Build schedule backwards from light-critical setups.
3. Circulate; confirm receipt from each person.
4. On the day, update and tick setups; record what slipped for the next plan.

## Verify
- [ ] Everyone has the call sheet and confirmed; critical setups scheduled against the light.
