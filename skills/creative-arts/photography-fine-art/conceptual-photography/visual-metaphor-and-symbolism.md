# Visual metaphor and symbolism

## Levels (after Panofsky)
1. **Primary**: what is literally there (a woman, an empty glass).
2. **Conventional**: culturally coded meaning (wilted flowers = transience; vanitas).
3. **Intrinsic**: what the image reveals about attitudes of its time and maker.

## Procedure
1. Write the idea as an abstract statement ("care is invisible labour").
2. List 20 concrete images associated with it - objects, actions, places. Push past the first 5 (cliches).
3. Mark each: obvious (cliche), readable, obscure. Aim for readable-but-fresh.
4. Combine one primary literal element with one displaced/unexpected element - tension makes meaning.
5. Test on two people without explanation: what do they read? Adjust.

## Gotchas
- Too many symbols turn the image into a rebus.
- Symbols are cultural: test readings with your intended audience.
- The punctum (Barthes) cannot be designed - leave room for detail you did not plan.
