# Working with non-professional sitters

## Casting
1. Approach with a printed card/portfolio and a one-paragraph project description.
2. Explain time required, what they will wear, where the work will be shown.
3. Offer payment or prints; follow through.

## On set
- Short sessions; frequent breaks; warm room; water.
- Explain each step before doing it; show them a frame on the camera/monitor early.
- Give simple physical directions (one at a time) rather than emotional ones.
- Repetition is tiring for non-actors; get the key setup ready before they arrive (stand-in for lighting).

## Consent and ethics
- Release signed; right to withdraw before publication discussed.
- Consider how the image represents them and their community; avoid humiliating staging.
- Send them the final image; invite them to the show.

## Gotchas
- Nervous sitters stiffen at the shutter sound: silent shutter, tethered display off, chat continuously.
- Children: guardian present, shorter sessions, follow safeguarding rules.

## Verify
- [ ] Sitter understood use, signed release, received image; session within agreed time.
