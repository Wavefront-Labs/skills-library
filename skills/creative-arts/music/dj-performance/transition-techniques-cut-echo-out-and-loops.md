# Transition techniques: cut, echo out and loops

**Goal:** a toolbox of transitions so mixes fit the music, tempo and genre.

## 1. Quick cut (on the one)
- Cue the incoming track at a strong downbeat (often a vocal or drop); on the playing track's phrase end, slam the crossfader or channel fader across exactly on beat 1.
- Best for open-format, hip-hop, and tempo/genre changes where blends would clash.

## 2. Echo out
- On the outgoing track, engage **Echo/Delay** (1/2 or 1 beat, high feedback) or "Echo Out" Beat FX, then cut or fade its channel: the echo tail carries over while the new track starts on the one.
- Use for tempo changes and ending tracks without awkward outros.

## 3. Loops
- **Loop extension**: loop 8-16 bars of a beat-only intro/outro to buy time for a long blend or to fix short intros.
- **Loop roll** (1/4 to 1/32 beat repeat, momentary) for build-ups into a drop.
- Auto-loop on beat grid (quantise on) so loops are seamless; check the beat grid first.

## 4. Filter transitions
- High-pass the outgoing track up while bringing the new one in; or low-pass the incoming and open it on the drop. Pair with EQ bass swap (see bass-swap skill).

## 5. Spinback / brake
- Spin the outgoing record backwards (or Spinback/Brake effect on CDJs/controllers), new track drops on the one. Energetic, used in hip-hop, drum and bass, and open format.

## 6. Drop swap
- Mix the build of track A, then cut to the drop of track B (same key/tempo). Dance music staple.

## Choosing
| Situation | Technique |
|---|---|
| Same genre, long intros/outros | long EQ blend |
| Different tempo or genre | echo out, cut, spinback |
| Short intro | loop extension, quick cut |
| Peak-time energy | drop swap, loop roll |

## Gotchas
- Echo outs with too much feedback clash with the incoming track; kill the tail quickly with the FX level.
- Loops off the grid cause skips; fix grids in prep.
- Same transition every time sounds predictable.

## Verify
- Recorded set: each transition lands on the downbeat with no gaps or clashes; variety across the set.
