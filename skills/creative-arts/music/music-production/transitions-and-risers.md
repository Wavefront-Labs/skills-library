# Transitions and risers

**Goal:** seamless or dramatic movement between sections that tells the listener something new is coming.

## 1. Transition tools
| Tool | How to make it | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **White-noise riser** | noise oscillator, high-pass/band-pass sweep up over 4-8 bars, volume up, reverb | builds |
| **Reverse cymbal** | reverse a crash sample; end exactly on the downbeat | into choruses |
| **Reverse reverb** | reverse a vocal word, add 100% wet reverb, bounce, reverse again: the reverb swells into the word | vocal entries |
| **Impact / boom** | layered low hit + crash on the downbeat | drops, chorus starts |
| **Downlifter** | noise or synth sweeping down after an impact | release after a drop |
| **Tape stop** | pitch and speed slow to zero over 1/2-1 beat (plugin or manual) | before a drop or stop |
| **Drum fill** | snare/tom fill on the last bar or two | live-sounding transitions |
| **Mute/drop-out** | stop everything for a beat (or keep only vocals) | before a chorus |
| Filter sweep | low-pass opening on the music bus | intros and builds |

## 2. Timing
- Snap the **end** (peak) of risers and reverse effects to the bar line, not the start.
- Pre-chorus to chorus: one-bar or two-bar transition is usually enough in pop; EDM builds 8-16 bars.
- Leave space: transitions compete with vocals; duck or high-pass them under vocal lines.

## 3. Layering
- Combine 2-3 elements (reverse cymbal + riser + snare roll) at lower levels rather than one loud one.
- Automate risers' filter and pitch rather than relying on one static sample.

## 4. Subtle transitions (non-EDM)
- A cymbal swell, a guitar slide, a vocal pickup ("ooh"), a bass walk-up, a single kick drop-out are often more musical than big FX.

## Gotchas
- Riser peaks landing after the downbeat sound sloppy.
- Every section having a big riser becomes predictable; vary.
- Long reverb tails crossing into the new section muddy the downbeat: cut or duck them.

## Verify
- Listen from 4 bars before to 4 bars after each section change: the downbeat hits cleanly.
- Mute all transition FX: if nothing feels lost, they were too subtle or unnecessary.
