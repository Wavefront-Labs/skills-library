# Sample clearance

**Goal:** release music containing samples without legal risk. This is practical guidance,
not legal advice; use a music lawyer or clearance house for real deals.

## 1. Two separate rights
| Right | Owned by | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| **Sound recording (master)** | record label / artist who owns the recording | master-use licence |
| **Composition (song)** | songwriters and their publishers | publishing licence (and a share of the new song) |
- Using the original recording needs **both**. **Interpolation** (re-recording the melody/lyric yourself) needs only the **publishing** licence.

## 2. Process
1. **Identify** the exact source (track, version, timestamp) and how much you use, where and how often.
2. **Find owners**: label via the release credits/Discogs; publishers via PRO databases (ASCAP/BMI Songview, PRS, SOCAN repertory search).
3. **Send a request**: your track (or a demo with the sample), the original, the sample's length and use, your release plan (label, territory, formats, expected sales/streams).
4. **Negotiate**: master side usually an upfront fee plus possibly a royalty; publishing side usually a percentage of the new composition (often substantial for prominent loops/hooks).
5. Get it **in writing before release**; register the agreed splits with your PRO/publisher.
- Clearance can take weeks to months; start early. Clearance houses do this for a fee.

## 3. Safer alternatives
- **Royalty-free libraries** (Splice, Loopmasters, Native Instruments Expansions): licence allows use in your music; check terms (usually no resale of samples as-is, and some disallow use in sample packs or Content ID claims).
- **Replay/interpolation** to avoid the master licence.
- Commission original material in the style of the record.

## Gotchas
- There is **no safe length** (the "under 2 seconds" myth is false; US Bridgeport v. Dimension held that any sampling of a recording needs a licence in that circuit).
- Uncleared samples are commonly caught by Content ID and distributor checks; tracks get pulled and earnings frozen.
- Royalty-free sample packs that themselves contain uncleared samples exist; buy from reputable sources.

## Verify
- Paperwork on file for both master and publishing (or a royalty-free licence) for every sample before distribution.
- Splits sheet updated to include sample owners' shares.
