# Energy arc, builds and drops

**Goal:** a track whose energy rises and falls on purpose, with builds that pay off.

## 1. Map the energy first
- Draw an energy graph (1-10) over time for the song before arranging. Typical pop: intro 3, verse 4, pre 6, chorus 8, verse 2 5, chorus 9, bridge 4-5, final chorus 10.
- EDM (club): intro (DJ-friendly, 16-32 bars of beat), breakdown, build, **drop**, second breakdown, build, drop 2, outro (16-32 bars). Sections in multiples of 8 bars.
- Import a reference track and mark its sections in the DAW; copy proportions, not content.

## 2. Ways to add energy (a build)
- **Add layers** (count active parts per section; each section up adds 1-2).
- **Rhythmic density**: snare/clap rolls accelerating (1/4 -> 1/8 -> 1/16 -> 1/32 over 8 bars).
- **Filter**: high-pass sweep up on the music bus (removes low end, creates suspense).
- **Pitch risers**: noise sweeps, synth risers, pitch-up automation (see transitions skill).
- **Harmonic tension**: sustain on V or a suspended chord; pedal bass.
- Volume automation rising slightly; reverb/delay sends up.

## 3. The drop
- Remove the kick and bass during the build, then bring everything back on the downbeat.
- **Pre-drop gap**: a beat or half-bar of silence or a vocal shout before the drop increases impact.
- First hit: crash + kick + sub together; undo all build automations exactly on the downbeat.
- Make drop 2 different (new bass sound, extra layer, half-time switch).

## 4. Removing energy (breakdowns and verses)
- Drop the drums or the low end, thin the harmony, or switch to half-time. Contrast is what makes the next chorus or drop feel big.

## Gotchas
- Everything at full energy from the start leaves nowhere to go.
- Builds that last too long (more than 16 bars in pop contexts) lose the listener.
- Forgetting to reset filters/automation after the build: the drop sounds thin.

## Verify
- Count layers per section in the arrangement view: the chart should match the energy graph.
- Listen at low volume from another room: section changes should still be obvious.
