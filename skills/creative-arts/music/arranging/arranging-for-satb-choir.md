# Arranging for SATB choir

**Goal:** an arrangement your choir can learn quickly and sing well in tune.

## 1. Comfortable ranges (amateur choir; stretch by a tone for skilled groups)
| Voice | Comfortable | Use sparingly |
|---|---|---|
| Soprano | D4-F5 | to A5 |
| Alto | A3-C5 | G3, D5 |
| Tenor | D3-F4 (sounds 8va lower than written treble) | G4 for climaxes |
| Bass | G2-C4 | E2/F2 low, D4 |
- Tenors are often weakest: keep them mid-range and doubled by altos or basses at exposed moments.

## 2. Texture choices
- **Homophonic** (all voices same rhythm): clear words, easy to learn; hymn style.
- **Melody + accompaniment voices** (oohs/aahs under melody): melody in soprano, or moved to tenor/alto for variety in verse 2.
- **Polyphonic/imitative** entries: interest, but harder for amateurs; keep short.
- **Unison** passages: strong and easy; good for openings and verse 1.
- Build density across verses: unison -> 2-part -> SATB -> divisi climax.

## 3. Voice leading for singers
- Inner parts should be **singable lines**: stepwise, common tones held, avoid augmented intervals and awkward leaps (7ths, tritones).
- Keep spacing: no more than an octave between S-A and A-T; T-B can be wider.
- Doubling: double the root; avoid doubling the leading note.
- Give every part some melodic interest; altos and tenors repeating one note for 16 bars get bored and go flat.

## 4. Score layout
- Open score (4 staves) for teaching complex parts; **closed score** (SA treble, TB bass) for hymn-like textures.
- Tenor part in treble clef with the small 8 below. Lyrics under every staff in open score.
- Add a piano rehearsal reduction (see piano-reduction skill) even for a cappella pieces.

## Gotchas
- Keys that put the melody's climax above F5 or basses below F2 for long; transpose the whole piece.
- Text setting: stressed syllables on strong beats in all parts (check prosody per part, not just melody).

## Verify
- Sing or play each part alone: it should be a sensible tune.
- Range check per part against the table; mark any note outside comfortable range and justify it.
