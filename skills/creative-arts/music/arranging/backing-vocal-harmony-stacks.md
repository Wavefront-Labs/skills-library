# Backing vocal harmony stacks

**Goal:** backing vocals (BVs) that thicken and lift the song without fighting the lead.

## 1. Types of BV part
| Part | Use |
|---|---|
| Lead doubles (unison) | thicken the lead in choruses; tight timing essential |
| Octave double (up or down) | weight or air |
| **Parallel harmony** (3rd above / 6th below) | classic pop/country lift on hook lines |
| Triad stack (3 parts) | chorus "wall"; root/3rd/5th of each chord, voice-led |
| Pads (oohs, aahs, mm) | sustained chords under verses or bridges |
| Call-and-response / echoes | answer lead phrases in gaps |
| Gang / group shouts | hooks, chants (many voices, loose) |

## 2. Choosing the notes
1. Start from the lead melody and the chord under each note.
2. **3rd above** the lead works when the result is a chord tone; where it isn't (passing notes), use the 4th or stay on a common tone, or switch to 6th below.
3. For 3-part stacks, voice-lead: each line moves by step or common tone; avoid all three parts leaping in parallel unless it's a deliberate "block" effect.
4. Keep harmony **below the lead's top note** in the chorus climax so the lead stays on top (or put the high harmony only on the last line for a lift).
5. Simplify the rhythm: BVs drop passing notes and ornaments of the lead; match consonants at phrase ends.

## 3. Tracking order and practice
- Record leads first, then doubles, then harmonies low to high. 2-4 takes per part, each a separate track, for width (pan pairs L/R).
- Sing BVs **less breathy and with matched vowels** (all "ah" not mixed "ah/aw"); cut final consonants ("s", "t") on all but one take to avoid clicks.
- Print a lyric sheet with harmony lines marked (arrows or notation); guide the singer by playing the part on piano.

## Gotchas
- Harmonies a 2nd away from the lead on held notes = clash; check every held note.
- Too many stacks in the verse leave nothing for the chorus; save width for the chorus.
- Timing: tighten BVs to the lead (VocAlign/Revoice, or manual edit), especially consonants.

## Verify
- Solo the BV stack: it should form correct chords on its own.
- Mute/unmute against the lead: chorus should lift without the lead getting buried.
