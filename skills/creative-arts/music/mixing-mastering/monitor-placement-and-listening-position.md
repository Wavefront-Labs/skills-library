# Monitor placement and listening position

**Goal:** hear an accurate stereo image and a low end you can trust, before buying any
treatment.

## 1. Symmetry
- Set up along the **long axis** of the room (facing the short wall), centred left-right. Asymmetric side walls skew the stereo image and bass.

## 2. The triangle
- Speakers and your head form an **equilateral triangle**, typically 1-1.5 m per side for near-fields.
- Toe the speakers in so each points at (or just behind) your ears.
- **Tweeters at ear height** (or tilt the speaker so its axis points at your ears); vertical speakers are generally preferred over sideways ones unless the manufacturer allows horizontal use.

## 3. Distance from walls (SBIR)
- Speaker-boundary interference causes notches in the low end. Either put the speaker **very close** to the front wall (< ~0.3 m; then reduce bass with the boundary/"half-space" switch) or **far** (> ~1.5 m); the in-between distances cause the worst cancellations in the low-mids.
- Avoid equal distances to the front wall, side wall and floor (their notches stack).

## 4. Listening position
- The **38% rule**: sit about 38% of the room length from the front wall as a starting point; avoid the exact middle of the room (bass null).
- Measure (REW with a measurement mic) or move in 10 cm steps while playing a bass sweep or a well-known track; choose the spot with the most even low end.

## 5. Isolation and surroundings
- Put speakers on stands or isolation pads (not directly on a resonant desk); angle down/up to your ears.
- Keep the desk surface and screens from reflecting into your ears (move speakers slightly forward of the desk edge).
- Same height and toe-in for both speakers; measure with a tape.

## Gotchas
- Speakers pushed into corners: massive bass build-up you'll mix away (thin mixes).
- Mixing off-centre (e.g. desk to the side): image and level offsets.
- Speaker back-panel EQ switches left at defaults regardless of placement.

## Verify
- Play a mono signal (pink noise or a centre vocal): it should image as a solid phantom centre.
- Measure with REW: frequency response at the listening position as even as placement allows (aim within about +/-6 dB below 300 Hz before treatment).
