# Album sequencing, spacing and level matching

**Goal:** an album that plays as a coherent whole, not a collection of loudest-possible singles.

## 1. Sequencing (with the artist)
- Opener: strong, representative, quick to engage. Track 2-3: often the singles. Vary tempo, key and energy to avoid fatigue; place a breather mid-album; end with a closer that feels final.
- Check key relationships between adjacent songs (a jarring key jump can be deliberate or awkward).
- Vinyl: plan sides (time per side limits; strongest tracks at the start of each side; quieter/brighter content not at the inner groove end).

## 2. Spacing and transitions
- Gaps between tracks typically 1-3 s; judge by feel (the tail of one song and the energy of the next). Measure from the end of audible decay, not the file end.
- **Crossfades** or segues for continuous albums; deliver **gapless** (DDP with index points, or gapless-tagged digital).
- Fades: shape fade-outs in mastering if requested; fade into silence (no truncated reverb tails), and trim leading silence consistently (a few milliseconds before the first transient, or a short pre-roll).

## 3. Level matching
- Match **perceived** loudness, not meters alone: a ballad should feel quieter than a rocker but not so quiet that listeners turn it up.
- Use integrated LUFS as a guide (ballads 1-3 LU below rockers is common) and adjust by ear in sequence playback.
- Check transitions: the start of each song against the end of the previous one.
- Streaming album normalisation (Spotify album mode, Apple Sound Check per album) keeps relative levels, so the album's internal balance matters.

## 4. Process
1. Master each track roughly to similar character.
2. Assemble in a sequence session (DAW playlist, WaveLab montage, Pyramix, Sequoia).
3. Play through start to finish at normal level; adjust levels, gaps, fades.
4. Re-check EQ consistency across songs (a song much darker than its neighbours stands out).

## Gotchas
- Mastering every song to the same LUFS: ballads end up too loud and squashed.
- Silence trimmed so tight the first transient is cut.

## Verify
- Full album playback without touching the volume knob: no song jumps out.
- Gaps and fades approved by the artist; delivered DDP/digital files play gapless where intended.
