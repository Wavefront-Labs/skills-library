# PIMA, rest stroke and free stroke

**Goal:** a reliable, beautiful-toned right hand for classical and fingerstyle repertoire.

## 1. PIMA
- p = thumb (pulgar), i = index, m = middle, a = ring (anular); c/ch = little finger (rare).
- Default assignment: p on strings 4-6 (bass), i-m-a on 3-2-1. Melody lines **alternate i-m** (never the same finger twice in a row on scales).

## 2. Strokes
| Stroke | Motion | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Free stroke** (tirando) | finger plucks and moves into the palm, clearing the next string | arpeggios, chords, accompaniment |
| **Rest stroke** (apoyando) | finger plucks through and comes to rest on the next lower string | melody notes, scales: fuller, louder tone |
| Thumb rest stroke | thumb rests on the next higher string | strong bass lines |

- Motion from the **knuckle joint** with fingertip joint relaxed but firm; follow-through small.

## 3. Planting
- Place the finger (or several) on the string **before** playing it: improves accuracy and speed in arpeggios; "sequential planting" plants each finger just before it plays.
- Planting also stops ringing strings (the finger is a mute).

## 4. Tone and nails
- Contact: flesh first, then nail leaves the string. Nails shaped to follow the fingertip with a smooth ramp, slightly longer on the left side for right-handed players; polish with 1000-2000 grit paper.
- Brighter: play nearer the bridge (sul ponticello) and with more nail; warmer: over the soundhole (sul tasto) and angled stroke.

## 5. Practice
- Giuliani 120 Studies (Op. 1): pick 5 patterns a day, slow, with metronome, planting.
- Scales with rest stroke i-m, then m-a, then i-a, 60-100 bpm.
- Tremolo (p-a-m-i) as an advanced goal (Recuerdos de la Alhambra).

## Gotchas
- Hand bouncing on each stroke wastes energy; the hand stays stable, only fingers move.
- Same-finger repetition ("walking" with one finger) in scales limits speed.
- Rough nails cause scratchy tone - check daily.

## Verify
- Record a scale: rest-stroke notes should be even and full; arpeggios even in volume with the melody note voiced louder.
- Mirror check: the hand stays still while fingers move.
