# Alternate, economy and hybrid picking

**Goal:** clean, even picking at speed, with a technique chosen for the line.

## 1. Grip and motion
- Pick held between thumb pad and side of the index finger; 1-3 mm of tip showing.
- Motion mostly from the **wrist** (and forearm rotation), small and relaxed; avoid big finger motion.
- Pick angle: slight edge-picking (tilted) reduces scraping and helps speed.

## 2. Techniques
| Technique | Rule | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Alternate** | strict down-up regardless of strings | consistent timing, metal/rock runs, 16ths |
| **Economy** | alternate on one string; when crossing, continue in the same direction (down to the next higher string) | 3-notes-per-string scales, fusion |
| **Sweep** | one continuous motion across strings, one note each, fretting hand mutes each after it sounds | arpeggios |
| **Hybrid** | pick plus middle/ring fingers for higher strings | country (chicken picking), wide intervals, banjo rolls |

## 3. String crossing (the hard part)
- Notice whether the pick needs to escape **over** the next string. Inside picking (crossing between the two strings) is harder than outside picking.
- Pick slanting (Troy Grady): downward pick slant (pick escapes on upstrokes) suits lines that change string after an upstroke; choose fingerings that match your slant, or learn both.
- Practise 2-string patterns at slow tempo, looking at the escape motion.

## 4. Speed plan
1. Find the fastest **clean** tempo (no misses, even volume) for a pattern: e.g. 16ths at 90 bpm.
2. Practise 5 minutes at 80% of it, then bursts at +10% for 1-2 beats only ("burst picking").
3. Raise the clean tempo by 2-4 bpm when 5 clean reps in a row happen.
4. Record and listen; even dynamics matter more than speed.

## 5. Hybrid picking drills
- Pick on the 4th string, middle finger on the 2nd: alternating (bass-note + snap) at 8ths.
- Chicken picking: pluck a muted string with a finger "snap" against the frets; muted pick notes in between.

## Gotchas
- Tension in the shoulder/forearm limits speed and risks injury; stop and shake out.
- Practising fast and sloppy trains sloppiness; clean tempo only.

## Verify
- Metronome test: 4 bars of 16ths at target bpm, recorded; every note audible and even.
- Same line with alternate vs economy: pick the one that's cleaner at tempo.
