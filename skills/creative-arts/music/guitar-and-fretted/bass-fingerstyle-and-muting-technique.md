# Bass fingerstyle and muting technique

**Goal:** even, clean notes with no unwanted strings ringing.

## 1. Plucking
- Alternate index and middle finger; rest stroke (finger ends on the string below).
- **Raking:** use the same finger when moving to a lower string, for economy.
- Pluck over the pickup or between neck and bridge for different tones.

## 2. Right-hand muting
- **Floating thumb** (Gary Willis): the thumb rests on the strings below the one being
  played and moves with the hand, muting them.
- Alternatively, thumb on a pickup with the palm muting low strings.

## 3. Left-hand muting
- Fretting fingers lightly touch adjacent strings.
- Release pressure to stop a note at exactly the right time.
- Extra fingers lay across higher strings.

## 4. Practice
- Slow scales and patterns with a metronome, listening for sympathetic ringing.
- Record and check for noise between notes.

## Gotchas
- Open strings ringing when moving to higher strings.
- Uneven volume between fingers.
- Tension in the plucking hand.

## Verify
- [ ] No ringing strings on recordings.
- [ ] Even volume between fingers.
- [ ] Note lengths controlled by both hands.
