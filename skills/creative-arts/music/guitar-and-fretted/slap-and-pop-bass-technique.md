# Slap and pop bass technique

**Goal:** a clear, rhythmic slap technique with even volume.

## 1. Thumb slap
- Strike the string with the side of the thumb near the end of the fingerboard, then let
  the thumb bounce off (like hitting a drum).
- Motion comes from forearm rotation, not the thumb joint.
- Low E and A strings for root notes.

## 2. Pop
- Hook the index or middle finger under the string (D or G), pull it away and let it
  snap back onto the frets.
- Small motion; do not pull too far.

## 3. Left hand
- Hammer-ons and pull-offs add notes between slaps.
- **Ghost notes:** mute with the left hand and slap, giving a percussive "dead" note.
- Mute unused strings with the left hand.

## 4. Patterns
- Octave pattern: slap root on E string, pop octave on D string.
- Combine slap - ghost - pop - hammer patterns with a metronome on 16ths.

## 5. Tone
Boost lows and highs, cut mids slightly; light compression helps even out levels.

## Gotchas
- Pops much louder than slaps; balance by technique, then compression.
- Strings ringing from lack of muting.
- Too much slapping in a mix that needs a simple groove.

## Verify
- [ ] Even volume between slaps and pops.
- [ ] Clean muting.
- [ ] Patterns locked to a metronome at 16ths.
