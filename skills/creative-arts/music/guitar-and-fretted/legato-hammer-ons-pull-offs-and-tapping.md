# Legato: hammer-ons, pull-offs and tapping

**Goal:** fluid lines where only the first note (or few) is picked, at even volume and time.

## 1. Hammer-ons
- Fret the first note, pick, then bring the next finger down **firmly on the fingertip just behind the fret** - speed of impact, not pressure, makes the note.
- Keep fingers close to the strings (hovering 1 cm) so they can drop quickly.

## 2. Pull-offs
- Not a lift: **flick the string slightly downward/sideways** as you release, plucking the string with the fretting finger.
- The lower note must already be fretted before the pull-off.
- Pull-offs to open strings are easier but ring; mute after.

## 3. Even legato
- Volume drops on each legato note; pick lightly so the picked note isn't louder.
- Practise 3-notes-per-string scales picking only the first note per string; metronome 8ths -> triplets -> 16ths.
- Trills (rapid hammer/pull between two notes) build endurance; 30 s per finger pair.

## 4. Slides
- Slide into notes from 1-2 frets below for vocal effect; legato position shifts slide on one finger and keep pressure the whole way.

## 5. Two-hand tapping
- Tap with the picking-hand middle (or index) finger hard on the fret, then **pull off** (flick) to a fretted note, then hammer/pull with the fretting hand. Classic triplet: tap 12, pull to 5, hammer 8 (Van Halen "Eruption" pattern).
- Hold the pick tucked in the palm or between thumb and index while tapping with the middle finger.

## 6. Muting (the secret to clean legato)
- Fretting hand: the index finger lies lightly across higher strings; fingertip undersides touch adjacent strings.
- Picking hand: palm rests on lower strings; for high-gain tapping, use a fret wrap or hair tie on the nut side.

## Gotchas
- Pressing too hard tires the hand and pulls notes sharp.
- High gain hides unevenness while exposing unmuted noise; practise clean and with gain.

## Verify
- Record a legato run clean (no distortion) and check note volumes are even.
- Tapping triplets at a fixed tempo with no open-string noise when you stop.
