# Locking bass with the kick drum

**Goal:** bass and kick sound as one instrument.

## 1. Learn the kick pattern
- Listen to (or ask) the drummer's kick pattern for each section.
- Play notes on the kick hits, at least at first; add notes between later.

## 2. Note length
- Release notes deliberately (end on a specific beat or 16th), not whenever. Short notes
  for funk and dance, longer for ballads.
- Ending notes together with the drummer's hi-hat or snare tightens the groove.

## 3. Pocket placement
- On the beat, slightly ahead or slightly behind: match the drummer's feel.
- Hip-hop and neo-soul: often laid back; rock: on or slightly ahead.

## 4. Drills
- Play only roots on the kick for a whole song.
- Record bass and drums, listen for flams between kick and bass.
- Mute the drums on one side of the headphones to hear alignment.

## 5. Dynamics
Follow the drummer's dynamics; lift in choruses together.

## Gotchas
- Busy lines that ignore the kick.
- Notes ringing over rests.
- Rushing fills.

## Verify
- [ ] Bass notes coincide with kick hits on a recording.
- [ ] Note releases are deliberate.
- [ ] Feel matches the drummer.
