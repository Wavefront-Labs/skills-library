# String bending and vibrato

**Goal:** bends that hit pitch every time and vibrato with a deliberate speed and width.

## 1. Bending mechanics
- Use **2-3 fingers together** (bend with ring finger supported by middle and index behind it).
- Power comes from **rotating the wrist** (like turning a doorknob), not pushing with the finger. Thumb hooks over the neck as a pivot.
- Push strings 1-3 **up** toward the ceiling; on strings 4-6 pull **down** (toward the floor).
- Mute: the picking hand palm rests on lower strings; the bending finger's underside mutes the strings it pushes over.

## 2. Bend types and how to check them
| Bend | Target | How to check pitch |
|---|---|---|
| Half step (1/2) | +1 fret | play the target fret first, then bend to match |
| **Whole step (full)** | +2 frets | most common; play fret+2 first |
| 1.5 / 2 steps | +3 / +4 frets | lighter strings help |
| Pre-bend and release | bend silently, pick, release | check the start pitch before picking |
| **Unison bend** | bend lower string to match fretted higher string | the beating stops when in tune |
| Quarter-tone "curl" | slightly sharp b3 | blues expression, not a full semitone |

Practice: play target, then bend, alternating, with a tuner on screen.

## 3. Vibrato
- **Wrist vibrato** (rock/blues): small repeated bends from the wrist; pitch goes up from the note and back (not below).
- Classical/axial vibrato (along the string) for nylon-string and subtle jazz.
- Control two variables: **width** (quarter tone to whole tone) and **speed**; match to tempo (e.g. 8th-note or triplet pulses) for a stable sound.
- Vibrato on a bent note: hold the bend then oscillate between bent pitch and slightly less.

## 4. Practice routine (10 min)
- 2 min target-match bends on each of strings 1-3, frets 7-12.
- 3 min unison bends.
- 5 min vibrato with metronome at 70 bpm: 8ths, then triplets, then 16ths, width constant.

## Gotchas
- Under-bending (flat) is the most common fault, especially in later frets and on whole-step bends.
- Floating tremolo bridges drop the pitch of other strings when bending; unison and double-stop bends need extra push, or block the trem.
- Heavy strings (11s+) on a long-scale guitar make 1.5-step bends hard; adjust gauge.

## Verify
- Tuner check: bends land within +/-10 cents of the target.
- Record vibrato on a held note; it should be even in width and speed.
