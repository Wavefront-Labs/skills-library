# Open and alternate tunings

**Goal:** retune quickly and accurately, and know the shapes that make each tuning useful.

## 1. Common tunings (low to high)
| Tuning | Strings | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | E A D G B E | |
| **Drop D** | **D** A D G B E | heavy riffs, folk bass; one-finger power chords on 6-5-4 |
| Half-step down | Eb Ab Db Gb Bb Eb | Hendrix, SRV, Van Halen; easier bends, vocal range |
| **DADGAD** | D A D G A D | Celtic, Pierre Bensusan, Jimmy Page "Kashmir"; Dsus4 open drone |
| **Open G** | D G D G B D | Keith Richards (5-string), blues slide (Robert Johnson) |
| **Open D** | D A D F# A D | slide (Elmore James), folk |
| Open E | E B E G# B E | slide; higher tension (use with care) |
| Drop C / C standard | lower tunings for metal | heavier gauges needed |

## 2. Retuning reliably
- Use a clip-on or pedal tuner; tune **up** to pitch (loosening then tightening) so the string doesn't slip flat.
- Stretch strings after big retunes; re-check after a minute.
- Live: dedicate a guitar to each tuning rather than retuning between songs.

## 3. Shapes and approach
- Open tunings: a straight barre = major chord (open G: 5th fret = C, 7th = D). Minor and 7th chords need small shapes; learn the I-IV-V barres and a few extra shapes.
- DADGAD: let open strings ring as drones against fretted melody; "power chord + drone" shapes; Dsus4 home.
- Drop D: low D drone under chords in D; one-finger power chords on the bottom three strings.
- Write tab with the tuning in the header.

## 4. Strings and setup
- Lower tunings reduce tension: floppy feel, buzzing, intonation shifts. Go up a gauge (e.g. 10s to 11s or 12s for drop C) and check intonation and relief.
- Raising tension (open E, open A) stresses the neck and strings; open D/G are gentler alternatives a tone lower.

## Gotchas
- Tremolo-bridge guitars go out of balance when one string is retuned (drop D on a floating trem flattens the others); re-check all strings or block the trem.
- Capo + open tuning: fine, but transposes all drones.

## Verify
- Tuner check every string after retuning and again after playing for a minute.
- Play I-IV-V in the tuning and confirm intonation at the 12th fret.
