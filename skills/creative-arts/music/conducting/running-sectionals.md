# Running sectionals

**Goal:** sections fix their technical problems in small groups, so full rehearsals
are spent on ensemble.

## 1. Choose the material
- List passages where a section is the problem (notes, rhythm, ensemble within the
  section, intonation, bowings).
- Give each section a written list with bar numbers and the goal for each.

## 2. Who leads
- You, a section coach or the principal player. Brief leaders on tempo and
  interpretation so they do not teach a different version.

## 3. Run the sectional
1. Tune and warm up together (5 min).
2. Play the passage at a slow tempo, fix notes and rhythm.
3. Fix ensemble: match articulation, bowings or breaths.
4. Bring up to tempo with a metronome.
5. Record the end result for the section.

## 4. Feedback loop
- Leaders report what was done and what remains.
- In the next full rehearsal, play the passages first to show the improvement.

## Gotchas
- Sectionals without a list become play-throughs.
- Different tempi from the leader than the conductor.
- Too long: 45-60 minutes is usually enough.

## Verify
- [ ] Each section has a list of bars and goals.
- [ ] Leaders know the tempi.
- [ ] Passages are checked in the next full rehearsal.
