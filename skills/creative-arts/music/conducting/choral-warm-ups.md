# Choral warm-ups

**Goal:** in 10-15 minutes, focus the choir and prepare the voices for the repertoire
you will rehearse.

## 1. Sequence
1. **Body** (1-2 min): shoulder rolls, stretch, neutral posture.
2. **Breath** (2 min): silent inhale, hiss on "s" for 8 counts then longer.
3. **Onset and SOVT** (2 min): lip trills, straw phonation or humming on sirens; gentle
   range extension.
4. **Vowels and resonance** (2-3 min): a 5-note scale on unified vowels (oo-oh-ah-eh-ee).
5. **Tuning** (2-3 min): sustained unison then chords; build a major triad by section.
6. **Link** (2 min): an exercise taken from the first piece (a tricky interval,
   rhythm or vowel).

## 2. Range and tempo
Start in the middle range, move up and down by semitone. Do not push top notes early.

## 3. Leading
Model the sound; keep instructions short; keep the choir standing for energy.

## Gotchas
- Generic warm-ups unrelated to the repertoire waste time.
- Loud warm-ups tire voices.
- No rest for singers after a heavy warm-up.

## Verify
- [ ] Warm-up includes body, breath, SOVT, vowels and tuning.
- [ ] At least one exercise previews the first repertoire item.
- [ ] Warm-up time stays within the plan.
