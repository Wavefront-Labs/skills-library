# Preparing parts and bowings

**Goal:** players arrive at the first rehearsal with parts that match the score and
agreed bowings, so rehearsal time is spent on music.

## 1. Check parts against the score
- Same edition as the score; rehearsal letters/bar numbers match.
- Check cuts, repeats and dynamics; correct errata in pencil.
- Ask the librarian (MOLA guidance) for the parts several weeks ahead.

## 2. Bowings
- Get bowings from the **concertmaster** and principal strings, or mark them yourself
  and ask the concertmaster to check.
- Mark **down-bow** and **up-bow** at the start of phrases and anywhere the default
  ("as it comes") would put the bow in the wrong place.
- Copy the master bowings into all parts of a section (violin I part bowings into every
  first violin part).
- Mark in pencil only.

## 3. Other markings
- Cues for long rests in winds and brass.
- Breath marks and phrase shapes the conductor wants.
- Divisi instructions (by stand or desk).
- Tempo changes and the beat (in 2, in 4).

## 4. Distribution
Bowed parts go out early (with a deadline for comments) or are placed on stands before
the first rehearsal.

## Gotchas
- Bowings made at the score without a string player often fight the phrase.
- Unmatched editions waste rehearsal time; check bar numbers before anything else.
- Never use ink or highlighters on rental parts.

## Verify
- [ ] All parts match the score's bar numbers and letters.
- [ ] String sections have identical bowings.
- [ ] Rental parts are marked in pencil only.
