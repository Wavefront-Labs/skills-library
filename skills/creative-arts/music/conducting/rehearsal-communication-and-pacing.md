# Rehearsal communication and pacing

**Goal:** more playing, less talking, and every stop improves something.

## 1. Plan with times
- Write a minute-by-minute plan: warm-up, hardest piece while fresh, run-through at the
  end. Post it so players know.
- Give rest to brass/winds and to singers; alternate demanding pieces.

## 2. Stopping
- Stop only when you have one specific thing to fix.
- Instruction order: **where** (letter, bar), **who**, **what**, then restart: "Letter C,
  bar 5, violas, shorter on the quavers. From C."
- Restart from a nearby clear landmark, a few bars before the problem.

## 3. Feedback that works
- Describe the sound you want ("lighter, like a whisper") or demonstrate by singing.
- Positive and specific: say what improved.
- One change at a time; check it, then add the next.

## 4. Pacing
- Keep stops under about 30 seconds of talk.
- Alternate detailed work and longer runs.
- Finish with something that goes well.

## Gotchas
- Talking during noise: wait for silence.
- Long explanations of history during rehearsal.
- Fixing the same problem repeatedly without new information.

## Verify
- [ ] Rehearsal plan has times and is posted.
- [ ] Stops have where/who/what and restart quickly.
- [ ] Playing time exceeds talking time on a recording of the rehearsal.
