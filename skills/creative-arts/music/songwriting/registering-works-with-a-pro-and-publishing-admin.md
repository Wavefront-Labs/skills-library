# Registering works with a PRO and publishing admin

**Goal:** every royalty stream from your songs is collected. Practical overview, not legal or
financial advice; rules differ by country.

## 1. The royalty streams for a composition
| Stream | Paid when | Collected by |
|---|---|---|
| **Performance** | radio, TV, streaming, live venues, public places | PROs: ASCAP/BMI/SESAC (US), PRS (UK), SOCAN, APRA AMCOS, GEMA... |
| **Mechanical** | reproductions: streams, downloads, physical | The MLC (US streaming/downloads), MCPS (UK), local societies |
| Sync | use in film/TV/ads/games | negotiated by publisher/writer directly |
| Print | sheet music | publisher |

## 2. Steps
1. **Join a PRO** as a writer (one per territory of residence; you can't belong to two for the same rights in most cases). Optionally set up a publisher entity, or use an admin publisher.
2. **Register each work**: title, writers and their IPIs, **splits** (must match co-writers' registrations), publishers, alternative titles, ISRCs of recordings if available.
3. **Mechanicals**: register with The MLC (US) directly or via your publisher/admin; MCPS or equivalent elsewhere.
4. **Publishing administrator** (e.g. Songtrust, Sentric, CD Baby Pro, Kobalt-type admins): registers works worldwide with societies (via CWR files), collects publisher shares and international royalties for a commission (typically 10-20%).
5. Live performances: submit setlists to your PRO (e.g. PRS "Report Your Gigs", ASCAP OnStage, BMI Live).

## 3. Identifiers
- **ISWC** identifies the composition (assigned after PRO registration); **ISRC** identifies each recording. Linking them improves matching.

## 4. Check statements
- Review statements quarterly: missing works, wrong splits, unmatched international income; dispute via the PRO/admin.

## Gotchas
- Registering with different splits from your co-writers: payments held.
- Self-published writers without a publisher entity or admin often miss the publisher share and international mechanicals.

## Verify
- Every released song appears in your PRO catalogue and The MLC (or local equivalent) with the correct splits and ISRCs.
