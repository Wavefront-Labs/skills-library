# Jazz comping voicings: shell, rootless and quartal

**Goal:** smooth, idiomatic comping voicings that leave room for the bass and soloist.

## 1. Shell voicings (left hand, with a bass-less texture or for solo piano)
Root + 3rd or root + 7th. ii-V-I in C: Dm7 (D+C), G7 (G+B), Cmaj7 (C+B). Alternate
root-7 and root-3 for smooth movement.

## 2. Rootless voicings (with a bassist)
Bill Evans **type A** and **type B**, four notes from 3, 5, 7, 9 (13 on dominants).
- Type A: 3-5-7-9. Type B: 7-9-3-5.
- ii-V-I in C: Dm7 A (F A C E), G7 B (F A B E), Cmaj7 A (E G B D).
- Alternate A and B through ii-V-I for minimal motion.
- Keep voicings in the range roughly C3-C5 (middle of the keyboard).

## 3. Quartal voicings
Stacked 4ths (So What voicing: three 4ths and a 3rd, e.g. E A D G B). Good for modal
tunes.

## 4. Comping rhythms
- Short, syncopated chords, anticipating beats ("and of 2").
- Leave space; answer the soloist.
- Avoid playing on every beat; listen to the drummer.

## Gotchas
- Rootless voicings too low (below about C3) are muddy.
- Playing roots in the left hand while the bassist plays them.
- Same rhythm every bar.

## Verify
- [ ] ii-V-I in all 12 keys with A/B rootless voicings.
- [ ] Voicings stay within the middle register.
- [ ] Comping rhythms vary and leave space.
