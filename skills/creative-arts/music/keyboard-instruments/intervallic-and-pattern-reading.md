# Intervallic and pattern reading

**Goal:** read faster by seeing shapes and distances instead of naming every note.

## 1. Landmark notes
Learn instant landmarks: treble G, middle C, bass F, the top and bottom C on each
staff. Read others as intervals from these.

## 2. Intervals by shape
- Step: line to space next door.
- Skip (3rd): line to line, or space to space.
- 4th, 5th: count lines and spaces; 5th = line to line with one line between.
- Octave: line to space (or vice versa) 7 positions away.
Practise: flash cards of intervals, playing them at sight without naming notes.

## 3. Pattern recognition
- Scale fragments, arpeggios, broken chords, Alberti bass.
- Chord shapes: root position (line-line-line or space-space-space), inversions.
- Sequences: a pattern repeated higher or lower.

## 4. Practice
- Daily reading of easy material, focusing on patterns: say "C major arpeggio" rather
  than "C E G C".
- Read ahead: eyes a beat or bar ahead of the hands.
- Use graded books (Paul Harris Improve Your Sight-reading).

## Gotchas
- Naming every note slows reading.
- Looking at hands breaks the flow; feel keyboard topography.
- Ignoring key signatures when reading by intervals.

## Verify
- [ ] Interval cards can be played without naming notes.
- [ ] A passage is described in patterns before playing.
- [ ] Reading speed improves on graded material.
