# Accompanying singers and instrumentalists

**Goal:** the soloist feels supported: the pianist breathes, slows and balances with
them.

## 1. Know the soloist's part
Read the vocal or solo line (and the text and translation for singers) as well as your
own part.

## 2. Breathe together
- Mark the singer's breaths; breathe with them and leave time at breaths.
- Follow rubato by listening to the line; anticipate phrase ends.

## 3. Balance
- Lid half-stick or down for voice in small rooms; full for big halls only with
  care.
- Voice the piano to support the singer's range; lighter in the same register as the
  voice.
- Bass line provides support; keep the inner parts light.

## 4. Rehearsing
- Agree tempos, breaths, fermatas and cuts.
- Play the introduction at the soloist's tempo.
- Give pitch discreetly for a cappella entries.

## 5. In performance
Watch the soloist, react to changes, cover mistakes (jump to where they are).

## Gotchas
- Playing too loudly in the singer's register.
- Ignoring text: words shape the rubato.
- Not watching the soloist at entries.

## Verify
- [ ] Breaths and fermatas marked in your part.
- [ ] Balance checked from the hall.
- [ ] Rehearsal covers all tempo changes.
