# Hammond drawbars and Leslie

**Goal:** dial in and play idiomatic tonewheel organ sounds on a B3/C3 or clone (Nord, Hammond XK, Crumar, VB3).

## 1. Drawbars
Nine drawbars, left to right: **16' 5 1/3' 8' 4' 2 2/3' 2' 1 3/5' 1 1/3' 1'** (brown, brown, white, white, black, white, black, black, white). Each 0-8 (loudness). Settings are written as 9 digits.

| Sound | Registration | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Jimmy Smith jazz | 888000000 + percussion 3rd, fast decay | jazz comping and solos |
| Full gospel/rock | 888888888 | climaxes, rock pads |
| "Green Onions"/ballad | 888800000 or 838000000 | blues |
| Soft pad | 008000000 / 006000000 | quiet comping under vocals |
| Bright solo | 888000008 | rock lead lines |
| Bass manual / pedal | 16' + 8' (8 and 4) | left-hand bass |

## 2. Percussion and vibrato
- **Percussion** (upper manual only; works with drawbar presets B): 2nd or 3rd harmonic, soft/normal, fast/slow decay. Only sounds on detached notes (single-trigger), so phrasing affects it.
- Using percussion silences the 1' drawbar on a real B3.
- **Vibrato/chorus** V1-3 / C1-3: C3 is the classic gospel/jazz shimmer.

## 3. Leslie
- Two speeds: **chorale (slow)** and **tremolo (fast)**. The ramp between them is part of the sound: switch speed for climaxes and let it spin up.
- Typical: slow for ballads/comping, fast on build-ups and held chords at climaxes, then back to slow.
- Overdrive: drive the preamp/Leslie for rock growl (clone "drive" knob).

## 4. Playing technique
- **Swell (expression) pedal** constantly: organ dynamics come from the foot, not touch.
- Palm smears and glissandos, drawbar moves while holding a chord, and short percussive stabs (palm/fingers) for funk.
- Left hand bass on the lower manual with staccato "thumped" notes for jazz walking bass.

## Gotchas
- Clones: drawbars on screen may be in a different order or use a single set for both manuals; check the manual.
- Too much 16' muddies with a bass player; drop it when a bassist plays.

## Verify
- Compare against a reference recording at matched level: drawbar timbre, percussion click and Leslie speed match.
- Play the same chord with Leslie slow-to-fast-to-slow: the ramp should be smooth and audible.
