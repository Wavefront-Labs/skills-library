# Keyboard and jazz piano improvisation

**Goal:** improvised lines that outline the harmony, develop ideas and swing.

## 1. Guide tones
Practise 3rds and 7ths through changes; the line moves by step. Base your solo lines
around them.

## 2. Chord scales
- ii: Dorian; V: Mixolydian (with altered or diminished options); I: Ionian or Lydian.
- Target chord tones on strong beats; use passing notes between.

## 3. Motif development
Start with a small idea and vary it: rhythm, sequence, inversion. Leave space.

## 4. Left hand while soloing
Rootless voicings or shells comping in the left hand; keep it sparse and rhythmic.

## 5. Transcription
Transcribe solos (e.g. Wynton Kelly, Bill Evans) and learn phrases in all keys.

## 6. Practice
Play-alongs, ii-V-I exercises, blues and standard tunes.

## Gotchas
- Running scales without targeting chord tones.
- Left hand too busy.
- No rests in lines.

## Verify
- [ ] Guide-tone lines on a standard.
- [ ] Solo with clear motif development.
- [ ] Left hand comping steady.
