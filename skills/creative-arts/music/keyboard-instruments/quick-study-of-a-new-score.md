# Quick study of a new score

**Goal:** a reliable performance of a new piece in hours or a day, as accompanists and
session players often need.

## 1. Scan (10 minutes)
- Form: sections, repeats, codas.
- Key and time signature changes; tempo changes.
- Hardest passages; write a list.

## 2. Prioritise
- Rhythm and bass line first, then the melody, then inner voices.
- Simplify: in hard passages, drop doublings and inner notes; keep the bass and top.
- Mark fingerings only in hard places.

## 3. Practice the hard spots
Slowly, small sections, then link into the surrounding material.

## 4. Logistics
- Page turns: copy and tape pages, or use a tablet with a foot pedal.
- Mark cuts and cues from other players or singers.

## 5. Run-throughs
Run without stopping to check continuity; fix only the biggest issues after.

## Gotchas
- Spending all time on the first page.
- Not checking repeats and codas.
- Trying to play every note in hard passages.

## Verify
- [ ] Piece is played through without stopping.
- [ ] Hard passages have a simplified version if needed.
- [ ] Page turns are solved.
