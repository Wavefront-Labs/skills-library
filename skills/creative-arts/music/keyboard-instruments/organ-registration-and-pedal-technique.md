# Organ registration and pedal technique

**Goal:** pick stops that suit the music and the building, and play a confident pedal line.

## 1. Reading stop names
- **Pitch** (footage): 8' = written pitch; 4' = octave up; 2' = two octaves up; 16' = octave down (pedal foundation); mutations (2 2/3', 1 3/5') add individual harmonics.
- **Families**: Principals/Diapasons (core organ tone), Flutes (softer, rounder), Strings (thin, quiet; Gamba, Salicional, often with a Celeste), **Reeds** (Trumpet, Oboe, Clarinet; solo or chorus), **Mixtures** (several high ranks; brilliance).
- Divisions: Great (main chorus), Swell (enclosed in a **swell box**, expressive), Choir/Positive, Pedal.

## 2. Registration recipes
| Context | Starting registration |
|---|---|
| Quiet accompaniment | Swell 8' flute (+ string), Pedal soft 16' + Swell to Pedal |
| Hymn, moderate | Great 8' + 4' principals, Swell 8' 4' + coupled; Pedal 16' 8' + Great to Pedal |
| Full hymn verse | add Great 2' and Mixture, Swell reeds coupled; Pedal 16' reed if available |
| Baroque plenum (Bach preludes and fugues) | Principals 8' 4' 2' + Mixture on Great; Pedal 16' 8' 4' principals (+ reed) |
| Solo melody | Reed or Cornet on one manual, soft flutes on another, light pedal |
- Always build from 8' foundation; avoid 4' without 8' except for special effects.
- Balance pedal to manuals: the bass should support, not boom (the room changes this hugely).
- Use pistons/combination action: set registrations before the service and test them.

## 3. Pedal technique
- Sit centred on the bench at middle D of the pedalboard; knees and ankles relaxed, heels together.
- **Alternate toes** for most lines; **heel and toe** of the same foot for legato steps; mark pedalling in the score (inverted V = toe, U = heel; above notes = right foot, below = left).
- Look down only at the start; navigate by feeling the gaps between sharp notes.
- Practise pedal alone, then pedal + left hand, then all three parts.

## 4. Hymn playing
- Play one verse as introduction (or the last line), clear tempo; lift all voices together at the end of lines to give the congregation breath; vary registration per verse by text meaning.

## Gotchas
- Every organ is different: always test registrations in the room with someone listening from the nave.
- Couplers left on from the previous player (check before playing).
- Mixtures without enough foundation sound shrill.

## Verify
- Record from the back of the building and listen for balance and clarity.
- Pedal: play the line legato at tempo without looking.
