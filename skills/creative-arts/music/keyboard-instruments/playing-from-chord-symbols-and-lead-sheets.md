# Playing from chord symbols and lead sheets

**Goal:** turn a lead sheet (melody + chord symbols) into a full piano arrangement.

## 1. Chord symbol syntax
- C = C major; Cm/C- = minor; C7 = dominant 7th; Cmaj7/CΔ = major 7th; Cm7b5/Cø =
  half-diminished; C°7 = diminished 7th; Csus4; C6; C9, C13 (with 7th implied).
- Slash chords: C/E = C major with E in the bass.
- Alterations: C7b9, C7#11, C7alt.

## 2. Left-hand patterns
- Roots and 5ths, octaves.
- Shell voicings (root + 7th or 3rd).
- Broken chord patterns (ballads), stride (bass on 1 and 3, chord on 2 and 4).

## 3. Right hand
- Melody on top with chord tones below (two to three notes).
- Fill gaps in the melody with chord tones or short runs.

## 4. Arranging on the fly
- Intro: last four bars of the tune.
- Play the melody once, then improvise or vary.
- Ending: repeat last phrase, add a tag.

## 5. Practice
Fake-book tunes, one per day, in various styles.

## Gotchas
- Ignoring slash chord bass notes.
- Too thick left hand covering the melody.
- Misreading C7 as Cmaj7.

## Verify
- [ ] Chord symbols are read correctly at sight.
- [ ] Left-hand patterns suit the style.
- [ ] Melody stays on top and audible.
