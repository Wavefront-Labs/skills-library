# Octave technique and velocity practice

**Goal:** fast, loud octaves and quick passagework without tension or fatigue.

## 1. Octaves
- Hand shape: thumb and 5th (or 4th on black keys for legato octaves) firm, other
  fingers relaxed and curved.
- **Wrist octaves** for light, fast repeated octaves; **arm octaves** for loud,
  heavier passages. Most passages mix them.
- Keep the hand close to the keys; the motion is small.
- Stop and shake out when tension builds; never push through pain.

## 2. Endurance
Short bursts (two bars) with rests, then longer sections. Build gradually over weeks.

## 3. Velocity (speed) practice
- **Grouping/impulses:** play groups of notes fast with a stop on the first note of the
  next group (e.g. 4 notes + stop). Shift the stopping note each time.
- **Rhythms:** long-short and short-long patterns.
- **Metronome ladder:** small increases, back off when it fails.
- **Rotation:** forearm rotation for alternating patterns (Alberti bass, tremolos).

## 4. Practice plan
Daily 10-15 minutes of technical work: octaves, scales in different rhythms, and
passages from repertoire.

## Gotchas
- Locking the wrist for octaves causes tension and injury risk.
- Practising fast too early.
- Playing loud for too long; vary dynamics.

## Verify
- [ ] Octave passages are played at tempo without wrist pain.
- [ ] Fast passages are even on a recording.
- [ ] Practice is in short, rested blocks.
