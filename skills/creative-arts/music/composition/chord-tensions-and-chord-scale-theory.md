# Chord tensions and chord-scale theory

**Goal:** know which extensions sound good on which chord and which scale goes with it.

## 1. Available tensions by chord type
| Chord | Chord scale (default) | Available tensions | Avoid note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Imaj7 | Ionian | 9, 13 (#11 if Lydian) | 11 (clashes with 3rd) |
| IVmaj7 | Lydian | 9, #11, 13 | none |
| ii-7 | Dorian | 9, 11 | 13 (sometimes, as it implies a half-dim sound) |
| iii-7 | Phrygian | 11 | b9, b13 |
| vi-7 | Aeolian | 9, 11 | b13 |
| V7 (to major) | Mixolydian | 9, 13 | 11 (sus4 sound) |
| V7 (to minor) | Mixolydian b9 b13 / Phrygian dominant | b9, b13 | |
| V7alt | Altered (7th mode of melodic minor) | b9, #9, #11, b13 | |
| subV7, bVII7 | Lydian dominant | 9, #11, 13 | |
| -7b5 | Locrian #2 | 9, 11, b13 | |
| dim7 | Whole-half diminished | each note a whole step above a chord tone | |

Rule of thumb (Berklee): a tension is **available** if it is a whole step above a chord tone; a note a half step above a chord tone (b9 against the root on non-dominants, 11 against the major 3rd) is an **avoid note** on sustained chords.

## 2. Voicing tensions
- Drop the root (bass plays it) and often the 5th; keep 3rd and 7th (guide tones), add tensions on top.
- **Upper-structure triads** over the dominant's 3rd and 7th (G7: B and F in the left hand):
  - II major triad, A-C#-E = 9, #11, 13 (Lydian dominant sound).
  - bVI major triad, Eb-G-Bb = b13, root, #9 (altered).
  - bV major triad, Db-F-Ab = #11, 7, b9 (altered).
  - VI major triad, E-G#-B = 13, b9, 3 (13b9 sound).
- Keep the minor 9th interval out of voicings except b9 over the root on dominants.

## 3. Choosing scales for improvisation or lines
Find the chord's function in the key, then choose the scale that contains the key's notes plus the chord's chromatic alterations (chord-scale = key + chord).

## Gotchas
- Natural 11 over a major or dominant chord with a 3rd = clash; use #11 or sus4.
- Tensions in the melody override your voicing; never voice a natural 9 against a melody b9.
- Theory says "available" but register matters: tensions low in the voicing become mud (keep below ~E3 to root/5th/7th).

## Verify
- Check each voicing for semitone (minor 9th) clashes against chord tones.
- Play the chord scale over the chord: avoid notes should only be passing, never held.
