# Melodic dictation with movable-do solfege

**Goal:** transcribe a 4-8 bar melody accurately in 3-4 hearings.

## 1. Solfege setup
- **Movable do**: do = tonic of the current key (do re mi fa sol la ti). Chromatic: raised = di ri fi si li; lowered = ra me se le te.
- Minor: choose one system and stay with it.
  - **La-based minor**: tonic = la (shares syllables with relative major; good for modulation).
  - **Do-based minor**: tonic = do with me, le, te (keeps tonic function = do; Berklee and many US programs).
- Fixed do (do = C always) is common in Europe and conservatoires; it trains pitch naming, not function. Dictation by function is easier in movable do.

## 2. Dictation routine
1. Before playing: get the key, metre and starting note (given in exams). Sing do-mi-sol-do in the key.
2. **Hearing 1:** don't write. Conduct, memorise the shape, note where the cadence lands (do? sol?). Mark the metre and number of bars.
3. **Hearing 2:** write the **rhythm** first (see rhythmic-dictation skill), plus the first and last notes and cadence notes.
4. **Hearing 3:** fill pitches by **singing back in solfege** in your head, chunk by chunk (one bar or motif at a time). Steps first, then leaps (identify leaps as chord tones: do-mi-sol or sol-ti-re).
5. **Hearing 4:** check; fix accidentals (chromatic syllables) and the leading-note in minor.

## 3. Memory strategies
- Melodies repeat: identify sequences and repeated motifs, write them once and copy.
- Most tonal melodies outline the harmony: hearing an implied V chord at the half cadence gives you sol-ti-re.
- Sing the whole melody back from memory after hearing 1; if you can sing it, you can write it.

## Gotchas
- Writing pitches before rhythm usually wastes a hearing.
- Octave errors after big leaps: check range and contour against what you sang.
- In minor, raised 6/7 (fi, si in la-based; la, ti in do-based) are the most commonly missed accidentals.

## Verify
- Play back your transcription and compare with the original by ear.
- Track accuracy per note (%) over weeks; target 90% at 4 hearings.
