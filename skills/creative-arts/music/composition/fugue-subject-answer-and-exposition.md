# Fugue: subject, answer and exposition

**Goal:** a correct 3- or 4-voice fugal exposition and the plan for the rest of the fugue.

## 1. The subject
- 1-4 bars, clear tonic, memorable head (a leap or rhythmic profile) and a more running
  tail that leads into the countersubject.
- Starts on 1 or 5 of the key; ends on 1 or 3 (or modulates to V in modulating subjects).
- Test: can it overlap with itself a beat or bar later (stretto potential)? Can it be
  inverted? Write these tests before committing.

## 2. Real vs tonal answer
- The answer enters in the dominant.
- **Real answer:** exact transposition up a 5th (down a 4th).
- **Tonal answer:** needed when the subject **starts on 5** or prominently moves **1-5
  / 5-1** near its head: answer 5 with 1 and 1 with 5 (scale degree swap) so the tonic
  stays tonic, then continue as a real transposition.
  Example in C: subject G-C-B-C... gives tonal answer C-G-F#-G..., not D-G-F#-G.
- Modulating subject (ends in V): the answer comes back to I.

## 3. Countersubject (CS)
- Accompanies each later subject/answer entry; contrast rhythm (moves when subject holds).
- Must be in **invertible counterpoint at the octave** (works above or below): avoid
  5ths on strong beats, treat 4ths carefully.

## 4. Exposition layout (3 voices, e.g. alto-soprano-bass)
| Voice | Entry 1 | Entry 2 | Entry 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Voice 1 | S (I) | CS | free |
| Voice 2 | - | A (V) | CS |
| Voice 3 | - | (codetta) | S (I) |
- A short **codetta** may link the answer to the next subject entry to get back to I.
- Exposition ends once every voice has entered; a counter-exposition is optional.

## 5. After the exposition
- **Episodes:** sequential passages from subject/CS fragments, modulate to related keys
  (vi, IV, ii, iii).
- **Middle entries** in related keys, possibly inverted or augmented.
- **Stretto** near the end: entries overlapping; **dominant pedal** then final entry
  over or ending on a **tonic pedal**.

## Gotchas
- Forgetting the tonal adjustment makes the answer sound like it is in the supertonic.
- CS that only works above the subject fails when voices swap in the bass entry.
- Episodes with no sequence or fragment derivation sound like filler.

## Verify
- [ ] Answer type justified (scale degrees 1/5 at the head checked).
- [ ] CS tested both above and below the subject.
- [ ] Every voice enters in the exposition in alternating I/V.
- [ ] Plan includes at least one episode, one middle entry in a related key, final tonic.
