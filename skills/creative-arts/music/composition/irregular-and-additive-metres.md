# Irregular and additive metres

**Goal:** notate and perform bars whose beats are unequal (twos and threes mixed) so the
grouping is unmistakable.

## 1. Decide the grouping first
An irregular bar is a sum of 2s and 3s of the bottom unit. The **order** is a musical choice:
- 5/8 = 2+3 or 3+2
- 7/8 = 2+2+3 (Balkan "lesnoto" is usually 3+2+2), 2+3+2, 3+2+2
- 11/8 = e.g. 2+2+3+2+2 (Bulgarian kopanitsa)
- 5/4 at slow tempo is felt as 5 crotchets or 3+2 / 2+3 (Take Five = 3+2).

## 2. Show it on the page
1. **Beaming** carries the grouping: 7/8 as 2+2+3 is beamed two quavers, two quavers, three quavers.
2. For complex cases write the grouping in the signature: "2+2+3 over 8", or put the grouping in brackets above the first bar.
3. **Dotted bar lines** inside a long bar make subgroups visible (Gould).
4. Rests follow the groups: a silent 3-group in 7/8 is a dotted crotchet rest; a 2-group is a crotchet rest.
5. If the grouping changes bar to bar, change the beaming, and consider re-barring into a sequence of 2/8, 3/8 bars or using mixed metres (e.g. 3/4 then 6/8 alternating as in hemiola writing).

## 3. Mixed metres
- Changing signatures every bar (Stravinsky, prog rock): keep the bottom number constant where possible so the pulse unit stays the same for players.
- If the bottom number changes (4/4 to 7/8), mark the relationship: quaver = quaver.

## 4. Counting and conducting
- Count with syllables per group: "1-2, 1-2, 1-2-3" or "ta-ka ta-ka ta-ki-da".
- Conductors beat the groups, not the quavers: 7/8 (2+2+3) is beaten as a 3-pattern with the last beat longer. 5/8 is a 2-pattern with one long beat.
- Practise with a metronome on the quaver first, then on the group starts (uneven click via a DAW or a programmable metronome).

## Gotchas
- Writing 7/8 beamed 2+2+2+1 hides the long beat; players will rush.
- Do not notate an irregular feel as 4/4 with a tied quaver; the accents disappear.
- At very fast tempi a 2+2+3 bar is felt as three uneven beats (short-short-long). Mark tempo in those beats (e.g. "long beat = dotted crotchet") if useful.

## Verify
- Have someone sight-read a bar: they should place accents on group starts without explanation.
- Every bar sums correctly and the beaming matches the stated grouping on every bar.
