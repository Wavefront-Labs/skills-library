# Interval recognition by ear

**Goal:** name any interval within an octave, melodic or harmonic, in under 2 seconds, and
use that to transcribe and sight-sing.

## 1. Two methods - use both
1. **Reference songs** (fast to start): one song per ascending and descending interval.
   | Interval | Ascending | Descending |
   |---|---|---|
   | m2 | Jaws | Fur Elise (opening) |
   | M2 | Happy Birthday ("hap-py") | Mary Had a Little Lamb |
   | m3 | Greensleeves | Hey Jude ("hey jude") |
   | M3 | Oh When the Saints | Swing Low |
   | P4 | Here Comes the Bride / Amazing Grace | O Come All Ye Faithful (bar 2 down) |
   | Tritone | Maria (West Side Story) | Black Sabbath riff |
   | P5 | Twinkle Twinkle (2nd note) | Flintstones |
   | m6 | The Entertainer (E-C) | Love Story theme |
   | M6 | My Bonnie / NBC chime | Nobody Knows the Trouble |
   | m7 | Somewhere (There's a Place for Us) | Watermelon Man |
   | M7 | Take On Me (chorus leap) | I Love You (Cole Porter) |
   | P8 | Somewhere Over the Rainbow | Willow Weep for Me |
2. **Scale-degree (functional) hearing** (lasts longer): hear the bottom note as "do" and sing up the scale to the top note. Eventually you hear "that's do to la" directly.

## 2. Drill plan (10 minutes a day)
- Week 1: ascending only, P4, P5, P8, M3.
- Week 2: add m3, M2, M6; then descending.
- Week 3: all melodic; then **harmonic** intervals (both notes together): listen for consonance (P5, P8, 3rds, 6ths) vs dissonance (2nds, 7ths, tritone).
- Use musictheory.net or teoria.com, random root, track percentage; move on at 90%.

## 3. Targeted confusions
- **m3 vs M3**: sing the chord - minor (sad) vs major (bright).
- **P4 vs P5**: P4 sounds "unfinished", wants to go up (sol-do); P5 sounds open and stable.
- **Tritone**: tense, "wants to resolve" inwards or outwards.
- **m6 vs M6**: sing the 3rd it inverts to (m6 inverts to M3; M6 to m3).

## Gotchas
- Reference songs anchor to the song's key; still sing the interval yourself rather than recalling the recording.
- Practising only on piano timbre limits transfer; vary instruments.

## Verify
- 20-question random test with both directions and harmonic intervals: aim for 90%+ correct and under 2 s.
- Transcribe a simple melody's first 8 notes by intervals; check against the score.
