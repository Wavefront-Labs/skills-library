# Vocal cool-down and daily voice use

**Goal:** recover quickly after heavy singing and manage total voice use across a day
or a tour.

## 1. Cool-down (5 minutes after singing)
- Gentle **straw phonation** or lip trills on descending slides from the middle range.
- Light humming in the speaking range.
- Speak a few sentences at a relaxed, low-medium pitch to return to the speaking voice.

## 2. Vocal load
- Voice use = time spent singing plus speaking plus loud speaking. Loud talking at a
  gig or bar after a show is a major source of fatigue.
- Plan the day: rest periods between rehearsals, limit talking on show days, avoid
  shouting over noise.

## 3. Speaking voice
- Use a comfortable pitch, adequate breath, no vocal fry habit.
- Use amplification for teaching and long talks.

## 4. Rest
- After demanding shows, short voice rest periods (not total silence for days unless a
  doctor says so).
- Sleep matters most.

## Gotchas
- Talking after the show undoes the cool-down.
- Skipping the warm-up and cool-down on tour days when tired.
- Ignoring early signs of fatigue: loss of top notes, breathiness, effort.

## Verify
- [ ] Cool-down routine after every performance.
- [ ] Daily voice plan for tour or show days.
- [ ] Fatigue signs tracked in a log.
