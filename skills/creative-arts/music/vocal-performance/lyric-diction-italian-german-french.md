# Lyric diction: Italian, German, French

**Goal:** sing a foreign-language song with correct pronunciation and full understanding of
every word.

## 1. Preparation routine
1. **Word-for-word translation** under each word (IPA Source, Nico Castel libretti, or a dictionary), then a poetic translation. Know what every word means.
2. **IPA transcription** of the whole text, written into the score.
3. Speak the text in rhythm, then intone on one pitch, then sing.
4. Check with a coach or native speaker before performance.

## 2. Italian - key rules
- Pure vowels; **open vs closed e and o** ([e]/[ɛ], [o]/[ɔ]) depend on the word: look up each stressed one.
- **Double consonants** are longer (fatto vs fato): take time from the preceding vowel.
- Single r between vowels is flipped; doubled or initial r is rolled.
- **Elision/synalepha**: adjacent vowels across words share one note (e.g. "mi a-" sung together); the main vowel gets the length.
- c/g soft before e, i (ce = [tʃe]); sc before e, i = [ʃ]; gli = [ʎi]; gn = [ɲ].

## 3. German - key rules
- **Glottal onset** before words beginning with a vowel (don't link like Italian).
- ch: [ç] after front vowels (ich), [x] after back vowels (ach).
- Final b, d, g devoice to [p t k] (Tag = [ta:k]); -ig ending = [ɪç].
- Umlauts: ü = [y]/[ʏ], ö = [ø]/[œ]; long vs short vowel length matters (Staat vs Stadt).
- Final -er = [ɐ] (schwa-like), -en/-el schwa; don't sing American r.

## 4. French - key rules
- **Nasal vowels** ([ɑ̃] [ɔ̃] [ɛ̃] [œ̃]) - no n sound after them.
- **Liaison**: linking a normally silent final consonant to a following vowel; obligatory in some cases (les amis), forbidden in others (after "et"). Mark each in the score.
- Mute e (schwa) is sung in song when set to a note, even if silent in speech.
- Uvular or flipped r (flipped is standard in classical singing).

## Gotchas
- Using English diphthongs (day, go) on pure vowels in all three languages.
- Over-aspirating consonants (English p/t/k) in Italian and French.

## Verify
- Record yourself speaking the text and compare with a native-speaker recording (IPA Source provides recordings for many texts).
- Coach checklist: every word's meaning known, vowels pure, consonant length correct.
