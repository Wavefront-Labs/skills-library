# Managing performance anxiety

**Goal:** turn nerves into usable energy and perform close to your practice level.

## 1. Preparation is the base
- Over-prepare the music so it runs on automatic.
- Practise performing: run the set for friends, record, perform in low-stakes venues.
- Simulate stress: run the piece after jogging on the spot to raise your heart rate.

## 2. Pre-performance routine
- Same steps every time: warm-up, mark the opening phrases, a quiet period.
- Eat lightly, hydrate, avoid excessive caffeine.

## 3. Breathing
Slow exhale-focused breathing (e.g. inhale 4 counts, exhale 6-8 counts) for a few
minutes lowers heart rate. Use it backstage.

## 4. Mind
- Reframe arousal as excitement ("I'm ready") rather than fear.
- Focus on the music's meaning or the first phrase, not on the audience's judgement.
- Accept that small errors are normal; keep going.

## 5. Mid-performance
If you lose focus: breathe out, feel your feet, focus on the next phrase.

## 6. Help
Persistent severe anxiety: performing-arts medicine services (BAPAM in the UK) and
psychologists. Medication such as beta-blockers is a medical decision for a doctor.

## Gotchas
- Avoiding performing makes anxiety worse.
- Alcohol before performing impairs control.
- Changing the routine on show day.

## Verify
- [ ] Pre-performance routine written and practised.
- [ ] At least three practice performances before the event.
- [ ] Breathing technique rehearsed.
