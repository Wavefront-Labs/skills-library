# Audition preparation and audition book

**Goal:** a book of ready songs and a clean audition where the pianist and panel can
focus on you.

## 1. Audition book
- 6 to 12 songs covering contrasting styles: ballad and up-tempo, legit (classic MT),
  contemporary MT, pop/rock, and anything specific to your casting.
- Each song fully prepared, plus a **16-bar** and **32-bar cut**.
- Sheet music in the right key, in a binder with plastic-free (non-reflective) pages or
  double-sided copies taped together for page turns.

## 2. Marking cuts
- Start and end bars clearly marked with a bracket and "START"/"END".
- Cuts end on a musically complete phrase, often the climax of the song.
- Mark tempo, key changes, repeats and fermatas.

## 3. Choosing material
- Show the vocal range and acting skills the role needs.
- Avoid songs the show itself uses unless asked.
- Choose songs you can sing even when nervous or ill.

## 4. In the room
- Greet the pianist; give the tempo by singing a bar quietly; point out the cut and
  tricky spots.
- Slate (name, song) if asked, sing, thank the panel.
- Bring water, a CV and a headshot.

## Gotchas
- Unmarked, loose photocopies cause pianist errors.
- Songs too high for your nervous voice.
- Cuts that start without an introduction need a bell tone (first note) from the pianist.

## Verify
- [ ] Book has contrasting songs with marked 16- and 32-bar cuts.
- [ ] Each cut is in the correct key with clear markings.
- [ ] You can sing each cut with a cold start.
