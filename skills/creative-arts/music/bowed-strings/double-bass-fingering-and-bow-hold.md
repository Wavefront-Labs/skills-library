# Double bass fingering and bow hold

**Goal:** accurate intonation and good tone on the double bass.

## 1. Simandl fingering
- In lower positions, the hand covers a whole step: fingers **1, 2, 4** (ring and little
  together as "4"). 3 is used only in thumb position.
- Positions named by Simandl (half, 1st, 2nd, 3rd...). Many shifts are needed in scales.

## 2. Rabbath and pivot
François Rabbath's system uses pivoting (rocking the hand around the thumb) to cover more
notes without full shifts. Good for fluid playing; requires solid geography.

## 3. Bow holds
- **German bow:** held like a saw with the frog in the palm, thumb on top. Powerful
  sound; common in many orchestras and in Central Europe and the US.
- **French bow:** like a cello bow (overhand). More agility, a lighter touch.
Choose with your teacher; both are used professionally.

## 4. Tone
- Weight from the arm, contact point closer to the bridge than on cello.
- Slow bow speed on the low strings.

## Gotchas
- Using 3 instead of 4 in lower positions (Simandl).
- Thumb tension.
- Too fast bow speed on low strings.

## Verify
- [ ] Simandl 1-2-4 positions in tune across scales.
- [ ] Even tone with chosen bow.
- [ ] Clean shifts on scales.
