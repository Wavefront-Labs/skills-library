# Double stops and chords

**Goal:** intervals that ring in tune and chords that sound full without crushing.

## 1. Intonation
- Tune the lower note first, then the upper. Then adjust both.
- Thirds and sixths: just intonation (major 3rds narrower) for ringing; listen for
  beats.
- Octaves and fifths: perfect fifths are a single finger across two strings - roll the
  finger to adjust.

## 2. Bow
- Bow level exactly between the two strings; equal weight on both.
- Slightly more weight and slower speed than single notes.

## 3. Chords (three and four notes)
- Break: two lower notes first, then two upper (2+2), or one + three; the lower notes
  are on the beat or before, per style.
- Bow starts near the frog with weight, pivots across the strings.
- Baroque style: arpeggiated rolls; Romantic: crunchier, more simultaneous.

## 4. Exercises
- Scales in thirds, sixths, octaves (Sevcik, Flesch).
- Simon Fischer's Double Stops.

## Gotchas
- Squeezing the left hand: tension makes intonation worse.
- Crushing chords by pressing too hard.
- One note louder than the other.

## Verify
- [ ] Double stops ring (no beats) on sustained notes.
- [ ] Chords sound full without scratch.
- [ ] Scales in thirds in tune at slow tempo.
