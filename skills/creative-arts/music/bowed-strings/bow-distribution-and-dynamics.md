# Bow distribution and dynamics

**Goal:** have enough bow for each note and the right place in the bow for each dynamic.

## 1. Divide the bow
- Map bow length to note value: a half note gets twice the bow of a quarter note at the
  same dynamic and speed.
- In uneven rhythms (dotted quarter + eighth, same bow direction alternating), save bow on
  the long note and spend it on the short one, or the bow drifts toward one end.

## 2. Dynamics
- **Crescendo** on a down-bow: start slow and near the fingerboard, add speed and weight
  and move closer to the bridge. Up-bows naturally crescendo (weight increases toward
  the frog).
- **Diminuendo:** reduce speed and weight, move away from the bridge.
- Plan where in the bow the phrase starts: soft starts near the tip, loud starts near the
  frog.

## 3. Marking
Mark bow plans in the part: "WB" (whole bow), "UH" (upper half), "LH" (lower half),
"save", "fr" (frog).

## 4. Exercises
- Whole bows on open strings for 4, 8, 16 beats at constant tone.
- Crescendo/decrescendo bows.
- Rhythm patterns with uneven bow use.

## Gotchas
- Running out of bow before the phrase ends.
- Unplanned accents from bow speed changes.
- Starting soft passages at the frog.

## Verify
- [ ] Bow plan marked in difficult passages.
- [ ] Phrase dynamics match the score on recordings.
- [ ] Bow reaches the planned position at phrase ends.
