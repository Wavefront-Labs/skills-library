# Cello thumb position and extensions

**Goal:** comfortable, in-tune playing in the high register and in extended neck
positions.

## 1. Extensions (neck positions)
- **Forward extension:** first finger moves forward (toward the bridge) a half step, the
  rest of the hand stays, giving a whole step between 1 and 2.
- **Backward extension:** first finger reaches back a half step.
- Extensions avoid shifts in scales; keep the thumb moving with the hand.

## 2. Thumb position
- Thumb lies across two adjacent strings (often D and A), forming a perfect 5th "bar".
- Fingers 1-2-3 above it; 4 is rarely used in thumb position.
- Finger spacing shrinks as you go higher; pitches are close together.
- Start on harmonics (halfway point of the string) where the thumb can find the octave.

## 3. Exercises
- Scales in thumb position on A and D strings.
- Popper's High School of Cello Playing (Op. 73), Cossmann exercises.

## Gotchas
- Thumb pressing too hard - painful and tense.
- Not adjusting finger spacing higher up.
- Collapsing the hand shape.

## Verify
- [ ] Octave between thumb and 3rd finger is in tune.
- [ ] Scales in thumb position in tune.
- [ ] Extensions without moving the thumb incorrectly.
