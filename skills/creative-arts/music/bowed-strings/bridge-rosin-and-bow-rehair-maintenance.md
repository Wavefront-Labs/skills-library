# Bridge, rosin and bow rehair maintenance

**Goal:** an instrument that stays playable and safe.

## 1. Bridge alignment
- The bridge leans toward the fingerboard as you tune (strings drag it).
- Check: the back of the bridge (tailpiece side) should be perpendicular to the top.
- Gently pull the top of the bridge back with thumbs and fingers on both sides, a small
  amount at a time, while the strings are at pitch.
- A warped bridge needs a luthier.

## 2. Rosin
- Apply a few strokes per day of playing; too much creates dust and a gritty sound.
- New hair needs more rosin initially.
- Wipe rosin dust off the strings and top after playing with a soft cloth.

## 3. Bow rehair
- Rehair every 6-12 months for regular players, or when hair is thin or does not grip.
- Loosen the bow after playing.

## 4. Humidity
Keep the instrument in its case with humidity control (40-60% RH); cracks and seams open
in dry conditions.

## 5. Luthier
Open seams, cracks, buzzes, soundpost adjustments.

## Gotchas
- Over-tightening the bow.
- Tuning with pegs abruptly - can snap strings or move the bridge.
- Cleaning with alcohol damages varnish.

## Verify
- [ ] Bridge perpendicular after tuning.
- [ ] Rehair schedule kept.
- [ ] Humidity controlled.
