# Orchestral sight-reading and following the conductor

**Goal:** play correctly and together at a first rehearsal.

## 1. Scan the part
Key signatures, time changes, tempo changes, repeats, cuts, hard passages.

## 2. Counting rests
Count bars in groups; follow cues printed in your part; listen for landmarks.

## 3. Watching the conductor
- Peripheral vision: eyes on the music, with the conductor's beat in the edge of your
  view. Look up at key moments (entries, tempo changes).
- Play with the ictus and the section leader.

## 4. Following the leader
In a section, follow the principal's bow and the concertmaster's lead.

## 5. Keep going
Do not stop on errors; drop notes, keep the rhythm.

## Gotchas
- Head buried in the part.
- Losing count in long rests.
- Following another section's tempo rather than the conductor.

## Verify
- [ ] Parts scanned before rehearsal.
- [ ] Entries correct at first read.
- [ ] Conductor watched at tempo changes.
