# Preparing orchestral excerpts for audition

**Goal:** excerpts played at the right tempo and style, with total security.

## 1. Get the list and parts
- The orchestra's list with specific bars. Use the orchestra's parts or a known edition.
- Standard violin excerpts: Mozart Symphony 39 (finale), Strauss Don Juan, Brahms 4,
  Mendelssohn Midsummer Night's Dream Scherzo.

## 2. Research
- Listen to several recordings (and especially the auditioning orchestra's).
- Know what happens around the excerpt: other parts, harmony, tempo.

## 3. Practice
- Slow practice with metronome, intonation with drones.
- Record and listen.
- Practise cold starts.

## 4. Mock auditions
- Play for colleagues and teachers in audition conditions.
- Screened rounds: no talking; the proctor gives instructions.

## 5. On the day
Warm-up, tune, play in order; take a moment between excerpts to set the tempo in your
head.

## Gotchas
- Excerpts played too fast.
- Rhythm unclear in fast passages.
- Not knowing the context.

## Verify
- [ ] Every excerpt played at the target tempo with metronome.
- [ ] At least three mock auditions.
- [ ] Recordings show clean rhythm and intonation.
