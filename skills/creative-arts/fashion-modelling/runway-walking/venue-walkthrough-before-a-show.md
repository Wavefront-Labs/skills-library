# Venue walkthrough before a show

**Goal:** no surprises on the route. Every new venue has a trap.

## What to check (walk it, in show shoes if possible)
- **Entrance**: where you wait, the reveal point, any step up onto the runway, the backdrop gap.
- **Route**: straight, L-shaped, U-shaped, loops, pillars, seats close to the edge.
- **Length**: count steps to the end mark.
- **Surface**: gloss, carpet, sand, grating, joins between platforms, slope (raked stages).
- **Edges**: height of the runway above the floor, whether edges are marked or lit.
- **Lighting**: where the front light blinds you, dark sections, strobes.
- **End mark and pit**: exact mark (tape), where the lenses are.
- **Exit**: same as the entrance or separate; where to go once off.

## Do it twice
Once at walking pace with eyes on everything; once at show pace with show focus, eyes up.

## Report hazards
Gaps between platforms, loose carpet, unmarked edges, wet floors: tell the producer.

## Verify
- [ ] Walked the full route at show pace.
- [ ] Hazards noted and reported.
