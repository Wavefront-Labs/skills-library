# Bags and accessories on the runway

**Goal:** accessories are often the product that sells; make them visible and keep them still.

## Bags
| Bag | Carry |
|---|---|
| Top-handle | In the hand, arm long by the side, or in the crook of the elbow with the forearm horizontal, wrist soft |
| Clutch | Held at the side or in front of the hip, fingers along the bottom, logo outward |
| Shoulder | Hand resting lightly on the strap or arm long; stop it swinging into the body |
| Cross-body | Bag at the hip on the side the stylist sets; arms swing freely |

- The **logo or front faces the pit**. Check as you set the pause.
- The bag arm swings little or not at all; the other arm matches the cast.
- Swap hands only if briefed.

## Sunglasses, hats, jewellery
- Sunglasses: worn as styled; dark lenses hide the eyes, so keep the chin line and head
  strong. Do not push them up unless briefed.
- Earrings and necklaces: keep the head still so they do not swing wildly; know where they
  hook to avoid snagging hair.
- Gloves: smooth the fingers before going out.

## Verify
- [ ] Each look's accessory carry confirmed with the stylist.
- [ ] Logo faces the pit in rehearsal photos.
