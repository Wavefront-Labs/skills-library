# Commercial runway walk

**Goal:** make the customer want the clothes and feel they could wear them.

## Where it's used
High-street brand shows, shopping-centre and consumer fashion events, bridal fairs,
trade presentations, charity shows, swimwear and resort shows.

## Technique
- Pace a little slower and softer than editorial; natural arm swing.
- Single-line feet, little or no crossover.
- **Expression**: relaxed, warm; a soft smile or full smile as briefed, especially at the
  pause. Eyes engage the audience and the pit, not a fixed point beyond.
- End poses more open: hand on hip, slight shoulder turn, half turn to show the back.
- Show features: open a jacket to show the lining, turn to show a back detail, lift a bag.

## Audience
Consumer audiences are close and at eye level. Real eye contact with the front row is
often welcome; ask.

## Faults
- A frozen "smile on, smile off". Think of something genuinely pleasant (see
  `genuine-commercial-smile`).
- Editorial stomp in a bridal show.

## Verify
- [ ] Expression and end pose confirmed with the producer.
- [ ] Rehearsal video reads relaxed and approachable.
