# Spacing marks and go cues

**Goal:** an evenly spaced show, so every look gets its own photographs.

## How shows space models
- A backstage **dispatcher** (producer or assistant) at the entrance releases each model
  with a tap or "go". Or the go is a visual trigger: when the model ahead reaches a
  taped mark (often the halfway point or the end), you go.
- Learn in rehearsal: your release point, your entrance mark (often a taped X behind the
  backdrop), the end mark, and the **passing side** (which side of the runway you walk
  back on; typically you keep to one side on the return so the next model has the centre).

## Keep the gap
- Walk the briefed speed. If the model ahead is slower, do not catch up and crowd their
  pit shot; ease off a fraction so the gap holds.
- If you are released late, do not run; walk at pace and the producer adjusts the next go.
- Never overtake.

## Exits
Return past the entrance line fully before slowing down; stopping in the doorway blocks
the next model's re-entry or blocks dressers.

## Verify
- [ ] Know release cue, entrance, end and passing side for this show.
- [ ] Gap to the model ahead stays constant in rehearsal.
- [ ] Clear the exit fully before stopping.
