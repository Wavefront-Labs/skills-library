# Editorial power walk

**Goal:** a strong, fast, confident walk that reads as fashion week, while still controlled.

## Technique
- Pace brisk and steady, often at a step per beat on a driving track.
- Purposeful stride from the hip, knees soft; womenswear single line or light crossover.
- Arms long, little swing, or a firm natural swing; shoulders down.
- **Face**: neutral and intense; mouth closed, jaw relaxed, eyes focused through the
  lenses. Intention comes from the eyes, not a frown.
- Pause short or none; quick pivot.

## Control
Strong is not stomping. Keep the foot strike quiet (see `heel-to-toe-roll-and-knee-use`)
and the head still. Aggressive walks bounce, which blurs the pictures.

## Adapting
The power walk is a base: designers dial it up or down ("more attitude", "softer",
"faster"). Being able to change on a single direction is what gets you rebooked.

## Verify
- [ ] Can do the walk at three different speeds while keeping head still.
- [ ] Face reads intense, not angry, in pit photos.
