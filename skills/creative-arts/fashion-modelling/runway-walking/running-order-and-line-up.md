# Running order and line-up

**Goal:** never be the gap in the show.

## The look board
Backstage has a **look board** or running order: every look in exit order, usually with a
Polaroid or photo of each model in each look, the look number and the model name. Each
model has a rail with their looks in order, labelled with name and look numbers.

## Learn your show
- Your look numbers (e.g. 4, 27, 51) and what is between them: the gap tells you how long
  your change is.
- Who walks before you (follow them in the line) and after you.
- Your rail location and dresser.

## Line-up
Before the show the producer calls a line-up in exit order, dressed in first looks. Stand
in order, stay still and quiet, phone away. A final check of shoes and accessories happens here.

## During the show
After each exit go straight to your dresser for the change, then join the line behind
the model with the previous look number. Ask the line producer if unsure; never guess.

## Verify
- [ ] Know look numbers, who is ahead of you for each, and your rail.
- [ ] In line on time for each exit in rehearsal.
