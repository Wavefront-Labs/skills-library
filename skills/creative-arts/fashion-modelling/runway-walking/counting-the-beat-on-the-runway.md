# Counting the beat on the runway

**Goal:** a walk that sits on the music when the show wants it to, and holds its own
pace when it does not.

## Find the beat
- Show tracks are often 110-130 BPM. In rehearsal, tap the beat on your thigh for 8 counts
  before you walk. Listen for the kick drum, not the melody.
- Typical runway pace is one step per beat at 110-125 BPM. At faster tempos (130+) walk
  **half time** (one step every two beats) or the walk becomes a march.
- Slow ambient or classical tracks have no clear beat: walk the briefed pace and ignore
  the music.

## Stay on it
- Take the first step on a downbeat as you leave the entrance mark.
- The pause and turn are counted too: e.g. arrive, hold 2 counts, turn on 3-4, walk on 1.
- If you drift off the beat, correct over several steps, never with a skip.

## When not to
Many designers want the cast walking at a fixed speed regardless of music, so that
spacing stays even. Ask the choreographer or producer: "on the music or own pace?"

## Verify
- [ ] Tapped the BPM before rehearsal and know step-per-beat or half-time.
- [ ] Filmed rehearsal: feet land on the beat for the whole length.
- [ ] Know whether the show wants music timing at all.
