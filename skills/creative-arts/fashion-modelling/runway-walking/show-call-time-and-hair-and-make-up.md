# Show call time and hair and make-up

**Goal:** be ready for the team the moment they need you.

## Call time
Your agency sends a call time, often 2-4 hours before the show starts. Arrive 10-15
minutes before it at the right entrance (backstage or model entrance, not front of house).
Late arrival can mean losing the show.

## Arrive ready
- **Clean face** (moisturised, no make-up) unless told otherwise.
- **Clean, dry, product-free hair**, brushed out. No ties that leave kinks.
- Nails clean and unpainted or neutral unless told.
- Shaved or not as requested by the agency; no fresh fake tan.
- Wear easy-to-remove clothes and neutral, seamless underwear (nude, thong or seamless
  briefs and strapless or no bra as briefed).

## Sign in and queue
1. Sign in with the producer or model coordinator; get your look numbers.
2. Go to hair and make-up in the order called. Hair and make-up often work at the same time.
3. Stay where they can find you; tell the coordinator if you must leave.
4. Eat and drink before the clothes go on; use a straw after make-up.

## Fashion week shuttling
When you have back-to-back shows, the agency negotiates a release time. Keep hair and
make-up teams informed; some looks are removed and redone for the next show. Leave with
your phone charged and your next address.

## Verify
- [ ] Call time, address and entrance confirmed the night before.
- [ ] Arrived with clean face and hair, appropriate underwear.
