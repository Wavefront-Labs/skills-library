# Runway walk drills

**Goal:** 20 minutes that improve the walk every session.

## The routine
| Drill | Reps | What it fixes |
|---|---|---|
| Wall posture: heels, seat, shoulders, head on wall, 60 s, then step away | 2 | posture |
| Line walk on tape, flats then heels | 5 lengths | foot placement |
| Book on head | 5 lengths | head bob |
| Tempo walk: metronome at 110, 120, 128 BPM | 2 lengths each | pace control |
| Pause-and-turn repeats at the end mark | 10 | turns |
| Blind count: count steps to a mark, then walk it with eyes on a point above it | 5 | landing on the mark |
| Style switch: commercial, editorial, slow | 1 each | range |

## Progressions
- Swap flats for 8 cm then 10 cm heels.
- Add a bag, a long skirt or a coat.
- Use a slippery floor once stable.

## Verify
- [ ] Routine done 4 times a week for 4 weeks.
- [ ] Faults from video review reduce week on week.
