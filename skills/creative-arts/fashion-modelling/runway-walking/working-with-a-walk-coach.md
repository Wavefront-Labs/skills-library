# Working with a walk coach

**Goal:** improve fast from expert feedback without paying for a fake shortcut.

## Sources of coaching
- **Your agency**: development agents and bookers usually coach new faces on the walk
  before fashion week, at no charge. Ask for it.
- **Show movement directors**: coach the cast in rehearsal for that show.
- **Independent coaches**: legitimate ones exist; check who they have trained and that the
  agency recommends them.

## Red flags
- An "agency" that will represent you only if you pay for its course or photos. In the UK,
  model agencies generally cannot charge an upfront fee for finding work, and courses sold
  as a condition of representation are a classic scam pattern.
- Guarantees of shows or contracts after the course.

## A good session
1. Coach films you first.
2. One or two priorities, not ten.
3. Drills for each (see `runway-walk-drills`).
4. Filmed again at the end to compare.

## Taking feedback
Write the notes down straight after. Practise the specific fix; re-film within a week.

## Verify
- [ ] Coach or course vetted with your agency.
- [ ] Before and after footage from each session.
