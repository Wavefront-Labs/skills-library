# Handling tempo changes in a show

**Goal:** stay smooth when the music or the briefed speed changes while you are on the runway.

## Where changes happen
- **Section changes**: shows often change track between groups (day to evening, a capsule).
  Your section may have a different tempo or mood. Ask which section you are in.
- **Track transitions**: a crossfade can leave a few bars with no beat. Keep the pace you had.
- **Finale**: usually a different, often slower or more emotional track, walked in a line
  at close spacing.

## Adjusting
1. Hear the change, do not react to it on the next step.
2. Spread the adjustment over 3-4 steps.
3. If the change lands at the pause, take the new tempo after the turn.

## Brief changes from the producer
Live instructions such as "faster" can be signalled from the side of the runway or by
the dispatcher before you go. Apply them on the next pass or from your next step, smoothly.

## Verify
- [ ] Know which section and track you are in.
- [ ] Rehearsed through at least one track change without a visible hitch.
