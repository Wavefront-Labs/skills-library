# Video self-review of the walk

**Goal:** see your walk as the pit sees it, and fix what you find.

## Filming
- Phone on a tripod at hip height at the end of a 10 m+ space (the pit view), landscape.
- Second angle: from the side, at 90 degrees to the walk line, mid-route.
- Use slow motion (120 or 240 fps) for foot placement and turns.
- Film in show shoes and a fitted outfit so the body line is visible.

## Checklist (watch once per item)
1. Head: does a point on the forehead stay level?
2. Shoulders: level and still?
3. Arms: swing straight, opposite to legs, soft hands?
4. Feet: on the line, consistent stride, no stutter step?
5. Knees: soft, never locked or deeply bent?
6. Eyes: up and focused the whole way?
7. Pause: settled, weight on the back leg?
8. Turn: compact, head last?
9. Exit: posture held until out of frame?

## Progress
Keep one dated clip a week. Compare week 1 and week 6 side by side.

## Verify
- [ ] Two angles filmed in show shoes.
- [ ] Checklist completed with one fault picked to fix this week.
