# Wardrobe malfunction on the runway

**Goal:** protect your dignity and the look, calmly.

## On the runway
- **Slipping strap**: a smooth hand to put it back is fine; do it as part of the walk.
- **Zip opening or seam splitting**: hold it closed with a hand placed naturally (on the
  hip, at the side), finish the pass.
- **Exposure**: you are entitled to cover yourself. Adjust or hold the garment and keep
  walking; leave the runway early if needed. Your dignity outranks the shot.
- **Garment dropping or a hem coming down**: gather it in one hand and continue.

## Afterwards
Tell the dresser and stylist immediately so the next exit is fixed (or the look pulled).

## Prevention
- Double-sided fashion tape on necklines and gapes, silicone or nipple covers under sheer
  or loose garments, seamless underwear. Ask the stylist before the show what they provide.
- Raise concerns about sheer or revealing looks at the fitting, not backstage.
  Consent for nudity and sheerness should be agreed in advance (see `nudity-consent`).

## Verify
- [ ] Taping and covers checked before each exit.
- [ ] Any revealing looks agreed at the fitting.
