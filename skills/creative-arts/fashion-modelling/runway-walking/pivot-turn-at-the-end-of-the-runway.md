# Pivot turn at the end of the runway

**Goal:** a small, quick, clean 180 degree turn that keeps the face on camera as long as possible.

## The turn
1. Arrive on the end mark. The last step places one foot slightly in front of the other
   (e.g. right foot forward).
2. Weight on the balls of both feet, heels just off the floor (in heels: weight forward
   over the ball).
3. Pivot 180 degrees on the balls towards the **back foot's side** (right foot forward -> turn left).
   The feet swap: the front foot becomes the back foot.
4. **Head last**: hold the face and eyes on the pit while the body starts to turn; the head
   whips round only at the end, like a dancer spotting.
5. Step off with the foot that is now in front, back into the walk.

## Direction
Turn towards the side you will walk back on, so you do not cross the next model's path.
Confirm in rehearsal: some shows want every model to turn the same way.

## In heels
- Narrow stilettos can twist; keep the heels up off the floor through the pivot.
- On slippery floors reduce the pivot and use a two-step turn instead (see
  `two-step-and-hip-turns`).
- Platforms: pivot is harder; take it slowly.

## Faults
- Big swinging turn: keep it compact, the feet barely move.
- Losing balance: weight too far back; look at a point at eye level as you finish.

## Verify
- [ ] 10 turns in show shoes without a wobble.
- [ ] Face is still on the pit at the start of each turn (video from the pit position).
- [ ] Turn direction matches the passing side.
