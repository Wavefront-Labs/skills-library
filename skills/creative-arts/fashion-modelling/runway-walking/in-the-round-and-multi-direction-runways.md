# In-the-round and multi-direction runways

**Goal:** stay oriented and photogenic when the runway does not go straight to one pit.

## Layouts
- **Loop / serpentine** through a room with seats on both sides of every section.
- **Square or round** central runway with audience all round.
- **Multiple pits** or photographers along the route.

## Technique
- Learn the route with its **corner marks**. Corners: turn the body smoothly through the
  corner in 2-3 steps, head and eyes leading slightly into the new direction.
- Focus points change per section: pick one for each straight.
- Where photographers are at several points, the producer may set mini-pauses; know them.
- No single "end" pause; often continuous walking.

## Spacing
Models can be on the route at the same time going different directions. Learn who passes
where and on which side.

## Verify
- [ ] Route drawn with corners and any pause points.
- [ ] Corner turns smooth on rehearsal video.
