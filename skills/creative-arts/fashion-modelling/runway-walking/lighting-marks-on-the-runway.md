# Lighting marks on the runway

**Goal:** stand in the light the lighting designer set for the cameras.

## How show light works
Show lights are focused on the runway centre line and especially on the **end mark**, where
the pit shoots. Off the centre line or short of the mark, your face drops into shadow and
the exposure is wrong.

## Marks
- **Spike marks**: small pieces of tape (often glow tape or coloured tape) at the end
  mark, entrance, and any stopping points. Learn them in rehearsal; they can be hard to see
  once the lights change.
- **Hot spot**: where the light is brightest. Feel it on your face and see it on the floor.
  Ask the lighting team where the end hot spot is.

## Blinding front light
Front light can make the end of the runway invisible. Count steps to the end mark in
rehearsal (see `stride-length-control`) and trust the count. Keep the eyes open and
focused at the light bank's centre; do not squint.

## Verify
- [ ] Know where every spike mark is and its count.
- [ ] Pit image from rehearsal shows the face evenly lit.
