# Heel height progression training

**Goal:** walk confidently in 10-12 cm stilettos, the height many shows hand out, without
learning it on the day.

## The ladder
| Stage | Shoe | Move up when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5-7 cm block heel | 15 min walking with a still head and no ankle wobble |
| 2 | 7-9 cm block or kitten | same, plus clean pivot turns |
| 3 | 8-10 cm stiletto | same on a smooth hard floor |
| 4 | 10-12 cm stiletto | full rehearsal routine (walk, pause, turn) x 20 |
| 5 | Platforms, mules, sculptural heels | see `platforms-and-unusual-shoes` |

## Practice volume
15-20 minutes, 4-5 days a week. Short frequent sessions beat one long one; fatigue
creates bad habits (bent knees, lean back).

## Surfaces
Practise on what runways are: smooth hard floors (gloss, concrete, wood). Carpet hides
faults. Add a slope or gravel only once stable (outdoor shows exist).

## Warning signs
- Ankle rolling or wobbling: drop back a stage.
- Forefoot pain lasting into the next day, numb toes, or blisters: rest, check fit, and
  see a professional if pain persists.
- Walking with bent knees and hips back: shoe too high for current strength.

## Verify
- [ ] Filmed walk at stage 4 shows head still, knees soft not bent, no wobble.
- [ ] 20 consecutive pivot turns in a 10 cm stiletto.
