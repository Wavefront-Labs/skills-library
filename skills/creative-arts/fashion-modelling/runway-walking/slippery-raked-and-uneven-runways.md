# Slippery, raked and uneven runways

**Goal:** keep walking safely when the set designer has made the floor part of the show.

## Surfaces
| Surface | Adjustment |
|---|---|
| Gloss, acrylic, mirrored | Scuffed soles or grip pads; shorter stride; two-step turn |
| Wet or water (shallow pools) | Very short stride, flat-footed placement; ask what the depth and slip risk are |
| Raked (sloped) stage | Lean from the ankle not the hips; shorter stride downhill |
| Sand, gravel, grass | Weight forward, stiletto heels sink: ask for block heels or bare feet; slow pace |
| Grating, decking with gaps | Stilettos catch: walk on the balls of the feet or ask for cover strips |
| Platform joins, carpet seams | Lift the toe more; learn where they are |
| Stairs and levels | Rehearse with eyes on the steps then without |

## Rehearse on it
Insist on walking the set in show shoes before the show. Any surface you cannot walk
safely after trying fixes: tell the producer; they can change shoes, add grip or change the route.

## Verify
- [ ] Every surface on the route walked in show shoes.
- [ ] Unsafe sections reported and resolved before the show.
