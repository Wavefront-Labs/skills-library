# Coat and jacket reveals on the runway

**Goal:** show both the outer layer and what is under it, exactly as the stylist planned.

## Ask first
The stylist decides: closed, open, worn on the shoulders, removed, or a reveal at the
pit. Get it explicit at the fitting and write it down per look ("look 12: open coat at
pit, hold, close on the way back").

## Open coats
- Unbuttoned coats swing with the stride; a little more stride and a fractionally
  stronger hip makes them move.
- Hands in pockets push the coat back and show the look underneath; only if styled.

## The reveal at the pit
1. Arrive and set the pause.
2. Open the coat with both hands at the front edges, smoothly, or slide it off the shoulders.
3. Hold for the pit, then close or put back as briefed on the turn.
Practise with the actual coat: heavy fabric needs more push; slippery linings slide off fast.

## Worn on the shoulders
Shoulders slightly back and still; any shrug drops it. Ask for a hidden stitch or tape
if it keeps slipping.

## Carrying a removed jacket
Over one forearm or held by the collar over the shoulder, folded neatly; it is still on show.

## Verify
- [ ] Each outerwear look's instruction written down.
- [ ] Reveal rehearsed with the actual garment.
