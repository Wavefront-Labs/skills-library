# Hats and headpieces balance

**Goal:** keep the headpiece on, level and safe while walking normally.

## Securing
Hair team secures with pins, combs, elastic or tape. Ask them to test: tilt your head
forward and to the side. It should not move. Never walk out with a piece you can feel slipping.

## Posture changes
- Head absolutely level: any nod shows up multiplied by a brim.
- Neck long, slower and smoother stride; avoid any bounce.
- Heavy pieces: brace through the core and upper back, keep the shoulders down.

## Sight lines
Brims and veils limit vision. In rehearsal, walk the runway in the piece and learn where
the edges are. Use peripheral cues (the runway edge, light lines).

## Width
Wide pieces need extra room at the entrance, at the turn and passing other models. Turn
more slowly; pass at the widest gap. Duck slightly through low doorways backstage.

## Verify
- [ ] Piece tested secure with the head tilted before going out.
- [ ] Rehearsal pass done in the piece.
