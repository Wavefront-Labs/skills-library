# Couture and slow walk

**Goal:** let a one-off garment be seen in full, slowly and with total control.

## The glide
- Slow steps, smaller stride, weight transferring smoothly so the body appears to float.
- Lead from the hip, soft knee, ball of the foot placed deliberately; no bounce.
- Slow walking is harder to balance; core firm, eyes fixed.

## Holds and turns
- Longer pause; half turns to show the back and construction are common.
- Turns slow and smooth, never whipped.

## The garments
- Couture pieces are one-off, often heavy (embroidery, structure) or fragile (feathers,
  beading). Move to avoid catching on doors, other models, chairs.
- Sit only if the dresser says you can; some pieces crush.
- No perfume, lotion or deodorant transfer onto garments; dress with a face cover if given.

## Salons
Couture can still be shown in small salons with guests close on gilt chairs. Walk
between them carefully; eyes ahead, no eye contact unless briefed.

## Verify
- [ ] Can walk the runway at half normal pace with head still.
- [ ] Know which looks need back holds.
