# Static presentation stillness

**Goal:** look alive and composed for an hour or more while guests walk round you.

## The format
Models stand or sit on a set (platforms, rooms, tableaux) while press and buyers circulate.
Often 1-2 hours, in rotations.

## Holding still
- Choose poses you can hold for minutes: weight even or on one leg with frequent swaps,
  joints unlocked (locked knees can cause fainting).
- Breathe low and slowly; small, slow shifts every 30-60 s prevent stiffness and are
  usually allowed. Agree with the director how much movement is wanted.
- Change poses slowly, as one continuous move, so photographs mid-change still look good.

## Eyes and guests
Eyes to a fixed point or slowly moving, as briefed. Guests and phones come within a
metre; stay in character, do not chat unless the format invites it.

## Breaks
Ask about rotation and break times before you start. Long static work in heels needs
breaks to sit and drink. Feeling faint: tell the coordinator or sit down; do not wait to collapse.

## Verify
- [ ] Rotation and break schedule known.
- [ ] Poses chosen are holdable with knees unlocked.
