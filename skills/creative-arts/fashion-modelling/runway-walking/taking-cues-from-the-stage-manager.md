# Taking cues from the stage manager

**Goal:** go exactly when told, so the show the producer planned is the show that happens.

## Who's who
- **Show producer**: runs the whole show; often gives the final brief.
- **Stage manager / dispatcher**: at the runway entrance, releases each model.
- **Line producer / runners**: keep the line in order backstage.
- **Movement director / choreographer**: sets the walk and staging.

## Cue language
- "Standby" or a hand on the shoulder: ready on the mark, focus.
- "Go" or a tap: walk on the next step (or on the music cue if briefed).
- "Hold": stay on the mark. Shows hold if something is wrong on the runway.
Some use hand signals only (raised hand = hold, drop = go) because it is loud.

## Missed cue
If you were not ready (shoe not on, change not done), tell the dispatcher immediately;
they will send the next model and re-slot you. Do not run out half-dressed.

## Verify
- [ ] Know who dispatches and how (word, tap or signal).
- [ ] Know the hold signal.
