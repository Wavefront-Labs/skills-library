# Conditioning for runway work

**Goal:** a body that can stand, walk in heels and change looks for 12-hour fashion-week
days, trained for strength and health, not for a number on the tape.

## What to train
- **Posture**: rows, face pulls, band pull-aparts (upper back); dead bugs and planks (core).
- **Hips and glutes**: glute bridges, side-lying leg raises, split squats - stability in heels.
- **Calves and feet**: see `ankle-and-foot-conditioning-for-heels`.
- **Stamina**: brisk walking, cycling, swimming; 150 minutes of moderate activity a week is
  the general adult guideline.

## Fashion-week season
Lighter training in show weeks; prioritise sleep, food and recovery. Stretch calves and hips daily.

## Health, not size
Training to lose weight to meet a measurement is a known path to harm. Fashion councils'
guidance (BFC, CFDA Health Initiative) asks agencies and brands to support healthy models,
and some laws require medical certification of health (see `health-certificate`). If you
feel pressure to change your body, talk to your agent or a clinician.

## Verify
- [ ] Two strength sessions a week including upper back, core, glutes.
- [ ] Recovery planned for show weeks.
