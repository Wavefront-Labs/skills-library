# Finale walk

**Goal:** a clean, synchronised last parade that frames the designer's bow.

## Setup
- The finale is usually in your **first look** or your last look; the producer decides.
  This is a fast change after the last exit; know it in advance.
- Line up in show order, closely spaced.

## The walk
- Close spacing: about 1-2 m between models, one continuous line.
- The pace is set by the first model and the music (often a different track). Match the
  model in front exactly; do not close up or open gaps.
- Usually no pause or turn at the end: the line walks down, loops round the end, and back.
- Expression as for the show, though some designers allow a soft smile.

## The bow
The designer comes out after the last model or at the head of the line; keep walking off
the runway as briefed. Do not stop to clap unless told to.

## Verify
- [ ] Know which look the finale is in.
- [ ] Rehearsed the loop at finale spacing.
