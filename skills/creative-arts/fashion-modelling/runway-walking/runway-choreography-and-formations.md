# Runway choreography and formations

**Goal:** perform a staged route accurately, in time with other models.

## Common formations
- **Pairs / groups**: walk side by side, matched stride and arm swing, same pause.
- **Criss-cross**: two routes crossing; one model has right of way, learn who.
- **Static tableau**: models walk to marks and hold, then exit in sequence.
- **Loops and figure-eights** in large spaces.
- **Simultaneous final line-up** where all models stand on marks.

## Learning it
1. The movement director gives your route, marks and counts. Write it down or draw it
   immediately: start mark, route, stops (with counts), exits.
2. Walk it slowly, then at pace, then with the group.
3. Note who you take cues from (the model ahead, a music cue, a lighting change).

## Matching
In pairs, the inside model sets the pace; match the foot. Use peripheral vision to stay
level, do not turn to look.

## Verify
- [ ] Route and counts written or drawn.
- [ ] Two clean group runs in rehearsal.
