# Ankle and foot conditioning for heels

**Goal:** stable ankles and resilient feet, so heels stay safe through long fashion weeks.

## Routine (10 minutes, 3-4 times a week)
1. **Calf raises**: 3 x 15 on both feet, then 3 x 10 single leg, slow up and down.
   Progress by doing them on a step edge.
2. **Single-leg balance**: 30 s each leg, then eyes closed, then on a cushion.
3. **Ankle alphabet**: trace the alphabet with the big toe, each foot.
4. **Toe work**: pick up a towel or marbles with the toes; spread and lift toes individually.
5. **Short foot**: draw the ball of the foot towards the heel to lift the arch, 10 x 5 s.

## After heel days
- Calf stretch against a wall (straight and bent knee), 30 s each.
- Roll the sole on a ball for 1-2 minutes.
- Elevate the feet if swollen.

## Fashion week
Several shows a day can mean hours in heels. Change into flats between shows and at
fittings unless told otherwise; wear trainers between castings.

## When to get help
Persistent heel or forefoot pain, numbness, a sprain that does not improve within a few
days, or recurring ankle rolling: see a physiotherapist or podiatrist.

## Verify
- [ ] 30 s single-leg balance with eyes closed each side.
- [ ] 10 slow single-leg calf raises each side without wobble.
