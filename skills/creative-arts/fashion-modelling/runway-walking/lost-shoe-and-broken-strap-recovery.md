# Lost shoe and broken strap recovery

**Goal:** finish the walk with composure; the audience remembers how you recover.

## Shoe comes off
1. Stop briefly only if needed; do not panic or look back first.
2. Options:
   - **Kick off the other shoe** too and walk barefoot, picking both up if easy, or
   - **Pick up the shoe** calmly and carry it, continuing the walk, or
   - On a short runway near the end, finish with the shoe in hand.
3. Keep the same face and pace. Do not limp in one heel; it looks worse and risks an ankle.
4. A shoe left on the runway is a hazard for the next model; pick it up if you can, or
   tell the dispatcher immediately on exit.

## Strap breaks but shoe stays on
Shorten the stride, keep the foot flexed to hold the shoe, finish the pass and tell the
shoe team. They will tape, swap or remove for the next look.

## Prevention
Check fit and straps at the fitting and before each exit; use grips (see
`sole-grip-and-shoe-fit-fixes`).

## Verify
- [ ] Know your plan before going out (barefoot vs carry).
- [ ] Reported to the shoe team as you come off.
