# Livestreamed and filmed shows

**Goal:** a walk that works for moving cameras as well as the pit.

## What changes
- Video cameras on tracks, gimbals or cranes follow the walk; you are on camera the whole
  route, from the backdrop to the exit. Posture and expression never drop.
- Head bob and arm faults are far more visible on video than in stills.
- Digital-only shows (filmed in advance) may involve several takes, marks and set moves:
  treat as a fashion film (see `fashion-film` skills in movement-for-camera).

## Cameras on the runway
Ignore moving cameras unless told to look at one; a track camera may run beside you.
Keep your line; the operator follows you.

## Usage
Livestreams and show films are usually covered by the show booking for the brand's own
channels, but advertising use is extra. Check with your agent what usage the show fee
includes (see `usage-rights` skills).

## Verify
- [ ] Rehearsal video reviewed for faults visible only on moving footage.
- [ ] Usage of the film confirmed by the agent.
