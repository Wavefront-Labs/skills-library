# Two-step and hip turns

**Goal:** have turns for when the pivot is unsafe or the brief wants something different.

## Two-step turn (safe turn)
1. Arrive at the end mark.
2. Step the outside foot across in front, turning the body 90 degrees.
3. Step the other foot round to complete 180 degrees and walk off.
It is slower and less stylish but safe in platforms, mules, very high heels, or on wet or
glossy floors.

## Half turn to show the back
For a garment with back detail (open back, train, embellishment):
1. At the end, pivot 90 or 180 degrees and **hold** so the photographers get the back.
2. Look over the shoulder to the pit if briefed.
3. Turn back to face front or continue round and walk back.
Ask the stylist: if a look has a back detail, they often want this.

## Hip turn (editorial)
Lead the turn with a small push of the hip into the direction of travel before the feet
turn, giving a swing to skirts and coats. Keep it small; big hip throws look theatrical.

## Choosing
| Condition | Turn |
|---|---|
| Normal floor, normal heel | Pivot |
| Unstable shoe or floor | Two-step |
| Back detail | Half turn with hold |
| Full skirt, editorial brief | Hip turn |

## Verify
- [ ] All three turns rehearsed in show shoes.
- [ ] Know which looks need a back-show half turn.
