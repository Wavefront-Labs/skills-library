# Outdoor and street shows

**Goal:** perform safely and well when the venue is a beach, a garden, a street or a rooftop.

## Conditions
- **Sun**: low sun blinds and makes models squint; find your focus point slightly off the
  sun line in rehearsal. Hydrate.
- **Wind**: hair and garments blow; lean very slightly into it, keep the head still, let the
  stylist decide whether to fight or use it.
- **Cold or rain**: ask for coats, heaters and covered waiting areas; clothing can be
  inadequate by design.
- **Ground**: cobbles, grass, sand, gravel (see `slippery-raked-and-uneven-runways`).

## Public spaces
Street shows can have the public near the route and no fixed barrier. Stay on the route,
ignore distractions; security should handle crowds. Know the exit route back to the safe area.

## Welfare
Outdoor shows should provide shade or warmth, water, toilets and a secure changing space.
Ask your agent to confirm before booking if the brief is outdoor.

## Verify
- [ ] Weather plan (cover, warmth, shade, water) confirmed.
- [ ] Route walked on the actual ground in show shoes.
