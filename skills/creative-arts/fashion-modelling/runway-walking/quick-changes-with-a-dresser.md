# Quick changes with a dresser

**Goal:** change looks in a minute or two without damaging the clothes or the make-up.

## Before the show
- Meet your dresser and walk through each change on your rail. Agree the order: shoes
  first or last, what is unzipped in advance, where accessories are.
- Looks are preset: zips open, buttons undone, shoes unbuckled, accessories clipped to hangers.

## The change
1. Come off the runway straight to your rail.
2. Stand still and let the dresser work; lift arms, step in and out when asked.
3. Head through necklines last off and first on with a **face cover** (a hood or scarf
   over the make-up) for over-the-head garments.
4. Shoes: often removed first and put on last while the dresser fastens the top.
5. Check yourself in the mirror for 2 seconds if time allows: hem, collar, tags hidden.
6. Into the line.

## Privacy and consent
Changes happen fast and often in shared space. Welfare charters (LVMH-Kering, BFC
guidance) call for private changing areas and no photography backstage while models
change. You are entitled to ask for privacy and to have photographers removed from changing areas.

## Verify
- [ ] Each change rehearsed with the dresser or at least walked through.
- [ ] Face cover used for over-the-head garments.
- [ ] No photographers in the changing area.
