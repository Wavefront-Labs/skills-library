# Pause hold at the end of the runway

**Goal:** a settled, photogenic stop that gives the pit a sharp frame of the look.

## Arrive and set
1. Last step lands on the mark with one foot slightly in front, toes angled a little out.
2. Shift weight onto the back leg; the front leg is relaxed, knee soft.
3. Hips square to the pit or angled 10-20 degrees (angled slims the silhouette).
4. Shoulders square to the pit, head level, eyes into the lenses.

## How long
- Typical: 1-3 seconds or a count of 2-4 beats. Some shows want **no pause** (walk to the
  end and turn), some a long editorial hold. Always ask.
- Couture and bridal often hold longer; fast commercial shows are short.

## End-pose options (only if briefed)
- Still: arms long at the sides.
- One hand on hip (back of the hip, fingers soft, elbow back not out sideways).
- Slight shoulder drop towards the camera, or a slow turn of the head.
- Commercial: a smile or small change of expression.

## Exit
Move straight into the turn from the set position; do not shuffle the feet before turning.

## Verify
- [ ] Pause length matches the brief in rehearsal.
- [ ] No foot shuffle or wobble between arrival and turn.
- [ ] Pit photos show a settled pose with weight on the back leg.
