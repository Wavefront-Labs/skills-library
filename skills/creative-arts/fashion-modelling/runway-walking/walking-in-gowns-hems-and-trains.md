# Walking in gowns, hems and trains

**Goal:** make a long dress flow and never step on it.

## Floor-length hems
- Use a small **forward flick** of the foot at each step so the toe pushes the hem
  forward rather than catching it. Keep it subtle; the kick should be invisible.
- Stride shorter than usual and keep to the single line (crossing tangles the skirt).
- If the stylist allows, lightly hold a fold of the skirt at the side, hand soft, lifting
  only enough to clear the toe.

## Trains
- The train follows you; never look back for it.
- **Turning**: turn wide enough that you do not step back onto the train. A half turn
  that walks round in a small arc is safer than a pivot. Some shows have an assistant
  arrange the train at the end.
- Walking back: the train can sweep across the next model's path; keep to your passing side.

## Stairs and steps
Lift the front of the skirt slightly, look down only with the eyes, and place the whole
foot on each step. Go down steps sideways slightly if the hem is very full.

## Rehearse in it
Walk at least one full runway pass in the actual gown at rehearsal or the fitting. Report
hems that are too long for the show shoe to the stylist (they may pin or hem it).

## Verify
- [ ] Full pass and turn in the gown without stepping on hem or train.
- [ ] Hem kick not visible on video.
