# Trip and fall recovery on the runway

**Goal:** stay safe first, then finish with composure.

## Saving a trip
Soft knees and a firm core make trips recoverable: let the knee bend, take a quick extra
step and re-set the pace. Do not grab at other models or the audience.

## If you fall
1. Stay down a second and check: ankle, knee, wrist. Pain on weight-bearing means stop.
2. Get up the safe way: onto one knee, then stand. Kick off unstable shoes first if
   needed and carry them.
3. Take a breath, re-find posture and focus, and finish the walk at pace, or walk straight
   off if hurt. Many models are remembered positively for a calm recovery.
4. On exit, tell the producer and get first aid to look at you if there is any pain.

## Afterwards
Do not walk the next look on a possibly sprained ankle to "be professional"; tell the
producer. Rest, ice, compression and elevation are the first steps for a sprain; see a
clinician if it does not improve.

## Prevention
Venue walkthrough, grip on soles, rehearse in the shoes (see the related skills in
this product).

## Verify
- [ ] Injury checked before continuing.
- [ ] Incident reported to the producer.
