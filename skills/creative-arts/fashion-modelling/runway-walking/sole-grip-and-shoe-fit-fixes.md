# Sole grip and shoe fit fixes

**Goal:** a shoe that grips the floor and stays on your foot.

## Slippery soles
New leather soles and glossy runways are the classic fall combination.
1. **Scuff** the sole: rub across rough concrete, sandpaper or a nail file in a cross-hatch.
   Ask the shoe team first: sample shoes may need returning unmarked.
2. **Grip tape / sole pads**: clear adhesive non-slip pads on the forefoot. Many show shoe
   teams carry them; carry your own in your model bag.
3. Wipe dust off soles right before going out; dust from the floor removes grip.

## Loose fit
- Heel grips (adhesive pads inside the heel counter) for heel slip.
- Toe inserts or tissue in the toe for shoes a size big.
- Gel ball-of-foot cushions also stop the foot sliding forward.
- Double-sided fashion tape between foot and footbed for mules.

## Pre-show check
- Heel tips (top lifts) are present and not worn to the metal pin: metal on a gloss floor
  slips.
- Straps and buckles fastened and checked by the dresser.
- Laces double-knotted and tucked.

## Verify
- [ ] Walked and turned on the actual runway surface in the shoe after fixes.
- [ ] Model bag has grip pads, heel grips and fashion tape.
