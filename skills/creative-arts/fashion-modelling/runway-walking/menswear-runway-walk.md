# Menswear runway walk

**Goal:** a confident, grounded walk that shows tailoring and volume without swagger.

## Placement and stride
- Feet land on **two close parallel tracks**, about hip-width or slightly narrower; no
  crossing. Toes straight ahead.
- Stride a little longer than everyday walking, heel strike then roll through; the step
  should feel purposeful, not marching.
- Knees soft; avoid a bouncing step (head bob shows more on taller men).

## Upper body
- Shoulders square and still; chest open but not puffed.
- Arms hang long; natural swing close to the body or almost still (common in tailoring
  shows). No exaggerated swing.
- Hands relaxed, slightly cupped. Hands-in-pockets only if styled.

## Face
Neutral, serious, eyes to the end point; jaw relaxed. Commercial menswear may allow a
slight smile at the pit.

## Differences from womenswear
- No hip sway; the line is straight.
- The pause is usually short or none; the turn is often a simple step-turn.
- Garment action: let coats and open jackets move; for buttoned tailoring keep the
  jacket buttoned as styled and do not hold the lapels.

## Common faults
- Swaggering shoulders or rolling the upper body.
- Looking down (tall models especially) - fix eyeline to the end wall.
- Walking too fast because the stride is long; keep the briefed pace.

## Verify
- [ ] Front video: two clean tracks, no crossing.
- [ ] Head stays level; shoulders do not roll.
- [ ] Matches the cast's pace when walking in a line-up.
