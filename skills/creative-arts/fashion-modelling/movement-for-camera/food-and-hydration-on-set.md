# Food and hydration on set

**Note:** general guidance, not medical or dietetic advice. If you have a health condition or eating concerns, speak to a doctor or dietitian; see the model-health skills.

## Practice
- **Eat before call** a balanced meal you tolerate.
- **Bring your own snacks and a water bottle** (fruit, nuts, bars, whatever suits you), since catering varies.
- **Drink regularly,** and avoid heavy food and large amounts of caffeine that cause bloating or jitters.
- **Avoid food that affects the look** on the day (salty or fizzy items that may cause bloating, strongly coloured drinks near white garments).
- **Respect the garment:** do not eat or drink in clothes unless the stylist agrees, and use a straw or change first.
- **Allergies and dietary needs:** tell production in advance.
- **Ask for breaks and meals** if they are not provided; welfare guidance generally asks that healthy food and drink be available on set (check the charter and your contract for specifics).
