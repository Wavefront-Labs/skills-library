# Jump shots

**Goal:** a clean airborne shape with a relaxed face, repeated without injury.

## Technique
- **Warm up** ankles, calves, quads and hips. Cold muscles and tired legs are where injuries happen.
- Check the **surface** (not slippery or uneven) and the **shoes**. Ask for flat shoes or a cushioned surface, or for the jump to be photographed in socks or barefoot if the set allows it.
- **Take off and land** through the balls of the feet with soft knees, and land one foot after another when appropriate.
- **Shape in the air:** pre-decide the arms and legs; lead with the chest and keep the neck long and the face relaxed.
- **Limit repeats** and rest between sets, since each jump is lower and sloppier when tired.
- **Agree** the number of jumps and what is behind you (props, hard edges, glass).
- You can decline stunts you do not feel safe doing. See the stunt-safety skill.
