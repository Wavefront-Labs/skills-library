# Digital twin consent and licensing

**Why it matters:** a digital replica can be used after the shoot without you being present. Rules are changing; for example the New York Fashion Workers Act and the SAG-AFTRA agreements contain provisions about digital replicas, so check the current text and seek qualified legal advice before you sign.

## What to check in any agreement
- **Clear and specific consent:** it should describe what the replica is, how it will be created and for which uses. Broad language such as "any media, in perpetuity" is a warning sign.
- **Scope:** the products, brands, media, territories and whether AI training is included.
- **Term and renewal:** a defined period, not an indefinite one.
- **Compensation:** separate from the day rate, with payment tied to uses.
- **Approval or review rights** for sensitive uses.
- **Ownership, storage and deletion:** who holds the scan data and what happens at the end.
- **Revocation:** whether and how consent can be withdrawn.
- **Your agency's role:** ask your agent or a lawyer or union rep to review.

## Habit
Do not sign on the day. Take the document away, and ask for changes in writing.
