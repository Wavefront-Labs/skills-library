# 3d body scan sessions

**Context:** brands and studios scan bodies for digital garments, avatars, fit models and games. A scan is personal data and can be reused, so the contract matters.

## Before
- **Ask what the scan is for,** who owns it, how it is stored and for how long, and whether it can be used to make a digital replica (see the digital-twin-consent skill). Get this in writing through your agent.
- **Confirm who attends** and that a private changing area is provided.

## On the day
- **Wear what is requested:** often fitted, plain or minimal clothing, with hair tied back and no shiny or reflective items.
- **Stand in the position requested** (often an A-pose or relaxed stance) and keep still while the rig captures.
- **Keep consistent** between sessions: weight, posture and clothing.
- **Request copies or deletion terms** where the contract allows.
- **Stop** if you are uncomfortable.
