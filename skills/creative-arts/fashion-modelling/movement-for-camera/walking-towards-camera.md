# Walking towards camera

**Goal:** a natural, repeatable walk that stays sharp and keeps the garment moving.

## Technique
- Ask the **start mark, end mark and the lens distance**. Focus is set for a zone, and walking out of it softens the frame.
- Walk on a **single line** toward the lens at a pace the garment suits: slower for heavy or long pieces, quicker for casual.
- Use a **normal heel-to-toe stride**, slightly shorter than a runway stride, with the chin level and the eyes at the lens or on a point just beside it.
- Let the **arms swing lightly** and keep hands relaxed; stiff arms look robotic.
- Let **fabric lead**: a skirt, coat or scarf moves best when you commit to the step rather than tiptoe.
- Repeat the walk **several times** and refine one thing each pass (pace, arm, expression). For video, keep the pass identical from take to take.
- Wear the right shoes and check the floor for grip.

## Check
Same start mark each pass, eyes consistent, garment visible, and you stopped on the end mark.
