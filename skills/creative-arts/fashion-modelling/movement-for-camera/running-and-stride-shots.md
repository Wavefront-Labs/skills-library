# Running and stride shots

**Goal:** convincing energy without losing the garment or your footing.

## Technique
- Ask the **distance, direction and stop point** and where the camera or crew are. Do not run toward people or equipment.
- Use an **athletic but controlled** pace; a full sprint is rarely needed and often blurs.
- **Arms** bent and swinging naturally; hands relaxed.
- **Head and eyes** level, with the face relaxed and not strained; breathe.
- **Repeat** with a rest between runs so form stays consistent.
- Check the **surface and shoes** (grip, ankle support) and walk the route first.
- **Hair and fabric** need space; agree with the stylist how much movement is acceptable.
- Warm up and cool down.
