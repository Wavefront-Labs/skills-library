# Brand ugc deliverables

**Context:** user-generated-style content is made to look like organic posts and is commonly used in ads.

## Technique
- **Read the brief:** product, key messages, format (vertical video, photos), length, and what you cannot say.
- **Confirm the deliverables:** number of clips, resolution, raw files or edited, deadlines and revisions.
- **Agree the usage and rights:** paid media or organic, platforms, territory and term, and exclusivity. UGC used in paid ads is often priced differently from organic posts.
- **Check the contract and your agency:** some agency agreements cover content work and some do not. See the model-contract skills.
- **Disclose properly:** see the ad-disclosure skill.
- **Keep quality high:** steady, good light, clear sound, one clear message and no filters the brand did not ask for.
- **Keep records:** brief, approvals and invoices.
