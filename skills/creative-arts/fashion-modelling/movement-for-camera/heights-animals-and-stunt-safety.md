# Heights animals and stunt safety

**Safety first:** professional guidance only. Rules and insurance requirements vary by country and production.

## Before the shoot
- **Check the booking:** stunts, heights and animals should be disclosed in advance, in writing, and covered by the agreed fee, insurance and conditions.
- **Ask who supervises:** qualified riggers, stunt coordinators, animal handlers and a medical or first-aid person should be named and present.
- **Ask for a risk assessment** and a rehearsal.

## On the day
- **Heights:** only on certified, secured platforms and harnesses as directed by qualified people; do not climb unsecured structures.
- **Animals:** follow the handler's instructions, stay calm and avoid sudden movement. Tell the team about allergies or phobias.
- **Stunts:** use a stunt performer or double for anything outside your experience.
- **Your decision:** you can decline or stop at any time. Contact your agent if pressured.
- **Report incidents** and injuries promptly.
