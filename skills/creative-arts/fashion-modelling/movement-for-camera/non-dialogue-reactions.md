# Non dialogue reactions

**Goal:** a clear emotional beat that reads on camera without words.

## Technique
- Ask the **moment** (what just happened) and the **emotion** (surprise, delight, relief), and what the camera will frame (close-up or wide).
- **Think the thought,** then let the face respond. Pushing the expression without a thought looks fake.
- **Scale to the shot:** a close-up needs a small, honest change in the eyes and mouth; a wide shot needs more body.
- Use **a clear beat structure**: notice, process, react. Give each stage a small pause.
- **Repeat consistently** across takes (same moment of looking, same hand movement) so the editor can cut.
- **Hold the reaction** a second after it peaks; editors often need that tail.
- Keep **product visibility** and eyelines as directed.
