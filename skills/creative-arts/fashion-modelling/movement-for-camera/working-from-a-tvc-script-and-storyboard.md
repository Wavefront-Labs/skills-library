# Working from a tvc script and storyboard

**Goal:** know your action in each frame and the shot the camera will use.

## Technique
- Read the **script** for scenes, actions and any dialogue, and the **storyboard** for frame size, angle and where you stand.
- Mark **your actions and cues**: what you do, when, with which hand and where you look.
- Note **timing** (a commercial is short, so each action often has a few seconds) and whether the director wants a specific rhythm.
- Ask about **eyeline** (lens or off-lens), **props** and the **product's position**.
- Check **wardrobe continuity** between scenes and ask for photos to match.
- Ask about **safety** (stunts, vehicles, water, animals) and **intimacy** or nudity before shooting day.
- Expect **changes on the day**; keep the notes updated.
