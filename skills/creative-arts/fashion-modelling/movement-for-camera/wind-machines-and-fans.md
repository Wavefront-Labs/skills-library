# Wind machines and fans

**Goal:** movement in hair and fabric without discomfort or injury.

## Technique
- **Protect your eyes.** Strong airflow can carry dust and grit. Ask for eyewear, keep eyes squinted-but-soft, or blink between bursts.
- **Ask the position and strength** of the fan and whether it can be reduced.
- **Plant your stance:** feet apart and weight steady, because wind from the front or side can push you.
- **Direct the hair:** keep it from covering your face and the product; the stylist can pin or spray it.
- **Face control:** keep your expression relaxed; wind makes you squint and tense.
- **Communicate:** hearing is limited near fans, so agree hand signals.
- **Respect the equipment:** do not touch moving blades or cables.
- **Fabric:** ask how far a garment can move, and avoid snagging on rough surfaces.
