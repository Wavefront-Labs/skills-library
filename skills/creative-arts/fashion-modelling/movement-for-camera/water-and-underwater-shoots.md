# Water and underwater shoots

**Safety first:** underwater work carries drowning and injury risk. This is professional guidance only; follow the production's safety plan and qualified supervision.

## Before the shoot
- Confirm in advance that the shoot is **in the booking**, with the agreed usage and conditions, and that **trained safety personnel** (for example safety divers and a medical or first-aid team) are present.
- **Be honest about your swimming and breath-holding ability.** Ask for a practice session.
- Agree **hand signals** for "stop" and "OK".

## On the day
- **Breath control:** do not push your limits, and never hyperventilate before a breath-hold. Rest between takes.
- **Eyes and ears:** ask about goggles, drops or protection and about water quality.
- **Warmth:** pool and tank water can be cold; have a warm robe, towels and warm drinks ready, and take breaks.
- **Hair, make-up and garments:** water-proof products and swim-ready fabrics.
- **Stop when unsafe.** You may end the take at any time.
