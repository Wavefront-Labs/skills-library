# Motion capture sessions

**Context:** mocap records your movement to animate a digital character or avatar. Check how the movement data may be used.

## Before
- **Ask the purpose, ownership and usage** of the data, and whether it will be used to create a digital replica. Get this in writing through your agent.
- **Ask what to wear:** commonly a fitted suit with markers or a sensor suit, and soft shoes.

## On the day
- **Calibration:** you will usually be asked for a neutral stance such as a T-pose or A-pose, and a range-of-motion routine.
- **Stay inside the capture volume** and avoid blocking markers with your own hands or props.
- **Perform clearly:** exaggerate slightly where the director asks; keep actions repeatable.
- **Reset between takes** and keep the same start position.
- **Take breaks:** marker suits can be warm and tiring.
- **Report** a loose marker or a suit that restricts you.
