# Behind the scenes content

**Goal:** authentic content for the brand or your own channel, without leaking confidential work.

## Technique
- **Ask first:** who may film, and what can be shown or posted, before you or others start.
- **Respect embargoes:** do not post unreleased garments, locations, campaign images or other people before release.
- **Do not film other people** (crew, models, children) without their consent and the production's.
- **Do not film** fittings, changing areas, nudity or private moments.
- Keep **your own phone clips** short and useful: a short clip of your look, the set and the team (with permission).
- Follow **brand handles, tags and hashtags** requested by the client.
- Check your **agency and contract terms** about posting and usage.
