# Cold and hot weather shoots

**Note:** professional guidance, not medical advice. Seek medical help for heat illness, hypothermia or frostbite symptoms.

## Cold
- **Layer** warm clothing between takes (robe, coat, thermal layers, gloves, hat) and keep feet warm.
- **Warm up** before shooting and use hand and foot warmers when allowed.
- **Cold shows:** red noses, blue lips and visible breath; ask for touch-ups and short takes.
- **Keep dry:** change wet clothes quickly.

## Hot
- **Hydrate,** take shade breaks and use sun protection.
- Watch for **heat illness** (dizziness, nausea, headache, confusion). Stop and tell someone.
- **Hair and make-up** will need touch-ups.

## All weather
- **Check the forecast and the contingency plan** (a weather day, a rebooking, a fee). See the cancellation-fee skill.
- **Wear safe footwear** for wet, icy or rough surfaces.
- **You can say no** to conditions that are unsafe for you.
