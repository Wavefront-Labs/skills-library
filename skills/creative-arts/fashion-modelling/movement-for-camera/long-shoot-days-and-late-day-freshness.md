# Long shoot days and late day freshness

**Goal:** the last frame is as good as the first.

## Technique
- **Get the call sheet** and know the day's length, looks and breaks. Sleep well beforehand and eat before call.
- **Pace yourself:** save peak energy for takes, and relax between set-ups; do not perform for the room.
- **Use breaks:** drink, eat, sit with your feet up, stretch your neck, shoulders, back and calves, and ask for a few minutes if needed.
- **Late in the day:** reset the face (breathe, shake out jaw and shoulders), refresh the eyes with a short pause, and re-check your posture and hands.
- **Keep posture:** long standing tires the knees and back; shift weight and avoid locking the knees.
- **Hair and make-up:** ask for touch-ups when shine, creases or tired eyes show.
- **Say so early:** tell the producer or your agent if breaks are missing, hours exceed the agreement or you feel unwell. See the working-hours skill.
