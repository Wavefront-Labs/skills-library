# Paid partnership disclosure

**Principle:** consumer-protection regulators in many countries require that paid or gifted brand relationships be disclosed clearly, so audiences can tell advertising from independent opinion. Examples include the US Federal Trade Commission's Endorsement Guides and, in the UK, the CAP Code enforced by the Advertising Standards Authority. Rules differ by country and are updated, so check the current text.

## Practice
- **Disclose anything of value:** payment, free product, commission, trips or other benefits.
- **Make it clear and obvious:** place it where it is seen, not hidden in a long block of hashtags or a "more" link. Use plain labels such as "ad", "paid partnership" or "sponsored". Use the platform's paid-partnership tool when available, but add your own label if the tool alone is not clear.
- **Put it in the video itself** for video content, as spoken and on screen.
- **Only say what you believe;** do not claim a product does what you have not experienced or what the brand cannot support.
- **Follow the brief and the contract,** and ask the brand or agency which wording they require.
- **When unsure,** ask the agency or take qualified advice.
