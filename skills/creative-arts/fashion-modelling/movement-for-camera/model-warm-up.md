# Model warm up

**Note:** general fitness guidance, not medical advice. If you have an injury or condition, ask a physiotherapist or doctor.

## A 5 to 10 minute routine
- **Pulse raiser:** brisk walking, light jogging on the spot or skipping for a couple of minutes.
- **Neck:** slow half circles and side tilts; avoid forcing.
- **Shoulders and upper back:** arm circles, shoulder rolls, cat-cow or a gentle spinal twist.
- **Hips and legs:** leg swings, hip circles, lunges and light squats.
- **Ankles and calves:** ankle circles, calf raises and a light calf stretch; important for heels and jumps.
- **Wrists and hands:** circles and finger spreads.

## When to do more
- Before **jumps, spins, dance, running or extreme shapes**, add dynamic movement of the joints involved.
- **Cold weather** needs a longer warm-up.
- Stop for **sharp or joint pain.**
