# Turntable and 360 video

**Goal:** a smooth, repeatable rotation that shows the garment from every side.

## Technique
- Ask the **rotation speed**, **direction** and **number of full turns**. The platform is often motorised and the speed is fixed.
- **Stand centred** on the platform with feet on marks, because off-centre positions swing in and out of focus and frame.
- **Hold your posture:** a relaxed standing pose with weight evenly spread, arms slightly away from the body to show the sides, and eyes on a fixed spot or the lens as asked.
- **Keep movement minimal** during rotation so the loop is smooth; do not shift weight or fidget.
- **Keep your face consistent** (neutral or a soft smile) and blink naturally.
- **Garment:** check hems, collar and back details before each pass.
- **Safety:** ask for a stop button, hold on to a rail if available, and step down only when the platform has stopped.
- **Reset between looks** at the same position and pose.
