# Vertical video framing

**Context:** vertical video is commonly 9:16, designed for phone screens.

## Technique
- **Keep the face and the product central,** with margins left on both sides.
- Allow for **platform overlays:** captions, buttons and username areas cover parts of the top, bottom and right edge, so leave those areas for background rather than key action.
- **Distance:** a close or medium frame works best; keep movement within a narrow width and avoid walking out sideways.
- **Move toward and away from the camera** rather than left to right when you want to stay in frame.
- **Eyeline:** direct address often works; look at the lens, not the screen.
- **Hold steady:** handheld movement shows more in a tight vertical frame.
- Check **lighting and sound** on a phone before filming.
