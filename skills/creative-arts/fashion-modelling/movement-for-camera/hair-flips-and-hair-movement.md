# Hair flips and hair movement

**Goal:** a clean arc of hair with the face still readable.

## Technique
- **Agree with the hair stylist** how much the style can move and who resets it between takes.
- **Move from the neck and core,** not by whipping the head: a smooth turn and a short stop lifts hair into an arc.
- **Time it** with the photographer's burst; repeat the movement several times and note which timing gives the best frame.
- **Control the face:** keep the eyes and mouth relaxed; do not squeeze the eyes shut.
- **Use wind or a fan** instead of repeated hard flips if the set has one. See the wind-machine skill.
- Protect the **neck**: limit the number of flips, avoid extreme speed, and stop for pain or dizziness.
- Keep hair **away from the product** (lips, jewellery, collar) when the shot needs it.
