# Product interaction on video

**Goal:** a believable use of the product that the camera can read.

## Technique
- Ask the **hero side**, the action and the timing.
- **Rehearse the action** several times, then with the product; keep each pass identical.
- Hold with **fingertips and edges**, keeping the label or logo to camera.
- **Move slowly and smoothly;** video exaggerates sudden movements.
- Be careful with **liquids, food and cosmetics** (hygiene, spills and stains, safe sampling); tell the team about allergies.
- **Match continuity:** use the same hand and the same start position for each take.
- Keep **eyes and expression** with the product or the lens as directed.
- Treat the product as a **breakable or borrowed** item; ask before changing how it is used.
