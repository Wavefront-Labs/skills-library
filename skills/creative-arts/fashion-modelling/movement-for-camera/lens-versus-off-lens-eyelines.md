# Lens versus off lens eyelines

**Goal:** the audience feels the intended relationship (talking to them or watching a scene).

## Technique
- Ask at the start: **lens or off-lens**? Direct address looks into the lens; most scene work stays off-lens.
- **Off-lens:** look at a specific mark beside the camera, usually a scene partner, an assistant or a marker; keep the eyeline height consistent.
- **Direct address:** imagine talking to one person through the lens, and keep the eyes steady and warm.
- **Do not drift:** the camera notices eyes sliding; reset after a take if you looked away.
- **Between takes:** keep the same eyeline when the camera angle changes, unless the director says otherwise.
- **Blink naturally** between lines or actions.
- Ask the **camera operator** whether you are close to the lens or in a wider frame, which changes how precise the eyeline needs to be.
