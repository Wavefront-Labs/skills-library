# Spins and turns for stills

**Goal:** a peak moment where the fabric flares and the face stays composed.

## Technique
- Agree the **direction** and the **stop pose** with the photographer.
- **Spin on the ball of the foot**, with your weight over it, and use a fixed spot with your eyes, turning the head last (spotting) to limit dizziness.
- Choose the **speed** for the garment: lighter fabrics flare at moderate speed, heavy fabrics need more.
- **Stop cleanly** (or slow sharply) in the stop pose; many photographers shoot the turn and the stop.
- **Repeat in small sets** and rest between, since dizziness and fatigue build.
- Check **footwear and surface**: smooth soles on grippy floors can twist ankles; ask for a safer surface or shoes if needed.
- Warm up ankles, calves and hips first.

## Check
Garment peak captured, face still composed, and no stumbling on the stop.
