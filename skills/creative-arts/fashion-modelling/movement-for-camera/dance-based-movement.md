# Dance based movement

**Goal:** fluid, intentional movement that supports the clothes.

## Technique
- Ask for the **style and reference** and whether you are improvising or following set choreography.
- **Warm up** and learn the **movement phrase** slowly first, then at speed.
- **Keep it simple:** one or two repeatable moves (a turn, a reach, a step and sway) produce better frames than complex combinations.
- **Use rhythm:** ask for music on set if it helps, and count the beat.
- Check the **garment and shoes** for range of movement; tell the stylist if the clothes restrict you.
- **Control the end of each move,** which is where the photographer wants the peak.
- Mark the **space** and keep safe distances from props and crew; take breaks.
