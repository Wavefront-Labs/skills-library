# Interpreting a mood board

**Goal:** deliver the intended attitude without literally copying another image.

## Technique
- Read the board for **attitude** first (confident, languid, playful, severe), then **body shape** (open, angular, curved), then **styling cues** (hands, gaze, movement).
- Notice **what repeats across references**; that is the brief. Single outliers are probably colour or light references.
- Ask the art director or photographer: "What should I take from this one?" and "Is there anything here you do not want?"
- Rehearse two or three poses from the board and adapt them to your own body; copying a pose made by another body can look wrong on you.
- Note how the board shows the garment, and keep that visible.
- Aim for the attitude of a reference, not a copy of an identifiable person's look.

## Check
Before shooting, you can say in one sentence what the mood is and what the garment must show.
