# Catalogue posing

**Goal:** accurate, appealing product images for print and web, shot efficiently.

## Technique
- Use a **small set of clean positions** (front, three-quarter, back, side, detail action) and repeat them consistently across looks.
- **Show the garment first:** hem, waistband, sleeve length, pockets and closures visible; arms away from the torso.
- Keep a **friendly, natural face** (often a gentle smile) and ask what level the brand prefers.
- Move through positions quickly but cleanly; hold each for the shutter.
- Adjust the garment (hems, collars) between frames only when you or the stylist see a problem.
- Keep posture and weight consistent so one look matches the next in a page spread.

## Check
Compare images across looks: similar positions, garment fully visible, face consistently warm.
