# Editorial pairs and groups

**Goal:** a composition where the group works as one image.

## Technique
- Ask the **concept**: relationship, hierarchy, narrative.
- **Vary levels and angles:** avoid lining up like a team photo; use triangles or layers.
- **Control overlap:** keep limbs from crossing faces and avoid hiding other garments.
- **Create tension or connection** through eyelines and spacing: some figures look to camera, some away.
- **Watch the negative space** between figures and keep it intentional.
- **Adjust to each body:** heights and builds differ, so ask where to stand.
- **Contact:** ask before touching or being touched, and tell the director if uncomfortable.
- Repeat the same **start and end positions** for each burst.
