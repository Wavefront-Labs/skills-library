# Character work in editorials

**Goal:** a believable person who belongs in the story.

## Technique
- Ask the **story** (who, where, what just happened) and use the **styling** as a cue.
- Choose a **specific attitude** and a **physical anchor** (posture, gaze, a small gesture) and carry them through the sequence.
- **Commit** to the choice, then vary it in small steps; hesitant half-measures read as unfocused.
- Keep **eyes** engaged with a thought, person or point in the scene.
- Keep the **garment visible** even in a strongly characterised pose; ask whether the character is overriding the clothes.
- Stay **directable:** adjust when asked, and step out of character between takes.
- Set **limits:** you can decline anything unsafe or not agreed in advance.
