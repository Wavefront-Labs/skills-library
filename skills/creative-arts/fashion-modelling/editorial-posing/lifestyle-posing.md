# Lifestyle posing

**Goal:** frames that look unposed but are designed.

## Technique
- Work from a **simple action**: walking, laughing, adjusting a sleeve, drinking coffee, sitting on a step. Repeat it with small variations.
- Keep **eyes engaged with the activity or another person**, not the lens, unless directed.
- Keep faces and key garment details **open to camera** even in candid moments.
- **Hands and body:** clean, relaxed hands; no mid-blink frames; avoid props covering the face.
- Stay **in the moment** between takes; many of the best frames come just after the instruction.
- Tell the team when you need to reset hair or a garment so continuity stays right.

## Check
Frames look natural at a glance, the product is visible and the same moment is not repeated.
