# Hand and parts modelling

**Context:** parts models are booked for a specific body part such as hands, feet, legs, lips or hair.

## Technique
- **Protect the part:** moisturise, wear gloves in cold weather or when washing up, and avoid cuts, burns, scratches and bites. Tell your agent about any change.
- **Nails and cuticles:** neat and cared for. Ask whether polish or bare nails are required.
- **Move slowly and steadily.** Small tremors show on close-up shots. Rest the forearm or elbow on a surface when allowed.
- **Match the product action** (open, pour, apply, hold) and repeat it identically for several takes.
- Keep **skin tone consistent**; avoid heavy tan lines from watches or rings.
- Expect **tight crops** and precise direction on finger position.
- Parts work is still modelling: check usage terms, fees and whether you could be identified.
