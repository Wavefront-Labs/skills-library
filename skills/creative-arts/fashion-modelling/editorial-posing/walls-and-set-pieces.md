# Walls and set pieces

**Goal:** use the set to create shape, not to prop yourself up.

## Technique
- **Lean from the shoulder or hip,** not the whole back; leaning flat widens the body and wrinkles the garment.
- Put **one foot against the wall** or use a crossed stance to form a triangle.
- Align your body with **lines in the set** (vertical, diagonal) for a graphic composition.
- **Stairs and steps:** vary levels but keep feet secure; wear the right shoes and ask before standing on anything.
- **Doorways and frames:** a hand on the frame or a lean against it; keep elbows away from the torso.
- **Surfaces:** check for rough, dirty or wet areas that could mark the garment or injure you.
- Ask whether a set piece is **solid, fixed and approved** for weight before using it.
