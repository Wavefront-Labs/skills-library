# Posing vocabulary on set

**Goal:** react to direction immediately, without asking for it to be repeated.

## Core terms (usage varies by photographer, so confirm once)
- **Camera left / camera right:** from the camera's point of view, which is the **reverse** of your own left and right when you face the lens. "Your left" means your left.
- **Three-quarter:** body turned about 45 degrees from the camera; also a crop from head to about mid-thigh.
- **Chin down / up / out / forward:** small movements of a few degrees; "forward and down" defines the jaw.
- **Shoulder to camera / drop the shoulder:** angle or relax the shoulder.
- **Hips to me / away:** turn the pelvis relative to the lens.
- **Give me more / less:** scale the last change up or down; do not reset the pose.
- **Hold it / keep moving / reset:** freeze, continue the motion, or return to the start.
- **Soften / lift / open:** relax tension, add energy, or create negative space.

## Habit
Confirm ambiguous terms once at the start of the day, then apply one change per instruction.
