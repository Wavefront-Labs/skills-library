# Using tethered review

**Context:** in tethered shooting the camera feeds a monitor so the team can review images. Whether the model sees it is the photographer's decision.

## Technique
- **Ask politely** at natural breaks ("Could I see a few?"), not mid-sequence.
- Look for repeated issues: hidden or tense hands, a chin angle, a garment problem, closed eyes.
- Look for **what works** and repeat it; the best frames are a reference for the rest of the shoot.
- Keep review to a few frames. Long monitor time slows the day and can make you self-conscious.
- Do not delete, edit or photograph the screen. Images belong to the client and are often under embargo.
- If you see a garment, hair or make-up issue, tell the stylist or artist rather than the photographer, unless it is urgent.

## Check
You asked at an appropriate moment, took away one or two adjustments, and returned to shooting quickly.
