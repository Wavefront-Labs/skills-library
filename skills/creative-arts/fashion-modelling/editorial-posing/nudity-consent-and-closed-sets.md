# Nudity consent and closed sets

**Principles** (consistent with model-welfare guidance such as the Model Alliance RESPECT materials and the LVMH-Kering charter, which calls for advance notice of nudity)
- **No surprises:** any nudity, partial nudity, swim or lingerie work should be disclosed **before** the booking is confirmed, in writing, with the degree and the usage.
- **Consent is specific** to the described images. You can decline an addition on the day, and you can withdraw consent during the shoot.
- **Keep your agent informed** and ask them to resolve disputes.

## Closed set
- A **closed set** limits those present to people essential to the work (for example photographer, one assistant, stylist, hair and make-up as needed).
- **No phones or extra cameras** except as agreed; ask who has access to the images and how they are stored.
- **Modesty garments** (robe, nude-coloured underwear, tape) are provided between shots, and there is a private changing space (see the changing-areas skill).

## If it goes wrong
Stop, leave the set and call your agent. For serious misconduct, see the harassment-reporting skill, and consider legal advice and, where relevant, the police.
