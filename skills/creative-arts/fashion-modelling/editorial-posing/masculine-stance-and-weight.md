# Masculine stance and weight

**Goal:** relaxed strength with a clear line.

## Technique
- **Feet:** about shoulder width or slightly wider, one foot turned out slightly. Very wide stances can look aggressive.
- **Weight:** shift to one leg and soften the other knee; avoid locked knees.
- **Angle:** turn the body 30 to 45 degrees from camera and bring the face back toward the lens.
- **Shoulders and neck:** keep shoulders down and back; extend the neck and push the face slightly forward.
- **Arms:** keep a small gap from the torso; avoid hanging stiffly at the sides.
- **Movement:** shift a step or lean in small adjustments instead of freezing for each frame.
- Check how the **garment fits** through hips and chest during the pose.
