# Avoiding pose repetition

**Problem:** photographers get many near-identical frames and the edit suffers.

## Technique
- Know your **default positions**: the hand on the hip, the same head tilt, the same smile.
- Use a **variety checklist** per look: three weight positions, three hand positions, three head positions, and at least two body angles.
- Change a **big element** (stance, level, direction) every few minutes and small elements in between.
- Keep a mental library of **sequences** and begin each look from a different start position.
- If asked to repeat a pose that worked, go back to it exactly, then vary.
- Review a past set and count how many frames look the same.

## Check
Compare three consecutive looks: different stance, different hand shapes and different angles.
