# Skin prep for close up work

**Disclaimer:** general professional guidance, not medical advice. For skin conditions or reactions, see a dermatologist.

## Technique
- Keep a **simple, consistent routine** in the weeks before work: cleanse, moisturise and use sun protection, with products your skin tolerates.
- **Avoid new products, strong exfoliants, peels, waxing or facials in the days before** a close-up job. Reactions appear fast and are hard to retouch.
- **Hydrate and rest;** both show on skin.
- **Arrive with a clean, moisturised face** unless the brief says otherwise.
- Tell the make-up artist about **allergies, sensitivities and recent treatments**.
- Protect yourself from **sunburn** and avoid fake tan unless the client wants it.
- Report a breakout to your agent; do not squeeze or treat it on the day.
