# Advertising campaign posing

**Goal:** convey the idea behind the campaign and carry the brand image.

## Technique
- Understand the **campaign concept** and **brand attitude** before the shoot, from the brief and mood board.
- Deliver **hero frames** (strong, simple, product visible), then **variations** with different expressions and energy.
- Allow space for **copy and layout**: the art director may ask you to hold a position, face a direction or leave space on one side.
- Match the energy of other talent when shooting as a pair or ensemble.
- Keep the product and logo visible and unobstructed.
- Campaigns carry defined usage terms; see the usage-rights skills.

## Check
You can state the campaign idea in one line and your hero frames show it clearly.
