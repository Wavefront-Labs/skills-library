# Menswear expression

**Goal:** an intentional expression that matches the brief.

## Technique
- **Jaw:** unclench; a relaxed jaw with a slight forward push of the face defines the line without tension.
- **Brow:** small changes (slightly lowered or lifted) make a big difference; heavy scowls read as angry.
- **Eyes:** soft but engaged. Look through the lens, not at it; for off-lens frames, look at a specific point.
- **Mouth:** relaxed, lips together or slightly open. For smiles, start with the eyes.
- **Grooming:** keep facial hair consistent with the booking and your digitals.
- Vary expression in **small steps** between frames and reset after long holds.
- Check frames for tension in the **neck, lips and forehead**.
