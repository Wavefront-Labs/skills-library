# Lingerie posing

**Before the shoot**
- Get the **brief, usage and degree of exposure** in writing through your agent. Confirm a **closed set** and who attends (see the nudity and closed-set skill).
- Check **fit and security** (straps, closures, lace) with the stylist before shooting. Use **modesty garments or tape** as arranged.

## Posing
- **Shape and line:** body angles of about 30 to 45 degrees, weight shift and long lines through neck and torso.
- **Fabric and detail:** pose to show lace, straps and construction, not only the body.
- **Hands:** soft and purposeful, not covering the garment's key features.
- **Expression:** follow the brief; many brands prefer a calm, confident face.
- Keep **movement small** and breathe out; held breath shows in the stomach and shoulders.
- Ask for strap marks and lines to be fixed.

## Professional boundaries
You can pause or stop if the set no longer matches what was agreed, and you should report concerns to your agent. See the welfare skills.
