# Tailoring and jacket posing

**Goal:** structure that looks sharp and unwrinkled.

## Technique
- **Buttoning:** the stylist decides. Common convention is to fasten only the top button of a two-button jacket and unfasten when sitting.
- **Square the shoulders** and keep the back flat; rounded shoulders spoil the jacket line.
- **Pocket hands:** thumb out, or hand in the trouser pocket with the jacket pushed back; avoid bunching the jacket.
- **Jacket pull:** gently draw the hem down or aside between frames to remove creases.
- **Sleeves and cuffs:** let the shirt cuff show as styled; keep arms slightly away from the body to avoid wrinkle lines.
- **Collar and tie:** check position and collar gap between frames.
- **Sitting:** sit on the front of the chair and keep the jacket draped, not bunched under you.
