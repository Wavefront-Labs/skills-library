# High volume shoot pacing

**Context:** some e-commerce days involve a large number of looks. Numbers vary widely, so ask for the day's plan.

## Technique
- Know the **shot list and look count** before the first look.
- **Pre-stage** yourself: shoes on, hair and make-up ready, the next look's accessories identified.
- Change **quietly and quickly** with the dresser and stay out of the shoot's way.
- **Reset every look:** consistent posture, face, hair and positions.
- **Pace yourself:** keep energy even, and use breaks to eat, drink, stretch and rest your feet.
- Check in with the stylist when garment details change (belts, tucks, sleeves).
- Watch for **fatigue creep**: tired eyes, drooping posture or rushed turns often appear late in the day.

## Check
The last look is as clean and consistent as the first, and the day finishes on schedule.
