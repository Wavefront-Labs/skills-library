# Lens and distance awareness

**Why it matters:** the same pose looks different at different distances. Models who understand this can adjust before the photographer asks.

## Basics (general photographic optics)
- **Wide-angle lenses** used close exaggerate whatever is nearest the camera: a foot, hand or face pushed forward looks larger.
- **Longer lenses** (portrait lengths such as 85 to 135 mm on full frame) compress depth and are generally more flattering for faces; the camera is further away.
- A **low camera** lengthens the legs and makes a knee or hand toward the camera loom; a **high camera** foreshortens the legs and enlarges the head.

## What to do
- Ask roughly where the camera will be and how wide the shot is.
- With a wide lens, keep extended limbs from pointing straight at the camera and keep control of chin position.
- Keep a steady distance to the lens between frames; leaning in changes proportions.
- If a pose looks distorted on the tether, adjust the body angle before abandoning the pose.
