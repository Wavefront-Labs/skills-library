# Genuine commercial smile

**Goal:** a smile that looks natural across many frames and does not get stiff over a long day.

## Technique
- Start from a relaxed face; smiles that begin from tension look forced. Think of something actually pleasant or amusing.
- A believable smile involves the **eyes and cheeks**, not only the mouth: a slight lower-lid lift without squinting.
- Decide how much **teeth** show (closed, slight, full) and ask which the brief wants. Keep the top lip from riding too high.
- **Reset between bursts**: let the face go neutral, breathe, then rebuild. A held smile goes stiff and tires the cheeks.
- Keep the chin slightly forward and down, as in other posing, so the smile does not push a double chin.
- For laughs, ask the photographer to shoot through the start and end of a laugh; the in-between frames often work.

## Check
Compare frames: the smile reaches the eyes, the teeth line is even, no frame shows a held-over grimace.
