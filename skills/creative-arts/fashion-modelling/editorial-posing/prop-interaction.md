# Prop interaction

**Goal:** a prop that supports the story without distracting from the clothes.

## Technique
- Ask what the **prop's job** is (character, mood, product) and what must stay visible.
- Handle the prop with **fingertips and edges** and relaxed wrists; avoid gripping tightly.
- Decide **start and end positions** and repeat the action cleanly for the burst.
- Keep props from **covering the face, logo or closure**.
- Treat props as **breakable or borrowed**: ask before handling and check weight and sharp edges.
- With **food and drink**, ask whether it is real, and take care near the garment.
- Give **attention to the prop** as well as the lens for a natural feeling.
