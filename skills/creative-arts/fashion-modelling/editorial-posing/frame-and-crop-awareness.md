# Frame and crop awareness

**Goal:** frames that survive a tight crop, a vertical re-crop and a web banner.

## Technique
- Ask for the **intended crop** (full length, three-quarter, waist-up, head-and-shoulders) and for any space left for copy.
- Avoid having the frame edge **cut through joints** (ankles, knees, wrists, elbows, neck); photographers usually cut just above or below them.
- Keep hands and elbows **inside the frame** when waist-up; a hand dropping out of frame looks like a missing limb.
- Hold key actions (hem lift, bag reveal) inside the safe area, not at the edge.
- For campaigns, shoot a few frames with more space left or right for layout, as the art director asks.
- If the shot will be re-cropped vertical for social, keep the face and product central.

## Check
Look at the tether and imagine the vertical crop. If the face or garment detail falls off, adjust position.
