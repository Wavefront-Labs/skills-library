# Couples and group commercial posing

**Goal:** a believable relationship that still shows every product.

## Technique
- Create **connection** with simple contact: an arm around a shoulder, held hands, leaning, or looking at each other, as the brief and your comfort allow.
- Use **levels**: avoid all heads at one height; sit or lean to create a triangle composition.
- Keep **overlaps clean:** no limbs across another person's face and no garment hidden by an arm or bag.
- Turn everyone slightly **toward the centre** of the group.
- Keep expressions **consistent but not identical**.
- With **children**, follow the guardian, chaperone and agency rules; see the underage-models skills.
- Ask before physical contact in any pairing, and raise discomfort with the director or your agent.

## Check
Every face visible, every garment readable, contact natural and comfortable.
