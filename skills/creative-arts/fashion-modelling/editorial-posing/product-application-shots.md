# Product application shots

**Goal:** a clean, believable application that can be repeated for stills or film.

## Technique
- Agree the **action**: what is applied, where, in which direction and at what speed.
- Rehearse **without product first**, then with it.
- **Hygiene:** use fresh or sanitised applicators and never share used products. Ask the make-up artist to prepare them.
- Keep the **product visible** and the label or shade turned to camera.
- Use **small controlled motions**; a smooth, slow stroke reads better than a fast one.
- Keep **fingers clean and still**; have wipes ready.
- **Repeat the same motion across takes** for continuity, and note the start and end positions.
