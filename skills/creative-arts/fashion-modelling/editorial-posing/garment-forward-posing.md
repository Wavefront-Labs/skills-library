# Garment forward posing

**Goal:** a shopper can understand the fit, length, shape and detail.

## Technique
- **Open the body** so sleeves, waist and hem are separated from the torso by negative space.
- **Reveal key details:** a hand in a pocket, a cuff showing, a back detail, a hem lift.
- **Keep arms and bags away** from closures, graphics and trims.
- **Show the fit:** stand naturally enough that fit problems are visible, not hidden; tell the stylist if the garment is not sitting well.
- **Show the fabric:** a slight step or twist shows drape in lightweight fabrics.
- Keep shoes and accessories in the shot only as the brief requires.
- Watch the **length line**: if a hem sits differently on your frame, say so.
