# Neutral editorial face

**Goal:** a face that lets the clothes and mood lead while still looking alive.

## Technique
- **Relax the mouth** (lips together or very slightly parted), unclench the jaw and drop the tongue from the roof of the mouth.
- Keep the **eyes engaged**: put a thought behind the lens rather than staring blankly. A changed thought alters the eyes more than widening them does.
- **Breathe out** just before the shutter; held breath tightens the face and neck.
- Vary in small steps between frames: chin a few degrees up or down, eyes at the lens, then slightly off it.
- Neutral is not sad or angry; check the frames for an unintentional frown between the brows.

## Practice
Phone camera, 20 frames, same pose, change only the thought behind the eyes (calm, curious, bored, confident). Compare which frames look intentional.
