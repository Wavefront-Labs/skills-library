# Holding products in stills

**Goal:** the product is the hero and the hand looks natural, not gripping.

## Technique
- Ask first which side is the hero (label, logo, texture) and which direction the photographer wants it to face.
- Hold the product with **fingertips or the pad of the fingers**, using the edges, so the front stays clear. Avoid wrapping the whole hand over it.
- Tilt the product slightly toward the camera; a perfectly vertical bottle or a flat-on box can catch glare.
- Keep your **wrist soft** and your forearm aligned with the product so the line of the arm leads to it.
- Check reflections: glossy packaging shows the room and your outfit. Small angle changes remove glare.
- Keep nails and cuticles clean; the product is often photographed close.

## Common faults
Death grip, label turned inward, thumb across the logo, arm blocking the garment.
