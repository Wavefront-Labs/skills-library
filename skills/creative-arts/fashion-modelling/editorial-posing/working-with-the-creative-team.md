# Working with the creative team

**Goal:** a calm set where you add to the day instead of slowing it.

## Roles in brief (they vary by production)
- **Photographer:** frame and light. Take pose direction mainly from them or the art director; if two people direct, ask who leads.
- **Art director / creative director:** the concept. They may speak through the photographer.
- **Stylist and assistants:** garments and fit. Raise comfort or fit issues with them, rather than pulling at the clothes yourself.
- **Hair and make-up:** keep the look intact; tell them if something is uncomfortable and avoid touching your face.
- **Digital tech and assistants:** manage the tether and lights; ask them when you want to review frames.

## Habits
- Learn names, say thanks, and keep phone use minimal.
- Give feedback briefly and positively ("Could I try it with the weight on my other leg?").
- Raise safety, nudity or comfort concerns early, and with your agent if needed. See the welfare skills.
