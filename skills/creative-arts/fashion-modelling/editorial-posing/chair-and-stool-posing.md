# Chair and stool posing

**Goal:** a seated pose with a clear line and a visible garment.

## Technique
- **Sit on the front of the seat** and lengthen the spine; sitting back collapses the torso.
- **Angle knees and hips** to one side of the camera, with shoulders turning back slightly for a diagonal line.
- **Legs:** one foot slightly forward, ankles crossed or one knee over the other; point toes lightly. Angle the thigh nearest the camera away so it does not flatten out.
- **Arms:** one hand on a knee or the chair back, the other resting or touching a collar. Keep gaps between arm and torso.
- **Garments:** smooth skirts and trousers before each frame and check hems and jacket fronts. Open a jacket if it bunches.
- **Stools and benches:** a perch with one foot on the floor gives more shape than a stiff, centred sit.
- Move **between positions** continuously for variety.
