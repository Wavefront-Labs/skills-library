# Swimwear posing

**Before the shoot**
- Confirm the **brief, usage and level of coverage** in writing through your agent, and ask for any changes in advance.
- Agree a **closed or limited set** (see the nudity and closed-set skill) and who will be present.
- Check the **fit** and tell the stylist if the swimwear gapes, rides or is not secure; fix it before shooting.

## Posing
- Use **angles and weight shift** as in other posing; avoid standing square to the lens.
- **Open arms away from the torso** to define the waist and garment, and keep hands relaxed.
- Keep **shoulders and neck long** and the stomach unstrained; breathe out.
- Move through **small changes** to avoid stiffness, and watch how light falls on water, sand or skin.
- On **location**, protect yourself from sun, cold and slippery surfaces, and take breaks to warm up and rehydrate.

## Your boundaries
You can stop if you are uncomfortable. Contact your agent about anything that departs from what was agreed.
