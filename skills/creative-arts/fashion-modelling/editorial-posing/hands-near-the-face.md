# Hands near the face

**When it comes up:** beauty-leaning editorials, jewellery and eyewear, and any frame where a hand touching hair, neck or cheek adds shape.

## Technique
- Use the **edge or side** of the hand and fingers, with small gaps between fingers; a flat palm to camera reads large next to the face.
- **Touch lightly.** Fingertips resting on the cheek, jaw or hairline look relaxed; pressing visibly pushes skin and can create a double chin or squashed cheek.
- Either make **true contact or keep a clear gap**. A hand hovering a few centimetres from the face looks accidental.
- Keep the wrist straight or gently curved; a sharply bent wrist looks broken.
- Do not cover the eyes, mouth or the product (earrings, glasses, lipstick) unless directed.
- Elbow position drives the shape: lifting the elbow slightly away from the body creates negative space.

## Check
Review a few tethered frames: eyes visible, no knuckles dominating, fingers not splayed or clawed.
