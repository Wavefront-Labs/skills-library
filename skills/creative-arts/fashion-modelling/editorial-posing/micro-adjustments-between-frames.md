# Micro adjustments between frames

**Goal:** many distinct, usable frames from one base pose.

## Technique
- Pick a **base pose** that works, then change only one element per frame: head tilt, chin, eye direction, shoulder, hand or weight.
- Use **small steps** (a few degrees or a finger's width). Large jumps produce unrelated frames and hide the one that worked.
- Move in a **loop**: change, hold a beat for the shutter, change again. Do not move during the click.
- After a strong frame is called, return to it and vary around it rather than starting again.
- Keep the garment line intact when you adjust: check that a hem, collar or strap still sits correctly.

## Practice
Phone on a tripod, twenty frames from one stance with one change each. Review which changes made a visible difference and which were wasted.
