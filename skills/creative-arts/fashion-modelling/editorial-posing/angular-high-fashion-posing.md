# Angular high fashion posing

**Goal:** striking, graphic shapes that look deliberate.

## Technique
- Think in **lines and angles** (elbows, wrists, knees, a sharp chin line) rather than smooth curves.
- **Break the body into planes:** shoulders in one direction, hips in another, head in a third, with the neck extended.
- **Be deliberate:** angular poses look wrong if loose or timid; hold the shape and keep breathing.
- **Joints:** avoid hyperextending elbows and knees and sharply bending the wrist or neck; listen to your body and ask to adjust.
- **Control the hands:** angular but not clawed.
- **Garment line:** keep seams, hems and structure working with the pose.
- **Warm up** before extreme shapes and stop if anything hurts.
