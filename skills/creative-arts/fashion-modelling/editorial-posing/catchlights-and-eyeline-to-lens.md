# Catchlights and eyeline to lens

**Definition:** a catchlight is the small reflection of a light source in the eyes. Eyes without one tend to look flat.

## Technique
- Know where the main light is. Turning your face a little **toward the key** usually puts a catchlight in the upper part of the iris; turning away often loses it.
- Raising the chin slightly can bring a light from above into the eyes; dropping it can bury them in brow shadow.
- **Eyeline to lens** gives direct engagement; **just off the lens** (toward a spot beside the camera) gives a more candid or editorial feel. Ask which the brief wants.
- Keep the eyes **soft but alive**; blink between bursts, not during them.
- If a reflector or window sits near the camera, find the frame where the light reads best on the tether and repeat that head angle.

## Check
Zoom the tether image on the eyes: catchlight present, not blown out, eyes not half-closed.
