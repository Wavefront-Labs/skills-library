# Menswear hands and accessories

**Goal:** hands that look easy and show product details.

## Technique
- **Pockets:** thumb out, or hand in with the jacket pushed back; vary hand and depth.
- **Cuffs and watches:** raise the wrist or adjust the cuff so the watch or cufflink shows; keep the wrist straight.
- **Tie and collar:** a light touch at the knot or collar tip, not a tug.
- **Hats and glasses:** touch the edge or brim with fingertips; do not cover the face.
- **Bags and straps:** hold lightly and show the strap or hardware; avoid clenching.
- **Rings and bracelets:** confirm with the stylist that jewellery is part of the look.
- Keep **hands clean and relaxed**, with natural finger spacing.
