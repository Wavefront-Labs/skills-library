# Posing sculptural and couture garments

**Goal:** let the garment lead and avoid damage.

## Technique
- Ask the **stylist or house representative** for handling rules before you move: how to sit, what not to touch and the garment's limits.
- **Learn the structure:** where the garment moves with you and where it does not, and move within its limits.
- **Use small movements** and slow changes; large movements can strain stitching or beadwork.
- Keep **hands, jewellery, hair and make-up** away from fabric that marks or snags. Use gloves if asked.
- **Show the silhouette:** angles and negative space that follow the garment's line.
- **Take breaks** when possible; heavy garments, corsetry and extreme shoes limit breathing and circulation. Say if you feel faint or in pain.
- Keep **food and drink** away from the garment.
