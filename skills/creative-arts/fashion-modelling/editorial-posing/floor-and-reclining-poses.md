# Floor and reclining poses

**Goal:** a relaxed shape that still looks designed.

## Technique
- Ask the **camera position**: overhead, from the side, or low at floor level; it changes the pose.
- **Support yourself** with an elbow, forearm or hand to lift the upper body and avoid a flattened torso.
- **Extend and layer the legs** and point toes lightly; avoid a knee pointing straight at the lens.
- **Keep the face visible:** chin slightly forward, neck long, hair arranged by the stylist.
- **Protect the garment:** ask the stylist about creasing and keep fabric away from dirt and make-up. Sit up between frames if asked.
- Use **mats or a clean surface** and check floors for splinters, cold or dust.
- Take breaks; staying in one position causes numbness and cramping.
