# Lip and eye macro shots

**Context:** in macro shots a small movement or flaw fills the frame.

## Technique
- Ask the **crop and distance**; macro shots are close, with shallow depth of field.
- **Eyes:** blink as needed between frames; use eye drops only if agreed with the make-up artist and safe for you. Keep eyes relaxed rather than wide.
- **Lips:** keep them hydrated beforehand, and avoid biting or licking them between frames.
- **Stillness:** exhale slowly and hold a short pause for the shot. Rest the head against a support if the photographer allows.
- **Expression:** soft and neutral, with small muscle changes only; big jaw or brow movements shift the whole frame.
- Keep hands away from the face between shots; the make-up artist will retouch.
- Say if lenses, lashes or products cause irritation; stop and tell the make-up artist.
