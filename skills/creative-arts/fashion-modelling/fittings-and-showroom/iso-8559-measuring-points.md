# Iso 8559 measuring points

**Reference:** ISO 8559-1:2017 gives anthropometric definitions for body measurement used in size designation for clothes. Check the standard for exact definitions; brands and agencies may use their own variations.

## Common points (descriptive; confirm against the standard and the brand's chart)
- **Height:** standing, without shoes, back straight.
- **Bust or chest girth:** horizontal tape around the fullest part of the chest, under the arms.
- **Waist girth:** at the natural waistline, the narrowest part of the torso, when relaxed.
- **Hip girth:** around the fullest part of the buttocks, horizontal.
- **Inside leg (inseam):** from the crotch to the floor or ankle, as specified.
- **Neck, shoulder width, sleeve length** and others for tailoring and fit work.

## Technique
- Keep the **tape snug, level and parallel to the floor,** not tight.
- Stand **naturally, breathing normally.**
- Measure over **thin underwear** or the agreed base layer.
- Record **units** (cm or inches) and the method.
- See the self-measurement and measuring-conditions skills.
