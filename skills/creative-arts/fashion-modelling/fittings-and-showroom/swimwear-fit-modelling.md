# Swimwear fit modelling

**Context:** swim fit is intimate work. Confirm in writing, through your agent, who attends, whether photos or video are taken, how images are stored and the usage. See closed-set and consent guidance.

## Tests
- **Dry fit:** coverage, gaping, riding, strap position, back and closure.
- **Movement:** squat, reach, bend, walk, twist and jump, checking coverage and security.
- **Wet test** where the brand offers it: fabric stretch and recovery, transparency, sagging, fit when wet, chlorine or sea water effects.
- **Straps and ties:** slippage, comfort and security.
- **Pads and linings:** shifting, bunching, drying time.
- **Seams:** rubbing or digging.

## Professional practice
- **Private changing and a robe** provided; you can pause or stop.
- **Report discomfort** without pressure, and contact your agent for concerns.
