# Showroom quick changes

**Context:** showroom garments are often single samples that must look right for every buyer appointment.

## Technique
- **Pre-stage** the next look on a rail in order, with shoes and accessories at hand. Get the running order from the sales team.
- **Handle samples with care:** clean hands, no deodorant or make-up on fabric, and use a cover over the head for tight necklines (a scarf or hood avoids marks).
- **Change in a private space** and tell the sales team when ready; use the dresser or assistant for closures.
- **Check the look** (tags tucked, collar flat, hems and creases) before stepping out.
- **Keep pace:** be quick but never rush a delicate sample.
- **Report damage immediately,** such as a pulled thread, stain or lost button, so it can be fixed before the next appointment.
- **Keep to the order** as buyers may ask for specific styles again.
