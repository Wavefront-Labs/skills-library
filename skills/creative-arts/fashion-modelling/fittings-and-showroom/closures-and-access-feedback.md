# Closures and access feedback

**Goal:** a garment people can actually put on and wear.

## Check each closure
- **Can you fasten it yourself,** and how long does it take? Is a dresser needed?
- **Does it open under movement:** buttons gaping, a zip drifting down, a hook pulling.
- **Position and comfort:** closure edges digging, a zip rubbing the skin, buttons that press when sitting.
- **Material and quality:** snags, sticking zips, sharp hooks, noisy fastenings.
- **Access:** can you use a toilet, take it on and off, and reach hidden pockets?
- **Safety:** drawstrings or loose ties that could catch, and fastenings that stop you moving freely.

## Reporting
Say **which closure, what you did and what happened**: "when I raise my arms the back zip pulls open about two centimetres at the waist." Offer no design instruction unless asked.
