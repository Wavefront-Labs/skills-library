# Working a trade show booth

**Context:** trade-show or exhibition models represent a brand at a booth, usually for several long days.

## Before
- **Confirm the booking terms in writing** (hours, breaks, rate, dress code, duties, any filming).
- **Get the brief:** brand message, product list, what visitors may ask, and what you must not say.

## At the booth
- **Dress and groom** as briefed; comfortable shoes matter.
- **Stand and move with purpose,** avoiding leaning, phone use or crossing arms.
- **Greet and engage:** a brief welcome, a question, then hand over to the sales staff for technical or price discussion.
- **Demonstrate** only what you have been trained for.
- **Hydrate, rest and eat** on your breaks.
- **Handle leads** as the brand requests (cards, scans, forms).
- **Escalate** any uncomfortable or inappropriate behaviour to your supervisor or agent. See the promotional-conduct skill.
