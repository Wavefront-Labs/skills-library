# Bra and lingerie fit modelling

**Context:** lingerie fit is intimate work. Agree conditions, privacy and photography rules in advance, and follow the model-welfare guidance. Professional guidance, not legal advice.

## Before the session
- **Confirm in writing** (through your agent) the brief, who attends, whether photos are taken, how images are stored and the usage. See closed-set and consent guidance.
- **Private changing space** and robe provided.

## Feedback points
- **Band:** firm but comfortable, level around the torso, not riding up; breath test.
- **Cups:** coverage, gaping, spillage, wrinkling or space; whether the shape stays the same when you move.
- **Centre gore:** sits flat against the sternum, or lifts away.
- **Straps:** slipping, digging, adjustments.
- **Wires and seams:** pressure points or rubbing.
- **Back and closure:** hook position and comfort.

## Movement tests
Raise arms, reach forward, bend, sit and walk; report rubbing, shifting, or gaps at each stage.
