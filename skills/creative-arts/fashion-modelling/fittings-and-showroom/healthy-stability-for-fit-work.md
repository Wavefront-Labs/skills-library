# Healthy stability for fit work

**Principle:** a fit model's body needs to stay within a range. That requirement should never come at the expense of health, and a body that is healthy and well will usually be more stable than one that is under pressure.

## Practice
- **Keep a regular routine:** sleep, steady meals, hydration and moderate exercise, rather than rapid changes.
- **Avoid crash diets, extreme training, and quick weight-cutting;** they create swings in measurements and harm health.
- **Speak to a doctor, dietitian or physiotherapist** if you need to change your body or your routine, or if you notice symptoms. See the eating-disorder awareness skill.
- **Log measurements** regularly (see the measurement-log skill).
- **Tell the brand and your agent** if your body is changing for a good reason (illness, pregnancy, injury or medication). They can then re-measure or adjust.
- **Say no** to pressure to alter your body to match a spec. Welfare guidance such as the BFC and CFDA health materials, and charters like LVMH-Kering, set a health-first approach.
