# Promotional conduct and boundaries

**Principle:** promotional work is public-facing and can involve unwanted attention. You are entitled to a safe working environment, and the job is what was booked.

## Practice
- **Agree scope and dress in writing** beforehand; refuse additions that were not agreed.
- **Know your supervisor and the agency contact** and have the venue or event address.
- **Alcohol and gifts:** follow the brand rules and your own limits; you do not have to drink or accept invitations.
- **Contact and conversation:** stay friendly and professional, and refer personal questions or invitations on politely.
- **Photographs:** ask the brand about photography rules and decline personal photo requests if not comfortable.
- **Harassment or unsafe behaviour:** move away, tell your supervisor or agent immediately, and record details. See the harassment-reporting skill.
- **Travel safely** to and from events; arrange transport where possible.
- **Leave** if you feel unsafe and tell your agent.
