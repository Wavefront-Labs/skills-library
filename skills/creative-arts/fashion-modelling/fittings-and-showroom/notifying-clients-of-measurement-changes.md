# Notifying clients of measurement changes

**Why:** the brand builds patterns on your measurements. Silent drift means wrong fits and wasted samples.

## When to notify
- **A change beyond the brand's tolerance** (ask them for it) or beyond your normal range.
- **Planned changes:** a haircut or colour, surgery, pregnancy, injury, medication or significant training.
- **Before a fit** if you suspect the numbers have changed.

## How
- **Tell your agent first;** they handle the client conversation.
- **Give new measurements** with the date, method and conditions, and say what caused the change if you are comfortable doing so; you are not obliged to give medical details.
- **Offer a re-measure** at the next session.
- **Keep it factual:** "my waist is now 2 cm bigger than the sheet".
- **Ask what the brand needs** (stay as before, move up a size, pause bookings).
- **If your health is involved,** seek medical advice. See model-health skills.
