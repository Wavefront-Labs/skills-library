# Line sheets and style numbers

**Definition:** a line sheet is a document listing a collection's styles for buyers, usually with images, style numbers, colours, sizes, wholesale and retail prices and delivery windows.

## What to learn
- **Style number:** the brand's code for each design, used in orders; sometimes with a suffix for colour or size.
- **Name and description:** what the garment is called and its key features.
- **Colourways and fabrics:** the available colours and the main fabric.
- **Delivery and drop:** when the style is expected to ship.
- **Hero pieces and bestsellers** which the sales team will push.

## Habits
- **Get a copy before the appointment** if the brand allows, and mark the order in which you will wear looks.
- **Use the correct style number** when buyers ask; do not guess prices or availability, and refer them to the sales team.
- **Keep confidential information** (wholesale prices, unreleased styles) to the showroom.
