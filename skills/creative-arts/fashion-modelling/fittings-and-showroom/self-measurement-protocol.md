# Self measurement protocol

**Goal:** numbers that agree with what the agency, client or brand measure.

## Setup
- A **flexible tape** (cloth or fibreglass), a **mirror,** and ideally a **helper** for back and shoulder measurements.
- **Thin base layer** or the agreed foundation garments.
- **Same time of day** and conditions each time.

## Method
- **Stand tall, relaxed, feet together, and breathe normally.**
- Keep the tape **level and snug,** not squeezing. Use a mirror to check it is horizontal.
- **Measure each point twice** and note both if they differ; use the average or the more consistent one.
- **Landmarks:** see the ISO measuring-points skill (bust or chest, waist, hips, height and inside leg).
- **Record immediately:** date, time, units and any notes (late meal, after exercise, before a period or any other known factor).

## When to ask for help
- Before sending numbers to an agency or brand for the first time, ask for a **professional measure** if possible, and compare.
