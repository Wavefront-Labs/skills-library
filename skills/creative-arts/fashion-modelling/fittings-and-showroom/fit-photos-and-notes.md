# Fit photos and notes

**Goal:** a clear visual and written record that the next fitting can build on.

## Fit photos
- Typical views are **front, side, back** and **detail** shots of areas under discussion, taken in a **natural stance** (not a pose).
- Keep **posture, shoes and foundation garments** consistent with previous sessions.
- Stand against a **plain background** if asked and keep arms still.
- Do not take your own photos unless the brand says you may. Fit photos usually show **unreleased designs**.

## Notes
- If you keep your own notes, record **date, style name or number, size, and the main comments** in your own words.
- Write down **what changed** between rounds.
- Keep notes **private** and avoid sharing them online; check your NDA.
- Keep a **measurement log** (see the measurement-log skill) alongside.

## Check
You can say which style, size and round each note refers to.
