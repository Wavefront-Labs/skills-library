# Technical outerwear and activewear fit

**Goal:** a garment that performs in use, not just on a stand.

## Technique
- **Wear the specified layers** (base layer, mid layer, backpack) and shoes; layering changes fit.
- **Dynamic tests:** squat, lunge, reach overhead, rotate the torso, climb a step, run in place, bend and simulate the activity (cycling posture, climbing reach, pack on).
- **Feedback points:** seam positions that rub or press, sleeve and hem riding up, restriction at shoulders, knees and hips, hood fit and vision, pocket access, closures with gloves.
- **Thermal and moisture:** report hot spots, ventilation, wet feel and condensation.
- **Report after sustained wear:** chafing and pressure appear after time, so ask for longer tests where feasible.
- **Safety:** garments with reflective, high-visibility or protective functions need correct fit; report any gaps.
