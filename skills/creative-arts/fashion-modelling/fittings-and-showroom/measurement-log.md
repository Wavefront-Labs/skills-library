# Measurement log

**Goal:** a record that shows what is normal for you and when something has genuinely changed.

## What to record
- **Date, time and conditions** (before eating, time of day, after exercise, relevant health factors).
- **Core measurements:** height, bust or chest, waist, hip, inside leg, shoe size, and any additions the agency asks for (shoulder, neck, sleeve, dress size).
- **Units and method** (see the self-measurement skill).
- **Photos or digitals dates** that match the measurements.
- **Notes:** haircuts, colour, surgery, tattoos or other changes that affect the card.

## How to use it
- **Measure on a regular schedule** (monthly is a common habit; fit models may do it more often, as the brand asks).
- **Look for trend rather than single points;** normal daily variation exists.
- **Tell your agent** when a change exceeds the tolerance for your work. See the measurement-change-notice skill.
- **Keep it private** and secure.
