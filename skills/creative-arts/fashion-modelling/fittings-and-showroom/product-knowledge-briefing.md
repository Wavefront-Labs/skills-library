# Product knowledge briefing

**Goal:** speak accurately about the product and avoid making unsupported claims.

## Technique
- **Take notes** on product name, features, prices if shared, availability and key messages.
- **Learn the claims you may make** and the ones you must not make (for example health, safety or performance claims the brand cannot support).
- **Practise the demo** and the short pitch aloud.
- **Anticipate questions:** where it is sold, how it differs, how to use it, and what to say when you do not know.
- **Refer on:** pass technical, pricing or contract questions to the brand's staff.
- **Ask for written material** (fact sheet, FAQ) and a contact for questions during the event.
- **Do not guess:** "I'm not sure, let me find someone who can answer that" is acceptable.
