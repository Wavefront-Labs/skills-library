# Fabric hand and stretch feedback

**Goal:** tell the team how the cloth behaves on a body, which the sample on a hanger cannot show.

## Vocabulary
- **Hand:** how the fabric feels (soft, crisp, scratchy, slippery, stiff).
- **Weight and drape:** light, heavy, fluid, stiff.
- **Stretch and recovery:** how far it stretches and whether it snaps back; "bags at the knees after sitting".
- **Cling or static** and transparency.
- **Itch or irritation** at the neck, seams or labels.
- **Warmth:** hot or cold when worn.

## Technique
- State **where and when**: "the knit stretches across the hips when I sit and doesn't recover".
- Test **movement** (sit, reach, walk, bend) and report changes.
- Report **seams and labels** that rub.
- Describe the effect, not the fix; the team decides whether to change the fabric, lining or construction.
