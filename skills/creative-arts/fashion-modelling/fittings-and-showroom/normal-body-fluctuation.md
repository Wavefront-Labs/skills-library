# Normal body fluctuation

**Principle:** body measurements vary through the day and month. Knowing normal variation stops you reading noise as change and helps you measure under repeatable conditions.

## Common sources of variation
- **Time of day:** the body often measures slightly larger in the evening than in the morning, especially at the waist and legs.
- **Meals, fluids and salt:** bloating can change girth, especially at the waist.
- **Menstrual cycle:** for many people it affects water retention and measurements; keep a note.
- **Exercise:** temporary swelling in muscles, and also fluid shifts.
- **Travel and sitting:** long journeys can cause swelling in legs and feet.
- **Posture and breath:** standing tall and relaxed gives a repeatable measure.
- **Clothes and tape position** also change numbers.

## What to do
- **Measure at the same time and in the same conditions** and keep a log (see measurement-log).
- **Report the pattern** to the agent or brand if they need numbers for a particular day.
- **See a doctor** for sudden or persistent unexplained changes.
