# Spec tolerances

**Definition:** a tolerance is the allowed difference between a target measurement and the measured value. Garment makers set tolerances for each point of measure on a garment spec, and brands expect a fit model to stay close to the body spec.

## What to know
- **Different points have different tolerances:** typically tighter for key girths and lengths and looser for others. Ask the brand for **its numbers** rather than assuming.
- **A fit model's body spec is the reference** for the base size; if your measurements drift outside what the brand accepts, the patterns and grading built from you are less accurate.
- **Measure under the same conditions** each time so differences reflect real change (see measuring-conditions).
- **Tell the brand early** if you approach the limit (see measurement-change-notice).
- **Do not diet or train** to hit numbers in unhealthy ways; if the brand's needs conflict with your health, say so (see model-health skills).
