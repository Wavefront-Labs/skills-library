# Plus petite and tall fit models

**Context:** many brands build separate blocks for extended size ranges because proportions differ and a simple grade of a sample size does not capture them.

## What to know
- **Separate fit model per range:** brands often use a dedicated plus, petite or tall fit model, because proportion (height, bust to waist, rise, inseam) matters as well as size.
- **Proportion is the product:** brands look for a body that matches the target customer's proportions, not only a size number.
- **Consistency:** same rules as other fit models (measuring-conditions, undergarments, shoes, posture).
- **Feedback:** report fit in terms of the proportion: "the rise sits short on me", "the sleeve hits at the wrist bone".
- **Comfort and ease:** ask for movement tests and seat testing. Extended ranges often need extra attention on comfort and stability.
- **Respect and professionalism:** work with teams who treat you as an expert; raise concerns about comments on your body and see the welfare skills.
