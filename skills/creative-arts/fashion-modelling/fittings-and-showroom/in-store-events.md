# In store events

**Context:** brands use models for appearances, launches and styling events in retail stores.

## Before
- **Confirm in writing:** time, duration, location, duties, rate, breaks, and whether images or video will be taken.
- **Get the brief:** products, offers, key messages, and the contact on site.

## On the day
- **Arrive early,** meet the store lead and walk the floor.
- **Engage** customers with a welcome, a question and a quick styling or product comment; keep to the facts you were briefed.
- **Handle products carefully** and keep demonstration items clean.
- **Photography:** follow the brand's guidance on selfies and social posts; many events want tags or hashtags.
- **Keep boundaries:** see the promotional-conduct skill; tell the store lead about unwelcome behaviour.
- **Take breaks** and stay hydrated; long standing is tiring.
- **Close out:** confirm hours worked and paperwork before leaving.
