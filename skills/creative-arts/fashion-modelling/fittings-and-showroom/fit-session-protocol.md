# Fit session protocol

**Context:** every brand differs; this is a typical flow for a fit model working with a technical designer and pattern maker.

## Typical order
1. **Arrive** at the agreed time, in the agreed foundation garments and shoes, with hair and body ready (see measuring-conditions and consistency skills).
2. **Measure:** the team may re-measure key points and record them.
3. **Dress in the sample** (often in the base or sample size) and stand naturally.
4. **Look-and-comment:** the team observes first; then you describe how it feels (see garment-feedback skills).
5. **Pin, mark and adjust:** stay still, then repeat the movement tests.
6. **Photograph and record:** front, side, back and detail photos, and written notes.
7. **Second round** for adjusted samples or other styles.
8. **Close:** confirm what changed and what is next; do not take samples away.

## Habits
- Ask the sequence of garments and the question each one answers.
- Stay quiet and still while they work, and speak up when something is wrong.
- Keep everything confidential.
