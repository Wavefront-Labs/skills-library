# Finding a legitimate agency

## Research
- **Look for established agencies** with a visible roster, a track record and a physical address. Check whether they belong to a recognised body where one exists (for example the Association of Model Agents in the UK) and whether the models listed are working.
- **Read the agency's own submission page;** many accept online applications and say what they want.
- **Ask other models** and check reviews with care, since scam sites also post fake praise.

## Submitting
- **Send what they ask for:** typically recent natural digitals, basic stats, age and contact details, and any reference. Follow their instructions exactly.
- **Do not pay to apply.** A legitimate agency earns commission from your work; see the scam-red-flags and model-law skills.
- **Parents or guardians** should be involved when a model is under 18.

## Before you sign
Read the contract, ask questions and consider independent advice. See model-contract skills.
