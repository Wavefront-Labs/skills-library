# Model apartments and accommodation

**Context:** agencies often arrange shared housing for visiting models and may deduct the cost from earnings. Conditions vary widely.

## What to ask before you accept
- **Cost:** how much, what is included, and how it is charged (an advance recouped from earnings, or paid up front). Get it in writing.
- **Contract:** who the landlord is, what the notice and exit terms are, and whether you can leave early.
- **Location and transport:** distance to the agency and safety at night.
- **Who you share with:** numbers, genders, ages, and whether minors live with adults.
- **House rules and supervision:** curfews, visitors, and who has access to keys.
- **Safety:** locks, fire safety, emergency contacts.
- **Privacy and belongings:** secure storage for documents and valuables.

## Practice
- **Keep records and receipts.**
- **Compare** costs with local prices, and ask your agent to justify any large charge.
- **If conditions are unsafe or unfair,** raise it with the agency and, if needed, seek independent help. Minors should have additional safeguards.
