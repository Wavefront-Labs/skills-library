# Placement agencies

**Definition:** a placement agency represents a model in a particular city or market on behalf of the model's mother agency. The mother agency manages the relationship and the placement agency finds and manages local work.

## How it commonly works
- **Mother agency** scouts or develops the model and negotiates placements.
- **Placement agency** books jobs locally and may handle housing, visas and local paperwork.
- **Money:** commissions and fees may be taken at each level, and earnings may first go against advances (travel, housing, test shoots). Terms vary, so check them.
- **Contracts:** the model is usually bound to the mother agency, and placement agreements are between the agencies. Ask who is the contracting party and who holds your payments.

## Questions to ask
- What commissions and fees apply at each level, and what costs are advanced?
- Who invoices clients, and when do I get statements and payment?
- Who can I call with problems, and what happens if I want to end the placement?
- What visa and insurance arrangements are made?
