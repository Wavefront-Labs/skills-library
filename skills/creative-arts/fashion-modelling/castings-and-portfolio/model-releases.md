# Model releases

**Definition:** a model release is a signed document giving permission to use your image, likeness and sometimes name, for stated purposes.

## Read before you sign
- **Purposes:** commercial, editorial, advertising, promotional or any use. A broad "any purpose, forever" release is risky. Check consistency with the booking's usage terms.
- **Editing:** whether the image may be altered, retouched or combined.
- **Digital replica or AI use:** make sure it is excluded or separately agreed (see the digital-twin-consent skill).
- **Compensation:** whether the release is part of the paid terms or a separate gift (for example a TFP test).
- **Parties:** who may use the image (photographer, client, publisher).
- **Term and territory.**
- **Minors:** a parent or guardian signs for a minor.
- **Moral or sensitive uses:** whether the image may be used in a way you would object to.

## Practice
- **Do not sign on the day if you do not understand it;** ask your agent.
- **Keep a copy** and a record of the job.
- Take legal advice for unusual or broad releases.
