# Phones social posts and embargoes

**Principle:** shoots and fittings often involve unreleased products and campaigns. Posting early can breach confidentiality, damage the client relationship and lead to legal consequences.

## Practice
- **Check what you signed:** NDAs, model releases and agency terms may restrict posting.
- **Ask the client or agent** what is allowed before you post anything from set.
- **Respect embargoes:** wait until the release date and the approved wording; some clients want tags or approvals.
- **Do not post** other people, locations, or confidential items without permission; avoid showing sketches, labels or screens.
- **Keep phones away from set** during takes unless allowed, and do not film or photograph the tether screen.
- **Do not leak** during travel or meal breaks, including to friends.
- **Be careful with location tags** and live content.
- **If you made a mistake,** tell your agent straight away and remove the post.
