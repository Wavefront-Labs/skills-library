# Harassment response and reporting

**First:** your safety comes first. You may say no, stop, leave and call someone, whatever the booking.

## Immediate steps
- **Say no clearly** if you can and move to a safe place or near other people.
- **Tell someone you trust on set,** such as the producer, the stylist or a chaperone, and call your agent.
- **Leave** if you do not feel safe.

## Document
- **Write down** what happened, when, where, who was present, and what was said. Keep messages, photos and call sheets.
- **Do it soon** while memory is fresh.

## Report
- **Your agent** should act; the agency should have a procedure. Some agencies and brands also provide a confidential line.
- **Industry and support routes:** for example the Model Alliance's resources and RESPECT Program, the LVMH-Kering charter's reporting mechanisms for signatories, and union support (such as Equity in the UK). Check what applies to you and what is current.
- **Employer or client** complaint procedures, and, where relevant, **police** or **labour authorities**.
- **Seek legal advice** about your options and time limits.

## Support
Look after yourself: talk to someone you trust and consider professional support. See model-health skills.
