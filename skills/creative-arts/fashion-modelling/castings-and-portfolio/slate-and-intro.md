# Slate and intro

**Definition:** the slate is the short introduction at the start of a tape that identifies you.

## What to include (check the brief)
- **Name,** agency, and often **height** and other stats requested (such as measurements or shoe size), and sometimes location or availability.

## Technique
- **Look at the lens,** smile or use a warm neutral expression, and speak clearly at a natural pace.
- **Keep it short:** a few seconds.
- **Show front and profile** if asked: turn when told, and say "front" and "profile" if the brief requires it.
- **Keep consistent lighting and framing** from slate to action.
- **Do not edit in effects,** filters or music.
- **Pause for a beat** at the end, and then begin the next section.
