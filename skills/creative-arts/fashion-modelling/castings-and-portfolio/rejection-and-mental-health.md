# Rejection and mental health

**Important:** if you feel persistently low, anxious, hopeless or unsafe, speak to a doctor or mental-health professional. In an emergency, use local emergency services.

## Understand the pattern
- Most castings end in no. Decisions are usually about fit to a brief, a client's taste or logistics, not your worth.

## Practical habits
- **Separate the job from yourself:** keep notes on the work (what you did well, what you learned), not a story about your value.
- **Routines:** sleep, food, movement, time outside the industry and friends who are not in it.
- **Limit comparison:** reduce time on social media if it hurts, and curate who you follow.
- **Boundaries:** you do not have to accept every comment about your body; see the welfare skills.
- **Plan beyond modelling:** study, skills or other income reduce pressure.
- **Support:** talk to your agent, family, a mentor or a therapist. Some organisations, such as the LVMH-Kering charter signatories, provide access to psychological support; ask what your agency offers.
- **Notice warning signs** (loss of interest, hopelessness, constant dread) and seek help early.
