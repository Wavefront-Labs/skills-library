# Trust accounts for minors earnings

**Context:** some US states require a portion of a child performer's earnings to be set aside in a protected account.

## Outline (verify with current law)
- **California:** Family Code sections 6750 to 6753 (the Coogan law) allow a court to approve a minor's entertainment contracts, and require a portion of gross earnings (commonly stated as 15 percent) to be set aside in a trust for the minor.
- **New York:** the Child Performer Education and Trust Act requires a portion of a child performer's gross earnings (commonly stated as 15 percent) to be held in a trust account for the child. Check how it applies to child models and in what circumstances.
- **Other places** may have different rules or none.

## What families should ask
- **Is a trust account required** for this job, and who sets it up?
- **How is money deposited,** by whom, and when?
- **Who controls the account,** and when can the child access it?
- **Does the agency or client** handle this correctly, and how is it recorded on statements (see agency-statement skill)?
- **Seek legal or financial advice** for how the rules apply to your situation.
