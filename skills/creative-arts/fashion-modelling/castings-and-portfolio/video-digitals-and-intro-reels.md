# Video digitals and intro reels

**Context:** many agencies and clients ask for short video alongside still digitals. Follow the specific brief if one is given.

## Typical contents (agency requirements vary)
- **Slate:** name, agency or location, height and measurements if asked, said clearly to camera.
- **Turn:** a slow full turn, arms relaxed, so the body is seen from all sides.
- **Walk:** to and from the camera, and across the frame if requested.
- **Movement or expression** clips if asked (a smile, a laugh, a simple action).

## Setup
- **Phone horizontal or vertical as requested,** on a tripod or steady surface, at about chest height.
- **Soft, even light** facing you, plain background, and quiet space.
- **Fitted plain clothes** as for still digitals.
- **Short clips,** not long takes, with a clear start and end.
- **Check sound and focus** before you start, and keep original files.

## Check
Face and full body visible, steady frame, clear voice, and clips labelled with your name.
