# New york fashion workers act

**Context:** the Fashion Workers Act (New York, Senate bill S.9832 / Assembly bill A.5631E; signed by Governor Hochul in December 2024) regulates model management companies and clients in New York. It took effect on 19 June 2025, and model management companies had to register with the New York State Department of Labor within a year of that date (by 19 June 2026). The earlier bill number S2477A in this skill was wrong. This skill is a summary only. Read the current statute and guidance from the New York State Department of Labor, and take legal advice, because details and enforcement evolve.

## Areas the Act addresses (verify in the text)
- **Registration** of management companies with the state.
- **Written agreements** and clear terms between management companies and models.
- **Fees and payments:** limits on what management companies can charge and rules about how and when models are paid.
- **Transparency** and record-keeping, for example statements of what was paid and deducted.
- **Digital replicas:** requirements for clear consent to create or use them.
- **Protections against retaliation** and routes to complain.

## How to use it
- **Ask your agency** how it complies, and read your contract with the Act in mind.
- **Keep records** of jobs, payments and communications.
- **Complaints and advice:** the NY State Department of Labor, advocacy groups such as the Model Alliance and a lawyer can advise on how it applies to you.
- **It applies to work with a New York connection;** it does not set rules in other jurisdictions.
