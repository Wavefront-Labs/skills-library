# Uk agency fees and cooling off rules

**Context:** in Great Britain, modelling agencies are regulated as employment agencies under the **Conduct of Employment Agencies and Employment Businesses Regulations 2003**. The regulations restrict what agencies may charge people looking for work, and include special provisions for those in entertainment and modelling. Read the regulations on legislation.gov.uk and current guidance from the Employment Agency Standards Inspectorate, and take advice if unsure.

## Points to check (verify the current text)
- **Fees for finding work:** agencies generally cannot charge a work-seeker a fee for finding them work.
- **Charges for other services** (such as photographs or listing in a directory) are subject to conditions, including written information and a period in which you can cancel. Confirm exact conditions and time limits in the regulations.
- **Written terms:** the agency should provide written terms of its relationship with you.
- **Pressure to pay up front** for portfolios, courses or listings as a condition of representation is a red flag (see the scam-red-flags skill).

## What to do
- **Read the terms** and ask what each charge is for, and whether it is optional.
- **Get advice** from Equity (for members), the Association of Model Agents' guidance or a lawyer.
- **Report concerns** to the Employment Agency Standards Inspectorate.
- **Other countries** have different rules; check locally.
