# Payment timelines and terms

**Basics:** the client usually pays the agency, and the agency pays you after deducting commission and other agreed amounts. Timing depends on the client's payment terms, the agency's process and the contract.

## What to check
- **Contract and confirmation:** payment terms, the date you should expect payment and whether payment depends on the client paying.
- **Rules:** some jurisdictions set maximum time limits for paying models. For example, New York's Fashion Workers Act (effective 2025) includes payment-timing requirements for management companies; check the current text.
- **Statements:** each payment should come with a statement (see the agency-statement skill).

## If you are paid late
- **Ask your agent** for the status and the date, politely and in writing.
- **Keep records** of messages and the booking.
- **Escalate** to the agency's accounts team, then seek advice from a union, a model-advocacy group or a lawyer, or the relevant labour authority.
- **Plan cash flow:** assume delays and avoid depending on a single payment.
