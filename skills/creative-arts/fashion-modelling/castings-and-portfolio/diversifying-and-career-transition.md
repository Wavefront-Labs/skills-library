# Diversifying and career transition

**Context:** modelling careers can be short or irregular. Planning early gives choices.

## Planning
- **Keep learning:** study, courses or certifications alongside modelling where possible.
- **Build finances:** save, understand your tax and avoid debt (see model-finances skills).
- **Develop skills:** writing, photography, styling, casting, production, coaching, business or content creation.
- **Adjacent roles in the industry:** agent or booker, scout, casting director, stylist, creative producer, brand or content roles, fit specialist, walk coach, and model advocacy.
- **Build a network** and keep relationships positive.
- **Reputation:** be reliable and professional; it travels.
- **Health:** look after your body and mind through the transition.

## When to move
- **Signs:** declining bookings, the market changing, or your wishes changing. Talk with your agent and people you trust, and set a timeline.
- **Leaving an agency:** see the contract-termination skill.
