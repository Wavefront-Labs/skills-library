# Planning a test shoot

**Goal:** a shoot that adds strong, usable images to your book.

## Steps
1. **Purpose:** what gap in the portfolio does it fill (beauty, commercial, editorial, movement)? Ask your agent.
2. **Photographer:** check portfolio and references (see photographer-vetting).
3. **Team:** stylist, hair and make-up if needed, and an assistant. Consider bringing a trusted person for safety.
4. **Concept and mood board:** simple and achievable; share with the team.
5. **Wardrobe:** loan or your own, confirmed in advance.
6. **Location and schedule:** daylight hours, safe public or studio location, travel and breaks.
7. **Agreement:** usage, credits and delivery in writing (see the TFP agreement skill).
8. **Delivery:** number of retouched images and a timeline.
9. **After:** send selects to your agent, credit the team, and respect agreed posting rules.

## Safety
Tell your agent or a friend the address and times; stop if the set changes from what was agreed.
