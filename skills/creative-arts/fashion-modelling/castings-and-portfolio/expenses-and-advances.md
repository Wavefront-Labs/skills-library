# Expenses and advances

**Definition:** an advance is money the agency pays on your behalf or gives you, such as travel, housing or tests, usually to be repaid from your future earnings.

## Common items
- **Travel and visas,** accommodation, test shoots, portfolio costs, courier and local transport.

## What to check
- **Written terms:** what is advanced, who approves it, what interest or markups apply, and what the cost really is.
- **Recoupment:** how and when repayment is taken from earnings, and whether you can repay directly.
- **Receipts and invoices** for each cost.
- **Cap or limit:** ask whether the agency limits the amount.
- **If you leave:** what happens to unpaid advances.
- **Legitimate agencies** should not charge hidden or excessive costs; some places restrict what can be charged to models. See the model-law skills.

## Habits
- **Ask before incurring costs,** and keep a running total.
- **Seek advice** if the balance grows faster than your earnings.
