# Character and real people models

**Context:** character and real-people divisions place people with distinctive looks, ages, backgrounds or occupations, for brands that want authenticity.

## Key points
- **Casting:** based on look, personality and fit to a story. Be ready with natural digitals and a short, honest introduction.
- **Work types:** commercials, campaigns, editorials, film and TV extras, and brand content.
- **On set:** be yourself, take direction well and be ready to act simple scenes (see commercial acting skills).
- **Agreements:** check usage, releases and fees; non-professionals may be offered poor terms, so ask your agent and take advice (see model-release and buyout skills).
- **Boundaries:** clear about what you will and will not do, especially for personal stories or sensitive images.
- **Privacy and safety:** be careful sharing personal information and be aware of how images could be used.
- **Reliability:** punctuality and professionalism matter as much as for any model.
