# Changing areas and privacy

**Principle:** model-welfare guidance, such as the BFC guidance, the Model Alliance's resources and the LVMH-Kering charter, expects reasonable privacy while changing.

## What to expect
- **A private or screened area** for changing, separate from public or crew walkways.
- **A robe or cover-up** between looks.
- **No photography or filming** in changing areas, and phones put away.
- **Gender-appropriate arrangements** where possible, and adult-only spaces where children are involved.
- **Limited access** during changes, with clear rules for dressers and crew.

## What to do
- **Ask in advance,** through your agent, what the changing arrangements are, especially for swimwear, lingerie, fittings and shows.
- **Speak up on the day** if the space is not private. Ask for a screen, a separate room or time.
- **Do not agree** to be photographed or watched while changing.
- **Report breaches** to the producer and your agent, and see the harassment-reporting skill.
- **Minors** should be accompanied by a chaperone or guardian (see the minors skills).
