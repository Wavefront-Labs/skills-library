# Callbacks and fittings before booking

**Context:** a callback is a second meeting after an initial casting. A fitting before booking is a try-on to see how you look in the clothes.

## What it usually means
- **You are a serious candidate,** but not yet booked. Ask your agent what the status is (option or hold).
- **Clients** may compare several models.

## Preparation
- **Confirm time and place** through your agent, and ask what to wear and bring.
- **Think about what changed:** the client may want a different look, shoes or walk.
- **Review the brief** and be ready to repeat what worked at the first meeting.

## At the fitting
- **Follow the stylist,** try garments and walk or pose.
- **Say if something does not fit or hurts,** politely.
- **Keep the garments clean** and be careful with the samples.
- **Do not agree to terms** at the fitting; tell your agent everything.

## After
Tell your agent what happened and keep other bookings clear if there is a hold. See the option-hold skill.
