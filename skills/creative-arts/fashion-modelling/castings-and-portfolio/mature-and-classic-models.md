# Mature and classic models

**Context:** many agencies have mature or classic divisions; age ranges vary by agency. Ask about yours.

## Key points
- **Work types:** commercial, lifestyle, healthcare, finance and luxury advertising, editorial and fashion campaigns that target older audiences.
- **Portfolio:** use natural, current images that show personality and range; avoid heavy retouching.
- **Agencies:** look for those with a mature division and credible rosters. Be cautious of anyone who charges to represent you (see the scam skill).
- **Skills:** acting and expression often matter more; consider commercial and video work.
- **Health and fitness:** keep your own routine; fit and health appear in the work.
- **Rates, usage and contracts:** the same principles apply; see booking and usage skills.
- **Confidence:** many clients value authenticity.
