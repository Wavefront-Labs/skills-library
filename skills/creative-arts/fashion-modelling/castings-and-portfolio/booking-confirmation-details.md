# Booking confirmation details

**Goal:** both sides have the same understanding in writing.

## What to check
- **Date, call time and wrap time,** and travel or turnaround.
- **Address and arrival instructions** (floor, door, parking).
- **Client and production contacts,** and your agent's contact.
- **Job description:** type of work, brand, nature of images (including any nudity, swim or lingerie; see nudity-consent).
- **Fee and payment terms:** rate, overtime, expenses, payment date and who pays.
- **Usage:** media, territory and term (see usage-rights skills).
- **Hair, make-up, wardrobe and what to bring.**
- **Cancellation and weather terms.**
- **Breaks, meals and welfare provisions.**
- **Other talent and chaperone** if relevant.

## Habit
Reply to confirm, ask questions through your agent before the day, and keep the document.
