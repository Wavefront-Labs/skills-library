# Planning a season abroad

**Context:** models often spend a season in one or more fashion cities. The main womenswear fashion weeks run in roughly New York, London, Milan and Paris, in that order, twice a year (around February to March and September to October). Menswear and couture have their own calendars; check current dates.

## Planning
- **Confirm the placement:** which agency, contract, costs and expected work, in writing. See the placement-agency skill.
- **Timing:** castings for shows usually start before fashion week, and lead times for travel and visas can be long.
- **Budget:** travel, housing, local transport, food, phone, insurance and a cushion; advances may cover some costs but are repaid from earnings.
- **Documents:** passport, visa or permit, insurance, contracts, medical certificate if required (see the health-certificate skill).
- **Health and safety:** vaccinations as needed, travel insurance, local emergency numbers and your agent's contact.
- **Portfolio and cards:** updated digitals, book and comp cards for the market.
- **Support:** a plan for mental health, contact with home and routines.

## Questions to ask
What do I owe if work is slow? Who handles my safety? How do I end the placement if it is not working?
