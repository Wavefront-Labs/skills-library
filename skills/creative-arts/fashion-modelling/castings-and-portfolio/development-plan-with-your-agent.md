# Development plan with your agent

**Goal:** a shared, realistic plan that you and the agency review regularly.

## Steps
1. **Goals:** what work you want (editorial, commercial, runway, parts), where, and what income and lifestyle you need.
2. **Strengths and gaps:** ask your agent for honest feedback on look, skills (walk, posing, movement) and book.
3. **Actions:** tests, digitals, training, markets, and a timeline.
4. **Costs:** what you will spend and what the agency will advance; see the advances skill.
5. **Boundaries:** your limits on nudity, travel, hours and health, noted clearly.
6. **Review:** set regular check-ins and adjust the plan.

## Habits
- **Write it down** and keep a copy.
- **Track progress:** bookings, feedback, and items completed.
- **Raise concerns early** if the plan conflicts with your health, school or family.
- **Remember** you can change direction.
