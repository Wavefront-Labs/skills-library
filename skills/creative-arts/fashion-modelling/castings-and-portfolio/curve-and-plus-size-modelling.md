# Curve and plus size modelling

**Context:** many agencies have a curve or plus division. Definitions vary by agency, brand and country, so ask for the range your agency uses.

## Key points
- **Division and requirements:** ask your agency for current size and measurement guidelines and what it expects.
- **Work types:** fashion and e-commerce, commercial, editorial, runway and fit work; see the plus-petite-tall fit skill.
- **Fit and wardrobe:** brands may have limited sample ranges, so check what will be available and tell the stylist about fit issues early.
- **Posing and movement:** the same fundamentals as other models; see posing skills.
- **Professionalism and welfare:** you are entitled to respect, privacy during fittings and protection from body-shaming comments (see welfare skills).
- **Health:** look after your health and avoid pressure to change shape for the job (see healthy-stability skill).
- **Representation:** check that the agency's contract, commission and marketing are fair.
