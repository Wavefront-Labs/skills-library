# Size and sample size policies

**Context (check each source for current wording):**
- The **LVMH-Kering charter** (2017) sets minimum sample sizes for the groups' brands; commonly reported as French size 34 for women and 44 for men, with the charter text as the reference.
- **Some countries and events** have had rules about body mass index or minimum weight (for example past rules in Spain and Israel). France removed BMI thresholds in 2017 and keeps a medical-certificate requirement (see the health-certificate skill).
- **CFDA and BFC** have published health guidelines for the industry, which are recommendations, not laws.

## What to know
- **Sample size** is the size designers use for samples, and clients cast models who fit them. Sizes differ by brand and market.
- **A model's health matters more** than fitting a sample. A healthy model who does not match a given sample size is not at fault.

## Practice
- **Ask your agent** about size expectations and keep your health first.
- **Report pressure** to lose weight or to alter your body; see harassment-reporting and welfare skills.
- **Discuss with a doctor** if you are asked to change your body for work.
