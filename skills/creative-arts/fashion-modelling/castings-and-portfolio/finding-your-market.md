# Finding your market

**Goal:** put your energy where your look and strengths book work.

## How to find fit
- **Collect feedback:** go-see and casting comments, and which jobs convert to bookings.
- **Study the market:** look at brands, campaigns and agencies; see who books models like you.
- **Match skills:** a strong walk fits runway, expressive movement suits video, hands or skin may suit parts and beauty.
- **Consider categories:** commercial, editorial, curve, petite, mature, fitness, parts and so on (see model-categories skills).
- **Test in the market:** targeted tests and digitals tailored to a category.
- **Ask your agent:** where they see demand and where they would place you.
- **Be willing to adjust:** your look changes and so does the market.

## Check
You can describe in a sentence the kind of work you are best placed for and the three clients or brands you want to target.
