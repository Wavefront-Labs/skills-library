# Key management agreement terms

**Warning:** this is general professional guidance. Contract law differs by place, and some jurisdictions have specific rules (for example New York's Fashion Workers Act, effective in 2025, which regulates management companies; check the current text). Have a lawyer, union representative or other qualified adviser review before you sign, especially if you are under 18 (guardians should read it too).

## Checklist
- **Parties and capacity:** who the agency is (legal name and address), and whether they act as your manager, agent or both.
- **Term and renewal:** length, automatic renewal and notice.
- **Exclusivity:** territory and scope (see the exclusivity skill).
- **Commission:** percentages and what they apply to (see the commission skill).
- **Fees and expenses:** what the agency may charge you and who approves them.
- **Payments:** when you are paid after the client pays, statements and audit rights (see finance skills).
- **Usage and releases** and who negotiates them.
- **Termination and post-term commission** (see the termination skill).
- **Governing law and disputes.**
- **Health, safety and conduct policies,** and how complaints are handled.

## Habit
Never sign under time pressure and keep a copy.
