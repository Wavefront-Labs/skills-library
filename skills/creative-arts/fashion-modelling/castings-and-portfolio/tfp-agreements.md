# Tfp agreements

**Definition:** TFP means a collaboration in which the model and photographer, and often a stylist and make-up artist, exchange their time for images instead of fees.

## Put in writing
- **Who,** where and when, and who is on the team.
- **What each side contributes** and any expenses.
- **Deliverables:** number of final retouched images, format, and delivery date.
- **Usage:** where the images may be used (portfolio, social media, competitions, commercial) and whether commercial use needs new consent and payment. A **model release** is a separate document and is not blanket permission for any use.
- **Credits:** how each person is credited.
- **Retouching and approval:** whether you approve edits.
- **Nudity or sensitive content:** agreed in advance (see the nudity-consent skill).
- **Copyright:** normally the photographer owns it, and you receive a licence for the agreed uses.
- **Cancellation** and what happens if someone does not deliver.

## Advice
- Check with your agent before any test if you are signed. Take legal advice for important or commercial uses.
