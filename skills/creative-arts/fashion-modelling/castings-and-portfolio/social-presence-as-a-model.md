# Social presence as a model

**Goal:** a presence that supports your career and protects your safety.

## Approach
- **Purpose:** use it as a secondary portfolio and a way for clients to see your personality, not as a replacement for your agency's book.
- **Content:** a consistent set of strong images, with credits, and genuine behind-the-scenes content where allowed (see behind-the-scenes and embargo skills).
- **Brand fit:** clients may check your profiles; remove content that conflicts with the work you want, and be aware of past posts.
- **Privacy and safety:** do not share your address or live location, think before sharing personal details, and manage who can message you.
- **Disclosure:** follow the rules on paid partnerships (see ad-disclosure skill).
- **Agency and contract rules:** check what you are allowed to post, and ask before announcing jobs.
- **Wellbeing:** limit comparison and handle abuse by muting, blocking and reporting.
- **Do not pay** for followers or "verification"; scams are common.
