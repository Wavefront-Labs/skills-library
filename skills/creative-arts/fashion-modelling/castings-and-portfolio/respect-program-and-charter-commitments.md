# Respect program and charter commitments

**Context (summary; check each source for the current text):**
- **Model Alliance RESPECT Program:** a program and set of resources offering an anti-harassment and complaint framework for people working in fashion, including models.
- **LVMH and Kering Charter (2017):** commitments by the two groups on the working relations and well-being of models. It covers matters such as age and size standards, health, nudity consent, working conditions and access to support. It applies to those groups and their suppliers, not to the whole industry.
- **British Fashion Council:** guidance and commitments on model welfare during London Fashion Week, including health, working conditions and under-age protections. Check the BFC's current guidance.

## Using them
- **Ask whether the client or event follows a charter** and what they commit to.
- **Use the wording** when you raise concerns, for example about nudity consent, backstage conditions, breaks or size.
- **Know the reporting mechanisms,** and your agent's role (see the harassment-reporting skill).
- **They are commitments, not laws:** legal rights come from law and contracts, so take advice where needed.
