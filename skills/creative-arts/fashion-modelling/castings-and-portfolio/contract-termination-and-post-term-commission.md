# Contract termination and post term commission

## Read before you decide
- **Notice period and method** in the contract (written, to whom, by when).
- **Outstanding payments:** the agency should account for jobs done and pay what is due; ask for a final statement.
- **Post-term commission:** many contracts allow the agency to take commission on jobs booked during the term, and sometimes on repeat or renewal work with the same clients for a period afterwards. Read the clause; it varies.
- **Advances and debts:** check what remains to be recouped and from which earnings.
- **Images and portfolio:** what you may keep or use, and how to retrieve digitals and tests.
- **Clients and notification:** who tells clients and how bookings in progress are handled.

## Practice
- **Keep records** of emails, statements and bookings.
- **Do not accept new representation** that conflicts with the old exclusivity until you know your position.
- **Take advice:** a lawyer, union representative (for example Equity in the UK) or model-advocacy group can review the terms. Where fashion-worker laws apply (for example New York's Fashion Workers Act), they may add protections; check the current law.
