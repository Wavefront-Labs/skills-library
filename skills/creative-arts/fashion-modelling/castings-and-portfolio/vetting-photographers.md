# Vetting photographers

**Goal:** reduce the risk of unsafe or exploitative situations.

## Checks
- **Ask your agent** whether they know or have worked with the photographer.
- **Review the portfolio and social profiles** for consistent, long-running work and credited collaborators, and check that the work looks real.
- **Ask for references** from other models or stylists, and contact them.
- **Search the name** and check for complaints. Model Alliance and other model-support organisations can be a place to seek advice.
- **Communicate through professional channels** and keep messages.
- **Confirm the team,** location, schedule and usage in writing.
- **Avoid private residences or hotel rooms** for first meetings; use a studio or public location, and bring a chaperone or friend if you can.

## Red flags
Pressure to shoot nudity not agreed, alcohol or drugs offered, secrecy, requests to avoid telling your agent, vague payment, or changes at the last minute.

## If it feels wrong
Leave, tell your agent, and see the harassment-reporting skill.
