# Commission structures

**Basics (varies by market, agency and contract):**
- Agencies typically take a **percentage commission** of the model's fee. Percentages differ by market and contract, so read yours.
- Agencies may also charge the **client a separate booking or agency fee** on top of the model's fee; this is not taken from the model, but check the contract.
- Commission may be calculated on the **gross fee** (before tax and some costs) or on another base. Ask which.
- **Exclusions and extras:** check how usage fees, buyouts, overtime and expenses are treated.

## What to check
- **Rate and base** for each type of work (editorial, commercial, runway, digital replicas).
- **Deductions** the agency may make beyond commission (advances, costs, fees) and the need for your approval.
- **Statements:** each job should show the gross amount, commission and net (see the agency-statement skill).
- **Legal limits:** some jurisdictions restrict commissions or fees charged to models; check local law and current guidance.
