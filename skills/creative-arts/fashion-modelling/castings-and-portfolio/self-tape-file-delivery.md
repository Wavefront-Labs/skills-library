# Self tape file delivery

**Goal:** the casting director can open, find and play your tape without trouble.

## Practice
- **Follow the brief exactly:** file type (commonly MP4 or MOV), maximum size, length, and where to upload (a link, an email or an agency platform).
- **Name the file clearly,** such as `Surname_Firstname_Agency_Height` or whatever the brief specifies, with no spaces if requested.
- **Combine clips in the requested order** (slate, front and profile, walk, movement) and trim excess.
- **Compress carefully** so quality remains acceptable, and test playback.
- **Upload to the specified service** (for example a file-transfer or video link) and check the link permissions.
- **Send before the deadline** and confirm receipt through your agent.
- **Keep a backup** of the original.
- **Privacy:** do not post the tape publicly unless allowed.
