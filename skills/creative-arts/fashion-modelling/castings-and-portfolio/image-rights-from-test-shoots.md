# Image rights from test shoots

**Basics (general; law differs by country):**
- In many countries the **photographer is the first owner of copyright** in a photograph unless a written agreement says otherwise. As the model you usually receive a **licence** to use the images for agreed purposes.
- A **model release** gives the photographer permission to use your image for stated purposes; it is separate from your licence to use the photos.

## What to agree
- **Your uses:** portfolio, comp card, social media, agency site, print or competitions.
- **Photographer's uses:** portfolio, social media, exhibitions, stock, commercial, and whether each needs payment or consent.
- **Retouching:** who does it and whether you approve changes to your body or face.
- **Credits** and tagging.
- **Term and territory** if limited.
- **Removal:** what happens if you want an image taken down.
- **Sensitive content:** how nude or private images may be used and stored.

## Habit
Keep your agreement and all emails. If you are signed, ask your agent to review before you sign. Take legal advice if an image is used outside the agreement.
