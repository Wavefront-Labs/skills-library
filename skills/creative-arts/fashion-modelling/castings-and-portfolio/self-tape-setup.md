# Self tape setup

**Goal:** a clean, professional tape that follows the brief.

## Setup
- **Phone:** use the rear camera, clean the lens, and put the phone on a tripod or a stable stack at about chest height. Orientation as requested (horizontal for many briefs, vertical for some social work).
- **Framing:** check the brief; commonly the full body is visible for a walk and head and shoulders for the slate.
- **Light:** soft light facing you, such as a window in front of you or lamps on both sides; avoid strong backlight and mixed colours.
- **Background:** plain, uncluttered wall.
- **Sound:** quiet room, doors closed and devices silenced.
- **Space:** enough room to walk, turn and step back; mark the floor.
- **Clothes:** as the brief specifies, usually plain and fitted.

## Test
Record a 10-second clip, check focus, exposure, sound and framing, and fix before you begin the real take.
