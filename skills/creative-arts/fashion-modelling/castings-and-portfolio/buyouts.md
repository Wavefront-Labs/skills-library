# Buyouts

**Definition:** a buyout is a one-off payment that licenses a client to use images or footage for a broad or unlimited scope, usually with no further payment for that scope.

## Points to check
- **What is bought out:** media (print, digital, social, TV, outdoor), territory (one country, region, worldwide), and time (a term or in perpetuity).
- **What is excluded:** many buyouts exclude certain media or products and still require new fees for them.
- **Exclusivity:** whether you are barred from working for competitors, and for how long.
- **Renewals:** if a term is limited, what happens when it ends.
- **Digital replica or AI use:** make sure these are not included unless separately agreed (see the digital-twin-consent skill).
- **Price:** a buyout normally costs more than limited usage because the client gets more value.

## Practice
- **Let your agent negotiate** and confirm the terms in writing.
- **Check the model release** for conflicts with the buyout.
- **Keep records** of what was agreed. Take legal advice for large or unusual buyouts.
