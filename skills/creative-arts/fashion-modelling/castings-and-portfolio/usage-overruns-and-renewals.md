# Usage overruns and renewals

**Definition:** an overrun is use of images beyond what was agreed or paid for.

## How to spot it
- **Track the usage terms** for each job (media, territory, term) and the end date. Your agent normally keeps this record.
- **Watch for the images** on websites, social media, packaging or advertising after the end date or in new media.
- **Compare** what you find with the booking and contract.

## What to do
- **Tell your agent;** they usually raise it with the client and negotiate a renewal or an additional fee.
- **Gather evidence** (screenshots, URLs, dates).
- **Do not threaten the client yourself.**
- **Renewals:** when a client wants to extend, the usage is renegotiated, and a new fee is normally agreed in writing.
- **If your agent does not act,** or you are unrepresented, take legal advice or seek help from a union or model-advocacy group.
