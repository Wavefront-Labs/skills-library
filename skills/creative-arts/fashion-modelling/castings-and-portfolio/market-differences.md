# Market differences

**Note:** markets change and are not uniform. These are broad generalisations; use your agent's current knowledge.

## Commonly described differences
- **New York:** large commercial and e-commerce work alongside fashion and editorial; fast-paced.
- **London:** strong editorial and high-fashion scene as well as commercial work.
- **Milan:** known for runway shows and fashion-house work in season.
- **Paris:** high-fashion shows and couture in season, with many agencies and strict casting.
- **Other markets,** such as Germany, Scandinavia, Asia and Australia, may focus on commercial, catalogue and e-commerce work, with varied rates and rules.

## What to research
- **Types of work** commonly available and the typical look the market books.
- **Rates, usage and commission** conventions.
- **Legal and welfare rules** (see welfare and law skills): medical certificates, age limits and fee rules.
- **Language, cost of living and visa rules.**
- **Agencies** with good reputations in that market.

## Practice
Ask your mother agency which market suits your look and stage of career.
