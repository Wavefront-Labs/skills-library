# Option holds

**Definition:** an option is a provisional hold on a model's date by a client. It is not a confirmed job.

## How it works (conventions; practices vary, so check with your agent)
- **First option** has priority; a **second option** is next in line. If the first option confirms, the second option is released or asked to confirm.
- **Time limits:** an option often has a deadline, after which the client must confirm or release.
- **Confirmation:** a booking becomes firm only when the client confirms with the agency (see booking-confirmation).
- **Conflicts:** if two clients want the same date, the agency decides in line with option order and contract rules.
- **Your role:** keep the date clear, tell your agent about other offers, and do not commit elsewhere without checking.
- **Cancellations of options** normally carry no fee; fees apply to confirmed bookings (see the cancellation-fee skill).

## Habit
Ask your agent which options you hold and the deadline for each.
