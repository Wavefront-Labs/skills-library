# Media territory and term

**Definition:** usage for modelling work is commonly defined by three dimensions: **media** (where the images appear), **territory** (where) and **term** (how long).

## Media
- Examples are print advertising, point of sale, online and social, television, cinema, outdoor, packaging and editorial. Ask for each to be listed separately.

## Territory
- A single country, a region or worldwide. Online use can be global by nature, so check how the contract treats online use.

## Term
- A defined period (for example months or years), a campaign period or in perpetuity. Check when it starts, whether it renews and what happens after.

## Practice
- **Get usage in writing** on the confirmation (see booking-confirmation).
- **Match the fee to the scope:** broader media, territory and term normally means a higher fee.
- **Watch for conflicts** with other brands in the same category.
- **Check what happens when the term ends:** images should be taken down or renewed (see usage-overrun).
- **Ask your agent** to track the usage period.
