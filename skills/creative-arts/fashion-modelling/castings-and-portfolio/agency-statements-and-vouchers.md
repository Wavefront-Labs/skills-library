# Agency statements and vouchers

**Definition:** a statement (or account) lists your jobs, what clients paid, and what the agency deducted. A voucher is a record of a single job.

## What to check on each line
- **Job:** client, date and description, and match it to your records.
- **Gross fee** and any overtime, usage or expenses billed to the client.
- **Commission and fees** deducted, and whether the percentage matches the contract.
- **Other deductions:** advances, travel, housing, testing costs, and whether you approved them.
- **Net payable** and date.
- **Running balance:** what you owe the agency and what you have been paid.
- **Currency and tax:** what is withheld or reported.

## Practice
- **Keep your own job diary** and expense records.
- **Ask for statements regularly** and query errors in writing.
- **Request supporting invoices** for large deductions.
- **Some jurisdictions give models rights to accounting;** check current law and your contract.
