# Go see etiquette

**Context:** a go-see is a short in-person meeting where a client looks at your book and look.

## Before
- **Confirm the time, address, and the contact** through your agent.
- **Plan the route** and arrive a few minutes early.
- **Bring:** your book on a device or print, comp cards if requested, the shoes and simple outfit asked for, ID, and water.
- **Look:** clean, simple and as in your digitals, unless told otherwise.

## There
- **Check in** politely and wait quietly.
- **Greet** briefly, say your name and agency, and hand over your book.
- **Do what is asked** (walk, turn, try on, photos) calmly and quickly.
- **Do not over-explain** or ask for feedback on the spot.
- **Thank them** and leave promptly.

## After
- **Tell your agent** how it went. Do not contact the client directly.
