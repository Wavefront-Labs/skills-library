# Cancellations and weather days

**Note:** terms differ by agency, client and country. Always check the confirmation and contract.

## Common concepts
- **Cancellation notice:** a client who cancels a confirmed booking close to the date may owe a fee; the notice period and amount are set in the terms (commonly stated in hours or days).
- **Postponement and weather days:** outdoor jobs may be moved for weather. Check whether a weather day carries a fee, how you are held for the next date, and what happens if you become unavailable.
- **Your cancellations:** if you cancel or are late, you may lose the job and may owe costs, and your agent should be told immediately. Illness and emergencies should be reported as soon as possible.
- **Force majeure** (for example events outside the parties' control) can change the position; read the clause.

## Practice
- **Ask your agent about the terms for each booking.**
- **Keep other work clear** until you know the date is firm.
- **Document** messages about postponements.
