# Working with hair and make up

**Goal:** a good working relationship and a look that lasts through the day.

## Before
- **Arrive with clean, product-free hair** and a clean, moisturised face unless told otherwise.
- **Tell the team in advance** about allergies, skin sensitivities and recent treatments.
- **Bring** your own hair ties, pins and any products you need for your skin or hair.

## In the chair
- **Be friendly and still.** Keep your head still and your eyes closed or open as asked.
- **Give feedback politely** if something pulls, stings or feels uncomfortable; say it early.
- **Hygiene:** use clean brushes and fresh applicators, and say if tools are not hygienic.
- **Phones:** keep your use to a minimum, and avoid distracting the artist.
- **Do not change the look** yourself or remove products; ask the team.

## Through the day
- **Avoid touching your face or hair;** ask for touch-ups.
- **Protect the make-up** when eating, drinking and changing (use a straw, cover your face for tops).
- **Thank the team** and ask before photographing the process.
