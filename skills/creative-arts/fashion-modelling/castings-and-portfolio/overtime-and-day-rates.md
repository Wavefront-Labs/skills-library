# Overtime and day rates

**Note:** rates and rules vary by market, client and contract. This skill describes concepts to check, not set numbers.

## Concepts
- **Day rate:** a fee for a defined day length, often with a stated number of hours; a **half-day rate** may apply to shorter jobs.
- **Overtime:** extra hours beyond the stated day, paid at a rate in the booking or contract. Some terms use an hourly rate or an increased rate; check yours.
- **Travel and waiting time,** **meal breaks** and **turnaround** (rest between days) may be treated differently.
- **Evening, weekend and early-start premiums** can apply.
- **Hours on set:** record start and end, including waiting and hair and make-up, because disputes arise from unclear timings.

## Practice
- **Check the confirmation** for the day rate, hours and overtime terms.
- **Ask your agent** before the day if the schedule looks longer than planned.
- **Keep a record** of times and tell your agent about overtime.
- **Health and welfare:** report excessive hours or missed breaks (see working-hours skill).
