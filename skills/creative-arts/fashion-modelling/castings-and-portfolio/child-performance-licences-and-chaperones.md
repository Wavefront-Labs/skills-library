# Child performance licences and chaperones

**Warning:** rules differ by country, nation and local authority. Parents or guardians and agencies should check the current rules with the relevant authority.

## UK (England) in outline
- The **Children (Performances and Activities) (England) Regulations 2014** govern when children may work in performances and modelling, with hours and breaks limits, and require a **licence** from the local authority where the child lives, or a **body of persons approval** for some one-off or short-term engagements. Details differ in Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland.
- **Chaperones** must be approved by the licensing authority where a licence applies. The chaperone's job is to supervise the child's welfare, and they must be with the child during the activity.

## What families and models should do
- **Ask the agency and the client** whether a licence or approval is needed and who applies, and keep copies.
- **Check that the chaperone is approved** and a suitable person, not just any adult.
- **Check hours, breaks, schooling and rest** against the licence terms.
- **A parent or guardian** should read contracts and attend where possible.
- **Raise concerns** with the agency and local authority.

## Other places
Many jurisdictions have child-labour or performer permit rules. Check current guidance from the local authority or labour department, and take legal advice where unsure.
