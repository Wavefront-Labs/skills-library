# Comp card design layout and formats

**Goal:** a card that shows range, reads quickly and matches the agency's template.

## Image selection
- **Front:** the strongest, clearest image of you (often a headshot or a clean full-length), usually with your name.
- **Back:** three or four images that show range (for example full-length, close-up, commercial and editorial), without repeating a pose or outfit.
- **Use current images** that look like you now.
- **Use images you have the right to use;** check credits and usage with the photographer. Ask your agent which images to use.

## Layout and stats
- Most agencies provide a **template** with logo and contact information; use it rather than your own design.
- **Stats:** height, key measurements, shoe size, hair and eye colour. See the model-stats guidance in the digitals and measurements skills.
- **Credits and contact** as the agency requires.

## Formats
- **Print:** commonly a card of around postcard to A5 size; ask your agent for the specification and resolution.
- **Digital:** a PDF or web card, often generated by the agency's system; check file size and that it displays on phones.
- **Update** when your look changes or you have better images.
