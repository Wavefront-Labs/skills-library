# Digital portfolio platforms

**Goal:** a current, consistent digital presence that agents and clients can find and trust.

## Options
- **Agency website and portfolio:** your agent controls this, so ask how updates happen.
- **A simple personal site or PDF book** if the agency allows it or you are freelance.
- **Social profiles** used as a secondary portfolio (see the social-presence skill).
- Industry platforms exist, and quality and reputation vary; check the platform's terms and fees before paying anything. Never pay a platform that promises representation or work.

## Practice
- **Keep the same set of top images** across platforms and remove outdated ones.
- **Credits:** name photographers, stylists and brands correctly.
- **Usage:** do not post campaign images before release or beyond what the contract allows. See the embargo and usage-rights skills.
- **Privacy:** do not publish home address or phone number; use agency contact details.
- **Check how it looks on a phone.**
