# Working hours and breaks

**Note:** hours and breaks depend on the contract, the client, local law and, for minors, specific regulation. Welfare guidance (such as the LVMH-Kering charter and BFC guidance) emphasises reasonable hours, breaks and rest; check current text and your contract.

## What to check
- **Call and wrap time** in the booking, and what counts as working time (including hair and make-up and waiting).
- **Meal and rest breaks:** when and how long, and whether food and drink are provided.
- **Turnaround:** a rest period before the next day.
- **Night and early work:** transport, safety and any extra pay.
- **Overtime:** see the overtime skill.
- **Minors:** stricter limits and chaperones apply (see minors skills).

## Practice
- **Keep a record** of start, finish and breaks.
- **Say early** if hours are running over or breaks are missed; raise it with the producer politely, and tell your agent.
- **Rest and health come first;** you can pause if you are unwell.
