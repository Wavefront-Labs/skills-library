# Retouched photo labelling

**Context:** France requires commercial photographs of models whose body has been modified by retouching to carry the label **'Photographie retouchée'**. The rule is in the Code de la santé publique (article L2133-2) and has applied since 2017. Check current French law and guidance for exact scope and penalties.

## Points to know
- **Scope:** commercial images where the model's silhouette has been altered or made thinner or larger by digital editing. Ordinary colour and lighting adjustments are not the aim of the rule.
- **Responsibility:** it generally falls on the advertiser or publisher, not the model.
- **Other jurisdictions** have proposed or adopted disclosure rules for retouched or digitally altered body images, and standards bodies and advertising authorities may have guidance. Check current local rules.

## For models
- **Ask about retouching** on your jobs, and what changes may be made to your body or face; include terms in your agreement or release.
- **Raise concerns** with your agent if images are altered in ways you did not agree to.
- **Keep records** of originals where you have them.
