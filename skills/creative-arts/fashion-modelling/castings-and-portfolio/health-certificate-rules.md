# Health certificate rules

**Context:** some jurisdictions require models to hold a medical certificate showing fitness to work. France is a well-known example: its Code du travail (article L7123-2-1) requires a medical certificate for models, and rules about measurements such as body mass index were removed in 2017 while the certificate requirement remained. Check current French law and decrees, as details change.

## What to know
- **Who requires it:** the law of the country, your agency, or a client or event; rules differ and some places have none.
- **What it covers:** generally the doctor's assessment of your overall health and fitness to work, not a public measure of weight alone. Check the specific requirement.
- **Getting one:** ask your agent where to go and what to bring; a doctor issues it.
- **Privacy:** the certificate is medical information. Ask who will see it and how it will be stored.
- **Validity:** it may have a limited period; keep track of renewal.
- **Other measures:** some organisations, such as the BFC and CFDA, have health guidelines separate from the law.

## Practice
- **Ask your agent** which markets require a certificate before you travel.
- **Do not alter your body** to pass an assessment; see the healthy-stability skill.
