# Age minimums for shows and adult work

**Context (summaries; check the sources):**
- **LVMH-Kering charter (2017)** sets restrictions on engaging very young models and on under-18s being portrayed as adults. Read the charter for exact ages and conditions.
- **CFDA Health Initiative** guidelines have included recommendations about the age of models used in shows. Check the current wording.
- **BFC** guidance addresses under-age models during London Fashion Week.
- **Law:** child-labour and performer rules apply to minors in many places (see the child-licence skill), and laws on adult content have their own age rules.

## Principles
- **Under 18 means protections apply:** guardians, chaperones, school, limited hours and no adult or sexualised content.
- **Check ages** of everyone on set and keep ID where required.
- **Report concerns** about the treatment of a minor to the agency, the client and the relevant authority.

## For models and families
- **Ask the agency** which age-related rules and charters apply to each job.
- **Do not accept** work that conflicts with those rules, and ask the agency to put terms in writing.
