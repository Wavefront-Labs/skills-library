# In the casting room

**Context:** a casting is a short audition for a specific job; time per model is often brief.

## Technique
- **Check in** and take the number or form if one is given.
- **Be ready** in the clothes and shoes requested, with your look as briefed.
- **Slate** (name, agency and sometimes height) clearly when asked.
- **Follow instructions exactly,** such as the walk, turn, pose, or expression, and do it with energy.
- **Be easy to work with:** friendly, calm, quick.
- **Nerves:** breathe out slowly, drop shoulders, and focus on the first instruction.
- **Do not apologise** or comment on your appearance.
- **Do not take it personally** if you are thanked quickly: many decisions are about fit to the brief.
- **Leave** with a thanks, and avoid lingering.
