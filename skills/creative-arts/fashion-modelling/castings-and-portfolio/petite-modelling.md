# Petite modelling

**Context:** petite modelling usually covers models shorter than the typical runway height requirement. Agencies set their own minimum and maximum heights, so ask yours.

## Key points
- **Work types:** commercial, e-commerce, beauty, lifestyle and petite-specific fashion lines, and some editorial; runway opportunities are more limited.
- **Proportions:** brands want models who match the proportions of petite customers, such as shorter torso, rise and inseam.
- **Posing:** use angles, lines and shoes (as allowed) to create height, and use camera angles agreed with the photographer.
- **Fit:** report proportion issues (sleeve, hem, rise) in fit sessions.
- **Market:** research where petite lines and campaigns exist.
- **Professionalism:** keep digitals and stats accurate, including height.
