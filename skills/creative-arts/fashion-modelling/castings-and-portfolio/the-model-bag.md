# The model bag

**Goal:** be ready for any look, any delay and any weather.

## Core kit
- **Underwear and basics:** nude seamless underwear and bras (including strapless), plain fitted tops, black and neutral basics, tights or socks.
- **Shoes:** neutral heels or the type requested, clean flats, and shoes for the weather.
- **Grooming:** clean hairbrush, hair ties and pins, deodorant (unscented if asked), lip balm, tissues, razor, tweezers, nail file, plasters.
- **Skin:** simple moisturiser, sun protection and cleansing wipes; avoid trying new products on the day.
- **Health:** water bottle, healthy snacks, any medication you need, pain relief, and a small sewing and safety-pin kit.
- **Tech:** charged phone, charger or power bank, headphones, a copy of your book and comp cards.
- **Documents:** ID, call sheet, agency contacts, and work or travel documents if needed.
- **Extras:** a robe or large shirt, spare layers, and a small towel.

## Habit
Restock after every job and keep the bag packed.
