# Exclusivity and contract term

**Definition:** an exclusive agreement means only that agency may represent you in the stated territory or field for the stated period.

## What to check
- **Scope:** which markets, countries or types of work (modelling, acting, social content, digital replicas) are covered.
- **Duration and renewal:** the term, automatic renewal and how to give notice.
- **Direct bookings:** whether you may accept work without the agency, and how commission applies.
- **Existing clients:** whether previous clients are covered and whether you can keep working with them.
- **Other agencies:** whether you can sign elsewhere for other territories (placement) or services.
- **Conflicts:** what happens with competing brand exclusivity (see usage rights).
- **Review rights:** whether the term can be shortened if the agency does not meet obligations.

## Advice
Longer or broader exclusivity should come with stronger commitments from the agency. Ask for advice before signing.
