# Flattening between blotters

**Goal:** dry and set paper flat without crushing texture or embossing.

## Setup
From the bottom: a rigid board, a dry blotter, a polyester or tissue release layer, the sheet, another release layer, a dry blotter, a board, and weight. Choose the weight by paper and stage: very light when damp, heavier when nearly dry.

## Procedure
1. Place the humidified or washed sheet between release layers; lay the blotters over them.
2. Add light weight evenly across the board.
3. Change the blotters after the first hour or two, when they become damp, and again later; repeat until the sheet is dry. Slow drying with fresh blotters reduces tidelines and cockling.
4. For larger distortions, stage the pressing with intermediate humidification.
5. Keep the sheet in the press until fully dry; a sheet released while damp curls.

## Cautions
- Heavy pressure can flatten impasto, embossing and plate marks; keep weight low for those.
- Do not press friable media.
- Blotters may be acidic or contaminated; use clean, good-quality ones.

## Verify
- [ ] Sheet lies flat when released.
- [ ] Texture and embossing preserved.
- [ ] Number of blotter changes recorded.
