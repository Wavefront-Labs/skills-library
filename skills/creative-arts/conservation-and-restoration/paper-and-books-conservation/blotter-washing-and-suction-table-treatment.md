# Blotter washing and suction table treatment

**Goal:** apply moisture only where needed and pull dissolved material out through the sheet.

## Blotter washing
1. Spot-test media first (see `spot-testing-media-solubility-before-washing`).
2. Place the sheet on a layer of absorbent blotter over a smooth support, with a protective web (Hollytex) between.
3. Apply deionised or adjusted water by brush or fine spray, from the centre outward, so water wicks into the blotter below and carries soluble material with it.
4. Replace the blotter as it takes up colour; stop when the blotter stays clean.

## Suction table
A suction table is a perforated plate or plenum with vacuum applied, covered with a porous membrane; the sheet is laid on it and treatment liquid is drawn through.
1. Set a low suction level (adjustable); lay a web layer then the sheet.
2. Apply liquid with a brush locally. The vacuum pulls it through to a blotter or the table, carrying degradation products.
3. Keep the sheet damp but not wet; control suction to avoid pulling media or fibres through.
4. Use for local stain reduction, tape-residue removal, and humidification as appropriate.

## Cautions
- Strong suction can pull media, pigment or paper fibres, or dent fragile paper.
- Overwetting causes tide lines.
- Avoid on friable or water-sensitive media.

## Verify
- [ ] Media stable after test.
- [ ] No tide line visible after drying.
- [ ] Settings (suction level, solution) recorded.

## Professional note
General guidance only, not a treatment recommendation for a specific object.
