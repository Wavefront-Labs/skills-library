# Chelators and surfactants in paint cleaning

**Goal:** boost a buffered aqueous solution just enough to release bound grime, without
extracting metal ions from the paint or leaving residues.

## Chelators
| Chelator | Typical starting range | Use and risk |
|---|---|---|
| Triammonium citrate (TAC) | 0.5-2% w/v, pH ~7-8 | the standard first step for grime on varnish and unvarnished oil; volatile ammonia helps clearance |
| Sodium citrate | 0.5-2% | similar strength, no ammonia; leaves sodium, clear well |
| EDTA (disodium/tetrasodium) | 0.1-1% | strong; binds calcium and lead - risks chalk grounds, lead white, lead soaps |
| DTPA | low tenths of a percent | stronger again, for specific mineral crusts; specialist use |

- Chelators work by binding metal ions (calcium, iron, lead) that hold grime to the
  surface. The same action removes metal from lead white, chalk, earth pigments and
  metal soaps if contact is long or concentration high.
- Start with the weakest chelator at the lowest concentration that works; raise pH only
  inside the paint's safe range.

## Surfactants
- Nonionic surfactants are the usual choice (Ecosurf EH-6 or similar ethoxylates, Tween
  20); Triton X-100 is restricted in the EU and being phased out.
- Use near the critical micelle concentration (CMC); far above it adds residue without
  more cleaning.
- Surfactant residues are hygroscopic and attract dirt; they demand thorough clearance.

## Procedure
1. Test water-only (buffered, conductivity-matched) first.
2. Add one additive at a time; record which addition made the difference.
3. Apply on small swabs or through a gel to limit contact time; work in short cycles.
4. Clear with the same buffer without additive, then a final water or hydrocarbon rinse,
   see `creative-arts.conservation-and-restoration.paintings-conservation.clearing-residues-after-aqueous-cleaning`.

## Gotchas
- EDTA on a chalk-glue ground exposed in losses will etch it; mask losses or avoid.
- Citrate on iron-containing earth pigments may lighten them.
- Ammonium citrates smell when fresh; mix small batches, they degrade.

## Verify
- [ ] Additive, concentration, pH and conductivity recorded for each test.
- [ ] Weakest effective system chosen; no pigment on swabs.
- [ ] Clearance completed and documented.
