# Choosing a consolidant for paintings

**Goal:** re-attach lifting paint with an adhesive that suits the painting's structure,
will not darken or gloss the surface, and can be re-treated later.

## Ask four questions
1. **Why is it flaking?** Canvas shrinkage, glue-size failure, impact, water damage,
   panel movement. The cause decides whether local consolidation is enough.
2. **Can the painting take water?** Old linen can shrink; water-sensitive paint swells.
3. **Can it take heat?** Impasto flattens; wax-resin linings re-melt; some modern paint
   softens near 40-60 C.
4. **What surface is it?** Matte, unvarnished or porous surfaces darken with resinous or
   glossy consolidants.

## Common consolidants
| Material | Typical prep | Strengths | Watch for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sturgeon glue (isinglass) | 3-5% in water, warm | penetrates fine cracks, strong, traditional; mixes with wheat starch paste for cupped flakes | water and warmth; mould risk if stored |
| Rabbit skin glue / gelatin | 3-10% warm | strong, cheap | stiff, shrinks; moisture responsive |
| Funori / JunFunori | 0.5-2% in water | matte, very low darkening | weaker bond |
| Aquazol 200/500 | 5-15% in water or ethanol | solvent choice, re-activates with heat or solvent | soft in high RH |
| BEVA 371 (solution or film) | heat-activated about 65 C | non-aqueous, reversible with hydrocarbons | needs heat; can fill surface texture |
| Plextol B500 / acrylic dispersions | diluted in water | flexible, low heat | water carrier; harder to reverse |
| Paraloid B-72 | 2-10% in solvent | stable, well known | solvent may affect paint; glossy |
| Klucel G | 1-3% in ethanol | weak, non-aqueous | low strength; for powdery surfaces |

## Test
- Small test on a detached flake area: penetration, darkening, gloss, bond after drying.
- Add a wetting agent (a drop of ethanol in aqueous adhesive) to help penetration if needed.

## Verify
- [ ] Cause of flaking noted; structural treatment considered if widespread.
- [ ] Consolidant, concentration and solvent recorded.
- [ ] No darkening or gloss change at test site after full drying.
