# Dry surface cleaning of paintings

**Goal:** lift loose and loosely bound soiling with no liquid, so later aqueous or solvent
cleaning (if needed at all) starts on a stable, known surface.

## Before you start
- Examine in raking light and under magnification: lifting, flaking or tenting paint is
  consolidated *first*. Any loose flake will catch a brush or sponge.
- Check whether the painting is varnished. Unvarnished modern paint, matte paint, gouache-
  like passages and powdery grounds can be burnished or picked up by sponges.
- Photograph before, and note the reference area you will compare against.

## Method, gentlest first
1. **Soft brush to vacuum:** a wide soft hake or goat-hair brush sweeps dust towards a
   HEPA vacuum nozzle held just off the surface (variable suction, gauze or screen over
   the nozzle to catch flakes). Never touch the nozzle to the paint.
2. **Air:** a hand bulb blower dislodges dust from impasto and frame rebates.
3. **Sponges, tested in a 1 cm square at the edge:**
   - cosmetic latex-free make-up sponges (very gentle);
   - vulcanised rubber "smoke" or chemical sponges (Absorene-type, Wishab) - dab and lift,
     do not rub; cut to a clean face often; they shed crumbs that must be brushed off;
   - polyurethane erasers or grated vinyl eraser are for robust varnished surfaces only.
4. Work in small sections on a grid; change sponge faces when they grey.
5. Brush and vacuum away every crumb; sponge crumbs left in interstices go grey and
   sticky over time.

## Stop and escalate when
- the swab or sponge shows *colour* (paint pickup) - stop immediately;
- the surface becomes shinier or more matte (burnishing or abrasion);
- soiling is greasy or bound in the varnish: dry methods are done; move to tested aqueous
  cleaning, see `creative-arts.conservation-and-restoration.paintings-conservation.aqueous-cleaning-with-controlled-ph-and-conductivity`.

## Gotchas
- Cracked paint catches sponge edges: dab perpendicular rather than dragging along cracks.
- Sponges and erasers vary between batches; retest a new pack.
- The reverse and stretcher bars hold years of dust; vacuum them (painting face down on a
  padded support only if the paint is stable) so it does not drift back onto the front.

## Verify
- [ ] Test squares recorded with result and material used.
- [ ] No colour on sponges; no change in gloss under raking light.
- [ ] Surface checked under magnification for crumbs before moving on.
- [ ] Treatment report records materials and areas cleaned.
