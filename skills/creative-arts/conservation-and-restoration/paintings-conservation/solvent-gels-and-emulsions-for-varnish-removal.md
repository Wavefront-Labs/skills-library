# Solvent gels and emulsions for varnish removal

**Goal:** reach the solvent strength the test ladder showed is needed while cutting
penetration into paint, solvent exposure and evaporation.

## Choose the delivery system
| System | What it is | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Carbopol solvent gels (Wolbers) | polyacrylic acid thickener neutralised with Ethomeen C-12 / C-25 amines | classic; amine residues must be cleared, often with the parent solvent and a hydrocarbon |
| Xanthan / Klucel G gels | cellulose or polysaccharide thickeners | for polar solvents and water; simpler residues |
| Pemulen TR-2 emulsions | polymeric emulsifier giving oil-in-water or water-in-oil emulsions | delivers water and solvent phases together |
| Silicone emulsions (Velvesil Plus with siloxane solvent) | water or polar solvent dispersed in silicone | low polarity carrier, low penetration |
| Loaded sheets (Evolon CR) | microfilament non-woven soaked with solvent | no gel residue; contact time controls action |
| Chemical hydrogels / organogels (Nanorestore, pHEMA/PVP) | retentive gels loaded with solvent | lift cleanly; reusable for a single job |

## Procedure
1. Base the solvent on the free-solvent test results (see
   `creative-arts.conservation-and-restoration.paintings-conservation.solvent-testing-and-varnish-removal`);
   gels typically allow one step *less* polar to work.
2. Test on a small window: apply, cover with Melinex to slow evaporation if needed, time
   it (30 s - 3 min typical), lift, swab off softened varnish with a dry or barely damp swab.
3. Clear residues with the clearance solvent recommended for the gel; check in UV.
4. Scale up in small panels following the form of the image.

## Gotchas
- Gels dwell; the same solvent can do more damage on a sensitive glaze in a gel left
  too long than on a swab. Time every application.
- Residue of thickeners and amine neutralisers has been found on surfaces years later;
  favour systems that lift cleanly (sheets, rigid gels) where possible.
- Cyclic siloxanes (D4/D5) are being restricted; check current suppliers.

## Verify
- [ ] System, solvent, contact time and clearance recorded.
- [ ] No residues visible in UV; surface even in raking light.
- [ ] Window reviewed dry before scaling up.
