# Hook-and-loop hanging for flat textiles

**Goal:** a hanging system that distributes load evenly and is removable.

## Components
- Soft loop side of hook-and-loop tape, sewn to a cotton tape which is stitched to the textile's top edge.
- Hook side fixed to a wooden or aluminium batten, covered with a barrier layer; the batten is fixed to the wall or frame.
- Never attach the hook side to the textile (it snags).

## Method
1. Assess the textile's strength; support weak edges first (see `stabilisation-stitching-onto-a-support-fabric`).
2. Sew cotton twill tape along the top, using fine stitches at close, regular intervals through the strongest thread-lines; use a curved needle.
3. Sew the loop tape to the cotton tape with machine or hand stitching.
4. Seal and cover the batten with barrier film or sealed wood, then fix the hook side on it; check wooden battens (see `oddy-test-for-display-materials`).
5. Press the loop tape onto the hooks, starting at one end and working across to share the load.
6. Check the textile hangs flat and square without ripples; also reduce load with side tapes if heavy.

## Cautions
- Heavy textiles need extra support; consider a lining or a sleeve plus hook-and-loop.
- Keep the system dust-free.

## Verify
- [ ] Load spread across whole top edge.
- [ ] Textile hangs square; no stress at the stitching.
- [ ] Textile can be removed without damage.
