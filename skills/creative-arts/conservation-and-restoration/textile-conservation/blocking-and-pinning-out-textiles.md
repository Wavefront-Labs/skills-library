# Blocking and pinning out textiles

**Goal:** return a textile to its square, flat form after wet or humidified treatment.

## Equipment
- A flat board covered with polyethylene foam and clean cotton or polyester, wide enough for the textile.
- Rustproof pins (stainless steel) and cotton tape or polyester strips for edges.
- Squares and a ruler; a record of original dimensions.

## Method
1. Lay the damp, supported textile on the board, face up.
2. Align the warp and weft with the board's grid; smooth gently from the centre outward; do not stretch.
3. Pin edges at intervals, or attach tapes along the edges and pin the tapes, rather than piercing the textile.
4. Compare measurements with the original; ease into shape but do not force.
5. Dry in a stable, ventilated room away from light and heat; avoid hair dryers.
6. Release only when dry; check dimensions.

## Cautions
- Pins in fragile edges leave holes and tears; use tapes.
- Leaving pins in damp textile can cause stains on rusting.
- Large textiles need multiple people.

## Verify
- [ ] Grain aligned and edges straight.
- [ ] Original dimensions restored without strain.
- [ ] Dried flat; pins removed without damage.
