# Treatment proposal and treatment report

**Goal:** a clear agreement before treatment, and a permanent record after it.

## Proposal (before treatment)
Contents, as the AIC Guidelines for Practice, ECCO and Icon standards describe documentation:
1. Object identification and owner.
2. Condition summary and findings of examination or analysis.
3. Aims of treatment and what will not be attempted.
4. Proposed steps and materials; alternatives and the option of no treatment.
5. Risks and expected changes in appearance.
6. Time and cost; who will do the work and any subcontracting.
7. Signature of the owner or agent giving informed consent, and the date.

## Treatment report (after treatment)
1. Identification, date, conservator.
2. Summary of condition before and after.
3. Materials with trade names, suppliers and concentrations where known.
4. Steps in order, with any variations from the proposal and why.
5. Results, unresolved issues and recommendations for future care.
6. Photographs and diagrams, referenced by file name.
7. Signature and date; stored permanently with the object's file.

## Practices
- Write the report during treatment, not from memory.
- Record any unexpected finding and communicate it to the owner.

## Verify
- [ ] Owner consent signed before work.
- [ ] Report matches what was done.
- [ ] Records filed and backed up.

## Professional note
General guidance only, not a treatment recommendation for a specific object. Work on heritage objects needs the judgement of a qualified conservator, working to the AIC Code of Ethics and Guidelines for Practice (or the ECCO / Icon equivalent) and the owner's consent; test, document and prefer the minimum intervention. Check current safety data sheets and local regulations for every material named.
