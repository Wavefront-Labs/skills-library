# Loan condition reports

**Goal:** show whether an object changed in transit or on display, and by how much.

## Stages
1. **Before packing:** full report and photographs, signed by the lender's representative.
2. **On arrival and unpacking:** the borrower checks against the departure report, with the courier present where possible.
3. **After installation:** a second check; record the display conditions.
4. **At de-installation and before repacking:** another check.
5. **On return:** the lender checks against the departure report.

## Practices
- Use the same template and terms (see `condition-terminology-rating-and-report-templates`) and the same lighting and viewing angles.
- Photograph overall, back, frame and each existing damage; use a scale and keep file names matched to the report.
- Mark areas of concern on diagrams.
- Discrepancies are written, dated and signed by both parties immediately; do not leave them for later.
- Keep copies with the object file and the loan agreement.

## Cautions
- Examine in good light on a safe surface, with support for fragile objects; do not move an object just to check it.
- Do not clean or treat during a loan check.

## Verify
- [ ] Reports exist for every stage and are signed.
- [ ] Photographs match the same views.
- [ ] Any change recorded and agreed.

## Professional note
General guidance only, not a treatment recommendation for a specific object. Work on heritage objects needs the judgement of a qualified conservator, working to the AIC Code of Ethics and Guidelines for Practice (or the ECCO / Icon equivalent) and the owner's consent; test, document and prefer the minimum intervention. Check current safety data sheets and local regulations for every material named.
