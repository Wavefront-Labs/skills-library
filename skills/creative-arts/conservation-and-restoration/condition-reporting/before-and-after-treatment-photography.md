# Before and after treatment photography

**Goal:** a set of images showing exactly what treatment did.

## Setup
- Fixed camera position on a tripod or copy stand; same lens and focal length; same lighting position for each stage.
- Include a neutral grey or colour target and a scale in the frame (see `colour-targets-and-image-metadata`).
- Shoot raw files, plus processed derivatives.

## Shots
1. Overall front, back and sides.
2. Details of each area to be treated.
3. Raking light and, where appropriate, UV (see `examination-under-raking-light-and-uv`).
4. During treatment: key steps, test areas and any discoveries.
5. After treatment: repeat every before shot.

## Practices
- Mark camera and light positions (tape marks or a diagram) so the after shots match.
- Name files with accession number, view and stage; record metadata.
- Back up on at least two media.

## Cautions
- Take care with UV and bright lights near sensitive objects; keep exposure brief.

## Verify
- [ ] Matched before and after views.
- [ ] Scale and colour target in each series.
- [ ] Files named and backed up.

## Professional note
General guidance only, not a treatment recommendation for a specific object. Work on heritage objects needs the judgement of a qualified conservator, working to the AIC Code of Ethics and Guidelines for Practice (or the ECCO / Icon equivalent) and the owner's consent; test, document and prefer the minimum intervention. Check current safety data sheets and local regulations for every material named.
