# Packing for an internal move

**Goal:** protect objects on short moves between rooms or buildings.

## Materials
Acid-free boxes and trays, polyethylene foam, tissue, polyester film, Tyvek, padded crates and trolleys with soft liners; labels and tags.

## Method
1. Sort objects by size, material and fragility; choose the right container for each group.
2. Check condition and stabilise loose parts before packing.
3. Place objects in trays or boxes with padding so they cannot touch each other or move; do not stack items that can crush each other.
4. Pack heavy items low and light items on top; do not overfill.
5. Label each container with IDs and contents, orientation and handling instructions.
6. Use trolleys with padding and brakes; check the route for thresholds, ramps and lifts; use enough people.
7. Unpack at the destination in the planned order, checking each item against the list (see `planning-and-tracking-a-collection-move`).

## Cautions
- Do not carry more than is safe in one trip.
- Keep sensitive items out of heat, damp and direct sun on the route.

## Verify
- [ ] Objects cannot touch or shift in containers.
- [ ] Route checked and clear.
- [ ] Labels and lists match contents.

## Professional note
General guidance only, not a treatment recommendation for a specific object. Work on heritage objects needs the judgement of a qualified conservator, working to the AIC Code of Ethics and Guidelines for Practice (or the ECCO / Icon equivalent) and the owner's consent; test, document and prefer the minimum intervention. Check current safety data sheets and local regulations for every material named.
