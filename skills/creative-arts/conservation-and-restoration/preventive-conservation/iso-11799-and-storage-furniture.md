# ISO 11799 and storage furniture

**Goal:** a repository and furniture that protect collections and staff.

## Standards
- **ISO 11799:2015** (Information and documentation, document storage requirements for archive and library materials): sets requirements for the building, environment and safety of repositories, with guidance on location, structure, fire, water, and conditions for different materials.
- **BS EN 16893:2018** covers heritage storage buildings in a European context.
Consult the standards for numeric values and apply them to your site.

## Furniture
- Prefer powder-coated or baked-enamel steel shelving, with fully cured coatings; avoid raw wood, particle board and fibreboard which emit acids and formaldehyde, unless sealed and tested (see `oddy-test-for-display-materials`).
- Shelves strong enough for the load, adjustable, and with edges that do not catch.
- Pull-out racks for paintings, flat files for works on paper, and drawers with inert linings.
- Compact mobile shelving saves space; check floor loading, fire code and safe operation.
- Keep the lowest shelf off the floor and keep space under ceilings and sprinklers.

## Layout
- Aisles for safe handling; clear access to exits; heavy objects at low levels.
- Seismic restraint where earthquakes occur.

## Verify
- [ ] Standards checked for the requirements.
- [ ] Furniture material emissions considered.
- [ ] Floor loading and fire rules verified by competent people.

## Professional note
General guidance only, not a treatment recommendation for a specific object. Work on heritage objects needs the judgement of a qualified conservator, working to the AIC Code of Ethics and Guidelines for Practice (or the ECCO / Icon equivalent) and the owner's consent; test, document and prefer the minimum intervention. Check current safety data sheets and local regulations for every material named.
