# Pollutant monitoring with A-D strips and sorbents

**Goal:** detect harmful gases and reduce them.

## Pollutants
Acetic and formic acid (from wood, adhesives, acetate film), formaldehyde, sulphur gases, nitrogen oxides, ozone, and particulates.

## Monitoring
- **A-D strips (Image Permanence Institute):** originally made to measure acetate film decay, they respond to acetic acid vapour by changing colour from blue to green to yellow; use inside closed containers or cases with a standard exposure time.
- **Passive diffusion tubes and badges:** laboratory-analysed samplers for acetic acid, formaldehyde and others.
- **Corrosion coupons:** metal coupons (silver, copper) exposed for a period then graded; the ISA 71.04 reactivity classification is used in environmental control.
- Record, locate and repeat on schedule.

## Reduction
1. Remove the source: replace emitting materials (see `oddy-test-for-display-materials`).
2. Ventilate or add filtered air supply.
3. Use sorbents inside cases and storage: activated carbon cloth or granules, zeolites for some acids, chemical media such as potassium permanganate on alumina for oxidisable gases. Keep them away from direct contact with objects, and replace them as they saturate.
4. Check results with a repeat measurement.

## Cautions
- Sorbents saturate; unreplaced sorbent can release pollutant.
- Do not seal degrading acetate film in sealed cases with other objects.

## Verify
- [ ] Baseline and follow-up readings recorded.
- [ ] Sorbent replacement schedule set.
- [ ] Sources identified and removed.
