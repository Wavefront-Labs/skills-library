# Display case specification and facility reports

**Goal:** cases and venues that meet the objects' needs.

## Display case specification
BS EN 15999-1 gives guidelines for the design of showcases for exhibition and conservation of cultural heritage. Points for a specification:
1. **Materials:** inert and tested (see `oddy-test-for-display-materials`); sealed finishes; low emission.
2. **Air exchange rate:** the rate at which case air is replaced by room air, stated as changes per day; many conservation cases aim for well under one change per day, tight cases lower. Measure with a tracer gas or humidity-decay method; seals and gaskets matter.
3. **Humidity buffering:** a silica gel tray or buffer panel as required (see `conditioning-silica-gel-and-using-dehumidifiers`).
4. **Lighting:** external or fibre-optic lighting to avoid heat; control UV and lux (see `uv-filtering-for-light-sources-and-glazing`).
5. **Security and access:** locking, laminated glass, secure fixing, easy but controlled access for staff.
6. **Stability and vibration:** rigid base, no resonance, earthquake restraint where needed.

## Facility reports
Loan agreements use a standard facility report (a widely used form is from the Registrars Committee of the American Alliance of Museums). Describe or request:
- building construction, fire detection and suppression, security staff and systems;
- environmental control and records;
- gallery layouts, handling and storage;
- couriers' access, and insurers' requirements.

## Verify
- [ ] Case specification includes materials, air exchange and light.
- [ ] Facility report completed and checked with registrars.
- [ ] Case test results recorded.

## Professional note
General guidance only, not a treatment recommendation for a specific object. Work on heritage objects needs the judgement of a qualified conservator, working to the AIC Code of Ethics and Guidelines for Practice (or the ECCO / Icon equivalent) and the owner's consent; test, document and prefer the minimum intervention. Check current safety data sheets and local regulations for every material named.
