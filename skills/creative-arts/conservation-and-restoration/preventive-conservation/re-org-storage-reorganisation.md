# RE-ORG storage reorganisation

**Goal:** improve storage conditions, access and use of space step by step, with limited budget.

## About RE-ORG
RE-ORG is a method developed by ICCROM with UNESCO and the Canadian Conservation Institute for museum staff to reorganise storage. It is documented in the RE-ORG handbook on the ICCROM site, which should be the source for the exact steps, forms and workshops. In broad terms it works through phases: prepare, assess, plan and implement.

## Outline of the work
1. **Prepare:** form a team, define aims and agree responsibilities and rules.
2. **Assess:** measure the storage space, list the collections by type and size, and record what is in poor condition, at risk or lost. Use survey forms to count volumes and shelf needs.
3. **Plan:** group collections by material and handling needs; decide the layout, shelving and furniture needed; draw plans and test them on paper.
4. **Implement:** clean, move and arrange in stages; label; update location records (see `planning-and-tracking-a-collection-move`).
5. **Review:** check that new storage works and fix problems.

## Practices
- Work from the largest improvements to the small ones.
- Keep a record of before and after, including photographs.
- Involve those who use the collection.

## Verify
- [ ] Team and aims agreed in writing.
- [ ] Space and collection measurements recorded.
- [ ] Locations updated in the register after each move.

## Professional note
General guidance only, not a treatment recommendation for a specific object. Work on heritage objects needs the judgement of a qualified conservator, working to the AIC Code of Ethics and Guidelines for Practice (or the ECCO / Icon equivalent) and the owner's consent; test, document and prefer the minimum intervention. Check current safety data sheets and local regulations for every material named.
