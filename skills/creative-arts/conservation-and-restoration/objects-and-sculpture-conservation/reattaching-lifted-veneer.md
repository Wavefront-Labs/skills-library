# Reattaching lifted veneer

**Goal:** re-adhere veneer or marquetry without damaging it or the finish.

## Preparation
- Photograph and record the layout. Check for cracks and missing pieces.
- Clean old glue from the veneer and ground gently with a scalpel or damp swab.
- Protect the finish with a release layer (polyester film).

## Method
1. Use traditional hide glue, which is reversible with heat and moisture; prepare it fresh and keep it warm.
2. Insert glue under the lifted area with a spatula or syringe; lightly moisten very dry veneer to avoid cracking.
3. Press down with a heated spatula or a warm caul through the release layer; the heat re-activates the glue, which grips as it cools.
4. Apply even pressure with a caul and clamps or weights until set; use padding to spread load.
5. Wipe squeeze-out immediately.
6. Fill small losses later if agreed.

## Failure modes
- Overheating and blanching or burning the finish.
- Glue squeeze-out staining the surface.
- Veneer cracking when forced flat.

## Verify
- [ ] Veneer sits flat with no cracks.
- [ ] Finish unmarked.
- [ ] Adhesive recorded.
