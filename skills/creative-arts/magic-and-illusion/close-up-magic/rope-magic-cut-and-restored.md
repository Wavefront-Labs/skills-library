# Rope magic

**Goal:** a rope is cut and restored, or three unequal ropes become equal, in a short
parlour routine.

## Principle
- **Cut and restored:** the rope is gathered so that what is cut is a small loop near one
  end, not the middle. The short piece is hidden or tied as a knot and then removed.
- **Professor's Nightmare:** three ropes of different length are made to look equal by a
  count that hides the real lengths, then shown unequal again.

## Routine structure (outline)
1. Show the rope (or three ropes); let it be examined before the method is set.
2. Set up the secret (the loop, the count).
3. Cut, or stretch.
4. Restore or equalise, with a clear visual moment.
5. Hand out the rope (or end clean).

## Common faults
- Small loop visible as it is gathered.
- Rope looks noticeably shorter after restoration; start long enough.
- Too many phases; rope routines tire quickly.

## Fine mechanics
Tarbell's rope lessons and Mark Wilson's course cover both effects in detail.

- [ ] Rope examined at the start without trouble.
