# Borrowed coin and note effects

**Goal:** perform strong magic with money borrowed from the audience, with no prepared
props visible.

## Principle
Borrowed money carries built-in conviction and personal interest. Coins allow the basic
vanishes and productions (French drop, retention vanish, palms). Notes allow tearing and
restoring, transformations to higher denominations, and the signed note found in an
impossible place (a lemon, a wallet). Most note effects rely on a **switch** to a
duplicate, which means you need a matching note of your own.

## Typical structures
- **Coin:** borrow, vanish, reproduce from the spectator's pocket or ear; return.
- **Note change:** a small note changes into a larger one (your own note substituted).
- **Signed note to impossible place:** note signed or serial noted, vanishes, found in a
  pre-set location.
- **Torn and restored:** your duplicate switched for the torn pieces.

## Care
- Never damage a borrowed note you cannot replace exactly.
- Return money visibly and quickly.
- Know the local currency (size, colour, coin weight) where you work.

## Common faults
- Switch duplicates that do not match the borrowed note's denomination or condition.
- Keeping money out of sight too long.

## Verify
- [ ] Effects ready for both coins and notes of the local currency.
- [ ] Return of the money scripted.
- [ ] Duplicate notes match denomination and wear.
