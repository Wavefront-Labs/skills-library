# Loading balls under cups

**Goal:** get a ball under a cup without the audience knowing, so it can "appear" there
later - the core mechanic of every cups and balls routine.

## The basic load
1. A ball is finger palmed in the right hand (curled third and fourth fingers).
2. The right hand picks up a cup by its base, mouth down, lifting it (often to show a ball
   underneath or to move it).
3. As the cup is lifted and brought towards the body, the ball is released from the
   finger palm into the mouth of the cup, which is tilted slightly so the ball rolls in.
4. The cup is set down; the ball is now under it.
The cup's movement covers the ball's journey.

## Stack loads (mouth-up nesting)
When cups are nested mouth-up, a ball can be placed or dropped onto the inner cup and
transferred under another cup during the "stacking" sequence, used for the "ball through
cup" penetration (the classic first phase in many routines).

## Timing
- Load on the **off-beat**: right after a revelation, when the audience looks at the ball
  that just appeared, or as you reach for the wand.
- Never load when attention is on the cup in your hand.

## Gotchas
- Ball sound: soft balls (crocheted or felt) are silent; hard balls click.
- Ball size: the ball must not be visible above the fingers when finger palmed.
- Loading too high above the table: the ball shows as it drops.

## Verify
- [ ] Ten loads on video from the front with no visible ball.
- [ ] Load always paired with a natural cup action and an off-beat.
