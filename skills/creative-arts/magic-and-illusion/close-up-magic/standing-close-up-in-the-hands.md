# Material that works standing in the hands

**Goal:** a repertoire that plays well to a standing group with no table, mat or pockets
of props.

## Principle
Without a table you lose a working surface, a place to hide things (lapping, mat), and
fixed sightlines. Material must work **in the hands**, with the **spectator's hand** or a
card case as the surface. Reveals must happen at chest height where everyone in the group
can see.

## Choosing material
- Prefer effects where the magic happens in a spectator's hand (sponge balls, card under
  the hand, ring onto a borrowed key).
- Avoid effects that need spreads, piles or a mat.
- Keep props few and small: a deck, coins, a sharpie, a small gimmick.
- Reveals at chest height, facing the whole semicircle.

## Adapting a table effect
- Replace table piles with cards held by spectators.
- Replace a spread with a hand-to-hand spread or a fan.
- Check angles again: standing spectators surround you more easily.

## Common faults
- Bending down to a non-existent surface.
- Reveals at waist height that half the group cannot see.
- Effects that need a dropped item picked up.

## Verify
- [ ] Every effect in the set runs with no table.
- [ ] Reveals visible to a group of six standing.
- [ ] Angles tested from 120 degrees around.
