# Building a parlour and close-up show set

**Goal:** a show where a seated or standing audience of 10-50 watches close-up and parlour
magic as a theatrical event rather than strolling encounters.

## Material that plays
- **Close-up for 10-20**: card and coin magic works if everyone can see the table (camera
  and screen for larger groups).
- **Parlour for 20-50**: larger props (ropes, silks, envelopes, cards shown big), effects
  with spectators on their feet, mentalism.
- Test: can someone in the back row follow the effect without seeing small details?

## Structure
1. **Opener**: quick, visual, establishes who you are (1-3 min).
2. **Middle**: 4-6 pieces varying type (cards, objects, mentalism), each with a spectator
   moment.
3. **Breather**: a slower, personal piece.
4. **Climax**: strongest, most involving effect.
5. **Closer and bows**.

## Spectator involvement
Choose helpers from different parts of the room; return them to applause. Plan which
effects need who (see spectator selection).

## Room set-up
- Seating in a shallow arc, rows raked if possible.
- A table at standing or seated height with a close-up mat.
- Light on you and the table, some light on the audience for interaction.

## Gotchas
- Close-up effects that only the front row sees lose the rest.
- Too long: 45 minutes is plenty for most parlour shows.

## Verify
- [ ] Each piece tested from the back of a room of the target size.
- [ ] Set list with timings totals within the booked length.
