# Maintaining and checking magic gimmicks

**Goal:** no trick fails because a prop wore out, a thread broke or a battery died.

## Pre-show checklist (written, every show)
- Each effect: props present, in the right pocket or case position, in reset state.
- Decks: correct stacks set, gaffs in place, no worn or bent cards.
- Gimmicks: thumb tip, thread, elastic, magnets, batteries, electronics charged.
- Spares: spare deck, spare thread, spare load items.

## Deck care
- Rotate performing decks: a deck used for daily shows lasts days to a couple of weeks
  before it clumps or bends. Fan powder helps handling.
- Keep stacked decks banded in their case.
- Replace gaffs with the deck so they match.

## Thread, elastic, magnets
- Replace invisible thread regularly, not just when it breaks.
- Elastic in pulls and holdouts loses tension; replace on schedule.
- Magnets can lose strength or chip; check pull.
- Electronics: fresh batteries before every show; carry spares.

## Repair kit
Spare thread, superglue, double-sided tape, small screwdrivers, scissors, rubber bands,
markers, spare batteries, a lighter (for thread ends), safety pins.

## Gotchas
- Humidity warps cards and props; store in sealed cases with silica gel on tour.
- Airline baggage: carry small essential gimmicks in hand luggage.

## Verify
- [ ] Written checklist signed before every show.
- [ ] Repair kit and spares packed.
