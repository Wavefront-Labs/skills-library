# The coin matrix

**Goal:** four coins at the corners of a square, each covered by a playing card, travel
one by one until all four are under one card.

## Principle
The cards provide cover for loading and stealing coins. As a card is lifted from one
corner and moved, the hand secretly steals the coin beneath it; as a card is placed on the
target corner, a palmed coin is added. The extra coin and the card handling are the whole
method; the audience sees cards simply being picked up and put down.

## Structure (outline)
1. Show four coins and four cards; cover each coin.
2. First coin travels: lift one card (stealing the coin), place it back; reveal two under
   the target card.
3. Repeat for the second and third coins, varying the handling.
4. Last coin: a visible or very clean passage.
5. End clean, with the extra coin disposed of.

## Common faults
- Card handling changes when a steal happens.
- Coins clink when loaded: use a soft mat.
- Extra coin left in the hand at the end.

## Fine mechanics
Schneider's own write-up and Roth's book give the classic handlings; learn card cover
loads from them.

## Verify
- [ ] Card pick-ups look identical with and without a steal.
- [ ] Silent loads on your mat.
- [ ] Hands clean at the finish.
