# Handling hecklers and burners

**Goal:** keep the performance on track with a spectator who interrupts, grabs props or
"burns" your hands, without losing the rest of the group.

## Principle
Most hecklers are not hostile; they want attention, feel challenged by being fooled, or
are showing off to friends. The group's sympathy is the deciding factor. If you humiliate
the heckler, the group sides with them. If you stay friendly and in control, the group
sides with you.

## Techniques
- **Give them a role:** hold the deck, be the "witness", sign the card. A person with a
  job is part of the show.
- **Burners** (staring at your hands): choose effects where hands are clean or the magic
  happens in their hands.
- **Light, friendly lines** rather than put-downs. Laugh at yourself.
- **Switch effect** to something they cannot interfere with.
- **Shorten the set** and leave gracefully if the group is not enjoying it.

## Things never to do
Grab back props roughly, insult, or argue about the method. Never expose a method to win
an argument.

## Common faults
- Ignoring early warning signs and continuing a vulnerable effect.
- Escalating with sharper insults.
- Staying too long.

## Verify
- [ ] Two heckle-proof effects ready in your set.
- [ ] Three friendly lines prepared and rehearsed.
- [ ] Exit line ready for groups that are not working.
