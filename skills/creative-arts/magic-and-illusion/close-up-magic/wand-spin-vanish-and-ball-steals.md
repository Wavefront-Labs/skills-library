# Wand spin vanish and ball steals

**Goal:** make a ball vanish from the hand (and secretly keep it) or secretly remove one
from under a cup - the other half of the load/steal cycle.

## Wand spin vanish (Vernon style)
1. A ball is held at the right fingertips; the wand is held in the same hand, along the
   fingers, or picked up.
2. The right hand apparently places the ball into the left hand (retention vanish or
   simple fake take), keeping it in the right finger palm.
3. The right hand picks up the wand and **spins** it (a twirl or tap), giving a reason for
   the hand to be busy.
4. The left hand opens: the ball has gone. The ball is in the right finger palm, ready to
   load.
The wand gives the right hand a reason to be closed and moving.

## Fake placement under a cup
Apparently place a ball under a cup while keeping it: the ball is pushed under the cup's
edge, but the fingers retain it as the cup is set down.

## Steal from beneath a cup
When a cup is lifted by its base and tilted back towards you, the ball that was under
it rolls against the curled fingers and is taken into the finger palm as the cup lifts
clear; the audience sees an empty space where they expect nothing anyway (or the steal
happens while attention is on a different cup). Each routine has its specific steals;
learn them in context.

## Using the wand
- The wand points to cups, taps them, and makes the magic gesture: every touch is a
  potential cover for a load or steal.
- Hold the wand naturally in the hand that has the finger-palmed ball.
