# Final loads in the cups and balls

**Goal:** the closing surprise: lifting the cups reveals large objects (lemons, limes,
potatoes, baseballs) that could not possibly have been there.

## Storage
- Final loads are kept in pockets (jacket side pockets, often specially enlarged), a
  bag, or a holder on the table edge or under the close-up mat.
- Each load must be accessible with one hand without looking.

## The load sequence
1. The audience believes the routine is over or attention is on another action (e.g. a
   ball vanished into the pocket).
2. The hand going to the pocket to "put away the ball" comes back with the large load
   finger-palmed or held in a curled hand, out of sight behind the cup or hand.
3. The cup is lifted (often to show it is empty, or casually moved) and the load is
   inserted as it is set down, mouth down over the load.
4. Repeat for each cup, using different off-beats (picking up the wand, putting balls away).

## Reveal staging
- Build the expectation that the routine is ending ("and that's the cups and balls").
- Lift each cup slowly, one at a time, increasing surprise; the largest load last.
- Let the audience handle the loads.

## Gotchas
- Load size must fit the cup with a little room; test them.
- Fresh fruit varies in size - carry spares.
- Bulging pockets: wear a jacket that hides the loads.

## Verify
- [ ] Each final load stolen and loaded with a natural reason, ten times.
- [ ] Loads fit the cups and pockets with no bulge on camera.
