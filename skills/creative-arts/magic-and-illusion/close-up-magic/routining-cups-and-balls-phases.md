# Routining cups and balls phases

**Goal:** a routine of 4-8 phases that escalates, never loses track of the balls, and ends in
final loads.

## Classic phase types
1. **Introduction**: show three cups and three balls (or one ball and multiplication).
2. **Penetration**: a ball passes through a cup (stack load).
3. **Vanish and reappearance**: ball vanishes from the hand and appears under a cup.
4. **Assembly**: all balls gather under one cup.
5. **Transposition**: ball travels from one cup to another.
6. **Multiplication / diminishing**: number of balls increases or decreases.
7. **Climax**: final loads.

## Tracking chart
Write a table for each phase:
| Phase | Visible | Under cup A | B | C | Hand (palmed) | Pocket |
Fill it for the beginning and end of each phase. If any cell is unknown, the routine is
not ready. This chart is how professionals rehearse.

## Escalation
- Each phase should be more impossible than the last.
- Change the type of magic: vanish, appearance, transposition, penetration.
- Vary pacing: one slow phase, then a quick one.

## The chop cup
A single-cup routine with a gimmicked cup and ball (magnetic) - a strong alternative or
addition for walk-around; many performers put a chop cup routine in their set.

## Verify
- [ ] Ball-position chart complete for every phase.
- [ ] Routine runs start to finish 10 times with no lost ball.
- [ ] Each phase is a different kind of effect.
