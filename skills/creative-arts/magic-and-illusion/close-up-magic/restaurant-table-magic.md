# Restaurant table magic

**Goal:** a residency where the restaurant sees value, diners enjoy short visits, and you
never interrupt a meal or a waiter.

## With the venue
- Agree fee, nights, hours, and **which tables** to visit (some areas may be off-limits).
- Brief staff: introduce yourself to the manager and servers; they will tell you which
  tables are celebrating or should be left alone.
- Agree how business cards and private bookings are handled.

## When to approach
- **Best**: after ordering and before food, or after mains before dessert.
- **Never**: when food is arriving, mid-meal, during a serious conversation, or when the
  bill is being paid.
- Ask: "Hi, I'm the restaurant's magician - may I show you something quick?" Accept a no
  gracefully.

## Set design
- 5-8 minutes per table, 2-3 effects.
- Material that works standing at a table edge, with little or no table space (plates,
  glasses).
- A strong closer that leaves something behind (a signed card) - a talking point.

## Working around food and staff
- Never put props on plates or near drinks.
- Step aside immediately when a server arrives.
- Keep pockets reset between tables (see walk-around reset).

## Repeat business
Regular diners: keep a list of what they have seen. Hand out cards when asked; many
private bookings come from residencies.

## Verify
- [ ] Staff briefed and table restrictions known.
- [ ] Set fits in 8 minutes with no table space needed.
- [ ] Record of regulars and what they saw.
