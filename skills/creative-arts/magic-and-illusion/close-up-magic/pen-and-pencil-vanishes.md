# Pen and pencil vanishes

**Goal:** make a borrowed pen or pencil vanish impromptu, without gimmicks.

## Principle
Pens are long and hard to palm, so vanishes rely on **misdirection and rhythm** more than
on grip. The classic structure counts "one, two, three" with the pen moving towards the
hand; on an early count the pen is secretly disposed of (tucked behind the ear, into a
sleeve, into the lap when seated), and the hand continues the rhythm empty. The audience
sees the hand open on "three" and the pen is gone. The structure is often played as a
sucker gag for a spectator standing at your side.

## Methods (outline)
- **Behind the ear / rhythm vanish:** count up and down; leave the pen on an off-beat.
- **Sleeving:** with a jacket, a throw action sends the pen up the sleeve.
- **Lapping:** seated, the pen drops to the lap as the hand moves forward.
- **Pen through note / coin:** a different effect, using a slit or a thumb tip.

## Common faults
- Breaking the rhythm at the moment of disposal.
- Disposing in a place the side audience can see.
- Using a pen too long for the method.
