# Svengali and stripper decks

**Goal:** use the two classic gimmicked decks well enough that they fool people who have
seen them sold on the street.

## Svengali deck
- Every other card is a slightly **short** card, all the same (e.g. all 4 of hearts); the
  others are ordinary, all different.
- Riffling from the **back end** (with the short cards' ends aligned) shows all different
  cards; riffling from the front shows only the force card, because short cards drop
  with the long ones.
- Spread or riffle to show "all different", then a spectator takes any card from a
  riffle-stop - it is always the force card.
- Classic effects: forcing a card, "the deck changes to all the same card".

## Stripper deck
- Every card is **tapered** slightly (one end narrower).
- A card turned around in the deck can be stripped out by pulling on the wide ends.
- Classic effects: a selected card is returned reversed and then found instantly, even
  after shuffles by the spectator (overhand shuffles do not change orientation).

## Hiding the limitations
- Svengali: never let a spectator spread or handle it freely; switch it for a normal
  deck at the end.
- Stripper: spectators can shuffle, but do not riffle-shuffle (it can reverse halves).
- Mix with sleight-of-hand effects so not every trick is the "trick deck" one.

## Verify
- [ ] Svengali shown as all different and forced 10 times without flashing duplicates.
- [ ] Stripper deck strip-out done invisibly after a spectator's shuffle.
