# Using a thumb tip

**Goal:** use the thumb tip, the most common magic gimmick, so the audience never suspects
the thumb.

## Choosing one
- Fit: snug enough not to fall off, loose enough to remove quickly. Soft vinyl tips are
  more natural-looking than hard plastic.
- Colour matched to skin as closely as possible.

## Getting it on and off
- On: kept in a pocket or in the fist (the "load" position); the thumb enters it while
  the hand does something natural (taking a silk out of a pocket).
- Off: during a closed-fist action, the thumb is withdrawn leaving the tip in the fist,
  then disposed of in a pocket.

## The golden rule: keep the thumb moving
A thumb tip is visible when stationary and pointing at the audience. It is invisible
when the thumb is behind the hand, in motion, or pointing towards the audience straight
on (end-on). Never wave it around side-on.

## Effects
- **Silk vanish**: a silk is pushed into the left fist, actually into the thumb tip inside
  the fist; the thumb steals it away.
- **Torn and restored paper**: a restored duplicate is in the tip.
- **Salt / liquid vanish**: poured into the fist into the tip.
- **Lit cigarette vanish** (older acts).

## Gotchas
- Pointing with the thumb.
- Keeping the tip on too long: do the effect, then ditch it.
- Close-up surrounded: risky; works best for audiences in front.

## Verify
- [ ] On/off steals invisible on camera.
- [ ] Silk vanish performed with the thumb never shown side-on.
