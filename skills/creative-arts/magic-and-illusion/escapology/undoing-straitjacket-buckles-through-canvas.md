# Undoing buckles through canvas

**Goal:** release the sleeve strap, back buckles and crotch strap after the arms are in
front.

## Principle
Once the arms are in front, the sleeve-end strap can be opened with the teeth or the
fingers through the canvas. The back buckles are released by working through the
canvas; the crotch strap is undone last or slipped. The sequence matters; Gibson describes
Houdini's order.

## Sequence (outline)
1. Sleeve strap released (often with the teeth).
2. Arms free inside the sleeves; work the buckles at the back through the material.
3. Crotch strap released.
4. Jacket pulled off over the head.

## Equipment
- Jackets with standard buckles; check for worn or stiff buckles before each show.
- Crotch strap adjusted to safe tension.

## Common faults
- Stiff buckles that cannot be worked through canvas.
- Losing time on the crotch strap.

## Verify
- [ ] Sequence rehearsed and timed.
- [ ] Buckles inspected before each show.
- [ ] Total escape within the act's time limit.
