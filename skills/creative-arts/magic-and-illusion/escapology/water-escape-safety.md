# Water escape safety

**Goal:** perform water escapes, if at all, with the safety team and engineering that the
risk demands.

## Principle
Water escapes are among the most dangerous in the repertoire and have killed performers. Drowning can follow a
blackout without warning; a struggling performer may not signal. Houdini's water torture
cell had assistants with axes ready. Modern practice relies on **safety divers**, **rapid
access** to the performer and **strict breath-hold limits** set in training.

## Requirements
- **Safety divers** trained in rescue, in or at the tank throughout.
- **Rapid break:** a tank or container that can be opened or broken in seconds.
- **Breath-hold training** under supervision; the time limit is well under your training
  maximum.
- **Medical cover:** a medic with resuscitation equipment present.
- **Rescue rehearsals** every show day.
- No hyperventilation before submerging (it raises blackout risk).

## Abort rules
The safety officer calls the abort at the time limit, regardless of signals.

## Common faults
- Treating the time limit as a target.
- Lacking a tested rapid-break method.

## Verify
- [ ] Safety divers and medic briefed each show.
- [ ] Rapid break tested.
- [ ] Time limit set with professional advice and enforced.
