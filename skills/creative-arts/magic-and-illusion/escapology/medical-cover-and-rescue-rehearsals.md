# Medical cover and rescue rehearsals

**Goal:** if an escape goes wrong, the right people are present and know exactly what to do.

## Principle
Event safety guidance asks organisers to match medical cover to the risk. For escapes,
the risk includes airway obstruction, circulation loss, falls, heat and, for water or
suspension escapes, drowning and inversion effects. A **rescue plan** for each escape,
rehearsed with the people who will use it, turns the abort signal into action.

## Medical cover
- Low-risk escapes (rope, cuffs at ground level): first aider on site.
- Higher-risk escapes (straitjacket suspension, enclosed escapes): paramedic or medic
  with equipment.
- Water escapes: medic with resuscitation equipment plus safety divers.

## Rescue plan per escape
- What can go wrong.
- Who acts, with what tool (keys, shears, bolt cutters, hoist).
- Time limits and abort triggers.
- Route for emergency services.

## Rehearsal
- Full rescue drill on each performance day.
- New team members drilled before their first show.

## Common faults
- Medical cover that cannot reach the performer quickly.
- Rescue plans never rehearsed.

## Verify
- [ ] Rescue plan written for every escape.
- [ ] Medical cover matched to risk and briefed.
- [ ] Drill completed and logged on show days.
