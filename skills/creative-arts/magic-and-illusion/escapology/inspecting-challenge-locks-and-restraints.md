# Inspecting challenge locks and restraints

**Goal:** accept challenge locks and restraints in a way that keeps the act safe and the
challenge fair, and refuse those that are dangerous or rigged.

## Conditions of acceptance (publish them)
- The lock or restraint must be a standard commercial product in working order.
- The **key** must be provided and demonstrated to open and lock it.
- The item is examined by the performer (and optionally a safety officer) privately
  before the show.
- Time limit and safety abort apply (see escape safety).

## Inspection checklist
- Lock opens and closes smoothly with the key several times.
- No glue, solder, broken key fragments or debris in the keyway.
- Shackle and body undamaged; no modifications.
- Handcuffs: correct double-lock function, no tampered pawl.
- Chains: links not welded shut where they should be padlocked.

## Refusing
- Refuse anything modified, jammed, or that you cannot verify with its key.
- Refuse restraints that could cause injury (overtight, sharp, choking risk).
- Announce politely; history shows challengers sometimes bring sabotaged locks
  (Houdini's famous Mirror handcuff challenge involved a specially made cuff).

## During the escape
- Bolt cutters and the key with the safety officer.
- Abort if the lock behaves differently from inspection.

## Verify
- [ ] Written acceptance conditions published with the challenge.
- [ ] Each challenge item inspected with its key before the show.
- [ ] Bolt cutters at hand during the escape.
