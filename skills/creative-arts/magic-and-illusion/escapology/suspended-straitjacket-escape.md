# Suspended straitjacket escape

**Goal:** escape from a straitjacket while hanging upside down from a crane or truss, with
the danger theatrical and the safety real.

## Principle
Houdini's suspended straitjacket escape turned the struggle into public spectacle. The
escape is the same as on the ground; the risks are added by suspension: blood rushing to
the head, ankle injuries, falls, and the rigging itself. **Professional rigging is
mandatory.** Donovan's Entertainment Rigging is a standard reference for the engineering.

## Safety requirements
- Rigging designed and installed by a qualified rigger with load-rated equipment.
- Padded ankle harness, rated and inspected.
- Strict inverted time limit; your medical team sets it.
- Lowering plan: the crane or hoist can lower you immediately at any time.
- Safety officer, rigger and medic on site.

## Staging
- Escape over open ground or a soft landing area.
- The audience sees the struggle clearly; the jacket drops at the end.
- Hold the final pose before lowering.

## Common faults
- Using non-rated rigging or amateur riggers.
- Overstaying the inverted time limit.

## Verify
- [ ] Rigging certified and documented.
- [ ] Time limit set by medical advice and enforced.
- [ ] Lowering plan rehearsed.
