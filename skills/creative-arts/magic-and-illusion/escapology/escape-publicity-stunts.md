# Escape publicity stunts

**Goal:** a public escape that draws press and crowds to promote a show, executed with the
same safety discipline as a stage act.

## Classic formats
- **Suspended straitjacket** from a crane or building (Houdini's signature publicity stunt).
- Escapes from boxes or chains in a public square.
- Water escapes (very high risk; specialist teams only).

## Permits and approvals
- Local authority permission for public space; police for crowd and traffic.
- Crane hire with certified operator and lift plan; rigging signed off by a competent
  rigger; performer suspension as a people-lifting operation.
- Insurance covering the stunt and public liability.

## Safety team
- Rigger, crane operator, safety officer with stop authority, medic.
- Rescue plan: how to lower the performer instantly (see suspension safety).
- Rehearsed at height in the actual rig before the day.

## Press and crowd
- Announce time and place with the show's details; give press a photo position.
- Crowd barriers; keep the fall zone clear.
- Run on time; delays lose crowds and media.

## If it goes wrong
- Abort, lower, and say so honestly. A safe failure can still be a news story; an injury
  ends a career.

## Verify
- [ ] All permits and lift plan in writing.
- [ ] Rescue rehearsed in the rig.
- [ ] Press brief issued with timing.
