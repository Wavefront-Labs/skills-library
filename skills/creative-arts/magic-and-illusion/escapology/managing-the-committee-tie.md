# Managing the committee tie

**Goal:** a committee of audience volunteers ties you as they wish, and the result is still
escapable.

## Principle
A committee tie raises conviction because the audience saw ordinary people do the tying.
The performer manages it through **briefing**, **rope choice** and **position**: the
amount of rope, the start position and polite guidance limit how severe the tie can be.

## Managing
1. Choose volunteers who look cooperative.
2. Hand them a set length of rope; less rope limits the complexity.
3. Offer your start position (wrists behind, seated) and suggest an order ("start with the
   wrists").
4. Comment and joke as they tie; keep it light and quick.
5. The safety officer watches every knot near joints, neck and circulation.

## Common faults
- Overenthusiastic volunteers with too much rope.
- Allowing a tie to drag on.
- Volunteers left standing awkwardly afterwards.

## Verify
- [ ] Rope length fixed and tested.
- [ ] Briefing lines scripted.
- [ ] Volunteers thanked and returned with applause.
