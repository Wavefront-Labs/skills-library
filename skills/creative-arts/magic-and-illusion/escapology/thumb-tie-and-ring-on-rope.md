# Thumb tie and ring on rope

**Goal:** thumbs are tied together tightly, yet hoops, rings and arms of volunteers pass
on and off them.

## Principle
The thumb tie is associated with the Japanese performer Ten Ichi. A tie that looks
inescapable leaves a moment where one thumb can be freed and replaced instantly. With that
moment, solid hoops or rings appear to penetrate the tie. **Ring on rope** effects use
related release principles with a rope held by both ends.

## Staging
1. Thumbs tied by a volunteer with string or tape.
2. Show the tie clearly.
3. Catches: hoops thrown and "caught" on the arms, a volunteer's arm passed through.
4. Each catch quick, with a visual pause.
5. Volunteer unties the thumbs to show the tie is still intact.

## Common faults
- Releasing the thumb too visibly.
- Too many catches.

## Fine mechanics
Tarbell teaches the classic thumb tie in detail.

## Verify
- [ ] Release and replacement under a second.
- [ ] Tie survives the volunteer's inspection.
- [ ] Routine runs under three minutes.
