# Arm-over-head release

**Goal:** get the crossed arms over the head so they are in front, making the buckles
reachable.

## Principle
With slack gained during strapping, the escapologist pushes the elbow of the top arm up
and over the head, bringing both arms in front. Houdini made this visible in his
suspended straitjacket escapes, turning the struggle into the show. It demands shoulder
flexibility and strength.

## Conditioning
- Shoulder mobility work daily; warm up thoroughly before every escape.
- Build the move gradually in rehearsal, never at full force cold.
- Stop if there is shoulder pain; dislocation is a real risk.

## Staging
- Visible struggle at the moment the arms go over sells difficulty.
- Bend forward at the waist; the movement reads as effort.
- Pause, breathe, then continue.

## Common faults
- Forcing the move without slack.
- Performing cold.

## Verify
- [ ] Warm-up routine done before each escape.
- [ ] Release done reliably without shoulder strain.
- [ ] Struggle choreography rehearsed for the audience.
