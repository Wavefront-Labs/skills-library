# The Hindu shuffle force

**Goal:** the spectator stops you during a shuffle, you show them the card "where they
stopped" - always the card on the bottom of the deck.

## The Hindu shuffle
1. The right hand holds the deck from above by the ends, thumb at the inner end and
   fingers at the outer end, face down.
2. The left thumb and fingers pull small packets off the **top** of the deck, letting
   them fall into the left palm.
3. The right hand moves back and forth, the left hand taking packet after packet, until
   the right hand has a small packet, which is dropped on top.
It looks like an ordinary, casual shuffle.

## The force
1. Force card on the bottom of the deck.
2. Start the Hindu shuffle: the right hand always retains the bottom of the deck, so the
   force card stays on the bottom of the right-hand packet throughout.
3. Ask the spectator to call "stop" at any time.
4. On "stop", raise the right-hand packet to show its bottom card: the force card.
   ("Remember this card.")
5. Drop the right-hand packet on the left-hand cards; the force card is now in the
   middle, and you can continue shuffling.

## Tips
- Start before asking for "stop"; the request comes naturally mid-shuffle.
- Show the card by tilting the right-hand packet towards the spectator, then lower it
  without looking yourself (mentalism: you "do not know" it).
- The Hindu shuffle also serves as a control: a selected card returned on the left-hand
  packet can be brought to the bottom.

## Gotchas
- Flashing the bottom card before the force: keep the deck face down and level.
- Shuffling too fast: the spectator cannot stop it; take your time.

- [ ] The shuffle looks casual on video.
- [ ] Force delivered at any stop point, including at the very start.
