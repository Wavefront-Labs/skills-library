# The ambitious card routine

**Goal:** a signed card inserted into the middle repeatedly appears on top, each phase
more impossible, finishing with a climax that cannot be repeated.

## Principle
Repetition only works when **each phase removes a solution** the audience may have formed.
Vary the method (double lift, tilt, pass, side steal) so no single explanation covers all
phases, and raise the conditions each time (inserted fairly, pushed through slowly, card
held by spectator).

## Structure (outline)
1. **Sign the card.** Signed cards cannot be duplicates.
2. **Phase 1:** card into the middle, appears on top (double lift).
3. **Phase 2:** inserted visibly and slowly (tilt), still on top.
4. **Phase 3:** card placed on the bottom or sides, rises again.
5. **Phase 4:** spectator's own hand on the deck.
6. **Climax:** something different, the card in a pocket, in a box, under the
   spectator's hand, folded, or stuck in the deck.

Three to five phases are typical; stop while it is still strong.

## Common faults
- Same method every phase, so the audience solves it by phase three.
- Too many phases: the routine drops after the high point.
- No climax: the last phase is just another rise.

## Verify
- [ ] Each phase uses a different method or condition.
- [ ] Final phase is different in kind from the rises.
- [ ] Routine runs under four minutes, signed card returned.
