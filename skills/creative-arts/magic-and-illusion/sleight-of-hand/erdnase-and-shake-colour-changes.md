# Erdnase and shake colour changes

**Goal:** the face of the top card visibly changes into a different card as the hand
passes over it or the card is shaken.

## Principle
Every colour change is a secret exchange covered by motion. In the **Erdnase change** a
card palmed in the hand is left on the face of the deck as the hand passes over it while
the old face card is removed. In the **shake change** a card held in the hand is
apparently shaken and transforms because the hidden card behind it is swapped in front.
The motion must be faster than the eye can follow, but read as "just a wave".

## Method (outline)
- **Erdnase-style change:** face card shown; the palming hand passes over the face, leaves
  a palmed card and removes the original. One pass, no pause.
- **Shake change:** two cards held as one at the outer end; a sharp shake releases the
  rear card forward while the front card is retained behind.

## Common faults
- Hand pausing on the deck.
- Card visible during the leave or the steal.
- No motivation for the passing hand: give it a reason (rubbing off ink, a wave).

## Fine mechanics
Erdnase's transformations chapter and Card College vol 2 teach the exact grips; practise
from these sources with video.

## Verify
- [ ] Change looks instant on video at normal speed.
- [ ] Hand passes over the deck in the same time it takes with no change.
- [ ] Repeatable ten times without flashing.
