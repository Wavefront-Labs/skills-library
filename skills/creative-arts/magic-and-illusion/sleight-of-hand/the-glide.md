# The glide

**Goal:** show a card on the bottom of the deck, then apparently take that card while
actually taking the one above it.

## Grip
- The left hand holds the deck **face down from above**: thumb on one long side, fingers
  on the other, the hand turned palm-down so the bottom card can be shown by tilting the
  hand up.
- The left third (or second) finger rests on the bottom card near its outer right corner.

## The action
1. Tilt the left hand to show the bottom card; lower it face down again.
2. The left fingertip draws the bottom card **back** (towards your wrist) about 1-2 cm.
   This is the glide; it is hidden by the hand.
3. The right fingers reach under the front of the deck and draw out "the bottom card" -
   actually the second card from the bottom, now exposed at the front.
4. Push the glided card back square later.

## Uses
- **Glide force**: the spectator calls stop as you deal cards from the bottom; you deal
  the card above the glided force card each time, then on "stop" deal the force card.
- **Switch**: show a card, deal a different one face down.
- Classic Royal Road effects (e.g. the "Glide" transpositions) use it repeatedly.

## Errors
- Gliding the card too far: it shows at the back.
- Pausing before drawing out: keep rhythm identical to a real deal.
- Squaring the glided card back with a visible push - do it during another action.

## Verify
- [ ] Ten glides on camera with no visible back step.
- [ ] Glide force delivered at any "stop".
