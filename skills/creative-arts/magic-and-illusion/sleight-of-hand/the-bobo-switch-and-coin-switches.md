# The Bobo switch and coin switches

**Goal:** apparently toss a coin from one hand to the other while a different coin arrives,
so a borrowed coin becomes a different coin or two coins swap.

## The Bobo switch
1. Coin A is visible on the right fingers; coin B is classic palmed in the right hand.
2. The right hand makes a tossing motion into the left hand.
3. As the hand turns over to toss, the visible coin A is retained (the fingers curl it in),
   while coin B is released from the palm and falls into the left hand.
4. The left hand closes on coin B. Coin A is now in the right hand.
The toss and the sound of a coin landing in the left hand sell it.

## Uses
- **Copper/silver transposition**: a copper coin in one hand, silver in the other, they
  change places.
- **Borrowed coin to foreign coin**: the spectator's coin "changes" into another.
- Loading a coin into a spectator's hand.

## Details
- **Sound**: the arriving coin should make one clean sound; avoid the two coins clinking
  in the right hand.
- **Hand distance**: short toss, hands close together.
- Look at the left hand as it catches.

## Other switches (for reference)
- The **thumb palm switch**: take a visible coin with one hand while a palmed coin is
  shown.
- Gimmicked switches using shells (see coin gimmicks).

## Verify
- [ ] Ten switches with no double clink.
- [ ] Toss looks identical to a real toss on camera.
