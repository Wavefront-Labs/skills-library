# Replacing palmed cards

**Goal:** get palmed cards back into the deck without any visible action - often the
moment palming is detected, because the hand finally has to "do" something.

## Top replacement when retrieving the deck
1. The spectator has shuffled the deck. Hold out the left hand for it.
2. The right hand (with the palmed card) takes the deck from the spectator from above,
   and as it places the deck into the left hand, the palmed card simply settles on top.
3. Square the deck - the replacement is hidden inside the natural squaring motion.

## Bottom replacement
The right hand comes over the deck; the palmed card is added to the bottom as the deck
is lifted or tabled. Useful when the card should end up at the bottom for a later force.

## During a cut
The palmed card is added to the top of the lower half as the right hand completes a
cut, putting it in the middle.

## Motivation
Replacement must be part of a real action:
- Taking the deck back after a shuffle.
- Squaring before a deal.
- Cutting the deck.
Avoid "extra" squaring with no reason.

## Gotchas
- Talking cards: a replaced card that sticks out (a "step") - square firmly.
- Replacing too soon after palming makes the hand's journey look purposeful; give the
  hand a moment of rest first.

## Verify
- [ ] Top replacement while taking a deck from a spectator, invisible from the front.
- [ ] Replacement always paired with a natural reason.
