# Double lift get-ready and the push-off break

**Goal:** separate exactly two cards from the top of the deck and hold a break beneath
them, invisibly and before the audience knows a card is about to be shown.

## Principle
Most double lifts are lost in the get-ready, not the turnover. The break must be taken
**early**, during an action with another purpose (squaring, talking, gesturing), so that
when the moment comes the hand simply turns "the top card".

## Methods (outline)
- **Push-off:** the left thumb pushes the top two cards slightly to the right as a block;
  the left little finger catches a break beneath them as they are squared back.
- **Thumb count / lift at the inner end:** the right thumb, in end grip, riffles up the
  inner end and releases cards until two remain above; the left little finger takes the
  break.
- **Buckle:** the left first finger buckles the bottom card(s) for get-readies from below
  (used for bottom doubles and some counts).

## Timing
Take the break while saying something that pulls attention up (asking a name, a joke), and
hold it while doing something else. The double lift itself then happens with no pause.

## Common faults
- Fiddling: several attempts at the push-off.
- Taking the break at the moment of the lift.
- Three cards instead of two: check with the thumb at the inner end.

## Verify
- [ ] Break of exactly two cards obtained twenty times in a row by feel.
- [ ] Get-ready done during speech and invisible on video.
- [ ] Lift follows with no hesitation.
