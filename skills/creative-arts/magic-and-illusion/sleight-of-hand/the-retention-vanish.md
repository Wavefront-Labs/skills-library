# The retention vanish

**Goal:** the coin is seen to be placed into the left hand, which closes on it - but the
coin never left the right hand. The audience "sees" it go in because the image persists
for a moment.

## The handling
1. The coin is held at the right fingertips (thumb below, first and second fingers above),
   face towards the audience.
2. The left hand is open, palm up. The right hand places the coin on the left palm, its
   lower edge touching the palm.
3. The left fingers start to close over the coin. As they cover it, the right thumb
   **pulls the coin back** into the right fingers (finger palm or classic palm) while the
   right hand appears to leave it.
4. The left hand closes completely and moves away slightly; the right hand drops away
   naturally.

## Why it works
For a fraction of a second the coin is seen on the left palm as the fingers close. The
eye keeps that image, and the closed left hand is assumed to contain the coin.

## Timing
- The coin must be visible on the left palm until the left fingers cover it.
- The retention happens as the fingers close, not before.
- Practise placing the coin genuinely many times; the fake must match speed and rhythm
  exactly.

## Selling
- Left hand closes gently around "the coin" and holds it with weight.
- Attention and eyes on the left hand.
- Vanish with a small crumble or rub of the left fingers, then open slowly.

## Verify
- [ ] Genuine placement and retention vanish identical on video side by side.
- [ ] Right hand relaxed after the vanish, no flash of the coin.
