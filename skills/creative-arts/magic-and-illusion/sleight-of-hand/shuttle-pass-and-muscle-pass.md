# Shuttle pass and muscle pass

**Goal:** two advanced coin techniques: an invisible transfer that is the backbone of many
coin routines, and an eye-catching coin jump.

## Shuttle pass
Effect: a coin is apparently tossed or placed from the right hand into the left, but
actually a coin already palmed in the left hand is revealed while the right hand keeps
its coin.
1. Left hand has a coin classic palmed; right hand shows a coin at the fingertips.
2. Both hands turn over together towards each other in a "pouring" action.
3. The left hand drops its palmed coin into its own fingers (shown as "arriving"), while
   the right hand retains its coin (classic or finger palm).
4. Result: the coin appears to move from right to left.
Used as a vanish, a transfer and for repeated "coins across" phases.

## Muscle pass
Effect: a coin jumps from the palm upwards or into the other hand.
- The coin is pressed into the classic palm, then a sudden contraction of the palm
  muscles shoots it out.
- Takes weeks to months to learn; hands and coin types matter (milled-edge half dollars
  are typical).
Used as a flourish or to make a coin "jump" to the other hand.

## Practice
- Shuttle pass: mirror and camera; match the motion to a genuine pour.
- Muscle pass: short, frequent sessions; stop if the hand cramps.

## Verify
- [ ] Shuttle pass on video looks like a genuine pour.
- [ ] Muscle pass propels the coin at least 10 cm reliably.
