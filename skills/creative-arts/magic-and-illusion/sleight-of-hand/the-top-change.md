# The top change

**Goal:** exchange a card held in one hand for the top card of the deck in the other hand,
during an apparent gesture.

## Principle
The two hands briefly come together as one hand gestures past the other. In that moment
the held card is left on the deck and the top card is taken. Because the action is a
**gesture** (pointing, turning to a spectator), the eye follows the gesture, not the
hands' contact. Big movement covers the small switch.

## Method (outline)
1. One hand holds a card; the other holds the deck, top card pushed slightly over.
2. During a gesture (turning to the side, pointing), the card hand passes over the deck.
3. Card left on top; the pushed-over card taken in the same instant.
4. The gesture continues to its end; nothing pauses.

## Common faults
- Hands meeting at a stop.
- Looking at the hands at the moment of the switch.
- No reason for the gesture.

## Fine mechanics
Erdnase teaches the top change in detail; Card College vol 3 adds modern handlings.

## Verify
- [ ] Switch invisible on video at normal speed and front angle.
- [ ] Gesture has a scripted motivation.
- [ ] Eyes and body follow the gesture, not the hands.
