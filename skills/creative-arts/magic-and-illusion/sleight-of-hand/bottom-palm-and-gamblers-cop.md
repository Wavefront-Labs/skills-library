# Bottom palm and gambler's cop

**Goal:** steal a card from the bottom of the deck, holding it in the palm or in a loose
"cop", while the deck is handed out or squared.

## Bottom palm (left hand)
1. Deck held in the left hand; the bottom card is the target.
2. The right hand takes the deck from above by the ends (as in a Biddle or end grip),
   lifting it slightly.
3. The left fingers press the bottom card's outer right corner into the left palm as the
   deck is lifted away, so the card stays in the left hand.
4. The right hand moves away with the deck (e.g. to table it or hand it over); the left
   hand relaxes with the card palmed.
Precise handling varies by source; Erdnase's descriptions are the classic reference.

## Gambler's cop
A looser hold: the card is held against the palm by the curled fingers, more like a
finger-and-palm clip, close to the heel of the hand. Used by card cheats because the hand
looks natural resting on a table. Less secure in standing work.

## Uses
- Card to pocket or wallet loads.
- Holding out a card in gambling demonstrations.
- Adding a card back to the deck later (see palm replacement).

## Angles and naturalness
- Visible from below and from the left side if the left hand opens.
- Keep the left hand doing something: resting on the table, holding a prop, gesturing.
- Never hold the palmed hand stiffly at your side.

## Verify
- [ ] Bottom palm executed while handing the deck out, invisible on front camera.
- [ ] Cop held while the hand rests on a table for 30 s naturally.
