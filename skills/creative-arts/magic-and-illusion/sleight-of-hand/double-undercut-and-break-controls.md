# Double undercut and break controls

**Goal:** bring a selection from the middle to the top with a simple, reliable control
that looks like squaring and cutting.

## Principle
A **little-finger (pinky) break** is a small gap held at the inner right corner by the
flesh of the left little finger. With a break above the selection, two undercuts move all
cards above the break to the bottom. The selection ends on top; the action looks like a
casual cut.

## Method (outline)
1. Return the selection and hold a pinky break above it as you square.
2. Right hand takes the deck in end grip; first undercut moves part of the cards below the
   break to the top (or simply moves a packet), second undercut moves the rest down to the
   break to the top.
3. Square and continue.
4. Alternative from the same break: a **double cut** to the bottom, or a simple cut at the
   break to the top.

## Common faults
- Break too wide: visible at the back or side.
- Hand freezes holding the break; keep moving and talking.
- Undercuts done with a different rhythm from an honest cut.

## Fine mechanics
Card College vol 1 teaches the pinky break and double undercut as a pair; most other
controls (pass, side steal) assume this break.

## Verify
- [ ] Break held for 20 seconds while talking, invisible from the front.
- [ ] Ten controls in a row with the selection on top.
- [ ] Laypeople describe it as "she cut the cards".
