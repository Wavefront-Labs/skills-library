# Card to pocket and card to wallet

**Goal:** a selected, signed card vanishes from the deck and is found in your pocket or
inside a zipped wallet - a strong closer for a card set.

## Method outline
1. Spectator selects and signs a card; return it to the deck.
2. **Control** it to the top (double undercut, pass, side steal).
3. **Palm** it (top palm) during a natural action such as handing the deck out to shuffle.
4. **Load** it into the pocket or wallet with an off-beat reason to reach there (taking out
   a pen, putting the card box away, reaching for a marker).
5. Continue with other business so the load is distant in time.
6. Reveal: the deck is searched (card gone), then the pocket or wallet.

## Wallet variants
- Ordinary wallet in an inside pocket: the card is loaded into the pocket next to it and
  brought out "from the wallet".
- Specially made **card-to-wallet** wallets (e.g. Kaps, Mullica styles) have a secret
  opening so a card can be loaded while the wallet is closed - allowing a zipped or
  sealed wallet reveal.
Follow the specific wallet's instructions.

## Timing (the real secret)
The load must happen well before the audience thinks about pockets. If the load and
the reveal are seconds apart, the spectator connects them. Fill the gap with another
phase of magic.

## Gotchas
- Signed card ink smudging in the pocket: use a dry pen or let it dry.
- Pocket visibility: an outside jacket pocket is visible to all; choose angles.
- Don't touch the pocket again between load and reveal.

## Verify
- [ ] Load done with a motivated reach, at least one phase before the reveal.
- [ ] Rehearsed reveal from the wallet without fumbling.
