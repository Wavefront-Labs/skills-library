# Pricing and contracts for magic gigs

**Goal:** charge sustainably and avoid disputes with a clear written agreement for every
booking.

## Pricing
- Research local market rates for similar performers (walk-around, stage, children's,
  corporate).
- Price by **event type and value**, not just time: corporate events pay more than private
  parties; weddings are mid to high.
- Include travel, preparation, and props consumption in your costing.
- Have a minimum fee; quote packages (e.g. 2 hours walk-around).

## Contract essentials
- Parties, date, venue address, contact on the day.
- Performance type, times (arrival, set times, breaks), audience size.
- Fee, deposit (commonly a percentage at booking), balance due date, payment method.
- Cancellation terms (by client and by performer), force majeure.
- Technical needs (space, power, sound, a table, lighting) - reference your rider.
- Meals, parking, changing room.
- Recording and photography rights.
- Insurance: your public liability details.

## Insurance and paperwork
- Public liability insurance (often required by venues; many magic societies offer schemes).
- DBS/background checks for children's work where required.
- Invoices with clear references; keep records for tax.

## Gotchas
- Verbal bookings without deposits lead to cancellations.
- Scope creep ("could you just do another 30 minutes?") - put extras and rates in writing.

## Verify
- [ ] Signed contract and deposit for every booking.
- [ ] Insurance certificate current.
