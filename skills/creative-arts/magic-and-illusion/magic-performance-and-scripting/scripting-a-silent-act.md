# Silent act scripting

**Goal:** a magic act performed without words, scripted to music, that still has character,
story and a climax.

## Principle
In a silent act, music and movement replace patter. The script is a **cue sheet**: every
move mapped to a musical count. Character is shown through movement, expression and
reaction. Manipulation acts and many stage illusion acts are silent.

## Writing the script
1. Choose music that fits the character; edit it to the act's length.
2. Map the music's structure (intro, verses, build, climax).
3. Place effects on the structure: openers in the intro, the biggest effect on the climax.
4. Write a two-column cue sheet: count or timecode, and action.
5. Add reactions: where you look, smile, pause.

## Rehearsal
Rehearse with the music from day one; the counts are your timing.

## Common faults
- Music chosen for taste, not for the act's structure.
- Moves that drift off the beat.
- No reactions: a silent act still needs character.

## Verify
- [ ] Cue sheet with every move on a count.
- [ ] Music edit matches the act's structure.
- [ ] Climax effect on the music's climax.
