# Joining magic societies and the Magic Circle exam

**Goal:** become part of a magic society for learning, feedback, lectures, libraries and
professional contacts.

## Main societies
- **The Magic Circle** (London, founded 1905): membership typically requires an
  examination (performance or a thesis) after an interview; grades include Member (MMC)
  and higher degrees (AIMC, MIMC). Check current requirements on their site.
- **International Brotherhood of Magicians (IBM)**: worldwide; local "Rings" hold
  meetings and competitions.
- **Society of American Magicians (SAM)**: US-based "Assemblies".
- Local magic clubs and youth programmes (e.g. Young Magicians Club).

## What membership gives
- Lectures by professional magicians, libraries, journals (e.g. The Magic Circular,
  The Linking Ring, M-U-M).
- Competitions and performance opportunities.
- Insurance schemes and codes of ethics.
- Peer feedback and mentoring.

## Preparing an exam performance
1. Choose a short act (often around 10-15 minutes) showing technique and presentation.
2. Polish it: script, rehearsal, run in front of others.
3. Focus on presentation, not just difficulty: examiners look for entertainment value.
4. Follow the society's specific rules (props, timing, permitted material).

## Verify
- [ ] Society requirements read and application started.
- [ ] Exam act timed and performed for an audience at least three times.
