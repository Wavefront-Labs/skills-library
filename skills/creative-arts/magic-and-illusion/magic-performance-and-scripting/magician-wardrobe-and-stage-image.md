# Wardrobe and image

**Goal:** a performing wardrobe that expresses your character, works with your methods and
looks right in the venue.

## Principle
Costume is the first thing an audience reads. It tells them who you are before you speak.
For magicians it also has a technical job: pockets, loads and holdouts must be built in,
and colour must contrast with the backdrop and black art zones.

## Choosing
- **Character first:** a formal suit, street clothes or a theatrical costume, each tells a
  different story.
- **Colour:** contrast with the backdrop; avoid black against black velvet unless you
  intend to vanish.
- **Fit:** tailored for movement; arms must move freely.

## Technical tailoring
- Pockets positioned for loads and ditches; reinforced linings.
- Hidden pockets (profondes, servantes) built by a tailor who understands magic.
- Weight distributed so loaded jackets hang naturally.

## Maintenance
Duplicates for touring; repair kit; check pockets before each show.

## Common faults
- Bulges from loads.
- Costume that clashes with the character.
- Shiny fabrics reflecting stage light.

## Verify
- [ ] Wardrobe described in one sentence matching the character.
- [ ] Loads hang without bulges.
- [ ] Checked against the backdrop under show light.
