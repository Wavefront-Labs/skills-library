# Build and climax within a routine

**Goal:** make each phase stronger than the last so the routine ends on its best moment.

## Build
1. **List the phases** and rank them by impact (clarity and surprise).
2. **Order for escalation**: weakest or simplest first, the strongest last. Each phase
   should be harder to explain than the previous one.
3. **Escalate along one line**: more objects, more freedom for the spectator, more
   impossible conditions, or a bigger reveal.
4. **Keep the effect clear**: do not let the build confuse what is happening (see
   `creative-arts.magic-and-illusion.magic-performance-and-scripting.clarity-of-effect-in-magic`).
5. **Do not give away the climax**: avoid showing the strongest method or prop early.

## Climax
- The climax is the **moment of magic** the whole routine has set up (see
  `creative-arts.magic-and-illusion.magic-performance-and-scripting.placing-the-magic-moment`).
- Give it **room**: a pause before, a clean display, and a still moment after.
- End on the effect, not on tidying up; put the secret clean-up before the climax or
  after the applause.

## After the climax
Finish with a clear signal that the routine is over (a bow, a step back or a line); do
not keep going after the audience reacts.

## Verify
- [ ] Phases are ranked and ordered from weakest to strongest.
- [ ] The strongest moment is last and uncovered early.
- [ ] No tidy-up action happens in the climax.
