# Large action covering small action

**Goal:** conceal a small secret action inside a larger, natural movement that holds the
eye.

## Principle
Attention goes to the larger and more meaningful movement. A turn of the body, reaching
for a prop, placing a deck on the table or putting on glasses draws the eye; the small
action of a palm, a load or a steal done inside it is lost. The big move must be
**motivated**: it has a reason the audience accepts, so it does not itself attract
suspicion.

## Applying it
1. Find the secret action's natural "carrier": the larger movement it could ride.
2. Give the carrier a reason in the script (fetching a pen, turning to the next
   spectator, pointing to the wall).
3. Time the secret to the middle of the larger movement.
4. Continue the big movement to its natural end; do not stop when the secret is done.
5. **Curved motion** is followed more smoothly than straight jerks; curved paths are good
   carriers.

## Common faults
- Unmotivated big moves that look like distraction.
- Freezing after the secret is done.
- The small action happening before or after the cover.

## Verify
- [ ] Each secret move has a named carrier movement.
- [ ] Each carrier has a scripted reason.
- [ ] Video shows the secret happening in the middle of the carrier.
