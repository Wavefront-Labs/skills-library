# Show reports and post-show notes

**Goal:** capture what happened while it is fresh, so each show improves the next.

## When
Write the notes within an hour of the show, and never later than the same day.

## Template (one page)
- **Date, venue, audience** (size, age, mood), booker contact.
- **Running time** by routine against plan.
- **What worked**: laughs, gasps, applause points.
- **What did not**: lines with no response, missed cues, angle problems.
- **Technical**: sound, lights, props, resets, anything broken.
- **Spectators and volunteers**: names worth using in callbacks, any problems.
- **Safety**: near-misses or concerns recorded and actioned.
- **Changes to make** (specific and assigned).

## Using recordings
Audio is enough for patter; video shows angles and movement. Check the venue's and
audience's consent for recording and tell the booker. Watch once with notes only for
timing, then once for the audience's reaction.

## From notes to changes
1. List each change with an owner and a date.
2. Rehearse the change before the next show.
3. After a few shows, look for patterns (the same line failing, the same prop breaking)
   rather than reacting to a single night.

## For crew shows
If there is a crew, share a short version so everyone knows the updated cues. For illusions,
a faulty or damaged prop must be reported and fixed before the next show; see
`creative-arts.magic-and-illusion.stage-illusion.illusion-maintenance-and-preshow-checks`.

## Verify
- [ ] Notes are written the same day.
- [ ] Every change has an owner and a rehearsal.
- [ ] Safety issues are logged and actioned.
