# Placing the magic moment

**Goal:** the audience perceives a single, clear instant when the impossible happens - and
that instant is far from the secret action.

## Separate method from moment
- The **method** (palm, switch, load) happens at one time.
- The **magic moment** (the audience believes the change happens) should be **later**,
  marked by a gesture, a snap, a breath, a word.
- The greater the gap, the less likely the audience connects them.

## Marking the moment
- A magic gesture: a wave, a snap, a blow, a squeeze of the spectator's hand.
- Have the spectator perform it: "You click your fingers."
- Hold a beat of stillness right after it.

## Visual vs delayed moments
- **Visual magic**: the change happens in full view (a colour change). The moment is the
  method moment, so the method must be invisible at that instant.
- **Delayed reveal**: the magic has "already happened" (prediction in an envelope). The
  moment is the reveal.

## Landing the reveal
- Build to it with a slower pace.
- Reveal slowly (turn the card at a measured speed).
- Allow the reaction; do not talk over it.

## Common mistakes
- Revealing before the conditions are clear.
- Two magic moments competing in one effect.
- No gesture: the audience is not sure when it happened.

## Verify
- [ ] For each effect, the method moment and magic moment written separately.
- [ ] Gap between them is at least one action or beat.
