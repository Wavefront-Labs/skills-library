# Safeguarding and parent liaison

**Goal:** perform for children safely and professionally, and show parents and venues
that you do. This is practice guidance only. Rules differ by country and venue, so check
the law and the booker's requirements, and take advice from a qualified source (the
venue's safeguarding lead, your insurer or a professional body).

## Before the booking
- Ask whether the venue or school requires background checks (in the UK, an enhanced DBS
  check, or PVG in Scotland; other countries have their own schemes). Do not take work
  that requires a check without one.
- Hold public liability insurance that covers children's entertainment, and ask whether
  the venue requires a particular level of cover.
- Agree who the responsible adult is: a parent or teacher should be in or near the room
  throughout.

## On the day
- Never be alone with a child or small group; keep doors open or the room in sight of
  adults.
- No private conversations, gifts or contact details exchanged with a child; send
  requests to the parent.
- Photos and video: agree with the booker first, and do not film or post images of
  children without parental consent or the venue's policy.
- Props: no sharp, hot or small-parts items (choking risk) in children's hands. Check
  allergies before using food, latex balloons or confetti.

## Liaison with parents
1. Confirm in writing: age range, numbers, running time, space, power, who will be
   present.
2. Brief the adults on helpers and noise levels at the start.
3. Give the booker a contact for after-event feedback.

## If you have a concern
Follow the venue's or organiser's safeguarding policy: report to the designated
safeguarding lead, write down what you saw and heard, and do not investigate or promise
confidentiality to a child. If a child is in immediate danger, contact the emergency
services.

## Verify
- [ ] Checks and insurance confirmed with the booker.
- [ ] A responsible adult is present for the whole show.
- [ ] Photo consent agreed before the show.
