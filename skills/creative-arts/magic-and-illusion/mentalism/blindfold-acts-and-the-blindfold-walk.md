# Blindfold acts and the blindfold walk

**Goal:** present convincing "eyeless vision" and blindfolded guidance safely.

## Blindfolds
- Audiences examine blindfolds; the more elaborate the covering (coins, dough, tape,
  hood), the stronger the effect.
- Methods for apparently seeing through them are published in Corinda and specialist
  texts; study them there. Whatever the method, practise until you can perform
  naturally, not peering.

## The blindfold walk
- Performer is blindfolded and led by touch (muscle reading) by a spectator thinking of a
  destination or path.
- Keep the route safe: clear floor, no stairs or stage edges, a spotter walking alongside.

## Blindfold driving (why to avoid it)
Historical publicity stunts had performers drive cars blindfolded through towns. Today it
is illegal in many places and dangerous to the public. If a stunt is ever considered, it
must be on a closed course with professional safety management - most modern performers
simply do not do it.

## Safety controls on stage
- Spotter assigned to the blindfolded performer at all times.
- Stage edges marked and guarded.
- Blindfold removable instantly.
- No sharp objects or fire while blindfolded unless method allows full sight and is
  rehearsed.
