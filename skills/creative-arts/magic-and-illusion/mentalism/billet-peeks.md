# Billet peeks

**Goal:** glimpse what is written on a folded billet without the audience realising the
billet has been opened.

## Hand peeks
- With a billet held in the fingers, the thumb opens the folds slightly inside the
  hand, enough to glimpse the writing, then closes it again.
- Done as the hand gestures or rests near your face (adjusting glasses, touching the
  temple - "concentration").
- Done while looking at the spectator for most of the time; a fraction of a second glance.

## Prop-assisted peeks
- **Peek wallets**: the billet is placed into a wallet that lets you see it while closing.
- **Clipboards and pads** with windows or impression devices (the writing is transferred).
- **Envelopes** with windows or that can be opened secretly.

## Time misdirection
- Peek long after the billet was handled, when attention has moved.
- Or peek before any "reading" begins, during a set-up moment.

## Reading the writing
- Know the word in a glance: practise reading upside-down and mirror-oriented writing.
- Have the spectator print in capitals ("so the spirits can read it").

## Ethics
Present as mentalism (entertainment), not genuine psychic powers - see mentalism
disclaimers.

## Verify
- [ ] Peek done in under a second, covered by a natural action.
- [ ] Words read correctly in any orientation.
