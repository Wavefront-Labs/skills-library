# The center tear

**Goal:** a spectator writes a word on a small slip, folds it, and you tear it up and burn
or discard the pieces - yet you secretly read what was written.

## Preparation
- A small square billet (around 7-8 cm) with a **circle drawn in the centre**; the
  spectator writes inside the circle.
- They fold it in half and in half again, so the writing is at the folded **corner** that
  becomes the centre of the folded packet.

## The tear
1. Take the folded billet, holding it so the corner with the writing is identified.
2. Tear it in half, put the pieces together, and tear again into quarters.
3. The **centre piece** (with the writing, still folded) is kept separate from the other
   pieces by the thumb as they are squared - it is retained in the fingers while the rest
   are dropped into an ashtray, envelope or pocket.

## The peek
- Later, under cover (reaching for a pen, putting on glasses, consulting a pad), open the
  centre piece and glimpse the word. Ditch it.
- The peek must have time misdirection: do it while attention is on something else or
  well after the tear.

## Presentation
- Burn the pieces or give them to someone to hold; this "proves" nothing was read.
- Reveal slowly, as impressions, not as a sudden certainty.

## Gotchas
- Spectators writing outside the circle or folding differently: give clear instructions
  and demonstrate the fold.
- Dropping the centre piece: practise the retention until automatic.

## Verify
- [ ] 10 tears with the centre piece retained cleanly on camera.
- [ ] Peek covered by a natural action with at least a few seconds' delay.
