# Dictionary and magazine tests

**Goal:** a spectator chooses a word, image or item from a dictionary, magazine or
catalogue and the mentalist reveals it.

## Principle
The prop sets the feel of the effect. A dictionary implies thousands of possible words;
a magazine offers images and pictures; a catalogue offers items. The method is usually a
page force or a prepared publication, with the reveal adapted to what is on the page.

## Choosing
- **Dictionary:** strongest impression of randomness; reveal by definition, then word.
- **Magazine:** images allow drawing reveals; current issues feel spontaneous.
- **Catalogue / menu:** objects and prices offer gradual reveals.

## Handling
- Introduce the publication casually.
- Know the target page in detail, including pictures and captions.
- Let the spectator keep the publication open to show the audience at the end.

## Common faults
- A dated magazine that looks prepared.
- Small text that cannot be shown to an audience.

## Verify
- [ ] Target page memorised in detail.
- [ ] Publication looks ordinary and current.
- [ ] Final display to the audience planned.
