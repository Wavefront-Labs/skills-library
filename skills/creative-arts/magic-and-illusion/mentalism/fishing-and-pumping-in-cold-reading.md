# Fishing and pumping

**Goal:** understand how readers obtain information from the subject while appearing to
give it, so you can use the techniques in entertainment and recognise them elsewhere.

## Principle
**Fishing** is presenting a question as a statement ("I'm getting a sense of a recent
change...") and watching for reaction. **Pumping** follows up on a reaction to draw out
details the subject then believes the reader produced. Hyman's 1977 article and Rowland's
book describe the mechanics. Memory does the rest: people remember hits and forget misses.

## Techniques (outline)
- **Statements as questions:** tone rises slightly, inviting confirmation.
- **Reading reaction:** nods, facial expression, posture, voice.
- **Narrowing:** from broad category to specific detail as confirmation arrives.
- **Reframing misses:** "perhaps not yet, but watch for it".

## Ethics
These techniques are widely abused in fraudulent psychic practice. Use them only in clearly
framed entertainment, never for "contact with the dead" or advice on health, finance or
relationships. Many performers include explanations of cold reading in their shows.

## Common faults
- Too many questions; the subject notices.
- Ignoring misses rather than reframing.

## Verify
- [ ] Reading script framed as entertainment.
- [ ] Practise narrowing with consenting friends.
- [ ] No private readings for vulnerable people.
