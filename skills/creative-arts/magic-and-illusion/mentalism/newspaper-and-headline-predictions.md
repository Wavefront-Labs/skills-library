# Newspaper and headline predictions

**Goal:** a prediction made days or weeks earlier, sealed and kept by a third party,
correctly matches a newspaper headline or major event.

## Principle
Headline predictions are a **publicity** genre. The method is one of a small number of
well-documented approaches, all of which work on the **audience's understanding of the
chain of custody**: who held the prediction, where it was, who opened it. The effect lives
in the story told before the reveal. Corinda treats it under publicity.

## Staging
1. Announce publicly that a prediction has been made and lodged (with a newspaper editor,
   a mayor, a bank).
2. Make the custody arrangements visible and memorable: sealed, signed, photographed.
3. On the day, build the reveal as an event with media present.
4. The custodian opens it; the headline is read; the prediction matches.

## Ethics and publicity
- Present as entertainment; avoid claiming genuine precognition, especially around
  tragedies.
- Never predict disasters or deaths.

## Common faults
- Overcomplicated custody story.
- A reveal without witnesses.
- Choosing a headline topic that could offend.

## Fine mechanics
Annemann and Cassidy discuss the classic methods; choose one and rehearse the custody
handling until it is effortless.

- [ ] Custody chain written as a script and rehearsed.
- [ ] Media and custodian briefed.
