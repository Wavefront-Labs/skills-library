# Tarot and palmistry as reading frames

**Goal:** use tarot cards or palm reading as an engaging structure for entertainment
character readings at events.

## Principle
A **reading frame** gives the reader a reason for every statement ("the Tower card speaks
of change"; "your heart line is long") and gives the subject something to look at. It
legitimises cold reading elements and helps structure the reading. Tarot provides symbols
and a spread; palmistry provides lines and mounts.

## Basic vocabulary
- **Tarot:** 78 cards, 22 Major Arcana and four suits of Minor Arcana; a simple spread
  (past, present, future) is enough for events. Pollack's book is a standard reference.
- **Palmistry:** heart, head and life lines; hand shape; mounts.

## Event readings
- Short (3 to 5 minutes), positive, light-hearted.
- Clear entertainment framing on signage and in your words.
- Rotate subjects; avoid queues of distressed people.

## Ethics
- Never predict illness, death, pregnancy or financial disaster.
- Refer distressed subjects to friends or support; stop the reading.
- Do not charge for private "genuine" readings.

## Common faults
- Long readings at busy events.
- Losing entertainment framing.

## Verify
- [ ] Spread and vocabulary memorised.
- [ ] Ethical rules written and followed.
- [ ] Entertainment framing on signage.
