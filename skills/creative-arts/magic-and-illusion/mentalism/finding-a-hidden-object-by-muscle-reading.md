# Finding a hidden object by muscle reading

**Goal:** while the performer is out of the room, an object is hidden; on return, the
performer finds it by contact with a spectator who knows where it is.

## Procedure
1. Leave the room (with an escort) while a committee hides an object (keys, a ring, a pin)
   somewhere in the room.
2. Choose a guide who saw the hiding; take their wrist (see contact technique).
3. Search: let their unconscious signals guide you to the area, then to the location.
4. Reveal and produce the object.

## The pin test
A classic variant: a pin is pushed into a specific spot (e.g. on a map or a board) and the
performer must find the exact place. Harder: the final location must be read precisely.

## Staging for drama
- Search slowly, with apparent concentration.
- Narrow down visibly: "It's on this side... at this height..."
- One or two "near misses" before the find heighten tension.
- Total time: under 3-4 minutes; long searches lose the audience.

## Protecting the outcome
- Limit the search area (one room, the stage).
- Ask for an object hidden "within reach, not locked away".
- Have an out: if signals are weak, switch to another guide or end with a partial result.

## Verify
- [ ] Rehearsed with strangers in a similar-sized space.
- [ ] Search completes within 4 minutes in rehearsal.
