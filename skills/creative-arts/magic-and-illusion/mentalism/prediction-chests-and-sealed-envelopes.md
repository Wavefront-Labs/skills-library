# Locked prediction chests and envelopes

**Goal:** the prediction is visibly isolated before the choice is made, and opened only at
the end by a spectator.

## Principle
The container's job is to create a **memory of isolation**: "it was locked and in view the
whole time". Commercial prediction chests, envelopes and wallets each have a method; what
matters to the audience is that a spectator held the key, signed the envelope, or kept it
in their pocket.

## Staging
1. Introduce the container at the start, ideally in a spectator's hands.
2. Make the isolation memorable: signatures, a padlock, a sealed bag.
3. Perform the selection process with as much apparent freedom as possible.
4. **Recap** before opening: summarise the conditions.
5. A spectator opens the container and reads aloud.

## Choosing equipment
- Buy reputable commercial chests or envelopes; test reliability and inspection
  resistance.
- Size appropriate to the venue (a small envelope does not read on stage).

## Common faults
- Container introduced late.
- Performer handling the container during the selection.
- Too many seals, which suggests a trick.

## Verify
- [ ] Container in view from the start.
- [ ] Spectator opens it and reads aloud.
- [ ] Recap scripted before every reveal.
