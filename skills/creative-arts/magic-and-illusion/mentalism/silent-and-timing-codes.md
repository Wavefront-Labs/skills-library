# Silent and timing codes

**Goal:** send information with no words at all, so the "silent" phases of a telepathy act
feel impossible.

## Timing (count) codes
- Both partners count silently at the same rate from a start signal (e.g. the moment the
  performer says "Now" or touches the object).
- The stop signal (a word, a step, a breath) ends the count; the count is the number.
- Practise with a metronome until both partners count identically (e.g. one count per
  half second).

## Position and posture codes
- Where the sender stands in the audience (row, side), how they hold their hands, which
  foot is forward - each a small table of values.
- The receiver must be able to see the sender (blindfolds are often see-through or
  peek-able in classic acts; modern acts use other methods).

## Breathing and sound cues
- Audible breath, a cough or a shoe scuff as a stop or digit signal. Risky; easily noticed.

## Combining codes
- Use verbal for categories, timing for numbers, posture for colours.
- Switching systems through the act prevents anyone fitting one explanation.

## Drills
- Daily drills with random items; track error rate.
- Rehearse under show noise and lighting.

## Verify
- [ ] Timing code transmits digits 0-9 with no errors in 30 trials.
- [ ] Each silent cue invisible to a watcher who knows to look for cues.
