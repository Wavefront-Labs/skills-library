# Transpositions and the substitution trunk

**Goal:** one performer is locked in a sack and trunk; the other stands on the trunk, a
curtain is raised for a moment, and when it drops the performers have changed places.

## Principle
The illusion is a **speed and choreography** piece. The exchange happens behind the
curtain in a very short time; the trunk and sack are designed so the inside performer can
leave and the outside performer can enter while locks and straps appear untouched. Houdini
and his wife Bess made Metamorphosis famous. Modern teams (the Pendragons and others)
turned the exchange into a near-instant visual moment.

## Choreography
- Curtain time counted to the second and rehearsed hundreds of times.
- Each performer's moves set to counts: who goes where, what is unlocked and relocked.
- Music and the curtain raise share the same count.
- Committee or volunteers can inspect locks and sacks before the exchange to raise
  conviction.

## Safety
- Air time inside the sack and trunk limited and timed.
- Abort procedure: trunk can be opened from outside instantly.
- Volunteers kept clear of moving parts.

## Common faults
- Curtain time longer than the audience can tolerate.
- Volunteers left on stage interfering with movement.
- Locks fumbled under pressure.

## Verify
- [ ] Exchange under the target time in fifty consecutive rehearsals.
- [ ] Emergency opening tested each show.
- [ ] Inspection sequence for volunteers scripted.
