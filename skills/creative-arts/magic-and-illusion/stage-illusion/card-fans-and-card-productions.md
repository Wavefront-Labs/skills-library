# Card fans and card productions

**Goal:** the visual heart of a classic manipulation act: cards appearing at the fingertips
and blooming into fans, readable from the back of a theatre.

## Fans
- **Two-hand pressure fan**: deck held in the left hand, the right thumb pushes and the
  fingers rotate the deck into an even arc.
- **One-hand fan**: thumb pushes across the face of the deck while fingers counter-rotate.
- Use **fanning cards** (white-faced or decorated fanning decks show well on stage) and
  **fanning powder** (zinc stearate) so cards slide evenly. Refresh powder regularly.

## Single card productions
- A card is held in the **back palm** (clipped between first and little fingers behind
  the hand). A flick of the fingers brings it to the front at the fingertips.
- Repeated productions use a stack in back palm, each released one at a time.
- Angle-sensitive: the audience must be in front; side seats and balconies may see.

## Fan productions
A small packet held hidden is produced and fanned in one motion; multiple fan productions
use loads from the body (see body loading).

## Making it read
- Gloves, contrasting costume and lighting so white cards pop.
- Hold productions at chest-to-head height, open towards the audience.
- Music-driven pacing; each production a clear beat.

## Practice
Mirror for shape, camera for angles. Practise to the act's music.

## Verify
- [ ] Even fans every time with the act deck and powder.
- [ ] Back-palm productions clean from all seats in a test room.
