# Training a new assistant

**Goal:** bring a new assistant (or a **swing**, who covers several roles) to show
standard safely and with secrets protected.

## Principle
Illusion assistants carry both safety-critical and secret-critical knowledge. Training
must protect the methods, build physical competence in each prop, and verify that the
person can perform under show conditions before they appear live.

## Training sequence
1. **Secrecy agreement** signed before methods are shown.
2. **Safety briefing:** abort signals, emergency procedures, pinch points, blade rules.
3. **Prop by prop:** walk-through with the prop open; then closed; then at speed; then
   with music.
4. **Cue sheet:** written counts, positions and cues for every illusion.
5. **Costume rehearsal** in every prop.
6. **Technical rehearsal** with lights and crew.
7. **Sign-off:** the magician and stage manager confirm readiness for each illusion.

## Swing specifics
A swing learns every track. Keep a separate cue sheet per role and rehearse each track
regularly, not just when needed.

## Common faults
- Rushing a replacement into a show without full runs.
- Verbal-only instructions.
- Training only on the magician's side; the assistant's side is different.

## Verify
- [ ] Signed secrecy agreement on file.
- [ ] Cue sheet for every illusion issued and tested.
- [ ] Sign-off recorded before the first live performance.
