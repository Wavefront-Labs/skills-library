# Assistant loading and concealment positions

**Goal:** the assistant gets into, holds, and leaves hidden positions in illusion props
reliably, silently and safely.

## Principle
Concealment positions are physical skills. The assistant must reach the position in a
counted number of beats, hold it still and silent for the required time, and leave it on
cue. Every position has a **comfort limit** (how long it can be held without strain or
breathing difficulty) and that limit sets the routine's maximum duration in that phase.

## Method
1. Learn each position slowly with the prop open and the builder's guidance.
2. Mark hand and foot placements inside the prop.
3. Count entry, hold and exit to the music.
4. Time the hold; set the routine's phase length well inside the comfort limit.
5. Rehearse silent entry: no knocks, no breath sounds on microphones.
6. Dress the position: costume pieces that cannot snag or show.

## Safety
- Ventilation in every enclosed space.
- Agreed abort signal and a crew member watching for it.
- Positions re-measured for any replacement assistant.
- Warm-up before performance; contortion positions demand it.

## Common faults
- Holding longer than rehearsed because the show runs slow.
- Costume changes not tested in the prop.
- Noise during entry.

## Verify
- [ ] Each position timed with comfort limit noted.
- [ ] Abort signal rehearsed in costume.
- [ ] Silent entry confirmed on a live microphone.
