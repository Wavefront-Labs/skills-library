# Body loads and holdouts

**Goal:** know exactly where each card fan, ball, silk or dove load is on your body, how you
get to it, and when - the logistics behind a manipulation act.

## Load locations
- **Jacket pockets and inner pockets** (tailored deeper or angled).
- **Holders** sewn or clipped inside the jacket, behind the lapels, under the tails.
- **Trouser pockets** and waistband holders.
- **Sleeves** (for small items, limited).
- **Holdouts**: devices that hold an item out of sight and present it to the hand
  (cards, balls); often mechanical.

## Load map
A diagram of your costume with each load's position, content and the act moment it is
used. Rehearse loading the costume from this map before every show.

## Timing loads
- Loads happen on **off-beats**: right after a production when the audience is looking at
  the object or applauding.
- Use **big movements to cover small ones**: turning to the side, a sweep of the arm.
- The non-loading hand often holds a visible prop to attract attention.

## Costume tailoring
- Work with a tailor experienced in magicians' costumes: reinforced pockets, holders that
  do not bulge, fabric that does not show outlines.
- Rehearse in costume from early on; loads change how a costume hangs.

## Verify
- [ ] Load map written and costume loaded from it before every show.
- [ ] No bulges visible on camera from the front in performance lighting.
