# Touring and load-in of illusions

**Goal:** move an illusion show from venue to venue so it plays to standard every night,
with props safe and secrets intact.

## Principle
Every venue is different: stage depth, wing space, height, trap availability, loading
access, sightlines. **Advancing** (finding out before you arrive) and a fixed load-in
order turn each venue into a known procedure.

## Advance
- Technical rider: stage dimensions, power, masking, lighting needs, crew numbers, dock
  access, dressing rooms.
- Venue plans and section; ask about rake, traps and balcony height.
- Confirm load-in time and curfew.

## Load-in and fit-up
1. Masking and lighting positions first.
2. Props in from the dock under covers, in an order that matches their stage position.
3. Each illusion assembled, checked against its preshow list.
4. Sightline walk of the house with lights on.
5. Technical rehearsal of any illusion affected by the venue.

## Transit
Road cases built for each prop; foam and strapping; humidity protection for wood and
mirrors.

## Common faults
- Arriving without venue plans.
- Skipping the sightline walk because "it was fine last week".
- Props damaged by poor packing.

## Verify
- [ ] Rider sent and venue response checked before travel.
- [ ] Load-in order sheet followed and timed.
- [ ] Sightline walk done in each venue.
