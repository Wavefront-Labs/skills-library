# Bringing volunteers on stage

**Goal:** volunteers come up, help, and return to their seats feeling great, while the
illusion stays protected and the show keeps moving.

## Principle
Volunteers bring authenticity, but they are unrehearsed people on a stage with hazards and
secrets. Choose well, guide every move, keep them safe, and give them applause.

## Process
1. **Choose** engaged, willing people; look for those who smile at you. Avoid the very
   drunk and children without a parent's consent.
2. **Invite** clearly and with a gesture; accept refusals instantly.
3. **Steps:** an usher or assistant meets them; well-lit stairs with a handrail.
4. **Position:** tape marks where they stand, chosen for sightlines; stand them where they
   cannot see the secret and the audience can see them.
5. **Instructions:** short, one at a time; a hand on the shoulder to guide position.
6. **Return:** thank them by name, lead the applause, escort them back.

## Safety
- Keep them clear of pyro, blades, traps and moving props.
- Never touch them without warning or permission.

## Common faults
- Volunteers wandering into secret areas.
- Too long on stage.
- No applause for their return.

## Verify
- [ ] Volunteer marks taped and checked for sightlines.
- [ ] Steps lit and attended.
- [ ] Return with applause scripted.
