# Penetrations with swords and blades

**Goal:** blades are pushed through a basket or box containing a person, who emerges
unharmed.

## Principle
Penetration illusions combine body displacement (the person moves into spaces the blades
will not occupy) with **routed blade paths** the performer knows exactly. The **Indian
basket** is the classic: an assistant enters a basket, swords are thrust through it, and
the assistant emerges. The drama depends on the blades looking dangerous and the person
appearing to fill the space.

## Staging
- Show blades as solid and sharp-looking (striking a stage surface, cutting paper).
- Insert blades in a fixed order and path; mark them so order cannot be confused.
- Pace slowly and dramatically.
- Remove blades in reverse order before the emergence.

## Safety
- Blades blunt; tips rounded; guides keep them on their path.
- Blade order labelled and checked by a second person.
- Assistant's positions rehearsed for every blade.
- Abort signal: if the assistant signals, all blades stop.

## Common faults
- Blade inserted out of order.
- Sharp edges on worn blades.
- Rushing.

## Verify
- [ ] Blade order labelled and verified before each show.
- [ ] Blade tips and edges inspected and logged.
- [ ] Abort signal rehearsed with the assistant.
