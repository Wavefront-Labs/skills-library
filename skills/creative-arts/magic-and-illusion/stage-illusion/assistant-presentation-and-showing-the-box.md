# Assistant presentation and displays

**Goal:** the assistant shows props as ordinary and fair through clear, motivated movement,
and is seen as a performer in the illusion.

## Principle
The **display** sequence is part of the method: it shows the audience exactly what the
illusion wants them to believe (the box is empty, the sides are solid) and nothing else.
A display done with choreographed confidence reads as fair; one done hesitantly reads as
hiding. The assistant is often the one making the display, so they need presentation
skill as much as the magician.

## Display principles
- **Show, then move on.** Each side or door opened once, clearly.
- **Motivated movement:** turning a cabinet is done "so you can all see", with a gesture
  and a look to the audience.
- **Open hands and open body:** stand to the side of the prop, never in front of it.
- **Focus:** look at the space being shown, then at the audience.
- **Rhythm:** displays set to music counts.

## Performer presence
- The assistant has character and reactions; react to the magic.
- Costume and movement style match the show's tone.

## Common faults
- Fussy, repeated showing.
- Standing in a sightline during the display.
- Neutral face when the magic happens.

## Verify
- [ ] Display sequence set to counts and rehearsed.
- [ ] Every display shown once, clearly.
- [ ] Assistant's reactions planned for each effect.
