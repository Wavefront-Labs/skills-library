# Lighting plot for black art and reveals

**Goal:** a lighting design that hides the secrets of each illusion and makes each reveal
land.

## Principle
In magic, lighting is part of the method. Most illusions need certain areas kept dark
(black art zones, gaps, upstage of a prop) while the apparent action is bright. Reveals
need a light change timed exactly with the moment. The lighting designer must know which
areas are secret, even if not how.

## Plot principles
- **Map secret zones** on the ground plan; mark them "no light".
- **Side light** from booms, shuttered off backdrops, models performers without spill.
- **Front light** kept off upstage velvet; check bounce from the stage floor.
- **Avoid heavy backlight** where it would silhouette edges of props.
- **Reveal cues:** a fast bump or snap to a brighter state at the moment of appearance.
- **Follow spots:** position and width rehearsed so they never cross a secret zone.

## Working with the designer
Share the secret-zone map under a confidentiality agreement; test every illusion in the
lighting state during technical rehearsal.

## Common faults
- Haze making light beams visible in black-art zones.
- Floor bounce lighting velvet.
- Reveal cue late by half a second.

## Verify
- [ ] Ground plan with secret zones agreed with the designer.
- [ ] Each illusion run in its lighting state from extreme seats.
- [ ] Reveal cues called on the exact count.
