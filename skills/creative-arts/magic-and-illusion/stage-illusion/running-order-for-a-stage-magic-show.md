# Running order for a stage magic show

**Goal:** sequence a full-length stage show so it opens strongly, varies in pace and scale,
resets in time, and builds to a climax at the end of each half.

## Principle
A show is a sequence of contrasts: large and small, fast and slow, comic and dramatic, solo
and with volunteers. **Practical constraints** (reset time for big illusions, set changes,
costume changes) dictate much of the order, and the art is to make those constraints look
like deliberate pacing.

## Building the order
1. **Opener:** fast, visual, sets the style in the first minute.
2. **Second spot:** introduce the performer's voice and personality.
3. **Alternate** large illusions with front-of-curtain pieces (comedy, mentalism, audience
   work) that cover resets behind.
4. **First-half closer** strong enough to bring people back after the interval.
5. **Second-half opener** re-energises the room.
6. **Penultimate** piece emotional or interactive; **closer** the biggest moment.

## Practical checks
- Reset time for each illusion noted; covering pieces at least as long.
- Costume changes and crew moves listed per transition.
- Total time with applause measured in run-throughs.

## Common faults
- Two similar illusions in a row.
- Long blackouts for resets.
- Closer that is not stronger than the first-half closer.

## Verify
- [ ] Running order sheet with reset times and covers.
- [ ] Full run timed within target with applause.
- [ ] No two consecutive items of the same type.
