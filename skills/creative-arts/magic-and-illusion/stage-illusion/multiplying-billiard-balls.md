# Multiplying billiard balls

**Goal:** a ball at the fingertips becomes two, three, four between the fingers, then they
vanish one by one - a classic manipulation routine.

## The props
- A standard set: three solid balls and a **shell** (a hollow half-ball that fits over a
  solid ball). Balls typically 40-45 mm, matte finish to reduce reflections.
- Some sets use a ball holder (load) on the body.

## Basic multiplication
1. Show one ball (actually ball + shell) between the right thumb and first finger.
2. A turn of the hand rolls the solid ball out of the shell into the next finger space
   while the shell stays in front: two balls.
3. A palmed solid ball is loaded into the shell to repeat.
4. Continue to four balls between the fingers (three solid + shell).

## Vanishes and colour changes
- Vanish: reverse the process; balls go back into the shell or are palmed and ditched.
- Colour change: a silk or a different-colour shell changes the visible colour.

## Loads
Extra solid balls come from a holder under the jacket or from a pocket, loaded on
off-beats (turning, a gesture).

## Angles and presentation
- Front-facing audience; the shell's open side must face you.
- Hold the hand at chest height, back of the hand to you, fingers spread for the
  multiplication pose.
- Choreograph each multiplication to a musical beat.

## Verify
- [ ] Full multiplication to four and back without the shell's open side flashing.
- [ ] Routine timed to music, each phase on a beat.
