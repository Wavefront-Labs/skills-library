# Pinch points and blade safety in illusions

**Goal:** illusions that look dangerous without being dangerous - for assistants, performers
and crew.

## Hazards to look for
- **Pinch and crush points**: hinges, lids, sliding panels, folding parts, wheels. An
  assistant's fingers in a lid hinge are the classic injury.
- **Blades, swords, spikes**: sawing, sword basket, spike cabinets. Even blunt blades
  injure when they hit a body at speed.
- **Confined spaces**: assistants in small compartments - heat, air, panic, getting stuck.
- **Trap doors and lifts**: falls, crushing, timing errors.
- **Pyro, flame and smoke**: burns, smoke inhalation (licensed operators only).

## Controls
- **Guards and stops**: hinge guards, limit stops so lids cannot close fully on a body,
  soft edges.
- **Blade design**: blunt, rounded edges, guides that only allow the blade to travel in
  the safe path; blades that cannot be inserted in the wrong slot.
- **Escape**: every compartment can be opened from inside; assistants can get out unaided.
- **Communication**: the assistant inside can signal "stop" (a knock, a tug); the
  performer must have a "stop" plan.
- **Training**: no new assistant goes into an illusion without rehearsing with lights on,
  then show conditions.

## Pre-show check
Every illusion: hinges and latches work, guides clear, blades in correct order, escape
release works, compartment clean and ventilated.

## Verify
- [ ] Hazard list and controls written for each illusion.
- [ ] Inside escape tested before every show.
- [ ] Stop signal agreed and rehearsed.
