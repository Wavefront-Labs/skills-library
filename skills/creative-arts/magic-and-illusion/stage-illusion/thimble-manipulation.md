# Thimble manipulation

**Goal:** a short manipulation sequence in which thimbles vanish, change colour and
multiply until one is on every finger.

## Principle
A thimble on a fingertip is easily hidden by curling it into the **thumb crotch**, where
it is gripped and released. Changes of colour use a second thimble of a different colour
nested or palmed. The act is visual and suits silent performance to music.

## Structure (outline)
1. Produce a single thimble.
2. Vanish and reproduce it from different places.
3. Colour change (often red to white or silver).
4. Multiplication, finger by finger, to eight on both hands.
5. A final vanish or a held pose.

## Handling principles
- Brightly coloured thimbles are needed even for parlour audiences; they are small.
- Hands angled so the thumb crotch faces the performer.
- Every move timed to the music.

## Common faults
- Thimbles too small for the space.
- Thumb tension visible.
- Act too long; thimbles are a short spot.
