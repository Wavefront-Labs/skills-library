# Illusion maintenance and pre-show checks

**Goal:** every illusion works and looks immaculate at every show - a fault in a large
illusion is visible to the whole house.

## Pre-show checklist (per illusion)
- Set in correct position on its spike marks.
- Secret panels, latches and magnets operate silently.
- Wheels lock; castors roll freely.
- Mirrors and glass clean; black art surfaces free of dust and lint (use a lint roller).
- Blades and swords in order; guides clear.
- Load items in place.
- Escape releases work.
- Lighting specials for the illusion focused (check with the lighting desk).

## Weekly / touring maintenance
- Tighten bolts and screws that loosen with transport vibration.
- Lubricate hinges and slides (dry lubricant near fabric to avoid stains).
- Touch up paint chips, especially on edges that reveal construction.
- Replace worn felt, velcro and elastic.
- Steam or replace creased black velvet.

## Fault log
Record faults, causes and fixes; recurring faults signal a design problem to fix with the
builder.

## Crew
- Only trusted crew handle secret parts; venue crew move cases, not open illusions.
- A designated illusion technician signs off the checklist.

## Verify
- [ ] Signed checklist for every show.
- [ ] Fault log reviewed weekly on tour.
