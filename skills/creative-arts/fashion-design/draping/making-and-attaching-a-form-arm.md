# Making and attaching a form arm

**Why:** sleeves, armholes, cuffs and jacket balance cannot be judged on an armless form.

## Pattern
1. Use a fitted one-piece sleeve block for the form's size, cut from the cap to the wrist.
   Remove most cap ease (keep under 1 cm) so the arm sits smooth in the armhole.
2. Cut the arm in two layers of firm muslin plus a gusset-shaped **shoulder cap** and an
   oval **armhole plate** (card or plastic covered in muslin) and an oval wrist plate.
3. Mark the grainline down the centre of the arm and a front/back notch.

## Make
1. Sew the underarm seam; stuff with polyester batting layered evenly, not loose fibre.
   Wrap batting around a core so the arm is firm and round, not lumpy.
2. Close the wrist with the wrist plate; set the armhole plate covered in muslin and
   whipstitch the arm to it so the cap stands slightly proud.
3. Add a cap extension (shoulder flap) that pins over the form's shoulder.

## Attach
- Pin the flap along the shoulder seam and the plate to the form's armhole plate
  (or the armhole tape) at front notch, back notch and underarm.
- Arm should hang slightly forward (following the form's sleeve pitch); a form arm hanging
  straight down gives sleeves that pull at the back.

## Gotchas
- Too much cap height makes the arm stick out; trim and re-stuff.
- Soft arms collapse under jacket sleeves; pack firmly.
- Mark a centre line on the arm so sleeve grain can be checked visually.

## Verify
- [ ] Arm hangs slightly forward, centre line vertical when viewed from the side.
- [ ] Arm removable without unpinning garments.
- [ ] Surface smooth enough to pin into.
