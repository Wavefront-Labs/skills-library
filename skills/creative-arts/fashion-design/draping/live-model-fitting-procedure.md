# Live model fitting procedure

**Goal:** confirm what the form cannot show (movement, posture, comfort) and leave the
session with clear, transferable corrections.

## Prepare
- Toile pressed, seams marked (CF, CB, waist, hip, bust lines drawn in pen), grain lines
  visible, closures working (tape or pins at CF are fine).
- Fit model in the intended undergarments and shoes (heel height changes hem and balance).
- Kit: safety pins, glass-head pins, marker, tape measure, notes sheet, camera.

## Order of checks
1. **Balance:** CF/CB plumb, side seams vertical, hem level (see balance skill).
2. **Shoulders and neck:** shoulder seam on top of shoulder, neckline lies flat.
3. **Bust/chest and back width.**
4. **Waist and hip:** ease and position of waist line.
5. **Sleeves:** pitch and length.
6. **Length** of body and hems.
7. **Movement:** reach forward, raise arms, sit, walk, bend. Note pulls and riding up.

## Corrections
- Pin out excess symmetrically on one side only, but note it is for both sides.
- Mark let-outs with a pen line and measurement; you cannot pin added fabric.
- Ask the model about comfort; models notice pressure points before they show.

## Record
- Photograph front, side, back before and after pinning.
- Write each change in pattern terms: "take in side seam 1 cm at waist tapering to 0 at hip".
- Date and sample stage on the sheet.

## Gotchas
- Pinning on the model's body: keep points away from skin, use safety pins under tension.
- Over-fitting to one model's asymmetry; fit the right side and check the left.
- Long sessions tire models; prioritise balance and key points first.

## Verify
- [ ] All changes written in pattern terms.
- [ ] Photos labelled by sample and date.
- [ ] Movement tests done.
