# Draping gathers and fullness ratio

**Goal:** gathers with the right amount of fullness for the fabric and look, falling where
the design wants volume.

## Fullness ratios (gathered length : finished seam length)
| Fabric | Soft look | Full look |
|---|---|---|
| Chiffon, georgette, voile | 2:1 | 3:1 |
| Lawn, poplin, crepe de chine | 1.5:1 | 2.5:1 |
| Medium cotton, linen | 1.5:1 | 2:1 |
| Heavy (denim, wool) | 1.25:1 | 1.5:1 |
Make a 30 cm gathered sample in the real fabric before deciding.

## On the form
1. Mark the seam that will hold the gathers (yoke, waist, bust seam) with tape.
2. Run two lines of long gathering stitches in the muslin within the seam allowance; or
   fold small pleats if gathering muslin is too stiff.
3. Draw up to the target length and pin to the seam line, **distributing fullness**:
   evenly, concentrated at the front, or away from the side seams as designed.
4. Mark the seam with distribution notches (e.g. "gathers between notches A and B").
5. Check the drop: gathers above the bust or hips add volume there; adjust.

## Translating to pattern
Slash-and-spread the flat pattern by the ratio where the gathers are, keeping un-gathered
sections unchanged; mark gather zones with notches.

## Gotchas
- Too much fullness in heavy fabric bulks the seam; use pleats or reduce ratio.
- Gathers sewn without notches end up unevenly distributed.
- Muslin gathers stiffly; judge the look in real fabric.

## Verify
- [ ] Ratio tested on a fabric sample.
- [ ] Distribution notches on seam.
- [ ] Volume falls where the design intends on the form.
