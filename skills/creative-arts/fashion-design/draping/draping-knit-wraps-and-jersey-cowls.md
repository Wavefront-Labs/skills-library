# Draping knit wraps and jersey cowls

**Goal:** soft fluid wraps and cowls in jersey that fall where intended and stay put.

## Why jersey is different
- Jersey drapes under its own weight; it grows lengthwise when hung. A cowl drawn on paper
  from woven rules is too deep in jersey.
- Use matte jersey, viscose or modal jersey for fluid falls; cotton jersey is too springy.

## Wrap front
1. Pin CB and side seams; take the front panel across the body from shoulder to opposite
   side waist.
2. Let the wrap edge lie on a diagonal; mark the overlap so it covers the bust point by at
   least 5-8 cm, otherwise it gapes when sitting.
3. Gather or pleat the excess into the shoulder or side seam; pin the folds evenly spaced.
4. Stabilise the wrap edge (clear elastic or stay tape cut 5-10% shorter than the edge) so it
   hugs the body.

## Cowl
1. Fold the top edge on bias for wovens; for jersey, the straight cross-grain usually works.
2. Pin the shoulders, lower the centre to the desired depth, and arrange folds.
3. Hang overnight on the form if possible; re-mark depth after growth.
4. Self-face the cowl (fold back 8-15 cm) so the inside does not show.

## Gotchas
- Tight gathering at the side seam bulks; spread folds across a longer seam.
- Heavy jersey pulls the cowl deeper over the day.
- Wraps without a tie or snap open with movement; add an inner tie or press stud.

## Verify
- [ ] Wrap covers the bust when seated.
- [ ] Cowl depth re-checked after hanging.
- [ ] Edge stabilising specified on the tech pack.
