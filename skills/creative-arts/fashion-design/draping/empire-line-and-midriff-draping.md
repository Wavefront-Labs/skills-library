# Empire line and midriff draping

## Empire line
1. Tape the underbust line on the form: just under the bust, rising slightly at CF if the
   design wants, level or dipping at the back. Check it from the side; it should follow the
   ribcage, not slope down at the side.
2. Drape the upper bodice (bust section) with grain on CF; bring all bust fullness to the
   underbust seam as gathers, pleats, or a shaped cup seam.
3. Drape the skirt from the underbust seam; grain on CF; let it hang from the bust point
   down, clearing the stomach (add flare or gathers so it doesn't cling).

## Midriff band
1. Tape a band from underbust to waist (or to below waist).
2. Drape the band in one piece per side, fitted closely with darts or shaped side seams;
   on curvy torsos add a seam at the side-front.
3. Drape the bust section above with fullness into the upper band seam.
4. Midriff bands usually need a stay or interfacing to keep from rolling.

## Gotchas
- An underbust seam too high cuts across the bust; too low reads as a dropped waist.
- Bust gathers placed too wide sit under the arms; keep them between the apexes and the side front.
- Empire skirts in stiff fabric tent at the front; add more at the back or use drapier fabric.

## Verify
- [ ] Underbust seam follows the ribcage from every view.
- [ ] Bust fullness controlled at the seam.
- [ ] Skirt hangs clear of the stomach; hem level.
