# Draping a shirt collar with stand

**Goal:** a two-piece collar (separate band and leaf) that sits close to the neck, closes at
CF without strain and rolls evenly over the band all round.

## Prepare
- Bodice drape trued, neckline marked with style tape at the finished seam (not cut edge).
- Mark CF and the button extension (usually half the button diameter plus 3-6 mm, so
  12 mm for a 10-12 mm shirt button on a 2.5-3 cm placket).
- Two strips of muslin on straight grain: band ~8 cm x half-neck + 5 cm; leaf ~12 cm deep.

## Band (stand)
1. Pin band CB on the form's CB, grain vertical at CB, lower edge on the neckline tape.
2. Work toward CF in small clips (every 1-1.5 cm) of the lower seam allowance so it follows the
   neckline. Keep the band upright and ~3-6 mm off the neck at the top edge.
3. The lower edge curves *up* toward CF (band "spring"); the more it curves, the closer the band
   hugs the neck. Typical rise at CF: 1-1.5 cm above the straight line.
4. Mark band height: 2.5-3.5 cm at CB, tapering slightly at CF; extend past CF by the button
   extension and round or square the band end.

## Leaf (fall)
1. Pin leaf CB to band top edge at CB, grain vertical.
2. Turn the leaf down so it covers the band seam by 6-10 mm at CB. Fall depth = band height
   plus that cover (e.g. 3 + 1 = 4 cm at CB).
3. Work forward, clipping the leaf neck edge to the band top. The leaf neck edge curves the
   *opposite* way (down toward CF) — that curve is what lets the outer edge spread.
4. Style the point: length, angle, spread. Point lands roughly over the band end, short of CF.

## True
- Remove, lay flat, smooth curves with a French curve; check band top = leaf neck edge length
  (leaf may be 2-3 mm shorter for a close roll if the fabric allows easing).
- Walk band lower edge against bodice neckline; notches at CB, shoulder point, CF.
- Add undercollar turn-of-cloth: undercollar 2-3 mm smaller at outer edge for shirting.

## Gotchas
- A straight band stands away from the neck; too much spring chokes the wearer.
- Leaf not covering the band seam shows the join at CB.
- Muslin is softer than fused shirting; check again in fabric with interlining.

## Verify
- [ ] Band closes at CF with a finger's ease.
- [ ] Leaf covers band seam evenly round to the front.
- [ ] Seam lengths match, notches marked.
