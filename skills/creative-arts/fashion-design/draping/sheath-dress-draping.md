# Sheath dress draping

## Prepare
Full-length form (to hem) or a stand with hip. Muslin length shoulder to hem + 10 cm.
Mark grain at CF/CB, cross-grains at bust and hip lines.

## Drape the front
1. Pin grain on CF from neck to hem; cross-grain level at the bust and at the hip.
2. Shape over the bust to the neck and shoulder; take bust fullness to a side or
   French dart (or armhole/shoulder dart).
3. **Contour (fish-eye) dart**: a double-pointed vertical dart through the waist, widest at
   the waist, tapering to just below the bust and to the high hip. Pin it in line with the
   apex.
4. Pin the side seam from underarm through waist to hip; keep the hip cross-grain level, then
   drop the side seam straight (or slightly in) to the hem.

## Drape the back
CB grain; shoulder blade dart or ease; back contour dart in line with the blade, tapering
to the high hip; side seam to match the front.

## Ease and length
- Leave about 1-2 cm ease at the waist and 2-4 cm at the hip in total (per Armstrong-type
  tables); more for wovens without stretch.
- If the hem is narrower than the hip, add a vent or slit at CB or side for walking
  (check stride).

## Gotchas
- Pulling contour darts too tight produces horizontal drag lines across the stomach.
- Hip cross-grain dipping at the side means the side seam is pulled; re-pin.

## Verify
- [ ] Grain vertical CF and CB; hip cross-grain level.
- [ ] Contour darts aligned and smooth at the ends.
- [ ] Walking ease or vent confirmed.
