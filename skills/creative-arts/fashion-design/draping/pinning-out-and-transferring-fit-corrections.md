# Pinning out and transferring corrections

**Goal:** turn what you see in a fitting into accurate pattern changes.

## Removing excess (pin out)
1. Pinch the excess fold and pin along it; taper to nothing at the ends.
2. Keep grain lines straight where they pass the correction (or note that the correction
   moves them).
3. Mark both sides of the pinned fold with a pen across the pin line (match marks).
4. Measure the depth of the fold at its widest: "pin out 1.2 cm across back at 10 cm
   below CB neck, tapering to 0 at armhole".

## Adding room (slash and spread)
1. Cut the toile where room is needed (perpendicular to the drag line), from the edge
   toward but not through the opposite edge (leave a hinge) or right through for length.
2. Let it spread naturally on the body; insert muslin behind and pin/tape.
3. Measure the gap.

## Re-marking seams
- Re-pin side seams, shoulders, armholes; mark new lines with a different coloured pen
  than the original.
- Mark new notches across seams.

## Transfer to pattern
1. Take the toile off; open the pinned folds as marked or measure the spreads.
2. On the pattern, draw the same correction lines at the same positions; fold out or
   cut and spread by the measured amounts.
3. Re-true affected seams (a correction in one piece may change the length of the seam
   it joins); walk seams.
4. Update grain lines if the correction rotated them.
5. Record the change in the pattern's revision note.

## Gotchas
- Corrections made only on the toile and not measured get lost.
- Correcting one side only when the body is symmetric; transfer to both halves.
- Changing a seam length without changing its partner seam.

## Verify
- [ ] Each correction has location, amount and direction written down.
- [ ] Affected seams re-trued and walked.
- [ ] A new toile confirms the corrections.
