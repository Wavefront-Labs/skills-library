# Reading drag lines and diagnosing fit

**Goal:** identify the cause of each wrinkle on a toile so the correction is made in the
right place, not just where the wrinkle shows.

## The two wrinkle types
- **Drag (strain) lines**: diagonal or horizontal lines **pointing toward** the area that is
  too tight or too short. Fix: add width/length at the point they point to.
- **Folds of excess**: soft, loose folds where there is too much fabric. Fix: pin out the
  excess.

## Common diagnoses
| Symptom | Likely cause | Typical fix |
|---|---|---|
| Diagonal drags from bust to side waist | Bust too small / dart too small | Full bust adjustment, bigger dart |
| Horizontal folds at back neck | Back too long above blades, or neck too low | Pin out across back, shorten CB |
| Drag lines from neck to armhole | Shoulder slope too square | Lower shoulder at armhole |
| Gaping armhole front | Excess at armhole | Pin out, move into dart |
| Horizontal pulls at hips | Hip too tight | Add at side seams |
| Skirt swings forward, side seams pull forward | Hip/waist balance off, prominent abdomen | Add length at CF waist or adjust balance |
| Smile folds at crotch (trousers) | Crotch too long/loose | Shorten crotch curve |
| Frown lines at crotch | Crotch too short/tight | Extend crotch, deepen curve |

## Procedure
1. Look from 2-3 m in neutral pose, then in movement.
2. Fix **balance first** (side seams vertical, CF/CB on body centre, hem level), then
   length, then width, then details.
3. Change one thing at a time; re-check.

## Gotchas
- Fixing a wrinkle where it appears rather than where it points to.
- Over-fitting a static form or posture; check in movement.
- Fabric matters: a soft fabric hides problems a stiff toile shows, and vice versa.

## Verify
- [ ] Each wrinkle classified (drag vs excess) with stated cause.
- [ ] Balance corrected before other fixes.
- [ ] Corrections confirmed on a re-fitted toile.
