# Yokes and asymmetric style lines

## Yokes
1. Tape the yoke line on the form (shoulder yoke, back yoke, hip yoke).
2. Drape the yoke piece first, grain usually straight at CF/CB (or shirt back yoke on the
   cross-grain for stripes); smooth it flat with no fullness.
3. Drape the lower piece; bring dart fullness up to the yoke seam. It becomes **gathers,
   pleats, or is shaped out** of the seam (a curved yoke seam absorbs a dart).
4. Mark the yoke seam and crossmarks on both pieces before unpinning.

## Asymmetric lines
1. Asymmetric designs need **both halves draped**; you can't drape half and mirror it.
2. Tape the full line across the form, crossing CF.
3. Decide grain for each piece: usually lengthwise grain parallel to CF on the largest piece;
   diagonal pieces may sit on bias for drape (note stretch).
4. Move darts into the diagonal seam where it passes near the apex.
5. Mark CF on every piece crossing it, so the pattern can be checked against the body.

## Gotchas
- Style line too far from the apex cannot absorb a dart cleanly; add a small dart or gather.
- Diagonal seams on bias stretch when sewn; stay-stitch or tape and note it on the pattern.
- Back yoke with ease: shoulder blade ease can go into the yoke seam.

## Verify
- [ ] Style tape line smooth and balanced from every view.
- [ ] Both halves draped for asymmetric styles.
- [ ] Grain marked on every piece; CF marked across.
