# Draping tools and pinning technique

## Kit
- **Dressmaker or silk pins** (fine steel, 0.5-0.6 mm, ~28-34 mm); glass-head for visibility.
  Pin cushion on the wrist.
- Shears and small snips (for clipping seam allowances).
- **Style tape** (narrow black or coloured twill or adhesive tape) for design lines.
- Soft pencil, fabric markers, tailor's chalk; L-square, hip curve, French curve, clear ruler.
- Tape measure, tracing wheel, notcher; steam iron.

## Pinning rules
- Pin **into the form** at anchor points (CF/CB, neck, waist, bust) at an angle so the pin
  head does not slide; secure grain first, then shape.
- **Seams**: pin edges together (pin-fit) with pins perpendicular to the seamline for easy
  adjustment, or lapped (fold one edge over the other) for a clean read of the line.
- **Darts**: pin along the fold, pins parallel to the dart line, heads toward the dart point.
- Fewer, accurate pins beat many; each pin holds a decision.
- Leave ease by pinching (a pin-tuck of ease) rather than pinning taut.

## Marking
- Crossmarks at seam junctions and notches before unpinning.
- Dots along seamlines at 2-3 cm intervals, dashes at style lines.
- Mark both layers when two pieces meet.

## Gotchas
- Pins pushed straight in pop out as you smooth the next area.
- Thick pins leave holes in silk; test.
- Unmarked drapes lose information when unpinned.

## Verify
- [ ] Grain anchors pinned before shaping.
- [ ] All seams marked with crossmarks.
- [ ] Ease pinned in, not pulled out.
