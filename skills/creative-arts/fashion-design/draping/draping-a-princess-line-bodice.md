# Draping a princess-line bodice

**Goal:** a fitted bodice shaped by vertical seams passing over or near the bust apex,
with each panel on its own grain.

## Prepare
- Style-tape the princess line on the form: from mid-shoulder (shoulder princess) or
  armhole (armhole princess) curving through or near the apex to the waist, roughly
  parallel to CF. The back princess line runs over the shoulder blade.
- Cut muslin for CF panel and side front panel, each with grain and cross-grain lines.

## Drape the CF panel
1. Grain on CF; cross-grain on the bust line. Pin at neck and waist.
2. Smooth across to the princess tape; clip neckline seam allowance.
3. Pin and mark along the princess style tape; mark the apex level as a notch.

## Drape the side panel
1. Place the grain line roughly vertical through the middle of the panel (parallel to CF),
   cross-grain level.
2. Smooth toward the princess seam and side seam; pin along the princess tape so that
   both panels meet. Don't stretch the side panel to fit.
3. Mark princess seam, side seam, armhole, shoulder, waist.
4. Place **notches**: at apex level, and a double notch above/below for matching the curve.

## Back
Same with CB and side back panels; back princess replaces the shoulder blade and waist darts.

## Gotchas
- The side panel goes off-grain if you pull it to the seam; let it hang.
- Over-curved seams at the apex create a pointed bust; soften the curve when truing.
- Notches are essential on curved seams; missing ones lead to twisted seams in sewing.

## Verify
- [ ] Each panel's grain vertical and cross-grain level.
- [ ] Princess seams marked with matching notches.
- [ ] Trued seams walk to the same length between notches.
