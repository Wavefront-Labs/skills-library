# Panelled dress draping

## Plan the panels
- Tape vertical panel lines from shoulder or armhole to hem: CF panel, side-front, side-back,
  CB. 6, 8 or 10 panels; lines pass through or near the bust apex and shoulder blade.
- Check from front, side and back that the lines look balanced and evenly spaced at the hem.

## Drape
1. Cut each panel wider than its taped width plus 5 cm allowances; mark the grainline in
   the centre of each panel.
2. Drape the CF panel first: grain on CF, smooth to the panel line, pin and mark.
3. Drape the side-front with grain **through the middle of the panel**, vertical to the floor
   (check with a plumb line). Pin to the CF panel along the line, shaping over bust and waist.
4. Repeat for side and back panels.
5. Add flare below the hip by letting each panel swing out equally at each seam; equal flare
   per seam keeps the hem balanced.

## Mark and true
- Crossmarks at bust, waist, hip on every seam; these must match after truing.
- True each pair of seams to the same length; walk them together from the hem up.

## Gotchas
- Grain not vertical in side panels gives twisting seams.
- Unequal flare per seam makes the hem swing to one side.
- Panel lines too close to the side seam look heavy on curvy bodies.

## Verify
- [ ] Grain vertical in every panel.
- [ ] Balance marks at bust, waist and hip match.
- [ ] Flare distributed evenly.
