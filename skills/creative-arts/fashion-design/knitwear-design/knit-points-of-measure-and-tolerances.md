# Knit points of measure and tolerances

**Goal:** a knit spec sheet that the factory and QC measure the same way, with realistic
tolerances for stretchy garments.

## Measuring conditions
- Garment finished (washed/steamed), conditioned 24 h if possible, laid flat on a smooth table,
  **relaxed**, no stretching. Smooth without pulling.
- Measure half chest, half hem etc. across the flat garment (1/2 measurements).

## Key knit POMs
- Body length from HPS; chest width 2.5 cm below armhole; across shoulder; armhole straight.
- Sleeve length from CB neck (raglan) or shoulder point (set-in).
- Hem width **relaxed** and **extended** (stretched to maximum without distorting); same for
  cuffs and neck opening.
- Neck opening stretched: must pass over the head; common minimum 56-60 cm extended for adults.
- Welt/rib height, band width.

## Tolerances (typical, brand specific)
| POM | Wovens | Knitwear |
|---|---|---|
| Chest, hem 1/2 | +/-1 cm | +/-1.5-2 cm |
| Body length | +/-1 cm | +/-1.5 cm |
| Sleeve length | +/-1 cm | +/-1.5 cm |
| Rib/trim height | +/-0.3 cm | +/-0.5 cm |
| Neck extended | min value only | min value only |

## Gotchas
- Measuring a sweater hung or stretched varies by 2-5 cm.
- Ribs measured extended by different people vary; define "extended" (stretched to hand force
  or to a set load).
- Measuring before finishing gives meaningless numbers.

## Verify
- [ ] Measuring method stated on the spec sheet.
- [ ] Relaxed and extended POMs both given for ribs.
- [ ] Tolerances agreed with the factory.
