# Neckbands and collars

**Goal:** necklines that sit flat, go over the head, recover after wearing and look clean.

## Types
- **Crew neck**: 1x1 or 2x2 rib band, 2-3 cm finished (often knitted double and folded).
- **V-neck**: band mitred at the V (overlap, mitre, or crossover), slightly stretched
  around the curve.
- **Mock/turtle/roll neck**: tall rib (turtle doubled over) or jersey that rolls naturally
  (roll neck - use the curl of jersey).
- **Polo collar**: fully fashioned collar with a placket; tipped edges as a design feature.
- **Shawl collar**: rib or full-cardigan stitch collar on cardigans.

## Ratios
- Band length is **shorter than the neckline** so it pulls in and lies flat: roughly 80-90%
  of the neckline length for rib, depending on rib stretch.
- The neckline opening, when stretched, must pass the head: about 56-60 cm stretched
  circumference for adults (check head circumference range).

## Attachment
- **Linking** (stitch-by-stitch onto the body loops): clean, elastic, premium.
- **Cup seaming/overlock**: faster, bulkier, less elastic.
- Fully fashioned shaping on the body neckline improves fit around the curve.

## Spec
Band type, finished height, rib structure, stitch count, linking method, stretch
circumference minimum.

## Gotchas
- Band too long waves; too short puckers the body.
- Overlocked necks lose stretch and crack the seam when pulled.
- Heavy collars drag necklines down; stabilise with tape at the back neck.

## Verify
- [ ] Band length ratio stated and swatched.
- [ ] Stretched opening passes the head.
- [ ] Attachment method specified.
