# Coned yarn, waxing and sourcing spinners

**Goal:** buy yarn in the form, quantity and timing a knitting factory needs.

## Where yarn comes from
- **Spinners** (e.g. Italian Biella/Prato spinners, Zegna Baruffa, Cariaggi, Todd & Duncan,
  Südwolle; Asian spinners for volume). Fair: **Pitti Filati** (Florence, twice yearly) shows
  seasonal yarn collections 18 months ahead of retail; also Spinexpo.
- **Stock service** colour cards: yarn held dyed in stock, small minimums (often 5-50 kg per
  colour), quick delivery.
- **Dyed to order:** minimums per colour (often 100-300 kg), 4-8 weeks plus.

## Cone specifics
- Cones typically 0.5-2 kg on paper or plastic tubes; specify cone weight for the factory.
- **Waxed** yarn (paraffin applied during winding) reduces friction for knitting; ask for waxed
  cones for wool/cotton flat knitting. Hand-knit yarns in skeins/balls are not suitable for
  industrial machines without rewinding and waxing.
- Ask for yarn on cones "for knitting" vs "for weaving" (weaving yarns may be unwaxed and
  twisted differently).

## Yarn spec sheet
- Count and ply, fibre content, micron (wool), twist, colour reference, finishes (superwash,
  anti-pill), certifications (RWS, GOTS, GRS), price per kg, MOQ, lead time.

## Consumption
- Estimate from garment weight plus waste: garment weight x 1.05-1.15 (fully fashioned) or
  x 1.3-1.5 (cut-and-sew from panels). Order a margin for sampling and re-knits.

## Gotchas
- Lot-to-lot shade differences: order a whole style's yarn from one dye lot.
- Superwash treatment changes hand and count; spec it from the start.

## Verify
- [ ] Yarn waxed, coned, lot noted.
- [ ] Consumption estimate and MOQ checked against order.
