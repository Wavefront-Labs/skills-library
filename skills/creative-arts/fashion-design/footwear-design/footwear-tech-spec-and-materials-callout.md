# Footwear tech spec and materials callout

**Goal:** a spec that a shoe factory can turn into a sample without guessing the
materials, construction or proportions.

## Views
- **Lateral (outside) side view** at 1:1 or fixed scale - the master drawing.
- Medial side, top view, back view, outsole view.
- Exploded view of the upper pattern parts for complex shoes.

## Parts callout table
For each part (vamp, toe cap, quarter, eyestay, tongue, collar, counter cover, heel
tab, lining, sockliner, midsole, outsole, laces, eyelets, logos):
material, supplier/ref, thickness, colour (Pantone or material colour code), finish,
and stitching (type, SPI, thread colour, rows).

## Last and bottom specs
- Last reference (code), heel height, toe spring, size base.
- Sole unit reference or new tooling drawing: thickness at heel/forefoot, material,
  hardness (Shore A), tread, colour.
- Construction method (cemented, strobel, welted).

## Branding and details
Logo placement with measurements, emboss/deboss/print method, size and colour; lace
length per size; aglet colour; packaging.

## Size run
Size system (EU, UK, US, Mondopoint) and range; sample size (often men's US 9 / EU 42-43,
women's US 7 / EU 37-38 - confirm with the factory).

## Colourways
One sheet per colourway with part-by-part materials and colours.

## Gotchas
- Missing medial view: the inside of the shoe differs (shorter quarters, no logo).
- Colours for leather, textile and rubber need separate approvals (different materials).
- Undefined stitch colour/rows leads to factory defaults.

## Verify
- [ ] All views and parts callout complete.
- [ ] Last, sole and construction specified.
- [ ] Colourway sheets per colourway.
