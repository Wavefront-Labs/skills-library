# Boot types (Chelsea, chukka, combat)

**Goal:** boots that look right and actually go on, stay on and fit the calf and instep.

## Types
| Boot | Height | Closure | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chelsea | Ankle | Elastic side gores, pull tab | Gore stretch must pass the heel; quality elastic |
| Chukka / desert | Ankle | 2-3 eyelets, open lacing | Desert = crepe sole, suede |
| Combat / work | Mid-calf or ankle | Lacing with hooks, padded collar | Lug soles, toe protection for safety |
| Lace-up hiker | Ankle+ | Speed hooks, gusseted tongue | Waterproof membrane |
| Riding/knee-high | Knee | Zip or pull-on | Calf fit critical |
| Western | Mid-calf | Pull-on, pull straps | Cuban heel, pointed toe |

## Fit design
- **Getting in**: the heel must pass through the opening. Pull-on boots need a high
  enough instep girth or elastic gores; otherwise add a zip.
- **Shaft**: specify height (from floor or from sole top at the back) and circumference at
  top and calf; offer wide-calf versions for knee-highs.
- **Instep and ankle**: boot lasts differ from shoe lasts (higher instep, heel seat).

## Design details
Pull tabs, gore placement, collar padding, lining (leather, shearling), welt vs cemented
soles, toe shape, heel type.

## Gotchas
- Chelsea boots on lasts with low insteps can't be pulled on.
- Knee-high shafts drawn too narrow; check calf range in the size chart.
- Shafts slouch without proper stiffeners/lining.

## Verify
- [ ] Shaft height and circumferences specified per size.
- [ ] Entry tested on a range of feet.
- [ ] Boot last (not a shoe last) used.
