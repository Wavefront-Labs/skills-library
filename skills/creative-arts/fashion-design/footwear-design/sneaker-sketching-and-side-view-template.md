# Sneaker sketching and side-view template

**Goal:** fast, accurate footwear drawings that sit on a real last shape so designs are buildable.

## Build a template
1. Photograph or scan the last (or a reference shoe) in pure side view at a fixed distance,
   lens zoomed to reduce distortion.
2. Trace the **last profile**: toe spring, heel curve, bottom line, instep.
3. Add the sole profile (midsole and outsole heights) for your platform/sole.
4. Mark key points: ball line, heel counter height, topline height, eyestay start.
5. Keep as a locked layer in Illustrator/Photoshop/Procreate; designers sketch on top.

## Drawing
- **Lateral side view** is the standard review view; add medial view for asymmetric designs,
  top view for lacing and toe shape, back view for heel.
- Three-quarter view in perspective for presentation: front toe slightly larger, both sides
  of the sole visible.
- Line weights: thick outer silhouette, medium panels, thin stitching (dashed) and details.
- Panels: vamp, quarter, eyestay, toe cap, counter, collar, tongue, heel tab, foxing/midsole.

## Colour-up
- Flat fills per material with simple shading; separate layers per part so colourways are quick.
- Callouts: materials, colours (Pantone), logos, sizes in mm.

## Gotchas
- Drawing freehand without the last profile gives impossible toe springs and heights.
- Inconsistent views between sketches mislead reviewers.

## Verify
- [ ] Sketch fits the template profile.
- [ ] Panels and materials called out.
- [ ] Layers organised for colourways.
