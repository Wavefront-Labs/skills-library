# Textile and knit uppers

**Goal:** textile uppers that look intended, hold the foot and survive wear.

## Materials
- **Canvas / cotton twill:** vulcanised sneakers; durable, cheap.
- **Mesh (warp-knit):** breathable, light; needs backing and reinforcement.
- **Engineered knit uppers** (flat-knitted one-piece, e.g. Flyknit, Primeknit type): zones of
  tight structure for support and open structure for breathability, knitted to shape
  with little waste.
- **Spacer mesh:** cushioned collars and tongues.

## Design for function
- Map zones: lockdown at midfoot and heel, flex at forefoot, ventilation at toe/vamp.
- Add support with **TPU overlays** (welded/fused films), skins, or tighter knit structures.
- Reinforce eyelet rows, heel counter area, toe cap (rubber toe bumper on canvas).
- Fusing/laminating a backing stabilises stretch; balance with comfort.

## Spec
- Yarn type (polyester, nylon, TPU-coated yarn for structure), gauge, colours, structure map.
- Knit uppers come off the machine as a flat shape; give a 2D pattern overlay with zones.

## Testing
- Tear strength (SATRA TM162), flex (TM55 Bally flex), abrasion (Martindale on linings),
  eyelet pull, colourfastness to rubbing.

## Gotchas
- Pure knit uppers stretch out; build in structure.
- Mesh tears around lace eyelets without reinforcement.
- Light canvas shows dirt; consider coated or dark options.
