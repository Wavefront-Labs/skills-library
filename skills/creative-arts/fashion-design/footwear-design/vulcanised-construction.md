# Vulcanised construction

**Goal:** design within the vulcanised sneaker process (canvas skate and basketball classics).

## Process
1. Canvas or suede upper lasted onto an insole board.
2. Rubber sole (uncured) cemented on; **foxing tape** (rubber strip) wrapped around the sole
   edge and lower upper; **toe bumper/cap** applied.
3. Shoe on last goes into an **autoclave** (heat and pressure, roughly 120-140 C for about
   an hour) to vulcanise the rubber, bonding sole, foxing and upper.

## Character
- Flexible, flat, grippy; board feel (skateboarding favourite); classic sidewall look.
- Heavier than cemented EVA; limited cushioning (add insole).

## Design points
- **Foxing tape** height (sidewall) commonly 25-40 mm; stripes and colours can be
  integrated as separate tapes.
- Toe bumper texture (diamond/ribbed) and mudguard.
- Upper materials must withstand autoclave heat: canvas, suede, some leathers; many
  synthetics and printed films distort.
- Tread: herringbone or waffle common.

## Gotchas
- Colour shifts during cure (white rubber yellows if formulated poorly).
- Glue stains on canvas; plan clean-up.
- Very complex sidewall shapes are limited compared to cupsoles.

## Verify
- [ ] Upper materials heat-tested.
- [ ] Foxing and bumper colours approved after cure.
- [ ] Sole bond and flex tested.
