# Hands, feet and shoes on the figure

## Sizes (9-head croquis)
- **Hand** = about 3/4 of a head (wrist to fingertip); an open hand covers the face.
- **Foot** = about 1 to 1.25 heads (stylised long); seen from the front it is foreshortened
  to roughly half that.
- Wrist falls at crotch level with the arm hanging; fingertips at mid-thigh.

## Hands
- Block as a mitten: palm wedge + finger mass + thumb wedge; split fingers only where needed.
- Fashion hands are elegant and slightly tapered; avoid knuckle detail and fingernails.
- Useful gestures: hand on hip (fingers behind, show thumb), hand in pocket (only the
  wrist line), relaxed hanging hand (slight curve, fingers together).

## Feet and shoes
1. Draw the foot inside the shoe: ankle bones (inner higher than outer), instep, ball, toe.
2. **Heel height sets the foot angle**: flats keep the foot nearly horizontal; a 10 cm
   heel tilts the foot steeply with the instep almost vertical and the ankle lifted.
3. Front view: the toe box is an ellipse foreshortened toward the viewer; the heel
   tip sits behind and slightly below the ankle.
4. Walking pose: the back foot shows the sole and heel, the front foot is planted.
5. Shoes follow the leg's perspective; both soles sit on the same ground plane.

## Gotchas
- Tiny feet on a heavy shoe make the figure topple; ground the weight-bearing foot under the pit of the neck.
- Heel and foot angle mismatch (high heel drawn on a flat foot) is the most common error.
- Hands in front of garments hide design detail; place them away from key features.

## Verify
- [ ] Hand and foot length in proportion to head unit.
- [ ] Foot angle matches heel height.
- [ ] Both feet on one ground line.
