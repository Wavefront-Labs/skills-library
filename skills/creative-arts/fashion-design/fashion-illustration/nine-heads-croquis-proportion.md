# Nine-heads figure proportion grid

**Goal:** a consistent, elongated fashion figure (croquis) that shows garments clearly and
can be reproduced in any pose. Real adults are roughly 7-7.5 heads tall; fashion uses
8.5-10, most commonly 9 (Riegelman, *9 Heads*).

## The grid (front view, standing)
Draw a vertical centre line and 10 equal horizontal lines (0 to 9 head units).
| Head line | Landmark |
|---|---|
| 0-1 | Top of head to chin |
| 1.5 | Shoulder line (about 2 head widths across for women, 2.5 for men) |
| 2 | Bust/chest (apex) |
| 3 | Waist (narrowest, about 1 head width plus a bit) |
| 3.5-4 | Hip/crotch (hips about as wide as the shoulders for women) |
| 5.5 | Knee (lengthened thighs; the extra length goes in the legs) |
| 8.5-9 | Ankle to sole |
Arms: elbow at waist level, wrist at crotch level, fingertips at mid-thigh.

## Steps
1. Grid lightly; draw head oval (1 unit).
2. Neck (about 1/3 head width), shoulder line with slight slope.
3. Torso as two trapezoids (shoulders to waist, waist to hips).
4. Legs as tapered cylinders; knee at 5.5, ankle narrow.
5. Arms with joints at the landmarks; hands about 3/4 head length.
6. Check symmetry by flipping the drawing (mirror or digital flip).

## Gotchas
- Lengthen legs, not the torso; a long torso looks odd and breaks garment proportions.
- Garments drawn on a 9-head figure look longer than on a person; when proportions
  matter for production, check on a 7.5-head realistic figure or a photo.
- Male croquis: wider shoulders, narrower hips, thicker neck, less waist definition.

## Verify
- [ ] Landmarks fall on the correct head lines.
- [ ] Mirror check shows no lean or asymmetry.
- [ ] The croquis traces cleanly for reuse as a template.
