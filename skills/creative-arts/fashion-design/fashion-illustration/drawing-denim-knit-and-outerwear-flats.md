# Denim, knit and outerwear flats

## Denim (5-pocket jeans as the reference)
- Show: waistband with belt loops (count them), fly with J-stitch, front scoop pockets
  with coin pocket, rivets as small dots, back yoke, back patch pockets with stitch design,
  back label, hem.
- Topstitching is the design; draw twin-needle rows as two dashed lines and call out
  thread colour, ticket and SPI.
- Wash effects (whiskers, honeycombs) as light callouts, not rendered.

## Knits (cut-and-sew and fully fashioned)
- Rib: fine parallel vertical lines in cuffs, hem and neckband; state 1x1 / 2x2.
- Cover-stitch hems: two or three parallel dashed lines.
- Flatlock seams: a ladder-stitch line.
- Fully fashioned: show fashioning marks along armhole and raglan lines.

## Outerwear
- Draw closed front plus an open-front or inside view to show lining, inside pockets and facings.
- Hoods: show up and down, or on a separate view; detachable hoods with snap/zip callouts.
- Show storm flaps, zip garages, adjustable cuffs, drawcords with cord locks, vents, seam taping.
- Quilting/baffles: draw channel lines and spacing; call out fill weight.

## Gotchas
- Missing back view on jeans: the back pocket stitch design is the brand ID.
- Knit flats drawn with woven-style seams confuse the factory about construction.
- Outerwear without an inside view gets sampled with the wrong lining and pockets.

## Verify
- [ ] All topstitch rows and rivets shown on denim.
- [ ] Rib, cover stitch and flatlock notated on knits.
- [ ] Outerwear inside view included.
