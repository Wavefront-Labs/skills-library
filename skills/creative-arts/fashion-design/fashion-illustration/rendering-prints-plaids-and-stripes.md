# Rendering prints, plaids and stripes

## Scale first
Measure the real repeat or check size against the body: a 10 cm check on a 170 cm
figure is roughly 1/17 of figure height. Get this right before drawing; wrong scale is
the most misleading error in a presentation.

## Stripes and plaids
1. Draw stripe/plaid lines as contour lines **following the body** (cylinders):
   horizontal stripes curve over bust, hip and around arms.
2. At folds, lines **break and shift**; they don't continue straight through a fold.
3. Plaid: draw the dominant lines first in both directions, then the secondary lines,
   then the crossing colour where they overlap.
4. Note grain: bias-cut plaid runs at 45 degrees; yokes and cuffs are often cut on the cross.

## Prints
- Lay base colour, then place the motif as a **suggestion**: main motifs in the
  well-lit areas, simplified and smaller toward the edges, lost in shadow.
- Motifs are cut by seams and folds; partial motifs at edges sell the realism.
- Digital: clip the print scan to the garment area, Warp/Liquify to follow the body,
  add a Multiply shadow layer over it.

## Gotchas
- Motif drawn the same size on the far side of the body; it should compress.
- Plaids unmatched across CF without reason (matching is a costing decision; show which).
- Over-detailed prints hide the silhouette; the garment still has to read.

## Verify
- [ ] Repeat scale matches the real fabric.
- [ ] Lines curve with the body and break at folds.
- [ ] Shadow over print, not under.
