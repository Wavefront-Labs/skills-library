# Line quality and expressive line

## Weight carries information
- **Thick lines** on the shadow side, under overlaps and at the outer silhouette.
- **Thin lines** on the light side, for interior seams and details.
- **Lost lines**: drop the line where light hits (shoulder top, bust highlight); the eye fills it.
- **Overlap rule**: the front object's line is unbroken; the line behind stops at it.

## Fabric through line
- Crisp fabrics: straight strokes with sharp corners at folds.
- Soft fabrics: flowing, tapering curves.
- Heavy knits: lumpy, broken contour.
- Sheers: very fine or interrupted lines.

## Tools
- Variable width: brush pens (Pentel Pocket Brush, Tombow), dip pen, pressure brushes in
  Procreate/Photoshop with size dynamics.
- Fixed-width fineliners for flats only.

## Practice drills
- The same figure in one line weight, then with weight variation; compare depth.
- 30-second gesture drawings with a single continuous line.
- Draw folds with one confident stroke rather than feathered strokes.

## Gotchas
- Hairy, re-stroked lines look unsure; commit to one stroke, undo digitally if needed.
- Uniform heavy outlines make figures look like colouring-book shapes.
- Expressive does not mean inaccurate: seams and closures stay where they are.

## Verify
- [ ] Line weight heavier on the shadow side and silhouette.
- [ ] Lost edges where light hits.
- [ ] Construction details still legible.
