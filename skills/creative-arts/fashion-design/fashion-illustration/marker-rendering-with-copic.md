# Marker rendering technique (Copic)

**Goal:** fast, clean, professional colour on fashion sketches with alcohol markers.

## Materials
- **Paper**: marker paper or smooth bleed-proof paper (Copic/Letraset marker pads);
  normal paper bleeds and drinks ink.
- **Markers**: Copic Sketch/Ciao (refillable). Code system: letter = family (YR, B, E for
  earth/skin), first number = saturation (0 vivid - 9 grey), second = lightness (0 light -
  9 dark). Blend within the same family and first number (e.g. E11, E13, E15).
- **Line art**: alcohol-proof fineliners (Copic Multiliner) or laser prints; inkjet smears.
- Colourless blender (0) for softening; white gouache or gel pen for highlights.

## Technique
1. Light base layer over the whole garment area in quick, even strokes following the
   form (vertical on a skirt, around a sleeve). Work while wet to avoid streaks.
2. Midtone in shadow zones (under bust, side of body away from light, folds).
3. Darkest tone sparingly in deepest folds and under collars.
4. Blend edges by going back with the lighter marker.
5. Highlights last: white gouache, gel pen or leave paper white.
6. Consistent light direction across the line-up (e.g. top left).

## Skin and hair
Skin with 2-3 tones of an E family; render under the garment edges for shadows.
Hair: light base, then darker streaks in direction of growth.

## Gotchas
- Going over many times lifts paper fibres and muddies colour.
- Marker colour on paper differs from cap colour; make a swatch chart.
- Alcohol markers fade in light; scan finished work promptly.

## Verify
- [ ] Bleed-proof paper and alcohol-proof line art used.
- [ ] At least three values per garment colour.
- [ ] Light direction consistent across illustrations.
