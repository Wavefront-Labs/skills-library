# Drawing collars, sleeves, pockets and closures

## Collars
- A collar **wraps around the neck cylinder**: the back of the collar is higher than the
  front; draw it rising behind the neck.
- Stand collars: a band standing up with a visible top-edge ellipse.
- Shirt collars: stand + fall; the fall overlaps the stand, points spread and lie on the chest.
- Lapels: roll line from top button to the neck; the lapel folds back and lies on the chest
  with the notch (gorge) about collarbone level.

## Sleeves
- Set-in: seam at the shoulder point, armhole curve visible.
- Raglan: diagonal seam from underarm to neck; no shoulder seam.
- Dropped / kimono: seam below the shoulder or no armhole seam; folds radiate from the underarm.
- Cuffs are cylinders: draw the opening as an ellipse; bent elbows bunch fabric on the inside.

## Pockets
- Patch pockets sit on the surface and follow the body curve; welt and jet pockets are slits.
- Place openings at hand height (front hip pockets) or on the chest line (chest welts).

## Closures
- **Womenswear laps right over left** (buttons on the wearer's right), menswear left over right.
- Buttons centre on the CF line; the extension beyond CF is about the button diameter.
- Zips: invisible = seam only; exposed = teeth; fly fronts show a J-stitch.

## Gotchas
- Buttons placed on the placket edge instead of on CF.
- Collars drawn flat as cut-outs instead of wrapping the neck.
- Wrong wrap direction is the error buyers notice first.

## Verify
- [ ] Collar rises at the back of the neck.
- [ ] Lap direction correct for the market.
- [ ] Pocket and sleeve details consistent between figure and flat.
