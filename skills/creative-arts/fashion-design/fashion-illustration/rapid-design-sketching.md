# Rapid design sketching and roughs

**Goal:** generate dozens of design ideas per hour on paper, at a quality that you and
your team can read, without polishing.

## Setup
- Print sheets of small croquis underlays (8-15 cm tall), front and back, 10-20 per A3.
- Use layout paper or tracing paper over them, or draw directly on light-grey printed figures.
- Tools: fineliner or brush pen, one grey marker for shading; no eraser.

## Method
1. Set a timer: 1-3 minutes per sketch.
2. Draw silhouette first, then main seams/closures, then one key detail.
3. Front and back side by side for anything with back interest.
4. Annotate in 2-5 words ("drop shoulder, raw hem, 2 welts").
5. Move on; don't redraw. Circle promising ones at the end of the session.

## Digital roughs
Procreate/Photoshop: croquis on a locked bottom layer at low opacity, one layer per
idea, duplicate-and-change for variations. Use a single brush (e.g. a dry ink).

## From rough to next step
- Selected roughs -> redraw larger with clear construction, then technical flats for
  anything going to sampling.
- Roughs on figures for mood; flats for making.

## Gotchas
- Slow perfect sketches kill idea flow; speed and quantity first.
- Using different croquis scales makes comparison hard; stick to one underlay.
- Unannotated roughs become unreadable after a week.

## Verify
- [ ] 20+ roughs per session with selections marked.
- [ ] Each selected rough has front and back and notes.
- [ ] Selected ideas move to flats.
