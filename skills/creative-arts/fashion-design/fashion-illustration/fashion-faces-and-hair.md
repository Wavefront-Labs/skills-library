# Fashion heads, faces and hair

**Goal:** faces and hair that support the garments and the collection's attitude without
stealing attention.

## Face proportions (front)
- Head oval about 2/3 as wide as long.
- Eyes on the horizontal halfway line; space between eyes = one eye width.
- Nose bottom halfway between eyes and chin; mouth a third of the way from nose to chin.
- Ears between eye line and nose bottom.

## Three-quarter view
- Centre line curves around the oval; the far eye is narrower and closer to the
  silhouette edge; nose breaks the contour slightly.

## Stylising
- Fashion faces are minimal: suggest eyes, brows, lips, a nose shadow; omit lines
  that age the face.
- Match the collection: sharp graphic features for tailoring, soft washes for romantic.
- Represent diverse features honestly; avoid one default face (see the skin-tone skill).

## Hair
- Draw as **mass and direction**, not strands: overall shape first, then 3-5 flowing
  lines in the growth direction, then a few accent strands.
- Hair style is part of styling: decide it once for the collection (slick, undone, cropped).
- Leave highlights white when rendering; darken near the parting and under layers.

## Gotchas
- Overworked faces date illustrations and distract from garments.
- Hair drawn larger than the shoulders changes the silhouette reading.
- Tiny heads exaggerate further; keep the head at 1 unit of a 9-head figure.

## Verify
- [ ] Features sit on the proportion lines.
- [ ] Hair reads as one shape with direction.
- [ ] Face/hair style consistent across the line-up.
