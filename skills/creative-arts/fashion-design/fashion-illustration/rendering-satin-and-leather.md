# Rendering satin, leather and sequins

**Goal:** show shine convincingly, which comes from contrast between highlights and darks,
not from bright colour.

## Satin and silk
1. Base tone (mid value).
2. Darks in the deep folds; shine fabrics have **darker darks** than matte ones.
3. Highlights: long, soft-edged streaks along the **crest of each fold**, following the
   fold direction; leave paper white or use white gouache/gel pen.
4. Reflected light: a thin lighter edge inside dark areas, near the body edge.
5. Few lines; let tone do the work.

## Leather
- Matte leather: base, soft darks, small highlights at creases (elbows, knees).
- **Patent**: extreme contrast, sharp-edged white highlights, almost black darks; reflections
  can include window shapes.
- Suede/nubuck: no highlights; soft, slightly textured tone (dry brush, stippling).
- Show thickness at edges and seams (topstitching, folded edges).

## Sequins and metallics
- Suggest with clusters: dots of white, light and dark across the area, concentrated on
  highlight zones; paint a few individual discs near edges.
- Metallic: hue in midtones, very dark darks, near-white highlights (gold: ochre,
  brown, pale yellow-white).

## Media notes
Markers: work light to dark, leave white for highlights. Gouache: paint highlights
last. Digital: Overlay/Add layer for highlights, soft brush for satin, hard for patent.

## Gotchas
- Highlights everywhere = plastic look; place them on fold crests only.
- Pure white highlights on dark colours can look like holes; tint slightly.

## Verify
- [ ] Value contrast stronger than for matte fabrics.
- [ ] Highlights follow fold crests.
- [ ] Leather type (matte, patent, suede) distinguishable.
