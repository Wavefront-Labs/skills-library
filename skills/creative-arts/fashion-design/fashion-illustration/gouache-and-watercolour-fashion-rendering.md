# Gouache and watercolour fashion rendering

## Materials
- Paper: 300 gsm (140 lb) cold-pressed watercolour paper, or hot-pressed for fine line work.
  Tape all edges to a board to stop cockling.
- Watercolour pans/tubes, a gouache set (primaries, white, black), round brushes size 2-8, a flat 12 mm.
- Transfer the drawing lightly with a hard pencil (H-2H); graphite under washes shows through.

## Watercolour order (light to dark, transparent)
1. Skin wash, diluted; let it dry fully.
2. Garment base washes: wet-into-wet for soft gradients (silks), wet-on-dry for crisp edges.
3. Shadow wash once dry: the same colour plus a touch of its complement, not black.
4. Leave white paper for highlights (masking fluid if needed).

## Gouache (opaque, matte)
- Mix to a creamy consistency; apply in one even layer for flat, poster-like colour.
- Highlights go last on top (opaque covers).
- Good for solid colour blocks, prints painted over a base, and correcting mistakes.
- Re-wets when painted over; use light strokes so the layer below does not lift.

## Combining
Watercolour for skin and fluid fabrics, gouache for opaque fabrics and details, then
fineliner or coloured-pencil line on top.

## Gotchas
- Overworking watercolour makes mud; limit to about three layers in one area.
- Gouache shifts value as it dries (darks dry lighter); test a swatch first.
- Scans of gouache can look flat; scan at 600 dpi and correct levels.

## Verify
- [ ] Paper taped and flat when dry.
- [ ] Highlights preserved.
- [ ] Colours matched to the colour story swatches.
