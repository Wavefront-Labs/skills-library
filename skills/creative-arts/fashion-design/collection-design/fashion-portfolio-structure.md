# Portfolio structure and project sequencing

**Goal:** a portfolio that shows how you think and what you can deliver for the specific
employer or course, in 20-40 pages.

## Content per project (4-10 pages each)
1. Concept page: title, one-line idea, key research image.
2. Research and development: sketchbook spreads, drapes, fabric tests (show process).
3. Design development: iteration sheets, line-up.
4. Final illustrations and technical flats.
5. Photographs of realised garments (studio/lookbook) where available.

## Sequence
- **Open with your strongest project**, close with the second strongest.
- 3-5 projects; vary to show range (womenswear and menswear, knit, tailoring, print),
  but keep one recognisable point of view.
- Tailor per application: a sportswear brand wants technical flats, fabric innovation
  and commercial sense; a luxury house wants craft and concept.

## Format
- **Digital**: PDF under ~10-15 MB for email, landscape, consistent grid; also a website
  or Issuu/Behance for links. Name: `Firstname-Lastname-Portfolio-2026.pdf`.
- **Print**: A3 landscape in a sleeve folder or bound book; same order as digital.
- Typography plain and consistent; let the work dominate.

## Gotchas
- Only finished illustrations: interviewers want process.
- Too many projects dilutes; quality over quantity.
- Uncredited team or photographer work; credit collaborators.

## Verify
- [ ] 3-5 projects, each with research-to-final flow.
- [ ] Order tailored to the target employer.
- [ ] Digital file size and naming suitable for email.
