# Trade fairs and swatch requests

**Goal:** come back from a fair with the right swatches, prices and contacts, and turn
them into ordered sample lengths on time.

## Before
- Know the calendar: Premiere Vision Paris (February and September), Milano Unica,
  Texworld (Paris), Munich Fabric Start, Kingpins (denim), regional fairs.
- Register as a buyer (proof of business needed). Prepare: concept boards, colour
  palette, target price per metre, quantities, deadlines, business cards.
- Shortlist exhibitors from the online catalogue by product type; book key meetings.

## At the fair
- Visit trend/forum areas first for an overview, then booths.
- Per booth, request swatches (hangers/headers) by article number; record on your
  sheet: mill, article, content, weight, width, price, MOQ, sample-length availability,
  lead time, stock service.
- Ask about **stock-service** fabrics for small brands (no or low MOQ).
- Photograph headers (with permission) as a backup.

## After
- Swatches arrive 2-6 weeks later; log them in on arrival.
- Request **sample yardage** (typically 3-10 m) for toiles/samples; confirm price and
  shipping. Some mills charge for sample lengths below a threshold.
- Follow up within a week with a written summary of what you asked for.

## Gotchas
- Small brands may be refused by mills with large MOQs; target agents and converters.
- Prices quoted at fairs may exclude finishing or dye surcharges; confirm in writing.
- Swatches vanish in studio chaos; log them immediately.

## Verify
- [ ] Every request logged with article, price, MOQ and lead time.
- [ ] Follow-up emails sent within a week.
- [ ] Sample lengths ordered in time for the sampling calendar.
