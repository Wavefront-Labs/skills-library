# Customer persona and lifestyle profile

**Goal:** a short, evidence-based description of who buys the line, so designers can test
each style against real needs, price tolerance and occasions.

## Inputs
- Existing brand data: sales by size, region, channel, price point; returns reasons.
- Surveys, interviews (5-10 customers yield clear patterns), store staff feedback.
- Social analytics (follower demographics), competitor customer reviews.

## The persona sheet (one page, 1-3 personas max)
| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| Snapshot | Name, age band, location, occupation, household, income band |
| Lifestyle | Week vs weekend, travel, climate, values (sustainability, status, comfort) |
| Wardrobe | Core items owned, gaps, size and fit issues, care habits |
| Occasions | Work, events, leisure: what each needs |
| Spend | Typical price per category, purchases per season, sale behaviour |
| Channels | Where they discover, buy and return |
| Quote | One real customer quote that captures the mindset |

## Using it
- Score each design against occasions and price: designs serving no occasion are cut or
  moved to show-only pieces.
- Size range and fit decisions come from persona fit issues and sales size curves.

## Gotchas
- Personas from imagination only are muses, not customers.
- Too many personas = no focus; pick a primary and at most two secondary.
- Refresh annually; customers age with the brand.

## Verify
- [ ] Every section rests on at least one data source.
- [ ] Price bands are stated per category.
- [ ] The line plan references persona occasions.
