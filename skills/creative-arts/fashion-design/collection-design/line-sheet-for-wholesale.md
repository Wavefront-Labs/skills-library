# Line sheets for wholesale

**Goal:** a document buyers can order from without asking questions: every style with
its data and the terms.

## Per style
- Photo or flat (front, back if important), style number, style name.
- Colours available (swatches or colour names/codes).
- Size range (and size ratio packs if sold in packs).
- Fabric content.
- Wholesale price and suggested retail price (MSRP/RRP) in the buyer's currency.
- Delivery window (e.g. "Delivery 1: 15-30 Aug").

## Terms page
- Order minimums (per order, per style, per colour).
- Payment terms (e.g. deposit with order, balance before shipping or net 30 for
  established accounts).
- Order deadline, delivery windows, cancellation date.
- Shipping terms (Incoterm), returns policy, contact details.

## Format
- PDF grouped by delivery or category; 4-9 styles per page.
- Wholesale platforms (JOOR, NuOrder, Faire) host digital line sheets and orders.
- Matching **order form** (spreadsheet) with the same style numbers.

## Gotchas
- Prices in the wrong currency or missing duty/shipping terms cause disputes.
- Styles in the line sheet that won't be produced (low orders); state the right to cancel
  styles below minimums.
- Mismatched style numbers between line sheet, order form and tech packs.

## Verify
- [ ] Every style has number, image, colours, sizes, prices, delivery.
- [ ] Terms page complete.
- [ ] Style numbers match the order form and production records.
