# Lab dip, strike-off and trim approvals

**Goal:** approve colour, prints and trims in parallel with fit samples, so bulk materials
are ready when the PP is approved.

## Lab dips (dyed solids)
- Mill sends 2-3 options (A, B, C) per colour against your standard.
- Review in a light booth under D65, then TL84/store light and incandescent.
- Comment in terms: "B: too red, slightly too dark, approve if 5% less red". Reject,
  approve, or approve with comment.
- Record delta E if instrumentally measured.

## Strike-offs (prints)
- Check: colour of each screen/ink vs standards, registration, repeat size and scale,
  print on the real base fabric, hand (stiffness) and wash test.
- Mark approved strike-off with date and signature; it becomes the bulk standard.

## Trims and yarns
- Buttons, zips, labels, hardware: colour, finish, size, logo, function (zip runs
  smoothly, snap strength). Yarn: colour and count.
- Test: colourfastness of trims (bleeding labels!), nickel compliance for metal.

## Tracker
One row per material per colourway: submit date, round, status, comment, approval date.
Highlight items without approval within 2 weeks of bulk fabric deadline.

## Gotchas
- Approving lab dips on screen photos only.
- Approving prints on a different base fabric than bulk.
- Bulk dye lots vary; request bulk shade bands for approval too.

## Verify
- [ ] Every colourway component has an approval date.
- [ ] Approved standards stored and labelled.
- [ ] Bulk shade band checked against the approved lab dip.
