# Lookbook and campaign planning

**Goal:** images that show every look clearly for buyers and press (lookbook) and/or
convey the brand mood (campaign), shot efficiently in one day.

## Decide the purpose
- **Lookbook**: every look, full length, front (plus back/details for key looks), clean
  background or simple location; consistent framing; used for wholesale and press.
- **Campaign**: fewer images, mood and story, location and art direction.
- **E-commerce**: separate standard (white/grey background, fixed poses and crops).

## Pre-production
1. Shot list: look number, garments, shots needed (full front, back, detail, movement).
2. Team: photographer, stylist, hair and make-up, model(s), assistant, digital tech.
3. Casting to the brand muse; check model sizes match samples (usually sample size).
4. Location/studio, permissions, weather backup.
5. Call sheet: times, addresses, contacts, schedule per look (~10-15 min per lookbook look).
6. Steam and prep all samples, label by look on rails with accessories bagged.

## On the day
- Tether to a laptop to review images live; check garments sit correctly (no clips visible).
- Shoot the hardest looks while the team is fresh; keep to the schedule.

## Delivery
- Select, retouch (garment colour accuracy matters more than skin smoothing).
- Export: high-res TIFF/JPEG for print, web JPEG (~2000 px long edge), with look numbers
  in filenames; caption sheet listing garments per image.
- Usage rights agreed in writing (duration, media, territory).

## Gotchas
- Colour-inaccurate images cause returns and buyer complaints; shoot a colour checker.
- Missing back views for looks with back details.

## Verify
- [ ] Every look shot per the shot list.
- [ ] Colour checked against garments.
- [ ] Captions and usage rights delivered with files.
