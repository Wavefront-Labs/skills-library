# Critical path and development calendar

**Goal:** one calendar that shows every milestone from concept to in-store, who owns it,
and which delays will move the delivery date.

## Typical milestones (worked backwards from in-store date)
| Milestone | Typical lead before in-store |
|---|---|
| Concept and research | 9-12 months |
| Line plan approved | 8-10 months |
| Design handover / tech packs out | 7-9 months |
| Proto samples in | 6-8 months |
| Fabric/trim orders (bulk) placed | 5-6 months |
| SMS / sell-in / buy finalised | 5-6 months |
| PP approved | 3-4 months |
| Production | 6-10 weeks |
| Ex-factory | 1-2 months |
| Shipping and warehouse receipt | 2-6 weeks |
Fast-fashion calendars compress to weeks; luxury may be longer. Use your suppliers' real
lead times.

## Build it
- Rows: style (or group), columns: milestones with planned date, actual date, owner.
- Mark the **longest chain** (usually fabric development -> bulk fabric -> production).
- Colour codes: on track, at risk (within one week), late.
- Review weekly in a cross-functional meeting.

## Recovering slips
- Fast-track: overlap steps (approve PP on bulk fabric strike-offs while fit finishes).
- Air-freight fabrics or finished goods (cost vs margin).
- Drop or move styles to a later drop.
- Use stock fabrics for late developments.

## Gotchas
- Calendars made from optimistic factory dates; build in buffer.
- A single late trim (custom button) can stop a whole production; track trims too.
- Chinese New Year and summer holidays close factories for weeks; include them.

## Verify
- [ ] Every style has dated milestones and owners.
- [ ] Critical chain highlighted.
- [ ] Factory holidays included; weekly review happening.
