# Designing pockets, closures and fastenings

**Goal:** details that work (hands fit, garment opens) and that carry the design idea.

## Pockets
| Type | Look | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Patch | Visible, casual | Cheapest; shape, flap and topstitch carry style |
| In-seam | Invisible | Follows side seam; bag must not show |
| Slant / jeans front | Casual trousers | Facing shows |
| Welt (single/double/jetted) | Tailored | Needs interfacing; costly |
| Flap | Formal or utility | Covers welt or patch |
| Cargo / bellows | Utility | Gusseted volume |
| Kangaroo | Sweatshirts | Two hand openings |
**Sizing**: hand opening at least ~15-17 cm for adult hands; bag depth to hold a phone
(~18-20 cm). Place hand pockets where the wrist falls with relaxed arms (roughly at or
just below the hip).

## Closures
- **Buttons**: size in ligne (L; 1 L = 0.635 mm). Shirts ~16-18 L, jacket fronts ~30-36 L,
  coats ~40 L+. Buttonhole length = button diameter + thickness (+ ~2-3 mm).
- **Zips**: coil (light, flexible), Vislon/moulded (sportswear), metal (jeans, workwear),
  invisible (dresses). Spec gauge (#3, #5, #8), length, open-end vs closed-end, puller.
- **Snaps/press studs, hooks and eyes, toggles, ties, magnets, Velcro**.
- Overlap/extension for buttons at least the button diameter (often 1.5-2 cm for shirts).

## Placement and function
Closure position changes dressing: back zips need help; side zips are hidden; centre
front is easiest. Check that the opening is big enough to pass the head/hips.

## Spec
Type, size, quantity, colour/finish, supplier ref, placement measurements from CF/HPS.

## Gotchas
- Pockets on lightweight fabrics drag; use stays or lighter pocketing.
- Too-small buttons on heavy coats pop; check shank and size.
- Zips shorter than the opening needed = garment can't be put on.

## Verify
- [ ] Hand and phone fit tested.
- [ ] Closure sizes and suppliers in the BOM.
- [ ] Dressing test done on a fit model.
