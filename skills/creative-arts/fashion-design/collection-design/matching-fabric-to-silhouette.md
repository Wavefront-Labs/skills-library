# Matching fabric to silhouette and drape

**Goal:** choose fabrics that will actually produce the drawn shape, before cutting toiles
in the wrong cloth.

## Properties to judge
- **Drape**: how it falls under its own weight (fluid: crepe, satin, jersey; stiff:
  taffeta, canvas).
- **Body/crispness**: holds shape away from the body (organza, taffeta, scuba).
- **Weight** (g/m2) and **thickness** (affects seams, darts, collars).
- **Stretch and recovery** (elastane content; mechanical stretch in twills).
- **Surface**: shine shows every fit fault; matte forgives.
- **Bias behaviour**: does it drape well on the bias (crepe, satin, soft wools)?

## Silhouette match
| Intended shape | Good fabrics | Poor fabrics |
|---|---|---|
| Fluid, body-skimming | Crepe de chine, viscose crepe, satin-back crepe, jersey | Canvas, taffeta |
| Standing volume | Taffeta, gazar, duchess satin, scuba, organza | Jersey, chiffon |
| Tailored | Wool suiting, gabardine, twill | Slippery satin, loose knits |
| Gathered softness | Voile, lawn, georgette | Heavy denim |

## Quick drape tests
1. Hang a 50x50 cm square from one corner, photograph the folds.
2. Pin a length on the dress form in the intended shape (pleats, gathers).
3. Gather a 1 m length to 30 cm: does it bunch or fall in soft folds?
4. Fold and crush for 30 s: crease recovery.
Measured drape coefficient (BS EN ISO 9073-9 / Cusick drape tester) is used in labs.

## Gotchas
- Calico toiles hide drape issues; final toile in the real or a similar fabric.
- Stretch fabrics need patterns with negative ease; a woven pattern in jersey bags.

## Verify
- [ ] Each look's fabric chosen for a stated property.
- [ ] Drape test photographed for the key fabrics.
- [ ] A final toile cut in the real or equivalent fabric.
