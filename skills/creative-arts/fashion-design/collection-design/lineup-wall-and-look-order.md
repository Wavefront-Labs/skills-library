# The line-up wall and look order

**Goal:** see the whole collection at once at the same scale, so you can judge balance,
order and gaps the way an audience or buyer will.

## Build the wall
1. Draw or print every look on the **same croquis, same pose, same scale** (e.g. 25 cm
   high), front view. Add back views below for key looks.
2. Colour them accurately (marker or digital) and add fabric swatches beneath each.
3. Pin in a row at eye level on a long wall. Leave space between groups.
4. Add photos of toiles/samples as they arrive, replacing drawings.

## Order the looks (show or lookbook order)
- **Opening look**: the clearest statement of the concept.
- **Groups/stories**: cluster by colour or fabric story; transition between groups with a
  look that shares elements of both.
- **Rhythm**: alternate silhouettes (volume, slim, volume) and intensity (simple,
  complex) so the eye stays engaged.
- **Colour flow**: light to dark or neutral to accent is common; avoid jumping back.
- **Finale**: the most spectacular or most emotional look.

## Spot gaps
Walk away 3-4 m and look for: missing categories (no coats?), repeated looks, colour
holes, too many similar silhouettes in a row, missing bottoms.

## Gotchas
- Mixed scales and poses make looks impossible to compare.
- The wall at desk distance hides balance; step back.
- Photos of the wall each week document the evolution for the portfolio.

## Verify
- [ ] All looks at same scale and pose.
- [ ] Order written with group and transition notes.
- [ ] Gap list produced from the wall review.
