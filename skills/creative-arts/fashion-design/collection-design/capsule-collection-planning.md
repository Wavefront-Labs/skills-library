# Capsule collection planning

**Goal:** a small, tightly focused collection (often for a collaboration, a launch, a
travel/occasion story or a sustainable "fewer, better" offer) where pieces multiply into
many outfits.

## Rules
- **Size**: 8-20 pieces (Donna Karan's 1985 "Seven Easy Pieces" is the classic reference).
- **Palette**: 2-3 neutrals + 1-2 accents; all tops combine with all bottoms.
- **Fabrics**: 3-5 qualities shared across pieces (eases minimums).
- **One story** in one sentence.

## Plan with an outfit matrix
1. List pieces by layer: base tops (3-5), bottoms (3-4), dresses/one-piece (1-2), mid
   layers (2-3), outer (1-2), accessories (1-3).
2. Build a grid tops x bottoms; mark each combination that works. Target 80%+.
3. Count outfits: a 12-piece capsule can deliver 30+ outfits; use the count in marketing.
4. Remove any piece that only works with one other piece.

## Collaboration capsules
Define what each party brings (brand codes vs partner signature), approval rights,
logo placement, and timeline; capsules often launch in a single drop with heavy marketing.

## Gotchas
- Including statement pieces that combine with nothing breaks the capsule premise.
- Capsules sell out fast or sit; plan replenishment or a clear limited-edition message.

## Verify
- [ ] Outfit matrix shows 80%+ combinable pairs.
- [ ] Palette and fabrics limited as planned.
- [ ] Single-sentence story exists and every piece serves it.
