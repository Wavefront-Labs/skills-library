# Assembling a fabric and trim board

**Goal:** one board (physical and photographed) that shows every material in the
collection, with the data needed to order it.

## Swatches
- Cut generous swatches: ~8x8 cm minimum for solids, larger (15x15 cm or a full repeat)
  for prints and textures. Pinked edges stop fraying.
- Mount so they can be **touched and flipped** (staple or tape on the top edge only);
  hand and drape matter more than look.
- Fold one swatch per fabric over itself to show drape.

## Layout
- Group by story (e.g. "workwear canvases", "night silks") or by weight (heavy to light).
- Place colour strip and key image from the moodboard for context.
- Trims below: buttons, zips, labels, threads, hardware, with sizes.

## Label every swatch
Supplier, article/quality number, content (e.g. 98% CO 2% EA), weight (g/m2), width,
price per metre, MOQ, lead time, finish. Supplier header cards often carry these.

## Link to looks
Number swatches (F01, F02...); put the numbers on design sketches and the line plan so
every look shows its fabrics.

## Gotchas
- Swatches without supplier info cannot be reordered; record before you lose the header.
- Glue on swatches shows through sheers and stains; use tape on the back.
- Screen photos of fabric are deceptive; the physical board travels to fittings.

## Verify
- [ ] Each swatch labelled with supplier, article, content, weight, price.
- [ ] Every look references fabric numbers.
- [ ] Trims shown with size and finish.
