# Runway show planning and running order

**Goal:** present the collection live in 10-15 minutes with every look shown as intended
and no backstage chaos.

## Format and calendar
- Runway, presentation (static models), film, or showroom only. Fashion week slots are
  managed by bodies (British Fashion Council, FHCM in Paris, CFDA, CNMI) with
  application deadlines months ahead.
- Venue: runway length (walk time ~30-60 s per look), audience seating, lighting rig,
  backstage space, power.

## Casting and fittings
- Casting 1-2 weeks before; model fittings 1-3 days before: each model tries their looks,
  alterations noted, photos taken per look.
- One model per look or doubles with quick changes (changes need 2-3 minutes minimum).

## Running order and boards
- Final order from the line-up; each look has a **look board** backstage: photo,
  garments, accessories, shoes, styling notes, model name, dresser.
- Running-order sheet at the entrance gate with look numbers and model names.

## Backstage
- Rails per model, labelled; dressers assigned (1 dresser per 1-2 models).
- Hair and make-up schedule; steamers, sewing kit, tape, safety pins.
- Line-up check (final walk-through in order) before doors open.

## Show
- Rehearsal for walk pace and lighting; music length = number of looks x walk interval
  plus finale.
- Finale lap; designer bow.

## Gotchas
- Late model arrivals from previous shows during fashion week; book backups.
- Shoes that don't fit cause stumbles; fit shoes at fittings.
- Unlabelled accessories get lost; bag per look.

## Verify
- [ ] Look boards and running order printed.
- [ ] Every model fitted with photos.
- [ ] Rehearsal timed against music.
