# Sample stages: proto to TOP

**Goal:** know what each sample is for, what it must be made of, and what approval it
needs, so problems are caught at the cheapest stage.

## Stages
| Stage | Purpose | Fabric/trims | Approval |
|---|---|---|---|
| Proto (1st, 2nd proto) | Check design, construction, basic fit | Substitute fabric acceptable | Design + technical |
| Fit sample(s) | Perfect fit at base size | Real or close fabric | Technical/fit |
| SMS (salesman/sales samples) | Selling to wholesale, photography | Real fabric and colour, often several sets | Merchandising |
| Size set | Check grading across sizes | Real fabric | Technical |
| PP (pre-production) | Final check of everything before bulk | Bulk fabric, trims, labels | Signed approval, kept as standard |
| TOP (top of production) | First units off the line | Bulk | QC compares to PP |
| Shipment sample | From the shipped goods | Bulk | QC |

## Rules
- Write comments per sample on a dated sheet; the factory answers comment by comment.
- Each stage has a deadline on the critical path; late protos compress fit rounds.
- Keep the approved PP sealed (sealed sample) as the contract reference.

## Gotchas
- Approving SMS in sample fabric when bulk differs leads to customer complaints.
- Skipping size sets causes grading failures in bulk.
- Endless proto rounds; cap at 2-3 and escalate design decisions.

## Verify
- [ ] Each style tracked by stage with dates.
- [ ] PP sealed and signed.
- [ ] TOP compared to PP with written results.
