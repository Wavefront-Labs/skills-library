# Glove design

**Goal:** gloves that fit the hand, allow movement and carry the design.

## Anatomy
- **Trank:** main front and back piece (one piece wrapping the hand for leather gloves).
- **Thumb:** separate piece; Bolton thumb (one piece with gusset) or quirk thumb.
- **Fourchettes:** narrow strips between fingers forming finger sides.
- **Quirks:** small triangles at the base of fingers for movement.
- **Points:** decorative stitching on the back (three lines classic).
- **Cuff / gauntlet:** length options (wrist, short, opera).

## Sizing
- Measure hand circumference around the knuckles (excluding thumb) in inches; size = that
  number (e.g. 7, 7.5, 8 for women; 8.5-10 men). Or S/M/L.
- Finger lengths matter; dress gloves fit close; leather stretches widthways (cut so stretch
  runs across the hand).

## Materials
- Leather: hair sheep (fine, strong), lamb nappa, deerskin, peccary (premium, CITES-dependent),
  suede. Usually 0.5-0.8 mm.
- Knit: wool, cashmere; fully fashioned or seamless glove machines (Shima glove machines).
- Linings: silk, cashmere, wool, fleece, thermal.

## Features
- Touchscreen fingertips (conductive thread or leather treatment).
- Wrist vents, buttons, snaps; elastic gathers for fit.

## Gotchas
- Leather cut with stretch running lengthwise makes long, baggy fingers.
- Thick linings require sizing up.
- Seams on the inside (inseam) look refined but are harder to make; outseams for casual.
