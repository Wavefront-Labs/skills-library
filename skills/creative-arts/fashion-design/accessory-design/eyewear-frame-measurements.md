# Frame measurements (eye size, bridge, temple)

**Goal:** design frames that fit the target faces, using the industry measurement system
printed on every frame (e.g. "52-18-145").

## Boxing system (ISO 8624)
- **Eye size (A)**: horizontal width of the lens box, mm (often 46-58 for optical).
- **Bridge (DBL)**: distance between lenses at the narrowest point, mm (often 14-22).
- **Temple length**: from hinge to tip, mm (135, 140, 145, 150 common).
- **B measurement**: lens height (vertical box) - progressive lenses need roughly 28-30+ mm.
- **Total frame width**: about 2 x A + DBL + 2 x end-piece width; should match face width at
  the temples (roughly 125-145 mm for adults).
- Marking on the inside temple: model, colour, A-DBL-temple, CE marking.

## Fit factors
- **Pantoscopic tilt**: lens plane tilts forward at the bottom (~8-12 degrees typical).
- **Wrap/face form**: curve of the front (sunglasses more wrap; high wrap affects optics).
- **Nose fit**: bridge shape and pad position; low-bridge (Asian fit) versions with deeper
  pads or lower bridge height.
- **Temple bend** location (length to bend) to sit behind the ear.

## Standards
- ISO 12870 (spectacle frames: requirements and test methods - nickel release, dimensional
  stability, endurance).
- Medical device rules for prescription frames in some markets (EU MDR, FDA).

## Gotchas
- Wide eye sizes with high wrap distort prescription lenses.
- One-size frames don't fit; plan 2-3 sizes for key models.

## Verify
- [ ] A, DBL, temple and B specified per size.
- [ ] Total width checked against target face widths.
- [ ] Tested for ISO 12870 compliance by the manufacturer.
