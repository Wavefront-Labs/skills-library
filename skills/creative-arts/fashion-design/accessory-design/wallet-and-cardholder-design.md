# Wallet and cardholder design

**Goal:** slim, durable small leather goods whose slots hold real cards and notes without
being too tight or too loose.

## Key dimensions
- Bank cards: **ISO/IEC 7810 ID-1 = 85.60 x 53.98 mm, 0.76 mm thick**.
- Card slot inner width: card width + ~3-5 mm (about 89-91 mm) for a snug fit; leather
  stretches, so test with the real leather.
- Slot depth: usually 55-65 mm visible pocket, stepped 10-15 mm between slots so cards
  are staggered and visible.
- Note compartments: longest common note (e.g. euro 500 notes are 160 x 82 mm, USD 156 x
  66 mm, GBP up to ~150 x 85 mm). Size for target markets.

## Types
Cardholder (2-6 slots), bifold (billfold), trifold, long/continental wallet with zip
around, coin pouch, passport holder, key case.

## Thickness control
- Each layer adds thickness; skive (thin) leather at folds and stacked edges to 0.6-0.8 mm.
- Count layers at the thickest point (often the fold with all slots) and aim for a total
  that closes flat.
- Use thin linings (lambskin, microfibre) inside.

## Construction and finish
- Turned edge (folded) vs raw painted edge (see edge finishing); stitching 7-10 stitches per
  inch with bonded polyester or linen thread.
- RFID-blocking layers if requested (adds thickness).

## Gotchas
- Slots made to card size in stiff leather won't accept cards; prototype in real leather.
- Too many layers at the fold prevent closing; redesign slot layout.
- Stretch over time loosens slots; leave slightly snug at first.

## Verify
- [ ] Slots tested with real cards, 1 and 2 cards per slot.
- [ ] Note compartment fits target currency.
- [ ] Wallet closes flat with full load.
