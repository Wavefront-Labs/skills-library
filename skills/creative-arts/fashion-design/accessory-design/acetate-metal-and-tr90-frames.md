# Acetate, metal and TR90 frames

**Goal:** pick frame materials that match the look, weight, price and adjustability needed.

## Materials
| Material | Character | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Cellulose acetate** | Rich colours and laminates (tortoiseshell, layers), warm hand, adjustable with heat | Cut from blocks (Mazzucchelli is a leading supplier); polished; heavier |
| **Bio-acetate** | Acetate with bio-based plasticiser | Check biodegradability claims |
| **Injection plastics** (nylon, TR90, grilamid) | Light, flexible, cheap to mould | TR90 very flexible; sport and kids |
| **Titanium / beta titanium** | Very light, strong, hypoallergenic | Premium; thin profiles |
| **Stainless steel** | Strong, cheaper than titanium | Can contain nickel |
| **Monel / nickel silver** | Easy to form and plate | Nickel allergy risk; plate well |
| **Wood, horn** | Craft, premium | Limited adjustability |

## Hinges and parts
- Barrel hinges (3, 5, 7 knuckles) in acetate; spring hinges for comfort.
- Core wires inside acetate temples allow adjustment.
- Nose pads on metal frames (adjustable) vs moulded bridge on acetate (fit by bridge size).

## Specs
- Material, colour code, front thickness (e.g. 6-8 mm acetate), finish (polished/matte),
  hinge type, core wire, nickel release compliance (EN 1811).

## Gotchas
- Asian fit vs global fit: low nose bridges need adjustable pads or a lower bridge fit.
- Thin acetate fronts flex and lose lenses.
- TR90 cannot be heat-adjusted like acetate.

## Verify
- [ ] Material and hinge specified.
- [ ] Fit tested on different nose bridges.
- [ ] Nickel compliance confirmed.
