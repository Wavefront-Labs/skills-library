# Head sizing and hat fit

**Goal:** hats in sizes that fit, with the right head shape and adjustment.

## Measuring
- Tape around the head, about 1 cm above the ears and across the forehead at the
  widest point at the back (where the hat sits). Keep the tape snug, not tight.
- Measure 2-3 times; use the largest.

## Size systems
| cm | US | UK | Alpha |
|---|---|---|---|
| 54 | 6 3/4 | 6 5/8 | S |
| 56 | 7 | 6 7/8 | M |
| 58 | 7 1/4 | 7 1/8 | L |
| 60 | 7 1/2 | 7 3/8 | XL |
| 62 | 7 3/4 | 7 5/8 | XXL |
- US size = circumference in inches / pi (approx); UK sizes run 1/8 smaller than US.

## Head shape
- **Intermediate oval** is the default head shape for most blocks; **long oval** and **round
  oval** exist for different heads. A round head in a long oval hat feels tight at the sides.

## Fit adjustments
- Sweatband (leather, grosgrain) adds finish and grip; size adjusters: foam tape behind the
  sweatband, internal drawstrings, elastic, or adjustable back straps (caps).
- Fascinators and headpieces are not sized; they attach with combs, clips, headbands or
  elastic (see headpiece skill).

## Gotchas
- Hat blocks labelled in one system and products sold in another; publish both.
- Felt hats shrink in rain; leave a small allowance or offer sizing tape.
