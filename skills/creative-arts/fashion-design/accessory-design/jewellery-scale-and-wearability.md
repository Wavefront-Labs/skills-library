# Jewellery scale and wearability

**Goal:** jewellery that looks right in proportion to the body and is comfortable to wear all day.

## Necklaces (lengths)
| Name | Length (cm) | Sits |
|---|---|---|
| Collar | 30-35 | High on the neck |
| Choker | 35-40 | Base of neck |
| Princess | 43-48 | Collarbone |
| Matinee | 50-60 | Above the bust |
| Opera | 70-90 | Bust or below |
| Rope | 100+ | Can double |
- Add a 5 cm extender chain for adjustable fit.

## Earrings
- Weight: aim under ~6-8 g per earring for all-day studs and drops; heavy statement pieces
  need clip-on or post with large backs, or lighter materials (hollow, resin, aluminium).
- Post length about 10-12 mm; posts 0.8 mm (20 gauge) common.

## Rings
- Sizes: US numeric, UK letters, EU circumference in mm (e.g. US 7 = UK O = 54.4 mm).
- Wide bands (> 6 mm) need about half a size larger; **comfort fit** (domed interior) eases on.
- Keep stone settings low and claws smooth to avoid snagging.

## Bracelets
- Wrist plus 1.5-2.5 cm for chains; bangles must pass over the hand (measure hand at widest,
  thumb tucked).

## Wearability checks
- Edges and burrs smoothed; claws snag knits and hair.
- Clasps: sizes matched to piece weight (see findings skill).
- Balance: pendants with the bail positioned so they do not flip.

## Gotchas
- Drawing jewellery at arbitrary scale; draw at 1:1 or on a body photo.
- Heavy earrings stretch earlobes; offer lighter alternatives.

## Verify
- [ ] Piece weighed and worn for a day.
- [ ] Sizes in all markets' systems.
- [ ] Snag test on a knit.
