# Hat shapes and face proportion

**Goal:** choose and adjust hat shapes so they flatter the wearer and fit the collection.

## Anatomy
Crown (height, shape: teardrop, pinch, telescope, flat top), brim (width, angle: snap,
flat, down-turned), band, tip, side band, under-brim.

## Classic shapes
| Shape | Features |
|---|---|
| Fedora | Pinched teardrop crown, 5-7 cm brim, snap brim |
| Trilby | Shorter crown, narrow brim angled down at back |
| Homburg | Single dent crown, curled brim |
| Boater | Flat crown and flat brim, stiff straw |
| Cloche | Bell shape close to the head, small brim (1920s) |
| Pillbox | Small, flat, brimless |
| Beret | Soft round flat crown |
| Wide-brim / picture hat | Brim 10 cm+ |
| Bucket | Sloping brim, soft |
| Baseball cap | Six-panel crown, visor |

## Proportion
- Brim width relates to shoulder width and overall silhouette: wide brims balance strong
  shoulders and full skirts; narrow brims suit slim tailoring.
- Crown height adds height; low crowns suit round faces less, taller crowns lengthen.
- Angle: tilting hats (forward, side) adds attitude; specify the wearing angle.
- Test on a range of faces; the same hat in three brim widths is a quick experiment.

## Gotchas
- Very wide brims are impractical (wind, doorways); weight balance matters.
- Crown height too low sits on top of the head instead of on it.

## Verify
- [ ] Crown and brim dimensions specified.
- [ ] Proportion tested on several face shapes and with outfits.
- [ ] Wearing angle described.
