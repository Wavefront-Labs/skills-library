# Bag closures (zips, clasps, turn-locks)

**Goal:** closures that suit the bag's look and use, attach securely and open easily.

## Options
| Closure | Character | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Zip** (top zip, zip-around) | Secure | Metal #5 common for bags; size tape and pull to the bag scale |
| **Turn-lock / twist-lock** | Signature, classic | Two parts: lock on front, plate on flap; flap thickness must match lock depth |
| **Push-lock / press-lock** | Easy one-hand | Spring wear over time |
| **Magnetic snap** | Hidden, quick | Low security; backing washers needed; can affect cards and phones near magnets |
| **Frame (kiss-lock, doctor bag)** | Structured opening | Bag pattern designed around the frame |
| **Drawstring / cinch** | Bucket bags | Use eyelets or channels |
| **Flap with buckle** | Satchels | Often decorative with a hidden magnet |

## Attachment
- Prongs through leather with washers, screws, or rivets; reinforce leather behind with a
  backing (bonded leather, board).
- For turn-locks, the flap must have the right thickness (lock's "leather thickness" spec,
  e.g. 3-4 mm); otherwise it rattles or will not close.

## Usability
- One-hand opening for crossbody and shoulder bags.
- Test with gloves and long nails.

## Gotchas
- Magnetic snaps through thin leather pull through; add reinforcement.
- Zips too short for the opening make the bag hard to use; zip end stops at least 1-2 cm from corners.
- Heavy locks on small soft bags pull the flap down.

## Verify
- [ ] Closure matches material thickness.
- [ ] Open/close cycle test passed (e.g. 5,000 cycles).
- [ ] Reinforcements in the BOM.
