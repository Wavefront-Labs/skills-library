# Frame shape to face shape

**Goal:** design eyewear ranges that flatter a range of faces, with shapes that carry
the brand's attitude.

## Classic shapes
| Shape | Features |
|---|---|
| Aviator | Teardrop metal, double bridge |
| Trapezoid acetate (Wayfarer-type) | Bold top line, tapering down |
| Round / panto | Circular or keyhole bridge; intellectual, vintage |
| Cat-eye | Upswept outer corners; retro feminine |
| Rectangle | Wider than tall; professional |
| Browline / clubmaster-type | Heavy top rim, thin lower rim |
| Square / oversized | Statement |
| Geometric (hexagonal, octagonal) | Fashion |

## Face shape guidance (contrast principle)
- **Round faces**: angular frames (rectangle, square) add definition.
- **Square faces**: round, oval or cat-eye soften the jaw.
- **Oval**: most shapes work; keep proportion.
- **Heart (wide forehead)**: bottom-heavy or rimless lower, light top lines.
- **Long faces**: deeper frames, decorative temples to shorten.
These are tendencies; individual features (brow, nose bridge) matter more in fittings.

## Proportion details
- Top rim should follow or sit just below the brow line.
- Frame width close to face width at the temples; too wide looks borrowed.
- Bridge height suits the nose; low bridges slide down.

## Range planning
Offer each key shape in 2 sizes and 3-5 colours (black, tortoiseshell, clear/crystal,
seasonal colour); tortoise and black usually lead sales.

## Gotchas
- Designing for an average face only; test on diverse faces including low nose bridges.
- Oversized fashion shapes can exceed lens blank sizes for prescriptions.

## Verify
- [ ] Each shape tested on several face shapes.
- [ ] Brow line and bridge fit checked.
- [ ] Sizes and colours planned per shape.
