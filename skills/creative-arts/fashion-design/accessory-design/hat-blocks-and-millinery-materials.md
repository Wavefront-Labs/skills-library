# Hat blocks and materials (felt, straw, sinamay)

**Goal:** design hats that can be made on available blocks with suitable materials, and
brief a milliner precisely. (Hands-on hat making sits with handcrafts millinery skills.)

## Blocks
- **Crown blocks** (wooden, by head size and shape) and **brim blocks** (flat, curved,
  down-turned); also one-piece blocks (cloche, pillbox).
- Custom blocks are carved by block makers (expensive, weeks of lead time); start by
  designing around existing blocks.
- Head size on block in cm (e.g. 57 cm).

## Materials
| Material | Character | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Wool felt hood | Warm, inexpensive | Blocks with steam |
| Fur felt (rabbit, beaver) | Fine, smooth, durable | Premium; velour and peachbloom finishes |
| Straw hoods (parasisal, panama/toquilla, buntal) | Summer | Panama woven in Ecuador; graded by weave fineness |
| Sinamay | Abaca fibre fabric, open weave | Layered and blocked for fascinators and brims |
| Buckram | Stiffened cotton | Foundation for structured shapes |
| Fabric-covered | Any fabric over buckram/wire | Matching outfits |

## Process (for briefing)
Steam/dampen material, stretch over the block, pin/tie with a string at the head line,
dry (hours to overnight), apply stiffener (felt or straw stiffener), remove, wire brims,
trim (headband/sweatband, ribbon band, lining).

## Design limits
- Felt hoods have a maximum size; very wide brims need capelines (hoods with large brims).
- Straw can't be blocked into very sharp angles without cracking.

## Gotchas
- Designing a shape no existing block makes: budget for a custom block.
- Stiffener amount changes hand; agree a sample.
