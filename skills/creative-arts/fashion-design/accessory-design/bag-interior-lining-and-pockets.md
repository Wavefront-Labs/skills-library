# Bag interior, lining and pockets

**Goal:** an interior that is useful, durable and on brand, without adding needless cost or weight.

## Lining materials
- **Textile:** cotton twill, canvas, polyester/nylon twill or satin, microsuede (Alcantara-type).
  Dark linings hide dirt; light linings help find items.
- **Leather or split-leather lining:** premium, heavy, costly.
- **Unlined / raw-edge** (painted edges): minimalist; requires clean back of leather.
- Reinforcements: bonded fibre board, foam, interfacing; base board for structure.

## Pockets and features
- **Slip pocket** (open, phone sized: e.g. 16-18 x 10 cm).
- **Zip pocket** (security, keys, cash), usually on the back wall.
- **Card slots** (8-9.5 cm wide for 8.56 x 5.4 cm cards).
- **Key clip / leash**, **pen loop**, **padded laptop sleeve**, **bottle pocket**.
- **Compartments / dividers**: zip divider in the middle (structured bags).
- Place pockets where the hand reaches naturally, not hidden under the top frame.

## Brand touches
- Embossed internal logo patch, printed lining, serial number tag.

## Cost and weight
- Every pocket adds parts and labour; count them on the BOM.
- Leather pockets on leather linings add weight; mix with textile.

## Gotchas
- Pockets on a slouchy wall sag unless stiffened.
- Zips without a pull tab are hard to open inside a bag.
- Lining slightly smaller than shell avoids bagging; size it 2-5 mm smaller.

## Verify
- [ ] Pocket sizes fit target items.
- [ ] Lining fits without slack.
- [ ] Interior parts listed on the BOM.
