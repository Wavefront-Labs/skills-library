# Leather tannages and finishes

**Goal:** select leather whose tannage and finish suit the product, budget and brand claims.

## Tannages
| Tannage | Character | Uses |
|---|---|---|
| **Vegetable (veg-tan)** | Firm, takes tooling and embossing, patinates, burnishable edges | Belts, structured bags, SLGs |
| **Chrome** | Soft, supple, colourfast, water resistant, faster and cheaper | Most bags, shoes, garments |
| **Chrome-free / wet-white** (aldehyde, synthetic) | Soft like chrome, no chromium | Automotive, kids, eco lines |
| **Combination (chrome + veg re-tan)** | Balance of both | Many bag leathers |

## Finishes
- **Full aniline:** dyed through, no pigment; shows natural marks; beautiful, marks easily.
- **Semi-aniline:** light pigment coat; more even, still natural look.
- **Pigmented / coated:** opaque finish; durable, uniform.
- **Corrected grain:** surface sanded and embossed; hides defects; cheaper.
- **Nappa:** soft full-grain (usually chrome), garment and bag.
- **Pull-up / oil-tanned:** colour lightens when flexed; casual and vintage.
- **Saffiano:** cross-hatch embossed and waxed; scratch resistant.
- Suede and nubuck are buffed (flesh side or grain side respectively).

## Sourcing
- Leather Working Group (LWG) audited tanneries (gold/silver/bronze) for environmental
  performance; ask for certificates.
- Check traceability to slaughterhouse for deforestation risk (cattle).

## Gotchas
- Aniline leathers vary between hides; approve a range.
- Veg-tan darkens with light; samples age differently.
- Chrome VI can form under poor conditions; test (ISO 17075).

## Verify
- [ ] Tannage and finish specified on the BOM.
- [ ] Hide-range samples approved.
- [ ] LWG certificate and chrome VI test on file.
