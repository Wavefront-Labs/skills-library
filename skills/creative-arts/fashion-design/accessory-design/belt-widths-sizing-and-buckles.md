# Belt widths, sizing and buckles

**Goal:** belts that fit, sit in belt loops and look in proportion.

## Widths (indicative)
| Use | Width |
|---|---|
| Dress (suit trousers) | 25-32 mm |
| Casual/jeans | 35-40 mm |
| Work/utility | 38-45 mm |
| Women's fashion | 10 mm (skinny) to 100 mm+ (waist cinchers) |
Check trouser belt loop height (often 40-45 mm) for casual belts.

## Sizing
- Belt size is usually measured from the **buckle fold (where the leather meets the
  buckle) to the middle hole**.
- Common rule: belt size = trouser waist size + about 5 cm (2 in) (e.g. trouser 32 in -> belt
  34 in), but customers vary; give a measuring guide.
- 5 holes, spaced 2.5 cm (1 in) apart is standard; middle hole = nominal size.
- Tip length beyond the last hole ~ 10-15 cm so it reaches the first loop.

## Buckles
Frame (prong) buckle, plate buckle, box frame/slider (no holes; ratchet/friction), D-ring
double-ring (fabric belts), reversible buckles with swivel. Specify inner width = strap
width (e.g. 35 mm buckle for 35 mm strap), finish and material (brass, zinc alloy, steel);
nickel compliance for skin contact.

## Strap construction
Single-ply bridle leather (thick, ~3.5-4.5 mm), layered with lining and edge stitching,
or webbing/fabric. Screw-back or snap attachment for interchangeable buckles.

## Gotchas
- Buckle too narrow for the strap: forcing ruins the edge.
- Belt size labels confused with waist sizes; state the measuring method.
- Soft leathers stretch at holes; reinforce or choose firm leather.

## Verify
- [ ] Width matched to use and loops.
- [ ] Sizing method and hole spacing specified.
- [ ] Buckle inner width matches strap; nickel compliance confirmed.
