# Fabric MOQs, lead times and minimums

**Goal:** get the fabrics you designed at quantities and dates a collection can actually use.

## Typical structure
- **MOQ** per article (e.g. 1000-3000 m for bespoke dyed wovens), plus **colour minimum** per
  colour (e.g. 300-800 m). Prints often have per-design and per-colourway minimums.
- **Stock service**: mill holds greige or finished stock; small cuts (sometimes from 1 m to
  50 m) at a higher price.
- **Lead times**: sampling 2-6 weeks; bulk 6-12 weeks from greige; 12-16+ weeks if yarn must be
  spun or dyed; add transit.

## Negotiating
1. Ask early, at the fair or first contact, for MOQ, colour min, surcharge for under-minimum,
   and stock availability.
2. Offer a **surcharge** (often 10-30%) for quantities below MOQ.
3. **Consolidate**: use one fabric across more styles; reduce colourways; share fabric between
   menswear and womenswear lines.
4. Choose dye-from-greige articles so the mill can dye small lots from existing greige.
5. Book capacity: give a forecast and confirm a booking date; release colours later.
6. Use agents/converters who buy at MOQ and sell smaller quantities.

## Plan in the critical path
- Fabric order deadline is usually the earliest bulk date in the critical path; tie it to range sign-off.

## Gotchas
- Lab dip and strike-off approvals delayed = bulk delayed day for day.
- Tolerance: mills ship +/-3-10% of ordered quantity; state limits in the PO.
- Under-minimum orders lose priority at busy times.

## Verify
- [ ] MOQ, colour min, price and surcharge recorded per article.
- [ ] Order dates in critical path.
- [ ] Quantity tolerance in purchase order.
