# Tossed and all-over repeats without lines

**Goal:** a repeat that looks random and continuous across the fabric, with no visible
grid, stripes of motifs, or empty holes.

## Tossed layout
- Motifs rotated in several directions (non-directional, so the fabric can be cut any way).
- Mix motif sizes (large, medium, small fillers) for natural rhythm.
- Distribute evenly: aim for similar ground areas between motifs.

## Building in Photoshop (Offset method)
1. Canvas at repeat size (e.g. 64 x 64 cm at print resolution; for digital any size; for
   rotary screen match the screen circumference, commonly 64 cm or 91.4 cm).
2. Place main motifs within the canvas, away from edges.
3. **Filter > Other > Offset** by half the width and height (wrap around): edges move to
   the centre, revealing the joins.
4. Fill the centre gap with more motifs; avoid touching the canvas edges.
5. Offset again to check; repeat until no holes or lines appear.

## Illustrator
Object > Pattern > Make; tile type Grid/Brick; place motifs crossing tile edges (the
pattern editor shows copies live). Check "Show Swatch Bounds".

## Check
- Tile 3x3 or larger and view at 25%: look for **tracking** (diagonal or vertical lines of
  motifs) and holes.
- Squint or blur the image to see density imbalances.

## Gotchas
- Too-small repeats reveal the grid on large garments.
- A distinctive motif repeated close together exposes the repeat; separate them.
- Motifs crossing the tile edge must be split exactly; misalignment shows as a cut line.

## Verify
- [ ] 3x3 tile view shows no lines or holes.
- [ ] Repeat size matches the printing method.
- [ ] Distinctive motifs well separated.
