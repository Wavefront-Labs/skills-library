# Fabric hand, drape and weight evaluation

**Goal:** judge and communicate how a fabric feels and falls, objectively enough to compare
mills and approve bulk.

## Weight
- **gsm** (grams per square metre); US uses **oz/yd²** (1 oz/yd² = 33.9 gsm). Denim is usually
  quoted in oz/yd² (12-14 oz typical).
- Linear weight (g per running metre) depends on width; compare gsm.
- Rough bands: voile 50-80 gsm, shirting 100-130, dress weight 120-200, suiting 220-320, coat 400+.

## Hand vocabulary (use consistently)
- Soft/firm, smooth/rough, dry/slick, warm/cool, crisp/limp, springy (resilient)/dead, full/lean.
- Record against reference swatches in the studio library.

## Drape
- **Drape coefficient** (ISO 9073-9 / BS 5058): circular sample over a disc; shadow area ratio.
  Low % = fluid (chiffon, crepe ~20-40%), high = stiff (canvas 70-90%).
- **Cantilever bending** (ASTM D1388): bending length; quick stiffness check.
- KES-F / FAST systems measure hand objectively (lab use).

## Practical checks
- Drape test: hold a 1 m length by one corner; watch fold count and size.
- Crush test: squeeze and release; observe crease recovery.
- Stretch and recovery by hand, then measured.

## Gotchas
- Finishes change hand; approve bulk after finishing, not greige.
- Same gsm, different yarn and weave = very different drape.

## Verify
- [ ] gsm measured with a cutter and scale.
- [ ] Hand described against references.
- [ ] Drape suited to silhouette.
