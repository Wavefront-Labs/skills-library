# Functional finishes: DWR, brushing, mercerising

**Goal:** choose finishes that deliver the claimed performance, survive washing and meet
chemical restrictions.

## Finishes
| Finish | What it does | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **DWR** (durable water repellent) | Water beads off | Use PFC-free (C0) chemistry; C8/C6 fluorocarbons restricted (EU PFAS rules, many brand RSLs) |
| **Mercerising** | Caustic soda treatment of cotton under tension | Lustre, strength, better dye uptake |
| **Brushing / raising** | Wire rollers lift fibres | Flannel, fleece; warmth, softness |
| **Peaching / sueding** | Emery sanding | Peach-skin surface on cotton, poly microfibre |
| **Sanforising** | Compressive shrinkage | Reduces residual shrinkage of cotton wovens to ~1% |
| **Easy-care / non-iron** | Resin cross-linking | Wrinkle resistance; check formaldehyde limits |
| **Anti-microbial / odour** | Silver, zinc or other agents | Claims are regulated (EU BPR, US EPA) |
| **Calendering** | Heat and pressure rollers | Gloss or chintz effect, often not durable |

## Testing
- Water repellency: spray test AATCC 22 / ISO 4920 (ratings 0-100), after 0, 5, 10 washes.
- Shrinkage: AATCC 135 or ISO 6330 + ISO 5077.
- Formaldehyde: ISO 14184-1 (limits per market, e.g. Japan babywear very low).

## Gotchas
- PFC-free DWR needs re-activation by tumble or iron; tell customers on care labels.
- Brushed fabrics pill more; check pilling.
- Resin finishes weaken cotton (tear strength).

## Verify
- [ ] Finish chemistry matches brand RSL.
- [ ] Performance tested after washes.
- [ ] Claims wording reviewed against local rules.
