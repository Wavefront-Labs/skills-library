# Garment wash and finish recipes

**Goal:** brief the laundry so washed garments hit the target look, hand and size.

## Common washes
| Wash | Process | Look |
|---|---|---|
| **Rinse** | water, softener | Removes starch, minimal fade |
| **Enzyme (bio-polish)** | cellulase enzymes | Soft hand, removes fuzz, mild fade; replaces stones |
| **Stone wash** | pumice in drum | Uneven fade, worn seams; damages machines and fabric |
| **Acid / snow wash** | pumice soaked in oxidiser (permanganate/chlorine) | High contrast mottling |
| **Bleach** | chlorine or peroxide | Overall lightening |
| **Pigment dye + wash** | garment dyed with pigment and binder, then washed down | Faded, chalky vintage look on seams |
| **Ozone / laser** | gas fade, laser abrasion (Jeanologia etc.) | Whiskers, fades with far less water and chemicals |

## Brief the laundry
- Send a **wash standard**: physical reference garment or approved wash panel, with front/back photos.
- Specify recipe name if known, target colour, abrasion areas (whiskers, honeycombs), hand.
- Request wash tolerances: shade bands (light/target/dark).

## Sizing
- Washed garments shrink; pattern must include wash shrinkage (often 3-8% length on denim,
  more on rigid). Get shrinkage from a test wash of the actual fabric and apply to the pattern.

## Sustainability
- Potassium permanganate sprays and sandblasting are hazardous; sandblasting is banned by many brands.
- Use laser and ozone where possible; ask for water-use figures (e.g. EIM score).

## Gotchas
- Each fabric lot washes differently; re-approve on bulk fabric.
- Metal trims discolour in acid and bleach washes; specify wash-resistant hardware.
- Threads and labels need to survive the wash.

## Verify
- [ ] Wash standard approved and signed.
- [ ] Shrinkage built into pattern.
- [ ] Trims tested through the wash.
