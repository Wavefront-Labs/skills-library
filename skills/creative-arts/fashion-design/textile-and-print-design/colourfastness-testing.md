# Colourfastness to wash, rub and light

**Goal:** make sure colours don't fade, bleed or rub off in use, by specifying the right
tests and minimum grades for each product.

## Main tests
| Property | AATCC | ISO | What it simulates |
|---|---|---|---|
| Washing | 61 (accelerated, e.g. 2A = 5 home washes) | 105-C06 | Fading and staining in laundering |
| Crocking (rubbing) | 8 (crockmeter) | 105-X12 | Colour rubbing onto other fabrics/skin, dry and wet |
| Light | 16.3 (xenon arc) | 105-B02 | Fading in sunlight |
| Perspiration | 15 | 105-E04 | Acidic/alkaline sweat |
| Water / sea / chlorine | 107 / 106 / 162 | 105-E01 / E02 / E03 | Swimwear, etc. |

## Grading
- **Grey scale for colour change** (1-5): 5 = no change.
- **Grey scale for staining** (1-5): rated on a multifibre adjacent fabric (acetate, cotton,
  nylon, polyester, acrylic, wool) to see which fibres pick up dye.
- Light fastness on ISO uses blue wool references (1-8).

## Typical minimums (set per brand and product)
- Washing colour change 4, staining 3-4.
- Dry crocking 4, wet crocking 3 (dark denim often 2-3 wet).
- Light: 4 or more for outdoor/swim; less for indoor items.

## Practical
- Test **dark and bright colours** first (navy, black, red, indigo) - highest risk.
- Test prints and contrast combinations (red trim on white body) for bleeding.
- Approve after tests on bulk fabric, not just samples.

## Gotchas
- Pigment prints often fail wet crocking.
- Indigo denim fades by design; disclose "wash separately" and set realistic grades.

## Verify
- [ ] Tests chosen per product use.
- [ ] Results graded and meeting minimums.
- [ ] Bleed-risk combinations tested as assemblies.
