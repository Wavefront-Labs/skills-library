# LMA Shape qualities

**Goal:** describe how the body's form changes in relation to itself and the environment -
the Shape category of Laban Movement Analysis.

## Modes of shape change
| Mode | Description | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Shape flow** | body changing in relation to itself | breathing, self-hugging, stretching on waking |
| **Directional - spoke-like** | straight lines out from the body to a point | pointing, punching |
| **Directional - arc-like** | arcs in space | a sweeping arm, a sideways swing |
| **Carving (shaping)** | moulding the body around space or objects in 3D | scooping, embracing a sphere |

## Shape qualities (direction of change)
| Quality | Plane affinity | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Rising / sinking | vertical | lifting the chest / collapsing |
| Spreading / enclosing | horizontal | opening arms wide / folding in |
| Advancing / retreating | sagittal | moving forward towards / pulling back |

## Using it
- **Observation:** note dominant shape qualities - a performer who mostly encloses reads as
  withdrawn.
- **Characterisation:** in narrative work, give each character a preferred shape quality.
- **Affinities:** Effort and Shape often pair (e.g. light with rising, strong with sinking),
  and crossing affinities creates interesting tension.

- [ ] Observer identifies the mode and quality in a short clip.
- [ ] Dancer shifts between carving and spoke-like on request.
