# Writing a Labanotation score

**Goal:** record choreography in Labanotation accurately enough for someone else to
reconstruct it. Reading is covered in `creative-arts.dance.dance-notation.reading-basic-labanotation`.

## Preparation
1. Watch the work repeatedly; obtain video, music score and the choreographer's access.
2. Map the music: measures and counts become the vertical time scale (bar lines across the staff).
3. Decide the unit of time per counted beat (symbol length).

## Writing order (for each measure)
1. **Starting position** below the double line at the bottom.
2. **Supports (centre columns):** direction and level symbols showing steps and weight.
3. **Leg gestures** (next columns out), then **torso, arms, head** in their columns.
4. **Path signs** for travel; turn signs.
5. **Floor plans** beside the staff for spatial patterns and group positions.
6. **Timing details:** holds, accents, relationship signs (contact, partner).
7. **Glossary/key** for any special symbols, and prose notes for style.

## Tools
- **LabanWriter** (free Mac software from Ohio State University) for clean scores.
- Pencil drafts in rehearsal; transfer later.

## Checking
- A different notator or dancer reconstructs sections from the score alone; compare with video.
- Revise ambiguous passages.

## Verify
- [ ] Measures align with the music map.
- [ ] Sections reconstructed by a second person match the original.
- [ ] Score includes floor plans, glossary and production notes.
