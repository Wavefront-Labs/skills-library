# Filming dance for the record

**Goal:** a record video is for restaging and archives, not for marketing. It must show the
whole stage and every dancer all the time, with sound, from the audience view, labelled so
future stagers know what they are looking at.

> **Status: draft.** Based on dance archive and documentation guidance (NYPL Jerome
> Robbins Dance Division, Dance Heritage Coalition) and Library of Congress format notes.

## 1. Camera placement
- **Main camera:** centre of the house, raised (front of circle or a high tripod in the
  stalls) so floor patterns and depth read; wide enough to include the full stage, wings
  edges included. **No zoom, no pans** - a fixed frame.
- **Second camera** (optional): a closer shot or a side angle for detail of hands, feet,
  partnering.
- For in-the-round or site-specific work, an overhead or several fixed angles.

## 2. Light and sound
- Ask lighting for a full-stage record state or record a dress with a work-light run
  too; dark stage looks have little reference value.
- Record clean audio from the desk plus a room mic; the music is how a stager finds counts.

## 3. Record what matters
- A full run, no stops, ideally the premiere cast or an approved revival.
- Separate clips of tricky moments from closer angles, and a spoken intro (title, date,
  cast, version, venue).
- Rehearsal videos of the choreographer giving corrections are valuable.

## 4. Files
- Keep the camera originals. Make a preservation master in a widely supported, preferably
  lossless or high-quality format (for example FFV1 in Matroska, or high-bitrate ProRes)
  and an access copy (H.264 MP4).
- Name files consistently: work_date_venue_cast_camera.
- Store at least two copies in different places; check them yearly.

## 5. Permissions
Union and contract rules usually restrict filming performances; record-only archival
filming must be agreed with the cast, musicians and rights holders (music especially).

## Gotchas
- Close-up "artistic" camerawork with cuts: useless for restaging.
- Recording a dark lighting state only.
- No log of cast and version: a video nobody can identify.

## Verify
- [ ] Fixed wide front shot of the whole stage for the whole work.
- [ ] Clean audio.
- [ ] Cast, date, version and venue recorded with the file.
- [ ] Master and access copies stored in two places.
