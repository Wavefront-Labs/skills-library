# Laban Effort factors

**Goal:** describe and change the dynamic quality of movement precisely using the Effort
category of Laban Movement Analysis.

## The four factors
| Factor | Indulging pole | Fighting (condensing) pole | Inner attitude |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Weight** | light | strong | intention |
| **Time** | sustained | sudden | decision |
| **Space** | indirect (flexible) | direct | attention |
| **Flow** | free | bound | progression/feeling |

## The eight basic effort actions (Weight, Time, Space; flow aside)
| Action | Weight | Time | Space |
|---|---|---|---|
| Punch (thrust) | strong | sudden | direct |
| Slash | strong | sudden | indirect |
| Press | strong | sustained | direct |
| Wring | strong | sustained | indirect |
| Dab | light | sudden | direct |
| Flick | light | sudden | indirect |
| Glide | light | sustained | direct |
| Float | light | sustained | indirect |

## Combinations
- **States:** two factors together (e.g. Time + Weight = rhythm/"near" state).
- **Drives:** three factors (action drive = the eight actions above; passion, vision and spell
  drives replace one factor with Flow).

## Using it
- **Observation:** note which factors are active and which pole; most movement uses one or two.
- **Coaching:** "make that a press, not a punch" is faster than long explanations.
- **Choreography:** vary efforts to build dynamic contrast (see phrase dynamics).

- [ ] Observer can name the effort action in a short movement clip.
