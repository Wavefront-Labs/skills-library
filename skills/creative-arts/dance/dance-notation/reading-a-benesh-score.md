# Reading a Benesh score

**Goal:** use a Benesh score to learn or stage choreography accurately, as company
choreologists and répétiteurs do.

## Orientation
1. Read the score's front matter: cast list, key/glossary of special signs, production notes,
   stage plan, version and date.
2. Find the music score alignment: Benesh staves usually run beneath the music.
3. Identify the role's stave (or group staves).

## Reading process
1. Read frame by frame: body position (stave), then location/direction (below), then timing
   (above).
2. Mark the movement lightly as you read; then full out.
3. For partnering, read both partners' staves together.
4. Note ambiguous moments and cross-check with video and any rehearsal notes.

## Staging use
- Learn the whole section before teaching it; teach in counts and music cues.
- Respect the version notated - different stagings may differ; the score documents one.

## Gotchas
- Scores are rights-controlled; access usually requires the rights holder's permission.
- Reading fluency comes from practice; begin with simple solos.

## Verify
- [ ] Section learned from score matches reference video.
- [ ] Ambiguities logged and resolved with a choreologist or source.
- [ ] Rights permission documented before staging.
