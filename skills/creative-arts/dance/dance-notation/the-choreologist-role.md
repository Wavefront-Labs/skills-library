# The choreologist role

**Goal:** understand and perform the choreologist's job in a ballet or dance company - the
person who records and safeguards the repertoire, usually in Benesh notation.

## Responsibilities
1. **Notate new works** during creation: sit in rehearsal, write the score live (pencil drafts),
   clean it up later; record the choreographer's revisions.
2. **Maintain scores** through revivals: note cast-specific changes, version history.
3. **Teach and rehearse** roles from the score - often to new casts and understudies.
4. **Restage abroad:** stage the company's repertoire for other companies under licence.
5. **Archive:** scores with video, cast lists, production notes, stage plans.

## In the rehearsal room
- Position yourself to see the whole stage and the choreographer.
- Note counts, music cues, spacing and corrections, not only steps.
- Clarify ambiguous moments with the choreographer at breaks - not mid-creation.

## Skills
- Fluency in notation (Benesh or Labanotation), music reading, ballet vocabulary.
- Diplomacy: the score can settle disputes ("what was originally set").

## Verify
- [ ] Score for a new work updated after each rehearsal.
- [ ] Version history and cast notes kept.
- [ ] Teaching a role from the score matches the choreographer's intent.
