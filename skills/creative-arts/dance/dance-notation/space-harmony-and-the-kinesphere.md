# Space harmony and the kinesphere

**Goal:** understand and train how the body moves in its surrounding space, using Laban's
Space category (choreutics).

## Kinesphere
- The sphere of space around the body reachable without stepping. Reach space: **near**
  (close to the body), **mid**, **far** (full extension).
- Moving the kinesphere through space = travelling; general space is everything beyond.

## Spatial structure
- **Dimensions (one-dimensional):** up-down, side-side, forward-back - the **octahedron**.
- **Planes (two-dimensional):** vertical (door), horizontal (table), sagittal (wheel) - the
  **icosahedron** is built from the corners of the three planes.
- **Diagonals (three-dimensional):** e.g. high-right-forward to low-left-back - the **cube**.

## Scales (movement sequences tracing the forms)
- **Dimensional scale:** up, down, left, right, back, forward (for the right side) - six
  points of the octahedron, establishing stability.
- **Diagonal scale:** the four cube diagonals - mobile, off-balance.
- **A and B scales, axis scales:** 12-point sequences on the icosahedron that weave through
  planes; train three-dimensional spatial clarity.

## Using it
- Train precise spatial pathways and reach.
- Choreography: plan a phrase through specific kinesphere points.
- Observation: note where in the kinesphere a mover lives (near and low vs far and high).

## Verify
- [ ] Dimensional scale performed with clear points.
- [ ] Diagonal scale shows mobility across the cube.
- [ ] Observation notes record reach space and spatial pulls.
