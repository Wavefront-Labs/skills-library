# Bartenieff Fundamentals

**Goal:** use the Body category of LMA to understand and retrain how the body connects -
foundational patterns that support efficient, expressive movement.

## Patterns of total body connectivity (developmental sequence)
| Pattern | What connects | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Breath** | the whole body through breathing | cellular breath, expanding and condensing |
| **Core-distal** | centre to the six limbs (head, tail, arms, legs) | starfish: gathering in and reaching out |
| **Head-tail** | spine from head to tailbone | spinal undulation, cat curls |
| **Upper-lower** | upper body with lower body (homologous) | frog-like push, both arms or both legs together |
| **Body-half** | right side vs left side (homolateral) | lizard-like crawl, side bending |
| **Cross-lateral** | diagonal connections | crawling, walking with opposition, spiral |

## The Basic Six (Bartenieff exercises, lying on the back)
1. Thigh lift.
2. Forward pelvic shift.
3. Lateral pelvic shift.
4. Body half (arm and leg on one side folding/extending).
5. Diagonal knee drop.
6. Arm circles (with spiral of the torso).
Each is done with breath support and clear initiation; teach from a certified source for detail.

## Using it
- Diagnose movement problems (e.g. a dancer who can't connect arms to the back lacks
  core-distal/upper-lower support).
- Warm-up sequence before technique class.
- Rehabilitation support with clinicians.

## Verify
- [ ] Observer identifies which pattern a movement relies on.
- [ ] Basic Six performed with breath and correct initiation.
- [ ] Patterns integrated into warm-up.
