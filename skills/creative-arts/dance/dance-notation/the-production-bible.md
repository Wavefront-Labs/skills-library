# The production bible

**Goal:** a production bible (show bible) holds everything needed to remount the work
elsewhere or years later without the original team in the room.

> **Status: draft.** Standard stage-management and repertory practice.

## 1. Contents
1. **Overview:** title, choreographer, premiere date and venue, duration, credits.
2. **Cast:** roles, original cast, current cast, covers.
3. **Choreography:** notation score if any, reference video list with timecodes, the
   counts sheet by section, style notes.
4. **Spacing:** charts for every formation, entrance and exit plot, track sheets.
5. **Music:** score, recordings with timings, tempos, musician list, rights.
6. **Lighting:** plot, cue sheet with call points (count and timecode), focus notes,
   colour list.
7. **Sound:** cue list, playback files and levels.
8. **Set and props:** ground plans, construction drawings, prop list and presets.
9. **Costume:** designs, fabric swatches, dressing guide, quick-change plot.
10. **Technical requirements:** minimum stage size, floor, wings, crossover.
11. **Rights and contacts:** licences, approvals, choreographer and designers' contacts.

## 2. Keep it current
Update after each change with the date; the dance captain and stage manager own their
parts. Lock a "final" version after opening and add revival notes later as amendments.

## 3. Format
A physical binder with tabs for the rehearsal room plus a digital folder with the same
structure (and video). Back up the digital copy off site.

## Gotchas
- Bible frozen at the premiere while the show changes on tour.
- Video referenced but not included.
- No rights section: a revival discovers the music is not licensed.

## Verify
- [ ] All sections present.
- [ ] Changes dated; current version clear.
- [ ] Digital copy backed up off site.
