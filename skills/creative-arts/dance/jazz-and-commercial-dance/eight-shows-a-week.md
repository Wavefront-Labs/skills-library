# Eight shows a week

**Goal:** perform at the same level every show across a long run without injury or vocal
damage. West End and Broadway schedules commonly run eight performances over six days,
with two-show days.

> **Status: draft.** General guidance; work with company physios and voice teachers.

## Daily routine
- **Warm-up** before every show (company warm-up or own 20-30 min).
- **Cool-down** and stretch afterward; hydrate.
- Sleep 8-9 hours; late shows mean shifted sleep - protect it.
- Eat enough: energy availability matters (see nutrition skill).

## Weekly
- Day off is recovery, not catch-up dancing.
- Cross-training at low intensity; strength twice a week to support loading.
- Vocal rest after two-show days.

## Reporting
- Tell the company manager/physio early about pain; most companies have physio clinics.
- Swing and understudy systems exist so you don't dance injured.

## Verify
- [ ] Pre-show warm-up and post-show cool-down every performance.
- [ ] Rest day protected.
- [ ] Any pain reported early.
