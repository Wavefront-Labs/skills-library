# Singing while dancing

**Goal:** deliver vocals that sound steady while performing full choreography - the core
skill of a musical theatre ensemble.

> **Status: draft.** From musical theatre training and practice; consult a voice teacher
> for individual technique.

## Breath planning
- Mark breath points in the score; match them to moments in the choreography (a hold, a
  turn finish, before a lift).
- Avoid heavy exertion (jumps, lifts) on the long notes; ask the choreographer to shift a
  move if it fights a phrase.
- Use low, expansive breath; shoulders stay down even in big arm work.

## Stamina training
- Rehearse singing full out with the choreography long before tech.
- Conditioning: sing along while doing cardio (running, stairs) to build aerobic capacity.
- Drill hard numbers twice back-to-back in rehearsal.

## Technique in movement
- Keep the larynx and jaw free; tension from arms and core should not reach the neck.
- Face out where possible on key lines; sound projects where the face points.
- Mic placement (forehead or cheek mic) is affected by sweat and hair; check with sound.

## Pre-records and splits
Some productions pre-record or double vocals; follow the music director - singing live
over a pre-record must stay in tune and time with it.

## Verify
- [ ] Breaths marked and matched to choreography.
- [ ] Number sung full out with choreography at least daily in rehearsal.
- [ ] No tension in jaw/neck when dancing.
