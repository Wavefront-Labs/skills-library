# Audition etiquette and presentation

**Goal:** make the panel's job easy and show your best dancing; how you behave in the room
is part of what they are casting.

> **Status: draft.** From common UK/US practice; follow any instructions in the breakdown,
> which override everything here.

## Before
- Read the breakdown: styles, shoes required (heels, jazz, tap, character), what to bring
  (CV, headshot, sheet music for singers).
- Arrive early - open calls fill and close; registration takes time.
- Pin your number where the panel can see it (usually front, sometimes back as well).

## Look
- Fitted clothing in which lines are visible; a colour that stands out but is not costume.
- Hair secured so it does not need fixing between combinations.
- Same look as headshot; for recalls, wear the same outfit so you are remembered.

## In the room
- Warm up in the corridor, not in the panel's space.
- Stand where you can see and learn; rotate lines when asked (front line moves to back).
- Learn fast (see `picking-up-choreography-fast-at-auditions`); ask questions only if it
  helps everyone, and briefly.
- Perform full out when it counts, with focus to the panel.
- Accept cuts graciously: thank the panel and leave quietly.

## Never
- Talk while the choreographer teaches, film without permission, or argue with a decision.
- Wear jewellery that can catch or fly off.

## Safety
Equity guidance: you should not be asked to perform dangerous tricks without warm-up or
proper flooring, or to undress beyond what was stated. You may decline.

## Verify
- [ ] Breakdown read; correct shoes and materials packed.
- [ ] Number visible; hair and clothing secured.
- [ ] Learning, performing and leaving done professionally.
