# Hitting marks on camera

**Goal:** land precisely on the floor positions set for the camera - so you are in frame and in
focus - while still dancing full out.

## Understanding marks
- Marks are tape (often T or V shapes, colour-coded per dancer) placed by the camera or
  assistant director team. They correspond to framing and focus distance.
- Missing a mark by even half a metre can put you out of focus on long lenses.

## Method
1. **Rehearse with marks** during blocking; learn how many steps/counts each travel takes.
2. **Use peripheral references:** a line in the set, a light, the camera's position - so you
   don't look down at the floor in the take.
3. **Count to marks:** "arrive on 5 of the third 8".
4. **Adjust choreography:** shorten or lengthen steps to hit marks; tell the choreographer if
   impossible.
5. **Feel the frame:** ask the director/DOP about the frame edges (where you'd be cut off).

## Across takes
- Repeat the exact same paths and arrival counts; editors need matching action.
- Note any changes on a personal sheet (which foot, arm, facing at each mark).

## Verify
- [ ] Marks hit on the correct count without looking down.
- [ ] Consistent across three takes.
- [ ] Choreography adjusted and approved for framing.
