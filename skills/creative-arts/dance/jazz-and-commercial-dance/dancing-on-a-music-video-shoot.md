# Dancing on a music video shoot

**Goal:** turn up ready, deliver the choreography under film-set conditions, stay safe,
and be booked again.

> **Status: draft.** Pay, buyouts and agreements differ by country and union; check the
> contract and union guidance.

## Before the shoot
- Rehearsals are usually 1-3 days; learn and record the choreography.
- Read the **call sheet**: call time, location, nearest hospital, wardrobe, catering.
- Confirm fee, hours, overtime and usage/buyout in writing (through your agent).

## On set
- Call time is when you start, not when you arrive.
- Coverage: the same section will be filmed many times - wide, mid, close, different
  angles, the artist alone and with dancers. Save energy between.
- Playback: listen for the cue ("playback... action"); start on the count given.
- Follow the first AD for set rules; do not wander off set.
- Hair, make-up and wardrobe touch-ups between takes - keep them consistent.

## Safety
- Location floors: concrete, sand, wet surfaces, slopes. Speak up if unsafe; ask for a
  dance floor or reworked choreography. Warm up again after long waits.
- Cold sets: layers between takes.
- Long days: water and food.

## After
- Check that you're credited where possible, invoice promptly, and keep your copy of the
  contract.

## Verify
- [ ] Fee, hours and usage agreed in writing.
- [ ] Choreography recorded; call sheet read.
- [ ] Floor and conditions checked for safety on arrival.
