# Tap shuffles and flaps

**Goal:** the basic sound-making actions of tap - two sounds per shuffle and flap - clean,
even and relaxed.

## Brush and spank
- **Brush (forward):** the ball of the foot strikes the floor as the leg swings forward.
- **Spank (backward):** the ball strikes the floor as the foot swings back.
- Action from a loose ankle and knee, not stiff from the hip.

## Shuffle
- Brush forward + spank back = **two sounds** ("shuf-fle", counted "and-a" or "1-and").
- Foot stays close to the floor; the knee relaxed and slightly lifted.

## Flap
- Brush forward + step (weight transfer onto the ball of the same foot) = two sounds, travelling.
- Forward flaps, backward flaps (spank-step), side flaps.

## Ball change
- Step onto the ball of one foot (usually behind) then the other - two sounds, weight transfer.

## Practice
1. Slow, count each sound out loud.
2. Metronome: start around 60-80 bpm; increase gradually.
3. Listen: sounds should be equal in volume and evenly spaced.
4. Combine: shuffle ball change; flap ball change; travelling flaps.

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| One sound instead of two | slow down, exaggerate both contacts |
| Stiff ankle | shake out, small relaxed movements |
| Heavy, loud stamping | tap with the ball, not the whole foot |
| Uneven rhythm | metronome and counting |

## Verify
- [ ] Two distinct sounds per shuffle and flap.
- [ ] Even rhythm at the target tempo.
- [ ] Shuffle ball change and flap ball change clean both feet.
