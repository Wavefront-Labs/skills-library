# Jazz turns in parallel

**Goal:** clean turns in jazz style, especially in parallel, with strong preparation and
controlled, stylish finishes.

## Parallel pirouette (outside/en dehors)
1. Prepare in a parallel 4th lunge: front knee bent, back leg straight or in plié, weight
   mostly forward; arms in a jazz 1st/2nd.
2. Push up to high relevé on the front leg; the back leg goes to **parallel retiré** (knee
   forward, toe at the front of the knee).
3. Turn away from the supporting leg; spot; close arms.
4. Finish in a lunge, a parallel 4th, or a stylised pose on the music accent.

## Inside (en dedans) parallel turn
- Turn towards the supporting leg; retiré in parallel; many jazz combos alternate inside and
  outside turns.

## Other turns
- **Pencil turn:** legs straight together, arms close to the body, fast single or multiple.
- **Jazz piqué/step turns:** travelling turns stepping onto a straight leg, parallel retiré.
- **Turns in second or attitude:** leg extended, turned out or parallel as styled.
- **Fan kick turn:** a fan kick that rotates the body.

## Teaching points
- Parallel retiré needs strong hip flexors and balance - practise relevé balances in parallel.
- Spot always; jazz often requires multiple turns on accent.
- Finish on the beat with attitude - the finish sells the turn.

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Retiré knee opens out (half turned) | keep knee forward, true parallel |
| Low relevé | full rise, supporting leg straight |
| Falling out of finishes | hold the finish for a count |

## Verify
- [ ] Clean double parallel pirouette both directions.
- [ ] Inside and outside turns in a combination.
- [ ] Finishes land on the music accent.
