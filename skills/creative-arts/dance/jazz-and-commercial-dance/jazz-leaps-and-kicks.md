# Jazz leaps and kicks

**Goal:** big, dynamic leaps and kicks typical of theatre and competition jazz.

## Leaps
| Leap | Shape |
|---|---|
| **Split leap (grand jeté)** | full split in the air, often with a forward-reaching torso |
| **Stag leap** | front leg bent (knee forward, foot near the back knee), back leg straight |
| **Switch leap** | front leg swings forward then switches to the back in the air before splitting |
| **Calypso** | turning leap with the back leg in attitude and the torso arched/tilted |
| **Hitch kick** | kick one leg, then the other kicks past it in the air (scissors) |

Preparation: usually a run, chassé or pas de bourrée into a deep plié on the take-off leg.

## Kicks
- **Fan kick:** a straight leg sweeps in an arc across the front of the body (inside or
  outside fan), passing high.
- **Battement / kick-line kicks:** straight, high kicks in parallel or turned out, often in
  unison in a line (Rockettes-style), with arms linked or on shoulders.
- **Développé kicks / layouts** in more advanced combos.

## Safety
- Warm hamstrings and hip flexors before kicking high.
- Land leaps through the foot into plié; jazz floors may be harder - watch volume.
- Kick lines: synchronise height and timing; avoid knee-locked supporting legs.

## Verify
- [ ] Stag, switch and split leaps distinct in shape.
- [ ] Fan kicks pass through a high arc with a straight leg.
- [ ] Soft, controlled landings.
