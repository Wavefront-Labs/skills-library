# Dancing in heels

**Goal:** perform commercial and tour choreography in heels with control and without ankle
or knee injury.

> **Status: draft.** Practice-based; individual anatomy matters - get a physio's input
> for recurring pain.

## Shoes
- Start with a **block heel 5-7 cm**; progress to taller and thinner over weeks.
- Secure ankle straps; snug fit; suede or roughed soles for turning on stage floors.
- Rehearse in the actual show shoes, not trainers.

## Technique
- **Weight over the balls of the feet**, heel barely touching; the heel is a lever, not a
  support.
- Knees soft; core engaged; ankles stacked over the shoe.
- Walks: step from the hip, cross slightly to a line, toe lands first or nearly flat
  depending on style.
- Turns: smaller and controlled; spot strongly; avoid big jumps unless choreographed with
  landing on the ball.
- Floor work: use the shoe as a design element, protect the heel tip from catching.

## Conditioning
- Calf raises (double then single leg), eccentric lowers.
- Ankle stability: balance on one foot, resistance band inversion/eversion.
- Intrinsic foot strength (doming, towel curls).
- Hip and glute strength for knee control.

## Injury prevention
- Build hours gradually; alternate with flat-shoe rehearsals.
- Stretch calves and hip flexors after.
- Report ankle pain or swelling early.

## Verify
- [ ] Rehearsed and performed in the show shoe.
- [ ] Weight carried over the balls; heels quiet.
- [ ] Conditioning programme in place; hours built gradually.
