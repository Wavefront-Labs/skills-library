# Fosse style

**Goal:** perform (or teach a class in) the movement signature of Bob Fosse - the
choreographer of *The Pajama Game* ("Steam Heat", 1954), *Sweet Charity*, *Pippin*,
*Chicago* and the film *Cabaret* - so it reads as Fosse and not as generic jazz.

> **Status: draft.** Fosse's actual choreography is licensed and staged by the Verdon Fosse
> Legacy; this skill covers the style vocabulary used in classes and auditions, not the
> copyrighted numbers.

## 1. The body
- **Turned-in or parallel knees**, feet often turned in too ("pigeon-toed") - the opposite
  of ballet's turnout. Fosse is widely reported to have built it around his own body.
- **Rounded, dropped shoulders** and a slightly concave chest; head often tilted, chin down,
  eyes up from under a hat brim.
- **Weight low and back**: sit into the hips, knees soft; walks are slinky and slow.
- **Hands:** the "jazz hand" spread wide, or the soft "Fosse hand" with a broken wrist;
  single-finger and thumb gestures; white gloves in many numbers make them read.

## 2. The movement
- **Isolation**: one body part moves while everything else is still - a hip pop, a shoulder
  roll, a finger snap. Stillness is the frame that makes the isolation visible.
- **Small, precise, slow.** Much of the vocabulary is tiny - a wrist flick, a hat tilt. The
  difficulty is control, not size.
- **Props** - bowler hats, canes, chairs - are part of the choreography: the hat tip and the
  hat roll down the arm are set moves, practised until they never fail.
- **Group texture**: tight clusters moving in canon or unison ("Rich Man's Frug", "Big
  Spender" leaning on the barre).

## 3. Rehearsing it
1. Start with posture: stand turned-in, shoulders rounded, knees soft - hold it for 8 counts
   and check in the mirror that nothing else is moving.
2. Drill isolations to a slow tempo (around 60-80 bpm): shoulders, ribs, hips, wrists one
   at a time, then layered.
3. Add the walk: slow, low, hips leading, with stops that land exactly on the count.
4. Add the face last: cool, knowing, under-played. Big smiles break the style.

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Dancing it "big" like a jazz combo | shrink the movement; hold the stillness longer |
| Turnout creeping back in | check knees and feet over every count; drill in parallel first |
| Isolations bleed into the whole body | slow it down; use a wall at the back to feel what moves |
| Hat falls off on the tilt | hat sized and secured; rehearse with the actual hat early |

## Verify
- [ ] Turned-in/parallel base held through the phrase.
- [ ] Each isolation happens with the rest of the body still.
- [ ] Props handled cleanly every run.
- [ ] For staging real Fosse numbers: licence and approved stager arranged.
