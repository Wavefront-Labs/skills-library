# Quick changes for dancers

**Goal:** change costume in seconds between numbers - sometimes under 30 - without
missing an entrance or damaging the costume.

> **Status: draft.** From theatre and tour wardrobe practice.

## Planning
- Wardrobe writes a **change plot**: who changes, what, where (booth location), time
  available, dresser assigned.
- Costume design for speed: Velcro, magnets, zips over hooks, pre-tied shoes or elastic
  laces, pieces sewn together as one garment.

## Choreograph the change
1. Preset: costume laid out in order of putting on, opened, shoes unlaced.
2. Decide who does what: dancer handles feet; dresser does back fastenings; dresser takes
   the old costume.
3. Rehearse the change with the dresser at the real time, several times, before tech.
4. Mic packs and IEMs transfer: plan where they go.

## Rules
- Silence and low light backstage; quick-change booths are lit with blue or dim lights.
- Return costumes to the dresser - never drop them on the floor where someone can slip.
- If a change fails, enter in what you have rather than missing the cue (unless told
  otherwise).

## Verify
- [ ] Change plot written and dresser assigned.
- [ ] Change rehearsed to time with the dresser.
- [ ] Preset order consistent every show.
