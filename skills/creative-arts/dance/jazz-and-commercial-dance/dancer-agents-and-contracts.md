# Dancer agents and contracts

**Goal:** get and keep representation that books you work, and never dance a job whose
terms you have not seen in writing.

> **Status: draft.** Commission rates, regulations and union agreements differ by country;
> this is general guidance, not legal advice. Check your union (Equity, SAG-AFTRA, AGMA,
> MEAA etc.) for current rates.

## Finding an agent
- Agents find you at showcases, graduation shows, or from a strong reel and CV.
- Check their client list and the work those clients book; meet before signing.
- **Red flags**: upfront fees for representation (illegal for UK agencies to charge
  performers for finding work, with limited exceptions), pressure to pay for photos with
  their photographer, no written agreement.

## The agency agreement
- Commission: commonly around 10-20% depending on country, sector and agent; confirm
  which earnings it applies to.
- Exclusivity and term; notice period to leave.
- How you are paid: agent client account, timeline for passing on fees.

## Every booking needs, in writing
- Dates, call times, rehearsal and performance days.
- Fee per day/week, overtime rate, holiday pay where applicable.
- **Usage/buyout**: where and how long the footage can be used (TV, online, territories,
  period). A low fee with worldwide perpetual usage is a bad deal.
- Travel, accommodation, per diems on tours.
- Cancellation terms; insurance.

## Verify
- [ ] Signed agency agreement with commission and notice period.
- [ ] Each booking confirmed in writing with fee, hours and usage.
- [ ] Fees checked against union minimums.
