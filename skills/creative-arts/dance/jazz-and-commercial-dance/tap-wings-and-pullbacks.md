# Tap wings and pullbacks

**Goal:** clean the two classic advanced tap jumps: **wings** (outward scrape and inward
brush while in the air) and **pullbacks** (spring back off the balls brushing backward).
Both appear in higher-grade tap syllabi and in audition combinations.

> **Status: draft.** Sound counts and names vary (e.g. 3-sound vs 4-sound wings) between
> teachers and syllabi; check the current syllabus or the choreographer's version.

## Pullbacks
- **Single pullback**: stand on the ball of one foot; spring up and back, brushing the ball
  backward against the floor as you leave it (one sound), land on the ball of the same foot
  (second sound).
- **Double pullback**: both feet together on the balls; same action with both feet, landing
  on both balls - two sounds per side clear, or flams if uneven.
- Keys: weight forward over the balls, knees soft, the brush is quick and backward (not
  downward); lift is small.

## Wings
- **Single wing**: from one foot, the supporting foot scrapes **outward** on the inside edge
  as you spring, brushes back **inward** in the air, and lands on the ball - commonly taught
  as 3 sounds (scrape, brush, land).
- **Double wing**: both feet at once, outward then inward, landing on both balls.
- Keys: the outward scrape is with the inner edge of the shoe; ankles relaxed; the jump is
  low, the work is in the feet.

## Progression
1. Pullbacks holding a barre with both hands, slow, counting sounds.
2. Pullbacks freestanding, then alternating feet, then in a phrase.
3. Wing scrape-outs on one foot holding the barre.
4. Full wings at the barre, then freestanding; doubles last.

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Only one sound | brush must be on the way up; practise slower with exaggerated brush |
| Landing on heels | weight forward; land on the ball and let heel lower after |
| Calves cramping | build with sets of 8 and rest; calf raises off class |
| Uneven double sounds | do singles on each foot first, then match |

## Verify
- [ ] All specified sounds audible, both feet, at tempo.
- [ ] Landings on the balls without heel drop noise (unless choreographed).
- [ ] Clean in a phrase, not just in isolation.
