# Musical theatre dance calls

**Goal:** be cast in a musical ensemble by surviving the dance call and then the singing
and acting rounds that follow.

> **Status: draft.** Structure varies by show and casting director; read the breakdown.

## Typical structure
1. **Ballet barre or centre** - quick check of technique (pirouettes, jumps).
2. **Style combination** from the show: jazz, Fosse, tap, contemporary, character.
3. **Cuts** after each combination.
4. **Singing**: 16-32 bars of a song in the show's style; sometimes material from the
   show sent in advance.
5. **Recalls**: scenes, more singing, the dance captain teaching actual choreography.

## Prepare
- Research the show's style and choreographer; drill that style.
- Bring all shoes: ballet, jazz, character heels, tap shoes.
- Sheet music marked clearly for the pianist (cuts, tempo, key), in a binder.
- Know your "type" for the show and dress to suggest it.

## In the call
- Treat the combination as a scene: who is this character?
- Keep vocal warm-up going between dance rounds; your voice must work after the
  dance call.
- If tap is called and you do not tap, say so honestly.

## Verify
- [ ] Style of the show researched and drilled.
- [ ] Shoes and marked music packed.
- [ ] Singing prepared in the show's style; voice warm after dancing.
