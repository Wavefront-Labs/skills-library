# Tap time steps

**Goal:** the standard time steps - a traditional phrase used to set tempo, warm up and test
tap dancers at auditions.

## Structure (one common version, starting on "8")
- **Single time step:** stomp (or step), shuffle, hop, step, flap... A widely taught version:
  "shuffle hop step, flap step" - counted "8 and 1, 2, and 3, and 4".
- **Double time step:** replace the single step with a flap ("shuffle hop flap step").
- **Triple time step:** replace with a shuffle step ("shuffle hop shuffle step").
- Each is done three times (alternating feet) followed by a **break** (a contrasting 2-bar
  phrase that ends the sequence).

Exact wording and counts vary by teacher, syllabus and style (Broadway vs rhythm tap);
learn the version in use.

## Practice
1. Say the sounds aloud in rhythm before dancing.
2. Metronome slow; ensure every sound is heard (count the sounds per bar).
3. Learn singles until clean, then doubles, triples.
4. Add the break; then perform the full sequence in 8 bars.

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Missing the hop sound | hop on the ball, lightly but audibly |
| Rushing | metronome; keep the swing feel consistent |
| Starting on the wrong count | time steps commonly start on "8" (pickup) |

## Verify
- [ ] Single, double and triple time steps with break, both sides.
- [ ] Every sound audible at the target tempo.
- [ ] Starts on the correct pickup.
