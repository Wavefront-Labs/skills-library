# Jazz isolations

**Goal:** move one body part independently of the others - the core skill of jazz technique,
traced to Jack Cole and African diasporic dance roots.

## Standard sequence (standing in parallel 2nd, knees soft)
| Part | Movements |
|---|---|
| **Head** | forward/back (nod), side to side (slide, ears over shoulders without tilting), tilts, half circles (never full circles dropping back) |
| **Shoulders** | up/down, forward/back, circles; one at a time then together, then alternating |
| **Ribcage** | slide side to side, forward/back, square, circle - hips stay still |
| **Hips** | side to side, forward/back (tilts), circles, figure-eight - ribcage stays still |

## Method
1. Slow, in 4 or 8 counts per movement, mirror in front.
2. Hold everything else still - check by watching one part that should not move (e.g. head
   during rib isolations).
3. Speed up to double time; then accents (sharp hit on 1, release on 2).
4. Layer: shoulder on the beat, hips on the off-beat; combine in short phrases.

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Ribs move with the hips | hands on the ribs to feel stillness |
| Head tilts instead of sliding | eyes stay level, ears over shoulders |
| Locked knees | soft knees allow hip movement |
| Full head rolls back | half circles only to protect the neck |

## Verify
- [ ] Each isolation performed with the rest of the body still (video check).
- [ ] Accented and double-time versions clean.
- [ ] Two-part layering at a steady tempo.
