# Dance captain duties

**Goal:** after opening, the choreographer leaves. The dance captain keeps the show as it
was set: choreography, spacing, style and safety, and teaches anyone going on. In US
Actors' Equity productions the dance captain is a contracted position with extra pay; the
same job exists in most professional companies.

> **Status: draft.** Duties summarised from common musical theatre and commercial
> practice; exact duties and pay are in each contract (Equity rulebooks).

## 1. Maintain the show
- Know every track in the numbers you look after: counts, spacing, style, intention.
- Watch from front of house regularly (several times a week on a long run) and note
  drift: timing, spacing, energy, added "improvements".
- Give notes by agreement with the resident director or company manager; keep them
  specific and private where individual.

## 2. Rehearsals
- **Clean-up rehearsals:** scheduled sessions for sections that have drifted.
- **Put-in rehearsals:** teach new cast members and swings, then a rehearsal with the
  company before they go on.
- **Understudy rehearsals:** rehearse covers regularly so they are ready.

## 3. Documentation
- Keep the **swing book** / track sheets and spacing charts current.
- Keep reference video (under the contract's recording rules).
- Record any change approved by the choreographer or director.

## 4. Show-day decisions
- With stage management and the resident director, decide re-spacing when someone is off
  and no swing is available ("cut tracks", close gaps).
- Brief the company before the show on any changes.
- Watch first entrances of anyone new.

## 5. Safety
Report unsafe floors, lifts done incorrectly, or injuries to stage management; never
change a lift or trick without the choreographer's approval.

## Gotchas
- Re-choreographing to suit yourself: the job is preservation.
- Notes in front of the whole cast that should be private.
- Swing book out of date the day a swing goes on.

## Verify
- [ ] Watched and noted shows on a regular schedule.
- [ ] Swing book and charts current.
- [ ] Put-ins rehearsed before going on.
- [ ] Changes approved and recorded.
