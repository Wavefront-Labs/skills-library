# Video review notes

**Goal:** video shows timing, spacing and shape problems nobody can see live. Turning it
into notes that dancers act on is a core rehearsal-director and dance-captain task.

> **Status: draft.** Common practice; check company and union rules on filming rehearsals
> and performances (many contracts limit recording and its use).

## 1. Shoot it usefully
- **Front of house, centre, wide**, from roughly mid-stalls height, the whole stage in
  frame. Fixed tripod, no zoom.
- A second angle (balcony, wings) only if needed for formations.
- Include audio so counts and music are clear; start before the first cue.
- Label files with date, run (studio run, dress, show #), and cast.

## 2. Log notes by timecode
Watch once through for overall issues, then again with pauses:
| Timecode | Section / count | Dancer | Note | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 04:12 | B-section bar 9 ct 5 | Ana | arm finishes late, hit full extension on 5 | 1 |
- Notes specific and actionable: what, where (count), and the target.
- Priority 1 = safety and spacing errors; 2 = timing and big shape; 3 = detail.

## 3. Sort and deliver
- Group by dancer for individual notes, by section for group notes.
- Give group notes in person or at the top of the next call; individual notes privately.
- Share the clip with the timecode where it helps; respect rules on distributing video.
- Limit to what can be fixed in the next rehearsal; carry the rest forward.

## 4. Follow up
Keep the log. At the next run, check the noted spots first. Repeated notes signal
something to re-teach or re-choreograph.

## Gotchas
- Filming from the side or the wings and noting spacing from it.
- 40 notes after a show; nothing changes. Prioritise.
- Posting video of a dancer's mistake to the group chat.

## Verify
- [ ] Front-of-house wide video labelled with date, run and cast.
- [ ] Notes logged with timecode, count, dancer and priority.
- [ ] Notes delivered before the next call; previous notes checked.
