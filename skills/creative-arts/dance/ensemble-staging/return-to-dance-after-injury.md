# Return to dance after injury

**Goal:** bring an injured dancer back to full work progressively, with the clinician, so they
don't re-injure.

## Stages (adapt with the clinician's plan)
1. **Rehab only:** clinic exercises; dancer may observe rehearsals (marking mentally, learning).
2. **Modified class:** barre with restrictions (no relevé, no jumps, limited range), floor barre,
   Pilates.
3. **Full class without restrictions** except jumps/specific movements.
4. **Jumps progression:** two-foot small jumps -> single-leg -> big jumps; increasing volume.
5. **Rehearsal partial:** selected sections, partnered work last.
6. **Full rehearsal and performance.**

Progress only when the current stage is pain-free (or within the clinician's agreed pain
limits) on the day and the next morning.

## Rehearsal director's role
- Get written guidance on restrictions from the clinician; share with teachers.
- Plan understudies/covers for the return period.
- Track workload (hours, jumps, partnering) and increase gradually.
- Keep the dancer involved (notes, video) to support mental readiness.

## Warning signs
Pain during or the next day, swelling, limping, loss of confidence - step back a stage and
consult.

## Verify
- [ ] Written clinical clearance and restrictions on file.
- [ ] Staged plan with criteria for each step.
- [ ] Workload log during return.
