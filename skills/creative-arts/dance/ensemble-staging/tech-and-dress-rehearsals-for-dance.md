# Technical and dress rehearsals for dance

**Goal:** bring the work from studio to stage: lighting, sound, set, costume and stage
management cues all set and run with the dancers, safely, before the first audience.

> **Status: draft.** Standard theatre practice adapted to dance; length and rules for
> tech calls come from the contract.

## 1. Before tech
- **Paperwork:** running order, music with timings, cue points by count and timecode for
  lighting and sound, spacing charts, entrance and exit plot, quick-change plot, props
  list.
- **Floor:** dance floor laid and taped; temperature checked.
- Lighting designer has seen a studio run and the cue points.
- Dancers know what tech is for: technical, not performance.

## 2. Cue-to-cue (tech)
- Stage manager calls cues; dancers jump from cue to cue rather than running whole
  sections.
- Dancers **mark** most material but go full out for anything affected by light or set:
  lifts, jumps, fast entrances, anything in a dark transition. Never full-out tricks
  in an unfocused blackout.
- Hold dancers in light for focus checks ("stand in your spot"), but keep them warm and
  rotate; give breaks.
- Rehearsal director watches spacing against lights; agree whether light or dancer moves.

## 3. Add costume
- **Costume parade** or first dress: check movement in costume under light.
- Rehearse quick changes with dressers at speed; time them.

## 4. Dress rehearsals
Full runs with all elements, as if a show: no stops unless safety. Notes afterwards
(see giving-notes-to-dancers). A final dress is often open to an invited audience.

## 5. Safety
- Glow tape on edges and steps; blackout transitions walked first.
- Haze and smoke checked for slipperiness.
- Clear offstage traffic routes; wings not blocked by booms or props.

## Gotchas
- Dancers dancing full out all day in tech: tired or injured by opening.
- Cues set from the studio count but music on stage runs at a different tempo.
- Quick changes first tried at dress.

## Verify
- [ ] Paperwork and cue points ready before tech.
- [ ] Cue-to-cue done; risky moves tested in real light.
- [ ] Quick changes timed.
- [ ] At least one full dress run with notes.
