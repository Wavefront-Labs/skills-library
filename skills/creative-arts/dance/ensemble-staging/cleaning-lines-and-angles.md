# Cleaning lines and angles

**Goal:** after timing and paths are clean, make every dancer's shape match: the same arm
height, elbow angle, leg height, depth of plié, head angle and facing. This is the layer
that makes a group look "tight".

> **Status: draft.** Rehearsal-director practice; complements cleaning-unison-and-spacing
> (which sets the overall order: timing, path, shape, dynamics).

## 1. Define the reference
Decide whose version is correct: the choreographer, the dance captain or the master video.
Correcting dancers to match another wrong dancer spreads the error.

## 2. Use concrete reference points
Replace "higher" with a landmark:
- **Body points:** hand at eye level, wrist in line with shoulder, elbow at 90 degrees,
  foot at knee height, fingers together or spread.
- **Room points:** look at the exit sign, arm points to the downstage left corner, face the
  second leg.
- **Clock and compass:** "arm at 2 o'clock", facing corner 2 (RAD/Cecchetti room numbering
  or the company's own).
- **Depth:** plié depth by a visible marker ("knees over toes, thighs half way").

## 3. Methods
- **Freeze-frame:** stop on the count and fix each shape held; then run into and out of it.
- **Sight down the line:** stand at the end of a line or diagonal; one head or arm out of
  the line jumps out.
- **Mirror off, then on:** dancers check in the mirror, then confirm by feel without it
  (stage has no mirror).
- **Small groups:** two or three at once; differences are obvious.
- **Video still:** pause the front-view video on the accent and compare shapes.

## 4. Body differences
Dancers have different limb lengths and ranges. Match the **angles and end points**, not
absolute heights: a tall dancer's arm at eye level is higher than a short dancer's. For
legs, set the height the whole group can hit safely, especially for hypermobile and
less flexible dancers together.

## Gotchas
- Cleaning shape before timing is clean: perfect shapes at different times still look messy.
- Vague notes ("sharper arms").
- Forcing a uniform leg height beyond some dancers' safe range.

## Verify
- [ ] Reference version named.
- [ ] Every corrected shape has a body or room landmark.
- [ ] Lines checked by sighting down them and on a video still.
