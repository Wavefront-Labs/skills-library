# Spacing charts

**Goal:** a spacing chart records exactly where every dancer is in every formation. It
lets you rescale for any stage, put on a swing, restage the work and settle arguments.
Rehearsal directors and dance captains keep them current for the life of a show.

> **Status: draft.** Common practice in ballet, musical theatre and commercial dance;
> numbering conventions vary by venue (centre-zero numbering is the most common).

## 1. Grid the stage
- **Width:** numbers along the downstage edge, **0 at centre**, then even numbers outward
  (2, 4, 6, 8...) left and right, usually around 0.6-1 m apart. Note the venue's spacing.
- **Depth:** named lines (downstage line, wing 1-4, or 1/4, 1/2, 3/4 depth). Write the
  distance from the setting line or the plaster line for each.
- Record stage left/right from the performer's point of view.

## 2. Chart each formation
For each formation one diagram plus a table:
| Dancer | Number | Depth | Facing | Count it starts | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sam | 4 L | Wing 2 | DSR diagonal | Bar 33, ct 1 | Behind Ana |

- Label formations by section and count ("Act 1 #3, A-section, bar 33").
- Mark facings with arrows and any prop or set piece positions.
- Formation apps or a spreadsheet with a stage grid both work; keep one master.

## 3. Track sheets
From the master, make a per-dancer **track sheet**: every formation that dancer is in,
in order, with entrances, exits, wings and costume changes. Swings learn from these.

## 4. Version control
- Date and version each chart; note what changed and why.
- Update the same day as any change in rehearsal.
- Keep a copy with stage management and in the production bible.

## 5. Rescaling for a new venue
Measure the new stage width and depth. Scale from centre: keep relationships (who is
next to whom, distances in proportion) and re-tape numbers on the new stage before the
spacing call.

## Gotchas
- Stage left/right written from the audience view by one person and the performer view
  by another. State the convention on every page.
- Charts updated in the rehearsal director's head but not on paper.
- Only formations recorded, not the facings and counts; the chart cannot rebuild the piece.

## Verify
- [ ] Stage grid and convention stated on every chart.
- [ ] Every formation charted with dancer, number, depth, facing and count.
- [ ] Track sheet exists for each dancer and swing.
- [ ] Charts dated and versioned, copy with stage management.
