# Sightlines and masking for dance

**Goal:** everything the audience should see must be visible from every seat, and
everything they should not see (dancers waiting in the wings, crossovers, crew) must be
hidden. Sightlines change with the venue, so check them for every theatre.

> **Status: draft.** Standard stage-management and production practice applied to dance.

## 1. Walk the house
Sit in the extreme seats before the spacing call: front row far left and far right, the
back of the stalls, the highest balcony seats at each side. From each, note:
- **Dead zones:** stage areas hidden by the proscenium edge, a box, or the front rows'
  heads (floor work downstage is invisible from the back of a flat stalls).
- **Exposed wings:** where the audience can see into the wings past the legs.
- **Balcony view:** from above, floor patterns read well; from the stalls, depth flattens.

## 2. Masking
- **Legs** (vertical black drapes) at each wing and **borders** (horizontal) overhead hide
  the wings and rig. Angle legs so the extreme seats cannot see past them.
- Leave dance wings wide enough for full-speed entrances and exits (dance companies often
  want 2 m or more between legs).
- Mask crossovers behind the cyclorama or upstage black; dancers crossing in view is a
  common error.
- Use dark floor tape for wing edges so dancers know where the audience view ends.

## 3. Adjust the staging
- Move floor work and key moments into the area everyone sees: usually centre, from
  mid-stage to near downstage.
- In a raked-seat venue floor work reads; in a flat floor venue raise it or bring it
  downstage.
- Tell dancers where the "visible line" is in each wing so they hold character until
  they are truly offstage.
- Waiting dancers stand behind the leg line, not in the gap.

## 4. Non-proscenium spaces
Thrust and in-the-round have no hidden side: there is always someone behind a dancer.
Rotate facings over time and keep the action moving so every side gets fronts.

## Gotchas
- Checking only from the centre of the stalls.
- Dancers relaxing in view of the side seats.
- Wings narrowed by lighting booms, leaving no room to enter at speed.

## Verify
- [ ] Extreme seats walked; dead zones and exposed wings listed.
- [ ] Legs and borders adjusted; crossover masked.
- [ ] Key moments placed in the fully visible area.
- [ ] Dancers know each wing's visible line.
