# Flexibility training for dancers

**Goal:** increase usable (active) range of motion safely, outside the pressure of class.

## Methods
| Method | How | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Static** | hold an end-range position ~30 s, 2-4 reps | best after activity, when warm |
| **Dynamic** | controlled swings through range | warm-up use |
| **PNF (contract-relax)** | stretch, contract the muscle against resistance ~5-6 s, relax, deepen | effective; requires control and a careful partner |
| **Active flexibility** | lift the limb with its own muscles and hold (e.g. développé holds) | converts passive range into usable extension |

## Planning
- Frequency: several sessions a week; improvements take weeks.
- Separate developmental flexibility sessions from performance days when possible.
- Combine passive gains with active strength at end range - otherwise extensions stay low and
  joints unstable.

## When not to stretch further
- Hypermobile dancers: focus on strength and control, not more range.
- Acute injury or pain at end range: stop, refer.
- Growing adolescents: tight muscles during growth spurts - reduce intensity.

## Verify
- [ ] Written plan with methods, frequency and target areas.
- [ ] Active range measured (e.g. held développé height) as well as passive.
- [ ] Hypermobile dancers on strength-focused programmes.
