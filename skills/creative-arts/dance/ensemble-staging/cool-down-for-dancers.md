# Cool-down for dancers

**Goal:** bring the body down gradually after intense dancing and use the warm state to
maintain flexibility, supporting recovery for the next day.

## Structure (10-15 minutes)
1. **Gradual reduction (3-5 min):** light movement - walking, gentle swings - instead of
   stopping abruptly after a big section.
2. **Static stretching (5-8 min):** while still warm, hold main muscle groups used (calves,
   hamstrings, hip flexors, quads, glutes, back) for ~20-30 seconds each; gentle, no bouncing.
3. **Breathing/down-regulation (2 min):** slow breathing lying or seated.

## Recovery habits
- Rehydrate; eat a carbohydrate and protein snack within a couple of hours.
- Change out of sweaty costume; keep warm.
- Note any pain and report it - don't wait until it worsens.
- After shows: sleep is the major recovery tool; manage post-show adrenaline.

## Gotchas
- Cool-downs get skipped when curtain calls and notes run long; schedule them.
- Deep developmental stretching is better in a separate session than right after an exhausting
  show.

## Verify
- [ ] Cool-down scheduled in the rehearsal/show call.
- [ ] Main muscle groups stretched while warm.
- [ ] Dancers know how to report pain.
