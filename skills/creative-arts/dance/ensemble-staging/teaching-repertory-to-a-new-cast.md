# Teaching repertory to a new cast

**Goal:** pass a finished work to dancers who have never done it, so it looks and means what
the choreographer made. This is the core job of a repetiteur or stager.

> **Status: draft.** Common stager practice in ballet and contemporary repertory;
> trust-licensed works have their own staging rules.

## 1. Prepare
- Gather sources: the score (Benesh or Labanotation) if one exists, the best videos
  (and which cast), the choreographer's notes, spacing charts, music score with counts.
- Learn it yourself fully, including all roles you will teach.
- Mark known variants and the version you are teaching.
- Make a rehearsal plan: sections, cast per section, hours.

## 2. Teach in layers
1. **Context first:** what the piece is about, the style, the music.
2. **Structure:** sections, music, formations and who is where.
3. **Steps with counts:** short chunks, mirror or facing direction stated, slowly then
   up to tempo.
4. **Style and quality:** the dynamics, use of weight, epaulement, focus.
5. **Intention:** what each role is doing and feeling.

## 3. Work with the new bodies
- Keep the choreography's intent but adjust detail to individual bodies where the work
  allows (a jump's height, an arm in a lift); never change structure or meaning.
- Record any adjustments in the notes.

## 4. Use video carefully
Video is one cast's version, mirrored or from a bad angle. Use it as reference, not the
final word; the score and the choreographer's notes take precedence.

## 5. Bring it to finish
Run sections, then the whole piece, clean (timing, path, shape, dynamics), and arrange
the choreographer's or trust's final viewing if required.

## Gotchas
- Teaching from a single video and copying that dancer's mistakes.
- Steps learned but no style; the piece looks like a different choreographer's.
- Changes made to suit the new cast without recording them.

## Verify
- [ ] All sources gathered; version chosen and noted.
- [ ] Taught in layers: context, structure, steps, style, intention.
- [ ] Adjustments recorded.
- [ ] Final run viewed by choreographer or trust where required.
