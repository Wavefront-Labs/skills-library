# Abhinaya

**Goal:** convey meaning and emotion through expressive dance (abhinaya).

> **Status: draft.**

## Four modes (Natyashastra)
- **Angika**: body, gestures.
- **Vachika**: speech, song.
- **Aharya**: costume, make-up.
- **Sattvika**: inner emotional states.

## Rasa and bhava
- Nine rasas (shringara, hasya, karuna, raudra, vira, bhayanaka, bibhatsa, adbhuta, shanta).
- Sthayi bhava (dominant) and sanchari bhava (transitory).

## Nayika types
Ashta nayika (eight heroines) by situation.

## Interpreting a padam
1. Translate lyrics; understand context.
2. Choose sanchari bhavas for each line.
3. Repeat lines with different interpretations.

## Verify
- [ ] Lyric translated and interpreted.
- [ ] Variations in sanchari bhava shown.
