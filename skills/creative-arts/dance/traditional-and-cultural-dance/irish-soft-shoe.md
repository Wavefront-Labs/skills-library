# Irish soft shoe

**Goal:** dance the soft-shoe (light-shoe) Irish step dances.

> **Status: draft.** Tempos and level rules differ between organisations (CLRG, An
> Comhdhail, WIDA, etc.) and change; check the current syllabus.

## Shoes
- **Ghillies**: soft lace-up shoes (traditionally girls).
- **Reel shoes**: soft shoes with a heel (traditionally boys).

## Dances
| Dance | Time signature |
|---|---|
| Reel | 4/4 (2/4) |
| Light jig | 6/8 |
| Slip jig | 9/8 |
| Single jig | 6/8 (or 12/8) |

## Technique
- High on the balls; crossed feet; turned out.
- Upper body still; arms by sides.

## Verify
- [ ] Each dance in rhythm with clear crossing.
