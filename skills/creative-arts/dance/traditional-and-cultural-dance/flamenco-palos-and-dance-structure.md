# Flamenco palos and dance structure

**Goal:** structure a flamenco dance (baile) for a given palo and communicate with guitar
(toque) and singing (cante).

> **Status: draft.** Structures vary by palo and artist; the following is common.

## Typical structure
1. **Salida/entrada**: entrance, often with marking.
2. **Letras**: verses sung; dancer marks and responds.
3. **Llamada**: call to cue musicians to a section change.
4. **Escobilla**: footwork section.
5. **Silencio**: (alegrias) lyrical section.
6. **Subida**: acceleration.
7. **Final**: often bulerias ending.

## Communication
- Llamadas and remates (closing phrases) cue musicians.
- Eye contact with guitarist and singer.

## Choosing palo
- Solemn (soleares, seguiriyas) vs festive (alegrias, bulerias).

## Verify
- [ ] Structure suited to palo.
- [ ] Llamadas cue musicians clearly.
