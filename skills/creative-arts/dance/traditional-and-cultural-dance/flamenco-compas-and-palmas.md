# Flamenco compas and palmas

**Goal:** feel and keep compas - flamenco's rhythmic cycles - and accompany with palmas
(hand clapping).

> **Status: draft.** Counting conventions vary; learn from musicians.

## Compas families
| Family | Cycle and accents |
|---|---|
| Soleares, alegrias, bulerias | 12 beats, accents on 3, 6, 8, 10, 12 |
| Seguiriyas | 12 beats grouped 2-2-3-3-2 (accents 1, 3, 5, 8, 11) |
| Tangos, tientos, farruca | 4-beat (binary) |
| Fandangos de Huelva, sevillanas | 3-beat-based (ternary) |

## Palmas
- **Sordas**: muffled, cupped hands.
- **Fuertes/claras/secas**: sharp, flat fingers into palm.
- Contratiempo: off-beat palmas.

## Practice
1. Clap 12-count soleares with accents.
2. Bulerias compas at faster tempo.
3. Practice palmas contratiempo with a partner.

## Verify
- [ ] 12-count accent pattern held for soleares/bulerias.
- [ ] Sordas and fuertes produced cleanly.
