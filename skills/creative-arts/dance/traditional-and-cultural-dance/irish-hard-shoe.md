# Irish hard shoe

**Goal:** dance the hard-shoe (heavy-shoe) Irish step dances.

> **Status: draft.** Tempos vary by organisation and level.

## Dances
- **Treble jig** (6/8, slow tempo for championships).
- **Hornpipe** (2/4 or 4/4).
- **Traditional set dances**: St Patrick's Day, Blackbird, Job of Journeywork, Garden of
  Daisies, King of the Fairies, Jockey to the Fair.

## Technique
- Clear trebles, clicks, drums; on the balls.
- Volume control; rhythm accuracy.

## Verify
- [ ] Trebles and clicks clean at tempo.
