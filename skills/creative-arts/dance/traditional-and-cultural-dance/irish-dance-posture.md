# Irish dance posture

**Goal:** the characteristic Irish step dance posture.

> **Status: draft.**

## Elements
- Upright torso, still upper body.
- Arms straight by sides, hands closed or relaxed.
- Turnout from the hips; crossed feet.
- High on the balls of the feet.

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Arms swinging | drills with arms pressed to sides |
| Turnout from knees | strengthen hip rotators |
| Sinking heels | calf strengthening |
