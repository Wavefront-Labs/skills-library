# Flamenco zapateado

**Goal:** produce clear, rhythmic footwork - zapateado - that plays as an instrument
within flamenco compas.

> **Status: draft.** Terminology and teaching vary between schools in Spain and abroad.

## Sounds
| Term | Sound |
|---|---|
| **Planta** | ball of the foot strikes |
| **Tacon** | heel strikes |
| **Punta** | toe tip (back of the shoe) strikes |
| **Golpe** | whole flat foot strikes |

## Technique
- Shoes with nails (clavos) at toe and heel; firm but flexible.
- Posture: upright torso, knees soft, weight centred; arms often held while feet work.
- Strike from the knee and ankle, not the hip.
- Clarity first: each sound distinct.

## Drills
1. Planta-tacon alternating, slow with metronome.
2. Patterns in 12-count compas (e.g. soleares).
3. Speed: increase tempo gradually; record.

## Floor
Wooden floor or tarima; protect knees and floors.

## Verify
- [ ] Each sound clear at slow and fast tempo.
- [ ] Patterns in compas with correct accents.
