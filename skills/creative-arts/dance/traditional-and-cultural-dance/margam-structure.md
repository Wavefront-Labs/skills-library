# Margam structure

**Goal:** structure a traditional Bharatanatyam solo recital (margam), codified by the
Thanjavur Quartet in the 19th century.

> **Status: draft.** Variations include pushpanjali at the start and other items.

## Order
1. **Alarippu**: invocatory pure dance.
2. **Jatiswaram**: pure dance to swaras.
3. **Shabdam**: first expressive piece.
4. **Varnam**: central, longest piece; nritta and abhinaya.
5. **Padam/javali**: slow expressive pieces.
6. **Tillana**: brisk pure dance finale.
7. **Mangalam**: closing.

## Programming
- Balance nritta and abhinaya; place varnam centrally.
- Coordinate with musicians (nattuvanar, vocal, mridangam).
