# Bharatanatyam adavus

**Goal:** learn the adavus - the basic step units of Bharatanatyam nritta (pure dance).

> **Status: draft.** Number, names and order of adavus differ between banis (Kalakshetra,
> Pandanallur, Vazhuvoor, etc.); follow your guru's bani.

## Stance
**Araimandi** (half-sitting): knees bent and turned out, heels together, back straight.

## Main adavu families (common names)
- **Tattadavu**: stamping footwork.
- **Nattadavu**: stretching of leg with heel strikes.
- **Kuditta mettadavu**: jumping and striking.
- **Mandi adavu**: deep sitting adavus.
- **Tirmanam**: closing patterns.
- Others: **Paraval**, **Sarukkal**, **Tattimettu**...

## Sollukattu and tala
- Syllables (e.g. "tai ya tai hi") set the rhythm.
- Practise in three speeds (kalam).

## Verify
- [ ] Araimandi held through adavus.
- [ ] Adavus in three speeds with sollukattu.
