# Ceili figure dances

**Goal:** dance and teach Irish ceili (group) figure dances.

> **Status: draft.**

## Dances
- Walls of Limerick, Siege of Ennis, Haymaker's Jig, Fairy Reel, Sweets of May,
  Eight-Hand Reel, Four-Hand Reel, High-Cauled Cap.
- Published in CLRG's ceili book (Ar Rinci Foirne).

## Structure
- Lead around, body, figures, repeats.
- Formations: lines, squares, longways.

## Teaching
- Walk through figures, then to music; calling cues for social dances.

## Verify
- [ ] Figures performed in order with correct formations.
