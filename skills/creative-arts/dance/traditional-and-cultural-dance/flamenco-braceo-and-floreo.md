# Flamenco braceo and floreo

**Goal:** dance flamenco arms (braceo) and hands (floreo) with control and expression.

> **Status: draft.** Gendered styles are traditional; contemporary flamenco blurs them.

## Braceo
- Arms carried from the back; shoulders down.
- Curved arms pass through positions above the head, side, front.
- Arm paths slower than feet; contrast.

## Floreo
- Wrist circles; fingers unfold in sequence (little finger to thumb or reverse).
- Traditionally, women's hands more elaborate floreo; men's more closed hands.

## Coordination
- Arms continue while feet work; isolate.

## Verify
- [ ] Arms carried from the back, shoulders down.
- [ ] Floreo smooth both directions.
