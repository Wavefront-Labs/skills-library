# Writing a programme note

**Goal:** a short text that helps the audience into the work and a complete credits list.

## The note
- **Length:** 100-200 words for a short work; up to 400 for an evening-length piece.
- **Content:** the starting point or question, an image or two, maybe a quote - enough to orient,
  not to explain every moment.
- **Tone:** plain language; avoid jargon and over-claiming ("explores the human condition").
- **Don't** describe the choreography step by step or tell the audience what to feel.

## Credits checklist
- Title, year of creation, premiere details.
- Choreography (and "created in collaboration with the dancers" if applicable).
- Music: composer, title, performer/recording, publisher/licence notes.
- Lighting, costume, set, sound designers.
- Dancers (and understudies/swings).
- Rehearsal director, producer, stage manager.
- Funders, commissioners, partners and required logos/acknowledgements.
- Running time.

## Marketing copy
- Write a 30-word and a 100-word version for brochures and websites; deliver images with
  photographer credit.

## Verify
- [ ] Note within the word limit, plain language.
- [ ] Credits complete and spelled correctly (check with each person).
- [ ] Funder acknowledgements meet their requirements.
