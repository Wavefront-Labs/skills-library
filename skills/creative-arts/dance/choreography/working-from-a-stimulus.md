# Working from a stimulus

**Goal:** develop a dance from a starting point - a piece of music, an image, an idea, an object
- so that the movement grows from it and the link is clear to the audience.

## Stimulus types (Smith-Autard)
| Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Auditory** | music, poem, sound recording, spoken text |
| **Visual** | painting, photograph, sculpture, film |
| **Ideational** | an idea or story (migration, memory, conflict) |
| **Kinesthetic** | a movement quality or action itself |
| **Tactile** | touching fabric, an object's texture or weight |

## Method
1. **Research:** brainstorm (mind map) everything the stimulus suggests - words, images,
   feelings, actions, structure.
2. **Decide the dance type:** pure (abstract), narrative, dramatic, comic, or a mix.
3. **Extract movement ideas:** actions (from verbs), dynamics (from adjectives), spatial ideas
   (from shapes/composition), relationships.
4. **Generate material** via improvisation and tasks; pick what fits.
5. **Structure** so the dance's form reflects the stimulus where useful (e.g. a poem's verses
   as sections).
6. **Check the link:** can someone who knows the stimulus see it in the dance?

## Tips
- Avoid illustrating literally beat by beat; translate.
- Write a one-line intention statement early and test material against it.

## Verify
- [ ] Mind map and intention statement written.
- [ ] Dance type chosen consciously.
- [ ] Link to stimulus visible to a viewer told the stimulus.
