# Funding applications for choreographers

**Goal:** a clear, credible application that meets the funder's criteria and pays everyone
fairly.

## Before writing
1. Read the guidance fully: eligibility, criteria, deadlines, maximum amounts, what they won't fund.
2. Check the decision timeline against your project dates (often 6-12+ weeks).
3. Secure partners (venues, producers) and letters of support or confirmed in-kind support.

## Sections
- **The idea:** what you will make and why now, in plain language. One clear paragraph first.
- **Your track record:** relevant previous work with links to video.
- **Activity plan:** dates, phases (R&D, creation, performances), who is involved.
- **Audience/public engagement:** who will see it and how you'll reach them.
- **Budget:** fees (choreographer, dancers at or above union/sector rates, designers), studio hire,
  production costs, marketing, access costs, contingency (~5-10 percent); income including
  in-kind and other funding.
- **Evaluation:** how you'll know it worked (audience numbers, feedback, artistic outcomes).
- **Risk:** what could go wrong and the mitigation.

## Tips
- Use the funder's language for criteria but avoid jargon.
- Budget and activity plan must match each other exactly.
- Get someone outside dance to read it for clarity.

## Verify
- [ ] Every criterion explicitly addressed.
- [ ] Budget balanced and fees at sector rates.
- [ ] Activity plan dates consistent with decision timeline.
