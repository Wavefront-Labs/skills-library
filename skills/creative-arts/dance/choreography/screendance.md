# Screendance

**Goal:** dance made for the camera, where framing, camera movement and editing are part of
the choreography.

## Thinking for the lens
- The frame replaces the stage: close-ups (hands, feet, faces) become choreography.
- Camera movement can dance (handheld following, a track, a drone).
- Editing creates time: cuts can repeat, reverse or join movements across locations.

## Process
1. **Concept and location:** choose locations with spatial and visual character; recce them
   (floor surface, light, permissions, power).
2. **Storyboard/shot list:** for each section, shot sizes, angles and camera movement.
3. **Choreograph in situ:** adapt material to the frame and location; small movements read on camera.
4. **Shoot:** multiple takes and coverage; continuity notes (costume, props, which foot).
5. **Edit:** cut on movement; respect the dance's rhythm; a good editor is a co-choreographer.
6. **Sound/music:** clear rights for online and festival screening (sync licences).

## Gotchas
- Floors outdoors are hard and uneven; plan safe material and warm-up.
- Location permissions and release forms for dancers/public.
