# Site-specific dance

**Goal:** a dance made for and with a specific place, so the location is not a backdrop but a
collaborator.

## Responding to site
1. **Visit repeatedly** at different times; observe how people move there, the light, sound,
   textures, history.
2. **Find the site's movement possibilities:** stairs, railings, walls, slopes, windows.
3. **Research its history and community**; consider how the dance relates to them.
4. Generate material on site whenever possible.

## Logistics
- **Permissions:** landowner, local authority, possibly licences for public performance and
  amplified sound.
- **Risk assessment:** surfaces (hard, wet, uneven), heights, traffic, public interaction, weather.
  Adapt choreography (no hard floor jumps on concrete; footwear).
- **Audience:** static or promenade? Plan sightlines, routes, stewards, accessibility.
- **Power and sound:** generators or battery speakers; neighbours.
- **Weather plan:** wet-weather alternative or postponement criteria.
- **Facilities:** changing, warm-up space, toilets, first aid.

## Verify
- [ ] Written permissions and risk assessment.
- [ ] Choreography adapted to surfaces and conditions.
- [ ] Audience flow, accessibility and weather plans documented.
