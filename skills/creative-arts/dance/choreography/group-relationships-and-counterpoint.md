# Group relationships and counterpoint

**Goal:** organise groups of four or more so the stage picture is rich but the audience knows
where to look.

## Group relationships
| Relationship | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Unison** | power, community, impact; best in short doses |
| **Canon** | texture, motion passing through the group |
| **Counterpoint** | sub-groups perform different material simultaneously; complexity |
| **Complementary** | shapes that fit together (one high/one low, inside/outside) |
| **Contrast** | one group still, another moving; one sharp, one smooth |
| **Leader/follower** | one initiates, others respond |

## Controlling focus
- The eye goes to: movement against stillness, the odd one out, the downstage/centre dancer,
  the highest point, unison after chaos.
- In counterpoint, give one group the "melody" (focus) and others the "accompaniment"
  (smaller, lower, slower).

## Method
1. Divide the cast into sub-groups for each section; name them (A, B, C).
2. Map relationships per section on a timeline (who does what when).
3. Draw formations and focus points; check from the audience.
4. Rehearse sub-groups separately, then together.

## Verify
- [ ] Section map showing sub-groups and their relationships.
- [ ] Focus is clear at each key moment (check video).
- [ ] Unison used deliberately, not as the default.
