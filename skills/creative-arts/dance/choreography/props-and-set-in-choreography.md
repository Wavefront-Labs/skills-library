# Props and set in choreography

**Goal:** integrate objects and set so they add meaning and movement possibilities without
becoming hazards or distractions.

## Choosing props
- Choose objects with **movement potential** (a chair to climb, a long cloth to swirl, a ladder
  as a frame).
- Every prop should earn its place; Humphrey warned of props as clutter.
- Explore the object through improvisation: its weight, balance, sound.

## Rehearsal
1. Use the real prop (or a replica with the same weight and size) as early as possible.
2. Choreograph handling exactly: who picks it up, when, where it goes.
3. Rehearse the failures (dropped prop, broken chair) and agree recoveries.

## Presets and stage management
- **Preset list:** where every prop is placed before the show, marked with spike tape.
- **Running props:** who carries them on/off; prop tables in the wings.
- **Clearances:** set pieces must not block exits, side lights or sightlines.

## Floor safety
- Water, glitter, petals, powders: slip hazards. Test, plan clean-up, warn dancers.
- Secure set pieces against tipping when climbed.

## Verify
- [ ] Props in rehearsal at least two weeks before tech.
- [ ] Preset and running props list with spike marks.
- [ ] Floor hazards assessed and mitigated.
