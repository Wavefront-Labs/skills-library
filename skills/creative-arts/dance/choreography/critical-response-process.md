# Critical Response Process

**Goal:** get useful feedback on a work-in-progress without the artist being flattened by
opinion - Liz Lerman's structured method.

## Roles
- **Artist:** shows the work, asks questions, stays in charge of what is discussed.
- **Responders:** audience giving feedback.
- **Facilitator:** guides the steps, helps rephrase opinions into questions.

## The four core steps
1. **Statements of meaning:** responders say what was meaningful, evocative, interesting,
   striking. No judgement of good/bad.
2. **Artist as questioner:** the artist asks questions about the work; responders answer
   honestly, staying on the artist's question.
3. **Neutral questions from responders:** responders ask questions without embedded opinions
   ("What were you going for with the red light?" not "Why was the red light so harsh?").
4. **Opinion time:** responders offer opinions only with permission: "I have an opinion about
   the ending, would you like to hear it?" The artist may say no.

## Facilitator tips
- Help reframe loaded questions into neutral ones.
- Keep step 1 genuine; it builds trust.
- Timebox: 30-60 minutes for a short showing.

## Verify
- [ ] All four steps run in order.
- [ ] Opinions offered only with permission.
- [ ] Artist leaves with notes they chose to hear.
