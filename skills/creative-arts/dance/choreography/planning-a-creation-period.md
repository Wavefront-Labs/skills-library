# Planning a creation period

**Goal:** a realistic schedule that gets from first idea to a finished, cleaned, teched piece.

## Phases
| Phase | Purpose | Typical length |
|---|---|---|
| Research and development (R&D) | test ideas, generate material, no pressure to finish | 1-3 weeks |
| Creation | build the full structure | 3-6 weeks |
| Cleaning and runs | precision, stamina, performance | 1-2 weeks |
| Production/tech week | in the venue with light, sound, set | 3-5 days |

## Rules of thumb
- Experienced choreographers often estimate several studio hours per minute of finished dance
  for new contemporary work (complex group sections take far more); track your own ratio.
- Hold the first **full run** at least a week (ideally two) before tech.
- Lock music length and set design dimensions early enough for designers.
- Schedule shorter days for intense physical work; plan rest days.

## Milestones
1. Concept and research shared.
2. Music/structure map locked.
3. First full rough run.
4. Design lock (costume fittings, set, lighting meeting).
5. Final run in studio with an invited audience/outside eye.
6. Tech, dress, opening.

## Verify
- [ ] Schedule with phases, milestones and rest days.
- [ ] First full run dated before cleaning week.
- [ ] Design deadlines aligned with creation progress.
