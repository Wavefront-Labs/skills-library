# Working with a composer

**Goal:** a commissioned score that serves the dance, delivered on time and usable in
rehearsal, with clear agreements on ownership and payment.

## Process
1. **Brief:** the concept, mood references, instrumentation/budget, duration, structure map
   (sections and approximate lengths), live or recorded.
2. **Order of work - choose one:**
   - music first (choreographer sets on finished sections),
   - dance first (composer scores to video),
   - parallel (both develop and exchange drafts weekly).
3. **Rehearsal tracks:** ask for early sketches/demos with the right tempo and length, even if
   rough; dancers need something to count.
4. **Structure lock:** agree when section lengths are frozen so choreography doesn't need
   re-counting late.
5. **Delivery:** final mixed tracks plus **stems** (separate instrument groups) for later edits
   and touring; a click track for live musicians if needed.
6. **Tech:** composer attends sound check or provides a level guide.

## Agreement (in writing)
- Fee and payment schedule; who owns the copyright (usually the composer) and the licence
  granted to the company (performances, territory, recordings, film).
- Credit wording; royalties/PRS registration (UK: PRS for Music handles performing rights).

## Verify
- [ ] Written brief and structure map shared.
- [ ] Rehearsal tracks available from early in the process.
- [ ] Signed agreement covering licence, fee and credit.
