# Making a trio

**Goal:** use the inherent drama of three - someone is always potentially left out - to build
relationships that shift.

## Dynamics of three
- **2+1:** two dancers together, one apart - instantly dramatic (exclusion, temptation, choice).
- **Shifting alliances:** the pair changes (A+B, then B+C, then A+C).
- **1 vs 2:** a leader and two followers, or one against two.
- **3 as one:** unison or tight contact as a unit.

## Spatial design
- **Triangle:** the base formation - change its orientation (point downstage or upstage), size
  and facings.
- **Line of three:** the middle dancer becomes focus.
- **Cluster:** close contact for weight-sharing and lifts with two bases.

## Method
1. Decide the relationships and how they change across the trio.
2. Build a shared phrase; distribute variations (unison 2, contrast 1).
3. Use three-way canon (start 1, 2, 3) for texture.
4. Contact: two lifting one gives secure lifts; design carefully.

## Verify
- [ ] At least two different 2+1 configurations.
- [ ] Triangle formation varied in orientation and size.
- [ ] Viewer can describe the relationships.
