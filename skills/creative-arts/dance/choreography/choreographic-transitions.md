# Choreographic transitions

**Goal:** link movements, phrases and sections so the dance flows - transitions are where
inexperienced choreography falls apart.

## Within a phrase
- **Momentum:** let the end of one movement launch the next (a swing finishing into a turn).
- **Shared shape or body part:** end a movement in a shape that the next starts from.
- **Weight shift:** plan which foot carries weight at the end so the next move is possible.
- **Level changes:** design the route to and from the floor (spirals, rolls), not a pause.

## Between phrases and sections
- **Overlap:** begin the new phrase in some dancers while others finish the old one.
- **Stillness as transition:** a deliberate held moment, not dead time.
- **Spatial transitions:** dancers travel to new formations as part of the choreography.
- **Entrances and exits:** choreograph them - how and where dancers arrive is part of the work.
- **Music changes:** use the musical transition or deliberately counter it.

## Method
1. Watch a run and mark every point where energy drops or dancers "walk to places".
2. For each, design a transition using one of the strategies above.
3. Rehearse transitions separately - they are often under-rehearsed.

## Verify
- [ ] No unplanned walking between sections.
- [ ] Each transition rehearsed at full speed.
- [ ] Energy maintained through section changes on video.
