# Costume for dance

**Goal:** costumes that support the concept and let dancers move, lift and partner safely.

## Briefing the designer
- Show the movement: video of floorwork, lifts, partnering, jumps.
- Note special needs: grips for lifts (no slippery fabric at the waist), floor sliding (fabric
  that slides), big extensions (stretch, gussets), quick changes.
- Agree the palette with lighting - colours change under coloured light.

## Fabric and construction
- Four-way stretch for movement-heavy work; check opacity under light.
- Avoid embellishments where partners hold (beading at the waist scratches and slips).
- Secure fastenings (hooks, poppers, sewn-in); no zips digging in during floorwork.
- Washable or easy to clean for long runs.

## Fittings and rehearsal
1. First fitting with movement tests (dancers do the hardest moves).
2. Costume in rehearsal at least a week before tech for partnering and floor work.
3. Quick changes rehearsed with dressers.

## Footwear
- Choose with the floor: bare feet, socks (slides), jazz shoes, trainers (grip) - test on the
  performance floor surface.

## Verify
- [ ] Designer briefed with movement video and special needs list.
- [ ] Costumes tested in partnering and floorwork before tech.
- [ ] Quick changes timed and rehearsed.
