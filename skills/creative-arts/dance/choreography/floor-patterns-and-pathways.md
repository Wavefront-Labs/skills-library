# Floor patterns and pathways

**Goal:** design the paths dancers trace across the floor (floor patterns) and through the air
with body parts (air patterns), so movement in space supports the meaning.

## Pathway types and effects
| Pathway | Effect |
|---|---|
| Straight lines | direct, purposeful, formal |
| Curves and circles | soft, continuous, ritual |
| Zigzags | agitated, unpredictable |
| Spirals | inward focus, intensity, gathering |
| Figure-eight | weaving, playful, continuous |
| Random/scattered | chaotic, natural |

## Method
1. **Draw a floor plan** for each section: a rectangle (stage) with each dancer's path as a line
   with arrows; use colour per dancer; mark counts at key points.
2. Vary pathways across sections; avoid everyone always travelling in straight lines.
3. Air patterns: design the shapes traced by arms or legs (circles, lines) to echo or
   contrast floor patterns.
4. Check spacing: paths must not collide unless intended; leave room for traffic.

## Tips
- A circle around the stage reads differently from a circle on the spot.
- Retrace a pathway backward for a sense of return.
- Software (e.g. ArrangeUs, StageWrite) or simple paper plans both work; keep them in the
  production bible.

## Verify
- [ ] Floor plans drawn for each section with counts.
- [ ] Pathway types varied and matched to intent.
- [ ] Paths tested in the studio for collisions.
