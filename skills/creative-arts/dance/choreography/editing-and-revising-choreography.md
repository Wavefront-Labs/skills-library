# Editing and revising choreography

**Goal:** turn a full draft into a tight work by cutting, reordering and refining.

## Method
1. **Film full runs** from the audience position; watch them a day later with fresh eyes.
2. **Mark attention drops** on a timeline - where did you look away or check the time?
3. **Ask of each section:** does it serve the intent? Is anything said twice? Is the
   transition working?
4. **Cut:** repetition that doesn't build; material you love that doesn't fit (Humphrey: "all
   dances are too long").
5. **Proportion:** check section lengths; the climax often comes too late or is too short.
6. **Reorder:** try sections in different orders in the studio - quick experiments.
7. **Outside eye:** invite feedback with a structured method (Critical Response Process).
8. **Version control:** keep each run's video and a log of changes so you can revert.

## Knowing when to stop
- When changes no longer make it better, only different.
- When the dancers need stability to perform well - freeze choreography before tech.

## Verify
- [ ] Attention-drop timeline from a full-run video.
- [ ] Logged cuts/reorders with reasons.
