# Devising with dancers

**Goal:** make a work with significant dancer input while keeping a coherent vision and fair
working relationships.

## Choose your mode (Butterworth's spectrum)
| Mode | Choreographer role | Dancer role |
|---|---|---|
| Didactic | expert, sets all movement | instrument |
| Pilot | sets most, some input invited | interpreter |
| Facilitator | sets tasks, selects | contributor/creator |
| Collaborator | shares decisions | co-owner |
Be explicit with dancers which mode you're working in - it can change per section.

## Process
1. **Share the concept** and research early so tasks make sense.
2. **Set tasks** (see choreographic-tasks-and-constraints); time-box them.
3. **Watch and select:** choose material; explain why when useful.
4. **Shape:** edit, combine, reorder - you hold the overview.
5. **Document:** film and name all material; keep a running order.
6. **Feedback loops:** invite dancer feedback at agreed points, not constantly.

## Ethics and credit
- Agree how dancer contributions are credited ("created in collaboration with the dancers").
- Personal material (stories, trauma) needs consent and care; dancers can withdraw it.
- Fair hours and breaks; devising is cognitively tiring.

## Verify
- [ ] Mode of working stated to the dancers.
- [ ] All generated material filmed and named.
- [ ] Credit agreement written in programme copy.
