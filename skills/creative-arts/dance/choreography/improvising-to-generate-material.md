# Improvising to generate material

**Goal:** turn improvisation into usable choreography - not just exploration - by framing,
capturing and selecting.

## Method
1. **Frame a prompt:** one focus at a time (a body part leading, an image, a quality, a
   spatial path). Vague prompts produce habitual movement.
2. **Warm into it:** a few minutes of free movement to get past the first predictable ideas.
3. **Improvise 3-5 minutes** while filming (wide, steady shot).
4. **Review the footage** immediately: mark timestamps of moments that surprise you or fit the
   work's intent - "keepers".
5. **Fix:** relearn each keeper from the video; give it counts or a cue; name it.
6. **Develop** (see `creative-arts.dance.choreography.motif-manipulation-devices`).

## Getting past habits
- Add a constraint mid-improvisation (only one arm; only on the floor; never repeat).
- Improvise with eyes closed, then with a fixed focus.
- Work from a partner's improvisation (copy and distort).

## Gotchas
- The best moments are often transitions, not "big" movements - watch for them.
- Memory loses detail fast: film every time.
- With dancers, credit their improvised material appropriately.

## Verify
- [ ] Improvisation filmed and reviewed the same session.
- [ ] At least 3-5 keepers fixed and named.
- [ ] Keepers can be repeated exactly the next day.
