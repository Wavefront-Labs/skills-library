# Hip hop dance theatre

**Goal:** create narrative or thematic theatre work using hip hop dance vocabulary.

> **Status: draft.**

## Elements
- Narrative or theme from the dancers' lives and culture.
- Characters built through movement styles.
- Dramaturgy: structure, arc, conflict.
- Music, spoken word, visuals.

## Process
1. Develop theme with dancers; workshops.
2. Build scenes; integrate freestyle.
3. Work with dramaturg and designers.

## Verify
- [ ] Clear narrative or thematic arc.
- [ ] Hip hop vocabulary central.
