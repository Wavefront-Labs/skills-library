# Tutting

**Goal:** create geometric shapes with arms and hands - right angles, boxes - inspired by
Egyptian art; a popping sub-style.

> **Status: draft.**

## Basics
- Right angles at wrists, elbows, shoulders; 90 degrees exact.
- Flat palms and straight lines.
- Move from shape to shape on beats; stillness between.

## Finger tutting
Small shapes with fingers - boxes, triangles, cubes.

## Practice
1. Learn 4 shapes; transition on each beat.
2. Mirror check angles.
3. Combine with pops (pop as you arrive at each shape).

## Verify
- [ ] Angles exact on video.
- [ ] Clean transitions on the beat.
