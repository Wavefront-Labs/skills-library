# Breaking freezes

**Goal:** stop dead in a balanced shape, usually on a musical hit, punctuating a round.

> **Status: draft.** Build strength progressively; wrist and shoulder injuries are common.

## Progression
| Freeze | Base | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Baby freeze** | hands, elbow tucked into hip, head side | first freeze for most |
| **Chair freeze** | one hand, elbow in back, legs crossed | stylish, many variations |
| **Turtle** | both elbows in stomach, body parallel | foundation for turtle-based power |
| **Headstand / headfreeze** | head and hands | head protection (beanie) |
| **Airchair** | one hand, body arched | advanced |
| **Hollowback** | handstand with arched back | advanced, back flexibility |

## Technique
- Enter from footwork with momentum, stop on the beat.
- Lock the elbow anchor into the hip/stomach.
- Hold for at least 2 counts; exit cleanly.

## Safety
- Wrist warm-up and conditioning; avoid hyperextension.
- Neck: train headstands progressively on soft surfaces.

## Verify
- [ ] Baby and chair freeze held 3+ seconds both sides.
- [ ] Freezes entered from footwork on a musical hit.
