# Waacking arm work

**Goal:** perform the arm rotations and whacks of waacking (punking), born in 1970s Los
Angeles gay clubs and popularised through Soul Train.

> **Status: draft.**

## Technique
- Arm rotations overhead and in front; shoulders relaxed.
- Whacks: arms snap overhead or behind, with control.
- Guarding: one arm covering the face/body.
- Speed and precision on the beat.

## Practice
- Rotations to disco at ~115-130 bpm.
- Alternate arm patterns; combine with poses.

## Verify
- [ ] Arm rotations clean and fast on the beat.
