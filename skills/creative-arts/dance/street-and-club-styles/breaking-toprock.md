# Breaking toprock

**Goal:** dance the upright entry of a breaking round - toprock - with style and musicality,
setting up the go-down to the floor.

> **Status: draft.** Step names and versions vary between crews and eras.

## Core steps
| Step | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Indian step** | cross-step with kick, from the original Bronx b-boys; arms open and close |
| **Crossover** | crossing step forward with alternating feet |
| **Bronx rock / Brooklyn rock** | rocking step with hops (Brooklyn rock overlaps with uprock) |
| **Kick step** | front kick and step back |
| **Salsa rock / Latin step** | side-to-side with Latin influence |
| **Charlie Rock**, **Front Rock**, **Side Step** | variations |

## Musicality and flavour
- Toprock rides the breakbeat: step on the drums, accent the snare.
- Arms, head and shoulders give flavour; facing the opponent communicates attitude.
- Transition: drop into the go-down on a musical phrase.

## Drills
1. Each step for 1 minute to a breakbeat at ~110 bpm.
2. Chain 3 steps for 16 counts, then go down.
3. Record and check rhythm.

## Verify
- [ ] At least five toprock steps clean on the beat.
- [ ] Smooth go-down from toprock into footwork.
