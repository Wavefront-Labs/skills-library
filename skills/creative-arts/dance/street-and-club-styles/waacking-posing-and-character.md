# Waacking posing and character

**Goal:** waacking is theatrical: poses inspired by Hollywood film stars and characters.

> **Status: draft.**

## Elements
- Poses on musical accents; held briefly.
- Character: glamour, drama, attitude.
- Facial expression and eye contact with the audience.

## Practice
- Pose on accents; build a sequence of poses and arm work.

## Verify
- [ ] Poses hit on accents with clear character.
