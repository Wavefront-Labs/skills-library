# Waving

**Goal:** create the illusion of a wave travelling through the body - a popping sub-style.

> **Status: draft.**

## Arm wave
1. Start with fingertips; lift wrist, elbow, shoulder in sequence.
2. Cross through the chest to the other shoulder.
3. Continue down the other arm to the fingertips.
Each joint moves once, in sequence, at even speed.

## Body wave
Head, chest, hips, knees in sequence, front to back or side to side.

## Practice
- Mirror work, slow then fast.
- Reverse direction; stop mid-wave on a beat.
- Mix with pops and tuts.

## Verify
- [ ] Wave travels joint by joint evenly.
- [ ] Waves in both directions and at different speeds.
