# Locking points

**Goal:** perform locking points - sharp, extended arm points with attitude.

> **Status: draft.**

## Technique
- Point straight with index finger; elbow extended.
- Directions: front, side, up, diagonal; facing changes.
- **Uncle Sam point**: point forward at the audience with attitude.

## Practice
- Points on every beat, then combine with locks.

## Verify
- [ ] Points sharp and in time.
- [ ] Combined with locks.
