# House footwork

**Goal:** fast, light, intricate footwork over the house groove.

> **Status: draft.** Names vary.

## Steps
| Step | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Shuffle** | quick heel-toe or skip-like slides |
| **Loose legs** | kicking legs loose in and out |
| **Heel-toe** | alternating heel and toe touches |
| **Skate** | gliding step resembling skating |
| **Stomp** | grounded stomping step |
| **Farmer** | traveling step with knees lifted |
| **Salsa hop** | Latin-influenced step |
| **Sway** | weight shifting side to side |

## Practice
- Each step 16 counts at ~120-125 bpm.
- Combinations of 3-4 steps.
- Keep upper body jacking.

## Verify
- [ ] Footwork light and clean at tempo.
- [ ] Combined with jacking.
