# Named locking steps

**Goal:** know and perform the classic named locking steps and who they honour.

> **Status: draft.** Versions vary; learn from locking teachers.

## Steps
| Step | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Scooby Doo** | kick-kick hop step; named for Jimmy "Scoo B Doo" Foster |
| **Skeeter Rabbit** | kicking skip; named for Skeeter Rabbit |
| **Scoobot** | combination of Scooby Doo and robot |
| **Stop and Go** | travelling stop-start movement |
| **Which-a-way** | look and turn in alternating directions |
| **Funky Guitar** | air-guitar motion |
| **Pacing** | walking steps with attitude |
| **Knee drop** | dropping onto knees (protect knees) |

## Practice
Learn one step at a time; combine into 8s with locks and points.
