# Hip hop bounce and rock

**Goal:** have a groove under every move - the bounce and rock that make hip hop dance
look like hip hop and not like jazz to rap music.

> **Status: draft.** From hip hop foundation teaching; terminology varies by teacher and
> city.

## The bounce
- **Down bounce**: knees and chest drop on the beat (on 1, 2, 3, 4), lift on the "and".
- **Up bounce**: the lift happens on the beat, drop on the "and"; lighter feel.
- Relaxed body: the bounce travels through knees, hips, chest, head.

## The rock
- Torso rocks forward/back or side to side, with the shoulders and chest leading.
- Combine with bounce; vary with head nods and arm swing.

## Drills
1. 2 minutes down bounce, 2 minutes up bounce to a 90 bpm track.
2. Same at 80 and 100 bpm; then switch bounce type every 8.
3. Add a step (e.g. two-step) while keeping the bounce; check video that bounce continues.

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Stiff upper body | relax shoulders; let the chest follow the knees |
| Groove stops in steps | drill steps slowly with bounce exaggerated |
| Off the beat | clap the beat first, then bounce to the clap |

## Verify
- [ ] Down and up bounce both clear at 80-100 bpm.
- [ ] Groove continues under steps on video.
