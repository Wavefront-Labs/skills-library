# Breaking footwork and six-step

**Goal:** move on the floor in breaking (downrock / footwork), with the six-step as the
foundation everything else builds on.

> **Status: draft.** Counts and teaching vary by teacher.

## The six-step
Hands on floor (one or both), body facing down, legs circle around:
1. Right leg steps behind/crosses under (from crouch).
2. Left leg steps out wide.
3. Right leg kicks through (sitting position, hand behind).
4. Left leg crosses over.
5. Right leg steps back out.
6. Return to crouch.
Repeat both directions.

## Variations
- **Three-step**: shortened version.
- **CCs**: kneeling step-step switches named after the CC crew.
- **Kick-outs**: legs shoot out from a crouch.
- **Sweeps**, **pretzels**, **coffee grinder** (one leg sweeps around on the floor).

## Technique
- Hips up and back; arm supporting weight close to body; switch hands smoothly.
- Speed comes from tightness, not big moves.

## Drills
1. Slow six-step 10 times each direction.
2. Speed sets of 30 seconds; rest.
3. Footwork into freeze transitions.

## Verify
- [ ] Six-step both directions at tempo.
- [ ] Two variations linked.
- [ ] Footwork ends cleanly in a freeze.
