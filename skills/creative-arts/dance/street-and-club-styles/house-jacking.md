# House jacking

**Goal:** jack - the continuous rippling groove of house dance, said to come from Chicago
house clubs - so that the beat lives in the torso under all footwork.

> **Status: draft.** Teaching varies by teacher and city.

## Technique
- Torso waves forward and back on each beat (forward jack), or side to side (side jack).
- Movement originates from the chest and hips; knees soft.
- Can be continuous (waves) or punctuated.

## Drills
1. Forward jack to a house track (~120-125 bpm) for 2 minutes.
2. Switch to side jack every 8 counts.
3. Add footwork while maintaining the jack.

## Verify
- [ ] Jack continuous on every beat.
- [ ] Jacking maintained under footwork.
