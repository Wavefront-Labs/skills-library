# Vogue femme five elements

**Goal:** understand and perform the five elements of vogue femme - from Black and Latinx
LGBTQ+ ballroom culture in New York.

> **Status: draft.** Ballroom culture has its own rules; learn from ballroom practitioners.

## The five elements
1. **Hands**: hand performance - gestures framing face and body.
2. **Catwalk**: stylized walk with cross steps and hip sway.
3. **Duckwalk**: squatting walk, kicking feet forward.
4. **Spins and dips**: turns ending in a dip (falling to floor with one leg bent).
5. **Floor performance**: moves on the floor.

## Context
- Old way, new way, vogue femme (soft and dramatic).
- Houses, categories, balls; *Paris Is Burning* (1990) documented the scene.
