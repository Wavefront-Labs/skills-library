# Musical phrasing in house

**Goal:** dance with the structure of house music so changes in movement match changes in
the track.

> **Status: draft.**

## Structure
- Four-on-the-floor kick at ~118-130 bpm.
- Phrases: 8 counts, 32-count phrases (4 x 8), 16/32-bar sections.
- Builds, breaks and drops; hi-hat and percussion layers.

## Dancing the phrase
- Change movement at the start of 32-count phrases.
- Build energy with builds; hit the drop with a big move.
- In breaks, use jacking or lighter steps.

## Practice
- Count 32s while listening; change steps on each phrase.

## Verify
- [ ] Movement changes on phrase boundaries.
- [ ] Energy follows builds and drops.
