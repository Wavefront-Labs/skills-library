# The lock

**Goal:** perform the signature move of locking - freezing in a position, then releasing -
created by Don Campbell in early-1970s Los Angeles.

> **Status: draft.** Credit Don Campbell and The Lockers.

## Technique
- From a movement, stop sharply, holding shoulders and arms.
- Typical lock position: arms bent, hands near shoulders or forward.
- Release on the next beat with flow.

## Funk character
Locking is playful and audience-facing; smile, play, interact.

## Practice
- Lock on 1, 3; then on syncopated beats.
- Combine with points.

## Verify
- [ ] Clean locks on the beat.
- [ ] Funk character in face and body.
