# Hip hop choreography

**Goal:** make choreography that is rooted in hip hop foundation and groove, readable on
stage or video, and musically rich.

> **Status: draft.** From choreographers' and competition practice.

## Music
- Pick tracks with clear beats and interesting layers (drums, bass, vocals, samples).
- Map the song: intro, verses, hooks, breaks, drops.
- Clear rights for performance or video.

## Building phrases
1. Start from a groove (bounce, rock) and party steps; build combinations.
2. Use musical **hits** (accent on a snare or vocal), **textures** (smooth vs hard),
   and **layers** (feet to drums, arms to vocals).
3. Contrast: a fast intricate eight followed by a simple groove.
4. Count for teaching but rehearse to the music for feel.

## Staging
- Formations change on phrase starts; transitions keep the groove going.
- Let individual flavour show in freestyle windows.

## Credit
Credit the vocabulary's originators and styles in class and programme notes.

## Verify
- [ ] Every phrase has groove underneath.
- [ ] Musical layers used, not only the beat.
- [ ] Taught in counts, rehearsed to music.
