# Boogaloo rolls

**Goal:** dance boogaloo - fluid, circular rolls of hips, knees, chest and neck, developed by
Boogaloo Sam (Electric Boogaloos) and danced alongside popping.

> **Status: draft.** Credit originators; terminology varies.

## Core elements
- **Hip rolls**: circular hip motion, knees bent.
- **Knee rolls**: knees circle in and out.
- **Chest rolls**: chest leads circles.
- **Neck rolls**: smooth circles of the head.
- **Twists** and **walk-outs**.

## Practice
1. Hip rolls slow and continuous; then on beat.
2. Combine with knee rolls; add pops at transitions.

## Verify
- [ ] Rolls smooth and continuous.
- [ ] Combined with pops in a sequence.
