# Building a crew routine

**Goal:** create a routine for a crew (typically 5-9 or more dancers) for a competition or
showcase.

> **Status: draft.** Check competition rules for time limits, crew size and judging criteria.

## Steps
1. Concept and music: edit a mix within the time limit (often 2-3 minutes).
2. Styles: highlight crew members' styles; foundation visible.
3. Formations and transitions on music changes.
4. Unison sections vs individual features.
5. Tricks placed at peaks.
6. Clean timing and spacing.

## Verify
- [ ] Routine within time limit; rules followed.
- [ ] Formations change cleanly; foundation visible.
