# Freestyling in the cypher

**Goal:** step into a cypher (circle) at a jam or party and dance a round that responds to
the music and the people, with respect for the circle's codes.

> **Status: draft.** Codes vary by scene and style; watch and ask locals.

## Entering
- Watch the energy; wait for the previous dancer to finish (they return to the edge).
- Don't cut someone off; don't hog the floor - a round is often 30-60 seconds.
- Enter with a clear start - a step in, a gesture - on a musical phrase.

## Building the round
1. **Groove first**: establish bounce and rhythm.
2. **Foundation**: steps from the style you dance.
3. **Musicality**: hit accents, play with breaks and drops.
4. **Peak**: a signature move or trick near the end.
5. **Exit**: clear finish, return to the circle.

## Responding
- Answer others' rounds with your own ideas, not copies.
- Call-outs (pointed challenges) are part of battle culture; keep it respectful.
- Hype others: claps, shouts.

## Verify
- [ ] Enters and exits cleanly on the music.
- [ ] Round has groove, foundation and a peak.
- [ ] Respects turn-taking and the circle.
