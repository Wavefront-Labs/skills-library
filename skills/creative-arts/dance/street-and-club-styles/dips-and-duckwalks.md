# Dips and duckwalks

**Goal:** execute dips and duckwalks safely and in time.

> **Status: draft.** Practise progressions; dips stress back and knees.

## Dips
- Fall backward, one leg bent under, other extended; land on back with control.
- Use arms and core to slow; time with musical hits.
- Progress from low to full height.

## Duckwalks
- Squat low, kick feet forward alternately; balance on balls.
- Build quad strength; keep back upright.

## Verify
- [ ] Dips landed safely on time.
- [ ] Duckwalk sustained with balance.
