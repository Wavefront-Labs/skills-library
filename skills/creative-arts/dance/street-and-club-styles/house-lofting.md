# House lofting

**Goal:** floor movement in house dance - spins, drops, slides - named after David
Mancuso's The Loft in New York.

> **Status: draft.**

## Elements
- Spins on the floor and while standing.
- Drops to the floor and quick returns.
- Rolls and slides.
- Integration with footwork.

## Safety
- Smooth floor; knee pads in training.

## Practice
1. Drops to floor and back on the beat.
2. Floor spin combinations.
3. Transition into footwork.

## Verify
- [ ] Smooth transitions between floor and standing.
