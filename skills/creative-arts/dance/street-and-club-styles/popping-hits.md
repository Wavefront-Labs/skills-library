# Popping hits

**Goal:** produce clean, rhythmic pops - quick muscle contractions on the beat - the core of
popping created by Boogaloo Sam and the Electric Boogaloos in 1970s Fresno.

> **Status: draft.** Teaching methods vary; credit the originators.

## Technique
- **Arms**: contract forearms and triceps on the beat, release immediately.
- **Chest**: push the chest out quickly and release.
- **Neck**: pop the neck forward or back.
- **Legs**: lock the knee quickly, contract thighs.
- Breathe: exhale on the pop to stay relaxed between.

## The fresno
Side-to-side step: step out, pop the arm and leg, return; foundational popping step.

## Drills
1. Arm pops to a funk track at ~100 bpm, every beat.
2. Pop on 1 and 3, then on syncopated beats.
3. Combine chest + arm pops in a fresno.

## Verify
- [ ] Clean, rhythmic pops in arms, chest, legs.
- [ ] Fresno step with pops on the beat.
