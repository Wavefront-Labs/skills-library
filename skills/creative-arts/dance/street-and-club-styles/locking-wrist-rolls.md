# Locking wrist rolls

**Goal:** perform wrist rolls (twirls) - a quick rotation of the hands around each other.

> **Status: draft.**

## Technique
- Hands circle around each other at chest height.
- Change direction; vary speed to the music.
- Finish in a point or lock.

## Practice
- Wrist rolls to funk at ~110 bpm; link with points and locks.

## Verify
- [ ] Rolls smooth, finishing cleanly on the beat.
