# Hip hop party steps

**Goal:** learn the social dances of hip hop from the 1980s and 1990s - the vocabulary
underneath most hip hop choreography and freestyle - with their names and the groove.

> **Status: draft.** Names and exact versions vary between cities and teachers; ask the
> class or scene for the version they use.

## A selection
| Step | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Running Man** | stationary run with a slide back on the standing foot |
| **Roger Rabbit** | like a reverse Running Man: kick back and skip back |
| **Cabbage Patch** | fists together circling in front of chest with the body rocking |
| **Kid 'n Play Kick Step** | partner kick step from the late-80s duo |
| **Biz Markie** | swinging legs in and out with arm swing |
| **Wop** | rhythmic shoulder and arm swing with knees bent |
| **Harlem Shake** (original) | shoulder and arm shake from Harlem, early 1980s; not the 2013 meme |
| **Bart Simpson** | knee and foot movement traveling sideways |
| **Steve Martin, Reebok, Happy Feet, ATL Stomp** | later-era steps |

## Learning approach
1. Learn the step with the bounce from the beginning.
2. Practise each to era-appropriate music (old-school tracks at ~90-110 bpm).
3. Mix steps in 8-count sequences; then freestyle transitioning between them.

## Context
Credit the eras, cities and dancers who popularised them; party steps are social dances
from Black and Latino communities.

## Verify
- [ ] Each step learned with bounce.
- [ ] Steps strung together smoothly.
- [ ] Origins named when teaching.
