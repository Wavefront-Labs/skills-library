# Crediting street pioneers

**Goal:** honour the originators of street styles - Black and Latino communities - in
teaching and staging.

> **Status: draft.**

## Practice
- Research: who created the style, where, when (e.g. locking - Don Campbell; popping -
  Boogaloo Sam; breaking - Bronx b-boys and b-girls; waacking - LA club scene; vogue -
  ballroom culture).
- Name originators in class and programme notes.
- Hire and pay culture-bearers; invite them to consult.
- Avoid presenting styles as "new" or without context.

## Verify
- [ ] Originators named in materials.
- [ ] Culture-bearers consulted and paid.
