# Breaking power moves

**Goal:** perform rotational power moves safely and integrate them into rounds as part of
breaking rather than gymnastics.

> **Status: draft.** High injury risk; train progressively with a coach or experienced crew.

## Core moves
| Move | Prerequisites |
|---|---|
| **Swipe** | strong handstand, explosive hip drive |
| **Windmill** | baby freeze, turtle; rolling across upper back |
| **Flare** | strong core and hip flexors; pommel-horse style circles |
| **Headspin** | headstand control; beanie; spin in a stable position |
| **Airflare** | flare and handstand strength; advanced |

## Progressions (windmill)
1. Baby freeze and turtle holds.
2. Back rolls across shoulders.
3. Baby mill (with knees), then full windmill.

## Conditioning
- Core strength, shoulders, wrists.
- Flexibility in hips and hamstrings for flares.

## Safety
- Floor: smooth, firm, non-slip (linoleum or marley as preferred).
- Spotters for early attempts; stop when fatigued.

## In a round
- Power as a peak, not the whole round; judges score foundation, musicality and creativity
  alongside execution (WDSF and battle judging).

## Verify
- [ ] Each move trained via progressions.
- [ ] Power integrated into rounds with toprock, footwork and freezes.
