# Cabriole

**Goal:** a beaten jump where the working leg is thrown to height and the supporting leg
rises to strike it from underneath, sending it higher, before the dancer lands on the
supporting (underneath) leg.

## Method (cabriole devant, effacé)
1. Preparation: a run-up step such as a chassé or a coupé into demi-plié on the take-off leg.
2. Throw the working leg forward (grand battement, straight, 45 degrees for petit cabriole, 90
   for grand).
3. Push off; the take-off leg rises in the air and **beats the calf of the working leg from
   below**; the working leg rebounds slightly higher.
4. The take-off leg drops back first and lands in fondu; the working leg remains extended in
   the air for a moment, then continues.
- **Cabriole derrière:** the same action behind in arabesque; the body inclines forward, and
  the beat is underneath the back leg.
- Double cabriole: two beats.

## Teaching points
- The underneath leg goes up to the working leg - not the working leg down to it.
- Both legs straight and turned out; calves meet.
- Hold the torso lifted; in derrière don't collapse forward.

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Working leg drops to meet the beat | keep it up; bring the underneath leg to it |
| Bent knees at the beat | straight legs from the thigh |
| Landing on the working leg | land on the take-off (underneath) leg |
| No visible beat | more elevation; turn out so calves meet |

## Verify
- [ ] Visible beat with straight legs.
- [ ] Working leg stays at or above its starting height.
- [ ] Lands on the underneath leg in fondu, working leg extended.
