# Développé

**Goal:** a slow unfolding of the leg into extension, building the strength to hold the leg
high with the pelvis level - the core of adage.

## Method
1. **Draw up:** from 5th, the working foot slides up the supporting leg through cou-de-pied
   to retiré (toe at the side/front of the knee), knee open to the side.
2. **Unfold devant:** the thigh lifts slightly, then the lower leg extends forward; heel
   leads, the foot finishes in a full point at the height you can hold with a level pelvis.
3. **A la seconde:** the knee stays back in line with the shoulder; the leg unfolds directly
   side. The hip of the working leg stays down.
4. **Derrière:** the knee opens behind into attitude line, then extends; the torso inclines
   slightly forward to keep the spine long, the pelvis opens only as much as the school
   allows.
5. **Sustain**, then lower the straight leg slowly to tendu and close 5th.

## Teaching points
- The height you hold is your height. A développé that kicks up and drops is a battement.
- Hamstrings must lengthen, hip flexors must not grip: think of the leg floating from
  underneath (the back of the thigh lifts it).
- Breathe; holding breath tightens the neck and shoulders.

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Hip hikes in 2nd | lower the leg; lengthen down the working side |
| Leg drops after unfolding | work lower, build sustain at 90 first |
| Knee drops before the unfold | lift the thigh first, then extend |
| Leaning back for height devant | stay vertical; height comes later |

## Verify
- [ ] Passes through a clear retiré with the knee open.
- [ ] Extension sustained at least 2 counts without dropping.
- [ ] Pelvis level and torso upright (front and side mirror).
- [ ] Controlled lowering to a clean 5th.
