# Partnered promenade

**Goal:** turn the supported dancer slowly on pointe in arabesque or attitude by walking
around her, so the line is shown to the whole house.

## Supported dancer
- Find balance on pointe first, then hold the position absolutely still.
- Keep the supporting leg straight and pulled up; let the partner rotate her, do not "help"
  by twisting.
- Keep the free arm in the set position and the head consistent.

## Partner
1. **Hold:** hand-in-hand (her hand over his, his palm up) or hand at the waist. Arm firm but
   not stiff - a stable post she can rest weight against minimally.
2. **Keep her on her axis:** the hand stays directly above her supporting leg or slightly in
   front; pulling sideways tips her off pointe.
3. **Walk:** small, even steps around her in a circle, staying the same distance, turning her
   via the hand or waist. Speed set by the music; do not rush the last quarter.
4. **Finish:** stop her facing the agreed direction, then into the next step (penché, release
   to balance, or lowering).

## Waist promenade
- Both hands at the waist (or one at the waist, one holding her hand). Used for attitude
  promenade; requires closer stance.

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| She comes off pointe | partner too far or pulling sideways - step in, hand above her axis |
| Jerky rotation | smaller, continuous steps |
| Her position changes during the turn | she holds; he does the turning |

## Verify
- [ ] Full circle on pointe with the position unchanged.
- [ ] Even tempo matched to the music.
- [ ] Clean finish on the agreed direction.
