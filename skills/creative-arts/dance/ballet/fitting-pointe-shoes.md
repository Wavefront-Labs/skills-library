# Fitting pointe shoes

**Goal:** a shoe that supports the foot over the box and matches the dancer's toe shape,
width and arch. Always fit with an experienced fitter, and have the teacher approve the first
pair before ribbons are sewn.

## Anatomy of the shoe
- **Box:** the hard toe enclosure; varies in width and taper.
- **Platform:** the flat end the dancer stands on.
- **Vamp:** the top of the box over the toes (length and height).
- **Shank:** the sole stiffener; strength (soft/medium/hard) and length (3/4 or full).
- **Throat/wings/heel:** opening shape, side support, heel fit.

## Fitting steps
1. **Foot shape:** Egyptian (big toe longest - tapered box), Greek (second toe longest - may
   need a pad or a shoe with a pointed box), square/Roman (toes even - square box).
2. **Width:** toes flat, not squeezed on top of each other; no gaping at the sides.
3. **Length (standing flat in 2nd plié):** toes just touching the end, no curling; a shoe
   that is too long lets the foot slide down and the toes jam.
4. **Vamp:** should cover the toe joints when en pointe; long toes need a longer vamp.
5. **Shank:** strong enough that the dancer is lifted out of the shoe, flexible enough that
   she can get fully over the box. Strong arches/feet can usually take softer shanks only if
   the box supports.
6. **On pointe:** the dancer should be over the box, the platform flat on the floor, no
   knuckling of the toes, heel not slipping excessively, no excess fabric at the heel.

## Fit faults
| Fault | Risk |
|---|---|
| Box too narrow | bunions, corns, toenail damage |
| Box too wide or shoe too long | foot sinks, toes jam, ankle instability |
| Shank too hard for the foot | dancer stays behind the box, sickles |
| Vamp too short | toes knuckle over the top |

## Gotchas
- Toe pads add volume; fit with the pads the dancer will use.
- Brands and makers differ widely; even the same model varies between makers at Freed.
- Feet grow and change: re-fit, don't reorder the same size blindly for growing dancers.

## Verify
- [ ] Toes flat, just touching in plié.
- [ ] Over the box en pointe with a flat platform.
- [ ] Teacher approves before sewing.
