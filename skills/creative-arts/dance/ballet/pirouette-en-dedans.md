# Pirouette en dedans

**Goal:** turn inward (towards the supporting leg) on one leg, usually from a 4th
preparation, with the working leg arriving in retiré as the turn begins.

## Method
1. **Preparation:** 4th position, front leg in demi-plié carrying most of the weight, back leg
   stretched (common in RAD/Cecchetti) or in plié. Arms: the arm on the front-leg side in 1st,
   the other in 2nd (or as set).
2. **Rise:** push up onto high demi-pointe on the front (supporting) leg. The back leg sweeps
   (through a small dégagé à la seconde in many schools) and closes to retiré as the body
   turns towards the supporting leg.
3. **Arms:** the 2nd arm closes to 1st to meet the other; no big wind.
4. **Spot** front, finish in 4th or 5th demi-plié behind as set.

## Differences from en dehors
- The supporting leg is the front leg of the preparation - the dancer turns into it.
- Dancers tend to over-rotate the hips into the turn: keep the hips square at the push.
- The working leg's retiré must arrive quickly; a late retiré makes the turn wobble.

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Hips turn before the rise | square the pelvis, rise straight up |
| Weight behind (on the back leg) in prep | 70-80 percent of weight on the front leg |
| Retiré arrives late or low | snap the leg up as the heel leaves the floor |
| Supporting knee soft on demi-pointe | full straight leg, rise held |

## Verify
- [ ] Clean single en dedans from 4th on both sides.
- [ ] Retiré in place on the first quarter of the turn.
- [ ] Controlled finish in the set position.
