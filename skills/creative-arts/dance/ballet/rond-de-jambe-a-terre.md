# Rond de jambe à terre

**Goal:** mobilise the hip joint in rotation, the leg circling in the socket while the pelvis
is a fixed frame.

## Method
1. **Preparation:** typically a demi-plié with the foot extending tendu devant, then
   straightening to tendu à la seconde (the "preparation" of 2 or 4 counts).
2. **En dehors (outward):** front - side - back, the toe tracing a half circle on the floor,
   then the foot passes through 1st position (flat heel down, both legs straight) to start
   again at the front.
3. **En dedans (inward):** back - side - front, through 1st to the back.
4. The working foot keeps full point at the extreme points; it passes through demi-pointe on
   the way into 1st.
5. **Finish:** usually a port de bras forward and back, or a balance with the leg in
   tendu/fondu.

## Teaching points
- The size of the circle equals the reach of a tendu in each direction - not bigger.
- Heel forward the whole way round; at the back the toes lead.
- Use a "demi-rond" (front to side only) for beginners before full circles.

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Hip swings to follow the leg | shorter circle; think of the pelvis as a still bowl |
| Leg loses rotation passing through back | press the heel down and forward at the back |
| Skipping 1st position | insist on the pass through flat 1st |
| Supporting knee bent | keep the supporting leg pulled up and rotated |

## Verify
- [ ] Pelvis level and square throughout (mirror at front).
- [ ] Passes through a true 1st each circle.
- [ ] Even tempo; circle shape consistent en dehors and en dedans.
