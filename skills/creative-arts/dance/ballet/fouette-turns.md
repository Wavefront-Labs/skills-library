# Fouetté turns

**Goal:** a series of turns on one leg, each powered by the working leg whipping out to 2nd
and into retiré while the supporting leg pliés and rises - famously 32 in the Black Swan coda.

## Mechanics of one fouetté
1. **Plié** on the supporting leg (heel down), working leg extending forward (devant).
2. The leg opens to **2nd** at about 90 degrees (or 45 degrees) as the supporting leg rises to
   relevé/pointe. The arms open to 2nd.
3. The leg **whips into retiré** behind then front of the knee as the body turns one full
   rotation; arms close to 1st.
4. Land back into plié facing front; repeat.

Laws' physics: the leg extended to 2nd stores angular momentum while the body pauses facing
front; drawing it into retiré transfers it to the body's turn.

## Teaching progression
1. Fouetté action at the barre without turning (plié-devant, relevé-2nd, retiré).
2. Quarter turns, half turns, then singles from a pirouette preparation.
3. 4, 8, 16, 32; add doubles (on every other or every fourth) once singles stay on the spot.

## Staying on the spot
- Spot the front every turn; the head is the brake.
- Supporting heel down in each plié; the knee over toes.
- The working leg goes exactly to 2nd, not forward, or the dancer travels forward.

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Travelling forward | leg to true 2nd; weight over the supporting foot |
| Losing height of the leg | extension at the same height each turn |
| Supporting heel not lowering | full plié each turn; otherwise calves fatigue fast |
| Arms flapping | arms open with the leg, close with the retiré |

## Verify
- [ ] 16 singles finishing within a small circle of the starting spot.
- [ ] Even rhythm matching the music.
- [ ] Doubles added without travel.
