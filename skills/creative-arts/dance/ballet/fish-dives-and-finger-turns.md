# Fish dives and finger turns

**Goal:** two signature partnering moves. Fish dive: the ballerina dives head-down into the
partner's arms in a fish shape. Finger turn: she pirouettes holding his raised finger.

## Fish dive (poisson)
1. Usually from a supported pirouette or a lift: she finishes facing the audience side.
2. He catches her: one arm around her waist/hip, the other supporting her thigh or under the
   upper body, his knee bent (often lunging) to take her weight.
3. Her shape: back arched, head up, arms open, one leg hooked behind his thigh or waist (the
   "hook"), the other extended in a curved line. She holds the shape firmly.
4. He controls the drop; the dive is quick but the catch must be stable before she releases.

## Finger turn
1. He raises his index finger (or two fingers) above her head; she holds it lightly in her
   fist from below.
2. She pirouettes under the finger on her own axis; his finger is a guide for alignment, not
   a crank.
3. He keeps the hand directly above her supporting leg; moving it pulls her off.
4. He stops her by catching her waist with the other hand on the agreed count.

## Safety and signals
- Fish dives must be learned low (from a standing position) before from height.
- Agree the signal for "abort": she keeps her legs under her and he catches the waist.
- Sweaty hands: rosin, or wipe before the pas de deux.

## Verify
- [ ] Fish shape held with the hook and arched back, catch stable before release.
- [ ] Finger turns with her axis vertical under his hand.
- [ ] Both know the abort signal.
