# Rond de jambe en l'air

**Goal:** an oval traced by the lower leg from a thigh fixed in 2nd. Trains the strength of
the turned-out thigh and quick, light lower-leg action used in allegro (e.g. in pas de
chat variants, fouettés and gargouillade).

## Method
1. Raise the leg à la seconde to 45 degrees (lower grades) or 90 degrees (advanced). The thigh
   does not move from now on.
2. **En dehors:** the foot travels from 2nd inward towards the calf of the supporting leg
   (behind, then front of the oval) and back out to 2nd - an oval, not a circle. The foot
   reaches roughly the side of the supporting calf for 45 degree ronds, the knee for 90.
3. **En dedans:** reverse direction - front of the oval first.
4. **Accent:** usually out to 2nd, with the leg fully stretched for a split moment.
5. Doubles: two ovals in the time of one, still finishing with a stretched leg.

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Thigh drops or moves | lower the height until the thigh can be held |
| Circle instead of oval | the inward point is close to the supporting leg, the outward point is 2nd |
| Hip hikes | lengthen the working side down |
| Sickled foot at the inside point | keep the heel forward throughout |

## Gotchas
- It is a knee-joint action on a fixed thigh; dancers who swing the whole leg strain the hip
  flexors and never build the needed strength.
- Teach at 45 degrees first, with the hand on the barre and a slow tempo.

## Verify
- [ ] Thigh motionless (watch the knee in the mirror).
- [ ] Oval shape, accent and stretch in 2nd.
- [ ] Hips level, supporting leg straight and rotated.
