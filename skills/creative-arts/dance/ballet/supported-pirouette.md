# Supported pirouettes

**Goal:** a pirouette where the woman (or supported dancer) provides her own turn and axis,
and the partner centres, adds rotation if needed, and finishes the turn precisely.

## Supported dancer
1. Prepare as for a solo pirouette (usually 4th), weight over the front foot.
2. Rise and turn on her own axis, pulled up, arms in 1st or 5th; do not lean back on the
   partner's hands.
3. Spot as usual; finish in the agreed position (often a held retiré or into attitude/arabesque).

## Partner
1. **Distance:** stand close enough to hold the waist with bent arms; too far makes her lean
   back, too close blocks her retiré.
2. **Hands:** fingers loosely around her waist above the hips, thumbs on the back - a ring
   she turns inside, not a grip.
3. **Placing:** before the turn, place her on her supporting leg (on pointe, over the box).
4. **During:** keep her vertical; if she falls off axis, correct gently towards vertical.
5. **Adding turns:** brush with the hands to add rotation on the second turn - alternating
   hands, timed, not shoving.
6. **Stopping:** hands close on the waist at the agreed point; she must stop facing front,
   still on pointe, often then dropping into a fish or a balance.

## Communication
- Agree in advance: number of turns, finish position, signal for an extra turn.
- The supported dancer reports whether she wants more or less help; partner respects it.

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| She leans back into his hands | stay over the supporting leg; partner closer |
| Partner grips (turn stops) | open ring of fingers |
| Turn ends off front | partner marks the stop and controls the final quarter |
| Partner standing too far | step in until his elbows are bent |

## Verify
- [ ] Doubles/triples with her axis vertical throughout.
- [ ] Clean stop facing front on the count.
- [ ] Both dancers can describe the agreed signals.
