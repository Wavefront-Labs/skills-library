# Assessing pointe readiness

**Goal:** put students on pointe when their body and technique can cope, not by birthday
alone. Readiness is a teacher decision informed by objective tests and, where available, a
dance physiotherapist.

## Traditional minimums (not sufficient alone)
- Usually not before about 11-12 years old (IADMS cites skeletal maturity concerns before this).
- Several years of regular ballet training, typically at least 3-4 classes a week at the time
  of starting pointe.
- Not during a rapid growth spurt, after recent injury, or with pain in feet/ankles.

## Functional tests (examples from dance-medicine literature)
| Test | What you look for |
|---|---|
| **Single-leg relevé (repetitions)** | 16-25 controlled relevés on each leg on flat, full height, no sickle or rolling |
| **Airplane test** | standing on one leg, tip forward to a T and back x5 with level pelvis and knee over foot |
| **Sauté test** | 16 single-leg sautés with good alignment, pointed foot in the air, controlled landings |
| **Topple test** | pirouette preparation and single en dehors turn with good placement and finish |
| **Plantar flexion range** | can align the top of the foot over the toes; knee and ankle line straight on demi-pointe |
| **Core control** | e.g. hold a plank/side plank with level pelvis |

## Red flags (delay pointe)
- Weak ankles that sickle or roll in on demi-pointe.
- Hypermobility without strength control.
- Persistent pain, recent fracture or sprain.
- Inconsistent attendance or poor placement at barre.

## Phasing in
1. First pointe classes at the barre only, 10-15 minutes at the end of class, facing the barre.
2. Rises in 1st and 2nd, échappés, then relevés on one leg, then centre over months.
3. Teacher checks the shoes' fit before first use (see
   `creative-arts.dance.ballet.fitting-pointe-shoes`).

## Verify
- [ ] Student meets age/training minimums and passes functional tests on both sides.
- [ ] No red flags; parents informed of the phased plan.
- [ ] Pointe time increased gradually with re-checks.
