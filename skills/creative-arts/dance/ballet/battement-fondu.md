# Battement fondu

**Goal:** a smooth single-leg plié coordinated with the working leg folding and unfolding.
It trains the supporting leg strength and timing needed to land jumps softly on one leg.

## Method
1. **Start:** working foot sur le cou-de-pied (RAD: wrapped or "conditional" position
   depending on syllabus; Cecchetti: cou-de-pied front with the heel forward).
2. **Fondu:** the supporting leg bends in demi-plié as the working foot stays at the ankle.
3. **Extend:** both legs straighten **at exactly the same time**, the working leg unfolding
   to tendu/45 degrees/90 degrees as set, the supporting leg arriving straight just as the
   working leg arrives.
4. **Return:** bend both legs together, the working foot closing back to cou-de-pied.
5. Variations: fondu with relevé, fondu with demi-rond, double fondu.

## Teaching points
- Melting quality: "fondu" means melting. No sharp accents unless the set exercise says so.
- Working knee stays out to the side as the foot comes in; the thigh does not drop.
- On fondu with relevé, the rise happens as the legs extend, not after.

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Legs arrive at different times | count both legs together: "bend-bend, stretch-stretch" |
| Supporting knee falls inward | turnout from the supporting hip, knee over toes |
| Working foot sickles at the ankle | heel forward in cou-de-pied |
| Sitting in the supporting hip | lift up out of the supporting side |

## Verify
- [ ] Both legs extend and bend simultaneously.
- [ ] Supporting knee over toes in plié.
- [ ] Smooth, continuous quality at the set tempo.
