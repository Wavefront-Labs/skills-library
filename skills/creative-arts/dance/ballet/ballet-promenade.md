# Promenade (tour lent)

**Goal:** turn slowly on the spot on a flat supporting foot while holding an adage position
(arabesque, attitude or 2nd) completely unchanged - a test of balance and placement.

## Method
1. Establish the position fully - weight forward over the ball of the supporting foot, hips
   placed, arms set.
2. Turn by **tiny lifts and replacements of the supporting heel** (heel moves a few
   centimetres each time), keeping the ball of the foot as the pivot. The heel barely leaves
   the floor; the knee stays straight.
3. Keep the rhythm even: many small shifts, not a few big jerks. A quarter turn per 1-2 counts
   is typical in syllabus work.
4. The working leg, arms and head do not change shape. The eyes travel with the body (no
   spotting - it is slow).
5. Finish facing the set direction, then continue the adage (e.g. into a fondu or close).

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Position drops as the turn starts | establish and hold before turning |
| Large heel jumps | smaller, quicker heel shifts |
| Supporting knee bends with each shift | keep the leg pulled up, move only the heel |
| Weight back on the heel | weight stays over the ball of foot |

## Gotchas
- Partnered promenade is a different skill (partner walks round, dancer stays on pointe or
  demi-pointe): see `creative-arts.dance.ballet.partnered-promenade`.
- Practise at the barre first, turning a quarter with one hand on the barre.

## Verify
- [ ] Full circle with the position unchanged.
- [ ] Even rhythm of small heel shifts.
- [ ] Ends on the correct direction.
