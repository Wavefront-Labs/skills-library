# Demi and grand plié

**Goal:** bend and stretch the knees in turnout with the pelvis still, the knees tracking over
the toes and the movement never stopping. The plié is the spring for every jump and the
cushion for every landing.

## Method
1. **Set up:** stand in your functional turnout (see
   `creative-arts.dance.ballet.turnout-and-placement-at-the-barre`), weight over all three
   points of each foot, hand lightly on the barre.
2. **Demi-plié:** bend until the Achilles tendon is fully stretched with heels still on the
   floor. Knees open outward over the 2nd/3rd toe. Descent and rise take the same time, with
   no pause at the bottom.
3. **Grand plié (1st, 4th, 5th):** pass through demi-plié, then the heels release only as far
   as necessary; thighs stay rotated, spine stays vertical. On the way up the heels press down
   as early as possible, passing back through demi-plié.
4. **Grand plié in 2nd:** heels stay on the floor throughout; stop when the thighs are roughly
   horizontal, or earlier if the pelvis starts to tip.
5. **Arms:** typical RAD/Cecchetti pattern: the arm lowers through bras bas as you descend,
   lifts through 1st and opens to 2nd on the rise. Eyes follow the hand.

## Counts
A classic grand plié takes 4 counts down and 4 up (2 and 2 for demi). Keep the tempo even:
continuous like a lift, never a drop.

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Knees fall in front of toes | reduce foot turnout until knees track over toes |
| Sitting at the bottom of grand plié | keep pulling up through the torso; rebound immediately |
| Heels lifting early in demi | release the ankles, do not force depth |
| Bottom sticking out or tucking | neutral pelvis; spine lengthens down towards the floor |
| Bouncing at the bottom | slow legato tempo; no ballistic loading of the knee |

## Gotchas
- Deep grand pliés in 5th load the knee heavily; teachers often omit them for young or injured
  dancers. That is correct practice.
- A pliable Achilles matters more than depth: a shallow demi-plié limits every jump.
- Grand plié in 4th: both heels release under control, weight centred between the feet.

## Verify
- [ ] Knees over toes at the deepest point (side and front mirror).
- [ ] Heels down throughout demi-plié and in grand plié in 2nd.
- [ ] No pause or bounce at the bottom; equal down and up timing.
- [ ] Pelvis level and still.
