# Preparing for RAD exams

**Goal:** students who know the set work cold, understand the marking criteria, and present
calmly on the day. Always use the **current exam specification** from the RAD website; details
(content, timings, dress) change between syllabus revisions.

## Choose the assessment type
- **Graded exam:** marked against technique, music and performance criteria, results
  graded (e.g. Pass, Merit, Distinction bands).
- **Class award:** taken as a group with the teacher present; good for students who need
  confidence.
- **Solo performance award:** dances only.
- **Vocational exams** (Intermediate Foundation onwards) require the student to meet the
  prerequisite and are examined in more detail.

## Timeline
1. Learn the syllabus over the year; aim to know all set work at least 2-3 months before entry.
2. Submit entries by the deadline for your region's session; candidates' ages and
   prerequisite exams are checked.
3. Last 6 weeks: mock exams with someone the students don't know, in exam order and timings.

## In class
- Use the official RAD music, at the correct tempi.
- Teach the free/unseen elements as well as set exercises (where the level requires).
- Coach the marking criteria: correct technique, musicality, performance (projection, épaulement).

## On the day
- Dress code per specification (leotard colour by level, hair in a bun, no jewellery).
- Arrive early, warm up properly (the exam warm-up is not the barre).
- Teach students to keep going after a mistake; stopping costs more than the error.

## Verify
- [ ] Current specification checked for the level.
- [ ] Mock exam run in correct order with official music.
- [ ] Entries submitted with prerequisites confirmed.
