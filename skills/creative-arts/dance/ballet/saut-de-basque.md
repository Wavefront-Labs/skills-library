# Saut de basque

**Goal:** a travelling turning jump: the body turns once in the air with one leg extended
then folded, the other in retiré, landing on the first leg.

## Method
1. **Preparation:** often a chassé or a step-coupé; the last step is onto the take-off leg in
   demi-plié, opening the body in the direction of travel.
2. **Throw:** the free leg brushes through 1st and throws forward (grand battement) in the
   direction of travel as the body begins to turn.
3. **Air:** the take-off leg folds into retiré in front of the knee of the thrown leg (which
   folds or stays as the style requires); the body turns once.
4. **Land** on the throwing leg in fondu, the other foot in retiré or cou-de-pied, then continue.

## Teaching points
- The turn is led by the throwing leg and the arm opening, not by twisting the shoulders early.
- Keep the axis vertical; lean comes only in manège.
- Spot the direction of travel.

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| No height | deeper plié on the take-off step, push up before turning |
| Retiré foot not placed | snap the foot to the knee as you leave the floor |
| Under-rotated landing | arms and head lead the turn |
| Heavy landing | land through the foot into fondu |

## Verify
- [ ] Full rotation in the air with retiré visible.
- [ ] Lands on the throwing leg facing the line of travel.
- [ ] Series travels in a straight line or a clean circle.
