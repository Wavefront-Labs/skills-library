# Balance and control in adage

**Goal:** sustain positions on one leg (flat and on demi-pointe) in slow centre work without
wobbling, and exit balances as deliberately as you entered them.

## Method
1. **Place first, balance second:** the balance is a consequence of correct placement. Over
   the ball of the supporting foot, hips level, ribs closed, shoulders over hips.
2. **Axis:** imagine the line from the crown down through the supporting leg into the floor.
   The working leg and arms balance each other around it.
3. **Rising (relevé) into a balance:** keep the position and push the floor away; the heel
   lifts straight up so the weight lands over the 2nd/3rd toes on demi-pointe - no sickling.
4. **Corrections:** small pressure changes through the toes and ankle, not arm-flapping or
   hip shifts.
5. **Exit:** lower the heel or the leg under control on the counted beat, close or continue.

## Training
- Rises on one leg at the barre, then letting go for 2, 4, 8 counts.
- Eyes closed balances on flat (low risk) to train proprioception.
- Single-leg ankle strengthening (theraband, heel raises) supports demi-pointe balance and
  reduces ankle sprain risk.

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Weight on the heel | shift forward until the heel is light |
| Sickling on demi-pointe | weight over the 2nd/3rd toe |
| Gripping toes | spread toes, press the metatarsals down |
| Holding breath | breathe; tension shakes |

## Verify
- [ ] Balance held for the set counts in each adage position.
- [ ] Relevé lands in a stable, unsickled demi-pointe.
- [ ] Controlled exit on the beat.
