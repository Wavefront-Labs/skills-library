# Attitude

**Goal:** the bent-leg extension where the curve of the leg continues the curve of the arm
above - one of the signature classical lines.

## Attitude derrière
1. Leg lifted behind with the knee bent at roughly 90-120 degrees (school dependent).
2. **The knee is at least as high as the foot** - ideally higher. A dropped knee kills the line.
3. Rotation: the knee faces out to the side, the foot is on or slightly above the knee line,
   not sickled.
4. Arm on the working side usually in 5th (en haut), the other in 2nd; spine lifted, with
   slight forward inclination as height increases.

## Attitude devant
- Knee lifted in front, lower leg curved so the foot is in line with or slightly crossing the
  midline, heel pressed forward to keep rotation. Shallower bend than derrière.

## Croisé and effacé
- **Attitude croisée (derrière):** the working leg is the upstage leg, line crosses; the head
  and arm frame the audience side.
- **Attitude effacée:** the downstage leg works; the line opens to the audience.

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Knee lower than the foot | lift from under the thigh; reduce height first |
| Foot sickles behind | heel forward, rotate from the hip |
| Torso tips forward excessively | lift sternum; lower the leg slightly |
| Hip of working leg hikes in devant | working hip down, lower the leg |

## Verify
- [ ] Knee level with or above the foot in attitude derrière.
- [ ] Rotated leg, unsickled foot.
- [ ] Correct croisé/effacé orientation and arm frame.
