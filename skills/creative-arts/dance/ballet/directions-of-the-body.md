# Directions of the body

**Goal:** place the body on the correct diagonal relative to the audience so the line reads -
the difference between an exercise and a classical pose.

## Room numbering (Cecchetti)
Cecchetti numbers the four corners 1-4 and the four walls 5-8; Vaganova numbers all eight
points 1-8 clockwise starting from the front wall (audience). The same call ("go to 2") points
somewhere different in each system, so **always confirm which numbering the class uses**.

## The eight directions (Cecchetti)
| Direction | Body facing | Working leg |
|---|---|---|
| Croisé devant | diagonal | front leg crosses the body line (the legs appear crossed from the audience) |
| A la quatrième devant | en face | straight front |
| Écarté (devant) | diagonal | leg to 2nd towards the downstage corner, body opened |
| Effacé (devant) | diagonal | front leg is the downstage one, line open |
| A la seconde | en face | to the side |
| Épaulé | diagonal, arabesque line with the downstage shoulder forward | back leg |
| A la quatrième derrière | en face | straight back |
| Croisé derrière | diagonal | back leg crosses the body line |

(Écarté derrière is in other schools; Cecchetti's eighth is épaulé.)

## Finding them fast
- **Croisé:** from 5th on the diagonal, the leg nearest the audience is the front leg -> the
  line crosses. **Effacé:** the leg farthest from the audience is in front -> the line opens.
- Head generally turns towards the downstage shoulder in croisé devant and effacé.

## Verify
- [ ] Dancer can show all eight from a single 5th on a called direction.
- [ ] Correct room numbering for the class's school.
- [ ] From the front, croisé reads crossed and effacé reads open.
