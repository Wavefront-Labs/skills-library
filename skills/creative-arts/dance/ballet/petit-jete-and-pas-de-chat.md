# Petit jeté and pas de chat

**Goal:** two staple petit allegro jumps: jeté transfers weight from one leg to the other
through the air; pas de chat draws both legs up under the body like a cat.

## Petit jeté (dessus/dessous)
1. From 5th demi-plié, the working foot brushes out to 2nd just off the floor (glissé).
2. Push off the supporting leg; spring towards the working leg.
3. Land on the working leg in demi-plié (fondu), the other foot arriving sur le cou-de-pied
   (front for dessus, back for dessous).
4. Series of jetés alternate legs; travel is slight.

## Pas de chat
1. From 5th demi-plié (back foot usually starting), lift the back foot to retiré (or a low
   raccourci) and spring sideways.
2. The second leg follows to retiré so both legs are drawn up under the body at the top.
3. Land on the first leg in demi-plié, the second closing quickly to 5th front.
4. Head and épaulement towards the direction of travel; light, playful quality.
- Variants: Russian (Vaganova) pas de chat with développé of the back leg; Italian pas de chat
  in some repertoire.

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Landing on a straight supporting leg | land through the foot into fondu |
| Cou-de-pied sloppy after jeté | foot wrapped and placed as in barre frappé work |
| Pas de chat legs unequal | both knees reach the same height at the top |
| Hitting the knees together | knees open to the side (turned out) |

## Verify
- [ ] One-leg landings soft, heel down, knee over toes.
- [ ] Clear cou-de-pied position after each jeté.
- [ ] Both legs visibly drawn up in pas de chat.
