# The five positions of the feet

**Goal:** place the feet precisely in every position so every step starts and ends cleanly,
using turnout the dancer actually owns.

| Position | Placement |
|---|---|
| **1st** | heels touching, feet turned out in a line |
| **2nd** | feet in a line, heels about one and a half of the dancer's own foot lengths apart |
| **3rd** | heel of the front foot against the middle (arch) of the back foot; used for young children and in some character work |
| **4th** | one foot in front of the other, about one foot's length apart. **Opposite (crossed) 4th**: heel of front foot in line with toe of back foot (Cecchetti/RAD standard). **Open 4th**: front heel in line with back heel (1st opened forward) |
| **5th** | front heel against the toe joint of the back foot (fully crossed), weight equally on both feet |

## Method
1. Find functional turnout in 1st (rotate from the hips; feet go where the knees allow).
2. For each position check: knees over toes, weight on three points of each foot, pelvis
   neutral, weight equally on both feet.
3. 5th: use an honest 5th - feet crossed to the point the knees still track the toes. A forced
   tight 5th rolls the arches.

## Gotchas
- In RAD lower grades, 3rd may replace 5th until turnout and strength allow 5th.
- Children: a "90-degree" turnout (each foot 45 degrees) is a perfectly good 1st.
- The width of 2nd and 4th is relative to the dancer's own foot, so it changes between
  dancers.

## Verify
- [ ] All five positions with knees over toes and weight centred.
- [ ] 4th is the crossed (opposite) 4th unless the exercise says open.
- [ ] No rolling in on any position.
