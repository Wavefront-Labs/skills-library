# Batterie

**Goal:** beaten jumps where the legs open slightly and the calves strike in the air. Beats
must be visible, so they are made from the thigh with the legs fully turned out.

## Mechanics
- The legs open a little (about a foot's width) and close, striking calf against calf, the
  front leg changing as counted. The beat is the closing, not the opening.
- Full rotation is essential: the calves can only meet if the legs are turned out from the hip.
- Height: you need enough elevation for the beats; a good demi-plié and push first.

## Main beats
| Step | Count |
|---|---|
| **Royale** (changement battu) | from 5th, beat the legs with the front leg still in front, then change and land with the other in front |
| **Entrechat quatre** | from 5th (right front), open, beat with right behind, open, land right in front - legs cross twice, no change |
| **Entrechat trois/cinq** | land on one leg, the other foot at cou-de-pied |
| **Entrechat six** | three crossings, land changed |
| **Brisé** | travelling: the working leg brushes out, beats behind/in front of the other in the air, lands in 5th (brisé dessus/dessous); brisé volé lands on one foot alternating front and back |

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Beats from the knees (bent legs) | straight legs, beat from the thigh |
| Legs open too wide | small opening, fast closing |
| Blurry count (no clear crossings) | practise beats at the barre as petits battements and sautés battus slowly |
| Landing open | close in 5th on landing |

## Training
- At the barre, facing it: jump in 1st, then small changements beaten holding the barre.
- Petits battements sur le cou-de-pied and frappés for lower-leg speed.

## Verify
- [ ] Each crossing visible from the front.
- [ ] Royale and entrechat quatre distinct (change vs no change at landing).
- [ ] Clean 5th landings, heels down.
