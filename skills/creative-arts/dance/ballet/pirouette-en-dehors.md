# Pirouette en dehors

**Goal:** turn outward (away from the supporting leg) on one leg in retiré, cleanly prepared,
spotted and finished.

## Method
1. **Preparation from 4th (crossed):** weight mostly over the front leg, back leg in plié too,
   front arm in 1st, other in 2nd. Both heels down in plié.
2. **Push and rise:** push from both legs; the back leg (working leg) snaps to retiré, the
   supporting leg rises straight up to high demi-pointe in one action.
3. **Arms:** the 2nd arm closes to meet the 1st arm in a rounded 1st - the closing provides
   the turning impulse; it is not a big swing.
4. **Spot:** the head stays on the front until the last moment, whips round and finds the
   front again (see `creative-arts.dance.ballet.spotting`).
5. **Finish:** land in 4th (or 5th) in demi-plié with control, arms opening, holding the
   finish for a beat.

From 5th: the preparation is demi-plié in 5th, the front (or back) foot goes to retiré.

## Mechanics that matter
- Turning force comes from the push of the legs plus the arm closing; overturning the
  shoulders at preparation makes the turn wobble.
- Keep the axis vertical; the retiré knee stays open to the side and the foot connected at
  the knee.
- Doubles: same turn, higher relevé and a faster spot - not more force.

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Falling out of the turn forward | weight over the supporting ball of foot, not ahead |
| Shoulders wind up in the prep | square shoulders in prep; arms provide the impulse |
| Low or sinking relevé | full rise and hold it through the turn |
| Retiré knee comes forward | rotate the working thigh, knee to the side |
| Landing on a straight leg | finish in demi-plié, back foot placed |

## Verify
- [ ] Clean single from both 4th and 5th, both sides.
- [ ] Holds the finish for a full count without hopping.
- [ ] Double with the same shape and spot.
