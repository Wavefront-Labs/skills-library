# Sautés, changements and échappés

**Goal:** the basic two-foot jumps that teach push-off, a stretched body in the air and soft,
controlled landings.

## Sauté (1st or 2nd)
1. Demi-plié with heels down, knees over toes.
2. Push through the whole foot: heel, ball, toes leave last. Legs and feet fully stretched in
   the air, in the same position.
3. Land toes - ball - heel into demi-plié; heels must touch down every time.
4. The demi-plié of the landing is the preparation of the next jump.

## Changement de pieds
- From 5th demi-plié, jump, the legs change in the air (the front foot finishes behind), land
  in 5th. Legs are fully stretched and close together in the air; change at the top.

## Échappé sauté
- From 5th, jump and land in 2nd (or 4th) in demi-plié, then jump and close in 5th (often
  changing feet). The legs open the same distance each side.

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Heels not touching on landing | full demi-plié, heels down - Achilles injury risk otherwise |
| Knees inside the toes on landing | less turnout, knees over toes |
| Unpointed feet in the air | push through the toes; practise rises and rises with points |
| Upper body bouncing | torso stays still; the legs do the work |
| Noisy landings | toe-ball-heel rolling through the foot |

## Gotchas
- Jumping on concrete or non-sprung floors increases load dramatically; count and limit
  jumps on poor floors.
- Children: limit repetitions; spread jump work through class.

## Verify
- [ ] Heels down on every landing.
- [ ] Feet pointed and legs straight in the air.
- [ ] Quiet landings, upper body still.
