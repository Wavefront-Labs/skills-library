# Reverence and curtain calls

**Goal:** close class and performances with the formal courtesy of ballet - set reverence in
class and exams, and a clean, rehearsed curtain call.

## Reverence in class and exams
- A set port de bras with a curtsey (women: step to the side, foot behind in a deep plié,
  head inclined) or a bow (men: step to the side, close, incline from the hips, arm gesture).
- Usually performed to the teacher/examiner, then to the pianist.
- In RAD exams, the révérence is a marked component of the exam presentation: it must be
  musical and shown with full épaulement.

## Curtain calls (stage)
1. **Order:** corps de ballet, then soloists, then principals; the leading couple last. The
   ballet master sets and rehearses it, usually after the dress rehearsal.
2. **Bows:** unison bows in groups; principals step forward, the man presents the woman, she
   curtseys, then they acknowledge each other, the conductor/orchestra pit and the company.
3. **Front cloth calls:** principals in front of the curtain, entering from the centre
   opening or the side.
4. **Flowers:** presented from the wings by a stage manager or child; the ballerina often
   gives one stem to her partner.
5. Keep it brisk and in character for the first call; stay in costume and composure.

## Verify
- [ ] Reverence performed with music and épaulement, acknowledging the pianist.
- [ ] Curtain call order rehearsed and known by every dancer.
- [ ] Bows in unison; principals' sequence agreed.
