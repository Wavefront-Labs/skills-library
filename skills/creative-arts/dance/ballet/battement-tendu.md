# Battement tendu

**Goal:** train the articulation of the foot and the rotation of the working leg while the
supporting leg and pelvis stay still.

## Method
1. From 5th (or 1st) the working foot slides along the floor: heel, then demi-pointe
   (metatarsals), then full point. The toes never leave the floor.
2. **Devant:** the heel leads forward and stays pressed forward so the foot does not sickle.
   Toes in line with the centre of the body.
3. **A la seconde:** to the side in line with the supporting heel; the knee faces the
   ceiling-side, not forward.
4. **Derrière:** toes lead back; pelvis stays square (some schools allow a slight forward
   inclination of the torso at full extension).
5. **Close:** reverse through demi-pointe and heel into a precise 5th. The closing is half
   the exercise.
6. **En croix:** front, side, back, side; the side closing alternates 5th front/back as set.

## Syllabus variations
Tendu with demi-plié on closing; tendu lowering the heel in 2nd; double-time tendus; tendu
relevé (lift and lower the heel at full point).

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Sickling at full point | heel forward, line through ankle to the 2nd toe |
| Working hip rising | lengthen down the working side; stop chasing distance |
| Supporting leg loses rotation | press supporting heel forward, rotate from the thigh |
| Weight shifts onto working leg | stay over the supporting ball of foot |
| Closing in 1st/3rd instead of 5th | slow the closings until clean |

## Gotchas
- Speed comes after articulation. Fast flat-foot tendus do nothing for jumps.
- Exact line of 2nd differs slightly between schools and bodies; work your real rotation.

## Verify
- [ ] The foot passes through demi-pointe on every exit and closing.
- [ ] Full point without sickle or toe-curling (check from the front).
- [ ] Pelvis and supporting leg unchanged throughout.
- [ ] Closing positions exact.
