# Preparing for Cecchetti exams

**Goal:** students who dance the Cecchetti method's set work with its stylistic precision and
can answer the theory the higher exams require. In the UK the method is examined by the ISTD
Cecchetti Classical Ballet faculty; in North America by the Cecchetti Council of America.

## What makes Cecchetti distinctive
- **Set daily exercises** (exercises for each day of the week in the higher grades) and set
  adages and allegros - the student must know them exactly.
- **Eight directions of the body** and Cecchetti room numbering (see
  `creative-arts.dance.ballet.directions-of-the-body`).
- **Cecchetti arm positions and port de bras** (the set ports de bras), its five arabesques.
- Emphasis on **balance, purity of line and logic of movement**; no exaggeration.
- Theory: definitions of steps, rules (e.g. which foot is front), and terminology in French.

## Preparation steps
1. Obtain the current ISTD (or CCA) syllabus outline and specification for the level.
2. Teach set exercises to the published music and tempi; check details against the manual.
3. Drill the theory questions orally across the term (terminology, positions, directions).
4. Mock exams in exam order; include unseen enchaînements where the level requires.
5. Enter through an ISTD-registered teacher by the session deadline.

## On the day
- Dress and hair per specification; neat, practical.
- Teach students to show the method's precision: correct épaulement in each direction, exact
  closings, clear arms.

## Verify
- [ ] Current specification and set exercises checked against the manual.
- [ ] Students answer theory questions for the level confidently.
- [ ] Mock exam run with the correct set exercises and music.
