# Rises, relevés and bourrées on pointe

**Goal:** get onto and off pointe safely and travel on pointe with tiny, even steps.

## Two ways onto pointe
- **Rise (roll through):** from flat, roll through demi-pointe to full pointe and back down
  the same way - RAD's foundational method; builds foot strength.
- **Relevé (spring):** from demi-plié, spring the feet slightly under the body onto pointe
  (Cecchetti/Italian style is known for springing), land on pointe with straight knees.

## Standing over the box
- Weight on the platform, centred; knees pulled up, kneecaps over toes.
- The heel lifts forward so the line runs knee - ankle - box. Not "on the knuckles", not
  sitting back behind the box.
- Abdominals and turnout active; standing on pointe is upward lift, not sinking.

## Progression
1. Rises in 1st and 2nd facing the barre; échappés to 2nd.
2. Relevés in 5th, sus-sous, then relevés on one leg (retiré, coupé).
3. Piqués onto a straight leg; then centre.

## Pas de bourrée couru (bourrées)
1. On pointe in a tight 5th (or parallel 1st), knees soft but legs very close.
2. Tiny, rapid alternating steps; the feet stay together so the dancer appears to glide.
3. Travel in any direction; arms and torso still.

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Knuckling over the box | stronger shank or more strength; vamp check |
| Sitting back/heel dropped | push over the box through the ankle |
| Bent knees on pointe | pull up; may be too much pointe too soon |
| Bourrées with legs apart | tight 5th, tiny steps, knees close |

## Verify
- [ ] Rolls through demi-pointe up and down with control.
- [ ] Stands over the box on straight legs.
- [ ] Bourrées travel smoothly with feet together.
