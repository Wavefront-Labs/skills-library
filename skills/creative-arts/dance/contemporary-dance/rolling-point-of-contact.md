# Rolling point of contact

**Goal:** the core skill of contact improvisation - two dancers keep a point of physical
contact that travels (rolls, slides) across their bodies, and the dance follows it.

## Method
1. **Start back to back or shoulder to shoulder,** standing or sitting. Find the point where the
   bodies touch.
2. **Roll it:** one dancer shifts slightly so the point travels - across the shoulder blade,
   down the arm, around the hip. The other follows to keep contact.
3. **Listen:** neither leads permanently; the point of contact itself suggests direction. Notice
   changes of pressure.
4. **Add weight:** gradually give more weight through the point; it begins to support and redirect.
5. **Change levels:** let the point lead you to the floor and up again.

## Exercises
- **Forehead-to-forehead walk:** keep the contact while moving through the space.
- **Rolling across the back:** one on all fours, the other rolls across their back keeping the
  point moving.
- **Hand dance:** palm to palm with eyes closed, then extend to forearms and shoulders.

## Teaching points
- Soft attention: eyes may be soft-focused or closed; information comes through touch.
- Contact is continuous but not constant pressure; it can be very light.
- Consent and check-ins before starting; any partner can stop at any time.

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| One partner always leading | alternate; then let the point decide |
| Losing contact | slow down |
| Pushing instead of sharing | give weight gradually, listen to response |

## Verify
- [ ] Contact maintained for a 2-minute duet with the point travelling.
- [ ] Both partners describe leading and following moments.
- [ ] Level changes achieved through the contact.
