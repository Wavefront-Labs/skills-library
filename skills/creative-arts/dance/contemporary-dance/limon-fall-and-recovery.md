# Fall and recovery (Limón)

**Goal:** movement that lives between balance and imbalance: give in to gravity (fall),
then recover with a rebound and suspension. Derived from Doris Humphrey's arc between two deaths.

## The arc
1. **Fall:** release the weight of a body part (head, arm, torso) or the whole body into
   gravity; the fall accelerates.
2. **Catch/rebound:** at the bottom the energy rebounds, like a ball - the movement reverses.
3. **Recovery and suspension:** the body rises past its starting point to a moment of
   suspension before the next fall.

## Exercises
- **Head falls:** drop the head forward, catch, rebound up; side to side.
- **Torso drops:** the spine releases forward from the hips with heavy weight, swings back up.
- **Arm drops:** lift the arm, release it from the shoulder, let it swing past and suspend.
- **Whole-body falls:** off-balance in a direction, catch with a step or lunge, recover.

## Teaching points
- Weight must be real - students who "place" the fall don't get the rebound.
- Breath supports it: exhale into the fall, inhale into the recovery/suspension.
- Successive flow: weight travels through joints one by one.

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Controlled lowering instead of a fall | release the weight, trust the catch |
| No rebound | let the momentum reverse naturally |
| Holding breath | breath shapes the phrase |

## Verify
- [ ] Visible acceleration in the fall and natural rebound.
- [ ] Suspension at the top before the next fall.
- [ ] Breath linked to the arc.
