# Cunningham back positions

**Goal:** the spine's five basic shapes in Cunningham technique, each clear and quickly
reachable, so the back can change direction as fast as the legs.

## The positions
| Position | Action |
|---|---|
| **Upright** | spine vertical and lifted, the neutral home |
| **Curve** | spine rounds forward from the upper back (thoracic), the pelvis stays still |
| **Arch** | spine lengthens and lifts up and back from the upper back, head continuing the line |
| **Twist** | rotation around the vertical axis, hips square |
| **Tilt** | the whole straight spine leans sideways (or forward) as a unit from the hips, one long line |

## Method
1. Start in upright, legs parallel or turned out as set.
2. Move into each position on a clear count, then back to upright: e.g. "curve 1, up 2, arch 3,
   up 4, twist 5, up 6, tilt 7, up 8".
3. Combine: curve while bending the knees; tilt with a leg extension in the opposite direction.

## Teaching points
- The curve starts high in the back; it is not a Graham contraction from the pelvis.
- The tilt keeps the spine straight; bending to the side is a different shape.
- Change from one position to another directly, at speed, without passing through upright
  when the exercise demands it.

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Curve from the pelvis | initiate in the upper back |
| Tilt becomes a side bend | keep one straight line from head to pelvis |
| Twist moves the hips | hips square; rotation above the waist |

## Verify
- [ ] Each position distinct and recognisable from the front and side.
- [ ] Clean transitions at the set tempo.
- [ ] Back positions coordinated with leg actions.
