# Kinesthetic listening in group improvisation

**Goal:** sense and respond to the whole group's movement - its timing, space and energy - so
an improvising ensemble moves as one organism or contrasts deliberately.

## Practices
1. **Soft focus:** relax the eyes to take in the whole room with peripheral vision; don't stare
   at one person.
2. **Flocking:** a group in a diamond or cluster follows whoever is at the front; when the group
   turns, the leader changes. Smooth handovers are the skill.
3. **Mirroring:** pairs face each other; one leads slowly, the other mirrors. Then no designated
   leader - both follow.
4. **Group start/stop:** without a signal, the group begins moving and stops together.
5. **Kinesthetic response (Viewpoints):** respond to a movement nearby immediately - contrast,
   join, or ignore as a choice.

## Ensemble choices
- **Join:** unison or similar movement.
- **Contrast:** opposite level, tempo or quality.
- **Space:** fill empty areas, leave room, create lines or clusters.
- **Stillness:** a powerful choice that frames others.

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Fixed focus on one person | soft focus, whole room |
| Laggy flocking handovers | slower tempo, clearer facings |
| Everyone does their own thing | name the relationship being chosen |

## Verify
- [ ] Group starts and stops together without a signal three times in a row.
- [ ] Flocking changes leaders smoothly.
- [ ] Dancers can name the relationship they chose at a given moment.
