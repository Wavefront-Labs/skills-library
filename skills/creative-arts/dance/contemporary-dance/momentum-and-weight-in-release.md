# Momentum and weight in release

**Goal:** dance with real weight and momentum so movement flows efficiently - redirecting
energy rather than stopping and starting it with muscle.

## Principles
1. **Drop and catch:** let a body part or the whole body fall, then catch it at the last
   moment with a step, plié or change of direction.
2. **Redirect, don't stop:** at the end of a swing the energy turns into the next movement
   (a curve, a spiral, a roll to the floor).
3. **Centrifugal force:** in turns, let limbs fly outward and use the pull; spiral down to the
   floor or up out of it.
4. **Off-balance travel:** lean the centre beyond the base of support and let the feet follow,
   like walking downhill.
5. **Pathways:** move along the most efficient route - curves rather than angles; through
   the floor rather than around it.

## Exercises
- **Swing-drop:** arm swing that drops the torso forward, catches in plié, swings up.
- **Falling walk:** tip forward and catch with a run, then decelerate through plié.
- **Spiral to floor:** turn on one foot, release into a spiral down, roll and rise.

## Safety
- Learn the catch before increasing the fall.
- Soft joints (knees, elbows) absorb force; never land on locked limbs.

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Muscle-led stops between movements | let one movement feed the next |
| Fear of falling (held body) | small falls first, build trust |
| Uncontrolled flinging | catch and redirect deliberately |

## Verify
- [ ] Phrase performed with visible flow and no dead stops.
- [ ] Falls caught smoothly.
- [ ] Turns use outward momentum and spiral into the next move.
