# Shoulder rolls

**Goal:** roll over the back diagonally across one shoulder - a safe alternative to rolling
over the head/neck - forward and backward, used constantly in contemporary floorwork.

## Backward shoulder roll
1. Sit, then roll back along the spine, legs folding over.
2. Turn the head to the side (away from the rolling shoulder) - the roll passes over the
   shoulder, **not the neck**.
3. Legs travel diagonally over that shoulder; knees land beside the ear or on the floor.
4. Come to kneeling or a lunge.

## Forward shoulder roll
1. From kneeling or a lunge, reach one arm diagonally under the body (thread the needle),
   placing the back of that shoulder on the floor.
2. Tuck the head away, roll diagonally across the back from shoulder to opposite hip.
3. Exit to sitting, standing or into the next movement.

## Progression
1. Rocking on the back in a ball.
2. Partial rolls with a spotter.
3. Full rolls slowly on mats.
4. On the dance floor at speed, then from standing.

## Safety
- Never roll directly over the head or cervical spine.
- Dancers with neck issues: substitute side rolls.

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Rolling over the neck | turn the head away; diagonal path |
| Bumping the spine | round the back, sequential contact |
| Stopping halfway | commit the momentum |

## Verify
- [ ] Rolls pass over the shoulder with the head turned aside.
- [ ] Smooth and silent, both sides.
- [ ] Exits into a standing or lunge position.
