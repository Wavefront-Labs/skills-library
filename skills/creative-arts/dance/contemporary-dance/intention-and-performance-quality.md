# Intention and performance quality

**Goal:** make each movement mean something to the performer and therefore read to an
audience - with clear focus, dynamics and presence rather than empty steps.

## Building intention
1. **Ask the choreographer** what each section is about: image, task, relationship, quality.
2. **Define per section:** where your **focus** is (inward, a partner, a point in space,
   the audience), the **dynamic** (Laban effort: sudden/sustained, strong/light, direct/
   indirect, bound/free), and an **inner image** or task.
3. **Score it:** write it in your notebook next to the counts.

## Presence without presenting
- Contemporary work rarely wants the "show face" of commercial dance. Neutral face, active
  attention.
- Eyes are part of the choreography: a fixed gaze, a soft gaze, eyes closed - decide them.
- Stillness must stay alive: breath and attention continue.

## Consistency
- Warm up into the intention (not just the body).
- Run sections with the inner score, even in marking rehearsals.
- After shows, note what drifted.

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Executing steps with no focus | assign a focus point and image |
| Same dynamic throughout | map effort changes in the score |
| Over-acting | trust the movement; less facial "emotion" |

## Verify
- [ ] Written score of focus, dynamic and image for each section.
- [ ] Rehearsal director sees clear differences between sections.
- [ ] Consistency across two consecutive runs.
