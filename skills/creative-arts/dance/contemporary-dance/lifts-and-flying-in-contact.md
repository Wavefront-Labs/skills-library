# Lifts and flying in contact

**Goal:** improvised lifts where the flyer is carried on the base's back, hips or shoulders,
built from rolling point of contact and weight sharing rather than set choreography.

## Base
- **Table:** on all fours or in a squat with a flat back; the back is a surface to roll over.
- **Pelvis power:** lifts come from the legs and pelvis beneath the flyer's centre, never the
  lower back alone.
- **Get under:** to lift, bring your pelvis under the flyer's centre of gravity (their pelvis).
- Keep knees soft and the spine long; release the flyer if overloaded.

## Flyer
- **Offer the centre:** place your pelvis over the base's centre (e.g. on the back of their
  hips); then let the arms and legs extend.
- **Stay active:** keep the body toned and spiralling; floppy flyers are heavy and unsafe.
- **Look for the floor:** have an exit path; reach for the floor with hands as you come down.

## Progressions
1. Rolling across the partner's back on all fours.
2. Flyer over the base's back in a table, base slowly rising to a squat.
3. Shoulder lifts from a lunge.
4. Dynamic lifts from a run (experienced pairs only).

## Safety
- Agree before: "no lifts" is always an option. Either partner can bail.
- Lower back pain = stop; check the base is under the flyer.
- Jams: be aware of other pairs; avoid flying near walls or others.

## Verify
- [ ] Base gets pelvis under the flyer's centre before rising.
- [ ] Flyer stays toned with a clear exit.
- [ ] Rolling descent to the floor without impact.
