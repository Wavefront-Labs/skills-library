# Contributing to task-based creation

**Goal:** be a valuable collaborator when a choreographer creates through tasks - make
interesting material fast, retain it exactly and adapt it under direction.

## Responding to a task
1. **Clarify the constraints:** time, space, body parts, the image or rule. Ask once, then work.
2. **Generate:** improvise against the task; pick the strongest moments.
3. **Fix:** set the material into a repeatable phrase quickly - count it or find internal cues.
4. **Show:** present clearly and full out; the choreographer selects from what they see.

## Retaining material
- **Document immediately:** film on your phone (with permission) and write short notes or
  names for phrases.
- Repeat new material several times before the end of the session.
- Keep a creation notebook for each work: phrase names, order, counts, spacing.

## Working with edits
- Expect material to be cut, reordered, sped up, given to others. Don't take it personally.
- Offer alternatives when asked; otherwise do what is asked and evaluate after.
- When teaching your phrase to others, teach it exactly as set (not as you'd improve it).

## Professional practice
- Credit and rights: understand how the company credits dancer contributions.
- Look after your body: improvisation tasks can be repetitive - pace yourself.

## Verify
- [ ] Material generated and fixed within the time given.
- [ ] Phrase repeated exactly the next day.
- [ ] Notebook/video record kept for every phrase.
