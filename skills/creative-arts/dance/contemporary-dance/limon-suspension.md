# Suspension (Limón)

**Goal:** the breath-held instant at the top of an arc when the body seems to float before
gravity takes over. It gives Limón phrasing its rise-and-fall musicality.

## Method
1. Build momentum from a fall, swing or rebound.
2. As the movement rises, **inhale** and let the energy carry the body past its usual height
   (often onto demi-pointe, with the chest and head lifting).
3. **Hang:** the body is fully extended with no forward motion - a moment of stillness that
   still contains energy (not a held pose).
4. **Release** into the next fall on the exhale.

## Where it appears
- Top of arm and torso swings.
- Rises on demi-pointe after a plié rebound.
- Turns that pause at the top before spiralling down.
- Jumps: the apex is extended in time by the upper body lift.

## Teaching points
- Suspension is in the music too: count it - e.g. a waltz-time swing where count 1 falls,
  2 rebounds, 3 suspends.
- It must be earned by momentum; static "holds" without momentum aren't suspension.
- The breath lifts the ribs; the shoulders stay soft.

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Stopping dead at the top | keep energy flowing outward; only travel stops |
| No rise before suspension | let the rebound carry higher |
| Held breath all phrase | inhale into the suspension only |

## Verify
- [ ] A visible hang at the top of swings and rises.
- [ ] Breath audible/visible on the suspension.
- [ ] Suspension placed on the agreed musical count.
