# Learning repertory from video

**Goal:** learn existing choreography from rehearsal or performance footage correctly -
including the details video hides - before working with the rehearsal director.

## Tools
- A player with **mirror (horizontal flip)**, speed control (0.25-0.5x) and frame stepping
  (e.g. VLC: Video > Effects > Geometry > Transform: Flip horizontally; playback speed menu).
- A notebook or spreadsheet for timestamps and counts.

## Method
1. **Watch the whole section** several times at full speed for intent and dynamics.
2. **Identify your track:** which dancer you are learning; note their entrances and exits.
3. **Chunk with timestamps:** e.g. 0:32-0:48 "phrase A", with counts or music cues.
4. **Mirror or not:** if the video faces you (audience view), flip it so you can learn facing
   the same way; check that left/right is right after learning.
5. **Slow playback** for detail: weight shifts, hands, head, breath.
6. **Map counts** to the music; mark where the dancers use internal counts or cues instead.
7. **Spacing:** note where on stage each phrase happens and facings (use stage numbers).
8. **Verify** with the rehearsal director or original cast; video angles distort and
   performances drift from the original.

## Gotchas
- Performance videos may have cuts or edits; prefer a fixed wide "archival" shot.
- Dancers in videos make individual choices or mistakes - don't copy idiosyncrasies blindly.
- Rights: rehearsal footage is often confidential; don't share it.

## Verify
- [ ] Section performed in sync with the video playback.
- [ ] Left/right and facings confirmed against the source.
- [ ] Count/cue sheet written and checked with the rehearsal director.
