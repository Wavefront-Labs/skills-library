# Graham falls

**Goal:** fall to the floor and recover in Graham style - the descent is driven by the
contraction and controlled by the legs, so it looks dramatic but is soft and safe.

## Back fall (basic)
1. Stand in parallel or turned out; contract.
2. Kneel down by lowering the body backward with the thighs and shins sliding to the floor -
   the contraction holds the torso; the weight descends through the legs.
3. Lower the back to the floor sequentially in the contracted curve, arms reaching forward.
4. **Recovery:** contract and rise back through the knees to standing in reverse, often
   with a release at the top.

## Side fall
- From standing, the body contracts and spirals; the outer thigh, hip and side slide along the
  floor in sequence, the supporting leg lowering the weight. Arms reach in opposition.

## Forward (front) falls
- Advanced: descend by bending the knees and lowering through the thighs, then hands absorb
  only the final contact in a push-up-like control; never land on locked wrists.

## Safety progression
1. Learn each fall slowly from the knees, then from a half-standing position.
2. Practise descending and recovering 8 times slowly before speed.
3. Use sprung floors; kneepads are allowed in early training.
4. Never drop onto the kneecap: the contact is through the shin/thigh with the knee folded.

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Dropping onto the knees | lower through the thigh, slow descent |
| Losing the contraction | the curve holds the body throughout |
| Using hands to catch | the legs control; hands only guide |

## Verify
- [ ] Back and side falls performed silently, both sides.
- [ ] Recovery to standing in rhythm.
- [ ] No knee impact on the floor.
