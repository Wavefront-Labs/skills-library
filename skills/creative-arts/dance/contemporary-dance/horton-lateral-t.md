# Horton lateral T

**Goal:** the signature Horton balance - the body forms a T: the supporting leg is the stem,
the torso and working leg form a straight horizontal line on opposite sides.

## Method
1. Start standing, parallel or slightly turned out; arms in a long line overhead or to the side.
2. **Prepare** with a lateral flat back (torso tilting sideways from the hip).
3. As the torso tilts to one side, the opposite leg lifts to the side so that head, torso
   and the raised leg form one straight horizontal line.
4. Arms extend overhead in line with the torso (or one arm along the line, the other to the side).
5. **Hold** on the supporting leg, then return through the lateral flat back to standing.

## Teaching points
- Everything stays in one plane (the frontal plane) - imagine dancing between two panes of glass.
- The supporting hip must not sink; lift out of it.
- Build height gradually: start with the torso at 45 degrees and leg at 45 degrees.
- Strength: side-lying leg lifts and lateral flat back holds build the required hip abductors
  and obliques.

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Body twists out of the plane | front of the body faces the audience |
| Supporting hip collapses | lift out of the hip; reduce the height |
| Leg drops below the torso line | build strength at lower heights |
| Bent supporting knee | straight, pulled-up leg |

## Verify
- [ ] Head, torso and leg in one horizontal line from the front.
- [ ] Held for 4 counts without wobbling.
- [ ] Controlled return through the flat back.
