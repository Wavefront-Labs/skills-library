# Graham floor series

**Goal:** the seated sequence (usually 20-30 minutes) that starts a Graham class, warming the
spine through breath, contraction and spiral before standing work.

## Typical order (varies by teacher and level)
1. **Bounces:** seated, soles together, the torso bounces gently forward over the legs in
   counts (often 8s), then with legs extended and in 2nd. Bounces are small, lifted rebounds,
   not forced stretches.
2. **Breathings:** contraction and release coordinated with breath, slow, in first position
   on the floor.
3. **Exercises "on six" / contractions:** percussive and sustained contractions with arm
   patterns, in counts.
4. **Spirals:** seated in 4th, spirals around the back, adding contractions.
5. **Exercises in fourth position:** back-and-forth weight shifts, spiral turns, rising to the
   knees.
6. **Pleadings:** on the knees, the contraction pulls the body back and the arms reach -
   a dramatic, expressive exercise.
7. **Knee work and transitions to standing.**

## Teaching points
- The floor series is technique, not warm-up filler: every exercise prepares a standing or
  travelling phrase.
- Sit on the sitting bones, spine lifted; if hips are tight, sit on a raised surface early on.
- Knee safety: kneeling work on hard floors needs gradual build-up; use padding in early days.

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Bounces become forced stretching | small, lifted, rhythmic bounces |
| Slumped seated posture | lift through the back, sit on the sitting bones |
| Rushing breathings | match the teacher's count; breath leads |

## Verify
- [ ] Student knows the order and counts of the floor series in their class.
- [ ] Contractions and spirals show correct initiation.
- [ ] Transitions to kneeling and standing are smooth.
