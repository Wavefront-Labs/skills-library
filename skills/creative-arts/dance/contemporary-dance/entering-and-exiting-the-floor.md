# Entering and exiting the floor

**Goal:** make transitions between standing and floor seamless and safe - the body pours
into and out of the floor instead of dropping or climbing.

## Entries
1. **Spiral down:** turn and bend the knees, letting the body spiral; the outer side of the
   shin/thigh, then hip, then back contact the floor in sequence.
2. **Slide through the outer thigh:** from a lunge, lower the back knee sideways so the outer
   thigh contacts first, sliding out into a side lying position.
3. **Sit and roll:** squat low, sit to one side of the sacrum, roll through the back.
4. **Hand-assisted:** place a hand, keep the elbow soft, transfer weight through the arm into a
   roll (hands absorb but don't catch body weight on a straight arm).

## Exits
1. **Rolling up:** roll onto one side, fold the knees under, and spiral up through a squat to
   standing.
2. **Momentum rise:** use a leg swing or roll to bring the centre over the feet, then stand.
3. **Through a lunge:** come to the knees via the shin (not the kneecap), step to a lunge, rise.

## Safety
- Contact the floor with the fleshy surfaces (outer thigh, buttock, back) - never knees,
  elbows or spine knobs.
- Practise slowly with control before using speed.
- Kneepads for learners; sprung floors preferred.

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Thumping onto the knees | lower through the shin/outer thigh |
| Stopping on the way down | continuous spiral |
| Climbing up with hands | use momentum and a squat |

## Verify
- [ ] Three entries and three exits without noise or stopping.
- [ ] No bony contact with the floor.
- [ ] Transitions done on both sides.
