# Weight sharing and counterbalance

**Goal:** two dancers support each other's weight so each is off their own balance but
stable together - by leaning in (counter-pressure) or pulling away (counter-tension).

## Counter-tension (pulling away)
1. Face each other, hold wrists (forearm grip - more secure than fingers).
2. Both lean back with straight arms until each is off-balance; the shared centre is between you.
3. Bend the knees together to sink low; rise together. Try one-arm holds and turning.

## Counter-pressure (leaning in)
1. Shoulder to shoulder or back to back, feet a little away from the partner.
2. Lean into each other until neither could stand alone.
3. Walk the feet out or slide down together into a sit; rise back up.

## Principles
- **Equal and gradual:** both give weight slowly; neither drops weight suddenly.
- **Skeletal support:** straight arms in pulls, stacked spine in leans - bones not muscles.
- **Communicate:** say "out" or tap to exit; release by both returning to their own balance
  simultaneously.

## Progressions
- Group counterbalances (three or more in a circle).
- Dynamic: counterbalance through a turn, release into a fall or roll.

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| One partner lets go suddenly | agreed exit; return weight gradually |
| Bent arms in pulls | straighten; hang from the skeleton |
| Feet too close | wider base and further distance |

## Verify
- [ ] Pair can sink to the floor and rise in counter-tension.
- [ ] Clean exits without stumbling.
- [ ] Back-to-back counter-pressure down to sitting and up.
