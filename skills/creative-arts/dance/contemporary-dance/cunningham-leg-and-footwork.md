# Cunningham leg and footwork

**Goal:** fast, articulate feet and legs that can switch between parallel and turned-out,
change direction instantly, and work independently of the back.

## Class elements
1. **Bounces:** standing, back bounces (curve) with straight then bent legs - warms spine and legs.
2. **Foot exercises:** articulate through the foot (heel, ball, toe), in parallel and turned
   out; rises, foot brushes, toe taps at speed.
3. **Tendus and battements:** in parallel and in turnout, often combined with back positions
   (e.g. tendu side with tilt away).
4. **Leg extensions:** développés and slow leg work with balance in changing directions.
5. **Across the floor:** fast changes of direction, triplets, skips, leaps, in irregular counts.

## Teaching points
- Parallel means truly parallel: knees and second toes forward.
- Cunningham often changes from parallel to turnout mid-phrase - train the rotation change at
  the hip, not the feet.
- Weight placement is always slightly forward to allow instant direction change.
- Counts are frequently irregular (5s, 7s); keep the feet precise regardless.

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Sloppy parallel (knees out) | align knees over second toes |
| Feet late on direction changes | weight forward, smaller steps |
| Legs and back move as one | isolate: back in position, leg moves independently |

## Verify
- [ ] Clean switches between parallel and turnout at speed.
- [ ] Foot articulation visible at fast tempo.
- [ ] Combines leg phrases with back positions independently.
