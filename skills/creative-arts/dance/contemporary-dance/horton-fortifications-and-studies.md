# Horton fortifications and studies

**Goal:** understand and teach how Horton technique is built: **fortifications** - numbered
strengthening exercises that target specific body areas - and **studies** - longer sequences
that combine them into dance phrases.

## Structure of a Horton class
1. Flat backs, primitive squats, lateral stretches (warm-up sequence).
2. Fortifications: numbered exercises (e.g. hinges, laterals, deep lunges, leg swings, coccyx
   balance work) each with set counts and progressions.
3. Studies: e.g. dimensional tonus (feet, ankles and legs working in parallel and turned out
   with precise rhythm), coccyx balance (balancing on the tailbone with legs lifted).
4. Across the floor: leaps, triplets, turns using Horton shapes (T, stag, lateral).

## Teaching approach
- Teach each fortification at a low level first, adding range and speed.
- The counts and order are fixed in the technique; follow a qualified teacher's syllabus
  (Ailey/Horton certified) for the exact numbering.
- Link every fortification to repertoire: the lateral T and hinge appear in Ailey works.

## Common studies
| Study | Focus |
|---|---|
| Dimensional tonus | foot/ankle articulation, rhythm, parallel/turnout changes |
| Coccyx balance | core strength, balance on the sitting bones/tailbone with legs extended |
| Lateral fortifications | side strength (lateral T preparation) |
| Deep lunges | thigh strength, hip flexor length |

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Skipping progression levels | follow the levels; build strength first |
| Loose counts | Horton studies are precise rhythmically |
| Losing straight lines | flat back principle governs every shape |

## Verify
- [ ] Class plan includes warm-up, fortifications, a study and across the floor.
- [ ] Exact counts used from a certified source.
- [ ] Students perform coccyx balance and dimensional tonus with correct alignment.
