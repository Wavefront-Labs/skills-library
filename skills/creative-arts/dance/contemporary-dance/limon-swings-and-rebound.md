# Swings and rebound (Limón)

**Goal:** use weight and momentum, not muscle, to drive large swings that pass through a
low point, rebound and suspend - a key part of Limón class.

## Torso swing (standing in parallel 2nd)
1. Arms overhead, spine long.
2. **Count 1 (drop):** release the torso and arms forward and down from the hips, knees bend in
   plié as the weight drops.
3. **Count 2 (rebound):** at the bottom, the energy swings through and back up, legs straighten.
4. **Count 3 (suspension):** the body rises to the top and suspends, arms overhead or
   continuing past.
5. Continue as a cycle; add side swings and figure-eight swings.

## Arm swings
- From the shoulder, the arm drops with weight across the body and rebounds up the other side;
  the torso responds with opposition.

## Leg swings
- Standing on one leg, the free leg swings forward and back from the hip; the supporting leg
  pliés at the bottom of each swing.

## Teaching points
- Swings live in 3/4 or 6/8; the down beat is the drop.
- The plié at the bottom of the swing absorbs and redirects weight.
- Keep the neck released; the head follows the spine.

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Muscling the swing up | let the drop do the work |
| Straight legs at the bottom | plié to absorb and rebound |
| Losing rhythm | count the drop on 1 every time |

## Verify
- [ ] Swings show drop, rebound and suspension each cycle.
- [ ] Plié coordinated with the low point.
- [ ] Dancer can keep a continuous swing for 8 bars of 3/4.
