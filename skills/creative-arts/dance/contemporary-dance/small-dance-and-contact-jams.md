# The small dance and contact jams

**Goal:** two practices that frame contact improvisation: the solo **small dance** that trains
perception of balance, and the **jam**, the open session where people dance contact together.

## The small dance (Paxton)
1. Stand still, feet comfortable, eyes open or closed.
2. Notice the tiny reflexive movements that keep you standing: shifts in the ankles, pelvis,
   breath.
3. Don't control them; observe. Paxton's guided cues include imagining the movement of the
   skeleton and the pull of gravity.
4. 5-15 minutes, often at the start of a class or jam.
- Purpose: tunes attention to the reflexes that support falling and weight-sharing.

## The jam
1. **Opening circle:** facilitator states guidelines and practical info.
2. **Entering a duet:** make eye contact or move near; a duet begins by mutual agreement.
3. **Leaving:** you can leave any dance at any time; a simple gesture or moving away is enough.
4. **Consent:** "no" or moving away must be respected immediately; avoid sexualised touch.
5. **Space:** watch for others; take big flying dances to open space.
6. **Witnessing:** sitting at the edge to watch is part of the jam.
7. **Closing circle** for feedback and announcements.

## Facilitator role
- Keep the space safe and welcoming; intervene when someone is uncomfortable.
- Post the jam guidelines; brief newcomers.

## Verify
- [ ] Can lead a 10-minute small dance with cues.
- [ ] Guidelines on consent and entering/leaving stated at the opening circle.
- [ ] Participants know how to exit a duet.
