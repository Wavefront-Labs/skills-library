# Hand supports and inversions

**Goal:** bear weight on the hands to pass the hips over the head or travel low - safely and
fluidly - as used in floor-based contemporary and breaking-influenced work.

## Hand placement
- Fingers spread, weight through the whole palm and finger pads (not just the heel of hand).
- Elbows soft but strong ("hugging" the joint); shoulders stacked over the hands where possible.
- Push the floor away (active shoulders) - collapsed shoulders strain the joint.

## Wrist and shoulder prep
- Wrist circles, rocking on all fours, finger-pad push-ups.
- Plank and side plank holds; downward dog pushes.

## Basic actions
1. **Monkey walk / hand-foot transfers:** travel sideways in a low squat moving weight from
   feet to hands and back.
2. **Cartwheel (low or floor-hugging version):** hand - hand - foot - foot, hips passing over
   the hands.
3. **Hip lifts over hands (frog/crow shapes):** balance knees on the elbows/arms.
4. **Handstand-style inversion:** brief vertical inversion with a soft fold back down.

## Safety
- Build gradually: weekly volume increases, especially for wrists.
- Use soft, sliding exits from inversions (roll out) rather than dropping.
- Never invert on a slippery floor or with sweaty hands.

## Faults and fixes
| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Weight in the wrist heel | spread fingers, use the fingertips |
| Locked elbows | soft elbows, active shoulders |
| Head bearing weight | the hands carry the weight; head clear |

## Verify
- [ ] Monkey walk travelling smoothly 8 counts each way.
- [ ] Low cartwheel with hips passing over hands.
- [ ] No wrist pain after the session.
