# Live video and shadow

**Goal:** shadow figures and live video share a screen.

## Setup
- A **camera** (webcam or video camera) on a small tabletop or hands; image sent to a **projector** focused on the same screen or a second screen.
- Check **latency**; keep it under a few frames and rehearse with it.

## Design
- Keep video high-contrast so shadows read over it.
- Use shadow for large forms, video for detail.
- Light the video scene separately from the shadow area.

## Check
Viewers can follow both layers without confusion.
