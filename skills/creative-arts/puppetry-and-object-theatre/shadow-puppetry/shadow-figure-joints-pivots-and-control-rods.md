# Shadow figure joints pivots and control rods

**Goal:** articulated figures that move without shaking and rods that vanish.

## Joints
- Use **eyelets, small rivets, knotted thread or brass paper fasteners** at shoulder, elbow, hip and knee.
- Place pivots where the limbs overlap; leave a small **hinge tab**.
- Test friction: the joint should hold a pose but move freely.

## Control rods
- Main rod fixed to the body, **perpendicular to the screen**, so its shadow shrinks to a dot; thinner rods for arms.
- Typical rod materials: bamboo or cane (traditional), dowel, wire, welding rod.
- Attach with a slot, two small holes and thread, or a tab and glue.

## Checks
Move the figure slowly through its range: no snagging, rod shadows barely visible, joints do not flop.
