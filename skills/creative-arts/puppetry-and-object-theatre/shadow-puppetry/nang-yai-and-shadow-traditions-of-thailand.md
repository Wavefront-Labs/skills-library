# Nang yai and shadow traditions of thailand

**Goal:** understand this large-scale tradition.

## The form
- **Nang yai** uses very large, carved leather figures held on two poles by dancers who move in front of a big screen lit by a fire or large lamp.
- Figures are intricately **perforated** and often painted on one side.
- The story is the **Ramakien**, the Thai Ramayana, accompanied by a **piphat ensemble** and a narrator.

## Related forms
- **Nang talung** from southern Thailand uses smaller jointed figures and a screen.
- Karagoz (Turkey) and wayang (Java) are related traditions in the shadow family.
