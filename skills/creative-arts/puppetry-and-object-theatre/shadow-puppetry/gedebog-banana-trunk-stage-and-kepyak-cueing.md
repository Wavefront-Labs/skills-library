# Gedebog banana trunk stage and kepyak cueing

**Goal:** know the dalang's set-up and signals.

## Gedebog
- Two banana-trunk logs, upper and lower, fix the **puppets' rods** by pushing them in; figures are arranged by size and alliance on the right and left of the screen.

## Cueing
- The **cempala**, a wooden mallet held in the dalang's foot, strikes the puppet chest (kotak) to cue gamelan changes.
- **Kepyak**, metal plates hung from the chest, are struck with the foot to give rhythmic accents to fights and dramatic moments.
- The gamelan players follow these signals as much as the voice.

## Check
You can explain what each sound signals and how the dalang uses his foot and hand together.
