# The kayon and gunungan

**Goal:** know the kayon's role and handling.

## Role
- A **leaf- or mountain-shaped** puppet with a tree, animals and gate.
- Placed upright at the centre before the show; its **raising and tilting** opens the play and ends scenes.
- Its moves can represent wind, fire, a forest or mountain.

## Handling
- Held by its single rod, moved gently side to side with a quivering motion for effect.
- When the story is **paused**, it is planted in the banana trunk in the centre.

## Respect
- The kayon is treated with ritual respect in Javanese practice.

## Check
You can describe the kayon's uses at opening, scene change and closing.
