# Shadowgraphy act and hand shadow props

**Goal:** a short act of rapid, surprising shapes.

## Structure
- A **series of transformations** from one animal or face to the next, each lasting a few seconds.
- Start simple, build to the most elaborate; finish on a clean, surprising change.

## Props
- A **hat, scarf, cane or cut-out** worn on the hand or held in the other hand adds characters.
- Keep props simple so the silhouette stays clear.

## Practice
- Practise the order and the hand positions so each change is a small move.
- Use music with clear beats for timing.

## Check
A four-minute run-through with no lost shape and no fumble between changes.
