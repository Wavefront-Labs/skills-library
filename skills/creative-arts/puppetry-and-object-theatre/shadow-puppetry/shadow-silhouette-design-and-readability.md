# Shadow silhouette design and readability

**Goal:** a figure the audience can identify at a glance.

## Principles
- Use **profile** views for heads and limbs; a front-on face reads poorly.
- Exaggerate the **distinctive outline**: nose, hair, hat, tail, stance.
- Keep **negative space** between arms, legs and body so the outline stays clear.

## Cutting and detail
- **Perforations** add internal detail (eyes, buttons, patterns); hole size should be larger than the card thickness.
- Cut with a craft knife on a cutting mat; use sharp blades and **cut inside lines first**, outside last.
- Leave bridges to keep islands connected.

## Testing
- Hold figures against the screen with the lamp on and check silhouette from the audience seat.
- Adjust proportions of important characters first.
