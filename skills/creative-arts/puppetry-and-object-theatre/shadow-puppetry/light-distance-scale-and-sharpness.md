# Light distance scale and sharpness

**Goal:** predict and use the scale and softness of a shadow.

## Rule
- Shadow size = figure size x (lamp-to-screen distance / lamp-to-figure distance). A figure halfway between lamp and screen casts a shadow twice its size.
- Edge softness grows with **lamp size** and with **figure-to-screen distance**: a figure touching the screen is crisp, one near the lamp is large and blurred.
- A small bright source (a clear halogen or a small LED cluster) gives crisp shadows; frosted or large sources soften them.

## Using it
- Fix the lamp at a measured distance; mark the floor lines for **sharp zone** and **giant zone**.
- A growing figure moving toward the lamp is a standard effect; keep the control rod stable as the shadow grows.
- Keep all figures at a constant distance from the screen when sharp focus is needed.

## Checks
Measure the lamp-to-screen distance and compute the shadow size for your biggest figure; confirm it fits the screen.
