# Wayang lakon repertoire and structure

**Goal:** understand how a night's performance is organised.

## Repertoire
- **Mahabharata and Ramayana** episodes, plus local Javanese **carangan** (branch) stories.
- The **Punakawan** (Semar, Gareng, Petruk, Bagong) are comic servants who comment and entertain.

## Structure
- A night-long performance has **three musical modes**: pathet nem (opening), pathet sanga (middle), pathet manyura (closing).
- Typical scene order: court audience, journey, battle, comic interlude, resolution.

## Practice note
- The dalang improvises dialogue within a known **lakon outline**.

## Check
You can describe one lakon and where each pathet falls.
