# Actors and figures and multiple screens

**Goal:** a layered stage with more than one screen.

## Actors and figures
- Actors cast shadows of **human scale** while figures show smaller or larger scale; use lamp distance to match.
- Let actors **step in and out** of the light.

## Multiple screens
- Use two or three screens at different angles or depths, each with its own lamp and zone.
- Co-ordinate with **light cues** and shared countdowns.

## Rehearsal
- Mark floor positions; film the arrangement from the audience side.

## Check
Scene changes between screens run smoothly with no light spill.
