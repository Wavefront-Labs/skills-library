# Hand shadow animals

**Goal:** clear, lifelike animal silhouettes with moving jaws and ears.

## Setup
- A small bright lamp or projector about 2-3 m from a white screen or wall; hands about 30-60 cm from the screen.
- Keep hands in **one plane** and the lamp stable.

## Animals
- **Rabbit:** fingers form ears, thumb and fingers the head; wiggle the ears.
- **Dog or wolf:** thumb and forefinger form the jaw; open and close for barking.
- **Swan or bird:** one arm and hand as neck and head, other hand as wing.

## Technique
- Practise the shape in a mirror, then in front of the light.
- Add **movement**: the jaw opens, ears twitch, head turns.

## Check
An audience unfamiliar with the shapes names each animal quickly.
