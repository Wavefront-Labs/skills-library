# Backstage choreography behind the screen

**Goal:** a crowded backstage that never casts a stray shadow.

## Plan
- Draw a **floor plan** with lamp, screen, performers and prop tables.
- Assign each performer a **zone** and path; keep bodies out of the light cone.

## Practice
- Keep a **quiet floor** and dark clothes.
- Pass figures from hand to hand at fixed points.
- Use **tap or light signals** instead of speech.

## Check
Run the full show in the dark with no collisions and no stray shadows.
