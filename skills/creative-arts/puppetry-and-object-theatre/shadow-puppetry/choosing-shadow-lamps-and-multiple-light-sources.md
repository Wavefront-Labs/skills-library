# Choosing shadow lamps and multiple light sources

**Goal:** the right lamp and light plan for the show.

## Lamps
- **Halogen** (50-100 W, clear): small bright filament, crisp shadows, but hot; keep clear of cloth and use a heat shield.
- **LED**: cool, controllable, but check the emitter size; a cluster can give multiple shadows.
- **Projector** (data or slide): gives crisp edges plus projected backgrounds.
- **Oil lamp (blencong)**: traditional wayang kulit light with flicker; fixed rig, fire safety needed.

## Multiple sources
- Two lamps at different angles give **two shadows**; use this for ghosts, echo or separate playing zones.
- Coloured gels on separate lamps produce coloured shadow overlaps (red + green giving yellow on white screen).
- Switch lamps by **dimmer or by flags** to change scenes.

## Safety
Keep flammable materials away from lamps; use guards and a fire extinguisher where flame is used.
