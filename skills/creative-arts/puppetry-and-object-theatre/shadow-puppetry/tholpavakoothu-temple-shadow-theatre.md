# Tholpavakoothu temple shadow theatre

**Goal:** know this ritual shadow tradition.

## The form
- Performed in a **koothumadam** (temple theatre) beside Bhagavathy shrines, usually during the temple festival season.
- A white cloth screen with a row of **coconut oil lamps** behind it; leather figures are held against the screen.
- The text is the **Kamba Ramayana** in Tamil-influenced Malayalam verse with percussion.

## Performers
- Traditionally hereditary families (Pulavars) perform it as an offering; a full cycle can run many nights.
