# Translucent colour shadow figures

**Goal:** coloured figures with strong outlines.

## Materials
- **Acetate or clear film** coloured with permanent markers or stained-glass paint.
- **Gels** (lighting filters) cut to shape and laminated.
- **Thin dyed leather or parchment** (as in Chinese and Turkish traditions).

## Technique
- Keep a dark **outline** for readability by cutting a black card frame and fixing the colour behind.
- Layer two colours for overlap shades; test on the screen.
- Avoid very dark colours that block the light.

## Checks
Colours show clearly with the actual lamp and screen; outlines remain visible from the back row.
