# Overhead projector layers and acetate

**Goal:** rich, animated projected scenes.

## Equipment
- An **overhead projector** (or a modern document camera and projector), acetate sheets, permanent markers, coloured gel, cut black card.

## Technique
- **Layers**: draw backgrounds on acetate and stack them on the stage; slide or swap sheets for scene change.
- Use **cut-outs and objects** on the glass for silhouettes and figures; move them by hand or on small rods.
- Focus: lift objects off the glass to blur; keep them flat for sharp edges.

## Tips
- Tape a **registration frame** on the glass to keep sheets aligned.
- Avoid hot lamp contact with plastics.

## Check
Layers register accurately and scene changes look clean.
