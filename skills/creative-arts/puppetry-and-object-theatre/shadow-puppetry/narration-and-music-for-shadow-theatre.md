# Narration and music for shadow theatre

**Goal:** sound that supports the images.

## Narration
- Short, vivid sentences that **complement** the image rather than describe it.
- Live narrators work from a side lamp; recorded voice allows the puppeteers to concentrate.

## Music and sound
- Use music to set **mood** and to mask small noises.
- Cue effects (thunder, steps) on a cue sheet.

## Check
Run with narration and sound together; the puppeteers can follow the cues.
