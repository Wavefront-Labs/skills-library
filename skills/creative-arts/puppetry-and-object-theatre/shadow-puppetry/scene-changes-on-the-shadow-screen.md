# Scene changes on the shadow screen

**Goal:** quick, silent scene changes.

## Methods
- **Blackout** (lamp dimmed) and swap cut-outs.
- **Sliding frames** or rolling scrolls behind the figures.
- **Flats or trees** on rods placed on the screen.
- A **traditional wipe** such as the kayon in wayang kulit.

## Practice
- List all scene changes on a cue sheet with who moves what.
- Rehearse in the dark with the lamp.

## Check
Scene changes take under five seconds without visible clutter.
