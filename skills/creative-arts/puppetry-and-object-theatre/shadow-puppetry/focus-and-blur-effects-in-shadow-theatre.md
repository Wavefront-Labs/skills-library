# Focus and blur effects in shadow theatre

**Goal:** expressive sharpness changes.

## Technique
- Move a figure **away from the screen toward the lamp** to blur and enlarge; return to sharpen.
- Combine with lamp movement for dramatic growth.
- Use a **second screen layer** or a piece of tracing paper to soften a background.

## Uses
- Memory or dream sequences (blurred), ghost or giant entrances, lamp-lit shock.

## Practice
- Record the same move at three speeds; choose the one that reads best.

## Check
Blur transitions look intentional, not accidental drift.
