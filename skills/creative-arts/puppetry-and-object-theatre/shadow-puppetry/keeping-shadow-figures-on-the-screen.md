# Keeping shadow figures on the screen

**Goal:** crisp, steady figures.

## Technique
- Hold the figure with **a light but constant pressure** toward the screen so its shadow stays sharp.
- Keep the control rods **perpendicular** and your hands low and out of the light path.
- Rest wrists on a bar, rail or the screen's lower frame for steadiness.

## Faults
- Figure drifting off the screen (blurs), hands showing at the screen edge, rod shaking.

## Practice
- Run a figure across the screen at a steady speed with no blur, then do turns and a bow.

## Check
From the audience side the figure edge stays sharp throughout the move.
