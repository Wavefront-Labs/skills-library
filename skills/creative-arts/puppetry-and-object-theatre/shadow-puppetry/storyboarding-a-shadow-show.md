# Storyboarding a shadow show

**Goal:** a clear plan before cutting figures.

## Method
- Divide the story into **scenes** and each into **frames**; sketch silhouettes in a screen-shaped box.
- Note **scale** (small near screen, big near lamp) and the **ground line**.
- Track **entrances and exits** and screen areas.

## Planning
- List figures and props per scene and who handles each.
- Time the scenes with a stopwatch.

## Check
Another person can run the show from your storyboard.
