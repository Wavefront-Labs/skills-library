# Walking shadow figures

**Goal:** a walk that looks like walking.

## Technique
- Keep the figure's feet along a **ground line** marked by the screen's lower edge.
- Use a **bob and tilt** of the main rod in time with each step.
- For jointed legs, a second rod or thread moves the legs; keep steps small.

## Turn and stop
- To turn, flip the figure (slide it out of the lamp path) or rotate the rod.
- Stop with a short settle.

## Check
Footfalls land on the ground line and speed matches the character.
