# Body shadow theatre

**Goal:** large silhouettes made from bodies.

## Setup
- A **large screen** with a strong lamp or projector behind the performers; performers work near the screen for sharp shapes.
- Mark floor positions for each performer.

## Technique
- **Solo:** use limbs and posture to make animals and objects.
- **Group:** several performers stack, link or compose a single figure, as in Pilobolus-style work.
- Agree a **count** for changes; leaders call shapes.

## Rehearsal
- Film from the audience side to check shapes.
- Rehearse transitions slowly.

## Check
Shapes are legible and transitions flow in time with the music.
