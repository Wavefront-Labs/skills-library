# Show repair kit and running repairs

**Goal:** fix any common failure in a few minutes.

## Kit
- **Sewing:** needles, strong thread, safety pins, scissors.
- **Adhesives:** hot glue gun, PVA, contact cement, super glue (with accelerator).
- **Tape:** gaffer, double-sided, duct, masking.
- **Fixings:** screws, cable ties, wire, spare eyes, Velcro, elastic.
- **Strings:** spare marionette thread and swivels.
- **Tools:** pliers, screwdriver, craft knife, spare batteries and bulbs.

## Triage
1. Safety: sharp ends, loose small parts.
2. Show function: strings, rods, voices.
3. Cosmetics: only if time remains.

## Spares
- Carry a **duplicate puppet or prop** for the most fragile parts.

## Check
Every item in the kit is labelled and re-stocked after each tour week.
