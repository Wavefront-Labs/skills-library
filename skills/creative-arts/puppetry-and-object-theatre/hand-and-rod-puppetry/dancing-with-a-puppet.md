# Dancing with a puppet

**Goal:** a dance where the puppet clearly leads or follows.

## Plan
- Choreograph in **counts**; assign the puppet its own steps.
- Decide **where the puppeteer moves** to make travel.

## Technique
- Dance with the **whole body**, with the puppet's moves at a smaller scale.
- Keep the puppet's focus on the partner.

## Rehearse
- Slow, half-time, full speed; film to check lines.

## Check
Counts line up between puppet and puppeteer on every musical hit.
