# Walking a rod puppet

**Goal:** a figure that appears to step rather than be carried.

## Legged figures
- With free-hanging legs, a small **side-to-side sway** and gentle **bob** of the central rod makes the legs swing and touch the ground in rhythm. Time the bob to each footfall.
- Keep the feet **light on the floor or playboard**, never dragging. A very small lift off the floor then a lowering gives contact.
- Use **leg rods** for precision; a second operator is common.

## Pace and rhythm
- Vary **step size** for mood: short and quick for nervous, long for confident.
- Accelerate in, decelerate out; stop with a settling bob.
- When turning, rotate the whole rod and lead with the head.

## Playboard figures without legs
- Use the glove-puppet bob technique (see `walking-and-running-a-glove-puppet`).

## Check
The figure's weight seems to land on the floor on each step and no leg crosses the other awkwardly.
