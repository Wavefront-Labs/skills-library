# Puppet hand offs between puppeteers

**Goal:** a transfer that the audience does not see.

## Principles
- Rehearse the hand-off as choreography: who, when, where, how.
- The **receiving puppeteer** matches the puppet's height and attitude in advance, then takes it on the moment the first lets go.
- Keep the puppet's head **still** during the exchange; use a count or a shared breath.

## Methods
- **Hand-to-hand**: the puppet is passed at a hidden point (under a table, behind a flat).
- **Cut-away**: the camera or audience focus moves while the swap happens.
- **Stay in character**: the hand-off puppeteer lets the puppet react and the new operator continues the line.
- Props handed between puppets use the pass-and-take pattern: eyes first, hand second.

## Check
A recording shows no change in height, tilt or breathing across the hand-off.
