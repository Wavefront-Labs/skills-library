# Voicing several characters in one show

**Goal:** every character is clearly distinct, even in fast exchanges.

## Contrasts
- Vary **pitch, rate, rhythm, accent, and breath**; vary two or more dimensions per character.
- Use **posture and placement** (chest, head, nose) to make the change quick.

## Technique
- Write a **voice sheet** per character: example line, tempo, key words.
- Practise **fast exchanges** with a partner and then alone.
- Assign signature **vocal tics** and catchphrases.

## Check
Listeners with eyes closed can name the speaker in every line.
