# Eight show week puppet maintenance

**Goal:** the puppet works on show 200 as on show 1.

## Daily
- Pre-show **function check**: mouth, eyes, joints, rods, glue.
- Post-show: wipe, dry, repair small faults.

## Weekly
- Deeper clean, stitch checks, hand-grip refresh, replace batteries.

## Records
- Keep a **repair log** and parts inventory.
- Schedule replacement of high-wear parts.

## Check
No on-show failure in the last 20 performances from a known wear point.
