# Two person puppets head and right hand

**Goal:** one character played by two people who act as one.

## Roles
- The **lead** operates the head, mouth and one arm. The **assistant** (right hand) operates the other arm with a glove or a rod.
- The assistant stands **close behind** the lead's right side (or left, for a left-handed puppet) with shoulders close enough to read breathing.

## Coordination
- The lead decides focus; the right hand **follows the lead's eyes**: look at the object, then the hand moves.
- Cue gestures with **silent signals**: a touch, a breath, or a head tilt.
- Practise together as a pair with the same pacing, in a mirror.

## Rules of thumb
- The assistant supports and does not upstage; the gestures complement the line.
- Agree **business** (point, hold, wave) in rehearsal; avoid improvised movements that confuse the lead.

## Check
In a take the hand moves on the lead's thought, never in advance.
