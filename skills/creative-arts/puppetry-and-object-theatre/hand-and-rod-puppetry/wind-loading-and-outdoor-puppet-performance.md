# Wind loading and outdoor puppet performance

**Goal:** make a go or no-go call before gusts take a puppet.

## Principles
- A big flat surface is a **sail**. Cloth, paper and light foam bodies catch more wind than open frames.
- Use **sail-reduction**: cut vents in fabric, use open cage frames, and keep large flat panels to a minimum.

## Thresholds (typical working practice, check the venue's rules)
- Check forecasts and an on-site anemometer. Many festivals stop big puppets in sustained winds around 8-10 m/s and gusts above that; smaller figures need earlier cancellation.
- Make a **named person** responsible for the stop decision.

## Rigging
- Add ballast or ground anchors to stationary figures; use guy lines that do not block the crowd.
- Keep the lowered figure with weight on it.

## Check
A written wind plan names the threshold, the decision-maker and the signal to ground the puppets.
