# Playboard height and sightlines

**Goal:** every spectator sees whole puppets and no puppeteer.

## Height
- A typical puppeteer stands with the playboard at **upper chest to chin height**, arms comfortably overhead. Adjust the booth height to the performer.
- For a seated audience of children, the playboard should be at or **just above eye level of the front row**; for adults on risers, raise it.

## Sightlines
- Sketch the room: audience seats, risers, windows, doors and light fall.
- The **front row** sets the lowest sightline; the **back row** the highest.
- Leave an **upstage depth** of at least the puppet's height so there is room to drop out of sight.

## Practical steps
- Test with people standing and sitting at the extremes of the audience.
- Raise or lower the playboard with leg extensions or adjustable stands.
- Use a **front panel** to hide the performers' heads and shoulders.

## Check
From every audience position a puppet's feet or hem are visible and no performer is.
