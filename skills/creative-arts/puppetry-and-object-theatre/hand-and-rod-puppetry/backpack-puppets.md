# Backpack puppets

**Goal:** a large figure carried for a parade leg without injuring the wearer.

## Build
- A **backpack frame** (aluminium tube or a repurposed pack frame) with a padded hip belt and shoulder straps carries the weight; arms are on long rods held by the wearer or an assistant.
- Keep the **centre of mass close to the spine and low**; use foam or lightweight cane and fabric for the body.
- Add a **quick-release strap** so the wearer can shed the frame in an emergency.

## Wearing
- Load from the **hips**, not the shoulders. Test with a half-hour carry before the parade.
- Practise turning slowly; the figure sweeps a large arc.
- Use a **partner** or a handler to spot the route and pull the figure's tail.

## Check
A 20-minute walk leaves no numbness; the figure stays stable in a light breeze.
