# Multi pole processional puppets

**Goal:** a large figure moved by several people who move together.

## Layout
- Head pole at the front, one or two **arm poles** and sometimes a **body pole** or frame for steadying.
- Poles of different heights need a team who agree **who leads**.

## Moves
- Use a **caller** who counts in loud, clear beats (one, two, lift) and changes direction on a word.
- Walk a **common pace**; the head leads turns, arms follow a half-beat later.
- Rest the poles on the ground using stand-off feet; rotate bearers.

## Rehearsal
- Practise slowly, then add the music.
- Mark the ground to rehearse path and spacing.

## Check
All poles lift, turn and rest together on a single count with no strain in the cloth.
