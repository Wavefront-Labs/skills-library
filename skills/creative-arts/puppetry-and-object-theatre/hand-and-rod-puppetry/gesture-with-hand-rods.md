# Gesture with hand rods

**Goal:** arms that gesture with purpose and never look like waving sticks.

## Setup
- Hand rods attach at the **wrist** (a wire loop or a screw eye) so the hand leads the gesture and the elbow follows.
- Rods bend at the wrist, not the hand, so the hand stays clean.
- Wire rods should be thin spring wire; add a bent end so the rod sits against the puppet arm and does not catch.

## Gesture
- **One clear gesture per line**: reach, point, wave, clasp. The other hand rests.
- Gestures start **before the word** and finish just after it; hold after the gesture for a beat.
- Both rods are held in one hand by an experienced operator if the other holds the central rod; otherwise, use a second puppeteer.

## Avoiding faults
- Keep rods **behind the arm, below the cuff, in the puppet's shadow**.
- Do not wiggle both hands constantly; keep them near the body unless needed.
- Practise with a lighter hand rod before final stringing.

## Check
Each gesture is readable from the back of the room and no rod is visible side-on.
