# Designing a sustainable puppet voice

**Goal:** a distinctive voice you can hold for hours.

## Design
- Start with **character facts**: size, age, temperament.
- Choose a **pitch** near the edge of your natural range, not beyond it.
- Add one defining feature: speed, accent, lilt, laugh.
- Record a signature phrase and keep a reference clip.

## Sustain
- Place the voice in the **resonators** rather than the throat; avoid pushing.
- Warm up and cool down; drink water; avoid whispering or shouting for long periods.

## Check
You can read a page in the voice after a rest and match the reference recording.
