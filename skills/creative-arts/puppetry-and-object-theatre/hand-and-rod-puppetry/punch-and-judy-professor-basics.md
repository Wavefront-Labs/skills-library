# Punch and judy professor basics

**Goal:** run a street Punch and Judy at traditional pace with the whole cast and rhythm.

## What the show is
A one-person glove-puppet show in a striped booth, built on short scenes pitting Punch against each character in turn: Baby, Judy, Joey the Clown, the Doctor, the Policeman, the Hangman and the Devil. A pitch show runs about 20-30 minutes.

## Principles
- Punch is a trickster; the audience laughs at him, so the violence stays stylised, with sharp rhythm and quick reactions.
- Repeating structure: a character enters, a dialogue built on misunderstanding, a stick fight, Punch wins. The audience anticipates and is rewarded.
- Participation is call and response ("behind you!").
- The professor works swazzle, puppets and props at once, so everything is pre-set in order.
