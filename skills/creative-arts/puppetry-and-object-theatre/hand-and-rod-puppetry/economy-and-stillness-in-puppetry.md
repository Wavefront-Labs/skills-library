# Economy and stillness in puppetry

**Goal:** every movement reads because the rest is still.

## Principles
- **One thing at a time:** head, then arm, then body.
- A puppet that is **still** on listening focuses the audience on the speaker.
- Use **small sizes**: a small movement plus a hold can read more than a large sweep.

## Methods
- Strip a scene of all movement and add back only what changes meaning.
- Use **breath** for low-level life (see `shared-breath-and-pulse-exercises` if available) rather than constant wiggling.

## Mistakes
- Fidgeting hands, shifting weight, repeated head nods.
- Moving the listener when the speaker should be the focus.

## Check
Film the scene: you can clearly point to the moment each character thinks.
