# School and library touring

**Goal:** reliable, repeatable venues that rebook you.

## Before booking
- Send a **tech rider**: space (width and depth), power, blackout, audience number and age, set-up time.
- Confirm **safeguarding and insurance** documents and any local check for adults working with children.

## On the day
- Arrive early, find the caretaker, test the room and the **sightlines**.
- Seat the youngest children at the front; leave an adult at each side.
- Allow **a clear turn-around** between sessions: time for a reset and repairs.

## After
- Provide **resource sheets** or a Q and A for teachers.
- Collect feedback and invoices promptly.

## Check
A packed show kit, a rider, a sheet of contact details, and a plan for a power fail.
