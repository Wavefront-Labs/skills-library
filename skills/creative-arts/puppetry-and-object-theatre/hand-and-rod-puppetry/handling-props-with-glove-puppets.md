# Handling props with glove puppets

**Goal:** props that the puppet seems to hold, not skewer.

## Grip
- Grip between the thumb arm and the middle-finger arm with the arms brought together. Practise with a pencil, a ball and a cup.
- Props with a ledge, loop or notch at hand height lock into the arm sleeves. A small pocket or Velcro tab helps with a letter or card.
- Heavy props: attach a hidden holding stick or loop so the puppet only appears to take the weight.

## Business
- Pre-set props on the playboard in sequence at a marked position; tape marks on the booth side keep them identical every show.
- Pick up: lower the head and look at the prop before the hands move; pause a beat after grasping; lift.
- Put down: look at the destination, place, release, look up.
- Passing between two puppets: both look at the item, one offers, the other takes. Never release before the other has gripped.

## Check
No prop falls in a full run; each prop has a labelled pre-set position and a spare in the kit.
