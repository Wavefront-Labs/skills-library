# Performing with limited vision

**Goal:** move confidently when you cannot see your feet or the crowd.

## Equipment
- Put a **vision port** at the mouth, neck or chest where a viewer can see through fabric mesh.
- Keep the mesh clean and clear of fog; use anti-fog treatment in cold weather.
- Wear shoes with grip and keep the route clear of cables.

## Technique
- **Pre-walk the route**; memorise obstacles and distances.
- Use a **handler** with a hand on the shoulder or back for stops.
- Move with **small steps**, scanning ahead.
- Avoid sudden turns; turn the whole body.

## Check
Blindfold-lite test: walk the route with partial blocking of the view and stop on a handler's signal.
