# Masking and backdrops for puppet stages

**Goal:** performers and equipment disappear while the puppets stand out.

## Masking
- **Front, sides and back** of the booth in black or dark neutral fabric; a front panel carries a **proscenium** frame.
- Add a **backcloth** at least one puppet-height behind the playboard so rods and arms are hidden by distance and shade.
- Keep the **inside** of the booth dark, with no shiny surfaces, to avoid reflections.

## Backdrop colour
- Choose backdrops that **contrast** with the puppets; avoid the exact colour of the puppets or props.
- Subtle painted or fabric backgrounds with low detail avoid fighting the puppets.

## Checks
- Walk the room: look for gaps at the sides and under the playboard.
- Look at the booth with the lights on to find light leaks.

## Check
From the audience position you cannot see the performers' faces or hands except where intended.
