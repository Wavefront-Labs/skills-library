# Parade route safety and spotters

**Goal:** a processional puppet route with no collisions and no unplanned stops.

## Survey
- Walk the route with the puppet's **height and width**: wires, branches, banners, tram lines, signal gantries.
- Mark **pinch points**, slopes and kerbs, and where the crowd narrows.
- Confirm permits, road closures and a marshal.

## Spotters
- One spotter **per pole or corner**, one at the head leading, and one at the tail.
- Agree a **stop word** and hand signals; the spotters call overhead hazards.
- Keep spare people to rotate bearers every 15-20 minutes.

## Crowd
- Keep a clear **buffer zone** in front of the figure; manage children and animals.

## Emergency
- Radios between the front and back; a plan for dropping the puppet safely on a signal.

## Check
Pre-walk the route at pace with the actual puppet before the event day.
