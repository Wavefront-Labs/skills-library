# Lighting the puppet stage

**Goal:** bright puppets, dark operators.

## Principles
- Light the **puppets**, not the performers: aim spots at playboard height and slightly above.
- Use a front-of-house lantern angled from above, with a second **side wash** for depth.
- Keep light off the back wall and the performers' faces.

## Choices
- LED lamps give no heat in a small booth; warm tungsten shows warm skin tones but heats up.
- Gels: pale ambers and blues for mood; avoid saturated colours that dull the puppets.
- Add **flags and barn doors** to stop spill into the operators' area.

## Safety
- Never leave lamps close to fabric; keep a safe distance.
- Secure cables and use RCD protection.

## Check
Black out performers: with the show lights on you can see only puppets and set.
