# Walking and running a glove puppet

**Goal:** convincing travel across a playboard with no legs.

## Walk
- Travel comes from the hand moving along the playboard with a small vertical bob on each step, about a finger-width and at a steady rhythm. Too high a bob reads as hopping.
- Add a slight forward tilt of the head into the walk and a small backward settle on stopping, so weight seems to arrive.
- Keep the hem of the body level and just above the playboard so the puppet appears to stand; below the board it seems to sink.

## Run
- Faster bob, lower amplitude, deeper forward lean, arms swung with thumb and middle finger.
- Stop with an overshoot and settle: travel a little past the mark, return half way, hold still.

## Stairs and entrances
- Rise on the bob in steps for stairs. Enter from below by rising, unless the character is meant to slide.
- Finish the move before dropping out of sight on exits.

## Check
Film from the audience side: constant rhythm, no legs implied below the hem, stops without sliding.
