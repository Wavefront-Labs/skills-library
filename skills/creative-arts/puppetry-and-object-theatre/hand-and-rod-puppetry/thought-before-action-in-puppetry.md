# Thought before action in puppetry

**Goal:** a puppet whose actions seem chosen.

## Principle
The audience reads **intent from focus**: the puppet looks at the target, reacts, and only then acts. Without the look and pause the movement seems mechanical.

## Sequence
1. **Notice:** the head turns or lifts.
2. **Think:** a beat of stillness, maybe a small breath.
3. **Act:** the move or the line.
4. **Settle:** a final hold.

## Examples
- Pick up a cup: look at the cup, pause, reach, grip, lift.
- Answer a question: turn to the questioner, look down or up while thinking, then speak.

## Drills
- Do each action first without the thought, then with it; compare.

## Check
Observers can tell when the puppet makes a decision.
