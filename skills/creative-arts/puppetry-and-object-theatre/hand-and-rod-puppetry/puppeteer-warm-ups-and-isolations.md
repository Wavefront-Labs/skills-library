# Puppeteer warm ups and isolations

**Goal:** a short routine (10-15 minutes) that prepares body and voice.

## Sequence
1. **Breath and posture:** stand, feel the feet, breathe low and slow.
2. **Joints:** neck rolls, shoulder circles, arm swings, wrist and finger stretches.
3. **Isolations:** move the thumb, each finger, the wrist, elbow and shoulder independently.
4. **Puppet work:** mirror drills, eye-line target game, quick entries and exits.
5. **Voice:** hums, lip trills, tongue twisters at puppet pitch.

## Tips
- Warm the **hand and forearm** for repetitive gripping tasks.
- Keep the routine consistent so you can run it in a corridor.

## Check
You can hold a clean eye-line and a steady head with a puppet after the routine.
