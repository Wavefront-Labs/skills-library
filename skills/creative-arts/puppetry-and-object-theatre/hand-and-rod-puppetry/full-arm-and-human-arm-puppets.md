# Full arm and human arm puppets

**Goal:** convincing human-scale arms on a character.

## Design
- A **full-arm sleeve** continues from the puppet's shoulder to a glove, sized to the performer's arm length; the sleeve matches the body fabric.
- Add a small **wire or foam stiffener** at the elbow if the sleeve collapses.
- The glove should be sized for the real hand; a larger glove can add cartoon scale.

## Use
- Hold the arm in a **relaxed curve**; avoid locked elbow or a bent wrist.
- Gesture from the shoulder and elbow, not the wrist only.
- Check the puppet's **centre of gravity**; the arm adds weight.

## Care
- Use ventilation and cotton liners in warm settings.
- Check cuffs and seams after each use.

## Check
Camera test: no bare skin, and the arm's gesture matches the puppet's body language.
