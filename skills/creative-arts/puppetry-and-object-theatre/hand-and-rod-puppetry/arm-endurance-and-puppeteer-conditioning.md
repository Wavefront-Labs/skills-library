# Arm endurance and puppeteer conditioning

**Goal:** hold a puppet overhead for a full take or show without strain.

## Why it fails
Overhead work keeps the shoulder abducted with the forearm loaded; the weakest links are the shoulder girdle, forearm extensors and neck.

## Routine (3 times a week, not an injury treatment plan)
- **Warm-up:** shoulder circles, arm swings, wrist circles.
- **Strength:** light overhead presses, band external rotations, scapular retractions, forearm extensor curls.
- **Core and posture:** planks and a neutral stance so the load goes through the legs.
- **Practice holds:** time-limited holds with a puppet, increasing in short steps.

## On the job
- Rest cycles: put the arm down between takes; shake out; alternate arms if the puppet allows.
- Use a **sling, a counterbalance or a forearm rest** for heavy puppets.
- Hydrate; keep the room warm.

## Warning signs
Numbness, tingling or sharp shoulder pain are reasons to stop and seek medical advice.

## Check
You can complete the longest scene twice in a row with no loss of height or shaking.
