# The traditional punch and judy scene sequence

**Goal:** know the spine so cuts and additions keep the show coherent.

## Typical sequence (variants exist)
1. Opening: Punch squeaks in and introduces himself.
2. Baby: Punch minds the baby and drops it.
3. Judy: she returns, a row, Punch beats her with the slapstick.
4. Joey the Clown, the sausages and the Crocodile.
5. Doctor and Policeman: Punch's chase and escape.
6. Hangman: Punch tricks him into the noose himself.
7. Devil: Punch beats the Devil and ends on his triumph.

## Edit notes
- A 15-minute show keeps four core scenes (Baby, Judy, Policeman, Hangman); add Crocodile, Ghost or Doctor for longer pitches.
- Keep the rhythm: short scenes, fast entrances and exits; always end on Punch's triumph.

## Check
Each scene can run alone, and every cut still has a clean entrance and exit.
