# Avoiding flapping in mouth puppets

**Goal:** a mouth that opens on syllables and closes cleanly without the head bobbing.

## The fault
Flapping is moving the whole head or wrist with every syllable, or opening the mouth continuously during a line. It makes the puppet look like a chattering toy.

## Fix
- Isolate the **thumb against the fingers** (the jaw). Hold the wrist and forearm still; practise opening and closing against a mirror with the head frozen.
- Open on each **stressed vowel**, not each letter. Short words in a phrase ("a", "the", "to") get a small movement or none.
- **Close the mouth** fully on plosives (b, p, m) and at the end of the line; leave it closed in pauses. A half-open mouth reads as dead.
- Vary opening size with volume and emphasis: whisper is small, shout is wide.
- Use a slight head movement (nod or tilt) on emphasis words instead of a bigger mouth opening.

## Drill
Say the alphabet and a counting sequence at a steady pace, then a nursery rhyme, matching jaw to syllable with the head still. Record and review.

## Check
Film the puppet with the sound off: you can still guess the stressed words.
