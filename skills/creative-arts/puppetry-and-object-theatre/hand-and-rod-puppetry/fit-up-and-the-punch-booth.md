# Fit up and the punch booth

**Goal:** a stable, quick booth that hides the professor and holds the props in order.

## Layout
- A frame of four uprights with cross-braces, a front proscenium, side wings and a striped cover. The professor stands inside with the playboard at about head height.
- A back shelf or pocket holds the cast in running order. The professor is the only person inside; the bottler works outside.
- Add sandbags at the base: a booth is a sail. A guy line adds safety.

## Fit-up routine
1. Check the pitch for flat ground and room for a crowd in a semicircle.
2. Erect the frame, fit the cover, set the playboard height.
3. Lay out cast, swazzle and props; check the backcloth for gaps.
4. Quick sound and sightline test.
- Pack in reverse order with each item in a labelled case.

## Check
The booth stands in a stiff breeze, set-up takes under 15 minutes, and no puppet is visible when resting.
