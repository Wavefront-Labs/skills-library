# Sound and voice amplification for puppets

**Goal:** clear voices that come from the puppet, with no feedback.

## Microphones
- Use **headset or lavalier mics** worn by puppeteers; angle capsules away from the booth wall.
- In a small booth, a boundary mic on the playboard edge picks up speech from both voices.
- Check each puppeteer's mic separately.

## Placement of speakers
- Put speakers **at or near the stage** so the voice seems to come from the puppet.
- Keep them forward of the mics to avoid feedback; use a monitor for the performers.

## Cues
- Use a cue sheet for playback music and effects; set levels with the performers inside the booth.
- Leave room under the voice; duck music during dialogue.

## Check
Feedback margin test: raise the gain until it just rings, then back off 3 dB and test.
