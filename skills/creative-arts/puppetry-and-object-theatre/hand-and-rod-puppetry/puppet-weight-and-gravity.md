# Puppet weight and gravity

**Goal:** a puppet that looks as though it has mass.

## Principles
- A puppet's weight is **shown by what moves second**. Lead with the head or lift, let the body follow, settle after.
- **Lag and settle:** a stop includes a small overshoot and return.
- Heavy character: slow start, strong stop; light: fast start, fast stop.

## Gravity in practice
- Objects dropped should land and **stay down**; do not lift them again as if they bounce unless intended.
- Bodies lean into walks and collapse in tired states.

## Exercises
- Lift a ball through different weights (a feather, a stone) with a single puppet.
- Sit and rise: show the effort by timing and head lead.

## Check
Viewers describe the puppet as heavy or light according to your choice.
