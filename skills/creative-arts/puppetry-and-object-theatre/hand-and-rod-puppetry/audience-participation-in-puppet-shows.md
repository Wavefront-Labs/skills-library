# Audience participation in puppet shows

**Goal:** engaged audiences who still follow the story.

## Types
- **Call and response**: "behind you!" or repeated phrases.
- **Help the puppet**: ask for a magic word, a count or a song.
- **Choices**: show a vote with hands.

## Control
- Teach the **stop signal** at the start (a hand up, a chime).
- Use the puppet to **hush** the crowd and re-set attention.
- Keep volunteers on the audience side and at a safe distance from the booth.

## Pacing
- Use participation as breaks between story beats, not during the climactic moment.

## Check
After a participation beat the room can be settled within five seconds.
