# Using the swazzle

**Goal:** the rasping Punch voice that stays intelligible.

## What it is
A **swazzle (swatchel)** is two thin metal strips with a tape-wrapped reed between them, bound with cotton tape. Professors make their own or buy one from another professor.

## Placement and breath
- Hold it against the roof of the mouth with the tongue, reed facing up, and keep the shape through the show without letting it slip back.
- Support from the diaphragm, not the throat; use short phrases and breathe between them. Slightly distort vowels and clip consonants to keep clarity; sibilant-heavy words need extra care.
- Rehearse the script without the swazzle first, then add it one scene at a time.

## Care and safety
- Never place it so it can slip toward the throat. Keep a spare in a tin.
- Dry and clean after use, replace tape when it frays, never share a used swazzle.
- Stop if you gag; build up in short sessions.

## Check
A listener 6 m away understands every word of a rehearsed scene.
