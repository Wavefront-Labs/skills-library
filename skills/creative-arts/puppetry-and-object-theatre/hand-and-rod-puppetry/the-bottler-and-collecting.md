# The bottler and collecting

**Goal:** a crowd gathered, kept in order, and paying.

## Role
- The bottler is the assistant outside the booth who draws the crowd, patters, and collects after the show. They are also the safety presence for children.
- Warm the crowd with a few lines of patter, set seating rules and bring children to the front.

## Collecting
- Mention the collection during the show and again at the end, then pass the hat or bucket and thank everyone.
- Keep a clear gap in front of the booth. After the show, hand out contact details for bookings.

## With the professor
- Agree cues: a word to start the crowd building, a gesture to speed or slow the show, and a way to stop the show if the crowd is unsafe or a child is hurt.
- Share takings by a pre-agreed split.

## Check
The bottler can start the crowd on time and the collection comes from at least half the audience.
