# Heat management and rotation for walkabout puppets

**Goal:** no one overheats inside a costume.

## Working limits
- Plan **short performance blocks**, often about 20 minutes in warm weather, with equal or longer rest in shade.
- Cooling vests, neck wraps and small fans inside the head help.
- Choose breathable fabrics and vents when designing.

## Rotation
- Use more performers than slots; keep a **roster and timer**.
- Rotate before symptoms, not after.

## Warning signs
- Headache, dizziness, nausea, stopping sweating: stop immediately, cool the person and seek medical help.

## Hydration
- Drink before and after each block; avoid alcohol and heavy meals before.

## Check
Each performer's shift is logged, and a first-aid person knows the heat illness plan.
