# Puppet fights and slapstick

**Goal:** a fight that is clear, funny and safe for puppets and puppeteers.

## Mechanics
- The attacker winds up (head and arm back), pauses, then strikes. The victim reacts a beat later by dropping, spinning or being knocked off the board. Wind-up and reaction sell the hit, not the contact.
- Make contact on a non-visible hard surface, such as the back of the playboard or a padded block, so the prop makes the noise and the victim moves after it.
- Use a **slapstick**: two hinged battens that clap together, held by the other hand or an assistant, for the sharp crack.
- Pattern: three blows with increasing speed, then a longer pause.

## Safety
- With two puppeteers on one board agree positions, distance and cues in rehearsal and keep arms below the playboard line.
- Cover striking props with felt; use soft foam for young audiences.

## Check
Run at half speed first; the fight repeats identically at full speed with the same count.
