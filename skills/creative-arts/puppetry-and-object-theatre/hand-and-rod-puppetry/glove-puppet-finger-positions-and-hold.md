# Glove puppet finger positions and hold

**Goal:** a glove puppet that holds its head up and gestures with two clean arms without cramping.

## Standard hold
- **Index finger** into the neck/head socket, **thumb** into one arm, **middle finger** into the other. Ring and little finger fold into the palm or grip the glove skirt.
- The head should sit at the first knuckle of the index finger; a head that rides on the second joint wobbles. Size the neck tube to the finger with a snug fit; pad with foam or tape if loose.
- The wrist is the puppet's neck and spine: keep the forearm vertical and the wrist straight so the head points where the body points. Bend the wrist only for deliberate bows.

## Variations
- Larger heads: use index and middle finger together in the head and thumb and little finger for the arms.
- Smaller hands: shorten the arm sleeves so the arms do not droop and the fingertips reach the cuffs.

## Common faults
- Dropped head: neck tube too long for the finger; add a finger stall.
- Both arms mirror each other: practise moving thumb and middle finger independently, then clap, wave one arm, point.
- Fatigue: raised forearms tire in minutes; rest the elbow against the playboard edge between speeches.

## Check
In a mirror the head stays steady while one arm moves, and the puppet can pinch a light prop between its two arms.
