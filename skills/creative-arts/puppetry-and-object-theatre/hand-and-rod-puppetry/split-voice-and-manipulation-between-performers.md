# Split voice and manipulation between performers

**Goal:** the voice seems to come from the puppet's body.

## Setup
- The puppeteer follows a **script** with beat markings; the voice actor sits near or watches a monitor.
- Mark **emphasis words** so the puppet moves on stress.

## Cueing
- Use **eye contact, a shared breath, or a light touch** to cue.
- Practise the lines with the mouth moves first, then speed up.

## Mistakes
- Voice rushing ahead of the mouth; voice actor drifting on tempo.

## Check
Record the performance and test the sync by watching with sound muted and on.
