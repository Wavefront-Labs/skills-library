# Wayang golek rod puppets

**Goal:** recognise and handle the wayang golek tradition's rod puppets.

## What it is
Wayang golek (West Java) uses carved, painted three-dimensional wooden puppets worked from below a screen by a **dalang**, with a central rod through the body and into the head, plus two thin rods for the hands.

## Handling
- The dalang grips the main rod and turns the head with fingers; the head can rotate and nod through the central dowel.
- Hand rods are thin and held in the other hand; gestures follow the character type (refined, strong, comic).
- Puppets are stuck in a **banana-trunk** log at the dalang's side when resting.

## Performance
- The dalang voices all characters and cues the gamelan; stories draw on the Mahabharata and Ramayana cycles, and also Menak tales.
- Clown servants (Punakawan, such as Cepot) are the audience favourites and carry comic business.

## Care
Handle carefully, protect the paint, and store puppets upright with heads supported. Seek out a master dalang for detailed repertoire and rules.

## Check
You can name the main puppet types and explain how the head turns on the central rod.
