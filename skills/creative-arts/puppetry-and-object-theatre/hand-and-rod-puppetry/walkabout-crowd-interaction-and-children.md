# Walkabout crowd interaction and children

**Goal:** delighted audiences with no scared or hurt children.

## Principles
- **Small, slow and open** gestures; large sudden movements can frighten young children.
- Let the child approach; offer a hand or a wave and wait for a response.
- Keep **soft contact only**: no grabbing, no lifting children.

## Handling the crowd
- Photographs: pose the character at a **safe height**, handler supervises.
- Keep a clear radius for the puppet's swing and tail.
- Have a script for common requests; stay in character.

## Safety
- Remove the puppet from a child who is upset; the handler guides parents.
- Follow venue child-safeguarding rules.

## Check
Observed groups of children approach without fear and nobody is struck by the puppet.
