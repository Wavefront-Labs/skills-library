# Actor puppeteer split focus

**Goal:** the audience sees the puppet as the character and the actor as a presence.

## Focus rules
- The actor looks at the **puppet's target** and mirrors its focus.
- Keep the face **neutral to supportive**; the puppet leads acting.
- Match the actor's breath with the puppet's.

## Moments of split
- Let the actor react to the puppet's emotion in the same moment, a beat later.
- Use stillness for the actor when the puppet speaks.

## Check
Audience members report watching the puppet during dialogue and the actor only in transitions.
