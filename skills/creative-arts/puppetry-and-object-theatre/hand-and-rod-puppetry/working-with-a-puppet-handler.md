# Working with a puppet handler

**Goal:** a performer inside or behind a character who is never at risk.

## Roles
- The handler is the performer's **eyes**: warns of steps, children, wires and bad ground; keeps the crowd at a safe distance; manages photographs.
- The performer is responsible for **staying in character**; the handler speaks for the character if needed.

## Signals
- Agree **hand signals** (stop, back up, turn, crowd, break) and a code word to end the performance.
- Handlers stay at the performer's shoulder, in front of the audience's sightline but not blocking it.

## Care
- Handler checks the performer's temperature and mood and schedules breaks.
- Keep a bottle and a towel ready.

## Check
You can run a 10-minute loop with signals only, and the handler can stop the walk instantly.
