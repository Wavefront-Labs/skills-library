# Show length by audience age

**Goal:** a show that suits the audience's attention.

## Rules of thumb (practice guide)
- Toddlers (2-4): about 15-20 minutes, short scenes, repetition, familiar songs.
- Early primary (5-7): about 30-40 minutes, a clear story, call-and-response.
- Older children (8-12): 40-60 minutes, more plot and humour.
- Adults: any length with a strong dramatic arc; consider an interval above an hour.

## Adapt
- **Scary moments** need a gentle approach for the young; show the puppet is safe.
- Use **language** and jokes that suit the age.
- Build **participation** into the structure.

## Check
Watch the audience; if wriggling starts, shorten or break up the scene.
