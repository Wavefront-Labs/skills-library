# Live hand gloves and props

**Goal:** a puppet with expressive hands that can actually do things.

## Concept
In live-hand work the performer's own hand, usually the right-hand assistant's, is fitted with a **glove matched to the puppet's colour** and operates as the puppet's hand.

## Gloves and props
- Use well-fitting stretch gloves or sewn sleeves, matched in colour and texture; cuff them into the puppet's sleeve.
- Keep fingers relaxed and curved; flat, spread fingers look mechanical.
- Props need **handles that suit the grip**: bigger loops or short sticks the glove can hold.
- Pre-set props within reach; use cues for pass-and-take with the other puppeteer.

## Direction
- Live hands only show **in the frame** when the hand is acting: the hand enters, does the business, leaves.
- Keep wrists below the frame or set edge.

## Check
Camera test: the glove reads as the puppet's own hand, with no skin showing at the cuff.
