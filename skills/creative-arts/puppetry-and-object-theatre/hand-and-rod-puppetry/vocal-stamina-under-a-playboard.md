# Vocal stamina under a playboard

**Goal:** keep a voice through many shows.

## Causes of strain
- Cramped posture, dry air, straining over noisy rooms and long runs.

## Practice
- Keep the head **forward and up**; use mic or amplification instead of pushing.
- Drink water and use steam or humidification.
- Rest between shows; avoid whispering and throat clearing.

## Warning
- Persistent hoarseness or pain needs a voice specialist.

## Check
Voice holds a constant tone at the end of a double-show day.
