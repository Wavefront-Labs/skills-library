# Singing while puppeteering

**Goal:** a singing puppet with clean sync and a steady voice.

## Breath
- Plan breaths in the music to match **puppet breaths**.
- Keep the arm steady while breathing low.

## Sync
- Open the mouth for **sustained vowels** and close on consonants; keep movements small on fast phrases.
- Add a head tilt for emphasis.

## Practice
- Sing the line without puppet, then with the puppet but no voice, then both.

## Check
Record video: the mouth matches the lyric and the puppet's head does not wobble on high notes.
