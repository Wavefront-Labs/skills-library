# Iron head rod operation

**Goal:** control a heavy figure with one rod.

## Mechanism
- An **iron rod** runs through the figure's head and body; a **thin rod** or string to the right hand moves the sword arm; a string works the left.
- The operator stands on a **bridge** above the stage and holds the rod.

## Technique
- Use both hands and the **body weight**; lift to take weight, lean to turn.
- Walk the figure with small lifts and **stamps** for sound.

## Check
You can lift, turn and set the figure down smoothly.
