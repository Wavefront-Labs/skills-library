# Musician marionettes playing instruments

**Goal:** a believable player.

## Method
- Attach the instrument to the **hands** with hooks.
- Operate **hand strings** for strum, bow or tap.
- Use head sway for emphasis.

## Tips
- Choose simple instruments: drum, banjo, trumpet.
- Mark **beats** in the music.

## Check
Hands move with the sound and the instrument stays steady.
