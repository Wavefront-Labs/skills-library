# Picking up and carrying objects with a marionette

**Goal:** reliable pick-up and carry.

## Preparation
- Place props at **marked positions** within reach of the hand.
- Use **magnets, small hooks, loops or velcro** on hands and props so the pick-up is dependable.

## Technique
- Look at the prop, lower the control so the figure bends, lift the **hand string** to the prop, grip, lift with the control.
- Hold the prop at **chest height**, away from the strings.

## Release
- Reverse the pick-up; place it down and drop the hand string.

## Check
Prop is picked up, carried and replaced in three attempts with no string snag.
