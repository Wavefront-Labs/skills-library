# Choosing thread and line for marionettes

**Goal:** strings strong enough, thin enough, and invisible.

## Options
- **Braided nylon or polyester line** (fishing-line type) is flexible, resists tangles and lasts well.
- **Monofilament nylon** is invisible but stiff, tends to coil and retains kinks.
- **Cotton or silk thread** is traditional and soft but weaker.

## Choosing
- Weight: fine line for small figures, heavier for large or heavy ones; test by hanging the figure with margin.
- Colour: dark thread against light background and vice versa; avoid shiny finishes.
- Match **diameter** to the stage lighting: the thinner, the less visible.

## Care
- Replace strings when they fray or kink; wax or lubricate lightly if needed.

## Check
Strings carry the figure with a clear safety margin and cannot be seen from the back row.
