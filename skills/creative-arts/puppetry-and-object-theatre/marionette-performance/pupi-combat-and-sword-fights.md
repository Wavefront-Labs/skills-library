# Pupi combat and sword fights

**Goal:** a loud, clear duel.

## Technique
- Two operators use **iron head rods** and a hand rod to swing the sword arm.
- Fights are **choreographed**: strike, parry, step, strike, with foot stamps on the boards.
- Metal swords clash for noise.

## Structure
- Rounds of escalating intensity ending with one knight falling.

## Safety
- Keep the audience at a safe distance and the swords secured.

## Check
The fight is repeatable and the sound matches the strikes.
