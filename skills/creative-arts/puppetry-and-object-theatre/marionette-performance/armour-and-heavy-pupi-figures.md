# Armour and heavy pupi figures

**Goal:** operate and maintain heavy armoured figures.

## Types
- **Palermo** figures are lighter and more flexible; **Catania** figures are larger and heavier, often 30 kg or more.

## Handling
- Use **two operators** for heavy figures, and a strong rod.
- Avoid dropping armour.

## Care
- Keep metal clean and dry; check hinges and attachments.

## Check
You can lift and move the figure under control with no damage.
