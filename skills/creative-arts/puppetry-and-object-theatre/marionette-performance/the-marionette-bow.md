# The marionette bow

**Goal:** a bow that reads as respect or comedy.

## Steps
1. Settle the figure and look at the audience.
2. **Tilt the control forward** from the wrist while the head string stays taut; the body folds.
3. Hold briefly at the bottom.
4. Rise slowly, returning to neutral.

## Variants
- **Formal bow:** slow, deep, hand at chest.
- **Comic bow:** exaggerated fall, a head bump.

## Faults
- Over-tilt causing the head to flop; fast rise jerking the figure.

## Check
The bow shows a visible start, bottom hold and return.
