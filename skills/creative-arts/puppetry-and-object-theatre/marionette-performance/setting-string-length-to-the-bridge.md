# Setting string length to the bridge

**Goal:** feet just touch the floor at the control's working height.

## Measuring
- Stand on the bridge at your working position; hold the control at **resting height**.
- The figure's feet should **just touch** the stage floor.
- Mark lengths and adjust at the **control end** for fine tuning.

## Adjusting
- Set the **head strings first**, then shoulders, hands and knees.
- A longer string lowers a part; a shorter one lifts it.

## Check
At the working height, the figure stands with correct weight and legs bend properly with a small control tilt.
