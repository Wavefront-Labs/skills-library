# Open stage marionette work

**Goal:** operators who frame the figure rather than distract.

## Choices
- Operators in **neutral costume** (blacks or contrasting).
- Stand **behind or beside** the figure, controls at a constant height.
- Use **lights** that favour the figures.

## Practice
- Keep faces calm, look at the figure.
- Decide whether to acknowledge the audience.
- Keep strings neat.

## Check
Audience members report watching the marionette, not the operator.
