# Turning and changing direction marionette

**Goal:** tidy turns that reveal character.

## Technique
- Lead with the **head** (rotate the control a few degrees), then the shoulders, then the hips.
- Plant a foot and **pivot** by rotating the control from the wrist.
- For a large turn, step in an arc.

## Tips
- Avoid spinning more than 360 degrees in one direction, as strings twist.
- Untwist by rotating the control the opposite way during a pause.

## Check
No strings cross after a half-turn and the head turn leads the body.
