# Hooks and perches for resting marionettes

**Goal:** figures that wait without damage.

## Methods
- **Control hook** on the bridge or rail: the control hangs by its ring or hook, with the figure straight beneath.
- **Perch** for seated figures: a small shelf or stool at stage level; strings slackened.
- **Rack or hanging rail** backstage for storage.

## Practice
- Lower the figure **feet first**, supporting the head.
- Keep strings **parallel** and avoid crossings.
- Check the stage side so the resting figure is not seen.

## Faults
- Hanging by the wrong string, twisting, or stretching strings while resting.

## Check
After resting, all strings are straight with no knots and the figure hangs symmetrically.
