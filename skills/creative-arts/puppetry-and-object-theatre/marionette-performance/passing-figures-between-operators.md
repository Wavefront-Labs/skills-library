# Passing figures between operators

**Goal:** exchange figures quickly and quietly.

## Method
- First operator **holds the figure still** on the floor; second takes the control by the **opposite side**.
- Pass the control **bar to bar**, not string to string.
- Verbal or touch cue for release.

## Tips
- Rehearse hand-overs with **count and pause**.
- Keep strings clear of operators' arms.

## Check
Passing takes under 5 seconds and the figure stays upright.
