# Marionette cabaret trick act

**Goal:** a polished short act.

## Structure
- **Opening**: figure enters with music, a signature walk.
- **Middle**: three tricks escalating in difficulty.
- **Finale**: a big transformation or collapse.
- Total running time about 4 minutes.

## Technique
- Plan each trick on a **cue sheet**.
- Keep reset time short.

## Check
Rehearse the act three times in a row without a failure.
