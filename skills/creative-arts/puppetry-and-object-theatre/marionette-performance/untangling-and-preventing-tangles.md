# Untangling and preventing tangles

**Goal:** tangle-free shows.

## Untangling
- Hold the control and let the figure **hang**; find the crossing point.
- Reverse the last movement that made the tangle.
- Use a **blunt probe** to open loops; do not pull knots tight.

## Prevention
- Keep strings **parallel** during moves.
- Do not turn the figure repeatedly in one direction.
- Store figures **hanging straight** or wrap strings round the control with care.

## Check
After untangling, each string moves its own part and none is stretched.
