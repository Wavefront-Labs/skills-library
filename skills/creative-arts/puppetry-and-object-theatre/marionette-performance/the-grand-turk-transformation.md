# The grand turk transformation

**Goal:** a reveal that surprises.

## Mechanism
- A figure with a **cloak or body that opens**, usually by a separate string that pulls panels apart, to reveal an inner figure, a dancer or a second character.
- Hidden **springs, hinges and pulleys** make the change.

## Routine
- Build tension, then open at a musical climax.

## Tips
- Test the mechanism often; keep a reset routine.

## Check
The reveal works every time in under two seconds.
