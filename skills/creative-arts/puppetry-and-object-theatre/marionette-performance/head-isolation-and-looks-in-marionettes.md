# Head isolation and looks in marionettes

**Goal:** a look that shows thought.

## Method
- Use the **head string** and small control rotation to turn the head alone.
- Let the body follow after a short delay.
- Fix the eye-line on a point (another figure, prop, audience).

## Variants
- Quick glance, long stare, double take (look, away, back).

## Check
Another person can tell what the figure is looking at.
