# Marionette dance

**Goal:** a dance that fits the music and the figure's limits.

## Method
- Count the music in **8s**; assign a step per count.
- Use **simple steps**: sway, step-touch, turn, bow.
- Add accents with arms and head on beats.

## Practice
- Rehearse **slowly**, then up to tempo.
- Use a mirror and record.

## Check
Each step lands within a beat and the dance is repeatable.
