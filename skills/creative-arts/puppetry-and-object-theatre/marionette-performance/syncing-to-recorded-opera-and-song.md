# Syncing to recorded opera and song

**Goal:** moves that land on the music.

## Method
- Listen and mark **cue points** (entrances, key words, climaxes) with times.
- Use **head and mouth accents** for sung phrases, and moves on musical bars.
- Rehearse with the actual track.

## Tips
- Provide operators with a **headphone** feed or a visible click.
- Keep moves simple during rapid passages.

## Check
Playback shows moves aligned to the score within a beat.
