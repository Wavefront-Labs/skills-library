# Transferring the control between hands

**Goal:** switch hands invisibly.

## Steps
1. Choose a moment when the figure is **still** (a held pose).
2. Bring the free hand to the control, grip the **opposite end**, keep the weight on the old hand.
3. Move your grip across, release the old hand slowly.
4. Confirm the figure stays upright.

## Tips
- Practise the **same grip** on both sides so the weight feels identical.
- The free hand can pick up strings first when needed.

## Faults
- Sudden lift or drop on release.
- Tilting the control forward.

## Check
A filmed transfer shows no movement of the figure's head or shoulders.
