# Bridge and leaning rail heights

**Goal:** a comfortable, safe working height.

## Principles
- The **bridge** is the platform behind and above the stage where operators stand. Choose its height so a figure at the control's working height has its feet on the floor.
- A **leaning rail** supports operators' forearms and hips at the front edge.

## Typical considerations
- Typical bridge heights run 1.2 to 2.0 m for standard figures; taller for long strings.
- Make the rail height **hip to waist**.
- Provide **steps, guard rails and non-slip surfaces**.

## Check
Operators can work without stretching and the bridge is stable under load.
