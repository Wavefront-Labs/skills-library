# Rocking the control for head nods

**Goal:** subtle head nods without the whole figure bouncing.

## Mechanics
- Tilt the **control** forward slightly; the head strings (at the ears or crown) tighten first and the head nods. Return slowly to vertical.
- Shake the head by rotating the control a few degrees left and right.
- A head string that is too short causes the head to sag; too long loses control.

## Practice
- Use a **mirror**; nod "yes", shake "no", look up and down.
- Keep the control's **height** constant; rocking should be a wrist movement only.

## Faults
- Head nodding with the **whole body** because the shoulder strings are slack or tight.
- Jerky nods from over-rotation.

## Check
The head nods independently with the body still, at a tempo you can vary.
