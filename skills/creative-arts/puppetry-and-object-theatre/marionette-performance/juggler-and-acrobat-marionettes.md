# Juggler and acrobat marionettes

**Goal:** an act that looks live and skilled.

## Juggler
- Balls attached to **hidden threads** to the control; the operator works them in rhythm, with a free hand.
- Use **three-ball cascade** timing; mark the beat.

## Acrobat
- Use **joined limb strings** and a wire frame; flips are done by moving the control in an arc.

## Practice
- Slow-motion practice, then speed up.
- Use clear marks for the handover.

## Check
The audience believes in the skill and strings remain untangled.
