# The paladins repertoire

**Goal:** know the story cycle behind the form.

## Sources
- Stories draw on the **Chanson de Roland**, **Orlando Furioso**, and Sicilian additions.
- Characters: Charlemagne, **Orlando**, **Rinaldo**, Ganelon, Angelica, Saracen enemies.

## Structure
- Performances were **serialised** over many nights, with cliffhangers and recaps.
- Narration and dialogue are spoken by the puppeteer.
