# The marionette walk cycle and contact

**Goal:** a walk with visible weight and floor contact.

## Cycle
1. Weight shifts to one foot by tilting the control **sideways**.
2. The free leg lifts with its **knee string** and swings forward.
3. The foot touches down **heel first**, the weight transfers, the other leg lifts.
4. Add slight sway and head bob.

## Tips
- Rock the control side to side in time with each step; the figure's foot should touch down as the control tilts back.
- Keep the **feet just touching** the floor; if they hover, it reads as floating.
- Walk speed is set by the control's rocking rate.

## Faults
- Knee strings too short (permanent bent legs) or too long (no lift).
- Leaning the control forward so the figure falls.

## Check
The audience sees footfalls and weight on each step.
