# Two marionettes interacting

**Goal:** two figures that interact cleanly.

## Setup
- Each operator stands a **comfortable distance** apart on the bridge; the figures are at least a figure-width apart.
- Position strings so they **do not cross**.

## Contact
- Agree **who leads**; the follower reacts a beat later.
- For embrace or handshake, use touch points set in rehearsal (hand strings meet).

## Cues
- Use a spoken or nodded count; breathe together.

## Check
A full scene passes with no tangles and clear eye contact between figures.
