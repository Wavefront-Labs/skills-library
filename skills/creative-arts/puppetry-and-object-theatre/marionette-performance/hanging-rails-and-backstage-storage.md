# Hanging rails and backstage storage

**Goal:** every figure is ready at its entrance.

## Setup
- Mount a **hanging rail** on the bridge with hooks spaced about a figure width apart.
- Arrange figures in **entrance order** and label each hook.

## Practice
- Hang each figure by its control hook with strings straight.
- Keep **spare figures** at the end of the rail.

## Check
A stage manager can hand any figure to an operator in under 10 seconds.
