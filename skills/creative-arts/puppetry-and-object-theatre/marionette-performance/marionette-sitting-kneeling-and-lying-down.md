# Marionette sitting kneeling and lying down

**Goal:** controlled postures with natural transitions.

## Sit
- Lower the control and **lift the knee strings** so the legs bend. Rock the control back slightly so the figure sits.
- A **seat-back string** at the base of the spine helps the torso stay upright.

## Kneel
- Lift one knee string, lower the other, tilt the body forward.

## Lie down
- Lower the figure onto the floor, back first or side first, then slacken the strings, keeping the head string lightly taut.

## Get up
- Reverse the sequence: lift the head, then the body, then lower the knee strings.

## Check
The figure lowers and raises without dropping or tangling.
