# Ergonomics and injury prevention for puppeteers

**Goal:** a show that does not cost performers their shoulders and backs.

## Principles
- Set **working heights** to the performer's body (adjustable tables, stools).
- Share loads: use **harnesses, slings and counterweights**.
- Rotate roles and schedule rest breaks.

## Routine
- Warm up before and stretch after; strengthen shoulders, core, and wrists.
- Monitor posture in rehearsal; film from the side.

## Warning
- Numbness, sharp pain or persistent soreness: stop and seek professional advice.

## Check
A written plan lists heights, rest rules and injury reporting.
