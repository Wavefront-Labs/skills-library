# Head operator signals and bunraku kata

**Goal:** understand how three people coordinate without words.

## Signals
- The **omozukai** (principal operator) controls the head and right arm and **leads** by body movement, breath and head position.
- The **left operator** and **foot operator** read these cues and follow in the same breath.
- Training teaches them to read the leader's **shoulder and breath**.

## Kata
- **Kata** are codified movement patterns (grief, anger, a bow, drawing a sword) that all operators know.
- Because the patterns are shared, the team needs only a slight cue to start a kata.

## Practice for Western ensembles
- Create your own **shared vocabulary** of cues and patterns and rehearse until they are automatic.

## Check
Three people can run a sequence in silence and arrive together at the final pose.
