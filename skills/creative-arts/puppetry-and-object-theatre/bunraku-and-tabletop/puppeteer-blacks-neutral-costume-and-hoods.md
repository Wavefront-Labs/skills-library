# Puppeteer blacks neutral costume and hoods

**Goal:** visible operators who recede or support, as the design requires.

## Options
- **Blacks:** black clothes, gloves optional, black shoes; best against a dark background.
- **Neutral:** grey or earth tones for a lighter world.
- **Character costume:** operators in period or story costume.

## Practical
- Check **lighting**: black reads differently in different light; avoid shiny fabric.
- **Hoods or masks** hide faces when needed.
- Keep **hands and forearms** matched to the puppet's colour.

## Check
From the audience position operators fade into the background.
