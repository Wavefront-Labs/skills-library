# Silent cueing and role handovers in ensemble puppetry

**Goal:** several people move one character and swap without a visible change.

## Cueing
- Use an agreed **count** (one-two-three-go) or a shared breath to start moves.
- **Touch cues** on the shoulder or back for the person with no view.
- Practice **silent counting** until the team moves together.

## Handover
- Swap positions during an **audience-focus moment** (another character speaks) and keep the puppet's focus and breath going.
- The outgoing operator gives the cue and the incoming operator **matches height and breath** before release.

## Check
Filmed handover shows no change in the puppet's focus or weight.
