# Puppet rehearsal process and notes

**Goal:** a rehearsal plan that builds puppet skill alongside staging.

## Stages
1. **Table and design:** read the script, assign puppeteers, agree the puppet's rules (how it breathes, looks, moves).
2. **Technique time:** short daily sessions on breath, focus, weight and walk before blocking.
3. **Blocking with puppets** from day one; avoid long rehearsals with only stand-in props.
4. **Runs:** stop-start for puppet clarity, then complete runs.
5. **Tech and dress:** fit light, sound and masking around puppeteers' needs.

## Notes
- Give notes to the **puppet** first ("Joey needs to look before he walks"), then to the operator.
- Be concrete: **focus, breath, weight, timing**, not "more life".
- Keep a note list per puppet and re-check each day.

## Check
By the first full run each puppet has a repeatable focus, breath and entrance.
