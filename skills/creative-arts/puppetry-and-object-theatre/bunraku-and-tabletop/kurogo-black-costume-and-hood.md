# Kurogo black costume and hood

**Goal:** understand the visual convention that makes operators fade.

## Meaning
- **Kurogo** refers to the black costume with hood that stagehands and puppet assistants wear; the black is understood as **invisible** by the audience.
- In bunraku, the principal operator may appear **unhooded**, showing the performer's face, while the others wear hoods.

## Use today
- Contemporary companies use **blacks** or hoods to focus attention, or choose to show faces to bring operator and puppet into dialogue.

## Check
You can describe when to cover faces and when to show them.
