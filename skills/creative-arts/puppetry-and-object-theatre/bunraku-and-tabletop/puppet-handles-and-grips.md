# Puppet handles and grips

**Goal:** handles that give control without blocking the figure.

## Design
- **Head handle** (a short stick at the head or neck), **back handle** (between the shoulders), **foot handles** (on each foot).
- Shape handles for the operator's grip; add rubber or tape for friction.
- Keep handles **short** and out of the audience's view where possible.

## Use
- Each operator has a **named handle**; practise swapping without moving the puppet.
- Add or reduce handles to suit the number of operators.

## Check
A swap of operator is invisible and the figure's balance is maintained.
