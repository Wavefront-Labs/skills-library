# Staging for puppet focus

**Goal:** the audience looks where the story needs them to.

## Principles
- Puppets are small; give them **space and a clean background**.
- Only one thing moves at a time; the still puppet is ignored.
- Use **levels** (a table, a ledge) to lift the puppet to the eye line.

## Visible operators
- Place operators **behind or beside** the puppet and make them look at it, which pulls the audience's eyes.
- Keep their movements minimal.

## Sightlines
- Check from the first row and the last; avoid puppets facing upstage.

## Check
A viewer unfamiliar with the show can say who is speaking from the stage picture alone.
