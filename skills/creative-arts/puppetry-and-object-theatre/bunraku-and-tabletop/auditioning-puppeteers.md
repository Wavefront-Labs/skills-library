# Auditioning puppeteers

**Goal:** find people who can bring a puppet to life and work in an ensemble.

## Tasks
- **Breath and focus:** give a simple puppet or object; ask them to make it wake up, look at something, and react.
- **Weight:** lift and set down the object; walk it across a table or floor.
- **Direction:** give a note and see whether they can adjust in a few minutes.
- **Group:** two or three work together to move one puppet.

## Look for
- Stillness, clear focus, willingness to hide, patience and listening.
- Physical stamina for the demands of the show.

## Practicalities
- Let candidates warm up; use the same puppet for all.
- Score on a sheet with the same headings for each candidate.

## Check
The shortlist includes people who made the puppet seem alive without relying on voice.
