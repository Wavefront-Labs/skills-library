# Kashira heads and mechanisms

**Goal:** know how a bunraku head is built and used.

## Design
- Heads are carved and painted (traditionally wood); a **handle** below the neck holds **levers and strings** that move eyes, eyebrows, mouth and sometimes eyelids.
- Different **types** of kashira represent roles: young woman, old man, villain, fox spirit.
- Some heads can **transform** (a beautiful woman becomes a demon).

## Operation
- The principal operator works the levers with fingers of the **left hand** inside the figure's body.
- Heads are **swapped** between roles and require care to maintain mechanisms.

## Check
You can describe what the levers control and how a head change affects performance.
