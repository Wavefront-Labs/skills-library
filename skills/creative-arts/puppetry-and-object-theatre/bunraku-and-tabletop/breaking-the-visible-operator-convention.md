# Breaking the visible operator convention

**Goal:** a reveal that has meaning.

## Techniques
- The **puppet looks at its operator** or the operator interacts with it directly.
- The operator **steps out** of neutral, speaks, or takes a bow.
- The puppet **takes control**, or the operator leaves.

## Use
- Use for comedy, tragedy, or to reveal theme.
- Re-establish the convention after with clear signals (return to focus on puppet).

## Check
Audience reaction shows the break is understood as intentional.
