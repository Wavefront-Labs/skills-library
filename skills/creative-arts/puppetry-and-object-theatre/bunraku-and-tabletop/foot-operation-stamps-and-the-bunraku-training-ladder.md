# Foot operation stamps and the bunraku training ladder

**Goal:** know the foot operator's job and the structure of training.

## Foot operator
- Holds the puppet's feet or hems and moves them with the leader's steps; produces **stamps** on the stage floor for strong movements.
- Feet convey **walking, running, kneeling and dance steps**.

## Training ladder
- Apprentices traditionally spend about **ten years on feet**, then about ten on the **left hand**, and only then work the **head and right arm**, with a lifetime of refinement.
- The ladder teaches rhythm, respect for the leader and the shared kata.

## Takeaways for others
- Build team skills by rotating roles and make everyone learn the foot job.
