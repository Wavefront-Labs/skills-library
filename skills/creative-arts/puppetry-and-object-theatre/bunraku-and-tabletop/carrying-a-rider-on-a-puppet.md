# Carrying a rider on a puppet

**Goal:** a safe ride on a puppet that moves like an animal.

## Design
- The **frame** must carry the rider's weight with a safety factor; use engineered steel or aluminium and test by static loading.
- Provide a saddle area and handholds.

## Procedure
- Define **mount and dismount** steps; operators brace.
- Rider and operators agree **cues** for start and stop.
- Check the floor and sightlines.

## Safety
- Test with sandbags then people; have a spotter.

## Check
Documented test and a signed-off procedure before public use.
