# Studying animal behaviour for puppets

**Goal:** a puppet whose behaviour comes from real animals.

## Observation
- Watch live animals (stable, zoo, farm) and film.
- Note **breath rate, gait order, head carriage, startle response, rest posture**.

## Distillation
- Choose **three or four behaviours** that define the species.
- Teach the whole team and rehearse them.

## Check
Watchers recognise the species from motion alone.
