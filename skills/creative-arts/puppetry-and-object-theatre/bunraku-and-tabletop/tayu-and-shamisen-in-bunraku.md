# Tayu and shamisen in bunraku

**Goal:** understand the sound world of bunraku.

## Roles
- The **tayu** chants narration and every character's speech in a stylised voice, sitting on a platform at the side of the stage.
- The **shamisen** player sits beside the tayu and provides the **rhythm, mood and cues**.

## Coordination
- The operators move **with the chant**, not to the words alone; the shamisen's phrases cue changes.
- The platform can **rotate** so the next pair enters as the first leaves.

## Lessons
- The voice is a **separate performance** from the puppets; the audience unites them.

## Check
You can describe how the tayu and shamisen shape the pace of a scene.
