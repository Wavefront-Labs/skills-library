# Operating a horse puppet and ear and tail emotion

**Goal:** a horse that breathes, walks and feels.

## Three operators
- **Head:** neck, ears and head. **Heart:** front legs and chest breath. **Hind:** rear legs and tail.
- The team shares a **breath cycle** and gait count.

## Behaviour
- Ears signal attention (forward alert, back anger, flicked uncertainty); tail signals mood (swish irritation, held high excitement).
- The head leads, the heart follows, the hind completes.

## Practice
- Walk, trot, canter patterns with counts.
- Study live horses for rhythm.

## Check
Observers describe the puppet's emotions and gait without being told.
