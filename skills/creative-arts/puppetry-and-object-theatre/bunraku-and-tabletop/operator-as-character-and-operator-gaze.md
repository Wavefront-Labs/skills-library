# Operator as character and operator gaze

**Goal:** the audience sees the puppet as the character.

## Gaze
- The operator **looks at the puppet** (or its target) and mirrors its attention; the audience follows.
- When the operator looks at the audience, focus shifts to the human.

## Operator as character
- Some shows give the operator a **role** (narrator, owner, double of the puppet); decide this in rehearsal.
- Keep the operator's facial expression **neutral** or supportive.

## Contract
- Establish early what the audience should do: watch the puppet.

## Check
Audience members, asked after the show, name the puppet and not the operators.
