# Table height surface and walking on the table

**Goal:** a stable table and a walk that shows weight.

## Table
- Height about **hip to waist** so the puppeteer can bend over the figure comfortably.
- Choose a surface that gives the right sound and friction (plain wood, felt, rubber).
- Keep the area **uncluttered** and well lit.

## Walk
- Hold the puppet by its **handles** (head, back, feet) and let the feet contact the table on each step.
- Rock the body slightly side to side; footsteps must have **audible weight**.

## Check
Film the walk: the feet land on the beat and the body moves with the steps.
