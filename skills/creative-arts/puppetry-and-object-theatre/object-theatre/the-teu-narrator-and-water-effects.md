# The teu narrator and water effects

**Goal:** know the narrator character and effects of the form.

## Teu
- **Teu** is a comic farmer figure who introduces and comments on the show.
- He interacts with the audience and the musicians.

## Effects
- **Spray, fire and smoke** effects accompany dragon dances.
- Splashes and water arcs are part of the choreography.

## Safety
- Controlled fire and pyrotechnics need licensed operators.

## Check
You can explain Teu's role and how effects support the story.
