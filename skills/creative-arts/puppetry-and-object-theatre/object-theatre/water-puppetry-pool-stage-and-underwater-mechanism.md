# Water puppetry pool stage and underwater mechanism

**Goal:** understand the structure of this Vietnamese form.

## Stage
- A **pool** serves as the stage; a screen at the back hides the performers who stand waist-deep.
- Pavilion-like fronts frame the performance; the musical group sits at the side.

## Mechanism
- Puppets are carved from **wood** and fixed to long poles and string systems under the water; operators move them through the water.
- Control of **poles and strings** allows boats, dragons and dancing figures.

## Practice note
- Operators need training and protection against cold and moisture.
