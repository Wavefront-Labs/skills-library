# Object gesture vocabulary and voice

**Goal:** a consistent language for each object.

## Gesture
- Give each object a **small set** of moves: tilt, slide, hop, spin, bow, shiver.
- Repeat them so the audience learns the code; vary tempo for emotion.
- Keep movements simple and **true to the object's weight and shape**.

## Voice
- Keep objects **silent** and let sound come from their own noise (a rattle, a scrape), or give them a **voice** from the narrator or a recording.
- Mixed approach: objects silent, the performer speaks for them in a distinct voice.

## Check
After a few minutes viewers can read the objects' moods from gestures alone.
