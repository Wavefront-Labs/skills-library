# Slider figures and toy theatre lighting

**Goal:** moving characters and atmosphere for a miniature stage.

## Sliders
- Cut figures, mount on card or **thin tin** strips, and attach to a **slider** so they glide through stage-slots.
- Keep figures **vertical** and keep slider strips out of view.

## Lighting
- Traditional lighting used **candles or oil lamps** in the footlights; modern theatres use **LED strips and torches** for safety and colour.
- Light from the **front and sides** to avoid shadows on the backcloth.

## Safety
- Never leave flames near paper; use LED when children are present.

## Check
Figures slide smoothly and light shows detail without hot spots.
