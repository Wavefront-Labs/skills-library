# Toy theatre sheets mounting and cutting

**Goal:** a neat, sturdy paper theatre.

## Materials
- Printed **toy theatre sheets** (historic reprints or new designs), thick card, PVA or spray adhesive, craft knife, ruler and cutting mat.

## Method
1. **Mount** sheets on card before cutting; weight under books to prevent curl.
2. Cut **inside windows first**, then outlines.
3. Score and fold tabs; assemble the **proscenium** and wings.
4. Slot wings at set distances; fix a backcloth.

## Tips
- Keep a blade sharp, and cut slowly.
- Colour the card edges for neatness.

## Check
The theatre stands square and wings slot in without wobble.
