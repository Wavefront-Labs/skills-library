# The narrator manipulator

**Goal:** a performer who tells the story and moves the objects with equal clarity.

## Roles
- The performer **speaks** to the audience and **moves** objects, sometimes in the same breath.
- Choose when to look at the object and when at the audience.

## Technique
- Plan **handover moments** where the objects lead and the performer goes quiet.
- Keep language **simple and rhythmic**.
- Do not talk over the best visual.

## Check
In rehearsal film, audience attention moves smoothly between performer and objects.
