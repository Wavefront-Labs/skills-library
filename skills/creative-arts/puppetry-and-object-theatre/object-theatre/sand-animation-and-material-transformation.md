# Sand animation and material transformation

**Goal:** images that form and dissolve as a story.

## Sand animation
- A **light table** or glass sheet under a camera feeds a projector.
- Use **fine dry sand** and fingers and brushes to draw and erase; keep surfaces clean.
- Plan **key images** in sequence and rehearse the transitions.

## Transformation
- Start with one material (flour, water, paper) and transform it into another image or object.
- Choose changes that read at **camera and audience scale**.

## Tips
- Keep hands clean between scenes.
- Use music to time the changes.

## Check
Each image reads clearly and transitions flow without gaps.
