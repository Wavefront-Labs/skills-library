# Choosing objects and kitchen table theatre

**Goal:** objects with strong shape, history and playability.

## Selection
- Gather a **box of candidates** from kitchens, workshops and attics.
- Test each for **shape** (a face or body), **sound**, **weight** and ease to hold.
- Prefer objects with **a history** that the audience recognises.

## Kitchen-table style
- Perform on a **table** with ordinary kitchen items, visible performer, close audience; the table is the whole world.
- Keep set and lighting simple and the stagecraft honest.

## Check
Every object in the show has a reason to be there and plays more than one role.
