# Newspaper and fabric puppetry

**Goal:** characters formed from sheet materials in front of the audience.

## Newspaper
- Roll and tear sheets into a **body and head** shape; tape or fold to hold.
- Use **tearing sounds** and rustle as part of the story.
- The figure is **moved by hand**, often by fingers underneath.

## Fabric
- A **scarf or cloth** held at points can become a body, wave or creature.
- Use **tension and release** to give breath and emotion.

## Tips
- Rehearse the **construction** so it is smooth and quick.
- Prepare spare sheets.

## Check
The material figure takes shape and moves convincingly within a minute.
