# Adapting a play for toy theatre

**Goal:** a play that fits the miniature stage.

## Method
- **Cut** the play to a handful of scenes and a few characters.
- Use **narration** for scenes you cannot show.
- Design a **small number of sets** and figures.

## History
- Nineteenth-century sheets reproduced scenes from popular melodramas and pantomimes; performers read the text aloud while moving slides.

## Practice
- Time the reading with the movement of figures; keep the changes simple.

## Check
Run-through of the whole play lasts under half an hour with smooth scene changes.
