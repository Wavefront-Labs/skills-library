# Hitting marks and adapting to shot size

**Goal:** repeatable positions and movement sized to the lens.

## Marks
- Use **tape or markers** on the set floor or in the puppeteer's area; rehearse each move to the mark.
- Look at the monitor to check the **end position**, not the start.

## Shot size
- **Wide:** larger moves and clear shapes.
- **Medium:** normal acting.
- **Close-up:** tiny movements and **stillness**; every flicker is magnified.

## Check
Match marks across takes; verify against playback.
