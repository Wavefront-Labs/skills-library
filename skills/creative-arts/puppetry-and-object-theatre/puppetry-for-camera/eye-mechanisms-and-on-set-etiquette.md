# Eye mechanisms and on set etiquette

**Goal:** reliable mechanics and a professional crew presence.

## Eye mechanisms
- **Blink and eye-line mechanisms** use levers or cables operated by the right hand or lead.
- Test for **smoothness and silence**; lubricate; carry spares.

## Etiquette
- Be on time, warm up away from set, and listen to the director and first AD.
- Do not **touch other puppets**.
- Keep quiet during takes and never leave rods on the floor.

## Check
Mechanisms work first take and the crew sees you as easy to work with.
