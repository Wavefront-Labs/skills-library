# Waldos and animatronic facial performance

**Goal:** expressive faces driven by a skilled performer.

## Waldos
- A **waldo** is a hand-worn or hand-held controller (named from a science-fiction story) whose sensors send positions to servos or cables.
- Calibrate its **range and speed**.

## Facial performance
- Map jaw, brows, eyelids, ears; **smooth** servo motion to avoid jitter.
- Practise **timing** of blinks and brow movement.

## Check
Observers read emotion from the face with no visible mechanics.
