# Live voice on set and puppet adr

**Goal:** a performance that works live and survives post.

## Live voice
- The voice performer stands near the puppeteer and **speaks the lines live**, giving a natural performance.
- Capture with **close mics** and consider noise from rods and movement.

## ADR
- Where audio is unusable, **re-record** while watching the take; match breath and mouth moves.
- Keep a record of takes.

## Check
ADR plays in sync with the visual take without audible changes in tone.
