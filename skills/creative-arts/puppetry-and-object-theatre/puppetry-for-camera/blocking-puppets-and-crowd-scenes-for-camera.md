# Blocking puppets and crowd scenes for camera

**Goal:** clear compositions and crowds that fit the space under the set.

## Blocking
- Work with the **camera frame**; mark positions per shot.
- Plan puppeteer positions so they do not collide.

## Crowds
- Stagger **heights and depths**; group puppeteers; **layer** foreground action and background reactions.

## Check
Run the scene with all puppets in frame and no operator clashes.
