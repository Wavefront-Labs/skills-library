# Real time capture and mocap puppeteers

**Goal:** a performer controlling a CG character live.

## Setup
- Use **mocap suit, gloves or controllers** feeding a game engine or real-time renderer.
- Map performer data to **rig controls** with smoothing.

## Performing
- Check **latency** and frame rate; practise with a monitor.
- Warm up and train in the rig as in a physical puppet.

## Check
Performer can hit marks and expression targets live.
