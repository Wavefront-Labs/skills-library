# Multi camera puppetry and puppet continuity

**Goal:** several cameras shoot one performance with consistent puppets.

## Multi-camera
- Plan **camera positions**, and where puppeteers hide for each.
- Provide **monitors** per puppeteer, with a switcher view.

## Continuity
- Keep a log: **puppet height, prop positions, costume details** per take.
- Photograph set-ups.

## Check
Cut between cameras with no visible mismatch.
