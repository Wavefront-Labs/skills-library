# Pre recording and syncing to playback

**Goal:** accurate lip-sync to a recorded voice.

## Pre-record
- Record the voice first in a studio, with the voice actor performing the **emotional shape**; keep alternates.
- Note timing and breath points.

## Playback sync
- On set, play the track through **earwigs or speakers**; puppeteers match mouth to the stressed syllables.
- Use a **clapper or count-in** to start together.
- Rehearse with the exact track and mark difficult words.

## Check
Review playback with sound on and off; mouth accents land on the stressed beats.
