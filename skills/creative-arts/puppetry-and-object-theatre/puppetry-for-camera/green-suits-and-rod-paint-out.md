# Green suits and rod paint out

**Goal:** puppeteers and rods vanish in post.

## Suits and rods
- Dress puppeteers in **chroma green or blue** (matching the screen) head to toe; cover rods in the same colour.
- Even lighting on the screen is vital.

## Paint-out
- Plan which rods **will be painted out** frame by frame, and avoid crossing the puppet's silhouette.
- Tell VFX when a rod cannot be avoided.

## Check
Test shot: key pulls clean with no green edges on the puppet.
