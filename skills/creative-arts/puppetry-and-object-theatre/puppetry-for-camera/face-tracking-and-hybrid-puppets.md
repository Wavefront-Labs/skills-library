# Face tracking and hybrid puppets

**Goal:** expressive digital faces and combined puppet workflows.

## Face tracking
- Use a camera or phone depth sensor; map expressions to **blendshapes**; calibrate neutral face.
- Add smoothing and thresholds.

## Hybrid puppets
- Combine a **physical puppet body** with CG face or effects.
- Align tracking and lighting between physical and digital parts.

## Check
Expressions track clearly and the combined puppet looks like one character.
