# Clean plates and tracking markers

**Goal:** give VFX what they need to remove rigs and track.

## Clean plate
- Shoot the same camera move **without the puppet and puppeteer** at the same lighting and lens; shoot several seconds.

## Tracking markers
- Put **small high-contrast markers** on puppets and rods, avoiding the face; photograph them and note positions.
- Keep a **VFX log** with lens, distance and settings.

## Check
VFX confirm the plates and markers are enough for clean removal.
