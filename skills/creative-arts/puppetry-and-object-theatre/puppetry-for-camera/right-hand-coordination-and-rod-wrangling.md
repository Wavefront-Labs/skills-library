# Right hand coordination and rod wrangling

**Goal:** support the lead puppeteer without being seen or in the way.

## Coordination
- Stand where you can see the lead's **head and eyes**; **follow the lead's focus**.
- Learn each gesture and prop before the take.

## Wrangling
- Manage **rods and cables** so they never cross the frame, pass behind the puppet and are ready for the next move.
- Take care of **costume**: fix cuffs, collars and fabric between takes.

## Check
Lead and assistant produce clean gestures with no snag or visible rod.
