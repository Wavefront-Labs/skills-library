# Monitor placement and eye line to lens

**Goal:** a puppeteer who can see the shot without moving the puppet.

## Monitors
- Put a small **monitor** where the puppeteer can glance without moving their head far, usually at chest height on a stand or clamped to the set.
- Set the image as **the camera sees it**; if the puppeteer works under the set, mirror or flip only if the crew has tested and agreed.
- Keep monitors out of lights to avoid glare.

## Eye-line
- **Look at the lens** only for direct address; otherwise the puppet's eyes go to the other actor's face, not their feet.
- Mark an **eye-line target** for a cutaway.
- Check that the puppet's eyes are visible in frame; adjust head tilt.

## Check
Play back a take; the puppet appears to look where the director intended.
