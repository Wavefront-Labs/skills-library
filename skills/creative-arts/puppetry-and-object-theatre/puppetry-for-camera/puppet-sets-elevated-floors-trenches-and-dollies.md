# Puppet sets elevated floors trenches and dollies

**Goal:** a set where puppets stand at the camera's eye while puppeteers work below.

## Elevated sets
- Raise the **set floor** to chest or head height so puppeteers stand underneath with arms up, or dig a **trench** in the stage floor.

## Hiding puppeteers
- Use **practical objects** (a counter, a pile of books) to hide puppeteers in the frame.

## Dollies and walls
- **Rolling stools or dollies** let puppeteers follow moving puppets.
- **Wild walls** lift out so operators and cameras can access.

## Check
Camera can move around the set with all puppeteers hidden and safe.
