# Cable and radio controlled animatronics

**Goal:** reliable mechanical or electronic control of a puppet head.

## Cable control
- **Bowden cables** run from lever handles to the head; adjust sheath lengths to avoid slack.
- Lubricate and keep curves gentle.

## Radio control
- **Servo heads** driven by a hobby RC transmitter or custom controller; map channels to eyes, brows, jaw.
- Use robust **batteries and fuses**; shield noise.

## Check
Test full range under load and note failure points; keep spares.
