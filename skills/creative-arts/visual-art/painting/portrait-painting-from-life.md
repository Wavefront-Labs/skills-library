# Portrait painting from life

**Goal:** an alla prima head study from a live sitter in one session (2-3 hours
with breaks), the core exercise of portrait painters (Portrait Society of America
competitions and workshops; Schmid).

## Setup
- One main light, high and to the side (about 45 degrees) so a clear shadow side forms;
  a plain mid-dark background.
- Sitter at your eye level, about 2-3 m away; easel beside so you can compare.
- Canvas or panel 30x40 cm to 40x50 cm, toned mid-value (thin burnt sienna or
  grey wiped on, dry).
- Breaks for the sitter every 20-25 minutes; tape the chair and mark the pose.

## Sequence
1. **Drawing (15-20 min)**: head envelope, tilt, centre line, eye line, nose base,
   mouth, chin, ear; shadow shapes. Thin paint.
2. **Shadow mass**: one transparent warm-dark mixture for the whole shadow side
   and the hair's shadow.
3. **Background** near the head, to judge edges and values.
4. **Light family**: big planes of the face (forehead, cheek, nose, chin) in
   their value and colour zones (see mixing-flesh-tones), side by side.
5. **Halftones** and transitions between light and shadow.
6. **Features**: eye sockets as shapes first, then lids, iris; nose base and
   nostril accents; mouth's corners and the line between lips. Keep them in the
   head's planes.
7. **Finish**: highlights (small, thick), darkest accents, edge decisions (hard at
   the focal point, lost into the background elsewhere).

## Working with the sitter
- Chat to keep the expression natural; draw the mouth when the sitter is relaxed.
- Keep a mirror to check the painting reversed.

## Gotchas
- Painting the eyes first: the head shape goes wrong around them.
- Chalky lights: too much white in flesh.
- Repainting the whole head after each break; check marks first.

## Verify
- [ ] Head shape and shadow pattern read as the sitter at a distance.
- [ ] Flesh zones and halftones present.
- [ ] Edges varied, focal point at the eyes or chosen feature.
