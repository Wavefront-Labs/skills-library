# Acrylic pouring and fluid techniques

**Goal:** controlled poured paintings that dry flat, uncracked and with clean
colour (Golden GAC 800 / Liquitex Pouring Medium guidance).

## Materials
- Fluid or soft-body acrylics, pouring medium (Liquitex Pouring Medium, Golden
  GAC 800, or Floetrol as a budget additive), cups, sticks, a level surface,
  gloves, plastic sheeting.
- Optional: silicone oil or dimethicone for cells; torch/heat gun.

## Consistency
- Mix paint with pouring medium (often 1 part paint to 1-3 parts medium depending
  on paint body). Add water sparingly.
- Target: like warm honey or motor oil; a drip from the stick should mound for a
  second before levelling.
- Let mixtures rest a few minutes to release air bubbles.

## Methods
- **Dirty pour / flip cup**: layer colours in one cup, place the canvas on top, flip,
  lift the cup; tilt to spread.
- **Puddle/ring pour**: pour colours in concentric puddles, tilt.
- **Swipe**: pour colours, then drag a thin layer of white across with a card.
- **Cells**: a few drops of silicone in some colours; brief heat from a torch brings
  cells up. Silicone residues must be removed (wipe with soap/alcohol) before varnishing.

## Drying
- Dry **level** and dust-free, 24-72 hours; thick pours can take longer.
- Prop on cups to let edges drip.

## Problems
| Problem | Cause |
|---------|-------|
| Crazing (cracks) | Too much water, thick layers, fast drying, wrong medium |
| Muddy colour | Overmixing complements; too much tilting |
| Paint slides off | Not level |
| Dull/oily patches | Silicone residue |

## Gotchas
- Large waste: mix a test quantity for a small canvas first and scale up from what it used.
- Pouring down drains: let waste dry and dispose as solid.

## Verify
- [ ] Consistent mixture consistency across colours.
- [ ] Dried level with no crazing.
- [ ] Silicone cleaned before varnish.
