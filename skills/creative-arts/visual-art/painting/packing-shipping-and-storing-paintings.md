# Packing, shipping and storing paintings

**Goal:** artworks arrive and survive in the condition they left the studio
(museum practice per NPS Conserve O Grams and conservation guidance).

## Before packing
- **Condition report**: photograph front, back, edges and any flaws; note date.
  Keep a copy; send one with the work.
- Paint must be dry enough; thick oil impasto can take months; do not ship
  touch-dry impasto wrapped tight.

## Wrapping order (a framed or stretched painting)
1. **Glassine** or acid-free tissue against the paint surface (never bubble wrap
   directly on paint: it imprints in warm conditions; never plastic on fresh oil).
2. **Corner protectors** (cardboard or foam).
3. **Rigid face board** (cardboard or foam board) over the front to stop punctures.
4. **Bubble wrap or foam**, bubbles facing out, sealed.
5. **Box**: double-wall cardboard with 5 cm of foam all round, or a **crate**
   (plywood with foam lining) for valuable or large works. Mark "fragile, this way up,
   do not lay flat" as appropriate.
- Glazed works: tape an X or grid across glass (low-tack tape) or use acrylic glazing
  for shipping.
- **Wet paintings**: travel frames / panel carriers that hold works apart face to face without contact.

## Shipping
- Fine art shippers for high values; for couriers, check whether they insure art
  (many exclude it) and buy separate fine art transit insurance.
- Keep tracking and photos; require signature.

## Storage
- Stable environment: roughly 18-22 C and 40-55 percent RH, avoiding rapid swings;
  away from outside walls, sunlight, radiators, basements and attics.
- Store vertically on padded racks, not leaning face-to-face; use dividers; keep off
  the floor.
- Cover against dust with breathable fabric or glassine, not sealed plastic.

## Gotchas
- Newspaper or bubble wrap touching paint.
- Stacking paintings face to back with stretcher bars pressing the canvas.

## Verify
- [ ] Condition report with photos made and sent.
- [ ] Surface protected by glassine and a rigid face.
- [ ] Transit insurance covers art.
