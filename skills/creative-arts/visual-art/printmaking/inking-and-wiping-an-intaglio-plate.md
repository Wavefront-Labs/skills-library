# Inking and wiping an intaglio plate

The plate is inked all over, then wiped so the ink stays only in the grooves and pits. Wiping controls the image as much as the biting does.

## Ink up
- Use stiff intaglio ink (e.g. Charbonnel, Gamblin, Akua for water-washable). Work it on the slab with a knife to loosen it; a drop of easy-wipe compound or plate oil if too stiff.
- Warm the plate slightly on a hotplate (optional) - ink flows into lines more easily.
- Push ink into the image with a **card scraper** (mat board offcut), rubber squeegee or dabber, in several directions so every line is filled.
- Scrape off the bulk of surface ink with the card.

## Tarlatan wipe
- **Tarlatan** (starched open-weave cotton) is bunched into a pad with a flat face. Use 2-3 pads: an inky one, a medium, and a cleaner one.
- Wipe in small circular or figure-eight movements with light pressure, the pad flat to the plate - never dragging across lines (that pulls ink out).
- Progress from the inky pad to the cleanest one until the image is clear and the plate surface has an even light film.

## Final wipe options
- **Hand wipe**: palm of the hand, lightly chalked with whiting, swept across the plate - polishes highlights to clean metal for crisp whites.
- **Plate tone**: leave a thin film for a warm tone overall, or selectively for atmosphere.
- **Paper wipe**: phone-book/newsprint pages flat under the hand for a clean surface without pulling lines.
- **Retroussage**: after wiping, lightly drag a soft muslin over the plate to pull a little ink out of the lines - softer, richer lines.
- Clean the **plate edges and bevels** with a rag - they print as dirty borders otherwise.

## A la poupee
- Apply different colours to different areas with small rolled felt or tarlatan "dolls" (poupees), wipe each area separately, and print in one pass.

## Gotchas
- **Overwiping** empties shallow lines and aquatint; underwiping prints a grey haze.
- Consistent wiping across an edition is the hard part - record the method on the proof.
- Lint from tarlatan if it isn't broken in; rub new tarlatan to soften it.

## Verify
- [ ] Lines fully filled before wiping.
- [ ] Wiping method noted for the edition.
- [ ] Bevels and edges clean.
