# Etching mordants

## Which mordant for which metal
| Mordant | Metals | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Ferric chloride** (FeCl3, a salt, not an acid) | Copper, brass (also zinc but messy) | No toxic gas; bites slowly and cleanly; sediment forms, so bite **face down** on supports or vertically in a tank. Stains everything orange-brown |
| **Edinburgh etch** (ferric chloride + citric acid) | Copper | Faster, cleaner bite than plain ferric, less sediment |
| **Saline sulphate** (copper sulphate + salt in water) | Zinc, aluminium, mild steel | Low hazard ("Bordeaux etch"); deposits copper on the plate - brush it off; bites quickly |
| **Nitric acid** (diluted, e.g. 1:8 to 1:10 for zinc) | Zinc, copper (stronger mix) | Traditional, fast; releases toxic nitrogen dioxide fumes - needs fume extraction; bubbles must be feathered off; generates heat. Many studios have dropped it |

Always **add acid to water**, never water to acid.

## Running a bath
- Label the tank with mordant, strength and date. Keep separate baths per metal: putting zinc in a copper ferric bath contaminates it.
- Ferric strength is often sold at about 40-45 Baume; it can be diluted for gentler bites.
- Keep a **test strip** per session; bites slow as the bath is exhausted or cold. Warmer baths bite faster.
- Rinse plates in water then dry before stop-out.

## Safety
- Nitrile gloves, eye protection, apron; ventilated area or extraction (essential for nitric).
- A spill kit with sodium bicarbonate or soda ash for neutralising.
- Read the safety data sheet; follow local hazardous-substance rules (e.g. COSHH in the UK).

## Disposal
- **Never pour spent mordant down the drain** - it carries dissolved copper and other metals.
- Ferric: neutralise with sodium carbonate (washing soda) to precipitate the metals as sludge; let settle, check pH, decant the clear liquid per local rules, dispose of the sludge as hazardous waste.
- Saline sulphate: add scrap aluminium or zinc to drop out the copper; then neutralise and dispose of solids as hazardous waste.

## Verify
- [ ] Mordant matches the plate metal; bath labelled.
- [ ] Test strip for timing this session.
- [ ] Neutralise-and-settle disposal, no drain disposal.
