# Mokuhanga (Japanese water-based woodblock)

Water-based pigments with rice paste, printed by hand onto damp washi with a baren. Transparent, luminous colour, no press.

## Materials
- **Blocks**: shina (Japanese basswood) plywood for most work, or cherry (yamazakura) for fine line keys.
- **Washi**: sized (dosa-treated) kozo paper such as Echizen hosho. Sizing (glue + alum, "dosa") stops pigment bleeding.
- **Pigment**: watercolour or dispersed pigments, sumi ink for black.
- **Nori**: rice or wheat starch paste - a small dab mixed with the pigment on the block gives even, flat colour.
- **Brushes**: maru-bake (round brush) to spread pigment and paste on the block.
- **Baren**: hon-baren (traditional coiled cord) or a ball-bearing baren.

## Kento
- An L-shaped notch (kagi kento) at one bottom corner and a straight notch (hikitsuke) along the same bottom edge, carved into every block in the same position. Paper is laid into them by hand - precise registration with no jig.

## Process
1. **Dampen paper** the day before: mist or brush sheets, stack between damp newsprint, wrap in plastic overnight so moisture is even. Paper must be evenly damp throughout printing.
2. Lightly moisten the block.
3. Brush on pigment and a dab of nori; spread evenly with the maru-bake.
4. Place paper into the kento, lower it, and print with the baren in a zigzag and circular pattern; for light colours use less pressure.
5. Print each colour across the whole edition while paper is damp; keep the stack covered between colours.
6. **Bokashi** (gradation): wet part of the block, apply pigment at one edge, and blend with the brush so colour fades - a hallmark of ukiyo-e skies.
7. Dry prints flat between boards and blotters.

## Gotchas
- Paper drying out mid-edition shrinks it and wrecks registration.
- Too much nori leaves a mottled, shiny surface; too little gives blotchy colour.
- Unsized paper blurs and feathers.

## Verify
- [ ] Kento carved identically on each block.
- [ ] Paper evenly damp, stack covered between passes.
- [ ] Test print each colour for paste and pressure.
