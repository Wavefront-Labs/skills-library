# Setting etching press pressure and blankets

**Goal:** even, correct pressure across the plate for clean, full prints
(Grabowski and Fick; press manufacturers' guides).

## Blankets (woven felts), top down on the press bed
1. **Pusher** (top, thicker, firmer, against the roller).
2. **Cushion** (thick, soft) - pushes damp paper into the plate's lines.
3. **Sizing catcher** (thin, fine, closest to the paper) - absorbs size and
   moisture; washed most often.
Some setups use two blankets or synthetic blankets; follow the press maker. Keep
blankets flat, dry and clean; wash in cool water without fabric softener.

## Setting pressure
1. Place a plate of the thickness to be printed (with tissue and paper) centred.
2. Lower both pressure screws equally until the roller just grips; then turn each
   down the same amount (e.g. a quarter turn) and do a test pull.
3. Check the proof: full ink transfer in lines (intaglio needs high pressure),
   even embossment, plate mark crisp on both sides. Uneven = adjust one side.
4. Record the setting (gauge readings or turns from contact) for each plate type.

## Adjusting per process
- **Intaglio**: high pressure, damp paper, full blankets.
- **Relief on an etching press**: much lower pressure, fewer or no blankets, use
  type-high packing or a thin board; too much pressure squashes ink.
- **Monotype**: moderate pressure.
- **Chine-colle**: same as intaglio.

## Maintenance
- Keep the bed and roller clean of ink; lift pressure when not in use (avoids flat
  spots on blankets and stress on the roller).
- Lubricate gears/bearings as the maker specifies.

## Gotchas
- Screws unequal: one side of the print light.
- Plate edges not bevelled: cut blankets and paper.
- Damp blankets left under pressure: mildew and shape loss.

## Verify
- [ ] Test print shows even, full transfer.
- [ ] Pressure settings recorded per plate thickness.
- [ ] Pressure released after the session.
