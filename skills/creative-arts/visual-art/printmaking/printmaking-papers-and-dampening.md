# Printmaking papers and dampening

## Choose
- **Cotton rag mould-made papers** (Somerset, Hahnemuhle Copperplate, BFK Rives, Arches 88): acid-free, strong; soft papers for intaglio, smoother for litho and screen.
- **Japanese papers (washi)**: kozo, gampi, mitsumata - thin, strong long fibres, for relief and mokuhanga, chine-colle.
- **Weight**: 250-300 gsm for intaglio; lighter for relief by hand; Arches 88 (unsized) for screenprint because it shouldn't be dampened.
- **Sizing**: internal or surface size (gelatin, alkyl ketene dimer) controls absorbency. Unsized papers soak quickly; hard-sized need longer.
- Mark the **grain direction** (tear test or bend test): run grain parallel to the press direction or the long edge for consistent stretch.

## Which processes dampen
- **Intaglio**: always damp - paper must be soft enough to be pushed into the lines.
- **Litho**: dry or slightly damp depending on paper and printer preference.
- **Relief**: usually dry; damp for some hand-printing on heavier paper.
- **Screenprint**: dry.
- **Mokuhanga**: damp, evenly.

## Dampening
1. **Soak** in a clean tray of water: soft unsized papers a few minutes; heavily sized papers 20 minutes to an hour (check maker guidance).
2. Drain, then **blot** between clean blotters with a roller or by hand until the surface is matt with no sheen of water.
3. Or make a **damp pack**: sponge or spray sheets, interleave, wrap in plastic overnight - very even moisture, good for editions.
4. Handle by diagonally opposite corners or with paper tabs (clean fingers only).

## Drying prints
- Stack between blotters and boards (or tissue/newsprint and blotters) under weight; change blotters after a few hours. Or tape to a board with gummed tape (affects margins).
- Relief and screen prints on dry paper go to a drying rack.

## Gotchas
- Shiny wet paper squeezes water into the lines - blot properly.
- Over-soaked soft paper tears and pills.
- Fingermarks on damp paper print permanently.

## Verify
- [ ] Paper chosen for process; grain marked.
- [ ] Soak time matched to sizing; blotted matt.
- [ ] Prints dried flat under weight.
