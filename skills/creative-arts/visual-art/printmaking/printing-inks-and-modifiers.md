# Printing inks and modifiers

## Ink families
| Type | Examples | Clean-up | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oil-based | Charbonnel, Gamblin, Graphic Chemical | Vegetable oil then soap; or solvent | Richest blacks, long open time; dries by oxidation over a day or more |
| Water-washable oil | Caligo Safe Wash, Cranfield | Soap and water | Behaves like oil ink, cleans with soap; common in schools and studios |
| Soy/vegetable-based | Akua (intaglio and monotype) | Soap and water | Doesn't dry on the slab easily; dries by absorption into paper |
| Water-based relief | Speedball, Essdee | Water | Fast drying, cheap; can be streaky and cockle thin paper |
| Water-based screen | Speedball, Permaset, System 3 | Water | Fast drying in mesh; use retarder |

## Modifiers (oil-based and safe-wash)
- **Transparent base / extender**: makes colours transparent without changing stiffness; essential for overprinting and reduction prints.
- **Burnt plate oil (light/medium/heavy)**: linseed-based; loosens stiff ink and makes intaglio wiping easier.
- **Easy wipe compound** (for intaglio): helps wiping without changing colour.
- **Magnesium carbonate**: stiffens ink, shortens it, reduces spreading; used in litho to fight scumming.
- **Tack reducer**: lowers tack so ink doesn't pluck paper fibres.
- **Driers** (cobalt): speed oxidation drying; use a few drops - too much makes ink skin on the slab.
- Water-based inks: **retarder** and printing medium/extender.

## Mixing colour
- Mix on a slab with palette knives; weigh or count measured dabs and **write the recipe** for re-mixing.
- Mix enough for the whole edition plus 25%.
- Test proof: colour shifts as ink dries, especially transparent layers on coloured paper.

## Gotchas
- Mixing incompatible types (water-based into oil) ruins the batch.
- Too much oil gives haloes and slow drying; too little stiff tacky ink tears paper.
- Skins on tins: press clingfilm onto the ink surface before closing.

## Verify
- [ ] Ink type suits process and paper.
- [ ] Recipe recorded, quantity sufficient.
- [ ] Proof checked when dry.
