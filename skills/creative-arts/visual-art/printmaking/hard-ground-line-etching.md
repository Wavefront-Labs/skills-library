# Hard-ground line etching

## Prepare the plate
1. **Bevel** all four edges with a file or bevel tool at about 45 degrees and round the corners - sharp edges cut paper and blankets.
2. **Degrease**: whiting (calcium carbonate) and a little water or ammonia, or soy-based degreaser, rubbed on and rinsed; water should sheet off without beading.
3. Dry; protect the back with contact paper or a stop-out (unless both sides go into the mordant).

## Lay the ground
- **Liquid hard ground** (e.g. asphaltum-based or acrylic hard ground): pour or brush on, let dry level.
- **Ball ground**: warm the plate on a hotplate, melt the ground on, roll thin and even with a leather or rubber roller; optionally smoke it with tapers for a black surface that shows lines clearly.
- The ground must be thin, even and pinhole-free.

## Draw
- Use etching needles (from fine to blunt) to scratch through the ground so the metal shows; don't need to dig into the metal. Line thickness comes partly from the needle, mostly from biting time.
- Transfer a design with carbon or chalk, or trace onto the ground.

## Bite
- Immerse in the mordant appropriate for the metal (see the mordants skill). Keep a **test strip** to time bites.
- **Stop-out**: after a light bite, paint over lines that should stay fine with stop-out varnish, then continue biting the rest. Repeat for 3-4 line weights. Typical ranges (ferric on copper): minutes for fine lines, up to around an hour for deep lines - calibrate with your bath.
- Brush away bubbles with a feather (nitric) or keep the plate face-down/vertical (ferric) so sediment falls away.

## Clean and proof
- Remove ground with the appropriate solvent (vegetable oil and soap for some safer grounds, or the maker's remover), clean, and pull a **state proof**.
- Rework: re-ground and add lines, burnish/scrape out unwanted ones.

## Gotchas
- **Foul biting**: pitting where the ground was thin, scratched or pinholed.
- **Overbiting** fine close lines merges them into a dark mass.
- Fingerprints under the ground resist it - degrease thoroughly.

## Verify
- [ ] Bevelled, degreased plate; even pinhole-free ground.
- [ ] Bite times tested on a strip.
- [ ] State proof pulled before reworking.
