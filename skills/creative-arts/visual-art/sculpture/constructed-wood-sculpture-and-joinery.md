# Constructed wood sculpture and joinery

**Goal:** sculpture assembled from cut and joined wood rather than carved from a
block (sculpture studio practice; USDA Wood Handbook for wood behaviour).

## Materials
- **Solid timber**: seasoned, stable species (pine, poplar, ash, oak, walnut);
  moisture content around 8-12 percent for indoor work.
- **Plywood / birch ply**: stable sheets for planes and boxes; edges can be shown or
  veneered.
- **MDF**: smooth for painted forms; heavy; weak at edges and with water.

## Joints
| Joint | Use |
|-------|-----|
| Butt + screws/glue | Fast, hidden internal structure |
| Dowel / biscuit / Domino | Aligned, strong panel joins |
| Mortise and tenon | Strong frames, visible craft |
| Halving / lap | Crossing members |
| Pocket screws | Quick hidden joins in frames |
- PVA wood glue (Titebond type) needs clamping and clean, close-fitting faces; epoxy
  for gaps and dissimilar materials.

## Stacked lamination
- Build big curved masses by stacking and gluing layers of ply or boards, each cut
  (bandsaw/jigsaw) to a contour from a sliced drawing, then carve/sand the stack
  smooth. Stagger joints and glue fully to avoid voids.

## Wood movement
- Solid wood swells and shrinks **across the grain** with humidity (USDA Wood
  Handbook gives shrinkage values per species); do not glue wide boards rigidly
  cross-grain; allow movement in long assemblies.

## Finishing
- Sand through grits (80-120-180-240); raise grain with water before final sanding
  for water-based finishes.
- Oils (Danish, hardwax), shellac, water-based lacquer, or paint over primer. Exterior
  work needs suitable timber (oak, cedar, treated) and exterior finishes.

## Safety
Push sticks, guards and dust extraction; hearing and eye protection; MDF and
hardwood dust masks.

## Gotchas
- Gluing end grain: weak; add dowels or mechanical fixings.
- Cross-grain glued panels cracking with seasonal movement.

## Verify
- [ ] Joints chosen for load and visibility.
- [ ] Laminations void-free.
- [ ] Movement allowed for in solid-wood assemblies.
