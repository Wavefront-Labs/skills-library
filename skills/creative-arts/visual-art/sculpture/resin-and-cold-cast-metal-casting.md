# Resin and cold-cast metal casting

## Resin types
- **Polyurethane casting resin** (e.g. Smooth-On Smooth-Cast 300 series): 1:1 by volume or weight, short pot life (minutes), demould in 10-60 minutes. Moisture-sensitive - humid air and damp moulds cause foaming. Casts are opaque.
- **Polyester resin**: cheaper, strong smell (styrene), shrinks more, used with fibreglass and for traditional cold-cast. Catalysed with MEKP at about 1-2%.
- **Epoxy**: low shrink, slow, for clear and small parts.
- Wear nitrile gloves, eye protection; organic-vapour respirator for polyester; ventilate.

## Cold-cast bronze (or copper, brass, iron, aluminium)
Real metal powder in resin, polished to look like cast metal.
1. Mould clean and dry (silicone needs no release for PU, but a light one extends life).
2. **Dust coat**: brush dry metal powder into the mould and tip out the excess, or mix powder into a first resin coat at a high loading (e.g. roughly equal volume, or as much as the resin wets - check the supplier's ratio).
3. Brush/slush a **filled gel coat** (resin + metal powder) to 1-2 mm over all surfaces.
4. Back it with plain resin (or resin + filler like aluminium trihydrate/calcium carbonate) to build strength, or fill solid for small pieces.
5. Demould, then **burnish**: fine steel wool (0000) or fine wet-and-dry to expose the metal; apply patina chemicals (real metal reacts - e.g. liver of sulphur on copper/bronze powder); wax and buff.

## Rotocasting hollow pieces
- Pour a measured small amount of resin into a closed mould and rotate it on all axes until it gels, repeat 2-3 times. Saves weight and resin, and fast PU resins make it practical by hand.

## Bubbles and defects
- Brush the first coat into detail; blow out bubbles; vibrate.
- **Pressure pot** (around 40-60 psi) during cure shrinks bubbles to invisible; vacuum degassing doesn't suit fast resins.
- Warm moulds slightly in cold studios; cold slows or prevents cure.

## Gotchas
- **Thick masses of fast resin** exotherm heavily - can crack, yellow or damage the mould. Build up in layers or use slower resin.
- Off-ratio mixing gives soft or sticky casts.
- Not burnishing enough leaves the cast dull grey-brown rather than metallic.

## Verify
- [ ] Mould dry; resin and parts at room temperature.
- [ ] Metal-filled coat continuous over the whole surface before backing.
- [ ] Burnished, patinated and waxed.
