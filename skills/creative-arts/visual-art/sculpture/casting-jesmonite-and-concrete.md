# Casting Jesmonite and concrete

## Jesmonite AC100
Water-based acrylic resin + mineral powder (gypsum-based). Low odour, no solvents, lighter and tougher than plaster, can be used outdoors with the right grade and sealer (follow maker guidance for exterior use).
- Ratio: **2.5 parts powder to 1 part liquid by weight** (base mix). Add powder to liquid and mix with a drill mixer for about 1-2 minutes until smooth.
- Working time around 15-20 minutes; demould roughly 1-2 hours at room temperature; full properties after days.
- **Laminating**: brush a gel coat (2-3 mm) into the mould; when tacky, apply a second coat with **quadaxial glass-fibre matting** torn into patches, stippled in; build 2-3 layers for strength. Thickened with a thixotropic additive for vertical surfaces.
- **Pigments**: Jesmonite pigment dispersions at a few percent; marbling and terrazzo with chips; metal powders for cold-cast effects.
- Moulds: silicone, polyurethane rubber, sealed MDF or plaster; no release needed for silicone.

## Concrete
- For sculpture use fine mixes: **sand:cement around 2:1 or 3:1**, water-cement ratio about 0.4-0.5; add superplasticiser to improve flow without extra water.
- **GFRC** (glass-fibre reinforced concrete): face coat sprayed or brushed, then backer coat with alkali-resistant (AR) glass fibres - thin, strong panels.
- Vibrate or tap to remove air; cast solid pieces with steel rebar or mesh kept at least 2-3 cm from the surface (corrosion causes spalling).
- **Cure damp**: wrap in plastic for at least 3-7 days; fast drying cracks and weakens it.
- Seal outdoor pieces with a breathable silane/siloxane sealer.

## Gotchas
- Jesmonite over-mixed or under-measured gives weak, dusty casts - weigh everything.
- Concrete is alkaline and caustic: gloves and eye protection; wash skin.
- Rebar too near the surface rusts and bursts the concrete.
- Heavy outdoor work needs engineered fixings to the plinth.

## Verify
- [ ] Powder/liquid or water/cement weighed.
- [ ] Laminate includes fibre layers for hollow casts.
- [ ] Concrete damp-cured, sealed for outdoors.
