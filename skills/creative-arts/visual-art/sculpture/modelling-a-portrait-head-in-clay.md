# Modelling a portrait head in clay

## Setup
- **Head armature**: a vertical pipe or wooden post on a heavy base with a loop of aluminium wire or a wooden peg in the cranium mass. Keep it well back from the face so you can carve features; hang a butterfly in the skull mass.
- Sitter's head at the same height as the clay; both on turntables, or move around the sitter.
- Callipers (large outside callipers) and a ruler. Life size means you measure the sitter directly.

## Key measurements (take on the sitter, transfer to the clay)
- Chin to top of the head, and chin to the pit of the neck.
- Ear hole (tragus) to ear hole across the head - sets the width.
- Ear hole to nose tip, to the brow, to the chin - fixes the face's projection, which is where beginners go wrong.
- Back of the skull to the brow (the head is much deeper front to back than people expect).

## Sequence
1. Build an egg-shaped mass to the measured dimensions, then set the **profile** from the side: brow, nose, upper lip, chin, the slope of the forehead and jaw.
2. Turn 90 degrees: set the width at temples, cheekbones and jaw; then the three-quarter views.
3. **Planes of the head**: block in the brow ridge, eye sockets, cheekbone plane, the front of the muzzle, the jaw corner. Check with raking light.
4. Place features: eyes in sockets (eyeballs are spheres set back; lids wrap over them), nose wedge, mouth around the barrel of the teeth, ears between brow and nose base lines and behind the jaw.
5. **Likeness** lives in proportions and the big shapes (skull shape, jaw, the spacing of features), not in eyelashes. Compare silhouettes against the sitter from many angles.
6. Finish: unify the surface; decide on eye treatment (incised iris and pupil, or smooth classical eye).

## Gotchas
- **Flat face**: the face sits on a curve; the eye corners are well behind the nose bridge.
- **Neck too thin or vertical**: the neck leans forward from the shoulders.
- **Ears too high/forward**.
- For firing, hollow the head (cut open, scoop to an even wall, rejoin) - no armature.

## Verify
- [ ] Ear-to-nose and ear-to-chin measurements match the sitter.
- [ ] Profile and front silhouettes match from several angles.
- [ ] Armature nowhere near the face surface.
