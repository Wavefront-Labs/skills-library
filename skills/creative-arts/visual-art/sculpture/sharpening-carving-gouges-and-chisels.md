# Sharpening carving gouges and chisels

A carving edge must be sharp enough to slice end grain cleanly. Dull tools tear, need force, and slip.

## Geometry
- **Bevel angle**: about 20-25 degrees for wood carving (lower for soft woods, higher for hard woods and mallet work). The bevel should be **flat or slightly convex**, not rounded over.
- A small **inside bevel** (micro-bevel on the inner face, a few degrees) helps gouges come out of deep cuts.
- Edge square to the shank (or the intended skew).

## Steps
1. **Grind** only if the edge is chipped or the bevel is wrong: slow-speed wet grinder or a coarse stone. Keep steel cool - blue colour means the temper is lost.
2. **Hone the outside bevel**: on a flat stone (1000 then 4000-8000 grit water stones, or oilstones/diamond plates), rock and roll the gouge across the stone so the whole sweep of the bevel contacts, keeping the bevel angle constant. Raise a small **burr** along the whole edge.
3. **Hone the inside** with a **slipstone** shaped to the sweep (or a rolled abrasive on dowel): flat to the inside face, removing the burr.
4. **Strop**: leather charged with honing compound (green chromium oxide), pulling the tool backward (edge trailing); profiled leather or MDF for inside curves. Strop frequently while carving.

## V-tool (parting tool)
- Hone each wing as a flat chisel. The point where they meet tends to form a **hook** (a small nub). Remove it by honing the outside of the keel at the junction until the two edges meet cleanly at a point.

## Test
- Cut across the end grain of soft pine: a sharp tool leaves a shiny, crumb-free surface.
- Look straight at the edge under light: any **reflected line** is a dull spot.

## Gotchas
- Rocking inconsistently rounds the bevel ("dubbed"), which raises the cutting angle.
- Overheating on a dry high-speed grinder ruins the temper.
- Fingers in line with the edge on the strop: work away from yourself.

## Verify
- [ ] Consistent bevel at the chosen angle.
- [ ] Burr removed from the inside.
- [ ] Clean end-grain cut and no reflecting edge.
