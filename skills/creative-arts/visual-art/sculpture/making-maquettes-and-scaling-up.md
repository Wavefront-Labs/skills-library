# Making maquettes and scaling up

A **maquette** is a small model used to decide the form, test views and structure, and show clients - before committing to full-size materials and cost.

## Make the maquette
- **Scale**: pick a simple ratio to the final (1:10, 1:5, 1:4). Write it on the base.
- **Materials**: oil clay, wax, card, foam board, wire, cardboard, wood offcuts, 3D print. Fast and changeable matters more than finish.
- Include a **scale figure** and the base/plinth, and for site works the surroundings (a photo backdrop or a model of the site).
- Photograph from eye level of a scaled viewer (low camera), not from above.
- For metal or constructed works make the maquette in the same logic (card for sheet, wire for rod) so it can be fabricated.

## Scale it up
- **Grid / slices**: draw a grid over front and side photographs (or slice the 3D model in software into contour profiles), enlarge onto plywood or foam sections, stack and fair them into a rough.
- **Frame and plumb lines** (see pointing-machine skill) for clay enlargements over an armature.
- **Proportional callipers** for key points.
- **3D scan + CNC foam**: scan, scale, mill in EPS/polyurethane foam in sections; coat with clay or hard-coat for moulding or direct finishing. Fast and accurate for large public work.
- **Fabrication drawings**: for steel, convert the maquette into dimensioned drawings and part lists; an engineer checks loads.

## Revise at full size
- Big forms read differently: surface detail becomes busy or too thin, edges need more weight, small gaps become dangerous traps. Plan time for revisions at 1:1.
- Check sightlines and silhouette at the real viewing distance.

## Gotchas
- Maquettes hide structural problems - a thin limb that stands at 1:10 bends at 1:1 (weight grows with the cube of scale, strength with the square).
- Clients approve the maquette: keep changes documented and agreed.

## Verify
- [ ] Scale written on the maquette and drawings.
- [ ] Structural check of the full-size version.
- [ ] Full-size viewing check before final finishing.
