# Stone carving tools and roughing out

**Goal:** remove stone efficiently and safely from a block toward a planned form
(Penny, *The Materials of Sculpture*; stone carving studio practice).

## Stones for starting
- Soft: soapstone (talc; check for asbestos-free sources), alabaster, some
  limestones; carve with rasps and hand tools.
- Medium/hard: limestone, sandstone, marble; need steel or carbide tools and a
  hammer (lump hammer, 1-1.5 kg).
- Inspect for cracks ("vents"): tap with a hammer; a dull sound or water tracking
  shows flaws.

## Tool sequence
1. **Pitching tool**: thick edge to break off large corners and flats.
2. **Point chisel**: held at about 45 degrees, struck to break stone in grooves;
   the main roughing tool.
3. **Claw (tooth) chisel**: refines the surface in cross-hatched strokes, shaping planes.
4. **Flat chisel**: final planes and edges.
5. **Rasps and rifflers**: shape and smooth.
6. **Abrasives**: wet-and-dry paper or diamond pads, coarse to fine (e.g. 120 to
   1500+) to polish.
Pneumatic hammers and angle grinders with diamond discs speed roughing; they
greatly increase dust and noise.

## Roughing out
1. Draw the main views (front, side, top) of the maquette onto the block faces.
2. Remove waste from the outermost points inward, working all around the block;
   do not finish one side.
3. Keep the form as big planes; leave material for changes.
4. Measure from the maquette with callipers or a pointing machine for accuracy
   (see pointing-machine skill when needed).
5. Leave a base or supports until late for stability.

## Safety
- **Silica dust** (sandstone, granite; some in marble/limestone) causes silicosis:
  wet methods, extraction, and an FFP3/P100 respirator; HSE and OSHA have
  stone-working guidance.
- Eye protection (flying chips), hearing protection with power tools, gloves for
  rasping, steel-toe boots for heavy blocks.

## Gotchas
- Striking toward the edge of the block: big unplanned breaks. Work from edges inward.
- Carving undercuts too early: weakens areas you may need.

## Verify
- [ ] Block checked for flaws before starting.
- [ ] Roughed out evenly from all sides.
- [ ] Dust and eye protection used throughout.
