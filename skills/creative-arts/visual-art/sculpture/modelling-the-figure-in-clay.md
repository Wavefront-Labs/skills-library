# Modelling the figure in clay

Classic studio sequence (Lanteri and academy practice) for a figure of roughly 45-90 cm from a model on a stand.

## Setup
- Model and sculpture on **turntables**, sculpture at the model's eye height relative to you.
- Mark the model's foot positions with tape so the pose can be reset after every break.
- Light from one main direction; a raking light shows planes.
- Armature fixed at the pelvis (see the armature skill); the supporting leg directly under the pit of the neck in a contrapposto pose.

## Sequence
1. **Gesture first**: press clay onto the armature to set the line of action, the tilt of pelvis and shoulders (opposite in contrapposto) and the head over the weight-bearing foot. Check the plumb line from the pit of the neck.
2. **Big masses**: ribcage, pelvis and head as three blocks; get their tilt and twist right relative to each other before any limbs are fleshed out.
3. **Profiles, not views**: turn both stands together in small steps (e.g. 8 or 16 positions) and correct the outer **silhouette** at each one. Sculpture is a sum of profiles.
4. **Proportion checks** with callipers or dividers: head units (about 7.5 heads standing), matching key points (nipples, navel, pubic arch, knee, malleoli) to the model's.
5. **Planes**: establish front, side and back planes and the corners between them before rounding. Square forms read better than balloons.
6. **Anatomy**: add muscles as they act in the pose - stretched side long and flat, compressed side folded.
7. **Surface**: finish with a wire loop or rake at cross-angles, then rubber kidney or soft brush. Keep the surface unified; over-detailing one area breaks the whole.

## Gotchas
- **Working only from the front** gives a flat relief-like figure. Turn constantly.
- **Starting the face or hands early**: they shift when the mass moves. Leave them as blocks until late.
- **Water clay drying**: wrap in plastic every break; dried arms crack off.
- **Thin ankles** carry no load in clay - the backiron must hold the weight.

## Verify
- [ ] Head is over the supporting foot (plumb from pit of neck).
- [ ] Silhouette checked from at least eight angles.
- [ ] Proportions checked with callipers against the model.
- [ ] Surface treatment is consistent over the whole figure.
