# Plaster casting from a mould

## Choose the gypsum
- **Plaster of Paris / pottery plaster**: soft, cheap, for studies and moulds.
- **Fine casting plaster**: smoother, harder.
- **Gypsum cements** (USG Hydrocal, Hydrostone, Ultracal): much harder and stronger, less water, good for durable casts and outdoor-ish use when sealed. Follow the maker's **water:powder ratio by weight** (e.g. around 32-45 parts water per 100 plaster depending on product) - less water = stronger.

## Mixing
1. Weigh water (clean, room temperature) into a flexible bowl.
2. **Sift plaster into water** by hand until it peaks above the surface, or weigh it to ratio.
3. Let it **slake** 1-2 minutes, then mix from the bottom without whipping in air (hand or slow drill mixer) for 1-3 minutes until creamy.
4. Tap the bowl to raise bubbles.

## Solid pour (small pieces)
- Brush or swirl a thin **print coat** into the mould detail, then pour from a low point in a thin stream, tapping or vibrating to release bubbles.

## Hollow cast (heads, figures)
- Pour a mix in, **slush** it around the mould, pour excess out; repeat 2-3 coats as it thickens.
- Lay strips of **scrim** (jute or fibreglass) dipped in plaster into the wet layer for strength; add a wooden or steel bar armature in long pieces.
- Reinforce seams where mould pieces join.

## Demould and dry
- Wait until the plaster has heated up and cooled again (the exotherm), usually 30-60 minutes for casting plaster, longer for gypsum cements.
- Demould gently; clean seam lines with a riffler or surform while still "green".
- Dry fully (days to weeks, depending on thickness) before sealing or painting; trapped water causes paint failure.

## Gotchas
- **Stirring after it starts to set** makes it weak and crumbly; mix a fresh batch instead.
- **Plaster in drains** sets in the pipes - wash bowls in a bucket and let it settle.
- **Hot burns**: never cast a body part in a closed plaster mould; heat of setting can cause serious burns.
- Plaster is not weatherproof; use gypsum cement sealed, or jesmonite/concrete for outdoors.

## Verify
- [ ] Weighed water:plaster ratio for the product.
- [ ] Print coat first; no voids at detail.
- [ ] Hollow casts reinforced with scrim and bar.
