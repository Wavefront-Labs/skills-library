# Making a plaster waste mould

A waste mould gives **one cast** and is destroyed getting it out. It is the traditional way to turn a water-clay original into a plaster positive, quickly and cheaply, while the clay is still wet.

## Materials
- Pottery or casting plaster (e.g. Fine Casting Plaster, Plaster of Paris), water, flexible bowls.
- **Brass shims** (thin brass strip cut into tabs) or a clay wall to divide the mould.
- Colouring for the first coat: a little blue/red powder pigment or ochre, so you know when you're close when chipping out.
- Scrim (jute/hessian) or fibreglass tissue and iron or wooden bars for reinforcement.
- Release for the casting stage: soft soap, or diluted washing-up liquid, or a wax/release agent.

## Steps
1. **Plan the seam**: along a line where both halves can pull off without undercuts (often over the ears and crown of a head). The back section may be smaller.
2. Push **shims** into the wet clay along the seam, overlapping, standing about 2-3 cm proud. Cut small notches in them for keys, or put keys in the plaster later.
3. **First (coloured) coat**: mix plaster (sift into water until islands form, rest, stir without whipping air), flick it onto the clay with fingers or a brush to get every detail and no bubbles, about 3-5 mm thick.
4. **Back coat** (uncoloured): build up to roughly 2-4 cm, embedding scrim, and lay iron or wooden bars across large spans.
5. When the plaster has set (warms then cools), remove the shims and prise the sections apart with wooden wedges and water poured into the seam.
6. Dig out the clay, wash the mould, repair any small holes.
7. **Release**: coat inside with soft soap (work it in, sponge off excess, repeat) so the cast parts from the mould.
8. Tie the sections together, seal seams with clay or plaster on the outside, and cast (pour/slush plaster, or lay up).
9. **Chip out** with a blunt chisel and mallet once set - work away from the cast. When you see the coloured coat, slow down; under it is the cast.

## Gotchas
- **Air bubbles** in the first coat become lumps on the cast. Flick, don't pour.
- **Inadequate release** fuses mould and cast; you can't tell where to stop.
- **Chipping straight toward the cast** gouges it. Hold the chisel at a low angle, small taps.

## Verify
- [ ] Seam line has no undercuts on either side.
- [ ] Coloured first coat is complete over the whole surface.
- [ ] Mould interior released before casting.
