# Welding steel sculpture

**Goal:** join mild steel parts into a sound, finished sculpture, using the
methods taught in sculpture metal shops (AWS and maker training material).

## Processes
- **MIG (GMAW)**: wire-fed, easiest to learn, fast; the studio default for mild
  steel. Flux-core MIG works outdoors without gas but is messier.
- **TIG (GTAW)**: precise, clean welds, stainless and aluminium; slower, harder.
- **Oxy-acetylene**: welding, brazing, heating to bend; needs training for gas safety.
- Stick (SMAW): rugged, thick sections, outdoor work.

## Workflow
1. **Design and cut**: plan with a maquette; cut with angle grinder (cut-off disc),
   bandsaw, plasma cutter or oxy-fuel.
2. **Clean**: grind mill scale, rust, paint and oil off weld zones (bright metal).
3. **Fit-up**: clamp parts with magnets/clamps; small gaps for thin stock, bevel
   thicker stock (above about 6 mm) for penetration.
4. **Tack**: small tack welds at intervals to hold the assembly; check alignment;
   adjust before full welding.
5. **Weld in sequence**: short welds alternating sides and locations (stitching,
   backstepping) to balance heat and reduce distortion.
6. **Grind and finish**: flap discs to blend welds where desired; or leave them
   expressive.
7. **Protect**: rust patina sealed with wax or lacquer; paint (zinc primer + topcoat)
   for outdoor works; or hot-dip galvanise.

## MIG settings
Set voltage and wire speed per the machine's chart for material thickness and wire
diameter (e.g. 0.8 mm wire for thin sculpture steel); test on scrap of the same
thickness; listen for a steady "bacon frying" sound.

## Safety
- Auto-darkening helmet (shade around 10-13 for MIG), leather gloves, flame-resistant
  clothing (cotton/wool; no synthetics), boots.
- Fume extraction (welding fume is a carcinogen per IARC; HSE requires control);
  never weld galvanised or painted steel without removing coating and extraction.
- Fire watch: clear flammables, have an extinguisher, check for smouldering after work.
- Grinder guards on; face shield for grinding.

## Gotchas
- Welding long continuous beads: warping.
- Dirty metal: porous, weak welds.
- Unbalanced sculpture: plan the base and centre of gravity; engineer outdoor work.

## Verify
- [ ] Welds penetrate (no cold lap); test pieces broken to check.
- [ ] Assembly true after welding.
- [ ] Finish suited to indoor/outdoor display.
