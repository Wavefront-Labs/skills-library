# Pouring a silicone block mould

For small originals (under ~20 cm) a poured block of silicone is the simplest flexible mould. For bigger pieces, brush-on with a mother mould uses far less rubber.

## Choose the rubber
- **Platinum-cure (addition cure)**, e.g. Smooth-On Mold Star, Dragon Skin: low shrinkage, long library life, but **cure inhibition** from sulphur clays, latex, some 3D-print resins, tin-cure silicone residue. Do a small test patch on the original.
- **Tin-cure (condensation cure)**, e.g. Smooth-On OOMOO, Mold Max: forgiving of most surfaces, but shrinks slightly and degrades faster.
- Shore hardness: about 10A-30A for undercuts and flexibility; 40A for flat simple shapes and durability.

## One-piece open block mould
1. Seal porous originals (plaster, wood) with shellac or a sealer recommended by the rubber maker, then a release (e.g. Mann Ease Release 200) if needed.
2. Hot-glue the original to a flat board (base becomes the mould opening).
3. Build a **mould box** (foamcore, Lego, acrylic, melamine) leaving about 1-1.5 cm clearance round and above the original. Seal box seams with hot glue or clay.
4. Calculate volume (box volume minus original) - or fill with rice/water first and measure.
5. Weigh parts A and B on a scale to the stated ratio; mix thoroughly, scraping sides and bottom.
6. **Degas** in a vacuum chamber if available, or pour in a thin high stream into one low corner and let it level - the stream stretches bubbles out.
7. Cure for the full stated time at room temperature (cold slows or stops some rubbers).
8. Demould; cut a pour hole and vents if not open-faced.

## Two-part block mould
- Embed the original halfway in plasticine (non-sulphur), add **keys** (marble-sized dimples) in the clay, pour part one, flip, remove clay, apply **release** to the cured silicone (silicone sticks to silicone), pour part two. Include a sprue (pour channel) and vents at high points.

## Gotchas
- **Tacky uncured surface** next to the original means inhibition - test first.
- **Off-ratio by volume** for rubbers meant to be weighed gives soft or uncured patches.
- **Thin walls** tear; keep 1 cm minimum.

## Verify
- [ ] Inhibition test passed on the original.
- [ ] Parts weighed, mixed and degassed.
- [ ] Release used between silicone halves.
