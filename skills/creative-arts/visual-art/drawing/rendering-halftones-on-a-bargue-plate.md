# Rendering halftones on a Bargue plate

**Goal:** turn a correct two-value map into a finished drawing that matches the
plate's turning of form without losing the big light-shadow split.

## Materials
Graphite (2H-2B; HB for most of the work) or charcoal pencil on smooth paper
(e.g. Strathmore 400/500 drawing, or the smooth side of Canson Mi-Teintes).
Kneaded eraser, sharp points (sandpaper block). Avoid stumps and tissues except
for very light, deliberate blending.

## Order of work
1. **Shadow first, as one value.** Darken the whole shadow to its average tone,
   slightly lighter than the plate so accents can sit inside it.
2. **Darkest accents.** Place core accents (darkest notes at the terminator and in
   deep crevices). They set the bottom of the value range.
3. **Halftones from the terminator outward.** Work the halftone band next to the
   terminator first, then progressively lighter halftones towards the light. The
   lightest halftones are the most often overworked; stay light.
4. **Reflected light** inside the shadow last, only if clearly visible on the plate.
   It must stay darker than the darkest halftone in the light.
5. **Highlights** are the paper. Lift with a kneaded eraser if covered.

## Technique
- Build tone with small, controlled strokes following the form or in one
  consistent direction; smudging fills the tooth and shrinks the value range.
- Work a small window (2-3 cm) at a time, comparing that window on the plate with
  yours; then step back and compare the whole.
- Compare the softness of the terminator on the plate with yours at every stage.

## Gotchas
- Polishing one area while the rest is untouched breaks value relationships;
  bring the whole drawing up together in passes.
- Graphite shine: heavy soft lead in the darks reflects light and reads lighter at
  an angle; build darks in layers of HB-2B rather than one 8B pass.
- Overworking lights flattens the form; when in doubt, leave it lighter.

## Verify
- [ ] At a distance the drawing and plate read the same light/shadow split.
- [ ] Darkest accent and highlight match the plate's value range.
- [ ] Reflected lights are darker than any halftone in the light.
- [ ] Terminator edges match the plate in softness.
