# Urban sketching on location

**Goal:** finished, truthful sketches made on site, following the Urban
Sketchers manifesto ("we draw on location, indoors or out, capturing what we
see from direct observation").

## Kit (fits in a small bag)
- Sketchbook with paper that takes washes (e.g. 200 gsm+ mixed-media or watercolour).
- Waterproof pen (fineliner 0.3-0.5 or fountain pen with waterproof ink, e.g.
  Platinum Carbon or De Atramentis Document), pencil, eraser.
- Small watercolour palette, water brush or travel brush, small water bottle,
  paper towel, clips for wind.
- Folding stool; hat; bag for rubbish.

## Process (30-60 minutes)
1. **Choose a view in 5 minutes**: something you find interesting, plus a place
   you can sit safely without blocking people.
2. **Thumbnail** (1 minute): decide crop and where the page edges fall.
3. **Big shapes first**: eye level line, main buildings' envelopes, perspective
   of 1-2 key lines. Fix the eye level and keep heads of standing people on it.
4. **Line**: commit to ink once the structure is light-pencilled or confident.
5. **People and traffic**: draw a person in 10-20 seconds as a gesture (head,
   torso, legs as a wedge). Draw cars when parked; wait for a similar one to fill
   details.
6. **Wash**: big light washes first (sky, sunlit walls), then shadows as one
   connected shape, then darks.
7. Write date, place and a note; urban sketches are also documents.

## Practicalities
- Weather: shade beats sun (glare on white paper misjudges values; washes dry too
  fast). In cold weather washes dry slowly and can freeze; work in shorter
  sessions and lean on line rather than wash.
- Crowds: sit with your back to a wall. People are usually curious, not hostile.
- Private property, transit, military sites: some ban sketching or tripods; ask.

## Gotchas
- Starting with details at one corner and running out of page.
- Chasing moving light: commit to the shadows you see in the first 10 minutes.

## Verify
- [ ] Drawn on site from observation.
- [ ] Eye level consistent; people's heads at eye level for standing figures.
- [ ] Shadows painted as one connected shape.
