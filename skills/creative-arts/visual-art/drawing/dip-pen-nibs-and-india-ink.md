# Dip pen nibs and India ink

**Goal:** confident ink line with controlled thick-thin variation, using the
traditional dip pen (Guptill, *Rendering in Pen and Ink*).

## Nibs
| Nib | Character |
|-----|-----------|
| Crow quill (Hunt 102, Gillott 659) | Very fine, flexible; detail and hatching; needs a crow-quill holder |
| Hunt 512 / Gillott 303 | Fine to medium, moderate flex; general drawing |
| G-pen (Zebra, Nikko) | Stiff-ish, wide swell under pressure; manga/comics inking |
| Maru pen | Very fine, even; detail |
| Hunt 22B / 56 | Bold, flexible |

## Prepare a new nib
New nibs carry a protective oil that makes ink bead. Wipe with a little
saliva, rubbing alcohol, or briefly pass through a flame (just a second; overheating
destroys temper). Ink should then flow evenly into the slit.

## Inks
- **India ink** (carbon black + shellac binder, e.g. Higgins Black Magic, Winsor
  and Newton): waterproof when dry, dense black; good for wash over the top.
- Non-waterproof inks (Higgins Eternal, sumi ink in many forms) re-wet with water;
  useful for line-and-wash softening, bad if washes follow.
- Acrylic inks are waterproof and lightfast in colour.
- Shellac inks clog nibs: clean regularly.

## Paper
Smooth, hard-sized papers: Bristol board (smooth/plate), hot-press watercolour,
marker or layout paper. Soft or cheap papers **feather** (ink bleeds along fibres)
and nibs catch.

## Technique
- Dip to just cover the vent hole; tap excess on the bottle rim.
- Pull strokes toward you or sideways; pushing a pointed nib upward digs in and
  spatters.
- Pressure opens the tines for a wide line; release for thin. Practise swelling lines.
- Rotate the paper rather than the hand for awkward directions.

## Care
Wipe the nib every few minutes and rinse at the end; dried shellac ink ruins
flow. Store dry to prevent rust.

## Gotchas
- Blots from overloading; keep a scrap sheet to test after dipping.
- Erasing pencil underdrawing before ink is fully dry smears it.

## Verify
- [ ] Ink flows from the first stroke (nib prepped).
- [ ] Line swells controllably; no feathering on the paper.
