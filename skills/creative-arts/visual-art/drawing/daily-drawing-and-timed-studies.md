# Daily drawing and timed studies

**Goal:** steady improvement from short, focused daily sessions rather than rare
long ones. Nicolaides' course is built on daily timed exercises; online programmes
like Drawabox use the same principle.

## Session structure (30-60 minutes)
1. **Warm-up (5 min)**: lines, ellipses, boxes, ghosted strokes from the shoulder.
2. **Timed studies (20-40 min)** on one target, a ladder of durations:
   - 10 x 30 seconds (gesture, big shapes)
   - 5 x 2 minutes (proportion and structure)
   - 2 x 5 minutes (adding value)
   - 1 x 15-30 minutes (a fuller study)
   Use a timer and stop when it rings.
3. **Review (5 min)**: compare with the subject or reference; write one
   thing to fix next session.

## Weekly rotation (example)
Mon figure gesture, Tue hands/heads, Wed perspective/environment, Thu still life
value, Fri master copy, Sat location sketching, Sun free drawing.

## Deliberate practice rules
- One specific target per session ("ribcage tilt", "ellipse degree").
- Work slightly above comfort; mistakes are data.
- Draw from life or high-quality photos; avoid copying other people's stylised drawings
  for fundamentals.

## Master copies
Copy a drawing by Ingres, Holbein, Sargent, Menzel: match proportions and marks
for 30-60 minutes. Note how they simplified.

## Tracking
- Date every page. Keep all drawings, including bad ones.
- Monthly: lay out four weeks of drawings and compare first and last.

## Gotchas
- Only finishing pretty drawings (no timed practice): slow growth.
- Overlong sessions that kill the habit; 20 minutes daily beats 3 hours weekly.

## Verify
- [ ] Sessions are timed and target one skill.
- [ ] Notes from each review drive the next session.
- [ ] Monthly comparison shows progress or changes the plan.
