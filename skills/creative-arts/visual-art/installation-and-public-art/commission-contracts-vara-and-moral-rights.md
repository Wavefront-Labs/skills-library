# Commission contracts, VARA and moral rights

This is practical guidance, not legal advice; have a lawyer experienced in arts contracts review any significant agreement.

## Clauses to find and negotiate
1. **Scope and deliverables,** with design approval stages and who approves.
2. **Schedule and milestones,** with the commissioner's responsibilities (site access, permissions, utilities).
3. **Payment:** amounts, triggers, late-payment terms, tax.
4. **Change orders:** written process for scope or site changes affecting cost and time.
5. **Ownership and copyright:** the physical work belongs to the commissioner; copyright generally stays with the artist unless assigned, and the commissioner usually gets a licence to reproduce the work for non-commercial or promotional use. Check this is stated.
6. **Maintenance, repair and conservation:** who pays; whether the artist is consulted before repairs.
7. **Relocation, alteration and removal (deaccession):** notice, consultation and the artist's right to remove their name or to buy back.
8. **Insurance and indemnity,** and liability for third-party injuries.
9. **Termination,** force majeure and dispute resolution.
10. **Credit and documentation:** attribution and permission for photography.

## Moral rights
- **United States (VARA):** the Visual Artists Rights Act of 1990 (17 U.S.C. 106A) gives the author of a qualifying "work of visual art" rights of **attribution and integrity**, including the right to prevent intentional distortion or destruction of a work of recognised stature. Works made for hire and applied art are excluded. The rights last for the artist's lifetime and can be **waived only by a signed written instrument** that identifies the work and the uses. For works incorporated in buildings, special rules require notice to the artist before removal. In the 5Pointz case (Cohen v. G&M Realty), a federal court awarded statutory damages of 6.75 million US dollars to graffiti artists for whitewashing and destroying their works, and the Second Circuit upheld the award in 2020; the case shows the rights are enforceable.
- **United Kingdom:** the right to be identified as author (it must be asserted) and the right to object to derogatory treatment exist under the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988.
- Other countries (for example France) recognise stronger or perpetual moral rights; check the local law and any applicable treaties.

## Gotchas
- A broad "work for hire" clause can strip VARA protection and copyright from you; resist or limit it.
- Waivers in a commission contract are valid only if specific; watch for blanket waivers.
- Verbal agreements on credit or maintenance are hard to enforce; write them down.

## Verify
- [ ] The contract states who owns copyright and what licence the commissioner gets.
- [ ] Alteration, removal and maintenance clauses are present and reviewed by a lawyer.
