# Spray paint mural technique

**Goal:** control line, fill and blend with aerosol cans on a wall. Always paint with permission; unauthorised graffiti is illegal in most places.

## Equipment
- Aerosol paints from art and graffiti makers (for example Montana, Molotow and others), assorted **caps** (fat caps for fills, skinny or needle caps for outlines), a respirator, nitrile gloves, eye protection and masking tape and paper.

## Technique
1. **Prime and base-coat** the wall (`creative-arts.visual-art.installation-and-public-art.mural-wall-assessment-and-surface-preparation`). Rough surfaces eat paint and need more coats.
2. **Practice** on cardboard: a steady arm movement parallel to the wall, with the can about 15 to 25 cm away for lines. Closer gives thinner, sharper lines and more drips; farther gives a softer, wider, speckled mist.
3. Start moving before pressing the nozzle, release at the end of the stroke, and keep the can upright to avoid spits and drips.
4. **Layer** from large fills to details; light colours over dark need an opaque base. Blend colours by overlapping soft passes while still wet, and keep thin coats to avoid runs.
5. Outline last or first as your style requires; use masking tape or stencils for hard edges.
6. **Clean caps** after use by turning the can upside down and spraying until it runs clear, to avoid blocked nozzles.
7. Seal with a protective coating if the mural is long-lived (`creative-arts.visual-art.installation-and-public-art.anti-graffiti-and-uv-coatings-for-murals`).

## Safety
- Wear an organic-vapour respirator with the correct cartridges and particulate pre-filter; dust masks do not protect against solvent vapours. Work outdoors or with strong ventilation, and not near flames or sparks. Cans are pressurised and flammable; do not heat or puncture them.
- Check the label and data sheet; some pigments and propellants are hazardous. Dispose of empty and part-full cans according to local hazardous waste rules.

## Gotchas
- Cold cans spit; warm to room temperature, avoid direct hot sun.
- Wind causes overspray; mask neighbouring surfaces.
- Mixed brands may not be compatible; test.

## Verify
- [ ] Test lines show consistent width and no drips at your working distance.
- [ ] Respirator cartridges are in date and fit-tested.
