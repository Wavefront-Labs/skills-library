# Wall assessment and surface preparation for murals

**Goal:** a stable, clean surface so the mural lasts. Poor preparation is the most common cause of mural failure.

## Assess
1. **Substrate:** brick, concrete block, poured concrete, render or stucco, metal, timber. Each needs a suitable primer.
2. **Moisture:** look for damp stains, efflorescence (white salts), blistering, or vegetation. Fix leaks and rising damp first, or the paint will blister.
3. **Existing coatings:** check for flaking, chalking, peeling and previous graffiti. Run a **tape test**: press strong tape on a scored cross-hatch of paint and rip it off; loose paint on the tape signals poor adhesion.
4. **Cracks and holes:** note and repair.
5. **Lead and hazard check:** older buildings may have lead-based paint (in the US, buildings before 1978). Do not dry-scrape or sand suspect paint without testing and local safety rules. Wear PPE and, where needed, a P100 respirator; follow local regulations.
6. **Condition of the mortar and render:** crumbling surfaces will not hold paint.

## Prepare
1. Remove loose paint by scraping and wire brush; wash with a pressure washer or scrub with a suitable cleaner, and rinse. Let it dry fully.
2. Treat mould or algae with a biocidal wash following the label.
3. Repair cracks and holes with a masonry filler or patching compound.
4. **Primer:** use a masonry or exterior primer suited to the substrate (alkali-resistant on new masonry or render). Fresh concrete and render need to cure before sealing; many guides say weeks, so follow the paint maker's advice. Use a stain-blocking primer over graffiti and tannin stains.
5. Apply the base coat in a uniform colour (often white or a mid-tone) to make colour matching easier. Test a patch before starting the mural.

## Gotchas
- Painting over damp walls traps moisture and blisters.
- Cold or wet weather slows cure; check the minimum application temperature on the paint label.
- Never mix incompatible coatings; check primers and paints with the same maker where possible.

## Verify
- [ ] Tape test on the primed surface lifts no paint.
- [ ] The wall is dry (and cured, if new) before painting.
