# Access equipment and safety at height

**Goal:** reach the work safely. Falls from height are a leading cause of serious injury; follow the law in your location (for example OSHA rules in the US and the Work at Height Regulations 2005 in Great Britain) and the equipment manual. This is general guidance and not a substitute for training.

## Choosing
- **Scaffold:** stable and good for long jobs and wide walls; must be erected, inspected and altered by competent people, with base plates, ties to the building, guardrails and toe boards, and a safe ladder or stair access. Check the inspection tag before use.
- **Scissor lift or boom lift (MEWP):** good for fast, flexible access. Operators need training or certification (in the UK, IPAF training is widely used). Boom lifts typically require the operator to wear a harness attached to the basket; follow the manual.
- **Ladders:** for short, light, low-risk tasks only. Keep three points of contact, place on firm level ground at the correct angle (commonly 1 horizontal to 4 vertical), tie off or have a second person foot it, and never overreach.
- **Rope access and swing stages** are specialist and need trained, certified operators.

## Controls
1. Plan access before the job and include it in the risk assessment (`creative-arts.visual-art.installation-and-public-art.risk-assessment-for-public-installations`).
2. **Overhead power lines:** keep well clear; OSHA uses a minimum 10 feet for lines up to 50 kV and more for higher voltages. Ask the utility for isolation when in doubt.
3. **Wind and weather:** every lift has a wind limit in its manual; stop in high wind, storms or lightning.
4. **Exclusion zone** below the work and signage; hard hats for people in the zone.
5. **Tools and paint** tied off or in a bucket on a lanyard to avoid dropped objects.
6. **Never** leave a lift basket by climbing over the rails; use the exit gate.
7. Inspect equipment daily before use and keep records.

## Gotchas
- Soft or sloping ground makes lifts and scaffolds unstable; use boards and levelling.
- Mixing hire companies and clients can leave nobody responsible; assign one person to supervise.
- Spray painting from a lift needs extra ventilation and PPE (`creative-arts.visual-art.installation-and-public-art.spray-paint-mural-technique`).

## Verify
- [ ] Equipment is inspected, level and used by trained people.
- [ ] Risk assessment and rescue plan exist.
