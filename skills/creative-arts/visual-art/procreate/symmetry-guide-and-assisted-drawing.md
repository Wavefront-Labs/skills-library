# Symmetry guide in Procreate

**Goal:** draw mirrored or radial artwork in real time.

## Steps
1. **Actions > Canvas** and turn on **Drawing Guide**, then tap **Edit Drawing Guide**.
2. Choose **Symmetry** from the options at the bottom (alongside 2D grid, Isometric, Perspective).
3. Pick **Vertical, Horizontal, Quadrant** or **Radial**. Under **Options**, you can also turn on **Rotational symmetry** to rotate rather than mirror the strokes (check the option labels in your version).
4. Drag the guide line to move the axis; adjust opacity, thickness and colour of the guide in the same panel. Tap **Done**.
5. Open the layer to draw on and tap **Drawing Assist** (the Assisted Drawing option in the layer menu). Strokes on assisted layers are mirrored; other layers draw normally.

## Tips
- Radial gives several-fold symmetry; zoom out to see the full design, and use a high canvas resolution if you plan to print.
- For faces and characters, use Vertical, then break symmetry on a separate layer for asymmetric details.
- Add QuickShape for perfect circles in mandalas (`creative-arts.visual-art.procreate.quickshape-and-streamline`).

## Gotchas
- Assisted Drawing is per layer; forgetting to turn it on makes the guide appear to do nothing.
- Moving the guide after drawing does not move existing strokes.
- Transform on a mirrored layer treats the layer as normal pixels.

## Verify
- [ ] A stroke on the Assisted layer appears mirrored across the axis.
- [ ] Layers without Assisted Drawing are unaffected.
