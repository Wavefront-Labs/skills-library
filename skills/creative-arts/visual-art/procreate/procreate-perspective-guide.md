# Procreate perspective guide

**Goal:** accurate perspective lines in Procreate using the Drawing Guide
(Procreate Handbook, Guides: Perspective and Drawing Assist).

## Turn it on
1. Actions (wrench) > Canvas > toggle **Drawing Guide** on, then tap
   **Edit Drawing Guide**.
2. Choose **Perspective** at the bottom (other guides: 2D Grid, Isometric, Symmetry).
3. Choose **One Point**, **Two Point** or **Three Point**.
4. **Tap** the canvas to place vanishing points; drag to move; tap a point to delete.
   The horizon line is created through the first two points.
5. Adjust guide **opacity**, **thickness** and **colour** with the sliders and colour bar.
6. Toggle **Assisted Drawing** (in the editor, or per layer via layer options >
   Drawing Assist) so strokes snap to the guide's directions.

## Working with it
- Assisted Drawing is **per layer**: create a separate layer for perspective
  construction with assist on, and paint freehand on others.
- Strokes snap to the nearest perspective direction (lines to a VP, verticals,
  horizontals); good for buildings, interiors.
- Turn assist off for organic elements on the same scene.
- Use the construction methods from the drawing skills (one-, two-, three-point,
  dividing in perspective) on the assisted layer.

## Limits
- Vanishing points must be on the canvas area or near it; very distant VPs are hard
  to place (enlarge the canvas temporarily or build a guide layer manually).
- Ellipses and curves are not snapped; draw them freehand or with QuickShape.

## Gotchas
- Forgetting assist is on: organic lines snap oddly.
- VPs too close together: distorted boxes (see two-point-perspective).

## Verify
- [ ] Correct perspective type and VP positions on the horizon.
- [ ] Assisted Drawing only on construction layers.
