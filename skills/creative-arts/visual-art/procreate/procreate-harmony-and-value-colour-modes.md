# Procreate Harmony and Value colour modes

**Goal:** pick and plan colours precisely in Procreate (Procreate Handbook, Colors:
Disc, Classic, Harmony, Value, Palettes).

## Modes (tabs at the bottom of the Colours panel)
- **Disc**: hue ring and saturation/brightness disc; double-tap snaps to clean values.
- **Classic**: square saturation/brightness field with hue, saturation and brightness sliders.
- **Harmony**: a colour wheel with linked points. Tap the mode name to choose:
  **Complementary**, **Split Complementary**, **Analogous**, **Triadic**,
  **Tetradic**. Drag the main point; the others follow. Brightness slider below.
- **Value**: numeric control: HSB and RGB sliders and a **hex** field for exact
  colours (brand colours, matching a reference value).
- **Palettes**: saved swatches (see palettes skill).

## Practical use
- Use Harmony to find a starting scheme, then mute most colours (lower saturation)
  and keep one accent high; see the painting skill colour-harmony-schemes.
- Use Value to set exact brightness steps (e.g. B at 20/40/60/80 percent) for a
  value-controlled palette, recognising HSB brightness is not perceptual lightness
  (yellow at 100 percent brightness is much lighter than blue at 100 percent).
- Check values: add a Colour or Saturation-blend layer filled with black at the top
  of the stack (or use Adjustments > Hue/Saturation/Brightness with saturation 0 on
  a duplicate) to view greyscale.

## Gotchas
- Trusting harmony geometry over the painting's value structure.
- Choosing all colours at full saturation in Disc mode: garish results.
- Colour profile differences (sRGB vs P3) shift hex appearance across devices.

## Verify
- [ ] Scheme chosen in Harmony and adjusted for dominance and saturation.
- [ ] Greyscale check confirms the value plan.
