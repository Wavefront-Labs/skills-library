# Proportional landmarks across the body

**Goal:** a set of internal relationships to cross-check a figure, which are more
robust than head counts because they hold across body types.

## Standing figure, arms hanging
- **Elbow** level with the **waist** (narrowest point / lower ribs to navel area).
- **Wrist** level with the **crotch** / greater trochanter region.
- Fingertips reach roughly mid-thigh.
- **Arm span** (fingertip to fingertip) ~ **height** (Vitruvian proportion).
- **Knee** sits about halfway between hip joint (greater trochanter) and sole.
- Upper arm (shoulder to elbow) ~ forearm (elbow to wrist) + a little; forearm
  ~ foot length.
- Head width ~ two-thirds of head height; neck width about half the head width
  on males (thicker), less on females.

## Torso
- Nipple-to-nipple distance ~ one head (male).
- Bottom of the ribcage (front) to top of the pelvis (iliac crest) is a gap of
  roughly a head's third to a half: the flexible waist.
- Shoulders: acromion to acromion ~ 2 heads (male), ~1.75 (female).

## How to use
1. Block in the figure.
2. Pick 3-4 relationships relevant to the pose (e.g. elbow-waist on a standing pose).
3. Check with sighting; if two disagree with the drawing in the same direction, a
   whole segment is too long or short.
4. Check on the model, too: individuals vary; trust the model.

## Gotchas
- Applying "arms hanging" relationships to raised arms: the shoulder girdle moves.
- Torso too long is the most common error; check navel and crotch positions.

## Verify
- [ ] At least three independent landmark checks agree.
- [ ] Deviations match the actual model, not habit.
