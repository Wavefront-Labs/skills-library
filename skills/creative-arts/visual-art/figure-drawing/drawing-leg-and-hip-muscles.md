# Drawing leg and hip muscles

**Goal:** legs with correct structure and rhythm, from the pelvis to the ankle
(Goldfinger; Bridgman).

## Hip
- **Gluteus maximus**: large, thick, from sacrum and iliac crest down to the femur
  and IT band; shapes the buttock. The gluteal fold changes with weight bearing:
  the weight leg's fold deepens, relaxed side flattens.
- **Gluteus medius**: above and to the side; the "hip pad" below the iliac crest.
- **Tensor fasciae latae (TFL)**: small muscle at the front-side of the hip,
  into the **IT band** running down the outer thigh to the knee: a flat groove.

## Thigh
- **Quadriceps** (front): rectus femoris down the middle; vastus lateralis
  (outer, large, sits high); vastus medialis (inner, teardrop just above the knee,
  sits low). Classic opposing rhythm.
- **Sartorius**: longest muscle, a strap from the ASIS spiralling to the inner knee;
  divides the front thigh from the inner thigh.
- **Adductors**: the inner thigh mass.
- **Hamstrings** (back): biceps femoris (outer) and semitendinosus/semimembranosus
  (inner); their tendons frame the back of the knee.

## Lower leg
- **Gastrocnemius** (calf): two heads, the inner lower than the outer; with
  **soleus** beneath, flowing into the Achilles tendon.
- **Tibialis anterior**: along the outside of the shin; shin bone itself is
  subcutaneous.
- **Peroneals** on the outer side.

## Rhythm
The whole leg forms an S: outer thigh high, inner knee low, outer calf high,
inner ankle high (medial malleolus) vs outer low. Legs angle inward from the hip
to the knee, then the lower leg is more vertical.

## Gotchas
- Straight vertical thighs from hip to knee: the femur angles inward.
- Calf heads equal height.
- Knee drawn as a round ball; it is a boxy structure with the patella in front.

## Verify
- [ ] Opposing rhythms at thigh, knee and calf are offset correctly.
- [ ] Gluteal fold reflects which leg bears weight.
