# Loomis head construction

**Goal:** a head in correct proportion at any angle, built on Andrew Loomis'
"ball and plane" method (*Drawing the Head and Hands*, *Fun with a Pencil*).

## Steps
1. **Ball**: draw a circle for the cranium.
2. **Slice the sides**: the sides of the skull are flatter; draw an ellipse on the
   side of the ball (its width depends on how much the head is turned). Its centre
   is near where the ear will sit.
3. **Centre line**: a vertical line around the ball through the middle of the face,
   curving with the head's turn and tilt.
4. **Brow line**: horizontal around the ball through the middle of the side ellipse;
   it curves with the up-down tilt.
5. **Thirds**: from the hairline to the brow, brow to the bottom of the nose, nose
   to the chin are roughly equal. The hairline sits near the top of the side
   ellipse; the bottom of the nose at the bottom of it; extend the centre line down
   one more unit for the chin.
6. **Jaw**: from the back of the side ellipse (below the ear) down and forward to
   the chin. Wider and squarer in men, narrower in women, but individual.
7. **Ears**: between the brow and nose lines, behind the vertical through the middle of
   the side plane.
8. **Eyes**: on a line about halfway between the top of the skull and the chin
   (lower than beginners expect); one eye-width apart.

## Rotating
Practise the construction in 9 views: front, 3/4, profile; each looking up, level,
down. Up-tilt makes the brow and nose lines curve upward (smile); down-tilt makes
them curve down (frown).

## Gotchas
- Cranium drawn too small: the back of the skull extends well behind the ear.
- Eyes placed in the upper third.
- Centre line straight on a turned head.

## Verify
- [ ] Hairline-brow-nose-chin in three roughly equal parts.
- [ ] Eye line near the middle of the whole head.
- [ ] Ear between brow and nose lines.
