# Ribcage and pelvis masses

**Goal:** a torso that bends, twists and carries weight, built from two rigid
masses joined by the flexible abdomen (Hampton, *Figure Drawing: Design and
Invention*; Bridgman's *Constructive Anatomy*).

## The masses
- **Ribcage**: an egg or barrel, narrower at the top, about 1.5 heads tall
  (from pit of neck to bottom of ribs). Front has the thoracic arch (an upside-down
  V/U opening below the sternum). Seen from the side it tilts back.
- **Pelvis**: a box or bowl about 1 head tall, wider than tall; front tilts
  forward. Landmarks: the iliac crests and anterior superior iliac spines (the
  hip bones at the front).
- **Gap**: the abdomen and waist; only the spine connects them. All bending of
  the torso happens here and in the spine.

## Movements
- **Tilt (side bend)**: the ribcage and pelvis tilt toward each other on one side
  (pinched) and away on the other (stretched). In contrapposto, shoulders and hips
  tilt in opposite directions.
- **Twist (rotation)**: the ribcage rotates relative to the pelvis around the
  spine; the centre lines (sternum and navel line) no longer align.
- **Forward/back bend**: the gap compresses at front or back.

## The bean exercise
Draw the torso as a bean: the pinched side and stretched side show bend; add a
centre line to show twist. Many quick beans (30 s) train torso gesture.

## Construction steps
1. Gesture line of the torso.
2. Ribcage box/egg with centre line and its tilt; pelvis box with its tilt and twist.
3. Connect with the abdominal and back contours (pinch and stretch).
4. Attach shoulders (girdle on top of the ribcage) and legs (at the hip sockets).

## Gotchas
- Ribcage and pelvis drawn parallel: stiff figure.
- Pelvis too small or too high; the hip joints are low, at the crotch level side.

## Verify
- [ ] Both masses have their own tilt and rotation.
- [ ] Pinch and stretch sides read in the waist.
