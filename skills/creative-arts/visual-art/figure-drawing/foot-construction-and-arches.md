# Foot construction and arches

**Goal:** feet that carry weight convincingly from any view.

## Proportion
Foot length ~ one head height (Loomis); width at the ball ~ one third of length.

## Construction
1. **Wedge**: from the side, the foot is a wedge: high at the ankle, sloping to
   the toes. From above, it widens from heel to ball.
2. **Inner vs outer side**: the **inner** side has a high arch (the medial
   longitudinal arch) and touches the ground at heel and ball only; the **outer**
   side is lower and almost flat along the ground. Draw this difference in
   every view.
3. **Ankle**: inner malleolus higher and further forward; outer malleolus lower
   and further back. The line between them slants down to the outside.
4. **Heel**: a rounded block projecting behind the ankle line; the Achilles
   tendon narrows into it.
5. **Toes**: the big toe is separate, angled up slightly; the other four step down
   in a curved arc and grip the ground with pads. Toe knuckles form an arc
   parallel to the ball.

## Weight
- Weight-bearing feet spread and flatten slightly; the arch lowers.
- On tiptoe, the heel lifts, the toes bend at the ball and the arch deepens.

## Gotchas
- Feet drawn too small (very common).
- Both ankle bones at the same height.
- Inner and outer profiles drawn symmetrical.

## Verify
- [ ] Foot ~ one head long.
- [ ] Inner arch high, outer edge flat.
- [ ] Malleoli inner high, outer low.
