# Life room etiquette and working with models

**Goal:** a session where the model is safe, respected and comfortable and the
artists get good poses. Models are professionals; treat the room as a workplace.

## As an artist
- **Never touch the model** or their belongings. Ask the session leader to relay
  requests ("could the arm come down a little?").
- **No photography or video** of the model, ever, unless the model has explicitly
  agreed in advance. Photos of your own drawing are usually fine; check first.
- Talk about the drawing, not the body. No comments on attractiveness.
- Stay put during poses; do not walk into the model's sightline or behind them
  without reason. Enter and leave quietly.
- Do not chat loudly; the model is holding still and can hear everything.
- Thank the model. Show them work if they want to see it, never insist.

## As session leader
- **Booking**: agree rate, hours, poses and nudity level in writing. Many
  regions have model registers (e.g. the UK Register of Artists' Models) with
  recommended rates. Pay promptly, including for cancellations at short notice.
- **Room**: warm (around 24-26 C at the platform; use a heater pointed at the model),
  private, with screens or a lockable changing area, a robe, a clean cloth or
  mat on the platform, water.
- **Timing**: standard pattern of short warm-up gestures then longer poses.
  Give the model a 5-minute break about every 20-25 minutes of holding. Long
  poses need **tape marks** around feet, hands and cushions so the pose can be
  resumed after breaks.
- **Poses**: let the model choose or offer suggestions; never demand a pose that
  is painful. Twisting or raised-arm poses are short only.
- **Safeguarding**: clear rules on photography, and a procedure if an attendee
  behaves inappropriately (ask them to leave). Minors require separate policy and
  usually clothed models.

## Gotchas
- Cold rooms make models shiver and change pose; heaters are not optional.
- Forgetting tape marks means the pose after the break no longer matches drawings.
- Informal "can I just take a quick photo" requests: always no unless pre-agreed.

## Verify
- [ ] Terms, fee and nudity agreed in writing with the model.
- [ ] Room warm and private; changing area provided.
- [ ] Breaks taken on schedule; poses marked.
- [ ] No photography of the model without explicit consent.
