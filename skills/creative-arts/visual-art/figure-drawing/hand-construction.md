# Hand construction

**Goal:** draw hands that are solid and correct from any angle (Loomis,
*Drawing the Head and Hands*).

## Proportion
- Hand length (wrist crease to middle fingertip) ~ face length (chin to hairline).
- Palm length ~ middle finger length (from knuckle at the back slightly more).
- Finger segments decrease toward the tip (roughly in a 1 : 0.7 : 0.5 relation,
  approximate; observe).

## Construction steps
1. **Palm block**: a slightly wedge-shaped, flattened box, thicker at the thumb
   side, wider at the knuckles than at the wrist. Show its plane changes; it
   arches slightly across the back.
2. **Knuckle arc**: the knuckles lie on a curve, not a straight line; the middle
   knuckle is highest. Fingertips also fall on an arc when fingers are straight.
3. **Fingers**: three segments each as small boxes or cylinders; the joints
   create arcs across the fingers when they bend. Fingers converge slightly
   toward a point when curled.
4. **Thumb**: starts near the wrist with its own mass (thenar eminence), as a
   wedge; it rotates about 90 degrees relative to the fingers, so its nail faces sideways when
   the fingers' nails face up.
5. Add the pads (fleshy, round on the palm side) and the bony knuckles (angular on
   the back).

## Practice
- Draw your non-drawing hand in 20 poses, 2-5 minutes each, boxes first.
- Draw a gloved hand or a mannequin hand to see the simple masses.

## Gotchas
- Hands too small: check against the face.
- Fingers as parallel sausages of equal length.
- Thumb attached at the side of the palm like a finger.

## Verify
- [ ] Hand ~ face length.
- [ ] Knuckles and joints on arcs.
- [ ] Thumb rotated and with its own base mass.
