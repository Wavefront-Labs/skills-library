# Photobashing and texture overlays

Photobashing combines photographs into a painting to get realism and speed, mainly in concept art. The skill is making them belong together.

## Source legally
- Use your own photos, paid stock with a licence fitting the use, texture libraries (e.g. Textures.com terms), or public domain / CC0. CC-BY needs credit; "non-commercial" licences can't be used for client work.
- Never build a commercial piece on an image found by web search without a licence.
- Keep a source log (file, URL, licence) with the project.

## Match before you blend
1. **Perspective**: set the horizon and vanishing points first; transform (Perspective/Distort/Warp) photos to fit. A photo shot from a different eye height never fits; choose better sources.
2. **Light direction**: all pieces lit from the same side, or flip/paint them to match.
3. **Value and colour**: use Curves/Levels and Color Balance or Hue/Saturation clipped to each photo to match the scene's black point, contrast and colour cast. Atmospheric perspective: distant elements lower contrast and shift toward the sky colour.
4. **Resolution and grain**: match sharpness; add a little noise to all layers so one isn't cleaner than the rest.

## Blend
- **Layer masks**, not erasing. Soft and textured brushes on the mask for natural transitions.
- Clip adjustments to individual photo layers.
- Use **Lighten/Darken/Multiply/Screen** to drop in skies, smoke and silhouettes without masking.

## Texture overlays
- Lay a texture (concrete, rust, paper, grunge) over a form with **Overlay, Soft Light or Multiply**; desaturate it first if you only want the value detail.
- Warp it to follow the surface's perspective; mask so it reads only where the material is.

## Paint over
- Paint over seams, edges and lighting with opaque strokes, unify with a gradient map or colour grade, and add atmosphere. The final image should read as one painted world.

## Gotchas
- Mismatched perspective and light are the giveaways - fix them, not the textures.
- Overuse of Overlay increases saturation and contrast unnaturally.
- Stock watermarks or recognisable landmarks can create legal issues.

## Verify
- [ ] Licences logged for every source.
- [ ] Horizon, light direction and black point matched across sources.
- [ ] Final unifying paint-over done.
