# Thumbnail to finish: the digital painting pipeline

Each stage answers one question; don't move on until it's answered, because later changes cost far more.

## 1. Brief and reference
- Confirm: format (aspect ratio, size, bleed), use (cover with type area, web banner), deadline, number of revisions, required elements.
- Collect reference into a board (PureRef or similar); shoot your own for key poses.

## 2. Thumbnails (composition)
- Small (a few cm wide on screen at 100%), fast, in 3-4 flat values. 10-30 options.
- Test silhouettes, focal point, eye path, the type/safe area. Flip horizontally to check.

## 3. Value comps
- Choose 2-3 thumbnails and develop them slightly larger with clear light and shadow families.

## 4. Client approval of sketch
- Present 2-3 options cleanly (not 20); state what's changeable. Get approval in writing before colour.

## 5. Colour comp
- Small colour studies over the chosen value comp (gradient maps, Color layers, or paint). Decide palette, light colour, temperature contrast.

## 6. Refined drawing
- Full size, clean line or tight sketch on its own layer; fix anatomy, perspective (rulers), design details. A second approval point for many clients.

## 7. Block-in
- Flat local colours and big light/shadow shapes under the drawing; keep it simple and correct in value.

## 8. Rendering
- Work from big to small: big forms, then edges (hard/soft/lost), then detail focused at the focal point. Zoom out often (or a second navigator window); flip; value check.
- Keep layers organised (background, midground, figure, effects); merge when decisions are made.

## 9. Final details and adjustments
- Highlights, accents, atmosphere, colour grade with adjustment layers. Fix tangents.
- Last flip and greyscale check.

## 10. Delivery
- Layered master (PSD/KRA/CLIP), flattened TIFF at required size and colour profile, JPG/PNG for web. Name files with version and date.

## Gotchas
- Rendering before composition is approved.
- Detailing everywhere equally: the focal point gets lost.
- Working zoomed-in for hours: proportions drift.

## Verify
- [ ] Sketch approved in writing before rendering.
- [ ] Flip and value checks at each stage.
- [ ] Delivery files in the agreed format and profile.
