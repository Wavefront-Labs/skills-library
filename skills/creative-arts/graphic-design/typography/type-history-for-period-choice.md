# Type history for period-appropriate choice

**Goal:** type that feels true to a period (a 1920s film poster, a Victorian-themed label, a mid-century
annual report) without anachronisms a knowledgeable viewer will spot.

## Timeline with representative faces and revivals
| Period | What was used | Revivals to reach for |
|---|---|---|
| 1450-1500 (Gutenberg, incunabula) | Textura blackletter; Venetian roman (Jenson 1470) | Textura revivals, Centaur, Adobe Jenson |
| 1500-1700 (Renaissance, Baroque) | Garalde romans and italics: Aldus, Garamond, Granjon, Caslon later | Bembo, Garamond Premier, Sabon |
| 1700-1800 (Enlightenment) | Caslon (Britain, colonial America), Fournier, Baskerville | Adobe Caslon, Baskerville |
| 1790-1850 | Didone: Bodoni, Didot; first fat faces, slab (Egyptian, 1815), first sans (1816) | Bodoni, Didot, Clarendon |
| Victorian 1850-1900 | Wood-type display, Clarendon, Tuscan and decorated faces, grotesques, mixed many faces on one poster | Clarendon, wood-type revivals |
| Arts and Crafts / Art Nouveau 1890-1910 | Golden Type, Jugendstil lettering, Eckmann | Morris-inspired, Arnold Böcklin |
| 1920s-30s Modernism and Art Deco | Futura (1927), Gill Sans (1928), Kabel, Broadway, Bifur | Futura, Gill Sans, Deco display faces |
| 1940s-50s | Times New Roman (1932) in print, script lettering in ads | Times, mid-century scripts |
| 1957-1970s Swiss/International style | Helvetica and Univers (1957), Akzidenz-Grotesk | Helvetica Now, Neue Haas Grotesk |
| 1970s | ITC faces: Avant Garde, Souvenir, Cooper Black revival, tight phototype spacing | ITC revivals |
| 1984-2000s digital | Emigre, bitmap faces, Verdana/Georgia (1996) for screen | |

## Method
1. Fix the exact year and place, and the medium (printed book, street poster, shop sign, newspaper).
2. Check reference material from the period (archives, museum collections, ephemera), not modern pastiche.
3. Pick a faithful revival; set it as it was set: Victorian posters centred and mixed, Swiss style flush left
   ragged right on a grid.
4. Match period details: ligatures (long s before c. 1800 in English), figures (old-style in books before 1900),
   spacing (tight phototype in the 1970s).

## Common anachronisms
- Helvetica or Futura in a story set before their release.
- Papyrus, Trajan or blackletter as generic "ancient" or "medieval" shorthand.
- Digital-perfect kerning on a supposedly hand-set wood-type poster: add irregular spacing and ink texture
  only if the period craft supports it.

## Verify
- [ ] Every face in the piece was available (or a faithful revival of one available) in that year and place.
- [ ] Setting style and details match period references.
