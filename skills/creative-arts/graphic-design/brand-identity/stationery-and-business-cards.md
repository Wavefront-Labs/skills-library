# Stationery and business cards

**Goal:** a coherent stationery set that prints well, posts properly and can be filled in by staff.

## Standard formats
| Item | Europe / ISO | US |
|---|---|---|
| Business card | 85 x 55 mm (UK/EU), 90 x 55 (AU) | 3.5 x 2 in |
| Letterhead | A4 210 x 297 mm | Letter 8.5 x 11 in |
| Compliment slip | 210 x 99 mm (1/3 A4) | 8.5 x 3.67 in |
| Envelope | DL 110 x 220 (A4 folded in 3), C5, C4 | #10 4.125 x 9.5 in |

## Design system across the set
- One **shared grid**: the logo position, margins and type sizes relate across all items, so the
  set reads as a family when laid side by side.
- Business card: name, title, phone, email, web, address - decide the hierarchy; 7-9 pt text
  minimum depending on typeface; keep 3-4 mm inside trim. Two-sided cards: logo/brand side +
  information side.
- Letterhead: logo and contact details framed around a clear **writing area**; leave room for
  the address and date. For window envelopes, the address must sit where the **window** is
  (DIN 5008 / DL window typically 20 mm from left, 45-50 mm from top for A4 - check the envelope
  supplier's window position). Fold marks at 1/3 positions as small ticks in the margin.
- Legal lines (company number, VAT, registered office) are mandatory in many countries -
  set them small at the foot.

## Production
- Letterhead is often printed blank by a printer **and** used as a Word/Google Docs template
  for laser printers. Design both: the printed sheet (bleeds, spot colours) and the template
  (no bleed; laser printers cannot print to the edge - keep 5 mm margins).
- Business cards: 350-450 gsm; finishes like spot UV, foil, edge painting or duplexed stocks
  (see special finishes skill).
- Proof names and phone numbers with the client per person; use Data Merge for teams.

## Templates for staff
- Word/Google Docs letter template with styles matching the brand typography (or the system
  fallback typeface named in guidelines).
- Email signature in HTML with live text, small logo image hosted online, no image-only signatures.

## Verify
- [ ] Address lines fall inside the envelope window when folded (print and test with a real envelope).
- [ ] Every card proof checked by its owner; legal lines present.
- [ ] Editable templates work on a machine without the brand fonts.
