# Writing the brand guidelines document

**Goal:** a reference that lets in-house teams, agencies and suppliers apply the brand correctly without
asking the designer, and that stays current.

## Structure (Wheeler's standard sections)
1. **Introduction:** purpose, brand story, positioning, values and attributes, contact for questions.
2. **Logo:** versions (primary, secondary, symbol), clear space and minimum size, colour versions, placement
   rules, co-branding, misuse examples.
3. **Colour:** palette with all specs, proportions, accessible pairs.
4. **Typography:** families, hierarchy, fallbacks, licensing notes.
5. **Imagery:** photography and illustration direction; iconography.
6. **Graphic devices** and patterns.
7. **Voice and tone** (with the copywriter): principles and examples.
8. **Applications:** stationery, digital, social, signage, packaging, templates; each with a layout spec.
9. **Assets and contacts:** where to download files, version history.

## Write rules so they are followable
- One rule per statement, with numbers: "Minimum logo width 20 mm print / 80 px screen."
- Every rule gets a correct example; important ones also a misuse example (red cross, labelled).
- Explain the why in one line where people will be tempted to break the rule.

## Formats
- **PDF** (20-80 pages) for small brands and supplier handoff; keep a one-page quick reference.
- **Online brand portal** (Frontify, zeroheight, Brandpad, or a website) for larger brands: searchable,
  asset downloads, always current, can embed Figma and code tokens.
- Large systems split: master brand book, plus detailed specs (signage manual, digital design system).

## Upkeep
- Version and date every edition; changelog. One owner in the client team.
- Review annually or at each new touchpoint type.

## Gotchas
- Guidelines that show only perfect hero applications do not help someone making a poster tomorrow; include
  templates and everyday examples.
- Contradictions between the PDF and asset files undermine trust; generate examples from the real assets.
- Too restrictive rules get ignored; allow flexibility where it does not harm recognition.

## Verify
- [ ] A new designer can produce a correct flyer using only the guidelines and assets.
- [ ] Version number, date and owner on the document; asset links work.
