# Proposals, scope and pricing

**Goal:** a proposal that shows the client you understand their problem, defines exactly what they get,
and prices it so the studio stays profitable.

## Structure
1. **Understanding:** the client's situation and goals in their words (from the brief call).
2. **Approach:** phases (research and strategy, concepts, refinement, system and applications, delivery),
   what happens in each, and client inputs needed.
3. **Scope and deliverables:** a precise list (e.g. "primary logo in 3 versions and 4 colourways; 20-page
   guidelines PDF; 3 stationery items"). Also what is **out of scope** (copywriting, photography, printing).
4. **Revisions:** rounds per phase (see handling-feedback-and-revision-rounds).
5. **Timeline:** dates per phase, dependent on client feedback times.
6. **Fee:** per phase or total; expenses (fonts, stock images, printing, travel) separate.
7. **Terms:** payment schedule, IP transfer on final payment, cancellation and kill fees, validity period.
   Base on a standard (AIGA Standard Form of Agreement) reviewed by a lawyer.

## Pricing methods
- **Time-based estimate:** hours per task x rate + contingency (10-20%). Track actual hours on past projects
  to estimate better.
- **Value-based:** fee related to the business value and usage (a national rebrand vs a local shop), still
  sanity-checked against hours.
- **Fixed fee per phase:** clients like certainty; protect with tight scope and change-request rates.
- Offer options (good / better / best scopes) to keep the conversation on scope, not discount.

## Payment terms (common practice)
- Deposit before work starts (often 30-50%), milestones, final payment before handover of master files.

## Gotchas
- Vague deliverables ("logo and branding") cause disputes; list items and quantities.
- Unpriced expenses (font licences for 200 seats) can exceed the design fee; flag them.
- Spec work (free pitching of designs) is discouraged by AIGA and many design bodies.

## Verify
- [ ] Every deliverable has a quantity and a phase; out-of-scope list present.
- [ ] Terms cover payment schedule, IP transfer, revisions and cancellation.
