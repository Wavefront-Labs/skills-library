# IP transfer and ownership of final art

**Goal:** a clear written agreement on who owns what at the end of a design project, so the client can
register and use their brand and the designer keeps what was not sold. Not legal advice: have contracts
reviewed by a lawyer in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Key concepts
- **Copyright** belongs to the creator by default (in most countries), or to the employer for employees.
- **Assignment:** ownership transferred in writing to the client. Usual for logos and identities, because
  the client must own the mark to protect it as a trademark.
- **Licence:** the designer keeps ownership and grants rights to use (exclusive or non-exclusive, by media,
  territory, duration). Usual for illustration and photography.
- **Work made for hire** (US): commissioned works only qualify in specific categories and with a written
  agreement (US Copyright Office Circular 30); otherwise use an explicit assignment.

## What transfers (AIGA Standard Form approach)
- **Final Deliverables:** the approved final art; rights transfer to the client **on full payment**.
- **Preliminary Works:** rejected concepts, sketches and alternatives stay with the designer unless the
  contract says otherwise.
- **Designer Tools:** templates, code libraries and methods the designer uses across clients stay with the
  designer, with a licence to the client where embedded.
- **Third-party materials:** fonts, stock images, illustrations: licensed from the third party, not
  transferable by the designer; list them and who pays for licences.

## Designer's retained rights
- **Portfolio use** of the final work (after publication; respect confidentiality periods).
- **Moral rights** (attribution, integrity) exist in many jurisdictions and may be waivable; clarify.

## Process
- State the terms in the proposal and contract; on final payment, send a short **assignment letter** listing
  the transferred works, signed by both parties.

## Gotchas
- Releasing master files before final payment removes the designer's leverage.
- Logos built on a licensed font cannot always be registered or modified freely; check the font EULA allows
  logo use (most do for outlined artwork).
- AI-generated elements may not be copyrightable in some jurisdictions; disclose their use.

## Verify
- [ ] Contract defines final art, preliminary works, third-party items and when transfer happens.
- [ ] Signed assignment letter issued after final payment.
