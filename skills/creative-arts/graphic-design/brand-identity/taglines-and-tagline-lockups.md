# Taglines and lockups

**Goal:** a short line that captures the brand's promise, and fixed artwork for when it appears with the
logo (Wheeler: taglines as a brand basic).

## Tagline types (Wheeler)
- **Imperative:** commands action ("Just do it" style).
- **Descriptive:** says what the organisation does or offers.
- **Superlative:** claims leadership ("the best...").
- **Provocative:** asks or teases.
- **Specific:** names the category or benefit.
Pick the type that fits the positioning and the brand voice.

## Developing
- Work with a copywriter from the positioning statement and attributes. Generate many (50+), shortlist 5-10.
- Criteria: short (2-6 words), distinctive, true, memorable, translatable for target markets, not in use
  by a competitor; check trademark if it will be registered.
- Test aloud and in context (signature, ad, website header).

## Brand tagline vs campaign line
- **Brand tagline:** long-lived, in the signature, approved by leadership.
- **Campaign line:** changes per campaign; set in campaign typography, not locked to the logo.
- Do not lock campaign lines into logo artwork.

## Tagline lockups
- Fixed position (below the wordmark aligned left, or to the right after a divider), in the brand typeface at
  a fixed ratio to the wordmark (e.g. tagline cap height = 0.35 x wordmark x-height) and fixed gap in logo
  units. Use only above a minimum logo size where the tagline remains legible (e.g. tagline at least 6 pt print,
  12 px screen); below it, drop the tagline.
- Supply lockup artwork per language if translated; translated lines change length, so design each.

## Gotchas
- Stretching the tagline to the logo width distorts spacing; set it naturally and align to one edge.
- Translations can be legally or culturally risky; check with local teams.
