# Merchandise and apparel graphics

**Goal:** branded t-shirts, caps, bags and promo items that look like the brand, survive washing, and are
produced right the first time by the supplier.

## Choose the decoration method
| Method | Good for | Artwork limits |
|---|---|---|
| **Screen printing** | runs of ~25+; bold flat colour | one screen per colour; spot colours (Pantone C or TCX); halftones coarse (35-55 lpi); min line ~0.5-1 pt |
| **Embroidery** | caps, polos, premium | no gradients; min text ~5-6 mm cap height; fine lines under ~1 mm fail; stitch count drives price; supplier digitises to DST |
| **DTG (direct to garment)** | small runs, full colour | RGB/CMYK raster 300 ppi at size; white underbase on dark garments; colours less vivid |
| **DTF / heat transfer** | full colour on varied fabrics | similar to DTG; feel is a film layer |
| **Laser / debossing / pad print** | promo hard goods | single colour usually; follow supplier template |

## Artwork prep
- Vector artwork, text outlined, at actual print size, one layer per colour for screen print, colours named by
  Pantone. Specify ink colour against garment colour (white ink on navy is a different look from the screen).
- Simplify the logo for embroidery: thicken thin parts, remove tiny counters, use the symbol for caps.
- Garment colour itself: match brand colour via Pantone TCX/TPX references; ask for a fabric swatch.

## Placement and sizes (typical)
- Left chest 75-100 mm wide; full front 250-300 mm wide; back neck 50-75 mm; sleeve 50-75 mm; cap front
  ~50-60 mm tall. Check supplier templates per product size.

## Approvals
- Ask for a digital proof with placement and colour callouts, and a physical sample for large runs.

## Gotchas
- Gradients and photos in screen print need simulated process or halftones; quote and test.
- Brand colours on coloured fabrics shift; approve on the actual garment.
- Tiny legal or URL text disappears in embroidery and on textured fabrics.

## Verify
- [ ] Artwork meets the supplier's method limits; colours named for ink and garment.
- [ ] Proof or sample approved before production.
