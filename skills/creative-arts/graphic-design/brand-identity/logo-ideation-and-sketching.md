# Logo ideation and sketching

**Goal:** many genuinely different ideas grounded in the brief, narrowed to a few strong concepts before any vector work.

## 1. Mine the brief for meaning
- From the strategy (attributes, positioning, story, name), write **word lists**: literal (what the company does),
  associative (what it feels like), name-based (letters, sounds, etymology), and contrast words (what it is not).
- **Mind map** from the name and the main attribute; push past the obvious first ring of ideas.
- Note category codes from the competitive audit (see audit skill) - which to follow, which to break.

## 2. Thumbnail fast
- Pencil/marker, small thumbnails (2-4 cm), no erasing; 50-100+ in a session. Quantity first - the first 20 are clichés.
- Explore every logo type: wordmark treatments, monogram/lettermark, abstract symbol, pictorial symbol, emblem,
  combination (see logo design process skill).
- Combine words: two concepts merged in one form (negative space ideas, letter + object).
- Draw the name in different letter structures (serif, geometric, script, stencil) to find a typographic idea.

## 3. Select and develop
- Pin all thumbnails up; step back; mark ideas that are **simple, distinctive, appropriate and memorable**.
- Test the shortlist at **small size** (1 cm) and in **one colour** - concepts that rely on colour or detail fail.
- Develop 3-6 directions in larger sketches, then rough vectors. Keep concepts in **black and white** until form is
  resolved; colour comes later.
- Quick check for obvious similarities with existing marks (image search) - not a legal clearance (see trademark search).

## Gotchas
- Jumping to the computer too early makes everything look finished and similar.
- Literal icons (a house for an estate agent) are rarely distinctive; meaning can be abstract.
- Keep sketches - clients sometimes ask how a concept came about; process shows value.

## Verify
- [ ] 50+ thumbnails across all logo types before vectors.
- [ ] Shortlist works at 1 cm in one colour and maps to the brief's attributes.
