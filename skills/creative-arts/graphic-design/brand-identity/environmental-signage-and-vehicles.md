# Environmental signage and vehicles

**Goal:** a brand that is recognisable and well made at building scale and on the road, where artwork
becomes metal, light, paint and vinyl.

## Sign types for brand presence
- **Fascia / storefront signs**, projecting (blade) signs, **monoliths and totems** at entrances, reception
  logos, window graphics (frosted or cut vinyl), and interior feature walls. (Wayfinding sign families are a
  separate system: see wayfinding-sign-family skill.)

## Design for fabrication
- Draw at scale with real dimensions; minimum stroke and gap widths depend on the method (routed letters,
  built-up channel letters, flat-cut acrylic, vinyl). Ask the fabricator for minimums (thin serifs and
  hairlines often need thickening).
- **Illuminated signs:** face-lit, halo-lit (reverse channel letters), or edge-lit. Brand colours shift on
  lit acrylic; specify translucent vinyl or acrylic colours and approve lit samples at night.
- **Materials and finishes:** paint (RAL, NCS, or paint brand codes), metals (brushed stainless, powder
  coat), vinyl (3M, Oracal series codes). Match brand colours to physical samples, not screens.
- Planning permission or landlord approval for external signs: size, illumination and heritage rules vary.

## Vehicles
- Get the exact vehicle model templates (vehicle template libraries or the wrap installer).
- Design all four sides plus top if seen from above; keep logos off door handles, panel gaps and curves.
- Contact details at a readable size from 10-20 m; avoid fine detail on rivets and corrugations.
- Full wrap (printed cast vinyl with laminate) vs partial graphics (cut vinyl); colours specified by
  vinyl code. Check legibility in motion and regulatory markings (fleet numbers, legal text).

## Handoff
- Vector artwork with outlined text, colour specs, dimensioned drawings, mounting notes, and approval of
  samples or a mock-up.

## Gotchas
- Logos designed for screens may have details that disappear at distance or cannot be fabricated.
- Reversed logos on dark fascias need their own approved version.
- Weather and sun fade colours; specify UV-stable materials and laminates.

## Verify
- [ ] Fabricator confirmed minimum sizes and materials; physical colour samples approved.
- [ ] Vehicle mockup on the exact model template reviewed from 20 m equivalent view.
