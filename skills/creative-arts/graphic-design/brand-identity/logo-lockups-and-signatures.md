# Logo lockups and signatures

**Goal:** a fixed set of approved arrangements of symbol, wordmark and tagline, so nobody recombines them freehand.

## What a lockup is
Wheeler calls the fixed combination of symbol and logotype the **signature**. A **lockup** is one approved arrangement:
- **Horizontal:** symbol left of the wordmark - headers, signage fascias, email signatures.
- **Stacked / vertical:** symbol above the wordmark - square or tall spaces, social, packaging.
- **With tagline / descriptor:** tagline locked at a fixed size and position (often only in larger versions).
- **Sub-brand / endorsed:** parent mark + division name (see brand architecture skill).
- **Co-branding / partner lockups:** two brands with a divider or "in partnership with", equal optical size.

## Specify proportions in units
- Define a unit from the mark itself (e.g. **x** = cap height of the wordmark or symbol stroke width).
- Gap between symbol and wordmark: e.g. 1.5x; tagline cap height 0.4x; tagline gap 0.75x.
- Alignment: wordmark vertically centred on the symbol, or aligned to its baseline/cap height - choose one and draw it.
- Clear space around the whole lockup in the same unit (see clear space skill).

## Produce artwork
- One artboard per lockup (and per colour version) in the master AI file (see multiple artboards skill); export all
  formats per lockup (see logo file delivery).
- Name files predictably: `brand_logo_horizontal_fullcolour.svg`, `brand_logo_stacked_white.png`.

## Co-branding rules
- Equal optical weight (not equal width): scale partner logos so they feel the same size; align on a shared centre line.
- Divider line (thin vertical rule) or spacing equal to the larger clear-space requirement.
- Respect the partner's clear space and colour rules.

## Gotchas
- Users stretching taglines to the wordmark's width or moving the symbol - prevent with locked artwork and misuse examples.
- Too many lockups dilute the system; 2-4 core lockups are typical.
