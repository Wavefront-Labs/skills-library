# Rebrand rollout planning

**Goal:** the new identity replaces the old across every touchpoint in a planned order, on budget, with
staff on board, and with no forgotten old logos left on invoices or vans years later (Wheeler: Managing assets).

## 1. Touchpoint inventory
- Spreadsheet of everything carrying the brand: digital (website, apps, social, email, ads), print
  (stationery, forms, brochures), environment (signage, interiors), vehicles, uniforms, packaging, products,
  legal documents, third-party listings, partner sites.
- Columns: item, owner, quantity or locations, supplier, replacement cost, lead time, visibility (high/med/low),
  regulatory constraints, natural replacement date.

## 2. Prioritise
- **Launch-critical (day one):** website, social avatars, email signatures, key digital ads, reception,
  press materials.
- **Phase 2:** printed materials as stock runs out (avoid waste), packaging on next production run,
  uniforms.
- **Phase 3:** expensive items on natural replacement cycles (building signage, vehicles), with interim
  solutions (vinyl overlays).

## 3. Launch approach
- **Big bang:** everything visible switches on one date; strong impact, high cost and risk.
- **Phased:** lower cost; needs rules for the transition period (old and new side by side acceptable where).
- Internal launch first: explain the why to staff, give them templates and the guidelines before the public sees it.

## 4. Supplier briefing
- Asset packages, guidelines, deadlines and approval process for printers, sign makers, agencies; collect proofs.

## 5. Track and close
- Status per inventory row (not started, in production, done); weekly reviews; final audit (photograph
  locations, search the web for old logos) after 6-12 months.

## Gotchas
- Legal and regulatory items (product labels, licences) have approval lead times of months.
- Destroying usable old stock wastes money; use until depleted unless brand risk demands otherwise.
- Domain, social handle and app store changes need technical owners and redirects.

## Verify
- [ ] Inventory with owners, costs and phases agreed with the client.
- [ ] Post-launch audit shows no high-visibility old-brand items remaining.
