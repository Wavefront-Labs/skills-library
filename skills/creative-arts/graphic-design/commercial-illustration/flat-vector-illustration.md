# Flat vector illustration

**Goal:** clean, scalable illustrations in a consistent style that clients can resize and
recolour without breaking them.

## Workflow
1. **Sketch** (paper or Procreate) and place it in Illustrator on a locked, dimmed template
   layer (File > Place, tick Template).
2. **Block in with shapes, not lines:** build forms from rectangles, ellipses and the Shape
   Builder, then refine with the Pen and Direct Selection. Fewer anchor points = cleaner shapes.
3. **Palette first:** 4-7 colours as **global swatches** in a group (see global swatches skill),
   including one dark for depth and one light for highlight. Every shape uses a swatch.
4. **Layer by depth:** background, midground, characters, foreground; name the layers.
5. **Simple light:** one light direction; shadows as a darker swatch shape clipped to the form
   (Draw Inside or clipping mask), highlights as a lighter shape.
6. Texture (optional): see the texture skill; keep it on a separate layer so a flat version exists.

## Style rules to fix early (write them on the artboard)
- **Shape language:** rounded (friendly), angular (dynamic/tech), geometric (systematic).
- Corner radius values, whether outlines exist (and their weight), how faces/hands are
  simplified, perspective (flat front, isometric, loose 3/4).
- Proportions for people (e.g. 6 heads tall, small heads for "corporate flat", big for friendly).
These rules are what make a set of 20 spot illustrations feel like one family.

## Clean files
- No stray points (Select > Object > Stray Points), no hidden junk, grouped by object.
- Overlapping shapes are fine for editing; for the client's master, keep them editable.
- Expand brushes and appearances only in an export copy.
- Recolour test: Edit > Edit Colors > Recolor Artwork with an alternate palette - if the result
  is coherent, your palette discipline works.

## Gotchas
- Gradient-heavy "flat" art loses its point and prints unpredictably.
- Very thin details disappear at small sizes; check at thumbnail size (e.g. 80 px wide).

## Verify
- [ ] All colours are global swatches; Recolor test works.
- [ ] Style rules documented; artwork reads at thumbnail size.
- [ ] No stray points; layers named.
