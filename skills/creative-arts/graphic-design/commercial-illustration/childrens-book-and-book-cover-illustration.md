# Book cover and children's book illustration

**Goal:** illustrations that serve the text, paginate well, and print without losing content at the trim or gutter.

## Picture book planning
- **Page count:** picture books are commonly 32 pages (24 and 40 also occur), and front matter and endpapers use some of them, leaving about 12-14 spreads for the story; confirm the count and imposition with the publisher or printer.
- Make a **storyboard** (thumbnail dummy) with each spread: composition, text position, page-turn reveal. Vary the pace: close-ups, wide shots, quiet spreads and action spreads.
- Decide trim size and orientation; they affect composition (portrait, landscape, square).

## Spread layout
- Leave **text space** (quiet area) for words on each page; avoid placing important detail in the gutter or the area close to it, and keep faces away from the fold on spreads.
- **Bleed:** extend the art by the printer's bleed (commonly 0.125 in or 3 mm, including KDP print books) beyond trim; keep key content inside a safe margin.
- Plan **page turns** so surprises land on the recto after turning.
- Keep **character continuity** with a model sheet (see `creative-arts.graphic-design.commercial-illustration.character-illustration-and-consistency`); keep the setting, props and colours consistent between pages.

## Book cover
- The cover has front, spine and back in a single wrap; get a **template** with exact dimensions from the printer, based on trim size, page count and paper (spine width depends on page count and stock).
- Front cover: title legible at thumbnail size (online retail displays covers small), a clear focal point, and genre signals; the type is usually set by the designer.
- Leave room for title, author name, and any quote or badge; check safe areas and barcode area on the back.
- Cover illustration is a commission with a brief from the publisher or author; deliver at the template size, 300 ppi, in the colour mode the printer asks for (usually CMYK for print, RGB for ebook).

## Production
- Work at final size or larger at 300 ppi; keep layered files; export a flattened PDF/TIFF for the layout and an RGB version for ebooks.
- Test the layout as a printed dummy.

## Failure modes
- Text-heavy page with no empty area.
- Faces in the gutter.
- Spine text or art placed too close to the trim.
