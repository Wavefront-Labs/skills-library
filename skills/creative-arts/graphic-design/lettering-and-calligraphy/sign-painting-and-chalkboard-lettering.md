# Sign painting and chalkboard lettering

**Goal:** clean, confident hand-painted or chalk lettering on a real surface, planned so it fits.

## Layout (both)
1. Measure the surface; draw a scaled sketch with baselines, margins and lettering sizes.
2. Transfer to the surface: **pounce pattern** (perforate the paper drawing and dust with chalk or pounce powder), light chalk grid, or projected image.
3. Use a level, straightedge or chalk line; mark centreline and baselines.

## Sign painting
- **Tools:** lettering quills or flat/chisel brushes, a mahl stick (to rest the hand without touching wet paint), lettering enamel (for example One Shot, a widely used brand) and a suitable reducer.
- **Technique:** load the brush; pull a stroke at a single pressure; make **single-stroke letters** with the brush width defining the stem; corners are turned in one motion.
- **Process:** paint the main letters, then any outlines, then shadows or 3D effects. Allow drying times between colours.
- **Surface:** clean, sand and prime; pick enamel or paint that suits the substrate; seal with a clear coat if exposed to weather.

## Chalkboard
- **Surface:** blackboard paint or a slate; **season** a new board by rubbing the side of the chalk over the whole surface and wiping it off, to avoid ghosting.
- **Tools:** chalk sticks, **chalk markers** (liquid chalk), or paint pens. Liquid chalk markers on non-porous boards wipe with a damp cloth; on porous surfaces they can stain, so test.
- **Technique:** sketch lightly in chalk, then work from the top, using a ruler or a stencil for straight lines. Fix mistakes with a damp swab or eraser for chalk markers.
- Keep the design legible at the viewing distance (see `creative-arts.graphic-design.information-design.sign-legibility-letter-height`).

## Design tips
- Limit to two or three styles; use a script for accent words and a sturdy sans or serif for information.
- Use colour contrast; light on dark for boards, dark on light for signs.

## Safety
Use ventilation and follow paint, solvent and brush-cleaning guidance on the product label; dispose of waste according to local rules.

## Self-check
Squint test: the main message reads; stand at the viewing distance and check for crooked baselines.
