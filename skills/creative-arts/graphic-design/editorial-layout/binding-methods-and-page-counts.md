# Binding methods and page counts

**Goal:** a binding that suits the page count, budget and use, and a layout that survives it.

## Methods
| Binding | Page count (typical) | Page multiple | Design implications |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Saddle stitch** (staples through the fold) | 8 to ~64-80 (paper dependent) | **4** | lays fairly flat; **creep** on thick books |
| **Perfect binding** (glued spine, EVA/hotmelt) | ~40+ (needs spine ~3 mm+) | 2 (sheets), signatures of 8/16 | gutter loses 3-6 mm; does not lie flat |
| **PUR binding** | as perfect | as perfect | stronger, flatter-opening; good for coated paper |
| **Section sewn** (thread, then glued or case bound) | books | 8/16/32 signatures | most durable, opens well; hardcovers |
| **Wire-o / spiral** | any | 1 | lies flat/folds back; holes need ~8-10 mm margin at spine |
| **Case binding** (hardcover) | books | signatures | cover board wrap, spine hinge allowances |

## Creep (saddle stitch)
Inner pages stick out further and get trimmed more, so content near the fore-edge on centre pages moves inward.
Printers apply **creep compensation** in imposition; keep text >= 5-6 mm from trim and avoid frames/borders close
to the outer edge in thick saddle-stitched jobs.

## Gutter loss (perfect binding)
The glued spine hides part of the inner margin. Increase inside margins (often +3-5 mm), avoid text or faces across
the gutter, and for spreads with images crossing the spine, shift elements slightly away or duplicate a few mm
("gutter bleed") as the printer advises.

## Spine width
Depends on page count and paper caliper: `spine = pages / 2 x caliper` (+ cover thickness). Get the exact value from
the printer's calculator for your stock; design the cover after it is known (see book cover skill).

## Gotchas
- Self-cover vs plus-cover: saddle-stitched brochures with a heavier cover add 4 pages of a different stock.
- Coated papers and EVA hotmelt crack more easily - prefer PUR.
- Page count must be confirmed before imposition; adding 2 pages to a saddle-stitched job means adding 4.

## Verify
- [ ] Binding chosen with the printer; page count fits its multiple.
- [ ] Margins allow for creep or gutter loss; spine width from the printer's calculator.
