# Magazine cover and coverlines

**Goal:** a cover that sells the issue on a crowded newsstand and as a tiny thumbnail on screen, while
staying recognisably the same title issue after issue.

## Anatomy
- **Masthead (nameplate):** top of the cover, same position every issue. The **top-left** area is the part
  visible when copies overlap on shelves; the masthead and main coverline start there.
- **Cover image:** one strong image, usually a face with eye contact, cropped so the subject can overlap
  the masthead slightly (masthead stays readable).
- **Main coverline:** the lead story, biggest type after the masthead.
- **Secondary coverlines:** 3-6 short lines in the **left third** (visible on racked stands).
- **Puffs, flashes, banners:** price, "exclusive", free gifts. Use sparingly.
- **Dateline, issue number, price, barcode:** barcode (EAN-13 with 2-digit add-on for issue number in many
  markets) at a size and quiet zone the distributor specifies, often bottom left or right.

## Writing and setting coverlines
- Coverlines are headlines: a few words, a number ("27 ideas"), a benefit. Work with the editor.
- Two to three type styles maximum; vary size and weight, not typefaces.
- Keep text off faces; place lines in calm parts of the image. Check contrast (scrim or colour change).

## Brand consistency
- Fixed masthead position, cover grid and type system; vary image and colour per issue.
- For subscriber copies, a cleaner version without many coverlines is common.

## Digital
- Check the cover at **thumbnail size (about 200 px tall)**: masthead and main line must still read.
- Supply RGB versions for digital storefronts and social; animated covers for digital editions if needed.

## Gotchas
- The printer and distributor have barcode specs (size, colour: black on white); a coloured barcode may
  fail scanning.
- Spine text on perfect-bound magazines needs the final page count and paper to set the spine width.
- Too many coverlines make the cover read as clutter at distance.

## Verify
- [ ] Masthead and main coverline readable at thumbnail size and in the left-third overlap.
- [ ] Barcode tested with a scanner app; details correct for this issue.
