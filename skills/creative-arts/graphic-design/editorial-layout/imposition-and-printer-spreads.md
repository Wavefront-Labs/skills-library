# Imposition and printer spreads

**Goal:** know how your pages are arranged on press sheets so you deliver what the printer needs and design spreads that survive.

## Reader spreads vs printer spreads
- **Reader spreads:** pages as the reader sees them (2-3, 4-5). Your InDesign layout.
- **Printer spreads:** pages that sit side by side on the printed sheet. In an 8-page saddle-stitched booklet: 8-1,
  2-7, 6-3, 4-5. Only the centre spread is the same in both.
- **Imposition** arranges pages onto press sheets (signatures of 8, 16, 32 pages) so that after printing, folding and
  trimming, they read in order. Printers do it with imposition software (Prinergy, Preps, Imposition Studio).

## What to deliver
Almost always **single pages** in a PDF in reading order (with bleed on all sides), not spreads, not imposed - unless the
printer asks otherwise. Their software adds creep, gutters, marks and colour bars. Covers are often supplied as a
separate spread (back-spine-front).

## Folding dummy
For anything folded or bound, make a **paper dummy**: fold a sheet the way the job will be folded, number the pages,
unfold it. You will see which pages share a sheet (useful for colour placement, special inks or varnish on some
pages only) and how gatefolds behave.

## Designing across spreads
- Images across the centre spread of a saddle-stitched job line up perfectly; other reader spreads are on different
  sheets - expect small misalignment (crossover) and avoid fine type across the gutter.
- In perfect-bound books, crossovers lose content into the spine (see binding skill).

## Imposing yourself (short-run, office or digital print)
InDesign: **File > Print Booklet** - type 2-up Saddle Stitch or Perfect Bound, space between pages, creep, bleed;
prints or exports a PostScript/PDF of printer spreads. Check page count is a multiple of 4.

## Gotchas
- Sending printer spreads created manually confuses the printer's workflow.
- A 4-page cover + 24-page text = separate signatures/stocks; deliver cover and text as separate PDFs if asked.

## Verify
- [ ] PDF delivered as the printer specifies (single pages, bleed all round).
- [ ] Folding dummy checked for folded/bound jobs; crossovers planned.
