# Print specification and quotes

**Goal:** quotes from several printers that are truly comparable, and no surprises in price or result.

## The spec (send the same to every printer)
1. **Job title** and short description.
2. **Quantity** (with breaks: 500 / 1000 / 2500 - unit costs fall steeply with offset).
3. **Finished (trim) size** and orientation; **flat size** if folded.
4. **Extent:** pages (text + cover), self-cover or plus cover.
5. **Stock:** text and cover separately - weight (gsm), finish (silk/gloss/matt/uncoated), brand if specified, FSC/recycled requirements.
6. **Colours:** 4/4 (CMYK both sides), 4/0, plus spots (e.g. 5/4 with one Pantone), varnish (sealer, spot UV).
7. **Finishing:** fold type, binding (see binding skill), lamination (matt/gloss/soft-touch), foil, emboss, die-cut,
   drilling, numbering, shrink-wrap.
8. **Proofs:** PDF proof, digital proof, contract proof, wet proof (see contract proof skill).
9. **Artwork:** supplied as PDF/X-4, date ready.
10. **Delivery:** address(es), date, packing (boxes of 250, pallets), split deliveries.
11. Terms: overs/unders tolerance (trade customs often allow up to +/-5-10% unless agreed), payment terms.

## Offset vs digital
- **Digital** (toner/inkjet): no plates, cost per copy flat - best for short runs (often < 500-1000), personalisation
  (variable data), quick turnaround. Colour gamut and paper range narrower.
- **Offset** litho: plates and make-ready fixed costs, cheap per copy at volume, spot colours, widest stocks, best
  consistency on long runs.
- The crossover point depends on format and pages - ask both on the same spec.

## Comparing quotes
Check every line was quoted as specified (stock substitutions are common: "equivalent stock"), what proofs are included,
delivery, and whether VAT/tax and shipping are included. Ask about lead time from artwork approval.

## Gotchas
- Changing quantity, stock or extent after quoting changes price - requote.
- Paper prices fluctuate; quotes may be valid only for 30 days.

## Verify
- [ ] One written spec sent to all printers; quotes compared line by line.
- [ ] Final purchase order restates the spec, proofs, delivery and tolerances.
