# Using white space

**Goal:** empty space that structures the page, groups related items, gives emphasis and signals quality
(Lupton and Phillips, Graphic Design: The New Basics).

## Two scales
- **Macro white space:** margins, space between columns, around images and between sections.
- **Micro white space:** leading, letterspacing, paragraph spacing, padding inside boxes.
Both must be consistent multiples of a base unit (the leading or a 4/8 pt unit).

## Principles
- **Proximity groups:** items closer together read as related. Space between groups should clearly exceed
  space within groups (at least 2x). A caption closer to its image than to body text belongs to it.
- **Active vs passive:** asymmetric empty areas create direction and tension; evenly spread leftover space
  looks like nothing was planned. Gather the space into one or two meaningful areas.
- **Emphasis by isolation:** one element surrounded by space becomes the focal point without needing size or
  colour.
- **Generous margins** signal calm and quality (books, luxury brands); tight margins signal urgency and
  density (newspapers, sale flyers). Choose to match the voice.

## Practical moves
- Remove boxes and rules; let space separate instead.
- Reduce the number of elements before reducing space between them.
- Use a consistent spacing scale (e.g. 1, 2, 3, 6 lines) for all vertical gaps.
- Align elements to shared edges so the space between them reads as shapes.

## Handling "fill the space" feedback
- Explain the job of the space (focus, legibility, premium feel) in brief terms the client agreed.
- Show the cluttered alternative side by side; it usually ends the discussion.
- If content must be added, re-plan the page count or format rather than shrinking space.

## Gotchas
- Trapped white space (small awkward gaps surrounded by elements) looks like errors; merge it into larger areas.
- White space in print costs paper; in budget publications make it purposeful, not uniform.

## Verify
- [ ] Squint test: groups read clearly; the focal point is obvious.
- [ ] All gaps come from the spacing scale; no trapped leftover space.
