# Liquify and retouching ethics

**Goal:** subtle shape corrections that nobody notices, and no corrections that mislead.

## Set up
Convert the layer (or a merged copy) to a **smart object** first: Liquify then becomes a smart
filter you can reopen and adjust or mask.

## Liquify (Filter > Liquify, Shift+Ctrl/Cmd+X)
- **Forward Warp (W):** push pixels. Large brush (bigger than the area), low pressure/density
  (Pressure 10-30), many gentle pushes.
- **Reconstruct (R)** brush and Reconstruct button restore areas.
- **Freeze Mask (F):** paint over areas that must not move (straight edges, nearby features,
  background lines); **Thaw (D)**.
- **Show Backdrop** with opacity to compare to the original; Show Mesh to spot distortion.
- Pucker/Bloat for small adjustments; Twirl rarely.

## Face-Aware Liquify
Detects faces: sliders for eyes, nose, mouth, face shape, or drag on-canvas handles. Useful for
minor expression corrections (a smile on one side) in group photos.

## Typical legitimate uses
- Smooth clothing bulges and creases, straighten a collar.
- Correct product shape distortions from wide lenses or packaging dents.
- Fix lens distortion on faces close to wide lenses.

## Gotchas
- Warping near straight lines (door frames, horizons, tiles) bends them - freeze them, or check
  the background afterwards. Bent backgrounds are the giveaway of manipulated bodies.
- Large brushes at high pressure create mush; work in several passes.

## Ethics and disclosure
- Editorial and news: shape changes to people or events are not acceptable (NPPA Code of Ethics,
  most agency policies: no adding, removing or reshaping content beyond tone and dust).
- Several jurisdictions require disclosure of body-altered commercial images (e.g. France's
  "photographie retouchée" label on commercial images where body shape is modified; Norway's
  marketing act amendment for social media ads). Check local rules for the market.
- Agree retouching scope with the art director and, where relevant, the subject.

## Verify
- [ ] Applied as a smart filter; backgrounds and straight lines undistorted.
- [ ] Changes within the agreed brief and disclosure rules for the use.
