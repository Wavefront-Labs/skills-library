# Development phase deliverables

**Development** is the phase where an idea becomes a financeable, producible project. It happens before pre-production and its output is the package on which a green-light decision is made.

## Typical deliverables
- **Concept and logline**, and a short **treatment** or synopsis.
- **Series bible** (for TV) or story outline (for film): world, characters, tone, episode formats and sample story ideas.
- **Scripts** or a pilot script, depending on the format.
- **Visual development**: character, environment and colour concepts, a style guide or style frames (`visual-development` in this field).
- **Pitch package**: deck, mood reels, sizzle or pilot teaser, and a pitch document.
- **Test animation** or look test to prove style, tools and pipeline are viable (`pipeline-architecture.md`).
- **Draft budget, schedule and delivery plan**, including the technique, crew model and outsourcing plan (`building-an-animation-budget.md`).
- **Rights and legal**: chain of title, music and voice talent intentions.

## Gates
1. **Greenlight**: decision-makers (studio, network or financier) approve the package. Record the decision, scope, budget cap and delivery dates.
2. After greenlight, move to **pre-production** with the lock list (`preproduction-lock-list.md`).

## Practice
- Keep a **deliverables list with owners and due dates** in the tracker (`production-tracking.md`).
- Version everything with dates; a pitch deck changes often.
- Keep tests honest: if the test animation uses shortcuts the production cannot sustain, say so in the notes.

## Gotchas
- Development scope creep: set a time and money limit.
- Unwritten decisions get re-argued; log approvals.

## Verify
- [ ] Each deliverable has an owner and a status.
- [ ] Greenlight decision, budget and dates are written down.
