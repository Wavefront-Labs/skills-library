# rigify-face-rig

Goal: a working face rig from the Rigify face rig types, fitted to your character.

## What you get
The modular face is built on the **skin system**: facial features are chains of `skin.stretchy_chain` or `skin.basic_chain` rigs (B-Bone chains that bridge control points); `skin.glue` reads or adjusts the state of a control generated by another skin rig; `skin.anchor` gives a control you fully own, with highest priority when controls merge; `face.skin_jaw` manages loops of mouth chains in response to jaw and mouth controls; `face.skin_eye` manages the top and bottom eyelid chains and generates the eye rotation mechanism and eye target controls; `face.basic_tongue` makes a simple tongue.

Legacy `faces.super_face` metarigs are deprecated: the *Upgrade Face Rig* button appears above *Generate Rig* in the metarig's Armature properties and converts to the modular system. Skinning stays compatible, but existing poses and animations will likely not.

## Fitting
1. Move face bones (Edit mode, X-mirror) to edge loops: lid chains along the lid edges, lip chains along the lip line.
2. Controls of two chains that completely overlap are merged automatically, so keep chain end points at matching positions where chains meet. Merge ownership uses parent depth, the `.T`/`.B` and `.L`/`.R` name markers, then alphabetical order; *Chain Priority* overrides it.
3. Name chains for the parent rigs: `face.skin_eye` needs two child chains tagged `.T` and `.B` joined at the eye corners; `face.skin_jaw` needs each lip loop made of four chains tagged `.T`/`.B` and `.L`/`.R`.
4. All face bones are in the *Face* bone collection by default; delete that collection's bones in Edit mode to drop the face.

## Binding
Deform bones are in the *DEF* bone collection. Automatic weights usually work well if bones are placed correctly and the mesh has enough topology. Legacy-face eyes and teeth do not deform; bind that geometry with Child Of constraints. `face.skin_eye` can generate eyeball and iris deform bones (option *Eyeball and Iris Deforms*).

## Gotchas
- Chain ends that should merge but are slightly apart leave duplicate controls.
- `skin.glue` needs its bone head on a control of another skin rig; it is an error if there is none.

## Verify
Pose the mouth open and the lids closed: no lip intersection, and the lids follow the eyeball.
