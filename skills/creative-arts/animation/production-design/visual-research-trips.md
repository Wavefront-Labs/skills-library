# Visual research trips and archives

Goal: the world is designed from observed specifics, not from other films.

## 1. Plan the research
- Write the questions first: what architecture, materials, light, vegetation, people, objects and
  rituals does the story need? List each with which sequences depend on it.
- Pick sources per question: a location trip, museum collections, archives, period catalogues, expert
  interviews, cultural consultants.

## 2. On a trip
- Shoot reference systematically: wide establishing, medium, detail and texture close-ups of each subject;
  include a scale object or person; note time of day and direction.
- Sketch on site (fast thumbnails and colour notes); sketches record what mattered, photos record everything.
- Record sound and short video of movement (water, crowds, foliage in wind) for animators and FX.
- Log permissions and contacts; respect restrictions on sacred or private sites.

## 3. Archives and museums
- Request high-res scans with licence terms; note accession numbers for traceability.
- Photograph construction details (joints, fasteners, wear) more than whole objects.

## 4. Library
- Ingest into a shared reference library with tags: location, subject, material, period, sequence.
- Make a one-page summary per trip: 10-20 best images and the design insights they gave.

## Verify
- [ ] Research questions linked to sequences.
- [ ] Shots include scale and context.
- [ ] Library tagged and searchable by sequence.
