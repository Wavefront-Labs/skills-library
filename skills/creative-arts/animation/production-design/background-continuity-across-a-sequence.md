# Background continuity across a sequence

Goal: the place stays the same place from shot to shot.

## 1. Master reference
- Paint or render a **master BG** (wide establishing) and an overhead floor plan of the set with
  furniture, windows and light sources.
- Every shot BG is derived from this: same objects, same positions, same light direction relative to the set.

## 2. Matching details
- Keep a list of continuity-critical items: window positions, a picture on the wall, a lit lamp, time of day,
  weather, damage state.
- Reverse angles: use the plan to work out what is behind the camera in the master.

## 3. Light and colour
- Same key direction in world space; sample palette from the master for each shot.
- Planned changes (lights switched off, sunset progressing) logged by shot.

## 4. Review
- Lay out all BGs of the sequence in cut order and check against the plan.
- Check against animation: props characters touch must match on both sides of a cut.

## Verify
- [ ] Master BG and floor plan exist.
- [ ] Continuity items list checked shot by shot.
- [ ] BGs reviewed in cut order.
