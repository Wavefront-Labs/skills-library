# Clarity of geography

Goal: in any action sequence the audience knows where everyone is, where they are going, and
where the danger is.

## Tools
1. **Establish the space** early with a wide that shows the layout and the key landmarks
   (exit, cliff edge, the goal).
2. **Landmarks**: distinctive elements that recur in closer shots so position is readable
   (a red door, a tower, a burning car).
3. **Overhead plan**: draw positions per beat; check every panel against it.
4. **Re-establish** after big moves or every 30-60 seconds of close action.
5. **Consistent screen direction** for movement toward/away from goals.
6. **Goal visibility**: keep the objective in frame in some shots (the closing door, the bomb timer).

## Common fixes for confused action
- Insert a wide shot.
- Add a landmark in the background of close shots.
- Reduce cuts; let a longer shot show the relationship.
- Use an overhead.

## Failure modes
- All close-ups and shaky framing ("chaos cam") - excitement without comprehension.
- Landmarks that change position between shots.
- Goals never shown.

## Verify
- [ ] Viewer can draw a rough map after the sequence.
- [ ] Overhead plan matches all panels.
- [ ] Landmarks consistent.
