# Trucks and dollies

Goal: move the camera toward, away from or alongside the subject with a story purpose.

## Terminology
- **2D truck in/out**: the camera moves closer/further on the rostrum (or digital camera scales);
  in 2D, perspective does not change - it is effectively a zoom of flat artwork. Noted in
  field sizes: "TRUCK IN 12F to 6F".
- **3D dolly in/out (push-in/pull-out)**: camera physically moves; perspective and parallax change.
- **Tracking / trucking (sideways)**: camera moves alongside a moving subject.
- **Crane / boom**: camera rises or descends.

## Story uses
- **Push-in**: increasing emphasis, realisation, growing tension - slow push on a face as it dawns.
- **Pull-out**: reveal context (the character is alone in a huge space), ending, release.
- **Track**: keep pace with a character; travel conversations.
- **Crane up**: departure, scale, ending shots.

## Boarding
- Draw the start frame and the end frame (inner rectangle for truck in) with arrows from corners.
- Note speed and ease ("slow push, 6 s, ease out").
- For dolly moves with parallax, indicate foreground elements and how they shift.

## Failure modes
- Push-ins on every emotional beat - they stop meaning anything.
- 2D truck-ins that magnify artwork beyond its resolution (paint BGs at the closest field size).
- Moves that end without a resolved composition.

## Verify
- [ ] Start/end frames and field sizes (2D) or path (3D) noted.
- [ ] Each move has a story reason.
- [ ] End composition is a good still frame.
