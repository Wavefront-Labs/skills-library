# Triangle staging

Goal: stage groups so every character reads, the composition has depth, and coverage stays
consistent.

## The triangle
- Place three characters at the corners of a triangle on the floor plan, not in a line.
- From most camera positions they appear at different depths and heights - no overlapping
  silhouettes, natural foreground/background.
- The character at the apex facing camera usually dominates; the two at the base can be OTS
  frames for coverage.

## Coverage
- Choose the main axis (often between the two characters in the most important exchange) and
  keep cameras on one side for that exchange.
- Shots: the master (all three), two-shots (pairs), singles; each pair has its own line - when
  focus moves to a new pair, re-establish.
- With larger groups, stage subgroups as triangles and use the speaking pair's line.

## Rearranging
- When focus moves to a different character, move characters (one walks to the apex position)
  or move the camera to put the new focal character at the apex.

## Failure modes
- Characters in a straight line facing camera ("police line-up").
- Silhouettes overlapping in the master.
- Losing track of eyelines with three people - use a plan.

## Verify
- [ ] Master shot shows all characters separated in depth.
- [ ] Focal character at the dominant position for each beat.
- [ ] Eyelines consistent with the floor plan.
