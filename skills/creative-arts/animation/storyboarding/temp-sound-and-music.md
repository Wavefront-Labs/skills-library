# Temp sound and music

Goal: make an animatic play like a film so the team can judge timing and emotion, using
placeholder sound and score.

## Temp sound effects
- Rough SFX for every impact, door, footstep that matters to timing; ambiences for location.
- Use a library you have rights to for internal use (studio library, licensed packs).
- Keep SFX on separate tracks from dialogue and music for editorial.

## Temp music
- Choose cues for **function**: tempo and emotional arc of the sequence.
- Edit music to fit (cut on phrase boundaries, crossfade at bar lines); do not let it dictate
  every cut unless the sequence will be scored to picture that way.
- Mark music-driven sequences (songs, montages) early - they may need final or pre-scored
  music before animation.

## Temp love
Teams get attached to temp music; the composer then has to imitate it. Mitigate:
- Use temp from many sources rather than one recognisable score.
- Share with the composer early what the temp is doing (function) rather than its sound.
- Periodically screen without music to check the visuals stand alone.

## Rights
- Temp music is for internal screenings only; never ship or publish a reel with unlicensed music.
- Track every cue used in an edit decision list or cue sheet so it can be replaced.

## Failure modes
- Music hiding pacing problems (sequence only works with the temp).
- Loud temp masking dialogue.
- Unlicensed temp leaking into public trailers or festival cuts.

## Verify
- [ ] Sequence also plays acceptably without music.
- [ ] SFX, dialogue, music on separate tracks.
- [ ] Cue sheet of temp music maintained.
