# Focal point composition

Goal: each panel has one clear point of interest that the audience sees first, followed by a
controlled secondary read.

## Contrast tools
- **Value**: highest contrast at the focal point (dark against light or light against dark).
- **Isolation**: negative space around the subject.
- **Size/position**: larger, on a thirds point or framed.
- **Detail**: most detail at the focal point, simplified elsewhere.
- **Lines**: leading lines and eyelines converge there.
- **Color** (color boards): saturation peak at the focal point.

## Method
1. Write the panel's point in a few words (what must the audience see?).
2. Thumbnail tiny (about 5 cm wide) in 2-3 values.
3. Make the focal point win on at least two contrasts.
4. Establish a secondary read (the person being talked to, the threat in the background) with
   less contrast.
5. Remove distractions: tangents, busy textures near the subject, competing bright spots.

## Thumbnail test
Shrink the panel to about 100 px wide or squint: the focal point should still pop. If not, push
value contrast or simplify around it.

## Failure modes
- Two equal focal points competing.
- Highest contrast on a background element.
- Focal point placed where a later camera move or text overlay will cover it.

## Verify
- [ ] One clear focal point per panel at thumbnail size.
- [ ] Secondary elements quieter.
- [ ] Focal point matches the story point written for the panel.
