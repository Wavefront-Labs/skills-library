# Pitch preparation

Goal: walk into the pitch with the sequence in its best order, rehearsed, with alternates ready.

## Steps
1. **Select panels**: remove redundant panels that slow the pitch; keep every story beat.
2. **Order and numbering**: panels numbered so notes can reference them ("panel 34").
3. **Setup**:
   - Wall pitch: pin boards in rows, left to right, top to bottom; space for pointer.
   - Digital: play in Storyboard Pro's playback, a slide deck, or an image viewer with
     keyboard flips; test the display beforehand.
4. **Rehearse** out loud at least twice with timing; mark where to pause.
5. **Alternates**: have alternate panels/versions ready for likely notes (a different gag, a
   shorter version) - showing you considered options builds trust.
6. **Context sheet**: script pages, previous sequence ending, any audio.
7. **Note-taking**: bring a partner or recorder (with permission) to capture notes.

## Timing target
Know the intended screen length (pages x about 1 minute per page as a rule of thumb, adjusted
for action) and rehearse to match.

## Failure modes
- Too many panels; pitch drags.
- Technical setup failing at the start.
- No numbering - notes cannot reference panels.

## Verify
- [ ] Panels numbered and in final order.
- [ ] Two timed rehearsals done.
- [ ] Alternates prepared for the riskiest beats.
