# Action continuity across cuts

Goal: everything that carries over a cut matches - pose, prop in hand, costume state, position,
weather and light - unless the change is intended and motivated.

## What to track
- **Pose and action phase**: the arm raised at the end of shot 1 is raised at the start of shot 2.
- **Props**: which hand, how held, full/empty, broken/whole.
- **Costumes and damage**: hat on/off, torn sleeve, wet clothes, dirt progression.
- **Positions**: who stands where; distances between characters.
- **Environment**: time of day, weather, light direction, doors open/closed.
- **Movement direction and speed** across cuts.

## Method
1. Keep a running continuity list per sequence (state of each character and prop at each cut).
2. When boarding a cut, look at the last panel of the previous shot before drawing.
3. For time jumps, signal the jump (transition, establishing shot, a costume change) so changes
   read as intended.
4. Mark continuity notes in the panel description ("cup now in left hand").
5. Revisionists: when a panel changes, check downstream panels for knock-on continuity.

## Failure modes
- Prop switching hands across a cut.
- Damage that heals and reappears.
- Light direction flipping between shots of the same moment.
- Revisions that fix one shot and break the next.

## Verify
- [ ] Continuity list maintained for the sequence.
- [ ] Each cut checked against the previous shot's last panel.
- [ ] Intended time jumps clearly signalled.
