# Visual movement

Goal: choose how things move on screen - and how the audience's eye moves - as part of the
visual plan. Block distinguishes object movement, camera movement and the audience's point of
attention moving across the frame.

## Types
1. **Object movement**: characters/props moving. Direction (horizontal calm, vertical, diagonal
   intense; toward camera most intense), speed, and quality (smooth vs erratic).
2. **Camera movement**: pans, tilts, dollies, cranes - shows the movement of the viewpoint.
3. **Eye movement (point of attention)**: the path the audience's eye follows inside a shot and
   across cuts.

## Contrast and affinity
- **Affinity**: movement continues in the same direction and speed across cuts (smooth,
  less intense).
- **Contrast**: opposed directions (left-right cut to right-left), speed changes, still vs moving
  (more intense).
- Eye movement across cuts: matching focus position = affinity; jumping focus = contrast.

## Method
1. For each sequence decide the movement plan (affinity for calm, contrast for conflict).
2. Board movement arrows; check direction and speed relationships at each cut.
3. Plan camera moves so they complement, not duplicate, object movement.

## Failure modes
- Random movement directions at cuts (unintended contrast).
- Camera moving constantly with no purpose.
- Fast object movement in calm scenes without intent.

## Verify
- [ ] Movement directions at cuts follow the plan.
- [ ] Camera moves motivated.
- [ ] Eye trace across cuts checked.
