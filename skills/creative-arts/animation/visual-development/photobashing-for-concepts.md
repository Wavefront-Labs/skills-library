# Photobashing for concepts

Goal: fast, believable concepts by combining photo fragments with painting, used in
environment and prop visdev to explore options quickly.

## Workflow
1. **Value/shape sketch first**: the composition is decided by a thumbnail, not by the photos found.
2. **Source**: use owned photos, licensed stock or CC0 libraries; record each source. Avoid
   recognisable copyrighted designs.
3. **Block in**: place photo pieces with layer masks (non-destructive) for big shapes and
   textures; use Transform > Perspective/Warp to fit the perspective grid.
4. **Unify**: match horizon lines and vanishing points; adjust values per depth plane with
   Levels/Curves; unify colour with gradient maps or a Color-mode layer; add atmosphere layers.
5. **Light**: repaint light and shadow direction to one source; photos lit differently
   betray the bash.
6. **Paint over**: paint across seams, simplify detail toward the show style, add design
   elements photos cannot give.
7. **Check**: flip, greyscale, thumbnail.

## When not to
- Final art for a strongly stylized 2D show: the photo texture will clash; use it only for exploration.
- When the design needs invention the photos cannot give (alien anatomy, unique architecture).

## Failure modes
- Mismatched perspective and light: collage look.
- Photo detail everywhere, no focal hierarchy.
- Unlicensed images in a delivered concept.

## Verify
- [ ] One light source, one perspective.
- [ ] Seams painted over and detail simplified.
- [ ] Source/licence list attached.
