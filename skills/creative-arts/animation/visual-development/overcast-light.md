# Overcast light

Goal: the soft, even light of a cloudy day, often chosen for melancholy, calm or
naturalistic mood.

## Characteristics (Gurney)
- The whole sky is the light source: soft, coming mostly from above.
- **Low contrast**: little difference between top planes and side planes; no strong light/shadow split.
- **Cast shadows** soft or absent; **occlusion shadows** do the grounding work.
- **Local colour** reads truer and more saturated (no glare, no warm/cool split). Greens of
  foliage look rich.
- **Tops lighter than sides**: up-facing planes catch the most sky; undersides dark.
- Colour temperature neutral to slightly cool; sky often lighter than any land value.

## Method
1. Light planes by orientation to the sky dome: top planes lightest, verticals mid,
   undersides darkest.
2. Keep the value range compressed except for tiny occlusion darks.
3. Let local colour carry the image; push saturation slightly in midtones.
4. Wet surfaces reflect the bright sky: lighter roads, sheen on leaves.
5. Keep the sky value high; reflections in water match it.

## Failure modes
- Adding hard cast shadows "for interest" - breaks the light logic.
- Grey, desaturated everything - overcast is low contrast, not low colour.
- Missing occlusion, so objects float.

## Verify
- [ ] No hard cast shadows.
- [ ] Plane values by orientation to the sky.
- [ ] Local colours saturated, contacts grounded.
