# Candlelight and firelight

Goal: intimate warm-lit scenes - campfires, candles, hearths, torches.

## Characteristics (Gurney)
- **Colour**: about 1800-2000K, deep orange-yellow; everything takes on warmth, blues go grey.
- **Steep falloff** (inverse-square): faces near the flame bright, a metre away noticeably
  darker, the room behind near black.
- **Small source**: crisp cast shadows that splay outward and grow fast with distance.
- **Low position**: campfires and candles on tables light from below or at chin level,
  creating unusual, often dramatic or eerie modelling.
- **Saturated shadows/edges**: warm light grading through red-orange into dark.
- **Flicker**: intensity and shadow edges move.

## Method
1. Place the source and mark its falloff circles (bright, mid, dark zones).
2. Light forms by distance and orientation; nearer planes brighter.
3. Keep the darkness large; contrast sells the warmth.
4. Add a secondary cool source (moonlight through a window) only for contrast and readability.
5. For animation, plan flicker as a few intensity keys and slight shadow shifts, not random noise.

## Failure modes
- Light reaching too far into the room.
- Shadows lit from above while the source is low.
- Uniform orange tint with no value structure.

## Verify
- [ ] Falloff zones visible.
- [ ] Shadow directions radiate from the flame.
- [ ] Darkness occupies most of the frame.
