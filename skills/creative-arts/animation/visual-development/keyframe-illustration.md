# Keyframe illustration of a story moment

Goal: a finished painting of a pivotal story moment that shows the team (and the studio)
what the film looks and feels like at its most important beats.

## Choosing the moment
- A turning point (inciting incident, midpoint reveal, climax) or a signature location.
- One idea per image: the emotion of the beat must be the first read.
- Camera from the board if available; otherwise propose one that layout can achieve.

## Method
1. Write the beat in one sentence and the emotion in one word.
2. Thumbnail 6-12 compositions (value thumbnails); choose with the director.
3. Design the light for the emotion (key/fill ratio, temperature, atmosphere).
4. Colour comp at small size; check against the colour script.
5. Paint at final aspect ratio and resolution with on-model characters and approved sets.
6. Edge control and focal hierarchy pass; check in greyscale and at thumbnail.
7. Deliver with notes on what is canon (lighting, palette) vs exploratory (props).

## Failure modes
- Pin-up illustration that ignores the film's camera language and aspect ratio.
- Off-model characters, causing confusion downstream.
- Multiple focal ideas in one frame.

## Verify
- [ ] Beat and emotion written and readable in the image.
- [ ] Matches the colour script and final aspect ratio.
- [ ] Characters and sets on model.
