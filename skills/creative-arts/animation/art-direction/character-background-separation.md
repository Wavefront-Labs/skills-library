# Character-background separation

Goal: the audience never searches for the character.

## 1. Pick a separation axis per show and per scene
| Axis | Typical choice |
|---|---|
| Value | characters lighter or darker than the BG area behind them by at least 2 steps (of ~9) |
| Saturation | characters more saturated; BG slightly greyed (classic 2D TV) |
| Temperature | warm characters on cool BGs, or vice versa |
| Detail / texture | flat characters on textured BGs (or the reverse) |
| Edges | crisp character lines against softer BG edges |
Use at least two axes; one is fragile when lighting changes.

## 2. Plan BG zones where characters act
- From layout, mark the action zones; paint those BG areas in a mid value and calm texture so characters
  of any colour will read. Push detail and contrast to areas where characters don't go.

## 3. Test
- Drop each cast member's colour model (for the right lighting state) onto the BG in each action zone.
- Greyscale check: character silhouette still reads.
- Squint/blur test at thumbnail size.

## 4. Fixes in shot
- Lift or darken the BG behind the character locally (soft vignette or BG paint).
- Add a rim or kicker (CG) or a coloured hold line (2D).
- Last resort: change costume colour for the episode, logged in costume continuity.

## Verify
- [ ] Two separation axes chosen and written in the bible.
- [ ] Action zones painted in calm mid values.
- [ ] Greyscale and blur tests pass for every cast member.
