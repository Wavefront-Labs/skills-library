# Running art dailies

Goal: a 30-60 minute session that turns submitted work into decisions and written notes the same day.

## 1. Before
- Artists submit by a cutoff (e.g. two hours prior) into a playlist in the tracker (ShotGrid, ftrack, Kitsu).
- Coordinator orders the playlist: blockers and approvals first, then work in progress, then FYI items.
- Room: calibrated display or projector, consistent dim lighting, keys and bible open on a second screen.

## 2. During
- The artist introduces the version in one sentence (what changed, what they want).
- The art director gives notes; a coordinator types them live into the version's note field.
- Each item ends with a status: approved, approved with notes, revise, or hold.
- Timebox about 3-5 minutes per item; longer debates go to a follow-up.
- Compare against the key or previous version side by side, not from memory.

## 3. After
- Notes published within the hour; statuses updated in the tracker.
- Draw-overs attached by end of day.
- Unresolved questions get an owner and a date.

## Failure modes
- No status at the end of an item: the artist leaves unsure whether to continue.
- Spoken-only notes: lost by tomorrow.
- Uncalibrated room: colour notes differ from what artists see at their desks.

## Verify
- [ ] Every item leaves the room with a status.
- [ ] Notes in the tracker the same day.
- [ ] Session ran within its timebox.
