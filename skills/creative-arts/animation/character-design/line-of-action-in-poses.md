# Line of action in poses

Goal: every pose built on one clear curve from head to foot (or through the
leading limb) that carries the energy of the gesture.

## Shapes of the line
- **C-curve**: bending, reaching, recoiling, sad slump.
- **S-curve**: graceful, relaxed weight shift, sly or seductive.
- **Straight diagonal**: thrust, push, stiff resolve, falling.
- **Vertical straight**: rigid attention, shock (use sparingly; reads static).

## Method
1. Draw the line of action first, one stroke, before any anatomy.
2. Hang the spine, head and supporting leg on it; the weight-bearing leg usually
   continues the line to the ground.
3. Push the curve 20-30% further than the reference; photo reference is always flatter
   than a good drawing.
4. Make secondary lines (arms, tail, hair) echo or counter the main line deliberately.
5. Check against the silhouette test; if the line does not read in black, open the
   limbs or rotate the pose.
6. In sequences of poses, alternate line directions between key poses (C one way, then
   reversed) for reversal energy.

## Failure modes
- Line of action drawn but body built straight on top of it.
- Every limb fighting the line (arms bending against the main curve without reason).
- Overcurved lines that break anatomy or balance.

## Verify
- [ ] The main line is visible when the drawing is reduced to gesture.
- [ ] Supporting leg and spine follow it.
- [ ] Pose reads in silhouette.
