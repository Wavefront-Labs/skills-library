# Heroic and stylized adult proportions

Goal: adult figures with a deliberate proportion canon, from the realistic 7.5 heads
to Loomis's heroic 8-8.5 and stylized fashion/action canons of 9+.

## Canons (head units)
| Canon | Use | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 7-7.5 | realistic adult | crotch near the 4-head mark (midpoint) |
| 8 | idealized/heroic | Loomis ideal; midpoint at the crotch, knees at 6 |
| 8.5-9 | superhero, action | extra length in legs and neck, smaller head |
| 9-10 | fashion, elegant villain | legs very long, narrow shoulders (women) |

## Gender and build cues
- Heroic male: shoulders about 2 to 2.3 head-widths, narrow hips, V taper.
- Heroic female: shoulders about 1.75 to 2 head-widths, hips close to shoulder width,
  waist narrow.
- Heavy build: widen torso and limbs, keep head unit the same so height still reads.

## Method
1. Decide the show canon once and write it on the style guide.
2. Build a head-unit grid; mark chin, nipples, navel, crotch, mid-thigh, knee, shin, feet.
3. Add length where the stylization wants it (legs for elegance, torso for power), and
   note every deviation from the canon on the model sheet.
4. Keep hands roughly face-length and feet about a head long even when stylizing;
   tiny hands on a hero read weak.
5. Check against the cast: a 9-head lead next to a 7-head realistic cast reads as a
   different show.

## Failure modes
- Small heads with no compensating neck/shoulder mass: pinhead.
- Inconsistent canon between characters in the same scene.
- Long legs drawn but knees left at the realistic height.

## Verify
- [ ] Canon written and grid drawn.
- [ ] Landmarks fall on the grid.
- [ ] Deviations noted and consistent across the cast.
