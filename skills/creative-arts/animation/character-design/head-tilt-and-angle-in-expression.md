# Head tilt and angle in expression

Goal: expressions that use the whole head, not just the features on a front-facing mask.

## Angle meanings (typical reads)
| Head move | Read |
|---|---|
| Chin down, eyes up | menace, determination, sulking, shyness (depends on brows) |
| Chin up, eyes down | pride, contempt, defiance |
| Side tilt (roll) | curiosity, sympathy, flirtation, confusion |
| Head pulled back | surprise, disgust, recoil |
| Head forward | aggression, interest, intensity |
| Turned away, eyes back | suspicion, coyness |

## Drawing it
1. Build the head as a ball plus jaw (Loomis method); rotate the ball first.
2. Curve the brow line and eye line around the sphere: chin down makes them curve
   upward (smile shape) when seen from front; chin up curves them down.
3. Foreshorten: chin down compresses the face below the eyes and shows more cranium;
   chin up shows under the jaw and nostrils.
4. Ears move opposite to the face tilt: chin down raises the ears relative to the eyes.
5. Combine with brow/eye shapes from the expression sheet; the angle multiplies their effect.

## Failure modes
- Features left on straight horizontal lines while the head tilts (pasted-on mask).
- Ears not moving with the tilt.
- Every expression drawn front-on; the sheet looks like emoji.

## Verify
- [ ] Expression sheet includes at least 3 head angles.
- [ ] Construction lines curve with the tilt.
- [ ] Ear position matches the tilt.
