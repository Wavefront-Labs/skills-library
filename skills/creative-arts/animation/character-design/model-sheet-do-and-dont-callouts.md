# Model sheet do and don't callouts

Goal: prevent the predictable off-model errors by showing them, marked wrong, next to
the correct version.

## Typical callouts
- Eye spacing or size drift ("eyes touch the nose bridge", not "eyes far apart").
- Head shape flattening in 3/4.
- Hair or ear position wrong in profile.
- Hand size, finger count.
- Tangent and silhouette problems (arm hiding the waist line).
- Costume details flipped (logo side, parting side).
- Expression mistakes (smile too wide, teeth showing when they should not).

## Method
1. Collect real errors: review the first rounds of storyboards, animation or outsourced
   work and list repeated mistakes.
2. For each, draw the correct version and the wrong version side by side at the same
   size and angle.
3. Mark with a clear check and cross (or green and red), plus a one-line written rule.
4. Keep to 6-12 callouts per sheet; prioritise by frequency.
5. Update after each review round; retire callouts that stop happening.

## Failure modes
- Hypothetical mistakes nobody makes, while the real ones are missing.
- Wrong drawings exaggerated so far they do not resemble actual errors.
- Notes without drawings ("keep eyes consistent").

## Verify
- [ ] Every callout comes from an observed error.
- [ ] Correct and wrong drawn side by side with a one-line rule.
- [ ] Sheet version-dated and updated after reviews.
