# Catalyst and debate

Goal: an event that knocks the hero's life off balance, followed by a short, real hesitation that raises the
question the act break will answer.

## 1. Catalyst (about 10% in; page 12 of 110)
- Something happens **to** the hero that makes the old life impossible to continue as is: a telegram, a
  firing, a death, a meeting, a discovery.
- It must be clear and on screen, not reported later.
- It should hit the hero's flaw or want specifically (it's this hero's problem, not a random disaster).
- In animation shorts and TV episodes, the catalyst often comes within the first 1-2 minutes.

## 2. Debate (from the catalyst to about 20-25%)
- The hero asks a question: "Should I go?", "Can I do this?", "Is it real?".
- Show the cost of each choice; let the hero try to return to normal and fail.
- Mentors, friends and setbacks argue each side.
- End the debate with a choice the hero makes actively (Break into Two); the hero must not be dragged
  into act two by events alone.

## 3. Tests
- Remove the catalyst: does the story still start? If yes, it is not the catalyst.
- Is the debate question one sentence? If the debate has no question, it is just delay.
- Does the debate take longer than the setup? Shorten it.

## 4. Common mistakes
- Catalyst too late (audience waits); multiple catalysts competing.
- Debate that repeats one beat several times.

## Verify
- [ ] Catalyst at roughly 10% and on screen.
- [ ] Debate question written as a sentence.
- [ ] Hero makes an active choice into act two.
