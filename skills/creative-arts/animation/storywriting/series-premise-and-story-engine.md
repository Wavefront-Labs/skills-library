# Series premise and story engine

Goal: a premise that produces 50+ episodes without repeating itself.

## 1. The engine
- A repeatable situation that keeps generating problems: a job (the workplace), a mission (monster of the
  week), a family dynamic, a world with rules that cause trouble, a goal that can't be reached quickly.
- Test: pitch ten episode ideas in ten minutes. If they come easily and differ, the engine works.

## 2. The lead
- A strong want and a flaw that can't be fully fixed (or fixes only slowly) - it causes episodes.
- Episodic shows reset; serialised ones evolve slowly. Choose and state which.

## 3. The cast
- Each main character has a distinct attitude to the lead's want; combinations create conflict.
- Map pairings: every pair of main characters should have a natural conflict or comedy dynamic.

## 4. The series bible
- Logline and premise; tone; world and rules; character profiles (want, flaw, voice, relationships);
  locations; episode format and length; sample episode springboards (10-20); season arc if serialised;
  visual style references.

## 5. Mistakes
- Premise that is a single story (ends when the goal is met).
- Lead without an internal problem: episodes become plot only.
- World rules that solve problems too easily.

## Verify
- [ ] Ten springboards written quickly and varied.
- [ ] Lead's flaw generates episodes.
- [ ] Bible covers premise, characters, world, format, springboards.
