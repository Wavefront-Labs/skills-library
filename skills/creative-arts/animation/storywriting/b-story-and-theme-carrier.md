# B story

Goal: a second line of story, usually a relationship, that holds the theme and helps the hero change.

## 1. What the B story is
- Starts just after Break into Two (about 27-30%), often with new characters from the act-two world:
  love interest, mentor, rival, friend.
- It's where the theme is discussed: the B-story character embodies or argues the need.
- Gives the audience a breather from the A-story plot.

## 2. Mechanics
- Cross the A and B stories at the midpoint (romance kiss, friendship pact) and at all-is-lost
  (the relationship breaks).
- The B story gives the insight in the dark night of the soul or break into three.
- B-story character often has the quality the hero needs.

## 3. In animation
- Sidekicks often carry the B story (the buddy who tells the truth); they must have their own want too.
- TV: the B plot of an episode is different (see A/B plot), but the same principle - B carries theme.

## 4. Test
- Remove the B story: does the hero's change still have a cause? If yes, the B story is decoration.
- Does the B story have its own beginning, middle and end?

## Verify
- [ ] B story begins near the act-two break.
- [ ] Crosses A story at midpoint and all-is-lost.
- [ ] Delivers or triggers the hero's insight.
