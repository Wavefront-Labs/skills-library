# Treatment writing

Goal: a prose version of the whole story that reads like the film feels, used to find structural problems
before writing script pages or boards.

## 1. Length and form
- Feature: about 10-30 pages; 22-min episode: 3-6 pages; 11-min: 1-3 pages; short film: 1-2 pages.
- Present tense, third person, scene by scene or sequence by sequence.
- Include occasional key lines of dialogue only where they carry a turn.

## 2. Contents
- Title, logline, short character list.
- Story in order, organised by acts or sequences, with headings if helpful.
- Every scene: what happens, what changes (the turn), how it moves the hero's arc.
- Tone through word choice: a comedy treatment has jokes on the page.
- Visual set pieces described vividly enough for artists to start exploring (animation teams often start
  visual development from the treatment).

## 3. Process
1. Start from the beat sheet.
2. Expand each beat into a paragraph; mark weak links ("then" instead of "because").
3. Read aloud; time it; cut scenes that do not turn a value.

## 4. Use
- Executives approve the story; artists and boards begin from it; writers script from it.

## Verify
- [ ] Every scene has a turn.
- [ ] Tone comes through in the prose.
- [ ] Length fits the format.
