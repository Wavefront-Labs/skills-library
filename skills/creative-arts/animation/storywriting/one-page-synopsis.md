# One-page synopsis

Goal: the whole story on one page that an executive or collaborator can read in two minutes.

## 1. Rules
- One page, about 300-600 words, present tense, third person.
- Tell the ending. A synopsis is not a teaser.
- Name only the main characters (3-5), in caps or bold on first mention with a 2-5 word description.
- Focus on the hero's want, the main turns and the arc; skip subplots unless essential.

## 2. Structure
1. **Hook and world** (1-2 sentences): hero, world, flaw.
2. **Inciting incident and goal**.
3. **Act two**: 2-4 major complications, the midpoint turn.
4. **Low point**: all is lost.
5. **Climax and resolution**: what the hero does and how they and the world have changed.
6. Optionally, a closing sentence linking to theme (no moralising).

## 3. Style
- Active verbs, concrete images; one paragraph per act.
- Convey tone (comedy synopses should be funny).
- Avoid camera directions and dialogue.

## 4. Check
- Can someone retell the story after one read?
- Is every paragraph about cause and effect ("because of that"), not "and then"?

## Verify
- [ ] Fits on one page with the ending included.
- [ ] Hero arc visible.
- [ ] Cause-and-effect between paragraphs.
