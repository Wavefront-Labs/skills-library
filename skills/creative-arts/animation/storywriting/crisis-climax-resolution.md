# Crisis, climax, resolution

Goal: an ending where the hero makes the most difficult choice of the story and acts on it.

## 1. Crisis
- A **true dilemma**: a choice between irreconcilable goods or the lesser of two evils, under the greatest
  pressure of the story. A choice between good and evil is not a dilemma.
- It's a decision, often a held moment: the audience sees the hero weighing it.
- It reveals the deepest character: true character is revealed in the choice under pressure.

## 2. Climax
- The action the hero takes as a result of the crisis decision. It produces an **absolute and irreversible
  change** in the story's core value.
- It should fulfil the obligatory scene projected by the inciting incident, but in a way the audience didn't
  predict (the gap between expectation and result at story level).
- The hero must cause it; rescue by others or chance breaks the contract.

## 3. Resolution
- Show the result: the new balance, how the world and relationships changed, subplots resolved.
- Keep it short in animation: a few shots to a couple of minutes. Long resolutions deflate.
- Often bookended with the final image.

## 4. Check
- Write the dilemma as "X or Y, both costly". If one choice is free, raise the cost.
- Does the climax change the core value from positive to negative or the reverse, irreversibly?

## Verify
- [ ] Crisis is a dilemma with costs on both sides.
- [ ] Climax caused by the hero's action and irreversible.
- [ ] Resolution brief and shows the new state.
