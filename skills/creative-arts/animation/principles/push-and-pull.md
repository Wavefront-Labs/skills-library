# Push and pull

Goal: show the resistance of an object being pushed or pulled through the angle of the body,
the timing of the start, and the momentum once it moves.

## Pushing
1. **Contact** - hands on object, arms bent, body close.
2. **Lean** - body leans into it; the heavier, the lower the angle (up to about 45 degrees);
   back leg straight, driving.
3. **Strain** - nothing moves at first (static friction). Feet may slip back slightly on a
   slick floor.
4. **Break-away** - the object jerks forward a little; the character stumbles forward with it.
5. **Push steps** - short, powerful steps with the body staying low and leaning; object moves
   at a slow, near-constant speed.
6. **Stop** - stop pushing; heavy objects stop quickly from friction; a rolling object
   coasts.

## Pulling
- Body leans back away from the object; arms straight; feet in front of the hips.
- Rope pulls: hand-over-hand, each re-grip a small loss of tension then a pull.
- If the object suddenly gives, the character falls backward - classic gag.

## Physics cues
- Static friction > kinetic friction: hardest moment is the start; once moving it is easier
  (the body rises a little).
- Heavier objects: lower lean, shorter steps, longer strain.

## Failure modes
- Upright posture while pushing something heavy.
- Object starting smoothly with no break-away.
- Feet planted flat behind with no driving extension.

## Verify
- [ ] Lean angle proportional to weight.
- [ ] Visible strain before movement starts.
- [ ] Momentum change at break-away.
