# Idle business and fidgets

Goal: keep non-focus characters alive and in character during holds, listening beats and
game idles - without pulling attention from the focus.

## Choosing business
- Must match character and situation: a nervous character picks at a sleeve, a bored one
  shifts weight and sighs, a soldier stays still with small breathing.
- Use **one** piece of business per hold; a character doing several fidgets looks restless.

## Timing
- Background/listening characters: small moves every 2-4 seconds, low amplitude, timed in the
  focus character's pauses, not on their accents.
- Breathing: 3-5 second cycle for calm adults (2-3 s when tense); chest and shoulders rise
  slightly; keep it subtle.
- Weight shift: every 4-8 seconds in long holds.

## Game idles
- Base idle loop 2-4 s (breathing, slight sway), seamless.
- Fidget variants (look around, stretch, check weapon) played randomly every 8-20 s,
  returning exactly to the base idle pose for blending.

## Listening characters in dialogue
- React to the speaker's beats, slightly after them (they are hearing it).
- Reactions smaller than the speaker's acting.
- A blink or small head move on the speaker's key line shows the listener absorbing it.

## Failure modes
- Fidgets on the focus character's accents - steals the beat.
- Identical idle loops on several crowd characters in sync: offset start frames and vary.
- Idles that drift away from the base pose so blends pop (games).

## Verify
- [ ] Watching only the focus character, the background never pulls the eye.
- [ ] Muting the background, the shot feels deader (they are doing something).
- [ ] Game idle variants return to the exact base pose.
