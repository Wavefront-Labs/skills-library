# Landings and impact recovery

Goal: the audience feels how far the character fell and how heavy it is from the landing.

## The landing sequence
1. **Pre-contact**: legs reach for the ground, arms rise to balance.
2. **Contact frame**: toes/balls of feet first (heel first only for walking-speed drops).
3. **Absorption**: knees, hips and ankles bend; spine curves; arms swing down and forward;
   head drops last (overlap). Deeper and longer for bigger falls/heavier characters.
4. **Lowest point**: a short hold or ease (2-6 frames).
5. **Recovery**: rise to standing, with overlap and settle.

## Calibrating
| Fall | Absorption depth | Frames to lowest | Recovery |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hop off a step | small | 2-3 | 4-6 |
| Jump from table height | knees deep | 3-5 | 8-12 |
| Big drop / heavy character | full crouch, hand may touch | 4-6 | 12-24 |
| Hard fall | roll-out or collapse | - | slow |

## Roll-outs and hard falls
- Forward roll: contact, absorb, tuck shoulder, roll diagonally across the back, come up.
- Hard fall (cannot absorb): body compresses, secondary bounce of loose parts, a delayed
  settle; often a camera shake or impact frame on contact.

## Hero landing
Three-point landing: one hand touches down, one knee low, head down, then a deliberate rise.
Long hold at the bottom for the pose, then a slow, powerful rise.

## Failure modes
- Same absorption for every fall height.
- Body continuing down at constant speed after contact (no deceleration).
- Overlap missing: head and arms stop with the hips.

## Verify
- [ ] Absorption depth and time proportional to fall height and weight.
- [ ] Extremities settle after the core.
- [ ] Contact frame clearly visible (stepped playback).
