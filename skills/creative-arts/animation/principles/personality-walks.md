# Personality walks

Goal: a walk that tells you who the character is and how they feel before they speak.
Williams spends a large part of the walks chapter on variations from the same four positions.

## Variables
1. **Vertical bounce** - size and where the high/low points fall (a double bounce adds an extra
   up on the passing position: happy, jaunty).
2. **Leading body part** - chest (proud), head (eager, curious), hips (sexy, swagger), feet
   (careful), belly (relaxed, heavy).
3. **Tempo** - frames per step.
4. **Stride** - long (confident), short (timid).
5. **Arm swing** - wide, tight, none (hands in pockets), asymmetric.
6. **Spine and head** - upright, slumped, head bob.
7. **Foot action** - heel-heavy, toe-first, shuffling, kicking out.

## Recipes (24 fps)
| Walk | Settings |
|---|---|
| Proud/strut | chest leads, 12-14 fpstep, long stride, high up position, arms wide, chin up |
| Sad | head down, 18-24 fpstep, small bounce, short stride, arms hanging, drag feet |
| Happy/jaunty | double bounce, 10-12 fpstep, bouncy toes, arms swinging high |
| Heavy/big | low down position, slow tempo, wide stance, side-to-side sway, long settle on each down |
| Sneaky | (see sneak walk skill) |
| Angry | fast, heavy heel strikes, short stride, arms tight and pumping, head forward |

## Method
1. Write the character's attitude in one word.
2. Pick 2-3 variables to push - not all.
3. Block the four positions with those changes; test as a cycle first.
4. Add personal details (a hand flick, a head tilt) on top only after the body reads.

## Failure modes
- Changing everything: a caricature that reads as a joke.
- Arms and legs contradicting (proud chest, timid arms) unless intended.

## Verify
- [ ] A viewer can name the attitude from a silhouette playback.
- [ ] Only 2-3 major variables pushed.
- [ ] Cycle loops cleanly.
