# Walk key poses

Goal: a walk that has weight, built from the four positions in Williams' Survival Kit.

## The positions (one step, right foot forward)
1. **Contact** - both feet on the ground, front heel just touching, back toe just leaving. Legs
   at their widest; arms at their widest (opposite arm forward). Defines stride length.
2. **Down (recoil)** - weight drops onto the front leg; front knee bends, body at its
   **lowest**. Back foot lifts. This pose gives weight.
3. **Passing** - the free leg passes the supporting leg; supporting leg near straight; body
   mid-height. Free foot is lifted (toe down or flat depending on style).
4. **Up (high point)** - the supporting foot pushes off on the toe, body at its **highest**;
   free leg reaching forward to the next contact.
5. **Contact** again with the other foot.

## Order of work
1. Key both contacts (start and end of the step) - stride and arm swing.
2. Key the passing position - sets the body height midway and foot clearance.
3. Key down and up - set the vertical bounce. Down lowest, up highest; contacts slightly above
   down.
4. Mirror for the second step.

## Vertical path
Hips trace a wave: contact (mid) -> down (low) -> passing (mid-high) -> up (high) -> contact.
The size of the wave sets the character - large bounce for bouncy or happy, almost flat for
sneaky, heavy and low for tired.

## Failure modes
- Lowest point on the passing position (a common beginner swap): reads as limping or floaty.
- Contacts with flat feet: no heel strike.
- Both arms swinging the same way as the legs: twinning; arms oppose legs.

## Verify
- [ ] Hip height: lowest on down, highest on up.
- [ ] Heel strikes at contact and rolls to flat by down.
- [ ] Opposite arm forward at contact.
