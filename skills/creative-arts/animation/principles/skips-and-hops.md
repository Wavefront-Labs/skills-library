# Skips and hops

Goal: rhythmic locomotion with airborne beats - happy skips, one-leg hops, bunny hops.
Williams covers skips alongside runs and jumps.

## The skip
A skip is a **step then a hop on the same foot**, alternating feet: step-hop (right),
step-hop (left).
- Rhythm is uneven: long-short, long-short (a dotted rhythm).
- Typical timing at 24 fps: step 6-8 frames, hop 4-6 frames.
- The hop lifts the body; the free knee drives high; arms swing big and opposite.
- Body leans slightly back and up for joy; forward for urgency.

## Hops
- **One-leg hop**: supporting leg compresses and extends each hop; free leg tucked; arms
  balance. Tires quickly - add wobble for comedy.
- **Two-foot hop (bunny hop)**: squash on landing, stretch on take-off, small parabolas; 8-12
  frames per hop.
- **Animal hop** (rabbit, kangaroo): see animal locomotion; hind legs drive, front legs/tail
  balance.

## Method
1. Block the centre of mass path: a series of arcs with the skip's uneven rhythm.
2. Key contacts and take-offs.
3. Add legs, then arms (opposite), then overlap on head and hair.
4. Loop as a cycle; check the rhythm against a clapped beat.

## Failure modes
- Even timing (step and hop same length): reads as a weird run.
- Same foot doing step and hop out of order.
- Missing air time on the hop.

## Verify
- [ ] Clap test: long-short rhythm audible in the contact timing.
- [ ] Each foot steps then hops before switching.
- [ ] Clear airborne frames on hops.
