# Trees and foliage stylization

Goal: trees and bushes that read as believable masses in the show style, not a noise of
individual leaves.

## Principles
- **Masses first**: a tree is 3-7 big foliage clumps on a branching structure. Design the
  clump shapes (round, layered, triangular, cloud-like) per species and style.
- **Light the mass as a form**: each clump is a sphere/egg with a light side, shadow side
  and core; the whole canopy also has an overall light and shadow side.
- **Sky holes**: gaps through the canopy, smaller and darker-skied than they look, varied in size.
- **Edges**: leaf texture only on the edges and transitions; centres of masses stay simple.
- **Trunk and branch rhythm**: taper, branch angles alternating, thinner branches lighter
  from atmosphere and translucency.
- **Species silhouettes**: conifer triangles, oak broad irregular dome, palm star, willow curtain.

## Stylization options
Flat graphic shapes (Eyvind Earle's Sleeping Beauty), gouache clumps (Ghibli), geometric
cones, line-only. Pick one and write it into the set style rules.

## Method
1. Silhouette the tree in one value; check readability and variety vs nearby trees.
2. Divide into clumps; block light and shadow per clump.
3. Add translucent light (yellow-green where light passes through leaves) and sky holes.
4. Edge pass: leaf-shaped breaks on the silhouette edge only.
5. Group distant trees into single masses.

## Failure modes
- Leaf-by-leaf painting; busy, flat.
- Lollipop trees with identical round heads.
- Every tree the same size and spacing.

## Verify
- [ ] Clump structure visible in a value thumbnail.
- [ ] Silhouettes vary.
- [ ] Texture limited to edges.
