# Merging and cutting characters

**Goal:** a cast where every character earns their place. A common developmental-edit
note is "too many characters": readers can't track them and each gets too little page time.

## Find candidates
1. List every named character with: function(s), scenes they appear in, and their want.
2. Flag characters who:
   - share a function (two best friends, two mentors, three cops);
   - appear in fewer than about three scenes but are named;
   - never change or affect a decision.
3. Ask for each flagged pair: if one disappeared and the other took their lines, what
   would be lost? If "not much", merge.

## Merge
1. Choose the **survivor**: usually the one with the stronger want or more vivid detail.
2. Transfer the essential **actions and information** of the cut character to the survivor.
3. Combine traits only if they make a more interesting person (a contradiction is
   a bonus); drop traits that conflict without meaning.
4. Re-check relationships: the merged character may now have tension with someone
   neither had before — use it.
5. Unnamed minor roles can often become functional roles with no name ("the
   receptionist").

## Execute without errors
- Search the manuscript for the cut character's name, nicknames, pronoun references and
  descriptions; fix each occurrence.
- Check timelines: the survivor can't be in two places at once now.
- Update the series bible / style sheet.

## Gotchas
- Merging characters who need to disagree (a debate needs two sides).
- Leaving orphaned references ("as Jake had said" after Jake no longer exists).
- Cutting all minor colour so the world feels empty; unnamed bit players are fine.

## Verify
- [ ] Every remaining named character has a distinct function.
- [ ] Manuscript search finds no references to cut names.
- [ ] Timeline checked for the merged character.
