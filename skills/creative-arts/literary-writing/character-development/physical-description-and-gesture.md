# Physical detail, gesture and mannerism

**Goal:** appearance and movement that reveal personality, state and relationship — not
a police description. Burroway: appearance is one of the methods of characterisation,
best when the detail is significant (it implies more than itself).

## Choosing details
- **Chosen vs unchosen:** what a character controls (clothes, haircut, tattoos, car)
  reveals choices and values; what they don't (height, scars, a lisp) reveals history
  and how they cope.
- **Specific over generic:** "a cardigan buttoned wrong" over "untidy".
- **Change over time:** a detail that alters (she stops wearing the ring) marks the arc.
- **Contradiction:** an immaculate suit with bitten nails.

## Gestures
- A **signature gesture** used sparingly (two to four times in a novel) becomes a
  shorthand for state: he takes his glasses off to lie.
- Gesture as **action beat** in dialogue replaces tags and adds subtext:
  `"I'm fine." She refolded the napkin into a smaller square.`
- **Involuntary vs deliberate:** blushing, trembling (involuntary) vs a pointed shrug (deliberate).
- Body language references (e.g., *The Emotion Thesaurus*) help, but pick the unusual
  over the default (avoid endless nods, sighs, shrugs, eyebrow raises).

## POV filter
- Description comes through someone's eyes; it characterises the observer. A jealous POV
  notices a rival's expensive watch.
- In the POV character's own body, describe from inside (heat in the face, not "her face reddened").

## Revision passes
- Search *nodded, sighed, shrugged, smiled, grinned, eyes, gaze* — reduce defaults by half;
  replace some with specific, character-revealing actions.
- Check each main character has 1-2 recurring physical motifs.

## Gotchas
- Head-to-toe inventories on first appearance.
- Eye colour as characterisation.
- Gestures that every character shares.

## Verify
- [ ] Each main character's key details are significant (imply character).
- [ ] Default gestures reduced; signature gestures used sparingly.
- [ ] POV character described only from inside.
