# Accentual and accentual-syllabic verse

**Goal:** know which metrical system you're writing in, and use it on purpose. Fussell
(*Poetic Meter and Poetic Form*) distinguishes systems by what they count.

## The systems
| System | Counts | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Accentual (strong-stress)** | Stresses per line only; unstressed syllables vary | Old English verse (*Beowulf*), nursery rhymes, rap, much ballad verse |
| **Accentual-syllabic** | Stresses *and* syllables, in feet | Iambic pentameter: 5 stresses, ~10 syllables (Shakespeare, Milton) |
| **Syllabic** | Syllables only | See `writing-syllabic-verse` |
| **Quantitative** | Long/short syllables | Greek and Latin; rarely successful in English |

## Accentual techniques
- **Old English alliterative line:** four stresses split by a caesura into two
  half-lines; alliteration links the third stress to one or both in the first half.
  Modern use: Auden's *The Age of Anxiety*, Richard Wilbur's "Junk".
- **Sprung rhythm** (Gerard Manley Hopkins): each foot has one stress, with any number
  of unstressed syllables; stresses can collide.
- **Four-beat / ballad metre ("dolnik"):** four (or alternating four and three) beats with
  one or two offbeats between — the natural metre of song and popular verse.

## Writing in each
1. **Accentual:** decide the beats per line (often 4); read aloud with a clap on each
   stress; vary unstressed syllables freely; strong stresses must be real speech stresses.
2. **Accentual-syllabic:** choose foot and line length (e.g., iambic tetrameter); count
   syllables and stresses; vary with substitutions (see `metrical-substitution-and-variation`).
3. **Loose iambic / dolnik:** keep beats regular, let syllable count float a little —
   much contemporary formal verse lives here.

## Gotchas
- Mixing systems unknowingly: lines drifting between strict iambs and loose four-beat.
- Promoting weak syllables to stresses to fill a count.

## Verify
- [ ] System chosen and stated (beats per line or foot type and count).
- [ ] Stresses fall on naturally stressed syllables.
- [ ] Clap-test read aloud confirms consistent beat.
