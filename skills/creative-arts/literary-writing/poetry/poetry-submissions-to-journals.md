# Submitting poems to journals

**Goal:** place individual poems in literary journals efficiently and professionally.
Journal publication builds the acknowledgments page that chapbook and book editors look for.

## Research markets
- Databases: Poets & Writers literary magazines database, Duotrope (paid), NewPages,
  The Submission Grinder.
- **Read the journal** (recent issues online) before submitting; match aesthetic.
- Note: reading periods, simultaneous-submission policy, fees, payment, response time.
- Tier your list: reach journals, good fits, emerging venues.

## The submission
- **Batch:** most journals ask for 3-5 poems (check limits, often by page count). Send
  your strongest, varied but coherent.
- **File:** one document, each poem starting on a new page, name and contact on the
  first page (unless reading is blind), standard font, single-spaced poems.
- **Cover letter:** 3-5 sentences: greeting to the poetry editor by name if known, titles
  of the poems, a short bio (prior publications if any), thanks. No explaining the poems.
- Most journals use **Submittable** or their own portal.

## Simultaneous submissions
- Allowed by many journals; when a poem is accepted elsewhere, **withdraw that poem
  immediately** from every other journal (Submittable allows partial withdrawal or a note).
- Never submit a poem already accepted.
- Track everything in a spreadsheet: poem, journal, date, status, response.

## Rights
- "Previously published" usually includes any public posting (blogs, social media);
  many journals won't take such poems.
- Typical acquisition: **first North American serial rights** (or first rights), reverting to
  you on publication; you credit the journal when reprinting in a book.

## Rejection
- Form rejections are normal; acceptance rates at many journals are low single-digit
  percentages. "Personal" rejections inviting resubmission are encouraging — resubmit
  in a later reading period.

## Verify
- [ ] Journal read and guidelines followed.
- [ ] 3-5 poems in one correctly formatted file.
- [ ] Submission tracker updated.
- [ ] Accepted poems withdrawn elsewhere the same day.
