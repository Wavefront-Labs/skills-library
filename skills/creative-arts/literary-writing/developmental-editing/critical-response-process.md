# Critical Response Process

**Goal:** give the artist feedback they have requested, in a structure that protects the work in progress. Choreographer Liz Lerman developed the Critical Response Process (CRP) and describes it in her writings and in training materials; this summary paraphrases the commonly taught steps, and her own materials are the authority.

## Roles
- **Artist** (here a writer) presents the work.
- **Responders** react.
- **Facilitator** keeps the steps and redirects; a writer should not facilitate their own session.

## The four steps
1. **Statements of meaning.** Responders say what was meaningful, evocative, interesting, memorable or compelling in the work. This is not a compliment contest; it is specific recognition.
2. **Artist as questioner.** The artist asks questions about the work (for example "Where did you feel the pace drag?"). Responders answer the questions asked, in response to the work.
3. **Neutral questions.** Responders ask questions about the work that do not hide an opinion. "What was the kitchen's role?" is neutral. "Why did you make the narrator so unlikeable?" smuggles an opinion in a question and should be reframed. The artist answers, and may choose not to.
4. **Opinion time.** Responders ask permission before giving an opinion: "I have an opinion about the structure. Would you like to hear it?" The artist may say no. The format is "I have an opinion about X" and not "You should."

## Using it with writing
- Give responders the pages ahead, or read aloud for short work.
- The artist states where the piece is (draft stage) so responders know what is fair.
- Record answers; the facilitator holds time.
- Use it for stuck or delicate work where traditional silence-and-critique has hurt the writer.

## Failure modes
- Responders slip back into judgment in the question stages; the facilitator reframes.
- The artist has no questions; prompt them to think before the session.

## Verify
- [ ] All four steps run in order.
- [ ] The facilitator is not the artist.
- [ ] Opinions were given only with permission.
