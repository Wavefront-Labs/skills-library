# Building an editorial style sheet

**Goal:** a document that records every style decision for a manuscript so the text
is consistent and later editors, proofreaders and series volumes follow the same
choices. CMOS (chapter 2) describes the editor's style sheet as a standard part of
manuscript editing.

## Sections
1. **Header:** title, author, editor, date, edition of references used — e.g.,
   *Chicago Manual of Style* 18th ed.; *Merriam-Webster* (first spelling unless noted).
2. **General style decisions:**
   - Numbers (spell out to one hundred; times; years) — see
     `creative-arts.literary-writing.novel-writing.chicago-style-conventions-in-fiction`.
   - Serial comma; em dash closed; ellipsis style.
   - Italics conventions (thoughts, foreign words, ships, signs/texts on screens).
   - Capitalisation (kinship terms, titles, invented institutions).
   - Text messages, emails, letters — how they're set.
3. **Word list (alphabetical):** spellings and hyphenations chosen, especially
   variants and compounds (*email*, *OK* vs *okay*, *grey/gray*, *T-shirt*, *front-runner*),
   plus brand names and real place names.
4. **Characters:** name, spelling of nicknames, age, eye/hair colour, relationships,
   first appearance (chapter/page) — continuity reference.
5. **Places and invented terms:** spellings, capitalisation, brief definitions.
6. **Timeline:** dates/days of key events by chapter; ages consistent with dates.
7. **Author preferences / deliberate deviations:** dialect spellings, stylistic fragments,
   "leave as is" items.

## Procedure
- Start on page one of the edit; add each decision the first time it comes up.
- Search the manuscript for alternatives as you decide (both "OK" and "okay").
- Share with the author for confirmation of preferences.
- Deliver with the edited manuscript; it passes to copyeditor and proofreader.

## Gotchas
- Recording decisions but not applying them retroactively to earlier pages.
- Leaving out page/chapter references in the character list (hard to verify continuity).

## Verify
- [ ] References and editions named.
- [ ] Word list alphabetical and applied consistently (search-checked).
- [ ] Character, place and timeline sections complete with references.
