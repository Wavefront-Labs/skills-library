# Setting rates and quoting a job

**Goal:** quotes that are fair to the author and sustainable for you, based on real pace
and clear scope.

## Benchmarks
- The **Editorial Freelancers Association (EFA)** publishes an editorial rates chart by
  service (developmental editing, line editing, copyediting, proofreading, manuscript
  critique) with typical per-word / per-page / hourly ranges and pace estimates.
- In the UK, the **CIEP** publishes suggested minimum hourly rates.
- Rates vary with genre, manuscript condition, turnaround and your experience — use the
  charts as a range, not a fixed price.

## Estimate your pace
1. Do (or reuse) a **sample edit** of 5-10 pages from this manuscript (see
   `the-sample-edit`) and time it.
2. Pages per hour x total pages = hours; for developmental edits add time for the
   full read(s), reverse outline and letter writing (often a large share of the total).
3. Multiply by your target hourly rate, then convert to the quote format.

## Quote formats
| Format | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| Per word | Transparent for authors; scales with length | Doesn't reflect manuscript difficulty |
| Per page (250 words) | Common in publishing | Need to define a page |
| Project fee | Author knows total; you're paid for value | Risk if scope creeps |
| Hourly | Fair for unpredictable work | Authors fear open-ended cost; give a cap |
Most freelance fiction editors quote **a project fee derived from a per-word rate** after
a sample.

## The written quote
- Service (exactly what's included: one read? letter length? margin comments? a follow-up call?).
- Word count it's based on (and what happens if the final count differs).
- Fee, deposit (commonly 25-50%), payment schedule, accepted methods.
- Start date and delivery date.
- What's extra (second rounds, rush fees).
- Validity period of the quote.

## Gotchas
- Quoting from word count alone without seeing pages.
- Underpricing developmental work by forgetting the letter-writing time.
- Agreeing to scope verbally only.

## Verify
- [ ] Pace measured on this manuscript.
- [ ] Fee checked against EFA/CIEP ranges.
- [ ] Written quote lists scope, fee, deposit, dates and extras.
