# The sample edit

**Goal:** a short edit of the author's pages that shows them what your edit will be
like, lets you assess the manuscript and your pace, and tests whether you work well together.

## Setup
- Ask for: the full manuscript (or synopsis + first chapters), word count, genre,
  goals (querying, self-publishing), deadline, and what kind of edit they think they need.
- Read enough to judge what level of edit the book actually needs (see the levels of
  edit in `creative-arts.literary-writing.developmental-editing.writing-an-editorial-letter`).
  The author may ask for a copyedit when the book needs a developmental edit — say so.

## Doing the sample
- **Line/copyedit samples:** edit about 5-10 pages (often ~1,000-3,000 words) in Track
  Changes exactly as you would in the full job, with comments explaining key changes.
- **Developmental samples:** pages alone can't show a structural edit; offer instead a
  short manuscript assessment of the opening chapters, or margin comments on a chapter
  plus a one-page note on big-picture observations, or share an anonymised sample letter.
- Add a short cover note: what you did, what you noticed overall, the level of edit you
  recommend, and the quote.

## Free or paid?
- Many editors offer a **free** short sample (a few pages) for line/copyedits.
- Longer samples, developmental assessments or samples for very long books are often
  **paid** (sometimes credited against the full fee).
- State limits in advance.

## Use it
- Time yourself for the quote (see `setting-editing-rates-and-quoting`).
- Note author reactions: do they accept suggestions thoughtfully or push back on all of them?
  A sample is also your screening tool.

## Gotchas
- Editing the author's most polished chapter only — pick pages from the middle as well.
- Doing a lighter sample than the real edit (or heavier) — mismatched expectations.
- Giving away a full developmental assessment for free.

## Verify
- [ ] Sample done at the same depth as the proposed edit.
- [ ] Cover note recommends the level of edit with reasons.
- [ ] Pace recorded for the quote.
