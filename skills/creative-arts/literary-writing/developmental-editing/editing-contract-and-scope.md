# Editing contracts and scope

**Goal:** a short written agreement that prevents misunderstandings — about what you'll
do, by when, for how much — and handles the awkward cases in advance. The EFA provides
a sample agreement to members; professional bodies recommend written terms for every job.

## Clauses to include
1. **Parties and manuscript:** title, word count, version/date of the file you'll edit.
2. **Scope of work:** level of edit (developmental, line, copy, proofread) and what that
   includes — e.g., "one full read, an editorial letter of approx. 10-15 pages, margin
   comments, and a one-hour follow-up call within 30 days". Also what it excludes
   (fact-checking, rewriting, a second read, formatting, querying advice).
3. **Deliverables and schedule:** file delivery date from author; your delivery date;
   what happens if the manuscript arrives late (new slot, not automatic delay of others).
4. **Fees:** total, deposit (often non-refundable once the slot is reserved), balance
   due date, late payment terms, currency, method.
5. **Changes in scope:** word count differing by more than X% -> re-quote; additional
   rounds at stated rates.
6. **Cancellation / kill fee:** by author before start (deposit retained); mid-project
   (payment for work done, pro rata or minimum percentage).
7. **Confidentiality:** you won't share the manuscript; author consent required for use
   of excerpts in your portfolio.
8. **Credit and testimonials:** whether the author may name you, and you them.
9. **No guarantee:** editing doesn't guarantee agent representation, publication or sales.
10. **Author's responsibility:** final text, legal content and permissions remain the author's.
11. **Signature/acceptance** (email acceptance can suffice; keep records).

## Managing scope creep
- When asked for extras ("Could you just look at my query letter?"), reply with the
  price and timing before doing it.
- Keep a change log in email.

## Gotchas
- Starting work on a handshake.
- Vague scope ("edit the book").
- No clause for manuscripts that arrive late or longer than quoted.

## Verify
- [ ] Scope lists inclusions and exclusions.
- [ ] Deposit, kill fee and late-file terms present.
- [ ] Signed or accepted in writing before work starts.
