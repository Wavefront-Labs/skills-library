# Book metadata, categories and keywords

**Goal:** the right readers find the book in store search and category browsing, and
the product page converts browsers into buyers.

## Subject codes
- **BISAC** (Book Industry Study Group) — standard US subject headings, e.g.
  `FIC022000 FICTION / Mystery & Detective / General`. IngramSpark and Bowker take up to 3.
  Pick the most specific code that is honest; `General` codes bury the book.
- **Thema** — international subject scheme used by many non-US retailers and distributors.
- **Amazon categories** — chosen in KDP (from Amazon's own browse tree); choose the
  most specific relevant ones.

## Keywords (KDP: 7 boxes)
- Use phrases readers type, not single words: "small town cozy mystery with cats",
  "enemies to lovers fantasy romance".
- Do not repeat words already in title/subtitle or category.
- Never use other authors' names, book titles, trademarks or claims like "bestseller" —
  against guidelines.
- Research: type the start of a phrase into Amazon search and note autocompletions;
  look at comparable books' categories.

## Description (back-cover copy for the web)
1. A one-line hook (bold allowed via KDP's limited HTML).
2. 2-3 short paragraphs: protagonist, situation, stakes — like a query pitch.
3. A line positioning the book (comps or tropes): "Perfect for fans of...".
4. Keep the first two lines strong; most stores truncate with "Read more".

## Other fields
- **Series** name and number exactly consistent across volumes (enables series pages).
- **Contributors:** same author name string everywhere (middle initials included or not).
- **Age range / grade** for children's and YA.
- **Publication date, price, page count** consistent across Bowker/Nielsen and platforms.

## Gotchas
- Categories that do not match the book: readers who arrive bounce, and reviews suffer.
- Changing metadata constantly; change one variable at a time and wait to measure.
- Different subtitles on cover vs metadata (KDP rejects mismatches).

## Verify
- [ ] 1-3 specific BISAC codes chosen.
- [ ] 7 keyword phrases, no prohibited terms.
- [ ] Description hook visible above the fold.
- [ ] Series and author strings identical across all editions.
