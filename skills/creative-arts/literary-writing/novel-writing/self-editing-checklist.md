# Self-editing checklist pass

**Goal:** a systematic final self-edit before beta readers, agents or a paid editor —
one concern per pass, big to small. Many items follow the chapter topics of Browne &
King's *Self-Editing for Fiction Writers*.

## Pass order
**A. Story level** (after structural revision is done)
- [ ] Every scene has a goal, conflict and turn.
- [ ] Opening starts where the story starts; ending resolves the central question.
- [ ] Setups paid off; no orphaned threads.

**B. Scene and POV**
- [ ] One viewpoint per scene (or deliberate omniscience); no head-hopping.
- [ ] Scene vs summary proportion fits importance.
- [ ] Each scene orients (who/where/when) early.

**C. Show and tell / exposition**
- [ ] No info-dumps; backstory in small doses.
- [ ] Named emotions replaced by concrete behaviour at key moments.

**D. Dialogue**
- [ ] "Said"/beats instead of said-bookisms and adverbs.
- [ ] No "as you know" exposition; characters sound distinct.
- [ ] Dialogue punctuation correct.

**E. Interior monologue**
- [ ] Thought rendered consistently (free indirect preferred; italics rule fixed).

**F. Line level**
- [ ] Echo/crutch words searched and reduced.
- [ ] Filter words, -ly adverbs, as/-ing constructions checked.
- [ ] Sentence variety; read-aloud done.

**G. Consistency**
- [ ] Names, spellings, timeline, ages, eye colours checked against a style sheet.
- [ ] Chapter numbering and scene breaks consistent.

**H. Format and proof**
- [ ] Standard manuscript format applied.
- [ ] Spellcheck plus a final proofread on a different medium (printout, e-reader, text-to-speech).

## Tips
- Do one category per read-through; trying to fix everything at once misses things.
- Keep your personal list of recurring faults and add it to section F.

## Verify
- [ ] Every box ticked or consciously waived.
- [ ] Personal fault list searched.
- [ ] Final proofread done in a different medium.
