# Chapter endings and chapter breaks

**Goal:** make each chapter end create a reason to start the next one, and use breaks
to control rhythm.

## Where to break
- A chapter does not have to end where a scene ends. **Breaking mid-scene, just after
  the turn** (the door opens, the letter's first line) is the strongest hook.
- Ending at the natural rest point of a scene ("She went to bed") gives the reader a
  place to stop. Use it deliberately for breathers.
- Within a chapter, mark a shift of time, place or viewpoint with a **scene break**:
  in manuscript a centred `#` (Shunn format) or `* * *` on its own line, never just an
  extra blank line (it disappears at page breaks and in conversion).

## Hook types (vary them)
| Type | Example ending |
|---|---|
| Revelation | "The handwriting was her mother's." |
| Decision | "He would go to the police in the morning." |
| Threat arrives | Footsteps on the stairs. |
| Reversal | The plan works — and that is the problem. |
| Open question | "Where's Danny?" |
| Emotional turn | A character finally says the thing. |

A cliffhanger at every chapter end trains readers to distrust them; mix in emotional
and decision hooks.

## Chapter length
- Genre norms vary: commercial thrillers often use short chapters (around
  1,500-3,000 words); literary fiction may run much longer. Rhythm matters more than
  uniformity: shorter chapters near the climax speed perceived pace.
- Re-orient (who, where, when) within the first paragraph of every chapter, especially
  after a POV switch.

## Gotchas
- A fake cliffhanger resolved trivially on the next page ("It was only the cat")
  costs trust.
- Ending on summary or reflection drains momentum.
- After a mid-scene break, open the next chapter in continuity unless the delay
  itself is the point.

## Verify
- [ ] Each chapter's last paragraph contains a turn, question or decision, or is a deliberate breather.
- [ ] No more than two consecutive chapters end at rest.
- [ ] Hook types vary across the book.
- [ ] Each chapter opening re-orients within one paragraph.
- [ ] Scene breaks are marked with a visible symbol.
