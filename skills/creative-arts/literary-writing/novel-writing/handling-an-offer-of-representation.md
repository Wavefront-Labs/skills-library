# Handling an offer of representation

**Goal:** turn one offer into the right agent for your career, without burning bridges.

## 1. The call
Thank them; do not accept on the spot. Ask:
- What do you love about the book; what revisions do you want before submission?
- How editorial are you? How many rounds before going out?
- Which editors/imprints would you submit to? How do you handle it if it doesn't sell?
- How do you communicate (email/phone), and how often during submission?
- Commission rates and agency agreement terms (ask to see the agreement).
- Can I speak to current clients?
- Are you interested in my career beyond this book (future projects, other genres)?

## 2. Notify every agent who has the manuscript or a pending query
- Email subject: "Offer of Representation – TITLE".
- Body: an offer has been made; you'd like to give them the chance to respond by
  [date — typically 1-2 weeks out]. Courtesy is to tell all agents with fulls/partials,
  and usually queried agents too.
- Tell the offering agent your deadline.

## 3. Read the agency agreement
Typical industry norms (check each point):
| Term | Common norm |
|---|---|
| Domestic commission | 15% |
| Foreign / translation (with sub-agent) | 20-25% |
| Film/TV | 15-20% |
| Termination | Either party, written notice (often 30-60 days) |
| Post-termination | Agent keeps commission on deals they made; check whether they claim rights to submissions in progress |
| Expenses | Only agreed, itemised costs; no upfront fees |
| Scope | Whole book-length work vs single title |

Under the AALA Canon of Ethics, members may not charge reading fees. Have the Authors
Guild (members get contract review) or a publishing lawyer review unusual clauses.

## 4. Decide
- Weigh enthusiasm and vision for the book, sales record in your genre, communication fit,
  and agency support (foreign rights, film, contracts department).
- Decline other offers graciously, by email, with thanks.

## Gotchas
- Accepting during the first call.
- Letting the deadline pass silently or extending it repeatedly.
- Agreements with indefinite claims on future work or termination fees.

## Verify
- [ ] All agents with your material notified with a date.
- [ ] Client references contacted.
- [ ] Agreement read; commission, term and termination understood.
- [ ] Written acceptance sent; others declined.
