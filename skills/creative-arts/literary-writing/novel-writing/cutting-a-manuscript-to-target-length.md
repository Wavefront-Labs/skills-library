# Cutting a manuscript to target length

**Goal:** bring a manuscript into its genre's word-count range (see
`word-count-targets-by-genre`) or a contract length, while making it better. Cut from
the top down: big structural cuts save thousands of words; line trims save hundreds.

## 1. Set the budget
- Target total; subtract from current = words to cut (e.g., 135k -> 105k = 30k, 22%).
- Allocate per act or chapter in proportion to where the bloat is (beginnings and
  middles usually carry most).

## 2. Structural cuts (biggest yield)
Using a reverse outline (see
`creative-arts.literary-writing.novel-writing.drafting-and-revising-a-novel`):
1. **Subplots** that don't intersect the main plot — cut whole.
2. **Scenes** that don't change anything, or duplicate another scene's turn.
3. **Characters** with redundant functions — merge (see
   `creative-arts.literary-writing.character-development.merging-characters`).
4. **Openings:** chapters that warm up before the story starts.
5. **Backstory and flashbacks** that the present can carry in a line.

## 3. Scene-level cuts
- Enter scenes later, leave earlier.
- Summarise routine action (see `balancing-scene-and-summary`).
- Cut repeated emotional beats (the third time she worries about the same thing).

## 4. Line-level trims (last 5-10%)
- Filter words, adverbs, stage directions ("she turned and walked to the door and opened it").
- Doubled descriptions; dialogue ping-pong; throat-clearing paragraph openers.
- See the prose-style line-editing skills.

## Tracking
- Snapshot or save a copy before cutting (keep a "cut" file — writers rarely restore,
  but it makes cutting easier emotionally).
- Log word count per pass; Scrivener's project targets or a spreadsheet.

## Gotchas
- Starting with line trims: weeks of work for little gain.
- Cutting all texture and voice to hit a number.
- Cutting setups whose payoffs remain (see
  `creative-arts.literary-writing.plot-structure.chekhovs-gun-and-planting`).

## Verify
- [ ] Word budget set and met.
- [ ] Structural cuts made before line trims.
- [ ] No orphaned payoffs or references to cut material.
