# Chronological and frame structure

**Goal:** a shape for a true story that creates momentum even when readers may know
how it ends. Gutkind (*You Can't Make This Stuff Up*) teaches building narrative
nonfiction from scenes arranged for dramatic effect, not simply in order of research.

## Straight chronology
- Works when events have natural rising tension (an expedition, a trial, a season).
- Decide **where to start** (the latest point that still includes the necessary
  setup) and **where to end** (the moment of change, not the last thing that happened).
- Compress uneventful stretches into summary; dramatise turning points as scenes.

## Frame (the most common nonfiction shape)
1. **Open with a dramatic present scene** — often from the climax or a crisis point
   (in medias res).
2. **Loop back** to the beginning and move forward chronologically.
3. **Return to the frame** scene at the moment the chronology catches up with it —
   now readers understand it fully.
4. Optionally continue past it to the resolution.
This gives suspense ("how did we get here?") and lets the narrator reflect from a
fixed later present.

## Variations
- **Alternating timelines:** present-day investigation chapters alternating with the
  historical story (common in narrative history and true crime).
- **Circular:** end where you began, changed.
- **Countdown:** chapters dated toward a known event (heightens inevitability).

## Procedure
1. Make a timeline of all events and research findings.
2. Mark the scenes with the most drama and meaning; pick the frame scene from them.
3. Outline: frame -> origin -> escalation -> catch-up -> resolution.
4. Check that each loop-back is triggered and clearly dated (headings, time cues).

## Gotchas
- Opening frame that gives away so much that the middle has no suspense — open on a
  question, not an answer.
- Starting with ancestry or childhood when the story is about later events.

## Verify
- [ ] Start and end points chosen for dramatic reasons.
- [ ] Frame scene (if used) returns when the chronology reaches it.
- [ ] Time cues make every shift clear.
