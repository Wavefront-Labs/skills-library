# Permissions and fair use for quotations

**Goal:** quote others' work legally and on schedule. Publishers typically make the
author responsible for clearing permissions and paying fees (check your contract).
General information only; CMOS chapter 4 (Rights and Permissions) is the standard
publishing reference; consult your publisher or a lawyer for specific cases.

## US fair use: the four factors (17 U.S.C. §107)
1. **Purpose and character** — commentary, criticism, scholarship, transformation favour fair use.
2. **Nature of the work** — factual works more open than highly creative ones; unpublished works less open.
3. **Amount and substantiality** — how much relative to the whole, and whether it's the "heart".
4. **Market effect** — does your use substitute for the original?
There is **no fixed word count** that is automatically fair use.
(Other countries use narrower "fair dealing" rules.)

## Usually needs permission
- **Song lyrics** — even a line or two; music publishers actively license and charge.
- **Poems** — a few lines can be a substantial portion.
- **Epigraphs** — decorative rather than commented on, so weaker fair-use claims.
- Long prose quotations, images, photographs, artworks, maps, charts.

## Usually doesn't
- Facts and ideas (not protected), titles, short phrases.
- **Public domain** works: in the US, works published more than 95 years ago (before 1931 as of 2026 — the line
  moves each 1 January), US federal government works, and others whose term expired.
  Check translations and editions separately (a modern translation is its own copyright).

## Requesting permission
1. Identify the rights holder (publisher's permissions department, music publisher,
   estate, agent). Allow 2-6 months.
2. Request specifying: exact text, page/source, your book's title, publisher, format(s)
   (print, ebook, audio), territory, print run, publication date.
3. Get the grant **in writing** and use the credit line they specify.
4. Budget for fees.

## Permissions log
Spreadsheet: quoted material | source | words/lines | rights holder | date requested |
status | fee | credit line | formats/territory granted.

## Verify
- [ ] Every quotation logged with a fair-use rationale or permission status.
- [ ] All lyrics and poem excerpts cleared or cut.
- [ ] Grants cover every format and territory of publication.
- [ ] Credit lines in place as specified.
