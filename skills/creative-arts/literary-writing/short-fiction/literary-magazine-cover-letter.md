# Literary magazine cover letter

**Goal:** a short, professional note that tells the editor what you're sending and who
you are, then gets out of the way. Editors decide on the story; the letter can only hurt
you by being long, odd or presumptuous.

## Template (paste into Submittable's cover-letter box or an email)
```
Dear [Editor's name / Fiction Editors],

Please consider my story "[Title]" ([word count] words) for [Magazine name].

[Optional, one sentence: why this magazine, if genuine and specific — e.g., you read
and admired a recent story in Issue 42.]

My fiction has appeared in [Magazine A], [Magazine B] and [Magazine C]. [One line:
relevant credential, MFA, award, or location.] [If none: "This would be my first
publication."]

This is a simultaneous submission; I will withdraw it promptly if it is accepted elsewhere.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

[Name]
[Email] | [Phone] | [Website optional]
```

## Rules
- **Use the editor's name** if listed on the masthead (fiction editor, not the publisher).
- **No summary or interpretation** of the story; don't explain the ending.
- **Bio:** up to ~3 publications, most prestigious or recent first; third person in the bio
  field if the form requests a contributor bio.
- **Simultaneous submissions:** only if allowed; withdraw within a day when accepted
  elsewhere (Submittable has a withdraw button; otherwise email).
- **Follow guidelines** exactly: file type, anonymous submission (strip your name from the
  manuscript if blind reading), word limits, reading periods.

## Tracking
Keep a spreadsheet or use Duotrope/Submittable history: story, market, date, status,
response time, any personal note from editors (a "please send again" is worth tracking).

## Gotchas
- Listing every publication, or self-praise ("a powerful story about...").
- Addressing "Dear Sir".
- Forgetting to withdraw after an acceptance elsewhere — damages relationships.

## Verify
- [ ] Letter under ~150 words; title and word count correct.
- [ ] Guidelines followed (blind reading, file format, reading period).
- [ ] Submission logged in the tracker.
