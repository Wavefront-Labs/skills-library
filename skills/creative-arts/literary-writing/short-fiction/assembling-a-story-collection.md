# Assembling a story collection

**Goal:** a book-length collection (commonly around 40,000-70,000 words; check publisher
norms) that reads as a deliberate whole, and a plan for placing it.

## Select
1. List all finished stories with word count and publication history.
2. Rank by strength honestly (published stories are evidence, not proof).
3. Pick the 10-15 best that **talk to each other** — shared themes, place, characters or
   concerns. A collection of unrelated strong stories is harder to sell than a cohesive one.
4. Leave out the outlier, even if good; it can go in the next book.

## Linked stories and story cycles
- **Linked collection / short story cycle:** recurring characters or place, each story
  standalone but building a larger arc (Sherwood Anderson's *Winesburg, Ohio*; Elizabeth
  Strout's *Olive Kitteridge*; Jennifer Egan's *A Visit from the Goon Squad*).
- Linked books are often marketed closer to novels; make each story complete and plant the
  connections lightly.

## Sequence
- **Open with a strong, representative story** — agents and judges may read only that.
- **End with a strong, resonant story**; place the title story where it does most work.
- Vary length, POV and tone between neighbours; avoid two similar stories back to back.
- For linked books, sequence for the cumulative arc (often chronological, not always).

## Front and back matter
Title page, contents, acknowledgments listing where stories first appeared (magazine,
issue, year) — required, as magazines usually hold first serial rights.

## Placing it
- **Agents** take collections less often than novels; many ask for a novel too.
- **Independent and university presses** read collections directly.
- **Contests:** e.g., Flannery O'Connor Award, Drue Heinz Literature Prize, Iowa Short
  Fiction Award, AWP Grace Paley Prize — check current guidelines and fees.

## Gotchas
- Including every story you've written; padding weakens the book.
- Forgetting rights: reprinted stories need the magazine's rights to have reverted (most
  revert on publication, but check each contract).

## Verify
- [ ] 10-15 stories selected for strength and cohesion; word count in range.
- [ ] First and last stories are among the strongest.
- [ ] Acknowledgments list first publications; rights checked.
