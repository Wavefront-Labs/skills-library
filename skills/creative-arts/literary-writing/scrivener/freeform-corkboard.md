# Freeform corkboard

**Goal:** use the corkboard as a two-dimensional planning surface (clusters, timelines, parallel plotlines) without altering the real document order. Menu names below follow Scrivener 3; shortcuts and exact labels differ between the Mac and Windows editions and by version, so confirm in the app's Help or the official user guide.

## How it works
The standard corkboard arranges cards in order, in a grid, and dragging a card changes the binder order. The **freeform** corkboard (corkboard options, or the view menu's freeform setting) lets cards sit anywhere, and positions are stored for that board view; moving cards on a freeform board does not reorder the binder.

## Uses
- **Brainstorm**: make cards for ideas, drop them anywhere, cluster by theme, then promote winners to real documents.
- **Parallel plotlines**: rows for characters or subplots, columns for time.
- **Timeline sketch**: arrange cards left to right by chronology while the binder holds reading order.
- **Revision map**: cluster cards by problem (needs cut, needs set-up).

## Steps
1. Select a folder with the documents to plan, then choose Corkboard view and switch it to Freeform in the corkboard options (the control is in the footer bar or the View menu).
2. Drag cards to positions. Use labels, status and card colours to show what type each card is.
3. Add new cards for ideas (Project > New Text) and write synopses.
4. When the arrangement should become the reading order, reorder the binder to match, or use the corkboard's apply-order option for the standard board if your version offers it. Confirm in the Outliner.

## Gotchas
- Freeform positions are a visual aid; they will not match the binder order unless you reorder.
- Cards for ideas that never become scenes should be moved out of Draft so they do not compile.
- Positions are saved per container; plan one board per folder.

## Verify
- [ ] Binder order unchanged after freeform arrangement unless intended.
- [ ] Idea cards live outside Draft.
- [ ] The final reading order is checked in the Outliner.
