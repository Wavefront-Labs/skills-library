# Clip Studio Assets and materials

**Clip Studio Assets** (assets.clip-studio.com, also inside the CLIP STUDIO launcher) is the official
marketplace for brushes, tones, 3D models, poses, panel layouts, colour sets and auto actions.

## Finding
- Search by keyword and filter by type (brush, image material, 3D, auto action), price (free, Clippy,
  Gold) and app version.
- Read reviews and preview images; check the update date for compatibility.

## Currency
- **Clippy** - earned points (logins, uploads); **Gold** - purchased; some materials are free.

## After download
- Image, tone, 3D and frame materials appear in **Material palette > Download** folder; move favourites
  to your own folders.
- Brushes and sub tools: drag from Material palette > Download into the **Sub tool** palette group you want.
- Auto actions: import into the Auto action palette.

## Licence terms
- Each material has usage terms; most allow use in your commercial artwork but forbid redistributing
  the material itself (e.g. inside a sold template or brush pack). Check before using in client work.

## Management
- Remove unused downloads to keep the palette quick to search.
- Sync materials across devices via a Clip Studio account (cloud) if you use multiple machines.

## Gotchas
- Materials made for newer versions may not load in old versions.
- 3D models with heavy polygons slow files; check poly counts.
