# Symmetrical ruler

## Create
1. **Ruler** tool > **Symmetrical ruler** sub tool.
2. Tool property: **Number of lines** - 2 for left/right mirror, 3+ for radial symmetry (mandalas,
   emblems, magic circles, flowers). Check **Line symmetry** for mirror reflection between segments
   (unchecked = rotational copies only).
3. Drag on the canvas to set the axis (Shift snaps to 45-degree steps).

## Draw
- Any brush on that layer mirrors across the axis. Snap must be on (**View > Snap to special ruler**;
  the snap button on the command bar).
- The ruler is attached to the layer; show it on other layers via the ruler icon in the Layer palette or
  Layer > Ruler/Frame > Show in all layers.

## Edit or remove
- **Object** tool to move/rotate the axis or change line count.
- Delete: drag the ruler icon in the Layer palette to the trash, or Layer > Ruler/Frame > Delete ruler.

## Uses in comics
- Mecha, armour and vehicles front views; logos and title emblems; character turnaround front faces
  (draw, then break symmetry by hand to avoid a dead face); kaleidoscope effects.

## Gotchas
- Drawing a face with mirror on gives a stiff, uncanny look; use it for the construction, then redraw.
- A forgotten symmetrical ruler on a layer mirrors later strokes; hide or delete after use.

## Verify
- [ ] Ruler deleted or hidden after the symmetrical element is done.
