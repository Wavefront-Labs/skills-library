# Daily strip format and layout

**Goal:** a daily strip template that reproduces cleanly whether printed tiny in a
newspaper or posted online.

## Format
- Horizontal strip, commonly about 3.5:1 to 4.5:1 width:height (syndicate specs
  vary; check if submitting).
- **1-4 panels**; 3-4 is classic: set-up, development, (beat), punchline.
- Drawn larger than print, often about 2-3x print width; many cartoonists draw on
  Bristol around 13-16 in wide, or digitally at high resolution.

## Template
- Fixed outer border and panel divisions; gutters consistent.
- Title/logo space if your strip carries one in the first panel.
- Copyright/signature space (small, in a panel corner).

## Readability at small size
- Newspapers print dailies small; letter large relative to the panel (test by printing
  at ~4-5 in wide).
- Simple backgrounds, clear silhouettes, minimal hatching (it fills in at small sizes).
- Fewer words: 1-2 balloons per panel.

## Web considerations
- Strips posted on social platforms get viewed on phones; consider a square or stacked
  (2x2) version for Instagram while keeping the horizontal master.

## Verify
- [ ] Template proportions fixed and consistent.
- [ ] Printed test at small size is fully legible.
- [ ] Web/stacked variant planned if publishing on social media.
