# Strip buffer and production schedule

## Lead times
- Syndicated dailies are typically due several weeks ahead of publication (commonly 4-6 weeks for
  dailies, longer for Sundays because colour and printing pre-press come earlier). Confirm your
  syndicate's calendar.
- Webcomics have no syndicate deadline, but a buffer protects against illness and burnout.

## Building the buffer
- Target: launch with 6-8 weeks banked; keep at least 4.
- To grow it, produce 8 strips a week while publishing 7 (or 6 dailies while publishing 5) until the target
  is reached.
- Track buffer depth (weeks banked) every week; when it drops below the floor, cut extras before cutting
  quality.

## Batch workflow
| Day | Work |
|---|---|
| Mon | Write: generate 15-20 gag ideas, pick 7. |
| Tue | Thumbnails and lettering placement for the week. |
| Wed-Thu | Pencils/inks for all 7 in assembly-line order (all panel borders, all lettering, all inks). |
| Fri | Sunday strip or colour; scan/export, file, schedule. |
Batching each step across strips is faster and keeps the drawing consistent.

## Holidays and breaks
- Bank extra strips before holidays; syndicates allow reruns ("classic" weeks) or guest weeks by
  arrangement.
- Seasonal strips (holidays, events) are written months ahead; keep a calendar.

## Gotchas
- A buffer spent on "just this week" never comes back without deliberate overproduction.
- Topical gags written 6 weeks ahead may be stale by publication: keep them evergreen or flag them for
  a late swap.

## Verify
- [ ] Buffer depth logged weekly and above the floor.
- [ ] Next three deadlines in the calendar.
