# Briffits, dust clouds and motion puffs

## The symbols (Mort Walker, Lexicon of Comicana)
- **Briffits** - the little puffs of dust left behind where a character took off or came to a sudden stop.
- **Hites** - horizontal speed lines trailing a moving object; **vites** - vertical ones (falling);
  **dites** - diagonal lines on glass and reflective surfaces.
- **Fight cloud** - a big dust ball with limbs, stars and objects poking out, standing for a brawl.
- **Agitrons** - wiggly lines around a shaking object.

## Drawing briffits
1. Draw 2-4 round lobed puffs at ground level where the action started, overlapping slightly.
2. Larger near the start point, smaller and fainter trailing back (they dissipate).
3. Add 2-3 hites from the puffs toward the departing character to show direction.
4. For a sudden stop, put the briffits in front of the feet with the character leaning back.

## Timing
- Briffits imply the action already happened. Use them in the panel after the departure (empty space +
  dust) for the classic "he's gone" gag.
- A puff plus a hat still spinning in mid-air extends the beat.

## Fight cloud
- Single dust mass, irregular scalloped outline, 3-6 protruding items (fist, boot, star, tail).
- Sound effects around the edge sell the noise.

## Gotchas
- Shading briffits heavily makes them read as smoke or explosions; keep them open and light.
- Speed lines and briffits pointing different ways contradict the direction.

## Verify
- [ ] Direction of travel is unambiguous from the symbols alone.
- [ ] Puffs touch the ground plane.
