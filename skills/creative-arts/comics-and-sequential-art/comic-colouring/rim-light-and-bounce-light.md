# Rim light and bounce light

**Goal:** figures that pop off backgrounds and sit in their environment, without
competing light.

## Rim light
- Light from behind the subject catching its edge. Thin band on the contour,
  opposite or perpendicular to the key light.
- Use a hard brush on a separate layer (Add/Glow Dodge or Screen) clipped to the
  figure flats; keep it narrow and broken on soft forms.
- Colour: the light source's colour (neon pink, moonlight blue), usually more
  saturated than the key.
- Excellent for night scenes and separating dark costumes from dark backgrounds.

## Bounce (reflected) light
- Light reflected from nearby surfaces into the shadow side, tinted by that surface:
  green from grass, warm from a red floor, cool from the sky.
- Always weaker than the key light; it lives inside the shadow and never reaches
  the highlight value.
- Paint on a separate layer in the shadow area, soft edge, low opacity.

## Gotchas
- Rim light on every figure in every panel becomes a gimmick.
- Bounce too bright flattens the form (reads as a second key light).

## Verify
- [ ] Rim colour matches a real source in the scene.
- [ ] Bounce stays darker than the lit side.
