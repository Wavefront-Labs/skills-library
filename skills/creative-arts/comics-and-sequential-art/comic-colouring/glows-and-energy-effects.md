# Glows and energy effects

**Goal:** effects that look like they emit light, rather than being painted with a
bright colour.

## Recipe
1. Darken the surround: glow needs contrast. Push nearby values and saturation down.
2. Core: near-white (slightly tinted) at the centre of the effect.
3. Halo: saturated hue of the effect around the core, falling off softly.
4. Outer bloom: low-opacity soft airbrush in the same hue on an Add (Glow) or Screen
   layer, extending past the shape.
5. Light spill: paint the effect's colour onto nearby surfaces and faces facing it
   (key light from the effect), and cast shadows away from it.

## Layer setup
- Effects on their own layers above the art, mode Add (Glow) / Glow dodge (CSP),
  Linear Dodge (Add) or Color Dodge (Photoshop).
- Coloured line hold (see colour holds) for energy lines drawn by the inker.

## Print vs screen
- Additive modes can produce RGB values outside CMYK; soft-proof and avoid relying
  on out-of-gamut neon. Check rich black and total ink limits near glows.

## Verify
- [ ] Effect is the lightest value in its panel.
- [ ] Light spill visible on the faces/objects nearby.
