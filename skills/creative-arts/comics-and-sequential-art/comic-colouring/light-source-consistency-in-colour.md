# Light source consistency in colour

**Goal:** colour rendering and inked blacks agree on where the light is, panel to
panel.

## Process
1. Read the inks: blacks and cast shadows show the penciller's light. Mark direction
   per scene on a notes layer or thumbnail sheet.
2. Where the inks are ambiguous, decide one key light per scene and ask the artist
   if it conflicts with the story (window, lamp, explosion).
3. Render: highlights on planes facing the light, form shadows on the far side,
   cast shadows away from it.
4. Secondary light (fill, bounce, rim) only in a different temperature and weaker.

## Panel to panel
- Camera reverses (shot/reverse shot) flip the light relative to the viewer but not
  in the world: if the window is behind A, A is backlit, B is front lit.
- Light sources that move in the story (torch, car headlights) must move
  consistently.

## Gotchas
- Colourist and inker using different lights: double shadows. Follow the inks or
  ask for corrections.
- Lighting every face from the front for clarity kills the scene's mood.

## Verify
- [ ] Highlights point the same way in all panels of a scene.
- [ ] Notes record light direction per scene for flatters/assistants.
