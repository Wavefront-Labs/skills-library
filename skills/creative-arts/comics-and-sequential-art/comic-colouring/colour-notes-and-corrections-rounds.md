# Colour notes and corrections rounds

**Goal:** editor and artist notes resolved quickly without breaking other pages.

## Receiving notes
- Ask for notes as page/panel references (p.07 pn3: "jacket should be green") or
  marked-up JPEGs/PDF comments.
- Consolidate notes from editor, writer and artist into one list; resolve
  conflicts with the editor, not between collaborators.

## Fixing
- Work non-destructively: flats layer kept; render and lighting on adjustment
  layers so a hue fix is one selection and a hue/saturation change.
- Re-export only changed pages; keep version suffixes (p07_v2.tif).
- Check whether a fix ripples (a costume colour error on p.07 likely appears on
  other pages).

## Tracking
- Corrections log: page, note, status, version. Share it with the editor.
- Typically 1-2 rounds are included in the page rate; agree in writing what extra
  rounds cost.

## Verify
- [ ] Every note in the log marked done or rejected with a reason.
- [ ] Final deliverables replaced in the shared folder with clear versions.
