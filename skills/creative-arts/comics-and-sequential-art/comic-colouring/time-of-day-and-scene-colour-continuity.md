# Time-of-day and scene colour continuity

**Goal:** a scene that starts at dusk stays dusk (or darkens plausibly) across
pages coloured on different days.

## Plan
1. Colour script: one row of small thumbnails for the issue, each tagged with time
   of day and light key.
2. Scene light key: a reference panel per scene showing the sky, ambient colour,
   key light colour and shadow colour.
3. Save the scene's light as adjustment layers (curves, colour balance, gradient
   map) or a layer set to copy onto every page of that scene.

## During colouring
- Sample from the reference panel, not memory.
- Time progression: if the script says the scene runs an hour into sunset, step the
  light key per page.
- Interior vs exterior in one scene: windows show the outside time of day.

## Catching drift
- Lay out all pages of the scene side by side (Bridge, CSP page manager, contact
  sheet) and compare before delivery.
- Monitor calibration and consistent viewing light; colouring at night on an
  uncalibrated laptop drifts warm/cool.

## Verify
- [ ] Contact sheet of the scene shows consistent ambient colour.
- [ ] Light key saved with the files for later issues.
