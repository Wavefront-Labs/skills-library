# Atmospheric perspective in comic colour

**Goal:** clear depth separation between foreground, midground and background.

## Rules
- With distance, values move toward the sky/air colour (usually lighter), contrast
  drops, saturation drops, and hue shifts toward blue in clear daylight.
- Foreground: full contrast, darkest darks; background: compressed value range.
- Exceptions: haze at sunset shifts warm; underwater shifts green/blue and darkens;
  smoke shifts grey-brown; night shifts toward dark blue.

## Method
1. Flat each depth plane on separate selections (flats by plane).
2. On the background plane, add a layer filled with the air colour clipped to the
   plane at 20-60 percent, or use a gradient map toward the atmosphere colour.
3. Lift black line art in the far background: set those lines to a colour hold
   (colour the lines a darker version of the air colour).
4. Keep figures in the foreground plane crisp.

## Gotchas
- Same saturation everywhere flattens the panel; inked lines alone cannot fix it.
- Inked black line in deep backgrounds fights the effect; ask for permission to hold
  lines in colour.

## Verify
- [ ] Each plane distinguishable in greyscale.
- [ ] Background contrast lower than foreground.
