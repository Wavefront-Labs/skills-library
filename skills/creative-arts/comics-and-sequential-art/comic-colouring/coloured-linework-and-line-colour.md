# Coloured linework and line colour

**Goal:** selective lines printed in colour (a "colour hold") so soft forms,
light sources and atmosphere are not cut by black outlines.

## Uses
- Light-emitting things (fire, energy, neon) and their edges.
- Distant background planes, clouds, smoke, water, reflections.
- Soft/lit skin edges in painted styles; whole-page line colour for watercolour looks.

## Setup
1. Line art on its own layer, transparent background (from 1-bit: select black,
   copy to a new transparent layer; CSP: Edit > Convert brightness to opacity).
2. Lock transparent pixels (Photoshop) or Lock transparency (CSP).
3. Paint over the lines with the hold colour; usually a darker, saturated version of
   the adjacent fill.
4. For the whole page tinted: a clipped colour layer over the lines, Normal or
   Color mode.

## Print
- Black line art normally prints on K plate only (overprint). Coloured lines are
  built from CMY(K) and need trapping; very thin coloured lines can misregister.
  Keep held lines at least moderate weight, or use rich-but-limited builds.

## Verify
- [ ] Held lines only where the story calls for it.
- [ ] Remaining black line art is still 100K overprint.
