# Colour holds for SFX and energy

**Goal:** sound effects and energy drawn in black line on the art become coloured,
light-filled shapes.

## Who does what
- Art-drawn SFX (by the penciller/inker): colourist fills and may hold the outline.
- Letterer-drawn SFX (separate layer): letterer usually styles them; agree if the
  colourist adds gradients or holds.
- Note in the script or editorial notes which SFX are art and which are lettering.

## Process
1. Separate the SFX lines from the rest of the line art (select, cut to its own
   layer) so holds do not touch character lines.
2. Fill the letterforms (gradient: hot core to saturated edge for impacts).
3. Hold the outline in a darker shade of the fill, or white for glowing.
4. Energy lines: hold in the effect colour, add glow layer (see glows).

## Corrections-safe
- Keep held lines as a separate layer group named "HOLDS" in the deliverable so
  later art corrections can re-apply them.
- Print: holds made of process colour, not K.

## Verify
- [ ] SFX readable against its background value.
- [ ] Holds on their own layer in the delivered file.
