# Montage and diegetic text

**Goal:** text that lives inside the image world (signs, screens, letters) or becomes
the image itself, scripted so it's legible and the right person produces it.

## Types
- **Diegetic text**: in-world signs, newspaper headlines, phone screens, tattoos,
  graffiti, handwritten notes.
- **Montage** (McCloud): words treated as integral parts of the picture, e.g. a scream
  rendered as a huge graphic shape filling the panel, a song's lyrics winding through
  the scene.

## Scripting
```
PANEL 2
Insert: Mara's phone screen. Text message thread.
  SCREEN TEXT (TEO): don't go to the harbour
  SCREEN TEXT (TEO): please
NOTE: letterer to set screen text as a separate layer for translation.
```
- Write the exact words; don't say "a headline about the fire".
- Decide **who produces it**: the artist draws it into the art, or the letterer sets it
  on a separate layer. Separate layers are far easier to fix and translate.
- Keep it short and legible at print size; a full newspaper page is unreadable.

## Translation and localisation
- Text drawn into the art must be redrawn or covered in foreign editions; prefer letterer
  layers for anything plot-critical.
- Note what can stay untranslated (background signs) vs must be translated.

## Gotchas
- Real brand names and logos: legal risk; invent brands.
- Diegetic text with spelling errors drawn into inks is expensive to fix.

## Verify
- [ ] Every in-world text item has exact copy in the script.
- [ ] Ownership (artist vs letterer layer) stated.
- [ ] Plot-critical text legible at print size and translatable.
