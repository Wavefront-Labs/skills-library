# Scripting silent panels and sequences

**Goal:** wordless storytelling that reads unambiguously and lands emotionally.

## When to go silent
- After a shock or loss: silence gives the reader time to feel.
- Action that the pictures already explain.
- Tension/stealth sequences where words would break the mood.
- Openings that establish place before anyone speaks.

## How to script it
- Mark each silent panel explicitly: `NO DIALOGUE` (or `SILENT`). Otherwise the letterer
  or editor may assume you forgot.
- Be more specific about action and expression than usual: there's no text to clarify.
- Each panel must answer "what changed since the last panel?" visibly.
- **Timing**: more panels = more time. Three panels of the same face with tiny changes
  slow the moment (a beat). One wide panel holds a long pause.
- Consider what sound you want: none, or a small SFX only.

## Example
```
PANEL 4
NO DIALOGUE
Medium. Mara sits on the pier edge, the ledger in her lap, unopened.

PANEL 5
NO DIALOGUE
Same framing. She has opened it. Her hand covers her mouth.
```
Same framing across panels makes the change the only thing the eye notices.

## Gotchas
- Ambiguous actions in silent sequences (is she throwing or catching?). Choose clear
  poses and say what's important.
- A silent page in a dense issue may still need a locator caption if the scene changed.

## Verify
- [ ] Every silent panel marked NO DIALOGUE.
- [ ] A test reader can narrate what happens without help.
