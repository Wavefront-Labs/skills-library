# Inking silhouette panels

**Goal:** solid black (or white) figure panels that read instantly and change the
page's rhythm.

## When to use
- Pacing: a beat of quiet or a dramatic pause between detailed panels.
- Violence or horror shown without detail; distance shots against a bright sky.
- Repeating layouts where only the silhouette changes (Eisner timing).

## Making it read
1. The pose must work as a filled shape: limbs separated from torso, profile faces,
   clear hand gestures. Repose the pencils if needed.
2. Fill the figure solid black; keep a strong background value contrast (white sky,
   coloured hold, or light tone).
3. Reverse silhouette: white figure on black ground; good for flashes and rim-lit
   night scenes. Ink the ground, leave the figure.
4. Interior lines: none, or a few white lines (white gel pen / white digital brush)
   for a face profile or rim light.

## Colour and letters
- Tell the colourist the silhouette is intentional ("black, no colour") or that it
  should take a colour hold.
- Balloons over silhouettes: white balloon with standard border; tails into black
  must stay visible (white tail outline).

## Verify
- [ ] Action reads from the shape alone.
- [ ] Colourist note included for silhouette or hold.
