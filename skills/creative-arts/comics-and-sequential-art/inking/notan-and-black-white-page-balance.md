# Notan and black-white page balance

**Goal:** the distribution of solid blacks across the page is designed, not a
by-product of individual panels.

## Notan study
1. Thumbnail the page at 2-3 inches tall.
2. Fill shapes in only two values: black and white. No lines, no grey.
3. Look at the page as an abstract pattern: where does the eye go first? Is there
   one dominant black mass, secondary masses and small accents?
4. Adjust: move a black from a panel interior to a background, invert a
   silhouette, open up a cluttered corner.

## Balance rules of thumb
- Avoid even distribution (checkerboard). Group blacks; let one area be dominant.
- Adjacent panels: alternate value (dark panel next to light panel) to separate
  them and to pace the read.
- Spread over two facing pages: check the spread notan, not just one page.
- Leave white for balloons; a black panel where heavy lettering lands needs a
  plan (white balloons on black are fine; captions in black areas need a border).
- Use the darkest area to frame the focal point, not to bury it.

## Failure modes
- Every panel spotted independently: the page reads grey and busy.
- Large blacks along the gutter edge merging neighbouring panels.

## Verify
- [ ] Two-value thumbnail has a clear dominant black shape.
- [ ] The eye's first stop on the notan is the page's key story beat.
