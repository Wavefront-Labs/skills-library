# Inking speed lines and focus lines

**Goal:** motion and impact lines that read as one designed effect, not a scribble.

## Speed (parallel) lines
- Direction of travel defines the lines; in perspective they converge to the
  motion's vanishing point.
- Ink with a ruler, flicking the pen off the end so each line tapers.
- Vary spacing: tight clusters and gaps; even spacing looks mechanical.
- Stop the lines short of the figure's leading edge; they trail behind.

## Focus (radial) lines
- Mark the focal point (often off the panel centre, on the face or impact).
- Ink from the panel edge inward, tapering to nothing before the subject.
- Leave a clean white area around the subject.
- Use a pin at the focal point and a ruler pivoting on it.

## Digital
- CSP Focus line and Speed line rulers or the Saturated line / Streamline figure
  tools give editable vector lines with taper and spacing jitter.
- Photoshop: Filter > Distort > Polar coordinates on noise for radial lines.

## Gotchas
- Lines in front of the figure: confusing; mask them out.
- Over-use: speed lines in every action panel flatten intensity.
- Coordinate with colour: tell the colourist whether to colour the background or
  hold it white.

## Verify
- [ ] Lines taper and converge correctly.
- [ ] Clear zone around the subject.
