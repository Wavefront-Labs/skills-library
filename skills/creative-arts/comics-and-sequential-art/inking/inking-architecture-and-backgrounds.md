# Inking architecture and backgrounds

**Goal:** backgrounds that place the scene clearly while staying behind the figures.

## Line weight for depth
- Foreground: heaviest lines; midground: medium; background: thinnest, or open
  lines and no blacks.
- Figures always get a heavier outline than the background they stand against.

## Architecture
1. Ink vanishing-point lines with a ruler first; check that edges converge.
2. Corners and edges heavier than surface detail lines.
3. Windows and repeating units: ink the nearest few fully, simplify toward
   distance.
4. Break lines where they would tangent a figure's contour; leave a small white gap.

## Texture economy
- Brick, wood, stone: a few patches, especially near edges and shadow, not the whole
  wall.
- Spot blacks in background windows and doorways to frame figures.

## Atmospheric tricks
- Drop the far background to line only and let the colourist add depth.
- Open lines (gaps in contours) on distant forms suggest haze.

## Gotchas
- Background lines touching or passing behind a head at the same weight create
  tangents and confusion.
- Over-rendered backgrounds compete with the acting; balance with the notan.

## Verify
- [ ] Figures separate clearly from backgrounds in a squint test.
- [ ] Perspective lines converge accurately.
