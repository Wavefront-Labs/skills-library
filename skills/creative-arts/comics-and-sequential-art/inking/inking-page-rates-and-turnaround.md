# Inking page rates and turnaround

**Goal:** a sustainable inking schedule that meets the book's deadline and is
priced professionally.

## Throughput
- Typical professional pace: 1-2 pages a day on standard interiors; detailed
  pages or heavy textures may be 1 page in 1.5 days. Covers 1-3 days.
- Monthly 20-22 page book: about 3-4 weeks of inking. Inking overlaps pencils:
  pages come in batches as the penciller finishes them.
- Track actual hours per page for a month; quote from data, not hope.

## Rates
- Rates vary widely by publisher and market. Consult the Graphic Artists Guild
  Handbook ranges and peers; work-for-hire page rates typically pay inkers less
  than pencillers. Clarify whether rates include backgrounds and covers.
- Get terms in writing: rate per page, kill fee, payment terms (net 30), who owns
  the originals, credit line, royalties if any.

## Schedule hygiene
- Build a buffer of at least a few days before the colourist's start date.
- Daily or batch delivery so colour can start early.
- Report slips early to the editor; late inks compress colour and letters.

## Invoicing
- Invoice per batch or per issue with page numbers, rate, total and payment details.

## Verify
- [ ] Written agreement with rate and deadlines before starting.
- [ ] Schedule shows deliveries per batch with buffer.
