# Rulers, French curves and ellipse templates

**Goal:** clean straight and mechanical curves without smears, with line weight
that still looks hand inked.

## Tools
- Ruler with an inking edge (bevelled or raised); or tape 2-3 coins or a second
  thin ruler under it so the edge lifts off the paper. Ink touching the edge wicks
  under and smears.
- T-square and triangles on a drawing board for verticals/horizontals.
- French curves (set of 3) for car bodies, flowing architecture, sweeping speed
  lines.
- Ellipse templates (by degree: 15 to 60 degrees) for wheels, cylinders, cups,
  rings. Match the degree to the eye level.

## Technique
1. Ink all ruled lines in one session; let them dry before freehand inking near them.
2. Pen held vertical against the raised edge; consistent speed.
3. Taper ruled lines by lifting pressure at the end, or by a brush pass along the
   ruler at a slight angle.
4. Curves: find the section of the French curve that matches 3+ points of the
   pencil, ink that segment, then reposition and overlap.
5. Wipe tools after each line.

## Digital
- CSP: ruler and special rulers; Photoshop: Shift-click line; Procreate:
  QuickShape. Add pressure tapering so lines do not look vector-flat.

## Verify
- [ ] No smears or ruler bleed along straight edges.
- [ ] Ellipse degrees consistent with the panel's eye level.
