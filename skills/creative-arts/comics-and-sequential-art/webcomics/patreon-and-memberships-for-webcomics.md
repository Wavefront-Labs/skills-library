# Patreon and memberships for webcomics

## Platforms
- Patreon (most used; tiered membership, posts, Discord integration; fees vary by plan and payment
  processing), Ko-fi (one-off tips plus memberships, lower fees), WEBTOON's and Tapas's in-app
  monetisation, YouTube/Substack-style memberships.

## Tier design
- 3-4 tiers maximum. Typical ladder:
  1. Low ($1-3): thanks, name in credits, patron-only feed.
  2. Mid ($5): early access (pages 1-2 weeks ahead), process sketches.
  3. High ($10-15): high-res wallpapers, PDF of each chapter, monthly bonus comic.
  4. Physical tier (optional): signed print or book; costs shipping and time.
- Every reward must be sustainable weekly for years. Prefer by-products of work you already do
  (thumbnails, sketches, timelapses) over new obligations.

## Early access
- Easiest high-value perk for webcomics: patrons see pages ahead of the public. Needs a buffer.

## Retention
- Post regularly to patrons even when the public comic is on break.
- Annual billing options and welcome posts reduce churn.
- Patron count grows with the free audience; Patreon is a conversion of readers, not a discovery tool.
  Typical conversions are around 1-5% of engaged readers.

## Admin
- Track income and fees for taxes; Patreon issues tax forms in some countries; VAT may apply to EU
  patrons (platform usually handles it).
- Keep the patron list exportable.

## Gotchas
- Paywalling the main comic usually shrinks the free audience that feeds the membership.
- Physical rewards on monthly tiers create a shipping job; prefer annual or one-off.

## Verify
- [ ] Each tier's deliverables fit your weekly schedule.
- [ ] Early access content scheduled from the buffer.
