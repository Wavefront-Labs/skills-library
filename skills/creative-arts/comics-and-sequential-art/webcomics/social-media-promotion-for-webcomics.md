# Social media promotion for webcomics

## Principles
- Post native content: the comic itself (a panel, a page, a carousel, a short video) on each platform,
  not only links. Algorithms penalise posts that send people off-site.
- Each post works standalone and ends with a pointer (link in bio, "read Ep. 12 on WEBTOON").
- Consistency beats volume: a schedule tied to the update day.

## Formats by platform
| Platform | What works |
|---|---|
| Instagram | 4:5 carousels of panels, Reels with page reveals/timelapses |
| TikTok / YouTube Shorts | vertical scroll-throughs with music, process timelapses, character intros |
| Bluesky / X / Threads / Mastodon | single striking panel + hook text; alt text |
| Reddit | subreddit-specific rules (r/comics, r/webcomics); post full strips, no spam |
| Tumblr | full pages, tags; long-tail reblogs |

## Launch
- Tease for 2-4 weeks before launch: character designs, cover, countdown.
- Launch day: post the hook page and the first-episode link everywhere.

## Engagement
- Reply to comments; repost fan art (with credit and permission).
- Cross-promote with creators of similar size (shout-out swaps, guest strips).

## Measuring
- Use UTM-tagged or per-platform links to see which platforms bring readers to the comic and mailing list.
- Track subscriber growth on the comic platform, not just likes.

## Gotchas
- Cropping a panel spoils the episode's reveal; preview setup panels instead.
- Posting identical text and hashtags everywhere looks automated; adapt per platform.

## Verify
- [ ] Each post has alt text, a hook and a path to the comic.
- [ ] Monthly check of which channel actually converts.
