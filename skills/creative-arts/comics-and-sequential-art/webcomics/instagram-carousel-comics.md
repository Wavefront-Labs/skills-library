# Instagram carousel comics

A carousel post holds up to 20 images (Instagram raised the limit from 10 in 2024); each swipe acts as a
panel transition or a page turn.

## Specs
- Portrait 4:5 at **1080 x 1350 px** gives the most screen space in the feed; square 1:1 at 1080 x 1080
  is safest for the profile grid. Every slide in a carousel is cropped to the first slide's ratio.
- The profile grid now displays posts at a 3:4-ish crop; keep the title and key art centred.
- Export sRGB JPEG or PNG; text sized to read on a phone without zoom.

## Structure
1. **Slide 1 = hook**: title, striking image, or the setup line. It is the only one many people see.
2. One beat per slide, or 1-4 panels per slide for a strip.
3. Put the reveal or punchline on its own slide so the swipe is the timing beat.
4. Last slide: punchline or call to action (follow, link in bio, next part).
5. Consistent borders/margins across slides so the set feels like one comic.

## Posting
- Alt text per image (Advanced settings) for accessibility.
- Caption with the first line as a hook, then credits and a few relevant hashtags.
- Reels and Threads crossposting need different ratios (9:16); make a separate export.

## Gotchas
- Mixing ratios crops later slides.
- Text near the edges is covered by the carousel dots and UI; keep a 60-80 px safe margin.
- Compression softens fine lines; thicken lines and avoid fine tones.
