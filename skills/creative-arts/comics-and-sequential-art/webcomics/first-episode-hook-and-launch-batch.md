# First-episode hook and launch batch

**Goal:** readers who click episode 1 keep reading through episode 3 and subscribe.

## Episode 1
- **First screens**: an arresting image, question or emotional moment. No title-card
  prologue of lore.
- **Protagonist** on screen early with a clear want or problem.
- **Premise** evident by the end of episode 1 (what kind of story is this?).
- **End on a hook** that promises the series' core conflict.
- Make it representative: the art/colour/tone readers get later.

## Launch batch
- Publish **several episodes at launch** (often 3 or more) so new readers can binge
  past the set-up; check platform guidance and promotions for launch counts.
- Keep a **buffer** of finished episodes (e.g. 5-10) before launch to keep a reliable
  schedule.

## Discovery assets
- Series thumbnail and title that communicate genre at a glance.
- Short summary: protagonist, conflict, hook; genre keywords.

## Measure
Watch episode-to-episode retention (subscriber/view drop-off between ep 1 and 3). A
big drop at ep 1 suggests the opening or thumbnail mismatches the story.

## Verify
- [ ] Hook visible within the first screens; premise clear by end of ep 1.
- [ ] Launch batch and buffer ready before going live.
