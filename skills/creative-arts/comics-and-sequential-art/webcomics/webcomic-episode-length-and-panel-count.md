# Webcomic episode length and panel count

**Goal:** episodes long enough to satisfy, short enough to sustain weekly.

## Typical ranges (observed practice; no fixed rule)
- Vertical-scroll episodes commonly run roughly **30-60 panels**; professional
  serialised webtoons often sit toward the higher end.
- Page-format webcomics: 1 page per update (2-3x weekly) or several pages weekly.
- Strip webcomics: one strip per update.

## Budgeting
1. Measure your real production time per panel (thumbnail to colour/letters).
2. Episode panel count x time per panel must fit your weekly hours with margin
   (illness, revisions). Burnout from over-long episodes is the most common reason
   series stall.
3. Hold length roughly consistent; readers notice short episodes.

## Content per episode
- **One main beat** (a scene or turn) plus an **ending hook**.
- Opening panels re-orient briefly (where are we, who's here).
- Avoid splitting a single conversation across many episodes without progress.

## Techniques to save time without shortening
- Reusable backgrounds/3D sets, simpler colour on transition panels, more silent
  atmosphere panels.

## Verify
- [ ] Panel count fits the measured weekly capacity with slack.
- [ ] Each episode has one beat and a hook.
