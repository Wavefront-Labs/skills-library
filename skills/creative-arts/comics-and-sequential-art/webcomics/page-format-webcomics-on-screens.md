# Page-format webcomics on screens

Many webcomics keep a print page shape (for later collection) rather than vertical scroll. The
challenge is legibility on phones, where a full page is shown at about 400 CSS px wide.

## Choosing a shape
- **Portrait page** (US comic 6.625 x 10.25 in, or manga B5/A5): best for print collections; small on
  phones.
- **Landscape or square strips/half pages**: fit desktop and phones held sideways; easy to assemble two
  per print page.
- **Panel-height tiers** that stack: design pages as 2-3 tiers that can later be sliced for vertical
  readers.

## Legibility rules for phones
- Fewer panels per page (4-6 rather than 7-9).
- Lettering at least about 1/40 of page height at final size, or test: readable on a phone without zoom.
- High contrast; avoid fine hatching that turns to grey at small size.
- Keep critical content away from the edges where platforms crop.

## Files
- Export about 1200-1600 px wide for the web version; keep print masters at 300-600 ppi CMYK/grey.
- Provide a click/tap-to-enlarge full resolution image, or panel-by-panel view on some apps.

## Gotchas
- Splitting pages into individual panels to post loses page composition; decide which you're designing
  for.
- Double-page spreads display tiny on mobile; post them as a separate wide image too.

## Verify
- [ ] Read one page on a small phone at arm's length without zooming.
- [ ] Same file works for later print at the planned trim.
