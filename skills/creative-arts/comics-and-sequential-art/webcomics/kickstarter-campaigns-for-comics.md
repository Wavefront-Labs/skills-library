# Kickstarter campaigns for comics

**Goal:** fund a print run without losing money or burning out on fulfilment.

## Budget before goal
List every cost, then set the goal to cover them with margin:
- Printing (get real quotes for 2-3 quantities), proofs.
- Platform and payment fees (check current rates on the platform's fees page;
  typically several percent plus payment processing).
- Shipping and packaging per region (often the largest surprise), pick-and-pack if
  outsourced.
- Rewards/add-ons (prints, stickers, pins) production.
- Taxes/VAT obligations where applicable, and a contingency (10-15%).

## Structure
- **Finish the book first** (or most of it); late delivery is the main backer complaint.
- Tiers: digital (PDF), physical book, signed/sketch editions, limited extras. Keep the
  tier count small to simplify fulfilment.
- Charge shipping separately or via a pledge manager after the campaign.
- Campaign length often ~30 days; build a pre-launch page and email list.

## Campaign page
Video or animated preview, sample pages, clear delivery timeline, risk section,
creator credentials.

## Fulfilment
- Pledge manager for addresses/add-ons; batch shipping by region.
- Communicate updates regularly, especially about delays.

## Verify
- [ ] Goal covers all costs including shipping and fees, with contingency.
- [ ] Book substantially complete before launch.
- [ ] Fulfilment plan and timeline written.
