# Fulfilment and shipping

After a Kickstarter or store sale, books must reach readers intact and on budget.

## Self vs outsourced
| Self-fulfil | Third-party (e.g. warehouse fulfilment, BackerKit/ProFulfillment partners, print-on-demand drop-ship) |
|---|---|
| Cheapest per order for small runs (under a few hundred) | Scales to thousands |
| Your time: hours per 100 orders | Pick/pack fees plus storage |
| Full control of packing | Integrated tracking and international hubs |

## Packaging
- Rigid mailers or corrugated book wraps; bubble mailers alone dent corners.
- Bag books in poly sleeves against moisture.
- Weigh a packed sample of every reward tier before setting shipping prices.

## Postage
- US: USPS Media Mail is cheapest for books (slow, inspected, only books/media); Ground Advantage for
  mixed rewards.
- UK: Royal Mail Large Letter/small parcel; EU and international via tracked services.
- International: charge shipping by region; collect it in the pledge manager, not the campaign base price.

## Customs and tax
- Customs declaration (CN22/CN23) with HS code for printed books (4901), value and description.
- EU orders: VAT collected at import or via IOSS for low-value goods; UK has its own rules. Pledge
  managers can collect VAT in advance.

## Process
1. Lock addresses in the pledge manager; export a CSV.
2. Print labels via a postage service (Pirate Ship, Click & Drop, Shippo).
3. Pack in batches by tier; scan tracking; mark shipped.
4. Keep spare stock (about 2-3%) for damaged/lost replacements.

## Verify
- [ ] Shipping charged per region covers postage plus packaging.
- [ ] Every international parcel has a correct customs form.
