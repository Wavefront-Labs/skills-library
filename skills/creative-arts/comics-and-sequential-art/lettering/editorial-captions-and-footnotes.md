# Editorial captions and footnotes

**Goal:** asides from the editor or translator that are clearly outside the story
voice and easy to ignore if the reader wants.

## Forms
- **Back-issue reference**: "*As seen in ASH TIDE #2. --Ed." Classic in superhero comics.
- **Translation note**: "*Translated from Spanish." on the first translated line.
- **Cultural/term footnote** in manga localisation: short explanation of a pun or term.

## Lettering
1. Put an **asterisk** after the word or balloon being referenced (or at the end of the
   balloon).
2. Letter the note in a small caption box, smaller size than dialogue, often italic,
   starting with the asterisk.
3. Place it near the referenced balloon, usually at a panel corner (bottom often),
   inside the safe area.
4. Sign-off style per house ("--Ed.", "--Trans."), consistent across the book.

## Restraint
- Use sparingly; frequent notes break immersion.
- Long explanations belong in back matter.

## Verify
- [ ] Every asterisk has its note on the same panel/page.
- [ ] Note style smaller and distinct from narration; sign-off consistent.
