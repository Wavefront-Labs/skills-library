# Stutters, whispers and non-verbal balloons

**Goal:** sounds and speech modes that aren't plain dialogue, lettered in recognised
ways.

## Stutters
- Hyphenate repeated initial sounds: "I-I DON'T KNOW." "W-WHAT?"
- Keep the repetition short (1-2 repeats) for readability.

## Whispers
- Dashed balloon outline (common convention) and/or smaller or grey text.
- Some books use a lighter grey balloon stroke instead; follow house style.

## Muttering / small text
- Smaller point size inside a normal balloon; balloon may be reduced too.
- Keep it legible: don't shrink below roughly 70-80% of normal dialogue size in print.

## Non-verbal sounds in balloons
- Sighs, grunts, gasps: "HAHH", "NNGH", "HUFF", lettered in balloons (they're voice),
  not as SFX (unless very loud).
- Laughter: "HA HA" or styled.

## Silent reactions
- A balloon containing only "..." = speechless/awkward silence.
- "?" or "!" alone = confusion or surprise without words.

## Singing
- Music note glyphs around or inside the text; some books use wavy balloon outlines.

## Gotchas
- Using SFX lettering for small vocal sounds (makes them feel loud).
- Overused stutters become comic unintentionally.

## Verify
- [ ] Each speech mode has one consistent treatment across the book.
- [ ] Small text still legible at print size.
