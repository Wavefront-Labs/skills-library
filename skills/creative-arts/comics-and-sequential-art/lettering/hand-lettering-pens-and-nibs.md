# Hand lettering pens and nibs

**Goal:** tools that give the consistent stroke weight comic lettering needs.

## Pens
- Speedball B-series nibs (round, mono-weight strokes): B-5 / B-6 for standard
  dialogue on original-size art, B-5 1/2 common. Bold emphasis: B-3 or B-4, or go
  over strokes twice.
- Crowquill (e.g. Hunt 107) for thin-line SFX and balloon outlines.
- Technical pens (Rapidograph 0.35-0.5 mm, or Pigma Micron 05/08) for even weight
  with no dipping. Pigment markers wear tips; replace often.
- Reservoir pens and some letterers' favourites: Pelikan Graphos (discontinued; find
  used), Rotring.

## Inks and paper
- Waterproof, opaque black (Higgins Black Magic, Deleter, Speedball Super Black).
- Smooth (plate) bristol for crisp strokes; vellum feathers more.

## Care
- Clean nibs after each session; new nibs: remove oil coating with saliva or a quick
  flame pass/alcohol wipe.
- Keep a sample sheet with each nib's stroke for matching later corrections.

## Verify
- [ ] Stroke weight identical for all dialogue on a page.
- [ ] Inks fully opaque in the scan.
