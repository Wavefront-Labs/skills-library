# Drawing balloons by hand

**Goal:** hand-drawn balloons with even line weight and good shape that suit the
art.

## Order
1. Letter the text first (in ink).
2. Pencil the balloon around it with even padding (about the height of one letter
   around the text block).
3. Ink with a technical pen or crowquill: ellipse template sections for smooth
   ovals, or freehand for organic styles.
4. Tails last: two tapering strokes meeting in a point aimed at the speaker's mouth.

## Shapes
- Speech: oval or rounded, slightly squarish for blocks of text.
- Thought: scalloped cloud with bubble trail (rare in modern books).
- Burst/shout: jagged spikes, irregular but balanced.
- Whisper: dashed outline.
- Captions: ruled rectangles.

## Line weight
- Balloon line lighter than panel borders; consistent across the page.
- Butted or connected balloons: join with a short neck or overlap the outlines, then
  white out the overlap.

## Corrections
- White-out (process white) for errors; or patch on new lettered paper and paste.

## Verify
- [ ] Even padding; no text crowding the balloon edge.
- [ ] Tails point clearly at the speaker.
