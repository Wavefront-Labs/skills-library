# Continued and to-be-continued captions

**Goal:** clean issue endings and continuations in the book's display style.

## Types
- **"TO BE CONTINUED..."** or "CONTINUED IN #5" at the end of a chapter.
- **"NEXT: ..."** blurb teasing the next issue (title of next chapter, a hook line).
- **"THE END"** for concluded stories.
- **"CONTINUED FROM..."** on openings that pick up another title's story (crossovers).

## Lettering
- Usually the last caption on the final page, bottom right (the last thing read).
- Use a display style: the caption font in caps with extra tracking, a slightly larger
  size, or the series' display face; consistent across issues.
- Keep it off the final image's focal point; a quiet corner or a dedicated strip.
- Coordinate exact wording with the editor (issue numbers, titles change late).

## Collected editions
In trades, "to be continued" captions between chapters are often kept or removed per
editor; deliver them on a separate layer so they can be dropped.

## Verify
- [ ] Wording confirmed with editor; issue numbers correct.
- [ ] Caption on its own layer for collection edits.
