# Dashes, ellipses and interruption

**Goal:** punctuation that signals timing the same way across the whole book.

## Conventions (Blambot's grammar guide and common practice; house styles vary)
| Situation | Mark | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Speaker is **interrupted** (cut off abruptly) | double dash or em dash | "WAIT, I CAN EXPLAIN--" |
| Speaker **trails off** / hesitates | ellipsis | "I JUST THOUGHT..." |
| Sentence continues into the **next balloon** (no pause) | dash or ellipsis at end and start (house choice) | "AND THEN WE--" / "--RAN." |
| Pause within a sentence | ellipsis | "IT WAS... BEAUTIFUL." |
| Shock question | ?! (question mark first, or a combined interrobang glyph if the font has it) | "YOU DID WHAT?!" |

## Details
- Many comic fonts render "--" as a proper dash; some use an em dash glyph. Pick one
  and use it consistently.
- Ellipsis: three dots, as the font's ellipsis glyph or three periods, consistently.
- Avoid multiple !!! or ??? beyond what the house style permits; one or two max.
- Periods vs exclamation marks: older comics avoided periods (they could vanish in
  print); modern books use them normally.

## Gotchas
- Mixing en/em dashes and double hyphens in one book.
- Interruption dash on a line that isn't interrupted.

## Verify
- [ ] One interruption style and one ellipsis style used throughout.
- [ ] Continuations across balloons follow the chosen convention.
