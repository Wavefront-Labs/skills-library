# Location and time captions

**Goal:** scene-setting captions that are instantly recognisable as "where/when", not
narration.

## Style
- Distinct from narration: e.g. small caps or a different weight, a darker box with
  light text, or text without a box over the art. Choose one locator style for the
  whole book.
- Short: place first, then time. "PORT CALDER. 2:14 A.M." or a two-line stack.
- Consistent formats for dates/times (12- vs 24-hour, numeral style) and names.

## Placement
- In the establishing panel of the new scene, usually top-left so it reads first.
- Avoid covering the establishing view itself; sit it on sky or a quiet area.
- For time jumps without a location change: time-only locator ("THREE DAYS LATER.").

## Special cases
- Flashback locators ("TWENTY YEARS AGO.") may adopt the flashback colour code.
- Multiple simultaneous scenes cross-cut: repeat locators the first time each place
  returns after a while.

## Verify
- [ ] One locator style used throughout, distinct from narrators.
- [ ] Formats for times/dates consistent.
- [ ] Locators don't cover key establishing art.
