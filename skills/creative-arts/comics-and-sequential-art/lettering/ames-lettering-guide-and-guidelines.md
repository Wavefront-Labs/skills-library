# Ames lettering guide and guidelines

**Goal:** consistent, evenly spaced guidelines for hand lettering on boards.

## The tool
- Ames Lettering Guide: a plastic frame with a rotating disc pierced by holes in
  columns. Slide it along a T-square or ruler; place a pencil in a hole and draw a
  line; move to the next hole.
- The disc number at the bottom sets the spacing (numbers are 32nds of an inch for
  the letter height in the standard columns).
- Comic lettering is typically ruled at setting around 3 to 3.5 on the guide using
  the centre column of equal spacing; check against your target cap height
  (original art letters were usually about 1/8 inch, which reduces in print).

## Process
1. Ensure the T-square sits square on the board's edge.
2. Rule lines across the balloon areas in light pencil or non-photo blue.
3. Letter, then ink balloons around the text.
4. Erase guidelines after ink dries (or leave non-photo blue to drop in the scan).

## Alternatives
- Lettering pad templates, Braddock-Rowe triangle, or printed guideline sheets
  under a lightbox.
- Digital hand lettering: CSP or Procreate guide layers with ruled lines at set
  spacing.

## Verify
- [ ] Letter height consistent across the page.
- [ ] Line spacing about half the cap height.
