# Cover trade dress and UPC box

**Goal:** a cover template with all required elements consistent across issues,
legible and print-compliant.

## Elements
- Series logo (fixed position per series), issue number, month or year as required.
- Price and publisher logo (bug); creator credits (often top or bottom band).
- Barcode: UPC (with 5-digit add-on for issue/variant) for direct market periodical
  comics, EAN-13 with ISBN for books/graphic novels. Usually bottom left corner, on
  a white box with quiet zone, never inside the bleed.
- Variant indications (Cover A/B), rating, cover copy or blurb if used.

## Layout
1. Template with trim, bleed (usually 0.125 in) and safe area (0.25 in inside trim).
2. Logo and trade dress on top layers; cover art beneath.
3. Cover copy: short punchy line, set in the series display type, never covering
   the main figure's face.
4. Check legibility over art: holding outlines, semi-opaque bands.

## Barcodes
- Barcodes from the publisher's GS1 allocation or the distributor; never resize
  below the minimum magnification (80 percent for UPC-A) and keep black on white.

## Verify
- [ ] Barcode scans and sits in the safe area.
- [ ] Logo position identical across issues.
