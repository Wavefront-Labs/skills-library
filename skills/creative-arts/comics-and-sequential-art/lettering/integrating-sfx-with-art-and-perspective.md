# Integrating SFX with art and perspective

**Goal:** SFX that feel part of the scene's space, near the source, without
covering key art.

## Placement
1. Start the effect at its source (gun muzzle, door, impact point) and let it
   travel in the direction the sound moves.
2. Never cover faces, hands doing the action, or the story-critical detail.
3. Read order: the SFX should be read at the moment it happens relative to the
   balloons in the panel (often before the reaction balloon).

## Space
- Perspective: distort with the panel's vanishing lines (Illustrator Perspective
  Grid or Free Distort).
- Overlap: part of the SFX behind a foreground object (mask it with a clipping path
  from the art) roots it in the scene.
- Size scale = loudness and distance.

## Borders and gutters
- Big effects can break the panel border and spill into the gutter for emphasis;
  keep inside the trim safety area.

## Collaboration
- Ask the artist to leave space or draw SFX they want integrated; check script notes
  for SFX the writer called for.
- Deliver SFX on a separate layer so colourist and translator can edit.

## Verify
- [ ] Nothing important covered.
- [ ] Perspective and overlap consistent with the scene.
