# Designing a comic series logo

**Goal:** a title logo that identifies the series on shelves and as a 200 px
digital thumbnail.

## Brief
- Genre and tone, era, key character, where it sits on covers (top band, vertical
  placements), long and short title variants.
- Study the market's shelf: what does the genre look like, how to stand apart.

## Design
1. Sketch 20+ thumbnails, mostly custom letterforms; avoid an unmodified font.
2. Choose 2-3 directions; refine in Illustrator with clean vector paths.
3. Unify with a strong shape (arc, perspective, overlapping letters, emblem).
4. Add a holding outline or drop shape so it sits on any cover art.
5. Test small: at 200 px wide (digital storefronts) it must still read.

## Variants
- Full colour, one colour (black and white), reversed (white), vertical or stacked.
- Issue number and creator credit placements built into the cover template.

## Delivery
- Vector AI/EPS/PDF with outlined text, plus PNG transparent at several sizes.
- Fonts used for any non-outlined text listed with licences.

## Verify
- [ ] Legible at 200 px and from 2 m on a printed cover.
- [ ] Works in one colour.
