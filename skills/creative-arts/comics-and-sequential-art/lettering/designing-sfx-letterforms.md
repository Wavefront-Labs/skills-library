# Designing SFX letterforms

**Goal:** sound effects that read as sound: the shape tells you how loud, sharp,
wet or mechanical it is.

## Match form to sound
| Sound | Letterform |
|---|---|
| Explosion, impact (BOOM, KRAKA) | huge, heavy, jagged or bulging, splayed outward |
| Sharp crack, gunshot (BLAM, KRAK) | angular, short, sharp serifs or spikes |
| Electric (ZZZT) | thin zigzag strokes, glow |
| Wet (SPLUT, DRIP) | drippy, rounded, irregular |
| Mechanical (KLIK, WHIRR) | geometric, condensed, even rhythm |
| Soft (tap, fwip) | small, light, lowercase allowed |

## Building blocks
- Start from an SFX font (Blambot, Comicraft display faces) or hand-draw letters
  in Illustrator; customise every major SFX (no stock unmodified fonts for hero
  effects).
- Distort: Envelope (Warp or mesh) in Illustrator, perspective along the action.
- Outline: inner fill colour, then a contrasting stroke, often a second outer
  stroke (white then black) for separation.
- Add texture or gradient only if it survives at print size.
- Repeated letters (KRAKKK) show duration; trailing size reduction shows fade.

## Gotchas
- Same SFX style for every sound: monotone soundtrack.
- Legibility: at least the first letter must be clearly readable.

## Verify
- [ ] SFX reads at thumbnail size.
- [ ] Style matches the sound's character.
