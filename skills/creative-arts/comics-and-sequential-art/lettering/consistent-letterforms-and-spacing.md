# Consistent letterforms and spacing

**Goal:** hand lettering where every A looks like every other A and the lines read
smoothly.

## Letterforms
- Comic lettering is mostly upper case, mono-weight with a slight lean or upright;
  pick one and hold it.
- Practice the full alphabet in fixed stroke order (e.g. E = vertical, then three
  horizontals left to right). Same order = same shape.
- Round letters (O, C, G, Q, S) slightly overshoot the guidelines.
- Crossbars: E, F, H at or slightly above the middle; consistent across letters.
- Crossbar I only for the pronoun "I" and initials, per convention.

## Spacing
- Optical not mechanical: equal area between letters, not equal distance.
  Round-to-round pairs closer, straight-to-straight further apart.
- Word spacing about the width of the letter O.
- Line spacing roughly half the cap height.
- Centre each line in the balloon; build text into a rounded diamond shape.

## Practice
- Letter a full alphabet and pangram daily; compare with a font from your own hand
  to spot drift.

## Verify
- [ ] Same letter identical across the page at a glance.
- [ ] No uneven gaps or collisions in a squint test.
