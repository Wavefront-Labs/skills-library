# Weekly serial schedule

A weekly shonen chapter is typically 17-20 pages (Weekly Shonen Jump standard is about 19), produced every
week, usually with a planned break schedule set by the magazine.

## The 7-day cycle (typical)
| Day | Work |
|---|---|
| 1-2 | Story meeting and **name** (storyboards) for next chapter; editor reviews and approves (uchiawase) |
| 3 | Pencils of characters and layout; assistants briefed |
| 4-6 | Inks; assistants do backgrounds, tones, effects; mangaka inks faces |
| 7 | Finishing, white correction, lettering checks; submit to editor; rest |
Days overlap: next week's name often starts while finishing the current one.

## Buffers
- Debut series often bank a few chapters (manuscripts in stock) before launch.
- Breaks: magazines schedule combined issues and rest weeks; author breaks (*kyuusai*) are announced in
  advance with the editor.

## Editor checkpoints
- Name approval before drawing starts.
- Chapter submission deadline; print-schedule deadline for colour pages (earlier).
- Reader survey (anketo) results feed back into story planning.

## Health and sustainability
- Fixed sleep and off-hours; long-running series show that burnout costs more chapters than it saves.
- Use 3D backgrounds and material libraries to cut background time.

## Gotchas
- Late name approval collapses the rest of the week. Push to finish name early.
- Colour pages (lead colour for chapter 1 or anniversaries) need separate lead time.

## Verify
- [ ] Schedule shows each week's name approval and submission deadline.
- [ ] Break weeks agreed with the editor in advance.
