# Page spread (mihiraki) control

**Goal:** design manga pages as the reader sees them: two facing pages, read right to
left, with reveals after page turns.

## Orientation
- Manga is bound on the right; the reader opens from what Western readers think of as
  the back.
- The **right-hand page is read first**, then the left, then turn.
- So in manga the page before a turn is the **left page**; reveals go on the next
  **right page**.

## Planning in the name
1. Mark each page R or L. In many tankobon layouts page 1 is a left page facing a
   blank or title; check the magazine/book's conventions for the first page.
2. End each left page on a prompt (a look, a door, a question).
3. Put reveals at the top-right of the next right page or as a full/large panel.
4. Avoid placing a big surprise on a left page whose right partner the reader reads
   first: they'll glimpse it.

## Spreads (two-page images)
- A two-page spread must occupy one opening: in a right-bound book that is a right
  page (read first, lower page number) plus the next page, which sits on the left of
  the same opening. Confirm the page numbering so the pair actually faces.
- Keep faces and text away from the gutter.
- CSP page manager shows spreads; set binding to right for manga files.

## Verify
- [ ] All pages marked R/L in the name with right-binding.
- [ ] Reveals land after page turns on right pages.
- [ ] Spreads fall on facing pairs.
