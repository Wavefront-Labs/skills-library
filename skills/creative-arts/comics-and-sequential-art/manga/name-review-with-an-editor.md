# Name review with an editor

**Goal:** a name that communicates the chapter clearly enough for an editor to judge
story and pacing, and a revision process that converges quickly.

## What the name is
The name (nemu) is the manga storyboard: every page with panel layout, rough figures,
camera, and **full dialogue** in balloons, read right-to-left. In Japanese magazine
practice the artist and their editor (tantou) meet (uchiawase) to review it before
any manuscript work.

## Preparing it
- Draw at a size the editor can read (often on copy paper or digitally at reduced
  manuscript size); dialogue legible.
- Figures rough but acting readable; expressions matter more than anatomy.
- Mark page numbers and spreads (mihiraki); mark planned colour pages if any.
- Include the chapter's opening and closing hooks clearly.

## What editors typically check
- Is the hook in the first pages strong; does the chapter end pulling to the next?
- Character clarity and motivation; is the protagonist sympathetic/active?
- Pacing: page budget, panel density, page-turn reveals.
- Readability of flow (right-to-left, balloon order).
- Fit with the magazine's audience.

## Revision rounds
- Take notes per page; ask what problem a note addresses.
- Redraw affected pages, not the whole name, unless structure changes.
- Expect multiple rounds for a new series; manuscript starts only after approval.

## Verify
- [ ] Every page has legible dialogue and clear flow.
- [ ] Hooks at start and end identified.
- [ ] Editor approval before manuscript pages begin.
