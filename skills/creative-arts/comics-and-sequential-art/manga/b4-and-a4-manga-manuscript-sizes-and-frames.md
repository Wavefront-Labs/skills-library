# B4 and A4 manga manuscript sizes and frames

Japanese professional manga is drawn on pre-printed manuscript paper (*genkou yoshi*) with blue
non-reproducing guide lines. Two sheet sizes are standard.

## Sheet sizes
| Sheet | Paper size | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **B4** (257 x 364 mm) | professional / submission standard | Printed at about 80-85% in B6/tankobon or larger in magazines |
| **A4** (210 x 297 mm) | doujinshi and amateur standard | Printed at 100% or reduced to B5/A5 |

## The guide lines (outside to inside)
1. **Bleed line** (tachikiri) - art that must run off the page extends to here (a few mm beyond trim).
2. **Finished trim line** (shiage) - where the page is cut.
3. **Inner frame / basic frame** (kihon waku) - the safe area that holds panel borders; text and
   important art stay inside it.
Typical B4 professional values: inner frame about 180 x 270 mm; trim about 220 x 310 mm; bleed about
3-5 mm beyond trim. A4 doujinshi (finishing to B5): inner frame about 150 x 220 mm, trim 182 x 257 mm.
Exact values vary by publisher and paper brand (Deleter, IC, Too); use the publisher's template.

## Digital equivalent (Clip Studio Paint)
- New > Comic: choose a preset such as "B4 manuscript (professional)" or set trim, bleed and base frame
  manually; 600 dpi for monochrome, 350 dpi for colour.
- Base frame shows as the inner frame; frame border tools snap to it.

## Gotchas
- Drawing panel borders to the trim line instead of the inner frame means borders get cut unevenly.
- Mixing A4 and B4 templates in one book changes reduction and line weights.
- Blue guide lines on scanned paper must drop out: scan in greyscale and remove with levels, or use
  a "blue drop-out" setting.

## Verify
- [ ] Text and faces inside the inner frame on every page.
- [ ] Bleed art reaches the bleed line, not just the trim.
