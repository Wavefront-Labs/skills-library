# Collecting chapters into tankobon

A **tankobon** collects magazine chapters into a standalone volume, typically ~180-200 pages: for a
weekly 19-page series about 9-10 chapters, for monthly 40-page series about 4-5.

## Steps
1. **Select chapters** so the volume ends on a strong hook (editors plan this during serialization).
2. **Corrections** - fix art errors, typos and continuity slips caught by readers; redraw rushed pages.
   Magazine-era cut corners are often redrawn for the volume.
3. **Colour pages** - magazine colour openings are often printed in greyscale in volumes; convert
   carefully (check tone values) or keep as colour inserts if the budget allows.
4. **Cover** - new cover illustration, spine art (series often build an image across spines), obi
   (promotional band) designed by the publisher.
5. **Extras (omake)** - author's notes (sidebars between chapters), character sheets, sketches, bonus
   4-koma, title page galleries; fill blank pages in signatures.
6. **Chapter title pages** - keep, compress or remove depending on page budget.

## Format
- Common Japanese sizes: shinsho (about 11.2 x 17.4 cm, most shonen) and B6 (12.8 x 18.2 cm, many
  seinen); page count must fit printer signatures (multiples of 16 or 32 pages).
- Margins and gutter differ from the magazine; check text near the spine.

## Gotchas
- Pages drawn for B5 magazines reduced to shinsho make small lettering illegible; re-letter if needed.
- Changing art between magazine and volume can conflict with already translated editions.

## Verify
- [ ] Page count matches signature count; ends on a hook.
- [ ] Every correction list item checked.
