# Beta flash and uni flash effects

## Beta flash (beta-furasshu)
A white burst shaped by hundreds of radial lines inked against a solid black ("beta" = solid fill).
Use: shock, revelation, dramatic entrance, a dark emotional moment.
1. Mark the centre and the outline of the white shape (oval or irregular).
2. Ink tapering lines from the black edge inward toward the centre, stopping at the white outline at
   varied lengths; lines must be closely packed and taper to points.
3. Fill the outer area solid black, letting it merge into the line bases.
4. Some artists ink with white over black instead (white flicks from the centre outward).

## Uni flash (uni-furasshu)
"Uni" = sea urchin. Radiating spiky lines around a white centre with *no* black background, forming a
soft-edged starburst. Use: shojo emotional emphasis, sparkle, a sudden thought, a character's aura.
1. Mark the centre and an inner circle that stays white.
2. Ink lines radiating outward from the inner circle edge, each tapering at the outer end, in varied
   lengths so the outer edge looks spiky.
3. Density: very tight near the inner edge; add a second layer of shorter lines for richness.

## Digital (CSP)
- Figure > Saturated line (focus line) tool with "draw on filled" option or reversed brush to make
  beta flash; set a polygon or ellipse reference shape, line count high (hundreds), jitter on.
- For uni flash, set the reference area to the inner circle with length randomisation, no fill.
- Asset store has flash brushes, check licence for commercial use.

## Gotchas
- Lines not converging on one centre look like a spill, not a flash.
- Uni flash behind text reduces legibility; put it behind the character, balloons outside it.

## Verify
- [ ] Taper points clean at print size; no fill gaps in the black of beta flash.
