# Manga chapter pacing and hooks

**Goal:** a chapter that grabs readers on its first pages and leaves them needing the
next one, within its page budget.

## Page budgets (typical; vary by magazine)
- Weekly serial chapters: often ~16-20 pages.
- Monthly: often ~30-50 pages.
- One-shots for contests/debuts: often ~31-45 pages, with a set count per contest.

## Structure
1. **Opening hook (pages 1-3)**: a striking image, question or action; for a first
   chapter, establish the protagonist's want quickly.
2. **Development**: alternate dialogue scenes and visual beats; manga allows more
   panels per moment (decompression) for emotion and action.
3. **Turn** in the last third: new information, a reversal.
4. **Final page hook**: a reveal or decision that sets up the next chapter. Place it
   so the reader turns the page into it (page-turn reveal, right-to-left).

## Manga-specific pacing tools
- Aspect-to-aspect sequences for atmosphere (McCloud notes their prevalence in manga).
- Large panels and spreads (mihiraki) for climaxes.
- Reaction panels and silent beats for emotion.
- Chapter title page and colour pages as ordered by the magazine.

## Gotchas
- Spending the first chapter on world explanation instead of character.
- Ending chapters at a resolution with no forward pull.

## Verify
- [ ] Hook on the first spread; hook on the last page.
- [ ] Page count matches the slot exactly.
- [ ] Climax beats get large panels or spreads.
