# Standard comic trim sizes

**Goal:** pick (or confirm) the trim before page one, so art never has to be cropped or
re-laid out.

## Common trims (approximate; always confirm with publisher/printer)
| Format | Trim | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| US comic (floppy) | ~6.625 x 10.25 in (168 x 260 mm) | modern standard; older books slightly larger |
| US trade paperback | often ~6.625 x 10.2 in (same as floppies) | collects issues without resizing |
| Manga tankobon (Japan) | B6 (128 x 182 mm) or similar | magazine serialisation often B5 |
| US manga / digest | ~5 x 7.5 in or 5.5 x 8.25 in | reduced from Japanese originals |
| European album (BD) | ~A4 (about 8.5 x 11.5 in / 216 x 292 mm) | hardcover, 46-64 pp typical |
| Graphic novel (trade) | 6 x 9 in, 7 x 10 in, 8 x 10 in | book-market trims; POD supports many |
| Zine/mini-comic | A5 or half-letter (5.5 x 8.5 in) | home/office printing |

## Sizing art
- Keep the art's aspect ratio equal to trim + bleed. Ratio for US comics about 1:1.55.
- Drawing at ~150% of print (e.g. US original art ~10 x 15 in image area) is standard
  practice; digital at print size, 600 dpi line art.
- If the book will be released in multiple formats (floppy then digest), design live
  areas for the smallest (keep text inside both safe areas).

## POD constraints
Print-on-demand services publish their supported trims and minimum/maximum page
counts; choose from their list.

## Verify
- [ ] Trim confirmed in writing before pencils.
- [ ] Art templates match trim + bleed proportion.
- [ ] Alternate formats considered for safe areas.
