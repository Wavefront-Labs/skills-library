# Costume design for comic characters

**Goal:** a costume that expresses the character, reads at small sizes and can be
drawn quickly and consistently for years.

## Principles
- Repeatability: count the details. Every stripe, pouch and rivet is drawn on every
  page. Cut what does not carry story.
- Big shapes first: cape, collar, boots, gloves define the silhouette.
- Colour blocking: 2-3 main colours with value contrast; complementary accents.
  Check in greyscale for black-and-white reprints.
- Emblem: simple enough to draw in 5 strokes, readable at 5 mm, works flipped
  only if symmetric (text and asymmetric logos break on mirrored panels).
- Contour lines: seams and colour breaks follow the anatomy (around the deltoid,
  down the lat) so they reinforce form.

## Character first
- Era, budget, profession: a street vigilante does not have tailored spandex.
- What the character would choose vs what the world gives them.
- Civilian wardrobe: 3-4 recurring outfits with a signature item.

## Deliverables
- Front/back costume turnaround on the model sheet.
- Flat emblem art; colour swatches with CMYK/RGB values for the colourist.

## Failure modes
- Detail creep from cover artists filtering back into interiors.
- Belts and straps that cross the waist at the wrong height between artists.

## Verify
- [ ] Costume readable as a 1-inch figure and in greyscale.
- [ ] Emblem drawable from memory in under 10 seconds.
