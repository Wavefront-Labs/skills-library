# Original art board sizes and markings

**Goal:** draw at a size that reduces cleanly to the printed page, with every boundary
marked.

## The US standard (common practice)
- Board: **11 x 17 in** Bristol (2-ply or 3-ply, smooth/plate for ink, vellum for
  pencil texture).
- Image area commonly about **10 x 15 in** (the live area within which panels sit)
  with bleed marks outside it. Many publishers supply pre-printed boards with
  non-photo-blue trim, bleed and live area lines.
- Printed US comic trim is about **6.625 x 10.25 in**, so original art is reduced
  roughly 65% (drawing about 150% of print size).

## The three boundaries
| Line | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Bleed | art for bleed panels extends to here; trimmed off |
| Trim | the final page edge after cutting |
| Live/safe area | keep all text and critical art inside |
Check the publisher's template; values vary by format (manga B4 manuscripts, European
albums, graphic novel trims).

## Reduction ratio
- Maintain the exact proportion of the trim + bleed when sizing art. Calculate:
  art height = art width x (print height / print width).
- Line weights shrink in print: lines under ~0.1 mm after reduction may drop out;
  test-print reduced.

## Digital equivalents
Set the canvas in print units at final size at 600 dpi (line art) or work at original
art size with the reduction in mind. CSP's New > Comic preset sets trim, bleed and
base (live) frame (see the CSP comic-file settings skill).

## Labelling
Write title, issue, page number and your name in the top margin outside the bleed
(non-repro or light pencil).

## Verify
- [ ] Board proportion matches trim + bleed.
- [ ] All three boundaries marked; text and key art inside live area.
- [ ] Margin labels on every page.
