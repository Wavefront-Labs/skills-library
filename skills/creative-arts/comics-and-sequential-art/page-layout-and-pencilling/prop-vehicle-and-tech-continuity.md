# Prop, vehicle and tech continuity

**Goal:** recurring objects look the same in every panel and sit correctly in
perspective.

## Prop sheets
- For each recurring prop (signature weapon, phone, car, ship): front, side, top,
  3/4, scale against a hand or figure, and detail callouts.
- Note which hand holds it and where it is carried when not in use.

## Drawing vehicles
1. Block the vehicle as a box in the panel's perspective (use the scene's vanishing
   points).
2. Cut the box into masses: hood, cabin, trunk; wheel arches at fixed positions.
3. Wheels: ellipses whose minor axis aligns with the axle; both wheels on a side
   share the axle direction and get rounder as they face the camera.
4. Add ground contact shadows; cars sit, they do not float.
- Use 3D models (SketchUp warehouse, CSP 3D) for complex machines; trace only for
  perspective, then redraw.

## Continuity tracking
- Script/continuity notes: where props were left, damage state, who has what.
- Damage persists: a dented car stays dented until repaired on-panel.

## Verify
- [ ] Prop matches its sheet in every panel.
- [ ] Wheel ellipses aligned with the axle and agree with eye level.
