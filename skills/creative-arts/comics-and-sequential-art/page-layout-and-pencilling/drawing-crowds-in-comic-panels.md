# Drawing crowds in comic panels

**Goal:** a crowd that reads as many distinct people, in perspective, without
drawing every one in full.

## Perspective scaling
- On level ground with eye level at standing height, every standing adult's head
  sits on the horizon line. Scale bodies down, keep heads on the line.
- For high or low eye levels, draw one reference figure and project heights to the
  vanishing point.

## Layered detail
1. Foreground: 2-4 fully drawn individuals, cropped by the panel edge.
2. Midground: simplified figures, varied silhouettes, partial overlap.
3. Background: head-and-shoulder shapes, then texture (ovals, hats, signs).
- Overlap people; gaps between figures look staged.

## Variety
- Heights, body types, ages, clothing silhouettes (hats, coats, bags, kids on
  shoulders).
- Different actions and gaze directions unless the story needs all eyes on one
  point; then add one or two exceptions for life.

## Shortcuts
- Spot blacks or drop to a single colour/tone for the back rows.
- CSP or 3D crowd assets for the background, redrawn and simplified.
- Panel choice: a low angle with a few big foreground heads implies a crowd.

## Verify
- [ ] Heads on the horizon line for level-ground crowds.
- [ ] No cloned figures visible.
