# Binding and page counts for comics

**Goal:** a page count and layout that suits the binding, so nothing is lost or
blank by accident.

## Saddle stitch (stapled; floppies, zines)
- Folded sheets nested and stapled at the spine.
- **Page count must be a multiple of 4** (including covers, if self-cover). A 24-page
  comic = 6 sheets; with separate cover = 24 interior + 4 cover pages.
- Practical upper limit around 48-64 interior pages depending on paper weight.
- **Creep**: inner sheets push outward so outer edges of centre pages get trimmed more;
  keep content away from outer edges (printers often compensate).
- Lies fairly flat; spreads in the centre lose almost nothing.

## Perfect binding (glued spine; trades, graphic novels)
- Pages glued at the spine; needs a minimum page count (often ~32-48 pages+, check
  printer) for the spine to hold.
- **Spine width** depends on page count and paper; printers provide a calculator or
  template for the cover.
- The gutter (inner margin) is partly hidden in the curve: keep text and faces out of
  ~0.5-0.75 in from the spine.
- Offset printing often works in **signatures** of 8, 16 or 32 pages; total counts in
  those multiples avoid blank pages. POD usually just needs an even number.

## Planning
- Count pages including front matter, ads, back matter.
- Fill odd remainders with sketchbook pages, pin-ups, or text pieces rather than blanks.
- Spreads across perfect-bound gutters need extra care (see spreads skill).

## Verify
- [ ] Page count valid for binding (multiple of 4 for saddle stitch; signature
  multiples or even for perfect bound per printer).
- [ ] Gutter-safe margins respected; spine width from printer template.
