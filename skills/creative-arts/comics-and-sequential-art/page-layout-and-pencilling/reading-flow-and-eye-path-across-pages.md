# Reading flow and eye path

**Goal:** readers move through panels and balloons in the intended order without
noticing the layout.

## Default path
Western: left to right within a tier, then down to the next tier's left (a "Z"). Manga:
right to left, top to bottom. Everything in the layout either supports the default or
must clearly override it.

## Layout rules
1. **Tiers read first**: keep horizontal gutters continuous across the page so the eye
   finishes a tier before dropping.
2. **Avoid stacked panels beside a tall panel** unless order is obvious; the eye may go
   down the stack or across. If you must, make the stack clearly a unit (the tall
   panel on the right, stack read first top-down).
3. **Don't align vertical gutters across tiers**; a continuous vertical gutter invites
   reading down a column.
4. **Balloons pull the eye**: place the first balloon top-left of each panel; let the
   last balloon sit near the right edge to lead into the next panel.
5. **Directional cues**: figures' gaze, motion and pointing lead toward the next panel;
   avoid characters facing "backwards" off the page at the end of a tier.

## Breaking the grid
- Diagonal or free layouts need a strong lead: overlapping panels in sequence, a
  figure breaking borders pointing onward, or numbered-feeling balloon chains.
- Test the page on someone who hasn't seen the script.

## Check before inks
- Draw arrows on the thumbnail through panels and balloons in order. Any arrow crossing
  another or backtracking signals a problem.

## Verify
- [ ] Arrow test has no crossings or backtracks.
- [ ] Fresh reader follows the intended order first time.
