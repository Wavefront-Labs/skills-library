# Photo reference and reference gathering

**Goal:** accurate places, objects and poses that still look drawn, sourced legally.

## Gathering
- Shoot your own: phone photos of locations, props, friends posing the script.
  Shoot several angles plus a wide establishing frame.
- Libraries: public-domain and CC0 sources (Wikimedia Commons, Library of Congress,
  Unsplash under its licence), Google Street View for location layout only.
- Organise by subject in folders or a tool like PureRef / Eagle; tag by script
  page so the next issue finds it.

## Using reference well
- Gesture first, reference second: draw your pose, then check anatomy and folds.
- Change the lens: photos have camera distortion; correct to the panel's
  perspective grid.
- Simplify: pick the 3-4 details that identify the object, drop the rest.
- Mix sources: one for pose, one for costume folds, one for lighting.

## Legal and ethical
- Do not trace or closely copy someone else's photograph; copied compositions have
  lost lawsuits and careers. Use for information, not composition.
- Celebrity likeness: reference is fine; using it as a commercial character may need
  rights.
- Credit photographers when a panel is close to a specific image.

## Verify
- [ ] No panel is a recognisable trace of a third-party photo.
- [ ] Reference folder saved with the issue files.
