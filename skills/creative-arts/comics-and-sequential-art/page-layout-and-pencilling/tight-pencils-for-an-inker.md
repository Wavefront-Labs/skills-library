# Tight pencils for an inker

**Goal:** pencils where every final line decision is visible, so the inker interprets
quality, not content.

## Tight vs loose
- **Tight pencils**: one chosen line per contour, textures indicated, blacks marked.
  The inker refines line quality.
- **Loose pencils/layouts**: inker (or finisher) draws much of the detail. Agree which
  you're delivering; rates often differ.

## Conventions
- **Solid black areas**: mark with an X (or fill lightly with tone). Fully filling with
  graphite smudges and scans poorly.
- **Line choice**: erase construction and alternate lines, or keep them visibly lighter
  than final lines.
- **Line-weight hints**: draw heavier where you want weight (shadow side, foreground)
  so the inker sees intent.
- **Textures**: indicate a small patch of hatching/feathering and note "texture: stone
  continues"; no need to draw every stroke if agreed.
- **Notes**: in margins, non-repro blue or light colour, e.g. "no holding line on
  clouds", "keep this light".
- **Panel borders**: drawn or indicated; agree who inks borders.

## Tools
- Traditional: 2H-HB lead for construction, HB-2B for final (soft leads smudge; fix
  with workable fixative if needed); non-photo blue for roughs.
- Digital: final pencils on their own layer, construction hidden; deliver at 600 dpi
  greyscale or the agreed spec. Blue-line printouts if the inker works traditionally.

## Gotchas
- Two competing lines on a contour: the inker picks the wrong one.
- Smudged graphite reads as tone; clean with a kneaded eraser before scanning.

## Verify
- [ ] Every contour has one clear final line.
- [ ] All blacks marked; textures indicated; notes legible.
