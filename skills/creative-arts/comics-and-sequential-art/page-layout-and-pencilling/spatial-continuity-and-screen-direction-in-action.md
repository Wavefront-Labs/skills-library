# Spatial continuity and screen direction in action

**Goal:** readers never get lost in a busy action scene.

## Tools
1. **Floor plan**: top-down sketch of the location with characters' positions and
   movement paths per beat. Draw cameras on it for each panel.
2. **The 180-degree line**: the line between two opponents (or along a direction of
   travel). Keep cameras on one side so left/right relations hold.
3. **Matching exits/entrances**: a character exits right in one panel, enters left in
   the next.
4. **Anchors**: a recognisable landmark (red door, pillar) appearing in several panels
   ties shots together.
5. **Damage and prop continuity**: broken window stays broken; the dropped gun stays on
   the floor; torn sleeve stays torn. Note them as you go.

## Crossing the line safely
- Show the move: a character walks around the other in-panel.
- A neutral shot on the line (head-on or overhead) as a bridge.
- A cutaway then re-establish with a wide shot.

## Multi-character scenes
Define a line per pair in each exchange; re-establish with a wide shot when the focus
switches pairs.

## Verify
- [ ] Floor plan exists for complex scenes; cameras plotted.
- [ ] No unmotivated left/right flips.
- [ ] Damage/prop states consistent panel to panel.
