# Fight choreography across panels

**Goal:** a fight a reader can follow blow by blow, that also tells a story (someone
gains, loses, changes).

## 1. Fight beat sheet
Before drawing, write 6-15 beats:
```
1 Establish: warehouse, crates, Mara vs two thugs, exit behind thugs
2 Thug A swings pipe - Mara ducks
3 Mara drives elbow into A's ribs (impact)
4 B grabs her from behind (reversal)
5 She uses crate to flip B (environment)
6 A recovers, knife out (stakes up)
7 Mara retreats, cornered (low point)
8 Pulls the fire alarm - sprinklers - escape (clever win)
```
Each fight needs **turns**: advantage shifts, stakes rise, environment used.

## 2. Geography
- Open with a wide panel establishing positions and the space.
- Keep screen direction: hero attacks left-to-right (forward reading direction) when
  winning; being driven right-to-left reads as losing ground.
- Re-establish with a wide shot after every 3-5 close panels.

## 3. Choose key poses
For each beat pick the clearest moment: anticipation (wind-up) or impact, rarely
the middle. Show **cause and effect** in sequence: hit, then reaction.

## 4. Page budget
A short scuffle: 1 page. A significant fight: 3-6 pages. Climactic: up to 10+. Use
panel size for weight: big panel for the decisive blow.

## Gotchas
- Fights with no story: two equals trading blows until one falls. Bored readers.
- Missing impact: cutting from wind-up to aftermath loses the hit.
- Too many tilted panels; clarity suffers.

## Verify
- [ ] Beat sheet with at least two turns.
- [ ] Geography established and re-established.
- [ ] A reader can narrate each blow and its result.
