# Crossfire dialogue

**Goal:** fast back-and-forth with a clear rhythm and jokes in each line.

## Principles
- Short lines, usually one or two sentences each.
- Each speaker has a distinct vocabulary and speed.
- Lines respond directly to the previous line, often by twisting its words.
- The figure interrupts or cuts off the performer to build momentum.

## Writing
1. Define the conflict: who wants what.
2. Write a list of jokes the figure can make about the performer.
3. Arrange as an exchange with escalation.
4. Put the punch word at the end of lines; see `tightening-a-joke-punch-word-last-and-word-economy`.
5. Avoid labial-heavy punch words where lip control is shaky.

## Rhythm
- Vary the length of lines so it does not become mechanical.
- Use pauses for reaction.

## Performance
- Switch voices clearly and use focus to show who is speaking; see `head-and-eye-controls-posture-and-life-between-lines`.

## Gotchas
- Dialogue where both characters are equally clever; keep contrast.
- Overlong exchanges on one joke.

## Verify
- [ ] Read aloud, the pace holds and each line builds on the last.
