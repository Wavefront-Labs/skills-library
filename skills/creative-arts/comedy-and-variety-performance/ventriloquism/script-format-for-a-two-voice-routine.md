# Script format for two voices

**Goal:** a script you can rehearse and revise easily.

## Layout
- Two columns or alternating lines labelled with the performer and the figure.
- Stage directions in brackets: [figure looks at audience], [turns head], [laughs].
- Mark the laughs and pauses: [pause], [hold for laugh].

## Marking problem sounds
- Underline or highlight labials (b, p, m, f, v, w) in the figure's lines and mark the substitution you plan, so you can see rehearsal risk.
- Rewrite lines with heavy clusters of labials where possible.

## Timing marks
- Note estimated runtimes per section.
- Mark moments for movement and for the transitions.

## Revision
- Keep dated versions.
- After each performance, mark which jokes worked and the actual timing.

## Gotchas
- Scripts that are not marked with the figure's actions.
- Writing the figure's lines as if for an actor, ignoring lip control.

## Verify
- [ ] The script includes directions for eye and head moves and highlights difficult sounds.
