# Vocal health for ventriloquists

**Goal:** sustain a figure voice without damaging your own.

## Habits
- Stay hydrated with water; avoid heavy caffeine and alcohol before performing.
- Warm up gently with humming and easy sirens.
- Keep the figure's voice at a pitch and effort that does not feel strained; a voice that sits in a comfortable placement lasts longer than one pushed high or low.
- Take breaks in long practice sessions.
- Rest your voice after long shows or days.

## Warning signs
- Hoarseness lasting more than a couple of weeks, pain, or loss of range should be checked by a doctor or voice specialist. I am not giving medical advice.

## Environment
- Dry air, smoke and loud rooms strain the voice; use a mic.
- Use a mic rather than projecting loudly.

## Technique
- Use support from the breath; see `daily-drills-and-breath-support-for-ventriloquists`.
- Avoid whispering and shouting for long periods.

## Gotchas
- A new voice that "feels fine" for ten minutes but is damaging at an hour.

## Verify
- [ ] You can speak normally after an hour of practice and are not hoarse the next day.
