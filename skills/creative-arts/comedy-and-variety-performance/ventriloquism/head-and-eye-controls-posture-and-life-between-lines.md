# Head and eye controls, posture and life between lines

**Goal:** a figure that looks like it is thinking.

## Controls
- Many figures have a head stick or a handle with levers for the mouth, eyes and sometimes eyebrows. Learn which lever does what and practise each separately.
- Move the head smoothly; avoid jerky movements.
- Use the eyes to direct attention: look at the audience, at you, at the person being talked about.

## Holding the figure
- Support its weight with a comfortable posture: elbow close to the body, forearm supported where possible.
- Keep the figure at chest or shoulder height so the audience sees its face and yours.
- Stand square to the audience with the figure at your side.
- Change hands only when needed, using a clean move.

## Life between lines
- Small movements while you or others talk: a head turn, a blink, a nod.
- React to the audience's laughter.
- Avoid constant movement; stillness is also alive.

## Practice
- Film the figure with the sound off; check whether it looks aware.

## Gotchas
- A figure that stares dead ahead.
- A tired arm that makes the figure droop; build strength.

## Verify
- [ ] With the sound off, the figure still seems to react and listen.
