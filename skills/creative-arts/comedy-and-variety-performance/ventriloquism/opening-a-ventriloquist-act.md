# Opening with the figure

**Goal:** establish figure, voice and relationship in the first minute.

## Entrance options
- The figure is already on stage in a case or on a chair; the performer brings it to life.
- The performer enters carrying the figure and sets it up.
- A short prop-based entrance (a box, a trunk) that leads into a first line.

## First moments
1. Establish the performer's status briefly.
2. Give the figure a strong first line that shows its character.
3. Let the audience see the figure's lively reaction.
4. Win an early laugh at the figure's cheek; see `crossfire-dialogue`.

## Voice
- Make the figure's voice distinct immediately; the audience should believe two characters.

## Practicalities
- Check the mic: one mic for both voices; keep a steady distance.
- Check that the figure and the stool are safe.

## Gotchas
- A slow entrance that needs a lot of setup.
- Hiding the figure's face with your body.

## Verify
- [ ] The audience laughs at the figure within the first 30 seconds.
