# Muffled and telephone voice

**Goal:** make the figure's voice sound as if it comes from a container or a phone.

## Concept
Classic ventriloquism texts describe throwing the voice as a mix of changing the sound's quality (making it small, muffled or distant) and directing the audience's attention to the source. You are making a voice that sounds muffled, and acting as though it is coming from elsewhere.

## Muffled voice
- Lower the volume and speak with a more constricted, darker sound, with the tongue pulled back in the mouth and the sound kept in the back of the throat. Teachers describe the technique differently; follow your teacher's approach and test what sounds convincing.
- Keep it comfortable; stop if it strains the throat.

## Telephone voice
- A thinner, higher, nasal quality, as if filtered through a small speaker. Place the sound further forward in the nose and reduce the low frequencies.
- Hold a phone to your ear to create the illusion and respond to the voice.

## Illusion
- Look at the box or the phone, react to what you hear, and place it near your hand.
- Use a prop to help: a box, trunk, a door, a phone.

## Practice
- Record and check that the quality changes without visible lip movement.

## Gotchas
- A muffled voice that is unintelligible; clarity is the priority.

## Verify
- [ ] Listeners understand the words and believe the source.
