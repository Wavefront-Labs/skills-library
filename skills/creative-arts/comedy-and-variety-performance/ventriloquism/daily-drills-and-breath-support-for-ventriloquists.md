# Daily drills and breath support

**Goal:** make lip control automatic and the figure voice easy to sustain.

## Breath
- Breathe low, from the diaphragm, with relaxed shoulders. A tense chest narrows the voice.
- Speak in short phrases at first; take a quiet breath between phrases.
- Practise breathing out slowly while holding the figure's pitch.

## A 15-minute routine (adjust to your needs)
1. **Warm-up (2 min)**: relax jaw and lips; hum.
2. **Vowels and easy consonants (3 min)**: say words without labials in a mirror, with a steady, slightly smiling mouth.
3. **Substitute drills (5 min)**: words and phrases with b, p, m, f, v and w using your chosen substitutions.
4. **Phrases and script (5 min)**: read a routine aloud in the figure's voice.

## Feedback
- Use a mirror and record video at close range.
- Review weekly for visible lip movement.

## Progression
- Start slow; increase speed and then add the figure's movement.
- Practise while moving and while holding the figure.

## Gotchas
- Practising only when tired.
- Pushing the voice; stop if it hurts.

## Verify
- [ ] Practice done most days and video shows no lip movement on the drill phrases.
