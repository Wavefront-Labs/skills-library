# Vent for children's audiences

**Goal:** an act that delights young audiences and respects their parents.

## Content
- Simple storylines, silly characters, and clear jokes.
- Avoid scary or adult themes.
- Songs and call-and-response work.

## Pace
- Short bits; vary activity every few minutes.
- Use big gestures and a clear voice.

## Interaction
- Invite children to answer the figure's questions.
- Keep volunteers on stage safe and supervised; get permission from adults.
- Keep the figure approachable; some children are shy or afraid of figures.

## Safety
- Keep the figure and equipment out of reach of children.
- Check venue child-protection policies and background-check requirements where required by law or the venue.

## Practicalities
- Check the room's acoustics: children are noisy.
- Keep the act's length appropriate to the age group.

## Gotchas
- Too much talk.
- Jokes aimed over their heads.

## Verify
- [ ] Children are engaged throughout and adults are comfortable.
