# Volunteer and human-dummy routines

**Goal:** an audience participation bit where the volunteer is a willing partner.

## Choosing a volunteer
- Pick someone who volunteers and seems relaxed; avoid anyone with signs of discomfort or alcohol impairment.
- Children need adult consent; check venue rules.

## The human-dummy bit (a standard in variety)
- A volunteer stands in place of the figure, and the performer speaks for them, creating a comic exchange, usually in a hat or with a prop. Agree on basic actions in advance.

## Process
1. Prepare a script with simple tasks for the volunteer.
2. Give clear, kind instructions.
3. Control the pace: keep it short.
4. Thank them; applause.

## Safety and care
- Avoid humiliation; make the volunteer look good.
- Do not touch them unless they agree.
- Give them an easy exit.

## Gotchas
- A volunteer who tries to take over; prepare lines to retake the stage.
- A bit that depends on the volunteer's cleverness.

## Verify
- [ ] The volunteer smiles at the end and the room is warm.
