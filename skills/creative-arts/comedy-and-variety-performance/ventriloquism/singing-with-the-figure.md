# Singing with the figure

**Goal:** a song where the figure sings or you duet with it.

## Song choice
- Pick songs with few labials in key lines or rewrite lyrics to avoid them.
- Simple melodies work best; a comic song suits the figure's character.

## Technique
- Use your substitutions as in speech, taking care to keep the vowel sounds clear.
- Hold sustained vowels with relaxed lips and jaw; the vowels are easy.
- Plan breaths so the figure does not appear to breathe while you sing.
- Keep the figure's singing voice distinct from yours.

## Duets
- Rehearse the handoff between voices.
- Keep the figure moving to the beat.

## Staging
- Check the mic setup so both voices are heard.

## Practice
- Film at close range to check lip control.

## Gotchas
- A song that needs the figure to hit a high note you cannot sustain.

## Verify
- [ ] On video, no visible lip movement during the figure's sung lines.
