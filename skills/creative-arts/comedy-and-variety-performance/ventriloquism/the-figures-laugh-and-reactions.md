# The figure's laugh and reactions

**Goal:** a distinctive laugh and reactions that make the figure feel alive.

## Designing
- Choose a laugh that suits the character: a cackle, a snort, a giggle. It should be in the figure's voice placement and different from your own.
- Prefer sounds without labials (the "ha" and "hee" sounds work without the lips).
- Practise the laugh alone until you can produce it without lip movement.

## Moves
- Pair the laugh with movement: the head tilts back, the mouth opens, and the body shakes.
- Use quick small reactions (gasp, "uh-oh", sigh) timed with the figure's expression.

## Comedy use
- Let the figure react to your lines to show opinion.
- Hold a reaction for a beat to get a laugh.
- Use the laugh as a button or a recurring gag, but avoid overuse.

## Gotchas
- A laugh that tires the throat.
- A laugh at the wrong moment that steps on the audience's.

## Verify
- [ ] The laugh is distinct, lip-still on video, and comfortable to repeat.
