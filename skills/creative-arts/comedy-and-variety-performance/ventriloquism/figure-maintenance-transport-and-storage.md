# Figure maintenance, transport and storage

**Goal:** a figure that works on every show and arrives in one piece.

## Maintenance
- Check cords, strings and springs for wear before each show; carry spares if the maker advises.
- Keep mechanisms clean and dry; lubricate sparingly with a lubricant the maker recommends.
- Check screws and joints.
- Keep paint and surfaces away from sunlight and moisture; clean gently.
- Dress the figure in clean costumes and wash them when needed.
- For any repair beyond simple adjustments, consult the maker or a specialist.

## Transport
- Use a padded case, ideally one made for the figure, supporting the head and body; remove any parts that could break.
- Never leave the figure in a hot car.
- Take it as carry-on when flying if allowed.

## Storage
- Store in a case in a stable environment: avoid damp, heat and sudden changes.
- Support the figure's weight so seams do not stretch.

## Gotchas
- Leaving the figure leaning on a stand for months.
- A broken string right before a show.

## Verify
- [ ] A checklist of pre-show checks exists and spare parts are packed.
