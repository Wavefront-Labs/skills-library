# Riffing and improvised lines

**Goal:** make off-script lines land and get back to the set without a seam.

## Habits that build the skill
1. Practise saying the first thing you notice aloud, in rehearsal and open spots.
2. Use improv basics (accept what the audience gives you, then heighten it); see `finding-and-heightening-the-game-of-the-sketch` for the language of heightening.
3. Keep a bank of reusable lines and templates ("That's the face of someone who ..." ) that fit many situations.
4. Use observations about the room: the venue, a sound, an interruption.

## Mechanics
- Repeat what was said so the room hears it; then add attitude.
- Heighten once or twice, then stop. Two good lines beat five.
- Return to material with a bridge: see `bridging-from-crowd-work-to-material` in `delivery-crowd-work-and-heckler-handling`.

## Safety
- Riff at the situation, not at the vulnerability of the person.
- If a riff dies, name it lightly and move on.

## Gotchas
- Riffing to avoid doing the set.
- Telling the audience a prepared line is spontaneous when it is repeated at every show.

## Verify
- [ ] Recordings show off-script moments return to material in under 30 seconds.
