# Discovering your stage persona

**Goal:** an on-stage version of you whose point of view generates jokes and is easy to sustain.

## Method
1. List 20 real traits: habits, fears, opinions, contradictions, roles (parent, nurse, immigrant).
2. Mark which ones make friends laugh. Real contradictions (anxious but bossy) are strongest.
3. Choose 2-3 to amplify and keep the rest private.
4. Choose a stance toward the audience: confiding, grumpy, bewildered, superior-but-failing. Link to `comic-status` and `the-non-hero-stance`.
5. Write premises from that stance; see `mining-premises-with-attitude-words-and-topic-lists`.

## Test
- Record a set; ask a stranger to describe the person on stage. If their description matches your target, it reads.
- Notice when delivery feels forced: that is usually a persona mismatch.

## Evolve
Personas change with life and confidence. Update when the old one stops feeling honest, and keep earlier material only if it still fits.

## Gotchas
- Copying a famous comic's stance.
- Choosing a persona that requires constant acting; it is tiring and inconsistent.
