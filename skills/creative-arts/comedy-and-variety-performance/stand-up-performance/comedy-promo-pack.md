# Promo pack, tape and headshots

**Goal:** a package a booker can judge in two minutes.

## Contents
1. **Clip**: a recording of a live set, clean audio, strong laughs, and usually a few minutes long. Keep it recent. Start with your best material.
2. **Bio**: 100-150 words in the third person; credits (venues, festivals, shows) and a hook. Only true credits.
3. **Headshot**: sharp, well lit, consistent with your persona; one or two images.
4. **Quotes**: only genuine ones, with source.
5. **Contact and links**: one page or email-ready PDF.

## Recording the clip
- Record at a well-attended gig with good sound.
- Use a stable camera and clean audio; see `taping-a-special-or-comedy-album`.
- Edit lightly; keep the laughs.

## Use
- Send a link rather than large attachments.
- Create a version for different bookings: club, corporate, festival.

## Maintenance
- Update after each major credit.
- Remove dated clips.

## Gotchas
- A clip filmed at a poor gig.
- Padding the bio with unverifiable claims.

## Verify
- [ ] A stranger can learn who you are, what you do and how to book you within two minutes.
