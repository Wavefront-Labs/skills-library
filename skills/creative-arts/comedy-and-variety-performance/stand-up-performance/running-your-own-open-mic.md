# Running your own open mic

**Goal:** a regular night where new comics can get time and an audience turns up.

## Set up
1. Find a venue that wants a night and can provide a stage, a mic and a PA. Agree day, time, deal (bar share, fee, or none) in writing.
2. Decide format: number of acts, set length (often 5 minutes), breaks, running order method.
3. Make a sign-up method (list at the door, online).
4. Check the venue's licensing and insurance requirements.

## Running the night
- Host the show yourself or recruit a reliable host; see `warming-a-room-and-running-a-live-show-as-mc`.
- State house rules at the start (listening, phones).
- Keep to time; use a light.
- Invite audience; promote each month.

## Care
- Keep the room safe; remove anyone who harasses acts.
- Treat every act with respect.

## Sustain
- Track attendance; ask performers for feedback.
- Have a guest spot of an established comic from time to time.

## Gotchas
- A night with more acts than audience.
- Not paying attention to the venue relationship.

## Verify
- [ ] Written venue agreement and a published schedule.
