# Getting venue staff to act on a heckler

**Goal:** make staff intervention a planned step, not an improvisation.

## Before the show
1. Ask the promoter or door manager who is responsible for removing audience members.
2. Agree a signal: a nod to the door staff, a phrase, or a gesture to the host.
3. Find out how much warning they need and whether a warning card is used.
4. Learn the venue's policy: some give a warning first, some remove immediately.

## During the set
- Make eye contact with staff and give the agreed signal.
- Tell the room briefly what is happening ("We'll sort this out; let's get on") to keep control.
- Avoid pointing the mic at the heckler during removal; let staff do it.
- Pick up the show with a prepared line about the interruption.

## After
- Debrief with the promoter; log what happened for the booker.
- If staff did not respond, tell the promoter afterwards, calmly.

## Gotchas
- Waiting too long: the room's good will drains.
- Using an unplanned signal that staff do not notice.

## Verify
- [ ] The signal was agreed and staff knew their part.
