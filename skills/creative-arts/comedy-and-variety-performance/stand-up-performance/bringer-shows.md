# Bringer shows

**Goal:** judge whether a bringer is worth your time.

## What it is
A show where performers must bring a number of paying guests to get a slot. It is a model used in some cities' comedy scenes; terms vary by venue, so ask for exact conditions.

## Considerations
- It can provide stage time where open spots are scarce.
- It places the cost on performers and their friends; audiences may be less comedy-literate.
- Some comics and promoters regard bringer shows as exploitative; others use them as one tool among several.

## Using them well
1. Ask how many guests are required, ticket price, drink minimums and how slots are assigned.
2. Bring friends who will enjoy comedy, not just obligation.
3. Treat the set as material development.
4. Do not pay for slots that offer no recording or length.

## Alternatives
Free open mics, running your own; see `running-your-own-open-mic`.

## Gotchas
- Spending more than the experience is worth.
