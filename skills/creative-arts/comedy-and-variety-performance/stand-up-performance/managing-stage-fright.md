# Managing stage fright

**Goal:** perform with the nerves you have.

## Before
- Know your first minute cold: the opening line and first two bits.
- Warm up your voice and body: gentle stretches, humming, walking.
- Eat lightly and hydrate. Limit caffeine and alcohol; alcohol dulls timing.
- Arrive early to learn the room.

## Techniques
- Slow breathing: in through the nose for a few seconds, out longer through the mouth.
- Name the feeling as excitement; physiological arousal is similar.
- Visualise the first minute going well.
- Focus on the audience and on the joke, not on yourself.

## On stage
- Pause and breathe before the first line.
- Slow your pace; nerves speed it up.
- Accept that a shaky voice usually settles in a minute or two.

## Long-term
- More stage time is the biggest cure; see `finding-and-logging-stage-time`.
- If anxiety is severe or disabling, a health professional can help; I am not giving medical advice.

## Gotchas
- Using alcohol to cope.
- Avoiding gigs after a bad one.
