# Bookers, avails and follow-ups

**Goal:** be a performer bookers can use and remember.

## First contact
- Introduce yourself in a short, professional email: who you are, link to a clip, where you have played, and what you are asking for.
- Do not pitch to a booker while they are working the room.

## Avails
- "Avails" are the dates you are free for work. Share a clear list, update it, and answer quickly.
- Say your travel range and conditions.

## Following up
- A polite follow-up after a reasonable gap (a week or two) is acceptable; two or three is the limit.
- After a gig, thank the booker and ask for feedback if the relationship allows.

## Professional habits
- Arrive on time; keep to the set length.
- Confirm details in writing; see `booking-acts-and-performer-contracts` for the paperwork.
- Never complain about a booker publicly.

## Gotchas
- Sending a long email with attachments; put a link.
