# Abandoning planned material mid-set

**Goal:** change course without panic.

## When to drop
- The room has not laughed in the last few jokes and the next bit needs a quiet, attentive audience.
- A topic is wrong for the room (a recent local event, someone in the audience affected).
- The previous act or the host used a similar subject.

## Prepare
1. Keep a short swap list: three bits that work in any room.
2. Know your time; a clean shorter set beats a long bad one.
3. Know your exits: a line that ends the current bit.

## Do it
- Finish the current joke, not mid-sentence.
- Move to the swap without comment, or with a light acknowledgement if the room knows.
- Avoid saying "I was going to do something else"; it flags the failure.

## After
- Log what you dropped and why; see `post-set-review`.

## Gotchas
- Dropping the closer because you are rattled; a strong closer ends the set.

## Verify
- [ ] Swap list written and rehearsed.
