# Rhythm, pace and rate of speech

**Goal:** a rhythm that suits each bit and that you can control under pressure.

## Principles
- Setups run at a conversational pace; punch lines hit with a slight slowing and clear emphasis.
- Tag runs speed up as they build; stories vary pace between description and incident.
- Silence is a tool: a pause before a punch word creates anticipation.

## Procedure
1. Time a 2-minute bit with a recording; count the words per minute for setup and punch.
2. Mark the points where the pace should change.
3. Practise with a metronome of your own: speak a line to a steady beat, then break it for the punch.
4. Write rhythm into the words: short words and hard consonants at the end of a punchline, longer phrasing in setups.

## Fixing a rush
- Breathe before your first line and at every chunk break.
- Plant your feet and slow the first sentence deliberately.
- Plan intentional pauses, so slowing feels like a choice, not a drag.

## Gotchas
- Speaking slowly the whole set; the audience reads it as low energy.
- Over-annunciating on every word.
