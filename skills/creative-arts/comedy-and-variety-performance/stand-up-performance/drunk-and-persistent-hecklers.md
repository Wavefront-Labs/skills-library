# Drunk and persistent hecklers

**Goal:** protect the show and everyone in the room, including the heckler.

## Assess
- Is this a friendly loud person or a hostile one? Drunk people often do not intend harm.
- Are other audience members becoming uncomfortable? If so, act sooner.

## Escalation
1. A light acknowledgement that shows you heard them.
2. A clear but friendly boundary: "You can talk after the show; the show is happening here."
3. If they continue, tell them calmly and firmly that they will have to be quiet.
4. Signal the venue staff (see `getting-venue-staff-to-act-on-a-heckler`).
Do not trade insults; the aim is to end the interruption.

## Mechanics
- Hold the mic; you control the room.
- Avoid long exchanges; each reply feeds them.
- Engage their group or friends gently: friends often quiet them.
- Move on to the next chunk quickly after the issue is solved.

## Safety
- Do not approach or touch them.
- If they become threatening, stop the show and let staff handle it.
- After the event, check with the venue.

## Gotchas
- Humiliating a drunk person who is clearly unwell: the room will turn on you.

## Verify
- [ ] The interruption ended without physical contact and the show resumed.
