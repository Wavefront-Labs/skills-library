# Theatre and big-room gigs

**Goal:** make a large room feel intimate and make every seat laugh.

## Physical
- Use more of the stage: broader gestures and walks, but still anchor on stillness for punches.
- Check sightlines: the back and the balcony see small details less; make faces readable.
- Do a proper sound check, including where the monitors are.

## Delivery
- Laughs take longer to move through a big room; give them more time before continuing.
- Slow slightly and enunciate; the audience hears you late via speakers.
- Pause for the second wave of laughter from the back.

## Material
- Material built on small observations works in small rooms; adapt with bigger images and stronger premises.
- Crowd work is harder: repeat what people say so everyone can hear.
- A longer show needs more shape; see `hour-structure-and-the-emotional-turn`.

## Practical
- Arrive early to learn the stage and entrances.
- Consider a house light up for crowd work.

## Gotchas
- Playing to the first few rows.

## Verify
- [ ] Laughs reach the back; check by asking the sound operator.
