# Road gigs and touring

**Goal:** make a tour pay and keep you well.

## Planning
- Start with a base: bookers, local promoters, and friends in each city.
- Route to minimise driving; cluster dates by region.
- Costs: transport, accommodation, food, insurance. Compare to fees; see `show-budget-and-door-split`.

## Agreements
- Written confirmation per date: fee, set length, travel/accommodation provided, technical needs, and cancellation terms; see `booking-acts-and-performer-contracts`.

## On the road
- Arrive early to avoid late-travel risk.
- Check the venue, the sound system, the host; see `international-and-second-language-crowds` for crowd adaptation.
- Keep a healthy routine: sleep, eating, exercise.
- Log each venue and booker.

## Material
- Adapt to local context; a local line wins the room.
- Keep the core set constant; test new material at smaller gigs.

## Gotchas
- Booking a date with no contract or deposit.
- Underestimating fatigue.
