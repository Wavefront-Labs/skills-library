# Late shows and stag crowds

**Goal:** control a noisy, drunk audience while giving the rest of the room a good show.

## Assess
- Check who is in before you go on: the size and mood of the party.
- Speak with the host about any problem tables.

## Approach
- Take control early with a strong, confident opening.
- Use faster, punchier jokes; avoid long stories.
- Include the party in the first few minutes: a friendly question, a toast.
- Do not tolerate disruption; use `drunk-and-persistent-hecklers` steps.

## Material
- Work from your strongest set; hold back experimental material.
- Keep the content within the venue's rules.

## Exit
- End when the energy peaks; late crowds can turn.
- Leave through the agreed route; do not stay around if there is trouble.

## Gotchas
- Letting the party take over the room.
- Insulting the party in a way that turns them against the show.

## Verify
- [ ] Opening gets control within a minute; set ends on a high.
