# Working the front row safely

**Goal:** use the nearest people for warmth without making them miserable.

## Choosing people
- Look for smiling, open faces. Avoid anyone who looks at their phone, avoids eye contact or turns away.
- Couples and groups are easier than lone people.
- Do not pick someone who is visibly vulnerable or in distress.

## Approach
1. Start with a simple question or comment, not a judgement.
2. Keep the exchange short unless they give you gold.
3. Treat them as an equal; you are in a partnership.
4. If they decline, say a quick light line and move on.

## Avoid
- Touching, pulling people on stage, or standing over them.
- Targeting the quiet person with a "joke" about their body, appearance or relationship.
- Asking people to stand or speak if they are with someone who may be harmed by attention.

## Aftercare
- Thank them; the room notices.
- If a bit goes wrong with a front-row person, resolve it with a small kindness and move on.

## Gotchas
- Singling out someone with a disability, or someone with a small child, as the source of comedy.

## Verify
- [ ] The person is smiling at the end of the exchange and the room is with you.
