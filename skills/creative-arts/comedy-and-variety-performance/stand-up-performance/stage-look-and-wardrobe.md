# Stage look and wardrobe

**Goal:** a look that tells the audience who you are before you speak, without distracting from the jokes.

## Principles
- The outfit is part of the persona. A rumpled jacket, a bold shirt, a suit: each says something. Choose a look consistent with your attitude.
- Dress one step above the audience in a club, unless the persona requires otherwise.
- Colour: strong solid colours read from the back of the room; busy patterns can look chaotic under stage light or on video. Stage lights bleach very pale or reflective fabrics.
- Avoid logos and slogans that compete with you.

## Practical
1. Pockets: you need one for a phone or setlist; keep it quiet.
2. Mic pack: if you wear a body mic, check where the pack sits (belt or waistband) and avoid noisy fabrics near the lapel.
3. Fit: choose clothes you can move in; check sitting on a stool.
4. Shoes: quiet on the stage floor.
5. Keep a spare shirt; a sweated shirt shows on stage.

## Gotchas
- Hair or jewellery that clicks on the mic.
- Dressing for the photo rather than the room.

- [ ] You look the same on video as you intend; the mic pack and cable have a plan.
