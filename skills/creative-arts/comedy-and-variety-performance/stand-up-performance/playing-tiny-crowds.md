# Playing tiny crowds

**Goal:** make a small audience feel like a good one.

## Adjust
- Acknowledge the room light-heartedly in the first line; pretending otherwise looks odd.
- Ask them to move closer together if the host or venue allows; spread audiences dilute laughter.
- Lower your volume and move to conversational delivery.
- Use crowd work more; small crowds love being included.

## Material
- Choose conversational, observational chunks over big act-outs.
- Keep jokes tight; fewer people laugh fewer times, so the silence is more obvious.
- Shorten the set if the room is quiet; the venue may thank you.

## Mindset
- Treat the people who came as the audience you want.
- Do not blame them.

## Gotchas
- Overplaying energy to fill the room; it can be hard to bear.
- Running a full slot for four people with no break.

## Verify
- [ ] Audience smiling by the first minute; set shortened if dying.
