# Open-mic etiquette

**Goal:** be a performer other comics and hosts want back.

## Before
- Arrive early and sign up per the host's system. Ask the host how long the set is.
- Bring a set list and a pen; phones in your pocket.

## During other sets
- Watch and listen; laugh when it is funny. Do not talk at the back of the room.
- Never heckle another act; do not leave in the middle of someone's set.

## Your set
- Respect the time limit; finish on the light.
- Do not run notes aloud on stage ("this is new") except where the room expects it.
- Be clean in the host's terms for the room.

## After
- Thank the host.
- Buy a drink if the venue expects it; open mics rely on bar sales.
- Do not pitch your show to other comics immediately after their set.

## Gotchas
- Stealing others' material; your set should be your own.
- Leaving right after your set; the room notices.

## Verify
- [ ] You finished on time and stayed to support others.
