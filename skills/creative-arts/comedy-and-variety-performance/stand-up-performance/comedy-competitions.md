# Comedy competitions

**Goal:** use competitions for exposure and stage time.

## Before
- Read the rules: set length, content limits, entry fees, judging criteria, and how the winner is chosen.
- Check the prize terms and any rights the organiser asks for.

## Preparing
1. Build a set to the length with your strongest chunks; see `building-a-set-to-5-10-20-and-40-minutes`.
2. Include a strong opener and closer; see `choosing-the-opening-line-and-first-laugh`.
3. Rehearse it to the second; going over may disqualify you.
4. Test the set in several rooms.

## On the day
- Find out the order and the audience mix (judges, public vote).
- Warm up; stay calm.
- Support the others.

## After
- Accept results; take feedback.
- Use the experience in your promo pack; see `comedy-promo-pack`.

## Gotchas
- Changing your set drastically before a final.
- Arguing with results.

## Verify
- [ ] Set timed under the limit and rehearsed in two or more rooms.
