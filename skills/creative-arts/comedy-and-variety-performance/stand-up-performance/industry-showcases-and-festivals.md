# Industry showcases and festivals

**Goal:** a short, strong set in front of people who book.

## Know the format
- Showcases give a few minutes to many comics before bookers, agents or producers. Ask the organiser who is attending and the slot length.
- Festivals vary: some have industry events, some are public only.

## The set
1. Choose the strongest material you can deliver in the time.
2. Start with your best premise and persona; there is no time to warm up.
3. Rehearse to the minute; see `writing-jokes-and-building-a-tight-five`.
4. Do not experiment.

## Behaviour
- Be pleasant to everyone; an industry person may be watching the green room.
- Do not push material at industry people; offer a card and a link.

## Follow-up
- Within a few days, email those you spoke to with a short thank-you and a clip.
- Keep a log of contacts.

## Gotchas
- Overselling.
- Wasting the slot on new material.
