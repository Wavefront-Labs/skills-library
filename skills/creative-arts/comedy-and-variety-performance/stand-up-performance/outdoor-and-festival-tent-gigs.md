# Outdoor and festival tent gigs

**Goal:** hold a crowd that came for something else.

## Conditions
- Ambient noise, drifting audience, bright light and wind. Laughter disperses in open air.
- Audiences may arrive or leave mid-set.

## Tactics
1. Open with your strongest material; do not build slowly.
2. Fewer subtle jokes; use big-image punches.
3. Slow the pace slightly and repeat key words.
4. Keep chunks self-contained so newcomers can follow.
5. Use physical energy; the audience may be far away.

## Practical
- Mic and wind: use a foam cover; ask the technician about monitoring.
- Weather: ask about the plan for rain.
- Timing: ask about overruns and the stage manager's signal.
- Alcohol and sunlight add rowdiness.

## Gotchas
- Material that requires hushed attention.
- Treating the gig as a failure when attention drifts; it is the format.

## Verify
- [ ] First two minutes get laughs; the set can be cut by a minute or two if needed.
