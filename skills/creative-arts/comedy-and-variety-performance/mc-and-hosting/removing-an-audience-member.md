# Removing an audience member

**Goal:** end serious disruption without escalation. Venue staff or security make the removal, not performers.

## When to act
- Harassment, threats, violence, or persistent disruption despite warnings.
- Safety first: if anyone is in danger, stop the show.

## Steps
1. Agree beforehand how the venue handles removals; see `getting-venue-staff-to-act-on-a-heckler`.
2. Give a calm, clear warning if it is safe to do so.
3. Signal staff with the agreed signal.
4. Let staff approach and handle it. Stay on stage, keep the mic, and avoid confrontation.
5. Use the mic to calm the room: a brief statement and a light line when the person has left.

## Your words
- Do not insult the person; state the behaviour was unacceptable.
- Avoid describing their reasons or identity.

## After
- Resume with a short reset.
- Report the incident to the venue.
- Check on any audience members who were affected.

## Gotchas
- Trying to remove someone yourself.
- Laughing at the person while they are removed.

## Verify
- [ ] The person was removed by staff and the room settled before continuing.
