# Moderating a panel

**Goal:** a lively discussion that serves the audience and treats panelists fairly.

## Preparation
- Learn the panelists' work and what they care about. Contact them beforehand for topics.
- Prepare a short introduction for each and a list of questions, with a few follow-ups.
- Decide time per question; plan time for audience questions.

## Running it
1. Welcome, explain the topic and format.
2. Introduce each panelist briefly.
3. Ask the first question to a panelist who will answer well.
4. Direct questions to different people; keep balance.
5. Listen and follow up; connect answers.
6. Move on when a topic is exhausted.
7. Take audience questions and repeat them for the room.
8. Close with a final round, thank everyone.

## Difficult situations
- Dominant speaker: thank them and turn to someone else.
- Quiet panelist: ask them directly.
- Off-topic: steer back politely.

## Time
- Use a clock; give signals to panelists.

## Gotchas
- Long introductions.
- Making yourself the star.

## Verify
- [ ] Each panelist spoke a fair share and the event ended on time.
