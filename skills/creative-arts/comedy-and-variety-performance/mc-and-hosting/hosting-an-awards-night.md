# Hosting an awards night

**Goal:** a smooth, warm event that keeps the audience engaged.

## Brief
- Get the running order, the script, the award categories, and pronunciation of names. Meet the producer and the stage manager.
- Clarify the host's role: introduce presenters, fill gaps, manage time.

## Script and prep
- Write short, tailored jokes about the organisation or the event.
- Check sensitivities with the organisers.
- Practise names and phrases aloud.

## During
- Keep links short; the audience came for the awards.
- Welcome presenters and winners warmly.
- Manage speeches: agree limits and signals, such as play-off music, with the producer.
- Keep energy up through lulls: a short joke or a prompt for applause.

## Mistakes
- Recover with composure: apologise briefly, correct, and move on.
- Technical delay: use prepared material to fill; see `vamping-through-technical-failure`.

## After
- Thank the sponsors and the winners; close promptly.

## Gotchas
- Spending time on your own material.
- Mispronouncing a winner's name.

## Verify
- [ ] Names rehearsed, links timed, and every sponsor mentioned as required.
