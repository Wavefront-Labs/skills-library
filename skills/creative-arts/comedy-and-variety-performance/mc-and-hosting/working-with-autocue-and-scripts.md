# Working with autocue and scripts

**Goal:** sound natural while reading.

## Setup
- Rehearse with the operator, on the real device if possible.
- Check font size, line length, and the scroll speed.
- Agree signals for a stop, a skip, or a fix.

## Reading
- Read the script beforehand so you know where the sentence is going.
- Look slightly ahead of the line you are speaking.
- Use natural pauses and eye contact with the audience.
- Mark the script with emphasis and pauses.

## Handling problems
- If the autocue stops, pause briefly, look at the hard copy, and continue.
- Keep a printed script available.
- Rehearse changes of last-minute edits.

## Pace
- Speak at the pace of your normal speech.
- Pause for applause and laughter.

## Gotchas
- A flat, fast read.
- Reading names you have never practised.

## Verify
- [ ] You can deliver the script from a printed copy if the autocue fails.
