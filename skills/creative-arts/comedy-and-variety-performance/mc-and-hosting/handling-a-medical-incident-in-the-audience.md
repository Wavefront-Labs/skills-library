# Handling a medical incident in the audience

**Goal:** get help fast and keep everyone calm. Follow the venue's own emergency procedure, which takes priority over this outline.

## Before the show
- Learn the venue's procedure: who is first aider, where the first-aid kit and the defibrillator are, how emergency services are called, and the address to give.

## If it happens
1. Stop the show and tell the audience calmly that you are pausing.
2. Alert venue staff or the first aider immediately; ask someone to call the local emergency number if staff are not already doing so.
3. Turn up the house lights if possible.
4. Ask the audience to stay seated and give space; ask for any medical professionals to identify themselves to staff.
5. Keep your own role simple: communicate and keep people calm. Do not give medical treatment unless you are trained.

## After
- Wait for staff to say the show can resume.
- Decide with the venue whether to continue, take an interval, or end.
- Resume gently; a short acknowledgement is better than jokes.
- Log the incident as the venue requires.

## Gotchas
- Joking too soon.
- Not knowing who the first aider is.

## Verify
- [ ] You know the venue's emergency contacts and procedure before every show.
