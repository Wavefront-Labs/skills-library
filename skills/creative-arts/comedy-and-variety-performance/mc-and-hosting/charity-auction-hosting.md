# Charity auction hosting

**Goal:** raise as much as possible while keeping the room happy.

## Preparation
- Review the lots, estimated values and starting bids with the organisers.
- Find out the format: live auction, silent auction, paddle raise; check any legal and tax requirements with the organisers.
- Brief spotters (people who identify bids in the room).

## Hosting a live auction
1. Introduce each lot with a short, funny story.
2. Start with a reasonable opening bid.
3. Build momentum: rhythm of increments, direct addressing of bidders, and humour.
4. Use a clear close: "Going once, twice, sold".
5. Thank the buyer.

## Raising money
- Use paddle raises, a fund-a-need appeal, and matching donors.
- Work the room's friendly competition without embarrassing anyone.

## Tone
- Keep it light; do not pressure or shame.
- Thank donors and sponsors.

## Payment
- Confirm how winners pay and who handles the money.

## Gotchas
- A slow pace that loses the room.
- Misreading a bid; use spotters and repeat the amounts.

## Verify
- [ ] Bidders understand increments and payment terms and spotters have been briefed.
