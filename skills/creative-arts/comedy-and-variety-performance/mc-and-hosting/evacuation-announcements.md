# Evacuation announcements

**Goal:** get people out calmly. The venue's own emergency procedure and script take priority.

## Before the show
- Ask the venue: the evacuation procedure, exits, assembly point, who gives the order, and the script (many venues provide a standard announcement).
- Know the fire alarm sound and the venue's lights.

## During an evacuation
1. Stop the show.
2. Use the approved wording; speak slowly and calmly into the mic. Typical content: there is an incident, please leave by the nearest exit, follow staff, do not collect belongings, do not use lifts, go to the assembly point.
3. Point to exits; repeat the message.
4. Do not joke or add speculation.
5. Leave with the crowd or as directed by staff, after checking the stage and backstage if safe and instructed.

## After
- Wait for staff or emergency services to confirm the all-clear before returning to the building.
- Decide with the venue whether the show continues.

## Gotchas
- Assuming the alarm means a drill.
- Improvising instructions.

## Verify
- [ ] You have read the venue's emergency plan and know the exits before going on.
