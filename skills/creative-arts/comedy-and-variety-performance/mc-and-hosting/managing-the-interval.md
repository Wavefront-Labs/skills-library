# Managing the interval

**Goal:** a break that lets people refresh and returns them on time.

## Before
- Agree the interval length with the venue and the stage manager (commonly around 15-20 minutes; use what suits the venue and the bar queue).
- Plan the closing act of the first half and the opener of the second.

## Announcement
- Say the length and the restart time clearly.
- Mention bar, toilets, raffle, merchandise.
- Give a reason to come back: a teaser for the second half.

## During
- Check with the bar about service delays and extend if needed.
- Run a raffle or prize draw as part of the break; see `charity-auction-hosting` for sales tips.
- Brief the second-half acts.

## Return
- Use a five-minute and a two-minute call (lights, music, bell).
- Start on time or within a minute or two; audiences learn to expect your schedule.
- Open the second half with energy.

## Gotchas
- Letting the interval run so long that the audience loses the mood.
- Not telling people the time to return.

## Verify
- [ ] The second half starts within a couple of minutes of the announced time.
