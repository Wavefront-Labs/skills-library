# Compere material, crowd mapping and the headliner

**Goal:** a compere who builds the room for the acts and delivers a headliner to a ready crowd.

## Compere material
- Write a small set of flexible material: openers, crowd work routines, transitions, and rescue lines; see `riffing-and-improvised-lines`.
- Keep material short and in your persona.
- Avoid topics that acts will cover; check the running order.

## Mapping the crowd
- Note who is in the room: groups, birthdays, hecklers, friendly responders.
- Keep a mental or paper list of names and details for callbacks later; see `calling-back-crowd-work-moments`.
- Use the map to place the risky tables away from the headliner's best moments or to include fans.

## Setting up the headliner
- Build anticipation: summarise the evening's high points, then promise the headliner's quality.
- Use the headliner's best credible credits; keep it short.
- Leave the stage clean and the mic stand set.
- Bring the room up: ask for applause as the headliner walks on.

## Care
- Do not overpromise.
- Do not use the headliner's material.

## Gotchas
- Ending the compere's section with a joke that the headliner then has to follow.
- Over-using one table.

## Verify
- [ ] The room is warm and the headliner's intro is short and accurate.
