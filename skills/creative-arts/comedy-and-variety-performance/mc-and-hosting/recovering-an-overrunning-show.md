# Recovering an overrunning show

**Goal:** finish close to time without making any act feel cut off.

## Spot it early
- Track elapsed time against the running order at each break.
- Decide the hard end time with the venue.

## Options (in order)
1. Cut your own material first.
2. Shorten between-act segments; keep introductions short.
3. Ask the stage manager to signal acts to wrap up; see `delivery-crowd-work-and-heckler-handling` for the light conventions.
4. Reduce the interval, with the bar's agreement.
5. If necessary, cut an act or a section, in consultation with the producer; tell the act yourself and as early as possible.

## Communication
- Brief acts quietly and respectfully.
- Keep the audience informed only if needed.

## After
- Note where time went; plan better for next time.

## Gotchas
- Cutting an act at the last moment without telling them.
- Letting early acts overrun.

## Verify
- [ ] Show finishes within a few minutes of the planned end.
