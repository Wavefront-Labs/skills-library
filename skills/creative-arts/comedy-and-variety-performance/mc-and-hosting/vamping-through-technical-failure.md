# Vamping through technical failure

**Goal:** keep the room while the problem is fixed.

## Prepare
- Have a few minutes of material that needs no tech: stories, crowd work, a short set.
- Know the venue's backup (a handheld mic, a backup track).

## When it happens
1. Stay calm; do not panic the room.
2. Acknowledge the problem lightly: "Technical difficulties; stay with me".
3. Switch to unamplified material if the sound is out and the room is small; move closer.
4. Ask the crew for an estimate of the delay.
5. Fill with crowd work or a game; see `studio-audience-warm-up-and-participation-games`.
6. If it is lengthy, tell the audience the plan.

## Types
- **Mic failure**: use a backup mic or project.
- **Lighting**: ask for house lights.
- **Music or track**: do a cappella or restart.

## After
- Restart with a short recap if needed.
- Debrief with the crew.

## Gotchas
- Blaming the crew publicly.
- Vamping so long that the audience is bored; decide a cut-off.

## Verify
- [ ] You have several minutes of tech-free material ready.
