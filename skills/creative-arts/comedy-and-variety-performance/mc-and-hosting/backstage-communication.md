# Backstage communication

**Goal:** everyone knows what happens next without shouting.

## Tools
- A printed running order in a visible place backstage with acts, times and intro notes.
- A cue sheet for sound and lights.
- Walkie-talkies or headsets for larger venues; a runner or a message to the MC otherwise.
- Agreed hand signals or lights (e.g., a light flash from the wings means wrap up).

## Roles
- A stage manager (or a designated person) controls the order and signals acts when to be ready.
- The MC receives time updates and changes.
- Sound and lights confirm cues.

## Briefing
- Brief all performers at the start: order, signals, entry and exit routes, house rules.
- Update changes in writing and verbally.

## During the show
- Keep backstage quiet.
- Confirm each act is ready before the MC introduces them.

## Gotchas
- Last-minute changes communicated to only one person.
- Noise backstage that can be heard on stage.

## Verify
- [ ] Every act knows their call time, cue and exit route.
