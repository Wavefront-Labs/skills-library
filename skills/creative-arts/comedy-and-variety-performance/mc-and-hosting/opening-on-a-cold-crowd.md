# Opening on a cold crowd

**Goal:** turn a silent room into an audience in the first few minutes.

## Assess
- A cold crowd may be tired, uncertain, or unfamiliar with comedy. Notice what they are doing (eating, looking at phones) before you begin.

## Tactics
1. **Start strong but calm**: confident walk-on and warm greeting.
2. **Small yeses**: ask easy questions that everyone can answer by clapping or cheering ("Who's here for the first time?").
3. **Name the situation lightly**: a relaxed comment about the quiet room can release tension.
4. **Local detail**: use the venue, the weather or the event as material.
5. **Short, reliable jokes first**; build up.
6. **Move closer** or invite them forward if the room allows.

## Avoid
- Blaming the audience.
- Starting with risky material.
- Pushing harder: more volume often makes a room more closed.

## Handoff
- When the room has warmed, bring on the first act; see `warming-a-room-and-running-a-live-show-as-mc`.

## Gotchas
- Ignoring the rest of the room to focus on one reactive table.

## Verify
- [ ] The room is laughing and applauding before the first act.
