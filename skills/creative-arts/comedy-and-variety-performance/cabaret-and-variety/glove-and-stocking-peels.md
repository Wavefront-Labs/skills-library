# Glove and stocking peels

**Goal:** a removal that is a mini-performance in itself.

## Glove peel
1. Frame the moment with the music and your eyes.
2. Release the closure (a button, or loosen a finger-by-finger pull).
3. Remove finger by finger or tug the fingertips; the pace follows the music.
4. Pull the glove from the wrist slowly, then let it go: toss, drop, or hand it to an assistant or a set piece.
- Use gloves with a smooth lining; test their friction.

## Stocking peel
1. Place the foot on a chair or a raised surface, which shows the leg line.
2. Unhook garters or loosen the top band.
3. Roll or slide the stocking down slowly, working with the music.
4. Remove it from the foot and complete the gesture: swing, drop, or hand.
- Choose stockings and garters that are easy to release and not too tight.

## Craft
- The tease is in the anticipation: look at the audience before and after.
- Rehearse the movement in costume; know which pieces release easily.
- Mind the venue's rules on what may be shown.

## Safety
- Check the chair's stability.
- Watch your balance on one foot in heels.

## Gotchas
- Tight gloves that stick.
- Rushing the peel.

## Verify
- [ ] Each removal is smooth, in time with the music, and can be repeated reliably.
