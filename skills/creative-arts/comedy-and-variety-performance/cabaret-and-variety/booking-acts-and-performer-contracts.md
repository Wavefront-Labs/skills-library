# Booking acts and performer contracts

**Goal:** clear agreement in writing so everyone knows what to expect. This is not legal advice; contracts are law-specific.

## Booking
- Describe the event: date, venue, audience, other acts, the slot, and the tech.
- Ask acts for a short video, technical needs, and references.
- Check the act's content for the audience.

## Contract basics (items commonly included)
- Parties, date, venue, arrival and sound check times.
- Fee, expenses, and payment date and method.
- Slot length and position on the bill.
- Technical rider: sound, lights, stage size, music format.
- Content rules.
- Cancellation terms for either side.
- Use of recordings and photography.
- Insurance and safety requirements (especially for dangerous acts).

## Process
1. Send a draft agreement.
2. Confirm in writing and store copies.
3. Send a final tech sheet and running order before the show.

## Care
- Pay on time.
- Treat acts with respect; communicate changes quickly.

## Gotchas
- Verbal agreements.
- Unclear arrival times.

## Verify
- [ ] Every act has a written agreement and has confirmed it.
