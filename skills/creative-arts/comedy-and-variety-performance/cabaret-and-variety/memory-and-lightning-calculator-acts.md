# Memory and lightning-calculator acts

**Goal:** a demonstration that is entertaining and honest about its methods.

## Memory techniques
- Common systems include the method of loci (placing items in imagined places), peg systems (associating numbers with images), and the major system (turning digits into sounds and words). Learn from a recognised book or teacher and practise daily.
- Build the act on a few reliable feats: memorising a list from the audience, a pack of cards, or names.

## Calculation
- Practise mental arithmetic techniques (squares, multiplication shortcuts, calendar calculation) and increase speed gradually.
- Have an assistant confirm results with a calculator.

## Presentation
1. Choose a clear challenge for the audience to see.
2. Use volunteers to supply random items.
3. Narrate to keep the audience engaged during pauses.
4. Add comedy through banter and reactions.

## Honesty
- Describe the act as skill and practice; avoid claims you cannot support. Some performers present methods as mentalism; decide which line you take.

## Practice
- Rehearse with distractions, as the audience may be noisy.

## Gotchas
- A long silence while you work; fill it with patter.
- Mistakes under pressure; have a graceful way to correct.

## Verify
- [ ] The feat works reliably with random input and an audience present.
