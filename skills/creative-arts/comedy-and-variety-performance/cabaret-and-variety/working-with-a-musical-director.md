# Working with a musical director

**Goal:** a partnership that supports your voice and your act.

## Before the first rehearsal
- Choose songs and keys; prepare clean charts: a lead sheet or piano-vocal score, with the key, tempo, repeats and cuts marked clearly.
- Send recordings and notes in advance.
- Agree fees and rehearsal times.

## Rehearsal
1. Run songs with the MD, set tempos and keys.
2. Mark cuts, dynamics, tempo changes, and where you will speak.
3. Agree **cues**: how you start each song, how you end it, and what happens if a laugh or a pause is added.
4. Rehearse the patter links as well as the songs.

## In performance
- Make eye contact or a small signal when needed; agree what that means.
- Check the stage position of the piano or band.
- Sound check with the MD.

## Etiquette
- Respect their time and credit them on the bill.
- Give constructive notes about the arrangement and listen to theirs.

## Gotchas
- Bringing charts in the wrong key.
- Changing the order of songs at the last minute.

## Verify
- [ ] Charts are clear, cues are agreed, and a full run-through has been done.
