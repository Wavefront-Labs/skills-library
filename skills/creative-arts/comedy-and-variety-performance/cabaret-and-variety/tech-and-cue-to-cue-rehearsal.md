# Tech and cue-to-cue rehearsal

**Goal:** make lighting, sound and stage changes work before the audience arrives.

## Prepare
- Collect each act's tech sheet: music, cues, lighting mood, props, and mic needs.
- Build a combined cue sheet in running order.
- Confirm crew roles: stage manager, sound, lights.

## Cue-to-cue
- The stage manager runs the show cue by cue, skipping the acting between cues. Performers stand in position for each cue, then skip to the next one.
- Check each light state, sound cue, and transition.
- Fix problems on the spot or note them.

## Run of the show
- After the cue-to-cue, run any complicated acts or transitions fully.
- Rehearse the opening and closing.
- Time the show and changeovers.

## Notes
- Give notes after the session to the crew and to acts; update the cue sheet.

## Safety
- Check fire exits, haze or fire effects approvals, and the stage floor.

## Gotchas
- Not scheduling enough time; each act adds minutes.
- Last-minute changes that are not written down.

## Verify
- [ ] The cue sheet is final and distributed.
