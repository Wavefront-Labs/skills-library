# Chapeaugraphy

**Goal:** a short act of quickly changing shapes from a single piece of felt.

## What it is
Chapeaugraphy is a variety turn in which a performer shapes a large ring of felt (or similar fabric) into many styles of hats, usually with a different character for each. I have not confirmed its detailed history; check specialist sources before you state it in programme notes.

## Materials
- A ring or loop of soft felt of appropriate size, often with a distinct nap; try different thicknesses.
- A mirror for practice.

## Technique
- Fold, pinch, roll and twist the felt to form crowns and brims.
- Practise each shape until you can produce it in a single motion.
- Pair each shape with a posture, accent, or facial expression.

## Act structure
1. Open with a simple shape.
2. Build to increasingly complex or humorous hats.
3. Use music or patter to link the shapes.
4. End with a surprise.

## Practice
- Film yourself; check speed and clarity.
- Practise in front of a small audience.

## Gotchas
- A felt that does not hold its shape.
- Too many similar shapes.

## Verify
- [ ] Each shape reads as a distinct character from a distance.
