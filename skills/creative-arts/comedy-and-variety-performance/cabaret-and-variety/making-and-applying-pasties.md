# Making and applying pasties

**Goal:** pasties that stay on through a number and come off without harm.

## Making
- Base: a firm material such as craft foam, felt, or a stiff interfacing, cut to a circle or shape.
- Decorate: rhinestones, sequins, tassels, fringe; see `rhinestoning`.
- Smooth the back so it does not irritate; add a layer of soft fabric if needed.

## Adhesives
- Use products intended for skin contact: adhesives designed for body tape, costume tape or pasties. Check labels and patch-test on a small area a day ahead.
- Do not use craft glue or superglue on skin.

## Application
1. Clean and dry the skin; avoid lotions.
2. Apply the adhesive on the back of the pastie; press on from the centre outward; hold a moment.
3. Check movement before the show.

## Removal
- Peel slowly from the edge, supporting the skin; use oil or the adhesive's recommended remover.
- Moisturise.

## Variety
- Have spare pairs and extra adhesive.

## Gotchas
- Sweat reduces adhesion; powder around the area or use stronger adhesive suited for skin.
- Heavy tassels pull at the skin; check weight.

## Verify
- [ ] Patch test done and the pasties stay in place when you shimmy.
