# Intimacy and the cabaret room

**Goal:** make a small room feel like a conversation.

## Principles
- The audience is close; they see every expression. Real feeling reads better than big gestures.
- Direct address: look at individuals, include the whole room, and take your time.
- Use a lower volume and rely on the microphone; avoid pushing the voice as if in a theatre.

## Using the room
- Learn the layout in advance: where the stage is, the sightlines, and how tables are set.
- Use space between tables if the venue allows.
- Use light changes and silences, since an intimate room allows stillness.

## Managing the room
- Drink and food service create noise; you may agree with staff when service pauses for songs.
- Handle talkers with a light line or a quiet look; keep the mood.
- Start songs with strong, clear first lines.

## Sound
- Sound check with the room empty and with a few people if possible; levels change when full.

## Gotchas
- Too much physical business.
- Ignoring the audience at the side or back.

## Verify
- [ ] Every table feels addressed at least once during the act.
