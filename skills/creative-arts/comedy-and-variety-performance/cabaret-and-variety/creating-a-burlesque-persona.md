# Creating a burlesque persona

**Goal:** a stage character with a clear identity.

## Elements
- **Stage name**: memorable, says something about the character, and checks for conflicts with other performers.
- **Character**: who is she or he or they: a vamp, a clown, a diva, an alien. Choose attitude, humour and energy.
- **Look**: signature colours, hair, makeup, accessories.
- **Music**: a taste that fits the character and the style of acts.
- **Story**: the narrative behind the character, if used.

## Process
1. List adjectives for the persona.
2. Choose a visual style that expresses them.
3. Choose one signature element that returns in every act.
4. Create acts that explore different aspects of the persona.

## Consistency
- Keep the name and image across social media and posters.
- Evolve the persona over time.

## Authenticity
- Draw on your own sense of humour or presence; audiences respond to confidence.

## Gotchas
- A persona that requires constantly acting a character you do not like.
- Using a name close to another performer's.

## Verify
- [ ] You can describe the persona in one sentence and the look supports it.
