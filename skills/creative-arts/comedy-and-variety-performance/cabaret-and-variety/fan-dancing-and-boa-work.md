# Fan dancing and boa work

**Goal:** smooth control of the prop so it frames the performer and builds the tease.

## Fans
- Feather fans (often ostrich) are held at the base; a pair is common.
- Moves: open and close, wave, sweep across the body, frame the face, cover and reveal.
- Keep wrists flexible; move the fan with the music; avoid hiding the face constantly.
- Plan the choreography for the fans so they move with the body.
- Handle carefully; feathers can break and moult.

## Boas
- Drape across the shoulders, pull across the back, wrap, throw, and use for the tease.
- Move the boa as an extension of an arm; use momentum, not strength.
- Be mindful of shedding on stage and how it affects the floor, other performers, and the venue.

## Care
- Store fans in a box to keep their shape; keep away from moisture.
- Check feathers and fixings before a show.
- Check the venue's fire and cleaning rules; avoid fire effects unless approved.

## Practice
- Film yourself; look at the shapes you make.
- Rehearse in costume with the music.

## Gotchas
- A boa that sheds on the floor at a venue that bans it.
- A fan covering the face too long.

- [ ] The prop looks controlled and the moves hit musical moments.
