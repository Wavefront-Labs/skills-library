# Building the tease

**Goal:** a routine where anticipation is as important as the reveal.

## Principles
- Burlesque tease is about control: the audience knows something will be removed and enjoys the build.
- Each removal gets its own preparation, the action, and the reaction.
- Pacing follows the music: slow build, accents on beats, and a peak.

## Structure
1. **Entrance**: set character.
2. **First removal**: small, to establish the language (a glove).
3. **Build**: escalating pieces with varied styles.
4. **Peak**: the most significant removal or surprise.
5. **Button**: the final pose and exit.

## Craft
- Use eye contact and facial expression to invite the audience.
- Use the hands to point at what will happen next.
- Vary the type of tease (slow, comic, dramatic).
- Be precise about what you reveal, within venue rules.

## Rehearsal
- Map costume pieces to the song.
- Practise timing against the track and in costume.

## Gotchas
- Removing too much too early.
- Choosing a song that does not build.

## Verify
- [ ] The routine has a visible build with a clear peak and button.
