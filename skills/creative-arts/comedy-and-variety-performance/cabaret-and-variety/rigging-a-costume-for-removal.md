# Rigging costume for removal

**Goal:** removals that look effortless and never stick.

## Closure options
- **Snaps, hooks and eyes, and Velcro**: reliable and quick, but check noise.
- **Ribbons or laces**: look good but slow; use for the tease.
- **Breakaway seams**: seams sewn with weak thread or basted stitches that tear with a tug; test on the real fabric.
- **Zips**: a long, smooth zip with a large pull.

## Layering
- Plan the order of layers and what is underneath.
- Check what is visible at each stage, within venue rules.

## Testing
- Practise each removal 20 or more times at performance speed.
- Rehearse with sweat and in heels or shoes.

## Fail-safe
- Have a backup plan if a piece sticks: a rehearsed move to turn away, a helper in the wings, or a prepared line.
- Keep safety pins and a small tool in a hidden place.

## Gotchas
- A closure that works in the studio but fails when your hands are damp.
- Too many layers for the length of the song.

## Verify
- [ ] Every removal works ten times in a row at speed.
