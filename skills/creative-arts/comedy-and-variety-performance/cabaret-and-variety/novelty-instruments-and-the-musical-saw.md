# Novelty instruments and the musical saw

**Goal:** a short novelty musical turn.

## Choosing
- Common novelty instruments include the musical saw, kazoo, ukulele, toy pianos, and the theremin. Choose one you can play competently and that fits your persona.

## Musical saw basics
- A flexible saw blade is held between the knees or on the lap, with the handle held in one hand and the tip in the other; bend it into an S-curve.
- A violin bow or a mallet makes the sound; changing the curve changes the pitch.
- Learn from a teacher or from reputable lessons; you must practise to hear pitch.
- Use a saw made for music if possible.

## Act design
1. Pick tunes with clear melodies that suit the instrument.
2. Add comedy through the premise: the setup (a serious musician), the object (a tool), and the surprise.
3. Keep it short; novelty fades.

## Care
- Protect the blade from rust and bending; carry it in a case.
- Handle sharp edges with care.

## Gotchas
- A weak ending: plan a strong last note or a final visual gag.

## Verify
- [ ] The act is under 5 minutes and the instrument can be played in tune.
