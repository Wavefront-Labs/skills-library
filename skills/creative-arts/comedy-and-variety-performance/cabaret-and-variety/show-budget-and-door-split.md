# Show budget and door split

**Goal:** a show that does not lose money and treats performers fairly.

## Costs to list
- Venue hire or share, tech and crew, performer fees, marketing, insurance and licences, props, travel, and contingency.

## Income
- Ticket sales, bar share, sponsorship, merchandise.

## Break-even
- Total costs divided by net ticket price gives the number of tickets to sell.
- Compare to the venue's capacity and your realistic sales.

## Door splits (check what each venue offers)
- A split divides the ticket income (after costs or before costs) between the venue and the producer; or it may be shared among performers.
- Agree exactly: percentage, what counts as income, and when payment is made.

## Performers
- Fees fixed or share of the door; guarantee a minimum when possible.

## Process
1. Build a spreadsheet of costs and scenarios.
2. Set the ticket price.
3. Agree the split in writing.
4. Reconcile after the show.

## Gotchas
- Forgetting fees or licences.
- Counting on full capacity.

## Verify
- [ ] Break-even number known and the split written down.
