# Quick-change (protean) act

**Goal:** a series of fast costume changes that surprise the audience.

## Concept
A protean act changes appearance rapidly in view of the audience, often through layers, breakaway pieces and hidden fastenings. Show the transformation as the entertainment.

## Design
1. Decide the sequence of looks and the story linking them.
2. Build each costume so that it comes off in one pull, usually with snaps, Velcro, or elastic closures; layers stack in order.
3. Plan hand-offs: where discarded pieces go (trap, bag, helper).
4. Use music with clear beats; the change happens on the hit.

## Rehearsal
- Practise each change in isolation, then in sequence.
- Time it; aim for repeatability.
- Rehearse with a helper if used.

## Safety
- Avoid fastenings that can trap fingers or be caught on props.
- Keep stage floors clear of discarded costume.

## Gotchas
- Changes that depend on clean hands and fail when sweaty.
- Overheating under layers.

## Verify
- [ ] The sequence works consistently at performance speed.
