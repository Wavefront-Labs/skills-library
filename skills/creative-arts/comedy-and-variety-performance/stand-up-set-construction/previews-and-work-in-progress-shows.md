# Previews and work-in-progress shows

**Goal:** use live rooms to shape an hour before it is reviewed or sold.

## Format
- A work in progress (WIP) is billed honestly: audiences accept scripts in hand and breaks in flow if told in advance.
- Start with 20-30 minute sets in friendly rooms, then 40-60 minutes in small venues, ending with full-length previews with the title and blurb final.
- Many comics preview in the weeks before a festival run; check how your festival counts previews against its schedule.

## What to capture
1. Audio or video of every run.
2. Per-bit laugh notes: timestamp, size, cause.
3. Audience feedback: a short form or a conversation, but ask specific questions (where did you lose interest?).
4. Your own timings.

## Iteration
- After each run, cut and reorder before adding. See `cutting-bits-that-do-not-work`.
- Change one major element at a time so you can tell what made the difference.
- Keep running the same hour in similar-size rooms; a hour that works for 20 may not at 200.

## Gotchas
- Previewing for friends only: honest data needs strangers.
- Fixing every note: you are looking for patterns across rooms, not one person's taste.

## Verify
- [ ] At least two full-length runs before opening, final order locked a few runs before.
