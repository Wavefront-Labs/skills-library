# Writing a clean version of a set

## Why
Corporate, cruise, TV, daytime radio and family shows require clean sets. A comic with a
clean 20 works far more.

## Method
1. **Mark every line** that contains swearing, sex, drugs, religion jabs or slurs.
2. For each, find the **mechanism** (what assumption the punch breaks). Usually the
   swear word is an intensifier, not the joke.
3. **Substitute:** replace swears with specific, vivid words ("absolutely furious" ->
   a precise action). Replace sexual targets with an adjacent embarrassment (money, parents, body).
4. **Re-test:** clean versions often lose 10-20% of the laugh initially and recover with delivery work.
5. Cut bits whose premise is the blue subject itself.

## TV standards
- Pre-watershed / network TV: no strong language; brands and real people may need clearing.
- Late-night network sets are typically vetted by the booker in advance; send the exact set.

## Gotchas
- Swapping a swear for a mild word ("frickin'") sounds coy; rewrite the line.
- Muscle memory: rehearse the clean set enough that blue words do not slip out.

## Verify
- [ ] Clean set performed in a regular club room keeps at least 80% of the laughs of the original.
