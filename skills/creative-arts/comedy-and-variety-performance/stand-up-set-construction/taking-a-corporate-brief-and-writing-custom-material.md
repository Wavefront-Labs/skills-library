# Corporate brief and custom material

## The brief call (30 minutes, a week or more before)
Ask:
- Who is the audience (company, roles, size, gender/age mix, nationalities)?
- What is the event (awards, conference after-dinner, sales kick-off) and the running order?
- What happened this year (wins, mergers, layoffs, new office)?
- Who can be teased (CEO? only with sign-off) and who must not be?
- No-go topics (competitors, recent redundancies, politics, religion, alcohol).
- Language level (fully clean is the default).
- Is it being filmed? Is there a client contact on the night?

## Writing custom material
1. Pick 3-5 shared facts the whole room knows (the office coffee machine, the CEO's
   catchphrase, the new software everyone hates).
2. Write jokes on them using your normal mechanisms — they get huge laughs because they are specific.
3. Keep custom material to 3-5 minutes at the top; then your clean act.
4. Read every custom line to the client contact; get explicit approval for names.

## On the night
- Arrive early, check the room (round tables, staggered dinner, lights up).
- Confirm last-minute changes with the contact.

## Gotchas
- An in-joke only one department knows divides the room.
- Layoffs or tragedies: never joke about them even if the brief contact laughs.

## Verify
- [ ] Written no-go list and approved custom lines on file before the gig.
