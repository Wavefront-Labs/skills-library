# Recording sets and mapping laughs

**Goal:** replace memory (which flatters) with data.

## Recording
- Audio is enough; a phone placed near the stage or a pocket recorder in your pocket works. Check levels once; clipping ruins the laughs.
- Ask the venue first if you are unsure of its rules on recording.
- Record every set including bad ones.

## Laugh map
1. Listen with a notebook or spreadsheet.
2. For each joke log: time, the line, laugh size (0 none, 1 titter, 2 solid, 3 big, 4 applause), laugh length in seconds.
3. Mark where you stepped on a laugh (talked into it) and where you waited too long.
4. Total laughs per minute for each chunk.

## Using the map
- Joke with a 3-4: keep it, consider a tag.
- Joke with 0-1: rewrite the setup or punch word before cutting; see `tightening-a-joke-punch-word-last-and-word-economy`.
- Chunks with low density: shorten or restructure; see `cutting-bits-that-do-not-work`.
- Compare the same joke across rooms to separate room effects from writing.

## Gotchas
- Judging a joke after one room.
- Listening only for laughs: notice your own timing and filler words.

## Verify
- [ ] Each rewrite is tested against the next recording, not assumed to work.
