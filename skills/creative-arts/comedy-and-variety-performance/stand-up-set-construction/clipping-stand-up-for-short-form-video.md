# Clipping sets for short-form video

**Goal:** turn one gig into several short clips that work with sound off and no context.

## Choosing clips
- Pick 15-60 second bits with setup, punch and a laugh; no reliance on an earlier callback.
- Start on the premise line, not on "so" or throat-clearing.
- Prefer bits with a visual (act-out, face) over long stories.

## Capture
- Record with a stable phone at the back or from a second camera at an angle; check the venue's recording policy first.
- Clean audio matters more than image; see `taping-a-special-or-comedy-album` for audio capture ideas.

## Editing
1. Trim to the first line and cut off just after the laugh.
2. Reframe to vertical (9:16) keeping the face in frame.
3. Add burned-in captions; many viewers watch muted.
4. Add a title card only if necessary.

## Posting
- Test the platform's current length limits and music policies; they change often.
- Post consistently with a stable format; link to a show or newsletter.
- Material on video is burned: audiences may know the joke. Consider releasing stale material, not the closer.

## Gotchas
- Using audience members in a clip without their consent.
- Over-posting your best bit before an hour show.

## Verify
- [ ] Clip is understandable muted, within the first two seconds, and ends on the laugh.
