# Cutting bits that do not work

**Goal:** remove weak material fast, without discarding a joke that only needed a fix.

## Decision test
1. Perform the bit in at least three different rooms (or the same room across a few shows). A bit that fails once may be the room.
2. Check the laugh map: see `recording-sets-and-mapping-laughs`.
3. Try one rewrite before cutting: a new punch word, a shorter setup, or a different position.
4. If it still fails and you do not love it, cut it.

## Reasons to cut regardless of laughs
- It conflicts with the persona.
- It no longer fits the through-line of the set.
- You are bored of it; delivery will suffer.

## How to cut safely
- Check for callbacks to the bit elsewhere in the set and remove or rewrite them.
- Bridge the gap with a new segue; see `writing-segues-between-bits`.
- Re-time the set after cutting.

## Archive
File the removed bit with a note on why it was cut and where it was tried; see `filing-and-versioning-jokes`. Old bits sometimes work in a new context.

## Gotchas
- Cutting the setup of a joke that earns its laugh from the pay-off later in the set.
- Keeping a bit out of loyalty to the work it took to write.

## Verify
- [ ] Set re-run end to end after the cut with no dangling references.
