# Association lists and comedy mind maps

**Goal:** generate raw comic connections fast when staring at a blank page.

## Two-list collision
1. Pick a topic (e.g. "gym").
2. Column A: everything literally associated (treadmill, protein shake, mirrors, locker
   room, personal trainer, January).
3. Column B: associations of a second, contrasting topic tied to your premise (e.g.
   "church": confession, pews, sermon, tithe, hymn, sin).
4. Collide items across columns: "personal trainer + confession" -> "My personal trainer
   asks what I ate this week like a priest who already knows."
5. Mark collisions that suggest a picture; those become setups or punches.

## Mind map
- Topic in the centre, five branches: people, places, objects, phrases/jargon, feelings.
- Expand each branch three levels. Jargon and specific phrases are gold for
  reinterpretation (they have connectors built in).

## Speed rules
- 10 minutes per list, no editing.
- Quantity first: 40 items per list before collisions.
- Do it daily; associations improve with a habit.

## Gotchas
- Lists of nouns only give puns; include verbs and feelings.
- Two topics with no tension between them give no jokes; choose a contrast.
