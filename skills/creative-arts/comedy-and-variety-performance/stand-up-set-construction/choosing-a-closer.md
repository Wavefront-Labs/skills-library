# Choosing a closer

**Goal:** leave on your biggest laugh so the booker and audience remember the end.

## Criteria
- Biggest *reliable* laugh in the set (works in most rooms, not just good ones).
- Ends on a punch or callback, ideally an applause break.
- Self-contained enough to be jumped to from anywhere.

## Structure
1. Last bit = closer bit; its final line = the last thing you say before "Thank you, I've been [name]".
2. Say goodbye immediately after the laugh peaks. Do not add "one more thing".

## Cut to the closer
- When you see the light (time warning) or the room is dying, cut straight to the closer.
- Rehearse a one-line bridge from anywhere: "Before I go, I have to tell you about..."
- Keep a **backup closer** for rooms where the main closer's premise does not suit (e.g. clean rooms).

## Gotchas
- Closers that rely on a callback fail if the plant died — have the standalone version.
- New material never goes in the closer slot.

## Verify
- [ ] Closer gets the set's biggest laugh in 4 of 5 recorded gigs.
- [ ] Cut-to-closer bridge drilled from three points in the set.
