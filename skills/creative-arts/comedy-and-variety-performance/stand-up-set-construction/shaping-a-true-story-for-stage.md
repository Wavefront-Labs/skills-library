# Shaping a true story for stage

## Steps
1. **Tell it badly first:** tell it aloud to a friend or recorder with everything in it. Note
   where they laughed or leaned in.
2. **Find the spine:** what changed for you? If nothing changed it is an anecdote; find the change.
3. **Compress:** three days become one night; four friends become one friend. The audience
   needs clarity, not accuracy of logistics.
4. **Keep sensory specifics:** the brand, the exact phrase someone said, the smell. Specifics
   make it feel true and give jokes their pictures.
5. **Heighten honestly:** exaggerate your inner reaction rather than inventing events the
   audience would find implausible.
6. **Write jokes on top:** attitude lines and act-outs at each beat.

## Ethics
- Comedians commonly embellish; avoid presenting invented harm by real, identifiable people as fact.
- If the story is billed as true (storytelling nights, The Moth), keep events true and compress only.

## Gotchas
- Inside references and names the audience does not know — replace with descriptions.
- Starting with "This is a true story" sets expectations you then must meet.

## Verify
- [ ] Story fits a set slot (3-8 min) with every beat serving the change.
