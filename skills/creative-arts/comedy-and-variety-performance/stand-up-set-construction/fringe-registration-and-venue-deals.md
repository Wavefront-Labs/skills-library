# Fringe registration and venue deals

**Goal:** get onto a fringe programme with a venue deal you can afford.

## Registration
- Fringes run an open-access registration: pay a fee and enter the show into the programme. Edinburgh's registration is run through the Fringe Society; check their participant pages for the current process, fees and deadlines. Other fringes have their own.
- You need: title, blurb, running time, venue, dates, an image, and a producer/performer contact.
- The programme copy deadline is earlier than the festival date; missing it can mean no print listing.

## Venue deals (typical structures, details vary by venue)
- **Box-office split**: you and the venue share ticket income.
- **Guarantee / venue hire**: you pay a fixed fee for the room and keep the income.
- **Free or "pay what you want"** shows: venue gets bar income, comic takes a bucket collection.
- Ask in writing: the room, technical support, publicity commitments, who sells tickets, what happens on cancellations, and payment dates.
- Compare the venue's capacity, location, and tech against your show size.

## Budget (see `show-budget-and-door-split`)
Include registration, venue, accommodation, marketing, a flyering plan, and a contingency. Know your break-even audience size before agreeing.

## Gotchas
- Choosing a venue because it is close to the action but too large for your audience.
- Agreeing verbally; get the deal in writing.

## Verify
- [ ] Deadlines and costs checked with the current fringe documents.
- [ ] Break-even audience calculated before signing.
