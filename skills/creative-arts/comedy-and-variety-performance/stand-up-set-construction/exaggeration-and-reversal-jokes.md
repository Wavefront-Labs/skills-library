# Exaggeration and reversal jokes

## Exaggeration
**Mechanism:** take a true trait and push it past reality into a precise image.
1. State the truth: "My flat is small."
2. Ask "how small?" and answer with a concrete, visual consequence, not an adjective:
   "My flat is so small the smoke alarm goes off when I make toast — in the bathroom."
3. Escalate in tags: each one bigger or more specific.
Rules:
- The image must be specific and visual; "really, really small" is not a joke.
- Keep logical consistency inside the exaggeration (the audience enjoys the internal logic).

## Reversal
**Mechanism:** flip an expected relationship — roles, cause and effect, who serves whom.
- Expected: parents worry about kids online. Reversed: "My teenager has put parental
  controls on my phone."
1. Write the normal relationship in one line.
2. Swap the roles or direction.
3. Describe the reversed world plainly, as if normal (deadpan sells reversal).

## Freshness
"So fat/so ugly" exaggerations and generic role swaps are worn out. Aim exaggeration at
yourself and reversal at a current, specific situation.

## Gotchas
- Exaggeration that becomes surreal too fast loses the truth anchor; start near reality.
- Reversal needs the audience to know the normal version; set it up if not.

## Verify
- [ ] Three exaggeration tags that escalate; one reversal premise that sustains three jokes.
