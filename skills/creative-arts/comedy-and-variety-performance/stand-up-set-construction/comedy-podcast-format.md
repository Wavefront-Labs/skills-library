# Comedy podcast format

**Goal:** a repeatable show whose structure lets the comedy come from the people.

## Premise
- One sentence: who talks, about what, and why it differs from existing shows. Choose a premise that generates material every episode (a game, a recurring topic, a character).

## Structure
1. **Cold open** (30-90 seconds): the funniest moment, or a quick hook.
2. **Recurring segments**: two or three segments with fixed names and rules so listeners know the format.
3. **Guest segment**: a standard set of questions plus room for tangents.
4. **Closer**: a recurring sign-off.
- Length: pick a consistent length that fits the listener's use (commute, workout). Many comedy shows run 45-75 minutes; no rule requires it.

## Recording
- Separate microphones per speaker, closed-back headphones, and a quiet room. Record each speaker to a separate track where your software allows.
- Do a short sound check each time; clap or count down to sync tracks.

## Editing
- Cut dead air and false starts; keep laughs breathing room.
- Do not over-edit: pace comes from keeping the conversation natural.

## Release
- Choose a release day and keep it. Write show notes with timestamps.

## Gotchas
- Too many hosts talking over each other; assign roles (driver, reactor).
