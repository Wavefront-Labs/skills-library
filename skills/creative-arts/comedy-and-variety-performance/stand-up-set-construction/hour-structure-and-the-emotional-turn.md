# Hour structure and the emotional turn

**Goal:** pace an hour so energy builds and the audience leaves moved as well as amused.

## Typical shape (a guide, not a rule)
1. **Opening 10 minutes**: establish the persona and the question; strongest, fastest material, a clear promise.
2. **Middle**: escalate. Alternate high-energy chunks with a story chunk; use callbacks to the opening so the room feels the show building.
3. **The turn**: roughly in the last quarter, the tone shifts (often the most honest or vulnerable material). It works only if the earlier laughs earned trust.
4. **Closer**: pays off the through-line, often with a callback or runner; end on a big laugh or a quiet button. See `choosing-a-closer`.

## Practical points
- Pre-plan breathers: after a heavy chunk, give a fast comedy run so the audience can release.
- Time the hour at 50-55 minutes of material if the venue slot is 60 and includes entry and exit; check your venue slot.
- Track laugh density per chunk using a recording; see `recording-sets-and-mapping-laughs`.
- Keep a short cut-list: bits you can drop if you are running long.

## Gotchas
- Moving into serious material too early or without a laugh to anchor it.
- Ending on a lecture; end on a laugh or a clean image.

## Verify
- [ ] Recording shows laughs through the turn, not a silent stretch.
- [ ] Runtime within slot with a margin.
