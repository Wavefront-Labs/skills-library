# Building a set to 5, 10, 20 and 40 minutes

## Typical slots
| Slot | Length | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Open mic / open spot | 5 min | strongest, cleanest five |
| Middle / paid 10 | 10 min | |
| Opening / middle spot (UK circuit) | 20 min | |
| Headline | 30-40 min (UK); 45-60 US headliner | requires flow and variety |

## Method
1. **Time each bit** from recordings (not from reading). Keep a spreadsheet: bit, duration,
   average laughs per minute, last performed.
2. **Build outward from the tight five:** keep the opener and closer; insert proven bits
   in the middle as length grows.
3. **Pacing across the set:** vary bit length and energy. Alternate quick one-liners or
   short bits with longer stories; place an emotional or personal bit around two-thirds in.
4. **Flow:** order bits so themes connect (segues), then check energy dips on the laugh map.
5. **Contingency:** for 20+ minutes keep 5 minutes of "flex" material that can be dropped
   or added without breaking flow.

## Testing
- Run the full length at a friendly room before a paid gig.
- The light: know where you are at the warning and what you cut.

## Gotchas
- Padding a 20 with weak bits: a strong 15 beats a sagging 20 — but do the booked time.
- Overrunning is a booking sin; under-running by more than a minute is also noticed.

## Verify
- [ ] Each length run with a timer; within 30 seconds of the booked time.
