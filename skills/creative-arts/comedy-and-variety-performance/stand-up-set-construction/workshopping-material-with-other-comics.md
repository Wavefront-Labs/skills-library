# Workshopping with other comics

**Goal:** get useful outside ears without having your act rewritten by committee.

## Format that works
- Small group (3-6), regular schedule, shared rules agreed up front.
- Each person reads or performs a short chunk (2-5 minutes). The group notes where they laughed before discussing.
- Feedback in this order: what landed, what was unclear, and one concrete suggestion (a line, a reorder, a cut).

## Giving notes
- Be specific ("the word 'actually' blunts the punch"), not general ("funnier").
- Notes are observations about the audience experience, not about the writer.
- Offer an alternative line only if asked.

## Taking notes
- Do not defend or explain. Write notes down and decide later.
- If three people mention the same problem, it is a problem. If one does, it may be taste.
- Your persona is yours: accept notes that sharpen it, reject ones that change it.

## Boundaries
- Agree that material shared is not to be reused by others; credit and ownership problems arise in joke-sharing circles.
- Keep sessions in a safe environment; harsh sessions burn people out.

## Gotchas
- Group notes written into a joke without testing; the joke may become less you.

## Verify
- [ ] Each session ends with a list of changes to test live, not just opinions.
