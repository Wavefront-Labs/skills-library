# Show title and blurb

**Goal:** copy that sells the show on the shelf.

## Title
- Short, spoken-aloud-friendly, searchable and distinct from other shows. Check for clashes with other shows and existing trademarks.
- It should hint at the through-line (see `theme-and-through-line-of-an-hour-show`).
- Avoid titles that are a joke only audiences who have seen the show would get.

## Blurb
1. **One-line hook**: the show's question or stake.
2. **Two or three sentences**: who the comic is and what happens, in the comic's voice.
3. **A promise**: tone, what the audience will experience.
4. Keep to the festival's word count; check the programme rules for limits and image format.
5. Review quotes only if you can verify them; fabricated or out-of-context quotes backfire.

## Photos and image
A clear, high-contrast image with your face and a hint of the tone. Check whether the programme requires text-free images.

## Process
Write three versions, test them on people unfamiliar with the show, choose the one they can repeat back.

## Gotchas
- A blurb that explains the jokes.
- Describing the show differently from what the audience gets.

## Verify
- [ ] Within the word count; a stranger can say what the show is about.
