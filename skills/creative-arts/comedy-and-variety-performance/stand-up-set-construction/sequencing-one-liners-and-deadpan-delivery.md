# Sequencing one-liners and deadpan delivery

## Sequencing
1. **Group loosely by theme** (family, jobs, animals) so the audience does not have to reset
   their world every 8 seconds.
2. **Alternate lengths:** a slightly longer joke, then two short ones.
3. **Put your strongest first and last**, test new ones in the middle.
4. **Mini-runs:** three jokes on the same word or idea build momentum.

## The reset
- Between jokes: a clear pause (beat after the laugh), sometimes a sip or a step.
- Do not talk over the laugh; start the next setup as the laugh peaks and begins to fall.

## Deadpan
- Keep face and voice neutral; the gap between flat delivery and absurd content is the laugh.
- No smiling at your own punch. Eyebrows still.
- Consistent rhythm — the same cadence for every joke makes the twist pop.

## When a joke dies
- Have saver lines ("That one's for the people at home"), but use sparingly.
- Move on immediately — the next joke is 8 seconds away.

## Gotchas
- Pure deadpan for 20 minutes can flatten; vary with a planned "break" moment.

## Verify
- [ ] Set recorded: average gap between laughs under 12 seconds.
