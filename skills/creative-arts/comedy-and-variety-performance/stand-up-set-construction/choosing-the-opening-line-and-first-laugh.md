# The opening line and first laugh

**Goal:** a laugh within 15-30 seconds of reaching the mic, and an audience that now knows who you are.

## What the opener must do
1. **Get a laugh fast.** The room decides about you in the first minute.
2. **Address the obvious.** Whatever the audience notices first about you (look, age,
   accent, a weird intro) — acknowledge it or they keep thinking about it.
3. **Establish persona.** The first joke tells them your attitude and status.

## Choosing it
- Your most reliable short joke, not your best long one.
- Short setup (one or two sentences).
- Works in every room (not topical, not crowd-dependent).
- Keep a second "insurance" opener in case the MC uses a similar line.

## Not to do
- No warm-up chat ("How's everyone doing?") unless you have a joke attached.
- No apologising ("I'm new, bear with me").
- Do not start with the mic stand fumble — move it before speaking.

## Local / room opener
Optionally a line about the venue, the MC or something that just happened. It shows
you are present, but only if it is funny; fall back to the written opener otherwise.

## Gotchas
- An opener that comments on the previous act can backfire if they bombed or killed.

## Verify
- [ ] First laugh time logged at every gig; median under 20 seconds.
