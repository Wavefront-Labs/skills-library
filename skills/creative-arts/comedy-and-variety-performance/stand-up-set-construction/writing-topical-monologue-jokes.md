# Writing topical monologue jokes

**Goal:** a page of news jokes ready by show time, each with a clear setup and one-line punch.

## Toplyn's monologue joke shape
1. **Topic:** a news story most of the audience has heard (if not, the setup must tell it).
2. **Setup:** one sentence, stated as fact, like a headline. Include the two "handles" —
   the most distinctive elements of the story.
3. **Punchline:** connect the handles via an association to make a surprising, true-feeling statement.

Example process: Story: "City installs talking bins." Handles: talking, bins. Associations of
"talking": nagging, podcasts, therapy. Associations of "bins": rubbish, collection day, foxes.
Collide: "...so now even the rubbish has a podcast."

## Daily routine
- Morning: list 10-15 stories from mainstream news (what the audience knows).
- Write 10+ punches per story; pick the best one or two.
- Read aloud to a partner; cut anything needing explanation.

## Shelf life
- Topical jokes die in days. Tag each joke with a date in your file.
- Some convert to evergreen material by removing the specific news hook.
- For club sets, retire topical bits after 1-2 weeks unless the story is ongoing.

## Gotchas
- Assuming the audience knows a story they do not — check its reach.
- Opinion without a joke is commentary.

## Verify
- [ ] 10 stories, 50 punches, 10 selected, tested within 48 hours.
