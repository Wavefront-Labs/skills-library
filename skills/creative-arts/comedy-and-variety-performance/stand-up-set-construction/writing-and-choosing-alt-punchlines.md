# Writing and choosing alt punchlines

**Goal:** never settle for the first punch; pick the best of many and confirm it live.

## Generate
1. Write the setup once at the top of a page.
2. Write at least 10 punches. Use different targets: different assumption, different
   character, a literal reading, an extreme, a personal admission.
3. Do not cross any out until you have ten.

## Rank
Score each 1-3 on: surprise, truth/attitude, brevity, picture. Keep the top three.
Writers' rooms call these "alts"; the room pitches alts until one wins the laugh.

## Test live
- Use alt A on Monday's mic, alt B on Tuesday's, with the setup word-for-word identical.
- Record both. Compare laugh length (seconds) and size (titter, laugh, applause break).
- Test across at least three rooms before deciding — one room is noise.

## Keep the losers
A losing alt is often a good tag after the winner. Note this in the joke file.

## Gotchas
- Changing the setup while testing alts confounds the test.
- Delivery differences (confidence on a new line) bias results; drill each alt equally first.
