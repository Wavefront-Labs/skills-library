# Structuring a comic story

**Goal:** a 3-8 minute true-ish story that keeps laughing all the way, not just at the end.

## 1. Beat outline
Write the story as 6-10 one-line beats: situation, want, obstacle, escalation 1, 2, 3,
turn (the moment it goes wrong or flips), consequence, button. Cut anything not on a beat.

## 2. Stakes and the turn
- State early what you wanted and what you risked (Kaplan: the non-hero who keeps trying).
- The turn is the story's big reversal; it should also be the biggest laugh or set it up.

## 3. Laugh density
- Mark the beats on a timeline. Any stretch over 30 seconds without a laugh needs a
  joke: attitude comment, act-out, tag on a detail.
- Front-load: a joke inside the first 15 seconds tells the room the story is worth waiting for.
- Small laughs along the way are fine; silence is not.

## 4. The ending
- The final line must be a punch or callback, not "...and that's what happened."
- If the true ending is flat, end at the funniest moment and cut the rest.

## Gotchas
- Too much exposition (who, when, where) up front: give only what the next joke needs.
- Telling the story as you remember it, in order, with every detail. Edit like a writer.

## Verify
- [ ] Laugh map: no gap over 30 seconds; biggest laughs at the turn and the end.
