# Talent segment

**Goal:** a number that shows skill and personality within the rules.

## Choose
- Pick what you do best: a lip sync with a story, singing, comedy, dance, or a speciality.
- Check the rules: time limits, music format, props and any restrictions.

## Structure
- A strong opening within seconds, a build, a peak, and a clean finish.
- Show technical skill and character.
- Consider what judges want to see: clarity of the act, difficulty, originality and performance.

## Rehearsal
- Rehearse with the exact music file and in the costume.
- Plan tech cues and the stage layout.
- Prepare a backup file.

## Gotchas
- Number exceeding the time limit.
- A number that depends on a prop forbidden by the rules.

## Verify
- [ ] The number runs in time, within the rules, in full costume.
