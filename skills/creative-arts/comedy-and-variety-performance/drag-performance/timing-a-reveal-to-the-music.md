# Timing a reveal to the music

**Goal:** the reveal happens on the strongest musical moment.

## Process
1. Map the song: intro, verses, chorus, bridge, drop, key change, final chorus. Note times.
2. Pick the hit: a drop, a stop, a key change or a downbeat.
3. Count backwards from the hit: how many beats does the action take?
4. Start the movement early so the reveal lands on the beat.
5. Rehearse with the track at performance volume.

## Performance habits
- Look for the cue in the song, not at the clock.
- Do not telegraph the reveal; keep eyes and posture consistent until the move.
- Hold a beat after the reveal for the audience reaction.

## Variations
- A false reveal first, then the real one.
- A reveal in silence if the edit has a pause.

## Gotchas
- A track with different timing in a venue sound system; test the file.
- Reveals placed before the climax.

## Verify
- [ ] On video, the reveal coincides with the beat within a fraction of a second.
