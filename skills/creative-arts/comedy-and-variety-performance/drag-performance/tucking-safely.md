# Tucking safely

**Goal:** reduce risk when tucking for performance. This skill gives general principles only, not medical advice.

## Principles
- Use garments designed for the purpose and fitted properly; avoid improvised methods with tight tape, elastic bands, or adhesives not made for skin.
- Pain, numbness, discolouration, or difficulty urinating mean stop and loosen. If symptoms persist, seek medical advice.
- Give yourself breaks; tucking for long periods is commonly discouraged in community health guidance, but safe durations are individual, so I am not stating a number. Look for guidance from trans-health organisations or a clinician.
- Stay hydrated and plan toilet access.
- Keep skin clean and dry; heat and sweat raise the risk of irritation and infection.

## Practical
- Test a new method at home before a show.
- Carry a change of underwear and a plan for removal.

## Aftercare
- Wash, check the skin, and rest.

## Gotchas
- Ignoring early discomfort during a long night.
- Using adhesive on delicate skin.

## Verify
- [ ] You know how to loosen quickly and when to remove.
