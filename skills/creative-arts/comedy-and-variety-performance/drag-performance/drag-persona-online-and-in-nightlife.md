# Persona online and in nightlife

**Goal:** a consistent character online and in person while keeping yourself safe.

## Identity
- Choose a drag name that is searchable and unique; check handles and domains across platforms.
- Decide how separate the persona and your private identity are. Some performers keep them separate for safety or work reasons.

## Content
- Use consistent photos and a recognisable look; show the process as well as the polished image.
- Post show dates and venues with tags.
- Keep clips short and showing the strongest moments; see `clipping-stand-up-for-short-form-video` for the editing principles.

## Safety and privacy
- Be careful about location and real-name information in posts.
- Block and report harassment; keep screenshots.
- Check platform rules on nudity, music, and links.

## In nightlife
- Arrive in time; be pleasant to staff and other performers.
- Tag the venue and promoters.

## Business
- Keep a booking email and a media kit; see `comedy-promo-pack` for the structure.

## Gotchas
- Posting a performance with copyrighted music that is removed.
