# Prop and confetti reveals

**Goal:** a visual surprise that suits the music and the venue.

## Types
- A prop pulled from costume: a fan, flag, balloon, or sign.
- Confetti or streamers released from a popper, a tube, a bag, or a cannon.

## Venue and safety
- Ask the venue before using confetti, glitter, or powders. Many venues forbid them or charge a cleaning fee.
- Avoid effects that can slip on the floor, hit eyes, or set off sensors.
- Pyrotechnics and flames need specialist approval; do not improvise.

## Design
1. Decide the moment; see `timing-a-reveal-to-the-music`.
2. Hide the prop in the costume; test access with a quick movement.
3. Practice the release and angle; aim away from faces.
4. Plan how it clears: sweep, remove props.

## Cleanup
- Bring a broom or arrange with staff; leave the stage as you found it.
- Use biodegradable or paper options where possible.

## Gotchas
- Confetti that sticks to wet wigs and floors.
- A prop that jams.

## Verify
- [ ] The venue has agreed and cleanup is planned.
