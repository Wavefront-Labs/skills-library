# Evening gown presentation

**Goal:** a polished entrance, walk and exit that shows the gown.

## The gown
- Fit and finish matter: check hems, seams, closures and movement.
- Check the train, sleeves and hair for safety on stage.
- Match the hair, makeup and jewellery.

## Presentation
1. **Entrance**: confident, with a pose.
2. **Walk**: slow, controlled and aimed at judges; see `runway-walk`.
3. **Turns**: smooth and timed with the music; show the back and sides.
4. **Stage use**: cover the stage so all judges can see; stay in the light.
5. **Exit**: finish with a pose and a clean exit.

## Practice
- Rehearse in the dress and shoes; see `walking-and-dancing-in-heels`.
- Practise navigating stairs, curtains and the stage edge.

## Gotchas
- A train that catches the heel.
- Not checking the lighting colour against the gown.

## Verify
- [ ] The presentation runs within time with no snagging.
