# Wig reveals

**Goal:** a clean surprise at the musical peak.

## Mechanics
- Layer one wig over another, with the top wig secured by a method you can release quickly (a small number of pins or an elastic).
- The bottom wig must be fully secure on its own; see `securing-a-wig-for-dancing`.
- Practise the pull: a smooth sweep up and away, hidden or shown deliberately.

## Staging
- Time the reveal to a musical beat; see `timing-a-reveal-to-the-music`.
- Prepare the hair underneath so it looks finished and not flattened.
- Decide what happens to the discarded wig: toss, hand to a helper, or drop to a prop.

## Rehearsal
- Practise many times in the real shoes and costume.
- Test with the speed and moves of the number.

## Safety
- Do not use too many pins that could hurt when pulled.
- Keep hair-pulling to a minimum; use clips with a quick release.

## Gotchas
- Top wig caught on the bottom.
- The bottom wig comes off with the top.

## Verify
- [ ] The reveal works in ten of ten rehearsals.
