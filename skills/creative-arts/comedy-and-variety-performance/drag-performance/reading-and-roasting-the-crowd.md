# Reading and roasting the crowd

**Goal:** playful insults that the target and the room enjoy.

## The craft
- A "read" is a witty, pointed remark about someone's appearance or behaviour; in drag scenes it is a traditional comedic form, usually delivered with affection.
- Delivery needs confidence, a clear target, and a button.

## Choosing targets
- Choose people who are enjoying the attention, ideally ones who have invited it.
- Avoid personal traumas, bodies the person is sensitive about, or anything you cannot know.
- Read things the person chose: clothes, a drink order, a group's behaviour.

## Structure
1. Observe.
2. Say the line with attitude.
3. Let the person respond; support them if they reply.
4. Finish with a warm or flattering line.

## Care
- Stop if the person looks uncomfortable.
- Check the venue's rules on content.
- Do not read children.

## Gotchas
- Being cruel under the guise of comedy.
- Reusing the same stock lines.

## Verify
- [ ] The target laughs and the room is with you.
