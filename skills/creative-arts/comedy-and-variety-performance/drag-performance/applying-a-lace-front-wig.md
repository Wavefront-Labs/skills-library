# Applying a lace front

**Goal:** a natural-looking hairline that stays secure.

## Preparation
- Prepare the skin: clean, dry, and moisturise away from the hairline.
- Flatten or cover your own hair with a wig cap.
- Trim the lace to the hairline, leaving a small margin; trim in small steps.

## Attachment
- Use adhesive or tape made for lace wigs and skin; follow the manufacturer's directions and patch-test for allergies.
- Apply adhesive on the skin at the hairline, wait until tacky, and press the lace into place from the centre outward.
- Add a layer of powder or a sealing spray if recommended.

## Blending
- Colour the lace with foundation to match the skin, or use concealer.
- Pluck or thin hairs near the hairline for a natural look (be careful).

## Removal
- Use the remover recommended for your adhesive; loosen gently, do not pull; clean the lace.

## Gotchas
- Using household glues.
- Cutting too much lace at once.

## Verify
- [ ] The lace is hidden at arm's length and holds through your number.
