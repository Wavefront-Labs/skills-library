# Masculine movement and swagger

**Goal:** movement that supports the king's character.

## Observation
- Watch real people and performers; note differences in stance, gait, and gestures. Masculinity is varied: choose a specific type.

## Elements (adjust to your character)
- **Stance**: weight grounded, feet a little wider, chest open.
- **Walk**: longer strides, less hip movement, often a rolling or heavy step.
- **Gestures**: economical, larger arm positions, stillness.
- **Face**: relaxed or deliberate expressions.
- **Voice**: lower pitch placement if you speak.

## Practice
1. Choose a character and film yourself walking.
2. Exaggerate and then scale back.
3. Practise sitting, standing, turning and touching objects in character.

## Care
- Avoid strain from lowering the voice or exaggerating postures.

## Gotchas
- A caricature that becomes mocking unless that is the intent.

## Verify
- [ ] On video the stance and walk differ clearly from your usual and match the character.
