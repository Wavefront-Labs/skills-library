# Working with backup dancers

**Goal:** dancers who support your number and enjoy doing it.

## Planning
- Decide what dancers add: frame the lead, add energy, or perform a section.
- Cast reliable dancers; agree on time, payment or exchange, and credit.

## Choreography
- Keep moves simple and clear for the stage size.
- Separate the dancers visually from the lead (positions, costume).
- Teach counts and cues; record rehearsals.

## Rehearsal
- Schedule time for several full runs.
- Rehearse in costume and in a space as big as the venue's stage.
- Plan entrances, exits and changes.

## Stage
- Check the stage dimensions and sightlines.
- Brief the sound and lighting person.

## Professional care
- Pay or compensate fairly; agree beforehand.
- Credit dancers on the bill and in posts.

## Gotchas
- Too many dancers for a small stage.
- Lead too hidden by the dancers.

## Verify
- [ ] Full run with all performers completed twice in the venue's or similar space.
