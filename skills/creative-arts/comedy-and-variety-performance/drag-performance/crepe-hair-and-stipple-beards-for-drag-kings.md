# Crepe hair and stipple beards

**Goal:** a believable beard or moustache that stays on.

## Options
- **Crepe hair**: braided wool sold for theatre; cut small pieces and apply them in layers with spirit gum or a skin-safe adhesive designed for this use; patch-test first.
- **Stipple**: dab a sponge with dark makeup or a stipple colour to create a shadow of stubble.
- **Pre-made pieces**: easier but more costly.

## Crepe process (typical)
1. Clean skin; apply adhesive in a small area.
2. Press small tufts of crepe in the direction of growth, from the neckline upwards.
3. Trim and shape with scissors; comb out.
4. Set with spray if needed.

## Stipple
- Dab a stipple sponge with brown or black makeup and press it lightly in the beard area; build gradually.

## Removal
- Use the remover recommended for the adhesive; avoid pulling; moisturise.

## Gotchas
- A very uniform beard looks fake; vary length and density.
- Adhesive fumes; ventilate.

## Verify
- [ ] The beard holds during movement and looks natural at distance.
