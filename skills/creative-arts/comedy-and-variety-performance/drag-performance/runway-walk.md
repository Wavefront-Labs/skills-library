# Runway walk

**Goal:** a strong, controlled walk that suits your persona.

## Elements
- **Posture**: tall spine, shoulders back, chin level.
- **Foot placement**: heel to toe, feet close to a line; keep the knees soft.
- **Pace**: tied to the music; slow to show the look, faster for energy.
- **Poses**: stops at set points (end of runway, turns).
- **Arms and hands**: relaxed, deliberate movement.

## Practice
1. Practise against a wall for posture.
2. Walk to music; film yourself.
3. Practise turns: pivot on the ball of the foot and move smoothly.
4. Practise in shoes and costume.

## Stage adaptation
- On small stages, shorten steps and add poses.
- On a runway, mark the stops and plan where judges are.

## Gotchas
- Looking at the floor.
- Walking too fast for the music.

## Verify
- [ ] On video, posture and pace are steady and the poses land on beats.
