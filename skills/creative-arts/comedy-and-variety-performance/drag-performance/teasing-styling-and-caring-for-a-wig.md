# Teasing, styling and caring for a wig

**Goal:** a big, shaped wig that lasts several shows.

## Styling
- Put the wig on a wig head or stand with pins.
- Tease sections by brushing back-and-forth toward the root with a teasing comb; shape the volume.
- Smooth the outer layer lightly.
- Use hairspray designed for wigs; lightly mist between layers.
- Synthetic fibres can melt with heat; check the fibre type and whether it is heat-resistant before using a hot tool, and test on a hidden section.

## Washing
- Brush gently first.
- Soak in cool water with a wig or mild shampoo; swish; rinse.
- Do not wring or rub; blot with a towel and air dry on a stand.
- Real hair wigs can be treated with products designed for hair.

## Storage
- Store on a stand or in a box with the hair smoothed; keep away from heat and sunlight.
- Use a wig bag for travel.

## Gotchas
- Washing a heavily teased wig; it may be unrecoverable.
- Brushing wet synthetics.

## Verify
- [ ] The wig keeps shape after a show and brushes out without matting.
