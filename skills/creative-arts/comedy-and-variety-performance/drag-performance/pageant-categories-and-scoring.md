# Pageant categories and scoring

**Goal:** enter a pageant knowing what will be judged.

## Common categories (vary by system)
- Evening gown, talent, interview or onstage question, and sometimes swimwear, casual, or other categories. Systems differ, so read the entry rules of the pageant you are entering.
- Judging typically scores each category with a points system; the weights differ by pageant.

## Preparing
1. Obtain the rulebook or the entry packet: categories, time limits, dress requirements, scoring weights, and eligibility.
2. Plan each category; see `pageant-talent-segment`, `pageant-interview-and-onstage-question` and `evening-gown-presentation`.
3. Budget for entry fees, costumes and travel.

## Etiquette
- Be on time, respect the production, and be gracious to other contestants.
- Follow rules on music, props and content strictly.

## After
- Ask for judges' feedback if offered.
- Accept results.

## Gotchas
- Missing a rule about props.
- Underestimating the weight of the interview.

## Verify
- [ ] You have read the current rules for this pageant and have a plan for each category.
