# Contour and highlight for stage

**Goal:** features readable from the back of the room.

## Principles
- Stage lights flatten faces; makeup is stronger than for daylight.
- Shade (darker) recedes and highlight (lighter) advances. Use them to reshape the nose, cheekbones, jaw and forehead.

## Process (typical)
1. Foundation and concealer; set with powder.
2. Contour with a matte shade a couple of tones deeper than the skin, in the hollows of the cheeks, the sides of the nose, along the jaw, and at the hairline.
3. Highlight on the centre of the forehead, the bridge of the nose, the high points of the cheekbones, the chin, and under the brow.
4. Blend edges thoroughly; sharp lines may be intentional but must be clean.
5. Set with powder.

## Adjusting
- Check the look from a distance and with a phone camera flash and under stage lights where you can.
- Warm lights can change colours; test.

## Gotchas
- Shimmer highlight that reads as sweat.
- Contour too low and heavy, reading as a mask.

## Verify
- [ ] The face reads from several metres away without hard edges up close.
