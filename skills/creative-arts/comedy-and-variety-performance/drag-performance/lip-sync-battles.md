# Lip sync battles

**Goal:** win a short head-to-head round on impact.

## Format
- Rules differ: song chosen by the host or by the performer, one or two rounds, time limits, and judging by the crowd or a panel. Ask the organiser.

## Preparation
1. Choose or prepare songs you know word for word.
2. Prepare a version cut to the allowed length with a clear build and peak.
3. Have a strong first 20 seconds and a memorable final moment.
4. Plan one or two tricks or reveals that suit the song.
5. Practise in costume and shoes.

## During
- Play to the room and the judges; make eye contact.
- Match the lyrics' emotions with your face.
- Keep the energy and avoid risky moves you have not rehearsed.

## Spirit
- Be gracious to your opponent; the audience and other performers notice.
- Accept the result.

## Gotchas
- A song with a long intro.
- Over-relying on a trick that fails.

## Verify
- [ ] The cut hits the peak within the time limit and you can perform it from memory.
