# Interview and onstage question

**Goal:** answer clearly, in character, and within the time limit.

## Preparation
- Learn the title's role and responsibilities: public appearances, charity work, representation.
- Write a short statement of your platform or goals.
- Prepare stories about your drag history, community involvement and plans.

## Practising
1. Gather likely questions; practise aloud.
2. Aim for short answers, with a clear opening point and an example.
3. Record yourself; check pace and clarity.
4. Practise onstage questions standing, with a microphone.

## On the day
- Breathe, take a moment, and answer the question asked.
- Stay in persona and be authentic.
- Do not insult other contestants.

## Gotchas
- Memorised answers that do not fit the question.
- Rambling.

## Verify
- [ ] Each answer is under the allowed time and ends with a clear point.
