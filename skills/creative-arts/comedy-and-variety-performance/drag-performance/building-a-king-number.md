# Building a king number

**Goal:** a number with a clear character and arc.

## Process
1. **Concept**: the character and story (a heartbroken crooner, a rock star, a comic villain).
2. **Track**: choose and edit; see `building-a-drag-persona-and-lip-sync-number` and the lyric tips in that skill.
3. **Structure**: entrance, build, peak, final pose.
4. **Moves**: movement in the king's style; see `masculine-movement-and-swagger`.
5. **Costume and look**: silhouette, hair, facial hair; see `king-silhouette-and-contour`.
6. **Reveals or surprises**: optional; see `timing-a-reveal-to-the-music`.
7. **Rehearse** in full costume.

## Staging
- Check stage size; plan travel.
- Use props that suit the character.

## Audience
- Consider tipping and interaction; plan how to hold the tip.

## Gotchas
- A strong costume with no number structure.
- A track that is too long.

## Verify
- [ ] The character is readable in the first ten seconds.
