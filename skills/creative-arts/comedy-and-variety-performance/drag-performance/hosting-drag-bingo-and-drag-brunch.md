# Hosting drag bingo and drag brunch

**Goal:** a lively show that fits the venue's routine.

## Drag bingo
- **Format**: a host calls numbers, the audience plays, and winners claim prizes; hosts fill the gaps with banter, performances and games.
- **Equipment**: cards, markers, a number-calling method, prizes, a microphone.
- **Rules**: explain clearly; confirm winners and check claims.
- **Pacing**: keep rounds moving and fill the gap between rounds with comedy.

## Drag brunch
- Performers entertain diners during a meal.
- Coordinate with the venue about service timing, show length (check the venue's), music and stage space.
- Keep numbers and talk away from peak serving moments if requested.

## Both
- Warm the crowd; see `warming-a-room-and-running-a-live-show-as-mc`.
- Keep content suitable to the audience, including mixed or family groups where relevant.
- Agree fee and tips with the venue beforehand.

## Gotchas
- Not reading the room: the audience may include guests unfamiliar with drag.
- Overruns affecting kitchen schedules.
