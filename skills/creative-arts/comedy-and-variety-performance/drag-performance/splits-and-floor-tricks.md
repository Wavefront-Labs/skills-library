# Splits and floor tricks

**Goal:** perform floor moves without injury.

## Preparation
- Warm up with cardio and dynamic movement.
- Build flexibility gradually: hamstring and hip flexor stretches over weeks, not days. A teacher or a dance class can guide you.
- Strengthen the core and legs to control descent.

## Technique
- Lower with control by the hands and legs; do not drop.
- In heels or tight costumes, check what the costume allows.
- Plan the exit: how you get up without losing the beat.

## Surface
- Check the floor for grip and cleanliness. Avoid wet or sticky floors and glitter.
- Use knee pads under costume for floor work if appropriate.

## Safety
- Stop at pain.
- Do not attempt a move on stage that you have not performed in practice.

## Gotchas
- Splits in a costume that rips.
- Doing floor tricks after drinking.

- [ ] You can do the trick cleanly in costume on the venue's floor type.
