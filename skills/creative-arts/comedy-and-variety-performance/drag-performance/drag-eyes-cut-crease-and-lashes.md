# Drag eyes, cut crease and lashes

**Goal:** eyes that read from afar and stay put.

## Cut crease
1. Prime the lid.
2. Define the crease with a bold, clean line above the natural socket, often drawn with a flat brush or a pencil.
3. Fill the lid with a bright or light shade, and blend colour above the line.
4. Clean the edges with concealer.

## Lashes
- Measure the strip against your lash line and trim the outer end if it is long.
- Apply lash glue to the strip, wait until tacky, and place as close to the lash line as possible.
- Press the corners and apply a liner to blend.
- Layering two pairs is a common stage choice; ensure comfort.

## Comfort
- Use lash glue designed for skin; check for sensitivity; avoid contact with the eyes.
- Remove with an oil-based or manufacturer-recommended remover.

## Gotchas
- Lashes too heavy for your lids.
- Cut crease drawn asymmetrically; check in a mirror.

## Verify
- [ ] Both eyes match from a distance and lashes stay on during movement.
