# Colour-correcting beard shadow

**Goal:** a smooth, even skin tone over a visible shadow.

## Principles
- Beard shadow reads as blue-grey to dark. Opposing colours cancel: an orange or peach corrector neutralises blue in lighter skin; a deeper red or orange suits darker skin. Test the shade on your own skin.
- Start from clean-shaven skin close to the show.

## Process (typical)
1. Moisturise and prime.
2. Apply the corrector only on the shadowed areas, patting not rubbing.
3. Layer foundation matched to your face; use a full-coverage product.
4. Apply concealer if needed.
5. Set with powder.
6. Repeat thin layers rather than one thick one.

## Tips
- Shave as close as possible; a fresh shave a few hours before.
- Check in daylight and under light similar to the venue's.

## Gotchas
- Too much orange showing through.
- Thick layers that crack when you move your jaw.

## Verify
- [ ] No grey patches visible at arm's length in bright light.
