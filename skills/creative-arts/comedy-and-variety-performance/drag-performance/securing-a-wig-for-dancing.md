# Securing a wig for dancing

**Goal:** a wig that stays on when you move.

## Layers (typical)
1. Pin your own hair flat, then add a wig cap.
2. Add a wig grip (velvet band) or similar.
3. Put the wig on from the front and settle it at the hairline.
4. Pin through the wig's built-in tracks or with wig clips and bobby pins into your own hair or the cap.
5. Use the wig's adjustable straps or elastic.
6. For a lace front, adhesive adds security; see `applying-a-lace-front-wig`.

## Testing
- Shake your head, spin, do your biggest movement, bend over.
- Adjust until nothing slips.

## Show preparation
- Carry extra pins and a small mirror.
- Plan wig reveals separately; see `wig-reveals`.

## Care for the head
- Avoid pins pulling hair; check comfort.
- Do not secure so tightly it gives you a headache.

## Gotchas
- A new wig not tested.
- Wigs with a high ponytail or large volume will move more.

## Verify
- [ ] The wig survives the biggest move at full speed.
