# Gluing down and blocking brows

**Goal:** a smooth surface to paint new brows on.

## Common methods
- **Glue stick** (water-soluble school-type): comb brow hairs flat against the direction of growth, apply thin layers of glue stick, let dry, repeat several times, then powder.
- **Soap**: a similar approach using a damp bar of soap worked into the brows, then sealed; details differ by performer.
- Both methods are widely used; individual skin reactions vary, so patch-test.

## Process (typical)
1. Clean and dry the brows.
2. Brush hairs flat upward and outward.
3. Apply thin layers of product; let each dry.
4. Seal with a translucent powder.
5. Apply a colour corrector or foundation and concealer to cover.
6. Redraw the brows higher or in the desired shape.

## Removal
- Use warm water and gentle cleanser; peel or wipe gently; avoid pulling.
- Moisturise afterwards.

## Cautions
- Do not use strong adhesives like superglue or skin-unsafe products on skin.
- Stop if irritation occurs.

## Gotchas
- Applying a thick layer in one go; it flakes.

## Verify
- [ ] The surface is smooth and flat under the foundation.
