# Cutting foam hip pads

**Goal:** a comfortable silhouette that stays in place.

## Materials
- Medium-density upholstery foam or similar, with a few thicknesses; spray adhesive; fabric or tights to cover; elastic or a base garment.

## Process (typical)
1. Decide the desired shape: curve at hips and bottom; sketch it.
2. Cut foam into pieces, with thicker sections where more volume is needed.
3. Taper edges by shaving them with scissors or a craft knife on a protected surface so the pad blends into your body.
4. Glue layers if needed and let it cure in a ventilated space.
5. Cover with stretch fabric or put into a garment.
6. Try under your costume and adjust.

## Wear
- Attach to a base garment like shapewear or tights so they do not shift.
- Watch heat: foam traps heat; take breaks and drink water.
- Cutting tools are sharp; use care.

## Gotchas
- Visible edges under a tight costume.
- Foam that absorbs sweat and does not dry; air it out.

## Verify
- [ ] The silhouette looks smooth and you can dance and sit without displacing the pads.
