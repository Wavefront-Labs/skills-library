# King silhouette and contour

**Goal:** a masculine shape and face that read on stage.

## Body
- Build shoulders and width with tailoring: jackets with structure, suits fitted to the shoulder.
- Binding: see `binding-safely`.
- Options for shape: layers, padded vests, or thicker fabrics.
- A packer is a prop some performers use; decide for your character.

## Face
- Contour to strengthen the jaw and brow and to define the cheekbones; see `contour-and-highlight-for-stage`.
- Add brows that are straighter and thicker; consider facial hair.
- Hair: short styles, slick or textured, or a wig.

## Check
- View yourself in profile and from the front in a mirror and on camera.
- Check from a distance.

## Variation
- Kings play many types: gentlemen, rock stars, comedic characters; choose the silhouette to match.

## Gotchas
- A silhouette that restricts breathing.
- Clothes that fit the body poorly.

## Verify
- [ ] The outline reads clearly from the back of the room.
