# Walking and dancing in heels

**Goal:** control, stamina and safe performances on stage.

## Choose
- A shoe that fits in length and width; a secure strap or boot design for dancing.
- A heel height you can control; build up gradually.
- A platform reduces the incline; a stable block heel is easier than a thin stiletto.

## Technique
- Posture: stand tall with the ribs over the hips; take smaller steps than flat shoes.
- Walk heel to toe, placing the foot under your centre of gravity.
- Keep knees soft and engage the core.
- Practise turns on the ball of the foot and check the floor surface.

## Conditioning
- Strengthen calves, ankles and feet; stretch after.
- Practise at home on a variety of surfaces.

## Care
- Blister protection (tape or pads), heel grips, and gel inserts.
- Rest your feet between numbers if possible.

## Safety
- Check the stage for gaps, tape, glitter and spills.
- Check heel tips and the strap before each show.

## Gotchas
- Wearing new shoes for the first time on stage.

## Verify
- [ ] You can do your number's moves in the shoes at full speed.
