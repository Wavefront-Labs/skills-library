# Binding safely

**Goal:** reduce risk when binding for performance. General principles, not medical advice.

## Principles
- Use a binder made for the purpose, in the correct size; avoid bandages, duct tape, or plastic wrap, which restrict breathing and can hurt ribs and skin.
- Wearing a smaller size to "flatten more" increases risk. Follow the maker's sizing and wear guidance.
- Avoid strenuous exercise and sleeping in a binder; take breaks, and give your body days off; guidance from trans-health organisations on duration varies, so check a current source.
- Stop for pain, trouble breathing, dizziness, or numbness.
- Keep skin clean and dry; check for irritation.

## Performance
- Plan breaks backstage; stretch and breathe.
- Wear a layer over the binder for sweat.
- Practise your number in the binder before a show.

## Cautions
- Pre-existing conditions (asthma, rib or back issues) may affect safety; consult a clinician.

## Gotchas
- A new binder worn first on a long night.

## Verify
- [ ] The binder fits and you can take a full breath while moving.
