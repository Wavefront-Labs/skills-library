# Character monologues

**Goal:** a solo piece where the character reveals themselves through speech.

## Structure
1. **Situation and listener**: who they speak to and why now.
2. **Objective**: what they want from the listener.
3. **Reveals**: each paragraph reveals more about their flaw or world.
4. **Heightening**: the stakes or oddness grow.
5. **Turn and ending**: a final revelation, a failed attempt, or a quiet button.

## Craft
- Write in the character's rhythm and vocabulary.
- Give the listener a presence: pauses, reactions, interruptions.
- Use specific details (names, numbers) for credibility.

## Performance
- Know the objective at each moment.
- Vary pace and volume to avoid monotone.
- Use a simple physical life: position, a prop, a gesture.

## Length
Short monologues often run 2-4 minutes; check your slot.

## Gotchas
- A list of jokes with no objective.
- A monologue that tells the audience what the character feels instead of showing it.

## Verify
- [ ] Each section reveals something new about the character.
