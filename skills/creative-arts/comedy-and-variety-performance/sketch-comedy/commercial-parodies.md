# Commercial parodies

**Goal:** a short sketch in an advertising voice that exposes something the real ads hide.

## Structure
1. Choose a format: testimonial, before-and-after, side-effects voiceover, celebrity endorsement, infomercial.
2. Invent the product or service with a built-in flaw or a dark truth.
3. Play the ad earnestly; the performers must commit to the format. See the commitment notes in `finding-and-heightening-the-game-of-the-sketch`.
4. Heighten with claims, then disclaimers, then the sting at the end.

## Details that sell it
- Slogan and jingle.
- Realistic phrases: "ask your doctor", "results not typical".
- A tidy button: the voiceover adds the damning line in the quick-read legal voice.

## Notes
- Use fictional brand names to avoid issues with real products.
- Keep it short; commercial parodies work in 30-90 seconds.
- Avoid naming real people or products in a way that could be defamatory.

## Gotchas
- A product with no clear flaw: nothing to heighten.

## Verify
- [ ] A listener hears the ad voice immediately and knows the product within 10 seconds.
