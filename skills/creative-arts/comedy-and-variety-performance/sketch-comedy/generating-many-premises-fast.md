# Generating many premises fast

**Goal:** a pile of ideas so you can choose the best rather than the first.

## Techniques
1. **Observations list**: write down annoying or strange rules of everyday life (waiting rooms, group chats, meetings). Ask "what if this were taken literally?"
2. **What-if**: take a normal situation and change one fact.
3. **Roles**: pick two people with conflicting goals in a shared place.
4. **Genre swap**: put a situation in a different format.
5. **News and trends**: jot topical items and look for a human angle.

## Process
- Set a timer (10 minutes); write one-line premises, no judging.
- Aim for 20 or more per session.
- Review later: circle those that make you smile and where you can state a game in one line.

## Filter
- Is there a clear first unusual thing?
- Can you find 3 or more beats?
- Do you have a clear character want?
- Is it a game or a single joke?

## Gotchas
- Spending the session on one idea.
- Choosing a topical premise with a short shelf life.

## Verify
- [ ] 20+ premises generated and a shortlist of three chosen.
