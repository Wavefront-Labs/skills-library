# Character point of view and want

**Goal:** a character the audience can predict and be surprised by.

## Elements
- **Point of view**: the lens through which they see the world (a rule they live by, a belief).
- **Want**: what they pursue in this scene or in life.
- **Flaw or blind spot**: what stops them, often tied to the point of view.
- **Status**: how they see themselves relative to others; see `status-in-stand-up` (the same status ideas apply).

## Procedure
1. Write a sentence: "This is a person who believes ___, wants ___, and cannot see ___."
2. Choose a physical or vocal anchor (posture, rhythm).
3. Put them in a situation where the point of view creates problems.
4. Let them pursue the want with commitment.

## Kaplan's lens
Steve Kaplan describes comic protagonists as non-heroes who pursue goals and fail; see `the-non-hero-stance`.

## Gotchas
- A character defined only by an accent or a prop.
- A want that is vague; make it specific and immediate.

## Verify
- [ ] The character's choices can be predicted from the sentence and still be funny.
