# Revue running order

**Goal:** a show that builds and varies from start to finish.

## Principles (common practice, adapt to your show)
- **Opener**: a strong, ensemble piece that sets the tone and shows the cast.
- **Alternate**: character vs premise sketches, big cast vs duos, loud vs quiet, topical vs absurd.
- **Placement**: strong sketch before the interval and a strong closer.
- **Spacing**: place heavy tech or costume changes between pieces that cover them.

## Process
1. Write each piece on a card with type, cast, tech, and runtime.
2. Try several orders in a table read.
3. Check that the same performers are not on in consecutive pieces without time to change.
4. Add transitions; see `transitions-and-blackouts-between-scenes`.
5. Total the times with changeovers; add a margin.

## Revision
- After previews, move pieces that sag; cut the weakest.
- Keep a record of the final order and cue sheet.

## Gotchas
- Two similar pieces in a row.
- A closing sketch that is the weakest.

## Verify
- [ ] The order alternates type and energy and is timed with margin.
