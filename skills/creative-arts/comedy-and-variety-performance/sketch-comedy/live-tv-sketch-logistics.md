# Live TV sketch logistics

**Goal:** a sketch that works under broadcast conditions with no retakes.

## Timing
- Time to the second; the director needs to know the runtime and the end cue.
- Build in a short hold at the end for a graphic or a transition.

## Staging
- Camera blocking: performers must hit marks so cameras can frame them.
- Plan entrances and exits for camera coverage.
- Avoid business that requires too much movement for camera operators.

## Quick changes
- Plan the order of sketches so costume changes fit the gaps; assign dressers.
- Rehearse changes with timing.

## Script support
- Use cue cards or teleprompter as the show provides; learn lines too.
- Mark cues for lines that must be on time.

## Rehearsal
- Dress rehearsal with cameras shows problems with sightlines and timing.
- Rehearse the transitions as well as the sketches.

## Contingency
- Agree what the cast does if a line is dropped, a prop fails or a cue is late. A rehearsed recovery phrase helps.

## Gotchas
- A sketch that runs over and cuts into the next segment.

## Verify
- [ ] A timed run with cameras and costume changes completes within the slot.
