# Ending options beyond the blackout

**Goal:** an ending that suits the game.

## Options
- **Button**: a final line or image that tops the last beat. Short and clean.
- **Reversal**: the game flips; the straight character joins in or the premise is revealed to be different.
- **Callback or runner payoff**: the last line returns to something planted earlier.
- **Exit line**: a character leaves with a line that sums up the game.
- **Escalation to chaos**: the situation explodes; works if staging allows.
- **Anticlimax**: the sketch ends quietly after a big build, when the contrast is the joke.
- **Blackout**: lights out on the strongest punch; only effective with clean tech cues.

## Choosing
1. State the game.
2. Ask: what is the most satisfying thing to do with it at the end?
3. Test two or three endings in a read-through.

## Staging
- Call the tech cue on a specific word.
- Freeze or hold for a beat so the audience can laugh.

## Gotchas
- Ending with "and they all leave".
- A twist that contradicts the premise.

## Verify
- [ ] The last line gets the biggest laugh or the clearest closure.
