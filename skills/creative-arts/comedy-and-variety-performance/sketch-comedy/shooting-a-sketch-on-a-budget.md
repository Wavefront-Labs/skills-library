# Shooting a sketch on a budget

**Goal:** a funny, watchable sketch with simple resources.

## Script for the budget
- One or two locations, a small cast, minimal props, and daytime shoots.
- Write the game so it plays in simple setups; see `finding-and-heightening-the-game-of-the-sketch`.

## Preparation
1. Shot list: wide, mid, close-ups on reactions.
2. Schedule the shoot with margin.
3. Gather props, costume and location permissions.

## Shooting
- **Sound first**: clean dialogue matters more than image; use a decent mic near the actors and monitor with headphones.
- Frame so faces are visible and eyes are readable.
- Shoot a few takes of each setup with variations in energy.
- Capture reaction shots and cutaways; they give you editing choices.

## Performance
- Screen acting is smaller than stage acting; keep reactions real.
- Hold for laughs in the script; do not step on lines.

## After
- Review footage promptly; see `editing-for-comic-timing`.

## Gotchas
- Windy outdoor locations with no windshield.
- Over-complicated camera moves.

## Verify
- [ ] Each line is audible and each key reaction is covered.
