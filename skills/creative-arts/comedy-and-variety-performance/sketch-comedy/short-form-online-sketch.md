# Short-form online sketch

**Goal:** a sketch that works in under a minute on a phone.

## Writing
- One premise and one game; no setup beyond a sentence.
- Open on the unusual thing in the first seconds.
- Plan an ending that makes the viewer replay or share.
- Characters can be played by one performer using costume or position changes.

## Shooting
- Choose the aspect ratio the platform uses (often vertical 9:16, but check current specs).
- Good light and clean sound outweigh camera quality.
- Frame faces close.

## Editing
- Trim to the beat; remove pauses.
- Add burned-in captions.
- End cleanly, loopable if possible.

## Posting
- Consistent format and series names build audience.
- Check platform rules on music, claims, and content.
- Respond to comments.

## Gotchas
- A sketch that needs the first ten seconds to explain.
- Using copyrighted music without permission.

## Verify
- [ ] The hook lands in 3 seconds and the sketch ends in about a minute or less.
