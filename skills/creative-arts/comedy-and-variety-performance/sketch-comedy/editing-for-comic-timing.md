# Editing for comic timing

**Goal:** cut so the jokes land.

## Principles
- Cut to the reaction at the moment of the punch rather than showing the speaker too long.
- Remove dead air between lines but leave a beat before key punchlines.
- A wide shot can hold physical gags; cut to close-ups for reactions.
- Match energy between takes; choose the best performance.

## Process
1. Assemble a rough cut following the script.
2. Trim each line's head and tail, then tighten the whole.
3. Check each joke: is the punch clear, is the reaction visible, is the pause right?
4. Add sound: remove noise, add music or effects sparingly, and mix dialogue clearly.
5. Test with viewers who have not seen it; watch their faces.

## Tools
Any editor (DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro, etc.) works; the principles are the same.

## Gotchas
- Cutting so tight that jokes have no room.
- Music that covers the laughs in a live-audience cut.

## Verify
- [ ] First-time viewers laugh at the intended points.
