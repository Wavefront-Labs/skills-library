# Punch-up sessions

**Goal:** raise the laugh density of a script that already works structurally.

## Setup
- The writer shares the script and identifies the lines or sections that need help.
- The group agrees the tone and the boundaries (the game and characters stay).

## Process
1. Read the script aloud.
2. Mark flat lines and transitional lines.
3. For each, pitch alternatives rapidly (aim for many quick ideas, not careful ones).
4. Record every suggestion in a shared doc.
5. The writer picks, and may adapt.
6. Reread with the changes.

## Roles
- A scribe records, a moderator keeps time, and the writer decides.

## Craft
- Replace generic words with specifics.
- Move the funniest word to the end; see `tightening-a-joke-punch-word-last-and-word-economy`.
- Add tags or heightening beats; see `writing-a-tag-run`.

## Gotchas
- Changing the structure in a punch-up.
- Pitching jokes that fit the pitcher's voice rather than the character's.
