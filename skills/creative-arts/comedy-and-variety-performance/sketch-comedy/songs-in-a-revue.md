# Songs in a revue

**Goal:** a song that adds a new texture and earns its place.

## Why include one
- It can change the energy, show off a performer, or deliver a sharper joke than dialogue.

## Writing
1. Start with a comic premise (a game) and a character want, as for a sketch.
2. Choose a style that serves the joke: ballad, patter, pastiche.
3. Write lyrics with the funniest word at the end of lines; see `comedy-song-structure`.
4. Decide accompaniment: piano, backing track, or a cappella.

## Staging
- Lighting or staging change signals the shift.
- Keep it short; many revue songs are 2-4 minutes.

## Placement
- Often used as a change of pace after dialogue-heavy sketches or before an interval.
- Check running order with `revue-running-order`.

## Rights
For parody songs, see `parody-and-copyright`.

## Gotchas
- A song that explains the joke.
- A strong singer with weak lyrics.

## Verify
- [ ] The song has a clear game and a payoff in its last line.
