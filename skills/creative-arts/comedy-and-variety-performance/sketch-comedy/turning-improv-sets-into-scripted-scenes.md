# Turning improv sets into scripted scenes

**Goal:** keep the discovery of improv and add structure.

## Process
1. **Record** the improv session (audio or video).
2. **Extract** the game, characters, and best lines.
3. **Outline** beats, using the order of discovery as a starting point.
4. **Tighten**: cut dead ends, merge repeated beats; see `mapping-heightening-beats`.
5. **Script** key lines; leave room for performers to find fresh moments if the show style allows.
6. **Rehearse** and test with the original performers.

## Notes
- Agree credit and ownership of material before it goes into a show; ask the people who made it.
- Improvised material often has a weak ending; write one.
- Scenes found in improv often have good character and game but need an economical start.

## Gotchas
- Scripting every word and losing the energy; keep the intent.
- Forgetting the base reality.

## Verify
- [ ] The scripted version retains the game and gets laughs in a read-through.
