# Catchphrases and recurring characters

**Goal:** a character the audience wants to see again.

## Catchphrase
- Short, rhythmic and tied to the character's want or view, so it is natural when they say it.
- Place it where the audience expects it (the button or a turn), then occasionally subvert.
- Do not force one into every character.

## Recurring characters
1. Fix the core: view, want, look, and voice.
2. Give each appearance a new situation that tests the core.
3. Raise the stakes or change the setting; avoid repeating the same scene.
4. Introduce supporting characters for variety.

## Fatigue
- Track audience response across appearances; note laughs for the catchphrase.
- Rest a character before the audience tires.
- Retire with a finale that pays off the character's arc.

## Practical
- Keep a character bible: voice, look, relationships, rules.
- Check the format: a sketch show with a weekly slot needs a plan for the number of appearances.

## Gotchas
- A catchphrase that carries all the laughs and stops the sketch.

## Verify
- [ ] Each appearance has a new situation and the character's core stays recognisable.
