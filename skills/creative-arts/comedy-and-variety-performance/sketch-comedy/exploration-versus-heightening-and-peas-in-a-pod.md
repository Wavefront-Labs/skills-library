# Exploration versus heightening, and peas in a pod

**Goal:** know which move you are making and choose it on purpose.

## Vocabulary (UCB manual usage)
- **Exploration**: after the first unusual thing, stay with it and ask what else is true about this world or this person (more detail, same level of absurdity).
- **Heightening**: raise the stakes or absurdity of the game (more extreme, bigger consequence). See `finding-and-heightening-the-game-of-the-sketch`.
- Many scenes alternate: explore to establish the logic, heighten once the pattern is clear.

## Peas in a pod
A pattern where several characters share the same unusual trait or behaviour, so there is no straight person pushing against the game. The comedy comes from the shared world and the specifics of the pattern, not from contrast.

## Writing
1. Decide your approach: contrast (straight character) or shared world (peas).
2. For peas, give each character a distinct version of the trait so it does not become repetition.
3. Explore the rules of the shared world in the first beats; heighten in later beats.
4. Add an outsider or a reality check late if you need a turn; see `the-turn-or-second-game`.

## Gotchas
- Peas scenes with no escalation read as a list of similar lines.
- Heightening before the audience understands the game.
