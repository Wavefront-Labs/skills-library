# Comedy song structure

**Goal:** a short song that escalates and ends on a laugh.

## Structure
- **Premise**: the verse establishes a character and the game.
- **Chorus**: the hook, a repeating line the audience can hold; make it rhythmic.
- **Second verse**: heighten the game.
- **Bridge**: the turn: a new fact or a change of tone.
- **Final chorus**: a variation of the chorus with a twist.
- **Tag**: a last spoken line or musical stinger.

## Craft
- Keep lyrics clear and place punches on rhymes at line ends.
- Use repetition for rhythm and variation for surprise.
- Tell a story across verses; avoid a series of unrelated jokes.

## Music
- Choose a style that fits the character (ballad, country, patter).
- Simple chords and strong rhythm let lyrics carry the comedy.

## Length
Short comic songs commonly run 2-4 minutes.

## Gotchas
- A strong chorus with weak verses.
- A song that repeats one joke.

## Verify
- [ ] The final verse or tag gets the largest laugh.
