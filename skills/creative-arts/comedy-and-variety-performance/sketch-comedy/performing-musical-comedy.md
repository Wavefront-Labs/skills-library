# Performing musical comedy

**Goal:** make every lyric clear and every laugh land inside the music.

## Preparation
- Learn the lyrics and music so well you can perform them as a character.
- Mark where laughs are expected and make room for them in the arrangement.

## Delivery
- Diction first: consonants clear, punch words emphasised.
- Commit to the character as in acting; do not wink.
- Use breath planning so you are not gasping on punch lines.
- Hold for laughs; the pianist or track needs a cue to vamp or wait.

## With accompaniment
- A live accompanist can wait for laughs; a track cannot. Plan a bar of vamp or choose a track with built-in pauses.
- Rehearse together; agree signals; see `working-with-a-musical-director`.

## Staging
- Keep movement simple and tied to the lyric; save big business for beats.
- Check the sound mix so the lyrics are audible.

## Gotchas
- Singing through laughs so the next line is lost.
- Over-singing at the expense of the joke.

## Verify
- [ ] A listener can understand every punch line on the first hearing.
