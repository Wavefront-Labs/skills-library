# Takes, double takes and slow burns

**Goal:** a reaction that shows thought and gets a laugh.

## Types
- **Take**: a reaction to something surprising: notice, register, react.
- **Double take**: look, look away, then look back with a bigger reaction when it registers.
- **Slow burn**: a gradual build of irritation or realisation across several seconds.

## Technique
1. **Notice**: eyes lead; the head follows.
2. **Register**: hold a beat; let the audience see the thought.
3. **React**: face, body, voice. Size the reaction to the situation.
4. For the double take, decide the delay and the size difference between the first and second look.
5. For a slow burn, plan the steps (calm, puzzled, annoyed, furious) and the timing between them.

## Staging
- Face the audience or camera so the eyes are visible.
- Keep the reaction clear without scenery chewing. On camera, smaller movements read.

## Gotchas
- Reacting before the cause is clear.
- Repeating the same take twice.

- [ ] On video, the stages are readable and the laugh comes at the register or the burst.
