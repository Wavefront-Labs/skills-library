# Genre mashups and anachronism

**Goal:** a clash that produces new jokes rather than a single gag.

## Method
1. Choose two worlds that have different rules (a medieval court and a customer service call; a western and an HR meeting).
2. List the conventions of each: language, props, power structure, goals.
3. Decide the collision rule: who is from which world, and who notices.
4. Generate beats from both sides: old-world logic applied to modern problems and vice versa.
5. Keep one world as the straight reality and let the other disrupt it.

## Craft
- Specific vocabulary from each world supplies the humour.
- Characters should want something real; the clash must affect their goal.
- Escalate: raise the stakes of the mismatch.

## Cautions
- Mashups can be one-joke ideas; plan the ladder before writing; see `mapping-heightening-beats`.
- Check that references are known to the audience.

## Gotchas
- Using the mashup as the sketch without a character want.

## Verify
- [ ] The sketch has at least four beats that come from the collision.
