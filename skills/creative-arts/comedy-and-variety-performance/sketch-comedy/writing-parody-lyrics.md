# Writing parody lyrics

**Goal:** new lyrics that fit an existing tune and make a joke about a new subject.

## Process
1. Choose a song the audience knows and a subject with a clear game (a mismatch between the song's tone and the new topic).
2. Mark the song's stresses and rhyme scheme for each line.
3. Draft the lyrics syllable by syllable to match the rhythm; sing them along with the track.
4. Place the funniest word on the rhyme at the line end; see `tightening-a-joke-punch-word-last-and-word-economy`.
5. Build a progression: set the premise in verse one, heighten in verse two, end with a payoff.
6. Test aloud.

## Craft
- Keep references from the original that the audience expects and subvert one or two.
- Check clarity: the audience hears the words once.

## Legal
Parody and copyright rules differ by country; see `parody-and-copyright`.

## Gotchas
- Lines that stretch the melody.
- A parody that only changes nouns.
