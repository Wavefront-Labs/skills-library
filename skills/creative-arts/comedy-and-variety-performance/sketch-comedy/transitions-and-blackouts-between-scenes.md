# Transitions and blackouts between scenes

**Goal:** keep momentum between pieces.

## Techniques
- **Blackout**: quick lights cut at the end of a piece; a clear cue to applaud.
- **Sound bridge**: short music or sound effect covers the change.
- **Pre-set**: props placed so the next scene starts quickly.
- **Front-of-curtain**: a short scene in front of a curtain while a set changes behind.
- **Cast changes**: performers exit in the dark while others enter.

## Process
1. List the required changes for each scene.
2. Assign people to move props; label items.
3. Rehearse transitions with lights and sound.
4. Time the change; aim for the shortest practicable.
5. Write a cue sheet; call cues on a consistent system.

## Safety
- Dark stage changes: use glow tape on edges and obstacles, brief the cast on routes.
- Check with the stage manager.

## Gotchas
- A long, silent change that kills energy; add music or a short bit.
- Overcomplicated sets.

## Verify
- [ ] Transitions run within planned time and without incidents in tech rehearsal.
