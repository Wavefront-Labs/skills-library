# Giving and taking notes

**Goal:** make notes useful and the writer's voice intact.

## Giving
- Note what you experienced: "I lost the game at the third beat."
- Reference a line or page.
- Say what worked first.
- Offer a fix only if asked; if you do, frame it as one option.
- Avoid "I would have done".

## Taking
- Listen without arguing. Write everything down.
- Ask clarifying questions.
- Wait before deciding.
- Look for patterns: when several people point to the same spot, fix it.
- Keep the intent of the piece; reject notes that change the game.

## Room norms
- Agree the type of notes (structure, jokes, casting) before the read.
- Notes belong to the room; do not relitigate after.

## Gotchas
- Defending the sketch in the room.
- Giving notes to a draft the writer considers a rough sketch.

## Verify
- [ ] After the session, you can list three changes to try.
