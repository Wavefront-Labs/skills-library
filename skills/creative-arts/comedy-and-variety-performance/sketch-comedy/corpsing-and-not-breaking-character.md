# Corpsing and not breaking character

**Goal:** keep the audience in the world.

## Causes
- A mistake or unexpected response from a partner.
- A rehearsed line that is suddenly funny.
- Tiredness or nerves.

## Prevention
- Focus on the objective rather than the funny.
- Look at your partner's eyes rather than their mouth.
- Hold an internal thought (a task or fact) that grounds you.
- Rehearse the funniest lines until they are familiar.

## In the moment
- Breathe out slowly.
- Turn upstage or use a prop to hide the face for a second.
- Convert the laugh into a reaction that fits the character if possible.
- Move to the next line; do not apologise.

## When breaking is fine
Some shows embrace it as part of the format; ask the director or the show's convention.

## Gotchas
- Agreeing with a partner to try to make you laugh; discuss it first.

- [ ] You have a personal technique and use it in rehearsal.
