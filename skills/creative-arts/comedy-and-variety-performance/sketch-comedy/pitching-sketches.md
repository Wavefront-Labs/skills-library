# Pitching sketches

**Goal:** make the room see the sketch quickly.

## Format
1. **Premise** in one sentence.
2. **Game**: what is unusual and what gets heightened.
3. **Two or three beats**: the best lines, in order.
4. **Ending**: how it finishes.
5. **Who plays whom**, if known.
Aim for under a minute; many rooms have limits on time per pitch. Check the rules of your room.

## Delivery
- Be concise and energetic; perform key lines.
- Pitch the funniest version, not the whole plot.
- Be ready for questions about structure and casting.

## After
- Take notes without defending.
- If chosen, write a draft; if not, ask whether it is a "no" or "not yet".

## Habits
- Keep a pitch list and update after each room.
- Bring several pitches in different styles.

## Gotchas
- Spending a pitch explaining background.
- Pitching an idea with no clear ending.

## Verify
- [ ] You can pitch each idea in 45-60 seconds with a clear ending.
