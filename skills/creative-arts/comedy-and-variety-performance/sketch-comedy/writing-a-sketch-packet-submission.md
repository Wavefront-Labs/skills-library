# Writing a sketch packet submission

**Goal:** a short bundle of your best writing that shows range and voice.

## Contents
- **Samples**: usually several sketches or scenes; check the submission guidelines for number and length. Select varied styles: character, premise, parody.
- **Spec material**: if the show has a known format, one sample may be written for it.
- **Cover note**: a short, professional note naming the pieces.
- **Credits and contact**: simple one-page bio if requested.

## Format
- Use standard script format and keep each sketch to a few pages, typically; follow the guidelines of the target.
- Proofread; typos suggest carelessness.

## Selection
1. Choose pieces that make the reader laugh in the first page.
2. Show you can write endings.
3. Lead with the strongest.

## Process
- Get outside readers, test in table reads; see `punch-up-sessions`.
- Keep versions per submission.

## Gotchas
- Submitting pieces that rely on obscure references.
- Ignoring the submission instructions.

## Verify
- [ ] Each piece is distinct in voice and game and follows the target's rules.
