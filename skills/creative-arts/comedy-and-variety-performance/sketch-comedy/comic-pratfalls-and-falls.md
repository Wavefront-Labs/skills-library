# Pratfalls and comedy falls

**Goal:** a fall that looks big and keeps you uninjured.

## Principles
- Learn from a qualified teacher (a physical comedy, circus or stage-combat instructor). Do not self-teach a high fall.
- Start low, on mats, and progress slowly.
- Land on padded or fleshy areas and avoid the head, spine and joints: the usual guidance is to spread impact across the body and keep the chin tucked and the head off the floor. Confirm technique with your instructor.
- Warm up thoroughly.

## The comic structure
- A pratfall has set-up (the obstacle), the trip or slip, the fall, and the reaction. The look after the fall often earns the laugh.
- Make the audience see the cause so the fall feels earned.
- A tight sequence: obstacle, near-miss, fall.

## Staging
- Check the floor: surface, slipperiness, edges, props.
- Clear the space; check the shoes.
- Rehearse in costume; padding under clothes if necessary.

## Safety
- Do not perform on a hard floor without proper technique and padding.
- Stop if you feel pain.
- Do not fall repeatedly in one night without rest.

## Gotchas
- Doing the fall for the sake of it with no comic structure.

## Verify
- [ ] A qualified instructor has checked your technique.
