# Live hint delivery by a game master

**Goal:** the team finishes (or nearly) with the feeling they solved it, and no hint
gives away an aha that was within reach.

## Channels
| Channel | Notes |
|---|---|
| Screen / text display | clear, can be reread; check it is visible from the whole room |
| Walkie-talkie / intercom | warm, fast; can break immersion |
| In-character actor or voice | most immersive; needs scripting |
| Clue cards or envelopes | self-serve; no monitoring, less flexible |

Nicholson's 2015 facility survey found most rooms use a game master watching via camera.

## When to hint
1. Before the game, ask the team's preference (on-request only, or pushed when stuck).
2. **Watch for stuck signals:** circling the same props, giving up on a puzzle, arguing,
   people sitting down, attempting the same wrong combination repeatedly.
3. Pace against the clock: the room's flow chart should give expected solve times per
   stage. Behind schedule by more than a threshold = proactive nudge.

## Wording tiers
1. **Attention:** "The painting might deserve a closer look."
2. **Direction:** "Have you compared the colours in the painting with the lock?"
3. **Solution:** "The colours in order give the code 4-1-7-3."
Always start at tier 1 unless the team has under 5 minutes. Never answer a puzzle they
have not found yet.

## Delivery rules
- Short, specific, and positive; one hint per message.
- Use the room's fiction and tone if possible.
- Confirm they got it (watch for the action), then move on.
- Reset and correct mistakes immediately (props used wrongly, broken locks).

## Log and improve
Record each hint: puzzle, tier, minute. Puzzles hinted in most games are design
problems; fix the room, not the script.

## Gotchas
- Hinting a puzzle already half-solved steals the aha; wait 30 seconds if they are close.
- One dominant player solving everything: direct hints to quieter players.
- Children's and corporate groups need different thresholds.

## Verify
- [ ] Tiered hint script exists for every puzzle.
- [ ] Hint log recorded for every session.
- [ ] Top-hinted puzzles reviewed monthly for design fixes.
