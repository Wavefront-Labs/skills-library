# Critiquing with design lenses

**Goal:** a design review that asks the right questions rather than trading opinions.
Schell's *Art of Game Design* presents 100+ "lenses", each a short set of questions
looking at the game from one perspective (also sold as a card deck and an app).

## Procedure
1. **State what you are reviewing and why** (a concept, a prototype, a level, a system
   that is not working).
2. **Choose 5-8 lenses** that match the problem. Useful clusters:
   | Problem | Lenses to pull |
   |---|---|
   | Concept unclear | Essential Experience, Problem Statement, Unification, Holographic Design |
   | Not fun yet | Fun, Curiosity, Toy, Flow, Surprise |
   | Decisions feel empty | Meaningful Choices, Triangularity, Skill vs Chance, Dominant strategy-related questions in the Balance chapter |
   | Pacing drags | Interest Curve, Time, Reward, Punishment |
   | Players lost | Interface lenses (Feedback, Transparency, Channels and Dimensions) |
   | Multiplayer issues | Competition vs Cooperation, Fairness, Community |
3. **Answer each lens's questions in writing**, one lens per 5-10 minutes, with evidence
   from playtests where possible ("observed", not "I think").
4. **Turn answers into actions:** for each lens, at most two concrete changes or
   experiments.
5. **Rotate lenses** across reviews so the team does not only ever see the game one way.

## Running it as a team session
- Deal one lens card per person; each presents the lens's verdict in 2 minutes.
- The lens owner argues from that perspective even if they personally disagree; this
  surfaces problems people avoid raising.

## Gotchas
- Lenses are questions, not rules; two lenses can recommend opposite changes. Resolve
  conflicts against the pillars and essential experience.
- Using every lens on everything is a time sink; pick by problem.
- Answers without evidence turn into taste debates. Bring playtest notes.

## Verify
- [ ] Lenses chosen to match a stated problem.
- [ ] Written answers per lens.
- [ ] Each lens produced at most two concrete actions, with owners.
