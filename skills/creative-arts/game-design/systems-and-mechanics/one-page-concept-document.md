# One-page concept document

**Goal:** capture a game concept on a single page that a teammate, publisher or future
you can read in two minutes and understand what the game is and why it is worth making.

Rogers (Level Up!) recommends a one-sheet as the first document, before a ten-pager and
GDD. Stone Librande's "One-Page Designs" (GDC 2010) shows the related practice of
diagram-heavy single-page design docs.

## Template
```
TITLE                       (working title)
HIGH CONCEPT                one sentence (see high-concept-and-x-statement)
GENRE / PLATFORM / RATING   e.g. co-op roguelite / PC + Switch / PEGI 7
PLAYER FANTASY              who the player is and what they get to do
CORE LOOP                   3-5 step diagram: verb -> feedback -> reward -> choice
PILLARS                     3 ranked statements
HOOKS                       3 bullets: what is different from comparables
COMPARABLES                 2-3 games and what we take / do differently
SESSION                     typical session length and player count
SCOPE NOTE                  team size, rough timeline, biggest risk
IMAGE                       one mood image or sketch
```

## Procedure
1. Fill the template in under an hour; leave blanks blank (they are useful signals).
2. Replace sentences with diagrams where possible (core loop, map, screen mock-up).
3. Cut every adjective that does not distinguish this game.
4. Give it to someone unfamiliar; ask them to explain the game back. Fix what they get
   wrong.
5. Version it; the one-pager is updated as the prototype teaches you things.

## Gotchas
- **Overflowing to two pages** means scope is not decided. The constraint is the point.
- Hooks that are features everyone has ("crafting", "open world") are not hooks.
- Avoid putting lore first; readers need to know what they *do*.
- Comparables must be recent and relevant for a business audience; classic games are
  fine for a design audience.

## Verify
- [ ] Fits one page (A4/Letter) including one image or diagram.
- [ ] A cold reader explains the core loop correctly.
- [ ] Biggest risk is named.
