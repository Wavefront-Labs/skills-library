# Level review and critique

**Goal:** reviews that find real problems early and leave the designer with a clear fix list.

## Review stages
1. **Paper/brief review** - goals, beats, metrics.
2. **Blockout review** - flow and combat spaces (most valuable stage; cheap to change).
3. **Alpha review** - pacing, scripting, encounters.
4. **Art-pass review** - readability and guidance preserved.
5. **Polish review** - bugs, performance, edge cases.

## Protocol
- Reviewer plays first without the designer talking; record video and a timer.
- Then a guided walkthrough by the designer.
- Notes written against a checklist, each with location (screenshot/coordinates),
  observation, severity, suggestion.

## Checklist
- Flow: is the next goal always clear? Any dead ends or backtracking pain?
- Guidance: landmarks, lighting, leading lines working?
- Pacing: intensity curve has peaks and valleys; any long empty stretches?
- Encounters: cover, sightlines, spawn fairness, readability of threats.
- Teaching: new mechanics introduced safely before tested.
- Metrics: jumps, cover heights, door widths consistent with standards.
- Story: does the space tell its story without text?
- Performance and collision: snags, stuck spots, frame drops.

## Etiquette
Critique the level, not the designer. Describe the player experience ("I didn't see the
door") before proposing fixes. The designer owns the solution.

## Verify
- [ ] Video and timings captured.
- [ ] Notes logged with location and severity.
- [ ] Fix list agreed and tracked.
