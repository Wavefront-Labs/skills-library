# Facilitator neutrality in playtests

**Goal:** testers behave as they would at home, and their reactions are not shaped by
the facilitator's hopes, hints or body language.

Fullerton (Game Design Workshop ch9) advises facilitators not to defend or explain the
game during tests; Nielsen Norman Group's guidance on talking to users during tests
covers neutral prompting.

## Briefing script (read verbatim)
> "Thanks for helping. We're testing the game, not you - there are no wrong answers.
> I didn't make it, so you won't hurt my feelings. [If true; otherwise: please be
> honest, it helps us.] Please think aloud as you play. I may not answer questions
> during play because we want to see what happens without help. Play as you normally
> would."

## During play
| Situation | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| Tester asks "What do I do?" | "What do you think you should do?" | explain |
| Tester silent | "What are you thinking right now?" | "Are you confused?" |
| Tester stuck beyond threshold (e.g. 3-5 min) | note time, give minimal hint, continue | rescue immediately |
| Tester criticises | "Tell me more about that." | justify the design |
| Tester succeeds | neutral "mm-hm" | praise ("Great!") |
Body language: sit slightly behind and to the side; neutral face; don't lean in at key
moments (it signals importance).

## Developers in the room
- Best: observe remotely or behind glass; otherwise sit silently out of the tester's view.
- Collect developer questions on paper for the post-session interview.

## Post-session interview
Start broad ("What stood out?"), then specific, with neutral wording; ask about
moments you observed ("I noticed you paused at the bridge - what was going on?").

## Gotchas
- Hints given to one tester invalidate comparisons with others; log every intervention.
- Testers please facilitators; positivity is inflated, so weight behaviour over praise.
- Friends and family testers are biased; use for early tests only.

## Verify
- [ ] Briefing script used verbatim.
- [ ] Intervention threshold defined; interventions logged.
- [ ] Developers didn't coach during sessions.
