# Scene framing and session pacing

**Goal:** the session spends its time on scenes the players care about, moves briskly
through the rest, and ends on a high point.

**Scene framing** is the GM's job of deciding where a scene begins, what is at stake and
when it ends - the tabletop equivalent of editing. Justin Alexander's writing (The
Alexandrian) discusses scene framing and "hard" vs "soft" framing among GM techniques.

## Techniques
| Technique | How |
|---|---|
| Start late | open at the door of the vault, not with the walk from the inn |
| End early | when the scene's question is answered, cut |
| Montage / abstraction | summarise travel, shopping, routine with one roll or a quick description |
| Hard frame | GM sets the scene directly ("You're in the duke's study; guards outside") |
| Soft frame | ask players where they go and what they do; slower, more player-driven |
| Bang | open with a provocation that demands a response |
| Cliffhanger | end the session on a question |

## Reading the table
- **Energy signals:** phones out, side conversations, repeated questions = scene
  dragging; leaning in, cross-talk about the game = engaged.
- **Spotlight balance:** track who has had a scene; frame the next around a quieter
  player (see spotlight management in session prep).
- **Clock watching:** know your session end time; aim the climax for ~20-30 minutes
  before it.

## Procedure for a session
1. List the 3-5 scenes you expect (prep), with a strong start (see session prep).
2. During play, at each scene end ask: "Is there more here the players want?" If not,
   cut.
3. Use montage for downtime and travel unless they're the point of the game.
4. At the 2/3 mark, steer toward a climax or decision point.
5. End on a hook; recap next session's start.

## Gotchas
- Cutting a scene players are enjoying (even if off-plan) loses more than it gains.
- Hard framing too often can railroad; mix with player-driven scenes.
- Long rules lookups kill pace; make a ruling and look it up after (see rules adjudication).

## Verify
- [ ] Session plan lists scenes with intended starts.
- [ ] At least one montage used for routine activity.
- [ ] Session ended on a hook within time.
