# Stars and wishes check-out

**Goal:** a short closing ritual that collects feedback and leaves everyone on a good note.

Stars and wishes is a simple feedback format used in education, workshops and games. I am not confident of its original source, so treat the format below as a generic technique.

## Procedure
1. **Set aside five minutes** at the end of a session or scene block, before packing up.
2. **Explain:** a star is something that worked or that you enjoyed; a wish is something you would like more or different next time.
3. **Go around the table** (voluntary participation). Each person gives one star, then one wish, about a sentence each.
4. **Receive quietly.** No debating wishes; the GM or facilitator thanks each person and notes them.
5. **Make wishes about play, not people:** "I wish there were more time for exploring" rather than "I wish Sam talked less". Redirect person-targeted wishes to behaviour or situation.
6. **Close with thanks** and a plan to apply one or two wishes next session.
7. **Record the results** in session notes and review them before the next prep.

## Variants
- **Roses and thorns** or **plus and delta:** similar structures with different vocabulary.
- **Silent version:** write stars and wishes on slips of paper when players are shy.

## Gotchas
- If a heavy scene occurred, run a proper debrief and aftercare first (see larp debrief formats).
- Not a substitute for safety tools; serious concerns need a private conversation.
- Doing it every session turns it into routine; keep it light.

## Verify
- [ ] Participation voluntary.
- [ ] Wishes focused on play, not people.
- [ ] At least one wish applied next session.
