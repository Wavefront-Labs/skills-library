# Larp risk assessment

**Goal:** identify what could hurt participants or crew at a larp and put proportionate
controls in place, documented so venue, insurers and the team can rely on it.

The UK Health and Safety Executive's general risk-assessment approach (identify hazards,
decide who might be harmed and how, evaluate risks and decide on controls, record
findings, review) is a widely used template; follow your own jurisdiction's
requirements.

## Hazard areas to walk through
| Area | Examples |
|---|---|
| Venue | uneven ground, stairs in darkness, water, fire exits, structural issues |
| Activities | combat (boffer), running, night games, physical contact |
| Fire and light | candles, fire pits, smoke machines, pyrotechnics |
| Food | allergies, hygiene, alcohol policy |
| Weather | heat, cold, rain, lightning (outdoor) |
| People | medical conditions, medication, exhaustion, psychological intensity |
| Equipment | props, weapons checks, electrics |
| Transport | access roads, parking, travel at night |

## Risk table
`Hazard | Who is at risk | Likelihood (1-5) | Severity (1-5) | Risk score | Controls | Residual risk | Owner`

## Controls examples
- Weapon checks before play; safety rules for combat; no combat in dark/uneven areas.
- Torches/lighting on paths; mark off-limits areas.
- First-aid trained crew with kit; known nearest hospital; emergency contact list.
- Medical info collected confidentially at sign-up.
- Psychological safety tools and safety team (see safety skills).
- Fire safety per venue rules; extinguishers; no open flames indoors unless permitted.

## Emergency plan
Who calls emergency services, where the address/access point is, how play is stopped
(an out-of-game "emergency" call distinct from game calls), and where people assemble.

## Gotchas
- Venue owners and insurers often require a written assessment; ask early.
- Night play outdoors multiplies risk; scout the site in the dark before the event.
- Assessments must be briefed to crew; a document nobody read controls nothing.

## Verify
- [ ] Site walked (including at night if relevant) and hazards listed.
- [ ] Risk table with controls and owners.
- [ ] Emergency plan and stop-play call briefed to all.
- [ ] Insurance and venue requirements confirmed.
