# Larp accessibility and inclusion

**Goal:** more people can take part comfortably and safely.

## Procedure
1. **Ask about access needs** at sign-up with an open question and an easy way to follow up privately. Explain what you will do with the answers.
2. **Physical access:** check step-free routes, accessible toilets and sleeping options; describe the site honestly, including distances and uneven ground.
3. **Sensory needs:** provide a quiet room, warnings about strobes, loud sounds, smoke or strong smells, and allow ear defenders and breaks.
4. **Communication:** provide documents in accessible formats (large print, plain text), clear schedules, and contact details for questions. Consider captioning or interpreters if needed.
5. **Dietary and medical:** collect allergies and requirements; designate first-aid contacts.
6. **Flexible participation:** off-game spaces, opt-out rules, adjustable physical activity, and alternative roles.
7. **Inclusive design:** avoid stereotyped characters, check content for discriminatory themes, and write a code of conduct with clear reporting channels.
8. **Safety tools:** lines and veils, calibration and stop words, accessible to everyone.
9. **Train staff** to respond respectfully; make adjustments without demanding justification.
10. **Learn from feedback;** publish what you can and cannot offer.

## Gotchas
- Do not promise what you cannot deliver; be honest about limits.
- Access needs are personal; keep them confidential.
- Accessibility is easier to plan in advance than retrofit.

## Verify
- [ ] Access needs form and private follow-up in place.
- [ ] Site and documents checked for access.
- [ ] Code of conduct and reporting route published.
