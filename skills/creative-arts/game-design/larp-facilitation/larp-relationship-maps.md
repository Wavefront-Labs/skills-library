# Larp relationship maps

**Goal:** a connected cast where every character has meaningful ties and conflicts.

## Procedure
1. **List the characters** and their groups or factions.
2. **Draw a graph:** characters are nodes; draw a line for each relationship (family, rivalry, debt, love, secret). Label lines with the tie and one line of detail.
3. **Aim for several ties per character,** at least three, with a mix of positive, negative and complicated ones. Common advice: fewer than three ties leaves a character without play, and many more than that can overwhelm.
4. **Avoid isolated clusters:** connect groups through at least a few cross-group ties, so scenes can span the cast.
5. **Add tension:** mark ties with a secret, unequal knowledge or opposing goals; these drive play.
6. **Check balance:** every player should have comparable hooks, and no player should be the hub of everything.
7. **Translate to character sheets:** each tie becomes a short line in the sheet (see larp-character-sheet-formats).
8. **Gather feedback in the workshop;** let players adjust ties together (see larp-relationship-workshops).

## Gotchas
- Pre-written ties that clash with a player's preferences need negotiation.
- Hidden ties break if players do not meet; provide a path to discovery.
- Avoid one-sided romance unless players opted in; check consent.

## Verify
- [ ] Each character has three or more ties.
- [ ] Map connected, with cross-group links.
- [ ] Ties appear in character sheets.
