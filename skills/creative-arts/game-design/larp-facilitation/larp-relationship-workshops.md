# Larp relationship workshops

**Goal:** make relationships feel real by creating shared experience before play.

## Procedure
1. **Prepare from the relationship map:** list the key pairs and groups.
2. **Pair exercise:** each pair discusses their relationship in neutral terms: how long, what they like, what they resent. They agree on two or three shared memories.
3. **Memory scenes:** pairs play or describe a short key moment (a first meeting, a quarrel, a promise). Keep them short and consent-based.
4. **Group exercise:** build group traditions, rituals, nicknames, inside jokes or conflicts.
5. **Photographs or tableaux:** players pose a freeze-frame of a shared moment; useful for groups.
6. **Negotiate boundaries:** each pair agrees what is on and off the table (intimacy, violence, secrets); use lines and veils and calibration tools.
7. **Capture the results:** each player jots notes; add them to the sheet.
8. **De-role at the end:** a short exercise to leave workshop scenes behind before the game.

## Gotchas
- Do not turn the workshop into a full game; short scenes suffice.
- Pre-written tension can be lost if players over-resolve it; remind them to keep conflicts alive.
- Watch for pressure to share more than players want.

## Verify
- [ ] Every key pair has a shared memory.
- [ ] Boundaries agreed and noted.
- [ ] Notes captured before play.
