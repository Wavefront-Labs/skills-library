# Larp warm-up exercises

**Goal:** loosen the body, connect the group and move players into the game's tone.

## Procedure
1. **Start with a short briefing:** purpose, voluntary participation, and the right to pass.
2. **Physical activation (5-10 minutes):** shake out limbs, stretching, walking at different speeds.
3. **Walk the space:** players move around the room, changing tempo and direction; add instructions like "fill the empty spaces" or "greet people with a nod".
4. **Name and gesture:** each player says their character name with a gesture; others repeat it.
5. **Voice:** gentle humming, counting together, or vowel sounds. Keep volume within comfort.
6. **Mirror work:** pairs mirror each other's slow movements, then swap the leader.
7. **Eye contact and trust:** only with consent and a clear opt-out; non-contact options are always available.
8. **Transition to character:** do a final exercise that moves from self to character, such as walking as the character and meeting others silently.
9. **Match intensity to the game:** a light comedy needs lively warm-ups; a heavy drama needs calm, grounding ones.

## Gotchas
- Physical contact exercises can be uncomfortable; offer alternatives.
- Do not force energetic activities on tired or injured players.
- Keep it short; the workshop has other jobs.

## Verify
- [ ] Participation voluntary, opt-outs stated.
- [ ] Exercises fit the game's tone and group.
- [ ] Time boxed.
