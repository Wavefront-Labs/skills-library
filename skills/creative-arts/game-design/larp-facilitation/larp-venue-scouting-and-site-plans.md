# Larp venue scouting and site plans

**Goal:** a site that supports the game design and keeps participants safe and comfortable.

## Scouting checklist
1. **Capacity:** number of players and staff for sleeping, eating, and playing.
2. **Spaces:** main game areas, quiet rooms, workshop room, off-game room, kitchen.
3. **Sanitation:** toilets and showers sized for the group.
4. **Access:** step-free routes, parking, public transport, emergency vehicle access.
5. **Safety:** fire exits, extinguishers and local fire regulations, first aid kit, mobile signal, hazards (water, cliffs, roads).
6. **Neighbours and noise:** local rules, quiet hours and nearby residents.
7. **Heating, power, water and lighting.**
8. **Permissions:** contract, permitted activities (props, candles, weapons) and insurance requirements. Check with the venue owner and your insurer.
9. **Costs:** rental, deposit, cleaning, damage terms.
10. **Weather plan** for outdoor games.

## Site plan
1. **Draw a map** showing buildings, rooms, paths, play zones, off-game zones, toilets, kitchen, first aid, assembly point.
2. **Mark hazards** and off-limits areas.
3. **Plan routes** for staff and participants, including accessible routes.
4. **Share the plan** with players and staff before the event.
5. **Walk the site with staff** in daylight and at night.

## Gotchas
- Venue photos hide acoustic and heating problems; visit.
- Get written agreement on candles, smoke effects, and prop weapons.
- Plan an emergency procedure with named roles.

## Verify
- [ ] Capacity, sanitation and access checked.
- [ ] Permissions and insurance confirmed in writing.
- [ ] Site plan with emergency points distributed.
