# Larp vision document

**Goal:** a short document that aligns the organising team and later the players on what
the larp is, so every design and logistics decision can be checked against it.

The larp design literature (e.g. *Larp Design: Creating Role-Play Experiences*, Koljonen,
Stenros et al. 2019, and Larpwriter Summer School materials) treats a clear vision as the
first design step: what experience, for whom, in what style.

## Sections
1. **Core experience:** one paragraph on what participants should feel and do.
2. **Themes:** 2-3 themes the larp explores (e.g. loyalty under pressure, grief, power).
3. **Tone and genre:** realism level, humour, intensity; reference works.
4. **Play style:** immersionist / gamist / narrativist leanings; amount of game mechanics;
   "play to lift" or "play to win"; transparency (is information open between players?).
5. **Format:** duration, number of players, venue type, chamber vs campaign.
6. **Content and intensity:** content that will appear, content that won't, opt-in
   mechanics (see safety skills).
7. **What players will actually do:** typical activities hour by hour.
8. **Design consequences:** decisions that follow (e.g. "transparent play -> all
   character secrets shared in advance"; "realism -> no boffer combat").
9. **Non-goals:** what the larp is not.

## Procedure
1. Draft as the lead designer; review with co-organisers until everyone can state the
   core experience the same way.
2. Derive the public pitch and sign-up text from it (with honest content info).
3. Check every major decision (characters, workshops, venue, mechanics) against it.

## Gotchas
- Vague visions ("an epic fantasy adventure") don't filter anything; be specific.
- Mismatched player expectations are a major source of bad larp experiences; publish
  the relevant parts honestly.
- Visions written after design starts tend to justify rather than guide.

## Verify
- [ ] Core experience, themes, tone, play style, content stated.
- [ ] Design consequences listed.
- [ ] Sign-up text derived from it, including content information.
