# Boffer combat and phys-reps

**Goal:** fun, repeatable combat that does not hurt people.

Boffer larp uses padded foam weapons. Well-known examples of rule systems include NERO and Dagorhir; each has its own detailed safety rules, which supersede this summary.

## Procedure
1. **Choose the rules system** (existing or custom) and publish it. Keep the combat rules short enough to learn in a briefing.
2. **Inspect all weapons:** every weapon is checked at check-in for a soft striking surface, no exposed core, no damage, and acceptable length and weight. Remove damaged weapons from play.
3. **Define target zones and banned zones:** typically torso and limbs; head, neck, groin and joints are off-limits for strikes. Specify the rule for accidental contact.
4. **Set contact level:** light or "pull-the-blow" contact; hits are acknowledged when felt; no full-force swings.
5. **Define calls and effects:** a limited set of verbal calls for damage or effects, taught in a short briefing.
6. **Use a stop signal:** a loud word such as "hold" or "time out" stops combat immediately when someone is hurt or unsafe; teach that everyone repeats it.
7. **Staff the fight:** marshals or referees for large battles, and first-aid-trained people nearby.
8. **Handle phys-reps:** physical representations of items (props that stand for weapons, potions, treasures) must be safe, clearly marked and agreed, e.g. tagged by owner.
9. **Run a short combat class** for newcomers before the game.

## Gotchas
- Tired or overconfident fighters hit harder; rotate and rest.
- Check terrain for hazards: wet ground, ditches, roots.
- Respect players with injuries or medical conditions; offer non-combat roles.

## Verify
- [ ] Weapon inspection done and recorded.
- [ ] Stop call, zones and contact rules briefed.
- [ ] First aid and marshals in place.
