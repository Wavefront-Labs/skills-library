# Larp budget and ticketing

**Goal:** a game that covers its costs without surprises.

## Procedure
1. **List fixed costs:** venue rental, insurance, equipment, props, printing, staff expenses, licences.
2. **List per-participant costs:** food, bedding or accommodation, workshop materials, costumes provided, per-head venue fees.
3. **Add a contingency,** commonly around ten to twenty percent of the total, for unexpected costs.
4. **Compute break-even:** ticket price = (fixed costs + contingency) / number of paying participants + per-participant cost. Test several attendance levels.
5. **Set the pricing structure:** a base ticket plus optional tiers (supporter pricing, subsidised tickets, staff tickets) can improve accessibility.
6. **Define payment terms:** deposit, final payment date, accepted methods, and a clear refund and transfer policy, including what happens if the event is cancelled.
7. **Track spending** in a simple spreadsheet and review it against plan monthly.
8. **Plan for volunteer and staff costs** (travel, food), so they are not hidden.
9. **Handle taxes and legal status** according to local law (for example, whether the event is run by a registered association); check with a local adviser.
10. **Report back** to participants after the event if you promised transparency.

## Gotchas
- Underestimating food and travel is common.
- Late drop-outs: a transfer or waitlist policy limits losses.
- Keep personal and event money separate.

## Verify
- [ ] Break-even computed at several attendances.
- [ ] Contingency and staff costs included.
- [ ] Refund policy published.
