# FAQ and errata

**Goal:** players get one authoritative answer for every rules question, and fixes reach
both current owners and future printings.

## Definitions
- **FAQ / clarification:** rules were right, explanation was unclear.
- **Erratum:** the printed rule or card text is changed.
- **Ruling precedence:** state it in the rulebook: card text overrides rulebook; specific
  overrides general; latest errata overrides print.

## Collecting questions
1. Monitor the game's BoardGameGeek rules forum, publisher Discord and email.
2. Log each question: component or rule section, question, answer, source, date.
3. Group duplicates; frequency shows which rule text is failing.

## Publishing
1. **One versioned document** (PDF and web page): "FAQ and Errata v1.3, 2026-10-16",
   sorted by rulebook section, then card number. Use the rulebook's numbered sections.
2. **Separate errata from clarifications** visually; errata first, they change play.
3. Post it on the publisher site and pin it in the BGG files and forum.
4. **Digital card databases** and apps get the updated text with an "errata" marker.
5. **Reprints**: apply errata to card text and the rulebook; mark the new printing on the
   box or rulebook ("Second printing incorporates errata v1.3") and update the online rules.

## When to issue errata
- Broken combo or infinite loop, dominant strategy, contradictory text, or a
  misprint that changes values. Prefer the smallest text change that fixes it.
- Avoid errata for mere balance preferences; every change splits the player base between
  printings.

## Gotchas
- Answers given casually by the designer on forums become de facto rules; route rulings
  through the official document.
- Errata that require replacement cards should come with a replacement policy (free
  upgrade pack, print-and-play).
- Translated editions need the same errata applied by each localisation partner.

## Verify
- [ ] Ruling precedence stated in the rulebook.
- [ ] FAQ document versioned and dated, sections match rulebook numbering.
- [ ] Reprint checklist applies all errata to cards and rules.
