# The alpha-player problem

**Problem:** in a co-op game with full public information, the most experienced or
assertive player can solve everyone's turn and tell them what to do. Others become
spectators. Pandemic is the standard example.

## Fixes (combine 1-2)
| Fix | How | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Hidden information | each player knows something others do not | Hanabi (you see everyone's cards but your own), Mysterium |
| Restricted communication | rules limit what can be said | The Crew (one card signal), The Mind (no talk at all) |
| Real-time pressure | sand timer or app; no time to direct others | Magic Maze, Space Alert, FUSE |
| Simultaneous play | everyone acts at once | Captain Sonar, The Mind |
| Traitor / semi-co-op | someone may be lying, so commands are suspect | Dead of Winter, Battlestar Galactica |
| Individual goals | personal objectives or scores alongside the team win | Dead of Winter secret objectives |
| Asymmetric expertise | each role's options are complex enough to need ownership | Spirit Island spirits |

## Procedure
1. Watch a test session with a dominant player; note decisions made by someone other
   than the player taking the turn.
2. Identify which information makes full planning possible.
3. Hide or delay that information, or add a communication limit, until each player
   meaningfully owns their own decisions.
4. Keep enough shared information that the team can still coordinate; total silence is a
   different, niche game.

## Gotchas
- Rulebook advice ("let players decide for themselves") does not fix a design that
  rewards one brain; solve it mechanically.
- Hidden hands in an otherwise open game get ignored by groups; give a mechanical reason
  to keep them hidden.
- Real-time fixes exclude some players (processing speed, accessibility); offer a
  relaxed timer.

## Verify
- [ ] In tests, most decisions are made by the player whose turn it is.
- [ ] New players report feeling responsible for outcomes in post-game interviews.
- [ ] Team can still coordinate within the limits.
