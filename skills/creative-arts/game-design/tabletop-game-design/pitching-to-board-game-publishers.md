# Pitching to board game publishers

**Goal:** get a publisher to request and evaluate your prototype, with materials that
show the game's appeal in under two minutes.

The *Kobold Guide to Board Game Design* (Open Design / Kobold Press) includes essays on
pitching and working with publishers.

## 1. Research fit
- Look at each publisher's catalogue: weight, player count, price point, theme.
- Read their **submission guidelines** (many publish them on their website; some only
  accept at conventions or through agents; some don't accept submissions).
- Pitch only games that fit; a mismatch wastes a meeting.

## 2. The sell sheet (one page)
| Element | Notes |
|---|---|
| Title + key image | photo of prototype or mock art |
| Hook | one sentence |
| Core numbers | players, age, play time, (estimated) components |
| How it plays | 3-4 bullets: the core loop |
| Unique selling points | 2-3 |
| Contact | name, email, website |
Readable in 30 seconds. PDF and printed.

## 3. Pitch video
2-3 minutes: the hook, a quick overview of a turn, what makes it special, footage of play.
Unlisted video link on the sell sheet.

## 4. Pitch meeting (often at conventions like Spiel Essen, Gen Con, UK Games Expo)
1. Book meetings in advance where possible.
2. Lead with the hook; let them handle components early.
3. Offer to play a short round or demo a turn; respect their time slot.
4. Have the sell sheet to leave behind.
5. Note follow-up actions; send the requested prototype/files promptly.

## Expectations
- Publishers may hold a prototype for months; ask about their evaluation timeline.
- Multiple simultaneous submissions are common; be transparent if asked.
- Signing typically means royalties on sales; read contracts carefully (see
  design royalty norms and get advice).

## Gotchas
- NDAs: most publishers will not sign them for pitches; idea theft is rare, and the
  industry runs on reputation.
- Overproduced prototypes are not needed; clear and functional is enough.

## Verify
- [ ] Target publishers researched and guidelines followed.
- [ ] Sell sheet readable in 30 seconds.
- [ ] Pitch video under 3 minutes.
- [ ] Follow-up tracker for each pitch.
