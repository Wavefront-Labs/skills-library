# Board game crowdfunding campaigns

**Goal:** fund manufacturing and delivery without losing money, and deliver on time.
Kickstarter and Gamefound dominate tabletop crowdfunding.

## Before launch (months, not weeks)
1. **Cost the game**: manufacturing quote at 2-3 print runs (e.g. 1,500 / 3,000 / 5,000),
   freight, fulfilment per region, platform fees (Kickstarter takes 5% plus payment
   processing of roughly 3-5%), VAT/duties, art, marketing, a contingency of 10%+.
2. **Funding goal** = the minimum that delivers the game at the smallest viable print
   run without loss. A low goal that funds fast builds momentum but must still be safe.
3. **Audience first**: mailing list, Kickstarter pre-launch page followers, reviewers'
   prototypes, BGG presence. Stegmaier's guidance: build the crowd before asking it.
4. **Finished rules and art** largely complete; backers expect a rulebook PDF and how-to-play video.

## The page
- First screen: game name, one-line pitch, player count, time, age, price, a gameplay
  image. A 2-3 minute video.
- Few reward tiers (base, deluxe, retailer); too many tiers confuse.
- Shipping: state costs per region or "charged in pledge manager"; EU- and UK-friendly
  shipping (duties prepaid) matters to backers.
- Risks and challenges section written honestly.

## During the campaign
- Length 2-4 weeks; Kickstarter recommends 30 days or less.
- The first 48 hours set the trajectory; launch with the audience ready.
- Stretch goals that improve the game for everyone (component upgrades) beat
  content bloat that delays delivery.
- Daily comments engagement; regular updates.

## After funding
1. Pledge manager (BackerKit, Gamefound, PledgeBox) for addresses, add-ons and shipping charges.
2. Pre-production proofs, then manufacturing (often 8-12 weeks), freight (4-8 weeks by sea),
   fulfilment hubs per region.
3. Monthly updates even when nothing happens; bad news early.

## Gotchas
- Stretch goals that add miniatures add cost and tooling; cost each one before announcing.
- Exchange-rate and freight swings: build buffer or quote in the manufacturer's currency.
- Exclusive content alienates retail; consider "Kickstarter edition" rather than exclusives.

## Verify
- [ ] Cost sheet covers every line through delivery, with contingency.
- [ ] Funding goal delivers safely at the minimum print run.
- [ ] Every stretch goal costed before the campaign.
