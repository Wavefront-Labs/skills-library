# Indirect control methods

**Goal:** players feel free yet reliably do what the story needs, because the design
steers their desires rather than forcing their actions.

Schell (2nd ed.) ch18 (Story and Game Structures Can Be Artfully Merged with Indirect Control)
argues that what matters is the **feeling of freedom**, and lists six methods.

## The six methods
| Method | How it steers | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Constraints | fewer options make the right one likely | two doors instead of an open plain; limited dialogue choices |
| Goals | clear goals make players pick goal-serving actions | "reach the lighthouse" makes players head toward it |
| Interface | what the controls allow shapes behaviour | a steering wheel makes players drive, not explore on foot |
| Visual design | eyes go where design leads them | light, lines, landmarks (see level-design guidance skills) |
| Characters | players follow characters they care about | a companion who walks toward the objective |
| Music | mood suggests behaviour | calm music invites exploration, tense music urges haste |

## Procedure
1. For each story-critical moment, write what the player must do and what they might
   do instead.
2. Pick 2-3 methods to steer toward the critical action; prefer invisible ones
   (visuals, characters, goals) over hard constraints.
3. Keep **some real choices** near the steering so freedom is not only an illusion.
4. Playtest: count how many players reach the critical action without hard blocking,
   and ask afterwards whether they felt forced.

## Gotchas
- Too many constraints feel like rails; too few and players miss story beats.
- Players notice manipulation that contradicts their goals (invisible walls); steer
  desire, don't block action.
- Companions who constantly nag ("We should go to the lighthouse!") become irritating;
  limit repetition.

## Verify
- [ ] Critical moments listed with chosen steering methods.
- [ ] Most testers reach critical beats without hard blocks.
- [ ] Post-session interviews show players felt in control.
