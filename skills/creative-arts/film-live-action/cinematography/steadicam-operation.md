# Steadicam operation

**Goal:** fluid moving shots that follow actors anywhere - with the operator briefed,
the rig balanced and the move rehearsed.

## 1. The operator's brief
Give the Steadicam operator: the shot's purpose, start and end frames, the path
(stairs, doors, uneven ground), lens and camera build, duration (long takes are
physically demanding - plan resets), whether it's high or low mode, and any handoffs.
Walk the path together.

## 2. The rig (operator's domain, but know it)
- **Vest + arm + sled** (post with camera on top, monitor and batteries at the bottom,
  gimbal at the balance point).
- **Static balance**: centre of gravity just below the gimbal.
- **Dynamic balance**: the sled spins flat without wobble (camera and battery
  masses aligned).
- **Drop time**: time for the sled to swing from horizontal to vertical, typically
  ~2-3 seconds - longer = more stable but sluggish; shorter = responsive but bouncy.
- Camera build stays fixed once balanced: changes (matte box, lens, wireless) need
  rebalancing - agree the build before the shot.
- **Low mode**: camera mounted under the post for ground-level shots.

## 3. Focus, video, wireless
Wireless follow focus (focus puller often walks nearby or works from a monitor), wireless
video to the village, wireless timecode/audio as needed. Battery hot-swap plan.

## 4. Rehearsal and execution
- Walk-through without camera, then with: operator's path marked, actors' marks, focus
  marks. The **grip/assistant** spots the operator (hand on the back on walking backwards,
  stairs).
- Operator rests between takes (docking stand); limit take counts on long moves.
- Keep horizons level; operator uses the monitor and frame lines.

## 5. Steadicam vs gimbal
Gimbals (DJI Ronin 2, Movi) stabilise electronically, are cheaper and can be handed to
cars/cranes; Steadicam gives the operator more organic control and handles heavier
builds for long takes. Many productions use both; hire an operator who knows the tool.

## Gotchas
- Adding accessories after balancing.
- No spotter when walking backwards.
- Long takes with no rest - shaky by take 8.
- Stairs and doorways not rehearsed.

## Verify
- [ ] Operator briefed and path walked.
- [ ] Build locked and balanced; drop time set.
- [ ] Wireless focus/video tested; batteries planned.
- [ ] Rehearsed with spotter; rest between takes.
