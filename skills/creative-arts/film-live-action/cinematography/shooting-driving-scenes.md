# Shooting driving scenes

**Goal:** convincing in-car scenes with good performances and lighting, shot without
actors driving while acting - the method chosen for safety, look and budget.

## 1. Choose the method
| Method | How | Pros / cons |
|---|---|---|
| **Process trailer / low-loader** | Picture car on a trailer towed by a camera/tow vehicle | Real backgrounds and light; actors don't drive; needs road control and permits |
| **Tow dolly / "pod" car** | Picture car towed, or driven by a stunt driver in a pod on the roof/side | Real motion; pod looks real for action |
| **LED volume / projection** | Car on stage in front of LED walls playing plates | Controlled, repeatable, interactive reflections; plates must be shot first |
| **Poor man's process** | Static car on stage, moving lights and shaking | Cheap; best at night with little visible background |
| **Green screen** | Static car, plates composited | Flexible; reflections and interactive light are hard |
| **Actor drives** | Only for simple, slow, legal driving with no dialogue complexity | Real; risky - avoid when performance is demanding |

## 2. Rigs
- **Hostess tray**: a platform clamped to the door sill for side angles through the window.
- **Hood mount**: rigged on the bonnet for a frontal two-shot through the windscreen.
- **Suction mounts** (Filmotechnic, Matthews) for small cameras on panels; always with
  safety straps.
- **Interior rigs**: speed-rail from headrests, small cameras on dash, back-seat
  positions. Remote focus/iris/record via wireless.
- **Camera car** with crane (Russian Arm) for exteriors of the moving car.

## 3. Lighting in the car
- Day: control harsh sun with **ND gels on windows** (where legal for the shot), small
  LED fill inside (panels on the dash, battery tubes on the headliner), bounce from
  white cards on the legs.
- Night: dash glow from LED strips, passing street lights simulated with flicker/chase
  cues on interior LEDs; on stage, moving lights on rails or LED walls for passing light.
- Match light direction between angles (sun side).

## 4. Safety
- Road closures or controlled routes with police/traffic management; camera car driver
  and picture car driver qualified; speed limits agreed.
- No crew riding outside the vehicles unless secured on designed platforms; all equipment
  safety-cabled.
- Communication: radios between camera car, picture car and lead/follow cars.
- Safety meeting before each run; follow the safety bulletin for camera cars.

## Gotchas
- Actor driving while acting and checking marks - dangerous and bad performances.
- Window reflections showing crew and rigs; position black flags and choose angles.
- Unmatched sun direction between master and singles.
- Suction mounts without safety lines.

- [ ] Rigs listed with safety straps; remote control tested.
- [ ] Interior lighting plan for day/night; sun direction consistent.
