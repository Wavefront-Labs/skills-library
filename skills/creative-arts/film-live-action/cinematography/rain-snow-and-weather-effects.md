# Rain, snow and weather effects

**Goal:** weather that reads on camera, is consistent across coverage, and is created
safely by the SFX department with camera and electrics protected.

## 1. Rain
- **Rain towers** (tall stands with sprinkler heads) or **rain bars** (horizontal pipes
  on cranes) supplied by water trucks or hydrants; SFX crew controls them. Rain must
  cover the **frame and foreground** - often the near background too.
- Real rain barely shows on camera: **backlight it** (hard source behind, 3/4 back);
  frontlit rain disappears. Dark backgrounds make it read.
- Drops size and density: bigger heads for close shots; mist for distance.
- **Wet down** the street for night exteriors - reflections double the light.
- Rain on actors: continuity of wetness (hair, costume) between takes - costume needs
  duplicates and drying; warm water for actors in cold weather.

## 2. Snow
- Falling snow: paper/foam/biodegradable snow (e.g. cellulose) from snow machines or
  dropped from above; consistent density in coverage.
- Ground snow: snow blankets, foam, paper, ice shaved; VFX for distant backgrounds.
- Breath in cold scenes (real cold, or VFX/ice in mouth).
- Exposure: snow is +2 to +2.5 stops; protect highlights.

## 3. Wind, fog and atmosphere
- **Wind machines** (Ritter fans) for hair/costume, with debris for storms.
- **Haze** (oil- or water-based hazers) for atmosphere and beams; **fog** (heavier, low-
  lying, dry ice or fog fluid). Keep levels consistent across takes - SFX "tops up" between takes.
- Check safety data sheets for haze fluids; ventilation and actor health.

## 4. Protect camera and electrics
- Rain covers / rain deflector (spinning glass) on the lens; umbrellas for camera crew.
- Electrics: RCD/GFCI, distro off the ground, fixtures rated for wet or covered; LEDs with
  IP ratings.
- Sound: rain is loud on props and umbrellas; plan ADR or soft surfaces.

## 5. Continuity and coverage
Shoot the wides with rain first (most water), then tighter shots in the same conditions;
the script supervisor logs rain intensity and wetness. Match natural rain days with
effects when weather changes mid-scene.

## Gotchas
- Front-lit rain that vanishes.
- Rain only in the background of close-ups.
- Haze levels drifting between takes - matching nightmare.
- Water near HMIs and distro without protection.

## Verify
- [ ] Rain/snow coverage includes foreground; backlighting planned.
- [ ] SFX, water supply and drainage arranged; actors' comfort and costume duplicates.
- [ ] Camera rain protection and electrical safety in place.
- [ ] Continuity of intensity logged.
