# HMIs, fresnels and hard light

**Goal:** daylight-balanced punch and crisp, controllable hard light - operated safely.

## 1. HMI basics
- **HMI** (hydrargyrum medium-arc iodide) lamps: ~5600 K, 3-4x the output of tungsten
  per watt. Sizes: 200 W, 575, 1.2 K, 2.5 K, 4 K, 6 K, 12 K, 18 K.
- **Ballast** required: electronic (flicker-free, square-wave) or older magnetic
  (flickers off-speed). Head cable between ballast and head; keep connections dry.
- **Strike**: lamp needs warm-up (~1-3 min) to reach full colour/output; colour shifts
  slightly with age and dimming. **Hot restrike** only on lamps/ballasts designed for it;
  otherwise wait for cooldown.
- **Dimming**: electronic ballasts dim to ~50%; colour shifts cooler when dimmed; use
  scrims/ND or distance for big changes.

## 2. Flicker and modes
On electronic ballasts choose **flicker-free** mode for high-speed or off-speed work (it's
louder - "silent" mode is quieter but only safe at standard frame rates/shutters).
Test at the shooting frame rate before the take.

## 3. Safety
- **UV**: HMI lamps emit UV - never run without the lens/glass (fresnel lens or UV
  protective glass) in place.
- Lamps under pressure: replace with gloves/eye protection, don't touch the glass.
- Ground fault protection, ballasts off wet ground; restrike can cause high voltage at the
  head - switch off before opening.

## 4. Fresnels
Fresnel lens with **spot/flood** (moving the lamp relative to the lens): spot = narrow,
intense; flood = wide, softer edge. Hard but controllable; use **barn doors** to shape and
cut, **scrims** (single/double, half) to reduce without changing colour, **snoots** for
small pools.

## 5. Other hard sources
- **PAR** HMIs (ARRI M-series, Joker): open-face with lens options (spot, narrow, medium,
  wide flood); more output than fresnels; through windows for "sun".
- **Bouncing** a hard unit into a card makes soft light; **through diffusion** makes it softer.
- Hard light reads as sun, street light, spotlight; shadows define shape and time of day.

## 6. Placement
Big HMIs outside windows for daylight interiors (on condors or rolling stands); use one
source for one sun - multiple hard sources make multiple shadows.

## Gotchas
- Running an HMI with the lens broken or removed (UV burns).
- Off-speed shot with a magnetic or silent-mode ballast - flicker.
- Dimming an HMI to 50% and wondering why it looks cool.
- Multiple hard keys giving double shadows.

## Verify
- [ ] Ballast mode set (flicker-free) and tested at shooting frame rate/shutter.
- [ ] Lens/UV glass intact; ballasts dry and protected.
- [ ] Hard light shaped with doors/scrims; single sun direction.
- [ ] Warm-up time allowed before rolling.
