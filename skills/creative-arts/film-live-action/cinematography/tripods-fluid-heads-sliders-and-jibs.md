# Tripods, fluid heads, sliders and jibs

**Goal:** steady frames and small controlled moves with the simplest support - set up
correctly so pans are smooth and moves land on their frames.

## 1. Sticks
Standard legs, **baby legs** (short), **high-hat** (ground level, mounted on a board or
apple box), spreaders (ground or mid-level). Level the bowl (75/100/150 mm) with the
bubble; legs at equal spread on uneven ground; sandbag in wind.

## 2. Fluid heads
- **Counterbalance**: set to the camera's weight and centre of gravity so the camera
  stays where you leave it at any tilt angle (balance the camera fore/aft on the plate
  first, then increase counterbalance until it holds). Rebalance after lens/accessory
  changes.
- **Drag** (pan and tilt, separately): enough resistance to damp jitter, not so much that
  starts jerk. Long lenses and slow moves need more drag.
- Pan technique: start the move from the body, ease in and ease out, finish slightly
  after the action; avoid strobing - a pan across detail should take ~5-7 seconds for
  an object to cross frame at 24fps with 180 degree shutter (judder rule of thumb).
- **Geared heads** (Arrihead, Oconnor-type geared): wheel-operated for precise, repeatable
  moves; feature-film standard for operators trained on wheels.

## 3. Sliders
1-1.5 m rails for short lateral/push moves that add parallax. Level and support both ends
(two stands or legs); motorised sliders for repeatable/timelapse moves. Foreground
objects make slider moves read; plain backgrounds hide them.

## 4. Small jibs
3-4 m counterweighted arms on a tripod/dolly: rise/fall reveals, over-the-table moves.
Balance with counterweights, lock when not in use, sandbag the base; a remote head or
fixed tilt. Operator watches a monitor; mark arm positions.

## 5. Choosing
Static/pans -> sticks + fluid head; parallax nudges -> slider; vertical reveals in small
spaces -> small jib; bigger moves -> dolly/crane (see those skills).

## Gotchas
- Unbalanced camera on the head - the tilt runs away.
- Drag too heavy - jerky starts.
- Pans too fast for the frame rate - judder.
- Slider on one support bending in the middle.

## Verify
- [ ] Bowl levelled; legs secure.
- [ ] Counterbalance and drag set for the current build.
- [ ] Pans tested for judder; slider and jib levelled and supported.
- [ ] Tool matched to the move.
