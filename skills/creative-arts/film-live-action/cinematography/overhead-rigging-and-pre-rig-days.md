# Overhead rigging and pre-rig days

**Goal:** big sets arrive lit - with overhead and through-window rigs installed by a
rigging crew before the shooting crew arrives, so shooting days aren't spent hanging lights.

## 1. Why pre-rig
Stages and large locations need overhead soft sources, window rigs, and distribution that
take a day or more. A **rigging gaffer** and **rigging key grip** with crews pre-rig
while the main unit shoots elsewhere. Schedule pre-rig (and de-rig/strike) days in the
calendar and budget.

## 2. The lighting plot
The DP and gaffer produce a plot: floor plan with fixture positions, heights, types,
DMX addresses, circuits/power, and purpose ("overhead soft ambience - 8 x SkyPanel S360
in a 20x20 silk box"). Include window rigs (outside units on condors, translights),
practicals and where cable runs go. The rigging crew works from this.

## 3. Overhead structures
- **Truss** (aluminium box truss) on motors or ground support - for heavy loads; motors
  operated by certified riggers.
- **Speed-rail / pipe grids** hung from the stage grid, studio ceiling or from stands
  (on location: wall spreaders, goal posts).
- **Overhead softboxes**: frames with fixtures above a diffusion (silk, grid cloth),
  skirted with black to control spill - the classic stage ambience.
- **Chain motors and spansets**: loads calculated; safety cables on every fixture.

## 4. Power and control
Distribution sized for the full load plus margin (generator or stage power); dimmers or
DMX for every rigged unit so levels can be adjusted from the floor. Label everything.

## 5. Handover
The rigging gaffer walks the shooting gaffer through: what's where, addresses, power,
quirks. Test every unit and every cue the day before. Leave access (ladders, lift) for
adjustments.

## 6. De-rig
Plan de-rig time; it often takes as long as rigging. Wrap the rig while the main unit
moves on.

## Gotchas
- No plot - rigging crew guesses; shooting crew re-rigs.
- Overhead rigs without dimming; adjustments require lifts on the day.
- Ignoring weight/load limits of location ceilings.
- Skirts missing; overhead ambience spills onto walls and floors.

## Verify
- [ ] Pre-rig and de-rig days scheduled and budgeted.
- [ ] Lighting plot with fixtures, heights, power and DMX.
- [ ] Loads calculated; safety cables everywhere.
- [ ] Handover walk-through and full test before shoot day.
