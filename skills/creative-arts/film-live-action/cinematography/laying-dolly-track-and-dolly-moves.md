# Laying dolly track and dolly moves

**Goal:** a perfectly smooth move, repeatable take after take, landing on its marks with
the actors.

## 1. Plan the move
With the DP and operator: start and end frame, the actors' marks, speed, any boom (arm)
up/down. Measure the track length needed (add run-up and stopping distance; most moves
need 1-2 m extra each end).

## 2. Laying straight track
1. Lay sections (steel or aluminium, 8 ft/2.4 m and 4 ft common) on the line; join
   tightly - the joints are where bumps come from.
2. **Level** with a spirit level across and along, building up low sections with
   **wedges** (hardwood, in pairs opposed) and **cribbing** (wood blocks) under the
   track sleepers. On uneven ground, start from the highest point and bring the rest up.
3. Check gauge (consistent width) and that wheels run without catching; push the dolly
   along and feel for bumps; "ride" the track with a level on the dolly.
4. Secure: sandbags or stakes on soft ground; tape joints on rough floors.

## 3. Curved track and floors
- **Curved sections** in standard radii; join to straight sections for arcs; levelling is
  more critical as the dolly's weight shifts.
- **Dance floor**: smooth panels (plywood/MDF with hard surface) laid and levelled for
  free movement in any direction with the dolly on soft wheels; for complex moves.
- Smooth concrete floors: dolly on its soft tyres without track.

## 4. Executing the move
- **Marks**: tape marks on the track for start, stop and intermediate positions (beats
  where the actor hits a mark).
- The dolly grip **times the move to the actors**, not to a count - watching the
  performer and feathering starts and stops (no jolts).
- **Boom/column**: the arm rises/falls hydraulically (Fisher, Chapman); combine with
  tracking for complex moves; mark the arm height too.
- Rehearse with stand-ins; the focus puller marks focus positions along the move.

## Gotchas
- Joints not tight; a bump on every take.
- Track on soft ground that settles over the day; re-level between set-ups.
- Moves that start or stop abruptly.
- Track visible in frame on wide lenses - check the frame at the far end.

## Verify
- [ ] Track length includes run-up; levelled in both directions.
- [ ] Ride test smooth; track secured.
- [ ] Marks set for dolly, arm and focus.
- [ ] Rehearsed with actors; track out of frame along the whole move.
