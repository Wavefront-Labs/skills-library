# Cranes and remote heads

**Goal:** big vertical and sweeping moves, chosen for the shot and executed safely by
a crane crew with a remote-head operator.

## 1. Types
- **Rideable cranes** (operator and focus puller ride the platform): large, heavy; less
  common now.
- **Remote cranes / jibs** (Jimmy Jib, GF-series): camera on a remote head at the end
  of a counter-balanced arm; fixed length.
- **Telescopic cranes** (Technocrane, Scorpio, Supertechno): the arm **extends and retracts**
  during the shot - push in through a window, track along without moving the base.
- **Small jibs** (3-4 m) for low-budget elevation (see sliders and jibs skill).

## 2. Choosing
Reach (arm length) for the frame and height needed, base footprint (doors, floors, ground
bearing), transport, set-up time (big cranes: 1-3 hours), crew (crane tech/grips),
payload (camera build weight), and whether telescoping is required. Techvis for complex
moves.

## 3. Remote heads
- 2-axis (pan/tilt) or 3-axis (with roll) heads: Libra, Scorpio, Oculus, Flight Head.
- Operated with **geared wheels** (camera operators trained on wheels get the most
  precise moves) or **pan bars/joysticks**.
- Stabilised heads compensate for arm vibration.
- Operator sits at a monitor; focus puller remote; set limits/ends to avoid hitting the
  arm.

## 4. Executing the move
- Crane grips (arm swing, telescoping, base move) and the head operator coordinate;
  rehearse with stand-ins. Mark positions.
- Timing to actors: the crane grip watches action, not counts.
- Watch for the shadow of the arm, reflections, and the arm entering the frame at
  extremes.

## 5. Safety
- Counterweights: add/remove weights with the camera **on/off** in the right order (the
  crane tech's sequence) - never unload the camera before counterweight is secured.
- Ground: level, firm; outriggers; wind limits per manufacturer.
- Exclusion zone around the swing; power lines - minimum distances.
- Only trained crane technicians operate.

## Gotchas
- Crane booked without checking site access or floor load.
- Arm visible in frame at the end of the move.
- Counterweight procedure ignored - tip-over risk.
- Remote head operator unpractised on wheels for precise moves.

## Verify
- [ ] Crane type chosen for reach, telescoping, site and payload.
- [ ] Head and operator (wheels/joystick) suited to the move.
- [ ] Rehearsed; frame checked at extremes.
- [ ] Safety: ground, wind, counterweight procedure, power-line clearance.
