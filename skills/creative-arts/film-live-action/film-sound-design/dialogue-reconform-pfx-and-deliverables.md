# Dialogue reconform, PFX and deliverables

**Goal:** the dialogue edit survives every picture change, production effects are
separated for the international version, and the mixer receives an organised, documented
session.

## 1. Reconforming
- Picture changes after "lock" arrive as **change lists** (EDL-based) and new AAF/
  reference movies.
- Tools: **Conformalizer** / EdiLoad (Sounds In Sync), Pro Tools' built-in options,
  **Matchbox**, or manual: apply the list event by event, moving/ trimming regions to the
  new cut, then check sync against the new reference with 2-pops.
- After a reconform, re-check every fade, fill and edit near the changes; new picture
  material needs new dialogue editing.
- Version sessions per picture version (`R3_v07_DX`).

## 2. Production effects (PFX)
- Sounds in the production tracks that aren't dialogue (footsteps, door closes, prop
  handling, clothing) are **PFX**. Move the good ones to **PFX tracks** so:
  - the mixer can balance them separately;
  - they can be used in the **M&E** (music and effects mix for foreign dubbing), where
    the dialogue tracks are removed - anything left only in DX would vanish.
- Mark which PFX are "M&E-safe" (no dialogue bleed); foley/effects must cover the rest
  (note "fill for M&E" items to the foley/ effects teams).

## 3. ADR list and notes
- Maintain the **ADR list**: cue number, character, TC in/out, line, reason (noise/
  performance/ added), priority; share with the ADR supervisor and producers.
- **Dialogue notes** for the mixer: problem areas, NR suggestions, alternate options on
  muted tracks, lines with both boom and lav provided (and why).

## 4. Delivering to the mix
- Session organised by track template; unused regions removed or muted on a "junk" track;
  clip gain rides to even out levels (the mixer appreciates pre-balanced clips).
- Consolidate/ copy media with the session ("Save Copy In" with audio files) for another
  facility; include reference picture, change notes.
- Pre-dub/premix stage: be present to answer questions.

## Gotchas
- Applying change lists without re-checking fades - clicks at new edits.
- Good PFX left on DX tracks - M&E missing footsteps.
- ADR list out of date with the latest picture.
- Session handed over with offline media.

## Verify
- [ ] Reconforms done per change list and checked against new reference.
- [ ] PFX split, M&E-safe items marked, fill notes sent to foley/FX.
- [ ] ADR list and dialogue notes current.
- [ ] Session consolidated, organised and delivered with reference.
