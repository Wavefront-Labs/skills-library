# Effects spotting and sound library management

**Goal:** a complete list of every sound the film needs, and a library system that finds
the right sound in seconds - with custom recordings for the sounds that make the film
unique.

## 1. Spotting session for effects
With the director, supervising sound editor and sound designer, reel by reel:
- **Hard effects** (sync events: doors, guns, cars, impacts), **backgrounds** (ambience
  per location/time), **design** moments (stylised, emotional, creatures, transitions),
  **foley** needs (footsteps, cloth, props), and places for **silence**.
- Note the director's intent ("the house should feel alive", "no music, sound carries the
  tension"), references, and any must-record items.

## 2. The SFX list
Spreadsheet/ database: reel, TC in/out, scene, category (HARD/BG/DESIGN/FOLEY), description,
source idea (library/ record/ design), editor assigned, status. Sorting by category
distributes work across the effects team.

## 3. Library management (Soundminer)
- Ingest libraries into **Soundminer** (or BaseHead, Soundly): it indexes files and
  **metadata** (description, category, keywords).
- Use the **UCS** (Universal Category System) naming and categories (e.g.
  `DOORWood_Door Old Creak Open_JD_Library.wav`) so search works across libraries.
- Add your own descriptions on import; rate favourites; build per-show **playlists/
  bins**.
- **Transfer** to the DAW with spot-to-timecode, embedded metadata, and optional
  processing (pitch, reverse) on the way.
- Licences: track which libraries are licensed for which seats/ productions.

## 4. Field recording for the film
Libraries are shared by everyone - custom recordings give a film its own sound:
- Plan sessions: the hero vehicle, period props, the actual locations' ambiences, weapons
  (armourer, permits), crowds (walla - see loop group).
- Recorders/mics: high sample rate (96/192 kHz) for pitch-manipulation headroom, stereo
  and multi-mic setups (close + distant), contact mics/ hydrophones for design textures.
- Slate verbally, log metadata immediately, ingest into the library with UCS names.

## Gotchas
- Spotting without the director - intent guessed.
- Libraries without metadata; editors waste hours searching.
- Same stock sounds as every other film ("Wilhelm" syndrome).
- Field recordings without slate/ metadata - unusable later.

## Verify

- [ ] Libraries indexed with UCS metadata; licences tracked.
- [ ] Custom recording plan for signature sounds executed and ingested.
- [ ] List status updated as effects are cut.
