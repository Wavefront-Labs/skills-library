# Backgrounds and hard effects editing

**Goal:** a world that sounds real and specific, and sync effects with weight - laid out
so the mixer can premix them quickly.

## 1. Backgrounds (BGs / ambiences)
- Per location/ time: a **base layer** (room tone/ air), **mid layers** (traffic, wind,
  birds, HVAC), **specifics** (distant dog, clock, neighbours) that tell story - placed,
  not looped carelessly.
- Stereo or multichannel (quad/ 5.0 ambiences for surround); avoid mono BGs except
  spot sounds.
- Cut BGs **across picture cuts within a scene** continuously; change with **location**
  changes, with a short overlap/ fade (or a hard cut for a shock).
- Use BGs emotionally: thin out before a key moment; shift as the story's mood shifts.
- Checkerboard BGs across tracks per scene so the mixer can treat transitions.

## 2. Hard effects (sync)
- Cut to the frame: align transients with the visual event (impact frame, muzzle flash).
- **Layer** big sounds: e.g. a gunshot = mechanical (action, trigger), **transient/
  crack**, body (low boom), tail (environment reflections); car = engine (onboard/
  exhaust by speed), tyres/ road, passes/ bys, doors, suspension; impact = transient,
  body, debris, sweetener (low-frequency).
- Match perspective: close vs distant versions; EQ/ reverb for distance; Doppler for passes.
- Vehicles: build engine runs that follow speed changes (cut between RPM steps with
  crossfades, or use specialised plugins).
- Weapons: consistent signature per weapon type; tails match the location (interior vs
  exterior).

## 3. Track layout and premix prep
- Template groups: BG (e.g. 6-12 tracks), HARD FX (8-24), DESIGN, PFX, FOLEY separate.
  Layers of one event on adjacent tracks.
- Name tracks by content; clip gain for rough balance; no plugins the mixer can't
  reproduce (print processing or provide settings).
- Notes for the mixer: intended panning (surround BGs), level intent at key moments.

## Gotchas
- BGs looping audibly (seams every 30 seconds).
- Hard effects a frame late - feels disconnected.
- Single-layer gunshots/impacts sounding thin.
- Unlabelled tracks and processing baked unpredictably.

## Verify
- [ ] BG layers per location; continuous across cuts; story-driven specifics placed.
- [ ] Hard effects frame-accurate and layered; perspective matched.
- [ ] Tracks organised by group; clip gains set.
- [ ] Mixer notes delivered with the session.
