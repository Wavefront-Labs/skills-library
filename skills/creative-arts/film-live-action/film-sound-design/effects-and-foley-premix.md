# Effects and foley premix

**Goal:** hundreds of effects and foley tracks reduced to a handful of balanced,
panned, perspective-correct predubs, so the final mix is about storytelling, not fader
wrangling - and the M&E is complete.

## 1. Order and prep
- Dialogue premix first (it sets the reference level), then BGs, hard FX, foley, design.
- Play each premix **against the dialogue premix** at low level; effects that fight
  dialogue get carved here.
- Predub outputs: e.g. BG 7.1, FX A/B 7.1, FOLEY feet/moves/specs, DESIGN - matching
  the mix template's stem plan.

## 2. Backgrounds premix
- Set the bed of each scene: level for the shot's world, wide in the surrounds for
  exteriors, tighter for interiors.
- Cross-fade scene changes on the cut or pre-lap as the editor intended.
- Roll off sub-bass rumble that adds mud; keep low end deliberate.
- Leave space (frequency and level) around dialogue; BGs breathe up between lines.

## 3. Hard effects premix
- Sync effects at picture perspective: on-screen = panned to the object; big moments
  get LFE and surrounds sparingly.
- Pan movers (cars, passbys) with automation that tracks picture; check centre
  channel isn't overloaded with effects masking dialogue.
- EQ and compress layers to sit together; keep impacts' transients.

## 4. Foley premix
- **Feet**: level so they're present but not "clicky"; per-character EQ consistency;
  perspective with distance (less high end, more room).
- **Moves/cloth**: just audible; it fills silence, never draws attention.
- **Specifics**: match the prop's on-screen size; key props can be featured.
- Add the same room reverb as dialogue so foley sits in the scene; mostly centre/
  on-screen panning.
- Check the **M&E**: mute dialogue and listen - foley must replace what production
  carried (PFX), or the foreign dub will feel empty.

## 5. Keep it flexible
- Don't over-commit: keep raw tracks under the predubs (or virtual premixes via VCAs)
  so notes in the final can reach individual sounds.
- Note any effect that only works with a specific music cue.

## Gotchas
- Premixing too loud: the final has nowhere to go when music arrives.
- Foley panned hard off-screen in wides that are centred - distracting.
- PFX lost when production dialogue is replaced by ADR, and nobody checked foley covers it.
- Surrounds full of effects on small TV downmixes become mush.

## Verify
- [ ] Each predub played against dialogue at mix level.
- [ ] M&E pass (dialogue muted) sounds complete.
- [ ] Pans follow picture; LFE and surrounds used deliberately.
- [ ] Raw tracks still reachable for final-mix notes.
