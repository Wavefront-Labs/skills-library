# Grants and broadcaster commissioning

**Goal:** a financing plan stitched from grants, broadcasters and co-producers - with
applications that win and relationships that last.

## 1. Grant applications
Typical components (e.g. Sundance Documentary Fund, ITVS, Catapult, Doc Society, regional
and national film funds):
- **Logline and synopsis**; **story/ approach statement** (why this story, why you, why now;
  access; visual approach; structure).
- **Work sample** (5-20 min) - the most important part: a cut that shows character, access
  and style, not a trailer.
- **Budget** (top sheet + detail) and **financing plan** (secured vs pending); timeline.
- Team bios; letters of support/ access agreements; impact/ engagement intentions.
Tailor every application to the fund's priorities (read their past grantees); follow
word limits exactly; submit early.

## 2. Funder reporting
Grants come with obligations: interim and final **reports** (progress, spending against
budget, cut status), credit requirements (logos, wording), sometimes screenings. Keep
receipts and a spend tracker per grant; report on time - future funding depends on it.

## 3. Broadcaster commissioning
- Research the **commissioning editor's** slots, strands and recent commissions (UK: BBC
  Storyville, Channel 4; US: PBS POV/Independent Lens, HBO Documentary; streamers).
- Pitch through the broadcaster's **commissioning process** - online submission portals
  and pitching forums (Sheffield MeetMarket, IDFA Forum, Hot Docs Forum) where many
  commissions start.
- Deal types: full commission (broadcaster owns more rights), **pre-sale/ licence**
  (you keep rights; they license territory/ window), **acquisition** (finished film).
- Deliverables, editorial compliance and insurance are contractual - budget for them.

## 4. Co-productions
Partnering with producers in other countries unlocks their national funds/ broadcasters.
**Official co-production treaties** (and the European Convention on Cinematographic
Co-production) give the film national status in each country if creative/ financial
contributions meet the treaty thresholds - plan contributions accordingly.

## Gotchas
- Generic applications sent to every fund.
- Work samples that are trailers without scenes.
- Late or missing funder reports.
- Signing a commission that takes all rights when a licence would do.

## Verify
- [ ] Fund list matched to the project; applications tailored with a strong sample.
- [ ] Grant reporting calendar and spend tracker.
- [ ] Broadcaster targets researched; forum pitches booked.
- [ ] Co-production treaty requirements checked where relevant.
