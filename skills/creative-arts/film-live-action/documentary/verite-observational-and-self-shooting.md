# Verite, observational and self-shooting

**Goal:** footage that cuts into scenes - with beginnings, middles and ends, usable
sound and a camera that was in the right place because the operator understood what
was about to happen.

## 1. Think in scenes, not footage
- Before any event, ask: what's the scene? Who wants what? Where will it start and end?
  Then position for the **people's faces** at the moment of change, not the activity.
- **Cover** like fiction without the control: a wide that establishes, mids on the
  people who matter, cutaways you *know* the editor will need (hands, listeners,
  details, the clock). Reactions are more valuable than actions.
- **Hold shots**: ten seconds minimum of steady framing before you move; the editor
  can't use a pan that never settles. Zoom/reframe *between* usable shots.
- **Beginnings and ends**: film people arriving and leaving, doors, the moment before
  and after; shoot the exit of a scene even when you think it's over.
- Let the moment finish; don't cut the camera because *you* feel awkward.
- Shooting ratio for observational work is 40:1-100:1; that's normal, but each day
  should produce 2-3 potential scenes, not 6 hours of waiting.

## 2. Anticipation and position
Stand where the light is behind or beside you and the action will come *toward* the
lens; know the room's geography; pre-focus and expose for the spot where the moment
will be. Read body language for who's about to speak. Move early and slowly; a
moving camera during a quiet moment destroys it. In confrontations, step back and
go wide; in intimacy, go close and still.

## 3. The one-person rig
Camera: Sony FX6/FX3, Canon C70/C300, Panasonic EVA1/S5II, or similar with **internal
ND**, **reliable autofocus** (eye AF for walk-and-talks), dual card slots, good low
light and a **servo zoom** or a 24-105 class lens; a fast prime for interviews.
Shoulder/Easyrig for long days. Settings: log or a high-DR profile with a monitoring
LUT, 180° shutter, 25/24 fps, 4K; auto-ISO/auto-ND when the light changes faster
than you can (fix in the grade beats missing it).
Sound (the self-shooter's weak point): a **top-mounted shotgun** (MKE 600/NTG5) into
one channel, a **radio lav** on the principal into the other; a **MixPre-3/6 or Zoom
F3 in 32-bit float** in the bag feeding the camera (or recorded separately with
timecode via Tentacle) when there are two or more lavs; headphones *always* on at least
one ear. Set levels in rehearsal with limiters on; check the lav for rustle before
every scene.
Batteries and media: two of everything, swap at 30%, offload nightly to two drives with
checksums, log clips (date/location/who) the same night.

## 4. Working alone with people
- Explain the rig once and then make it boring; eat with them; the camera goes
  everywhere so it stops meaning anything.
- Ask permission the first time you follow someone into a private space; after that
  read the room.
- Don't direct - but you may *ask* ("would you two normally talk about that here?")
  and you may **wait**. Reconstructions must be labelled as such on screen.
- Speak only when necessary and never over the moment; your questions in verite are
  cut unless you're a character.
- Keep a **daily log**: scenes captured, missing pieces, release status, follow-ups.

## 5. Safety and judgement
Situational awareness: exits, hostile crowds, police, roads, weather. Don't film into
danger for a shot; the footage is worthless if you're hurt. Tell someone where you are;
check in on a schedule; local fixers decide what's safe. Know when to stop filming
because a person needs help more than the film needs the moment (duty of care).

## Gotchas
- Constant zooming/reframing; nothing holds long enough to cut.
- Filming the activity instead of the person's face when it matters.
- Scratch camera mic only; the key line is lost under traffic.
- Shooting every minute of a ten-hour day; the editor drowns and the shooter burns out.
- Leaving before the scene actually ends.

## Verify
- [ ] Each day's log lists scenes with beginnings/ends, plus cutaways and reactions.
- [ ] Shots held 10 s+; moves between shots, not during.
- [ ] Two sound sources (shotgun + lav) with levels checked; headphones on.
- [ ] Media offloaded to two drives with checksums; batteries/cards rotated; safety check-ins kept.
