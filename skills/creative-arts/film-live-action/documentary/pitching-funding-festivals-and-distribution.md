# Pitching, funding, festivals and distribution

**Goal:** money to make the film from sources that leave you the film you wanted, a
premiere that gives it a life, and a release plan that reaches the audience the film
is for - planned from development, not after lock.

## 1. The pitch package
- **Logline** (one sentence, with the engine and the stakes) and a **one-pager**.
- **Treatment** (5-12 pages): the story as a film - opening, characters, the arc/
  process, the ending you expect (and alternatives), visual/sonic approach, access
  secured, why you, why now, comparable films.
- **Sizzle / teaser** (2-5 min): real footage showing the characters alive and the
  tone; a proof-of-concept scene beats a montage. Fund the sizzle shoot first.
- **Deck** (10-15 slides): logline, synopsis, characters (photos), the world, style
  frames, team bios, access, production plan and schedule, budget top line, **finance
  plan** (what's raised, what's asked), distribution/impact plan, contact.
- **Budget** at the level the funder expects (doc features typically $300k-$1.5m;
  broadcast hours less) and a **finance plan** that balances: commissions, pre-sales,
  funds, equity, tax credit, in-kind.
- Rehearse a **7-minute verbal pitch** with a 2-minute version; forums enforce the clock.

## 2. Where the money is
- **Broadcasters / streamers** (BBC Storyville, Arte, ZDF, PBS/ITVS, NHK, Netflix,
  etc.): commissions or pre-sales/licences for territory; they fund a share and
  want editorial involvement and delivery specs. Approach via commissioning editors
  at markets or through a producer with a track record.
- **Public funds and institutes**: BFI Doc Society Fund (UK), Sundance Documentary
  Fund, Screen Australia/NZ, Telefilm, national film institutes, Creative Europe
  MEDIA (development/co-dev), regional funds - development, production and completion
  grants with reporting obligations.
- **Foundations and impact funders**: issue-aligned (Ford/JustFilms, MacArthur,
  Bertha, Catapult, Perspective, Chicken & Egg): grants, often with an impact
  campaign expectation; preserve editorial independence in writing.
- **Co-productions**: partners in other countries unlock their national funds and
  broadcasters (treaty co-pros have rules on spend and creative share); a co-producer is
  a creative partner, not just money.
- **Equity / private investors**: recoupment waterfalls, rarely profitable in docs;
  be honest about returns.
- **Crowdfunding**: good for sizzle/finishing money and audience-building; it's a
  campaign job in itself.
- **Tax credits/rebates** on qualifying spend; **in-kind** post deals; **educational
  pre-sales**.
Track every application: deadline, requirements, decision date, outcome, feedback.

## 3. Pitch forums and markets
IDFA Forum, Sheffield MeetMarket, Hot Docs Forum, CPH:FORUM, Sunny Side of the Doc,
Dok Leipzig Co-Pro Market, Good Pitch (Doc Society, impact), regional forums. Apply
with the package; if selected, you pitch to a table of commissioners and funders, then
take one-to-one meetings. Go with a producer; follow up within a week; expect money to
arrive over 12-24 months of meetings.

## 4. Festival strategy
- Decide the **tier-one target** for the **world premiere** (Sundance, IDFA, CPH:DOX,
  Tribeca, SXSW, Hot Docs, Sheffield, Berlinale/Cannes sections, Venice, TIFF) by the
  film's type and audience; premiere status (world/international/North American) is the
  currency - don't screen anywhere (including online or for funders' public events)
  before. Submit early-bird to the primary and have a tiered plan B/C.
- A **festival consultant/publicist** for the premiere; a trailer, stills, press kit
  (EPK), director's statement, subtitled DCP/ProRes screeners, Vimeo links with
  passwords and expiry.
- Awards-qualifying runs (Academy doc rules require a theatrical run or qualifying
  festival award) if that's the aim.
- Festivals are the market: sales agents and buyers watch; plan the sales strategy to
  peak at the premiere.

## 5. Distribution
- **Sales agent** (Dogwoof, Cinetic, Submarine, Autlook, Cinephil, Met Film Sales…):
  sells territories and platforms for a commission (20-30%) plus expenses; signs
  before/at the premiere; assess their slate and what they'd actually do.
- **Distributors** per territory (theatrical, TV, VOD), or an all-rights streamer deal
  (money up front, no back end, global reach).
- **Self-distribution / hybrid**: event cinema, community and educational screenings
  (Tugg/Demand.film style), educational distributors (Kanopy, Good Docs, Bullfrog),
  TVOD platforms via aggregators, impact-led screenings with partners.
- **Impact campaign** (if the film wants change): goals, partners, screening tour,
  policy targets, measurement - funded separately (Doc Society's Impact Field Guide).
- **Deliverables**: DCP, ProRes masters, HDR where asked, 5.1 and stereo mixes, M&E,
  textless, closed captions/subtitles, dialogue lists, music cue sheets, E&O certificate,
  chain of title and releases, archive log, key art/stills/trailer, press kit, QC
  reports; a deliverables budget line (5-10%) in the finance plan from the start.

## Gotchas
- Pitching a subject, not a story; commissioners ask "what happens?"
- Giving a funder editorial approval or a broadcaster all rights worldwide for the price of one territory.
- Screening the "work-in-progress" publicly and losing premiere status.
- No deliverables budget; the sale is held up by an uninsurable archive log.
- Waiting for the festival to "discover" the film with no publicist or sales plan.

## Verify
- [ ] Pitch package complete: logline, treatment, sizzle, deck, budget, finance plan, 7-minute pitch rehearsed.
- [ ] Funding tracker with applications, deadlines and outcomes; editorial independence protected in contracts.
- [ ] Premiere target chosen; nothing screened publicly before; publicist/consultant and EPK ready.
- [ ] Sales/distribution plan decided (agent, distributor, self/hybrid, impact) and deliverables budgeted.
