# Impact campaigns and outreach

**Goal:** the film doesn't just screen - it changes something specific (minds,
behaviours, communities, policies), with partners who carry the work beyond release,
and evidence of what happened.

## 1. Define the impact
- Doc Society's Impact Field Guide frames four impact dynamics: **changing minds**,
  **changing behaviours**, **building communities**, **changing structures**
  (policy/law). Pick one or two primary goals; tie each to a target audience.
- Write an **impact strategy**: issue landscape, goals, audiences, partners, tactics,
  timeline, budget, how you'll measure.
- Start during production; strong campaigns shape access and release choices.

## 2. People and money
- An **impact producer** leads it - strategy, partnerships, campaign events, evaluation.
  Budget them from development where possible.
- Funding: impact funds and foundations (Doc Society, Ford JustFilms and others),
  NGO partners, individual philanthropic donors; Good Pitch-style forums connect
  films with funders and organisations.

## 3. Partners
- NGOs, community groups, educators, faith groups, unions, policymakers already
  working on the issue - the film is a tool for their work, not the other way round.
- Involve affected communities and contributors; nothing about them without them.
- Agree partner roles, branding and messaging boundaries (editorial independence).

## 4. Tactics
- **Community and grassroots screenings** with Q&A and a call to action.
- **Policy screenings** (parliament, congress, agencies) timed to decisions.
- **Discussion/educational guides**, short versions and clips for classrooms and
  social media; educational licensing.
- Petitions, pledges, donation routes owned by partners.

## 5. Distribution timing
- Coordinate with distributors: windows and **holdbacks** may limit free community
  screenings or online clips. Negotiate impact carve-outs in the distribution deal.

## 6. Evaluate
- Set indicators in advance (screenings, attendees, partner actions, policy citations,
  survey change); collect stories and data; report to funders.

## Gotchas
- Campaign bolted on after release with no budget or partners.
- Goals too broad ("raise awareness") to measure or achieve.
- Distribution deal blocking the campaign's screenings.
- Contributors exposed to backlash by campaign publicity without consent.

## Verify
- [ ] Written impact strategy with specific goals and audiences.
- [ ] Impact producer and budget in place.
- [ ] Partner agreements and distributor carve-outs agreed.
- [ ] Indicators defined and evaluation reported.
