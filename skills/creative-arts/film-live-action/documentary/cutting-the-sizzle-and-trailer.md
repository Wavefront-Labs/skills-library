# Cutting the sizzle and trailer

**Goal:** a short piece that makes a funder, commissioner or audience want the film:
a sizzle/pitch trailer to raise money, a teaser to build interest, a trailer to sell
the finished film.

## 1. Know which one you're cutting
| Piece | Audience | Length | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sizzle / pitch trailer / work sample | Funders, commissioners, pitch forums | 2-5 min (or the funder's limit) | Prove story, access, character and the filmmaker's voice |
| Teaser | Festivals, socials, crowdfunding | 30 s-1.5 min | Intrigue; one idea, no resolution |
| Trailer | Distributors, audiences | 1.5-2.5 min | Promise the experience and tone, sell the release |
Check each funder's work-sample rules (length, whether it must be a scene or can be a
trailer) - they differ.

## 2. Structure for a sizzle
1. **Hook** in the first 10-20 seconds: the most arresting moment or question.
2. **Character**: who we follow, in their own voice/action.
3. **Stakes and conflict**: what's at risk, why now.
4. **Access proof**: intimate verite moments only you could get.
5. **Approach**: style and tone (how the film will feel).
6. Close on the unanswered question; title card, filmmakers, contact.
Don't explain the whole film; a sizzle that resolves gives buyers no reason to fund it.

## 3. Cutting
- Use the best real moments, even if they're early footage; no voiceover crutch unless
  the film will have narration.
- Let scenes play long enough to feel real (a sizzle of 2-second shots reads as a
  promo, not a film).
- Music: drives pace, but tone must match the film.
- Supers: minimal, readable.

## 4. Music and clearance
- Sizzles shown privately often use temp music; risky if it leaks or goes online, and
  some funders/forums require cleared material. Prefer library/production music with
  a pitch/promo licence or the composer's music.
- Anything public (teasers on socials, trailers) must be fully cleared: music,
  archive, releases from people shown.
- Trailer audio mixed for its platform (online loudness vs cinema limits).

## Gotchas
- Sizzle too long: decision makers stop at 3 minutes.
- Showing every contributor; the piece needs a clear protagonist.
- Uncleared music in a public teaser: takedowns and a bad first impression.
- Using moments contributors consented to only for the final film, not promotion -
  check releases.

## Verify
- [ ] Hook within 20 s; protagonist and stakes clear.
- [ ] Length within the destination's limits.
- [ ] Music and archive cleared for the use.
- [ ] Ends with title and contact; tested on someone who doesn't know the project.
