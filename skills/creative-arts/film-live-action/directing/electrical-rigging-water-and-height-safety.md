# Electrical, rigging, water and height safety

**Goal:** the most dangerous set conditions are risk-assessed, staffed by competent
people, and controlled - so that the director's shot never relies on luck.

## 1. Electrical
- Distribution by qualified electricians (gaffer, best boy electric); generator earthed
  and positioned away from crowds; cables protected at crossings (cable ramps/mats).
- **RCD (UK) / GFCI (US)** protection on any circuit near water or wet conditions and
  on all handheld/practical circuits; HMI heads and ballasts kept dry, with protected
  connections.
- Tie-ins to building supply only by licensed electricians.
- Rain: cover distro, raise connections off the ground; stop work if water reaches
  connectors.

## 2. Rigging
- Rigging grips/riggers calculate loads; use rated hardware, safety cables on every
  overhead fixture, sandbags on stands (two on large stands, more in wind).
- Overhead work: exclusion zone beneath; announcement "rigging overhead".
- Wind limits for large frames (butterflies, 20x20s) and cranes - set by the key grip
  and manufacturer.

## 3. Water work
- **Water safety coordinator** and **safety divers** for anything in water (tanks,
  rivers, sea); lifeguards; rescue plan and equipment.
- Water quality tested (rivers/lakes - leptospirosis); tank heated for long work.
- **Hypothermia** limits: time in water per temperature; warming tents, wetsuits under
  costume, scheduled breaks; medic on set.
- Electrical exclusion near water (low-voltage/battery fixtures in or near water).
- Actors' swimming ability confirmed; doubles for risky work.

## 4. Work at height
- **MEWPs** (cherry pickers, scissor lifts) operated by trained/licensed operators
  (IPAF/PAL cards in UK); harness and lanyard in booms; ground conditions checked.
- Scaffolds/towers by competent erectors with handrails and toe boards.
- Actors near edges: barriers out of frame, harness with hidden anchor, or VFX
  extension.
- Rescue plan for anyone suspended (suspension trauma).

## 5. Director's role
Agree the risk assessment and the method statement before the day; schedule time for
safety briefings; never push a department to skip a control for a shot.

## Gotchas
- Mains-voltage fixtures near a tank or rain bars without RCD/GFCI.
- Unsecured overhead units over cast.
- Cold-water scenes with no time limits.
- MEWP used by untrained crew "just to reach the light".

## Verify
- [ ] Electrical protection (RCD/GFCI) and cable management checked by gaffer.
- [ ] Rigging loads, safety cables and wind limits set.
- [ ] Water safety coordinator, divers, medic and time limits arranged.
- [ ] Height work by trained operators with rescue plan; risk assessment signed.
