# Crew rates and equipment quotes

**Goal:** budget lines based on real current rates and real quotes, with the
overtime, kit and fringe assumptions written down.

## 1. Crew rates
- **Union scale**: US IATSE/Teamsters/DGA/SAG-AFTRA agreements publish minimums by
  position and budget tier (low-budget agreements have reduced scales). UK: **Bectu rate
  cards** by budget band (major film, low budget), with standard working day (e.g. 10+1
  hours) and overtime rules; PACT/Bectu agreements.
- **Market rates** above scale for experienced HoDs - ask line producers and agents.
- Rate basis: daily or weekly, **guaranteed hours** (e.g. 12-hour day guarantee, or
  10+1), overtime multipliers (1.5x/2x), sixth/seventh-day premiums, night premiums.
- Record each line's assumption: `1st AC: $X/week (50 hrs guarantee) + kit $Y/week`.

## 2. Kit and box rentals
Many crew rent their own kit (camera assistant's kit, make-up kit, sound package from
the mixer, location manager's car). Budget **kit fees** per week; sound packages often
rented from the mixer at a weekly rate.

## 3. Equipment quotes
- Send rental houses a **package list** (camera bodies, lenses, support, lighting/grip
  list from gaffer and key grip) with dates and shoot/prep days.
- Typical deal: **day rate x 3 = weekly rate** (a "3-day week") and longer shoots
  discounted further - ask; compare quotes on identical lists.
- Check: insurance requirements (certificate naming the house), loss & damage waiver,
  delivery/collection, prep days free, expendables included or not.
- Get two or three quotes; leverage but don't trade away reliability or support.

## 4. Put it in the budget
Use globals for weeks; separate **labour** and **rentals**; apply fringes to labour only.
Add **overtime allowance** (e.g. 10% of crew labour on long days) and box rentals as
their own lines (taxable treatment differs).

## Gotchas
- Using old rate cards; minimums rise annually.
- Forgetting guaranteed hours: budgeting 10 hours when the agreement guarantees 12.
- Equipment quotes for different lists, so the cheapest is the most incomplete.
- No overtime allowance on location-heavy schedules.

## Verify
- [ ] Current union/rate-card figures with source and date noted.
- [ ] Rate basis, guarantees and overtime recorded per line.
- [ ] Kit fees and box rentals listed.
- [ ] Two or three equipment quotes on the same list; terms compared.
