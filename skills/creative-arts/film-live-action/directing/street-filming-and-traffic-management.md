# Street filming and traffic management

**Goal:** shoot in public space legally and safely, with the least disruption to the
public - so you can come back.

## 1. Permits and film office
Apply through the local **film office** (e.g. FilmLA, Film London borough film
services): location, dates, times, crew size, vehicles, parking, equipment on the
pavement, special requests (SFX, weapons, drones, closures). Lead times range from
days (small units) to weeks (closures). Provide public liability insurance certificate
(e.g. GBP/USD 5-10m) naming the authority.

## 2. Choose the level of traffic control
| Need | Method |
|---|---|
| Brief holds of passing traffic for takes | **Intermittent Traffic Control (ITC)** - police or licensed traffic officers hold traffic for short periods (often max 3-5 minutes) |
| Picture cars driving, stunts, big set dressing | **Full road closure** - traffic regulation order / permit, signed diversions |
| Pavement only | Pedestrian management with marshals; keep a clear walkway |
Requirements are set by the authority; police presence may be mandatory for holds or
weapons.

## 3. Traffic management plan
Prepared by a qualified traffic management company: signage (per local standards such as
UK Chapter 8), cones, diversion routes, marshal positions, emergency vehicle access,
timings. Brief all marshals; radios on a dedicated channel.

## 4. Neighbours and the public
- **Letter drop** to residents and businesses (dates, times, contact number) per film
  office rules; obtain signatures if required.
- Marshals politely ask (not order) pedestrians to wait; never block emergency access
  or disabled access.
- Background: members of the public who appear identifiable may need releases or notices
  (signage "filming in progress").

## 5. Unit base and parking
Unit base (trucks, catering, honeywagons) off the street where possible; parking
suspensions arranged with the authority; generators placed away from homes; no idling.

## 6. On the day
Location manager on site; permit copy on hand; start and wrap on time (curfews);
leave the street cleaner than found; log any complaints.

## Gotchas
- Holding traffic longer than allowed - officers will release it mid-take.
- Starting before the permitted time; complaints threaten future permits.
- Forgetting emergency access routes.
- Picture cars driving in live traffic without control.

## Verify
- [ ] Permit and insurance certificate obtained; conditions read.
- [ ] Traffic control method agreed and TM plan prepared.
- [ ] Residents notified; signage posted.
- [ ] Unit base, parking and curfews planned; on-site liaison named.
