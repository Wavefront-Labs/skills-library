# Exhibit G, timesheets and production reports

**Goal:** the records that pay people correctly, prove union compliance and tell
producers exactly what was shot - completed accurately every day.

## 1. Start paperwork (before day one for each person)
Deal memo, tax forms (US: W-4, I-9 with ID; UK: starter checklist / right-to-work
checks), union forms, emergency contact, NDA if applicable. Payroll won't pay without it;
the 2nd AD or production coordinator collects for cast and crew.

## 2. Exhibit G (SAG-AFTRA Performers Production Time Report)
Daily form (2nd AD) listing each performer's: **report time on set, makeup/wardrobe
in-out, meal periods (start-finish), dismissed time, travel times**, stunt adjustments,
and signature at the end of day. It's the legal record for performer pay and
penalties (meal penalties, forced calls, turnaround). Send copies to SAG-AFTRA per
the agreement; keep originals. Errors cost money and claims - complete it in real time,
not from memory.

## 3. Crew timesheets
Weekly (or daily) timesheets per crew member: call, meal breaks, wrap, with
department head approval. Must agree with the **production report** times. Note
meal penalties and short turnarounds.

## 4. Script supervisor's daily report
Scenes and set-ups shot, pages completed, minutes (screen time estimate), number of
set-ups, takes, circled takes, wild tracks, added/omitted scenes, retakes - plus
cumulative totals versus schedule.

## 5. Daily production report (DPR / progress report)
Prepared by the 2nd AD and signed by the UPM/1st AD:
- Crew call, first shot, lunch, first shot after lunch, camera wrap, last person out.
- Scenes scheduled vs completed; pages, minutes, set-ups (from script supervisor).
- Cast times (from Exhibit G), background counts and times.
- Film/media used (cards/TB), equipment and vehicles.
- **Delays, accidents, incidents** and their causes - insurers rely on this.
- Ahead/behind schedule statement.
Sent to producers and the studio/financier the next morning. A **wrap report** at
the end of the shoot summarises totals.

## Gotchas
- Exhibit G times reconstructed later; disputes with no defensible record.
- DPR times not matching timesheets.
- Incidents omitted from the DPR, then an insurance claim fails.
- Missing start paperwork delaying pay for the first week.

## Verify
- [ ] Start paperwork complete for everyone before day one.
- [ ] Exhibit G filled live and signed by performers daily.
- [ ] Script supervisor report and DPR agree on scenes, pages, set-ups.
- [ ] DPR distributed next morning, with delays and incidents recorded.
