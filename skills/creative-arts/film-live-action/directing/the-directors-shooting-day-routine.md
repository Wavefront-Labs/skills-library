# The director's shooting-day routine

**Goal:** get the first shot up fast, stay where you can direct performance best, and
leave every night ready for tomorrow.

## 1. Before call
- Read today's scenes and your notebook; know the **first shot** and the day's
  must-haves (A-priority shots).
- Arrive before crew call. Meet the 1st AD and DP for a 5-minute walk of the set:
  first set-up, any changes overnight, weather, cast status.

## 2. First shot of the day
The first shot sets the day's pace - crews say "first shot by 9" or within an hour of
call. Choose something **simple and already planned** (often the master or a wide
that's pre-lit). Process: **private rehearsal** with actors (director, actors, DP, 1st AD,
script supervisor), **marking rehearsal** for camera/focus/boom, then hand over to the DP
to light while actors go to hair/make-up. **Crew show** (watching rehearsal) lets
departments see the blocking at once.

## 3. Monitor or by the camera?
- **By the camera**: actors see you; notes are instant and private; you feel the
  scene's energy. Many directors stay here for intimate scenes and use a small handheld
  monitor (wireless video on a director's monitor).
- **At the monitor (video village)**: you see exactly the frame and can watch
  multiple cameras; better for complex moves, VFX, action. Downside: distance from actors
  and an audience of producers/clients commenting.
- Practice: rehearse by camera, watch takes on a monitor near set (not 30 m away),
  walk to actors for notes. Keep the village small and quiet; producers' notes come to
  you privately.

## 4. Through the day
Check progress against the schedule with the 1st AD at lunch; decide early what to
simplify if behind (combine set-ups, drop C-priority shots) rather than losing the
last scene.

## 5. Wrap
- Last shot called by the AD ("martini shot"). Thank cast and crew.
- **Next day**: confirm first shot and any changes with 1st AD and DP; check the call
  sheet.
- **Dailies**: watch yesterday's (or today's) dailies on the platform the post team
  uses; note circled takes and performance moments for the editor; send notes to the
  editor the same night.
- Review the notebook for tomorrow's scenes; rest - the schedule is long.

## Gotchas
- Arriving with the first shot undecided; the crew waits.
- Directing everything through a monitor speaker from a distant village.
- Watching dailies late at night every night and running on no sleep.
- Not checking tomorrow's call sheet - surprises on the day.

## Verify
- [ ] First shot planned and walked through with AD and DP before call.
- [ ] Private rehearsal -> marking -> crew show done per set-up.
- [ ] Monitor position close to set; notes given in person.
- [ ] Dailies reviewed and editor notes sent; tomorrow's first shot confirmed.
