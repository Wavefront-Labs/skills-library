# Scheduling minors, animals and weather days

**Goal:** a schedule that respects the hard limits of children, animals and weather -
so they don't break the day or the law.

## 1. Minors
- Hours vary by **age band and jurisdiction**. Example (California): ages 6-9 may be on
  set 8 hours with 4 hours work and 3 hours school on school days; 9-16, 9 hours on set
  with 5 work and 3 school; 16-18, 10 hours with 6 work. Infants (15 days-6 months) only
  20 minutes of work in a 2-hour window. UK limits differ by age and are set out in the
  2014 regulations (e.g. maximum performance hours and breaks; no work before 07:00
  or after 19:00 for under-13s, with variations).
- Build **school time** into the day for school-age children; the studio teacher /
  chaperone tracks it.
- Schedule children **early** and their coverage first; use **doubles** (age-appropriate
  or adults for wides) and twins to stretch hours.
- Night shoots with children are heavily restricted - plan day-for-night or stage nights.
- Turnaround (e.g. 12 hours) applies.

## 2. Animals
- Follow **American Humane Guidelines** (US) or local animal welfare law; notify the
  monitoring body in advance on covered productions.
- Animals need **rehearsal/training days** before shooting and handlers (wranglers) on
  set; working time and rest by species; temperature limits.
- Schedule animal scenes early in the day and allow extra time; trained doubles for
  different behaviours.
- Record animal scenes on the DOOD (category: animals).

## 3. Weather days
- Mark weather-dependent scenes (exteriors, specific weather needed or must avoid).
- Place them with an **adjacent cover set** (interior at or near the location, cast in
  common).
- Include **weather days** (unassigned contingency days) in the calendar for long
  exterior stretches; insurance may require.
- Check **sun times** and season (golden hour, short winter days); a 10-hour exterior day
  in December in the UK is impossible.

## Gotchas
- Scheduling a child for a full adult day.
- Animals on set without trained doubles or rehearsal.
- Exterior-heavy weeks with no cover sets.
- Ignoring daylight hours when estimating exterior pages.

## Verify
- [ ] Child hours and school time per jurisdiction built into each day; teacher/chaperone booked.
- [ ] Animal handler, rehearsals and welfare monitor arranged.
- [ ] Weather-dependent scenes paired with cover sets; contingency days present.
- [ ] Daylight hours checked against exterior workloads.
