# Background and extras casting

**Goal:** the right number and type of background people, booked, dressed and
placed, so the world looks real and the budget holds.

## 1. Count from the breakdown
On each breakdown sheet list background by type and number ("12 commuters, 2 police
uniforms, 1 dog walker with dog"). Note **matching background** (same people across
scenes for continuity) and **featured** extras (close to camera, specific action). Use
the director's staging plan to justify numbers - "how many do we see in the widest
shot?" Use repeat crossings and camera tricks to stretch numbers.

## 2. Brief the background agency
Send per day: scene numbers, period/style, counts by type, age/look, any skills
(driving, dancing, swimming), wardrobe needs, call time/location, rates. Ask for
photos for featured roles. Book **10% over** for no-shows on big days.

## 3. Union rules (check current agreements)
- **US SAG-AFTRA**: covered zones require a minimum number of union background
  per day before non-union can be hired on covered productions; special-ability,
  stand-in and wardrobe-change bumps apply.
- **UK**: Equity/FAA (Film Artistes' Association) agreements set daily rates, overtime
  and supplements (own wardrobe, props, haircuts). Smaller productions use non-union
  agencies with agreed rates.
Budget per head including supplements and overtime; meals.

## 4. Wardrobe and continuity
Specify "bring 3 options" per type (colours to avoid: white, red, logos, green on a
green-screen day). Costume fits for period work need a separate fitting day. Track
who wore what in matching scenes (photos at holding).

## 5. On the day
- **Holding area** with sign-in (vouchers/timesheets), toilets, food, shade/heat.
- The **2nd AD** (or crowd AD) places and cues background; the director sets the
  intent ("busy commute, nobody looks at camera").
- Background begins action **before** "action" for principals ("background action!").
- Mime dialogue (no real sound); separate wild tracks for walla later.
- Release them as early as possible to save overtime; collect vouchers.

## Gotchas
- Booking by headcount without types; everyone shows up the same age.
- Non-union hires in a union-covered situation; claims later.
- Logos and branded clothing needing clearance in post.
- Featured background accidentally upgraded to a speaking role (rate change) by giving them a line.

## Verify
- [ ] Counts and types per scene from the breakdown, with matching flagged.
- [ ] Agency brief sent with wardrobe notes; over-booking buffer.
- [ ] Union obligations checked; supplements budgeted.
- [ ] Holding, sign-in and continuity photos organised for the day.
