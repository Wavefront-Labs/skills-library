# Building the stripboard: sorting, banners and calendar

**Goal:** a board that groups scenes into efficient shooting days and a calendar that
turns those days into real dates.

## 1. Strip colours (standard)
| Colour | Meaning |
|---|---|
| White | Day interior |
| Yellow | Day exterior |
| Blue | Night interior |
| Green | Night exterior |
| Black (banner) | Day break / end of day |
| Others (orange, red, grey) | Banners, notes, travel, holidays - production's choice |
In Movie Magic Scheduling (MMS) colours derive from INT/EXT and DAY/NIGHT fields;
customise in the board design if needed.

## 2. Sorting principles (in priority order)
1. **Location/set** - shoot everything at one location together; company moves cost hours.
2. **Cast** - group an actor's scenes to minimise their days (and holds).
3. **Day/night** - don't flip from night to day work without turnaround; group nights
   into blocks at the end of a week.
4. **Exterior before interior** at a location (cover sets for weather).
5. **Constraints**: actor availability, location dates, children's hours, stunts and
   VFX days, special equipment rentals, sun direction, story continuity (haircuts, weight
   change, destruction).
MMS: *Board > Sort* by multiple fields (e.g. Location, then Cast, then I/E, then D/N),
then drag strips manually to refine.

## 3. Day breaks and banners
Insert **day breaks** after each day's work: target page count (~3-5 pages/day for
features, 7-10 for TV) and hours (estimated time per scene). MMS shows totals per day.
Add **banners** for notes (company move, travel day, holiday, "weather cover"). Mark
**cover sets** in banners.

## 4. The boneyard
Strips not yet scheduled (cut scenes, uncertain scenes, pick-ups) go in the **boneyard**
(in MMS a separate board area) - visible but not on any day. Review it before
publishing; nothing that's in the script should hide there.

## 5. Calendar
Set the **start date** and calendar (*Design > Calendar* in MMS): working week (5- or
6-day), holidays, travel days, days off. The calendar assigns dates to the shooting
days so the DOOD and call sheets have real dates. Check union **turnaround** (e.g. 12
hours crew, 12 cast) and weekly rest days when placing nights near weekends.

## Gotchas
- Sorting by script order out of habit.
- Day breaks with 9 pages one day and 1 the next - check page counts and timings.
- Scenes lost in the boneyard.
- Night shoot ending Friday morning with Monday day call - turnaround OK, but crew body clock wrecked; plan a split day.

## Verify
- [ ] Strip colours standard; every strip has I/E, D/N, set, cast, pages, estimate.
- [ ] Sorted by location -> cast -> D/N with constraints honoured.
- [ ] Day breaks with balanced page counts; banners for moves and covers.
- [ ] Boneyard reviewed; calendar set with holidays and turnaround.
