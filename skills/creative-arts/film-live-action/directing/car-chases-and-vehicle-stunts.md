# Car chases and vehicle stunts

**Goal:** an exciting, legible chase built from shots that are physically safe to
execute and fit the schedule and road permissions.

## 1. Design with the stunt coordinator
- Write the **story of the chase**: goal, escalations, reversal, outcome (like any set piece).
- The **stunt coordinator** and **precision/stunt drivers** design the gags: near
  misses, slides, jumps, crashes. Each gag is a separate set-up with its own risk
  assessment.
- **Techvis** or a stunt-vis (a filmed rehearsal with phones/small cameras on the actual
  route) proves timings, speeds and camera positions.

## 2. Control the road
Full road closure with police/authority permit and traffic management; rolling road
blocks for moving shots; marshals at junctions; emergency vehicles on standby. Never
shoot vehicle stunts in open traffic.

## 3. Camera vehicles and rigs
- **Russian Arm / crane car** (U-Crane etc.) on a high-performance camera car - dynamic
  tracking at speed.
- **Hard mounts** on hero cars (hood, door, bumper), crash cams (armoured boxes) for impacts.
- **Picture cars** duplicated (hero, stunt, process/tow) - matching dressing; art and
  transport manage them.
- Drones for overheads (licensed operator, closed area).
- Actors in close-ups on **process trailers/low-loaders** or LED volumes; stunt drivers
  for the action, faces hidden or doubled.

## 4. Coverage plan
Hero action wides from multiple cameras (each gag is expensive - cover once from 3-6
angles), dynamic Russian Arm runs, interiors (actors, POV out), inserts (pedal, gear
stick, speedometer), reactions. Keep screen direction consistent (see travel-direction
skill).

## 5. Safety chain
- Stunt coordinator has authority to stop anything; the 1st AD runs the safety meeting
  before each gag; everyone knows the abort signal.
- Speeds, distances and positions of crew/camera marked; crew behind barriers; unmanned
  cameras for close crash positions.
- Rehearse at slow speed, then build up.

## Gotchas
- Director asking for "a bit faster" on the take - only the coordinator changes stunts.
- Actors driving in action they're not trained for.
- Uncontrolled public roads.
- Too few cameras on a one-time crash.

## Verify
- [ ] Chase story and gag list agreed with stunt coordinator; techvis done.
- [ ] Road closure permit and traffic management confirmed.
- [ ] Camera vehicles, rigs and picture-car duplicates booked.
- [ ] Safety meeting per gag; multi-camera coverage on one-time events.
