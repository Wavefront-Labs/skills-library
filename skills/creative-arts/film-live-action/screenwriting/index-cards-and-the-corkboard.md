# Index cards and the corkboard

**Goal:** see the entire film on one wall, where moving a scene costs a pushpin, not a
rewrite.

## 1. Board layout
Snyder's board: **four horizontal rows** - Act One (p.1-25), Act Two first half
(25-55), Act Two second half (55-85), Act Three (85-110). Mark the beat-sheet
milestones (Catalyst, Break into Two, Midpoint, All Is Lost, Break into Three) on
cards of a distinct colour first. Aim for **~40 cards** total, ~10 per row; a row with
15 or 4 shows the imbalance immediately.

## 2. What goes on a card
- Top: slug location (`INT. GARAGE - NIGHT`) - keeps you thinking in places.
- Middle: one sentence of what happens - **the event**, not the dialogue.
- Bottom: the scene's emotional change `+/-` (Snyder) and the conflict `><` (who
  opposes whom). A card with no `+/-` change and no `><` is a scene that doesn't turn.
- Colour or marker for the storyline (A red, B blue, C green) or character.

## 3. Working the board
1. Pin milestones; fill between them chronologically; leave blanks as blank cards
   ("??") rather than inventing filler.
2. Read each row aloud as a story. Look for: two cards in a row with the same `+/-`
   polarity; storyline colours absent for long stretches; a character missing for
   20+ pages.
3. Move, combine, cut. Combining two half-scenes into one is the commonest fix.
4. Photograph the board after each session (version history).

## 4. Digital equivalents
Final Draft Beat Board, Scrivener corkboard, Highland's navigator cards, Milanote,
Trello columns per row. Same rules; keep cards to one sentence and resist the urge to
type dialogue into them.

## 5. When the board is done
Every card has a place, a sentence, a `+/-` and a `><`; rows are balanced; you can tell
the story from the cards to a friend in ten minutes. Then write the step outline or
go to pages.

## Gotchas
- Cards that are summaries of dialogue - the board reads as talk, not events.
- 80+ cards: you're writing scenes too small; combine.
- Falling in love with the board and never writing pages.
- Act two, half two is always the thinnest row - fix it here, not in draft.

## Verify
- [ ] Four rows, ~40 cards, milestones placed at the right rows.
- [ ] Every card has location, event, `+/-` and `><`.
- [ ] Storyline colours appear throughout act two.
- [ ] Photographed/saved version of the final board.
