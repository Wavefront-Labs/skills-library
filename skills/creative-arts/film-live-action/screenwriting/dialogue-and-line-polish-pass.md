# The dialogue and line polish

**Goal:** a script that reads effortlessly - every line distinct, every page tight,
no typos - with the story unchanged.

## 1. Character voice passes
Use your software's character filter/report (Final Draft: *Tools > Reports > Character
Report*, or the Navigator's character list) to read **one character's dialogue only**,
start to finish. Check: consistent vocabulary and rhythm, an arc audible in the speech,
no lines another character could say. Do it for every principal.

## 2. Dialogue trims
- Cut **greetings, goodbyes, names in address** ("Look, John, ..."), and "on-the-nose"
  lines where the subtext was already clear.
- Cut the first and last line of many speeches - the first is throat-clearing, the last
  restates.
- Replace answers with **non-answers** or actions where possible.
- Kill **repeated information** - check each reveal happens once.
- Watch for **echo words** across neighbouring speeches.

## 3. Action-line tightening
Delete "we see", "begins to", "starts to", "is -ing" (use present simple). Break paragraphs
over four lines. Replace generic verbs (walks, looks) with specific ones. Remove camera
directions and parentheticals that direct the actor ("(angrily)") unless counter-intuitive.

## 4. Format and typo sweep
- Spellcheck, then read for homophones (their/there, its/it's), names spelled two ways.
- Software checks: orphaned character cues, (MORE)/(CONT'D) correctness, scene headings
  consistent (`INT. KITCHEN - NIGHT` vs `INT. MARTA'S KITCHEN - NIGHT`).
- Final Draft: *Tools > SmartType* list - clean duplicates of locations/characters
  that reveal inconsistencies.

## 5. Out loud
Hold a **table read** (actors or friends) or read aloud yourself. Mark every stumble,
every laugh that doesn't come, every place eyes glaze. Polish those lines only.

## 6. Scope discipline
A polish changes lines and texture, not story. If you find yourself moving scenes,
stop: that's a rewrite and needs a new plan.

## Gotchas
- Polishing away subtext - characters start explaining.
- Uniform voice after over-trimming; everyone becomes terse.
- Introducing new typos in late changes; always run the sweep last.
- Using the polish to sneak in story changes nobody approved.

## Verify
- [ ] One-character read for each principal done.
- [ ] Greetings, names-in-address and restating lines removed.
- [ ] Action lines tightened; no camera directions.
- [ ] Typo/format sweep and a read-aloud done after final changes.
