# Wind protection and weather noise

**Goal:** usable dialogue outdoors - wind and rain controlled at the mic - and a clear
call when conditions make it impossible.

## 1. Wind protection, by strength
| Condition | Boom mic protection |
|---|---|
| Indoors / still air | Foam windscreen only |
| Light breeze | Basket/ **blimp** (Rycote Cyclone or classic windshield) |
| Moderate wind | Blimp + **windjammer** (furry cover) |
| Strong wind | Blimp + windjammer + body shielding; position downwind; consider hi-wind covers |
Typical blimp + windjammer gives ~25-35 dB wind noise reduction (manufacturer data).
Furry covers cut some high frequencies - compensate slightly in post if needed.

## 2. Lavs in wind
Lavs under clothing are naturally protected; exposed lavs need **miniature furs**
(Rycote Overcovers, Bubblebee). Bury lavs a layer deeper on windy days.

## 3. Filters and settings
- **High-pass filter** at 80-100 Hz (sometimes higher for strong wind) on the recorder input
  - removes low-frequency rumble before it overloads. Note it on the report.
- Limiters on to catch gusts; keep iso tracks unfiltered if your recorder allows
  pre/post-fader isos with different settings (post can filter later; can't unfilter).

## 4. Rain
- Rain on a blimp's fur is loud ("drumming"): use a **rain cover** over the windjammer
  or a hair/ fur designed to drip; or an umbrella/ flag held above (out of frame/ shadow).
- Rain effects (towers) are especially loud - plan **wild lines** or ADR for rain scenes;
  record **wild tracks** of the rain for the effects editor.
- Protect gear: recorder bag rain cover, connectors sealed, silica gel.

## 5. Cold and heat
Batteries drain fast in cold - keep spares warm; condensation when moving indoors - bag
the mics; capsules can crackle with moisture.

## 6. Make the call
If wind/rain noise is masking dialogue, tell the director and 1st AD **before the take**:
options are re-angle away from wind, use a windbreak (flags), shoot tighter so the boom
can get closer, record wild lines after the take, or plan ADR. Log it on the sound report.

## Gotchas
- Windjammer on in calm interiors - dulled sound.
- High-pass on the iso tracks permanently removing low end.
- Rain drumming on fur unnoticed until post.
- Not flagging likely ADR to the production.

## Verify
- [ ] Protection matched to conditions; lav covers for exposed mics.
- [ ] High-pass and limiters set; settings noted.
- [ ] Rain covers and gear protection; wild tracks of weather.
- [ ] Problems flagged with options; ADR notes on the report.
