# Lav placement, clothing noise and blending with the boom

**Goal:** hidden lavs that sound clean and natural - and boom and lav tracks that cut
and blend together in the dialogue edit.

## 1. Placement
- Centre chest, at the sternum, 15-25 cm below the mouth - consistent tone; head turns
  don't drop the voice dramatically.
- Alternatives: in the hairline/ over the ear (theatre style - great for hats, period
  costume), in a collar, in a tie knot, in hats.
- Mic capsules: miniature omni (DPA 4060/6060, Sanken COS-11D, Countryman B6) - omnis are
  forgiving of position.

## 2. Beating clothing noise
- **Isolate the capsule** from fabric movement: Rycote **Stickies** + **Undercovers**,
  moleskin, tape "triangle" or "sandwich" mounts.
- Tape a **cable loop** (strain relief) so tugs don't reach the capsule.
- Avoid noisy fabrics (silk, nylon, leather); work with **costume** in fittings - they can
  add lining, sewn-in pockets for transmitters, or recommend placement.
- Keep the transmitter quiet: in a pouch on the waist/ thigh/ back, antenna straight.
- Listen to the actor moving (sit, stand, hug) before the take.
- Respect: explain what you're doing, ask permission, use the same-gender sound team
  member where requested; keep wiring time short.

## 3. Recording for blending
- Record **ISO tracks** for every mic (boom and each lav) plus a **mix track** (for
  editorial). Polyphonic WAV with iXML track names (`BOOM`, `LAV_MARTA`).
- **Time/ phase**: boom and lav pick up the same voice at different distances (lav
  arrives earlier - ~1 ms per 34 cm). Summing them can comb-filter. In the live mix, favour
  one per moment or use a **delay** on the lav to align with the boom (some recorders
  offer input delay); note settings in the report.
- Keep levels conservative on lavs (shouting spikes); limiters on.

## 4. Mix track practice
The mix track is what editors hear for months: favour boom for natural perspective,
bring up lav where the boom can't reach (wides, off-axis), avoid having all lavs open at
once (noise, phasing). Mute lavs of non-speaking actors.

## Gotchas
- Lav rubbing on chest hair or a bra strap - pre-check.
- Polarity inverted on one input - the blend cancels. Check with a test clap.
- No ISO tracks - post can't fix the mix track.
- Actor's radio pack audible when they sit.

## Verify
- [ ] Capsules isolated with mounts; costume consulted; movement test done.
- [ ] ISO tracks named; mix track recorded.
- [ ] Phase/time alignment considered; polarity checked.
- [ ] Mix favours boom with lavs where needed.
