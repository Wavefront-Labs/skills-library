# Shuttle drives and data reports

**Goal:** an unbroken, documented chain from camera card to the post facility, with
at least two verified copies in different places at all times.

## 1. Shuttle drive system
- Rugged drives (SSD or rugged HDD), **labelled** with production, drive number and
  contents date range. Rotation: set A goes to post while set B is on the cart; post
  returns cleared drives after confirming their copy.
- Encryption if the production requires (studio security).
- Drives travel with a named person (runner/ courier) and a **transfer form** signed at
  each handover (chain of custody).
- Never send the only verified copy; keep a second copy on the cart RAID until post
  confirms its own verification/LTO.

## 2. Data reports
Generated by the offload tool and the DIT each day:
- **Offload report**: per card - clip list, sizes, checksums, destinations, verification
  status, time.
- **Media log**: card/roll IDs vs camera reports (roll A012 = card ID), any issues
  (dropped frames, corrupted clips).
- **Daily data summary**: total TB, running total, drive status, what's been sent where.
Send PDFs/CSVs to post, the camera team and production; file with camera reports.

## 3. Card cycle and sign-off
1. Camera hands card to DIT with camera report.
2. Offload + verify to 2-3 destinations.
3. Spot-check clips play back (beginning, middle, end of card).
4. Report generated; card marked "offloaded/verified" (tape colour code agreed with
   camera team).
5. Card returned for formatting **only** after sign-off - the 2nd AC formats in camera.

## 4. Archive
Post or the DIT writes **LTO** tapes (two copies, stored in separate locations) - the
long-term archive. Keep LTO logs (barcodes, contents, checksums).

## Gotchas
- Cards returned before verification "because we're short of cards".
- Unlabelled drives; mix-ups at the post house.
- No transfer forms - nobody knows where a drive went.
- One copy left on site when a drive fails in transit.

## Verify
- [ ] Shuttle rotation with labelled (encrypted if required) drives.
- [ ] Offload report, media log and daily summary sent every day.
- [ ] Card colour code and sign-off before formatting.
- [ ] Two copies at all times; LTO archive logged.
