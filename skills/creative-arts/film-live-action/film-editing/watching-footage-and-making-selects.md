# Watching footage and making selects

**Goal:** know the material completely - every take, every unexpected moment - and
capture your first emotional reactions before familiarity dulls them.

## 1. Watch everything
Watch all dailies at least once, in full, before cutting the scene (start to end of each
take including what happens after "cut" and before "action" - real moments live there).
Murch notes the value of the **first viewing**: you only see it fresh once.

## 2. Log as you watch
- Notes per take: timecode of moments that moved you, line readings, problems (focus,
  boom, continuity), physical details (a look, a breath).
- Systems: markers in the NLE (colour-coded: green = great performance moment, red =
  technical problem, blue = alt line reading); or a spreadsheet; or pen notes with TC.
- Rate takes quickly (1-3 stars) but don't commit to "the take" yet.
- In Avid: locators with text; Premiere: markers and the **favourite** (star) flag in
  the Project panel / ratings in metadata; Resolve: markers and flags.

## 3. Selects sequences
For each scene build a **selects sequence**: the best moments from every set-up in
script order (or grouped by line/beat), with markers carrying notes. For documentary-
style or improv-heavy material, selects by **theme/character** too. Selects are the
raw material of the first cut and invaluable when re-cutting months later.

## 4. Favourites and circled takes
Note the director's circled takes (from the script supervisor) but don't be bound by
them - the editor's job is to find the best moments, wherever they are. Mark both in
different colours so you can show the director the alternatives.

## 5. Habits
- Watch at full speed with sound; skimming at 2x misses rhythm.
- Watch in a dedicated block without interruption; take breaks every 60-90 minutes.
- Re-watch dailies for a scene before re-cutting it.

## Gotchas
- Cutting from the circled takes only.
- Skipping the ends of takes; missing the best reaction.
- Notes without timecode.
- Selects sequences never updated after pick-ups/reshoots.

## Verify
- [ ] Every take watched in full at least once.
- [ ] Moments logged with TC and a colour-coded marker system.
- [ ] Selects sequence per scene built.
- [ ] Director's circled vs editor's favourites both marked.
