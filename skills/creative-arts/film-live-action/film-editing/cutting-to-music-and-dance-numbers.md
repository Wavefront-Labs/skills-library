# Cutting to music and dance numbers

**Goal:** picture that moves with the music's phrasing and energy - and dance that
shows the choreography and the performers' skill rather than chopping it up.

## 1. Mark the music
- Lay the track first; add **markers** on beats/bars (tap the marker key while playing -
  `M` in Premiere/Resolve, locator in Avid). Mark downbeats, phrase starts (every 4/8 bars),
  accents, lyric lines and section changes (verse/chorus/bridge).
- Use a different marker colour for phrases vs beats.

## 2. Cutting technique
- Cut **slightly before** the beat (1-2 frames) for cuts to feel on the beat - perception
  lags; test both.
- Don't cut on every beat - **phrase** the edit: cut on downbeats and accents, hold
  through phrases, accelerate cutting into the chorus/climax.
- **Off-beat** cuts create syncopation and tension.
- Cut on **movement within the frame** that matches musical accents (a door slam, a turn).
- Let the music breathe: one long shot through a held note can beat ten cuts.

## 3. Dance and musical numbers
- Shot to **playback** (see playback skill): every take synced to the same track and
  timecode. Sync all angles to the music (TC or waveform) - a multicam group with the
  music track as the reference.
- **Show the choreography**: favour wides/full-figure for footwork; cut to closer shots on
  emotional beats or details (hands, faces). Don't cut in the middle of a move unless
  match-on-action carries it.
- Choose takes per **section** for performance energy; check continuity of costume/
  position across takes.
- Respect the choreographer and director's priorities - review with them.
- Lip sync: check every sung line frame-accurately against the playback track.

## 4. Music edits
If the music needs shortening, edit the music on phrase boundaries (bar lines), matching
key/tempo; crossfade on downbeats. The music editor refines later (see music-editing skill).

## Gotchas
- Cutting exactly on every beat - mechanical, music-video cliche.
- Chopping dance into close-ups; the dancing disappears.
- Lip sync drifting by a frame or two.
- Music edits that skip a beat and break the bar.

## Verify
- [ ] Beats, phrases and sections marked.
- [ ] Cut points tested slightly ahead of beats; phrasing follows the music.
- [ ] Dance angles synced to playback; choreography readable.
- [ ] Lip sync checked; music edits on bar lines.
