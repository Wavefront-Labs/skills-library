# Entrances, exits, inserts and transitions

**Goal:** movement between shots, places and times that reads effortlessly - and
transitions chosen for meaning, not decoration.

## 1. Entrances and exits
- **Clearing frame**: let a character fully leave frame before cutting to the next
  location, or cut **just before** they clear (on movement) for pace. Leaving the empty
  frame a beat gives a pause; cutting early keeps momentum.
- **Entering**: cut in on an empty frame and let them enter, or cut to them already in
  frame mid-movement. Exits frame left -> enter frame right to maintain direction.
- Exit/entrance cuts compress time: walking down a corridor needs only the exit and the
  arrival.

## 2. Inserts and cutaways
- **Inserts** (details within the scene: the letter, the hand on the gun) direct attention
  and carry plot information; cut them **short** - as long as needed to read them, no more.
- **Cutaways** (something outside the main action: a reaction, a clock, the window)
  bridge continuity gaps, compress time, cover jump cuts, and adjust rhythm.
- Overuse makes scenes fussy; each insert should earn its frames.
- Ask for inserts in the assembly feedback if story information is unclear.

## 3. Choosing transitions
| Transition | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Cut** | Default; continuous time or simple change |
| **Dissolve** (12-48 frames typical) | Time passing, memory, lyrical connection; montage softness |
| **Fade to/from black** | End of a chapter/act, a significant time jump, death/sleep |
| **Fade to white** | Dream, revelation, death (stylised) |
| **Smash cut** | Abrupt contrast, comedy, shock |
| **Match cut** | Visual/sound rhyme connects ideas |
| **Wipe/iris** | Period or genre-pastiche styles |
Dissolves need usable handles in both shots; choose the midpoint where images combine
interestingly.

## 4. Sound bridges
**J-cut** (incoming sound before the picture) and **L-cut** (outgoing sound continues over
the new picture) smooth transitions and pull the audience forward; often better than a
visual dissolve.

## Gotchas
- Dissolves used to hide bad cuts - viewers feel the softness.
- Inserts held too long.
- Character exiting frame right and entering the next scene frame right (unless intended).
- Fades to black mid-scene in a feature (feels like a TV act break).

## Verify
- [ ] Exits/entrances timed for pace and direction.
- [ ] Inserts and cutaways each serve clarity or rhythm, trimmed tight.
- [ ] Transitions chosen for meaning; handles checked.
- [ ] Sound bridges used where they help flow.
