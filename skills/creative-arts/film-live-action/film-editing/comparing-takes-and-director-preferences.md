# Comparing takes and director preferences

**Goal:** pick the best performance moment for every beat - fast, systematically,
and with the director's preferences known and respected (or knowingly challenged).

## 1. Line strings ("radio cuts" of readings)
For a key line, build a sequence of **every reading** of that line back to back across all
takes and set-ups (ScriptSync makes this quick in Avid; otherwise markers/subclips).
Watch them in a row: differences in rhythm, emphasis and truth jump out. Mark the top 2-3.

## 2. Side by side
- Two viewers/ monitors or a split-screen sequence (picture-in-picture) to compare
  reactions or physical business between takes at the same moment.
- Compare at the same point in the line, with sound.
- Watch reactions with the off-camera dialogue audible (it's what the audience will hear).

## 3. Circled takes and director preferences
- **Circled takes** (from the script supervisor's notes) are the director's on-set
  choices - mark them in bins. Ask the director in prep how strongly they want them used.
- Keep a **preferences doc** per scene: "Sc 24 - D prefers T5 for her, T3 for him;
  dislikes the laugh in T2". Directors' preferences shift after seeing the cut - log changes.
- Show alternatives when you believe a non-circled take is better: cut both versions and
  let them see it in context rather than arguing in the abstract.

## 4. Choosing
Murch's priorities: **emotion** first, then story, rhythm, eye-trace, 2D/3D continuity.
A take with a continuity error but true emotion usually wins. Consider the whole scene's
arc - a big reading early may leave nowhere to go.

## 5. Keep the alternatives
Maintain an **alts track** or alternate sequence with the second-best readings above the
cut; when notes come ("can she be warmer here?"), you can try options in seconds.

## Gotchas
- Choosing per take instead of per moment (whole-take thinking).
- Ignoring off-camera audio while judging reactions.
- Forgetting director preferences; re-litigating them every review.
- Losing alternates when cleaning up sequences.

## Verify
- [ ] Line strings built for key lines.
- [ ] Circled takes marked; preferences doc maintained.
- [ ] Alternates kept on a track/sequence.
- [ ] Choices weighed emotion-first in the scene's context.
