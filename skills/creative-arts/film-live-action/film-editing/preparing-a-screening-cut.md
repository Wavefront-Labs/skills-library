# Preparing a screening cut

**Goal:** an audience sees the film as close to finished as possible, so their
reactions reflect the story - not the missing sound or unfinished shots.

## 1. Lead time
Plan 1-2 weeks of prep for a recruited test screening: temp mix, VFX temps, titles,
deliverable file, venue tech check. Internal screenings need less, but always a temp mix.

## 2. Picture
- Temp VFX in place for every shot (no green screens or rigs visible) - or a clear
  "VFX TEMP" title card if not.
- Temp **titles and end credits** (a simple crawl or card).
- Basic colour balance (a quick grade or dailies looks) so shots match.

## 3. Sound - the temp mix
- Dialogue cleaned (fills, crossfades, noise reduction on the worst), levels consistent.
- Temp music and key sound effects/ambiences laid; transitions smoothed.
- A temp mix (often 5.1 or stereo depending on venue) done by the sound team or
  editorial's sound assistant - **mixed at the venue's reference level** if possible.
- Bad audio is the number one cause of misleading screening reactions.

## 4. Screening cards and research
- Opening card: "Work in progress - unfinished visual effects, temporary music and sound".
- For recruited screenings: research company (e.g. NRG/ OTX) runs recruitment, cards
  (questionnaires) and focus group; agree questions with the director/producers.

## 5. Technical QC of the screening file
- Export DCP or high-quality ProRes per the venue; check frame rate, aspect ratio,
  audio channel layout, levels and sync.
- Watch the **whole file** in the venue (tech check) before the audience arrives - check
  projection, sound level, subtitles if any.
- Bring a backup on a separate drive.

## 6. During and after
Editor sits where they can feel the audience (not just watch the screen); note laughs,
restlessness, walkouts, silences by timecode. Debrief with the director the same day;
compare to cards later.

## Gotchas
- Screening with dailies sound; audience can't hear lines.
- Missing temp VFX making audiences laugh at green screens.
- DCP never tested in the venue.
- Over-reading individual comments vs patterns.

## Verify
- [ ] Temp mix, temp VFX, titles and basic colour done.
- [ ] Work-in-progress card and research questions prepared.
- [ ] Screening file tech-checked in the venue; backup present.
- [ ] Audience reactions logged by TC; debrief held.
