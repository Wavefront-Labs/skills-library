# Reworking openings and endings

**Goal:** an opening that hooks and orients fast, and an ending that lands - the two
places audiences judge a film hardest and cuts change most.

## 1. Diagnose the opening
Symptoms from screenings: restlessness in the first 10-15 minutes, confusion about who
the protagonist is, "it took a while to get going". Usual causes: setup scenes that
explain what later scenes show anyway, too many characters introduced, the inciting
incident arriving late.

## 2. Opening fixes
- **Cut setup scenes** whose information is restated later; trust the audience to catch up.
- **Move the inciting incident earlier**, or open with a later scene as a **cold open**
  (flash-forward or a strong standalone scene) - Rosenblum describes famous openings found
  this way in the cutting room.
- **Combine** character introductions into fewer scenes.
- **Title placement**: main title after a cold open, or over the first scene; decide
  where the audience should "settle in".
- Re-watch the first 10 minutes as a stranger: by minute 10, do we know whose film it is and
  what they want?

## 3. Diagnose the ending
Symptoms: multiple false endings ("I thought it was over three times"), climax that
feels rushed, epilogue drags, emotional payoff missed because the audience is
reaching for coats.

## 4. Ending fixes
- **Trim the denouement**: one or two scenes after the climax, not five. Combine tag
  scenes.
- Give the climax room: slow the key moment, let the reaction play.
- **Final image**: test alternatives (from existing footage) - the last shot sets the
  memory of the film.
- Remove scenes that resolve things the audience no longer cares about.
- Alternative endings: build versions in separate sequences and test them at screenings
  (with producers' agreement).

## 5. Keep track
Version openings and endings as separate sequences (`OPEN_v4_coldopen`), so you can swap
and compare quickly; note which version screened where.

## Gotchas
- Cutting so much setup that payoffs lose meaning.
- Cold opens that spoil the climax.
- Endings trimmed until the emotional landing has no time.
- Losing track of which ending version played at which screening.

## Verify
- [ ] Opening reviewed for protagonist/goal clarity by minute 10.
- [ ] Setup scenes trimmed/combined; title placement decided.
- [ ] False endings removed; climax given room; final image chosen.
- [ ] Opening/ending versions tracked against screenings.
