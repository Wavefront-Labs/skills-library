# Fixing performances and flagging ADR

**Goal:** the best possible performance in the film - shaped invisibly in the cut, and
ADR requested only where the edit can't solve it.

## 1. Editing tools for performance
- **Reaction stealing**: use a reaction from another moment/take (even another scene, if
  continuity allows) to change how a line lands.
- **Lifting lines**: take a better reading from another take and lay it over the
  on-screen picture when the mouth isn't visible (OTS, back of head, wide) or sync
  closely matches.
- **Pace**: tighten pauses to make a character sharper; open them to let a moment land.
  Cut the "acting" before and after the genuine moment.
- **Cutting away**: play a weak reading off the speaker's face, on the listener.
- **Removing lines** that over-explain; trust the face.
- **Re-ordering** beats within a scene, cautiously (continuity).

## 2. Off-camera replacements
When the speaker is off camera, any take's audio can be used - choose the reading that
best supports the on-camera actor's reaction. Check background sound/room tone matches;
the dialogue editor will smooth it.

## 3. When to flag ADR
- **Technical**: noise (planes, generators), mic problems, distortion.
- **Performance**: a reading the director wants changed and no take has it.
- **Story/clarity**: an added line off camera to explain plot, or a changed line for legal/
  ratings reasons.
- **Off-camera/back-of-head** opportunities to add lines.
Flag with a marker: character, line, TC, reason (TECH/PERF/STORY/ADDED). Prefer lines
where the mouth isn't clearly visible for performance changes - ADR to on-camera close-ups
is the hardest to sell.

## 4. Temp it
Record a **scratch** of the new line (editor, assistant or the actor on a phone) to test
whether the change works before the real ADR session.

## 5. Keep it honest
Show the director when you've steered a performance materially; actors and directors
should know.

## Gotchas
- Lifted lines slightly out of sync on visible mouths.
- Over-stealing reactions until the character feels inconsistent.
- ADR list bloated with "maybe" lines; costly sessions.
- Changing a performance's meaning without the director's knowledge.

## Verify
- [ ] Performance fixes attempted with editorial tools first.
- [ ] Lifted lines sync-checked; room tone noted for dialogue editing.
- [ ] ADR markers with reason codes; scratch ADR tested.
- [ ] Director informed of material performance changes.
