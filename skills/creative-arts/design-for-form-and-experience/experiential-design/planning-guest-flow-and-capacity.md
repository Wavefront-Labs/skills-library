# Guest flow and capacity

## Capacity (space per person; check local fire code first)
Typical planning densities: **standing reception 0.5-0.7 sq m (5-7 sq ft) per person** (comfortable mingling 1 sq m), **theatre seating 0.5-0.6 sq m**, **banquet rounds 1.2-1.5 sq m**, **exhibit hall or gallery 3-5 sq m per person** for good viewing (fire code may allow about 3 sq m net for exhibit areas), **queues 0.5 sq m per person** in line. The maximum is the **lowest** of fire occupant load (`creative-arts.design-for-form-and-experience.experiential-design.planning-egress-and-occupant-load`), comfort, exit width, and toilet/catering capacity.

## Flow rates
Corridor or door flow about **60-80 people per minute per metre of clear width**; stairs lower; turnstile about 20-30 per minute; manual ticket check 8-12 per minute per lane; registration desk 2-4 people per minute per station; coat check 3-5 per minute. Compute arrival waves (bell curve around start time; 40-60 percent arrive in the 20 minutes before) and number of stations needed so queue times stay under 5-10 minutes.

## Plan
1. Draw the **guest journey** (arrival, security, registration, circulation, experiences, F&B, toilets, exit) on the plan with entry and exit points separate.
2. Estimate **peak occupancy by zone** and check densities; widen or split pinch points; avoid placing attractions at corridor bottlenecks.
3. **Queue design:** serpentine lines inside space, shaded or weather-protected outside, signage with estimated waiting time, virtual queue or timed tickets for hero experiences.
4. **Zones and wayfinding:** clear signage, floor markings, staff at decision points; separate paths for accessibility and VIP.
5. **Simulate:** spreadsheets; or tools such as Legion, MassMotion, or AnyLogic for large venues.
6. **Monitor:** counting at entries, density cameras or stewards, thresholds to hold entry; communication plan and a stop-entry decision maker.

## Verify
- [ ] Maximum capacity is the lowest of fire, flow and comfort limits.
- [ ] Peak-wave throughput and queue times calculated per entry station.
- [ ] Monitoring and crowd-control actions defined with owners.
