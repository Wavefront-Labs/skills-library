# Organising a drawing set

## Sheet order and discipline designators (NCS UDS)
General (G), Hazardous Materials (H), Survey/Mapping (V), Geotechnical (B), Civil (C), Landscape (L), Structural (S), **Architectural (A)**, **Interiors (I)**, Equipment (Q), Fire Protection (F), Plumbing (P), Mechanical (M), Electrical (E), Telecommunications (T), Resource (R), Other (X). Interiors and exhibit work typically uses **A** and **I**, with E (lighting and power), M, P, F by engineers, and specialist sheets (graphics, AV, millwork).

## Sheet numbering (NCS)
Prefix + sheet type digit + sequence: `I-101`. Type digits: **0 general** (legends, notes, schedules cover), **1 plans**, **2 elevations**, **3 sections**, **4 large-scale views** (enlarged plans, stairs), **5 details**, **6 schedules and diagrams**, **7-8 user defined**, **9 3D representations**. Leave gaps for later insertions.

## Standard sheets in an interiors set
Cover and sheet index; general notes, abbreviations and symbols legend; code and life-safety plan; demolition plan; construction (partition) plan with types; dimension plan; furniture plan; finish plan; reflected ceiling plan (`creative-arts.design-for-form-and-experience.experiential-design.drawing-a-lighting-plan-and-reflected-ceiling-plan`); power and data plan; interior elevations; sections; details; schedules (doors, finishes, fixtures; `creative-arts.design-for-form-and-experience.experiential-design.writing-a-finish-schedule`); millwork details.

## Drawing mechanics
- **Title block:** project, client, sheet title and number, scale, date, revision, drawn/checked initials, seal.
- **Scales:** plans 1/4 in = 1 ft (1:50) or 1/8 (1:100); elevations 1/4 or 1/2 (1:25); details 1-1/2 in or 3 in = 1 ft (1:10-1:5).
- **Callouts:** section, elevation and detail bubbles with sheet cross-references; north arrow; consistent line weights and layers (NCS layer names, e.g. `I-WALL`, `I-FLOR-FNSH`).
- **Revisions:** delta clouds and a revision log; **issue register** by date (bid set, permit set, addenda, bulletins).
- **Coordination:** cross-check references, dimension chains, schedules versus plans, and consultant drawings before issue; publish a PDF with bookmarks and sheet index matching.

## Verify
- [ ] Sheets use consistent prefix and number scheme and a current index.
- [ ] Every callout resolves to an existing sheet; schedules match plans.
- [ ] Issue register and revisions are maintained.
