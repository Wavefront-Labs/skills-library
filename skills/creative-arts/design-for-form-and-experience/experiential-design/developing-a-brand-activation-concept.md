# Brand activation concept

**Goal:** a participation idea that makes the brand experienced, remembered and shared.

## Steps
1. **Brief:** business objective, target audience, brand promise, budget, dates, locations, KPIs, constraints (permits, safety, legal for alcohol, sampling and prizes).
2. **Insight:** a human truth connected to the brand ("people love their sneakers but never see how they are made"); mine research, social listening and observation.
3. **Big idea in one sentence** that turns insight into participation: "Customers shape their own sneaker in a 3-minute live-print booth." Test for relevance (brand link), simplicity, ownability, participation, shareability, scalability.
4. **Mechanics:** what visitors do (see, touch, make, win, compete, share), time to experience (3-5 minutes for street, longer for destination), throughput (`creative-arts.design-for-form-and-experience.experiential-design.planning-guest-flow-and-capacity`), staff roles and scripts, safety and accessibility.
5. **Moments:** attract (visible from 30 m), engage, climax (the memorable beat), take-away (product, data capture with consent, content).
6. **Amplification:** design for photos and video (lighting, backdrops, hashtags, shareable output), influencers and PR, content capture crew, post-event edits; paid media to extend reach.
7. **Measurement:** footfall, participants, dwell time, sign-ups, sample redemption, sales lift, social impressions and sentiment, brand lift surveys; compare with targets; cost per engagement.
8. **Production brief:** concept visuals, flow diagram, bill of materials, schedule (`creative-arts.design-for-form-and-experience.experiential-design.writing-a-run-of-show-and-production-schedule`), staffing plan, risk assessment, budget and approvals.

## Verify
- [ ] Idea is one sentence, tied to insight and brand.
- [ ] Mechanics, throughput and safety specified; amplification designed in.
- [ ] KPIs set before launch and reported after.
