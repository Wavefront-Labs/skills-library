# Specifications with CSI MasterFormat

## Structure
- **MasterFormat 2018:** 50 divisions in Procurement and Contracting Requirements and Specifications groups; six-digit numbering **NN NN NN**. Relevant to interiors and exhibits: **Division 06** Wood, plastics, composites (06 40 00 Architectural Woodwork), **Division 08** Openings, **Division 09 Finishes** (09 21 16 Gypsum board assemblies, 09 30 00 Tiling, 09 51 13 Acoustical panel ceilings, 09 65 00 Resilient flooring, 09 68 00 Carpeting, 09 91 23 Interior painting), **Division 10** Specialties (signage 10 14 00), **Division 11** Equipment, **Division 12** Furnishings, **Division 26** Electrical (lighting 26 51 00).
- **Three-part SectionFormat:** **Part 1 General** (summary, references, definitions, submittals, quality assurance, delivery, warranty), **Part 2 Products** (manufacturers, materials, accessories, fabrication, finishes), **Part 3 Execution** (examination, preparation, installation, field quality control, cleaning, protection).

## Writing method
1. List the sections from the finish schedule and details (`creative-arts.design-for-form-and-experience.experiential-design.writing-a-finish-schedule`); one section per product family.
2. Start from a master (MasterSpec, NBS Chorus, or firm master) and **edit for the project**; delete options not applicable; never leave bracketed text.
3. **Language:** imperative, streamlined ("Install carpet tiles using the quarter-turn method"); precise, measurable requirements; cite standards (ASTM E84 for flame spread, ASTM F1869 moisture tests, ANSI/BIFMA for furniture) with year.
4. **Specify one way:** **proprietary** (named products with "or equal" and substitution procedures), **descriptive** (properties without names), **performance** (results to achieve), or **reference standard**; do not mix contradictory methods.
5. **Submittals:** product data, samples (size and quantity), shop drawings, LEED/EPD/HPD documents, warranty, maintenance data.
6. **Quality assurance:** installer qualifications (for example 5 years experience), mock-ups, pre-installation meeting, tolerances, testing.
7. **Coordinate with drawings:** do not repeat dimensions or contradict the schedule; use "drawings show, specifications describe"; check references and section numbers.

## Verify
- [ ] Section numbers and titles match MasterFormat and the project manual index.
- [ ] Parts 1-3 complete; no bracketed or unedited options.
- [ ] Specs agree with schedules, plans and details.
