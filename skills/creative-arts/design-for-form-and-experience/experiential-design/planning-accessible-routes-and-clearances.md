# Accessible routes and clearances

Based on the 2010 ADA Standards (US); local codes and state rules can be stricter, so confirm with the authority having jurisdiction.

## Route
- **Accessible route** connects all accessible spaces and elements; **clear width 36 in** minimum (403.5.1). **Passing spaces** of 60x60 in (or a T-shaped intersection) at intervals of 200 ft maximum where the route is under 60 in wide (403.5.3).
- **Turning space:** a 60 in circle or a T-shaped space within a 60 in square with 36 in wide arms (304.3.2); **clear floor space** 30x48 in (305.3).
- **Floor surfaces:** stable, firm, slip-resistant; vertical changes in level up to 1/4 in; 1/4 to 1/2 in must be beveled no steeper than 1:2 (303.3); over 1/2 in needs a ramp or curb ramp. Floor openings must not pass a 1/2 in sphere (302.3).
- **Slope:** walking surface running slope not steeper than 1:20, cross slope not steeper than 1:48 (403.3); **ramp runs not steeper than 1:12** (405.2) with rise of 30 in maximum per run (405.6); landings 60 in long minimum (405.7.3); ramp runs with rise over 6 in need handrails (405.8); handrail top 34-38 in above the surface (505.4).
- **Protruding objects:** objects with leading edges between 27 and 80 in above the floor protrude 4 in maximum; vertical clearance 80 in minimum (307.2, 307.4).

## Doors
- **Clear opening** at least 32 in (404.2.3).
- **Maneuvering clearances** come from Table 404.2.4.1 and depend on approach direction, latch side and whether the door has a closer; dimension them from the table. Thresholds 1/2 in high maximum (404.2.5); operable hardware usable with one hand without tight grasping, 5 lbf maximum force, mounted 34-48 in above the floor (309.4, 404.2.7).

## Reach and counters
- **Unobstructed reach:** 15 in minimum to 48 in maximum above the floor, forward or side (308.2.1, 308.3.1); obstructed reach has depth-dependent limits in 308.2.2 and 308.3.2.
- **Service counters:** a portion 36 in long minimum and 36 in high maximum for a parallel approach; 30 in long minimum for a forward approach with knee and toe space (904.4.1, 904.4.2).
- **Knee clearance:** 30 in wide minimum, 11 in deep at 9 in above the floor and 8 in deep at 27 in above the floor (306.3.3, 306.3.5).
- **Dining and work surfaces:** 28-34 in above the floor (902.3).

## Process
1. Mark routes on the plan and verify widths at the tightest points (furniture, displays).
2. Place 60 in turning circles at dead ends and door approaches.
3. Check door maneuvering clearances and hardware against the table.
4. Review exhibit and retail layouts for aisle widths (36 in minimum).
5. Include accessible signage and toilet room clearances (water closet clearance 60 in from the side wall; grab bar 33-36 in high where the standard specifies).
