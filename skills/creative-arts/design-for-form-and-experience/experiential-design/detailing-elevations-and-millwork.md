# Interior elevations and millwork details

## Interior elevations
- Draw each wall surface in the orthographic view with **floor, ceiling and head heights**, door and window openings, switches and outlets with heights, wall finishes (tagged to the finish schedule), millwork, artwork and equipment, fixtures, and **dimension strings** vertical and horizontal.
- Reference **section and detail cuts** to their sheets. Show furniture only where it affects design. Include reflected ceiling reveals and soffits.
- Typical scale 1/4 in = 1 ft (1:50); keep a **key plan** for orientation and number elevations by room and wall.

## Millwork (casework and architectural woodwork)
1. **Design intent drawings** (elevation, plan, section): overall dimensions, materials, finishes, drawer and door layout, hardware (hinges, slides, pulls, locks), clearances for appliances and equipment, reveals and shadow gaps, tolerances, scribe allowances at walls and floors.
2. **Sections and details** at 1-1/2 in or 3 in = 1 ft (1:10-1:5): panel thickness (3/4 in), edge banding, toe-kick height (4 in), shelf depth and span (check deflection; 3/4 in shelves at about 36 in spans for typical loads), countertop overhang, backing and blocking in walls, attachment to structure, **finish end panels**, panel-to-panel joinery.
3. **Quality grade** per AWS: **Economy, Custom, Premium** (grade affects tolerances, materials, matching). Call out the grade and finish system (veneer species, cut, matching: book/slip/random; sequence-matched), laminate (HPL manufacturer/code), solid surface, stone.
4. **Fabrication details:** corner joints (rabbet, dado, mitre, dovetail), concealed fasteners, expansion allowances for solid wood, grain direction, fire-treated plywood where required, **access panels** and cable management.
5. **Spec notes** link to the specification (`creative-arts.design-for-form-and-experience.experiential-design.writing-specifications-with-csi-masterformat`; Section 06 40 00 Architectural Woodwork).
6. **Shop drawings and mock-ups:** require shop drawings, samples (finish, hardware), and a mock-up for critical pieces; review dimensions on site before fabrication; check against field-verified measurements.

## Verify
- [ ] Elevations dimensioned with heights, finish tags and cross-references.
- [ ] Millwork has sections with construction details, hardware and quality grade.
- [ ] Shop drawing and sample approval process is specified.
