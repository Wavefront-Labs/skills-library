# Reflected ceiling plan (RCP) and lighting plan

## Concept
An RCP shows the ceiling **as if reflected in a mirror on the floor**, so it aligns with the floor plan orientation. It combines ceiling construction, lighting, and ceiling-mounted services.

## Contents of an RCP
1. **Base:** walls (thin, cut at ceiling), columns, soffits, bulkheads, dropped ceilings, coves, skylights.
2. **Ceiling types** hatched or tagged: acoustic tile (grid 2x2 or 2x4 ft, show layout centred on the room), gypsum board, open ceiling, wood slats; **heights (AFF)** tagged with elevation changes and section markers at steps.
3. **Fixtures** by standard symbols with letter tags matching a **lighting fixture schedule** (type, manufacturer, model, source, lumens, CCT, CRI, voltage, mounting, dimming driver, finish, notes).
4. **Other ceiling devices:** diffusers and returns, sprinklers, speakers, smoke detectors, access panels, projectors and screens, signs.
5. **Switching and circuits:** switches drawn on the floor/RCP with lines to controlled fixtures (arcs); letters per circuit; dimmers; occupancy and daylight sensors; scenes table for control systems.
6. **Dimensions and notes:** locate fixtures by dimensioned centrelines to walls or grid; centre to symmetrical features; keyed notes for details; **legend**.

## Process
1. Start from the finalised space plan and ceiling heights; decide ceiling strategy.
2. Layout general lighting evenly (spacing to mounting height ratio about 1.0-1.5 for downlights), then layered accent, wall washing, decorative lighting (`creative-arts.design-for-form-and-experience.experiential-design.setting-illuminance-targets-and-colour-quality`).
3. Coordinate with MEP (sprinklers, ducts) and structure; resolve clashes in a coordination overlay.
4. Write schedule, controls narrative, and details (coves, recessed housings, drywall returns).
5. Check against energy code lighting power density and emergency lighting; issue with the drawing set (`creative-arts.design-for-form-and-experience.experiential-design.organising-a-drawing-set`).

## Verify
- [ ] RCP orientation matches the plan; heights and ceiling types shown.
- [ ] Every fixture symbol has a schedule entry; switching and circuits drawn.
- [ ] Coordinated with MEP and checked against energy code.
