# Retail layout, zones and fixtures

## Layout principles
- **Decompression zone:** the first 3-5 m inside the entrance where shoppers adjust; keep it clear; place key messages beyond it; do not put displays or sales points here (Underhill).
- **Right-hand drift:** most shoppers turn right; place appealing items on the right and a strong "power wall" on the back-right.
- **Circulation pattern:** grid (supermarkets, efficient), **loop or racetrack** (department stores, guides along feature walls), **free-flow** (boutiques, flexible), or herringbone (long narrow shops). Pick by dwell time desired and assortment.
- **Sightlines:** from the entrance, see the back wall and the cash wrap; keep fixtures at 1.2-1.4 m in the centre and taller (1.5-1.8 m) at the walls so the store reads as open; keep theft-sensitive goods visible.
- **Zones:** window / threshold, feature/impulse, primary categories, destination items at the rear, service (fitting rooms, cash wrap near exit, stock), seating and experience areas.
- **Adjacencies:** place complementary categories together; high-margin and new items on best sightlines; clearance and sale at the rear.
- **Aisles:** main 1.2-1.5 m, secondary at least 0.9-1.0 m (ADA 0.91 m minimum), 1.5 m turning clearances at ends; width tuned to expected traffic and trolleys.

## Fixtures
- **Types:** gondolas, wall systems (slatwall, gridwall, rail), tables and plinths, mannequins, hanging racks (round racks, T-stands), shelving, display cases, end caps, bins.
- **Heights:** eye level 1.5 m, hand reach 0.9-1.7 m prime; bottom shelf (below 0.5 m) and top above 1.8 m weak; kids' goods lower.
- **Flexibility:** modular, castor-mounted, power and data points; fixture schedule listing type, dimensions, finish, load capacity and quantity; **planogram** per fixture.
- **Cash wrap:** accessible counter section (0.9 m high), near the exit with queue space; impulse items; bag storage hidden.

## Lighting and signage
Layer ambient plus accent (3:1 to 5:1) (`creative-arts.design-for-form-and-experience.experiential-design.aiming-accent-lighting-and-beam-angles`); category signage at eye level readable from 10 m; digital screens sparingly.
