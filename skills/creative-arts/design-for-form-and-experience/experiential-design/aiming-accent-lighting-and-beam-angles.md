# Accent lighting and beam angles

## Beam geometry
- **Beam angle:** where intensity falls to 50 percent of the peak (FWHM). Typical: **spot 10-15 degrees, narrow flood 20-25, flood 36-40, wide flood 60**. Field angle is wider (10 percent).
- **Beam diameter** at distance d: D = 2 x d x tan(angle / 2). A 24-degree beam at 2.4 m makes about 1.0 m diameter.
- **Illuminance at centre:** E = candela / distance squared (lux from cd and m); check with photometric files (IES/LDT).
- Choose beam to cover the object with a little spill (about 1.2 x object size) for crisp accent, or tighter for a framed projection.

## Aiming
- **30-degree aiming** from vertical for wall and artwork: balances glare, shadows and reflections; closer to the wall emphasises texture (scallops).
- For artworks on walls, place track or spots at about **one-third of the ceiling height from the wall** for 2.7 m ceilings (0.9 m setback) and aim 30 degrees; use **framing projectors** with shutters for exact edges.
- 3D objects: key light from 45 degrees up and to the side, with fill at lower ratio (3:1); avoid flat frontal light; use **cross-lighting** from two sides for sculpture.
- **Cases:** fibre-optic or micro LED spots inside; keep heat and UV away from sensitive objects; avoid reflections in glass by lighting from beyond the angle of reflection.

## Wall washing
Use asymmetric wall washers (distance from wall about 0.3-0.5 x wall height) with spacing of about 0.5-1 x distance from wall to give even vertical illuminance (uniformity 0.7).

## Control
Dimmable to adjust hierarchy; locking aiming and focusing sessions at night after installation: record focus on a cue sheet; use glare shields, snoots, honeycomb louvres; shield view of the lamp source at eye level (cut-off angle of 30-45 degrees).

## Verify
- [ ] Beam angle matches object size and distance (calculated).
- [ ] Aiming avoids glare and veiling reflections; focus session documented.
- [ ] Accent-to-ambient ratio meets design intent.
