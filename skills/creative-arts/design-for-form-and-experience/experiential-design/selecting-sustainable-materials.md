# Selecting sustainable materials

## Evidence documents
- **EPD (Environmental Product Declaration,** ISO 14025, EN 15804): life-cycle assessment results (global warming potential, kg CO2e per declared unit); **product-specific, Type III** EPDs earn more credit than industry-wide ones.
- **HPD (Health Product Declaration):** full ingredient disclosure with hazard screening (GreenScreen).
- **Declare label** (ILFI): ingredients, source, end-of-life; **Red List Free / Compliant** shows absence of worst-in-class chemicals. **Cradle to Cradle Certified** levels; **Environmental certifications:** FSC (wood), GREENGUARD Gold or CDPH Standard Method v1.2 (VOC emissions), SCS Indoor Advantage, UL Ecologo, FloorScore (flooring), NSF/ANSI 140 (carpet), BIFMA LEVEL (furniture).

## Criteria (LEED v4.1 ID+C, WELL, LBC)
- **LEED MR:** building product disclosure and optimisation: EPD, sourcing of raw materials, material ingredients; **IEQ Low-emitting materials** (paints, adhesives, flooring, ceilings, furniture). **WELL** Materials concept (Restrict foodware, material transparency).
- Low-VOC content limits: paints at or below **50 g/L (flat) and 100 g/L (non-flat)** (SCAQMD Rule 1113 sets 50 g/L), adhesives per SCAQMD 1168.
- **Wood:** FSC certified or reclaimed; avoid illegal logging; formaldehyde-free (NAF/ULEF) or CARB Phase 2 for composite panels.
- **Recycled and bio-based content**, regional sourcing (within 100 miles gets weight in LEED), rapidly renewable, durable.
- **Embodied carbon:** compare EPDs per functional unit; reuse and refurbish furniture and fixtures first; specify salvaged materials.
- **End of life:** take-back programmes (carpet tile reclamation), design for disassembly (mechanical fasteners rather than adhesives), recycling streams.

## Procedure
1. Set project targets (LEED level, WELL, client policy) and a **banned chemicals list**.
2. In specs, require EPD/HPD/Declare and emissions certificates in submittals (`creative-arts.design-for-form-and-experience.experiential-design.writing-specifications-with-csi-masterformat` Part 1).
3. Build a material tracker (product, certifications, cost, recycled %, distance, EPD GWP) and compare alternatives.
4. Balance with durability and maintenance (`creative-arts.design-for-form-and-experience.experiential-design.specifying-finish-durability-and-maintenance`): the greenest finish is the one that lasts.
5. Verify on site (delivery tickets, product labels) and document for certification.

## Verify
- [ ] Targets and banned list set; submittals require EPD, HPD and emissions proof.
- [ ] Tracker compares alternatives with data, not marketing claims.
- [ ] Durability and end-of-life considered.
