# Run of show and production schedule

## Production schedule (macro, weeks to days)
Backward-plan from event date: concept lock; design approval; vendor contracts and permits; fabrication and graphics sign-off; AV and staging orders; rehearsals; **load-in** (hours or days); tech and rehearsal; doors; show; **load-out/strike**. Include gates (approval deadlines), lead times (fabrication 3-6 weeks, printing 5-10 days, trucking), crew call times, venue access windows, union and overtime thresholds, and a critical path. Tools: spreadsheet, Gantt (Smartsheet, Asana).

## Run of show (micro, minute-by-minute)
Columns: **time (clock and countdown)**, duration, element, location, owner, content/script ref, **AV cue** (video, audio, lighting scene numbers), stage action, notes. Group by blocks (doors, welcome, keynote, break, reception). Rules:
- Use **24-hour time**, with a "T-minus" version for the show caller.
- One owner per line; **transitions** are lines too (reset, mic check).
- Number cues (LX1, SX2, VX3) to match the programmer's cue list; include standby ("standby LX5") and go calls.
- Build **buffers** (5-10 percent) and a **cut list** (what to drop if late) and a **contingency plan** (weather, no-shows, power, speaker late, medical).

## Supporting documents
Cue sheets per department, contact sheet (radio channels, phone), site map, load-in/out schedule, rehearsal schedule, VIP movement plan, run sheet for catering, risk assessment and emergency plan, accessibility arrangements.

## Execution
- Distribute the final version with version/date; one master owned by the production manager; update only via the manager.
- **Production meeting** the day before; **walk-through** of the flow; stage manager calls the show with timed cues over comms; log actual times for post-event review.

## Verify
- [ ] Production schedule has gates, lead times and critical path.
- [ ] Run of show cues are numbered, owned and timed with buffers and a cut list.
- [ ] Contingency and emergency plans are included and briefed.
