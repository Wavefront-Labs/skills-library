# Finish schedule and legend

## Room finish schedule (table)
Columns: **room number, room name**, **floor** (material, base), **base**, **walls** (N, E, S, W if different), **ceiling** (type, height), **doors**, **notes**. Use short **finish codes** referencing the legend (FL-1, WL-3, CL-2, BS-1). One row per room; group by floor.

## Finish legend (materials schedule)
For every code: type, **manufacturer, product, series, colour/pattern name and number**, size or thickness, finish/sheen, installation (direction, pattern, grout colour), VOC or certification requirement, **specification section**, and notes. Example: `FL-2 Carpet tile: Interface, Walk on By, Flannel 101, 24 x 24 in, quarter-turn, 0.9 ratio, Section 09 68 13`.

## Finish plan
Floor plan with finish tags: hatch patterns for flooring, transitions drawn (threshold type), wall finish tags at elevations, paint scheme by colour and sheen (eggshell walls, semi-gloss trim, flat ceilings), feature walls, base heights and corner conditions.

## Transitions and coordination
List transition strips between materials (carpet to LVT, tile to wood), height differences (edge treatments within accessibility limits `creative-arts.design-for-form-and-experience.experiential-design.planning-accessible-routes-and-clearances`), substrate prep notes, moisture testing requirement for slabs.

## Samples and approvals
Maintain a **sample board and sample log** (submitted/approved with date), lead times, minimum order and dye lot requirements, attic stock quantity (typically 3-5 percent), and alternates. Check that colour codes in schedule, plan, elevations and specification match; run a cross-check before issue.

## Verify
- [ ] Every room has finish codes and every code exists in the legend with a product.
- [ ] Transitions and substrate requirements noted.
- [ ] Schedule, plans, elevations and specs agree.
