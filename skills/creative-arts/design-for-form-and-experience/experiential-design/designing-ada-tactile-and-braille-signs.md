# ADA tactile and braille signage

The US 2010 ADA Standards for Accessible Design (section 703 Signs) and the model standard ICC A117.1 set requirements for signs; the building code and local amendments apply too. The points below are a working summary: **verify every dimension against the current published text and your authority having jurisdiction before you issue drawings.**

## Which signs
- **Permanent room and space identification** (room numbers, restrooms, exits) must have tactile characters, braille and visual characters.
- **Directional and informational signs** (overhead directories, directional arrows) need visual characteristics but not tactile, though many projects choose to add them.
- Signs for temporary use are generally not covered.

## Tactile characters (summary)
- Raised **uppercase**, sans-serif, not italic or script; raised a minimum of 1/32 inch; character height roughly 5/8 inch to 2 inches; stroke thickness and spacing limits.
- **Braille:** Grade 2, placed below the corresponding text; dome-shaped dots with specified dimensions and spacing; no braille on the visual-only parts.
- **Pictograms** need a field of at least 6 inches high with a tactile and braille description beneath (for example an accessible symbol).

## Visual characteristics
- Non-glare (eggshell, matte) finish; characters and background contrast strongly (light on dark or dark on light); proportion and spacing rules.

## Mounting
- **Location:** on the wall adjacent to the latch side of the door; tactile characters between 48 and 60 inches above the floor to the baseline; clear floor space beside the sign so a person can approach and touch.
- Not on the door leaf where doors may be open, unless no wall space exists.

## Practice
- Specify to the fabricator by standard, and require shop drawings and a sample before production.
- Test with a person who reads braille where possible.
- Local rules, other countries' standards (for instance in the UK and EU) and language requirements differ.

## Verify
- [ ] Each room identification sign has raised characters and braille.
- [ ] Mounting location and height are dimensioned and match the code.
- [ ] A physical sample was approved.
