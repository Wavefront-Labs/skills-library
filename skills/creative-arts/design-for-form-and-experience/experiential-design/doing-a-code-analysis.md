# Code analysis

**Goal:** a documented, authority-reviewed basis for the design, usually a drawing sheet (G-001) plus calculations.

## Steps
1. **Identify adopted codes and amendments:** jurisdiction (state/city), edition of IBC or IEBC (existing buildings), NFPA 101 where adopted, ADA 2010 and ICC A117.1, energy code (IECC / ASHRAE 90.1), IPC plumbing, IMC, NFPA 13/72, local fire and zoning rules; confirm with the building department early.
2. **Classify occupancy (IBC Ch. 3):** A assembly, B business, E educational, F factory, H high hazard, I institutional, M mercantile, R residential, S storage, U utility; list **mixed occupancy** (separated or non-separated) and accessory uses.
3. **Construction type (Ch. 6):** I-V with A or B (protected or unprotected); check **allowable height and area** (Table 504.3, 504.4, 506.2 with frontage and sprinkler increases) and fire-resistance ratings of structure.
4. **Separations (Ch. 7 and 508):** fire barriers and walls between occupancies and for corridors (0 or 1 hour), shafts, opening protectives (door ratings 20-minute to 3-hour), penetrations firestopping, smoke barriers (I-2).
5. **Sprinklers and alarms (Ch. 9):** automatic sprinkler thresholds by group, fire alarm, standpipes, smoke control; note how they affect other limits (travel distance, interior finish).
6. **Egress (Ch. 10):** occupant loads, exits, widths, travel distance, common path, dead ends, door hardware (`creative-arts.design-for-form-and-experience.experiential-design.planning-egress-and-occupant-load`).
7. **Interior finishes (Ch. 8)** by class (`creative-arts.design-for-form-and-experience.experiential-design.specifying-interior-finishes-for-fire-and-flame-spread`).
8. **Accessibility (Ch. 11, ADA):** accessible routes, entrances, toilets, signage, parking (`creative-arts.design-for-form-and-experience.experiential-design.planning-accessible-routes-and-clearances`).
9. **Plumbing fixtures (IPC Table 403.1)** counts by occupant load and use; **energy** compliance (lighting power density).
10. **Existing building work:** use IEBC compliance method (prescriptive, work area, performance) and determine level of alteration.

## Document and review
Present a table with code section, requirement, project condition and compliance; **life-safety plan** showing exits, ratings, occupant loads, travel distances, fire extinguishers and alarm devices; request pre-application meetings, **variances or alternate means** where needed; carry notes to permit drawings (`creative-arts.design-for-form-and-experience.experiential-design.organising-a-drawing-set`).

## Verify
- [ ] Codes and editions confirmed with the jurisdiction.
- [ ] Occupancy, type, area and egress calculations documented per section.
- [ ] Life-safety plan and compliance table issued with the permit set.
