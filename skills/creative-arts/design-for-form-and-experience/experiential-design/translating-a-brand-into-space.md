# Translating a brand into space

**Goal:** an environment where people feel the brand's promise through what they see, touch, hear, smell and do, not through logos alone.

## Method
1. **Extract the brand essence:** purpose, 3-5 attributes (for example *precise, warm, playful*), tone, audience insights (brand book, interviews, audit).
2. **Make attributes spatial:** for each attribute, a concrete translation across sensory and spatial levers:
   - **Layout and flow:** open and generous vs intimate and curated; sightlines and reveal sequences.
   - **Material and texture:** timber and wool for warmth; brushed steel and glass for precision; tactile samples.
   - **Colour and graphics:** brand palette used proportionally (not everywhere), scale of graphics and typography.
   - **Light:** colour temperature and contrast (`creative-arts.design-for-form-and-experience.experiential-design.setting-illuminance-targets-and-colour-quality`).
   - **Sound and scent:** music tempo and acoustics; signature scent where appropriate and allergen-aware.
   - **Behaviour:** staff uniforms, language, rituals (handing over a product, a welcome drink).
3. **Create signature moments:** 2-3 memorable beats (threshold, product reveal, a hero installation, a shareable spot) placed in the journey (`creative-arts.design-for-form-and-experience.experiential-design.designing-an-exhibition-visitor-journey`).
4. **Develop a spatial brand toolkit:** a kit of parts for rollouts (fixtures, finishes, graphics rules, lighting types, dos and don'ts), with flexibility for different footprints.
5. **Prototype and test:** mood and materials board (`creative-arts.design-for-form-and-experience.mood-boarding.building-a-mood-board-and-style-tiles`), full-size mock-up or VR walkthrough; rate with semantic-differential scales against brand attributes (`creative-arts.design-for-form-and-experience.mood-boarding.measuring-attributes-with-semantic-differential-scales`).
6. **Govern:** design guidelines, supplier specs, and a post-opening audit vs brand and operations.

## Verify
- [ ] Each brand attribute has a concrete spatial, material and behavioural expression.
- [ ] Signature moments are defined and placed in the journey.
- [ ] Mock-up tested against attributes; rollout toolkit documented.
