# Pop-up experience

**Goal:** a temporary space that delivers a measurable result in days or weeks.

## 1. Objective and measures
One primary aim: sales, sampling, lead capture, launch buzz, or research. Set KPIs (footfall, conversion, dwell time, sign-ups, social reach, cost per acquisition) and a budget split (rent, fit-out, staff, marketing, logistics, contingency 10-15 percent).

## 2. Site and permissions
Shop-in-shop, mall kiosk, street vacancy, container, or event space. Check landlord and local permits (temporary use, signage, food and alcohol licences, fire and occupancy approvals, insurance, power and data). Visibility, footfall, adjacency to brand audiences, loading access and storage.

## 3. Experience design
- **Journey in three acts:** attract (facade, signage, scent or sound), engage (hands-on product, demo, photo moment), convert and remember (purchase, sample, sign-up, take-away).
- One **hero idea** (an installation, a limited drop, a workshop) rather than many small ones; shareable photo moments with good lighting.
- Plan flow and queue capacity (`creative-arts.design-for-form-and-experience.experiential-design.planning-guest-flow-and-capacity`).

## 4. Physical design
- **Modular kit** that fits different sites: panels, plinths, flat-pack counters, lighting trusses, graphics on tension fabric, rental furniture; designed for <2 person install, standard vehicle loading, and reuse.
- Materials: durable, lightweight, fire-rated (flame-spread rules, `creative-arts.design-for-form-and-experience.experiential-design.specifying-interior-finishes-for-fire-and-flame-spread`), cleanable.
- Power and POS: mobile POS, cashless, Wi-Fi backup; storage hidden; security.

## 5. Operations
Staffing (brand ambassadors trained on script and data capture), opening hours, stock management, cleaning, health and safety, daily reports, and live adjustments.

## 6. Teardown and learning
Schedule installation (typically 2-5 days), take-down, reinstatement obligations, asset storage; post-event report with KPIs, learnings and content.

## Verify
- [ ] Objective, KPIs and budget set; permits and insurance in place.
- [ ] Journey and kit designed for quick install and reuse.
- [ ] Post-event report compares results with KPIs.
