# Egress and occupant load (IBC, US practice)

Check the adopted edition and local amendments; this is the planning method, not a substitute for the authority having jurisdiction (AHJ).

## 1. Occupant load
Occupant load = area / load factor (Table 1004.5; **gross** or **net** per the table). Typical values: business areas 150 gross sf per person; assembly unconcentrated (tables and chairs) 15 net; concentrated (fixed seats, no tables) 7 net; standing space 5 net; mercantile 60 gross (sales floor); exhibit gallery and museum 30 net; classroom 20 net; kitchens 200 gross; storage/stock 300 gross. Use the greater of calculated and actual (fixed seats). Round up.

## 2. Number of exits (Table 1006.2.1)
Typically **2 exits** for occupant loads up to 500; 3 for 501-1,000; 4 for over 1,000. Single exit allowed only for small occupant loads and short travel distances (business: up to 49 occupants, 75 ft travel).

## 3. Exit separation
Where two are required, separate by not less than **one-half the diagonal** of the area served (one-third with automatic sprinklers and approved arrangement).

## 4. Width
Capacity widths: **0.3 in per person** for stairs, **0.2 in per person** other components (egress width from occupant load; 2015+ editions, some older use 0.2 and 0.15 with sprinklers), minimum clear door width **32 in** (opening), corridors 44 in (36 in when occupant load under 50).

## 5. Distances
- **Exit access travel distance:** business and mercantile typically 200 ft (250 ft sprinklered); assembly 200/250; check Table 1017.2 for the group.
- **Common path of egress travel:** 75 ft (100 ft sprinklered business and mercantile; 20 ft for many assembly and educational uses with small loads).
- **Dead-end corridors:** 20 ft (50 ft sprinklered business in many cases).

## 6. Doors and hardware
Doors in the path swing in the direction of egress when occupant load is 50 or more (and high-hazard); panic hardware for assembly (A) and education (E) with load 50+ (A) or 100+; no keyed locks that require a key from inside (delayed egress needs approval); egress illumination at 1 foot-candle minimum along paths with emergency power 90 min; illuminated exit signs.

## 7. Draw and document
Mark exits, travel paths with dimension chains to the farthest point, occupant loads by room on a life-safety plan, and put calculations in the code analysis (`creative-arts.design-for-form-and-experience.experiential-design.doing-a-code-analysis`).

- [ ] Travel distance and common path measured on plan.
- [ ] Door swing, hardware, signage and emergency lighting specified.
