# Finish durability and maintenance

## Fabric and upholstery
- **Wyzenbeek (double rubs):** light residential about 3,000+, **general commercial 15,000+ (medium duty 15-30k), heavy duty 30,000+**, with cotton duck or wire mesh as specified.
- **Martindale (cycles):** domestic 10-15k, **general contract 25-40k**, heavy contract 40k+, heavy 100k+ (healthcare, hospitality).
- **Colourfastness:** lightfastness (AATCC 16, 40 hours or more; 60+ in sunny areas), crocking (AATCC 8, rating 4+), pilling (ASTM D3511), seam slippage and tear strength; **liquid repellency / cleanability codes (W, S, WS, X)** for cleaning method.
- Flame performance and stain resistance (Nanotex, Crypton) as needed; consider vinyl alternatives with antimicrobial treatments only when required.

## Flooring
- **Hard surface:** porcelain tile **PEI wear rating** (I-V; IV/V for commercial) and **DCOF at least 0.42** wet (ANSI A326.3) for level interior wet areas; coefficients per ADA recommendations; sealed grout; epoxy grout in wet and kitchen areas.
- **Resilient:** LVT wear layer **20 mil** for heavy commercial, 12 mil medium; sheet vinyl heat-welded for healthcare; rubber for gyms.
- **Carpet tile:** face weight and density, soil-hiding patterns, backing, **ANSI/NSF 140**; walk-off mats at entrances (6 ft or more).
- **Wood:** hardness (Janka), finish (aluminium oxide urethane), moisture constraints; **polished concrete** densified and sealed.

## Wall, ceiling, surfaces
- **Paint:** scrubbable and washable (ASTM D2486), semi-gloss at high-touch; **wallcoverings** Type II (13-20 oz) vinyl commercial; protection with chair rails and corner guards.
- **HPL (laminate):** NEMA LD3 grades (general purpose 0.048 in; postforming); solid surface repairable; **quartz** low maintenance, granite and marble need sealing and acid avoidance.
- **Metals:** anti-fingerprint, powder-coat; consider patina.

## Maintenance plan
Write a **cleaning and maintenance guide** per finish (cleaner pH, tools, frequency, restoration), specify attic stock (3-5 percent) and consider **lifecycle cost** (initial plus cleaning, repair, replacement over 10-15 years). Match to **traffic**: entrances and corridors heaviest, so spec toughest there; check warranty exclusions.
