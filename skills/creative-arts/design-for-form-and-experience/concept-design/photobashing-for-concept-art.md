# Photobashing workflow

Photobashing builds concept images by compositing and painting over photographs or 3D renders, reaching realistic detail quickly.

## Workflow
1. **Sketch the composition** in simple shapes first (see `...concept-design.painting-an-environment-keyframe`). A sketch beats a random pile of photos.
2. **Collect source images** with clear rights: your own photos, public-domain or licensed stock. Keep a source log. Do not publish work built on images with unclear licences.
3. **Cut out and place** with non-destructive layer masks; fit perspective with Free Transform, Perspective Warp or Warp.
4. **Unify:** match light direction and colour temperature with curves and colour balance; add a global grade layer above everything.
5. **Atmosphere:** add haze and depth with soft gradients and low-opacity lighter layers on distant elements.
6. **Paint over:** remove photographic clues (repeating textures, wrong shadows) and add edges and light. The painted pass makes it your design.
7. **Match sharpness and noise** across pieces; add subtle grain.

## Pitfalls
- Raw photo texture with no design intent looks like collage; design the shapes first.
- Perspective and horizon disagree between parts; draw perspective guides.
- Licence: confirm the licence allows modification and commercial use (see `...mood-boarding.licensing-and-clearing-reference-images`).

## Verify
- [ ] The source log lists a licence for every image.
- [ ] Light direction is consistent across parts.
- [ ] No obvious repeats of the same texture remain.
