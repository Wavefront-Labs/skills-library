# Vehicle proportions and stance

**Goal:** a side view and plan stance that make the vehicle look planted and give it the intended
character (premium, sporty, rugged) before any surface detail.

## Side-view proportion variables
| Variable | Effect |
|---|---|
| Wheel diameter to body height | Bigger wheels relative to body look sportier and more expensive; typical premium cars sit at about 2 wheels tall for the whole car height |
| Dash-to-axle (front wheel centre to base of windscreen) | Longer = rear-wheel-drive premium look; short = front-drive, cab-forward |
| Front and rear overhangs | Short overhangs look agile; long front overhang looks heavy |
| Greenhouse (glass) to body (DLO to shoulder) | Lower greenhouse looks sportier; more glass looks friendlier and roomier |
| Cabin position | Cab-rearward (long bonnet) vs cab-forward |
| Ground clearance and wheel-arch gap | Small gap = planted; big gap = off-road |

## Stance in plan and front views
- Wheels pushed out to the body corners (track close to body width) look planted.
- Body tucked in above the wheels (tumblehome) and shoulder over the wheels gives muscular stance.

## Method
1. Lay in the package: wheelbase, wheel size, occupants (H-point mannequins), ground line.
2. Draw side-view thumbnails at small size varying one variable at a time.
3. Fill silhouettes black; compare against competitor silhouettes scaled to the same wheelbase.
4. Pick candidates and check the front and rear 3/4; stance faults show there first.

## Gotchas
- Drawing wheels too small is the most common student error; it makes the car look cheap.
- Ellipses of the wheels must sit on parallel axles to the side VP.

## Verify
Silhouette matches the package hard points, the chosen proportions are recorded as ratios, and
the 3/4 view keeps the intended stance.
