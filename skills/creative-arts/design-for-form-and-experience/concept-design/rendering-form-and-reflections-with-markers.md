# Rendering form and reflections with markers

Goal: turn a clean line sketch into a form that reads as glossy, matt or metallic in about 15 minutes, using alcohol markers (Copic is a common brand; the technique is brand-agnostic).

## Kit
- Marker paper (bleed-proof, coated layout pad) so ink stays crisp; plain copier paper bleeds.
- One grey family (cool or warm) in 4-6 values from light to dark, plus a black. Do not mix warm and cool greys in one object.
- One accent colour pair (mid and dark), a white gel pen or white pastel, a fineliner for edge lines.

## Steps
1. Draw line work in light pencil; ink only the silhouette and key edges. Photocopy clean copies to try several colourways.
2. **Fix the light direction** (usually top-left, slightly front). Decide which surfaces face up (lightest), face the light, or turn away (darkest).
3. **Lay the lightest grey flat** over the whole shadow side with broad strokes that follow the surface direction. Keep a wet edge so strokes merge without lines.
4. **Build form with graded values:** mid grey in the turning zone, dark grey in the core shadow. Overlap while still wet. Marker only darkens, so go light to dark.
5. **Reflections:** on glossy surfaces leave white paper (or knock out with white later) for the sharpest highlight, and add a dark horizon band. Top surfaces reflect the sky (light), vertical sides reflect the horizon (a sharp dark band), undersides reflect the ground (dark to mid). Keep reflection edges soft on matt parts and sharp on gloss.
6. **Accent colour:** apply the mid tone, then shade with the dark tone; do not outline everything in black.
7. **Ground shadow:** darkest directly under the product, fading outwards; a contact shadow gives weight.
8. **Finish:** white gel pen for specular points on edges, fine black line to re-sharpen the silhouette where ink spread.

## Failure modes
- Streaks: the edge dried; work faster and cover large areas in one pass.
- Muddy colour: too many layers on cheap paper; stop earlier and recover with white.
- Flat result: no value contrast; the core shadow must be clearly darker than the mid tone.

## Verify
- [ ] Squinted, the sketch shows three clear values plus highlights.
- [ ] Reflection bands follow the surface curvature and agree with one light direction.
- [ ] Gloss, matt and metal parts differ in edge hardness.
