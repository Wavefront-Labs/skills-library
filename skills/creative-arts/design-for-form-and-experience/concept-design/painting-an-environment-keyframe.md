# Environment keyframe painting

A keyframe is a single finished image that sets the look and mood of a location or scene for a film, game or product story.

## Process
1. **Brief and references:** story beat, time of day, mood, scale; collect references.
2. **Thumbnails (grayscale, 5-10):** explore compositions with big shapes; use leading lines and a clear focal point.
3. **Value study:** choose three to five values; keep foreground, middle ground and background clearly separated, with the background lighter and lower in contrast for depth.
4. **Colour:** a limited palette with a dominant hue and a contrasting accent; apply on the value sketch with multiply, colour and overlay layers.
5. **Light:** decide key light direction and temperature; pair warm light with cooler shadows or the reverse.
6. **Atmospheric perspective:** distant objects lose contrast, lighten and shift toward the sky colour.
7. **Detail the focal area first;** keep the periphery loose; add scale cues (people, vehicles).
8. **Finish:** check values in grayscale, vary hard and soft edges, add subtle texture.

## Verify
- [ ] The image works as a grayscale thumbnail.
- [ ] The eye goes to the intended focal point.
- [ ] Depth cues (value, colour, edges) are consistent.
