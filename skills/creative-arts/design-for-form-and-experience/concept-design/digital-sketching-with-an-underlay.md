# Digital sketching with an underlay

Goal: a clean, correctly proportioned concept sketch in a tablet app (Procreate, Photoshop, Sketchbook, Krita) by building on an underlay rather than a blank canvas.

## Layer stack (bottom to top)
1. **Underlay:** a perspective grid, an orthographic photo or drawing, or a screenshot of a rough 3D blockout from the same camera angle. Lock it; set opacity to roughly 20-40 percent.
2. **Construction layer:** loose, low-opacity lines (ellipses, centre lines, boxes). Lower this layer's opacity once you ink.
3. **Line layer:** confident final lines in one dark colour, in two or three weights (heavier on silhouette and underside, lighter for interior edges).
4. **Tone layers:** clipped to the line layer or on multiply: base fill, shade, then highlights on a separate normal layer.
5. **Detail layer:** screens, buttons, logos.

## Settings and habits
- Use moderate stabilisation/streamline for long curves; reduce it for quick gesture lines.
- Draw from the shoulder with the canvas zoomed out; long sweeps and ellipses come out cleaner at modest zoom.
- Use a symmetry guide for products with a centre plane; remember it makes perspective errors obvious.
- Flip the canvas horizontally often; a mirrored view exposes proportion errors.

## Workflow
Thumbnails on a small canvas, pick two, enlarge, redraw over the grid underlay, tone, add a ground shadow, export a flattened PNG and keep the layered file.

## Verify
- [ ] Layered file keeps the underlay hidden but recoverable.
- [ ] Vanishing points agree between construction and final lines.
- [ ] Export is flat RGB at a size suitable for the board.
