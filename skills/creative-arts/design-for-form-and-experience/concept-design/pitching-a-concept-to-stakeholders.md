# Concept pitch to stakeholders

Goal: get a clear decision on a concept, in a short time, from people with different priorities (marketing, engineering, finance, executives).

## Structure (10-20 minutes)
1. **Frame:** restate the brief and goal on one slide so everyone agrees on the question.
2. **Insight:** the user problem, with one real observation or quote.
3. **Concept:** show the hero image or model early. Name the idea in a phrase.
4. **In use:** a scenario storyboard or short video (see `...concept-design.storyboarding-use-scenarios-for-concepts`).
5. **Why it works:** form, ergonomics, CMF, brand fit; compare with options considered.
6. **Feasibility:** manufacturing route, cost drivers, risks and how to test them.
7. **Ask:** the decision, next steps, timeline and budget implication.

## Delivery tips
- Bring physical models if you have them and let people hold them.
- Anticipate objections by function and prepare a backup slide for each (cost, tooling, schedule, regulatory).
- Present two or three options with a recommendation, not one take-it-or-leave-it design.
- Capture feedback live in writing; confirm decisions and owners at the end.
- Send a follow-up summary the same day.

## Verify
- [ ] The decision requested is on the last slide.
- [ ] Each stakeholder concern has an answer or an owner.
- [ ] Minutes and decisions were circulated.
