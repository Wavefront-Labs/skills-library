# Package drawing and hard points

The package is a side-view (plus plan and front) layout that fixes the non-negotiable positions: wheels, occupants, mechanical components, fuel or battery, crash structures. Styling is drawn around it.

## Hard points and references
- **Wheelbase, track, tyre size, ground clearance and overhangs** set stance.
- **H-point** (hip pivot) and the **seating reference point** place the seated occupant; the **accelerator heel point** and ball of foot position the driver's leg; **eye position** (eyellipse, SAE J941) governs visibility.
- **Hard points** are fixed points that the styling must respect: steering wheel, pedals, seat, windshield base, instrument panel, powertrain, battery pack, suspension towers, roof headroom and door entry.
- SAE J1100 gives standard dimension names (L lengths, W widths, H heights, numbered) so everyone measures the same way. Confirm current editions of J1100, J826 and J941 before use.

## Steps
1. Draw tyres and wheelbase to scale in side view.
2. Place the H-point and manikins (small and large cases) and eye ellipses.
3. Add headroom, legroom, visibility angles; check door apertures.
4. Place powertrain or battery, steering, front and rear crash volumes.
5. Draw the **package envelope** and the minimum body-surface offset.
6. Hand over to sketching: the silhouette must not intrude on any hard point (see `...concept-design.setting-vehicle-proportions-and-stance`).

## Verify
- [ ] All hard points are dimensioned from one origin.
- [ ] Manikin envelopes fit inside the cabin volume.
- [ ] Styling sketches were checked against the package lines.
