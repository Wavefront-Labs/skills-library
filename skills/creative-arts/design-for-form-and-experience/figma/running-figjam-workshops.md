# FigJam workshops

## Prepare the board
1. Start from a **template** (Figma Community or FigJam templates: retros, journey maps, brainstorm, affinity, dot vote), or build with **sections** (Shift+S) labelled with steps and timing.
2. Pre-fill instructions as **text in large type** and **stickies** with examples; place a **Start here** section and an **agenda** with times; add a **parking lot** section.
3. Lock instructions (**Ctrl+Shift+L**) so participants do not move them; set the **frame/section** order to match the agenda.
4. Share link with **Can edit** for participants (in the organisation or by link); allow **guest access** where needed; check permissions (`creative-arts.design-for-form-and-experience.figma.sharing-and-permissions-in-figma`).

## Tools to use
- **Sticky notes (S)** with colour by role/theme; **text, shapes, connectors (Shift+C)** for flows; **stamps, emotes, stickers** for reactions; **images and Figma links** to embed designs; **tables** for matrices; **code blocks**.
- **Voting (V):** set **votes per person** and a timer; hide votes until finish for unbiased voting (dot voting; `creative-arts.design-for-form-and-experience.ux-design.crazy-eights-and-dot-voting`).
- **Timer** (T): timeboxed silent brainstorming; **music** to fill silence; **cursor chat** (/) for quick comments; **Spotlight** or **Observation** to bring the group to a section.
- **Cluster** stickies automatically with **selection grouping** or use FigJam AI to summarise or group (check the output).
- **Open sessions** let participants join live and see cursors.

## Facilitate
Explain the exercise and timebox; silent individual work first, then sharing; keep one voice on the mic; capture decisions and owners in a **summary section**; collect feedback with a quick stamp poll.

## After the session
Export as **PNG/PDF** (section selection), copy decisions to Slack/Docs, **convert stickies to Jira or Asana tickets** via integrations, and duplicate the board as a template for repeats.

## Verify
- [ ] Board has agenda, instructions, timers and a parking lot before participants arrive.
- [ ] Voting settings fit the exercise; outputs exported and shared with owners.
