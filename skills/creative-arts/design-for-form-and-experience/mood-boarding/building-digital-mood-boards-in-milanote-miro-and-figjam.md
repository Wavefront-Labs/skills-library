# Digital boards in Milanote, Miro and FigJam

Features change often; verify details in each tool's help centre.

## Which tool
- **Milanote:** image-first boards with notes, columns and nested boards; good for solo and small-team creative work.
- **Miro:** large infinite canvas with frames, templates, voting, comments and many integrations; good for workshops and big groups.
- **FigJam:** whiteboard in the Figma family with stickies, sections, stamps and voting; convenient when the design work lives in Figma (see `...figma.running-figjam-workshops`).

## Workflow
1. **Structure first:** create sections or frames for themes (materials, colour, form, environment), plus an "inbox" area for unsorted images.
2. **Import** by drag and drop, paste, a browser extension or an image URL; keep source links in captions.
3. **Curate:** move candidates into sections, delete the weak ones, then compose a presentation frame (see `...mood-boarding.composing-and-curating-a-mood-board`).
4. **Collaborate:** invite team and stakeholders as commenters; use sticky notes for reactions and dot voting; set a deadline for feedback.
5. **Version:** duplicate the board before big changes, or keep a "v1 / v2" naming; save exports for milestones.
6. **Export** to PDF or PNG for sharing and archive; check resolution and that credit lines are included.
7. **Permissions:** limit edit access; remember that guests with a link may see everything.

## Gotchas
- Image-heavy boards load slowly; downscale originals and keep full-size files elsewhere.
- A board that is never curated is a dump.

## Verify
- [ ] A presentation frame exists separate from the working area.
- [ ] Sources are recorded.
- [ ] Access is limited to the right people.
