# Presenting directions and gathering feedback

Goal: leave with a decision, not a pile of opinions.

## Before
1. **Agree the criteria** with stakeholders (brand fit, audience, differentiation, accessibility, feasibility) and who decides.
2. **Share context:** brief, audience and attributes.

## During
1. **Restate the goals** and criteria.
2. **Present one direction at a time** with its name, rationale, and the mood board and tile (see `...mood-boarding.exploring-divergent-design-directions`); show them in the same order and format.
3. **Frame feedback requests:** ask "What does this say about the brand? Which words match? What feels off for the audience?" rather than "Do you like it?"
4. **Hold silence** after each question; capture words exactly.
5. **Collect individual reactions in writing** (stickies or a form) before group discussion so loud voices do not dominate.
6. **Separate** feedback on strategy from taste: redirect "I do not like blue" to what blue communicates.
7. **Converge:** dot-vote or rank against criteria, then discuss; decide, or choose elements to combine, with an owner.

## After
- Send a summary the same day: decision, rationale, next steps, what is out of scope.
- Log feedback by element for the next round.

## Verify
- [ ] The decision maker and criteria were agreed in advance.
- [ ] Feedback was recorded individually and then discussed.
- [ ] A written summary with next steps went out.
