# Affinity diagramming

**Goal:** turn a pile of raw observations into themes the team agrees on, grouped by content rather than by categories decided in advance.

## Steps (NN/g)
1. **Generate notes:** each participant writes ideas on separate sticky notes, ideally independently to avoid bias. Set a time limit, typically 5-10 minutes depending on the amount of data. Colour-coding is optional.2. **Cluster:** the team sorts notes collaboratively, optionally led by a facilitator. Group by content first, then choose a clear label. Do not force clusters; when in doubt, duplicate a note into two clusters.
3. **Prioritise:** review each cluster, document next steps, and rank or vote. Use dot voting or dot coding if consensus is hard. Name a point person for action items.

For large groups, split into smaller ones.

## Gotchas
- Sorting top-down into feature areas rediscovers your own org chart; label after grouping.
- Counting notes as evidence strength: one talkative participant can produce many notes, so count participants.

## Verify
Every theme cites notes from several participants; each cluster has a clear label and next step.
