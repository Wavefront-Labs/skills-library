# Service safari

**Goal:** build first-hand empathy and spot good and bad practice by going through real services as a customer.

## Plan
1. **Pick services:** your own, direct competitors, and analogous services from other fields (an airline check-in for a clinic, a hotel for a hospital ward).
2. **Define what to experience:** specific scenarios (book, arrive, ask for help, complain, leave), and what to look for (waits, handoffs, signage, tone).
3. **Form teams of 2-3,** give each a service and a capture sheet or app.

## Run
- Experience it exactly as a customer: book, queue, pay, call support, return an item. Time each phase; note emotions at each step.
- **Capture:** photos (where allowed), screenshots, recordings of calls only with legal consent, receipts, a log of touchpoints and how you felt, and moments of delight and frustration.
- Keep a neutral demeanour; avoid revealing you are researching if the goal is a mystery-shopper view, and respect staff, privacy and local law.

## Synthesise
- Lay out the captured touchpoints on a wall in journey order per service.
- Mark highs and lows; cluster patterns and write 3-5 insight statements (`creative-arts.design-for-form-and-experience.ux-design.writing-insight-statements-and-how-might-we-questions`).
- Bring back **inspiration** (good ideas to adapt) and **warnings** (failures to avoid).

## Gotchas
- A single visit is anecdote; repeat at different times or with different teammates when the service varies.
- Take no photos of other customers; get permission in sensitive places.

## Verify
- [ ] Scenarios and observation focus set before going out.
- [ ] Every team captured evidence and emotions per touchpoint.
- [ ] Findings synthesised into insights and shared with the team.
