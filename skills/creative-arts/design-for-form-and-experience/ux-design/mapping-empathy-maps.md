# Empathy maps

**Goal:** a quick, shared picture of one user type's perspective built from research.

## Canvas (four quadrants around the user or persona)
| Quadrant | Content |
|---|---|
| Says | Ideally verbatim, direct quotes from user research |
| Thinks | What goes through the user's mind during the experience |
| Does | What they do, as opposed to what they say |
| Feels | The user's emotions |

## Steps (NN/g)
1. Define scope and goals.
2. Gather materials.
3. Collect research.
4. Individually write sticky notes for each quadrant; do not see each other's notes in this phase.
5. Converge: cluster and synthesise.
6. Polish and plan.

When working from interview transcripts, set a clear scope and timebox the effort.

## Limits
- A sparse map signals missing data: if you cannot fill a quadrant, do more research before moving on.
- A map can be sketched before research only if the team clearly separates evidence from assumptions; label it as hypotheses and plan the study to test them, and mark assumption notes as such.

## Verify
Notes trace to research; assumption notes are labelled; empty quadrants are turned into research questions.
