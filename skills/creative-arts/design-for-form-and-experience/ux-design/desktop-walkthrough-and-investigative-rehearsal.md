# Desktop walkthrough and investigative rehearsal

**Goal:** test a service concept physically and early, before building it, by acting it out.

## Desktop walkthrough
A scale model of the service environment on a table.
1. Build the setting from paper, LEGO or cut-outs: rooms, counters, kiosks, devices.
2. Use small figures for customers and staff. Move them through a scenario while a narrator describes each step.
3. Place touchpoint cards (letter, app screen, sign) where they appear.
4. Watch for crowding, long walks, missing info, and dead ends; rearrange and rerun.
Cheap, fast, good for layouts and process flow, less good for feeling.

## Investigative rehearsal
Full-scale role-play to learn rather than perform.
1. Write 2-3 **scenarios** including a failure case, and roles (customer, staff, system).
2. Prepare **props**: printed forms, mock screens on phones, chairs as a counter, signage on paper.
3. Staff and customers (real or team members) act it out; a facilitator can pause, rewind and replay.
4. Observers record breakdowns, confusion, time, and emotional reactions; stop at moments to ask what the actor expected.
5. Debrief: list what to change, then **re-rehearse** the revised version.

## Tips
- Include frontline staff in the cast; they find backstage problems fast.
- Rehearse failures as well as the happy path.
- Film short clips (with consent) for stakeholders.

## Verify
- [ ] Scenarios cover at least one failure case.
- [ ] Observers recorded breakdowns, not only opinions.
- [ ] A second round tested the changes.
