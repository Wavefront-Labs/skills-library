# Content inventory and audit

An **inventory** is a list of what content exists. An **audit** evaluates its quality and fit. Halvorson and Rach describe quantitative inventories and qualitative audits as separate steps.

## Inventory
1. **Scope:** the whole site or a section; choose full or sample.
2. **Collect** URLs by crawling (a crawler such as Screaming Frog, the CMS export, or the sitemap) into a spreadsheet.
3. **Columns:** ID, URL, title, type/template, section, owner, last updated, word count, and analytics (views, entry pages, exits) if available.

## Audit
1. **Define criteria** from goals and users: accurate, current, clear, complete, on-brand and on-voice, findable, accessible, and aligned with a business goal.
2. **Rate each page or sample** on a short scale with notes; use two reviewers on a sample to calibrate.
3. **Decide:** keep, update, merge, move, rewrite or remove (redirect old URLs).
4. **Spot patterns:** duplicated content, orphan pages, outdated material, gaps against user tasks.

## Report
A short summary with counts and examples, a prioritised action list and owners, and recommendations for governance (who owns what, review dates).

## Gotchas
- Do not remove pages with traffic or links without redirects.
- Auditing without knowing user tasks gives vanity cleanup.

## Verify
- [ ] Every row has an owner and a decision.
- [ ] Removed URLs have redirects or a reason.
- [ ] Criteria were written before rating.
