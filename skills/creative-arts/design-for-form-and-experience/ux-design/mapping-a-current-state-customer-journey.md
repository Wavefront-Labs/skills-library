# Current-state journey map

**Goal:** a research-based picture of how one type of customer experiences one scenario today.

## Components (NN/g)
- **Actor:** the persona or user whose journey is mapped. Use one actor per map (a university needs separate maps for students and faculty).
- **Scenario and expectations:** the situation, tied to the actor's goal and what they expect. Journey maps suit sequential, process-based scenarios that may span several channels.
- **Phases:** high-level stages that organise the rest of the map; the organisation usually has data to define them.
- **Actions, mindsets, emotions:** actions are a narrative of steps within each phase, not a granular log. Mindsets are thoughts, questions and information needs, ideally customer verbatims from research. Emotions are a single line across phases showing highs and lows.
- **Opportunities:** insights for improvement, with ownership and metrics.

## Process
1. Compile user actions into a timeline, rooted in data.
2. Add user thoughts and emotions to build a narrative.
3. Condense and polish it into a visualisation.
4. Ask what needs doing, who owns each change, where the biggest opportunities are and how improvement will be measured.

## Gotchas
- Mapping the company's process instead of the customer's experience.
- One map for every customer type; use one actor per map.

## Verify
Actions and mindsets trace to research data; each opportunity has an owner and a measure.
