# Five-day design sprint

**Goal:** answer a critical business question by building and testing a realistic prototype in one week.

## Setup
- A **Decider**, a **Facilitator**, and 5-7 people with diverse roles (design, engineering, marketing, support, finance). Clear their calendars for the week; devices off during sessions.
- A real problem with a long-term goal and a hard question, plus a room with whiteboards, timers, sticky notes, markers.

## The week
- **Monday - Map:** long-term goal and sprint questions; map the customer journey; ask experts (How Might We notes); pick a **target** moment.
- **Tuesday - Sketch:** lightning demos of existing solutions; individual sketching: notes, ideas, **Crazy 8s**, then a three-panel solution sketch (see `creative-arts.design-for-form-and-experience.ux-design.crazy-eights-and-dot-voting`).
- **Wednesday - Decide:** art museum (silent review), heat map dot vote, speed critique, straw poll, Decider votes; then a **storyboard** of about 10-15 frames.
- **Thursday - Prototype:** "fake it": a Keynote or Figma facade that looks real. Roles: Makers, Stitcher, Writer, Asset Collector, Interviewer. Trial run by end of day.
- **Friday - Test:** five one-on-one interviews with target customers, one interviewer, team watching live and taking notes on a grid; then review patterns for each sprint question.

## Gotchas
- Without a Decider, the week stalls at Wednesday.
- Prototype only the storyboard path, not the whole product.
- Five tests show patterns (about 85 percent of major problems); they do not give rates.
- Variants exist (4-day, remote with Miro/Figma, "sprint 2.0"); keep the same arc.

## Verify
- [ ] A clear target and sprint questions exist by Monday evening.
- [ ] Prototype is ready and rehearsed before Friday.
- [ ] Friday ends with answers to the sprint questions and next steps.
