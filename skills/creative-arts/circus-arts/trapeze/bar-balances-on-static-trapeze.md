# Bar balances on static trapeze

**Goal:** hip balance, back balance and seated hands-free positions on the bar. The bar
is round and rocks; balance comes from locating your centre exactly over it.

> Coach-led. Learn hands-free balances on a low bar with a spotter; plan how to fall out
> of each before trying it.

## Hip balance (front of hips on the bar)
1. From front support, lean forward, legs extended back, body horizontal.
2. Find the point where the body balances over the hip bones; hands hover then release.
3. Arms extend sideways for small corrections.

## Back balance (lower back on the bar)
1. From sitting, lie back so the bar sits across the lower back, legs and arms extended.
2. Tiny adjustments of head and feet control the balance.
3. Ropes stay within reach; the coach spots under the shoulders and legs.

## Coaching points
- Balance is found in millimetres; move the whole body as one, don't bend at the waist.
- Keep the bar still before entering; a swinging bar makes balances unrepeatable.
- Squeeze the legs together; an open body has more to control.

## Fall planning
- Front: hands grab the bar or the body rotates into a hocks catch.
- Back: hands reach for the ropes or bar; a spotter controls the first attempts.

## Verify
- [ ] Each balance held 5 s hands-free at low height.
- [ ] Student demonstrates the planned exit from each balance.
