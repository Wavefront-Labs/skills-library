# Mounts to sitting and standing on a static trapeze

**Goal:** get onto the bar safely and with control. Mounts set the vocabulary for
everything else on static trapeze.

> Coach-led. Learn on a low bar (reachable from the floor) over mats.

## Bar setup
- Bar at a height where the student can hang with feet just off the floor. Ropes
  checked, bar padding or tape intact, rigging logged.

## Tuck-through to sit
1. Hang from the bar, overhand grip, active shoulders.
2. Tuck knees to chest and pass the legs between the hands, hooking the knees over the
   bar (hocks hang) or continue to an inverted hang.
3. From hocks, reach hands to the ropes and sit up onto the bar.

## Pullover
1. Hands on the bar, pull the bar to the hips, rotate back over it.
2. Finish in a front support on the bar, then turn to sit holding the ropes.

## Standing on the bar
- From sitting, hands on ropes, bring one foot to the bar, then the other, stand with
  hands on ropes at shoulder height.
- Feet close to the ropes give more stability; the centre of the bar rocks.

## Coaching points
- Hands never let go of the bar until a knee is hooked or a rope is held.
- Hips stay close to the bar in the pullover.

## Faults
- Kicking the tuck-through: swing and loss of grip.
- Standing with feet in the bar's centre and hands too high: bar tips.

## Verify
- [ ] Mount to sit and to stand, controlled, 3 times each.
- [ ] Coach-steadied bar before unsupported standing.
