# Safety lines for swinging trapeze

**Goal:** a safety line (lunge) lets an operator take a falling trapeze artist's weight
while they learn. Lines only protect if the rig and the operator are right.

## Rig
- Performer wears a properly fitted harness with attachment points at both hips (side
  lines) so a fall stays upright.
- Lines run up to pulleys on the rig above the swing's pivot so the line follows the
  arc, then down to the operator.
- All hardware rated and logged; ropes inspected for wear at the pulley contact.

## Operator
- Stands where they can see the performer and keep the line tensioned.
- Takes in slack as the performer rises; never lets a loop of slack form.
- On a fall, catches the weight, slows, and lowers to the mat.
- Knows the calls and is not doing any other job.

## When lines come off
- Coach decision only, after the trick is consistent in lines over several sessions.
- Lines are back on for any new trick, after injury, or after a long break.

## Faults
- Too much slack: the line doesn't arrest the fall before the floor.
- Too tight: lifts the performer and spoils the timing.
- Pulleys placed off the swing's pivot: the line pulls the performer sideways.

## Verify
- [ ] Operator has caught test falls in rehearsal.
- [ ] Harness fit checked every session.
