# Release tricks (splits, shooting star, saltos)

**Goal:** release tricks leave the fly bar in free flight to the catcher. The release
point and timing must be exact; this is where calls and lines matter most.

> Coach-led only. Every release trick is learned to the net in lines first, then to an
> experienced catcher, with the lunge operator and coach in position.

## Common release tricks
- **Splits:** at the end of the swing, legs straddle over the bar; release forward into
  the catch.
- **Shooting star:** legs hooked over the bar briefly then released, body extended,
  arms reaching to the catcher.
- **Back salto / layout:** release at the back of the swing, rotate backward, arrive
  at the catcher's hands.
- **Double, triple saltos:** expert work requiring years of progression.

## Release point
- Most tricks release near the top of the forward swing, when the bar is slowing. The
  coach calls the release; the flyer does not decide alone.
- Hips lead the release; a late release sends the flyer low.

## Flight
- Eyes find the catcher as early as the trick allows.
- Arms reach for the catcher's wrists, straight.

## Progression
1. Trick to the net in lines.
2. Trick with "to the catcher" timing, but to the net.
3. Catches with an experienced catcher, lines on.

## Faults
- Early release: flyer travels too far; catcher can't reach.
- Late release: flyer drops short.

## Verify
- [ ] Consistent net landings and timing calls.
- [ ] Catcher and coach agree before first catch attempt.
