# Catcher's lock and leg position

**Goal:** the catcher hangs from the catch trap by the legs, leaving the hands free to
catch. The lock must hold the catcher's weight plus the flyer's dynamic load.

> Coach-led. Catchers are trained by experienced catchers, initially without a flyer.

## Lock
- Catcher sits on the catch bar, then hangs down with knees over the bar.
- Legs wrap the ropes or a wrapped section of the bar (often with tape or a wrap for
  friction); feet hooked behind the ropes or each other.
- Many catchers wear wraps or grip tape on the backs of the knees and lower legs.

## Body position
- Body hangs below the bar with the hips under the knees; arms extend down for the
  catch.
- The lock is tested by the catcher swinging alone and building to performance
  amplitude before any flyer.

## Checks before each catch
- Wraps are in place and tight.
- The catch bar and ropes are inspected; tape or padding not worn through.
- Legs feel solid; any slip in a solo swing means the lock is rebuilt.

## Faults
- Hips swinging behind the bar: the lock opens on the catch.
- Worn wrap or sweaty skin: slip under the flyer's load.

## Verify
- [ ] Solo swings at performance amplitude without lock movement.
- [ ] Coach checks lock before first catches.
