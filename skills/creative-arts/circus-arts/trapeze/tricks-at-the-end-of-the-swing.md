# Tricks at the end of the swing

**Goal:** swinging trapeze tricks (drops, balances, releases to the bar) are timed at the
ends of the swing, where the bar momentarily stops and the forces are lowest.

> Coach-led only. Every new trick is attempted in safety lines with a lunge operator and
> coach calling.

## Why the end of the swing
- At the end of each swing the bar is nearly still and forces are near body weight;
  at the bottom the body is heaviest (largest load). Tricks that change grip or
  position are done at the end; the bottom is for holding on.

## Timing cues
- Feel the bar decelerate; the instant before it stops is the cue.
- Coach calls ("ready", "hep" or the school's word) until timing is automatic.

## Progression
1. Learn the trick on a static bar.
2. On a small swing, in lines, with the coach calling.
3. Increase swing amplitude only once the trick works at small swing.

## Faults
- Early or late: the body is moving relative to the bar and lands off-centre.
- Looking down: the head pulls the body out of line.

## Verify
- [ ] Trick works on static bar and small swing.
- [ ] Coach and lunge operator agree each trick before adding amplitude.
