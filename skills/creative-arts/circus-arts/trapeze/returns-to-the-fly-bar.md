# Returns to the fly bar

**Goal:** after a catch, the flyer can return to the fly bar instead of dropping to the
net. The return needs the catcher's throw, the flyer's turn and the fly bar to arrive
together.

> Coach-led only. Returns are taught with experienced catchers, a coach timing the fly
> bar and lines on.

## The return
1. The catcher swings with the flyer and, at the top of the swing, throws the flyer
   back toward the fly bar.
2. The flyer performs a half turn (pirouette) in the air to face the fly bar.
3. The fly bar is sent back by the board staff (or arrives on its own swing) at the
   right moment; the flyer catches it with both hands.
4. The flyer swings back to the board and lands.

## Timing
- The board staff controls the fly bar: hold it, then send it at the call, so it
  reaches the meeting point as the flyer does.
- Coach calls the throw and the turn.

## Coaching points
- Turn direction is fixed for each flyer; practise on the ground.
- Eyes find the bar early; arms reach straight.

## Missed return
- Flyer tucks and lands in the net. Plan this every time; lines stay on until the
  return is consistent.

## Verify
- [ ] Half turn drilled on a trampoline or the ground with a spot.
- [ ] Bar timing rehearsed by the board staff without a flyer.
