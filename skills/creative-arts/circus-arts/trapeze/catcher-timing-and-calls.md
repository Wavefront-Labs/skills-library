# Catcher timing and calls

**Goal:** the catcher controls the timing of most catches. The catcher's swing must meet
the flyer at the right point, and the call tells the flyer when to leave the board.

> Coach-led. Calls are agreed by the whole flying team before rehearsal.

## Synchronising swings
- The catcher starts swinging first and builds to a consistent amplitude.
- The catcher calls the flyer off the board (often "listo" / "ready" then "hep") so
  the two swings meet at the catch point.
- Flyers and catchers count swings the same way; the coach confirms.

## Reading the flyer
- Catcher watches the flyer's takeoff and first swing; a low or early takeoff changes
  the meeting point.
- If the meeting looks wrong, the catcher calls the flyer to the net or does not
  reach. No improvised catches.

## Calls
- "Hep" (or the team's word): leave the board / perform the trick.
- Abort call: one word, practised, always obeyed.
- After the catch, the catcher calls the flyer's release to the net or the return.

## Faults
- Calling off rhythm: flyer arrives early or late.
- Two people calling: flyer doesn't know which to follow. One caller per trick.

## Verify
- [ ] Calls written down and rehearsed without tricks.
- [ ] Abort call practised in every rehearsal.
