# Swinging trapeze rigging and ropes

**Goal:** a swinging trapeze puts much larger, cyclic and directional loads into the
roof than a static one. Rigging is done by a qualified rigger; the artist provides the
right data and checks the equipment.

## Points
- Two separate rated points, spaced to the bar width or wider, with known working load
  limits and approved for dynamic, swinging loads.
- Rigger needs: performer weight, maximum swing angle, tricks with drops or catches,
  number of shows per day.

## Ropes and hardware
- Steel cable core with cotton or synthetic cover is common; ropes the same length,
  terminated with thimbles and swaged or rated fittings.
- Swivels are normally not used (no spin wanted).
- Bar: steel with weighted ends on many swinging rigs; check welds, tape and padding.

## Clearance
- Calculate the swing arc: rope length and maximum angle give the forward and back
  travel; add body length and margin to lighting, set and audience.
- Safety line pulleys above the pivot points.

## Inspection
- Before every session: thimbles seated, no broken wires or cover damage, shackles
  moused or locked, bar welds intact.
- Log inspections and any replacements.

## Verify
- [ ] Rigger has the load data and signed off the points.
- [ ] Swing clearance measured and marked.
