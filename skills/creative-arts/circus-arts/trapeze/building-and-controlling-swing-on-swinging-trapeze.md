# Building and controlling swing on swinging trapeze

**Goal:** a swinging trapeze artist builds and controls the swing from their own body.
Control of the swing comes before any trick.

> Coach-led. Swinging trapeze is learned in a safety lunge (see the safety-line skill)
> with a qualified operator.

## Pumping mechanics
- Swing is built by shifting the body's centre of mass in time with the swing: at the
  back of the swing, extend; as the bar passes the bottom, pull in and rise (stand or
  pike); at the front, extend again. Same idea as a playground swing.
- Standing on the bar, a squat-and-stand rhythm (bend at the ends, stand through the
  bottom) builds height.

## Amplitude limits
- Coach sets the maximum amplitude for the student (e.g. bar ropes no more than a set
  angle from vertical) based on the rig and the trick.
- The rig is designed for a certain swing; exceeding it can put the bar into the
  rigging or the ropes slack at the top (dangerous).

## Damping the swing
- Reverse the pump (pull in at the ends) to remove energy.
- Coach can slow the bar from the ground using a tail rope on some rigs.

## Calls
- Coach: "build", "hold", "down", "stop".
- Student never begins a trick without the coach's go.

## Verify
- [ ] Student builds to the set amplitude and damps back to still on command.
- [ ] No slack-rope moments at the top of the swing.
