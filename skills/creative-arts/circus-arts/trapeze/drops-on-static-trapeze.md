# Drops on static trapeze

**Goal:** a drop moves from one position to a lower catch (seat to hocks, hocks to
ankles). Drops are high-reward and among the highest-risk static trapeze elements.

> Coach-led only. Drops require a rigger-rated point for dynamic loads, thick mats and
> a coach who has taught that drop before.

## Prerequisites
- The catch position is held confidently statically (e.g. hocks hang, ankle hang).
- The student can do the slow, hands-on version of the drop.

## Common drops
- **Seat drop to hocks:** from sitting, fall backward, hooking the knees on the bar.
- **Hocks to ankles (heel or ankle catch):** an advanced drop; the feet must already be
  in a hooking shape before the knees leave the bar.
- **Back balance drop:** from back balance, roll off to a catch. Path must be known.

## Progression
1. Slow version, hands controlling the descent.
2. Small, fast version with coach spotting at low height.
3. Full drop at low height, multiple sessions.
4. Height raised only after consistent catches.

## Load and rigging
- Drops create a sudden load on the bar and ropes; tell the rigger which drops are in
  the act.

## Faults
- Starting the drop before the catch shape is set.
- Rope on one side catching the arm: plan which side of the rope the body passes.

## Verify
- [ ] 5 consecutive clean catches at low height.
- [ ] Rigger informed of dynamic elements.
