# Catcher swing and the return throw

**Goal:** the catcher builds a swing that meets the flyer, absorbs the catch, and either
releases the flyer to the net or throws them back to the fly bar.

> Coach-led only. Taught with experienced flyers and lines on.

## Building the swing
- From the lock, pump by bending and extending at the hips (the same principle as a
  swinging trapeze).
- Match the flyer's swing: amplitude agreed before each trick.

## Absorbing the catch
- At the catch, the catcher's arms stay strong but soft; the body continues the swing
  with the flyer's weight added.
- Both swings continue together; the catcher keeps the lock tight through the bottom
  of the swing (highest load).

## The return throw
1. At the top of the swing back toward the fly bar, the catcher throws the flyer by
   releasing the wrists while pushing slightly.
2. The flyer turns and catches the fly bar.
3. If the timing is wrong, the catcher releases the flyer to the net instead.

## Faults
- Throwing early: flyer short of the bar.
- Not releasing cleanly: flyer twisted in the air.

## Verify
- [ ] Throw timing practised to the net before attempting returns.
- [ ] Catcher and board staff agree the call for sending the fly bar.
