# Partner calls and bail-out cues for duo aerial

**Goal:** both partners always know what happens next, and what happens if it goes wrong.

## Build the vocabulary
Keep it short and unambiguous; each word means one thing.
- "Ready" - I am locked and can take load.
- "Hep" (flying) / "Go" - commit now.
- "Hold" - stay where you are.
- "Down" - come back to the bar or base position.
- "Stop" - abort; follow the bail-out plan.
Agree who calls each transition (usually the base for weight transfers, the flyer for
releases). Both can call "stop".

## Bail-out plan per transition
For every move, write: if it fails here, where does each person go? Examples:
- Flyer slips from hands-to-hands: base keeps one wrist, flyer re-grips the rope.
- Base loses lock: base calls stop, flyer returns to bar first.
Rehearse the bail-out deliberately, not only when it happens.

## Performance cues
- Music hides voices; use squeezes (two squeezes = ready), taps, or eye contact.
- Keep at least one audible word for "stop"; the audience will not mind.

## Gotchas
- New calls added late in rehearsal get confused with old ones; rewrite the sheet.
- Replacement partners: teach the call sheet before any height work.

## Verify
- [ ] Written call sheet with every transition, caller and bail-out.
- [ ] Each bail-out rehearsed at low height.
- [ ] Silent cues tested with show music playing.
