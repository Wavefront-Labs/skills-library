# Operating flying trapeze safety lunges

**Goal:** the person on the lines keeps a learning flyer from landing badly in the net
(or missing it) without interfering with the trick.

## Equipment
- **Safety belt** with side rings at hip level, fitted snug (two fingers under the belt
  at most), buckle doubled back.
- Two lines, one to each ring, running up through pulleys on the rig and down to the
  operator at the back or side of the net.
- Gloves for the operator.

## Fitting
1. Belt on, low on the waist above the hip bones, tightened, tail secured.
2. Clip each line, gates locked, lines not crossed.
3. Flyer lifts arms; operator takes slack and confirms the lines run free.

## Handling through the swing
- Keep **light tension**: slack enough not to pull the flyer, tight enough that you can
  take weight within a fraction of a second.
- Walk with the swing if needed; pay out on the forward swing, take in on the return.
- On release tricks, follow the rotation without yanking.

## Catching a miss or bad landing
- If the flyer releases early or is rotating towards a head-or-neck landing, pull to
  slow the fall and orient the body to a seat or back landing.
- Lower the flyer into the net; do not drop them from the top of a pull.

## Calls
- Operator calls "ready", "hep" (or the coach does), and "let go" / "hold" as agreed.
- Flyer must know that "hold" means stay on the bar.

## Gotchas
- Lines catching the fly bar: keep them outside the bar ropes at takeoff.
- Operator distracted by the crowd: one job only during a turn.
- Wet lines on outdoor rigs slip in gloves; adjust grip.

## Verify
- [ ] Belt checked by a second person before every turn.
- [ ] Operator has practised catches with a staff flyer this season.
- [ ] Calls agreed and repeated before each new trick.
