# Static, swinging and flying trapeze — technique and safety

**Goal:** know what each trapeze discipline demands, how its safety system works, and
what order to learn things in so the swing never outruns your control.

> **Safety.** Trapeze injuries come from mistimed releases, bad net landings and
> rigging faults. Swinging and flying trapeze must only be learned on a professionally
> rigged apparatus with trained line-handlers. Nothing here replaces a coach on the lines.

## 1. Three different apparatus
| | Static | Swinging (solo) | Flying |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bar moves? | No (performer may rock) | Yes, large arc | Yes, flyer swings to a catcher or bar |
| Main risk | Falls from shapes, shoulder strain | Release at wrong point of arc | Missed catch, net landing |
| Safety | Crash mat, spotter | Mechanic (lunge) lines, mat | Net + safety lines on the flyer |

Static skills (knee hangs, ankle hangs, balances on the bar and ropes) transfer to the
others; that is why most schools start there.

## 2. Static fundamentals
- **Mount:** pull-over or tuck-through to sit; never hop up with momentum.
- **Hocks (knee hang):** lower with hands still on the bar until knees are locked
  around it; ankles flexed. Release hands only when the coach confirms.
- **Ropes work:** standing and sitting on the ropes, "angel", birds-nest. Wrap the
  rope over the forearm or ankle before shifting weight onto it.
- **Ankle hangs / heel hangs** are advanced and need a coach-led progression and a
  spotter every time while learning. *Opinions differ on how long to wait; ask your coach.*

## 3. Flying trapeze: the swing and the call
- The **board** is the platform; the flyer leaves on the **"hep!"** call from the line
  operator or catcher. Never leave on your own count: timing belongs to the caller.
- **Swing technique:** a forceful "sweep" through the bottom and a "seven" (pike) at the
  back end build amplitude; the trick happens near the peak of the forward swing,
  where velocity is lowest.
- **Safety lines:** the flyer wears a belt attached to two lines run by a ground
  operator who can take weight and slow a fall. Clip-in is checked by two people.
- **Progression:** knee hang off the board › knee hang to backflip dismount into the
  net › knee hang **catch** with the catcher › then releases (layout, splits, shooting
  star) › flips and twists to the catch.

## 4. Landing in the net — practise it first
- Land on your **back or seat**, chin tucked, arms out to the side or folded across the chest;
  never feet-first, head-first or on the knees, and never put a hand back to break the fall.
- After landing, roll to the edge, grip it, and flip down to the floor; do not stand
  and walk on the net.
- Drill net landings from low before any trick; most beginner injuries are landings.

## 5. Swinging trapeze (solo)
- Higher, faster swings with mechanic lines on the performer. Releases, catches back
  on the bar and heel/ankle catches happen at the dead point of the arc.
- The lines operator stays on throughout; the rigging includes a tensioned
  frame or guy lines that the rigger signs off.

## Rigging and inspection
- Rated points and hardware (see creative-arts.circus-arts.aerial-arts.silks-and-lyra-foundations-and-safe-progression
  for the hardware chain logic).
- Check ropes for abrasion at the bar thimbles, bar tape for wear, net for broken
  meshes and correct tension (sagging nets bottom out).
- Line pulleys run free; lines checked for glazing and fraying.

## Gotchas
- **Grip on tape:** chalk helps on the bar; never on the ropes you need to slide.
- **Shoulder load at the bottom of the swing** is the highest; engaged shoulders, not passive hanging.
- **Early release** is the dangerous error: the flyer travels forward toward the frame.
- Fear makes flyers "muscle" the timing — trust the call, or don't go.
- Hands blister; use grips/tape as your coach advises.

## Verify
- [ ] You can say which apparatus you're on and which safety system it uses.
- [ ] Net landings practised and consistent before any trick.
- [ ] Lines checked and clip-in confirmed by a second person on flying/swinging.
- [ ] You leave only on the caller's "hep".
- [ ] New skills follow the progression: static › knee hang › catch › releases.
