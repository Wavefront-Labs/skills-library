# Landing in the net and dismounts

**Goal:** every flyer must land in the net safely before they learn any trick. Net
landings are taught and rehearsed first and on every new trick.

## Landing position
- Land on the **back or seat** with the chin tucked to the chest, arms out to the
  sides or crossed on the chest. Most schools teach a seat-to-back landing.
- Never land on the hands, knees, feet or head; never reach down to break the fall.
- Legs together, knees slightly bent.

## Dropping from the bar
1. At the coach's call (usually at the back of the swing or a specific point), release.
2. Tuck the chin, bring the knees up, land on the back.
3. Lie still until the bounce stops.

## Getting off the net
- Roll to the edge, grab the net edge, flip over to a hang, lower to the floor or a
  mat. Never jump down from the edge.
- Watch for the next flyer before crossing the net.

## Faults
- Reaching with the arms: wrist and elbow injuries.
- Head back on impact: whiplash.
- Landing near the edge or on a cable: net tension and position wrong for the trick
  (tell the coach and rigger).

## Verify
- [ ] Student shows the landing position on a mat first.
- [ ] Clean net landings after release from the bar on the first session.
