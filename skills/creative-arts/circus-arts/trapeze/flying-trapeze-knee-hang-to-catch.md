# Knee hang to catch (flying trapeze)

**Goal:** the knee hang catch is the first trick most flyers take to the catcher. It
teaches the call sequence, the timing and the hand-off that every later trick uses.

> Coach-led only. Taught in safety lines over a net with a qualified lunge operator and
> catcher. Learn it first to the net, then to the catcher.

## Sequence
1. **Ready / hep:** on the board, hands on the fly bar, the call is given; take off
   with hips forward.
2. **Legs up:** on the first forward swing, at the coach's call, bring the legs up
   between the hands and hook the knees over the bar.
3. **Hands off:** let go of the bar and hang from the knees, arms reaching back.
4. **Arch at the front:** on the next forward swing, at the call, arch with arms
   extended toward the catcher.
5. **Catch:** catcher takes the flyer by the wrists (wrist-to-wrist grip); the flyer
   unhooks the knees as the catcher's swing takes the weight.

## Coaching points
- Knees hook at the knee pit, toes flexed, hard squeeze.
- Arms are passive and straight for the catch; the catcher does the catching.
- Look at the catcher's hands.

## Faults
- Unhooking the knees early: flyer drops before the catch.
- Grabbing at the catcher: elbows bend, grip lost.
- Late arch: flyer passes under the catcher's hands.

## Verify
- [ ] 5 clean knee hang to net sequences with correct timing.
- [ ] First catches only with an experienced catcher and lines on.
