# Work in the ropes and ankle hangs

**Goal:** rope figures and ankle/heel hangs are where static trapeze gets its height and
line. They rely on precise foot hooks and the ropes' grip.

> Coach-led only. Ankle and heel hangs are taught after a secure hocks hang, with a
> spotter and mats, at low height.

## Rope figures
- Hand-in-rope wraps (rope around the wrist), elbow hooks and knee hooks give holds high
  in the ropes. Each wrap is checked by the coach before body weight goes on it.
- Standing in the ropes: feet on the bar against the ropes, hands high; body inside or
  outside the ropes.

## Ankle hang (feet hooked around the ropes at the bar)
1. From a hocks hang, hook both feet around the ropes where they meet the bar,
   toes flexed and pulling the ropes in.
2. Coach spots at the shoulders and hips.
3. Slide the knees off the bar so weight transfers to the ankles while the feet keep
   the hook.

## Heel hang (heels hooked on the bar)
- Flexed feet hooked over the bar from a hocks hang, body hanging below. Higher risk;
  taught only after reliable ankle hangs.

## Coaching points
- Feet stay flexed hard; a pointed foot slips.
- Practise the return to hocks before letting the knees go.

## Stop rules
- Any foot slip at low height: back to the previous step.
- Cramp in the feet or calves: come down.

## Verify
- [ ] Return from ankle hang to hocks, spotted, 5 times.
- [ ] Coach sign-off before unspotted attempts.
