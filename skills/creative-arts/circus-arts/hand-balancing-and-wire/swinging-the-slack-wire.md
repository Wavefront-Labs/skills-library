# Swinging the slack wire

**Goal:** the classic slack wire act swings the wire in large arcs while the performer
stays upright, often juggling or balancing objects.

> Coach-led. Amplitude is limited by the rig and clearance; agree a maximum before
> starting.

## Building swing
- From a steady two-foot stand, push the wire sideways with the feet and knees while
  keeping the head and shoulders still; the wire pendulums under you.
- Add a little each swing, in time with the wire's natural period (set by sag and
  length; it does not change much with amplitude).

## Body position
- The body stays near vertical; the wire and lower legs travel. Leaning into the swing
  throws you off at the end.
- Arms out, slight bend at the knees and hips to absorb the ends.

## At the ends of the swing
- The wire momentarily stops and rises; this is where turns or throws are timed.

## Stopping
- Damp by letting the knees absorb at each end (resist the push), reducing the swing
  over several cycles.
- Never step off at full swing; reduce first.

## Clearance
- Measure the full arc with the performer standing; nothing (posts, set, audience) in
  the swept area plus margin.

## Verify
- [ ] Builds to agreed amplitude and damps to still in under 6 swings.
- [ ] Clearance marked on the floor.
