# Cane handle grip and height

**Goal:** handstand canes are stands with a block or handle on top. The grip on the
cane differs from the floor and changes how corrections work.

## Handle types
- **Flat block:** like a small platform; fingers wrap the edges, palm flat.
- **Handle:** a grip bar; the hand wraps around it; more grip, less finger correction.

## Grip
- On a block, the heel of the hand on the block, fingers over the front edge pressing
  down for corrections.
- Fingers do the corrections (pressing to prevent overbalance), the heel of the hand
  corrects underbalance.

## Height
- Start on low canes (a few centimetres), then 30-50 cm, then higher.
- Higher canes increase consequences of a fall but feel the same in balance.

## First balances
- Kick or press to handstand on low canes; balance holds as on the floor.
- Spot from a coach for higher canes.

## Faults
- Hand too far forward on the block: no room to correct.
- Gripping too hard: tension slows corrections.

## Verify
- [ ] 30 s handstand on low canes.
- [ ] Grip consistent left and right.
