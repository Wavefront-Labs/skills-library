# Rola bola falls and stage surface

**Goal:** rola bola falls happen in a fraction of a second, and most injuries are ankle
sprains or the board striking the shin. Learn the exit first and control the surface.

## Falls
- When the board goes, **step off to the side the board is falling to**, landing on both
  feet; let the board and cylinder go.
- Do not try to save a lost balance with the ankle or by stepping on the cylinder.
- Practise deliberate step-offs both sides at the start of every session.

## Ankle and shin protection
- Ankle strength and proprioception work (single-leg balance, wobble board) in
  conditioning.
- Some performers wear ankle supports in training; shins are protected from a flying
  board in early sessions.

## Surface checks
- Level: a slope makes the cylinder drift.
- Non-slip: rubber mat or performance mat taped down; no haze residue, dust, water or
  paint.
- Street: gravel, grates and cracks rule out rola bola; carry a board of plywood with a
  rubber top as a base.

## Rule-outs
- Wet or uneven surfaces, wind for tall stacks, a raised stage edge closer than the fall
  zone.

## Verify
- [ ] Step-offs practised every session.
- [ ] Surface checked and mat taped before each show.
