# Straddle press to handstand

**Goal:** lift from standing (or a straddle stand) to handstand with straight arms and no
jump, legs open in straddle. A press shows control; it is also the foundation for
most hand-balancing transitions.

## Prerequisites
- Freestanding handstand 30 s plus.
- Pike fold with chest close to legs; pancake straddle near the floor.
- Compression: seated straddle leg lifts (pancake lifts) and L-sit.

## Technique
1. Hands shoulder-width on the floor, legs straddled wide, hips high.
2. **Lean:** shift the shoulders forward over the hands until the feet get light.
3. **Lift:** hips rise over the shoulders while the legs stay low and wide; the feet
   leave the floor last.
4. Bring the legs together once the hips are stacked over the shoulders.

## Drills
- **Elevated press:** feet on a box; reduces range needed.
- **Negatives:** from handstand, lower slowly to straddle stand.
- **Wall press:** back to the wall, hips sliding up the wall.
- **Band-assisted:** band from above around the hips.

## Faults
- Bending the arms to lift: becomes a strength press; keep arms straight.
- Lifting the legs first: hips don't rise.
- Not enough lean: the feet can't leave the floor.

## Verify
- [ ] 5 slow negatives from handstand.
- [ ] Elevated press clean before floor attempts.
