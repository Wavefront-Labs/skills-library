# Handstand shapes (straddle, tuck, diamond)

**Goal:** shapes are the hand balancer's vocabulary. Each changes where the centre of mass
sits, so the hands correct differently.

## Shapes
- **Straddle:** legs wide, hips over the shoulders. Lowers the centre of mass slightly;
  often easier than straight.
- **Tuck:** knees to chest, hips over the shoulders with the back rounded. Needs strong
  shoulders.
- **Diamond:** soles of the feet together, knees out; a balance between straddle and
  tuck.
- **Pike:** legs together toward the floor; needs compression.
- **Split / stag:** one leg forward, one back; check hips stay square.

## Balance changes
- Moving the legs changes the centre of mass; the shoulders or hips compensate.
- Practise each shape statically, then transitions.

## Transitions
- Straight to straddle to straight; straight to tuck and back.
- Slow and controlled; the transition is the trick.

## Common errors
- Dropping the hips in tuck: shoulders collapse.
- Arching in straddle: banana shape.

## Verify
- [ ] Each shape held 10 s.
- [ ] Transitions straight-straddle-straight controlled.
