# Chest-to-wall handstand drills

**Goal:** use the wall to train the straight line and build holding endurance before
freestanding work.

## Chest-to-wall (facing the wall)
1. Start in a plank with feet at the wall.
2. Walk the feet up the wall and the hands in until the body is nearly vertical, hands
   10-20 cm from the wall.
3. Only toes touch the wall; body straight, shoulders open.
4. Walk down the same way.
Why: you cannot arch to compensate when facing the wall, so the line is honest.

## Back-to-wall (kick up)
Kick up with back to the wall, heels resting. Good for kick-up practice, but encourages
banana if the hands are far from the wall. Keep hands close.

## Progressions
- Holds: 5 x 20 s to 5 x 60 s chest-to-wall.
- **Toe pulls**: from chest-to-wall, pull the toes off the wall by pressing the fingers
  into the floor; hold balance a few seconds, return.
- **Heel pulls** (back-to-wall): pull heels off by pressing with the palms/heels of hands.
- **Shoulder taps**: shift weight, tap a shoulder with one hand.
- **Wall walks** sideways.

## Gotchas
- Wrist pain: warm up wrists and keep sessions short at first.
- Exiting chest-to-wall: walk down, or cartwheel out; practise the cartwheel bail early.

## Verify
- [ ] 60 s chest-to-wall hold with straight line.
- [ ] 5 toe pulls holding 3 s off the wall.
