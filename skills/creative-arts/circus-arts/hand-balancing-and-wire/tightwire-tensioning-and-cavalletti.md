# Wire tensioning and cavalletti

**Goal:** a tightwire is tensioned high so it barely moves under the walker. Tension
creates large forces on anchors; rigging must be designed.

> Rigging by a qualified rigger. Tensioning a wire stores large energy; a failing anchor
> or a snapping wire can injure people.

## Wire
- Steel wire rope, typically 10-16 mm; terminated with thimbles and rated fittings.

## Anchors and posts
- End posts (A-frames) or anchor points with guys; anchors rated for the horizontal
  load from wire tension plus the walker's weight.
- Tension creates horizontal and vertical forces at the posts; the rigger calculates.

## Tensioning
- Turnbuckle or ratchet at one end; tension set by the rigger to an agreed value.
- Check that the wire is level.

## Cavalletti
- Guy lines from the wire to the floor at intervals along the wire; stop the wire
  swinging side to side. Usually two or more per side.

## Inspection
- Wire for broken strands, kinks, corrosion.
- Posts and anchors for movement.

## Verify
- [ ] Rigger signs off the tension and anchors.
- [ ] Cavalletti in place and tight.
