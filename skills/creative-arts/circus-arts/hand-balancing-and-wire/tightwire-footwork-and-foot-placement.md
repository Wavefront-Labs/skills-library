# Tightwire footwork and foot placement

**Goal:** walking the tightwire depends on placing the foot along the wire from the big
toe to the heel, with the leg turned out slightly.

## Footwear
- Soft leather slippers (wire shoes) with thin soles; let the wire sit in the arch.

## Foot placement
- Place the wire between the big toe and the second toe, along the arch to the heel.
- Leg slightly turned out; weight over the ball of the foot.

## Walking
- Small steps; each foot placed in front of the other.
- Backwards: same placement, feel for the wire with the toes.

## Arms and gaze
- Arms out to the sides for balance; slow movements.
- Eyes on the end of the wire, not on the feet.

## Drills
- Walk a line on the floor, then a low wire (knee height), then higher.

## Verify
- [ ] Walk forward and backward the full length of a low wire.
- [ ] Foot placement consistent.
