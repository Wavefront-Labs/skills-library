# Straight-line handstand alignment

**Goal:** a handstand where wrists, shoulders, hips and ankles stack in one vertical line,
the shape that lets you balance with minimal effort and build every other hand-balancing
skill.

## The shape, bottom to top
- Hands shoulder-width, fingers spread, index fingers forward.
- **Arms locked**, shoulders fully **open** (elbow pits forward), pushing tall - "push
  the floor away".
- **Ribs in**, lower back flat: a slight hollow, not an arch.
- **Glutes squeezed**, pelvis tucked (posterior tilt).
- Legs together, knees locked, feet pointed.
- Head neutral: look at the floor between the hands or slightly ahead.

## Floor drills
- **Hollow body hold** lying on the back, arms overhead: 3 x 30 s.
- **Wall shoulder flexion**: back against wall, arms overhead touching the wall with ribs
  down.
- **Shrug push-ups / pike shrugs**: push tall from the shoulders.
- **Plank with posterior tilt**: learn the pelvis position while horizontal.

## Common faults
- **Banana**: arched lower back, ribs out; cause = closed shoulders or weak hollow.
- **Closed shoulders**: arms angled forward of the head; fix with flexibility and
  pushing tall.
- **Bent hips (piking)**: weak glutes or fear; squeeze and tuck pelvis.
- **Bent arms**: wrist or shoulder weakness; return to wall work.
