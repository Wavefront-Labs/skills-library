# Compression strength for the press

**Goal:** the press depends on pulling the legs close to the torso with the hip flexors
and abs while the hips rise (compression). Stalder and pike presses need even more.

## Drills
- **Seated pike leg lifts:** sitting with legs straight, hands beside the knees, lift
  the heels off the floor; hold 2 s, lower.
- **Pancake lifts:** straddle sit, hands in front, lift both legs.
- **L-sit and V-sit holds:** on the floor or parallettes.
- **Hanging pike compression:** toes to bar, slow.
- **Elevated pike press negatives:** feet on a box.

## Loading
- 3 sessions per week, 3-5 sets of short holds or lifts; compression responds to
  frequency.
- Combine with flexibility work (pike and pancake), which reduces the strength needed.

## Progress tests
- Seated pike lift height and hold time.
- Pancake lift: number of reps.
- L-sit hold time.

## Stalder press
- The stalder press starts from a straddle with hands between the legs (the body
  folded); it needs more compression and a deeper straddle. Build the regular straddle
  press first.

## Verify
- [ ] Monthly compression tests logged.
- [ ] Pike and straddle flexibility work alongside strength.
