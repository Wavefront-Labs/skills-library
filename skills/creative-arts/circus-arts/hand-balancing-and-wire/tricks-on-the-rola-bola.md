# Tricks on the rola bola

**Goal:** add manipulation and body tricks once the stance is automatic. The legs work
continuously; tricks happen above a moving base.

## Juggling
- Cascade with vertical, slightly lower throws; the head stays level. Add clubs or rings
  later.
- Rings and hoops read well from a raised rola bola.

## Turns and jumps
- **Half turn:** small jump, turn 180° in the air, land in the same stance with the
  cylinder centred; drill on a line on the floor first.
- **Jumps over a rope or hoop:** keep the board level at take-off; the cylinder will
  move during flight, so land with knees soft.

## One-leg stand
- Shift weight slowly to one foot near the centre of the board; the free leg
  counterbalances.

## Objects
- Hula hoops on the waist or limbs, spinning plates, balancing an object on the head.

## Practice ladder
1. Trick on the floor.
2. Trick on a balance board with a fixed pivot.
3. Trick on the rola bola with a spotter.

## Verify
- [ ] Each trick 5 times clean on the rola bola before adding to the act.
- [ ] Dropped-prop plan rehearsed.
