# One-arm handstand straddle and flag positions

**Goal:** expand a basic one-arm balance into the shapes that make an act: straddle,
flag and straight.

## Shapes
- **Straddle OAH**: the learning shape. Legs wide lower the centre of mass; small leg
  movements correct balance.
- **Flag OAH**: body leans to the side, legs together or one leg bent, free arm out. The
  hips move further off the hand line and the working shoulder works harder. Visually
  strong.
- **Straight OAH**: legs together vertical; the hardest, smallest base of correction.
- **Tuck / diamond OAH**: knees bent; useful in transitions.

## Transitions
- Straddle to straight: close the legs slowly while keeping the hip over the hand.
- Straddle to flag: lean the body to the free-arm side, legs follow.
- Changing hands: from two-arm, shift to one, back to two, to the other side.
Each transition moves the centre of mass; the working hand corrects in advance.

## Training
- One shape per session block; 3-5 attempts each side.
- Hold criteria for show use: 10 s in rehearsal for a shape you will hold 3-5 s on stage.

## Faults
- Hips drifting during leg closure: close more slowly.
- Bent working arm under fatigue: end the set.

## Verify
- [ ] Straddle OAH 10 s each side.
- [ ] One transition (straddle to flag or straight) held after transition 3 s.
