# Finger and wrist balance correction in handstand

**Goal:** balance the handstand with small hand corrections instead of walking or
bending.

## The controls
- **Overbalancing** (falling towards your back/feet going over): press the **fingertips**
  into the floor ("claw"). This pushes the body back.
- **Underbalancing** (falling back towards the start): press the **heel of the hand**,
  open shoulders a little more.
- Ideal: weight slightly towards the fingers, constantly micro-adjusting.

## Bigger corrections
When fingers cannot save it:
- Overbalance: slight shoulder lean back, then bend the hips (pike) as last resort, or
  pirouette/cartwheel out.
- Underbalance: lean the shoulders forward slightly, or come down.
Aim to need these less over time.

## Drills
- **Wall toe pulls / heel pulls** (see chest-to-wall drills).
- **Kick to balance, count, come down**: 10-20 attempts per session, count seconds.
- **Spotter holds ankles lightly**, releasing for seconds at a time.
- **Finger push-ups / claw holds** in a plank to strengthen fingertip pressure.

## Gotchas
- Wrist bent at more than 90 degrees for long periods aggravates the wrist; keep sessions
  sensible and warm up.
- Students "walking" the handstand never learn finger control; set a no-walk rule for
  balance drills.

## Verify
- [ ] Student can describe which part of the hand corrects which direction.
- [ ] 10 s freestanding with visible finger corrections, no walking.
