# Handstand kick-up and controlled entry

**Goal:** reach the balance point the same way every time, so practice time is spent
balancing, not chasing.

## Lunge kick-up
1. Stand in a lunge, arms up by the ears.
2. Reach long to the floor, hands land a comfortable distance in front.
3. Back leg swings up straight, front leg pushes and follows.
4. **Scissor**: the top leg arrives first and waits; the bottom leg joins.
5. Shoulders stay over the hands; do not let them travel past.

## Calibrating force
- Underkicking: legs never reach vertical; kick a little harder.
- Overkicking: falling over; kick less, and use finger pressure as the top leg arrives.
- Practise at a wall's distance first, then away from the wall.

## Tuck-up entry (more controlled)
1. Squat, hands on the floor shoulder-width.
2. Lean the shoulders forward over the hands until the feet float.
3. Tuck balance, then extend legs to vertical.
This builds the balance point and leads to the press handstand.

## Drills
- **Kick to a "hover"**: kick until the top leg is just past vertical and return,
  10 reps.
- **Tuck holds** (crow to tuck handstand).
- Counted attempts: 20 kick-ups per session, track how many reach balance.

## Verify
- [ ] 7 of 10 kick-ups reach balance without falling over or walking.
- [ ] Tuck-up entry held 3 s.
