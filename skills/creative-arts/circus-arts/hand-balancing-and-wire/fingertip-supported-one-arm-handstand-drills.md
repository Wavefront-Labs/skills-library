# Fingertip-supported one-arm handstand drills

**Goal:** bridge between a weight-shifted straddle and a true one-arm handstand by
reducing support from the free hand step by step.

## Progression
1. **Whole hand light**: free hand flat but carrying little weight.
2. **Five fingertips**: free hand on fingertips only, hold 10 s.
3. **Three fingers**, then **two**, then **one** (often the index or thumb).
4. **Hover**: free hand a few millimetres above the floor, touching only to save.
5. Free hand lifts and moves to the side or hip.
Move on when 3 x 10 s holds are clean at the current step.

## Placement
Place the free hand **further away** from the working hand as you progress - it carries
less weight at a distance and forces the working side to do more. Typical start at
shoulder-width, moving out.

## Session structure
- 3-5 attempts per side per set, 3-4 sets; long rests (1-2 min) between attempts.
- Alternate sides each attempt.
- Video regularly; finger support usually carries more weight than you think.

## Faults
- Pressing hard through the fingers (not really supported by the working hand): check
  by lifting the fingers briefly.
- Holding breath.
- Short-changing the weaker side.

## Verify
- [ ] One-finger support 10 s each side.
- [ ] Hover of 2 s with clean line.
