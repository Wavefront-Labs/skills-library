# Blocks and stacked bases

**Goal:** blocks are stackable bases; a classic act adds or removes blocks under the
hands while in handstand, or balances on a tower of blocks or chairs.

## Blocks
- Wooden or metal blocks, square; stacked in interlocking or flat patterns.
- Blocks must be square and flat; a block that rocks is out.

## Stacking during the act
- The artist shifts weight to one hand while the other moves a block; or an assistant
  adds blocks.
- Practise each stacking step low.

## Stability
- Check each block for rocking; floor level.
- Stacks taller than a set height require spotters.

## Falls plan
- Know which direction you will come down from each height; the stack can tip with you.

## Verify
- [ ] Each block flat and checked.
- [ ] Falls plan rehearsed for every height.
