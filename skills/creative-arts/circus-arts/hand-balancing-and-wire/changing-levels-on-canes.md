# Changing levels on canes

**Goal:** many cane acts move the artist between canes or heights during balances.
Each transition is a skill, practised low first.

## Transitions
- **Step across:** from one cane pair to another, shifting weight to one arm while the
  other hand moves.
- **Height change:** canes of different heights; moving from low to high changes the
  angle of the shoulders.
- **One-arm transitions:** a one-arm handstand while the other hand moves to a new
  block.

## Practice
- On floor blocks first, then low canes, then performance height.
- Spotter at higher heights.

## Faults
- Moving the hand before the weight is fully shifted.
- Looking at the new cane: head movement shifts the balance.

## Verify
- [ ] Each transition clean on floor blocks.
- [ ] Spotted at height before unspotted.
