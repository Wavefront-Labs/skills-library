# Rola bola mount and basic stance

**Goal:** stand on a board balanced across a cylinder. The trick is to move the board
with the knees while the upper body stays still.

## Equipment and surface
- Board with a grippy top and a strip or stops under the ends so the cylinder cannot
  roll out; cylinder with rubber or grippy surface.
- Flat, level, non-slip floor (rubber mat on stage).

## Mount
1. Cylinder under the centre of the board; one end of the board on the floor.
2. Step on the **low end** first, foot near the end.
3. Place the second foot on the high end, then press it down so the board levels over
   the cylinder; spotter holds both hands in front.
4. The cylinder rolls toward the high side; the knees let it.

## Stance
- Feet near the board ends, shoulder-width or wider; knees soft; hips stay over the
  centre of the cylinder while the board moves.
- Eyes forward on a fixed point; arms out or relaxed.

## Drills
- Hold 10 s with a spotter, then one hand, then free.
- Rock side to side deliberately to learn the range.

## Faults
- Hips follow the board: whole body sways off.
- Standing on the toes: ankles lock.

## Verify
- [ ] Unassisted mount and 30 s stand.
- [ ] Step-off practised both sides.
