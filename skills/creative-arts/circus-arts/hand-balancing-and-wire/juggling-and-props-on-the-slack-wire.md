# Juggling and props on the slack wire

**Goal:** juggling while standing or swinging on the slack wire is a traditional act. The
challenge is keeping the throws steady while the legs absorb the wire's movement.

## Pattern choice
- Start with a 3-ball cascade or 3 clubs singles; keep throws lower and more vertical
  than on the floor. Rings read well and fly straight.
- Avoid patterns with large plane changes until the base is quiet.

## Practice ladder
1. Juggle standing on one foot on the floor.
2. Juggle on a balance board or rola bola.
3. Juggle on the slack wire at low height, still.
4. Juggle while the wire swings gently, throws timed to the ends of the swing.

## Object balancing
- Balancing a club or plate on the forehead or chin while on the wire; the eyes stay on
  the object, so wire balance must be automatic first.
- Spinning plates or hoops on the limbs are lower-attention options.

## Drops
- A dropped object falls under the wire; an assistant returns it or a spare is on the
  wire's end post. Never step off to retrieve mid-act.

## Verify
- [ ] 60 s cascade still on the wire before trying it swinging.
- [ ] Spare props placed where they can be reached from the wire.
