# Mounting and standing on the slack wire

**Goal:** stand on a wire that hangs in a curve and swings freely. Unlike tightwire,
balance comes from moving the wire under the body; the body stays quiet above it.

> Coach-led for first sessions; a spotter stands beside the wire offering a hand.

## Rig for learning
- Lowest point of the sag at about knee height over a mat; wire long enough that the
  ends are well away from the learner.
- Some schools learn on a webbing slackline first (similar feel, wider foot).

## Mount (centre mount)
1. Stand beside the wire at the low point, facing along it or across it as taught.
2. Place one foot on the wire at the low point, foot along the wire, wire under the
   ball of the foot to the heel.
3. Hold the spotter's hand or a pole; push down until the wire is loaded and still.
4. Stand up onto that leg in one smooth motion; the free leg hangs as a counterweight.

## First stand
- Knees soft, hips square, arms high and wide; eyes on a fixed point at the end of the
  wire or ahead, not down.
- Let the wire wander under the foot; correct with the ankle and knee, not by throwing
  the arms.
- Progress: one foot 10 s each side, then bring the second foot on beside or in front.

## Faults
- Stiff knee: the wire throws the body.
- Leaning to chase the wire: fall to that side.
- Looking at the feet.

## Verify
- [ ] Unaided mount and 30 s two-foot stand at low height.
- [ ] Controlled step-off to the side on command.
