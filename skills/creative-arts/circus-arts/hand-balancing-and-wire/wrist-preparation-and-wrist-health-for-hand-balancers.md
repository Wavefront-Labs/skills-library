# Wrist preparation and wrist health for hand balancers

**Goal:** wrists that tolerate hours of handstands a week for years.

## 5-minute warm-up (every session)
1. Wrist circles both ways, 10 each.
2. On all fours: rock forward and back with fingers forward, then sideways, then
   fingers pointing back, then on the backs of the hands (gently). 10 each.
3. Finger extensions with a rubber band, 20.
4. Palm lifts: on all fours, lift the palms keeping fingers down, 10.
5. A few easy handstands against the wall.

## Strength and mobility (2-3 x week)
- Wrist curls and reverse curls with light weight, 3 x 15.
- Weighted wrist extension stretch in a kneeling position.
- Forearm pronation/supination with a hammer.
- Fingertip planks.

## Load management
- Increase weekly handstand time gradually (rough rule of thumb: no more than ~10-20%
  more per week).
- Split long sessions into blocks with breaks.
- Use parallettes or canes to vary wrist angle if one position hurts.

## Pain patterns
- General ache after a hard session that fades by next day: normal.
- **Pain on the little-finger side** (TFCC area) or with twisting: rest and refer if it
  persists.
- **Pain on the back of the wrist** when loading in extension: reduce volume, use canes
  or fists, refer if persistent.
- Young athletes: persistent wrist pain can indicate growth plate stress; always refer.

## Verify
- [ ] Warm-up done before every session.
- [ ] Weekly handstand volume logged.
- [ ] Pain over a week is referred, not trained through.
