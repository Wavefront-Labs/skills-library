# Tricks on the wire (turns, kneel, jumps)

**Goal:** add tricks to wire walking. Each trick is learned low with a spotter before
height.

> Coach-led. A spotter walks alongside the wire for new tricks.

## Turns
- Pivot on the balls of both feet, 180°; arms balance.
- Practise on a line on the floor first.

## Kneeling and sitting
- Kneel by lowering one knee onto the wire behind the front foot.
- Sitting: lower to sit on the wire, legs either side or in front.

## Jumps
- Small vertical jumps landing on the wire in the same placement; needs a very tight
  wire.

## Splits
- From kneeling, extend one leg along the wire.

## Falls plan
- Step off to the side, landing on both feet; mats below.

## Verify
- [ ] Each trick clean on a low wire.
- [ ] Falls plan rehearsed.
