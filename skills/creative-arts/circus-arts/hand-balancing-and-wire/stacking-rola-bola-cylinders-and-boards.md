# Stacking cylinders and boards

**Goal:** classic rola bola acts build towers of alternating cylinders and boards, or
stand on rola bolas balanced on tables. Each extra level adds a degree of freedom and
height.

> Coach-led. Spotters on every new configuration, mats around, and only after the single
> rola bola is completely automatic.

## Configurations
- **Two-level parallel:** cylinder, board, cylinder, board; both cylinders rolling the
  same direction.
- **Crossed:** the upper cylinder at 90° to the lower; balance in two directions at once.
- **Mixed objects:** cylinders on a pipe, on a ball, on a table: harder and higher.

## Building the stack
- Built on the floor by the performer or an assistant, each level checked flat.
- Mount like a single rola bola onto the top board, with the assistant stabilising the
  lower levels until the performer says "go".

## Equipment checks
- Board stops intact, cylinder surfaces clean (no talc, sweat or haze residue).
- Boards free of cracks; the top board's grip surface not worn smooth.

## Falls plan
- Lower levels shoot out sideways when a stack fails; spotters stand where they can
  catch the performer, not where the equipment flies.
- The performer steps or jumps down to the side, landing two-footed.

## Verify
- [ ] Each configuration held 10 s with a spotter before attempting alone.
- [ ] Assistant and spotter positions written into the act notes.
