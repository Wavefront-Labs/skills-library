# Static poses in and on the lyra

**Goal:** build a library of secure poses that read clearly from the audience, each
with known contact points and a known exit.

> Coach-led. Each new pose is held at low height first, coach close, before any spin.

## Core poses and contact points
- **Bar sit / bar stand:** sitting on or standing on the bottom bar holding the sides.
  Base for most entries.
- **Man in the moon:** body inside the hoop, back against one side, feet against the
  opposite side; tension through the legs pins the body. Check feet are flat and legs
  straight before letting go with one hand.
- **Mermaid:** one hip/thigh hooked over the bottom bar, opposite hand on the upper hoop,
  body arched outside. Contact: back of the knee, hand.
- **Gazelle / back-balance variations:** contact through the back of the knee and the
  bar; teach only after hocks hang is solid.
- **Standing in the hoop:** feet on the bottom bar, hands on the hoop or tabs, never on
  the swivel.

## Building each pose
1. Name the contact points out loud before entering.
2. Enter with both hands on; find the pose; remove one hand only when contacts are
   loaded.
3. Hold 3-5 s, exit the same way you entered.

## Coaching points
- Tension in every limb even in a "relaxed" pose; slack limbs slide.
- Use skin on contact points (backs of knees, inner thighs); costume can slip.
- Shape is judged from the audience side: check silhouettes from the front.

## Faults
- Hooking at the calf instead of the knee pit.
- Arching for the shape before contacts are secure.
- Hands near the tabs/swivel: pinch and snag hazard.

## Verify
- [ ] Student lists contacts and exit for each pose.
- [ ] Each pose held 5 s at low height both sides before spinning.
