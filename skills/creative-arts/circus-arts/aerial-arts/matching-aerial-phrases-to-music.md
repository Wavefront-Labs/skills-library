# Matching aerial phrases to music

**Goal:** aerial movement has fixed durations (climbs, wraps) that cannot be rushed.
Musicality means placing the flexible moments to the music and leaving slack around the
fixed ones.

## Map the music
1. Count the track in 8s; mark phrases, accents, builds and quiet sections.
2. Mark big accents where a drop, a reveal or a pose could land.

## Map the act
1. Time each figure and wrap with a stopwatch, several runs; note the range.
2. Mark which moments are fixed (climb, wrap) and which can stretch (poses, slow
   lines, reaches).

## Fit them together
- Put drops on accents but start the drop's wrap early enough; the drop must not be
  rushed to hit the beat.
- Place climbs in sections with a steady pulse, not a big accent.
- Leave a couple of counts of slack before each accent; a pose can be held longer
  if you are early.
- If the act and track disagree, edit the music, not the safety of the wrap.

## Rehearsal
- Rehearse to the final edit at the final tempo; a new edit changes everything.
- Plan what happens if you are behind: which pose shortens, which section can be cut.

## Verify
- [ ] Count sheet with figures marked against bars.
- [ ] Three full runs hitting key accents without rushing a wrap.
