# Training new skills on a lowered apparatus

**Goal:** learn new shapes, beats and transitions where a coach can reach you and a fall
is short, then move them up only when they are reliable.

## Height guide
- **Hoop / static trapeze**: bar at the student's chest to head height for first mounts;
  standing reach for spotting hangs and balances.
- **Silks**: start wraps with feet just above the mat (see drop progression skill).
- **Straps**: hand loops at a height where feet can touch the floor for first rolls.
A coach should be able to reach the student's hips without stretching.

## Rigging for adjustable height
- A rated rope-and-pulley or chain-hoist system lets the coach change height safely
  between skills. Ask the rigger; never improvise with knots in a span set.
- Daisy-chain lengths or adjustable span sets give fixed height steps.
- Re-check the chain after every height change.

## Moving to full height
All of:
- Skill done cleanly 3 times in 2 separate sessions at low height.
- Student can enter and exit the skill and has a bail-out.
- No spotting needed for the last 3 reps.
- Endurance: the skill can be done after a full warm-up and some fatigue.

## Gotchas
- Low height brings the floor close for swinging skills: beats on a low hoop can drive
  feet into the mat. Adjust skill or height.
- Students rush height for social media. Keep the gates explicit.

## Verify
- [ ] Each new skill has a recorded low-height sign-off before going up.
- [ ] Height change procedure written and rigger-approved.
