# Rope climbs and foot locks on corde lisse

**Goal:** climb and rest on a single, thicker, stiffer rope. The rope does not wrap like
fabric, so foot locks bite differently and burns come faster.

> Coach-led. Set a ceiling mark on the rope; raise it only after controlled descents.

## Rope basics
- Corde lisse is a single cotton or synthetic rope, often 25-40 mm, hung from a rated
  point, sometimes with a swivel. Stiffer and heavier than silks; burns more easily.

## Foot locks
- **J-hook (S-wrap):** rope runs down the outside of one leg, under that foot; the
  other foot steps on the rope on top of the first foot, pinching it. Fast and
  common.
- **Classic footlock (wrap):** one turn of rope around the foot, other foot clamps.
  Holds better for poses.

## Climbing cycle
1. Hands high, pull and lift knees above hips.
2. Set the lock, stand, reach, repeat.
3. Descend by reversing: release lock, hands lower, re-lock. No sliding.

## Coaching points
- Long sleeves or leggings at first for burns on shins and inner thighs; skin contact
  is added for grip as tolerance grows.
- Rope swings more than fabric when kicked; keep the knees in line with the rope.

## Faults
- Clamp too loose: rope slips through the feet.
- Sliding down with the hands: friction burns.

## Verify
- [ ] Climb and reverse-climb to the ceiling mark with both foot locks.
- [ ] Stand hands-free in a footlock for 10 s.
