# Spanish web hand loop and spinning

**Goal:** the Spanish web is a cotton-covered rope with a hand or foot loop near the top;
a ground spinner turns the rope so the performer spins in poses hung from the loop.

> Coach-led only. Spinning from one wrist puts high load on the hand, wrist and
> shoulder; volume is built over months.

## Loop setup
- Loop sized so the wrist sits in it with the hand closed around the rope above; too
  big and the hand slips through, too small and it cuts circulation.
- Padding inside the loop; artists often wrap the wrist as well.
- Loop stitching and the loop-to-rope join are inspected every session.

## Entering the loop
1. Climb to the loop, set a foot lock to rest.
2. Hand through the loop, rotate the wrist to tighten, grip above.
3. Load the loop before releasing the foot lock.

## Spinning
- The spinner turns the rope from the floor; speed builds slowly. Poses in the loop
  (arabesques, one-arm hangs) use body shape to control speed: limbs in speeds up.
- Agreed calls between performer and spinner: faster, hold, slow, stop.

## Coaching points
- Hanging shoulder active throughout.
- Short spins at first, 10-20 s; dizziness tolerance builds over weeks.

## Stop signs
- Hand numbness or colour change, wrist pain, nausea.

## Verify
- [ ] Performer loads and unloads the loop from a foot lock without help.
- [ ] Spinner and performer rehearsed the stop call.
