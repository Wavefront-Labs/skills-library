# Russian and French climbs on silks

**Goal:** teach a climb that uses the legs, so the arms are not spent before the first
figure. Both climbs repeat one cycle: hands high, knees up, wrap with the feet, stand,
reach.

> Coach-led. Climbs are the gateway to height; the coach sets a ceiling (tape mark on the
> fabric) and raises it only when the gates below are met.

## Russian climb (one fabric pole, or both held together)
1. Hands high, pull, lift knees to hip height or above.
2. The fabric runs down the **outside of one shin**; the other foot's instep comes under
   the fabric and lifts it so it crosses over the top of the first foot.
3. Pinch the fabric between the instep of the top foot and the arch/heel of the bottom
   foot; feet stay **together and flexed**.
4. Stand up through the legs, hands walk up, repeat.

## French climb
- The fabric is wrapped around one foot (outside to inside over the instep) and the
  other foot steps on the wrap to clamp it. Faster for some bodies, harder on the
  ankle. Teach both; let the student pick a main climb, but keep both working sides.

## Coaching points
- Knees reach **above the hips** before wrapping; a low wrap gives a tiny step and
  burns the arms.
- Arms stay bent-to-straight with active shoulders; no dead hang between steps.
- Look up at the hands, not down at the feet once the wrap is learned.
- Climb down by reversing the cycle; never slide down the fabric (friction burns).

## Faults
- Feet separate: the clamp opens and the student slips a step.
- Wrapping across the toes instead of the instep: the fabric rolls off.
- Pulling with the arms only: stops working after three steps.

## Readiness gates
- [ ] 3 climb cycles each side at low height, controlled descent by reversing.
- [ ] Can stop mid-climb and hold 10 s standing in the wrap, arms bent.
- [ ] Only then raise the climb ceiling mark.
