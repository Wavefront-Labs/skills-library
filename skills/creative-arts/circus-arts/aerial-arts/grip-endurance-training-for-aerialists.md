# Grip endurance training for aerialists

**Goal:** enough grip endurance that the last minute of a 5-minute act is as safe as the
first. Grip fails suddenly; training must push the failure point well beyond show length.

## Specificity
Train on the grip you perform with: fabric (wide, soft), rope (round, thick), hoop bar
(25 mm steel). Bar hangs alone transfer poorly to fabric.

## Session menu
- **Fabric or towel hangs**: 3-5 x max hold minus 5 s, 2 min rest.
- **Climb ladder**: climb 1, 2, 3, 2, 1 lengths with no rest at top, controlled descent.
- **Density set**: as many climbs as possible in 10 min with self-chosen rests; track the
  number weekly.
- **Grip changes**: hang and shift hand positions (open, closed, one hand) for 60 s.
- **Antagonists**: finger extensions with a rubber band 3 x 25 to protect elbows.

## Load management
- 2-3 grip-focused sessions a week, 48 h between hard ones.
- Elbow pain on the inside (medial epicondyle) or outside: reduce volume, not just
  intensity.

## In class and in the act
- Hardest skills first in the session, conditioning last.
- Build rests into choreography: footlocks, hip keys, sits on the hoop, where the hands
  can shake out.
- Rosin and grip aids: venue policy; some fabric manufacturers advise against them.

## Verify
- [ ] Full act run-through followed by 2 extra climbs without grip failure.
- [ ] Density number trending up over 6 weeks.
- [ ] No unmanaged elbow pain.
