# Teaching silks drops low and raising height

**Goal:** a progression where the first time a student feels a drop, the worst case is
landing on a mat from a short height.

## Prerequisites
- Wrap is learned and narrated without errors (see wrap-check skill).
- Student can hold the starting position for 10 s without fatigue.
- Rest/abort positions are known.

## Stages
1. **Floor walk-through**: wrap made with feet on the floor; coach slowly pulls the
   fabric to show the unroll direction and where the body ends up.
2. **Hip-height release**: wrap made with feet just off a thick mat. Coach stands at
   the hips, outside the travel line. Release at half speed by letting the hands slide.
3. **Chest height**, 2-3 clean reps.
4. **Head height**, 2-3 clean reps.
5. **Full height**, only in a later session.
Increase height **between sessions**, not within the session the drop was first done.

## Gates (all must pass before the next stage)
- End position correct and controlled every rep.
- Student can describe what the fabric did.
- No new pain, no fabric burns in unexpected places.
- Coach confident in student's abort under fatigue.

## Calculating fabric needed
A drop consumes fabric. Before going high, check the end position will still be above
the mat with margin: measure the drop length at low height (mark the fabric with tape)
and add it to the release height.

## Gotchas
- Drops that unroll sideways (star drop, some saltos) travel; mat the whole path.
- Different fabric stretch between studio and venue changes drop length - re-measure.

## Verify
- [ ] Drop recorded at each height with date in the training log.
- [ ] Measured drop length plus margin fits the performance height.
