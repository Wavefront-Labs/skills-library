# Beats and dynamic transitions on lyra

**Goal:** add swinging beats, drops and rolls on the hoop safely. Dynamic moves multiply
the load on the rigging and on the shoulders; both must be planned for.

> Coach-led only. Dynamic lyra moves are learned on a low hoop over thick mats, with the
> rigger informed of the dynamic loads.

## Before any dynamic move
- Rigger has rated the point for dynamic aerial loads (peak loads several times body
  weight are normal on drops and beats).
- Every component of the static version is solid: the catch position must be a pose
  the student already holds confidently.
- Mats cover the swing or drop path.

## Beats
- A beat is a swing generated from a hang (hands, or hocks) on the bottom bar. Build:
  small swing, full extension, front and back of the swing, then the trick at the end
  of the swing.
- Body hollow-arch drives the swing; arms stay active.

## Drops
- Drops move from one contact to another (e.g. from bar sit to hocks hang). Teach the
  slow version first (lower through the movement with the hands), then speed up.
- The catch contact (knee pits, ankles) must be in position before the hands release.

## Progression
1. Slow motion with hands on.
2. Small dynamic with coach spot at low height.
3. Full speed at low height.
4. Raise height only with consistent catches.

## Stop rules
- Any missed catch at low height: back a step.
- Shoulder pain or clicking: stop the session.

## Verify
- [ ] 5 consecutive clean catches at low height.
- [ ] Rigger told about the dynamic element before it goes in the act.
