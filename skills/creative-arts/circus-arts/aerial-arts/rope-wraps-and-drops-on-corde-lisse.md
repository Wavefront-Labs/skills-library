# Rope wraps and drops on corde lisse

**Goal:** perform wraps and drops on a single rope. A rope drop has one line to unroll
around instead of two fabric poles, so wraps grip harder and burns are worse.

> Coach-led only. Every rope drop is learned low over mats with the coach checking the
> wrap before each release.

## How rope differs from silks
- One pole only: wraps cross themselves; a wrong crossing can lock instead of unroll.
- Higher friction and stiffness: the rope decelerates the body faster at the end of
  the drop (harder catch) and burns exposed skin.
- Rope swings and twists as it unrolls; mats must cover a wider area.

## Wrap check (before every release)
- Name every wrap in order, top to bottom, and where the rope exits.
- Confirm the catch wrap (often the waist or a foot lock) is under the body.
- Coach sees the wrap from the side; any doubt, unwrap and rebuild.

## Progression
1. Build the wrap at standing height and slowly unroll by lowering, hands on.
2. Release from just above the floor.
3. Raise in small steps once the unroll path is consistent.

## Coaching points
- Clothing covers the skin where the rope runs during the drop; skin where you grip.
- Legs and arms tight; loose limbs catch the rope and spin.

## Faults
- Crossed wrap that locks: student stops suddenly, high load on the body.
- Rope around the neck at any point: stop immediately and rebuild with the coach.

## Verify
- [ ] Student narrates the wrap correctly 5 times running.
- [ ] Clean drops at low height before the coach raises it.
