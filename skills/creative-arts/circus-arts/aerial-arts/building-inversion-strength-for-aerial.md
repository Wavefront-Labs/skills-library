# Building inversion strength for aerial

**Goal:** an inversion (hips over head while hanging) done with control and active
shoulders, the gateway skill for silks, hoop, trapeze and straps.

## Progression ladder
Advance when the current step is clean for 3 sessions.
1. **Hollow body hold** on the floor 3 x 30 s (lower back flat).
2. **Hanging knee raises** 3 x 10, active shoulders, no swing.
3. **Hanging tuck to 90 degrees** with a 2 s hold.
4. **Hanging straight-leg raise** to 90 degrees, then **toes-to-bar** 3 x 5.
5. **Tuck inversion on a low bar** (feet can touch floor), slow both ways.
6. **Straddle inversion** with a 3 s descent.
7. Inversion on the apparatus from standing, then from a climb, then in the air.

## Compression accessories
- Seated pike pulses (hands beside knees, lift heels) 3 x 15.
- Pancake stretch: straddle flexibility makes the straddle-up far cheaper.
- Negative inversions: start inverted (spotted) and lower slowly.

## Technique points
- Lead with the hips going backwards over the hands, not the feet going up.
- Arms stay slightly bent and pulling; straight-arm inversion comes later.
- Exhale on the lift.

## Common faults
- Kicking with a swing: unsafe at height, the body loses the apparatus line.
- Arching the back at the top: strain on the lumbar spine; cue "ribs in".
- Shrugged shoulders: return to step 2.

## Verify
- [ ] 5 strict toes-to-bar, no swing.
- [ ] Straddle inversion on a low bar up and down in control, 3 reps.
- [ ] Coach signs off before first inversion in the air.
