# Salto and back-balance drops on silks

**Goal:** understand the mechanics and coaching checkpoints of rotating drops, so a
coach can progress a student who already has reliable static wraps.

> Coach-led only. Rotating drops put the head below the hips at speed. Learn each
> specific wrap from a qualified coach; this file is the checklist, not the recipe.

## Shared mechanics
- **Back-balance drops**: the fabric runs across the back of the hips; the body rotates
  backwards around it and the fabric unrolls from the waist. Control comes from the
  hands on the poles until release and from a tight hollow through the rotation.
- **Salto drops**: a forward or backward roll around the fabric where a wrap around the
  waist or thigh catches the end. The catch is the critical part: wrong side = no catch.
- In both, the **final wrap** (what catches) is set *before* the rotation. Identify it.

## What the coach watches
1. Which pole is in front at the hip before release.
2. Hands release together or in the planned order.
3. Body shape through rotation (tight tuck or hollow; loose shapes slow the unroll and
   change the catch point).
4. Head position: chin neutral, not thrown back.

## Common faults
- Releasing one hand early: body twists and the catch wrap moves.
- Opening the body early: extra fabric slides through, catch is lower than expected.
- Arching in a back-balance drop: lower back strain.

## Stop conditions
- Any unexpected catch position, even if "fine".
- Student cannot say which wrap catches.
- Fabric burn on bare skin in a new place: wrap path changed.

## Verify
- [ ] Student names the catching wrap before every attempt.
- [ ] Low-height reps consistent before raising height (see progression skill).
