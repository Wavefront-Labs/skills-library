# Star drop and hip-key drops

**Goal:** coach drops that start from a hip key or star position, which travel sideways
and rely on the hip key being correctly built.

> Coach-led only. Learn the wrap from a coach; use this as the safety checklist.

## Entry checks
- The **hip key** must be complete: fabric crosses the hips the planned way, the
  correct pole is between the legs. A half-built hip key looks right from the front.
- For the star (open sideways position held by a hand and a foot), confirm which pole
  the body will roll off and where it catches.
- Feel the weight shift: in a correct hip key the fabric takes body weight when the
  hands loosen slightly.

## Travel and matting
Hip-key and star drops roll the body **sideways** around the fabric. The landing
position is offset from the apparatus line; mats must extend in that direction. Mark the
travel direction on the floor in training.

## Coaching points
- Release smoothly; a jerked release adds spin and changes the catch.
- Keep legs together through the roll unless the drop requires otherwise.
- Look along the fabric, not at the floor.

## Faults
- Hip key built on the wrong side: the drop unrolls the wrong way or does not catch.
- Rolling too early before full release: student ends tangled at the waist.
- Fabric across the neck in the star entry: stop immediately.

## Verify
- [ ] Student can build the hip key both sides and identify each pole by colour or tape.
- [ ] Mats cover the measured lateral travel plus margin.
