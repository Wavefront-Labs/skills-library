# Aerial warm-up protocol

**Goal:** arrive at the first climb warm, with shoulders, wrists, core and grip switched
on, in 15-20 minutes. Uses the RAMP structure: Raise, Activate, Mobilise, Potentiate.

## The sequence
1. **Raise (3-4 min)**: skipping, jogging, jumping jacks, until lightly sweating.
2. **Mobilise (4-5 min)**: arm circles both ways, thoracic rotations, cat-cow, hip
   circles, lunges with a twist, wrist circles and wrist rocks on all fours, neck nods.
3. **Activate (4-5 min)**:
   - Scapular push-ups and scapular pull-ups x 10 each.
   - Band pull-aparts and external rotations x 15.
   - Hollow and arch holds 20 s each.
   - Active hang 20 s.
4. **Potentiate (3-5 min)** on the apparatus at low height: two climbs, a floor
   inversion, the first few shapes of today's work at half effort.

## Variants
- **Pre-show**: same, then a mark-through of the act's tricky transitions at floor
  height. Time it to finish no more than ~15 minutes before going on; re-warm if longer.
- **Cold venue / tent**: double the Raise, keep layers on until the last minute, and add
  a second short potentiation just before the entrance.

## Gotchas
- Long static stretches belong after training, not here.
- Skipping wrist prep before hoop and trapeze is a common source of wrist pain.
- Warm-ups get skipped on tour; build a 5-minute emergency version and do at least that.

## Verify
- [ ] Light sweat, shoulders set easily, grip feels "awake" before the first climb.
- [ ] Warm-up logged in training diary for the week.
