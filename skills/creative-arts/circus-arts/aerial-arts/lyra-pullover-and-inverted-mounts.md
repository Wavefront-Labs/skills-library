# Lyra pullover and inverted mounts

**Goal:** get from the floor into the hoop under control. The mount sets the tone of the
act; a struggling mount reads as a struggling act.

> Coach-led. Learn mounts with the hoop low (bottom bar at hip to chest height) and the
> swivel locked or a non-spin rig before adding spin.

## Hoop setup
- Bottom bar height: start at hip height for pullovers, raise to head height for the
  inverted mount later.
- Single-point (spins) vs two-point (more stable) rigging changes the feel; learn on
  two-point or a damped swivel.

## Pullover mount (low hoop)
1. Hands on the bottom bar, shoulder width, overhand grip.
2. Pull the bar to the hips, tuck knees to chest, rotate backwards over the bar.
3. Finish sitting on the bar with hands on the sides of the hoop.

## Inverted (straddle) mount
1. Hands on the bottom bar, hoop above head height.
2. Invert to a tuck or straddle under the hoop (as on silks), hips above hands.
3. Hook one or both knees over the bar, or bring the legs through to sit.
4. Release one hand only after a knee is hooked.

## Coaching points
- Active shoulders throughout; hanging passively before the invert strains the joint.
- Hoop will swing on the pull; the coach steadies it until the student can control.
- Feet never touch the hoop tabs or the rigging point.

## Faults
- Kicking the legs through: the hoop swings away and the student loses the bar.
- Knee hook behind the bar only at the calf: slips off. Hook at the back of the knee.

## Verify
- [ ] 3 clean inverted mounts with no swing at low height.
- [ ] Coach-steadied mount before mounting on a free swivel.
