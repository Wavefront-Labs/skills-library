# Straps conditioning and shoulder load

**Goal:** straps is one of the highest shoulder-load apparatus in circus. Training is
planned around how much the shoulder can absorb, not how much the artist wants to try.

## Weekly structure (example for a training artist)
- 3 apparatus sessions, separated by a day where possible.
- 2 strength sessions off-apparatus: pull, push, core, scapular control.
- Prehab every session (10 min): external rotation with band, scapular pull-ups,
  Y-T-W raises, wrist mobility.

## Volume control
- Count one-arm holds and dynamic elements per session and per week.
- Increase weekly volume by small steps (roughly 10% is a common guide), not jumps.
- Every 4th to 6th week: a lighter (deload) week.

## Before an act enters the season
- Full act run-throughs build in frequency gradually; do not go from rehearsal to two
  shows a day without a build-up.

## Warning signs (see a physio)
- Shoulder pain that persists into the next day.
- Night pain, loss of range, clicking with pain.
- Tingling or weakness in the hand.

## Faults
- Training straps hard on consecutive days.
- Skipping prehab once the act is "done".

## Verify
- [ ] Weekly load log kept with holds counted.
- [ ] Planned deload weeks in the calendar.
