# Rolls and dislocates on straps

**Goal:** rolls (rotating the body around the line of the straps) and dislocates
(rotating the body through the arms while the hands stay in the straps) give straps its
fluid, rotating look. Both rely on shoulder mobility built over a long time.

> Coach-led only. A dislocate is not a dislocation; forcing it can become one. These
> skills are taught to performers with established straps training.

## Mobility prerequisites
- Wide-grip stick or band "shoulder dislocate" drill with control, gradually narrowing
  grip over months.
- German hang and skin-the-cat on rings or bar with control both ways.
- No history of shoulder instability without physio clearance.

## Progression
1. Skin-the-cat on low straps, feet near the floor, slow.
2. Dislocate with feet supported (straps low), coach guiding the hips.
3. Small swing dislocate, then full.
4. Rolls: rotate around the body's long axis with one hand rewrapping; practise the
   rewrap on the floor first.

## Coaching points
- Arms wide; narrowing the arms increases shoulder strain.
- Hollow body through the rotation; the hips lead.
- Never force range; the shoulder must move through range it already has on the floor.

## Faults
- Bent arms through the rotation: elbow and shoulder strain.
- Fast dislocate before slow is clean.

## Stop signs
- Clicking with pain, any feeling of instability, pins and needles.

## Verify
- [ ] Slow dislocate controlled both ways at low height.
- [ ] Physio check for anyone with prior shoulder injury.
