# Single and double footlocks

**Goal:** a footlock is the silks rest position and the base for many figures. A
correct one lets the student stand hands-free; a wrong one unwraps under load.

> Coach-led. Check every new footlock with the student standing at low height before any
> hands-free work.

## Single footlock
1. From a climb or held in the air, fabric runs down the **inside** of the working leg.
2. Take the fabric under and around the working foot, bringing it from inside to
   outside across the top of the foot (one wrap around the ankle/instep).
3. Step the other foot onto the wrap and stand: the standing foot clamps the tail
   against the working foot.
4. Test by loosening the hands slightly; weight should sit on the feet.

## Double footlock
- From a single footlock, take the tail around the same foot a second time (repeat the
  wrap direction), then stand on it again. Holds better for long poses and is the
  usual base for hands-free figures.
- Release: unwrap the tail with the free foot or hand, in reverse order.

## Coaching points
- Wrap direction must be consistent; wrapping the opposite way for the second turn
  undoes the first.
- The wrap sits on the **instep**, not the toes or the bony ankle.
- Stand straight-legged into the lock; a bent standing leg lets the tail slip.

## Faults
- Wrap around the toes: rolls off when the foot points.
- Tail not under the standing foot: lock carries no load.
- Fabric twisted between the poles above: the lock tightens unevenly.

## Verify
- [ ] Student stands hands-free in a double footlock for 10 s at knee height.
- [ ] Builds both feet; can say which way the wrap travels each side.
