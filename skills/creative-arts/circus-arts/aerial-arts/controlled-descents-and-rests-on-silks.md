# Controlled descents and rests on silks

**Goal:** every student can come down safely from any height they climb to, and can
rest without letting go. A planned exit is part of every sequence.

> Coach-led. No student climbs higher than they can descend controlled when tired.

## Descents
1. **Reverse climb:** release the foot wrap, lower hands, re-wrap lower. Slow and the
   safest; the default when fatigued.
2. **Hand-over-hand with a footlock slide:** feet in a loose wrap acting as a brake,
   hands lowering alternately. Teach only after the reverse climb.
3. Never slide with the fabric running through bare hands or skin; friction burns
   happen in under a second.

## Rest positions
- **Double footlock stand:** hands free or one hand on the fabric.
- **Hip key sit** (once learned): weight on the hip key, legs relaxed.
- **Thigh wrap / waist wrap:** for long pieces, a known wrap that takes weight off
  the arms.

## Fatigue rules
- When grip fades (shaking forearms, opening fingers) the student descends
  immediately using the reverse climb; the coach calls it if the student doesn't.
- In choreography, plan a rest every 45-60 s of hanging work.

## Faults
- Descending faster as the floor approaches: the last metre is where hands give out.
- Holding breath on the way down.
- Letting the tail swing into the face during descent.

## Verify
- [ ] Reverse climb from the working height after a full session.
- [ ] Rest in a footlock hands-free 20 s before every hands-free figure session.
