# Working the web spinner from the ground

**Goal:** the person on the ground (spinner, sitter) controls the speed of the web and is
the performer's first safety observer. A bad spinner can injure a good aerialist.

## Position and grip
- Stand at the base of the rope; hold the tail at a height that lets you walk a circle
  with the rope slightly angled. Keep the tail clear of your feet and any tripping
  hazard.
- Holding the tail tensioned also steadies the rope for climbs and entries.

## Spinning
1. Start slowly by walking a small circle; build speed by walking faster and widening
   the circle a little.
2. Hold speed by matching the rope's rotation.
3. Slow by reducing the circle and walking more slowly; never stop the rope dead.

## Calls (agree before every rehearsal)
- Performer to spinner: "more", "hold", "slow", "stop".
- Spinner watches for the performer's non-verbal stop sign (e.g. a tap or open hand)
  and acts on it immediately.

## Watching for distress
- Loss of shape, eyes closed, grip shifting, or a long time without breathing.
- On any doubt, slow and stop, then ask.

## Faults
- Stopping abruptly: whip load on the performer's wrist and shoulder.
- Spinning while the performer is still setting a lock.
- Watching the audience instead of the performer.

## Verify
- [ ] Spinner runs a full act sequence with the performer calling changes.
- [ ] Controlled stop from top speed in a rehearsal.
