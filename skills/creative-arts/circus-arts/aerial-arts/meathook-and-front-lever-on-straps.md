# Meathook and front lever shapes on straps

**Goal:** the meathook (hanging from one wrist, body horizontal or angled sideways) and
the front lever are signature straps strength shapes. They are built through
regressions over months, not attempted cold.

> Coach-led. These shapes put large loads through one shoulder; strength gates come
> before attempts.

## Strength prerequisites (typical coaching gates)
- Strict pull-ups x8-10, controlled negatives.
- Front lever tuck hold 10-15 s on a bar.
- Hanging leg raises, side planks and oblique work for the meathook.

## Front lever progression
1. Tuck lever, then advanced tuck (back flat).
2. One-leg lever, straddle lever.
3. Full lever: body straight, horizontal, arms straight, scapulae depressed.

## Meathook progression
1. Two-strap hang, rotate the body sideways with knees tucked.
2. Single-wrist hang held briefly with the other hand on the strap.
3. Extend the legs gradually toward horizontal; the free arm can counterbalance.

## Coaching points
- Shoulder of the hanging arm stays active (pulled down); never hang passively in a
  one-arm shape.
- Squeeze glutes and legs; a loose body adds lever length and load.
- Short holds, high quality. Count holds per session; overuse injuries are common.

## Stop signs
- Pain at the front of the shoulder or the elbow.
- Wrist numbness from the strap.

## Verify
- [ ] Each regression held clean 5 s before moving on.
- [ ] Weekly volume of one-arm holds logged.
