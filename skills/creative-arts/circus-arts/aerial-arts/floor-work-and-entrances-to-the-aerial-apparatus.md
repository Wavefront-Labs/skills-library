# Floor work and entrances to the apparatus

**Goal:** the first 20-30 seconds set the character and the audience's attention before
the performer leaves the ground. Floor work and the first contact are choreographed,
not improvised.

## Entrance choices
- Start on the apparatus already (revealed by light), walk in, run in, or rise from the
  floor. Each sets a different tone; choose on purpose.
- Know where the apparatus will be when you arrive: is it held by a stagehand, tied
  off, or hanging free?

## First contact
- The first touch of the apparatus is a choreographic moment: a hand on the fabric, a
  lean, a spin of the hoop.
- Floor-based figures (wrapping the fabric around the body, hanging from the hoop with
  feet on the floor) bridge to the air.

## The first climb or mount
- Pick the cleanest, most reliable climb or mount; nerves are highest here.
- Plan the climb in the music; it is a fixed duration.

## Practical checks
- Stage floor: shoes or barefoot? Is the floor slippery from haze or dance?
- Tail of fabric on the floor: who manages it, where does it lie when you climb?
- Lighting: front light on entrance; is the apparatus visible before you touch it?

## Verify
- [ ] Entrance rehearsed with the stage manager and lighting on stage.
- [ ] First climb or mount completed cleanly in five consecutive runs.
