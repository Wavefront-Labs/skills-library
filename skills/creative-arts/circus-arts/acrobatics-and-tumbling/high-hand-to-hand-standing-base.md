# High hand-to-hand (standing base)

**Goal:** the flyer balances in handstand on the hands of a standing base, arms
extended overhead. The fall height is now the base's height plus arm length.

> Coach-led only. Taught after a solid low hand-to-hand, with one or two spotters and
> mats, often with a spotting rig at first.

## Base stance
- Feet hip-width, slightly turned out; knees locked but not hyperextended; pelvis
  neutral; ribs down; arms locked overhead with elevated shoulders.
- Head through the arms, eyes on the flyer.

## Entry
- From the low hand (base stands up with the flyer in balance), from a foot-to-hand
  throw, or with the flyer jumping/pressing from the base's shoulders. Each entry is
  a separate skill.

## Corrections
- Small: fingers and wrists. Medium: base shifts their weight. Large: base takes a
  step under the flyer's fall. The flyer stays tight and does not correct with bends.

## Spotters
- Spotter at each side or a spotting rig with belt lines; spotters watch the flyer's
  hips.

## Fall planning
- Flyer bails forward or sideways into a step-down or a spotter catch; never onto the
  base's head.

## Verify
- [ ] Repeated holds with spotters hands-off.
- [ ] Bail drilled at height with spotters.
