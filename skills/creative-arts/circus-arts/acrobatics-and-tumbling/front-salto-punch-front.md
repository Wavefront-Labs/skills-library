# Front salto (punch front)

**Goal:** the punch front rotates forward from a two-footed take-off after a hurdle. The
landing is blind (you don't see the floor until late), which is why progression matters.

> Coach-led only. Taught on trampoline, into a pit, or with a spot or overhead rig before
> the floor.

## Prerequisites
- Hurdle to a two-footed rebound jump with straight legs and arms up.
- Forward roll from a jump; front drop on trampoline.

## Technique
1. Hurdle: one-legged step, feet come together ahead of the body.
2. **Punch:** rebound off both feet with legs nearly straight, arms driving up.
3. At the top, round the back and pull the knees to the chest; tuck tight.
4. Open by extending the legs downward, look for the floor and land.

## Spotting
- Coach beside the take-off, hand on the stomach and lower back to help rotation and
  height.

## Faults
- Diving forward instead of punching up: low rotation, risk of landing on the back.
- Throwing the arms down at the take-off: low height.
- Opening too early: landing seated.

## Verify
- [ ] Clean landings on a mat pit or trampoline first.
- [ ] Spotted on the floor before unspotted.
