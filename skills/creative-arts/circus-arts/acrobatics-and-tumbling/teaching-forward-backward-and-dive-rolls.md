# Teaching forward, backward and dive rolls

**Goal:** rolls that never load the neck, because rolls are the safety skill every
acrobat uses to get out of a failed trick.

## Forward roll
1. Squat, hands shoulder-width on the mat in front of the feet.
2. Tuck chin to chest ("look at your belly button").
3. Push with legs, place the **back of the head/upper back** down, never the top of the
   head. Arms take weight briefly.
4. Roll along a round back and stand up without hands.
Use an **incline (cheese) mat** for beginners so gravity helps.

## Backward roll
1. Squat with back to the incline; hands by the ears, palms up, fingers pointing back.
2. Sit back, roll onto a round back, hands land flat beside the head.
3. **Push through the hands** to lift the head out of the way as the hips pass over.
4. Land on feet in a squat.
No push = weight on the neck. Students who cannot push yet stay on the incline, or roll
over the shoulder instead.

## Dive roll
Prerequisite: clean forward roll from standing.
1. From a small jump, reach forward, hands land first, arms absorb, chin tucks, roll.
2. Increase distance over a rolled mat or soft obstacle in small steps.
3. Never dive over obstacles taller than the student can clear with arms fully absorbing.

## Faults
- Top of head touches the mat: chin not tucked, hands too close to feet.
- Rolling crooked: uneven push; place hands evenly and look straight.
- Dive landing on the head: arms not reaching first, too much height too soon.

## Verify
- [ ] Forward and backward roll from standing on flat mat, no hands to stand.
- [ ] Backward roll with visible push, head clear.
- [ ] Dive roll distance progressed only with clean rolls at each step.
