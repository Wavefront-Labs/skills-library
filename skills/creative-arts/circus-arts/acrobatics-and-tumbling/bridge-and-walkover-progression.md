# Bridge and walkover progression

**Goal:** a back and front walkover with the bend shared by shoulders, upper back and hips,
not crushed into the lower back.

## Preparation
- **Shoulder flexion**: arms overhead against a wall, ribs down; wall slides.
- **Thoracic extension**: over a foam roller.
- **Hip flexor length**: lunge stretch.
- Bridge must be built with straight arms and shoulders over (or past) the hands.

## Progression
1. **Bridge** from lying: push to straight arms, shoulders over wrists, hold 20 s.
2. **Bridge rocks**: shift shoulders forward over the hands.
3. **Bridge kickover**: from bridge, lift one leg and push off the other over to stand.
   Use an incline mat at first.
4. **Back walkover** with spot: from standing, one leg forward, arms up, reach back to
   bridge while the front leg lifts and kicks over. Spotter at the side supports lower
   back and thigh.
5. **Front walkover**: handstand to bridge with one leg leading, push through the
   shoulders to stand. Needs good shoulder push and front-split flexibility.

## Spotting
Spotter kneels or stands beside, one hand under the lower back, the other guiding the
thigh over. Remove the spot gradually.

## Faults and warnings
- Bend in the lower back only, shoulders closed: back pain, collapse.
- Bent arms in the bridge: risk of falling on the head.
- Any persistent lower back pain: stop backbends and refer; spondylolysis is a known
  risk in young acrobats who do a lot of extension.

## Verify
- [ ] Straight-arm bridge with shoulders over hands, 20 s.
- [ ] Kickover from bridge both legs.
- [ ] Walkover unspotted with arms straight throughout.
