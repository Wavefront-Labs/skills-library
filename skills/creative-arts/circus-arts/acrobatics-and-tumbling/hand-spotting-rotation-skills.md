# Hand spotting rotation skills

**Goal:** a spot that adds height and rotation when needed and prevents a head or neck
landing, without injuring the spotter.

## Principles
- **Position** close to the performer, side-on, feet apart, knees bent - at the point
  where the skill rotates, not where it starts.
- **Contact points**: one hand on the lower back (lifts and supports), the other on the
  back of the thighs (rotates). Never grab arms or ankles.
- **Timing**: contact from take-off through the inverted phase; release on landing.
- **Move with the skill**: step in the direction of travel.

## Back handspring spot
Spotter kneels or stands beside the take-off point. Lower-back hand supports as the
performer sits and jumps back; thigh hand pushes the legs over. Spotter slides along
with the performer.

## Back tuck spot
Spotter beside, hand on the lower back lifts at take-off; other hand turns the back of
thighs to complete the rotation; follow to landing ready to catch the chest if they
under-rotate.

## Communication
Agree before: "I'm spotting you on this one", and what the performer must do (e.g.
"jump up, then tuck"). Never remove a spot without telling the performer.

## Spotter safety
- Keep your head out of the swing path of legs.
- Lift with legs, not back.
- Do not spot skills heavier/faster than you can control; use a rig or mats instead.

## Verify
- [ ] Spotter can state stance, contact points and timing for the skill.
- [ ] Spotter has spotted the skill on an experienced performer first.
