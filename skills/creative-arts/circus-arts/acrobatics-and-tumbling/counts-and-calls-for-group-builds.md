# Counts and calls for group builds

**Goal:** a group build depends on everyone moving on the same count. One caller, agreed
words and silent versions for performance.

## Who calls
- One caller, usually a base or the captain who can see the whole build. Nobody else
  calls movement; anyone can call "down".

## Standard counts
- "Ready... and one, two, up" (or the team's version). The movement happens on the
  last count.
- Each mount has its own count.

## Calls
- "Set": I am stable.
- "Go": next level mounts.
- "Down": dismount or emergency.

## Silent cues
- In performance with music, counts are matched to the music or replaced by a squeeze
  of the hand or a breath. Rehearse the silent cue as much as the spoken.

## Verify
- [ ] Calls written in the build sheet.
- [ ] Rehearsed with music in performance conditions.
