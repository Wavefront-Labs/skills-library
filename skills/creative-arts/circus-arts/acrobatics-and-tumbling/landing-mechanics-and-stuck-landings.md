# Landing mechanics and stuck landings

**Goal:** most tumbling injuries happen at the landing. Good landing mechanics absorb force
safely and finish the skill cleanly.

## The landing position
- Feet hip-width or together depending on the skill; land on the balls of the feet,
  heels following.
- Ankles, knees and hips bend together to absorb; knees over the toes (not collapsing
  inward).
- Chest up, arms forward or up, eyes forward.

## Sticking
- A stuck landing has no step. Train by jumping down from low boxes to a stuck
  landing, then from skills onto soft mats.
- Hold the landing for 2 s in training; the hold becomes the presentation.

## When the landing is lost
- Under-rotated (falling backward): sit and roll back; never reach behind with straight
  arms.
- Over-rotated (falling forward): step out or forward roll.
- Practise these falls as drills.

## Faults
- Knees collapsing inward (valgus): knee ligament injury risk.
- Stiff-legged landing: high impact on the spine.
- Reaching back with straight arms: wrist and elbow injuries.

## Verify
- [ ] Students land 10 box jumps with knees tracking over toes.
- [ ] Fall drills practised in each tumbling session.
