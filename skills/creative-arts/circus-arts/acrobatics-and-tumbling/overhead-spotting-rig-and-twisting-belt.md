# Overhead spotting rig and twisting belt

**Goal:** let an acrobat attempt new rotations and twists with the coach holding them
from above through ropes and pulleys.

## Equipment
- Two overhead pulleys (fixed above a trampoline or tumbling spot) or a **travelling rig**
  on a track along a tumbling line. Points rated for dynamic loads.
- **Spotting belt**: rings at each hip for saltos.
- **Twisting belt**: inner belt rotates inside an outer ring, so the acrobat can twist
  while the ropes control the salto.
- Ropes to the coach, who stands to the side or behind.

## Fitting
- Belt snug on the waist above the hip bones; buckle doubled back.
- Ropes clipped to the rings, untwisted, gates locked.
- Check the twisting bearing turns freely.

## Rope handling
- Keep light tension to feel the acrobat; take in on the take-off.
- For saltos: as the acrobat leaves the floor, pull to add height and time.
- For a bad landing: hold the acrobat up, let them reach their feet or a seated position.
- Never let the ropes go slack at the top of a salto.

## Limits
- Rigs change rotation feel; transition off the rig with hand spots and mats.
- Ropes can wrap the body in twists; with a standard belt, twisting is dangerous.
- The coach must be strong enough to hold the acrobat's full weight; use counterweights
  or a second operator for heavier acrobats.

## Verify
- [ ] Points and pulleys inspected and rated for dynamic use.
- [ ] Belt fit checked by a second person.
- [ ] Coach has practised catching a deliberate under-rotation.
