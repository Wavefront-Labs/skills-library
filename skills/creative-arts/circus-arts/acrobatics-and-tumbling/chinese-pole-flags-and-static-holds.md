# Chinese pole flags and static holds

**Goal:** a horizontal human flag and related holds that read as signature moments on the
pole.

## Flag mechanics
- Top hand **pulls**, bottom hand **pushes**; arms straight.
- Grip spacing roughly shoulder-width or a bit wider; bottom hand's palm can face away.
- Body tight from shoulders to feet; obliques and lats hold the line.
- Shoulders active: bottom shoulder pushed away from the ear, top shoulder pulled down.

## Progression
1. **Vertical flag** (body inverted alongside the pole): build lat and push strength.
2. **Tuck flag**: knees to chest, horizontal hips.
3. **Single-leg / straddle flag**: extend one leg or straddle.
4. **Full flag**: legs together, horizontal.
5. **Negatives**: from vertical, lower slowly towards horizontal.
Hold 3-5 sets of 5-10 s; quality over duration.

## Other static holds
- **Pole sit**: seated sideways, thighs lock, hands free.
- **Pole plank / elbow lever**: horizontal with elbow hooked.
- **Inverted thigh hang**: head down, thighs locking.

## Shoulder health
- The bottom shoulder takes a lot of stress; build up volume slowly.
- Train both sides; one-sided flags create imbalances.
- Elbow pain inside the bottom arm: reduce volume.

## Verify
- [ ] Tuck flag 10 s each side.
- [ ] Full flag 3 s with straight arms before using in choreography.
