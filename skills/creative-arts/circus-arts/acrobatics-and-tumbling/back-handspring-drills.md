# Back handspring drills

**Goal:** the back handspring (flic-flac) is taught through drills that build each
part: the sit back, the arm swing, the hand placement, the snap down.

> Coach-led only. Use spotting and drills; never learn by "just trying" on a hard floor.

## Prerequisite skills
- Bridge with straight arms and open shoulders.
- Handstand snap-down (handstand to a hollow, feet landing under the hips).
- Standing back jump to the back onto a mat stack.

## Drill sequence
1. **Sit-back and jump:** from standing, sit back as if into a chair, then jump back
   onto a raised mat, arms swinging up.
2. **Barrel / back handspring trainer:** roll back over a barrel or a shaped trainer,
   placing the hands, coach guiding.
3. **Incline to flat:** down an incline wedge, spotted.
4. **Spotted on floor:** coach hand on the lower back and the back of the thighs.
5. **Handstand snap-down:** the last part of the skill, drilled separately.

## Coaching points
- Arms swing up and back hard; eyes find the floor when the hands are over the head.
- Hips push back, not up; jumping straight up gives a "back tuck" shape.
- Snap down from the hands with the legs together.

## Readiness gates
- [ ] Bridge, snap-down and sit-back jump all clean.
- [ ] 10 spotted consecutive repetitions with no assistance needed.
- [ ] Coach decides when the spot is removed.
