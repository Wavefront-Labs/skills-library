# Rebound and connection between skills

**Goal:** a tumbling pass is skills linked without a pause. Each skill's landing is the
next skill's take-off; the rebound carries momentum through the line.

## The rebound
- Land on the balls of the feet with legs nearly straight and the body tight; the floor
  contact is short.
- Arms finish forward or up depending on the next skill.
- Chest up, slight hollow; landing arched or piked kills the rebound.

## Typical connections
- Round-off to back handspring: round-off snap-down feeds the backward sit.
- Back handspring to back handspring: each snap-down feeds the next.
- Back handspring to back tuck: the last snap-down gives a high rebound and a set.

## Keep the line
- Hands and feet stay on the line of the floor; drifting sideways compounds through
  the pass.
- Mark the line on the floor in training.

## Coaching points
- Build passes by adding one skill at a time.
- Fatigue shows first in the rebound; stop the pass if rebounds get lower.

## Stop rule
- A low or crooked rebound into a salto is aborted: stand up instead of rotating.
  Teach and practise the abort.

## Verify
- [ ] Each connection drilled as a pair before full passes.
- [ ] Students demonstrate the abort.
