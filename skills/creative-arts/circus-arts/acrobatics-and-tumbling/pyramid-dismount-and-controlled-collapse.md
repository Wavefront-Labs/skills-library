# Pyramid dismount and controlled collapse

**Goal:** coming down is where pyramids injure people. Dismounts are planned as carefully
as the build, including an emergency version.

## Dismount order
- Reverse of the build: tops first, then middles, then bases release.
- Each dismount has a landing spot and a spotter.

## Jumps and steps
- Tops jump forward to the floor or to spotters, with a stuck landing.
- Middles step down via their mount path.

## Controlled collapse
- A designed collapse (bases kneel, then lie) can be choreography. It is rehearsed at
  low height first; nobody falls onto anyone.

## Emergency dismount
- One call ("down") means the planned emergency exit: tops to spotters, middles step
  down.
- Rehearse it every session.

## Verify
- [ ] Dismount order on the build sheet.
- [ ] Emergency dismount rehearsed.
