# Low hand-to-hand handstand

**Goal:** the low hand-to-hand is a handstand by the flyer on the hands of a lying base.
It is the first hand-to-hand balance and teaches communication and corrections.

> Coach-led. A spotter stands beside the pair for all early attempts.

## Setup
- Base lies on their back on a mat, arms vertical, hands up.
- Flyer stands at the base's head, facing the base's feet (or at the side depending on
  the entry).

## Entry
1. Flyer places hands in the base's hands (hand-in-hand grip).
2. Flyer presses or kicks up to handstand; the base's arms stay locked.
3. Base finds the balance with the fingers and wrists; the flyer keeps a straight,
   tight body.

## Communication
- Flyer: "up", "down", "bail". Base: "ready", "hold", "down".
- Agree who calls the dismount.

## Spotting
- Spotter stands beside the pair, hands ready at the flyer's hips and shoulders.

## Bail
- Flyer steps down on the side planned (usually toward the base's feet), pirouette or
  cartwheel exit. Base keeps arms locked until the flyer's weight is off.

## Verify
- [ ] 5 s balance with spotter hands off.
- [ ] Bail practised on every session.
