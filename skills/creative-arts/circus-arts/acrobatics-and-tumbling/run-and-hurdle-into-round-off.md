# Run and hurdle into round-off

**Goal:** the run and hurdle turn horizontal speed into a powerful round-off, the entry
to most backward tumbling passes.

## Run
- A short, consistent run: 3-5 steps for most circus tumbling lines; measure and mark
  the starting point.
- Accelerate steadily; the last steps are the fastest.

## Hurdle
1. A low, long skip from one foot, arms rising to overhead.
2. Land on the front foot into a lunge, arms still up beside the ears.
3. The hurdle is low and travelling, not high.

## Round-off
- Hands placed one after the other in a line, the second hand turned (the "T"
  placement); the body turns a quarter on the first hand and a quarter on the second.
- Legs snap together over the top, feet land behind the hands.
- Push from the shoulders (block) as the legs come down; land with the chest up,
  facing back, ready to rebound.

## Coaching points
- Consistent hurdle step: measure hurdle length on the floor.
- Arms stay by the ears through the hurdle; arms dropping lowers power.

## Faults
- Stutter steps: inconsistent hurdle foot.
- High hurdle: loss of speed.
- Feet landing apart: twisting rebound.

## Verify
- [ ] Hurdle foot lands on the same mark 5 times in a row.
- [ ] Round-off lands with feet together, ready to rebound.
