# Throwing the flyer from hands (banquine)

**Goal:** the bases throw the flyer up from the basket for a jump, salto or twist. Timing
between the bases and the flyer decides the height and direction.

> Coach-led only. Banquine throws are taught with experienced coaches and spotters; first
> rotations with an overhead rig or into a pit.

## The throw
1. Flyer stands in the basket, arms ready.
2. Count: bases dip together (bend the knees), flyer dips with them.
3. Bases drive up with the legs and lift the basket; arms extend up at the end.
4. Flyer pushes off the basket with straight legs at the top, arms driving up.

## Direction and height
- Throw is vertical unless a travelling skill is planned; a forward throw drifts the
  flyer away from the bases.
- Height is consistent; bases count aloud.

## Spotters
- Spotters watch the flyer and are positioned for the expected landing.

## Progression
1. Straight jumps.
2. Jumps with tuck shapes.
3. Saltos in a rig.
4. Saltos with spotters.

## Faults
- Bases out of time: flyer twisted sideways.
- Flyer pushes early: low throw.

## Verify
- [ ] Straight jumps land back in the basket consistently.
- [ ] Coach decides when the rig comes off.
