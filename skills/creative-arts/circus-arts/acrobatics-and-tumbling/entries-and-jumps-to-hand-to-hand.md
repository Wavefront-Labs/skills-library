# Entries and jumps to hand

**Goal:** the entry is how the flyer arrives in hand-to-hand. A poor entry makes a good
balance impossible; jumps to hand are among the most demanding pair skills.

> Coach-led only. Each entry learned separately, with spotters and mats, low before high.

## Entry types
- **Press to hand:** flyer presses up to handstand from standing on the floor or the
  base's thighs. Slow, controlled; needs strong compression.
- **Kick to hand:** flyer kicks up from the floor; base receives.
- **Jump to hand (from the floor or the base's feet):** the base drives the flyer up
  by the hands while the flyer jumps, ending in handstand.

## Timing
- Base and flyer count the same; the base's drive starts as the flyer jumps.
- Base keeps arms close to vertical; drive is up, not forward.

## Spotting
- Spotters at both sides; for jumps, a spotter behind to stop over-rotation.

## Faults
- Base drives forward: flyer overshoots.
- Flyer piking on the way up: arrives with hips off the line.
- Mismatched count.

## Verify
- [ ] Each entry consistent at low height before high.
- [ ] Counts agreed and written down.
