# Headstand and handstand forward roll

**Goal:** a stable tripod headstand and a controlled roll out of a handstand, the
standard way to bail when a handstand overbalances.

## Tripod headstand
1. Hands and forehead (hairline, not the crown) form an equilateral **triangle** on the
   mat, hands shoulder-width.
2. Walk the feet in until hips are over the head; place knees on elbows (frog stand).
3. Lift knees, then extend legs up slowly; most weight on the hands, not the neck.
4. Come down the same way.
Limit holds to short durations for beginners; neck discomfort = stop.

## Handstand forward roll
1. From a kicked handstand (spotted at first), let the shoulders lean slightly forward
   past the hands.
2. **Bend the arms and tuck the chin** as the hips come over.
3. Place the back of the head/upper back down, roll on a round back, stand.
Drill on a soft mat; spotter supports the hips and guides the tuck.

## Bail-out habit
Teach: overbalancing in a handstand = tuck and roll, or step one hand forward and
pirouette to a cartwheel. Practise deliberate overbalances every session.

## Faults
- Crown of the head in headstand: neck compression. Move contact to the hairline.
- Straight arms in the roll: crash onto the back of the head.
- Not tucking the chin: head hits first.

## Verify
- [ ] Tripod headstand with straight legs 10 s, controlled descent.
- [ ] Handstand forward roll unspotted with head tucked and arms bending.
- [ ] Student overbalances on purpose and bails correctly.
