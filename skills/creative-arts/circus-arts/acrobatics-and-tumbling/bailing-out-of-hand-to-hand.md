# Bailing out of a hand-to-hand balance

**Goal:** every hand-to-hand pair practises the bail (planned exit from a failed balance)
before working on balance quality.

## Planned directions
- **Over-balance (falling toward the flyer's back):** flyer steps down forward, base
  lets go once the flyer's weight leaves.
- **Under-balance (falling toward the flyer's front):** flyer pirouettes or cartwheels
  to the side.
- Direction agreed for each skill.

## Base's role
- Base keeps arms locked until the flyer's weight is off; letting go early drops the
  flyer onto the base.
- High hand: base may step forward or sideways to guide the flyer.

## Spotters
- Spotters catch the flyer's hips or shoulders and lower them; they practise catches
  in every session.

## Calls
- "Bail" or "down": both partners act immediately; no saving at all costs.

## Practising
- Bail from low hand on a mat, then from high hand with spotters, then with each new
  entry.

## Verify
- [ ] Bails practised at the start of every session.
- [ ] Each partner can say the planned direction for each skill.
