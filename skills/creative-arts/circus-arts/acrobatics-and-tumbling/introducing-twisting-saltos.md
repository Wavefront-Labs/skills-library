# Introducing twists

**Goal:** add rotation around the long axis to a salto. Twisting is introduced after a
stable layout and in controlled environments.

> Coach-led only. Twists are learned on trampoline and in a twisting belt / overhead rig
> before any floor attempt.

## Find the twist direction
- Most people twist consistently one way. Test with standing half and full turns, a
  jump full turn and a back-drop half twist on trampoline. Use the same direction for
  every twisting skill.

## Mechanics
- Twist starts from a straight, tight body with arms close; arms pull into the
  chest and the body rotates around its long axis.
- Twist comes *after* the take-off, not from twisting the feet on the floor.
- Body stays straight; pike or arch slows the twist and changes rotation.

## Progression
1. Jump full twists on the trampoline.
2. Back drop to feet with a half twist, then full.
3. Layout to full twist in the twisting belt with a coach on the ropes.
4. Floor attempts only after consistent rig landings.

## Faults
- Twisting early from the floor: the salto loses height and rotation.
- Arms wide: twist slows.

## Verify
- [ ] Twist direction recorded and consistent.
- [ ] Coach sign-off at each stage.
