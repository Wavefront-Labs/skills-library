# Back tuck and layout

**Goal:** a back salto rotates backward in the air with no hand contact: first tucked,
later in a layout (straight body). Height comes before rotation.

> Coach-led only. Back saltos are taught with a hand spot or an overhead rig, on
> trampoline or into a mat pit before the floor.

## Prerequisites
- Vertical jump with arms driving up and a tight body.
- Back roll to stand; back handspring is not required but common.
- Trampoline back drop and controlled bounce.

## Back tuck technique
1. Arms swing up and stop overhead (the **set**); the body jumps up, not back.
2. At the top of the jump, knees pull to the chest; hands grab the shins.
3. Spot the floor as it comes round; open and land with knees bent, arms forward.

## Layout
- Same set, but the body stays straight, rotating from a slight hollow; requires more
  height and a stronger set. Taught on trampoline or with a rig first.

## Spotting
- Coach hand under the lower back on the set, other hand helping rotation at the
  back of the thighs; or an overhead spotting rig.

## Faults
- Throwing the head back early: low, travelling salto.
- Tucking before the top of the jump: under-rotation, landing on the knees.

## Verify
- [ ] Consistent spotted landings on mats over several sessions.
- [ ] Coach sign-off before reducing the spot.
