# Teeterboard jumping and pusher timing

**Goal:** the jumper (pusher) controls the launch height. Consistency is the jumper's
job; a variable jump means variable flyer height.

> Coach-led only.

## The jump
- Jumper steps from a platform or a raised position onto the high end of the board.
- Lands with both feet, straight-ish legs, body tight, arms down.
- Height of the jump determines force; it is marked and repeated.

## Consistency
- Jumpers use the same platform height, same step, same landing point on the board.
- Two jumpers jumping together increase force; they jump in sync.

## Timing
- Flyer and jumper count together; the jumper calls the jump.

## Safety checks
- Board inspected (cracks, pivot, padding); mats in place; the flyer ready.
- Spotters in place.

## Verify
- [ ] Jumper's launch height consistent over many repetitions.
- [ ] Counts agreed.
