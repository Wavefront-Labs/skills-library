# Russian bar

**Goal:** the Russian bar is a flexible pole held on the shoulders of two bases; the
flyer bounces and rotates on it. It needs exact timing between the bases and the flyer.

> Coach-led only. Flyers learn in an overhead rig with lines; first on a low bar or a
> bar on the floor.

## Bar
- A flexible fibreglass or composite pole, typically 4-5 m long; bases hold the ends on
  their shoulders.
- Checked for cracks before every session.

## Bases
- Shoulders under the bar, hands holding it.
- Bounce rhythm: bases bend their knees together to push the bar.

## Flyer
- Stands on the middle of the bar, feet together.
- Bounces with the bar; the set happens at the bottom of the bar's bounce.

## Landing
- Feet back onto the bar; stuck landing, knees bent.

## Rig
- Overhead rig with lines; spotters ready.

## Verify
- [ ] Bases' bounce in sync.
- [ ] Flyer lands rig skills consistently before lines come off.
