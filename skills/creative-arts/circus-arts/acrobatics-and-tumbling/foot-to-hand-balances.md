# Foot-to-hand balances

**Goal:** a flyer standing in the base's hands, first with the base lying down, then
standing - the key step towards high hand-to-hand.

## The grip
- Base holds each foot with the heel in the palm, fingers wrapped round the instep,
  thumb on the inside.
- Flyer keeps feet flat and ankles strong; pointed or rolled ankles make the platform
  wobble.

## Low foot-to-hand (base lying)
1. Base lies on back, arms vertical and locked over the shoulders.
2. Flyer stands at the base's head, steps one foot into one hand, then the other, using
   spotter support or hands on the base's knees.
3. Base keeps arms vertical; flyer stands tall, tight, hips over feet.
4. Base steers balance by **moving the hands as one platform**, small shifts.
Exit: flyer steps forward off, or base lowers to the chest and flyer steps down.

## High foot-to-hand (base standing)
Prerequisites: solid two-high and low foot-to-hand.
1. Entry via shoulders: flyer stands on the shoulders, base takes feet in hands one at a
   time and presses up to locked arms.
2. Or from a jump with the base's hands as a step (advanced).
3. Base stands under the load, arms locked, legs doing steering.
Always with a spotter, and an overhead lunge for learning high entries if available.

## Faults
- Base bends arms under load: collapse; press stays locked, legs do the work.
- Flyer bends at the hips to correct: base cannot read the balance.

## Verify
- [ ] Low foot-to-hand 10 s, base arms vertical.
- [ ] High foot-to-hand entry and exit with spot, three clean reps.
