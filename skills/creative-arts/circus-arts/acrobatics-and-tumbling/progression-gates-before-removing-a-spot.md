# Progression gates before removing a spot

**Goal:** remove support based on evidence, not on how brave the acrobat feels.

## Stages of support
1. Rig / overhead belt.
2. Full hand spot (spotter does real work).
3. Light touch (spotter in contact, no lifting).
4. Shadow spot (spotter present, hands ready, no contact).
5. Unspotted on soft surface.
6. Unspotted on performance surface.
Move one stage at a time.

## Gates for each stage change
- **Consistency**: 5 consecutive clean reps at the current stage, in at least 2 sessions.
- **Self-sufficiency**: spotter reports no force needed for the last reps.
- **Correct shape**: take-off, rotation and landing positions match the model.
- **Bail-out**: acrobat can abort mid-skill safely (e.g. stop on hands, open out to a
  roll).
- **Health**: no new pain, not fatigued.

## Surface change
Moving from crash mat to landing mat to sprung floor to stage is a stage change in
itself; expect a temporary drop in consistency.

## Fear management
- Agree the stage in advance; never surprise-remove a spot.
- Give the acrobat a veto; fear is information.
- Short, frequent exposure beats one heroic session.

## Verify
- [ ] Stage and rep log for each skill per acrobat.
- [ ] No stage skipped; surface change logged as a stage.
