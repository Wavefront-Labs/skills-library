# Cartwheel to round-off technique

**Goal:** a cartwheel on a straight line, then a round-off that lands both feet together
facing the way you came, ready to rebound into back handsprings.

## Cartwheel
1. Stand side-on or in a lunge facing the line.
2. Hands go down one after the other **on the line**, fingers turned slightly.
3. Legs pass straight over the top, as close to vertical as possible.
4. Feet land one after the other on the line; finish in a lunge facing the start.
Drill: hands and feet in chalk boxes on a line; the cartwheel over a small block.

## Round-off
1. Lunge forward facing the line, arms up.
2. First hand down on the line, second hand turned in (T-shape).
3. As the body passes vertical, **legs snap together** and the body turns a quarter more.
4. **Snap down**: push from the shoulders (block), pike slightly from the hips and land
   both feet together, chest up, facing back towards the start.
Drill: handstand snap-downs from a wall or block onto a mat.

## Faults
- Hands off the line: everything after goes crooked.
- Legs bent or apart at landing: no rebound.
- Landing too far from the hands: weak snap; work snap-downs.
- Arching at landing: lower back stress, cannot connect to handspring.

## Verify
- [ ] Cartwheel with hands and feet on a 10 cm line, both sides.
- [ ] Round-off landing feet together, chest up, 5 reps in a row.
- [ ] Round-off rebound into a stretched jump straight up.
