# Columns and three-high work (banquine)

**Goal:** a column is a vertical stack of performers standing on shoulders: two-high,
three-high, sometimes more. Columns are built and dismounted in practised order.

> Coach-led only. Three-high is learned with an overhead rig or spotters for the top and
> thick mats.

## Building
1. Bottom base stands, legs wide and strong.
2. Middle mounts to the bottom's shoulders (from a basket throw, or climbing via the
   thigh).
3. Top mounts to the middle's shoulders via a throw from other bases or climbing.
4. Each person grips the calves of the one above.

## Holding
- Every level stacks straight: hips over feet, chest up.
- Middle and top keep legs straight and tight.

## Dismounting
- Top first: jump down forward to spotters or mats, or a cradle catch.
- Middle next.

## Falls plan
- The top falls forward to spotters; never backwards onto the bases.
- The bottom does not move unless the column is collapsing.

## Verify
- [ ] Two-high stable before three-high.
- [ ] Dismount order rehearsed every session.
