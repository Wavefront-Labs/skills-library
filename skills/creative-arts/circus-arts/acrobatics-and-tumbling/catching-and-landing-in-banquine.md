# Catching and landing on the base (banquine)

**Goal:** after a throw, the flyer lands back in the basket, on a base's shoulders, or
in a cradle catch. The catch protects both flyer and bases.

> Coach-led only.

## Catches
- **Basket catch:** flyer lands on feet in the basket; bases absorb with legs bending.
- **Shoulder landing:** flyer lands standing on a base's shoulders; base grips the
  flyer's calves and absorbs.
- **Cradle catch:** for a failed rotation, the bases catch the flyer across the arms
  (under the back and knees) instead of a standing catch.

## Absorbing
- Bases bend knees and hips as the flyer lands; arms stay straight.
- Flyer lands with straight but not locked legs, body tight.

## Spotting
- Spotters stand ready to catch a flyer who misses the basket or the shoulders.

## Faults
- Bases reaching to catch with bent arms.
- Flyer landing with soft body: basket collapses.

## Verify
- [ ] Cradle catch practised before saltos.
- [ ] Catches consistent in rig before rig comes off.
