# Front handspring

**Goal:** a front handspring rotates forward through a handstand with a push from the
shoulders. It is the gateway to forward tumbling and the punch front.

> Coach-led. Learn with a coach spot and mats or an incline before trying on a floor.

## Prerequisites
- Strong handstand with straight arms and open shoulders.
- Bridge kick-over / front walkover range.
- Hurdle into a lunge.

## Technique
1. Hurdle, lunge, reach long with straight arms.
2. Hands land shoulder width, fingers forward; arms straight, shoulders open (body
   straight over the hands).
3. Kick the back leg, then the front leg, to a fast straight-body handstand.
4. **Block:** push from the shoulders (not by bending the elbows) as the body passes
   vertical.
5. Land on two feet, arms up beside the ears, slightly arched or straight, depending
   on whether the next skill follows.

## Spotting
- Coach kneels beside the hands, one hand under the shoulders/upper back, the other on
  the lower back, helping rotation and preventing collapse.

## Faults
- Bent arms on the block: crash onto the head or neck.
- Closed shoulders: the body slows and lands sitting.
- Looking at the hands too late: arched landing that hurts the lower back.

## Verify
- [ ] Clean spotted handsprings on an incline mat.
- [ ] Coach sign-off before unspotted on the floor.
