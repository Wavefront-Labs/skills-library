# Standing on shoulders (two-high)

**Goal:** a stable standing two-high, the building block for columns, pyramids and banquine.

## Base posture
- Feet hip-width or slightly wider, knees soft, pelvis neutral, chest up.
- Load path: flyer's feet on the **top of the shoulders close to the neck** (on the
  trapezius over the collarbone), straight down through the spine to the feet.
- Base grips the flyer's **calves** (hands wrapped behind) and pulls down slightly to
  lock the flyer's feet in place.

## Mount from the thigh (front)
1. Base in a lunge, flyer steps onto the base's front thigh near the hip, holding hands.
2. Flyer climbs: other foot to the base's shoulder, then the first foot.
3. Base stands up straight, transfers hands to calves.

## Mount from behind
Base squats; flyer steps onto the shoulders from behind holding the base's hands, then
the base stands. Easier for heavier flyers, harder for the base's legs.

## Flyer alignment
Tight legs, squeezed glutes, hollow body, eyes forward on a fixed point. Small
corrections come from the ankles; the base corrects bigger drift by stepping under.

## Dismounts
- Forward jump: base holds hands up, flyer jumps forward to the floor, base absorbs
  with hands.
- Step down via the thigh (reverse mount).
Spotter always behind, ready to catch under the flyer's arms.

## Verify
- [ ] Two-high held 10 s with hands released to calves only.
- [ ] Mount and dismount both ways with spotter.
- [ ] Base stands upright with no lower back arch.
