# The banquine wrist grip and basket

**Goal:** banquine bases throw and catch the flyer from interlocked hands (the basket).
The grip must not slip under dynamic loads many times the flyer's weight.

> Coach-led only. Banquine is a high-risk discipline; learn grips and baskets at standing
> height without throws.

## Wrist grip
- Each base grips their partner's wrist (not the hand): right hand grips the partner's
  left wrist, left hand grips the partner's right wrist (or as agreed), forming a
  square or "X".
- Gripping wrists rather than hands is stronger and doesn't slip.

## Basket
- Two bases face each other, gripped, arms low (about hip height) with elbows straight.
- The flyer stands with both feet in the basket (feet on the bases' forearms or hands).
- Bases stand in a split stance, knees bent ready to drive.

## Load path
- Load goes through straight arms into the bases' legs; the bases drive with the legs,
  not the arms.

## Checks before throwing
- Grip is consistent for both bases.
- Basket stays level as the flyer steps in.
- A third person (spotter) is assigned.

## Verify
- [ ] Bases hold a flyer in the basket for 10 s without movement.
- [ ] Grip and stance identical across repetitions.
