# Trampoline and trampowall for circus

**Goal:** trampoline acts and trampowall (trampoline against a wall) use bounce for
height and rotation. Control of the bounce comes before tricks.

> Coach-led. Frame pads, end decks and spotters in place for every session.

## Safety
- Frame pads cover springs and frame; end decks or mats beside the bed.
- Spotters at the sides; nobody under the bed.
- One person on the bed at a time unless the act is designed for more.

## Bounce control
- Bounce in the centre of the bed; arms circle to build height.
- **Kill the bounce:** bend the knees on landing to stop; practise first.

## Trampowall
- A trampoline set against a wall with a platform at the top.
- Skills: drops from the platform to the bed, rebound to the wall, wall runs.
- Drops are learned from low positions with spotters.

## Progression
1. Bounce control and kill.
2. Seat, front and back drops.
3. Saltos with rig.

## Verify
- [ ] Students kill the bounce on command.
- [ ] Frame pads checked every session.
