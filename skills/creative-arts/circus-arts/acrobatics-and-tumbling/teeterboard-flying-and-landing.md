# Teeterboard flying and landing

**Goal:** the teeterboard flyer is launched by jumpers landing on the other end of the
board. Flyers rotate and land on mats, a catcher's shoulders, or a chair.

> Coach-led only. Teeterboard is one of the highest-risk acts; flyers learn in an overhead
> rig with lines, on mats, with experienced jumpers.

## On the board
- Flyer stands on the end of the board at the agreed position, feet together, knees
  slightly bent.
- Arms up or ready; eyes forward.

## The launch
- At the jumpers' landing, the board launches the flyer. The flyer pushes through
  straight legs, sets the arms and stays tight.

## In the air
- Same principles as tumbling saltos: set, rotate, spot the landing.

## Landing
- Mats: stuck landing, knees bent.
- Catcher's shoulders or a chair on a base: feet land on the shoulders, catcher holds
  the calves.

## Rig
- An overhead rig with lines operated by an experienced spotter is standard for
  learning; lines come off only after many consistent landings.

## Verify
- [ ] Consistent rig landings before lines come off.
- [ ] Flyer and jumpers agree counts.
