# Designing a pyramid for load and silhouette

**Goal:** a pyramid must hold up (load) and look good from the audience (silhouette).
Design both on paper before trying it.

## Load
- Count the load on each base: performers they support, directly and indirectly.
- Heaviest load on the strongest, most experienced bases.
- Middle performers need both strength (supporting tops) and lightness.

## Matching bodies
- Bases: strong, stable. Middles: strong, mid-weight. Tops: light, flexible.

## Silhouette
- Symmetry reads as stability; asymmetry reads as drama.
- Check from the audience view; draw it.
- Height ratio: wide bases for tall pyramids.

## Fall paths
- For each top position, draw where they would fall and place a spotter there.
- Remove positions that would fall onto other performers.

## Verify
- [ ] Load diagram with each base's support count.
- [ ] Fall path diagram with spotter positions.
