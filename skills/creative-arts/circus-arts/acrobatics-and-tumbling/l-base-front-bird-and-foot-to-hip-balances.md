# L-base: front bird and foot-to-hip balances

**Goal:** the first balances of partner acrobatics, where the base lies on their back with
legs vertical (the "L") and supports the flyer on the feet.

## Base set-up
- Lie flat, legs vertical, **ankles over hips** - stacked bones carry weight with little
  muscle.
- Feet flexed, toes slightly turned out.
- Arms ready, hands up to receive the flyer's hands.

## Front bird
1. Flyer stands at the base's hips, facing the base's head, holding hands.
2. Base places feet on the flyer's **hip bones** (anterior iliac crest), toes pointing
   out, heels together-ish.
3. Flyer leans forward onto the feet; base lifts legs to vertical.
4. Flyer opens arms when stable: tight hollow body, legs together, chest up.
5. Return: base bends knees to lower, flyer lands on feet.

## Back bird
Flyer faces away; base's feet on the flyer's lower back/upper glutes, flyer arches gently
with arms reaching back. Easier to fall forward off; spotter in front.

## Foot placement errors
- Feet on the belly: painful, unstable.
- Feet too low on the thighs: flyer pivots forward.
- Base's legs angled (not vertical): base's hips and core fatigue fast.

## Spotting
Spotter stands beside the flyer's shoulders, hands ready under shoulders and thighs.
The usual fall is forward over the base's head; spotter catches the shoulders.

## Verify
- [ ] Front bird held 10 s with hands free, base legs vertical.
- [ ] Controlled return to standing three times.
- [ ] Spotter positioned on every attempt by beginners.
