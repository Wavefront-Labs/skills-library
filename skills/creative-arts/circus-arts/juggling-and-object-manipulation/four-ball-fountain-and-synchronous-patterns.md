# Four-ball fountain and synchronous patterns

**Goal:** a steady four-ball fountain (siteswap 4), the first step past three balls,
in both asynchronous and synchronous timing.

## The pattern
Four balls = two in each hand, each hand juggling its own two-ball column/circle. The
hands never exchange balls. Async: hands alternate (R, L, R, L). Sync: both hands throw
together, siteswap (4,4).

## Step by step
1. **Two in one hand**, right: throw, throw, catch, catch in a small circle (inside
   throws, i.e. throw on the inside and catch on the outside, is the usual starting
   form). 30 catches.
2. Same with left hand. The weak hand usually needs double the practice.
3. Sync: both hands two-in-one together, mirror images.
4. Async: offset the hands by half a beat; feels odd at first.
5. Height: roughly 1.5-2 times your three-ball cascade height.

## Drills
- **Flash**: throw all four, catch all four (async). Builds confidence.
- Counting catches out loud; target 20, 50, 100.
- Columns vs circles: practise both to add tricks later.

## Common faults
- Balls drifting forward: throws too far out; face a wall.
- Collisions in the middle: crossing accidentally; keep each hand's balls on its own side.
- Weak hand lower: drill the weak hand alone.

## Verify
- [ ] 50 catches of two-in-one each hand.
- [ ] 20-throw run of the 4-ball fountain async and sync.
