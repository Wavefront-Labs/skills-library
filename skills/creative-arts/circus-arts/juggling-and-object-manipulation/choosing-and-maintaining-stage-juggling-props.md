# Choosing and maintaining stage juggling props

Props are your instruments. A set that behaves the same way every time makes the act reliable.

## Choosing
- Choose props to suit the stage: visibility (colour against the backdrop), noise (bouncing balls versus beanbags), and the surface.
- Matched sets: same weight, size and bounce within a set. Mismatched props make the pattern uneven.
- Grip: choose handles with the surface you are used to; replace worn tape or wrap before it becomes slippery.
- Consider the venue floor: bounce props need a hard floor; beanbags are friendly to carpet and stage.

## Maintenance
- Inspect before each show: seams, splits, leaks, dents, loose handles and worn grips.
- Clean props; dust and sweat change the feel.
- Keep spares of the key props in the same condition as the main set.
- Store props in a case, away from heat, and label sets.

## Replacement rules
- Replace any prop that has changed weight or balance.
- Retire props that have repeated damage; never perform with a prop you do not trust.

## Travel
- Carry props in hand luggage when you fly: lost luggage is a disaster for an act.
- Check the venue's rules for prop use before arrival.

## Verify
- [ ] Each set is matched and has a labelled spare.
- [ ] A pre-show inspection checklist is in the prop case.
