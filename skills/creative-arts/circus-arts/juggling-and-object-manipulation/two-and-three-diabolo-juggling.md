# Two and three diabolo

Multiple diabolo work uses two or three diabolos on one string or alternating between strings. The spin management is the main challenge.

## Prerequisites
- Excellent single-diabolo acceleration, throw and catch.
- The ability to control tilt on each diabolo.

## Two diabolos on one string
1. Place two diabolos on the string at about the same distance from the centre.
2. Accelerate them in alternate pumps so each gets equal spin.
3. Keep the string taut and the diabolos separated; they tend to collide if the string slackens.

## Throwing and catching with two
1. Throw one diabolo and catch it while the other stays on the string.
2. Throw both together, then catch both; keep the sticks level.

## Three diabolos
Three diabolos need fast acceleration and short, reliable throws. Learn the transitions from two diabolos with small, repeated drills before attempting three.

## Practice rules
- Use diabolos of matching weight and size.
- Keep the spin equal; the one with less spin will drop first.
- Clear the practice space: tangled strings or collisions can send a diabolo anywhere.

## Faults
- Uneven spin: pump the slower diabolo.
- String tangled around a stick: unwind before the next trick.

## Verify
- [ ] Two diabolos on one string at a steady hum for 30 seconds.
- [ ] A throw and catch of one diabolo while the other remains on the string, 5 times in a row.
