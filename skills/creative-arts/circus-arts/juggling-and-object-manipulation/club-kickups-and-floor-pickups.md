# Club kickups and floor pickups

A kickup is a foot-flicked pickup that sends a club from the floor back into the hands and into the pattern, saving a stoop and keeping the rhythm.

## Set-up
- Floor: smooth, non-slip, clean, and level. Wet or dusty floors cause slides.
- Shoes: light shoes with a flat sole; the toe must not catch the floor.

## Technique (one club first)
1. Place the club on the floor.
2. Hook the toe under the head or barrel, pull it back slightly, and flick it upward into the juggling plane.
3. Catch it with the hand as it rises, in the same position as a normal throw.
4. Learn with each foot.

## With a pattern
1. Juggle three clubs; deliberately drop one and use the kickup to restart it.
2. Time the kick with the pattern so that the club rejoins at the right beat.
3. Practise sequences: drop, kickup, catch, continue.

## Faults
- Too strong: the club rises over the head.
- Foot too late: the club has already lost momentum.
- Staying in the stoop: the knees stay bent; stand tall.

## Safety
Keep ankles warm and flexible. Do not kick clubs near spectators. Use a low-gloss floor for practice.

## Verify
- [ ] 10 clean kickups of a single club in each foot.
- [ ] A drop-and-kickup sequence in a three-club pattern, 3 times in a row.
