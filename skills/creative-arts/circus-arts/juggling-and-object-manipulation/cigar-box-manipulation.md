# Cigar box manipulation

Cigar boxes (also called box juggling) is a manipulation form in which two or three boxes are held, moved and exchanged, typically by balancing one on top of another and moving them between the hands.

## Equipment
- Use matched boxes of the same dimension and weight, with smooth surfaces and rounded edges; plastic or wood are common.
- Keep corners smooth and check edges for splinters or cracks.

## Basic holds
1. Hold two boxes, one in each hand, flat and parallel.
2. Learn to place one on top of the other and hold them as a single stack.
3. Hold three boxes by combining stacks and singles.

## Switches
1. Start with two boxes: swap the boxes by moving one from below to above the other.
2. Slide the top box from one hand to the other with a small shove.
3. Add a third box and a rhythm.

## Drills
- Practise the exchange pattern slowly, with a metronome, and speed it up only when it is clean.
- Use a mirror for symmetry.

## Faults
- Boxes slide off: the surface is too slick; keep them dry and clean.
- Dropping a corner on the floor: use a mat while learning.

## Verify
- [ ] 20 consecutive exchanges between two boxes.
- [ ] A short sequence with three boxes repeated three times.
