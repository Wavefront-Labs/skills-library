# Club flats and helicopters

A helicopter is a club thrown so that it spins horizontally (flat, parallel to the floor) like a helicopter rotor. Flats are throws that spin in a flat plane across the juggler's front instead of the normal vertical plane.

## Getting started
1. With a single club, throw the club so that it rotates flat about its centre of mass and returns to the same hand. Use a gentle throw at about head height.
2. Catch the club by the handle. The hand must meet the handle as it passes; this takes practice.
3. Reduce the body rotation: the throw comes mostly from the wrist and forearm, not the torso.

## In a pattern
- Replace one cascade throw by a flat; the other clubs keep their normal spin.
- Keep the height of the trick throw slightly higher to give the trick time.
- Practise in both hands and in reverse.

## Faults
- Club drifting forward: the throw is leaning; check the flick.
- Catches on the knob end: the number of rotations is wrong; adjust height.
- Missing the handle: practise single-club drills until the catch is automatic.

## Safety
Horizontal spinning clubs sweep a wide area. Clear a wide space around you and any bystanders, and keep faces and windows out of reach.

## Verify
- [ ] 10 clean single-club helicopters with a handle catch.
- [ ] 5 flats slotted into a three-club cascade without a drop.
