# Fire prop safety and fuel handling

Fire spinning is high-risk work. This is practice guidance, not a licence to perform. Learn from an experienced fire performer, follow local law, and obtain the venue's and the authority's permission and insurance cover. In the US, flame effects before an audience are covered by NFPA 160; check the equivalent local rules elsewhere.

## Before you light anything
- Master the move with unlit practice props, and rehearse the full routine in the same space with unlit props.
- Do not perform fire moves that you cannot do reliably without fire.

## Props, fuel and wicks
- Use props built for fire (rated wicks, secure attachment of wick and cord); inspect for loose or frayed material before each session.
- Use a fuel intended for fire props; never use petrol or gasoline.
- Store fuel in a closed, approved container away from any flame, audience and spectators; refuel only in a separate area, with the props unlit and cooled.
- Soak the wick briefly and remove excess fuel before lighting; excess fuel runs and drips.

## Safety person and kit
- At least one trained safety person who is not performing, watching the performer and the surrounding area.
- A fire blanket or wet towels ready; a suitable extinguisher for the fuel in use, within reach.
- A planned way to extinguish the props at the end of the routine (such as a fire blanket or damp towel in a safe place).

## Performer
- Tie back and cover hair; no loose, synthetic or flammable clothing or hair products; natural fabrics (cotton, wool) are preferred.
- Remove jewellery that could catch.
- Know the stop-drop-and-roll or smother procedure and rehearse it.

## Space
- Clear ceiling height and a clear radius around the performer, non-combustible surface, spectators well back, no wind that can fan flame toward people.

## Verify
- [ ] Permission, insurance and local rules confirmed in writing.
- [ ] Safety person, fire blanket and extinguisher in position, and rehearsed.
