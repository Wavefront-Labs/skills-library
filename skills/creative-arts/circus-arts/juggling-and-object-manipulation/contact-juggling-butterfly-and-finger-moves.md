# Contact juggling butterfly and finger moves

Finger moves keep the ball moving on and between the fingertips, with the palm as a base for resets. "Butterfly" is a name used for a move in which the ball is passed between the hands while the wrists rotate; the exact move named butterfly differs between juggling communities, so look at a demonstration on the Library of Juggling (https://libraryofjuggling.com) and agree the name with your coach.

## Finger rolls
1. Hold the ball on the fingertips with the thumb alongside.
2. Roll the ball from the index fingertip across the others by tilting the hand.
3. Finish with the ball on the little finger, then tilt back.

## Hand-to-hand transfers
1. Start with the ball in the left palm; rotate the palm so the fingers point to the right.
2. Place the right palm under the ball as the left hand slips away.
3. Keep the ball in contact with a hand at all times; do not toss it.
4. Practise transferring slowly, then with the two forearms crossing and uncrossing.

## Practice rules
- Work over a soft surface.
- Keep fingers relaxed; flex only where the ball touches.
- Practise both hands equally.

## Faults
- The ball sticks: wipe the ball and the palms.
- Tight fingers: the ball squirts forward; keep a soft hand.

## Verify
- [ ] 10 slow hand-to-hand transfers in each direction.
- [ ] A one-minute sequence of finger rolls and transfers.
