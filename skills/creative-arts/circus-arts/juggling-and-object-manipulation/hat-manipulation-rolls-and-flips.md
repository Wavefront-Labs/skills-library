# Hat rolls and flips

Hat manipulation uses a hat as a juggling object and a character piece. Tricks include rolling the hat along the arms and across the shoulders, and flipping it to the head or to the other hand.

## Choosing the hat
- A stiff-brimmed hat with a good, firm crown (felt or a stage hat) is much easier to control than a floppy one.
- Keep a matched spare: the performance hat will bend.

## Rolls
1. Hold the hat in the palm with the brim vertical.
2. Roll it along the arm by tilting the arm; keep the rolling surface level and slightly tilted so the hat moves under gravity.
3. Roll it across the shoulders: keep the shoulders level and the head upright.
4. Catch with the other hand or place on the head.

## Flips
1. Throw the hat with a small lift and a spin so that it turns over once.
2. Catch it by the brim with the other hand, or aim it onto the head.
3. Practise with each hand; use an easy distance.

## Timing
Plan each trick as a sequence of three beats: set, move, finish. Repeat them as a rhythmic phrase.

## Faults
- Hat flying off the arm: the tilt is too steep.
- Brim catching on the nose: the head is too forward.

## Verify
- [ ] 5 rolls across both shoulders in one direction without a drop.
- [ ] 5 flips onto the head in a row.
