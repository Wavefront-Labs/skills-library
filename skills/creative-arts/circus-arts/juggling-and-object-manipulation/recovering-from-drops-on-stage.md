# Recovering from drops on stage

Every juggler drops. The skill is making the recovery invisible or part of the act.

## Plan the drop
- Decide in rehearsal how each trick is recovered: kickup, a quick pickup, a replacement prop, or a clean restart.
- Place a spare prop on the stage in a clear spot (a prop stash), and tell the stage crew so it is not moved.
- Learn the safe fall of props: a ball that rolls away is gone for the act; use a soft floor or a stage mat.

## The recovery beat
1. Pause only as long as needed to find a solution.
2. Stay in character: keep the same energy, look at the audience, and let the pause speak.
3. Make the recovery fit the act; a clown can play it; a classic act smooths it.

## Techniques
- Kickups and foot pickups where the stage surface allows (see club kickups).
- Quick pickups that continue the pattern from a bent knee.
- Replacement: step to the stash and continue.

## Rehearsal
- Rehearse planned drops: deliberately drop in each section and recover.
- Rehearse unplanned drops from varied tricks in each run-through.
- Run the act with a hostile floor (slippery, slightly tilted) to test.

## Verify
- [ ] Every trick has a recovery plan.
- [ ] Prop stash agreed with crew and cleared before each show.
