# Flash training and qualifying runs

**Goal:** build towards a new number of objects with measurable milestones.

## Definitions (community conventions)
- **Flash**: every object thrown once and caught once (n throws, n catches).
- **Qualify**: every object thrown at least twice and caught - i.e. 2n catches for n
  objects. The usual "I can juggle n" threshold.
- **Run**: number of catches before a drop or stop.
Record conventions for official records (video, controlled start and finish) are
published by juggling record-keeping groups; check them before claiming.

## Progression for a new number
1. **Half flashes**: throw part of the pattern (e.g. 3 of 5) at the right height.
2. **Flash** repeatedly until 8 of 10 attempts are clean.
3. **Flash plus**: flash and keep one or two extra throws.
4. **Qualify**.
5. Extend runs.

## Logging
For each session: attempts, flashes made, best run, average run. Plot best and average
weekly - averages move before personal bests.

## Practice tips
- Fresh arms: numbers work first in a session.
- Collect drops quickly (a bucket or bag) to keep attempt density high.
- Film attempts to check heights and drift.

## Verify
- [ ] Flash success rate over 80% before moving to qualify attempts.
- [ ] Log kept for each new number.
