# Contact juggling palm spins and circles

Contact juggling uses a ball that always stays in touch with the body (or the floor) rather than being thrown. Acrylic balls are the usual prop; common sizes are around 70 to 100 mm, and many people start around 76 mm. Sizes vary; choose a ball that suits your hand.

## Palm spin
1. Hold the ball in the palm with the fingers curved slightly.
2. Keep the hand level; rotate the forearm and wrist so that the ball rotates around its own axis while staying on the palm.
3. Keep the palm slightly cupped at the beginning, then flatten gradually.

## Palm circle
1. Roll the ball in a small circle on the palm; the hand moves in a circle under it so that the ball stays almost at the same point in space.
2. Practise both directions; practise with each hand.
3. Combine it with the palm spin.

## Practice tips
- Sit on the floor or above a soft surface so that a dropped ball does not chip or roll away.
- Keep the ball clean and dry: dust and sweat change friction; wipe with a soft cloth.
- Use a mirror to check that the ball does not drift.

## Faults
- Gripping: keep the hand relaxed.
- Wrist stiffness: use the forearm and shoulder to make the movement.

## Verify
- [ ] 1 minute of palm circles in each direction with each hand without a drop.
