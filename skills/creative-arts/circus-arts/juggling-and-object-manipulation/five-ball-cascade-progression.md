# Five-ball cascade progression

**Goal:** a sustained five-ball cascade (siteswap 5), the benchmark of a serious toss
juggler.

## Prerequisites
- Solid 3-ball cascade, 100+ catches.
- 4-ball fountain and comfortable high throws.
- 5s with 3 balls: siteswap `5` throws are cascade throws at 5-ball height.

## The progression (siteswap drills)
Ball count = average of the digits; check any drill with the validity rules.
1. **3 balls - `50505`**: every other beat a 5-height cascade throw. Builds height and
   accuracy with lots of time.
2. **3 balls - `55500`**: three 5s in a row, then two empty beats. This is the 5-ball
   rhythm with only three balls in the air.
3. **4 balls - `5551`**: three 5s and a quick 1 pass; adds a fourth ball.
4. **5 balls - flash**: throw all five as 5s, catch all five. Repeat until clean.
5. **5 balls - `744`**: a 3-throw pattern that builds 5-ball height and timing.
6. **5 balls - `645`**: the step between 744 and the cascade.
7. **Run the cascade**: 5 balls, aim for 10, 15, 20 catches.

## Practice structure
- Short sessions (20-30 min) of focused attempts; quality > volume.
- Count catches every attempt; log best and average.
- Juggle facing a wall to stop forward drift.

## Faults
- Throws drifting forward: wall drill.
- Uneven heights: return to 3-ball 5s and `55500`.
- Rushing (throws too low): count out the rhythm.

## Verify
- [ ] Clean 5-ball flash (5 throws, 5 catches) 10 times.
- [ ] 20-catch 5-ball cascade run.
