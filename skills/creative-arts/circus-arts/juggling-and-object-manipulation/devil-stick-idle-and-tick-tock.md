# Devil stick idle and tick-tock

A devil stick is a tapered central stick, controlled by two handsticks held one in each hand.

## Set-up
- Hold a handstick in each hand; hold the devil stick between them with the thumbs and fingers loosely.
- Use rubber or foam-tipped handsticks for a quieter, softer feel.

## Idling
1. Hold the devil stick vertically in front of the body.
2. Tap it alternately with the handsticks near its centre, in short, alternating hits.
3. Keep the stick near vertical and let it move only a short distance from side to side.
4. Learn the sound and rhythm of hits and adjust the strength: soft taps keep it up; hard hits cause it to move away.
Idling is the basic holding pattern, and every trick starts and ends with it.

## Tick-tock
Tick-tock is a rhythmic rocking of the stick between the handsticks: each handstick hits the stick in turn to rock it from side to side. Practise it slowly, keeping the stick near the centre of the body.

## Common problems
- The stick drifts forward: the handsticks are hitting too low or too hard.
- Dropping toward one side: one handstick is hitting later than the other.
- Gripping: let the stick move between the handsticks.

## Training
1. Dry run without the stick: just the hands' rhythm.
2. Idle for 10 seconds, 30 seconds, 1 minute.
3. Add tick-tock for 10 rocks, then 20, then a minute.

## Verify
- [ ] 1 minute of idling without a drop.
- [ ] 20 consecutive tick-tock rocks.
