# Juggling under stage light and ceiling height

Venues rarely look like the studio, and lighting and ceilings change what a juggler can do.

## Ceiling and rigging
- Measure the clear height at your working spot in each venue, including lights, curtains and trusses.
- Know your throw peak height for each trick and keep it well below the lowest obstruction.
- If the ceiling is low, replace high throws with lower patterns; do not risk hitting equipment.

## Light
- Spotlights and front light can create glare directly over the top of your throws. Practise looking at the pattern with the stage light in your eyes.
- Test the stage with the rig turned on before the show, with the actual lights, not the work lights.
- Props with a dark colour disappear against a dark background; choose a colour that reads against the backdrop.

## Background and contrast
- Bright props against a dark curtain read well; dark props against a bright background read poorly.
- Reflective or glow props help in dim light but test them under the real rig.

## Preparation
1. Visit the stage early and run the act at working speed under the lights.
2. Adjust the throw height, the tricks and the position as needed.
3. Brief the lighting operator on your position and your tricks.

## Verify
- [ ] Ceiling height measured and recorded for each venue.
- [ ] A run under the real lighting before the show.
