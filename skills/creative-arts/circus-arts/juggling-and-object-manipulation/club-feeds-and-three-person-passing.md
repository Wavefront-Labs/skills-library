# Feeds and three-person club passing

Feeding and three-person patterns extend passing beyond a pair. In a feed, one or more jugglers pass clubs to a main juggler whose pattern is larger than anyone could sustain alone, and who works as the centre of the pattern. Three-person patterns put three jugglers in a triangle, line or circle with agreed pass routes.

## Foundations
- All jugglers can sustain a stable pair pattern with at least two partners.
- Learn the individual pair patterns first, then the three-person link.

## Roles
- **Main juggler / centre:** concentrates on the large pattern and calls the start.
- **Feeder(s):** pass at the call; their timing depends entirely on the main juggler.
- **Spotter/caller (optional):** a non-juggling person who calls the pattern to keep everyone in step.

## Set-up
1. Draw the geometry on the floor with tape: positions, distances, and each pass path.
2. Walk the passing paths without clubs to verify there are no crossing paths at the same moment.
3. Add one club per person, then the full number.

## Calls and stop rule
Use one start call and one stop word that anyone can say. After a stop call everyone lets their clubs go to the floor or holds the pattern, as agreed beforehand.

## Notes
Feeding is usually learned with an experienced main juggler. I have not described specific throw sequences because they differ by pattern; read the reference for the specific pattern.

## Verify
- [ ] Geometry marked and walked through at slow speed before clubs are used.
- [ ] Start and stop calls agreed and rehearsed.
