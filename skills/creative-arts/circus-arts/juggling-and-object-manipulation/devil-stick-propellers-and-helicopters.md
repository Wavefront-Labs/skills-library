# Devil stick propellers and helicopters

Propellers and helicopter-style moves spin the stick end over end (propeller-like) or in a flat plane (helicopter-like) with the handsticks controlling it. The names are used differently by different jugglers, so check a reference (such as the Library of Juggling, https://libraryofjuggling.com) and agree the name with your coach.

## Prerequisites
- A reliable idle and tick-tock.
- Short spins of the stick in the air with a controlled catch.

## General method
1. From idle, give the stick a controlled flick with one handstick so that it starts rotating.
2. Follow it with the other handstick to keep the spin going or to stop it.
3. Keep the spin small first: a half rotation, then a full rotation.
4. Always plan the exit: the handstick that catches the stick should be ready.

## Progression
- One-handed flick and catch.
- Two flicks per rotation to keep a continuous propeller.
- Slow acceleration and deceleration.

## Faults
- The stick drifts away: hit closer to its centre.
- Mistimed catch: slow the spin and count the rotation.
- Hitting the face with the stick: keep the pattern low and clear.

## Space and safety
Keep the practice space clear; devil sticks travel and strike. Wear eye protection if you practise with others close by.

## Verify
- [ ] 5 controlled half-rotations returning to idle.
- [ ] A full rotation caught and returned to idle, 5 times in a row.
