# Poi planes, weaves and timing

Poi are weights on cords swung in circles. Most beginner moves can be placed on a simple grid of plane, time and direction.

## Planes
- **Wall plane:** circles in front of the body, facing the audience.
- **Wheel plane:** circles at the sides, like a wheel beside the body.
- **Floor plane:** horizontal circles around the body parallel to the floor.
Learn each plane with one poi first, then with both.

## Timing and direction
- **Same-time:** both poi are at the same point of their circles at the same moment.
- **Split-time:** the poi are half a revolution apart (one at the top when the other is at the bottom).
- **Same-direction / opposite-direction:** both circle the same way or opposite ways.
That gives four basic combinations for each plane.

## Weave
Weaves are patterns in which the poi pass in front of the body in an alternating, crossing way. The exact definition of 'the weave' differs by community, so look up a demonstration and agree the name with your teacher.

## Training
1. Practise each plane with one poi in each hand, with a count: "one, two, three, four" on the quarter points.
2. Add the second poi with a metronome or music at a slow tempo.
3. Link planes with a turn of the wrist or body, keeping the poi circles regular.

## Tips
- Keep the arms and wrists relaxed; the poi's weight does the work.
- Use soft (practice) poi, such as sock poi or tennis balls in socks, and stand in a clear area.
- Film yourself to check the circles are round and the planes are clean.

## Verify
- [ ] 8 bars of each plane in same-time and split-time, no wraps or collisions.
