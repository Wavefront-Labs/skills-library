# Contact juggling body rolls

A body roll moves the ball across the arms, shoulders and chest on a continuous path. The body forms a ramp that the ball rolls along.

## Principles
- The ball needs a continuous surface; keep the contact points joined so no gap opens under it.
- Tilt the arm gradually so the ball rolls, not slides.
- Move the support slightly ahead of the ball to stay under it.

## Arm roll
1. Hold the arm straight and slightly tilted from the shoulder, ball in the hand.
2. Tilt the hand so the ball rolls toward the elbow; raise the shoulder as the ball arrives.
3. Roll the ball across the shoulder and chest to the other arm.
4. Keep the tilt shallow; steep angles cause the ball to run away.

## Chest roll
1. Round the chest and shoulders slightly forward to create a trough.
2. Roll the ball from one shoulder to the other with the chest as the ramp.
3. Practise slowly, then add a turn of the body.

## Practice tips
- Practise over a bed or soft mat. Acrylic balls chip and bounce.
- Wear a plain long-sleeved top: sleeves with zips or buttons scratch the ball and catch it.

## Verify
- [ ] 10 arm-to-arm rolls across the shoulders without a drop.
- [ ] A complete chest roll in each direction.
