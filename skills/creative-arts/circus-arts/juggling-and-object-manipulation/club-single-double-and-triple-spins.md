# Single, double and triple club spins

A juggling club is thrown with spin so that it returns to the receiving hand handle-first. The number of spins depends on the throw height and the wrist flick.

## Grip and release
- Hold the club by the handle near the taper (not by the knob), thumb along the side.
- The wrist snaps to give rotation; the arm lifts to set the height. Throw the club upright, not slanted.
- A single spin makes one full turn between hands: the normal three-club cascade.

## Increasing spins
- A double spin needs more height and more wrist snap; a triple needs more again.
- Practise with one club at a time. Aim to catch the handle: if you catch the head, the spin count is wrong.
- Many jugglers use double spins for the five-club cascade and triples for larger numbers; use whichever suits the height and your hands.

## Drills
1. One club: single spins from hand to hand for 50 throws, then 20 doubles.
2. Two clubs: one hand throws double, the other throws single, to learn to match airtime to spin count.
3. Three clubs: combine singles, then doubles where one throw per cycle is a double.
4. Add triples one at a time.

## Faults
- Clubs wobbling: the club is thrown with the head tilted; check the throwing plane.
- Handle catches missed by a half-turn: add or reduce height rather than changing the wrist alone.

## Verify
- [ ] 20 consecutive single-spin throws in each hand.
- [ ] 10 doubles in each hand with the handle caught correctly.
