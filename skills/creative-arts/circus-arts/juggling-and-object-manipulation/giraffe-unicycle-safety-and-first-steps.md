# Giraffe unicycle: safety and first steps

A giraffe is a chain-driven unicycle with the seat high above a small wheel, so the fall is from height and the chain is a pinch/entanglement point. Treat it as height work: train with a qualified coach or an experienced giraffe rider, with spotters.

## Prerequisites
- Comfortable on a standard unicycle: free mount, riding, idling, backward riding, and a sound controlled dismount.
- Fit and loose-limbed enough to dismount quickly on the spot.

## Equipment checks (every session)
- Chain tension and condition; a chain guard if the design has one; the chain should not be loose enough to jump the sprockets.
- Frame, forks, cranks, seat clamp and wheel mounting checked for cracks and play.
- Tyre pressure.
- Keep the performer's costume clear of the chain and sprockets (no loose cuffs or ribbons).

## Learning set-up
- Smooth, level floor; clear zone around the riding area; helmet, wrist guards and pads.
- Ceiling clearance: measure the height of rider plus apparatus plus the arm and prop reach; lights, trusses and rigging must be well above it. Check this in each venue.
- A helper holding a rail or walking alongside; a wall for the first mounts.

## Mounting
Methods vary by height and design: mounting from a wall, from a helper holding the frame, or later a free mount by stepping onto the pedal with the wheel rolling. Learn the method with an instructor; do not improvise.

## Falls and dismounts
- Plan the exit: dismount forward by stepping off, or slide off the back; do not fight a fall by gripping the seat.
- Practise controlled forward dismounts on the lowest possible giraffe first and raise height in stages.
- Never perform with an apparatus you have not practised on at that height.

## Performance notes
Balance props (juggling on a giraffe) multiply the risk: first train each prop skill on a regular unicycle. Check venue ceiling height every time and place spotters.

## Verify
- [ ] Rider can mount, ride a circle, stop and dismount in a controlled way, five times in a row, at the working height.
- [ ] Ceiling and floor checked and recorded for the venue.
