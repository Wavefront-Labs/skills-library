# Passing tricks: tomahawk and early double

Trick passes alter one throw in an otherwise steady passing pattern. Both partners must know in advance which throw is the trick.

## Names (and a caution)
- A **tomahawk** pass is thrown from above the head with a chopping, overhand motion so that the club turns in a vertical plane towards the partner, like throwing an axe.
- An **early double** is a pass that is thrown early and with a double spin. Definitions vary between groups, so check the Library of Juggling (https://libraryofjuggling.com) or ask the partner exactly which they mean.

## Preconditions
- Both jugglers can hold the base pattern for several minutes without drops.
- The tricks are rehearsed with one club first, with the partner catching it as a pass.

## Learning order
1. One club: thrower works on the trick throw; the partner catches it by the handle.
2. Two clubs: throw the trick then the self throw.
3. Whole pattern: the trick on one beat of the cycle, then on repeat.
4. Swap roles; let the partner introduce a trick.

## Rules
- Agree the call word for the trick ("trick" or its name) and call it before the throw.
- A bad trick throw is dropped or let go; the receiver does not lunge for it.
- Keep spacing wide enough so that tomahawk clubs have clear air; no bystanders in the plane of the throw.

## Faults
- Too early or late: slow the base count and retime the trick.
- Throwing at the partner's head rather than the hand.

## Verify
- [ ] Each trick lands with the handle in the catcher's hand five times in a row.
- [ ] Both partners can name the trick and the beat at which it occurs.
