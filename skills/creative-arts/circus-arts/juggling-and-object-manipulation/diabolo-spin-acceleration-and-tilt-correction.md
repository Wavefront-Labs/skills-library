# Diabolo spin acceleration and tilt correction

A diabolo is a spinning spool that rides on a string held between two sticks. Spin gives it gyroscopic stability, so keeping the spin up is the foundation of every other trick.

## Setting the diabolo up
- Place the diabolo on the floor with the string passing under the axle.
- Hold one stick in each hand, string taut and level.

## Acceleration
1. Start the diabolo rolling slightly with the string.
2. Lift one stick, then the other, in alternate small movements so the string pulls the axle round. Use short, quick, whipping pulls that drag the string along the axle and speed it up.
3. Keep the diabolo centred on the string.
4. Stop pumping only when the diabolo reaches a consistent hum or steady spin.

## Tilt
A tilted axle (one cup lower than the other) causes the diabolo to slide to one side of the string. Correct tilt by changing the relative height of the sticks: raise one stick slightly relative to the other. Which hand to raise depends on the direction of the lean and your spin direction; watch the axle at slow speed to learn which one works for you, and adjust a little at a time.

## Training drills
1. Accelerate to a hum, hold 30 seconds.
2. Deliberately tilt, then correct.
3. Move the diabolo along the string, left and right, keeping it centred.

## Faults
- Pulling too hard and dropping the diabolo off the string.
- Letting the string go slack: keep tension.
- Spin direction mismatch with your habits: practise in both directions.

## Safety
Hard or heavy diabolos can hurt: keep space around you; do not practise near windows or people.
