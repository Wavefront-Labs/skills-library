# Unicycle idling and backwards riding

**Goal:** control the wheel in both directions while staying on the spot, then ride backwards.

## Prerequisites
Reliable free mount, riding forward, turning, and stopping. Work next to a wall or rail at first.

## Idling
Idling means rocking the wheel a few centimetres forward and back while the pedals stay level (horizontal, cranks roughly parallel to the floor).
1. Mount, ride forward, then stop with the pedals level.
2. Press the front pedal slightly to roll forward, then press the rear pedal to roll back. The motion is tiny and rhythmic; the wheel travels only a few centimetres each way.
3. Keep your weight on the seat. Keep the upper body quiet and the hips loose so the wheel moves under you.
4. Build the count: five rocks, ten rocks, then a minute.
Typical faults: cranks drifting off horizontal (let them drift and you will fall in one direction), over-large movements, and gripping the seat.

## Backwards riding
1. Idle with growing amplitude until you can roll back a full half revolution.
2. Lean the hips very slightly back and keep pedalling backwards; look over the shoulder to find a direction, keep the head upright.
3. Steer by turning the shoulders and hips; a small wobble is corrected by pedalling a little more.
4. Practise along a line with a wall or rail to touch.

## Progressions
- One-foot idling: take one foot off the pedal while idling, rest it on the frame (the crown), idle with the other.
- Idling while holding the seat in one hand, then both hands free.
- Linking idling into forward and backward riding in a sequence for performance.

## Verify
- [ ] 30 seconds of continuous idling with pedals staying level.
- [ ] 10 m backwards with one controlled stop.
