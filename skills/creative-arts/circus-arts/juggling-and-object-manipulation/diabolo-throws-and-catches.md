# Diabolo throws and catches

A throw sends the diabolo up, with its spin, so that it returns to the string. It is the base for most aerial diabolo tricks.

## Prerequisites
A diabolo that is accelerated, centred, and held stable for at least a minute.

## The vertical throw
1. Accelerate and settle the diabolo near one end of the string.
2. Lift the sticks together in a smooth upward movement and then spread them slightly so that the string flicks the diabolo into the air.
3. Keep the sticks level and wide; the diabolo should rise vertically, not spin off sideways.
4. Watch the diabolo, not the sticks.

## The catch
1. As the diabolo falls, move the sticks so the string meets the diabolo with the axle centred.
2. Keep the string taut and level at contact.
3. Let the string absorb the fall; move the hands in the same direction as the fall for the first few centimetres.
4. If the diabolo comes down on a tilt, correct the tilt before the catch.

## Variants
- Sideways throws and catches to the other end of the string.
- Higher throws as strength and control allow.
- Throw and catch behind the back, only after the base throw is consistent.

## Faults
- The diabolo flies off sideways: the sticks are not level or equal.
- It bounces off the string: the string was loose at contact.

## Safety
Keep the face and eyes out of the fall line, and use a diabolo with a rubber or soft outer edge in busy areas.

## Verify
- [ ] 10 throws and catches in a row at moderate height.
