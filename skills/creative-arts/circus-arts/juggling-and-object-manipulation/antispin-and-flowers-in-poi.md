# Antispin and flowers in poi

A flower is a poi pattern in which the head makes petal-shaped loops around a larger circle made by the hand.

## Terms
- **Inspin:** the poi head and the hand travel around their circles in the same direction, producing loops that point toward the centre (the 'inside').
- **Antispin:** the head and the hand circle in opposite directions, making loops pointing outward.
- The number of petals depends on how many head rotations occur for each hand rotation.

## Learning antispin (one poi)
1. Make a slow hand circle in the wall or wheel plane.
2. Spin the poi head the opposite way around the hand circle.
3. Adjust the speed so the hand circles once while the poi makes the number of petals you chose.
4. Practise with each hand.

## Learning inspin (one poi)
1. Hand and head circle in the same direction.
2. Match the hand speed to the head speed so the petals line up.

## Two poi
- Start with same-time same-direction flowers.
- Move to split-time flowers once the two hands are accurate.

## Faults
- Uneven petals: the hand speed is varying; slow down and count.
- Collisions between the poi: stagger the poi at the start.
- Wrapped cords: practise unwinding before starting.

## Safety
Use practice poi until you can control the flower without hitting yourself; keep a clear space.

## Verify
- [ ] A clean 3-petal and 4-petal antispin in each hand, held for 8 turns.
