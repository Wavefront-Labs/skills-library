# Walkabout character interaction

**Goal:** create brief, memorable encounters with passing audiences as a roving
character, without making anyone uncomfortable.

## The encounter cycle (30 s to 3 min)
1. **Approach** within the person's view; never from behind.
2. **Offer**: a look, a gesture, a prop, a small game.
3. **Read**: smiles and eye contact = continue; turning away, children hiding = leave
   gracefully.
4. **Play**: one small game or bit.
5. **Exit** while they still want more; leave them with a gift moment (a bow, a sticker,
   a photo).

## Consent and care
- Children: interact through the parent; never pick up or touch without clear consent.
- Wheelchair users and people with assistance dogs: approach at eye level, no grabbing.
- Never touch anyone's belongings without permission.
- Fear of clowns is real; respect it immediately.

## Working in pairs
A partner watches your back, manages crowd and space, handles photos and keeps time.
Agree a cue to move on.

## Practicalities
- Hydration and breaks: walkabout sets are usually short (30-45 min) with breaks.
- Stilts or large costumes need a minder always.
- Know where first aid, lost children point and organisers are.

## Gotchas
- Staying too long with one group while others wait.
- Character drift into normal chat; keep the character.

## Verify
- [ ] Clear exit cue with partner.
- [ ] Children approached only via parents.
- [ ] Break schedule agreed with organiser.
