# Pleasure and play states in clown

In clown teaching, the clown's pleasure in playing is what draws the audience in. A clown who is enjoying the moment makes the audience want to enjoy it too.

## What play looks like
- The body is relaxed, the breath is open, and the clown is interested in what happens next.
- Actions are simple and fully committed.
- Mistakes and surprises are welcomed.

## Finding the state
1. Warm up with games: tag, ball games, clapping rhythms.
2. Take the playful state into a simple task (stack three chairs, pick up a hat).
3. Notice what stops pleasure: self-judgement, trying to be funny, planning ahead.
4. Return to the simple game each time pleasure disappears.

## Keeping it alive
- Treat each repetition as the first: look for something new in the task.
- Take breaks and change the game when it becomes mechanical.
- Use the audience: their reaction is part of the play.

## Exercises
- A simple game with a partner for two minutes, with one rule: have fun.
- A solo task (open a door) where the only aim is pleasure.
- Observers watch and note when the pleasure is real.

## Faults
- Forcing laughter or a smile.
- Showing off rather than playing.
- Planning the next gag during the current one.

## Verify
- [ ] An observer can identify a moment of real play.
- [ ] The clown can return to play after a failed beat.
