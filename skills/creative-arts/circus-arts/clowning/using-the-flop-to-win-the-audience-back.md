# Using the flop to win the audience back

In clown teaching, particularly in the Lecoq lineage, the flop is the moment when the clown's attempt fails and the audience does not respond. The flop is treated as part of the work, not an accident, because it shows the clown honestly and invites the audience's sympathy.

## What happens in a flop
- The clown tries something; there is silence.
- The clown feels it, and the audience sees them feel it.
- The moment is awkward, and that is where the audience can start to like the clown.

## Playing the flop
1. Stop. Do not rush to the next gag.
2. Let the feeling show: embarrassment, disappointment, bafflement.
3. Share it with the audience in an open look.
4. Wait for a response; often a laugh arrives from the honesty.
5. Offer something simple and different, not a bigger version of the same thing.

## What not to do
- Do not explain or apologise.
- Do not repeat the failed gag louder.
- Do not blame the audience or the partner.

## Exercises
- Exercise on purpose: do a deliberately dull action, accept the silence, and share it.
- Partner exercise: one partner is silent; the clown must work with the lack of reaction.
- Observers say when the clown became likeable.

## Verify
- [ ] The clown can sit in silence for several seconds without pushing.
- [ ] After the flop, the next offer is simpler and different.
