# Hospital and therapeutic clowning

**Goal:** bring play into hospitals and care homes in a way that supports patients and
staff, follows clinical rules, and never adds to distress.

> Healthcare clowning is a professional specialism with organisation-led training. This
> skill outlines principles; work under an established programme.

## Working with the ward
- Start each visit with the **handover** from nurses: who is well enough, who is in
  isolation, who wants privacy, what to avoid.
- Never interrupt clinical procedures unless invited (some programmes support
  procedures by distraction, by agreement).

## Infection control
- Hand hygiene between every room; follow ward PPE rules.
- Props that can be wiped down; no shared items between patients without cleaning.
- Respect isolation signs.

## At the bedside
- **Consent** first: knock, wait, ask (often non-verbally). A child saying no is a gift:
  play with the "no" and leave gracefully - it gives them control in a place where they
  have little.
- Lower energy, smaller play than on stage; adapt to fatigue and pain.
- Work in pairs: one plays, one reads the room.
- Include family and staff.

## Adapting play
- Bed-bound: play with what is in reach, small magic, music, puppets.
- Older adults / dementia care: songs from their youth, slow pace, dignity.
- Never make jokes about illness or procedures.

## Debrief
Each visit ends with a debrief with your partner and notes for staff. Emotional load is
high; programmes provide supervision.

## Verify
- [ ] Handover taken before entering any room.
- [ ] Hand hygiene logged between rooms.
- [ ] Debrief completed after the visit.
