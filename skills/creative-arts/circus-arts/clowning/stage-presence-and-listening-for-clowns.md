# Stage presence and listening for clowns

A clown needs presence: a quality of attention that holds the room. Listening is its foundation.

## Stillness
- Stand on stage and do nothing, with the weight even and the breath easy.
- Notice what the audience does when you are still.
- Do not fill the silence; trust that the pause has value.

## Breath
- Breathe low and slowly. Short, high breaths show tension.
- A visible breath shows the audience that the clown is alive and responding.

## Listening
1. Listen to the partner: watch, wait, receive the offer.
2. Listen to the audience: sense when they are with you or drifting.
3. Listen to yourself: notice the impulse before you act.

## Exercises
- Stand and receive: the group looks at you for one minute; do nothing else.
- Pairs: A makes a small action, B waits, then answers with a small response.
- Group: walk the room and stop together without a cue by listening to each other.

## Faults
- Acting before receiving: the clown ignores what has happened.
- Holding the breath.
- Looking busy to avoid the silence.

## Verify
- [ ] The clown can hold a still presence for 30 seconds without discomfort.
- [ ] A partner can describe the offer they received.
