# Clown nose work and the first entrance

The red nose is often called the smallest mask in the world. Putting it on changes how you play: you stop being a person acting and start being a clown reacting.

## Nose work
1. Put the nose on in front of a mirror and see what changes.
2. Wear the nose and take a moment to look at the room and the people in it.
3. Take it off, then put it on again: the transition is the performance.
4. Work with the nose as a prop: the clown may be surprised that the nose is on, or that the audience is looking.

## The first entrance
1. Enter from the side with a simple walk.
2. Stop at the edge of the playing space and look at the audience.
3. Take in the audience: discover them.
4. Let a reaction arrive (pleasure, surprise, shame) and let it be seen before the next action.
5. Only then do the first action.

## Principles
- The entrance should be simple and clear.
- Take the time to let the audience see the clown and the clown see them.
- The clown is always honest about what they feel.

## Common mistakes
- Rushing the entrance: the audience has not seen the clown yet.
- Putting on a face: the clown must actually feel the reaction.
- Looking at the floor rather than the audience.

## Exercises
- Entrance and exit repeated, each with a different feeling.
- Entrance with a partner watching and saying when they believe the clown.

## Verify
- [ ] A new first entrance each rehearsal, with a different first reaction.
- [ ] A partner observer can name the clown's feeling at each stage.
