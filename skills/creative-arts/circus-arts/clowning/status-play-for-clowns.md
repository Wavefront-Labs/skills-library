# Status play for clowns

**Goal:** use status - who is up and who is down in a moment - as the engine of clown
comedy, especially in duos.

## Status markers
**High status**: still head, sustained eye contact, slow movement, takes space, speaks
without hesitations.
**Low status**: moving head, broken eye contact, small quick movements, apologises,
touches face.
Clowns usually play low status, but the comedy comes from **changes** in status.

## Status in clown pairs
- Classic **whiteface** (high, authority) and **auguste** (low, chaos). The whiteface
  sets rules, the auguste breaks them innocently, the whiteface loses status trying to
  restore order.
- The **status reversal** at the end - the low clown accidentally triumphs - is the
  pay-off of many entrees.

## Status with the audience
- A clown who tries for high status with the audience and fails gets the biggest laugh
  (the flop is a status drop).
- Never punch down at volunteers: they must end higher than they started.

## Drills
- **Status walk**: walk with numbered status 1-10 called by coach.
- **See-saw scenes**: two clowns, each line raises one's status and lowers the other's.
- **Master-servant**: Johnstone's exercise, with the servant secretly undermining.

## Verify
- [ ] Clown can switch status on a cue with body and voice.
- [ ] A duo scene contains at least one clear status reversal.
