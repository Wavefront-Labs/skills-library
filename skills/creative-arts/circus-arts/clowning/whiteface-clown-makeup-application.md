# Whiteface makeup application

Whiteface is a classic clown face with a white base, with the features (eyes, mouth, brows) drawn in colour on top.

## Products and skin safety
- Use cosmetic-grade, theatrical greasepaint or makeup made for skin. Do not use craft paint, acrylic or poster paint.
- Check the product's labelling and your local rules for cosmetic products.
- Patch test every new product on the inner forearm and wait 24 hours before using it on the face.
- Keep products clean and do not share sponges, brushes or sticks between performers.
- Take care around the eyes; use products made for the eye area for lines near them.

## Application
1. Clean and moisturise the face; tie back hair.
2. Apply the white base with a clean sponge or fingers in thin, even layers, covering the face to the hairline and under the chin.
3. Blend the edge into the neck or use a clear edge, depending on the design.
4. Set with translucent powder, pressed on with a powder puff, brushing off the excess.
5. Draw the features on top: eyebrows, eyes, mouth, in the design you have chosen.
6. Set the colours with powder again, if needed.

## Design
- Keep the design simple and visible from a distance.
- Test in the venue lighting if possible.
- Draw a design on paper first.

## Removal
- Use a makeup remover or oil, then cleanse; take off the makeup before sleeping.

## Hygiene and care
- Do not wear makeup for longer than necessary.
- Stop immediately if the skin reacts.

## Verify
- [ ] A 24-hour patch test is done for each new product.
- [ ] A photograph of the finished face is taken and checked in the venue light.
