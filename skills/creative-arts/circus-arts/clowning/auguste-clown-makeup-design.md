# Auguste and character makeup design

The auguste clown traditionally wears a flesh or pink-tinted base with exaggerated features. The character or tramp clown has a more naturalistic base with added details (for example, stubble).

## Design process
1. Look at your own face and note the features you can exaggerate: the mouth, the eyes, the brows.
2. Draw on paper in several variations.
3. Choose one that reads clearly from a distance and suits the character.

## Base
- Choose a base colour close to your skin tone or a chosen tint.
- Apply thinly and evenly with a sponge; set with powder.

## Features
- Mouth: widen and outline it in a strong colour.
- Eyes: use outlines or shapes that open the eyes.
- Brows: raise, arch, or lower to show the character.
- Add any special features: a beauty mark, stubble, freckles.

## Colours
- Limit the palette to three or four colours; high-contrast colours read well from the audience.
- Test the colours under stage light; some pigments change.

## Record
- Photograph the finished face from the front and side.
- Keep a reference chart showing where each colour goes and the products used, so you can repeat it exactly.

## Safety
- Patch test products; use cosmetic-grade makeup near the eyes and mouth.
- Do not share sponges or brushes.

## Verify
- [ ] The face reads from the back of the room.
- [ ] A reference photograph and product list are kept.
