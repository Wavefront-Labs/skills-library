# Playing with mistakes and accidents on stage

**Goal:** when something genuinely goes wrong, the clown makes it the best moment of the
show instead of covering it up.

## The sequence
1. **Notice** - stop. Do not pretend it did not happen; the audience saw it.
2. **Share** - look at the audience. This look is the clown's admission and invitation.
3. **React truthfully** - embarrassment, surprise, delight. Small and honest beats big
   and faked.
4. **Play the consequence** - try to fix it, make it worse, blame the prop, try again.
5. **Return** - get back to the material with the audience on your side.

## Examples
- A juggling drop: look at the ball, then the audience; scold the ball; it rolls away;
  chase it; return with a flourish.
- A costume tear: discover it; hide it badly; it becomes the running gag.

## Keeping accidents
If an accident gets a huge laugh, consider rehearsing it into the show - but it must
still look and feel accidental, which is hard. Gaulier's warning: the audience laughs at
the truth of the moment; a planned "accident" that is played as planned dies.

## Gotchas
- Some accidents are safety events (a rigging noise, an injury). Stop the show; clown
  play is not appropriate.
- Over-milking: one accident, one sequence.

## Verify
- [ ] In rehearsal, coach introduces an unplanned mistake; clown runs the five steps.
- [ ] Show notes record accidents and whether they were kept.
