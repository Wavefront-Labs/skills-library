# The hat line and collecting from a street show

**Goal:** a collection that pays for the show, without losing the audience before the
finale.

## When
The hat speech goes **just before the finale**, while the audience is waiting for the
promised big moment. After the finale, people walk away.

## The speech
- Thank them, explain that this is how you earn your living.
- **Set the expectation** with humour: "If you enjoyed the show, the suggested price is
  the same as a coffee... a large one."
- Mention all payment options: cash, card reader, QR code.
- Make it easy and guilt-free: "If you can't give, a round of applause is great too."
- Keep it short (under a minute) and in character.

## Collection logistics
- Hat or bucket at the front, plus a helper or the performer walking the edge after the
  finale.
- A contactless card reader or QR sign visible; increasingly most income in some cities.
- Count later, out of sight; never count money in front of the crowd.

## Holding the crowd
- Finale straight after the speech.
- Thank everyone, stand by the hat; the walk-around after is where much of the money comes.

## Gotchas
- Pitch rules sometimes restrict asking for money or volume; know the local rules.
- Security: keep cash safe and minimal; empty the hat regularly.

## Verify
- [ ] Hat speech scripted, under a minute, rehearsed.
- [ ] At least two payment options visible.
- [ ] Finale immediately follows the speech.
