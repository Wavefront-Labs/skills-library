# Choosing and fitting a clown nose

The clown nose is both a costume piece and a performance tool. A good fit keeps it comfortable and secure.

## Choosing
- **Size:** a larger nose reads from further away but changes your breathing and your face more; a small one is more subtle.
- **Material:** foam noses are light and comfortable; rubber and plastic are firmer; check the edges for sharp or rough areas.
- **Colour:** red is traditional; check how it looks under stage light.

## Fitting
1. Place the nose over your own nose so that the nostrils are not blocked.
2. Hold it with a fitted elastic around the back of the head, or a skin-safe adhesive (check the product and patch test).
3. Check that you can breathe freely through your mouth and nose.
4. Check that your vision is not blocked, and that the nose does not move when you smile or speak.

## Use
- Put the nose on as part of the performance ritual.
- Take it off at the end; keep it clean.

## Hygiene
- Clean the nose after each use with a gentle antibacterial wipe (check it is safe for the material).
- Do not share a nose between performers without cleaning it.

## Safety
- Do not wear the nose if you have difficulty breathing or if it causes irritation.
- Stop at the first sign of a skin reaction.

## Verify
- [ ] The nose stays secure through the performance and your breathing is not restricted.
- [ ] The nose is cleaned after each use.
