# Clown costume silhouette and shoes

The costume tells the audience who the clown is before the clown moves. It must also be safe and workable.

## Silhouette
- Choose a clear shape (wide, narrow, tall, round) that the audience can read at a distance.
- Use contrast: oversized with undersized, bright against plain.
- Test the silhouette by looking at a photograph in black and white.

## Layers and fit
- Build the costume in layers so you can add or remove pieces during a show.
- Keep clothing loose enough to move in: check you can reach, bend, kneel and sit.
- Add pockets or hidden places for props.
- Choose breathable fabrics; clown costumes can be hot.

## Shoes
- Large clown shoes change your gait and your sense of the floor. Practise in them before a show.
- Check the soles for traction on the venue's floor.
- Practise stairs and any ramps; avoid wearing the shoes on stairs unless you have practised carefully.
- Consider the weight of the shoes on your ankles; stop if you feel strain.

## Safety
- Avoid items that catch on equipment or trip you.
- Check any props sewn into the costume.
- Keep a repair kit (safety pins, thread, tape) at hand.

## Cleaning and care
- Wash the costume after use; clean the shoes.
- Replace worn or damaged parts.

## Verify
- [ ] You can move, kneel and run in the costume and shoes.
- [ ] A repair kit is in the costume bag.
