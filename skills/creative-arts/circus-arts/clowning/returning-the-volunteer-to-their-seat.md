# Returning the volunteer to their seat

A good volunteer moment ends with the volunteer feeling like a star, not a victim.

## Principles
- The volunteer is a guest on the stage. Their dignity and comfort come before the gag.
- Ask for willing volunteers and read their body language; let someone step back at any time.
- Check consent: say clearly what will happen, in a sentence, before they come up.
- For children, check with the parent or carer.

## Ending the moment
1. Stop while the volunteer is still enjoying it.
2. Thank them by name, with a clear statement of what they did well.
3. Lead the applause for them.
4. Offer a small gift or token if it fits the act.
5. Give a clear exit: point the way, accompany them to the edge of the stage, or walk them back to their seat.

## Do not
- Leave the volunteer alone on stage.
- Make them the butt of a joke that makes them feel foolish.
- Require physical contact they have not agreed to.
- Ask the volunteer to do something unsafe.

## Safety and access
- Watch for steps and cables on the way off the stage.
- Offer an easy return for a person with a mobility need.
- Do not pull a person up; invite them and wait.

## Verify
- [ ] The volunteer knows what will happen before coming up.
- [ ] The volunteer is thanked and led back to their seat with applause.
