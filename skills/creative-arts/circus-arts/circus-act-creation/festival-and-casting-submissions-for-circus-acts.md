# Festival and casting submissions

A good submission is complete, precise and easy for the selector to read.

## Read the call
- Note the deadline, format, fees and what the festival provides (stage, rigging, accommodation).
- Check any restrictions: act length, apparatus, height limit, fire rules.
- Make a checklist of every item requested.

## Prepare the package
- **Act description:** a short summary of the act and its concept.
- **Video:** a link as described in the promo video skill; add a password only if requested.
- **Technical rider:** apparatus, rigging points and loads, stage size, lighting and sound needs (see the tech rider skill).
- **Biography and photographs:** short bio; high-resolution photos with credits.
- **Contact and references:** who can speak about your work and reliability.
- **Safety documents:** insurance certificate and risk assessment if requested.

## Submit
1. Submit before the deadline, ideally a few days early.
2. Follow the format exactly (file type, naming, size).
3. Save a copy of everything you send and a confirmation email.

## After
- Track the applications in a spreadsheet: festival, date, status and follow-up.
- Follow up politely if no answer arrives after the stated date.
- If rejected, ask for brief feedback if the festival offers it.

## Verify
- [ ] Every item on the call's checklist is attached.
- [ ] A copy of the submission and confirmation is saved.
