# Writing a circus act tech rider

**Goal:** a 2-4 page document that tells a venue exactly what the act needs, so nothing
is a surprise on the day.

## Sections
1. **Act summary**: name, duration, number of performers, apparatus, one-line
   description, contact for technical questions.
2. **Stage**: minimum width x depth, surface (sprung floor, dance floor, hard surface
   OK?), flatness, whether mats are brought.
3. **Height and rigging**: minimum clear height, number of points with loads (attach the
   rigging plot), acceptable bridles, who rigs (act rigger or house), hardware brought.
4. **Floor**: ground anchors or stage weights needed, positions and loads.
5. **Lighting**: general wash colour/temperature, no strobe or haze if it affects the act
   (jugglers lose objects), specials, a cue list or a simple cue description.
6. **Sound**: playback format, number of cues, microphone needs, who operates.
7. **Time**: get-in and rigging time, rehearsal time on stage at height, warm-up space
   and time before the show.
8. **Crew**: stagehands needed for set/strike or tail/rope handling.
9. **Hospitality**: warm dressing room, water, towels, space for warm-up mats.
10. **What we bring**: apparatus, mats, hardware, costume.
11. **Documents**: risk assessment, rigging certificates, insurance.

## Writing tips
- Mark **essential** vs **preferred**; venues negotiate.
- Include diagrams and photos of the set-up.
- State units (metres and feet, kN and lbs).

## Gotchas
- Juggling acts need ceiling height and specific lighting angles - say so loudly.
- Smoke/haze and fog alarms: tell venues if you use fire or haze.

## Verify
- [ ] Rider includes rigging plot, RA and insurance references.
- [ ] Essential requirements clearly marked.
- [ ] Contact number for the technical lead.
