# Building a running order and pacing

The running order decides how the audience's energy rises and falls across the show.

## Gather the facts
For each act: duration, discipline, apparatus, rigging and changeover time, energy (quiet, medium, high), and any safety constraints (for example aerial rigging that cannot be changed during the interval).

## Principles
1. **Open with clarity:** an act that tells the audience what kind of show this is.
2. **Vary the energy:** avoid two acts of the same type or energy in a row.
3. **Place the big acts:** the most spectacular act before the interval and at the end, with variation between.
4. **Use quiet acts:** to let the audience rest and to build contrast.
5. **Consider the apparatus:** group acts that share rigging, and put long changeovers where a clown, a break or the interval can cover them.
6. **Respect performers:** do not put two physically demanding acts for the same performer close together.

## Making the order
1. Write each act on a card with its duration and changeover time.
2. Arrange the cards to make an energy curve.
3. Check total show time and interval.
4. Run the full order in a dress rehearsal and take notes.

## Adjusting
- Be ready to change the order after the first audiences.
- Record the order in the show bible with dates.

## Faults
- All the big tricks at the start.
- Long changeovers with no cover.
- No quiet moment before the finale.

## Verify
- [ ] The order has an energy curve noted on paper.
- [ ] Changeover times are listed and covered.
