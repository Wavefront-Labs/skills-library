# Shooting an act promo and casting video

A video is often the first thing a producer or booker sees.

## Content
- Show the act as it would be performed, with the best material first.
- Include the full performance of the key tricks without cuts so that viewers can see they are real.
- Keep it short: bookers commonly prefer a short reel plus a longer full-act video; check what each recipient asks for.
- Add your name, discipline and contact details at the start and end.

## Filming
1. Film in a clean, well-lit space with a plain background.
2. Place the camera at the audience's viewpoint and keep it steady.
3. Frame the whole performer and apparatus; avoid cutting off hands or feet.
4. Record sound clearly; use the actual music at the performance level.
5. Film several takes and choose the best.

## Editing
- Edit simply; avoid effects that hide the performer.
- Keep any cuts between tricks, not inside them.
- Use the same costume and set as the live act.

## Hosting
- Upload to a video platform and use a link that does not require a login.
- Test the link on another device.

## Music
- Make sure you have the right to use the music in the video (see the music licensing skill).

## Updating
- Replace the video when the act changes or after a year or two.

## Verify
- [ ] The link plays without a login on a phone and a laptop.
- [ ] Key tricks appear in unbroken takes.
