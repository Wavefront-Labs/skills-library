# Long-term training plan and periodisation

A performer's year has a rhythm: build, peak for the show, recover. A plan keeps training sustainable and reduces injury risk. Consult a coach or a medical professional for individual advice.

## Structure
- **Macrocycle:** the whole year or season, working back from the key performances.
- **Mesocycles:** blocks of a few weeks, each with a focus (for example strength base, skill acquisition, act polish, show run).
- **Microcycles:** the week, with hard days, skill days and rest.

## Building blocks
1. **Base:** general conditioning, mobility and strength.
2. **Build:** specific skills, increased intensity and volume on apparatus.
3. **Peak:** run-throughs and show preparation; lower volume, higher specificity.
4. **Recovery:** planned lighter periods after a block or a run of shows.

## Weekly structure
- Place high-intensity apparatus days apart.
- Add a conditioning day for strength and flexibility.
- Include at least one full rest day each week.

## Load tracking
- Keep a training log: date, work done, and how it felt.
- Note pain, fatigue and sleep.
- Reduce the load when warning signs accumulate.

## Adapting
- Shift the plan when shows are added or when an injury happens.
- Plan a deload week every few weeks, or when fatigue is high.

## Verify
- [ ] The plan is written back from key performance dates.
- [ ] A training log is kept and reviewed monthly.
