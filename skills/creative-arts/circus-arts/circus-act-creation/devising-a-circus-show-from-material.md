# Devising a circus show from material

Devising builds a show from the company's own material rather than from a script.

## Starting
1. Agree a theme, question or image that will drive the work.
2. Gather the skills and apparatus the company has.
3. Set a calendar of creation periods with goals and reviews.

## Generating material
- Use prompts: a word, an image, a task, a constraint.
- Run short improvisation sessions with a time limit (five to fifteen minutes).
- Film everything; review and tag the clips.
- Build a bank of scenes, moves, sequences and sounds.

## Selecting and shaping
1. Review the bank and choose pieces that speak to the theme.
2. Cluster them into sections.
3. Order the sections and look for links.
4. Fill gaps with new work, and cut material that does not serve the show.

## Testing
- Show work in progress to small audiences and ask questions.
- Use structured feedback (see the feedback skill).
- Revise based on what the audience saw, not only what the company intended.

## Documentation
- Keep a show bible: scene descriptions, counts, cues, rigging and safety notes.
- Date each version, and keep the earlier ones.

## Safety
- New material using apparatus or height must pass the same safety checks as any act, before it is added to the show.

## Verify
- [ ] The bank of material is filmed and tagged.
- [ ] A written version of the show exists for each performance period.
