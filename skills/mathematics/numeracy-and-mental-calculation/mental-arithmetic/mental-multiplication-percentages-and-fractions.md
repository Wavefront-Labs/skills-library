# Mental multiplication, percentages and fractions

**Goal:** do everyday calculations in your head quickly *and* correctly, using a few
reliable strategies instead of the paper algorithm done blind.

## 1. Foundations to make automatic
- **Number bonds to 10 and 100:** 7 + 3, 64 + 36, 0.35 + 0.65. These drive change-giving
  and most addition shortcuts.
- **Times tables to 12 × 12**, recalled not computed. Drill only the ones you hesitate on
  (for most adults: 6×7, 6×8, 7×8, 8×9, 7×12).
- **Doubles and halves** of two-digit numbers (double 37 = 74; half 86 = 43).
- **Place value moves:** ×10, ×100, ÷10 shift digits — the decimal point does not "move".

## 2. Adding and subtracting
- **Left to right** (how you'll remember it): 47 + 38 → 47 + 30 = 77, + 8 = 85.
- **Compensation:** 47 + 39 = 47 + 40 − 1 = 86. 503 − 198 = 503 − 200 + 2 = 305.
- **Counting up for subtraction** (shop-keeper's method): 1,000 − 637 → 637 → 640 (3)
  → 700 (60) → 1,000 (300) = 363.
- Hold intermediate results by saying them silently; working memory holds ~4 chunks, so
  reduce the problem early.

## 3. Multiplying
- **Partition (distributive law):** 23 × 7 = 20×7 + 3×7 = 140 + 21 = 161.
  46 × 12 = 46 × 10 + 46 × 2 = 460 + 92 = 552.
- **Compensation:** 19 × 6 = 20×6 − 6 = 114. 98 × 25 = 100×25 − 2×25 = 2,450.
- **Double and halve:** 16 × 35 = 8 × 70 = 560. 4.5 × 18 = 9 × 9 = 81.
- **Factor:** × 25 = × 100 ÷ 4 (36 × 25 = 900). × 5 = × 10 ÷ 2. × 50 = × 100 ÷ 2.
  × 9 = × 10 − self (× 99 = × 100 − self).
- **Near squares:** 49 × 51 = 50² − 1 = 2,499 (since (a−b)(a+b) = a² − b²).

## 4. Dividing
- Turn it into "what times divisor?": 156 ÷ 12 → 12 × 13 = 156.
- **Halve repeatedly** for ÷ 4, ÷ 8: 520 ÷ 8 = 260 → 130 → 65.
- ÷ 5 = × 2 ÷ 10: 340 ÷ 5 = 68. ÷ 25 = × 4 ÷ 100.
- Simplify first: 84 ÷ 14 = 42 ÷ 7 = 6.

## 5. Percentages
- **10%** = shift one place (10% of 340 = 34); **1%** = two places (3.4); 5% = half of 10%.
- Build any percent: 17.5% of 340 = 10% (34) + 5% (17) + 2.5% (8.5) = 59.5.
- **Swap trick:** x% of y = y% of x. 8% of 25 = 25% of 8 = 2.
- **Percentage change** = change ÷ original: 40 → 50 is +25%; 50 → 40 is −20%.
- **Reverse a percentage:** a price of 120 including 20% tax was 120 ÷ 1.2 = 100, not
  120 − 24 = 96. A 20% increase then a 20% decrease leaves you at 0.96 (−4%), not even.

## 6. Fractions and decimals
Know these cold: 1/2 = 0.5, 1/3 ≈ 0.333, 1/4 = 0.25, 1/5 = 0.2, 1/6 ≈ 0.167,
1/8 = 0.125, 3/8 = 0.375, 5/8 = 0.625, 1/9 ≈ 0.111, 1/12 ≈ 0.083.
- Fraction of an amount: divide by the bottom, multiply by the top. 3/8 of 64 = 8 × 3 = 24.
- Compare fractions by converting to decimals or cross-multiplying: 5/8 vs 3/5 →
  25 vs 24 → 5/8 is larger.
- Add with a common denominator: 1/3 + 1/4 = 4/12 + 3/12 = 7/12.

## 7. Always sanity-check
- **Estimate first** (see mathematics.numeracy-and-mental-calculation.estimation.fermi-estimation-and-plausibility-checks):
  48 × 21 ≈ 50 × 20 = 1,000, so 1,008 is plausible, 10,080 isn't.
- **Last digit check:** 23 × 7 must end in 1 (3 × 7 = 21).
- **Casting out nines:** the digit sums of the factors, multiplied and reduced, must match
  the answer's digit sum (catches most slips but not transposed digits).

## Gotchas
- Errors cluster in carrying and holding intermediate results — say them aloud quietly.
- Percentage *points* vs *percent*: 4% → 5% is +1 point but +25%.
- Speed comes after accuracy; practise 5–10 minutes daily rather than in long bursts
  (a common recommendation; evidence for exact dosing is limited).

## Verify
- [ ] Number bonds and tables recalled instantly (no counting).
- [ ] You can do 46 × 12, 17.5% of 340 and 1,000 − 637 in under 10 seconds each.
- [ ] Reverse percentages divided, not subtracted.
- [ ] Every answer checked by an estimate or last-digit test.
