# Needs-assessed CME modules with fair balance and independence

**Goal:** education that closes a real gap in clinical practice, is scientifically balanced,
and is demonstrably independent of commercial influence — so it can be accredited and
trusted by the clinicians who use it.

Accreditation rules differ: in the US, the ACCME's Standards for Integrity and Independence;
in Europe, EACCME (UEMS) and national bodies; in the UK, royal colleges' CPD schemes. Check
the accreditor's current requirements; this describes the common core.

## 1. Needs assessment: start from the gap
A **practice gap** is the difference between current and best practice. Evidence sources:
- guideline updates vs. audit data or published practice-pattern studies;
- quality indicators, registry data, prescribing data;
- surveys or interviews of the target learners;
- outcomes of previous education; expert faculty input.
Write the gap precisely and say whether it's **knowledge** (don't know), **competence**
(know but can't apply), or **performance** (can but don't do it in practice). The type
dictates format: lectures fix knowledge gaps; cases and simulation build competence; audit
and feedback or tools shift performance.

## 2. Learning objectives
Measurable, learner-centred, tied to the gap: "By the end of this module, participants will
be able to select appropriate first-line therapy for a newly diagnosed adult patient with
<condition> according to the current guideline." Use action verbs (select, interpret,
apply, calculate); avoid "understand", "be aware of".

## 3. Content development
- **Evidence-based and balanced:** present the best available evidence, including
  guidelines and comparisons across treatment options (generic names). Where evidence is
  weak or contested, say so.
- **Fair balance** of benefits and risks, efficacy and safety, for every intervention
  discussed. Off-label uses may be discussed in independent education where clinically
  relevant, but must be identified as such.
- **Case-based** formats (realistic patient vignettes with decision points) work well for
  competence gaps.
- Slide decks: one idea per slide, references on each data slide, readable figures,
  speaker notes for faculty.
- **Medical/scientific review** of every item for accuracy; check numbers against sources.

## 4. Independence from commercial interests
Core principles in most standards (paraphrased from ACCME's):
- The accredited provider and faculty — **not** the commercial supporter — control needs,
  objectives, content, faculty selection and evaluation.
- Collect **financial-relationship disclosures** from everyone who controls content
  (faculty, planners, reviewers); identify relevant relationships and **mitigate** them
  (e.g. peer review of their content) before they take part; disclose them to learners.
  Owners/employees of ineligible companies generally can't control content (with narrow
  exceptions).
- **Commercial support** (grants) is under a written agreement; supporters cannot influence
  content; support is acknowledged to learners.
- No marketing, logos, or product promotion within the education; promotional activity is
  kept physically/temporally separate.

## 5. Evaluation (outcomes)
Moore's framework describes levels: participation (1), satisfaction (2), learning —
declarative (3a) and procedural (3b), competence (4), performance (5), patient health (6),
community health (7). Aim beyond satisfaction:
- pre/post knowledge or case questions (levels 3–4);
- intent-to-change and follow-up surveys at 1–3 months (proxy for 5);
- chart audits or data where feasible.
Use results to inform the next needs assessment.

## Gotchas
- "Needs assessment" written after the content.
- One-sided data reviews that read like a product presentation.
- Faculty slides arriving the night before, unreviewed.
- Supporter staff "suggesting" speakers or topics.

## Verify
- [ ] Gap stated with evidence and classed as knowledge, competence or performance.
- [ ] Objectives measurable and tied to the gap.
- [ ] All content referenced, balanced, generic-named, off-label flagged.
- [ ] Disclosures collected, relevant relationships mitigated and shown to learners.
- [ ] Evaluation measures at least learning/competence, not only satisfaction.
