# Queue triage, de-escalation and escalating threats

**Goal:** clear the queue in the right order, make the same call another mod would make,
calm fights before they spread, and never sit on something dangerous.

For moderation at platform scale (SLAs, QA sampling, staffing) see
law-government.platform-and-community-governance.content-moderation-operations.running-review-queues-quality-calibration-and-moderator-wellbeing.

## 1. Triage the queue by severity, not by age
Scan everything once, then act in this order:
1. **Imminent risk to life** — credible threats of violence, a member saying they're about to
   harm themselves. Act within minutes (section 4).
2. **Illegal / non-negotiable** — child sexual abuse material, doxxing (posting someone's
   address, phone, workplace), non-consensual intimate images. Remove and escalate.
3. **Active harassment or raids** — a pile-on happening now.
4. **Spam and scams** — phishing links, crypto/"free Nitro" scams; remove fast, they spread.
5. **Ordinary rule breaks** — off-topic, rudeness, self-promo.
6. **Low-quality reports** — "I disagree with this post". Dismiss politely.

## 2. Consistent calls under volume
- Ask three questions per item: *Which rule? Which rung of the ladder (check the member's
  history)? Is there context that changes it?*
- Use the mod log every time: user, action, rule, short reason. If it isn't logged, it
  didn't happen — and the next mod can't escalate a repeat offender.
- When unsure, **remove-and-discuss** for clearly harmful content, **leave-and-discuss** for
  borderline speech; ask in the mod channel instead of guessing.
- Don't moderate threads you're personally arguing in; hand off.
- Use saved replies so explanations are consistent and fast.

## 3. De-escalating a heated thread
In rough order of intervention:
1. **Public, neutral nudge**: "Keeping this to the topic, please — critique the argument,
   not the person." No sarcasm, no taking sides.
2. **Private messages** to the two or three people driving it. Often they cool down when not
   performing for an audience.
3. **Slow mode / rate limits** (on platforms that support them) to break rapid back-and-forth.
4. **Remove the worst replies** and explain why.
5. **Lock** the thread with a note on why and whether it will reopen.
6. Timeouts for individuals who continue.
Watch for **brigading** — sudden arrivals from outside, new accounts, links from another
community. Treat it as a raid (lock, restrict new members, require verification).

## 4. Escalating threats, self-harm and illegal content
- **Threats of violence:** screenshot with timestamp and username/ID, remove, ban if
  appropriate, report to the platform's trust & safety. If the threat is specific and
  credible (named target, time, place, means), advise contacting or contact local law
  enforcement — your community rules should say who does this.
- **Self-harm / suicide risk:** respond privately and kindly, share crisis resources
  relevant to the person's region (keep a list of hotlines in the handbook), don't argue or
  debate, use the platform's self-harm reporting tool, and loop in a lead moderator. Mods are
  not counsellors — connect, don't treat.
- **CSAM:** do not download, share or re-post it, even to other mods. Report via the
  platform's tools; the platform reports onward (in the US, platforms report to NCMEC;
  other countries have equivalent hotlines such as the IWF in the UK). Then remove/ban.
  Exact legal duties for volunteer mods vary — check the platform's guidance.
- **Legal threats or requests** (subpoenas, takedown notices, defamation claims): don't
  respond yourself; pass to the community owner or platform.

## 5. Look after yourself
Rotate off the worst queues, blur or greyscale images where tools allow, take breaks after
graphic content, and debrief with another mod. Burnt-out mods make inconsistent calls.

## Gotchas
- Working the queue oldest-first while a threat sits unread.
- Arguing with a rule-breaker in public.
- Forwarding illegal images to "show" another mod.
- No regional crisis list ready when someone discloses suicidal intent.

## Verify
- [ ] Queue processed in severity order; risk-to-life items handled first.
- [ ] Every action logged with rule and reason.
- [ ] Heated threads got a nudge before a lock unless severe.
- [ ] Threat, self-harm and CSAM procedures written in the handbook with contacts.
- [ ] Mod team has a rotation and a debrief channel.
