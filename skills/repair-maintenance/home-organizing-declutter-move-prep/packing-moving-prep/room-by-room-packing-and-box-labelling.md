# Room-by-room packing and box labelling

**Goal:** everything arrives intact, every box goes straight to the right room, and you
can find the kettle and the bedding on the first night.

## 1. Materials (rough quantities for a 3-bed home: 60–100 boxes)
- **Small boxes** (~35–45 L) for heavy things: books, tins, tools, records. A box one
  person can't lift comfortably is a dropped box; keep each under ~20 kg (≈45 lb).
- **Medium** (~60–70 L) for kitchenware, toys, small appliances.
- **Large** (~100 L+) for light, bulky items only: bedding, cushions, lampshades.
- Wardrobe boxes for hanging clothes; dish-pack (double-wall) boxes for china.
- Unprinted packing paper (newspaper ink transfers), bubble wrap, 48 mm packing tape
  and a tape gun, thick markers, coloured stickers/tape per room, stretch wrap,
  furniture blankets, zip bags for screws.

## 2. Timeline
- **6–8 weeks out:** declutter first — every item you move costs time and money. Book
  movers/van; start a box inventory sheet.
- **3–4 weeks:** pack rarely used rooms and items (loft, garage, spare room, books,
  off-season clothes, decor).
- **1–2 weeks:** most of the house except daily essentials. Dismantle non-essential furniture.
- **Last 2 days:** kitchen except a minimal kit; pack the **Open First** boxes.
- **Moving day:** bed linen, toiletries, and the last-used items.
Pack one room at a time and finish it — half-packed rooms everywhere is how things get lost.

## 3. Packing technique
- Heavy at the bottom, light on top; fill voids with paper so nothing shifts — a box
  that rattles or caves when pressed on top is underpacked.
- Tape the bottom with an H-pattern (centre seam plus both edge seams).
- **Plates:** wrap individually, stand them **on edge** like records, never flat — they
  survive vertical loads much better. Crumpled-paper layer at the bottom and top.
- **Glasses/stemware:** stuff the bowl with paper, wrap the stem, wrap the whole thing;
  use cell dividers if available. Pack upright.
- **Framed art/mirrors:** masking-tape an X on glass (helps contain shards; it doesn't
  prevent breakage), corner protectors, bubble wrap, then sandwich between cardboard or
  use a mirror box. Transport on edge, never flat with weight on top.
- **TVs:** original box if you have it; otherwise blanket + stretch wrap, screen never
  laid flat, nothing pressing the panel.
- **Furniture hardware:** screws in a zip bag taped to the piece (or in one labelled
  hardware box). Photograph cable setups behind TVs and computers before unplugging.
- Liquids: tape caps, bag them, or don't move them. Movers often refuse flammables,
  gas bottles, paint and aerosols — check the contract.
- Valuables, passports, keys, medications, jewellery and hard drives travel **with you**.

## 4. Labelling and inventory
Label **two sides and the top** (the top is hidden once stacked). Each label carries:
- **Destination room** (the room in the *new* home), ideally colour-coded with a matching
  sign on the new room's door.
- **Box number**, sequential across the house (e.g. `KIT-14` or just `47`).
- Brief contents and flags: `FRAGILE`, `THIS WAY UP`, `HEAVY`, `OPEN FIRST`.
Keep an inventory list (spreadsheet or notes app): number, room, contents, value flag.
On delivery tick off numbers — a missing number shows up immediately.

## 5. Open First box (one per person + one kitchen)
Kettle, mugs, tea/coffee, a pan, plates/cutlery for one meal, phone chargers, toilet
roll, soap, towels, bedding, medications, basic tools (screwdriver, Allen keys, box
cutter), torch, bin bags, cleaning spray, snacks, pet food. Label it on all sides and
load it last (or in your car).

## Gotchas
- Overfilled large boxes of books break bottoms and backs.
- Unlabelled boxes all end up in the garage.
- Movers' insurance may exclude owner-packed boxes — check before relying on it.
- Defrost the freezer 24–48 h ahead; leave the door ajar to avoid mould.

## Verify
- [ ] No box over ~20 kg; none rattle or crush when pressed.
- [ ] Every box has room, number and flags on two sides and the top.
- [ ] Inventory count matches the boxes loaded and unloaded.
- [ ] Valuables and documents travelled with you.
- [ ] Open First boxes were loaded last and found first.
