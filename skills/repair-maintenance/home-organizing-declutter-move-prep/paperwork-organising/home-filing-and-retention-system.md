# Home filing and retention system

**Goal:** every piece of paper is handled once at arrival, anything needing action is in
one place, reference documents are findable in under a minute, and the rest is
scanned or shredded on a schedule.

## 1. The three-stream structure
1. **Action** — one tray or folder only: bills to pay, forms to sign, things to reply to.
   Emptied weekly.
2. **Reference (live files)** — current-year and ongoing documents in a hanging-file
   drawer or box, one folder per category.
3. **Archive** — closed years, in labelled boxes by year, plus vital documents in a
   fire/water-resistant box or bank deposit box.

Suggested reference categories (broad beats granular — 10–20 folders, not 80):
Home (mortgage/tenancy, insurance, utilities), Vehicles, Banking, Credit cards,
Investments & pensions, Tax (this year), Income/payslips, Medical (one per person),
Insurance (life, health), Children/school, Pets, Warranties & manuals, Identity &
vital docs (copies), Charity/subscriptions.

## 2. Mail triage (do it at the door or daily)
Open everything immediately over the recycling bin and shredder:
- Junk → recycle (shred if it has your name + account details).
- Needs action → Action tray, with the due date written on the top corner.
- Keep for reference → straight into its folder (or the "to file" pocket, filed weekly).
- Has an online copy and isn't a legal original → recycle or shred.
Switch statements to paperless where offered, then file the PDFs (see section 4).

## 3. Retention guide (typical; check your jurisdiction)
| Keep | Documents |
|---|---|
| Until replaced / next one arrives | Utility bills (unless used for tax/business), monthly statements once reconciled with the annual one |
| ~1 year | Payslips (until reconciled with the annual tax summary), receipts for returns |
| As long as you own it (+ the claim period) | Warranties, manuals, receipts for valuable items (insurance claims), vehicle docs |
| ~6–7 years after the tax year | Tax returns and their supporting records. US IRS: generally 3 years, up to 6–7 in some cases. UK HMRC self-assessment: at least 22 months after the tax year end, longer if self-employed (5 years from the 31 Jan deadline) — many people keep 6–7 to be safe |
| Ownership + several years after sale | Property purchase, improvements and sale documents (capital gains) |
| Permanently (originals, secure) | Birth, marriage, death, adoption certificates; passports; wills (or note of where the original is); deeds; citizenship; pension details; divorce decrees |
*The table is a common-practice simplification; confirm with an accountant or the tax authority for business, self-employed or unusual situations.*

## 4. Scanning and digital files
- Scanner app or sheet-fed scanner at **300 dpi**, greyscale or colour, output
  **searchable PDF (OCR)**.
- File naming: `YYYY-MM-DD_Source_Description.pdf` (e.g. `2026-03-31_Bank_Annual-statement.pdf`)
  so files sort by date. Folder tree mirrors the paper categories.
- Back up with the **3-2-1** rule: 3 copies, 2 media, 1 off-site (cloud). Encrypt or use
  an encrypted cloud for identity documents.
- Keep paper originals of anything legally required as original (wills, deeds, certificates).

## 5. Shredding and disposal
- Cross-cut (P-4 level or better under DIN 66399) for anything with account numbers,
  ID numbers, signatures or medical info. Strip-cut shredders are easily reassembled.
- Large backlogs: a commercial shredding service with a certificate of destruction.

## 6. Routines
- Daily: mail triage (5 min). Weekly: empty Action tray, file the "to file" pocket.
- Annually (after tax filing): move last year to Archive; purge archive boxes past retention.
- Keep a one-page "where things are" sheet (accounts, policies, adviser contacts,
  location of the will) for family/executors — no passwords on it; point to a password manager.

## Gotchas
- Over-categorising: if filing takes thought, it won't happen.
- Scanning a backlog before purging wastes hours — purge first.
- Don't put the only copy of the will in a bank box the executor can't open.

## Verify
- [ ] One Action tray, emptied weekly; no bills found outside it.
- [ ] Any reference doc retrievable in under a minute.
- [ ] Vital originals in a fire/water-resistant location; scans backed up 3-2-1.
- [ ] Archive purged annually per the retention table; shredding is cross-cut.
