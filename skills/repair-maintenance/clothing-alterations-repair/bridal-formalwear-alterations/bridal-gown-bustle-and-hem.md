# Bridal gown: fittings, layered hems, bustles and bodice

**Goal:** a gown that fits at the bodice, clears the floor in the wedding shoes, and
bustles up securely for the reception — delivered on time with no damage to costly
fabric.

## 1. Schedule the fittings
- **First fitting** 6–10 weeks before the date (bring shoes, the actual undergarments,
  and any petticoat). Pin bodice, hem, straps; agree bustle style.
- **Second fitting** ~3–4 weeks out: check sewn bodice, hem; pin bustle points.
- **Final fitting/pick-up** 1–2 weeks out: teach the person who will bustle the gown
  (often a bridesmaid) and let them do it twice.
Weight changes before weddings are common — leave seam allowance, don't trim early.

## 2. Bodice take-in
- Most bodice alteration happens at the **side seams** (and centre back if zipped or
  laced). Open the lining, adjust shell, interlining and lining separately.
- **Boning** sits in channels on the seams: remove the bone, take in, recut it
  (spiral steel with a cutter + new end cap; plastic/Rigilene with scissors, then
  melt or tip the end) so it ends ~1 cm short of the stitch lines.
- Lace or beaded bodice: move the appliqués across the seam — unpick the motif edge,
  sew the seam, then hand-appliqué the motif back over it so the seam vanishes.
- Cups: sew-in cups go in the lining layer; tack at the edges, not through the shell.

## 3. Hemming a layered gown
Hem **each layer separately**, each finishing at its own length — usually the outer
layer just clears the floor (~1 cm) at the front in shoes, and inner layers slightly
shorter so they don't peek out.
- Mark at the front on the body; take the train shape from the existing hem, removing
  the same amount evenly around (or from the front only, blending, to keep the train).
- **Tulle:** cut, no finish needed. **Chiffon/georgette:** rolled hem (rolled-hem foot
  or overlocker rolled hem). **Satin/crepe:** narrow turned hem or a horsehair braid
  insertion for body.
- **Horsehair braid** edges: unpick, remove, re-sew the braid at the new edge,
  easing on curves (pull the braid's gather thread).
- **Lace-edged hems:** do not cut the scalloped edge off. Remove the lace border,
  shorten the underlying layer, then re-attach the border by hand.
- **Beaded edges:** remove beads 2–3 cm from the cut line first (tie off each strand)
  so the machine needle doesn't hit them; re-bead by hand afterward. Never press over beads.

## 4. Bustles
Choose by train length, fabric weight and dress style:
- **Over (American) bustle:** hooks/buttons on the outside of the gown at the waist,
  loops on the train. Simple; 1–3 points for a short train, 5+ for cathedral.
- **Under (French/Victorian) bustle:** ribbons tied under the skirt, colour-coded or
  numbered pairs. Good for heavy trains and satin; looks like a waterfall.
- **Ballroom bustle:** many under-points making the train appear to be a full skirt.
Fitting the bustle: with the bride standing in shoes, lift the train to the point where
the hem is level with the front. Pin the pickup points, check from all sides and while
walking. Reinforce each attachment with a small square of matching fabric or tape on
the inside — a heavy train will tear lace at a single stitched point.

## 5. Handling
- Clean hands, white cotton sheet on the cutting table, no pens near the gown.
- Steam from the inside or at a distance; test heat on a hidden seam allowance —
  polyester satin melts and silk water-spots.
- Store hanging by the internal hanger loops, in a breathable bag.

## Gotchas
- Shoes not yet bought = no hem marking. Insist.
- Don't trim seam allowance on the bodice until the final fitting.
- A bustle that relies on tiny thread loops will fail on the dance floor — use
  ribbon or sturdy loops and reinforcement.
- Draft note: timing windows are common shop practice; adapt to your workload.

## Verify
- [ ] Bodice stays up without lifting the arms; no gaping at the back or neckline.
- [ ] Front hem clears the floor in the wedding shoes; no inner layer shows.
- [ ] Bustle lifts the train level with the front; holds after walking and sitting.
- [ ] Bustle helper has done it themselves at the final fitting.
- [ ] No pressing marks, water spots or missing beads.
