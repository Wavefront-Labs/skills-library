# Leather jacket and waterproof outerwear repair

**Goal:** fix the damage that ordinary sewing makes worse — leather that keeps every
needle hole, and waterproof or down garments where each stitch is a leak. Zips are in
`repair-maintenance.clothing-alterations-repair.zipper-fastener-replacement.replacing-zippers-and-fasteners`;
cleaning and conditioning leather is in
`repair-maintenance.laundry-textile-care.leather-suede-care.cleaning-conditioning-and-salt-stains`.

## 1. Leather garments
**Equipment:** leather (wedge/"LR") needles 90/14–110/18, polyester or bonded nylon thread
(Tex 40–70), a **walking foot** or roller foot, stitch length 3.5–4 mm (short stitches
perforate and tear like a stamp sheet), binder clips or tape instead of pins.
- **Split seam:** unpick a few cm beyond the split, glue the allowances back with a thin
  smear of contact cement (e.g. Barge), restitch **in the original holes** where
  possible, hand-sewing with two needles (saddle stitch) if the machine can't hit them.
  Lock off by tying ends and dabbing glue; never backstitch on leather.
- **Tear or hole in a panel:** small → underpatch with thin garment leather or fusible
  leather-repair tape glued from inside, edges butted, then flexible leather filler and
  colour (filler technique in
  `repair-maintenance.furniture-restoration-upholstery.leather-furniture-repair.leather-sofa-tear-scratch-and-recolour-repair`). Large or across a
  stress point → replace the panel: unpick it, use it as a pattern (+seam allowance),
  cut from matched skin, grain direction matching, sew in with original seam allowances.
- **Hems and facings** are usually glued, not stitched: re-glue with cement, hammer with a
  leather mallet or roller, clamp with clips 30 min.
- Pressing: no direct iron. Use a warm dry iron (low/synthetic setting) on brown paper,
  or just a hammer/roller.

## 2. Waterproof shells (Gore-Tex, eVent, PU-coated)
- **Peeling seam tape:** clean the inside seam with isopropyl alcohol. If the tape is
  intact but lifting, re-bond with a hot iron at the setting the maker suggests through
  baking paper (Draft note: many 3-layer fabrics tolerate a short pass at low–medium;
  test on a hidden seam). If the tape is gone, peel it off fully and paint a thin line of
  **urethane seam sealer** (Seam Grip WP or similar) over the stitches, 24 h cure.
  Replacement hot-melt tape needs a seam-tape press for a reliable bond.
- **Tears and punctures:** round-cornered patch of self-adhesive repair tape (Tenacious
  Tape, Gore-Tex patches) on the **outside**, 1–2 cm beyond the damage, warmed with a
  hair dryer and rubbed down. For tears >5 cm, tape inside and out or seal the edges with
  Seam Grip before patching. Avoid sewing through a membrane — if you must, seal the stitches.
- **Wetting out (not a leak):** if the outer fabric darkens, DWR has failed — wash with
  tech wash and reproof (spray-on or wash-in, then tumble warm if the label allows).

## 3. Down jackets
- **Feather leak through fabric:** pull the feather back in from the inside — don't pull
  it out, the hole grows.
- **Cut or burn hole:** tape first to stop loss, then a round ripstop nylon patch with
  repair tape or an iron-on patch at low heat (nylon melts ~200 °C — use a press cloth).
- **Sewn repair** (a burst baffle): use fine needle (70/10), fine thread, follow existing
  stitch lines; down-proof fabrics are tightly woven so new holes leak — cover with tape.

## 4. Hardware
- Buckles, cord locks and toggles: match the webbing width (20, 25, 38 mm common) and
  type (side-release, ladderlock). Many snap on; sewn ones are unpicked and box-X stitched
  with bonded nylon.
- Leather jacket snaps and rivets: set with the correct die size; back with a leather or
  interfacing washer if the hole has torn.

## Gotchas
- Every needle hole in leather is permanent — plan once, sew once.
- Contact cements and sealers are solvent-based: ventilate, no flames, gloves.
- Iron-on patches on membranes can delaminate the fabric if too hot.
- Suede shows glue stains — apply adhesives sparingly with a fine spatula.

## Verify
- [ ] Leather seams restitched at ≥3.5 mm, no perforation tears, ends glued not backstitched.
- [ ] Patched shell passes a shower or hose test from the inside view (no damp spots).
- [ ] Seam tape fully bonded along its length; patch corners not lifting after flexing.
- [ ] No feathers escaping from the repaired area after squeezing.
- [ ] Hardware matches webbing width and locks under load.
