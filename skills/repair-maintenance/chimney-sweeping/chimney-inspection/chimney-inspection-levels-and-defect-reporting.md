# Chimney inspection levels and defect reporting

**Goal:** an inspection that answers "is this chimney safe to use with this appliance?",
records evidence and grades every defect so the customer knows what's urgent. The US
uses NFPA 211's three levels; UK practice (HETAS, NACS, Guild of Master Sweeps,
Building Regulations Approved Document J) covers the same ground with smoke and
draught tests. Inspectors should hold the relevant certification (e.g. CSIA in the US).

## 1. Pick the level (NFPA 211)
- **Level 1:** routine — appliance and flue in continued use with no change. Readily
  accessible parts of the chimney exterior/interior and the appliance connection;
  confirm basic soundness and no obstruction or combustible deposits.
- **Level 2:** required on **change** — new appliance, change of fuel, relining, after a
  chimney fire/earthquake/operating malfunction, and on **sale/transfer of property**.
  Includes Level 1 plus accessible attics, crawl spaces and basements, and a **video
  scan** of the whole flue interior.
- **Level 3:** when a Level 2 finds a hidden hazard that can't be evaluated otherwise —
  removing parts of the building or chimney (e.g. cutting into a chase or wall).

## 2. Exterior
Using binoculars, a pole camera or a drone where ladder access is unsafe:
- **Crown:** cracks, missing sections, no overhang/drip edge.
- **Pots, caps, terminals:** secure, appropriate guard, no blockage.
- **Masonry:** spalling, missing mortar, leaning stack, efflorescence (water getting in).
- **Flashing:** lifted lead/steel, sealant-only flashing.
- **Height/terminals:** meets the rules for the fuel (US: 3 ft above roof penetration and
  2 ft above anything within 10 ft; UK Approved Document J tables).

## 3. Interior and appliance
- Appliance: correct installation, hearth size, clearances to combustibles, connector
  pipe slope and joints, register plate sealed, data plate/installation certificate.
- Flue: deposits (creosote stage), obstructions, liner type and condition.
- **Clearances:** wood beams/joists touching the chimney breast — a classic hidden
  hazard (US code generally requires 2 in clearance from interior masonry chimneys).
- CO alarm present and working in the room (UK requirement for new solid-fuel installs).

## 4. Video (camera) survey
1. Sweep first — soot hides cracks.
2. Lower or push the camera from top or bottom with a light; record the whole run,
   narrating depth/position.
3. Look for: cracked or missing clay tiles, gaps at liner joints (mortar loss), offsets,
   collapsed sections, liner crushing or perforation (stainless), signs of a past chimney
   fire (puffy/honeycomb creosote, cracked tiles, warped metal, discoloured cap).
4. Save the footage and timestamp key frames for the report.

## 5. Smoke and draught tests
- **Smoke (flue integrity) test** (UK practice for lined/unlined flues): seal the top,
  burn a smoke pellet at the bottom for the specified time; watch for smoke escaping
  into rooms, roof space and adjacent flues. Any leak = fail.
- **Draw test:** smoke pellet in the fireplace/appliance with the top open; smoke should
  be drawn up promptly.
- **Draught reading:** with the appliance running and stable, a digital manometer at the
  flue pipe test point; compare with the appliance maker's range (many stoves want about
  −10 to −25 Pa — confirm). Too high → consider a draught stabiliser; too low → check
  height, size, blockages, house depressurisation (extract fans).

## 6. Write the report
For each defect: **location, photo/video reference, description, grade, action.**
Use a clear grading, e.g. *Immediately dangerous — do not use* / *At risk — repair
before next use* / *Not to current standard — advise* / *Maintenance*. Include the
level performed, appliance and fuel, test results with numbers, and what wasn't
accessible (and therefore not inspected).

## Gotchas
- "Passed" on a Level 1 isn't a Level 2 — make limits explicit on sale inspections.
- Never smoke-test a flue with an appliance in use connected to the same flue.
- Unlined old flues serving new stoves often fail smoke tests; recommend relining.

## Verify
- [ ] Level chosen and stated on the report with the reason.
- [ ] Exterior, interior and appliance all checked; inaccessible areas listed.
- [ ] Camera footage saved; defects time-stamped.
- [ ] Smoke/draw test and draught reading recorded.
- [ ] Every defect graded with a clear action and urgency.
