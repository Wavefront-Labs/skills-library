# Sterndrive bellows and gimbal bearing service

**Goal:** keep water out of the boat and the drive train smooth. On a sterndrive
(I/O), the **U-joint bellows** is the only thing between the sea and the bilge at the
transom — a split bellows can sink a boat at the mooring and lets water rust the U-joints
and gimbal bearing.
Common intervals: inspect bellows every haul-out; replace every ~2–3 seasons or at
the first crack; gimbal bearing check every season. *Written around MerCruiser Alpha/
Bravo-style drives; Volvo Penta SX/DP and others differ in detail — use the model's manual.*

## Signs it's due
Cracks or brittleness at bellows folds (flex them to see), water in the bilge after
sitting, rust on U-joint yokes, grinding/rumbling when turning or at certain trim
angles, play or noise from the gimbal bearing, coupler noise at idle (alignment).

## Kit
Bellows kit (U-joint, exhaust, shift cable bellows + clamps + adhesive per kit),
gimbal bearing and **bearing puller/driver** for your transom, **engine alignment tool**
(model-specific bar), U-joint grease (marine, high-water-resistance), coupler/spline
grease, new drive gasket/O-rings, torque wrench, drive dolly or a helper — drives weigh
roughly 40–70 kg.

## Procedure
1. Boat out of the water, drive **trimmed full down**, in **forward** gear (Alpha: shift
   in gear helps the shift cable/slide disengage cleanly — follow your manual).
2. Remove the drive: trim cylinder pins (support the drive), shift linkage/cable as
   specified, then the six drive nuts (Alpha). Pull the drive straight back; the U-joint
   splines slide out of the coupler.
3. **Inspect U-joints**: no notchiness, no play, no rust bleeding from caps. Grease via
   zerks (if fitted) until fresh grease shows.
4. **Bellows replacement**:
   - Cut off old bellows and clamps; scrape flanges clean (gasket remover, no deep
     gouges on aluminium).
   - Apply the **specified adhesive** to the bellows' flange contact areas (many kits:
     bellows adhesive on the transom side only).
   - Install from inside the bell housing using the installation tool or careful
     hand work; orient drain holes/air vents as the manual shows (the exhaust bellows
     often has a drain slit at the bottom).
   - Clamps tightened to spec; clamp screw positions where they won't foul the drive.
   - Replace shift cable bellows and check the shift cable sleeve for corrosion.
5. **Gimbal bearing**: spin it with a finger through the bell housing — rough, notchy
   or rusty = replace. Use the puller (slide-hammer type) to extract; drive the new one
   in square, seated fully against its shoulder.
6. **Engine alignment** (do it whenever the drive is off, and always after a new
   gimbal bearing or engine mount work):
   - Insert the alignment tool through the gimbal bearing into the engine coupler.
     It should slide in and out **freely by hand**.
   - If it binds, adjust the front engine mounts (raise/lower) until it slides freely;
     re-check after tightening the mount lock nuts.
7. **Reinstall**: grease drive splines and coupler splines (coupler type per maker,
   some use a specific coupler grease), new drive gasket and O-rings, guide the drive
   on, rotating the prop to align splines — don't force. Torque drive nuts to spec in
   sequence; refit trim pins, shift linkage.
8. Check **shift adjustment** (forward/neutral/reverse at the helm vs drive) and trim
   operation through full range.

## Test
- Bilge dry, check the bellows from the engine bay with a torch after launch and after
  the first run.
- Run in the water: no coupler noise at idle, smooth through full trim and steering lock.
- Recheck clamps after the first outing.

## Gotchas
- Never leave a boat with a cracked bellows in the water "for the weekend".
- Pressure-washing the drive bell area or leaving the drive trimmed up on a trailer for
  months shortens bellows life.
- Alignment that is "close enough" kills couplers and gimbal bearings — the tool must
  slide freely.
- Some drives (e.g. Bravo with **continuity/ground wire**) have bonding wires across
  the bell housing — reconnect them or galvanic corrosion follows.

## Verify
- [ ] All three (or more) bellows new, adhesive applied as kit specifies, clamps torqued.
- [ ] Gimbal bearing smooth; U-joints greased with no play.
- [ ] Alignment tool slides freely by hand into the coupler.
- [ ] Shift and trim correct through full range; bilge dry after first run.
