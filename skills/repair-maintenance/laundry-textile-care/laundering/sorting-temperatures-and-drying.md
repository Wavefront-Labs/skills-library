# Sorting, temperatures and drying — laundering without damage

**Goal:** clean clothes that come out the same size and colour they went in.

## 1. Read the care label (ISO 3758 / GINETEX symbols)
- **Tub**: number = max wash temperature in °C (30, 40, 60, 95). In the US, dots mean
  the same thing: 1 dot ≈ 30 °C cold, 2 ≈ 40 °C warm, 3 ≈ 50 °C, 4 ≈ 60 °C hot.
  - No bar = normal cycle; **one bar** under = permanent press/synthetic (gentler
    spin); **two bars** = wool/delicate. Hand in tub = hand wash. Crossed tub = do not wash.
- **Triangle**: bleach. Empty = any; two diagonal lines = oxygen/non-chlorine only; crossed = none.
- **Square with circle**: tumble dry. 1 dot = low heat, 2 dots = normal; crossed = don't.
  Square with a curved line at top = line dry; vertical line = drip dry; horizontal = dry flat.
- **Iron**: 1 dot ≈ 110 °C, 2 ≈ 150 °C, 3 ≈ 200 °C.
- **Circle**: professional cleaning (letters P/F = dry-clean solvent, W = wet clean).
Labels are the manufacturer's safe maximum — washing cooler and gentler is fine.

## 2. Sort
Sort on three axes, in this priority:
1. **Colour**: whites / lights / darks / reds-and-brights. New dark denim and reds wash
   separately for the first few washes.
2. **Fibre and construction**: cotton/linen towels and bedding; synthetics and
   sportswear; delicates (silk, lace, lingerie) in mesh bags; wool on a wool cycle.
   Keep towels away from fleece and dark synthetics (lint).
3. **Soil level**: heavily soiled workwear apart from lightly worn office clothes.
Prep: empty pockets (tissues!), close zips and hooks, unbutton shirts, turn printed
and dark items inside out.

## 3. Temperature and detergent
| Load | Temp | Detergent |
|---|---|---|
| Everyday mixed colours, lightly soiled | 30 °C (or 20 °C with a modern liquid) | Colour/non-bio or bio liquid |
| Whites, towels, bedding, underwear | 40–60 °C | Bio powder (contains bleach activator) |
| Hygiene loads (illness, cloths, baby) | 60 °C | Bio powder |
| Wool, silk | 30 °C or cold, wool cycle | Wool/silk detergent — no enzymes |
| Sportswear/synthetics | 30–40 °C | Sports or colour detergent; no softener |
- **Enzymes** (protease, amylase, lipase) in "bio" detergents digest protein/starch/fat
  stains — great on cotton, **harmful to wool and silk** (they're protein fibres).
- Dose for your water hardness and load; overdosing leaves residue, stiffness and smell.
- Fabric softener coats fibres: avoid on towels (less absorbent), sportswear/wicking
  fabrics, microfibre cloths and flame-resistant children's sleepwear.

## 4. Loading
- Fill the drum about **¾ full for cottons** (a hand's width at the top), **half for
  synthetics and delicates**, a third for wool. Overloading = poor rinsing and creasing;
  underloading a single heavy item (a duvet, a rug) causes unbalanced spin.
- Lower spin speed (800–1000 rpm) for synthetics to reduce creasing.

## 5. Drying without damage
- **Shrinkage** in cotton and wool comes from heat + agitation while wet. Tumble on low,
  or line dry; take cottons out slightly damp.
- Wool knitwear: roll in a towel to remove water, dry **flat** in shape, out of sun.
- Line dry whites in sun (mild bleaching); dry darks and brights inside out in shade.
- Clean the dryer **lint filter every load** — a blocked filter lengthens drying and
  is a fire risk. Condenser dryers: rinse the condenser unit monthly.
- Never tumble rubber-backed mats, items with foam, or anything stained with oil
  (cooking oil residue can self-heat and ignite).

## 6. Machine care
Monthly maintenance wash: empty, 60–90 °C with powder or machine cleaner. Wipe the door
seal, leave the door and detergent drawer ajar to prevent mould, and clean the pump
filter every few months (towel on the floor — water will come out).

## Gotchas
- One red sock in a white wash: re-wash immediately without drying, using a colour-run
  remover; heat sets dye transfer.
- Colour-catcher sheets reduce, not eliminate, dye transfer.
- "Cold wash" with powder can leave undissolved residue — use liquid below ~20 °C.

## Verify
- [ ] Every item's care label was checked; wool/silk didn't see enzymes or heat.
- [ ] Colours sorted; no dye transfer.
- [ ] Load size left room to tumble; no detergent residue or smell.
- [ ] Clothes came out the same size; lint filter cleaned.
