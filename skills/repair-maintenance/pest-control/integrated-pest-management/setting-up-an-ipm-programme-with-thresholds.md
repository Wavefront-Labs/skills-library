# Setting up an IPM programme with thresholds

**Goal:** a pest programme for a food plant, school, hospital, warehouse or office
that prevents problems, catches them early through monitoring, escalates only as far
as needed, and survives an audit because every decision is written down.

> Draft: thresholds below are illustrative starting points. Audit standards (BRCGS,
> AIB, SQF, local school IPM laws) set their own documentation requirements —
> match them.

## 1. Site risk survey
Walk the site with the facility manager. Record:
- **Pest pressure from outside:** neighbours (food waste, waterways, fields), past
  pest history, seasonal patterns.
- **Entry routes:** dock doors, door seals, drains, pipe penetrations, vents, roof.
- **Conducive conditions:** spillage, waste handling, standing water, clutter,
  vegetation against walls, external lighting attracting insects to doors.
- **Sensitive zones:** open food, sterile areas, kitchens, classrooms, where toxic
  baits are not acceptable.
Output: a risk register (hazard, location, risk level, owner, fix).

## 2. Device map and monitoring schedule
- Draw a **site plan with every device numbered**: external tamper-resistant stations
  around the perimeter (commonly every 15–30 m, and either side of doors), internal
  traps (non-toxic monitors or snap traps in stations) along walls and at doors every
  6–12 m, insect light traps away from open food and not visible from outside,
  stored-product pheromone traps where relevant.
- **No toxic rodenticide inside food production areas** in most food standards —
  internal devices are monitors/traps.
- Service frequency: commonly monthly baseline, increased (fortnightly/weekly) during
  activity. Insect light trap glue boards analysed and changed at set intervals,
  tubes replaced annually.

## 3. Set action thresholds
Write them down before there's a problem. Example:
| Pest / device | Threshold | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Rodent — any internal sign | **1** | investigate same day, increase traps, proof |
| Rodent — external station take | > 10% of stations in a month | inspect perimeter, proofing review |
| Cockroach — monitor | any in food area | crack-and-crevice bait, deep clean, re-monitor 7 d |
| Flying insect — ILT board | > 20 flies/board/period, or a trend up 2 periods | find source |
| Stored product moth — pheromone trap | > 5/trap/week | inspect stock, rotate, clean |
Zero tolerance zones (sterile areas, open product) have threshold 1.

## 4. Escalation ladder (least-toxic first)
1. **Prevent:** sanitation, proofing, stock rotation, door discipline, waste management.
2. **Monitor:** devices, sighting log, staff reporting.
3. **Physical/mechanical:** traps, ILTs, exclusion, heat, vacuuming.
4. **Targeted low-risk chemical:** baits in cracks, IGRs, desiccant dusts.
5. **Broader chemical** (residual sprays, fumigation) — justify in writing, notify
   site, note re-entry intervals, choose timing when site is closed.

Every step up is recorded with the reason it was needed.

## 5. Documentation (the audit trail)
Keep in a site file or digital system:
- Contract, scope, technician licences/training records.
- Site plan with device numbers (kept current).
- Product list with labels and safety data sheets; usage records (product, batch,
  quantity, location, date, operator).
- Service reports with device-by-device findings, and **recommendations with
  owners and due dates**.
- Sightings log for staff.
- **Trend analysis** — quarterly at least: activity by device/zone over time.
- Corrective actions closed out with evidence (photo of the fixed door seal).

## 6. Review
Quarterly review meeting: trends, open recommendations, threshold breaches, season
coming up. An IPM programme that only ever adds bait is just pest control with paperwork.

## Gotchas
- Recommendations nobody owns never get done — name the person.
- Devices moved by cleaners break your map; number and fix them to walls.
- ILT next to an open external door pulls insects in.
- "No activity" for a year can mean devices aren't being checked — verify servicing.

## Verify
- [ ] Risk survey and register exist with owners.
- [ ] Numbered device map matches what's on the walls.
- [ ] Written thresholds per pest/zone and an escalation ladder agreed with the client.
- [ ] Every chemical use justified, recorded, and on an approved product list.
- [ ] Trend report and open-actions list reviewed at least quarterly.
