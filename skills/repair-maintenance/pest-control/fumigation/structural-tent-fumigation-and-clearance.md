# Structural tent fumigation and clearance

**Goal:** kill every target pest throughout a structure (typically drywood termites or
wood borers) with a gas, then return the building safe to occupy — documented at every step.

> **Draft and safety-critical.** Fumigants are restricted-use; this work requires a
> licensed fumigator with the specific category, the product's fumigation management
> plan (FMP) and label, and calibrated monitoring equipment. Jurisdiction: United
> States (EPA); the current label and the manufacturer's dosage calculator govern. People have died from short cuts here.

## 1. Pre-job survey and plan
- Confirm the pest and that fumigation is justified (widespread drywood termites,
  inaccessible infestation). Localised activity may only need spot treatment.
- Measure the structure's **volume** (m³ / ft³): footprint x average height incl.
  roof space. Under-calculating volume = under-dosing.
- Identify hazards: connected buildings, shared walls/voids (townhouses), crawlspaces,
  underground utilities and conduits that can carry gas, nearby occupied buildings.
- Write the **FMP**: sealing method, dosage, monitoring points, aeration plan,
  emergency contacts, notice to occupants/neighbours, local authority notifications.
- Client prep: everyone out (people, pets, plants), **food and medicines** removed or
  double-bagged in the fumigant bags the label specifies, gas pilot lights off, utility company
  notified to shut gas at meter.

## 2. Seal
- Tarps overlapping and clamped, sand snakes on ground seal; tape or seal
  penetrations; shared walls sealed separately or the job isn't viable.
- Soil around the perimeter wetted where label calls for it (reduces loss downward).
- Place **monitoring lines** to representative points: top of attic, centre of
  living space, a closed room, subfloor.
- Lock doors, fit warning placards on every side and entrance, secure with
  secondary locks (lock-outs) where required.

## 3. Introduce and hold
- **Warning agent first** (chloropicrin with sulfuryl fluoride per label) — a few
  minutes before the fumigant, distributed by fans, so anyone who enters is driven out.
- Dose from the calculator using **target pest, temperature, volume and expected
  half-loss time (HLT)**. The quantity that matters is the **CT product**
  (concentration x time, oz-h/1000 ft³ or g-h/m³). Lower temperature → higher dose.
- Introduce gas through a hose to a fan-driven distribution point; never directly
  onto surfaces (frost/ residue damage).
- Monitor concentrations at intervals (e.g. 1 h after introduction and then as plan
  requires) with a fumigant-specific monitor. Recalculate HLT; top up if CT will fall short.
- Exposure time and CT come from the label and dosage calculator for the target pest and temperature.

## 4. Aerate and clear
- Remove tarps / open structure per the label's aeration procedure (often staged:
  open, run fans for set hours, close/check).
- Anyone entering before clearance wears the respiratory protection the label requires.
- Clear with a **clearance device** that is on EPA's list of effective devices (EPA's 2023
  mitigation removed "approved clearance device" wording from labels because some
  previously listed devices did not read accurately). Every room, closed cupboard and void sampled.
- Re-entry only when readings are at or below the label's clearance level (the sulfuryl
  fluoride label requires 1 ppm or less, confirmed with a clearance device; approved
  respiratory protection stays on until then). Record readings, times, instrument and serial.

## 5. Commodity fumigation (briefly)
Containers, silos, stacks under sheets: phosphine (aluminium/magnesium phosphide)
is common. Follow the product label for exposure time, minimum temperature and
gas-tightness. Same monitoring and clearance discipline.

## Gotchas
- Gas follows conduits, drains and shared voids — neighbours have been killed.
- Temperature drop overnight cuts efficacy; dose for the lowest expected temperature.
- A torn tarp on a windy night = fast HLT and failed CT; monitor and repair.
- Fumigation has **no residual** — re-infestation is possible next season.

## Verify
- [ ] FMP written; volume, temperature and dose calc recorded.
- [ ] Occupants, pets, plants, food/meds removed or bagged; gas off.
- [ ] Warning agent (chloropicrin) introduced first; placards and locks in place.
- [ ] Concentration readings show the required CT achieved.
- [ ] Clearance readings at or below the label limit in every space before handover; site log completed.
