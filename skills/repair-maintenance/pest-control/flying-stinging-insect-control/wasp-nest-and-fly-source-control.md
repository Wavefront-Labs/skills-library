# Wasp nest treatment and fly/mosquito source control

**Goal:** remove stinging-insect hazards without getting the operator or client
stung, protect bees, and solve fly and mosquito problems at the breeding source
rather than spraying adults forever.

> Which species are protected and which products are permitted near water varies by
> country and state. Check before acting.

## 1. Identify first — it changes everything
| Insect | Look | Nest | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Honey bee | hairy, golden-brown | wax comb in cavity, or a hanging swarm cluster | **Do not kill.** Refer swarms to a local beekeeper; established colonies in walls need a bee removal specialist (cut-out) |
| Bumblebee | big, fuzzy | small, in ground/compost | leave alone unless a real hazard; colonies die out in autumn |
| Yellowjacket / common wasp | smooth, bright yellow-black | papery, in ground, wall voids, lofts | treat |
| Paper wasp | long dangling legs | open umbrella comb under eaves | treat or knock down |
| Hornet | large, brown/yellow | big paper nest, often in trees/sheds | treat with extra care; in Great Britain report suspected Asian (yellow-legged) hornet with a photo and location to the Non-native Species Secretariat and do not disturb an active nest |

## 2. Wasp/hornet nest treatment
**PPE:** full bee suit with veil, gauntlets, boots taped; never on a job if you know
you're allergic and alone. Carry the client's/your own adrenaline auto-injector if
prescribed; know the emergency number.
**Timing:** dusk or early morning, when foragers are home and less active.
**Method:**
1. Locate the **entrance** by watching flight lines during the day. Don't block it
   first — trapped wasps chew into the house.
2. Apply **insecticidal dust** (e.g. a permethrin- or similar dust; follow label)
   with a bulb or extension duster directly into the entrance. Workers carry it
   through the nest. Aerosols with long jet reach are for exposed nests.
3. Leave the entrance open so returning foragers contact the dust.
4. Remove exposed nests once inactive (bag them). Nests in voids can often be left,
   but remove them if debris will attract other pests.
- Never treat from the top of a ladder without a plan to retreat; use extension poles.
- Ground nests: dust into the hole; don't stand on the entrance.

## 3. Flies — find the breeding source
Adult control without source removal fails. Match fly to source:
- **House/blow flies:** bins, rotting food, dead animals (a sudden heavy blowfly
  problem indoors often means a dead rodent or bird in a void).
- **Fruit flies:** fermenting produce, drains, mops, recycling bins, bar drip trays.
- **Drain/moth flies:** gelatinous film inside drains, broken pipes under slabs.
  Test by taping a clear cup over a drain overnight.
- **Cluster flies:** overwinter in lofts — exclusion and timing, not sanitation.
Fixes: clean bins and drains mechanically (brush, enzyme/bio cleaner — boiling water
or bleach alone don't remove the film), remove the dead animal, fix the leak.
Then **UV light traps with glue boards** (not zappers above food — they scatter
fragments), away from competing light and doorways' line of sight.

## 4. Mosquitoes
- Tip and drain anything holding standing water, even small amounts (EPA): saucers,
  gutters, tyres, buckets.
- Larvicide water you can't drain (ponds, drains, water features) with **Bti**
  (Bacillus thuringiensis israelensis) or an IGR like methoprene per label.
- Adult barrier sprays on resting vegetation are supplementary and harm pollinators —
  avoid flowering plants.

## Gotchas
- Spraying a wasp entrance at noon = being chased.
- Sealing an active void nest pushes wasps indoors.
- "Bees" reported by clients are usually wasps — but check; killing honey bees is
  often illegal and always bad practice.
- Fly lights near doors attract flies *in*.

## Verify
- [ ] Insect identified; bees referred, not killed.
- [ ] Nest treated at entrance, dusk/dawn, full PPE; entrance left open.
- [ ] Activity rechecked after the dust has had time to work; inactive exposed nests removed.
- [ ] For flies, the breeding source was found and removed, not just adults killed.
- [ ] Standing water eliminated or larvicided for mosquitoes.
