# Two-stroke top-end rebuild

**Goal:** fresh piston, rings and small-end bearing in a cylinder that's in spec, so the
engine makes full compression and doesn't seize. Common on dirt bikes at set hour
intervals (e.g. every 30–100 h depending on model and use — check the manual).

## 1. Decide: compression and leak-down
- **Compression test**: warm engine, throttle wide open, kick/crank until the gauge peaks.
  Compare with the manual's figure; on many 125–300 cc dirt bikes healthy is roughly
  **~120–190 psi**. A drop of ~10–15% from new = rebuild due.
- **Crankcase leak-down** (pressure/vacuum test, ~6 psi pressure and ~6 inHg vacuum
  with intake and exhaust blocked): should hold for minutes. A leak points at **crank
  seals**, base gasket or reed block — fix during the rebuild, because an air leak
  leans the engine and seizes it.

## 2. Strip
1. Clean the bike; drain coolant (liquid-cooled); remove tank/seat, pipe, carb, head.
2. Lift the cylinder straight up while supporting the piston — catch it so it doesn't
   hit the cases. **Stuff a clean rag in the crankcase mouth** immediately.
3. Remove circlips (point down/away, eye protection), push out the wrist pin, remove piston
   and small-end needle bearing.
4. Inspect: piston crown (detonation pitting, holes), skirt scuffing/seizure marks,
   ring land wear, **rod small-end play** and big-end (up-down play = bottom-end job).

## 3. Measure the cylinder and piston
- **Bore**: bore gauge or inside micrometer at three heights and two axes (front-back
  and side-side). Check taper and out-of-round vs the service limit.
- **Plated bores (Nikasil)**: look for flaking, scoring or exposed aluminium — replating,
  not honing. Light scuffs can be cleaned with a soft brush hone or Scotch-Brite;
  don't hone a plated bore with stones unless the maker says so. Many cylinders have a
  size letter/grade (A/B/C) stamped — match the piston grade.
- **Piston**: measure at the point the manual specifies (often ~10–15 mm above skirt base,
  90° to the pin). **Piston-to-wall clearance** = bore − piston; typically in the
  ~0.03–0.08 mm range on many dirt bikes — use the manual.
- **Ring end gap**: push the ring squarely into the bore with the piston crown, measure
  with feeler gauges. Must be within spec (often ~0.2–0.5 mm). Too small = ring butts
  when hot → scuffing/seizure.

## 4. Assemble
1. Clean everything; new **base gasket**, head gasket/O-rings, circlips and small-end
   bearing. Lightly oil bearing, pin and bore with **two-stroke oil**.
2. Fit rings with markings up; **ring ends must sit over the locating pins** in the
   ring grooves (two-strokes pin rings so ends can't catch a port).
3. Piston **arrow toward the exhaust**. Fit wrist pin; new circlips with the gap away
   from the removal notch; spin each to confirm seated.
4. Lower the cylinder, compressing each ring by hand at the chamfer; don't force.
5. **Torque** cylinder base nuts then head bolts in a cross pattern, in stages, to spec.
6. **Squish** (head-to-piston clearance): some engines specify checking with soft solder
   through the plug hole; adjust with base gasket thickness per the manual.
7. Reinstall power-valve linkage (if fitted) — check it moves freely.
8. Inspect **reed petals**: flat, no chips, minimal light gap; replace if cracked.

## 5. Premix and break-in
- Mix fuel/oil at the **manufacturer's ratio** (commonly 32:1–60:1). More oil isn't always
  safer — it can foul plugs and leans the mixture slightly by displacement.
- Warm up gently; heat-cycle 2–3 times (run, cool fully). Then 15–30 min of varied
  throttle without full-load lugging. Retorque head if the manual says so.
- Check plug colour; re-check jetting if the top end failed by seizure (lean jetting,
  air leak or wrong mix are usual causes).

## Gotchas
- A seized piston rarely "just happens" — find the cause (air leak, lean jetting, coolant
  loss, no oil in fuel, over-rev) or it'll seize again.
- Debris from a broken ring or circlip in the cases = split the cases; don't hope.
- Over-torqued plated cylinders distort the bore.

## Verify
- [ ] Bore, piston, clearance and ring gap measured and in spec.
- [ ] Ring ends on locating pins; piston arrow to exhaust; circlips seated.
- [ ] Leak-down passes after assembly.
- [ ] Compression close to new spec after break-in; plug colour light tan.
