# Room-by-room, deep and move-out cleaning

**Goal:** the same high result every visit, in a predictable time, without damaging
surfaces. The difference between an amateur and a pro is a system: fixed order, the right
chemical with enough dwell time, and a final walkthrough.

## 1. Agree the scope (write it down)
- **Routine clean** (weekly/fortnightly): dust reachable surfaces, kitchen counters and
  fronts, hob, sink, bathrooms, floors, bins, beds made. Typical 2–3 bed home: 2–3 hours
  for one cleaner.
- **Deep clean:** routine plus inside microwave, skirting boards, door frames and
  switches, light fittings, behind/under movable furniture, limescale removal, tile grout,
  window sills and tracks, extractor filters. Roughly 2–3× routine time.
- **Move-out / end-of-tenancy:** empty property, everything inside: oven, fridge/freezer,
  all cupboards and drawers, inside windows, walls spot-cleaned, appliances pulled out where
  safe. Landlord/agent inventories often specify it — get the checklist.
- Out of scope unless agreed: carpets, outside windows, high ladder work, mould remediation,
  biohazards.

## 2. Kit
Colour-coded microfibre (e.g. **red = toilets/urinals, yellow = bathroom other surfaces,
blue = general areas, green = kitchen** — common UK scheme), flat mop with separate pads
per area, vacuum with HEPA filter, extension duster, scraper with fresh blades (for glass
and ceramic hobs only), non-scratch pads, spray bottles labelled.
Chemicals: neutral all-purpose (pH ~7), degreaser (alkaline) for kitchens, **acid
descaler** for limescale in bathrooms, glass cleaner, disinfectant for touchpoints and
toilets, oven cleaner (caustic — gloves/eye protection).

## 3. The system
- **Top to bottom, dry to wet, clean to dirty, left to right** around each room so nothing
  is skipped and dust falls onto unclean areas.
- **Order through the house:** start chemicals dwelling first (spray toilets/showers with
  descaler, oven cleaner on the oven), then dust bedrooms and living areas, then kitchen,
  then bathrooms (come back to dwelling products), floors last working toward the exit.
- **Dwell time:** let products sit (often 2–10 min — read the label) instead of scrubbing
  harder.

## 4. Room notes
- **Kitchen:** degrease hob and splashback; fronts of appliances and cupboard handles
  (touchpoints); sink and tap with descaler if limescale; inside microwave (steam a bowl
  of water with lemon for 3 min first); empty and clean bins.
- **Bathroom:** descale shower screen, taps, tiles; toilet: flush, apply cleaner under rim,
  dwell, brush, then wipe outside top to bottom ending with the base, using the red cloth;
  mirror with glass cleaner and a dry microfibre.
- **Bedrooms/living:** dust high to low, including tops of doors, picture frames, lamp shades;
  vacuum edges with the crevice tool, under beds.
- **Floors:** vacuum before mopping; mop with a neutral cleaner, damp not wet, in a figure-8
  toward the door.

## 5. Surface matching (the expensive mistakes)
| Surface | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Marble, limestone, travertine | Any acid (vinegar, lemon, descaler) — etches |
| Granite, quartz | Harsh alkalis and abrasive pads; use pH-neutral |
| Stainless steel | Bleach/chloride; wipe with the grain |
| Wood floors | Excess water, steam mops |
| Natural stone/brass/gold-plated taps | Strong descalers |
Test a hidden spot when in doubt.

## Gotchas
- **Never mix bleach with acids or ammonia** (toxic chlorine/chloramine gas). One chemical
  per surface, rinse between.
- Dirty cloths spread grime: change cloths per room/area; launder hot (60 °C+).
- Keep a "client notes" card: allergies, pets, keys, alarm, surfaces to avoid.

- [ ] Room order followed; dwell products applied first.
- [ ] Colour code respected; no toilet cloth used elsewhere.
- [ ] Stone and special surfaces cleaned with compatible products.
- [ ] Final walkthrough against the checklist, lights on, from the client's view; photos for move-outs.
