# Rough, final and touch-up clean phases

**Goal:** hand over a new or renovated space ready to occupy — no dust on any surface, no
paint specks, labels or glue — without scratching new finishes. Construction dust keeps
settling for days, so the work is done in **phases**, timed with the trades.

## Phases
1. **Rough clean** (after drywall/major trades, before finishes): remove debris and
   offcuts, scrape heavy spills, sweep and HEPA vacuum, empty skips/bins. Goal: stop dust
   being ground in and make finishing work possible.
2. **Final (light/detail) clean** (after trades finish, before inspection/handover): every
   surface top to bottom — detailed below. The big one: often 2–4× a deep residential
   clean in labour.
3. **Touch-up (sparkle/punch) clean** (just before handover/move-in, after the final
   snag fixes): re-dust settled dust, floors, glass smudges, items on the punch list.

## Safety
- Hard hat, hi-vis, safety boots on an active site; site induction.
- **Fine dust contains silica** (from concrete, brick, tile cutting) and gypsum — dry
  sweeping raises it: use **HEPA-filtered** (H-class/M-class in the UK) vacuums and damp
  methods; FFP3/N95+ masks.
- Check with the site manager before using water near electrics or unfinished finishes.

## Final clean method (top to bottom, back to front of each room)
1. **High level:** HEPA vacuum and then damp-wipe ceilings edges, light fittings,
   vents/grilles (remove covers if accessible), tops of door frames and cupboards.
2. **Walls and woodwork:** dust then wipe skirtings, door frames, switches and sockets
   (power off/dry cloth on electrics). Spot-remove marks with a melamine sponge gently —
   it's abrasive on flat/matt paint; test first.
3. **Windows and glass:** remove manufacturer labels and protective films (films left in
   sun bake on — remove early), clean frames and tracks (vacuum then wipe), glass by
   squeegee. Paint specks: scraper with a fresh blade wet, pushed forward only — **test
   on tempered glass first** (see
   repair-maintenance.cleaning-services.window-cleaning.squeegee-and-water-fed-pole-technique).
4. **Kitchens and bathrooms:** inside every cupboard and drawer (sawdust collects), remove
   stickers from appliances and sanitaryware, clean grout haze, silicone smears, polish
   chrome.
5. **Floors last:** HEPA vacuum, then damp mop; hard floors may need several passes for
   fine dust. Carpets: vacuum slowly with a beater bar; extraction only if specified.

## Residue removal (gentlest first)
| Residue | Approach |
|---|---|
| Sticker/label adhesive | Peel slowly; citrus/adhesive remover; plastic scraper |
| Fresh latex paint | Warm water and a cloth; dried: plastic scraper, then a latex paint remover on non-painted surfaces |
| Grout haze on tile | Grout haze remover appropriate to grout type (acid only on cement-grouted ceramic, not stone) |
| Mortar/plaster splash | Soak, plastic scraper; acid brick cleaner only on brick and only diluted as directed |
| Silicone smear | Silicone remover/plastic blade; avoid solvents on acrylic baths |
| Tape residue on wood | Mild solvent tested first; never on unsealed wood |

## Ventilation and HVAC
Ask the contractor to replace or clean HVAC filters after the final clean (and protect
returns during construction) — running the HVAC during work blows dust everywhere again.

## Punch list and handover
Walk the space with the site manager/client, list every item missed, fix it the same day;
photograph completed rooms. Agree when the touch-up clean happens relative to final
trades' snagging.

## Gotchas
- Cleaning finals before trades finish = paying to clean twice. Get the phase schedule
  in the contract.
- Melamine sponges, scourers and blades damage new finishes; test everything.
- Dust in drawer runners and window tracks is the most common punch item.

## Verify
- [ ] Phase scope agreed in writing with dates.
- [ ] HEPA vacuums and damp methods used; masks for silica dust.
- [ ] All labels, films, paint specks and residue removed without marks.
- [ ] Inside cupboards, drawers, tracks and vents clean.
- [ ] Punch list walked and closed; photos taken at handover.
