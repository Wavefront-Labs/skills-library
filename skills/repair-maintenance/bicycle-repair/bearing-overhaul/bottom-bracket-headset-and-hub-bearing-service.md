# Bottom bracket, headset and hub bearing service

**Goal:** bearings that turn smoothly with zero play and no creak. Identify the
standard before buying parts or picking up a tool — the wrong guess is expensive.

## 1. Diagnose
- **Play:** BB — grab both cranks and push/pull sideways. Headset — front brake on, hand
  around the upper headset cup, rock bike forward/back; a knock = play. Hub — wheel in
  frame, rock the rim sideways at the brake.
- **Roughness:** remove the chain and spin the cranks; turn the bar with the front wheel
  off the ground — notchy straight-ahead steering = indexed (pitted) headset bearing.
- **Creak:** isolate before blaming the BB. Common real causes: pedals, chainring bolts,
  seatpost/saddle rails, quick-release, thru-axle, spoke crossings, stem/bar interface.

## 2. Bottom bracket standards (check frame shell width and ID)
| Standard | Shell | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| BSA / English threaded | 68 or 73 mm, 1.37 in x 24 tpi | **Drive side is left-hand thread** (tighten anticlockwise) |
| Italian threaded | 70 mm, 36 x 24 | Both sides right-hand |
| T47 | 68/86 mm, 47 mm threaded | Both sides usually right-hand; check |
| BB86 / BB92 | press-fit, 41 mm ID | Shimano press-fit cups |
| BB30 / PF30 / BB386 | 42 / 46 mm ID | Bearing pressed in frame (BB30) or in cups (PF30) |
Crank spindle (24 mm Hollowtech II, 29 mm DUB, 30 mm) must match the BB.

## 3. Threaded BB swap
1. Remove cranks (Hollowtech II: loosen pinch bolts, remove preload cap; SRAM DUB:
   8 mm self-extractor anticlockwise).
2. Remove cups with the right splined tool; remember the drive-side BSA direction.
3. Clean and inspect threads; chase only if damaged (facing/tapping is a shop tool job).
4. Grease threads (or anti-seize on titanium; thread lock where the maker specifies),
   fit any spacers per the chart for shell width and chainline.
5. Torque: commonly **35–50 N·m** for threaded cups — use the BB maker's figure.
6. Crank: Hollowtech II preload cap finger-tight (0.5–0.7 N·m) just to remove play, then
   pinch bolts alternately to ~12–14 N·m.

## 4. Press-fit BB
- Remove with the correct drift/extraction tool, tapping evenly round the cup.
- Clean the shell; measure ID with calipers — oval or oversized shells cause creaks.
- Press in **square** with a headset/BB press and correct adaptors, never a hammer.
  Retaining compound only where the BB maker allows it. Thread-together press-fit BBs
  (e.g. Wheels Manufacturing, Praxis) cure many creaky PF frames.

## 5. Headset
1. Loosen stem bolts, remove the top cap; hold the fork as it can drop out.
2. Note the order of spacers, compression ring, seals. Clean bearings and seats.
3. Cartridge bearings: if rough after cleaning, replace with the exact size (e.g.
   41.8 mm 45x45° — measure OD, ID, angles or read the bearing stamp).
4. Grease bearing seats and races, reassemble. **Preload:** top cap bolt just until play
   disappears and steering is free; then align stem and tighten stem bolts to spec
   (often 5 N·m — carbon steerers are torque-critical).
5. Steerer must sit 2–3 mm below the top of the stack so the cap can preload.

## 6. Cup-and-cone hubs
1. Remove axle locknut on the non-drive side while holding the cone with a thin cone
   wrench (13–17 mm). Keep drive side assembled to preserve axle position.
2. Clean balls, cups and cones; pitted cones/cups → replace cones (cups usually can't be).
3. Pack fresh grease, set balls (count them), reinsert axle.
4. **Adjust:** a hair of play on a QR hub disappears when the skewer clamps it; on
   thru-axle conversions aim for zero play/no bind. Lock cone and locknut against each other.
5. Cartridge hubs: press bearings out/in with a bearing press on the **outer race**
   (in the hub) or inner race (onto an axle) — never through the balls.

## Gotchas
- Cleaning sealed cartridges with solvent flushes their grease; replace instead.
- A "fixed" headset that's tight wears an index into the bearing in weeks.
- Carbon frames: no metal hammering, follow torque values exactly.

## Verify
- [ ] Standard identified and recorded (shell width, ID/thread, spindle size).
- [ ] No play on rock test; cranks spin freely with chain off.
- [ ] Steering free with no notch; no headset knock under front brake.
- [ ] All fasteners torqued to the maker's values.
- [ ] Creak gone on a test ride under load (out-of-saddle climb).
