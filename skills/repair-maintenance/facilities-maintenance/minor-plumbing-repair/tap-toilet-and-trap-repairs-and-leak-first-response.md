# Taps, toilets, traps and leak first response

**Goal:** stop drips, running toilets and slow drains in a facility with the fewest
parts and least downtime, and respond to a leak in a way that limits damage and gets
the right people involved.

## 1. Leak first response (do this in order)
1. **Isolate:** the local service/isolation valve under the fixture (quarter-turn
   slot or lever), then the zone valve, then the building stopcock. Know where these
   are before you need them — mark them on the rounds map and in the CMMS.
2. **Electrics:** if water is near sockets, lights or panels, keep people away and
   have the circuit isolated by someone authorised. Never wade into water near live
   equipment.
3. **Contain:** buckets, absorbent socks, wet vac; move stock and IT kit; sign the
   wet floor.
4. **Find the source:** water travels along pipes and slabs; the drip on the ceiling
   is often metres from the leak.
5. **Log and escalate:** photos, time, valve isolated, areas affected; call the
   plumbing contractor for pressurised pipe failures, and tell insurers/landlord as
   the site procedure says.

## 2. Leaking taps
- Isolate, open the tap to release pressure, and plug the basin so screws don't
  vanish.
- **Rising-spindle taps (washer type):** drip from spout = worn washer (common sizes
  12.5 mm and 19 mm); leak round the spindle = gland packing/O-ring.
- **Quarter-turn ceramic cartridge taps:** drip = worn ceramic discs or seals; take
  the old cartridge to match spline count (e.g. 20-spline) and length. Replace as a
  unit; check hot/cold rotation direction.
- **Single-lever mixers:** 35 or 40 mm cartridges, brand-specific; record the model in
  the asset record so next time the part is on the van.
- **Sensor taps:** check battery/power supply, sensor window cleanliness, and the
  inline filter and solenoid; low flow is often a blocked filter or flow straightener.
- Threads: PTFE tape (clockwise, 5–8 wraps) on taper threads only; compression and
  fibre-washer joints don't need it.

## 3. Toilets and urinals
- **Running cistern:** overflow running = fill valve shuts too late; lower the float
  or replace the fill valve. Water trickling into the pan = flush valve seal (drop
  valve or siphon diaphragm) worn — replace the seal/washer.
- **Flushometers (Sloan-type, common in commercial washrooms):** continuous run or
  short flush = diaphragm or relief valve; use the matching rebuild kit; set the stop
  valve so the flush cleans the bowl without splashing.
- **Urinals:** auto-flush cisterns should be controlled (PIR or timer) to avoid
  wasting water overnight; clear blocked spreaders and descale.
- Check the pan-to-floor fixing and the pan connector for seepage, which shows as
  stained flooring.

## 4. Traps and drains
1. Plunge first (cup plunger for basins with the overflow sealed with a wet cloth).
2. Remove and clean bottle/P-traps over a bucket; hair and soap in basins, fat in
   kitchen sinks. Replace trap seals; hand-tight plus a quarter turn on plastic nuts.
3. Use a hand drain auger (6–10 mm cable) for blockages beyond the trap.
4. Avoid chemical drain cleaners — they damage traps, splash, and harm the next
   person to open the pipe.
5. Recurring or multi-fixture blockages point to the main drain: call a drainage
   contractor with CCTV and jetting.

## Gotchas
- Old gate valves often fail to seal or leak at the gland when operated; have the
  next isolation point ready.
- Overtightened plastic fittings crack hours later — check again before leaving.
- Little-used outlets are a Legionella risk; follow the site's flushing regime and
  water-hygiene plan. (Draft note: requirements vary — e.g. UK ACoP L8/HSG274,
  US ASHRAE 188.)
- Work on gas water heaters, backflow devices and pressurised mains is for licensed
  plumbers in most jurisdictions. See
  repair-maintenance.building-services-equipment-maintenance.building-plumbing-maintenance.booster-pumps-hot-water-and-backflow-testing.

## Verify
- [ ] Fixture dry after 10 minutes on full pressure; joints wiped and rechecked.
- [ ] Cistern refills and stops at the waterline; no trickle into the pan.
- [ ] Basin drains freely with the tap full on.
- [ ] Isolation valves reopened fully and left in the correct position.
- [ ] Parts and model numbers recorded on the work order.
