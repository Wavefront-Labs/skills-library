# Informal reviews and desk checks

**Goal:** cheap, frequent static testing that catches obvious defects before anything heavier. CTFL v4.0 3.2.4: informal reviews follow no defined process and need no formal documented output.

## Informal review
No defined process and no formal documented output; the main objective is detecting anomalies.
## Escalate to a walkthrough, technical review or inspection when
The syllabus says the required level of formality depends on:
- the SDLC being followed and the maturity of the development process;
- the criticality and complexity of the work product;
- legal or regulatory requirements;
- the need for an audit trail.

The same work product can be reviewed informally first and more formally later. A walkthrough is led by the author; a technical review is performed by technically qualified reviewers and led by a moderator.

## Verify
The review type chosen matches the formality factors above.
