# Design input fallbacks and accessibility
**Goal:** Support seated play, one-handed use and alternative input.

- Offer seated and standing modes, adjustable height and reach, and one-handed interaction.
- Support controllers, hands and gaze/pinch where possible, with audio and haptic cues in addition to visuals.
- Provide subtitles and colour-blind-safe cues.

## Gotchas
- Do not require fast head turns or precise two-handed gestures to progress.

## Self-check
A user can complete the core flow using one controller while seated.
