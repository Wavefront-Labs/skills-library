# Configure queues, routing and SLAs
**Goal:** Route and time-box cases.

- Queues hold cases; use unified routing for rules and workstreams, or simple routing rules.
- SLA items track first response and resolve-by with pause/resume on hold status.
- Entitlements govern support levels.

## Gotchas
- SLAs need business hours calendars to be accurate.

## Self-check
A case shows an SLA timer and moves to the right queue.
