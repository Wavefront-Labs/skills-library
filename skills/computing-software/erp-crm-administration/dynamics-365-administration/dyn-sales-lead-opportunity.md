# Configure Sales lead and opportunity process
**Goal:** Set up business process flows.

- Business process flows guide stages for lead to opportunity; qualify creates account, contact and opportunity records.
- Customise stages and add fields; manage product catalog and price lists.
- Use sales insights if licensed.

## Gotchas
- Process flows are per table and role; check availability for users.

## Self-check
A lead qualifies and creates an opportunity.
