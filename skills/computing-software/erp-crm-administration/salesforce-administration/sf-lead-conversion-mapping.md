# Configure lead conversion
**Goal:** Control how leads convert to accounts, contacts and opportunities.

- Lead Settings and lead conversion field mapping (Setup > Lead Custom Fields Mapping) decide what carries to each object.
- Lead assignment rules and queues distribute leads; web-to-lead captures them.
- Convert with or without creating an opportunity.

## Gotchas
- Mapping applies only to custom lead fields you map.

## Self-check
Converted lead data lands in the right account and contact fields.
