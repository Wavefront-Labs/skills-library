# Set up opportunities, products and price books
**Goal:** Model products and prices on opportunities.

- Define stages/sales processes, products, the Standard Price Book entries, then custom price books.
- Opportunity products need a price book; once products are added the price book cannot change.
- Configure forecasts (Collaborative Forecasts) by categories and hierarchy.

## Gotchas
- A product needs a standard price book entry before any custom price book.

## Self-check
Adding a product to an opportunity shows the right price.
