# Understand company codes and charts of accounts
**Goal:** Know the FI organisational structure.

- A company code is the smallest unit for which financial statements are produced; it is assigned a chart of accounts and fiscal year variant.
- G/L accounts are maintained with FS00; posting periods are opened in OB52.
- Currency settings in the company code.

## Gotchas
- Posting in a closed period fails with a period error.

## Self-check
A posting appears in the correct company code and period.
