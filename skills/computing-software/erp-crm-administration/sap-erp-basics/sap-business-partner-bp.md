# Use Business Partner in S/4HANA
**Goal:** Understand customer/vendor master consolidation.

- In S/4HANA the Business Partner (transaction BP) is the single entry point for customers and vendors; Customer-Vendor Integration (CVI) keeps the classic master data in sync.
- A partner has roles (customer, vendor) and one general data record.
- Plan CVI during conversion.

## Gotchas
- CVI configuration is mandatory in S/4HANA conversions.

## Self-check
Creating a BP with a vendor role makes the vendor available for purchasing.
