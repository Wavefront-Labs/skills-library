# Amazon two-step verification

**Goal:** your Amazon account (stored cards, addresses, order history, Alexa, Ring) needs more than a password.

## Turn it on
Account > Login & security > **2-Step Verification (2SV) Settings** > Turn on.
- Add an **authenticator app** as preferred method (scan QR, enter code).
- Add a backup method (second phone number or authenticator on another device).
- Passkey: Login & security > Passkey > Set up (sign in with device unlock; 2SV still applies when you use a password).

## Trusted browsers
"Don't require OTP on this browser" remembers the device. Review and clear in 2SV settings > Devices you've
trusted, especially after selling a computer.

## Related settings
- Login & security: email, phone, password.
- Your Payments: remove old cards.
- Amazon Household: who shares your Prime and payment methods; teens get their own logins.
- Alexa privacy settings and Ring account share the Amazon login; Ring has its own 2FA prompt.

## Gotchas
- Amazon impersonation calls/texts ("suspicious purchase") are common; Amazon won't ask for 2SV codes.
- Removing SMS entirely isn't always possible; make the authenticator the preferred method.
