# Tech safety plan when leaving an abusive relationship

**Goal:** abusers often monitor through shared accounts and devices they set up.
Sequence the changes with safety in mind; work with an advocate where possible
(e.g. NNEDV Safety Net's techsafety.org resources, local domestic violence services).

## Phase 1: before leaving (quiet)
- Use a **safe device** the partner has never touched (library computer, new phone) for
  planning and new accounts.
- Create a **new email** on the safe device; don't link it to old accounts yet.
- Inventory shared things: phone plan (account holder can see call logs, locate device),
  family sharing (Apple/Google), shared cloud photo albums, smart home, car apps, banking,
  streaming accounts, password manager families.
- Note signs of monitoring but don't remove stalkerware or trackers yet if that could
  escalate.

## Phase 2: at or after leaving
1. Apple **Safety Check** (Settings > Privacy & Security > Safety Check > Emergency
   Reset or Manage Sharing & Access): stops location sharing, resets privacy permissions,
   signs out other devices, changes Apple Account password. Google: Family Link/Family
   group, Location sharing, Find Hub devices.
2. Change passwords + 2FA on email, Apple/Google, social, banking; remove partner's phone
   numbers and devices; check email forwarding rules.
3. Leave the **family phone plan**; get your own number (or port yours).
4. Check car (connected car app), bags and car for **Bluetooth trackers** (AirTag alerts,
   Android unknown tracker alerts).
5. Smart home: remove partner from Google Home/Alexa/Ring, change Wi-Fi password.

## Verify
- [ ] No location sharing, no unknown devices in accounts, own phone plan.
- [ ] Advocate or trusted person aware of the plan.
