# Family security plan

**Goal:** a one-page plan that keeps the whole household's accounts and devices safe, including the least
technical members.

## Roles
- **Tech lead:** the person who sets things up and helps others (and a backup person).
- **Recovery holders:** who holds emergency kits, legacy contacts, recovery codes.

## Baseline for every member
| Item | Standard |
|---|---|
| Password manager | Family plan; each person's own vault plus a shared household vault |
| MFA | On for email, Apple/Google/Microsoft account, bank; passkeys where possible |
| Phone | Passcode 6+ digits, auto-updates, Find My on |
| Recovery | Each adult's recovery phone/email is another adult they trust, not an old number |
| Scams | Family **safe word** for emergency calls; "hang up and call back" rule |

## Kids
Managed child accounts (Family Link / Screen Time), app approval, location sharing with parents only, privacy
settings on games and social apps, teach "tell us if someone asks for codes or photos".

## Older relatives
Credit freezes, call blocking, simplified phone, bank alerts, trusted contact at the bank, regular check-ins about
"strange calls".

## Shared infrastructure
Router admin password, guest Wi-Fi for visitors/IoT, backups for each computer and phone, shared calendar of
renewals (domain, password manager).

## Write it down
Store the plan as a note in the household vault; review yearly or when someone joins/leaves the home.

## Gotchas
- Don't share one email/Apple Account between people; separate accounts make recovery and privacy possible.
- Teenagers need privacy too; agree on what's monitored and why.
