# LinkedIn privacy settings

**Goal:** a professional presence that does not hand scammers, data brokers and AI trainers more than you intend.

## Visibility (Settings & Privacy > Visibility)
- **Edit your public profile**: limit what non-members and search engines see; hide photo, headline details,
  current position, or turn public visibility off entirely.
- **Profile viewing options**: Private mode when researching people (you lose seeing your own viewers).
- **Who can see or download your email address**: Only you or 1st-degree connections; never "Anyone on LinkedIn".
- **Who can see your connections**: Only you (connection lists are harvested for social engineering).
- **Profile discovery using email address / phone number**: Nobody or 1st-degree.
- **Profile visibility off LinkedIn** (partner apps): off.
- **Share profile updates with your network**: off when job hunting quietly.

## Data privacy
- **Data for Generative AI Improvement**: off (stops your posts/profile training LinkedIn's models; affects future use only).
- Job seeking preferences: "Open to Work" set to **Recruiters only**, not the public photo frame.
- **Get a copy of your data** to see what is held.

## Advertising data
Turn off: ads beyond LinkedIn, profile data for ad personalization, interests and traits, data from third parties
(partners and Insight Tag).

## Scam awareness
- Fake recruiters move you to Telegram/WhatsApp, offer jobs without interviews, or ask for ID and bank details.
- Verify recruiters via the company careers page; LinkedIn's verification badge helps but is not proof of intent.
- Do not list your phone number or personal email publicly.

## Verify
- [ ] View your profile as public (Edit public profile preview) and confirm only intended fields show.
- [ ] Generative AI and ad data toggles off.
