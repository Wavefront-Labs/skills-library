# AI assistant training and history settings

**Goal:** know what happens to what you type into chatbots, and set each service to keep
and reuse as little as you're comfortable with.

## The levers (names vary; check each service's current settings)
| Lever | What it does |
|---|---|
| Model training / "Improve the model for everyone" | Whether your chats may be used to train future models |
| Chat history / activity | Whether conversations are saved to your account and for how long |
| Temporary / incognito chat | One-off chats not saved to history (often retained briefly for abuse monitoring) |
| Memory / personalisation | Assistant remembers facts about you across chats |
| Connected apps | Access to email, drive, calendar |
| Human review | Some services have reviewers read samples |

## Where to look
- **ChatGPT**: Settings > Data controls (training toggle, Temporary Chat), Personalisation >
  Memory.
- **Gemini**: Gemini Apps Activity (on/off, auto-delete period); human reviewers may see
  chats when activity is on; Connected apps.
- **Claude**: Settings > Privacy (training on chats setting), delete conversations.
- **Microsoft Copilot**: Privacy settings for model training on conversations, personalisation.
- **Meta AI**: interactions may be used to improve AI; limited controls; avoid sensitive use.

## Habits
- Delete old conversations you don't need.
- Use temporary chats for anything personal.
- Remember shared chat links are public to anyone with the link.
