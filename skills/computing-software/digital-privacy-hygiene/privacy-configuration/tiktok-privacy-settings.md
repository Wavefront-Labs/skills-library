# TikTok privacy settings

**Goal:** control who sees your videos, finds your account and contacts you on TikTok.

## Settings and privacy > Privacy
| Setting | Recommend |
|---|---|
| Private account | On (unless you're building an audience) |
| Suggest your account to others | Off for contacts, Facebook friends, mutual connections, people who open/send links to you |
| Sync contacts and Facebook friends | Off; remove previously synced |
| Location services | Off in phone settings for TikTok (TikTok can still infer approximate location from IP/SIM) |
| Activity status | Off |
| Profile views (history) | Off |
| Downloads | Off |
| Comments | Friends or No one |
| Mentions and tags | Friends / No one |
| Direct messages | No one or Friends |
| Duet / Stitch | Only you or Friends |
| Liked videos / Following list | Only me |

## Ads
Settings and privacy > Ads: personalised ads off where available; Ad interest categories review.

## Teens
Accounts for 13-15 are private by default with DMs disabled; parents can use **Family Pairing** to manage
settings.

## Data
Settings > Account > Download your data; Deactivate or delete account.

## Gotchas
- Videos posted while public may already be saved or reposted.
- The phone number used to sign up can make your account findable unless suggestions are off.
