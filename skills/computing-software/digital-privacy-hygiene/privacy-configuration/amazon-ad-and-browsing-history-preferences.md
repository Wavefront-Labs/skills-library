# Amazon ad preferences

**Goal:** limit how Amazon uses your shopping and viewing for ads on and off Amazon.

## Ad preferences
Go to **amazon.com/adprefs** (Your Account > Advertising preferences) and choose **Do not show me interest-based ads
provided by Amazon**. This applies to the browser/device you're signed in on; repeat on each browser and in the app
settings. You still see ads, just not targeted by Amazon's data.

## Browsing history
Your Account > **Browsing History** > Manage history: turn **Browsing history** off and remove items. Hidden items
stop driving recommendations.

## Purchases
Archive orders you don't want visible to others in the household (Your Orders > Archive). This hides, it doesn't
delete; Amazon keeps the data.

## Other surfaces
- **Alexa**: Alexa Privacy settings: review and delete voice recordings, turn off use of recordings for improvement.
- **Prime Video / Fire TV**: Fire TV Settings > Preferences > Privacy settings: turn off Collect App Usage Data and
  Interest-based Ads; reset the advertising ID.
- **Kindle**: reading data syncs; turn off public notes and highlights in profile settings.
- **Sidewalk** on Echo/Ring devices: separate skill.

## Deleting data
Request your data (Account > Request your data) to see what's held; for deletion, use Amazon's privacy request page or
close the account.

## Gotchas
Clearing cookies resets the ad opt-out on some browsers; recheck after cleaning.
