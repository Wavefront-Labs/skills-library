# Loyalty program data

**Goal:** get the discount without handing over a detailed record of everything you buy.

## What's collected
Every purchase (item, time, store, payment card), app location, email opens, and inferences (health, pregnancy,
income, household size). Retailers share or sell this to advertisers and retail media networks; some run their own ad
networks using it. Card-linked offers tie bank transactions to profiles.

## Limit at sign-up
- Use an email alias and a phone number you're comfortable giving (cashier lookup is by phone number).
- Skip optional fields (birthday, household members, interests).
- Don't link the app to location "always"; deny or "while using".
- Use cash or a card not linked to the profile for sensitive purchases (pharmacy, alcohol, pregnancy tests).

## Opt out and delete
- Account privacy settings: marketing emails, personalised offers, "share with partners".
- California and other state laws: request "do not sell or share" and deletion; companies offering programme
  discounts must give a **notice of financial incentive** describing what data the discount buys (CCPA).
- EU/UK: object to profiling for marketing (absolute right) and request access to see the inferences.

## Decide
For a store you visit weekly, the discount may be worth it with the limits above. For occasional shops, decline and
pay the regular price.

## Gotchas
- Pharmacy loyalty data is usually not covered by health privacy laws like HIPAA.
- Programs merge after acquisitions; your data moves with them.
