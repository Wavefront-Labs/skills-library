# Posting about children (sharenting)

**Goal:** family and friends see the kids; strangers, data brokers and future classmates don't get a searchable record.

## Before posting, leave out
- Full name together with birth date (key identity theft details).
- School name, uniform logo, team jersey, street signs, house number, car plates.
- Real-time location; post after you leave.
- Bath, nappy, medical or embarrassing content the child may hate at 14.
- GPS metadata (most social apps strip it, messaging apps and email may not).

## Audience
- Use private accounts, close friends lists, or a shared private album (iCloud shared album, Google Photos shared
  album) instead of public posts.
- Ask relatives not to repost publicly; a grandparent's public account undoes your settings.
- Review tags so others can't tag the child's name.

## Consent
Ask older children before posting; respect "no". Some countries now protect children's image rights explicitly (France
passed a law on children's image rights in 2024).

## School and clubs
Check photo consent forms; you can opt out of public website or newsletter photos. Ask how photos are stored and who
sees class apps.

## Clean up
Search your own profiles for older posts with identifying details; archive or restrict them. Use Instagram/Facebook
"Limit past posts".
