# Facebook groups exposure risks

**Goal:** treat each group as an audience you cannot fully control, and keep sensitive membership and posts
out of reach.

## Know the group type
| Type | Who sees posts | Who sees members |
|---|---|---|
| Public | Anyone, on or off Facebook | Anyone |
| Private, visible | Members only | Members (anyone can see the group exists) |
| Private, hidden | Members only | Members; group not searchable |
Even in private groups any member can screenshot, and membership questions/answers go to admins.

## Higher-risk groups
- Health, addiction, sexuality, immigration, and survivor support groups: membership alone reveals sensitive
  facts. Prefer hidden groups with vetted admins, or platforms built for anonymity. Use **Post anonymously** where
  admins allow it.
- Neighbourhood and parenting groups: posts reveal home location, routines, children's schools.
- Buy/sell and rental groups: magnets for marketplace and rental scams, fake admins DMing members, and
  "hacked friend" posts.

## Before joining or posting
- Check About: privacy type, creation date and **name history** (a renamed group may have been bought to
  repurpose its audience).
- Assume posts are permanent; edit history is visible to members.
- Never answer "fun" group quizzes (first car, pet name) that mirror security questions.

## Clean up
- Groups > **Your groups** > Leave groups you no longer use; leaving removes notifications but past posts may
  remain; delete them first if needed.
- Activity log (Settings > Your activity) > Groups: review comments and posts.
- Profile: limit who sees your group activity via audience settings and keep **Who can see your friends list**
  on Only me.

## Verify
- [ ] Sensitive groups are hidden with known admins, or left; old posts deleted.
