# Google About me profile visibility

**Goal:** know which personal details anyone who emails you, sees your reviews, or shares a document with you can see.

## Open it
myaccount.google.com > **Personal info** > "Choose what others see" (opens aboutme.google.com).

## Review each item
| Item | Typical exposure | Suggested |
|---|---|---|
| Name and profile photo | Visible to anyone you interact with | Keep name; choose a photo you're comfortable being public |
| Birthday | Can be set to public | Only you |
| Gender | | Only you |
| Work, education, places lived | Older Google+ data may linger | Remove or only you |
| Email and phone | | Only you, unless needed |
| Links / websites | | Remove anything sensitive |

Use **"Visible to"** controls per field; "Your organisation" applies to work accounts.

## Related surfaces
- **Google Maps contributor profile**: Maps > your profile > settings > restrict profile; reviews and photos show your
  name and photo publicly.
- **YouTube channel** name may be your real name; change in YouTube Studio.
- **Google Calendar**: shared calendars show event details; check default visibility.
- **Google Docs**: shared documents show your name and photo to viewers.

## Verify
On aboutme.google.com use the **profile preview** to see the public view while signed out.
