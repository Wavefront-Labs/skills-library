# X privacy settings

**Goal:** lock down an X account to the exposure you actually want.

## Settings and privacy > Privacy and safety
| Area | Setting |
|---|---|
| Audience and tagging | **Protect your posts** (followers only) if not public-facing; Photo tagging off or only people you follow |
| Your posts | Add location information off; mark media as sensitive if applicable |
| Direct messages | Allow message requests from: No one / people you follow |
| Discoverability and contacts | Let others find you by email off; by phone off; delete synced address book contacts |
| Ads preferences | Personalised ads off |
| Off-X activity / data sharing with business partners | Off |
| Location information | Personalise based on places you've been off |
| Grok & third-party collaborators | Allow your public data and interactions to be used for training: off |

## Security and account access
Two-factor with authenticator app or security key (SMS 2FA is restricted to paid tiers); review apps and sessions;
connected apps remove unused.

## Old posts
Download your archive (Your account > Download an archive), then delete or restrict old posts with a tool you
trust or manually.

## Gotchas
- Protected posts already retweeted/screenshotted stay out there.
- Display name and bio are public even when posts are protected.
