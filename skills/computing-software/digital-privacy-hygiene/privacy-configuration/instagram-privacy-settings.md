# Instagram privacy settings

**Goal:** limit who sees your posts, location patterns and social graph on Instagram,
which strangers mine for stalking, scams and doxxing.

## Settings and activity (profile > menu)
- **Account privacy** > **Private account** on. Existing followers stay; review them and
  remove strangers.
- **Close Friends** list for personal stories.
- **Story, live and location**: hide story from specific people; turn off sharing stories
  to Facebook if unwanted; don't allow resharing.
- **Activity in Friends tab / Activity status** (Messages and story replies > Show activity
  status) off.
- **Tags and mentions**: allow tags from People you follow; **Manually approve tags**.
- **Messages and story replies**: message requests only from people you follow; hide
  offensive/unknown requests.
- **Comments**: limit to followers; hidden words filter.
- **Accounts Center > Your information and permissions**: **Upload contacts** off (and
  delete previously uploaded contacts); ad preferences and "Activity information from ad
  partners" off.

## Posting habits
- Post location **after** you've left; avoid geotagging home, school, gym routines.
- Check photo backgrounds (house numbers, car plates, uniforms).
- Separate public "creator" account from personal private account if you need both.

## Verify
- [ ] Logged out view of your profile shows "This account is private".
- [ ] Tag approval on; contacts upload off.
