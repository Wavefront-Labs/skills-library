# Carrier CPNI and location data opt-outs

**Goal:** your phone company uses your calling records and location only to provide service, not for advertising.

## Background
- **CPNI** (Customer Proprietary Network Information): who you call, when, how long, services you use. FCC rules let
  carriers use it for marketing unless you opt out (or with opt-in for some uses).
- In 2024 the FCC fined the major US carriers for selling customers' location data to aggregators without proper
  consent.

## Where to opt out (names change; look for privacy choices)
| Carrier | Where |
|---|---|
| Verizon | My Verizon > Account > Privacy preferences / Custom Experience programs; CPNI choice |
| AT&T | Privacy Choices: Enhanced Relevant Advertising, CPNI marketing opt-out |
| T-Mobile | Privacy Center > Your choices / Privacy Dashboard: analytics and reporting, relevant ads |
| MVNOs/prepaid | Privacy policy page; call customer care to opt out of CPNI marketing |

Opt out of: advertising programs, analytics/insights "using my data", CPNI marketing use, sharing with affiliates.

## Also
- Delete the phone's advertising ID and limit app location permissions; carriers aren't the only collectors.
- Set an account PIN to protect CPNI from pretexters.

## Gotchas
- Opt-outs are per line; check each line on a family plan.
- Some opt-outs need renewing after plan changes or carrier mergers.
