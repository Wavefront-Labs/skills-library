# Prescreened offer and marketing opt-outs

**Goal:** less junk mail with your personal data (pre-approved credit offers are an identity-theft risk if stolen
from your mailbox) and fewer marketing calls.

## Prescreened credit and insurance offers (US)
- **optoutprescreen.com** (official, run by the credit bureaus) or call 1-888-5-OPT-OUT.
- Online: opt out for 5 years; for permanent opt-out, sign and mail the form it generates.
- Asks for SSN and date of birth (optional but improves matching); that's expected on the official site.

## Marketing mail
- **DMAchoice.org** (Association of National Advertisers): opt out of catalogs, magazine offers and other mail
  categories from member companies; small registration fee, valid for years.
- Contact specific catalogue senders to remove you; many honour requests within weeks.

## Telemarketing
- **donotcall.gov**: register landline and mobile numbers; it's free and permanent. Illegal robocallers ignore it;
  use carrier spam filtering too.

## Email
Unsubscribe from legitimate senders; use aliases for shopping.

## Gotchas
- Opt-outs take weeks to months to take effect.
- Look-alike "opt out" services that charge for this are unnecessary.
