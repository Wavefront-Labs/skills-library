# Snap Map ghost mode

**Goal:** Snapchat friends (or strangers) can't follow where you go.

## Settings
1. Open the Map (swipe right from the camera or tap the location pin) > gear icon (**Settings**).
2. Choose:
   - **Ghost Mode**: nobody sees your location; can be set for 3 hours, 24 hours or until turned off.
   - **My Friends, Except...**: exclude specific people.
   - **Only These Friends**: share with a short list.
3. Turn off **Allow friends to request my location** if you don't want prompts.

Snapchat's location sharing is off by default and only ever with friends; check it wasn't turned on.

## Other location leaks in Snapchat
- **Our Story / Spotlight** submissions can appear publicly on the map at the place taken.
- **Location stickers and filters** reveal the town or venue.
- Phone permission: Settings > Apps > Snapchat > Location: set "While using" or "Never"; Ghost Mode still gets your
  location to show you the map.

## Teens
Family Center lets parents see friend lists and, if the teen agrees, location sharing; discuss Ghost Mode rather than
forcing it, and teach that a friend's screenshot of the map shares location further.

## Verify
Ask a friend to check whether your Bitmoji appears on their map.
