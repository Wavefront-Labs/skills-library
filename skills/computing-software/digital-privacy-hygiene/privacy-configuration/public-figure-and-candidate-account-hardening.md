# Public figure and candidate account hardening

**Goal:** a public role makes you a target for phishing, takeover, impersonation and harassment; plan for it before
it happens.

## Accounts
- Enrol primary email in **Google Advanced Protection** or use Microsoft account with security keys; programmes like
  Microsoft AccountGuard and Defending Digital Campaigns (US, nonpartisan) offer free security keys and support.
- Passkeys or hardware keys on every social account; remove SMS recovery.
- **Separate personal and official accounts**; keep personal ones private and minimal.

## Staff access
- Use platform roles (Meta Business Suite, X delegate, YouTube Studio permissions) rather than sharing passwords.
- Password manager organisation for shared credentials; offboard staff the day they leave.
- One person owns the recovery methods; document it.

## Impersonation
Request verification where available, monitor for lookalike accounts, and report them; tell followers which accounts
are official on your website.

## Doxxing and harassment
- Opt out of data brokers; remove home address from public records where law allows (many US states have address
  confidentiality for officials).
- Lock down family members' accounts too; attackers pivot to them.
- Plan for coordinated harassment: moderation tools, who reports, when to involve police.

## Devices
Lockdown Mode on phones for high-risk periods, updates, no unofficial apps, travel devices for high-risk trips.
