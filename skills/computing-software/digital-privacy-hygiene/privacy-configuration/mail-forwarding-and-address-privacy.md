# Mail forwarding and address privacy

**Goal:** your home address spreads through deliveries, account sign-ups, subscriptions and public records into
people-search sites. Give out a different address wherever you legally can.

## Options
| Option | Notes |
|---|---|
| **PO Box** (USPS) | Cheap; some post offices offer Street Addressing so UPS/FedEx can deliver there. Requires ID and a physical address on file with USPS. |
| **Private mailbox (CMRA)** e.g. UPS Store | Real street address with "PMB"/"#" suffix; accepts all carriers. Requires **USPS Form 1583**, notarised or verified, with two IDs. |
| **Virtual mailbox** | Scans envelopes and contents; convenient for travellers and survivors, but the provider opens your mail; vet them. |
| **Address Confidentiality Program (ACP)** | State programs for survivors of domestic violence, stalking, trafficking: a free substitute legal address accepted by state agencies, often including voter and DMV records. |
| **Parcel lockers** | Amazon Hub, carrier lockers: packages without revealing home. |

## Where to use it
Online shopping, subscriptions, magazine and loyalty programs, business registrations (use a registered agent),
non-government sign-ups. Some uses legally need a residential address (driver's licence in many states, voter
registration outside ACPs, banks' KYC); do not misstate it there.

## Avoid leaks
- A USPS **change of address** feeds the National Change of Address (NCOA) data that licensed mailers use; data
  brokers pick up new addresses this way. Consider temporary forwarding plus updating key accounts directly.
- Property records list owners: some states allow trusts/LLCs or confidentiality for certain professions.
- Wedding/birth announcements, package photos with labels, and selling items with pickup addresses all expose it.

## Verify
- [ ] Online accounts and shipping profiles use the alternative address; people-search results checked afterwards.
