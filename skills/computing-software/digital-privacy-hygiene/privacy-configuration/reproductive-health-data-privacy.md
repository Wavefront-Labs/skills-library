# Reproductive health data privacy

**Goal:** limit the digital trail around pregnancy, contraception and abortion where it could be used against you or
someone helping you.

## Who is and isn't covered
HIPAA covers doctors, clinics and insurers, not most apps, searches, retailers or your phone. Data held by apps and
brokers can be sold or obtained by legal process.

## Apps
- Period trackers: choose apps that store data **locally only** with no account (e.g. drip., Euki) or turn off cloud
  sync; check the privacy policy for sharing with third parties.
- Apple Health data is end-to-end encrypted in iCloud with two-factor; Android health apps vary.

## Searches and browsing
Use a private browser or Tor Browser, a private search engine, and don't sign into accounts while researching. Clear
or auto-delete Google Web & App Activity.

## Communications
Use Signal with disappearing messages for sensitive conversations; avoid SMS and social DMs. Talk with the people
involved about what not to write.

## Location
- Turn off location history (Google Timeline settings, iPhone Significant Locations) and limit app location to "while
  using".
- Ad SDK location data has been sold showing visits to clinics; delete the advertising ID and deny location to
  non-essential apps. Consider leaving the phone at home or turning it off for clinic visits in high-risk places.

## Purchases
Pay cash or with a card not linked to loyalty programmes for tests and medication.

## Get help
Organisations like EFF and Digital Defense Fund publish up-to-date guidance; laws change quickly by state.
