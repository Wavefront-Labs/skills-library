# Limiting past posts and reviewing tags

**Goal:** ten years of public posts reveal schools, relatives, addresses, routines and
opinions you no longer hold. Reduce that archive.

## Facebook
- Settings & privacy > Settings > **Posts** (Audience and visibility) > **Limit who can see
  past posts**: changes all past Public/Friends-of-friends posts to Friends in one click.
- **Activity log** > Your activity across Facebook > Posts: filter by year; select and
  **Archive** (hidden, restorable) or **Trash** in bulk.
- **Profile and tagging**: Review posts you're tagged in before they appear on your profile
  (on); Review tags people add to your posts (on). Activity log > "Photos and videos you're
  tagged in" > remove tags.
- Profile details (Intro): remove workplace, school, hometown, relationship, family members
  or set to Only me.

## X (Twitter) and others
- Download your archive first (Settings > Your account > Download an archive).
- Delete old posts in bulk with a reputable tool that uses your archive, or protect the
  account (Protected posts) to hide them from non-followers.
- Instagram: Your activity > Photos and videos > bulk Archive/Delete.
- LinkedIn: Settings > Visibility to limit who sees your connections and activity.

## Gotchas
- Others' copies (screenshots, shares, archive.org) remain.
- Deleting tags doesn't delete the photo from the poster's account; ask them or report.

## Verify
- [ ] Logged-out or "View as" profile shows no old public posts or personal details.
