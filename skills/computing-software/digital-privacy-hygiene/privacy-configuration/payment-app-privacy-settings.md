# Payment app privacy settings

**Goal:** payment apps can publish who you pay, when and why. Make transactions private and keep these apps for
people you know.

## Venmo (most exposed)
Settings > Privacy:
- **Default privacy setting**: Private.
- **Past transactions**: Change All to Private (old payments stay public until you do).
- **Friends list**: Private (and untick "appear in other users' friends lists").
Settings > Friends & social: **Sync contacts** off, Facebook connection off.
Payment notes are visible to the counterparty and, if public, to everyone: keep them bland.

## Cash App
Profile > Privacy & Security: turn off **Contacts** sync; limit who can find you by phone/email; enable Security
Lock (PIN/biometric for payments). Your $Cashtag is a public identifier; do not post it alongside your real name.

## PayPal
Settings > Data & privacy: review **Manage shared info** (contacts sync, social activity); turn off personalized ads
and data sharing where offered. Remove saved authorisations for merchants you no longer use (Payments > Manage
automatic payments).

## Zelle
No social feed, but payments go bank-to-bank instantly and are rarely reversible. Fraud protection is weak when you
authorise the payment yourself.

## Safe-use rules
- Only pay people you know in person; for strangers and marketplaces use a credit card or the platform's
  protected checkout.
- "Accidental payment, please send it back" and "pay to verify you are real" are scams.
- Turn on app-level biometric/PIN lock and notifications for every transaction.

- [ ] Venmo default and past transactions Private; friends list hidden; contact sync off in every payment app.
