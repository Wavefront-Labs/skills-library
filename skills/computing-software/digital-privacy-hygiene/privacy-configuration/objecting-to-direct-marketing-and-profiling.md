# Objecting to direct marketing and profiling

**Goal:** stop companies using your data for marketing and profiling they justify as "legitimate interest".

## Rights (GDPR text, Articles 21 and 22)
- **Article 21(2)-(3) direct marketing**: you may object at any time, including to profiling related to that
  marketing. Once you object, the personal data shall no longer be processed for such purposes. There is no
  balancing test in the text.
- **Article 21(1) processing based on Article 6(1)(e) or (f)** (public interest task or legitimate interests, including
  profiling based on them): you object on grounds relating to your particular situation, at any time. The controller
  must stop unless it demonstrates compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, rights and freedoms,
  or needs the data for legal claims.
- **Article 21(4)**: the right must be brought to your attention at the latest at first communication, clearly and
  separately from other information. **Article 21(5)**: for information society services you may object by
  automated means using technical specifications.
- **Article 22(1)**: right not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing, including
  profiling, that has legal effects or similarly significantly affects you. It does not apply where the decision is
  needed for a contract, authorised by Union or Member State law with safeguards, or based on your explicit
  consent. In the contract and consent cases the controller must at least allow human intervention, let you express
  your view and let you contest the decision (22(3)).

## How
- Unsubscribe links and marketing preferences handle email; for profiling and data sharing write explicitly:
  > I object under Article 21(2) GDPR to all processing of my personal data for direct marketing, including profiling.
  > I also object under Article 21(1) to processing for [analytics/ad targeting] based on legitimate interests.
  > Please confirm in writing that you have stopped.
- Ask for the controller's response deadline in its privacy notice or the regulator's guidance; the Article 21 and
  22 texts do not set one.

## Escalate
No satisfactory response: complain to your data protection regulator with your request and dates.
