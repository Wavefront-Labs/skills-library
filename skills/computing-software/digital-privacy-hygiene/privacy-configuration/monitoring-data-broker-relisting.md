# Monitoring data broker relisting

**Goal:** opt-outs that stay done, since brokers rebuild profiles from new data and relist people.

## Tracking sheet
| Broker | Opt-out URL | Date submitted | Confirmation | Email/alias used | Recheck due | Status |
Keep it in the vault or a spreadsheet.

## Schedule
- Recheck major people-search sites every **3 months** for the first year, then every 6 months.
- Recheck after events that create new records: moving house, marriage/name change, buying property, new phone
  number, voter registration changes.

## How to recheck quickly
- Search your name + city + age on each broker and on a search engine.
- Google **Results about you** monitors search results for your phone, email and address and alerts you when new
  ones appear.
- Paid removal services re-scan automatically; still spot check a few.

## Resubmitting
Use the same email alias for all opt-outs so you can filter confirmations and spot sites that sell that alias.
Save screenshots of the listing and the confirmation.

## Escalate
Brokers that ignore opt-outs: in California use DROP and file complaints with the CPPA; in other states with
broker registries, report to the attorney general.

## Gotchas
- Some brokers own multiple sites (opt-out on one doesn't cover the others unless stated).
- Opting out can require providing more data (to match); give the minimum and use an alias.
