# Filing a privacy regulator complaint

**Goal:** when a company ignores an access/deletion request or misuses your data, get a regulator involved
effectively.

## First, exhaust the company route
Most regulators expect you to have complained to the organisation and waited:
- GDPR/UK GDPR access or erasure: response due within **one month** (extendable by two for complex requests).
- CCPA/CPRA: **45 days** (extendable by 45 with notice).
- Australia OAIC: complain to the organisation and wait 30 days.
Send the request in writing, keep a dated copy, and send one reminder citing the deadline.

## Pick the regulator
| Where / what | Regulator |
|---|---|
| EU | National Data Protection Authority where you live (it routes to the lead authority, e.g. Ireland DPC for many tech firms) |
| UK | Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) |
| California consumer rights | California Privacy Protection Agency (CPPA) or the Attorney General |
| Other US states with privacy laws | State Attorney General |
| US deceptive privacy practices | FTC (reportfraud.ftc.gov) |
| Credit reports, financial data | CFPB |
| Health data held by providers/insurers | HHS Office for Civil Rights (HIPAA) |
| Student records | US Dept of Education Student Privacy Policy Office (FERPA) |
| Robocalls, carrier data | FCC |
| Canada / Australia | OPC / OAIC |

## Build the complaint
- Who you are, the company, what you asked for and when, what happened (or did not).
- Attach: your request, proof of sending, replies, screenshots, account identifiers (no passwords).
- State the remedy you want (data provided, data deleted, processing stopped).
- Keep it factual and chronological; one page plus attachments.

## After filing
Regulators may take months and often do not resolve individual cases quickly; the complaint still matters for
enforcement patterns. For GDPR, non-profits such as noyb can represent you, and courts are an alternative route.

## Verify
- [ ] Company deadline passed with written evidence; complaint filed with the correct regulator; reference number saved.
