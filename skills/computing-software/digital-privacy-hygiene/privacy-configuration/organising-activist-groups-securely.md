# Organising activist groups securely

**Goal:** the group keeps working when a phone is seized, a member leaves angry, or someone isn't who they claimed.

## Group threat model
Agree as a group: what you protect (member list, plans, donor data), adversaries (police, counter-groups, doxxers,
employers), and acceptable effort. Revisit before major actions.

## Communication
- Signal groups with **disappearing messages** (e.g. 1 week), admin-only member approval, invite link off, and
  separate groups for public outreach versus core planning.
- Usernames instead of phone numbers; ask members to hide numbers in Signal settings.
- Assume any group of more than a handful has someone who screenshots; say sensitive things in small groups or in
  person.

## Data and roles
- Minimal records: don't keep full member lists with addresses unless needed; store them encrypted (CryptPad, Proton
  Drive) with limited access.
- Role-based accounts for social media and email, with a password manager organisation and 2FA, not shared passwords.
- Offboarding checklist: remove from groups, rotate shared passwords, transfer account ownership.

## Infiltration and doxxing
- Vet new members gradually; access to sensitive channels comes with time and trust.
- Remove members' personal data from data brokers before high-profile actions.

## Actions
Protest phone guidance, legal support numbers written on skin or paper, buddy system, plan for arrests (who has
which passwords, what to do with devices).
