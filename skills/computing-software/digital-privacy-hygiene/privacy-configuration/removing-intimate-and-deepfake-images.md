# Removing intimate and deepfake images

**Goal:** remove non-consensual intimate imagery (real or AI-generated) from search results and limit its spread on participating platforms.

## Hash-based blocking
- **Take It Down** (free, run by NCMEC) is for nude, partially nude or sexually explicit images or videos taken of you under 18, whether already shared or only feared to be. You can stay anonymous. It generates a hash (digital fingerprint) of an exact copy on your device; the image is not uploaded and only the hash goes to NCMEC's list, which participating platforms use to scan their public or unencrypted services.
- Do not send, share or download the image to submit it; only use files already on your device. Don't re-post it afterwards: it may be flagged and could block your account.
- Platforms may be limited in removing content already posted; NCMEC's CyberTipline can add help, including if someone is threatening you.
- For images from when you were 18 or older, Take It Down points to stopncii.org (its page could not be read for this check).

## Google Search removal ("Remove personal sexual content from Google Search")
- Covers sexual content showing you nude, in a sexual act or in an intimate state (real, consensual or not, if not commercialised), fabricated content (deepfakes, fake nude photos), and results that associate your name, alias or handle with pornographic sites or ads that have nothing to do with you.
- For fakes, all three must hold: you are identifiable in the images, audio or video; the content falsely depicts you nude or sexually explicit; it was distributed without your consent.
- You or your representative can request, listing the URLs (only those are reviewed) and a screenshot if asked. Never take or share screenshots of sexual content showing anyone under 18. Anyone can report such content of minors.
- Google tries to remove duplicates; you can opt out of that on the form. You get an email with the outcome: full removal or partial removal (hidden for searches containing your name).
- Public interest can keep content up. Content outside the policies may be covered by local law via the Legal Help Center. Copyright concerns can use DMCA.
- Removal covers Search only; the content may remain on the site and be found via links, social media or other engines, so also contact the site owner.

## Gotchas
- Hash matching identifies exact copies; edited versions may not match.
- Ask a trusted person to help search and report so you do not have to view everything yourself (advice, not from the sources).
