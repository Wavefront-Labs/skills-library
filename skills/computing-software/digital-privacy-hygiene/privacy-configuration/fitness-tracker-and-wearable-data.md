# Fitness tracker and wearable data

**Goal:** share workouts if you want, without revealing where you live, your routine, or health data to advertisers.

## Strava
- Settings > Privacy Controls: profile page, activities, group activities, flybys set to **Followers** or **Only
  You**.
- **Map visibility**: hide start/end points of activities (choose a distance) and add hidden addresses for home and
  work.
- Opt out of **Aggregated data usage** (heatmaps, Metro). Strava's 2018 global heatmap revealed military base layouts.
- Approve followers manually.

## Garmin Connect
Privacy settings per activity type; default to "Only Me"; use privacy zones; review connected apps (Strava, insurance
programmes).

## Fitbit and Google
Fitbit accounts are moving to Google accounts; review Fitbit privacy settings and Google's data controls; Fitbit data
is not used for Google ads per Google's commitments.

## Apple Health and Watch
Health data in iCloud is end-to-end encrypted with two-factor; review apps with Health access (Settings > Health >
Data Access & Devices) and remove unneeded ones.

## Sharing with insurers and employers
Wellness programmes can collect detailed data; read terms, share summaries if possible, and know that consumer health
apps are usually not covered by HIPAA.

## Clean up
Delete old activity history around your home, export and delete accounts for devices you no longer use.
