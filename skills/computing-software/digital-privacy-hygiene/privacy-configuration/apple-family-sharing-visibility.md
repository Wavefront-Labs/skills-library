# Family Sharing visibility

**Goal:** share subscriptions and storage with family without sharing more than you intended.

## What can be visible
| Feature | Who sees what |
|---|---|
| Location Sharing (Find My) | Members you share with see your device location continuously; can be turned on automatically for the family |
| Purchase Sharing | Members can see and download apps, books and media you bought; the organiser pays and sees purchases on the card |
| Ask to Buy / Screen Time | Organiser/parents see child's app requests and usage reports |
| Shared iCloud+ storage | Members see total use, not your files |
| Shared photo albums and calendar | Everything posted |
| Apple Cash Family | Parents see child transactions |

## Adjust
- Settings > [name] > **Family** > Location Sharing: turn off for specific members, or stop in Find My > Me > Share My
  Location.
- Settings > Family > **Purchase Sharing**: turn off for yourself; hide individual purchases in App Store > account >
  Purchased > swipe left > Hide.
- Screen Time applies only to child accounts (under 18 in most regions); adults can't be monitored by Screen Time.

## Leaving a family
Settings > Family > your name > **Leave Family** (adults). Shared purchases and subscriptions stop; your own data
stays. If you're in an abusive situation, use **Safety Check** (Settings > Privacy & Security) to stop all sharing at
once and review devices.

## Gotchas
- The organiser can remove members and sees the payment method charges for everyone.
- Turning off sharing notifies nobody, but family members may notice your location disappear.
