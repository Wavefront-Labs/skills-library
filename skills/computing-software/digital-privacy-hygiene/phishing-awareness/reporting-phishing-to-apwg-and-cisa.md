# Reporting phishing to APWG and CISA

**Goal:** get phishing sites taken down and blocked for others, not just deleted from your inbox.

## Email phishing
- **APWG:** forward the phishing email **as an attachment** (keeps headers) to `reportphishing@apwg.org`. APWG shares
  with browser makers, banks and security firms.
- **CISA (US):** use the reporting options at cisa.gov/report (includes a phishing reporting address).
- In Gmail/Outlook/Apple Mail: also use the client's **Report phishing** button (trains filters, shares with the
  provider).

## Forward as attachment
- Gmail: three dots > Forward as attachment (or download "Show original" > save .eml and attach).
- Outlook: More actions > Forward as attachment.
- Apple Mail: Message > Forward as Attachment.

## Phishing websites
- Google Safe Browsing report form (safebrowsing.google.com/safebrowsing/report_phish/).
- Microsoft: report unsafe site in Edge (Help and feedback > Report unsafe site).
- The impersonated brand: most banks and big companies have `phishing@` or `abuse@` addresses or forms.

## SMS
Forward to 7726 (SPAM) in the US/UK and other countries; also use the phone's Report Junk.

## Gotchas
- Don't click the link to "check it first"; report the URL as text.
- Remove any personal data you don't want shared (your own info in the body) only if it doesn't destroy the headers.
