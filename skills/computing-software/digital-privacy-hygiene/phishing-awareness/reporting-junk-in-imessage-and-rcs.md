# Reporting junk in iMessage and RCS

**Goal:** report scam texts to the people who can block the sender, and keep them out of your main inbox.

## iPhone (Messages)
- A message from an unknown sender shows **Report Junk** under it: tap it to send the sender and message to Apple
  (and to your carrier for SMS) and delete it.
- Settings > Apps > Messages > **Screen Unknown Senders** / Filter Unknown Senders puts them in a separate list.
- Don't reply STOP to obvious scams: it confirms the number is live.

## Android (Google Messages)
- Long-press the conversation > **Block** > tick **Report spam** > OK.
- Settings > Spam protection on; RCS spam is reported to Google, SMS spam to Google and the carrier.
- Samsung Messages: conversation menu > Block number and report spam.

## Forward to 7726
In the US, UK, Canada and some other countries, forward the spam text to **7726** (SPAM) for your carrier, then send
the sender's number when asked. Works for any phone.

## Report fraud
- US: reportfraud.ftc.gov; for impersonation of a specific company, also report to that company.
- UK: forward to 7726; report to Action Fraud if you lost money.

## Gotchas
- Report Junk only appears for senders not in your contacts.
- RCS/iMessage messages from email addresses (Apple ID) can't go to 7726; use Report Junk.
