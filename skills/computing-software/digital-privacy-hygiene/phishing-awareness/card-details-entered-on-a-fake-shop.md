# Card details entered on a fake shop

**Goal:** limit the damage within the first hour.

## Right now
1. **Lock or freeze the card** in the bank app (most have a toggle), then call the number on the back to report it
   compromised and get a replacement with a new number.
2. Ask about any **pending** transactions; some can be stopped before they settle.
3. If you also typed a password used elsewhere or a one-time code, treat those as compromised too.
4. Screenshot the shop, order confirmation and payment page; note the URL.

## Disputes
- **Credit cards (US):** under the Fair Credit Billing Act, dispute in writing within 60 days of the statement;
  liability for unauthorised use is capped at $50 and most issuers waive it.
- **Debit cards:** report within 2 business days to keep liability at $50 (EFTA); later reporting raises it. Money
  leaves your account immediately, so act faster.
- If goods never arrive from a real but dodgy shop, dispute as "goods not received".

## After
- Update the new card on subscriptions; stored cards elsewhere still have the old number.
- Expect phishing calls "from your bank's fraud team" asking for the new card number or a code: hang up and call the
  card's number.
- Report the shop to reportfraud.ftc.gov and to Google Safe Browsing.
- Watch statements for several months; small test charges come before big ones.

## Next time
Virtual card numbers (merchant-locked or single-use) and checking shop reviews and domain age before buying from
an unknown site with prices that are too good.
