# Calendar invite spam

**Goal:** scam invites don't land on your calendar with phishing links and fake reminders.

## Google Calendar
Settings > Events from Gmail / **Add invitations to my calendar**: choose **Only if the sender is known** or
**When I respond to the invitation in email**. Report spam invites: open event > three dots > Report as spam.

## Outlook / Microsoft 365
Settings > Calendar > Events and invitations: turn off automatic processing of invites from unknown senders where
available; mark the email as junk; delete the event without responding.

## iCloud / iPhone
- Invites from unknown senders: tap **Report Junk** (iCloud calendar), which deletes without notifying the sender.
- **Subscribed calendar spam** (from clicking "Subscribe" on a website): Settings > Apps > Calendar > Calendar
  Accounts > Subscribed Calendars > select and **Delete Account**. These push fake "virus" event alerts.

## Don't
- Respond (Accept/Decline) to spam invites: it confirms your address.
- Click links in event descriptions from unknown organisers.

## Gotchas
- Spam invites can be sent from real (compromised) accounts; the organiser name isn't proof.
- Teams/Zoom links in spam invites may be credential phishing pages; join only from known meetings.
