# Friend and family impersonation scams

**Goal:** the most convincing scam message comes from someone you trust, because their account was taken over or
cloned. Verify people, not profiles.

## Common scripts
- **"Hi Mum/Dad, this is my new number"**: phone broke, need a bill paid urgently.
- **"I sent you a code by mistake, can you send it back?"**: the code is your WhatsApp/Instagram/Google Voice
  verification; sharing it hands over your account, which then targets your contacts.
- **Cloned profile**: a new friend request from someone already in your list, followed by a money or investment pitch.
- **"Is this you in this video?"** links to a fake login page.
- Votes for a contest, "I made money with this crypto trader", emergency abroad, bail money.

## Verify
- Contact the person through a **different, known channel**: call their saved number, ask a family member.
- Ask something only they would know (not on social media), or use a family safe word.
- Never share verification codes, even with real friends; no legitimate reason exists.
- Urgency + secrecy + new payment method = stop.

## Protect your own accounts
- Turn on two-step verification PIN in WhatsApp (Settings > Account > Two-step verification) and MFA on social accounts so
  a shared code alone is not enough.
- Lock down friend lists so cloners cannot copy your network.

## If an account is compromised or cloned
- Report the fake profile; ask friends to report it too.
- Warn contacts through another channel; recover the account through the platform's recovery flow and enable MFA.
- If money was sent, contact the bank immediately and report to the FTC/Action Fraud.

- [ ] Family agrees: any money or code request by message is confirmed by voice on a known number first.
