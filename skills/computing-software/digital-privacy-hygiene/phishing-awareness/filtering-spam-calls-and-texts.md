# Filtering spam calls and texts

**Goal:** most phone scams start with an unsolicited call or text. Filter them before you have to decide.

## iPhone
- Settings > Apps > Phone > **Silence Unknown Callers** (unknown numbers go to voicemail) or, on newer iOS,
  **Screen Unknown Callers**, which asks the caller to state a name and reason first.
- Phone > **Call Blocking & Identification**: enable carrier or trusted third-party apps.
- Settings > Apps > Messages > **Filter Unknown Senders** / Unknown & Spam; texts from unknown numbers go to a separate list
  with links disabled.

## Android
- Pixel Phone app > Settings > **Spam and Call Screen**: See caller & spam ID, **Filter spam calls**, and automatic
  **Call Screen** for unknown numbers.
- Samsung Phone > Settings > **Caller ID and spam protection** > Block spam and scam calls.
- Google Messages > Settings > Spam protection on; scam detection where available.

## Carrier tools (free tiers)
T-Mobile Scam Shield, Verizon Call Filter, AT&T ActiveArmor; UK carriers block many spoofed numbers by default.
"Verified" call labels come from STIR/SHAKEN; they show the number was not spoofed, not that the caller is honest.

## Report and register
- Forward scam texts to **7726** (SPAM) in the US/UK and Canada; then delete and block.
- Report robocalls at reportfraud.ftc.gov and the FCC; register at donotcall.gov (stops legal telemarketers only,
  so anyone calling despite it is suspect).

## Habits
- Do not "press 1 to be removed" or reply STOP to obvious scams; it confirms a live number.
- A caller claiming to be your bank: hang up and call the number on your card.

## Verify
- [ ] Unknown-caller screening/silencing on; carrier spam filter on; unknown-sender text filtering on.
