# Celebrity and giveaway impersonation

**Goal:** never pay or log in to claim a prize from someone famous.

## Common forms
- **Crypto doubling:** a deepfake livestream of a tech CEO says "send 0.1 BTC, get 0.2 back". Nobody gives away
  money this way; every coin sent is lost.
- **"You won" DMs** from an account that copies a creator or brand (name with an extra letter, recent creation
  date, few posts), asking for a shipping fee, card details, or for you to log in via a link to "verify".
- **Comment replies** under a real influencer's post from a lookalike account saying you were picked.
- **Free gift card / airline ticket** pages shared by friends whose accounts were hacked.

## Checks
- Verify on the creator's real account (from the official site) that the giveaway exists and how winners are
  contacted. Most announce winners publicly and never charge.
- Paid verification badges are not proof of identity.
- Any request for crypto, gift cards, a fee, a login or a 2FA code ends the conversation.

## Respond
Report the account as impersonation on the platform (Instagram/TikTok/X/YouTube have dedicated forms); block it. If
you paid, contact your bank or the exchange and report to reportfraud.ftc.gov. If you logged in through their link,
change your password and revoke sessions.
