# Rental and housing scams

**Goal:** don't lose a deposit to someone who doesn't own or control the property.

## Typical patterns
- Real listing copied from Zillow/Craigslist/Facebook with a lower price and a different contact.
- "Owner" is abroad (missionary, military, job) and will mail the keys after payment.
- Pressure: many applicants, pay now to hold it.
- Payment by wire, Zelle, crypto, gift cards or a payment app to a person.
- Vacation rentals booked off-platform at a discount.

## Verify
1. **Reverse image search** the photos (Google Lens, TinEye): the same photos under another address/agent = copy.
2. Check **property ownership** in the county assessor/recorder records; does the name match?
3. Search the address + "for rent" to find the original listing and contact that agent directly.
4. **Tour in person** (or via a trusted local) and meet the landlord/agent; ask for ID and confirm with the
   property manager's office.
5. Read the lease before paying anything.

## Pay safely
Pay deposits by check or through a property management portal after signing; for vacation rentals stay on the
platform (Airbnb/Vrbo) for payment and messages.

## If scammed
Contact your bank/payment app immediately to try to recall; report to the FTC (reportfraud.ftc.gov), IC3 and the
listing site.

## Gotchas
- Scammers may "show" a vacant house via a lockbox code they got from a real listing.
- Application fees to unknown parties are a common smaller scam.
