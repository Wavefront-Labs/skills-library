# Family safe word against voice clones

**Goal:** a few seconds of a relative's voice from social media is enough to clone it.
"Grandparent" and "virtual kidnapping" scams use that voice to demand urgent money.

## Set it up (one family conversation)
1. Choose a **safe word or phrase** that isn't on social media or guessable (not a pet's
   name): e.g. two random words.
2. Rule: anyone calling in an emergency asking for money or secrecy must give the word;
   the person receiving asks for it calmly.
3. Share it in person, not by text/email; write it on paper for older relatives.
4. Add a **callback rule**: hang up and call the person back on their saved number, or
   call another family member to check where they are.

## The scam patterns
- "Grandma, it's me, I crashed the car / got arrested, don't tell Mum", then a "lawyer"
  asks for bail by cash courier, gift cards or crypto.
- Virtual kidnapping: screaming voice, then a "kidnapper" demands ransom while keeping you
  on the line so you can't check.
- Executive/boss voice clone asking an employee for a transfer (same defence at work).

## Reduce exposure
- Limit public videos/voice notes for children and older relatives' accounts.
- Teach: urgency + secrecy + unusual payment = scam, even if the voice is perfect.

## Verify
- [ ] Safe word set and known by every family member, including grandparents.
- [ ] Everyone has each other's numbers saved for callbacks.
