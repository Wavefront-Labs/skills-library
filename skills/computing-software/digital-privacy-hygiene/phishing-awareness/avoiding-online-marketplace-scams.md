# Avoiding online marketplace scams

**Goal:** peer-to-peer marketplaces have no buyer protection outside their own checkout. Recognise the standard
scripts and trade in ways that cannot be faked.

## Scripts aimed at sellers
- **Verification code scam**: "I need to make sure you are real, I'll text you a code, read it back." The code sets up a
  Google Voice number or account takeover in your name. Never share any code.
- **Fake payment confirmation**: email "from Zelle/PayPal" says the payment is pending until you "upgrade to a business
  account" or pay a fee. Check your actual bank/app; no money is held pending.
- **Overpayment**: buyer sends a cheque or payment for more than the price and asks for the difference back; the payment
  later bounces or is reversed.
- **Shipping agent**: buyer insists on their courier and a "shipping fee" link.

## Scripts aimed at buyers
- **Deposit to hold** an item, pet, car or rental via Zelle, Cash App, gift card or crypto; item never exists.
- **Rental listings** copied from real listings at low rent; "owner abroad, can't show it".
- Tickets, phones and consoles at below-market prices; counterfeit or nonexistent.

## Safer trading
- Meet in person in public, ideally a police "safe exchange zone", in daylight; inspect before paying.
- Cash (counterfeit pen for large sums) or the platform's own checkout with purchase protection; avoid Zelle/Cash App with
  strangers.
- Check the profile: age, other listings, ratings; brand-new profiles with stock photos are a red flag.
- Never click payment or shipping links sent in chat; keep the conversation in the platform's messenger.

## If it happens
Report the profile in the app, report to the FTC/IC3, contact your bank immediately to attempt a recall, and if you
shared a code, secure the account it belonged to.

## Verify
- [ ] You never share codes, never accept overpayment, and confirm payments only inside your own banking app.
