# Recognising fake customer support accounts

**Goal:** complaining publicly about a bank, airline, crypto wallet or game summons impersonators within minutes.
Real support must be reached on your terms.

## Where fakes appear
- **Replies on X, Facebook, Instagram, Reddit** to your complaint: "@Brand_HelpDesk: please DM us your details".
- **Search ads and results** listing a "support number" that is a call centre run by scammers (common for airlines,
  printers, antivirus, crypto).
- **Edited Google Maps / business listings** with replaced phone numbers.
- **Discord/Telegram "moderators"** DMing after you ask for help in a server.
- Fake "Meta support" pages warning of copyright violations or account deletion, linking to a login form.

## How to tell
- Handle created recently, few followers, no verification, slight spelling differences, or a generic name.
- Moves you to DM, WhatsApp or Telegram; asks for login, 2FA code, card number, seed phrase, or to install
  remote-access software. Real support never asks for passwords, codes or seed phrases.
- Offers a "support form" on a domain other than the company's own.

## Reach real support
- Open the company's app or type its website; use **Contact us/Help** from there.
- Phone numbers: from the back of your card, your account page or a statement.
- Assume any account that contacts you first is not support.
- Do not post order numbers, account emails or phone numbers in public complaints.

## If you engaged
Change the password and revoke sessions; remove remote access tools; call your bank on the card number if you shared
card data; move crypto if a seed phrase was disclosed.

## Verify
- [ ] Support contacts for your bank, carrier and key services saved from official sources in your contacts or password manager.
