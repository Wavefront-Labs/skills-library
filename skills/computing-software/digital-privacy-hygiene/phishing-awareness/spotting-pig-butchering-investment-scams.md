# Pig-butchering investment scams

**Goal:** these scams cost victims life savings; they work because they're slow and
friendly. Spot the pattern early.

## The arc
1. **Contact**: "wrong number" text ("Hi Amy, are we still on for golf?"), a LinkedIn or
   dating match, or a WhatsApp group. Friendly, attractive, successful persona.
2. **Grooming** (weeks): daily chats, life stories, photos of luxury lifestyle; moves to
   WhatsApp/Telegram.
3. **The tip**: their uncle/mentor has a crypto or forex strategy. They guide you to a
   "platform" (a fake website or app, sometimes in real app stores briefly).
4. **Fake gains**: small deposits show big profits; a small test withdrawal may work to
   build trust.
5. **The squeeze**: big deposit; then withdrawal blocked by "taxes", "fees", "anti-money-
   laundering deposit". Then silence, or a recovery scam.

## Red flags
- A stranger who won't video call (or only briefly) yet pushes investing.
- Platforms you can't find in regulator registers (SEC/FINRA, FCA register), domains
  registered weeks ago, customer support only by chat.
- Any fee to withdraw your own money.

## Actions
- Don't reply to wrong-number texts beyond blocking/reporting (7726 in the US/UK).
- Check platform domains with WHOIS age and regulator warnings lists.
- If involved: stop sending, collect transaction hashes and chats, report to IC3/FTC and the
  exchange you used.

## Verify
- [ ] Family knows the "wrong number to investment tip" pattern.
