# Shoulder surfing and privacy screens

**Goal:** people and cameras behind you on trains, planes and cafes can't read your screen or your passcode.

## Physical
- **Privacy filter** (micro-louver film, e.g. 3M) for laptops and phones: the screen blackens from the side. Some
  laptops have a built-in electronic privacy screen toggle (e.g. HP Sure View).
- Sit with your back to a wall; on planes, tilt the screen and lower brightness.
- Hide passcode entry with your hand; CCTV records high-resolution footage.

## Unlocking in public
Use Face ID/fingerprint so no one sees your passcode. A longer alphanumeric passcode makes a glimpse less useful.
Thieves watch for passcodes then steal the phone (Apple's Stolen Device Protection and Android Theft Protection limit
the damage).

## Notifications
Hide previews on the lock screen; turn off message previews on laptop notifications when presenting (Focus/Do Not
Disturb).

## Fast locking
- Windows: Win+L; Dynamic Lock; **presence sensing** on supported laptops locks when you walk away.
- Mac: Ctrl+Cmd+Q or a hot corner.
- Linux: Super+L.
- Phones: short auto-lock (30 seconds to 1 minute).

## Presenting and screen sharing
Share a single window, not the whole screen; close password manager and email before presenting.
