# Identity theft recovery plan

**Goal:** methodically undo fraud committed in your name. US-centric steps; other countries have their own
reporting bodies.

## Day 1
1. **Contact the companies** where fraud occurred and ask them to close or freeze the accounts; note names, dates
   and reference numbers.
2. **Fraud alert**: contact one of the three credit bureaus (Equifax, Experian, TransUnion); the FTC page says it
   must notify the other two, and describes a free one-year alert.
3. **Credit freeze** with each of the three bureaus; the FTC page says it is free to place and lift and that you
   contact each bureau.
4. **Report at identitytheft.gov** to get a free personal recovery plan with next steps.

## Next days
- Follow the recovery plan's steps and letters, and check the FTC page for the current guidance on disputing
  fraudulent items on your credit reports.
- Change passwords and enable multi-factor authentication on accounts that may have been accessed.
- Tax-related identity theft has its own IRS process; see the IRS identity theft pages.

## Keep a log
Date, organisation, person, what was said, reference number, follow-up date. Recovery can take a long time; the
log is your record.
