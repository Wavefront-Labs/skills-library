# Facebook legacy contact and memorialisation

**Goal:** decide now what happens to your social accounts after death, so family
doesn't need your password (sharing it violates terms and may be illegal in places).

## Your choice (Facebook)
Settings > Accounts Center (or Settings & privacy > Settings) > Personal details >
**Account ownership and control** > **Memorialisation**:
- **Choose a legacy contact**: a Facebook friend who can manage a memorialised profile
  (pinned tribute post, profile picture, respond to friend requests, request removal).
  They **can't** log in or read your messages. Optionally allow them to download a copy of
  what you shared.
- Or **Delete account after death**: Facebook deletes the account when notified.

## When someone has died
- **Facebook**: family or friends submit the memorialisation request form with proof
  (obituary/death certificate link); verified immediate family can request removal instead.
- **Instagram**: memorialisation request form (proof of death) or removal request by
  immediate family with documentation.
- Memorialised accounts show "Remembering" and stop appearing in birthday reminders and
  "people you may know", which reduces impersonation scams using the dead person's account.
