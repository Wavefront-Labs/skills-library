# Opting out of bank voice ID

**Goal:** your voice isn't the thing that unlocks your phone banking.

## The problem
Some banks and brokerages offer "voiceprint" or "my voice is my password" authentication on phone lines. AI voice
cloning from a few seconds of public audio (videos, voicemail greeting, podcasts) has been shown to pass such
systems in journalist tests. Voice is public, unchangeable and easy to sample.

## Opt out
1. Call the bank's number from your card/statement (not a number in an email).
2. Ask to **disable voice ID / voice biometrics** on your profile.
3. Ask for an alternative: a **verbal password/PIN** set on the account, or authentication via the bank's app push.
4. Ask them to note on the account that voice recognition must not be used.

## Reduce samples
- Use a generic voicemail greeting (the carrier default).
- Be aware public videos give enough audio; that's why opting out matters more than hiding.

## Also
- Set up a **family safe word** for calls claiming to be relatives in trouble.
- Turn on transaction alerts so a phone-channel takeover shows up quickly.

## Gotchas
- Some banks re-enrol voice automatically on calls ("we're creating your voiceprint"); say no each time.
- Voice ID at government agencies (some tax agencies) can usually be declined the same way.
