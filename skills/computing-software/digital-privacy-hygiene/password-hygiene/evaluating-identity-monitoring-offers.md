# Evaluating identity monitoring offers

**Goal:** know what you're getting from "free 2 years of identity protection" or a paid subscription.

## What monitoring does
- Alerts **after** something appears: a new account on a credit report, your email in a breach, your SSN on
  dark-web lists.
- Some include insurance for recovery costs and access to restoration specialists.

## What it doesn't do
- Prevent new accounts (a **freeze** does that, for free).
- Stop tax, medical or benefits identity theft (IP PIN, SSA account, insurer checks help there).
- Remove data from brokers (separate service).

## Decision table
| Situation | Do |
|---|---|
| Breach offers free monitoring | Enrol (free alerts are useful) **and** freeze credit |
| Considering paying | Freezes + free weekly reports + HIBP alerts cover most of it |
| Identity theft already happened | Restoration service can save time; check the provider's scope |

## Read the terms
- Data sharing: some "free" services monetise your data or upsell.
- Arbitration clauses: check whether accepting a breach settlement offer waives claims.
- Auto-renewal to paid after the free period.

## Gotchas
- Monitoring needs your SSN and personal data; choose reputable providers (often the big bureaus).
- Alerts sent by SMS/email can be imitated by phishers; log in directly to check.
