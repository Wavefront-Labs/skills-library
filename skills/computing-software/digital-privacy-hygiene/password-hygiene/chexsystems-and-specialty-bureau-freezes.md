# ChexSystems and specialty bureau freezes

**Goal:** close the identity-theft routes that a normal credit freeze at Equifax, Experian and TransUnion misses.

## Why
- New **bank/checking accounts** are often screened through ChexSystems, not credit bureaus. Fraudsters open
  accounts to launder money in your name.
- New **phone, cable and utility** accounts check NCTUE (National Consumer Telecom & Utilities Exchange).
- **Innovis** is a fourth credit bureau some lenders use.

## Place freezes (free under US federal law)
| Company | Covers | How |
|---|---|---|
| ChexSystems | Bank/deposit account openings | chexsystems.com security freeze (online, phone or mail); you receive a PIN |
| NCTUE | Telecom, utilities, pay-TV | nctue.com consumer section, freeze request |
| Innovis | Credit | innovis.com freeze |
| Big three | Credit | Each bureau's website/app |

The CFPB keeps a list of consumer reporting companies, including others for tenant screening and insurance.

## Record
Store each freeze PIN/login in your password manager (Identity or secure note). You need it to lift the freeze.

## Lifting
Temporarily lift ChexSystems before opening a new bank account (or ask the bank which bureau they use); lift NCTUE
before switching carriers or opening utilities.

## Gotchas
- Freezes don't affect existing accounts.
- Account opening may silently fail when frozen; banks often just say "can't verify identity".
