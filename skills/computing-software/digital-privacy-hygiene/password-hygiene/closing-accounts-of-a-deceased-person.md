# Closing accounts of a deceased person

**Goal:** secure the estate's digital accounts, stop billing, preserve what the family needs, and prevent the
deceased's identity being used.

## Gather
- Death certificate copies (several), proof you're the executor/next of kin, the person's email addresses and
  phone number.
- Their password manager emergency kit or legacy access, if they set one up.

## Priority order
1. **Keep the phone number active** for a few months (codes still arrive there).
2. **Email** (resets everything else): use Legacy Contact / Inactive Account Manager if set; otherwise provider
   request processes.
3. **Financial** accounts: notify banks directly; they don't close via online login.
4. **Subscriptions** charging cards.
5. **Social media**: memorialise or remove.
6. **Cloud photos/files**: download before closing.

## Provider routes
| Provider | Route |
|---|---|
| Google | "Submit a request regarding a deceased user's account" (close, get funds, or obtain data) |
| Apple | Legacy Contact access key, or court order; request to delete |
| Facebook/Instagram | Memorialisation or removal request with proof |
| Microsoft | Contact support for closure; inactive accounts close after prolonged inactivity |

## Identity protection
- Notify the credit bureaus with a death certificate to place a "deceased - do not issue credit" flag.
- SSA is usually notified by the funeral home; confirm.
- Watch for mail about new accounts in the deceased's name.

## Gotchas
- Logging in with their password may breach terms or laws in some places; prefer official routes.
- Don't cancel the phone number until email and banks are handled.
