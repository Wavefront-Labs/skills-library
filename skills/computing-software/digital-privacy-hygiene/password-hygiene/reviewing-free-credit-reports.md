# Reviewing free credit reports

**Goal:** spot accounts, inquiries or addresses that aren't yours before they do real damage.

## Get them
annualcreditreport.com is the official free source: reports from Equifax, Experian and TransUnion, available
**weekly** for free. Requesting doesn't affect your score and works even with a freeze in place.

## Read each report
| Section | Look for |
|---|---|
| Personal info | Addresses you never lived at, name variants, unknown employers |
| Accounts (tradelines) | Accounts you didn't open, wrong balances, accounts reported as late |
| Hard inquiries | Credit applications you didn't make |
| Collections | Debts you don't recognise |
| Public records | Judgments or bankruptcies not yours |

## Rotate or check all three
Some people check one bureau every 4 months; after a breach or identity theft, check all three monthly.

## Dispute errors
- Online dispute at the bureau that shows it (each has a dispute portal); attach evidence.
- Also contact the company that reported it.
- If it's fraud: identitytheft.gov report first, then disputes referencing it.

## Gotchas
- Lookalike sites ("free credit report" with paid subscriptions) are not the official site.
- Soft inquiries (pre-approvals, your own checks) are normal and don't show to lenders.
