# Removing stored payment methods from accounts

**Goal:** a hacked shopping or gaming account should not be able to spend your money.

## Where cards hide
| Place | Path |
|---|---|
| Browser autofill | Chrome: Settings > Autofill and passwords > Payment methods; Firefox: Settings > Privacy > Autofill; Edge: Profiles > Payment info; Safari: Settings > AutoFill > Credit Cards |
| Google | pay.google.com > Payment methods (also used by Play Store, YouTube) |
| Apple | Settings > [name] > Payment & Shipping; Wallet |
| Amazon | Your Account > Your Payments |
| PayPal / Venmo / Cash App | Wallet: cards and bank links |
| Streaming, gaming, food delivery, ride-hailing, airlines | Each account's billing page |
| Password manager | Card items (keep only if the vault is well protected) |

## Decide per account
- **Delete** cards on sites you buy from rarely; type or autofill from the password manager each time.
- **Keep, but protect**, where needed for subscriptions: then use a virtual card number with a merchant lock or limit
  where your bank supports it.
- **Remove bank account links** (ACH) wherever a card works; card disputes are easier than bank transfer disputes.

## On closing an account
Delete stored payment and addresses **before** requesting deletion; deletion requests can take weeks and data may
be kept for legal reasons.

## Also
- Turn on purchase notifications from your card issuer for every transaction.
- Require a password for purchases on app stores and consoles.

## Verify
Card issuer apps (and Visa/Mastercard click-to-pay portals) list merchants holding your card on file; check for any
you forgot.
