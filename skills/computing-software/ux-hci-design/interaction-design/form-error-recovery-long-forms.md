# Long forms
- Chunk into steps of related questions; show progress and names of steps.
- Allow save and resume; autosave.
- Guest checkout option; defer account creation to after purchase.
- Offer address lookup, field defaults, and avoid duplicate entry.
- Final review page with edit links.

## Verify
- [ ] Funnel analytics: drop-off per step identifies troublesome fields.
