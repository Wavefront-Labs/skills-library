# Touch design
- Primary actions in easy-reach zones (lower half on large phones); avoid critical controls at top corners.
- Gestures are not discoverable: always pair swipe/pinch/long-press with a visible control (WCAG 2.5.1).
- Spacing between targets to avoid mis-taps; cancelable on touch-up.
- Avoid hover-dependent UI; do not override system edge gestures.
- Keyboards cover the screen; keep the focused field and submit button visible.
