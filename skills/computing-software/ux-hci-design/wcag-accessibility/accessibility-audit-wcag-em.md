# WCAG-EM steps
1. Define the scope: what is included, the goal of the evaluation, and the WCAG conformance level (A, AA, AAA).
2. Explore the product: key views, key functionality, types of content, designs and functionality, and required technologies.
3. Select a representative sample: structured and randomly selected views, when evaluating every view is not feasible.
4. Evaluate the sample: determine successes and failures against WCAG, check accessibility support for specific features, and record the evaluation steps.
5. Report the findings: aggregate and report findings, make evaluation statements, and calculate overall scores.

## Practice
WCAG-EM recommends involving real users with disabilities during evaluation. Do preliminary checks (Easy Checks) and fix obvious barriers before applying it. It is a supporting resource and adds no WCAG requirements. WCAG-EM 2 (published 23 July 2026) also applies to apps and other digital products; WCAG-EM 1 was for websites and web pages.
