# Severity rating
Three factors: **frequency** (how common), **impact** (how hard to overcome), **persistence** (one-time or repeated).
Nielsen's scale:
- 0 not a usability problem
- 1 cosmetic only
- 2 minor
- 3 major, important to fix
- 4 usability catastrophe, must fix before release

Rate independently then average across evaluators; rate after the evaluation so rating does not bias discovery.
