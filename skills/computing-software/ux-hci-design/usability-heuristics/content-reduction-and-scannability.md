# Scannability
- Users scan rather than read; front-load key words in headings and links.
- Use meaningful subheadings, lists, one idea per paragraph.
- Aim for short line lengths (around 50-75 characters is a common guideline).
- Cut promotional language ("welcome to...") and redundant instructions.

## Verify
- [ ] Readers can answer a key question after scanning for 10 seconds.
