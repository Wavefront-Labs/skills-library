# Report structure
- Executive summary: top 3-5 issues and overall success rates.
- Each issue: title as outcome, evidence (quote, clip, count e.g. 4 of 6 failed), severity, recommendation.
- Include positive findings to keep what works.
- Tie to metrics (task success, time, errors) where measured.

## Verify
- [ ] A developer could implement each recommendation without a meeting.
