# Empty, error and 404 states
- **First-use empty**: explain what the area is for and offer the primary action ("Create your first project").
- **No results**: show the query, suggest spelling fixes, broaden filters, keep the search box.
- **404**: say the page was not found, offer search and top links; return a real 404 status (not 200).
- **Permissions error**: say who can grant access.

## Verify
- [ ] No dead end: every error or empty screen has a forward action.
