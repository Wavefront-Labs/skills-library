# Context of use
Describe: users (roles, skills, accessibility needs), goals and tasks, technical environment (devices, bandwidth), physical and social environment.
Stakeholder interviews: business goals, constraints, success metrics, assumptions to test.
Output: a brief that turns assumptions into research questions.

## Verify
- [ ] Each research question maps to a decision the team must make.
