# Moderation
- Brief: "We are testing the design, not you."
- Ask participants to think aloud; prompt "What are you thinking?" when silent.
- Do not help, defend the design or lead; use neutral probes ("What would you expect?").
- Observers silent and separate; record success, errors, time, quotes.
- Debrief with satisfaction question (SEQ) and open feedback.

## Verify
- [ ] Moderator talk-time is low and no leading questions appear in recordings.
