# Contextual inquiry (Beyer and Holtzblatt)
Principles: context (go to the work), partnership (master-apprentice), interpretation (share and check), focus.
Steps: short interview, observe work with interruptions, interpret aloud, wrap-up.
Capture artifacts (photos with permission), workarounds, interruptions, tools.
Analyse with affinity diagramming and work models.

## Verify
- [ ] Findings cite observed behaviour, not recalled behaviour.
