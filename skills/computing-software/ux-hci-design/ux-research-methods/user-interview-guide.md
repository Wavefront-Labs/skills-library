# Interview craft
- Goal and 5-8 participants per segment for qualitative themes (rule of thumb; stop when new insight plateaus).
- Guide: warm-up, past behaviour ("Tell me about the last time you..."), tasks, wrap-up.
- Ask open and non-leading questions; avoid "would you" future speculation; ask "why" and "can you show me".
- Listen more than talk; silence is a tool.
- Record with consent; note quotes; debrief right after.

## Gotchas
- What people say differs from what they do; pair with observation.

## Verify
- [ ] Transcripts contain concrete stories, not opinions only.
