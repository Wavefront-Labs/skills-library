# Behavioural analytics
- Funnels: drop-off by step; segment by device and source.
- Heatmaps and scroll maps: attention; clicks on non-links show false affordances.
- Session replays for specific issues; mask personal data.
- Define event taxonomy up front; respect consent and privacy law.
- Quantitative shows where, qualitative shows why.

## Verify
- [ ] Each analytic finding is followed by a qualitative check or experiment.
