# Personas
- Derive from interviews, analytics, surveys; cluster by goals and behaviours.
- Include goals, tasks, pain points, context, accessibility needs; one photo and quote optional.
- Limit to a few primary personas.
- Mark assumption-based "proto-personas" and validate.

## Gotchas
- Stereotypes and made-up demographics mislead.

## Verify
- [ ] Each persona statement is traceable to data.
