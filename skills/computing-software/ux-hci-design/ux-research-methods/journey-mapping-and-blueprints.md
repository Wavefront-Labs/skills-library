# Journey map
Lanes: persona and scenario, phases, actions, thoughts, emotions, touchpoints, opportunities. Based on research; include a clear scope.
# Service blueprint
Adds frontstage and backstage actions, support processes, and lines of interaction, visibility and internal interaction.
Use to align teams and find pain points and gaps; workshop with cross-functional participants.

## Verify
- [ ] Each opportunity links to an evidenced pain point.
