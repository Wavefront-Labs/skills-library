# Diary study
- Use for habits, journeys over time, infrequent events.
- Define duration (days to weeks), entry prompts (event- or time-triggered), and tool (messaging app, form, dedicated app).
- Keep entries lightweight; send reminders; incentives scaled to effort.
- Hold a final interview to expand on entries.

## Gotchas
- Attrition grows with burden; pilot first.

## Verify
- [ ] Participants submit at expected rate; follow-up interview covers odd entries.
