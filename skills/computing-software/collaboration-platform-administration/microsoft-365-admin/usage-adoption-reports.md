# Usage and adoption reports
**Goal:** Read Reports > Usage and Adoption Score for rollout insight.

- Reports > Usage lists active users per product for 7/30/90/180 days.
- User-level names can be concealed: Org settings > Services > Reports > hide identifiable information (privacy choice).
- Export to CSV; Graph reports endpoints for automation.
- Adoption Score has a delay of days and requires the Reports Reader role or equivalent.

## Gotchas
- Concealed names break per-user follow-up; decide with HR/privacy.
- Report data lags up to 48 hours.

## Self-check
Download the Active users report and compare the count to licensed users.
