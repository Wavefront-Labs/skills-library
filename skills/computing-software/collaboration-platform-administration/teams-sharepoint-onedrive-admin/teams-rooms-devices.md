# Teams Rooms and devices
**Goal:** Manage Teams Rooms and phone devices.

- Teams admin center > Teams devices; assign configuration profiles.
- Create a resource account (room mailbox) with a Teams Rooms licence.
- Update policy and restart/diagnose remotely.
- Use Teams Rooms Pro Management for monitoring (licence, confirm).

## Gotchas
- Resource account must have a password that doesn't expire.
- Check device-specific firmware update windows.

## Self-check
Device shows Healthy with the current app version.
