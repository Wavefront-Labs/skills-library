# Meeting settings, webinars and town halls
**Goal:** Configure meeting settings and large event options.

- Meetings > Meeting settings: anonymous join, caller ID, QoS.
- Webinars and town halls (replace live events; confirm retirement dates) require Teams Premium or a licence variant (confirm).
- Meeting templates and branding need Teams Premium.
- Event policies control who can schedule.

## Gotchas
- Live events retire in favour of town halls; check current dates.
- Recording governance applies to events.

## Self-check
Schedule a test event with the policy-allowed user.
