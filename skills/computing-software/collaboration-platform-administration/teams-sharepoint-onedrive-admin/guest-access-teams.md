# Guest access in Teams
**Goal:** Enable guest access end to end.

- Teams admin center > Org-wide settings > Guest access; also Entra external collaboration and SharePoint sharing must allow guests.
- Guest meeting and calling toggles are separate.
- Review guests with access reviews (Entra, reference).
- Remove guests who have no group membership.

## Gotchas
- Changes take several hours; tenant, Teams and SharePoint settings must agree.
- A guest can see team members by default.

## Self-check
Invite a test guest and confirm they can join one team only.
