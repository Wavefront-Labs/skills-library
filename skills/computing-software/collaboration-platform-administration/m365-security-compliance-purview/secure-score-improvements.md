# Microsoft Secure Score
**Goal:** Use Secure Score to prioritise hardening.

- Defender portal > Secure score: Overview, Recommended actions, History, Metrics.
- Filter by product and status, mark actions to address or accept risk.
- Tackle MFA, legacy auth block, DKIM and safe links before low-impact items.
- Track score change after each step.

## Gotchas
- Score is a guide; business risk comes first.
- Some recommendations require licences you lack.

## Self-check
Score history shows an increase after the change.
