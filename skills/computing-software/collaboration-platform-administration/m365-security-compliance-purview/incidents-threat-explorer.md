# Incidents and Threat Explorer
**Goal:** Investigate email threats and automated actions.

- Incidents & alerts groups alerts across Defender products.
- Email & collaboration > Explorer (Plan 2) or Real-time detections (Plan 1) to find messages and URLs.
- Actions: soft delete, move to junk, submit to Microsoft.
- Automated investigation and response (Plan 2) with approvals in Action center.

## Gotchas
- Threat Explorer features depend on plan.
- Remediation across tenant should be scoped to specific recipients.

## Self-check
A test phish appears in Explorer and the remediation shows in Action center.
