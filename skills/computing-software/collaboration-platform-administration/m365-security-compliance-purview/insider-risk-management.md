# Insider risk management
**Goal:** Set policies, privacy and review workflows for insider risk.

- Enable insider risk analytics first and review the baseline.
- Policy templates: data leaks, data theft by departing users, security violations.
- Pseudonymize usernames by default; assign investigator roles tightly.
- Involve HR and legal before enabling.

## Gotchas
- Privacy and legal requirements vary by region; seek advice.
- Needs E5 or the compliance add-on licensing (confirm).

## Self-check
Alerts show for a test activity and are pseudonymized.
