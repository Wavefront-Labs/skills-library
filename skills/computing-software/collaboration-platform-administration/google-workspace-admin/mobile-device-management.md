# Mobile device management
**Goal:** Enable basic or advanced mobile management.

- Devices > Mobile & endpoints > Settings > Universal settings: basic or advanced management.
- Require screen lock, encryption; block jailbroken/rooted devices.
- Account wipe or full wipe (advanced) from Devices list.
- Android Enterprise work profile policies in Advanced.

## Gotchas
- Basic management supports account wipe only.
- Advanced management installs a device policy app.

## Self-check
A test device shows in the console with the policy applied.
