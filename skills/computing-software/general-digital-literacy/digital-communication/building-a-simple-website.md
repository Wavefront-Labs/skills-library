# Build a simple website

**Goal:** a clean, mobile-friendly site with your key information, live in an afternoon.

## Choose a builder
| Builder | Good for | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Google Sites** | clubs, class or team pages, internal sites; drag and drop from Google Docs/Drive | free (custom domain possible) |
| **Wix / Squarespace** | small businesses, portfolios, booking and shops | free tier with their branding/subdomain; paid plans for custom domain |
| **WordPress.com** | blogs and content-heavy sites | free tier; paid for domain and plugins |
| **Carrd** | one-page profiles and link pages | low cost |
| Instagram/Linktree-only | not a website; you don't control the platform | free |

## Steps
1. **Plan pages:** Home (who/what/where), About, Services/Events, Contact. Write the text first.
2. Pick a simple template; replace demo text and images with your own (use photos you own or free-licensed ones).
3. Put key facts (opening hours, location, phone/email, prices) on the home page and in text, not only inside images.
4. **Preview on mobile** (builders have a phone-view toggle); most visitors use phones.
5. **Publish**; connect a **custom domain** (buy from a registrar or the builder; renew yearly; turn on auto-renew and domain privacy).
6. Add basic SEO: page titles, short descriptions; claim a Google Business Profile for local businesses.

## Accessibility and law
- Alt text on images, heading levels in order, good colour contrast, link text that says where it goes.
- A privacy policy if you collect form data or use analytics/cookies; cookie consent banners in the UK/EU when using non-essential cookies.

## Keep it safe
Strong password and 2-step verification on the builder account and the domain registrar; keep the domain's contact email current so renewals don't lapse.

- [ ] Site loads on a phone, contact details correct, custom domain works with HTTPS, a test contact form message arrives.
