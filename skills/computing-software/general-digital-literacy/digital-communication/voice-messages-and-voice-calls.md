# Voice notes and voice calls

**Goal:** use voice when it's quicker or warmer than typing, without annoying the listener.

## Voice messages
- **WhatsApp:** voice messages can be sent as **view once**: the recipient must open it within 14 days, can play it once, and cannot forward, copy, save or star it.
- **iMessage:** audio messages expire from the conversation 2 minutes after you send or listen unless you tap **Keep**; set Settings > Apps > Messages > Audio Messages > Expire to Never to keep them.
- Other apps (Signal, Telegram, Messenger, Instagram, Teams) record in a similar press-and-hold way; check each app's help for limits and options, which differ.

## Etiquette
- Keep them under a minute; put key facts (addresses, times, numbers) in text too, so they can be searched and copied.
- Ask colleagues before using voice notes for work; many prefer text in meetings or open offices.
- Start with the point: "Quick one about Friday's delivery...".
- Listen in private or with headphones.

## Voice calls
- WhatsApp supports voice and video calls with up to 32 people (per its desktop help page); mute when not speaking.
- Poor quality: switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data, whichever is stronger.

## Gotchas
- Voice messages can be recorded or shared by the listener; don't say anything you wouldn't put in writing.
- Voice-cloning scams use short audio samples; families can agree a safe word for urgent money requests.
