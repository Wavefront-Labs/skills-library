# Recording and transcribing meetings

**Goal:** a record of the meeting that people agreed to and that only the right people can open.

## Start and stop
- **Teams (desktop):** More actions > **Record**, set the spoken language and what to record, then Confirm; More actions > Recording stops it, or Transcribing stops only the live transcript. Everyone is notified when recording starts. One person can record at a time. Recordings do not capture more than four people's video at once, whiteboards, annotations, shared notes, or some shared content. Who can record may be limited by the organiser's meeting options (Teams Premium or a Copilot licence). Your admin settings determine a recording's expiration.
- **Teams (mobile):** More options > Start recording.
- **Zoom:** computer recordings are saved by default to a Zoom folder in Documents (Settings > Recording > Local recording storage changes it) and are available to free and paid accounts; cloud recording needs a paid plan (Pro, Business or Enterprise).
- **Google Meet (computer):** Meeting tools > **Recording**. Recordings upload to the organiser's Google Drive, which needs enough free space. Hosts can record by default unless host controls are on. Google states that from July 2026 the "Meet Recordings" folder is renamed "Legacy Meet Recordings" inside a new "Google Meet" folder, with new recordings in meeting-specific sub-folders.

## AI recaps and note-takers
Summaries and action items from your meeting platform or third-party note-taker bots can misattribute and miss nuance: check them. A bot that joins is a participant too: announce it or block it.

## Consent and law
- Tell participants at the start that you are recording and why. Rules on consent differ by country, state and employer; check yours before recording.
- Don't record sensitive HR, medical or legal conversations without clear agreement.

## Access and retention
Share recordings with attendees only (link permissions), set an expiry where your admin allows it, and delete when no longer needed.
