# Presence and status messages

## Presence
- **Teams** statuses: Available, Busy, Do not disturb, Be right back, Away, Offline. Busy still lets notifications through; Do not disturb suppresses them; Be right back is never set automatically. Teams can switch Available to Away when your computer locks, idles or sleeps (on mobile, when the app is in the background). Focus time booked through the Outlook add-in sets the status to Focusing and silences notifications.
- Set a duration from your profile picture > current status > **Duration** (Reset status after...). Durations work for any status except Available, and a more available status (Busy) will not override a less available one (Away). When it ends, Teams resets based on activity, calendar and device state.
- **Slack:** set a status, choose **Remove status after...** (mobile: **Clear after**), and tick **Pause notifications** to silence alerts until it clears.
- Presence is not a measure of work.

## Status messages
- Teams: profile picture > **Set status message**, choose how long before it clears (*Clear after* on mobile) and *Show when people message me*.
- Slack: update your status with emoji and text and an end time (for example "In workshop until 3pm", "Out sick").
- Include what matters: when you're back and who to contact.

## Respect others' signals
- Do not disturb or focus time: send a message they can read later; don't call unless urgent.
- Different time zones: schedule messages for their working hours.
