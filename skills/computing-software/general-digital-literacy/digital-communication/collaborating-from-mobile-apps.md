# Collaborate from mobile apps

## Apps to install
- **Microsoft 365 Copilot app** (Word/Excel/PowerPoint in one), **OneDrive**, **Teams**, **Outlook**.
- **Google Docs/Sheets/Slides**, **Drive**, **Gmail**, **Google Chat**.
- **Slack**, **Zoom**, **Notion**, **Loop** as used by your team.
- Work accounts may require Intune/Company Portal; follow IT's setup.

## What works well on a phone
- **Reading and commenting**: open from the link, tap-and-hold text > *Comment*; @mention to notify.
- **Light edits and approvals**: fix a typo, accept suggestions (Docs *Suggesting* mode), approve a request in Teams/Approvals.
- **Scanning** paper into the shared folder (OneDrive/Drive scan button).
- Joining calls and sharing your screen from the phone.

## What's painful
- Big formatting changes, complex spreadsheets and slides layout: leave for a computer.

## Offline
- Google Docs: *Available offline* per file; OneDrive: *Make available offline*. Edits sync when reconnected; others' edits merge in real time when both online.
- Avoid editing the same section offline on two devices: conflict copies appear.

## Security
- App lock (Face ID/PIN) on work apps; don't save work files to the phone's personal storage or share them via personal WhatsApp.
- Turn on notifications only for mentions and direct messages.

## Verify
- [ ] You can open a shared doc from a chat link, leave a comment with an @mention, and it appears on the desktop.
