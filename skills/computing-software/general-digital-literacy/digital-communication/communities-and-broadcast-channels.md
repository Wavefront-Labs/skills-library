# Communities and broadcast channels

## Which tool
- **WhatsApp Channels**: one-way updates to an unlimited audience; followers can react with emojis or vote on polls but cannot reply. Channel updates are public and channels are not end-to-end encrypted, so never post private material.
- **WhatsApp Communities**: an umbrella over related groups (a school with class groups). Every member is added to an **announcement group** where only community admins can post; members can reply, react, vote in polls and mute it. Leaving the announcement group removes you from the community. An optional **general chat** group can be joined and left separately.
- **Telegram channels**: for broadcasting public messages to large audiences; channels can have an unlimited number of subscribers, and posts are signed with the channel name, not the poster's. You can appoint additional administrators.
- **Broadcast lists, Instagram and Facebook broadcast channels**: check each app's help centre for current limits before relying on them.

## Good practice
- Pick a channel or an announcement group for large audiences instead of one big group chat where everyone posts.
- Moderate: admin-only posting, remove spam quickly.
- Keep posting predictable and relevant; people mute or leave noisy channels.
- Follow data protection rules if it's a business or organisation (consent, opt-out).

- [ ] A test member receives posts, can mute or leave easily, and you know who can post and reply.
