# Post and share responsibly

**Goal:** what you post doesn't spread falsehoods, embarrass others, or put you at risk later.

## Before sharing something you didn't create
- **Read beyond the headline**; check the date (old stories resurface).
- **Check the source:** who posted it originally? Is it covered by reliable outlets?
- Images and videos: reverse image search (Google Lens, TinEye) to see if they're old or from elsewhere; look for AI-generation signs and labels.
- Strong emotional reaction (outrage, fear) is the moment to pause; that's what misinformation exploits.
- If unsure, don't share. Sharing "just in case it's true" spreads it anyway.

## Before posting about others
- Ask permission before posting photos of friends, colleagues, and especially other people's **children**.
- Respect "please don't post this" without argument; remove promptly if asked.
- Avoid tagging people in situations that might affect their jobs or safety.

## Protect yourself
- Don't post holiday plans while away, home address, children's school uniforms or names, boarding passes (barcodes reveal booking details), or keys/cards in photos.
- Turn off location tagging; check the audience selector (Public / Friends / Close Friends) before each post.
- Assume anything can be screenshotted and kept forever, even "disappearing" stories.
- Employers, universities and landlords look at public profiles; review old posts periodically.

## At work
Follow your employer's social media policy: don't share confidential information, and make clear personal opinions are your own.

## Correct mistakes
If you shared something false, delete it and say so; a correction post reaches some of the same people.

## Verify
- [ ] Source checked, audience chosen, nobody in the post objects, no location or routine revealed.
