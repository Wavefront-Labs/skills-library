# Record audio and podcasts

## Gear
- Phone voice memo app is fine to start; a **USB microphone** (dynamic mics like Samson Q2U or Shure MV7 reject room noise better than condenser mics) improves a lot.
- Headphones to monitor and to stop echo when recording guests.

## The room matters most
- Soft furnishings reduce echo: record in a carpeted room, near curtains, or even a wardrobe. Avoid kitchens and bare rooms.
- Turn off fans, fridges where possible, notifications; mic 10-15 cm from mouth, slightly off-axis to reduce plosives (or use a pop filter).

## Record
- **Audacity** (free, Windows/Mac/Linux): select mic, set level so peaks reach about -12 to -6 dB (never red). Record a few seconds of silence for noise reduction.
- **GarageBand** (Mac/iPhone): *Voice* track; **Audacity**/**Reaper** on Windows.
- Remote interviews: **Riverside**, **Zencastr**, or each person records locally ("double-ender") while on a call.

## Edit
- Audacity: *Effect > Noise Reduction* (get noise profile from silence, apply gently), cut mistakes, *Effect > Compressor*, then **Loudness Normalization** to -16 LUFS (stereo podcasts) / -19 LUFS mono.
- Keep edits natural; remove long pauses and false starts, not every "um".

## Export and publish
- Export **MP3** 128 kbps mono (speech) or WAV for archiving.
- Podcast hosting (Spotify for Creators, Buzzsprout, Libsyn) creates the RSS feed and distributes to Apple Podcasts and Spotify. Add title, show notes, cover art (3000x3000 px).
- Music in podcasts needs a licence.

## Verify
- [ ] Playback on phone speaker and headphones sounds clear, without echo or clipping, at a consistent volume.
