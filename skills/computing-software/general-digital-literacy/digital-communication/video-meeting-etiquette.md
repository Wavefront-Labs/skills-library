# Video meeting etiquette

**Goal:** meetings where everyone can hear, contribute and finish on time.

## Before
- Test audio and camera in the app's preview or test call; headphones stop echo.
- Join 1-2 minutes early; use the display name people know you by.
- Close noisy apps and notifications (Focus/Do Not Disturb), and tabs you'd rather not share.

## During
- **Mute when not speaking** in groups larger than ~4; `Ctrl+Shift+M` (Teams), `Alt+A` (Zoom), `Ctrl+D` (Meet). Hold Space to talk briefly in Zoom/Teams.
- **Camera:** follow the meeting's norm; on for small discussions and introductions helps engagement; off is fine for large webinars or poor connections. Say why if you turn it off ("bandwidth is bad").
- **Raise hand** (Teams `Ctrl+Shift+K`, Zoom `Alt+Y`) rather than talking over others; host calls on people in order.
- **Chat:** for links, questions and "+1"s; don't run side conversations that ignore the speaker. Private messages can still appear on screen when sharing.
- Look at the camera when speaking; pause a second for latency before replying.
- Don't eat on camera; step away with camera off if needed.
- Don't visibly multitask (typing noise, looking at another screen); if you must, keep camera and mic off.

## Hybrid meetings
- Room participants: use the room system's mic, avoid side chats remote people can't hear.
- Chair checks in with remote people first; share documents on screen, not paper.

## Recording
Announce before recording or transcribing; many platforms notify automatically, but courtesy (and sometimes law) requires asking.

## After
Send notes and actions; share the recording link with the right permissions.

## Verify
- [ ] You join on time, muted when silent, and every remote participant had a chance to speak.
