# Family sharing for purchases and subscriptions

## Apple Family Sharing
- The organizer, who must be an adult, sets it up: Settings > [your name] > **Family** > Continue, then follow the prompts. You and up to five family members can share services such as iCloud+, Apple Music and Apple TV without sharing an Apple Account.
- Invite more people: Settings > Family > **Add Member**; check or resend an invitation by tapping the person's name.
- You can be in only one Family Sharing group at a time and can switch groups once per year.
- Adults can be set as **Parent/Guardian** (tap the member's name), which lets them manage parental controls and respond to Ask to Buy and Ask to Browse requests.
- A Child Account can be created from Add Member; in some places a child's account must stay in a group until they are 18.

## Google Family Link
- Supervises a child's Google Account and supervised devices: apps they may download or purchase, screen time, and Android or compatible Fitbit location.
- Purchase approval settings apply only to purchases through Google Play's billing system.
- iPhones, iPads and computers other than Chromebooks can't be supervised; Google Workspace for Education accounts can't be managed.
