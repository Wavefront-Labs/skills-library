# Profile photo and display name

**Goal:** colleagues and contacts recognise you instantly and see the right name and availability.

## Where to set it
- **Microsoft 365 (Teams, Outlook):** your initials/photo top right › **Change photo** (applies everywhere in Microsoft 365). Name and title usually come from your organisation's directory; ask IT to fix them. Teams › Settings › **Profile** for pronouns and personal note (if enabled).
- **Google:** myaccount.google.com › **Personal info** › photo, name, nickname; *About me* controls what others see; workspace admins may lock the name.
- **Slack:** profile picture › **Edit profile**: full name, display name, title, pronouns, name pronunciation recording, time zone.
- **Zoom:** zoom.us › Profile › edit photo, display name, pronouns; rename during a meeting from the Participants list.
- **WhatsApp:** Settings › profile › photo, name, About; Privacy › *Profile photo*: My contacts / Nobody / Everyone.

## Good choices
- **Photo:** recent, head and shoulders, face clearly visible, plain background, good light. Logos or pets for work accounts make you harder to recognise.
- **Display name:** the name people call you, plus surname at work; add a short qualifier for common names (team or location).
- **Pronouns and pronunciation:** optional; helpful in diverse and remote teams.
- **Status message:** Teams/Slack status for "On leave until 3 Nov, contact Sam" with an expiry so it clears itself.
- **Time zone** set correctly so people see your local time.

## Privacy
- Personal apps: limit profile photo to contacts; strangers use photos for impersonation and romance scams.
- Don't include phone numbers or birthdays in public bios.

## Verify
- [ ] Photo and name appear correctly in a colleague's view (ask) or in your own profile card preview.
