# Consistent usernames and handles

## Choosing one
- Short, easy to spell when said aloud, no confusing characters (l vs 1, double underscores), and the same everywhere if possible.
- Professional/brand use: your name or business name; add a short modifier if taken (`janesmithdesign`, `thejanesmith`, `janesmith.uk`).
- Check across platforms at once before committing (namechk-style tools, or search each platform).

## Reserve it
- Register the handle on major platforms you might use (Instagram, TikTok, X, Threads, Bluesky, YouTube, LinkedIn custom URL, Facebook page) even if dormant, to prevent impersonation.
- **Bluesky**: you can use your own domain as your handle (Settings > Account > *Handle*), which verifies identity.
- **LinkedIn**: *Edit public profile & URL* for linkedin.com/in/yourname.

## Privacy trade-offs
- Same handle everywhere makes you easy to find, including linking anonymous accounts to your real identity. Use a **different** handle for private, hobby or anonymous accounts.
- Don't include birth year, location or school in handles.

## Changing a handle
- Most platforms let you change; the old name becomes available to others and old links may break (some redirect briefly). Update bio links and business cards.

## Security
- Each account still needs a unique password and 2-step verification; popular handles are targets for takeover.

## Verify
- [ ] Your professional handle is consistent across platforms and private accounts use unrelated names.
