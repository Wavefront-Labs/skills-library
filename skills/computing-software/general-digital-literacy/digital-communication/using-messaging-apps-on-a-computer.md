# Use messaging apps on a computer

## Link a device
- **WhatsApp:** link from the phone (WhatsApp help: "How to link a device"; there is also a link-with-phone-number option). One primary phone can link up to four companion devices, and you must use WhatsApp on the primary phone at least every 14 days to keep linked devices connected.
- **Signal:** install Signal on the new device, then on your phone go to Settings > **Linked devices > Link a new device** and scan the QR code (unlock with biometrics or your device passcode, not your Signal PIN). You can transfer message history at link time (all chats and the last 45 days of media). Your phone must come online at least once every 30 days, linked devices also unlink after 45 days of inactivity, and the limit is 5 linked devices.
- For Telegram, Google Messages and Apple Messages on a computer, follow each provider's help centre.

## Use it well
- Keyboard typing for long messages; drag files in to send; notifications on the desktop (manage in OS settings).

## Security
- Review the **Linked devices** list regularly (Signal: Settings > Linked devices) and unlink anything unknown. A scammer who gets you to scan their QR code or read out a pairing code can read your chats.
- On shared or work computers, log out when done.

## Verify
- [ ] Messages sent from the computer appear on the phone, and the linked devices list shows only your own devices.
