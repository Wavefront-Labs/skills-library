# Scheduling and hosting an online meeting

**Goal:** a meeting that starts on time, stays on track and keeps uninvited people out.

## Schedule
- Create the meeting from your calendar or the platform's schedule button, and send the invite with an agenda.
- Set meeting options before it starts: who bypasses the lobby, who can present, who can record, and whether attendees can use microphone and camera.

## Teams host controls (Microsoft Support)
- **Lobby:** the organiser chooses who skips the lobby, from only organisers and co-organisers, through invited people and people in your organisation, to everyone including dial-in callers. Admin policy can still send guests, people from untrusted organisations and dial-in callers to the lobby. By default organisers, co-organisers and presenters can admit people; the organiser can narrow this with "Who can admit from the lobby".
- **Co-organisers:** can change meeting options, manage breakout rooms, bypass the lobby, admit people and change other participants' roles; they cannot remove the organiser, manage recording or edit invitation details. They must be in the organiser's organisation, up to 10 can be added.
- **Remove someone:** open the participant list (People), hover over the name, then More options > **Remove from meeting**.
- Zoom and Google Meet have equivalent waiting-room, co-host, mute and remove controls; use each platform's help centre for the exact menus.

## Security
Don't post meeting links publicly; require a lobby or waiting room for public events; restrict screen sharing and presenting to the host and co-hosts; use webinar formats for large audiences.

## Running it well
Start on time, state the agenda and outcome, ask people to mute when not speaking, use chat or raise-hand for questions, summarise actions at the end and send notes.
