# Shared photo albums

**Goal:** everyone from the wedding or holiday can see and add photos in one place.

## iCloud Shared Albums (Apple)
- Photos app: create a new shared album, name it and invite people by Apple Account.
- Size limits depend on software version (Apple Support "Save photos from a shared album"): on iOS, iPadOS, visionOS or macOS 26 or earlier, shared photos are reduced to 2048 pixels on the long edge (panoramas up to 5400 pixels wide) and videos are delivered at up to 720p; on version 27 or later, photos in a shared album are stored in iCloud in original formats at full resolution. Apple's limits page also lists a 100-participant maximum for albums created in version 27 or later.

## Google Photos
- Select photos, add them to a **new shared album**, give it a title, then share with people or create a link.
- The owner chooses whether others can add photos (**Collaborate**) and whether comments and likes are allowed: open the album > More > Options (iPhone: Sharing settings).
- **Link sharing**: anyone with the link can view, and can add photos if Collaborate is on. Only the owner can change link sharing; turning it off and on resets the link. Removing a person while link sharing is on does not stop people who hold the link.
- **Partner sharing** automatically shares photos with another person (a Google Account holder), either everything or photos of chosen people or from a start date.

## Other cloud folders
OneDrive or Dropbox folder links with view or edit permission suit collecting full-resolution originals from photographers; check each service's help for permission options.

## Privacy
- Link-shared albums can be forwarded; use invite-only sharing for family photos of children.
- Remove location data before sharing publicly.
