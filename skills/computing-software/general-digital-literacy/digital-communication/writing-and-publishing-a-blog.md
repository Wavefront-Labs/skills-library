# Write and publish a blog

## Choose a platform
- **WordPress.com** (hosted, free tier, custom domain on paid plans) or self-hosted **WordPress.org** (most control, you maintain it).
- **Substack** (newsletter-first, email subscribers, paid subscriptions), **Ghost** (newsletter + site, paid hosting or self-host), **Medium** (built-in audience, less control), **Blogger** (free, simple), **Squarespace/Wix** blogs if you already have a site there.
- Own your list: platforms that let you **export posts and subscriber emails** protect you if you move.

## Write posts people read
- Clear, specific title; first paragraph says what the reader gets.
- Short paragraphs, subheadings, bullet lists; 600-1500 words is plenty for most posts.
- Images: your own or licensed (Unsplash, Creative Commons with attribution); compress them; add **alt text**.
- Link to sources; correct mistakes openly with an update note.

## Publish
- Preview on phone; set a featured image and excerpt; categories/tags for organisation; schedule for a consistent day.
- Custom domain makes it portable and credible.

## Readers
- Email subscription form; share each post where your audience is; reply to comments.
- Basic SEO: descriptive title and URL slug, headings, internal links; no keyword stuffing.

## Responsibilities
- Comments moderation, privacy notice if collecting emails (GDPR), disclosure of affiliate links or sponsorship (FTC/ASA rules).

## Verify
- [ ] A first post is live, readable on mobile, with alt text, and someone else can subscribe successfully.
