# The share menu on phones

## Find it
- **iPhone/iPad**: the **Share** icon (square with an up arrow) in Safari, Photos, Files, and most apps.
- **Android**: the **Share** icon (three connected dots) or *Share* in a menu.

## What's in it
- **Top row**: suggested people and recent chats (one tap sends via that app); nearby devices (AirDrop/Quick Share).
- **App row**: Messages, Mail, WhatsApp, Notes, Drive, etc.
- **Actions** (iPhone): *Copy*, *Add to Reading List*, *Save to Files*, *Print*, *Add to Home Screen*, *Find on Page*, shortcuts. Android: *Copy*, *Nearby/Quick Share*, *Print* in the app's menu.

## Options before sharing (iPhone)
- Tap **Options** at the top of the sheet in Photos: turn off *Location*, *All Photos Data*, or send as *iCloud Link*.
- Safari: Options lets you send as PDF, web archive or link.

## Customise
- iPhone: scroll the app row to the end > **More** > Edit: add/remove/reorder apps; *Edit Actions* at the bottom.
- iPhone Settings > Siri > *Suggestions when Sharing* to turn off contact suggestions.
- Android: long-press an app in the share sheet > *Pin*; Samsung Settings > Advanced features > *Direct share* toggles people suggestions.

## Gotchas
- An app only appears if it's installed and supports sharing; open it once if missing.
- Sharing a photo to some apps strips quality; see the full-quality sharing skill.

## Verify
- [ ] You can send a webpage link to a contact in two taps and share a photo without location.
