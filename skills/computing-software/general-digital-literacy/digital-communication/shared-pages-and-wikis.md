# Shared pages and wikis

## Microsoft Loop
- **Loop workspaces** (loop.cloud.microsoft): pages for projects, meeting notes and plans; everyone edits live.
- **Loop components**: a table, checklist or paragraph inserted in a Teams chat or Outlook email (*Loop* icon in compose). Edits sync everywhere it appears.
- Stored in OneDrive/SharePoint (.loop files); permissions follow those files.

## Notion
- Pages nest inside pages; **databases** (tables, boards, calendars) for tasks and docs; templates for meeting notes and wikis. Share with a teamspace or invite guests; *Publish* makes a public web page.

## Confluence
- Spaces per team; page trees; templates (meeting notes, decision log, how-to). Common in organisations using Jira.

## Google Docs as a simple wiki
- A Drive folder with an index doc linking to others; or **Google Sites** for a navigable internal site.

## Make it work
- One home page with clear navigation; owner and *last reviewed* date on each page.
- Write so a newcomer can follow: purpose, steps, links to sources.
- Archive stale pages instead of letting them mislead.
- Check sharing: internal wikis accidentally published publicly are a common data leak.

## Verify
- [ ] A new teammate can find the key how-to page from the home page in two clicks.
