# Tone in written messages

**Goal:** people understand your intent, not just your words.

## Why text goes wrong
No voice or face: readers fill gaps with their mood. Short messages read as cold; jokes read as
serious; "Can we talk?" causes panic.

## Practical rules
- **Context first:** "Quick question about the invoice: is the date right?" beats "Is this right?"
- **Soften requests:** "Could you…", "When you have a moment…", "Thanks!" costs nothing.
- **Avoid loaded phrases:** "Per my last email", "As I already said", "Just to be clear" sound passive-
  aggressive. Restate the point kindly instead.
- **Punctuation:** a full stop on a one-word chat reply ("Fine.") can read as annoyed; exclamation marks
  in moderation sound warm; ALL CAPS is shouting; "???" is impatient.
- **Specific praise and thanks** in public, criticism in private and by voice.
- **Re-read before sending** as the recipient: would it worry, hurt or confuse?
- **Group chats:** reply in threads, @mention only those who need to act, don't argue in the group.

## Switch channel when
Disagreement after two exchanges, bad news, emotional topics, complex decisions: call or meet, then
confirm the outcome in writing.

## Different cultures and languages
Directness varies; be clear and polite, avoid idioms and sarcasm with international colleagues.
