# Joining a meeting from a link or code

**Goal:** arrive on time, with working sound, looking and sounding the way you intend.

## From the invite
- Click the **link** in the email or calendar event; it opens the app or a browser page.
- **Teams, without the invite link:** in the desktop app open Calendar and use the dropdown beside Meet now to choose **Join with an ID**, then enter the meeting ID and passcode (shown in the invite or the meeting details). This option needs an Exchange Online mailbox. On mobile it is under the Calendar tab > More > Join with meeting ID. People without an account can join as guests; Microsoft has a separate article on joining without an account.
- **Phone dial-in:** many invites list numbers and a conference ID; useful with poor internet.

## Pre-join screen (always look)
- Choose camera, microphone and speakers; in Google Meet, speak and watch the mic bar move, and use the speaker test button. If the browser asks for camera and microphone access, allow it.
- Join **muted** if the meeting has started; turn on video if expected.
- Check your background and the name that will be shown.

## If something fails
No sound? Check the speaker selection, then rejoin. A lobby or waiting-room message means the host will admit you. If the app won't open, use the browser option.

## Gotchas
- Join 2-5 minutes early for first-time setups (installs, updates, permissions).
- Signed in to the wrong account (personal vs work) can block entry; Microsoft has an "I can't join a meeting" article for this.

## Verify
- [ ] You hear others, they hear you, and your display name is correct.
