# Feed algorithms and content controls

## How ranking works
- Feeds predict what you'll engage with from signals: who you interact with, what you watch to the end, rewatch, like, comment, share, pause on, and what people like you engage with.
- Watch time matters more than likes: lingering on content you dislike teaches the feed to show more.
- Platforms publish explanations (Meta *Why am I seeing this post?*, TikTok *Why this video*, YouTube).

## Steer it
- **Not interested** / *Show fewer posts like this* / *Don't recommend channel*; long-press or the three-dot menu.
- **Topics**: TikTok *Manage topics* sliders and *Filter keywords*; Instagram *Suggested content* settings, Hidden words; Threads *Not interested*; X *Not interested in this post*.
- **Following feeds**: Instagram *Following/Favourites*, Facebook *Feeds*, X *Following*, Bluesky custom feeds.
- **Reset**: TikTok *Refresh your For You feed*; Instagram *Reset suggested content*; YouTube clear or pause **watch and search history** (paused history empties the home feed recommendations).

## Teen and sensitive settings
- Instagram/Facebook *Sensitive content control*; YouTube Restricted Mode; TikTok *Restricted mode*. EU users can choose non-personalised feeds under the Digital Services Act.

## Healthy habits
- Follow sources deliberately; unfollow accounts that make you feel worse.
- Remember the feed shows what keeps you scrolling, not what's most important or true.

## Verify
- [ ] After a week of steering, the feed shows more of the topics you chose and fewer you rejected.
