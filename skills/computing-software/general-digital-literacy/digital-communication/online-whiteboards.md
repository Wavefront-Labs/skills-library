# Online whiteboards

**Goal:** a remote group can brainstorm, sort and decide visually, as on a wall of sticky notes.

## Tools
- Common whiteboard tools include Microsoft Whiteboard, Miro, FigJam, Mural and Zoom Whiteboard. Check each one's help centre for plan limits, templates and export formats.
- **Google Jamboard has been discontinued.** It went view-only on 1 October 2024 and could not be accessed after 31 December 2024; Google recommended FigJam, Lucidspark and Miro, and said jams would be converted to PDFs. Do not plan new sessions on Jamboard.

## Running a session
1. Prepare the board before: title, instructions, a frame per activity, a template (retrospective, affinity map, SWOT, journey map).
2. Share the link with **edit** access; ask everyone to open it a few minutes early and test adding a note.
3. Silent brainstorm: each person adds sticky notes for 5 minutes (timer).
4. Group similar notes (affinity clustering), label clusters.
5. **Vote** (dot voting feature, or each person adds 3 dots), discuss the top items.
6. Capture decisions and actions in a clear box.

## Afterwards
Export or screenshot the board, share it read-only, and move actions to the task tool.

## Gotchas
- Too many people editing at once is chaos; give each person a colour and an area.
- Boards shared "anyone with link can edit" can be vandalised or leak; restrict access for sensitive topics.
- Keyboard-only users need text-friendly activities.
