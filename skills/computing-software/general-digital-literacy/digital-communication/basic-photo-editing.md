# Basic photo editing

**Goal:** a straight, well-lit, well-framed photo, ready to share, without specialist software.

## Built-in editors
- **Windows Photos:** open photo › **Edit** (`Ctrl+E`) › Crop (aspect ratios, rotate, flip, straighten),
  Adjustment (light, colour), Filter, Markup; *Generative erase* on recent versions; Background blur.
- **Mac/iPhone Photos:** **Edit** › Adjust (Auto wand, Light, Colour), Filters, Crop (straighten
  wheel, aspect), **Clean Up** removes distractions (Apple Intelligence devices); Retouch on older Macs.
- **Google Photos (Android/web):** Edit › Suggestions (Enhance), Crop, Tools (Magic Eraser, blur),
  Adjust.
- **Snapseed** (free): more control (selective adjust, healing).

## Order of work
1. **Straighten and crop**: level horizon; crop to strengthen composition (rule of thirds) or to a
   required ratio (1:1 for profile photos, 16:9 for slides).
2. **Light**: exposure/brightness, then shadows (lift) and highlights (recover sky).
3. **Colour**: warmth for skin tones, saturation lightly; avoid neon.
4. **Clean up** small blemishes or a stray object.
5. Export/share at a sensible size (see resize skill); keep the original.

## Gotchas
- Photos apps keep originals: *Revert to Original* undoes edits, but edits saved as a copy or exported
  are separate files.
- Heavy filters and "beauty" smoothing look fake; for ID or official photos, don't edit features at all.
- Editing doesn't remove location metadata; strip it when sharing publicly (share options › exclude location).

## Verify
- [ ] Horizon level, subject well exposed, original still available.
