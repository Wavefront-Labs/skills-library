# Shared task boards and lists

**Goal:** everyone sees who is doing what, by when, without status emails.

## Tools
- **Microsoft Planner** (Microsoft 365; a tab in Teams channels), **Trello** (free tier, boards and
  cards), **Asana**, **Notion** databases; for households, shared lists in **Microsoft To Do**, Google
  Keep or Apple Reminders.

## Set up a board
1. Columns as stages (Kanban): **To do › Doing › Done** (add *Waiting* if work stalls on others).
2. One card = one task with a verb: "Book venue for 12 Nov", not "Venue".
3. Each card: **one owner**, a **due date**, checklist for sub-steps, attachments/links, comments for
   updates (instead of email).
4. Labels for category or priority; filter by person to see your own work.

## Keep it alive
- Review in a weekly 15-minute check-in: move cards, unblock, reassign.
- Limit work in progress: too many cards in Doing means nothing finishes.
- Archive Done cards monthly.
- Turn on notifications for cards assigned to you (and few others).

## Gotchas
- Boards nobody updates are worse than none; agree that the board is the single source of truth.
- Guests/external people need explicit access; check sharing before adding client names or personal data.

## Verify
- [ ] Every card in To do/Doing has an owner and a date; the team can find its board in one click.
