# Buy and sell on marketplaces safely

**Goal:** get the item or the money, without scams or unsafe meetings.

## Buying
- Prefer platforms with **buyer protection** and pay through them (eBay Money Back Guarantee, Vinted Buyer Protection, Facebook checkout with *Purchase Protection* where offered).
- **Never pay by bank transfer, gift cards, crypto or PayPal "Friends and Family"** to a stranger; these have little or no protection.
- Too-cheap prices for phones, consoles, tickets, puppies and cars are the classic bait.
- Check the seller: account age, ratings, other listings; reverse-image-search the photos.
- Collect in person and inspect before paying where possible; for phones check the IMEI isn't blacklisted and that Find My/Google lock is off.
- Vehicles: check history (DVLA/MOT history UK, Carfax/VIN check US).

## Selling
- **Overpayment scam:** buyer "accidentally" sends too much and asks for a refund of the difference; the original payment then bounces. Refuse.
- **Fake payment emails** saying "payment pending, send tracking first" don't come from the platform; check the payment in the app itself.
- Buyers asking you to click a link to "receive the money" or verify with a code are phishing.
- Use tracked postage; photograph the item and packing; keep proof of posting.
- Don't share your phone number or address publicly; message within the app.

## Meeting in person
- Meet in a busy public place (some police stations offer safe trade zones), in daylight, bring someone, tell a friend.
- For large items at home, have someone with you and keep valuables out of sight.
- Count cash on the spot; use contactless/instant transfer only once it shows in your own banking app.

## If it goes wrong
Report the user on the platform, open a claim through the platform's process within its deadline, contact your bank or card issuer, and report fraud (Action Fraud UK / ReportFraud.ftc.gov).
