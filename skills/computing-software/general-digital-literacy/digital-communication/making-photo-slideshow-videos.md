# Photo slideshow videos

## Quick, automatic
- **Apple Photos**: select photos > Share > *Slideshow* (plays only) or create a **Memory movie** (*Memories* > + or tap ... > *Create Memory Movie*); change music, mood and length; share/export.
- **Google Photos**: *Create > Highlight video* (or Movie): choose people/dates, pick music; edit clip order and length.

## More control
- **Microsoft Clipchamp** (free with Windows 11): drag photos into the timeline, set each to 3-5 seconds, add transitions, text titles, music from the stock library; *Export* 1080p.
- **iMovie** (Mac/iPhone): Create > Movie or Magic Movie; Ken Burns pan/zoom on stills.
- **Canva** video: templates for birthdays and memorials.
- **PowerPoint**: photo album + *Export > Create a Video* also works.

## Tips
- 3-5 seconds per photo; 60-90 photos makes about 5 minutes, which is enough.
- Order chronologically or by theme; start and end with the strongest images.
- Mix portrait and landscape carefully: blur-fill backgrounds or crop to 16:9 for TV.
- Music: use the app's licensed library; commercial songs may be muted on YouTube/Facebook.
- Check faces aren't cropped by pan/zoom effects.

## Export and share
- 1080p MP4 plays on most TVs (USB stick, Chromecast/AirPlay) and phones; share via cloud link for large files.

## Verify
- [ ] Watch the full export on the target screen: music plays to the end and no photos are cut off.
