# Trim and assemble simple videos

**Goal:** a tidy one-to-three-minute video from several clips, with captions, sized for where it will be shared.

## Quick trim
- **iPhone/iPad:** Photos › video › **Edit** › drag the yellow handles on the timeline › Done › *Save as New Clip* (keeps the original).
- **Android:** Google Photos › video › **Edit** › trim handles › Save copy.
- **Windows:** Photos app › video › **Trim** (or *Edit in Clipchamp*). **Mac:** QuickTime Player › Edit › **Trim** (`Cmd+T`).

## Assemble several clips
- **Clipchamp** (built into Windows 11, free web version): Create a video › drag clips to the timeline › split (`S`) and delete gaps › add text, transitions, music from the stock library › **Auto captions** › Export 1080p.
- **iMovie** (Mac, iPhone): Create New › Movie › add clips › trim by dragging clip edges › titles and transitions › Share › File.
- **CapCut** (phones, desktop) and Google Photos *Highlight video* for quick automatic edits.

## Good habits
- Shoot horizontally for YouTube/presentations, vertically (9:16) for Reels/TikTok/Shorts/Stories.
- Cut the first and last few seconds of fumbling; keep shots short (3-8 s).
- **Audio matters more than picture:** record close to the speaker, use an external mic or earbuds mic, reduce background music under speech.
- Add **captions** (Clipchamp Auto captions, CapCut, YouTube auto-subtitles; then correct them); many people watch muted and it aids accessibility.

## Music and rights
Use the editor's licensed stock music, YouTube Audio Library, or Creative Commons tracks with credit. Commercial songs get muted or blocked on most platforms.

## Export
- 1080p, 30 fps, MP4 (H.264) plays everywhere. 4K only if needed; files are 4× bigger.
- For email or messaging over ~25 MB, share a cloud link instead.

## Verify
- [ ] Final video plays on a phone with sound off and captions readable; file size suits where it's going.
