# Live captions

**Goal:** read what's said in real time, for accessibility, noisy places, accents or another language.

## In meeting apps (each person turns them on for themselves)
- **Teams:** in the meeting controls select **More > Turn on live captions** (More > Turn off live captions to stop). Teams does not save captions.
- **Zoom:** click **Show captions** in the toolbar (or More > Show captions). The host must have enabled manual or automated captions; if the host has restricted who can start them, click **Request captions** (tick *Send request with translation* to ask for translated captions) and the host or a co-host approves.
- **Google Meet (computer):** hover over the captions and translation control at the bottom and select **Turn on captions**, or press `c`. More options > Settings > **Captions** sets language and appearance; translated captions are a separate option.

## System-wide (any app, even videos)
- Windows 11: press `Windows key + Ctrl + L`, or Settings > Accessibility > Captions > Live captions. The first use asks consent and downloads language files for on-device recognition.
- iPhone (iPhone 11 or later): Settings > Accessibility > **Live Captions**.
- Mac (Apple silicon only): System Settings > Accessibility > **Live Captions**. Apple says availability varies by language and region and warns not to rely on it in high-risk or emergency situations.

## Tips
- Set the spoken language correctly; captions in the wrong language are gibberish.
- Speakers: one at a time, close to the mic, say names and acronyms clearly.
- For formal events or deaf attendees, ask for human captioning (CART) or a sign language interpreter; automatic captions miss words.

- [ ] Captions appear in the expected language when someone speaks.
