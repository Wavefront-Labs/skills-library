# Building a professional profile

**Goal:** when an employer, client or contact searches your name, they find an accurate, professional
picture.

## LinkedIn essentials
- **Photo:** recent, head and shoulders, plain background, good light, you smiling.
- **Headline** (220 characters): what you do and for whom, not only a job title: "Payroll specialist
  helping SMEs stay compliant | CIPP".
- **About:** 3-5 short paragraphs in first person: strengths, achievements with numbers, what you're
  looking for.
- **Experience:** role, dates, 3-5 bullet achievements ("cut month-end close from 8 to 5 days").
- **Skills** (keywords recruiters search), certificates, education; ask colleagues for a recommendation.
- Custom profile URL: Edit public profile & URL (`linkedin.com/in/firstname-lastname`).

## Job seeking
*Open to work*: choose **Recruiters only** to avoid announcing it to your employer, or all members.
Settings › Visibility › Profile viewing options (private mode) when browsing others.

## Privacy and safety
- Settings › Visibility: who sees your connections, email, last name; what's on your public profile.
- Don't list home address or date of birth; watch for fake recruiter profiles asking for money or ID.
- Turn off *Share profile updates with your network* while making many edits.

## Consistency
Same name and photo across professional platforms (portfolio site, GitHub, Behance); old or
unprofessional public profiles: tidy, make private or delete.

## Verify
- [ ] Viewing your profile as public ("View as") shows what you intend and nothing private.
