# Test audio and video before a call

## Built-in tests
- **Zoom:** join a Zoom test meeting (zoom.us/test) to check audio, video and your connection. In the desktop app, Settings > **Audio** has **Test speaker** and **Test microphone**.
- **Teams (desktop app, work or school account):** Settings > Devices > Audio settings > **Make a test call**. Microsoft answers describe this as desktop-only, so it may be missing on the web or with a personal account.
- **Google Meet:** the green room preview shows your camera; use the microphone control and speak (the level bar should move), and the speaker control's test button. More options > Settings > Audio or Video changes devices. Allow the browser to use camera and microphone when asked.
- OS checks: look at the input level in your system sound settings.

## Checklist 10 minutes before
- Right **microphone** and **speaker** selected (headsets, monitors and webcams all add audio devices).
- Camera at eye level, light in front of you not behind, background tidy or blurred.
- Close bandwidth-heavy apps (cloud sync, streaming); plug into power; sit near the router or use Ethernet.
- Notifications off (Focus or Do Not Disturb).
- Software updated; updates mid-meeting are a common delay.

## Common fixes
- Nobody hears you: wrong microphone, hardware mute, or the app lacks microphone permission in your device privacy settings.
- Black camera: another app is using it, a privacy shutter is closed, or camera permission is off.
- Echo: use headphones.

- [ ] Test playback sounds clear and the preview shows you well lit.
