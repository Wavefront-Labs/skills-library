# Live streaming

## From a phone (YouTube)
- Mobile live streaming on YouTube requires a verified channel with live streaming turned on, at least 50 subscribers, no live-streaming restrictions in the last 90 days, and you must be at least 16. You may have to wait 24 hours before your first stream. Below 1,000 subscribers YouTube may cap viewers and archived streams default to private.
- Other platforms (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Twitch) have their own age, follower and feature rules; read each help centre before planning an event.

## From a computer
- Streaming software such as OBS Studio sends video to a service using a **stream key**. Treat the key as a password: anyone with it can stream to your channel. Follow the platform's encoder guide for settings.
- Browser tools such as StreamYard and Restream exist for multi-platform streaming; check their plans.

## Quality
- Audio first: an external microphone usually beats the built-in one.
- Light from the front; phone on a tripod.
- Test with a private or unlisted stream before going public.

## Moderation and privacy
- Assign moderators, block words and hold links for review where the platform offers it.
- Check what's in frame: house number, mail, school uniforms, screens with notifications.
- Music: streams with copyrighted music can be muted or taken down.

## After
- Save or download a copy for your archive; check how long the platform keeps replays.
