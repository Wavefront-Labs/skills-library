# Group chat etiquette

## Before you post
- Is this for **everyone**? Personal replies ("Thanks!", "Me too") go to the person directly or as a reaction.
- Stay on the group's purpose; a school class group isn't for selling things or political forwards.
- One complete message beats five fragments that each trigger a notification.

## Threads, replies and mentions
- Use **reply/quote** (WhatsApp swipe right) or **threads** (Slack, Teams) so answers stay attached to questions.
- **@mention** only people who must act. @everyone/@channel only for genuine announcements.

## Timing
- Avoid non-urgent posts late at night and early morning; schedule them (Slack/Teams *Schedule send*, WhatsApp doesn't schedule natively).
- Don't expect instant replies; people mute groups.

## Content
- Ask before adding someone to a group or sharing their number.
- Don't forward screenshots of the group elsewhere without thinking; it breaks trust.
- Check forwarded "news" and chain messages before sharing (WhatsApp marks *Forwarded many times*).
- Large files and many photos: use a shared album or link.

## Conflict and leaving
- Take disagreements to a direct message or call.
- Leaving: a short note ("Leaving to keep my phone quieter, message me directly anytime!") then *Exit group*; or mute quietly. WhatsApp lets you exit quietly (only admins are notified).

## Admins
- Pin group rules; set *Only admins can send* for announcement groups; remove spam.

## Verify
- [ ] Your posts are on topic, threaded, and sent at reasonable hours.
