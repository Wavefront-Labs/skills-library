# Webinars and large online events

## Meeting vs webinar
- **Meeting**: everyone can talk and share video. Fine for small groups.
- **Webinar / town hall**: hosts and presenters present; attendees watch, ask in **Q&A** and can be given speaking rights. Check your platform's attendee limits before you promise a number.

## Teams capacity (Teams Enterprise, per Microsoft Learn)
- Up to 1,000 attendees with attendee microphone and camera, interactive chat, reactions, raise hand, polls and Q&A.
- Up to 3,000 attendees with large-event meeting chat, reactions, raise hand, polls and Q&A.
- Up to 10,000 attendees in a view-only experience with Q&A; an Attendee Capacity Pack add-on can scale events to 100,000. Features vary by licence, so read the events licensing page.
- Teams has separate **Webinar** (with registration) and **Town hall** event types.
- Other platforms (Zoom, Google Meet, YouTube Live) have their own limits and plans; check their help centres.

## Organiser checklist
1. Registration with confirmation and reminder emails; a calendar file.
2. Roles: host, co-host or producer (handles tech, admits, Q&A), presenters.
3. **Practice session**: presenters join early; test screen sharing and audio.
4. Captions on; share slides in advance for accessibility.
5. Q&A moderation: one person curates questions; answer live or type replies.
6. Recording and follow-up email with the recording, slides and links.
7. Security: registration required, attendees muted, screen sharing limited to hosts.

## Attendee tips
- Join via your personal registration link; use Q&A rather than chat for questions; recordings usually follow.
