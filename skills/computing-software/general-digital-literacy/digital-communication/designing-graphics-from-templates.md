# Design graphics from templates

## Start
- **Canva** (free tier is ample) or **Adobe Express**: search the size first ("Instagram post", "A4 poster", "Facebook cover", "invitation"), then pick a template close to what you want.
- Custom size when needed (e.g. 1080x1350 px for Instagram portrait, 1920x1080 for a slide or screen).

## Edit
- Replace text and photos (drag your image onto a placeholder frame); *Uploads* for your own logos and photos.
- **Brand kit** (Canva Pro / Express premium) stores colours, fonts and logos; without it, note your hex colours and reuse them.
- Keep two fonts at most; align elements (smart guides); leave space around text.
- Contrast: dark text on light or vice versa; text over photos needs a dark overlay or a solid band. Check with a contrast checker (4.5:1 for small text).
- Big, short headline; details smaller; one clear call to action (date, link, QR code).

## Images and licences
- Template photos and elements marked free are licensed for most uses; premium items need a paid plan. Canva's licence restricts some uses (e.g. trademarking a logo made from stock elements).

## Export
- **PNG** for graphics with text (sharp), **JPG** for photo-heavy images (smaller), **PDF Print** for printing (tick *crop marks and bleed* for professional printers), **MP4/GIF** for animated posts.
- Add **alt text** when posting to social media.

## Collaborate
- Share with edit or view links; comment on designs; templates for your team to reuse.

## Verify
- [ ] Viewed at actual size on a phone, the headline reads in under three seconds and the export dimensions are correct.
