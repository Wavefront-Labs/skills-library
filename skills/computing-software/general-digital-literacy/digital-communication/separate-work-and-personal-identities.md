# Separate work and personal identities

**Goal:** work data stays in work systems, personal life stays private, and leaving a job doesn't mean losing your photos or contacts.

## Accounts
- Use a **work email** only for work; a **personal email** for banks, shopping, family, and anything you need after you leave.
- Never use a work account as the recovery email/phone for personal accounts, or vice versa.
- Professional profiles (LinkedIn) on the personal email: they belong to you, not the employer.

## Devices and apps
- **Browser profiles:** separate work and personal profiles with their own passwords and bookmarks.
- **Android work profile:** work apps show a briefcase badge; the employer manages only that profile; pause it after hours (Quick Settings › *Work apps*).
- **iPhone:** company apps managed through MDM/User Enrolment; use **Focus** modes (Work/Personal) to show different Home Screen pages and silence work apps after hours.
- **Teams/Slack:** separate work and personal accounts in the app's account switcher; set working hours and quiet times.
- **Password managers:** separate vaults or accounts for work and personal.

## Content rules
- Don't save personal files to work OneDrive/Google Drive (employer can access and it disappears when you leave).
- Don't forward work documents to personal email or upload them to personal AI tools; it may breach policy and data-protection law.
- Separate phone numbers (dual SIM/eSIM) if you give your number to clients.

## Leaving a job
Before your last day, move any personal items you saved on work systems (with permission), update recovery details, and remove work accounts from personal devices.

## Verify
- [ ] No personal account uses a work email for login or recovery; work apps silenced outside hours.
