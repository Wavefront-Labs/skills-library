# After-hours messages and the right to disconnect

## Sending without intruding
- **Schedule send**: Outlook (arrow beside Send > *Schedule send*), Gmail (*Schedule send*), Teams and Slack (right-click Send / clock icon). Write when it suits you, deliver at 9am their time.
- Signature line: "I send email at times that suit me; please reply during your working hours."
- Urgent really means urgent: call for emergencies rather than expecting someone to watch chat.

## Protecting your own time
- Set **working hours** in Outlook/Google Calendar so others see when you're available; Teams/Slack **notification schedules** (Slack Preferences > Notifications > *Notification schedule*).
- Phone: Focus/Do Not Disturb with work apps silenced in the evenings; separate work profile (Android Work Profile can be paused).
- Remove work email from your personal phone if allowed, or turn off its notifications.

## The law
- **Australia**: right to disconnect in the Fair Work Act from August 2024 (small businesses from August 2025): employees can refuse unreasonable out-of-hours contact.
- **France** (2017), **Belgium**, **Portugal**, **Ireland** (code of practice), Ontario (written policy required) have rules. UK: no specific law yet; working time rules and contracts apply.
- Follow your employer's policy and agree team norms for on-call situations.

## Managers
- Model it: schedule your late-night emails; say explicitly that replies can wait.

## Verify
- [ ] Your working hours are visible to colleagues and work notifications are off outside them.
