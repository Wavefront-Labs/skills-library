# Shared drives and SharePoint sites

## Why not personal drives
- Files in someone's My Drive/OneDrive belong to them; when they leave and the account is deleted, the files can go too. Shared drives and SharePoint sites are owned by the **team**.

## Google shared drives (Workspace)
- Drive > **Shared drives > New**; add members with roles: *Manager*, *Content manager* (add, edit, move, delete), *Contributor* (add and edit), *Commenter*, *Viewer*.
- Files moved in from My Drive become owned by the organisation.
- Sync with Drive for desktop; shared drives appear under the G: drive (Windows) / Google Drive volume (Mac).

## SharePoint and Teams
- Each **Teams team** has a SharePoint site; the channel **Files** tab is a folder in its document library.
- SharePoint: *Members* (edit), *Visitors* (read), *Owners* (full control). Add via the site's Settings > *Site permissions* or by adding people to the team.
- **Sync** or *Add shortcut to My files* in the library toolbar to see files in File Explorer through OneDrive.

## Organise
- One shared drive/site per team or project, not per person. A short folder structure and a README file explaining it.
- Use links, not attachments, when sharing internally; everyone edits one copy.
- External guests: check if allowed by your organisation; share only specific folders.

## Gotchas
- Moving files between shared drives or out of SharePoint can change permissions and break links.
- Deleted files go to the shared drive's Trash / site Recycle Bin (managers can restore).
- Sync very large libraries selectively; syncing everything can overwhelm the OneDrive client.

## Verify
- [ ] Team files live in a shared drive or site with at least two owners/managers.
