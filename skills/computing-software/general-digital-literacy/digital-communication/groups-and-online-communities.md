# Groups and online communities

## Types
- **Facebook Groups**: local, hobby, parenting, buy/sell; public, private or hidden.
- **Reddit**: topic subreddits with upvoted threads; pseudonymous.
- **Discord** servers: chat channels and voice; gaming, creators, study groups.
- **Forums** (Discourse-based, specialist sites), **Nextdoor** (neighbourhood), **WhatsApp Communities**, **LinkedIn groups**.

## Joining well
- Read the **rules** and pinned posts; lurk a bit to learn norms.
- Search before asking; include details when you do ask.
- Use a profile that reveals only what you're comfortable with; Reddit/Discord usernames needn't be your name.

## Safety in groups
- Scams: fake sellers in buy/sell groups, "DM me for investment tips", fake admins messaging you, giveaway scams, rental scams in local groups. Never pay by bank transfer to strangers; use protected payments or meet in person.
- Anyone in a public group can see your posts; private groups can still be screenshotted.
- Leave and report groups that turn abusive or extreme.

## Running a group
- Clear rules (pinned), membership questions, post approval for new members, at least two admins/moderators.
- Tools: Facebook *Admin assist*, Reddit *AutoModerator*, Discord *AutoMod* and verification levels.
- Remove spam quickly; be consistent; keep a mod log.

## Verify
- [ ] You know the rules of each group you're in and your profile shows only what you intend.
