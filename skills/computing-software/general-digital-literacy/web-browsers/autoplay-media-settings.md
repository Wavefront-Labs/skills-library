# Autoplay and sound settings (Chrome)

## Default sound permission
More > Settings > Privacy and security > Site settings > under "Content" Additional content settings > **Sound**, then choose the default for all sites.

## One site only
Select the icon next to the web address to open that site's saved settings; change its permissions there without changing your defaults. The Site settings page also lists per-site permissions.

## Control playing media
- Media control (top right of Chrome) pauses or skips media playing in any tab and returns you to its tab.
- Picture-in-picture: Media control > Enter picture-in-picture. Automatic picture-in-picture (only where the website enables it) is managed at Settings > Privacy and security > Site settings > Additional permission settings > Automatic picture-in-picture; it is off in Incognito by default.

## Also
Many sites have their own autoplay switch in their settings; turn it off there.

## Verify
- [ ] Open a news site with video and confirm the behaviour matches the Sound setting you chose.
