# Vertical tabs

## Turn on
- **Edge**: tab actions menu (top-left icon) > **Turn on vertical tabs**, or Ctrl+Shift+, . Settings > Appearance > *Show vertical tabs*.
- **Firefox** (version 136+): Settings > General > Browser layout > **Vertical tabs**; or right-click a tab > *Turn on vertical tabs*. The sidebar also gives history, bookmarks and synced tabs.
- **Chrome**: right-click the tab strip > *Show tabs to the side* where rolled out (2025+); otherwise use an extension.
- **Vivaldi, Arc, Brave**: built in (Vivaldi Settings > Tabs > *Tab bar position: Left/Right*).
- **Safari**: no vertical tabs; the tab overview (Shift+Cmd+\) and the sidebar Tab Groups list are the alternative.

## Use them
- Collapse to icons only (Edge pin/collapse button, Firefox *Expand/collapse*) to save space; hover to peek.
- Pinned tabs sit at the top; tab groups appear as collapsible headings.
- Drag tabs to reorder; Shift-click to select a range and move or close them together.

## Why bother
- Wide monitors have spare horizontal space and too little vertical; titles stay readable with 30+ tabs.

## Verify
- [ ] Tabs show down the side with readable titles and the browser remembers the setting after restart.
