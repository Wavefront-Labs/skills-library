# Sign out of the browser and remove synced data

## Why
Leaving a shared or work computer, selling a device, or separating accounts.

## Chrome (computer)
- Profile icon > **Sign out of Chrome**. When you sign out, your info stays safe in the Google Account and is removed from the device. If you signed out of a Google web service such as Gmail or the account is in an error state, select **Remove account from Chrome**.
- Delete saved info from the Google Account: go to chrome.google.com/data > **Delete data** (Chrome's instruction for deleting saved info from the account).
- Sign-in prompts: Chrome notes it is optional to sign in to Chrome when you sign in to a Google service, and you can change the preference at any time. On Android: More > Settings > Google services > turn off **Allow Chrome sign-in**.
- To remove everything a profile holds from a computer, remove the profile (profile icon > Manage Chrome profiles > More > Delete; this erases its bookmarks, history, passwords and settings from the computer).

## Edge (computer)
- Settings > Profiles > **Sign out** stops syncing.
- To delete sync data first: Clear browsing data. Microsoft warns that deleting data while signed in and syncing removes it from all synced devices.
- Phone: select your profile image > Sign out and turn off sync.

## Verify
- [ ] On the old computer the browser shows no signed-in account; your other devices still have your data.
