# Organising bookmark folders and the bar

**Goal:** the five sites you use daily one click away, everything else findable.

## Show the bar and open the manager
- Chrome: Ctrl+Shift+B shows or hides the bookmarks bar (also More > Bookmarks and lists > Show bookmarks bar); Ctrl+Shift+O opens the Bookmark Manager (Mac: Cmd+Shift+B and Cmd+Option+B).
- Edge: Ctrl+Shift+B shows or hides the favorites bar; Ctrl+Shift+O opens Favorites (Mac: Command+Shift+B).
- Safari: Control-Command-1 shows or hides the Bookmarks sidebar; Bookmarks > Edit Bookmarks also manages them.

## Organise
- **Chrome folders:** Bookmark Manager > More > **Add new folder**, or right-click the bookmarks bar > **Add Folder**. Try short folders like *Daily*, *Work*, *Money*, *Reference*.
- **Move:** drag a bookmark into a folder (or, in the Bookmarks side panel, select it > More > **Edit** and choose the folder > Save).
- **Rename/edit:** Bookmark Manager > More > **Edit**. Use short names ("Bank") rather than long page titles.
- **Sort (Chrome):** Bookmark Manager > More > **Sort by name**; the side panel's Organize menu also offers newest, oldest, last opened, A to Z and Z to A.
- **Safari:** Control-click a bookmark or folder in the sidebar to rename or edit it, change its address, copy or delete it, create a folder, or move it to another folder; Show Folders on Top is in the sidebar's options menu.

## Find
- Chrome: type `@bookmarks` then Tab or Space in the address bar and enter keywords, or choose Search bookmarks in the suggestions.
- Safari: type the name in the search field at the top of the Bookmarks sidebar.

## Gotchas
- Chrome's help says a deleted bookmark cannot be recovered, so export a backup first (see `computing-software.general-digital-literacy.web-browsers.importing-and-exporting-bookmarks`).
- Bookmark the real login page of banks and use that, rather than search results.
