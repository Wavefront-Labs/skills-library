# Open PDFs in the browser or download them

**Goal:** PDFs open the way that suits the job: quick reading in the browser, or a separate PDF app.

## Computer (Chrome)
- **Change the behaviour:** More > Settings > Privacy and security > Site settings > Additional content settings > **PDF documents** > Download PDFs or Open PDFs in Chrome.
- **Save one PDF without opening it:** right-click the link > Save link as.
- **Scanned PDFs:** in Chrome's PDF viewer, scanned PDFs are automatically converted with OCR to be searchable and selectable. Chrome shows "Extracting text from PDF..." and a multi-page scan may take time.

## Android (Chrome)
Tap a PDF link to open it without a separate reader (needs Android 12 or later for these features). You cannot open a PDF in Chrome while in Incognito mode; you are prompted to download it. More > Open with... opens it in another viewer. Find in page, zoom and Download are in the menu.
