# Send tabs to other devices

**Goal:** open the page you are reading on your phone or another computer.

## Requirements
Signed in to the same browser account on both devices with sync on (Chrome: Google account; Firefox: Firefox account; Safari: same Apple ID with Safari in iCloud). The receiving device must be online, running, and the browser open or notifications allowed.

## How
- Chrome: right-click the page > **Send to your devices** (or the share icon in the address bar), pick the device. A notification arrives on the receiver.
- Firefox: right-click a tab > **Send Tab to Device**, or the page actions menu.
- Safari: use the Share button, or open the Tabs overview > iCloud Tabs on the other device.
- Alternative: email/message the link to yourself.

## Not working?
1. Confirm both devices show up in the account's device list (Chrome: Settings > You and Google > Sync; Firefox: Account menu).
2. Turn sync on, with open tabs included, on both; update both browsers.
3. Sign out and back in on the missing device; check time/date are correct.
4. Check notification permission for the browser on the receiver.
5. Work profiles managed by an organisation may block it.

## Verify
The tab appears on the other device within seconds of sending.
