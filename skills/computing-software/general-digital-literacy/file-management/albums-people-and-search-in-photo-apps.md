# Albums, people and search in photo apps

## Search by content
- **Google Photos**: search "beach", "receipt", "dog 2019", a place, or text in a photo; *Ask Photos* (AI) answers natural questions. Searches run on your library server-side.
- **Apple Photos**: Search tab for objects, places, dates, people, text (Live Text) and natural phrases ("Maya skateboarding in Paris" in iOS 18).
- **Windows Photos**: search by people, places and objects when the library is in OneDrive (cloud-based recognition).

## People and pets
- Google: **Collections > People & pets**; name a face to search by name; merge duplicates (*Same or different?*). Turn face grouping on/off in Settings.
- Apple: **People** album/collection; tap *Add name*; review *suggestions* for more photos of the same person.

## Albums
- Manual albums for events; **smart/live albums** fill themselves (Apple *Smart Album* by rules on Mac; Google *Live album* of chosen people/pets).
- Favourite (heart) the best shots; filter later.
- An album is a view: removing a photo from an album keeps it in the library; deleting it from the library removes it everywhere.

## Places
- Map view uses GPS metadata; photos without location won't appear (add it in Info).

## Privacy
- Face recognition is processed differently per service; turn off where regionally sensitive. Shared albums reveal names you added only to you.
