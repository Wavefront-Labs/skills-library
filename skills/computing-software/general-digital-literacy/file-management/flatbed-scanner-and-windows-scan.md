# Flatbed scanner and Windows Scan

## Windows
- Install the printer/scanner (Settings > Bluetooth & devices > *Printers & scanners*).
- **Windows Scan** app (Microsoft Store): choose the scanner, *Source* (Flatbed or Feeder), *File type* (PDF, JPEG, PNG, TIFF), *Show more* for colour mode and resolution, *Preview*, then **Scan**. Saves to Pictures > Scans.
- Alternatives: the maker's software (HP Smart, Epson ScanSmart, Canon IJ Scan Utility), often with multipage PDF and OCR.

## Mac
- **Image Capture** (or Printers & Scanners > Open Scanner): select scanner, *Show Details* for kind (Text/Black & White, Colour), resolution, format (PDF combines pages with *Combine into single document*), destination folder.
- Preview: **File > Import from Scanner**.

## Settings that matter
- Documents: **300 dpi**, greyscale or black-and-white, PDF. Photos: 600 dpi colour, TIFF or JPEG.
- **Feeder (ADF)** for many pages; duplex if supported. Flatbed for books, fragile or odd-sized items.
- Multi-page PDF on flatbed: use the "scan another page"/*Add* button before saving.

## Troubleshooting
- Scanner not found: same Wi-Fi network, restart printer, reinstall the maker's full driver (not just the basic printer driver), or connect by USB.
- Dark lines: clean the glass and the ADF strip.

## Verify
- [ ] Scanned PDF opens with all pages in order and text readable at 100%.
