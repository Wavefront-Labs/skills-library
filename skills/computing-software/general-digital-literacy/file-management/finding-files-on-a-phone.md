# Find files on a phone

## iPhone and iPad: Files app
- **Browse** tab: *Locations* lists **On My iPhone**, **iCloud Drive**, and connected providers (Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox once their apps are installed and enabled via *...> Edit*).
- **Recents** shows files opened recently; the search bar searches names and content of all locations.
- Safari downloads go to **iCloud Drive > Downloads** by default (Settings > Apps > Safari > *Downloads* to change).
- Mail attachments: open > share button > **Save to Files**.
- Photos and videos are in the Photos app, not Files, unless you *Save to Files*.

## Android
- **Files by Google** (most phones) or **My Files** (Samsung): categories *Downloads*, *Images*, *Videos*, *Documents*, *Apps*; search bar at top.
- Browser and app downloads usually land in **Internal storage > Download**.
- WhatsApp media: *Internal storage > Android > media > com.whatsapp > WhatsApp > Media* (hidden from some file apps; use WhatsApp's own *Storage* screen).
- Cloud files: open the Drive/OneDrive app, or add it via the file manager's *Cloud* section on Samsung.

## Tips
- Sort by **date modified** to find what you just saved.
- Star/favourite folders for quick access (Files: drag a folder to *Favorites*).
- Can't see a file another app saved: some apps keep files private in their own storage; use that app's export/share.
