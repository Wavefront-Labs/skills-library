# Print photos at home or online

## Resolution for size
- Aim for **300 pixels per inch** (200 acceptable). 6x4 in needs about 1800x1200 px; 10x8 in about 3000x2400 px. Phone photos (12 MP, 4000x3000) are fine up to A4 / 10x8.
- Images from messaging apps and social media are downsized; use the original.

## Home printing
- Windows: right-click > **Print** (Print Pictures wizard) or Photos app > Print: choose paper size, **paper type** (glossy photo), quality *Best/Photo*, and *Fit picture to frame* (crops) vs fit (borders).
- Mac: Photos > File > **Print**: size, layout, paper; *Printer Options* to match paper type.
- Use the printer maker's photo paper and set the matching paper type; the wrong setting gives smeared or dull prints.
- Aspect ratio: phone photos are 4:3, 6x4 prints are 3:2, so edges get cropped; crop yourself first.

## Order online
- **Google Photos**: *Print store* (photo books, prints, canvas; varies by country). **Apple** stopped its print service; use extensions like Motif or retailers.
- High-street/online labs (Snapfish, Boots Photo, Walgreens, Costco, Photobox): upload, pick sizes, collect in store or deliver.
- Turn off "auto-enhance" if colours must match your edit.

## Colour accuracy
- Screens are brighter than paper; prints usually look darker. Lower screen brightness when editing for print, or order a test print.

## Verify
- [ ] Test print at the final size shows no pixelation or unexpected cropping.
