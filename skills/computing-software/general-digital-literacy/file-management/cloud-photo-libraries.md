# Cloud photo libraries

**Goal:** every photo is safe off the phone, searchable, and you understand what deleting does.

## Google Photos
- App › profile picture › **Photos settings › Backup** › on; choose **Storage saver** (slightly compressed) or **Original quality**; both count against your Google storage.
- Backup works on iPhone and Android; deleting a backed-up photo in Google Photos deletes it from the cloud and the phone gallery (Android). **Free up space** removes only device copies.
- Search by things, places and faces; share albums and partner sharing.
- Download everything: **takeout.google.com** › Google Photos.

## iCloud Photos
- iPhone Settings › Apple Account › iCloud › **Photos › Sync this iPhone** (on). **Optimise iPhone Storage** keeps small versions on the phone.
- It's **sync**: deleting on one device deletes on all (after 30 days in *Recently Deleted*).
- Access on Windows with the iCloud for Windows app or at icloud.com/photos.
- Export originals: Mac Photos › File › Export › *Export Unmodified Original*, or privacy.apple.com data copy.

## OneDrive camera upload
OneDrive app › Photos › **Camera backup** on; it's a one-way upload, deleting on the phone doesn't delete the cloud copy.

## Habits
- Pick one main service to avoid triple storage use.
- Keep a second copy (external drive or another cloud) once a year: sync services mirror deletions.
- Turn on backup over mobile data only if you have a big data plan.

## Gotchas
- Backup pauses on low battery, Low Power Mode or without Wi-Fi; check the status line in the app.
- Shared albums are often lower resolution than originals.

## Verify
- [ ] Take a test photo; it appears on the web version within minutes; last backup time is recent.
