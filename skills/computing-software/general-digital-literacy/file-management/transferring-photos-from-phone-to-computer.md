# Transferring photos from phone to computer

**Goal:** get full-quality photos and videos onto a computer reliably.

## Windows
1. Connect the phone by USB cable and unlock it. iPhone: tap **Trust**. Android: pull down the
   notification › USB › **File transfer / Android Auto**.
2. Open the **Photos** app › **Import** › From a connected device › choose items and destination
   folder. Or File Explorer › This PC › the phone › `DCIM` folder and copy.
3. iPhone HEIC files: iPhone Settings › Apps › Photos › Transfer to Mac or PC › **Automatic** converts
   to JPG on the fly; *Keep Originals* keeps HEIC (install HEIF/HEVC extensions to view them).

## Mac
- iPhone: **Photos** app › select the phone in the sidebar › Import. Or **Image Capture** to copy
  files to any folder without the Photos library.
- Android: use a USB-C card reader, a cloud service, or Android's *Quick Share* to Mac (third-party
  apps); macOS doesn't mount Android phones natively.
- AirDrop for a handful of photos.

## Cloud (no cable)
Google Photos or iCloud Photos on the phone, then download at photos.google.com / iCloud for Windows.
Download "originals" (not reduced versions).

## Gotchas
- Large imports over USB fail if the phone locks; keep it unlocked/awake.
- Don't tick "delete after import" until you've checked the copies and backed them up.
- Bluetooth transfers recompress or are very slow; use cable or cloud.

- [ ] Photo count on the computer matches; a sample video plays; dates are correct.
