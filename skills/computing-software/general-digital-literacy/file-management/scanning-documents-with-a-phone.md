# Scanning documents with a phone

**Goal:** a flat, readable, multi-page PDF of a paper document, without a scanner.

## Apps
- **iPhone:** Notes › new note › camera icon › **Scan Documents** (or Files app › three dots ›
  Scan Documents). Auto-captures when the page is steady; drag corners to adjust; Save. Share › PDF.
- **Android:** **Google Drive** › + › **Scan** (or Google Camera/Lens suggests "Scan document").
  Add pages with +, choose filter, Save as PDF to Drive.
- **Microsoft Lens** (Document mode) and the OneDrive app's Scan button save to OneDrive/Word/PDF.

## Good scans
- Dark, plain background contrasting with the paper; even light, no shadow of your phone
  (hold it directly above, or turn off the flash and use daylight).
- Fill the frame; let the app straighten and crop.
- Choose *Black & white* or *Greyscale* for text (smaller files), *Colour* for forms with stamps/photos.
- Name files clearly: `2026-10-16 Council tax bill.pdf`.

## Gotchas
- A scan for official submissions usually needs every page, including blank backs with text; check
  requirements (max size, PDF vs JPG).
- Files of 10+ pages in colour can exceed upload limits; rescan in greyscale or compress.
- Sensitive documents (ID, bank) live in your cloud account; delete from the phone/cloud when done.

## Verify
- [ ] Open the PDF on a computer: all pages present, straight and legible at 100% zoom.
