# Shared with me and adding shortcuts

## Find shared items
- **Google Drive**: left menu **Shared with me**; filter by person, type or date; search `to:me` or `owner:name@example.com`.
- **OneDrive**: **Shared** (or *Shared with you* in the Microsoft 365 app); *People* view groups by who shared.
- **Dropbox**: **Shared** page lists folders and files; shared folders must be added to your Dropbox to sync.

## Keep them handy
- **Google Drive**: right-click > **Organise > Add shortcut** to put a link in My Drive; it then shows in Drive for desktop. The file still belongs to the owner.
- **OneDrive / SharePoint**: on a shared folder, **Add shortcut to My files**; it syncs via the OneDrive app (needs edit access for some libraries).
- **Dropbox**: open the invite and click **Add**/*Add to Dropbox*.

## Ownership facts
- The **owner's storage quota** is used, not yours.
- If the owner deletes it or leaves the organisation, your shortcut breaks; copy anything you must keep (*Make a copy* / *Copy to*).
- Removing a shared item from your view does not delete it for others.
- Edits you make in a shared file change the owner's file for everyone.

## Gotchas
- Old Drive "Add to My Drive" items became shortcuts in 2022; duplicates in two places are the same file.
- A link that says "You need access": click *Request access*, or the owner shared it with your other account (check which account you're signed into).

## Verify
- [ ] Shared folder appears in your Drive/OneDrive sidebar and syncs to the desktop app.
