# Warranty repair and support options

**Goal:** the device gets fixed at the lowest cost, with your data safe.

## 1. Find the facts
- **Serial number / service tag:** on the label underneath or in settings (Windows: `wmic bios get serialnumber` or Settings › System › About; Mac: Apple menu › About This Mac; phones: Settings › About).
- **Coverage check:** Apple checkcoverage.apple.com; Dell/HP/Lenovo support sites take the serial; Samsung Members app.
- Proof of purchase (receipt, order email) and the date.

## 2. Which route
| Situation | Route |
|---|---|
| Fault within the maker's warranty (often 1 year) | Manufacturer support: online chat, mail-in, authorised service centre |
| Fault after warranty but goods should have lasted (UK Consumer Rights Act up to 6 years; EU 2-year legal guarantee) | Claim against the **retailer** who sold it, not the maker |
| Accidental damage, liquid | Extended cover (AppleCare+, retailer plan) or home/gadget insurance; otherwise paid repair |
| Old device, cheap fix (battery, screen) | Authorised or reputable independent repair shop; get a quote first |
| Credit card purchase | Some cards extend warranty or give claims (UK Section 75 over £100) |

## 3. Before handing it over
- Back up and test the backup.
- Note or remove the passcode as asked; sign out of Find My/Find My Device only if the repairer requires it.
- Remove SIM, memory cards, cases; photograph the device's condition.
- For laptops, ask whether the drive will be replaced or wiped.

## 4. During
- Get a case/ticket number; keep chat transcripts.
- Repair is usually free under warranty; unauthorised opening or third-party parts can void it (check local right-to-repair rules).

## Gotchas
- Scam "support" numbers in search ads: use only numbers from the maker's own site.
- Repair vs replace: if the quote is more than half the price of an equivalent new device, compare options.

## Verify
- [ ] Device returned working, data restored, receipt for repair kept (repairs usually carry their own 90-day warranty).
