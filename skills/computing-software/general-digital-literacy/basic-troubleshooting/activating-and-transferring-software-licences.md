# Activate and transfer software licences

## Windows
- Windows can be activated with a product key or a **digital licence**. Microsoft advises linking your Microsoft account to
  the digital licence so you can reactivate with the **Activation troubleshooter** after changing hardware.
- Check activation status in Settings, System, Activation (path may vary by Windows version).
- Microsoft's activation page describes the licence types (for example a copy bought from an authorised retailer comes with a
  product key on a label in the box). Read it for rules on moving a licence between PCs: licence terms differ between
  PC-maker (OEM) and retail licences, so check the terms you bought under before transferring.

## Office and subscriptions
Sign in with the Microsoft account the product was bought under, and manage installs from the account's services page.
Device limits differ by plan: read your plan's current terms. For Adobe and similar per-seat apps, sign out or deactivate
on the old computer before retiring it (check the vendor's help for the current limit).

## Other software
- Keep licence emails and keys in your password manager with the purchase date and store.
- Before retiring a PC: deactivate per-seat apps so you don't run out of activations.
- App store purchases (Microsoft Store, Mac App Store, Steam) are tied to the account: sign in on the new machine.

## Fake keys
Very cheap keys from marketplace sites are often resold volume or stolen keys that can be revoked. Buy from the maker or
authorised retailers.

## Verify
- [ ] Activation shows "active" and the old device no longer counts against your limit.
