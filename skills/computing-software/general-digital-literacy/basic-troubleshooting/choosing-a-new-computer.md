# Choose a new computer

## Start with the tasks
- Web, email, documents, video calls: any current mid-range machine or a Chromebook.
- Photo/video editing, programming, gaming, large spreadsheets: more RAM, faster CPU, maybe a dedicated GPU.
- Apps you must run (work software, a game, a printer driver) decide the platform first.

## Platform
- **Windows**: widest software and price range. Needs a supported CPU for Windows 11.
- **Mac**: long support (typically 6-8 years of macOS updates), excellent battery on Apple silicon; fixed RAM/storage, pay for upgrades at purchase.
- **Chromebook**: cheap, simple, secure; web/Android apps only. Check the **Auto Update Expiration** (AUE) date (now up to 10 years from release).

## Specs that matter (2026)
- **RAM**: 8 GB minimum, **16 GB** recommended for Windows and heavy browser use; 32 GB for video/dev.
- **Storage**: SSD only, **512 GB** if you keep files locally; 256 GB with cloud storage.
- **CPU**: recent Intel Core Ultra 5/7, AMD Ryzen 5/7, Snapdragon X, or Apple M-series. Check app compatibility for ARM Windows.
- **Screen**: 1920x1080 minimum; IPS/OLED; 300+ nits for bright rooms.
- **Ports**: USB-C with charging, at least one USB-A, HDMI if you present; headphone jack.
- **Battery**: read independent tests, not the maker's claim.

## Value
- Buy last year's model or certified refurbished with warranty; avoid lowest-end eMMC storage and 4 GB RAM.
- Repairability and warranty length matter; check local consumer rights (UK 30-day short-term right to reject).

## Verify
- [ ] Every must-have app runs on the platform, and RAM/storage meet the minimums above.
