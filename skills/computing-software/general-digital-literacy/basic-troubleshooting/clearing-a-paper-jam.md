# Paper jams

## Clear it
1. **Cancel the job** and turn the printer off; on lasers wait 10 minutes (the fuser is hot).
2. Open every door the manual shows (front, rear access panel, duplexer, input tray). Many printers show an animation on the screen of where to look.
3. Pull paper **slowly with both hands in the direction it travels**. Tearing leaves scraps that cause the next jam; if it tears, find every piece (torch helps).
4. Check under the cartridge carriage (inkjets) and in the rear duplex unit.
5. Close all doors firmly (a door sensor not closed shows as a jam), turn on.

## "Paper jam" with no paper visible
- A small scrap or a label stuck deep inside; a foreign object (paper clip, pen cap).
- Carriage can't move freely (inkjet): check for packing tape on new printers.
- Sensor dirty: power off, gently clean the visible paper path; restart. Still showing → service.

## Prevent
- Fan the stack, keep paper flat and dry, don't overfill (below the max line), adjust guides to touch the stack without bending it.
- Use the paper weight the tray supports; heavy card/envelopes go through the rear/manual feed.
- Don't mix paper types in one tray; no crumpled or previously printed paper in duplex jobs.
- Frequent misfeeds/multiple sheets: clean the **pickup rollers** with a damp lint-free cloth; replace worn rollers (kits are cheap).

## Verify
- [ ] Error clears; a 10-page test (and a duplex page) prints without jamming.
