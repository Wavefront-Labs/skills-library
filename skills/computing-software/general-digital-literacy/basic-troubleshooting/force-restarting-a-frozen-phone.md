# Force restart a frozen phone

A force restart does not erase anything, but anything unsaved in an open app is lost. Try it only when the screen does not respond.

## iPhone (recent models with volume buttons and a side button)
Per Apple's iPhone User Guide: press and quickly release **Volume Up**, press and quickly release **Volume Down**, then press and
**hold the Side button**. Release it when the Apple logo appears. Older iPhone models and iPads use different button
combinations: use the instructions for your exact model on Apple Support rather than guessing.

## Android
Button combinations differ by manufacturer and model. Search your manufacturer's support page for "force restart" plus the
model name. Holding the power button for a long time is the usual first attempt. If the screen is black, charge the phone for
a while before concluding it is dead.

## Still stuck
- **Stuck on the logo or restarting in a loop:** do NOT choose Erase, Factory reset or Restore unless the manufacturer's
  steps say so and you have accepted losing data not backed up. On iPhone, Apple's recovery mode offers Update (which
  aims to keep data) or Restore (which erases): choose Update first, following Apple's steps for your model.
- **Freezes repeatedly:** update the operating system, free some storage, remove recently installed apps, and have the battery checked.
- Back up first whenever the phone is responsive enough to do so.

## Verify
- [ ] Phone restarts to the lock screen; apps and data are intact.
