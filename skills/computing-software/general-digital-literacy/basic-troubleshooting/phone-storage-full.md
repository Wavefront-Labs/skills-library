# Phone storage full

**Goal:** room for updates, photos and apps without losing anything important.

## See what's using it
- **iPhone:** Settings › General › **iPhone Storage** (bar chart and recommendations).
- **Android:** Settings › Storage, or the **Files by Google** app › Clean.

## Biggest wins
1. **Photos and videos:** turn on iCloud Photos with *Optimise iPhone Storage*, or Google Photos
   backup then *Free up space*. Confirm the backup finished first. Delete duplicates, screenshots,
   bursts; empty **Recently Deleted** (photos stay there 30 days, still using space).
2. **Offload unused apps:** iPhone Storage › app › *Offload App* (keeps data) or enable
   *Offload Unused Apps*. Android: Files by Google › Delete unused apps.
3. **Chat media:** WhatsApp › Settings › Storage and data › **Manage storage** › largest files and
   forwarded media. Turn off auto-download of media for groups.
4. **Downloaded music, podcasts, Netflix/Spotify offline content.**
5. **App caches** (Android: Settings › Apps › app › Storage › Clear cache; never Clear storage unless
   you accept losing that app's data).
6. Messages: keep messages for 1 year instead of forever (iPhone Settings › Apps › Messages).

## Gotchas
- iPhone "System Data" shrinks after a restart and over time; you can't delete it directly.
- Don't delete photos from the phone before they're safely in the cloud *and* a second copy.

## Verify
- [ ] At least 5-10 GB free (enough for an OS update).
