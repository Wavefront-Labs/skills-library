# Wiping a device before selling or recycling

**Goal:** the next owner gets a clean device and none of your data or accounts.

## 1. Back up and transfer
Check the backup or move data to the new device first (photos, chats, authenticator apps!).

## 2. Sign out and unlink
- **iPhone/iPad:** unpair Apple Watch; Settings › [name] › **Sign Out** (turns off Find My and
  Activation Lock). Remove cards from Wallet.
- **Android:** remove your Google account (Settings › Passwords & accounts) to avoid Factory Reset
  Protection locking the buyer out; Samsung account too.
- **Mac:** sign out of iCloud and Messages; deauthorise Music. Apple silicon/T2: System Settings ›
  General › Transfer or Reset › **Erase All Content and Settings** does it all.
- **Windows:** sign out of Microsoft account apps; remove the device from account.microsoft.com/devices.

## 3. Wipe
- iPhone: Settings › General › Transfer or Reset iPhone › **Erase All Content and Settings**.
- Android: Settings › System › Reset options › **Erase all data (factory reset)**.
- Windows 11: Settings › System › Recovery › **Reset this PC** › *Remove everything* › and choose
  **Clean data** (Yes) for drives leaving your hands.
- Encrypted devices (all modern phones, BitLocker/FileVault drives) are effectively unrecoverable after
  reset because the key is destroyed.

## 4. Physical
Remove the SIM and memory card. Old hard drive that won't boot? Remove it and physically destroy
or use a certified recycler.

## Verify
- [ ] Device boots to the "Hello"/setup screen and asks for no account of yours.
