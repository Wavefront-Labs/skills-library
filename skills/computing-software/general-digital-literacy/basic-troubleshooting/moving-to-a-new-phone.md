# Move to a new phone

## Before you start (on the old phone)
- Update the OS; make a fresh backup (iCloud / Google One).
- **Authenticator apps**: Google Authenticator › Transfer accounts (QR); Microsoft Authenticator cloud backup; Authy syncs. Don't wipe the old phone until every 2FA code works on the new one.
- **WhatsApp**: Settings › Chats › Chat backup (or use the built-in transfer during setup).
- Note bank apps that need re-registration; some tie to one device.

## Same platform
- **iPhone → iPhone: Quick Start**: place the phones together, follow prompts, choose *Transfer from iPhone* (direct, includes apps and data). Keep both on charge.
- **Android → Android**: during setup choose *Copy apps & data* › cable or wireless from the old phone; Samsung **Smart Switch** for Galaxy.

## Switching platform
- **Android → iPhone**: install **Move to iOS** on Android; on the new iPhone at *Apps & Data* choose *From Android*, enter the code. Moves contacts, messages, photos, calendars, free apps, WhatsApp.
- **iPhone → Android**: Pixel/Samsung setup offers *From iPhone* with a cable or the **Switch to Android** app. **Turn off iMessage/FaceTime** (Settings › Messages) or deregister at selfsolve.apple.com/deregister-imessage, otherwise texts from iPhone users vanish.

## Phone number
- Move the physical SIM, or transfer the **eSIM** during setup (iPhone eSIM Quick Transfer; carriers' apps/QR codes).

## After
- Sign in to each app; check photos fully synced; then **erase the old phone** (sign out of Apple/Google accounts first).

## Verify
- [ ] Calls, texts, 2FA codes, banking and WhatsApp work on the new phone before the old one is wiped.
