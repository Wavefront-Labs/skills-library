# Find, lock or erase a lost phone

**Goal:** get the phone back, or at least keep your data and accounts safe.

## Set up beforehand
- iPhone: Settings › [your name] › Find My › **Find My iPhone** on, plus *Send Last Location*.
- Android: Settings › Google › **Find My Device / Find Hub** on; location on.
- A screen lock (passcode/PIN) is essential: without it remote lock means little.

## When it's missing
1. From another device or computer: **icloud.com/find** (iPhone) or **google.com/android/find**
   (Android). Sign in with the account on the phone.
2. **Play sound** if it's probably nearby.
3. **Lost Mode / Secure device:** locks it, shows a message and contact number on the screen,
   suspends Apple Pay/Wallet cards.
4. **Erase** only when you're sure it won't be recovered: it wipes data. iPhone remains activation
   locked to your Apple Account, so a thief can't reuse it.

## If stolen
- Don't try to recover it yourself; give the location to police and get a crime reference.
- Call your mobile provider to block the SIM (and the handset IMEI).
- Change passwords for email and banking; banking apps: call the bank to freeze cards in wallets.
- Stolen Device Protection (iPhone, Settings › Face ID & Passcode) and Android Theft Protection
  limit what a thief can change even with your passcode: turn them on now.

## Verify
- [ ] Your phone appears on the map in Find My / Find Hub from another device.
