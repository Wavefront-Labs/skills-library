# Google Account recovery

## Recover
1. Go to **g.co/recover** (accounts.google.com/signin/recovery) on a device and network you've used before (home Wi-Fi, your usual phone/laptop): this weighs heavily.
2. Enter the email or recovery phone. Follow prompts: code to recovery phone/email, *Tap Yes* on a signed-in phone, last password you remember.
3. Don't skip questions; a close guess beats "I don't know". Answer once carefully rather than many rapid attempts (repeated tries can lock recovery for a while).
4. If asked for a contact email, give one you can check now; Google may reply in a few days.
5. After getting in: change password, review **Security** › *Recent security activity*, *Your devices*, third-party access, Gmail forwarding/filters.

## When it fails
- Google support **cannot** manually restore personal accounts; keep trying from a familiar device over a few days.
- Workspace (work/school) accounts: the organisation's admin resets them.
- Child accounts: the parent in Family Link resets.

## Set up now
- myaccount.google.com › Security › **Recovery phone** and **Recovery email** current.
- Turn on 2-Step Verification; save **backup codes**; add a **passkey**.
- Inactive Account Manager for long absences.

## Scams
- "Google account recovery service" phone numbers and paid recovery helpers are scams. Google never calls you to recover your account.

## Verify
- [ ] Security Checkup at g.co/securitycheckup shows recovery info confirmed and no issues.
