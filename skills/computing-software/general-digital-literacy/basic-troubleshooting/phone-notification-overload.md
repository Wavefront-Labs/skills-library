# Phone notification overload

## Audit per app
- **iPhone**: Settings > Notifications > [app]: *Allow Notifications*, Lock Screen/Notification Centre/Banners, sounds, badges, and *Time Sensitive*. Long-press a notification > **Turn off** or *Mute for 1 hour/today*.
- **Android**: long-press a notification > *Turn off notifications* or choose which **categories** (channels) stay on: Settings > Notifications > App notifications > [app]. Mark low-value ones *Silent*.
- Rule of thumb: alerts only for people and things that need action now (calls, messages from humans, calendar, banking security). Everything else silent or off.

## Batch them
- iPhone **Scheduled Summary** (Settings > Notifications): non-urgent apps delivered at chosen times.
- Android **Notification history** and *Snooze*; Pixel *Adaptive notifications*, Samsung *Notification categories*.

## Focus and Do Not Disturb
- iPhone **Focus** (Settings > Focus): Work, Sleep, Personal with allowed people/apps, schedules and location triggers; *Share across devices*.
- Android **Do Not Disturb** / **Modes** (Android 15): exceptions for starred contacts and repeat callers; *Bedtime mode*.

## Chats and websites
- Mute busy group chats (WhatsApp/Messages > group > *Mute*), keep mentions on.
- Browser site notifications: Chrome Android > Settings > Notifications > Sites; iPhone Settings > Notifications for web apps added to Home Screen.
- Email: notify only for VIPs (Mail VIP, Gmail *High priority only*).

## Verify
- [ ] A day later, every notification received was one you wanted to see.
