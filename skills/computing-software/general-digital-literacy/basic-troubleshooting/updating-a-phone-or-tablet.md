# Update a phone or tablet

**Goal:** the device runs a supported system version with the latest security patches.

## iPhone and iPad
1. Back up (iCloud or computer).
2. Settings › General › **Software Update** › *Update Now* (or *Upgrade to iOS XX* for a major version).
3. **Automatic Updates** › turn on *Download iOS Updates*, *Install iOS Updates*, and **Security Responses & System Files** (rapid fixes).
4. Needs Wi-Fi, a few GB free, and 50%+ battery or a charger.
5. Not enough space: Settings › General › iPhone Storage › offload apps, or update from a computer (Finder or Apple Devices app on Windows).

## Android
1. Settings › **System › Software update** (Samsung: Settings › *Software update › Download and install*).
2. **Google Play system update**: Settings › Security & privacy › System & updates › *Google Play system update* (separate monthly component).
3. Also update apps in the Play Store.
4. Updates arrive in waves; a friend with the same phone may get it days earlier.

## How long is support?
- Apple supports iPhones for roughly 6-7 years of iOS updates.
- Google Pixel 8 and later and Samsung Galaxy S24 and later: 7 years. Many cheaper Android phones get 2-4 years.
- When security updates stop, banking and messaging apps eventually drop support; plan a replacement.

## Gotchas
- Don't interrupt an update (no restart, keep charging); a stuck Apple logo for over an hour needs recovery mode via a computer.
- Beta programs give unfinished software; leave them on main devices.

## Verify
- [ ] Settings shows "up to date" and a security patch level within the last 2-3 months (Android: Settings › About phone › Android version).
