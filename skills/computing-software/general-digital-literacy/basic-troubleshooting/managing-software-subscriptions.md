# Manage software subscriptions

## Find them
- **iPhone/Mac**: Settings > [your name] > **Subscriptions** (or App Store > account > Subscriptions).
- **Android**: Play Store > profile > **Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions**.
- **Microsoft**: account.microsoft.com > Services & subscriptions.
- **Direct billing** (Adobe, Netflix, antivirus, cloud storage): the provider's website account page.
- Bank and card statements are the ground truth; search for recurring merchants monthly. Some banking apps list subscriptions.

## Cancel
- Cancel where you **subscribed**: an app-store subscription can't be cancelled by deleting the app or on the provider's website.
- You usually keep access until the end of the paid period.
- Annual plans: note the renewal date in your calendar a week before.
- Hard-to-cancel services: use the provider's online cancellation (US FTC and UK DMCC rules require easy cancellation as rules take effect); if blocked, stop the payment with your bank and keep evidence.

## Free trials
- Set a reminder the day you start; cancel immediately after starting if you only want the trial (access usually continues until the end).

## Avoid paying twice
- Bundles overlap: Microsoft 365 includes 1 TB OneDrive; Apple One includes iCloud+; Amazon Prime includes Prime Video; phone plans may include streaming.
- Antivirus renewals at full price: Windows Security is free and adequate for most home users.

## Verify
- [ ] Every recurring charge on last month's statement matches a subscription you want.
