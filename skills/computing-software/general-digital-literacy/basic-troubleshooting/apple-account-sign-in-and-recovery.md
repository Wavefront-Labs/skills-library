# Apple Account sign-in and recovery

## Forgot the password
- Fastest: on a **trusted device** already signed in (your iPhone): Settings › your name › Sign-In & Security › **Change Password** (uses the device passcode).
- Another person's Apple device: **Apple Support app** › Reset Password › *Help someone else*.
- Web: **iforgot.apple.com** › enter Apple Account email/phone › confirm via a trusted phone number or device.

## Account Recovery (no trusted device, can't reset)
- iforgot.apple.com starts **Account Recovery**: Apple verifies you and there's a **waiting period of days or longer**. You get texts with updates; using a signed-in device during this may cancel it. Apple Support cannot speed it up.

## Set up now so recovery is easy
- Settings › your name › Sign-In & Security › **Account Recovery** › add a **Recovery Contact** (a trusted person's Apple device generates a code for you).
- Keep a current **trusted phone number**; add a second (partner's).
- **Recovery Key** (28 characters): strongest security, but if you lose it *and* trusted devices, Apple cannot help. Only for those who will store it carefully.
- **Legacy Contact** for after death (see digital legacy).

## Sign-in problems
- *Verification failed*: check date & time are automatic; try another network.
- Two-factor codes arrive on your other Apple devices; choose *Didn't get a code?* › text/call.
- Apple never asks for your password or codes by phone; unexpected sign-in codes → change password.

## Verify
- [ ] Sign-In & Security shows a current trusted number and a recovery contact.
