# Patient portals and telehealth visits

**Goal:** manage healthcare online and get the most from a remote appointment.

## Patient portals
- UK: **NHS App** (prescriptions, GP appointments, test results, records, NHS number). US: your
  provider's portal (often **MyChart**). Sign up with identity verification.
- Typical tasks: book/cancel appointments, order repeat prescriptions and choose a pharmacy, read
  test results and letters, message the practice (non-urgent), manage proxy access for a child or
  someone you care for.
- Use a strong unique password and app biometrics; health data is sensitive.

## Before a video visit
1. Test the link/app a day before: camera, mic, speakers, and allow permissions in the browser.
2. Charge the device; use Wi-Fi or good 4G/5G; close other apps.
3. **Private, quiet, well-lit** room (light in front of you). Headphones help privacy.
4. Have a list: symptoms and when they started, medications with doses, questions, pharmacy details;
   your phone number in case the video drops.
5. Join a few minutes early; you may wait in a virtual waiting room.

## During and after
- Be ready to show the problem to the camera (rash, swelling) with good light; take photos beforehand
  if the portal accepts uploads.
- Repeat back instructions; check the portal for the visit summary and prescriptions.

## Gotchas
- Emergencies: call emergency services (999/911/112), not the portal.
- Don't use public Wi-Fi or shared computers for health portals; sign out after.
