# Reduce the environmental impact of digital use

## Biggest lever: devices
- Most of a phone's or laptop's lifetime emissions come from **manufacturing**, not use. Keeping a phone 4-5 years instead of 2 roughly halves its yearly footprint.
- Repair before replacing: batteries and screens are cheap to swap; check iFixit repairability and manufacturers' self-repair programmes.
- Buy refurbished; sell, donate or recycle old devices (wipe first). Never bin batteries or electronics; use WEEE/e-waste collection points.
- E-waste is the fastest-growing waste stream (62 million tonnes in 2022, under a quarter documented as recycled).

## Use less energy
- Enable power saving and sleep; turn off monitors and set PCs to sleep after 15 minutes.
- Laptops use far less than desktops; ENERGY STAR-rated devices.
- Turn off "always on" standby on TVs and consoles (instant-on modes can use more than a light bulb all day).

## Streaming and online habits
- Streaming at lower resolution on small screens saves data and energy; download for repeated viewing rather than streaming again.
- Wi-Fi is more efficient than mobile data.
- Online impact per email or search is small; the larger effect is buying fewer devices. Cleaning up cloud storage matters mostly for cost and organisation.
- Generative AI uses more energy per query than search; use it where it adds value.
