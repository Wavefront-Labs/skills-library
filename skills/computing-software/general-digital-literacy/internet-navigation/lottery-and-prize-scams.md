# Lottery and prize scams

This page follows US FTC guidance; use your own country's regulator elsewhere.

## Tell-tale signs
- The FTC lists "prize or problem" as one of four signs of a scam: you are told you won a lottery or sweepstakes but must pay a fee to collect it.
- Pressure to act now, so you do not have time to think or check the story.
- Unusual payment methods: cryptocurrency, wire transfers, payment apps or gift cards.
- A message "from" a government agency or a business you know that you did not expect.

## Check
- Contact the organisation through a website or number you find yourself, not the message.
- Search the exact wording plus "scam".
- If you did not enter, you cannot have won.

## If you engaged
- Stop contact and do not pay more.
- Credit card or debit card: report it to the issuer or bank immediately and ask for a refund. Gift card, wire transfer or cryptocurrency: contact the issuer, wire company or exchange right away.
- Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.

## Verify
- [ ] Any "prize" message involving payment is ignored and reported.
