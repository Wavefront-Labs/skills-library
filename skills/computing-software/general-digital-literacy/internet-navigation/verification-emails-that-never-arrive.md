# Verification emails that never arrive

## Check, in order
1. **Wait a few minutes**, then press *Resend* once rather than repeatedly; if several emails arrive, use the newest one.
2. **Spam/Junk** folder; Gmail **Promotions** and **Updates** tabs (Updates holds automated confirmations and notifications); Outlook.com **Other** tab if Focused Inbox is on, plus Deleted Items and Archive. Microsoft says Junk Email is kept for 30 days. Search all mail for the company name.
3. **Typo** in the address you entered (`gmial.com`, missing letter). Many sites show it on the "check your email" page.
4. **Filters and rules** you set: Gmail Settings › See all settings › Filters and Blocked Addresses; Outlook.com rules (Inbox or Sweep rules can move mail). **Blocked sender** lists.
5. **Mailbox full**: a personal Google Account has up to 15 GB shared across Gmail, Drive and Photos; Microsoft says mail sent while your storage is full bounces back to the sender. Free up space.
6. If you used an alias or forwarding address, try your main address.
7. **Work/school email**: ask IT whether a company filter is holding the message.

## Codes by SMS instead
- Check the number you entered, including the country code, and your signal.

## If still nothing
- In Outlook.com, if a message was misfiled in Junk, select it and choose Report > Not junk. Add the sender to your contacts.
- Contact the site's support from its official Help page.

## Safety
- Never ask a stranger to forward you "your" code, and never give a code you didn't request to anyone: that's how accounts are hijacked.

- [ ] The email arrives in the inbox and the link/code works within its expiry time.
