# Generate images with AI

## Tools
- Chat assistants with image generation (ChatGPT, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot/Designer), **Adobe Firefly** (trained on licensed content, built into Photoshop/Express), **Midjourney** (Discord/web), **Canva** Magic Media.

## Prompting
- Describe: **subject**, action, setting, **style** (photo, watercolour, flat vector, 3D render), lighting, composition/camera (close-up, wide shot, top-down), mood, colour palette, and **aspect ratio** (16:9 banner, 1:1 post, 9:16 story).
- Example: "Flat vector illustration of a grandmother video-calling her grandson on a tablet, warm kitchen, pastel palette, simple shapes, 16:9."
- Iterate: change one thing at a time ("same image but evening light"). Use edit/inpaint to change a region.
- Text in images is improving but check spelling; add text afterwards in a design tool for reliability.

## Common flaws
- Hands, teeth, jewellery, reflections, background text, extra limbs, inconsistent characters across images. Zoom in before using.

## Responsible use
- Don't create images of real people in false or sexual situations (illegal in many places), fake news photos, or imitations of living artists' styles for commercial use.
- Many tools add **C2PA Content Credentials** or watermarks; don't strip them. Label AI images when posting (platforms require it for realistic content).
- Check licence terms for commercial use.
