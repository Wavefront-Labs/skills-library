# Sextortion and intimate image abuse

You are not to blame. This page covers US routes (NCMEC) and the StopNCII service; if you are in danger, contact your local emergency services, and use your own country's police and support services.

## If you are being threatened
1. **Preserve evidence**: screenshots of profiles, messages, usernames and payment requests; note URLs. Save before deleting any chat.
2. **Block and report** the account on the platform.
3. If someone is blackmailing you, create a CyberTipline report with NCMEC (cybertipline.org, or 1-800-THE-LOST), even if you have already submitted a hash.
4. Tell someone you trust; help is available and you are not alone.

## Getting images blocked
- **Take It Down** (NCMEC) is for nude images or videos of yourself taken when you were under 18. It creates a hash value (a digital fingerprint) on your device, and the image is not uploaded. Participating public or unencrypted platforms can use the hash to detect and remove matching content. Images from age 18 or older are directed to stopncii.org.
- **StopNCII.org** also creates a hash on your device; the image stays on your device. It works only on the participating platforms on its partners page.
- Limits: Take It Down will not prevent uploads on other sites, cannot guarantee removal, does not work on encrypted platforms, and cannot notify you of matches.

## Parents
- Stay calm and supportive; the priority is safety, not punishment. Help preserve evidence and report.

## Verify
- [ ] Evidence saved, accounts blocked and reported, a CyberTipline or police report made, and a hash submitted where eligible.
