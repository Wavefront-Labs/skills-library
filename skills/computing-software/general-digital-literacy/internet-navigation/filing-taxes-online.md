# File taxes online

## Use the official route
- **UK**: Self Assessment via gov.uk (sign in with Government Gateway or GOV.UK One Login); HMRC app for tax code and PAYE. Deadline 31 January for online returns.
- **US**: IRS **Free File** (income limits) via irs.gov, IRS Direct File where offered, or commercial software; state returns separately.
- **Canada**: NETFILE-certified software; **Australia**: myTax via myGov.
- Always reach these by typing the address or from the government's main site; ads in search results can be paid-for lookalike or fee-charging services.

## Before starting
- Gather: employer statements (P60 / W-2), other income (interest, self-employment records, 1099s), pension and charity contributions, previous year's return, your tax reference (UTR, SSN/ITIN).
- Set up the sign-in early; identity verification can take days.

## During
- Save as you go; many services time out after inactivity.
- Check pre-filled data; it's not always complete.
- Pay what's owed through the official payment options; never by gift card, crypto or wire to an individual.

## Scams
- Tax agencies don't text or email about refunds with links asking for card details, or threaten arrest by phone. Forward UK scams to phishing@hmrc.gov.uk; US to phishing@irs.gov.

## Afterwards
- Download the submission receipt and the return PDF; keep records (UK 5 years after the deadline for self-employed, US generally 3 years).

## Verify
- [ ] Submission reference saved and the account shows the return as received.
