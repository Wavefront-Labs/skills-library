# Smart speaker and voice assistant privacy

## How it works
- Devices listen for the wake word; Google notes assistants can activate by mistake on sounds like "Hey Google", and Amazon says audio is sent to the cloud when it detects the wake word. Alexa recordings and transcripts are associated with your Amazon account.

## Amazon Alexa
- Alexa app > More > **Alexa Privacy**: review and delete voice recordings, transcripts and typed requests; you can choose to have recordings deleted automatically after 3 or 18 months, or not save them (Amazon warns that features such as Voice ID then stop working). Also available at amazon.com/alexaprivacysettings.
- You can also manage whether your voice recordings are used to improve Amazon's services in the same place.

## Google Assistant / Gemini
- In your Google Account, *Voice and audio activity* is a Web & App Activity setting that saves audio recordings from Search, Assistant and Maps; you can turn it off and review or delete activity in My Activity. Gemini Apps Activity lets you review and delete activity, change the auto-delete period and control whether data is used to improve Google AI. Human reviewers may see some data.

## Siri
- Apple says Siri requests are linked to a device-generated random identifier, not your Apple Account. On iPhone: Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements > *Improve Siri & Dictation* (sharing choice); Settings > Siri > *Siri & Dictation History* to delete stored history (Apple's legal page says data may be kept up to two years).

## Practical controls
- **Echo microphone button**: Amazon says pressing it disconnects power to the microphones and lights a red indicator, so the device cannot record or stream audio; use it for private conversations. Check your own device's manual for its mute control.
- Before selling: factory reset and remove it from your account (check the maker's instructions).

- [ ] Voice history is empty or auto-deleting, and you know where each assistant's mute control is.
