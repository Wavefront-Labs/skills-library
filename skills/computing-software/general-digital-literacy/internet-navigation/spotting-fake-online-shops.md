# Fake online shops

Fake shops appear in social media ads and search results, often copying real brands, selling at huge "closing down" discounts, then deliver nothing or a cheap knock-off.

## Red flags
- Prices 50-90% below everywhere else on in-demand items (trainers, designer goods, tools, Dyson, PlayStation).
- **New domain** with a brand name plus extra words (`nike-outlet-sale.shop`), odd endings (`.top`, `.shop`, `.store` aren't bad alone).
- No real address or phone; only a contact form or WhatsApp; returns policy copied from another site with the wrong company name.
- Payment by **bank transfer, crypto or gift card** only, or the card payment "fails" and they push transfer.
- Poor grammar, stock photos, social media accounts with comments disabled.

## Checks (5 minutes)
1. **Get Safe Online Check a website** or **ScamAdviser**: trust score and domain age.
2. WHOIS lookup (who.is): domain registered weeks ago is a warning.
3. Search `shop name + scam` / `+ reviews` on Trustpilot and Reddit.
4. UK companies: check Companies House; EU: the imprint/Impressum details.
5. For brands: go to the brand's official site and use its store locator / authorised resellers list.

## Pay safely
- **Credit card** (UK Section 75 protection over £100) or PayPal Goods & Services; never Friends & Family.
- Paid and nothing arrived: card chargeback via your bank; report to Action Fraud / reportfraud.ftc.gov and the ad platform.

## Verify
- [ ] Domain is more than a year old or the brand lists it as official, and you paid by card or protected method.
