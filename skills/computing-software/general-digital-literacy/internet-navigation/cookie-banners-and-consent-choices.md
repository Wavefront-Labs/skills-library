# Cookie banners and consent choices

## What you are choosing
- The ICO says organisations must give clear information and, for any cookie that is not strictly necessary, an appropriate means of consenting before it is set on your device.
- Strictly necessary cookies (such as a login or basket) are the exception.

## Quick choices
- **Reject all** if the banner offers it.
- No reject button: open **Manage options / Preferences**, switch off non-essential categories, then save or confirm your choices.
- Check any other tab on the banner (some list vendors separately) for a matching objection option.

## Pay or consent
- Some sites offer "accept tracking or subscribe"; read what each choice does before picking.

## Change your mind later
- Look for *Cookie settings* / *Privacy choices* in the site footer.
- Clearing that site's cookies in your browser will usually make the banner appear again.

## Reduce the banners
- Mozilla's Firefox for Android help says its cookie banner blocker is no longer available; do not rely on it. Any extension that answers banners needs broad page access, so check its permissions first.
- **Global Privacy Control** is a browser signal meant to convey a Do Not Sell or Share request under the California CCPA and similar US state laws.

## Verify
- [ ] After choosing, the site's cookie settings page shows only necessary cookies enabled.
