# Peer-to-peer payment apps

## Treat payments like cash
P2P payments to individuals (Zelle, Venmo Friends, PayPal Friends and Family, Cash App, UK bank transfers) are usually **instant and irreversible**. Use them for people you know and trust.

## Set up safely
- Turn on app **PIN/biometric** lock and 2-step verification.
- **Venmo:** Settings › Privacy › **Private** (default is public feed of who you pay); also Friends list private.
- Link a credit card or bank you monitor; turn on payment notifications.

## Sending
- Double-check the recipient: phone/email/username and the **profile photo**; send £1/$1 first to new contacts.
- UK bank transfers show **Confirmation of Payee** ("name doesn't match"): stop and check.
- Buying from strangers (Marketplace, tickets, pets): use **PayPal Goods and Services** or card checkout; protection applies only there. Zelle, Venmo Friends and Cash App have little or no purchase protection.

## Common scams
- "Accidental" payment then "please send it back" (the original was from a stolen card and will be reversed).
- Seller asks for Friends and Family "to avoid fees".
- Fake payment confirmation emails/screenshots; check the app itself.
- Impersonators of your bank asking you to "move money to a safe account" via Zelle/transfer.

## If something goes wrong
- Contact the app and your bank immediately. UK: authorised push payment (APP) fraud reimbursement rules give many victims refunds (claim within 13 months). US: report to the bank and reportfraud.ftc.gov.

- [ ] Account private and PIN-locked; purchases from strangers made with a protected method.
