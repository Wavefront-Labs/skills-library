# AI voice cloning and family emergency scams

This page follows an FTC consumer alert (March 2023). Scammers may use AI to clone a loved one's voice from a short audio clip posted online, then call as a relative in distress.

## The pattern
- An urgent crisis story with pressure to act.
- Requests to pay in ways that are hard to reverse: wiring money, sending cryptocurrency, or buying gift cards and reading out the card numbers and PINs.

## What to do
1. Don't trust the voice.
2. Hang up and call the person back on a number you already know is theirs.
3. If you cannot reach them, try another family member or their friends.
4. Agree a family code word in advance and never post it (a precaution, not an FTC requirement).

## Reduce exposure
- Audio and video posted online can be used, so review who can see your public posts.

## If you paid
- Contact your bank, card issuer, wire company or exchange immediately and ask for a refund, and report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.

## Verify
- [ ] Your family knows to call back on a saved number before sending money.
