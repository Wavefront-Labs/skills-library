# Spot fake reviews

This page follows US FTC guidance.

## What the FTC says to check
- **Timing:** look at how recent reviews are and watch for a burst of reviews over a short period.
- **Reviewer:** check whether they have written other reviews; an account that exists only to post one review for one product may be fake.
- **Do not trust your eyes alone:** you cannot reliably tell a real review from a fake by looking.
- **Fakes are not always five stars:** some fake positive reviews give less than the top rating to seem believable, and fake reviews are not always positive (a company might post negative ones about a competitor).
- **Use multiple sources,** starting with well-known websites that have trustworthy, impartial expert reviews.
- **Consider who is speaking:** what you know about the reviewer and the site hosting the review.
- **Incentive labels:** some sites label reviews where the reviewer got something, such as a free product; how much weight to give them is up to you.

## Rules and reporting
- The FTC announced a final rule on 14 August 2024 banning fake or false reviews and testimonials (including AI-generated ones), paying for reviews with a set sentiment, undisclosed insider reviews, misleading company-controlled review sites, review suppression through threats, and fake social media indicators. Use the platform's report link for suspected fakes.

## Verify
- [ ] Your decision rests on at least one independent source, not just the star average.
