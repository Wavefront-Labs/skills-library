# Manage feeds and doomscrolling

## Why it happens
- Endless feeds with variable rewards and negative news, which grabs attention. Scrolling at night worsens sleep and mood.

## Change the feed
- Choose **Following**/chronological feeds: Instagram *Following* and *Favourites*; X *Following* tab; Facebook *Feeds > Friends*; YouTube turn off watch history to stop recommendations on the home page.
- **Mute** words and topics (X muted words, Instagram *Hidden words*, Threads/Bluesky mute lists); *Not interested* on recommended posts; unfollow accounts that leave you anxious or angry.
- Instagram *Sensitive content control* to *Less*; TikTok *Manage topics* and *Refresh your For You feed*.

## Add friction
- Screen Time / Digital Wellbeing **app limits** (e.g. 30 min/day for social apps) and **downtime** at night.
- Move social apps off the home screen or into a folder; log out after use; delete the app and use the browser.
- **Grayscale** mode reduces the pull: iPhone Accessibility > Display > Colour Filters (set as Accessibility Shortcut, triple-click); Android Bedtime mode.
- Phone out of the bedroom; a real alarm clock.

## Healthier news
- Pick 1-2 trusted sources and check them at set times (morning digest, newsletter) instead of continuous feeds. Turn off breaking news alerts unless they affect you.

## Verify
- [ ] Screen Time shows less daily social media use after a week, and nights are phone-free.
