# Music in videos and social posts

## Safe sources
- **In-app sound libraries**: TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and Facebook licence songs for use *inside their app*. Business/brand accounts often get a smaller commercial library.
- **YouTube Audio Library** (Studio > Audio library): free tracks, some need attribution.
- **Royalty-free subscriptions**: Epidemic Sound, Artlist, Musicbed: licence covers your channels while subscribed (check whether it covers past videos after you cancel).
- **Creative Commons** music (Free Music Archive, ccMixter): follow the licence (BY needs credit; NC bans commercial use; ND bans edits).
- Public domain recordings are rare; old compositions may be public domain but modern recordings of them aren't.

## What isn't permission
- "I bought the song/CD", "only 10 seconds", "no copyright intended", "I credited the artist". None of these license use in a video.

## What happens
- **YouTube Content ID**: matched music leads to a claim: monetisation goes to the owner, the video may be muted or blocked in some countries. Remove or replace the song in Studio's *Editor*, or dispute if you have a licence.
- Instagram/Facebook may mute audio or remove the post; repeat issues limit the account.

## Licensing commercially
- Ads and client work need sync and master licences from rights holders, or a stock licence that covers advertising.

## Verify
- [ ] Every track used has a documented source and licence covering this platform and purpose.
