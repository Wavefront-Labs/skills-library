# Digital wallets and contactless payments

## Set up
- **iPhone**: Wallet app > **Add Card**; hold the card near the phone or enter details. Your bank may ask for more information or an app before approving the card.
- **Android**: **Google Wallet** app, add a payment card. The first card you add becomes the default for tap to pay; change it from the card's Details. Set Wallet as the default payment app in Android Settings (search for NFC > Contactless payments > Payment default > Google Wallet).
- Android contactless payments require a screen lock.

## Pay
- In shops, follow the wallet's own prompts, then hold the phone near the reader.
- Online and in apps, wallet buttons skip typing card numbers.
- Transit: Apple says an eligible travel card may be set to Express Mode automatically when added.

## Why it's safer
- With Apple Pay, merchants receive a device account number (shown in the card's details) rather than your card number.

## If the phone is lost
- Contact your bank to block or replace the cards, and use your Apple or Google account tools to remove the cards from the device. In Google Wallet, removing a card removes it only from that device. Apple says signing out of iCloud or removing the passcode removes cards from the device. Check each provider's current lost-device steps.

## Gotchas
- Some banks or cards may not be supported; the bank may need extra verification.
- Never share a verification code your bank sends with anyone who contacts you.
