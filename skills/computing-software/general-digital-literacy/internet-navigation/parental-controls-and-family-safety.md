# Parental controls and family safety

**Goal:** protect children online with controls that match their age, plus conversation.

## Device and account controls
- **Apple:** set up Family Sharing and a Child Account (each child needs their own Apple Account), then manage from your own device: Settings › Family › child's name › Screen Time. Controls include Downtime, App Limits, Communication Limits and Content & Privacy Restrictions (age ratings, Game Center, Siri, privacy), plus **Ask to Buy** and Ask to Browse. Ask to Buy and Ask to Browse are on by default for under-13s and Limit Adult Websites for under-18s; in some regions some controls cannot be turned off. Apple's pages describe new parental controls in iOS 27 and later, so menus may differ.
- **Android/Chromebook:** **Google Family Link**: create a Google Account for a child under 13 (or the age in your country) or add supervision to an existing one; approve or block apps, set daily limits and bedtime, check the location of an Android or compatible Fitbit device. iPhones, iPads and computers other than Chromebooks cannot be supervised with Family Link, and it may not be available in every country. Purchase approval covers only Google Play billing.
- **Windows/Xbox/Android:** **Microsoft Family Safety**: screen time limits across devices, app and game limits, activity reports and web filtering in Microsoft Edge. App and game content filters work only on Windows and Xbox.
- **Consoles:** each console maker has its own parental controls; check the maker's help centre.

## Network and apps
- Router or broadband provider filters cover devices on your home network but not mobile data.
- Check each social or gaming app for private-account, messaging and contact settings, and its minimum age.

## Make it work
- Agree rules together (family media plan); explain why. Loosen controls as children show responsibility.
- Devices in shared spaces for younger children; no devices in bedrooms overnight.
- Make sure they know they can tell you about anything upsetting without losing the device.
- Controls can be bypassed (other devices, friends' phones); conversation matters more than settings.
