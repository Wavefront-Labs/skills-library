# Book travel and use digital tickets

## Booking safely
- Book on the operator's site/app or a well-known agent; check the URL. Lookalike booking sites add fees or are scams.
- Pay by credit card for chargeback protection (UK Section 75 for £100-£30,000).
- Event tickets: official seller or authorised resale (Ticketmaster resale, Twickets, AXS). Social media "spare tickets" offers paid by bank transfer are a common scam.
- Check names exactly as on passport/ID for flights.

## Storing tickets
- Add to **Apple Wallet** or **Google Wallet** (*Add to Wallet* button in the confirmation email or app); boarding passes update with gate changes.
- Train apps (Trainline, national rail apps, Amtrak) hold barcoded e-tickets; some must be **activated** before travel.
- Many event tickets use rotating barcodes (SafeTix) that only work in the app; screenshots won't scan.

## Backups
- Phone fully charged plus a power bank; download tickets for offline use.
- Save the PDF or confirmation code too; know the operator's rule on paper copies.
- Brightness up at the gate; remove cracked screen protectors if scanners struggle.

## Gotchas
- Wallet passes can disappear if you reset the phone; re-add from the app.
- Changes and refunds go through where you booked, not always the operator.

## Verify
- [ ] Ticket opens offline in airplane mode and the names and dates are right.
