# Voice assistants on phones and speakers

## Set up
- **Siri** (iPhone): Settings > **Apple Intelligence & Siri** (or Siri): *Listen for "Siri" or "Hey Siri"*, press side button, *Allow Siri when locked*, voice. Type to Siri for quiet places.
- **Android**: Gemini is replacing Google Assistant as the default; Settings > Google > *Search, Assistant & Voice* or the Gemini app > *Hey Google & Voice Match*.
- **Alexa**: Alexa app sets up Echo devices; Alexa app on the phone also works as an assistant.

## Useful commands
- Timers and alarms: "set a 20-minute timer called pasta".
- Calls and messages: "call Mum on speaker", "text Sam I'm running late", "read my messages".
- Reminders: "remind me to take my tablets at 8 every morning", "remind me to buy milk when I leave work" (location-based).
- Navigation: "directions home", "where's the nearest pharmacy".
- Smart home: "turn off the living room lights", routines like "Good night".
- Questions and conversions; "what's this song?".

## Accessibility
- Hands-free use helps people with limited mobility or vision; combine with Voice Control (iPhone) or Voice Access (Android) for full device control.

## Safety and limits
- Lock-screen access lets anyone send messages or calls; turn off *Allow when locked* if that's a concern.
- Emergency: "Hey Siri, call emergency services" works, but know your phone's emergency SOS buttons too.
- Answers may be wrong; verify medical or financial info.
