# Screen time limits

**Goal:** use devices on purpose rather than by habit.

## Measure first
- **iPhone:** Settings › **Screen Time** shows how you use your device.
- **Android:** Settings › **Digital Wellbeing** (newer pages: Digital Wellbeing & parental controls) shows how often you unlock the phone and how long you use each app. Steps vary by device.
- Microsoft Family Safety reports screen time for Windows, Xbox and Android devices.

## Set limits
- **App Limits** (iPhone) / **App timers** (Android: Settings › Digital Wellbeing › App timers › Set timer): e.g. 30 min/day for social media. On Android the app closes and its icon dims when time runs out; timers reset at midnight, and some work or school accounts do not work with app timers.
- **Downtime** (iPhone): only phone calls and the apps you choose are available. **Bedtime mode** (Android: Digital Wellbeing › Bedtime mode) silences the phone and removes the screen's colour.
- **Focus mode** (Android: Digital Wellbeing › Focus mode): pick apps to pause; you cannot use them and get no notifications from them. iPhone has its own Focus feature.

## Habits that help
- Turn off non-essential notifications (badges and sounds) per app.
- Charge the phone outside the bedroom; use a cheap alarm clock.
- Move tempting apps off the home screen; log out of social sites on the computer.
