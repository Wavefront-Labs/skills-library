# Sharing photos of children

## Before posting
- **Who can see it?** Public posts can be copied, scraped, searched by face, and misused. Prefer private audiences.
- Would the child be embarrassed at 15 (bath, tantrum, potty photos)? Older children: ask them first.
- Ask other parents before posting their children.

## Hide identifying details
- School name/uniform/badge, house number or street, car number plate, the child's full name and birthday together.
- Turn off location on the camera or strip it (iPhone share sheet *Options > Location* off; Android Photos *Remove location*). Don't tag the location of home, school or regular clubs; post holiday photos after you return.

## Safer ways to share
- **Private shared albums** (iCloud Shared Albums, Google Photos shared albums with specific people, Tinybeans, family WhatsApp group).
- Instagram/Facebook: *Close Friends* or custom friend lists; make the account private.
- Turn off face recognition tagging and limit who can tag you.

## Schools, clubs and events
- Many schools ask parents not to post photos of other children from events; respect it.
- Check your own consent choices for school/club photo use.

## Future
- AI tools can now turn innocent public photos into abusive images; public child photos carry more risk than before.
- Some countries (e.g. France's 2024 law) give children image rights against their parents; growing children may ask you to delete old posts. Do it.

## Verify
- [ ] Recent posts of children are visible only to chosen people and contain no location or school details.
