# Online and mobile banking safely

**Goal:** manage money online without falling for fraud.

## Set up
- Install the bank's **official app** from the App Store/Google Play (check the publisher name), or
  type the bank's address / use a bookmark. Never sign in through links in emails or texts.
- Use a unique strong password and the app's biometric login; keep phone OS and app updated.
- Turn on **alerts** (every payment, large transactions, new payee, login from new device).

## Paying people
- New payees: check the **Confirmation of Payee** (UK) result showing whether the name matches the
  account. A "no match" or "close match" warning is a strong reason to stop and phone them.
- Changed bank details by email or message ("we've changed banks") are classic fraud; verify by
  phone on a known number.
- Bank transfers are hard to reverse; for purchases from strangers use card payments or protected
  methods.

## What banks will never do
Ask for your full password/PIN or one-time code by phone, text or email; ask you to move money to a
"safe account"; send someone to collect your card. **Stop, hang up, call the number on the back of
your card** (from another phone if possible).

## If something goes wrong
Call the bank immediately (UK: **159** connects to your bank's fraud line), freeze the card in the app,
report to Action Fraud/Report Fraud (UK) or reportfraud.ftc.gov (US).

- [ ] Alerts are on and you know your bank's fraud number and 159 (UK).
