# AI translation

## Text
- **Google Translate**, **DeepL** (often more natural for European languages), **Microsoft Translator**: paste text or upload documents.
- Chatbots (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) handle tone and context well: "Translate into polite Spanish for an email to a landlord".

## Camera and signs
- Google Translate / Google Lens **Camera** mode overlays translations on menus and signs; iPhone **Translate** app or *Live Text* > Translate in Camera/Photos.

## Conversations
- **Conversation mode** (Google Translate, Apple Translate, Microsoft Translator): each person speaks in turn; the phone speaks the translation.
- Live translation in calls and earbuds: Pixel *Live Translate*, Samsung Galaxy AI *Interpreter* and call translation, AirPods Live Translation with recent iPhones.
- Meetings: Teams/Zoom translated captions (licensing varies).

## Offline
- Download language packs before travel: Google Translate > profile > *Downloaded languages*; Apple Translate > *Downloaded Languages*.

## Accuracy
- Fine for travel, gist and casual chats. Risky for medical, legal, immigration, contracts and safety instructions: use a professional or certified translator.
- Check names, numbers, dates, negatives ("not") and formality level. Back-translate key sentences.
- Idioms and humour translate badly; write simple, short sentences when you know text will be machine-translated.

## Privacy
- Free services may keep text; avoid confidential documents unless your organisation approves (DeepL Pro deletes texts after translation).

## Verify
- [ ] Key phrases back-translated correctly, and offline packs downloaded before travel.
