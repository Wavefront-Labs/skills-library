# Government services online

**Goal:** do tax, benefits, licences and applications on the official sites, safely and once.

## Find the real service
- Start from the national portal: **gov.uk** (UK), **usa.gov** (US), **europa.eu/youreurope** (EU
  links to each country), **my.gov.au** (Australia), **canada.ca**.
- Official UK addresses end in `.gov.uk`, US federal `.gov`. Paid search ads at the top may be
  copycat agents charging extra for passports, driving licences, EHIC/GHIC cards or ESTA/ETA.

## Sign in
- UK: **GOV.UK One Login** (replacing Government Gateway for many services), proving identity with
  passport/driving licence photo and a selfie in the app. US: **Login.gov** or ID.me for many
  federal agencies.
- Save recovery methods and the authenticator app/codes; losing them locks you out of tax accounts.

## Doing a task
1. Read the eligibility and "what you need" list first; gather documents (National Insurance number,
   tax reference, passport).
2. Save progress where offered; sessions time out after inactivity (often 15-30 minutes).
3. Keep the **reference number** and confirmation email/page (save as PDF).
4. Help: the service's contact page, a library or community digital-help centre, or someone with
   delegated access (UK "trusted helper", US authorised representative).

## Gotchas
- Government bodies don't text links asking for bank details for refunds; that's phishing.
- Free services don't require payment; fees (e.g. passport) are stated on the official page.

## Verify
- [ ] You reached the service from the official portal and saved the confirmation reference.
