# Read-later apps

## Options
- **Safari Reading List**: Share > *Add to Reading List* (or Shift+Cmd+D on Mac); syncs via iCloud; *Save offline automatically* in Settings > Apps > Safari.
- **Chrome Reading list**: bookmark star > *Add to reading list*; side panel. Syncs with your Google account.
- **Edge Collections**, **Firefox** (Pocket closed in July 2025; use bookmarks or another service).
- **Instapaper**: clean text view, highlights, offline, send to Kindle.
- **Readwise Reader**, **Matter**, **Raindrop.io** (bookmarks plus read later, tags, full-text search on paid plan).

## Save quickly
- Install the browser extension or use the phone **Share** sheet; many apps accept forwarded emails (each account has a save-by-email address), handy for newsletters.

## Read
- Reader view strips ads; adjust font size and theme.
- Highlights export to note apps (Readwise syncs to Obsidian, Notion).

## Keep it useful
- Weekly: read, archive or delete. Archive anything older than a month that you haven't opened.
- Tag by purpose (*work*, *recipes*) not by source.
- Pick one service; scattered lists across apps never get read.

## Gotchas
- Paywalled articles may save only the teaser.
- Services shut down (Pocket did); export regularly (CSV/HTML) if the list matters.

## Verify
- [ ] Articles saved on the phone appear on the computer and open offline.
