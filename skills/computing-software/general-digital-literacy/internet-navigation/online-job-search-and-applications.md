# Online job search and applications

**Goal:** find relevant jobs and submit applications that reach a human.

## Searching
- Sites: national services (UK Find a job, US CareerOneStop/USAJOBS), LinkedIn Jobs, Indeed,
  company career pages, sector-specific boards.
- Search with job titles plus location; filter by date posted, salary, remote/hybrid.
- Set **job alerts** (daily email) for saved searches.

## Your documents
- Keep a master CV and tailor it per role, mirroring keywords from the advert (skills, tools,
  qualifications). Many employers use an **applicant tracking system (ATS)** that scans text.
- Upload as **DOCX or text-based PDF**, simple layout: no text in images, tables or text boxes, standard
  headings (Experience, Education, Skills). Name it `Firstname-Lastname-CV.pdf`.
- Professional email address (`first.last@…`).

## Application forms
- Read the whole form first; draft long answers in a document and paste them in (sessions time out).
- Use the STAR structure (Situation, Task, Action, Result) for competency questions.
- Save a copy of the advert (it disappears after closing) and your answers.
- Track applications in a spreadsheet: role, company, date, link, status, contact.

## Job scams
Red flags: interview only by chat, offer without interview, paying for training/equipment/background
checks, cheque for "equipment" you must forward money from, requests for bank details or ID before an
offer, vague company. Verify the company and recruiter independently.

## Verify
- [ ] Your CV opens cleanly when pasted as plain text (ATS check) and your tracker is up to date.
