# Follow sites with RSS and alerts

**Goal:** new posts from the sites you trust, and new mentions of a topic, arrive in one place.

## RSS feeds
- An RSS/Atom feed is a list of a site's latest posts that a feed reader checks for you.
- Look for a "Subscribe" or RSS link on the site; many feed readers can find a site's feed when you paste its home page address.
- Organise feeds into folders (work, local news, hobbies) and prune them regularly.

## Google Alerts
1. Open Google Alerts and enter a topic you want to follow, for example your name or an organisation in quotes.
2. Click **Show options** to change how often you get notifications, the types of sites, language, region, how many results, and which account receives the alert.
3. Click **Create Alert**; you get emails when Google finds matching search results.
4. To edit, click **Edit** next to an alert, change options, then **Update Alert**. To remove one, click **Delete**, or use **Unsubscribe** at the bottom of an alert email.

## If alerts do not arrive
Check you are signed in to the right account, that alerts have not been disabled (click **Enable** if prompted), and that the emails are not in spam or the inbox is not full. Gmail users can add googlealerts-noreply@google.com to contacts.

## Gotchas
- Too many feeds becomes another firehose.
- Alerts only cover what Google Search finds, so they miss closed social media and paywalled content.
