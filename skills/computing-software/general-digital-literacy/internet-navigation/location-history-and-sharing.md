# Location history and sharing

## Location history (where you've been)
- **Google Timeline**: off by default (you must opt in), and the data comes from your device, so it is not available in Maps on a computer. Back up is optional and stores an encrypted copy on Google's servers. You can turn Timeline off, set auto-delete or delete all of it in My Google Activity; to delete only parts, use the Google Maps app on your phone.
- **iPhone Significant Locations & Routes**: Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › System Services › **Significant Locations & Routes** (turn off). Apple says this data is encrypted and cannot be read by Apple.
- Google **Web & App Activity** may also store places from searches: myactivity.google.com.

## Live sharing (where you are now)
- iPhone **Find My** › People; Messages › contact › *Share My Location*. Stop: Find My › People › person › Stop Sharing.
- **Google Maps location sharing**: Maps › profile picture › Location sharing › pick the person › Stop. Turning the Android Location Sharing toggle off only pauses existing shares; stop each share to end it. A shared real-time location link can be viewed for up to 24 hours.
- Snap Map, WhatsApp live location, Life360 and family apps have their own sharing settings; check each.
- If you suspect someone tracks you, check Google Maps sharing and Apple's Personal Safety guide (linked in sources).

## App permissions
- Phone settings › Location: set apps to **While Using** and turn off **Precise Location** for weather, shopping and social apps.

## Gotchas
- Fitness apps can publish route maps; check each app's privacy zones and visibility settings.
- Other apps and devices you use may also record location; review each one.

## Verify
- [ ] Timeline/Significant Locations are off or auto-deleting; only people you choose see your live location.
