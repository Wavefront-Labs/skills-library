# Maps, directions and local search

## Directions
- Search a destination > **Directions** > choose mode: car, **transit**, walk, cycle, ride-hailing. Set *Depart at / Arrive by* for future trips (traffic and timetables differ).
- Options: avoid tolls, motorways, ferries; multiple stops (Google: *Add stop*, up to 9).
- Share a route or **live location/ETA** with someone (Google *Share trip progress*, Apple *Share ETA*).

## Local search
- Search "pharmacy", "petrol near me", "pizza open now"; filter by *Open now*, rating, price.
- Check the business listing: **hours** (holiday hours often wrong; call ahead), phone, website, photos, *Popular times*, recent reviews.
- Listings can be hijacked: a scam phone number on a "locksmith" or "customer service" listing is common. Cross-check the number on the business's own website.

## Save and organise
- Star/save places into lists (Google *Saved*, Apple *Guides*); label **Home** and **Work** for quick routes.
- Google **Timeline** (location history) now stored on your device; Apple *Significant Locations* in Settings > Privacy.

## Offline and travel
- Google Maps: profile > **Offline maps > Select your own map**, download the area on Wi-Fi.
- Apple Maps (iOS 17+): profile > *Offline Maps*.
- Street View/Look Around to recognise an entrance before arriving.

## Gotchas
- Walking routes may use paths that are closed or unsafe at night; use judgment.
- Battery: navigation drains fast; carry a charger.

## Verify
- [ ] Route checked with the right departure time and an offline copy downloaded for areas with poor signal.
