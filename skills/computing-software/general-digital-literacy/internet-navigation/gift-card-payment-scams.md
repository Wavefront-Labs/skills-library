# Gift card payment requests

**Rule:** the FTC says no real business or government agency will ever tell you to buy a gift card to pay them. A request to do so is a scam. This page follows US FTC guidance; use your own country's regulator elsewhere.

## Common stories
- A government agency says you owe money and face arrest. "Someone who works for the government will never demand payment."
- "Tech support" says your computer has a problem and asks for payment by gift card; real security pop-ups never ask you to call a number.
- A boss, romance contact or prize "organiser" asks for card numbers and PINs.
- Anyone who tells you to read out the card numbers and PINs is running a scam.

## If you already bought cards
1. Don't send any more codes.
2. Contact the gift card company right away, even if it happened long ago, and ask for your money back; some companies refund. Use the phone number on the card or the issuer's support page.
3. Keep the gift card and store receipt to support your report.
4. Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
5. If you gave a scammer a password, change it and any account that reused it.

## Help others
- Talk to older relatives about this scam; shop staff may also ask whether someone told you to buy the cards.
