# Creative Commons licences

**Goal:** reuse others' images, music and text legally, and share your own on your terms.

## The building blocks
| Element | Means |
|---|---|
| **BY** (Attribution) | credit must be given to the creator |
| **SA** (ShareAlike) | adaptations must be shared under the same terms |
| **NC** (NonCommercial) | only noncommercial use is permitted |
| **ND** (NoDerivatives) | no derivatives or adaptations are permitted |

Six licences: CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC BY-ND, CC BY-NC, CC BY-NC-SA, CC BY-NC-ND. **CC0** is not a licence; it is a public domain dedication. The **Public Domain Mark** marks a work already in the worldwide public domain.

## Can I use it?
- Business or marketing use? Avoid **NC**.
- Need to edit, crop, translate or remix? Avoid **ND**.
- Mixing into a work you will license differently? Watch **SA**.

## Attribution: TASL
Include the **T**itle, **A**uthor, **S**ource and **L**icence, linking each where possible. If you changed the work, say so, for example "Cropped from original".

## Licensing your own work
CC licences are not revocable: you can stop distributing under one, but people who already have a copy may keep redistributing it under its terms.

## Gotchas
- CC licences do not license trademark rights, or the publicity, personality and privacy rights of third parties; a person in a photo may still need to agree to your use.

## Verify
- [ ] Every reused CC item in your work has a full TASL credit and matches its licence terms.
