# News search and specialised verticals

## Verticals (filters under the search box)
- Google lists filters such as Books, Forums, Images, Maps, News, Shopping, Short Videos, Videos and Web. They change with the query; choose **All** to see others.
- **News** focuses on articles from news publishers; **Books** and **Maps** search those collections; **Shopping** lists products (treat it as commercial).
- Other engines have their own news and image sections.

## News search technique
- Put names and key terms in quotes: `"Bank of England" interest rates`.
- Limit to a source with `site:` and a domain, for example `site:example.com`.
- Narrow by date with `after:` and `before:` (documents last updated after or before a year or date).
- Date matters: an old story resurfacing is a common misinformation pattern. Read beyond headlines and check the date, byline and whether it is news, opinion or sponsored.

## Balanced reading
- Compare several outlets with different perspectives.
- Primary sources (court documents, official statistics, press releases) beat commentary.
- Libraries may give free access to news archives; ask yours.
