# Organise household documents digitally

## Folder structure (simple beats perfect)
- Top-level: `Home`, `Money`, `Tax`, `Health`, `Car`, `Insurance`, `Identity`, `Work`, `Warranties-Manuals`, `Kids`.
- Year subfolders where volume is high (`Money/2026`).
- File names start with the date: `2026-10-03_Electricity_Bill_OctoEnergy.pdf` so they sort chronologically.

## Getting paper in
- Phone scanning (Notes, Google Drive, Microsoft Lens/Office app, Adobe Scan) with OCR so text is searchable.
- Opt into e-statements and save PDFs directly; set an email rule to label bills.
- Photograph receipts for big purchases with the warranty card; note serial numbers.

## Security
- Store in an encrypted, backed-up place: cloud drive with 2-step verification, plus an encrypted local copy (BitLocker/FileVault drive or encrypted archive).
- Identity documents (passport scans, tax ID): restrict sharing; consider a password manager's secure file storage.
- Share an "In case of emergency" folder or instructions with a trusted person (see digital-legacy skill).

## What to keep on paper
- Originals with legal force: wills, deeds, birth/marriage certificates, passports, share certificates, vehicle titles where required.
- Tax records retention: UK self-assessment at least 5 years after the 31 January deadline; US IRS generally 3 years (longer in some cases).
- Shred paper with personal data after scanning (cross-cut shredder).

## Maintenance
- Monthly 10 minutes: file the inbox folder; annually purge expired warranties and old statements past retention.

- [ ] You can find last year's tax summary and the boiler warranty in under a minute, on any device.
