# Online identity verification

## Typical flow
1. Photograph the front (and sometimes back) of a passport, driving licence or national ID; some apps read the passport **NFC chip** by holding the phone to it.
2. Take a **selfie** or short video (turn head, blink) to prove liveness and match the ID photo.
3. Answer knowledge questions or confirm via bank details/credit file in some services.
- Examples: GOV.UK One Login app, ID.me (US IRS, VA, states), bank account opening, Yoti and Post Office EasyID, age verification on platforms.

## Get a clean capture
- Bright, even light, no glare on laminated cards; plain dark background; whole document in frame, nothing covering corners.
- Remove glasses and hats for the selfie; face the light.
- Passport chip scan: phone case off, hold the top of the phone on the photo page and keep still.

## When it fails
- Retry with better light; try a different document; use the in-person or video-call alternative (Post Office, ID.me video agent).
- Name mismatch between ID and account (middle names, recent marriage) commonly fails checks; use the name exactly as on the ID.

## Protect yourself
- Only verify inside the official app or website you navigated to yourself. A link in a text asking you to "verify your identity" with ID photos is a common identity-theft scam.
- Never send ID photos by email or chat to strangers, employers you haven't confirmed, or landlords before viewing.
- Check how long the service keeps your images (privacy notice) and delete accounts you no longer need.
