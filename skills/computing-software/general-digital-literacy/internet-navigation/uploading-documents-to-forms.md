# Upload documents and photos to forms

**Goal:** the application isn't rejected because the upload was blurry, too big or the wrong format.

## Read the rules first
Most forms state: accepted formats (PDF, JPG, PNG), **maximum size** (often 2-10 MB per file), whether colour is required, and whether all pages must be in one file.

## Capture well
- Use a **scanner app** (Notes on iPhone › Scan Documents, Google Drive scan, Microsoft Lens, Adobe Scan) rather than a plain photo; it straightens, crops and makes a PDF.
- Flat surface, good daylight, no shadows or flash glare, all four corners visible, text readable when zoomed.
- ID photos: follow the official photo rules (plain background, no filters).

## Prepare the file
- Combine pages into one PDF if asked (scanner apps do multi-page; or combine PDFs).
- **Too big:** export at lower quality/resolution, Medium size in the scanner app, or resize the image (aim 1500-2000 px wide).
- **Wrong format:** HEIC iPhone photos often fail; export as JPG.
- Name clearly: `Surname-ProofOfAddress-2026-10.pdf`.

## Uploading
- Click **Choose file/Browse** › pick it; on phones, choose *Files* or *Photo Library* (or take a photo directly).
- Wait for the progress bar and a thumbnail/tick before pressing Continue.
- Failed: check size/format, try another browser, turn off VPN, use a stable connection, close other tabs.

## Safety
- Upload only to the official site (check the address); never email ID unless the organisation's official process requires it.
- Delete copies of ID from Downloads/phone afterwards, or keep them in an encrypted folder.
- Watermarking a copy ("For X application only, date") deters misuse where the form allows.

## Verify
- [ ] Upload shows as attached with the right file name; the confirmation page or email lists it.
