# Verifying AI answers

**Goal:** use AI as a starting point without passing on its mistakes.

## Why it's needed
Chatbots generate fluent text by predicting likely words; they can state wrong facts, invent
citations, quotes, statistics, court cases or product features ("hallucinations"), and sound equally
confident either way. AI search overviews can misread their sources.

## How to check
1. **Click through the sources** it cites. Does the page exist? Does it actually say that?
2. **Cross-check** key facts with a primary or authoritative source: official sites (.gov, NHS, the
   manufacturer's manual), reputable publications, the original study.
3. Ask the same question differently or in a new chat; inconsistent answers flag weak ground.
4. Check dates: the model may not know recent changes (prices, laws, software menus).
5. For calculations, redo key numbers in a spreadsheet or calculator.
6. For code or formulas, test them on sample data.

## High-risk areas: always verify with a professional/official source
Health and medication, legal rights and deadlines, tax and benefits, financial decisions, safety
(electrical, chemicals), anything you'll publish under your name.

## Red flags
Very specific numbers with no source, references you can't find, quotes not found anywhere else,
answers that perfectly confirm what you hoped.

## Verify
- [ ] Each fact you'll rely on is confirmed by at least one independent authoritative source.
