# Live subtitles while presenting

## PowerPoint (Microsoft 365 and PowerPoint 2024; Windows, Mac, web)
1. **Slide Show › Subtitle Settings**: *Spoken Language* (what you speak), *Subtitle Language*
   (same, or another language to translate into), the microphone, and placement (overlaid on the top or
   bottom of the slide, or docked above or below it; default *Below Slide*). Windows: *More Settings*
   for colour, size, transparency and font.
2. Tick **Always Use Subtitles**, or during the show click **Toggle Subtitles** on the toolbar below the
   slide (or press **J**).
3. Speech is processed by Microsoft cloud speech services; you need a reliable internet connection.
   Windows needs Windows 10 or later.

## Google Slides
Start the slideshow, then **Ctrl+Shift+C** (Mac ⌘+Shift+C) or More options › Captions preferences ›
Toggle captions. The drop-down next to **CC** changes text position and size. Works in current Chrome,
Edge and Safari with a working microphone; the shortcut list marks captions as English only. Captions
have no punctuation and are not stored.

## Keynote
macOS Live Captions (Apple menu › System Settings › Accessibility › Live Captions) can caption spoken
audio on screen; it is available only on Macs with Apple silicon and not in every language or region.

## Tips
- Microsoft recommends a headset microphone; reduce background noise.
- Speak names and jargon clearly; put key terms on the slides.
- Leave space at the bottom of slides for captions, or choose a docked position.
- Live captions are machine-generated and may be inaccurate; for formal accessibility needs, book human
  captioning.

## Verify
- [ ] Rehearse 30 seconds with subtitles on: text appears promptly and is readable from the back of the room.
