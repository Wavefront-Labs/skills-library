# Present slides in a video meeting

## Options
| Method | Notes for you? | Details |
|---|---|---|
| **Teams PowerPoint Live** (Share › PowerPoint Live section › pick the file) | Yes, next to the slides | Uses much less bandwidth than screen sharing; .pptx/.ppsx only; attendees may browse slides unless you select *Turn off participant navigation* |
| Teams/Zoom **share a window** running the slide show | Yes, Presenter View stays on your screen | Share the slideshow window only, not the whole screen |
| **Zoom: Share › Screens › Application windows › Microsoft PowerPoint**, then a presenter layout | Not documented | Your video appears over the slides ("Sharing slides as a Virtual Background"); needs the Zoom desktop app on Windows or macOS and PowerPoint (or Keynote on macOS) installed; web-client viewers see a normal share |
| **Google Meet: Present now › Chrome Tab** › the Slides tab › Share › *Start slideshow* | Yes, speaker notes panel in Meet | Needs an eligible work or school account, Chrome, desktop |
| Share entire screen | Risky | Shows notifications and other windows |

## PowerPoint with one monitor
Slide Show › **Set Up Slide Show** › *Browsed by an individual (window)*, start the show, then share
that window.

## Two monitors
Start Presenter View; the slideshow goes to the second monitor. Share that monitor (or the slideshow window).

## Checklist
- Turn on Do Not Disturb / Focus; close email and chat.
- Videos in Zoom: tick *Share sound* / *Optimize for video clip* when sharing.
- Google Slides captions show up on the shared screen; tell the audience they come from Slides and caption
  only your voice.
- Ask "can you see the slide-show view, not the editor?" in the first seconds.

## Verify
- [ ] Test meeting with yourself on a second device: slides fill the screen and advance when you click.
