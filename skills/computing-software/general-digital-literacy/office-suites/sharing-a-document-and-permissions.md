# Share a document and set permissions

## Word / Excel / PowerPoint (OneDrive or SharePoint)
1. Save to OneDrive or SharePoint first, then use **Share**.
2. Link types: **Anyone** (whoever holds the link, including outside your organisation), **People with existing access** (resend a link without changing permissions) and **Specific people** (named individuals; forwarded invitations only work for people who already have access). Admins can grey out options.
3. **Can review** lets people comment and suggest changes without editing; it applies to Word documents only, and people who already have edit rights can still edit.
4. Anyone links can have an expiration date and a password, depending on your subscription and admin settings; check the OneDrive sharing page.

## Google Docs
**Share**, add people as **Viewer**, **Commenter** or **Editor**. **General access**: *Restricted* (only people you added), *Anyone with the link* (opens without signing in; Viewer by default), or *Your Organization* on work or school accounts. In Share > Settings, untick **Allow editors to change permissions and share** and the options that let viewers and commenters download, copy and print. **Add expiration** (click the arrow beside a person's name) sets a date within one year, for work or school accounts only; on folders only for Viewer.

## Rules of thumb
- Share with named people for anything personal or confidential; "Anyone with the link" links get forwarded.
- Restricting download is friction, not security.

## Verify
- [ ] Open the link in a private window (not signed in): it should ask you to sign in unless you meant it to be public.
