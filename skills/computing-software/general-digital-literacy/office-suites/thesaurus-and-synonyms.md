# Thesaurus and synonyms

## Microsoft Office
- Select a word, then **Review > Thesaurus**; point to a word in the results, open its arrow and choose **Insert** to replace the selected word.
- In the desktop versions of Word, PowerPoint and Outlook, right-click a word and choose **Synonyms** for a quick list.
- **Shift+F7** opens the Thesaurus task pane (Windows Word shortcut list).
- The Office web apps do not have a synonym finder; Word for the web has no thesaurus.

## LibreOffice Writer
Click the word and choose **Tools > Language > Thesaurus** or press **Ctrl+F7** (Command+F7 on Mac); click an entry in Alternatives to copy it to the Replace with box, then **Replace**.

## Choose well
- Synonyms differ in register (purchase vs buy), connotation (thrifty vs stingy) and grammar. Read the sentence aloud after replacing.
- Prefer the plainer word in work writing; use the thesaurus for repetition and precision, not decoration.
- Check an unfamiliar synonym in a dictionary with example sentences before using it.

## Verify
- [ ] The replaced word fits the sentence's meaning and tone when read aloud.
