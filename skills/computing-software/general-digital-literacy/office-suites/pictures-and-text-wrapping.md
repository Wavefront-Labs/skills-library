# Pictures and text wrapping

**Goal:** images that sit where you want and do not jump around when text changes.

## Word
- Select the picture and use the **Layout Options** button or Picture Format > Wrap Text.
- **In Front of Text** plus **Fix position on page** keeps a picture at a set spot on the page.
- **Square** plus **Move with text** wraps text round the picture and lets it shift as text is added or deleted.
- **Move object with text** keeps an object attached to its paragraph; it only works for objects that are not In Line with Text and not at a fixed position, and turns on Move with Text in the Wrap Text menu.

## Google Docs
- Insert sources include stock images, upload from computer, web search, Drive and Google Photos.
- Click the image for **In line, Wrap text, Break text, Behind text, In front of text**; Wrap text and Break text let you choose whether the image moves with text or stays fixed on the page. **Image options > All image options** has Size & Rotation, Text Wrapping, Position and Recolor.
- Wrapping and position settings are unavailable in pageless documents; switch to pages format first.

## Alt text
Word and other Microsoft 365 apps: right-click the picture and choose **View Alt Text** or **Edit Alt Text**. Google Docs: Alt text command (Ctrl+Alt+Y).
