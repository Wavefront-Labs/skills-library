# Assign tasks with comments

## Microsoft 365 (files in the cloud)
1. Add a comment or reply, type `@` and the colleague's name, and tick **Assign to** before posting.
2. The person gets an email and sees the task in the comment; the task history is kept across reassignments.
3. Reassign with an @mention in a reply and the **Reassign to** box. The assignee marks it done with the check-mark circle on the comment; it can be reopened from the comments pane.
4. @mentions need modern comments (Microsoft 365 builds), files stored in the cloud, and a tenant that allows them; conditional-access tenants may block creating @mentions.

## Google Docs, Sheets, Slides
1. Add a comment, type the person's email address with `@` or `+` before it, tick **Assign to [name]** and click **Assign**.
2. The assignee gets an email. Reassign by replying with the new email and ticking **Reassign to [name]**. Click **Done** at the top right of the comment to close it.
3. Assigning from comments uses a work or school account. In Drive, files with action items show a **Follow up** number.
4. The comments panel has tabs for all comments or comments for you, and an **All types** filter for open or resolved.

## Making it work
- One clear action per comment: what, by when.
- The assignee needs access to the file.

## Gotchas
- Comments travel with copies sent externally; resolve or remove them before sharing (see the Document Inspector skill).

- [ ] Assignee confirms they received the notification and the task shows on the comment.
