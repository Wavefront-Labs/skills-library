# Read aloud and Immersive Reader

## Word
- **Review > Read Aloud** reads from the cursor. Windows shortcuts: **Ctrl+Alt+Space** starts it, **Ctrl+Space** plays or pauses. Playback speed is a control; **More (...) > Voice selection** changes voice.
- Read Aloud uses the document's proofing language, and is available in Office 2019, Office 2021 and Microsoft 365. On Mac you can use Read Aloud or Speech. **Speak** (Windows only) reads only selected text.
- **View > Immersive Reader** (Windows; on Mac **View > Learning Tools**): Column Width, Page Color, Line Focus (one, three or five lines), Text Spacing, Syllables and Read Aloud. Word for the web adds Picture Dictionary, Translate and Reading Coach under Reading Preferences.

## Other apps
- **Edge:** **Ctrl+Shift+U** (Command+Shift+U on Mac) starts or stops Read Aloud; **F9** enters or exits Immersive Reader.
- **Windows:** Narrator starts and stops with **Windows key+Ctrl+Enter**.

## Uses
- **Proofreading:** hearing text exposes missing and repeated words that eyes skip.
- **Reading support:** line focus and wider spacing reduce crowding.

## Gotchas
- A document with the wrong proofing language is read with the wrong pronunciation; set it first.

## Verify
- [ ] Read Aloud starts at the cursor and the chosen voice and speed are comfortable.
