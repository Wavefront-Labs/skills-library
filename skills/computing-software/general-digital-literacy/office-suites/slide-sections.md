# Slide sections and reordering

**Goal:** a 60-slide deck you can navigate and rearrange in chunks (Intro, Results, Q&A).

## PowerPoint
- Thumbnail pane: right-click between slides › **Add Section** › type a name › Rename. Mac: select the first
  slide of the section › Home › **Section** › Add Section.
- Click the triangle next to a section name to collapse or expand it; the number shows how many slides it holds.
- **View › Slide Sorter**: drag a section title to move the whole section; right-click › Delete to remove a section.
- PowerPoint for the web: add sections from Slide Sorter (right-click the first slide › Add Section).
- Present one part: a **Section Zoom** or **Summary Zoom** (Insert › Zoom), or a custom show.

## LibreOffice Impress
Right-click between two slides in the Slide pane › **Add Section** ("Untitled Section"); right-click the name ›
Rename Section, or Remove section (keeps the slides).

## Keynote
Keynote groups slides in the slide navigator; a collapsed group can be skipped as a whole by skipping its first
slide (see Apple's "Group or ungroup slides" topic).

## Google Slides
Use grid view (button at the bottom left) to see and drag many slides at once; a "divider" slide plus
skipped slides works as a sectioning workaround.

## Gotchas
- Co-authors see sections; assigning each colleague a section makes slide ownership clear.
