# Cell borders, fill and cell styles

## Excel
- **Home › Borders** arrow: pick a preset border for the selected cells.
- **More Borders...** opens Format Cells › *Border* tab: choose line style and colour under *Line*, then
  click a preset or the edges (diagonal borders are available there too).
- **Draw Borders** / **Draw Border Grid** for freehand, with *Line Color* and *Line Style* on the same
  menu; **Erase Border** to remove.
- **Cell Styles** (Home › Styles group): built-in styles such as those under *Good, Bad, and Neutral*.
  Right-click a style › *Modify* changes it wherever it is used; *Duplicate* makes a custom copy.

## Google Sheets
- **Format › Alternating colors** for banded rows, and the toolbar border and fill-colour controls for
  cell borders and border colour.

## Good practice
- Gridlines do not print unless you tick Page Layout › Sheet Options › Gridlines › **Print**; add borders
  to anything that must print as a grid.
- Light fills and thin grey borders read better than heavy black boxes.
- Don't rely on colour alone to carry meaning (colour-blind readers, black-and-white printing).
