# Themes and design ideas

**Goal:** a consistent look set once, not formatted slide by slide.

## Apply a theme
- **PowerPoint:** Design tab › Themes gallery. The Variants drop-down › **Colors**, **Fonts**, **Effects**,
  **Background Styles** (each with Customize options) sets them for the whole deck; Themes gallery ›
  *Save Current Theme* keeps your version.
- **Google Slides:** Slide › **Change theme** (right pane); *Import theme* at the bottom right reuses a theme
  from another Slides or PowerPoint file; Slide › Edit theme › Colors changes theme colours.
- **Impress:** Slide › **Change Slide Master** › Load, or the Master Slides deck in the sidebar.

## Designer / Design Suggestions
PowerPoint (Microsoft 365 subscribers): **Design › Design Suggestions** (formerly Designer) proposes layouts for
the current slide's pictures and text. It needs an internet connection and works best with a built-in theme
and the Title or Title + Content layout. Pick an idea, then check text is still in placeholders.

## Slide size
PowerPoint: Design › **Slide Size** › Custom Slide Size (width, height, orientation). Change it before
building content; when changing later, PowerPoint asks you to **Maximize** (content may fall off the slide)
or **Ensure Fit** (scales content down).

## Gotchas
- Fonts not on the presenting computer get replaced; embed them (Windows: File › Options › Save ›
  *Embed fonts in the file*; Mac: PowerPoint › Preferences › Save) or stick to common fonts. Some fonts'
  licences forbid embedding.

## Verify
- [ ] Switching to another theme variant recolours every slide consistently.
