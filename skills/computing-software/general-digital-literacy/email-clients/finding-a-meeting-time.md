# Finding a time for a meeting (Google Calendar)

**Goal:** pick a slot people can attend, without an email ping-pong.

## Find a time
1. Google Calendar (computer) > Create > Event > add guests > **Find a time**.
2. You can compare up to 50 guests; beyond 50, only 50 schedules are shown. Guests must have shared their calendars with you (use **Search for people** on the left to look someone up).
3. **Filter and view** shows or hides rooms and filters guests (No guests, Required guests, All guests). Add a room with Add rooms or Add room booking.
4. To compare schedules next to yours in the main grid: Settings > View options > **View calendars side by side in Day View**, then use Day view.

## Help it work
- Work accounts: set **working hours** (calendar.google.com in a computer browser). They are a recurring weekly schedule and adjust to your working location or time zone. If the option is missing, your admin may not have turned it on.
- Events appear in each invitee's own time zone, so "14:00 London" shows as 09:00 for someone in New York. State the zone in the invite text for external guests.

## Gotchas
- If the person has not shared their calendar you cannot see their availability; send a request to share, or ask them for times.

## Verify
- [ ] Before sending, no required guest shows a conflict in the chosen slot.
