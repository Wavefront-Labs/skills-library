# Unsubscribe and cut newsletter clutter

**Goal:** fewer unwanted emails, safely.

## Legitimate mailings
- Gmail and Outlook show an **Unsubscribe** link next to the sender name for mailing lists that
  support one-click unsubscribe; use that instead of hunting in the footer.
- **Gmail:** Menu › **Manage subscriptions** lists senders by volume with an Unsubscribe button.
- **Outlook.com / new Outlook:** **Sweep** (or right-click › Sweep): delete all from a sender and
  block future ones, or keep only the latest.
- **Apple Mail:** banner "This message is from a mailing list" › Unsubscribe.

## Filter what you still want
Send newsletters to a folder/label automatically: Gmail search `unsubscribe` or `from:news@…` ›
Create filter › Skip the Inbox, Apply label "Reading"; Outlook rules › Move to folder. Read them in
one batch weekly.

## Spam, not newsletters
If you never signed up and it looks shady, **don't click unsubscribe** (it confirms your address and
may be a phishing link). Mark as **Spam/Junk** and block the sender.

## Gotchas
- Unsubscribing can take up to 10 working days for some senders (allowed under US CAN-SPAM).
- Use a separate address or alias for shop sign-ups (Gmail `name+shop@gmail.com`, iCloud Hide My
  Email) so you can filter or abandon it.

## Verify
- [ ] Two weeks later, the top senders in Manage subscriptions/Sweep are ones you want.
