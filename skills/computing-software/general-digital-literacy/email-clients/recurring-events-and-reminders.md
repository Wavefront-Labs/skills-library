# Recurring events and reminders (Google Calendar)

**Goal:** routine meetings and bills appear automatically, with a reminder in time.

## Create a repeat
- Computer: Create, add the title and details, click the arrow next to **Does not repeat**, choose how often and when it ends, then Save.
- Existing event: click it > Edit > the same drop-down > Save.
- Repeating events are limited to 730 occurrences.

## Edit
After you change a repeating event and Save, Calendar asks which events in the series the update applies to. Choose only the occurrence for a one-off change and the series for a permanent one.

## Reminders
- Per event: open the event > Edit event > next to Notifications choose notification or email and the lead time; **Add notification** for more; Remove to delete one.
- Defaults: Settings > under Settings for my calendars choose the calendar > **Event notifications** and **All-day event notifications**.
- Notification settings are personal to your account. Desktop notifications need the browser to allow calendar.google.com and Calendar to be open.

## Gotchas
- Events with no end date accumulate; set an end and review periodically.

## Verify
- [ ] Look three months ahead: occurrences fall on the right days and the next reminder fires.
