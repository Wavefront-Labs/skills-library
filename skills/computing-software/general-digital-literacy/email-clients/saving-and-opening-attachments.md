# Save and open attachments

**Goal:** get the attached file into the right folder, and know where it went.

## Gmail (web)
- Hover over the attachment chip: **Download** (arrow) saves to your browser's Downloads folder; **Add to Drive** saves to Google Drive; click the chip to **preview** in the browser without downloading.
- Several attachments: **Download all** (zip) at the bottom of the message.
- Phone app: tap the attachment › download or save to Drive / Files.

## Outlook
- Attachment's down arrow › **Preview**, **Download/Save as**, **Save to OneDrive**. Classic desktop: right-click › *Save All Attachments*.
- Double-clicking opens a temporary copy in a hidden cache folder; edits to it are lost or hard to find. Save first, then open and edit.
- Office files from email open in **Protected View**; only click *Enable Editing* if you trust the sender.

## Apple Mail and iPhone
Mac: drag the attachment to a Finder folder, or **File › Save Attachments**. Mail downloads to `~/Downloads` by default (Mail Settings › General › Downloads folder). iPhone/iPad: touch and hold › **Save to Files** or Save Image.

## Where did it go?
- Browser downloads list: `Ctrl+J` (Windows) / `Cmd+Option+L` (Safari) › *Show in folder*.
- Sort the Downloads folder by Date modified, newest first.

## Safety before opening
- Expected file from a known sender? If not, confirm by another channel.
- Show file extensions; be wary of `.zip`, `.html`, `.iso`, `.exe`, `.js` and macro-enabled `.docm/.xlsm`.
- Preview in the browser first when unsure; don't enable macros.

## Verify
- [ ] File saved to a named project folder, not left in Downloads or the mail cache.
- [ ] Edited copy reopened from that folder shows your changes.
