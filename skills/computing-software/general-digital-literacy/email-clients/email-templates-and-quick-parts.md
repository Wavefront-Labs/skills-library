# Email templates (Gmail)

**Goal:** answer the same question for the tenth time in seconds.

## Gmail (computer only)
Templates can be turned on and used only from Gmail on a computer.
1. Settings > **See all settings** > **Advanced** > next to Templates click **Enable** > Save Changes.
2. Compose, type the template text, then at the bottom click **More options** > **Templates** > **Save draft as template** > **Save as new template**.
3. Change one: Save draft as template, then under Overwrite Template choose it and Save. Delete one: Templates > **Delete template** (deleted templates cannot be recovered).
4. Insert: Compose > More options > Templates > under Insert template choose one, finish the message and Send.
5. Automatic reply: Show search options > enter criteria > Create filter > tick **Send template** and choose it.

## Good templates
- Leave placeholders in brackets such as `[Name]` and `[date]`, and scan for leftovers before sending.
- Keep a short and a detailed version, and review them regularly so prices and links stay current.

## Verify
- [ ] Insert the template in a new message: subject and body arrive intact.
- [ ] No placeholder brackets left in the sent copy.
