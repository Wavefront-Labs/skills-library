# Sharing calendars and setting permissions

**Goal:** others see or edit what they need of your calendar, and no more.

## Google Calendar (computer)
- My calendars > hover over the calendar > **More** > **Settings and sharing** > **Shared with** > **Add people and groups** > enter the address > choose permission > Send.
- Permissions: **See only free/busy (hide details)**; **See event details**; **Make changes (see private events as free/busy)**; **Make changes and see event details**; **Make changes and manage sharing**.
- The person gets an email and must click its link to add your calendar. If you share a calendar someone else owns, you need Make changes and manage sharing.
- Everyone: under **Access permissions for events** choose **Make available to public** (people can find it in search and use it in other apps) or, for work or school, **Make available for {company name}**. Your admin may control sharing and may be able to see event details anyway.
- Stop sharing: turn off those settings or click Remove next to the person.
- Make a separate calendar (for example Family) and share that rather than your main calendar.

## Outlook on the web (Microsoft 365 / Exchange Online)
Choose permission per person: **Can view when I'm busy** (no event location or details), **Can view titles and locations**, **Can view all details**, **Can edit**, and a delegate option. Items marked Private show most people only the time, not title or location.

## Subscribe to a feed
Google: Other calendars + > **From URL** (only for public calendars; non-Google calendars cannot be subscribed to with "Subscribe to calendar").
