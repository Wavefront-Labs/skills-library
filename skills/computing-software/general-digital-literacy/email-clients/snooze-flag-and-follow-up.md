# Snooze and follow up (Gmail)

**Goal:** an inbox that holds only what needs attention now.

## Snooze (computer)
- Point to the email > on the right click **Snooze** > choose a later day and time. The email leaves the inbox and returns to the top at that time.
- Several messages: select them > **Snooze** at the top (if missing, More > Snooze).
- Find snoozed mail: left side > Mail > **Snoozed**, or search `in:snoozed`.

## Habits
- Snooze what you will act on at a known time; archive the rest.
- Review the Snoozed label when something you expected has not come back.

## Verify
- [ ] Your inbox shows only messages needing action now; the rest are snoozed with dates.
