# Manage contacts and the address book

## Add and edit (Google)
- Go to Google Contacts on a computer, click **Create contact** (or **Create multiple contacts**), enter details, Save.
- Contacts saved to your Google Account sync with Google Contacts and your Android devices.

## Automatic "Other contacts"
- If you email someone who is not a contact, Google Contacts saves their address in **Other contacts**, so it autocompletes next time.
- Stop this: Gmail > Settings > "Create contacts for auto-complete" > **I'll add contacts myself** > Save changes.
- Find or delete them: Google Contacts > **Other contacts** on the left; point to names, tick the boxes, then More > Delete. Other contacts are not shown on iPhone, iPad or Android.
- A contact's update history appears at the bottom right of the contact; older contacts may have none.

## Wrong autocomplete suggestion in Gmail
In the To field, right-click the suggested recipient's profile photo and click **Hide suggestion**; to find a hidden one, type the name and click **Show hidden suggestions**.

## Other apps
Outlook and Apple Contacts have their own add, merge and import/export features: check the vendor's help for exact menus rather than trusting remembered paths.

## Gotchas
- Pick **one** home account for new contacts so they are not scattered across accounts.
