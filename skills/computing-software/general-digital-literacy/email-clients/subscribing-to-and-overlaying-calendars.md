# Subscribe to and overlay calendars (Google Calendar)

## Subscribe to a public calendar by URL
- Computer only: next to **Other calendars** click Add other calendars > **From URL**, paste the published calendar URL, click **Add calendar**. It works only if the other person's calendar is public. The calendar then shows under Other calendars.
- You cannot subscribe to calendars in the Google Calendar mobile app, and you cannot subscribe to non-Google calendars with **Subscribe to calendar**.
- If you subscribe to more than 400 calendars you may see performance issues.

## Ask for access to someone's calendar
Other calendars > Add other calendars > **Subscribe to calendar** > enter the person's email. The owner gets an email and chooses what you can do: see only free/busy, see event details, make changes, or make changes and manage sharing.

## Import a file (one-off copy)
Computer: Settings > **Import & Export** > Select file from your computer (.ics or .csv) > choose the calendar > Import. Imported events do not stay in sync with the source; repeat events imported from .csv may arrive as separate one-time events.

## Overlay and tidy
- Tick or untick calendars in the left list to show or hide them without unsubscribing.
- To see calendars side by side: Settings > View options > **View calendars side by side in Day View**.

## Verify
- [ ] The calendar shows on the computer and the phone, and hides when unticked.
