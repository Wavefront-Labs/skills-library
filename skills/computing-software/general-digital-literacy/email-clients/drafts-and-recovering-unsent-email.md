# Drafts and recovering unsent messages

## Lost a message?
- Check **Drafts**, then **Outbox**, then **Sent**, then **Deleted/Trash**. Gmail's own guidance for a missing message is to check Sent and Drafts first; you may have deleted it before sending.
- With several accounts, look in the Drafts of the right account.
- Writing long or important emails: draft in a document or notes app and paste at the end.

## Undo a send (Gmail)
Right after sending, "Message sent" appears bottom left with **Undo**. In Settings > See all settings, "Undo Send" lets you pick a cancellation period of 5, 10, 20 or 30 seconds; click Save changes.

## Mail that did not send
- Make sure you are connected to Wi-Fi or mobile data; a slow connection can delay sending.
- If you sent through an email client such as Apple Mail or Outlook and it is stuck, Google suggests sending from the Gmail app or mail.google.com instead.
- Wait a few hours for a delivery error message; ask the recipient to check Junk/Spam.

## Verify
- [ ] Outbox is empty and the message appears in Sent.
