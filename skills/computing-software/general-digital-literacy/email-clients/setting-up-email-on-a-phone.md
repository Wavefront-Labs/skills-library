# Setting up email on a phone

**Goal:** email on your phone that syncs, notifies sensibly and is protected.

## iPhone
- **Mail app:** Settings › Apps › Mail › Mail Accounts › Add Account (older iOS: Settings › Mail ›
  Accounts) › choose Google / Microsoft Exchange / iCloud / Outlook.com / Other.
- Or install the **Gmail** or **Outlook** app from the App Store and sign in.

## Android
- Gmail app is built in: profile picture › **Add another account** › Google / Outlook, Hotmail and
  Live / Exchange and Microsoft 365 / Other.
- **Outlook** app from Google Play for work accounts.

## Work accounts
Many organisations require the **Outlook** app and **Microsoft Authenticator** or Intune Company
Portal. Follow the IT instructions; you may be asked to set a screen lock and allow management of
the work profile (not your personal data).

## Notifications
- Turn notifications on only for important accounts or VIPs (iOS Mail: VIP list; Gmail: Settings ›
  account › Notifications › *High priority only*; Outlook: *Focused inbox only*).
- Check: phone Settings › Notifications › app.

## Security
- Phone **passcode/biometrics** required: anyone with your unlocked phone can reset your passwords
  through email.
- Turn on Find My iPhone / Find My Device for remote wipe if lost.
