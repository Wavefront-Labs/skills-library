# Conversation view and threading

## Turn it on or off
- **Gmail**: Settings (gear) › Quick settings › **Conversation view** toggle (web); app: Settings ›
  your account › Conversation view.
- **Outlook (new/web)**: View › **Layout › Message organisation**: *Show email grouped by
  conversation* / *Show email as individual messages*. Classic: View › **Show as Conversations**
  (choose this folder or all folders).
- **Apple Mail**: View › **Organise by Conversation**. iPhone: Settings › Mail › Organise by Thread.

## Working with threads
- Newest message at top or bottom: Outlook reading pane setting; Gmail always oldest-first with older
  ones collapsed.
- Mute a noisy thread: Gmail `m` (Mute); Outlook **Ignore** (classic) moves current and future
  messages to Deleted Items.
- Reply to one specific message in the thread: expand it and use that message's Reply.

## Problems
- Gmail groups by subject + references; same subject from different people can merge ("Invoice").
  Turn off conversation view or change the subject when starting a new topic.
- More than 100 messages: Gmail starts a new conversation.
- Deleting a conversation deletes every message in it; expand and delete individually if needed.
- Filters/rules apply per message, so parts of a thread can be in different folders (Outlook shows
  them greyed from other folders).

## Verify
- [ ] Your preferred view is consistent on phone and computer.
