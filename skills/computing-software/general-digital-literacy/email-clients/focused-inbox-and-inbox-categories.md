# Focused inbox and inbox categories

**Goal:** important mail is on top; newsletters and notifications wait until you want them.

## Outlook Focused Inbox
- Classic Outlook: View tab > **Show Focused Inbox**; the Focused and Other tabs appear at the top. In new Outlook, select the Focused or Other tab on the inbox.
- Retrain: right-click a message in either tab. Move > **Move to Other inbox** or **Move to Focused inbox**; choose **Always move to ...** to apply to all future mail from that sender.
- In Outlook for Windows, Focused Inbox is available only for Microsoft 365, Exchange and Outlook.com accounts.

## Gmail categories (computer)
- With the **Default** inbox type, Gmail sorts mail into Primary, Social, Promotions, Updates and Forums. You can only choose from these; you cannot create categories.
- Show or hide categories: Settings > Quick settings > Inbox type > Default > **Customize**, tick categories, Save.
- Retrain by dragging a message to another tab (Undo appears at bottom left), or create a filter for a sender.
- Search with `category:social` and similar. Archived mail does not appear in category tabs; use the All mail label.
- New-mail notifications cover only the Primary category. The Default inbox type cannot be used with more than 250,000 emails in the inbox.
- Use **labels** to organise beyond the five categories.

## Gotchas
- Receipts and password resets can land in Other, Promotions or Updates; search before assuming mail never arrived.
