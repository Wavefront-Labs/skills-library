# Tasks and to-dos alongside the calendar

## Outlook / Microsoft To Do
- Flagged emails appear as tasks in **To Do** (Outlook's *My Day* pane and the To Do app) under *Flagged email*.
- New Outlook: **My Day** (top right) shows today's events and tasks; drag a task onto the calendar to block time for it.
- Tasks assigned to you from Planner or document comments appear under *Assigned to me*.

## Gmail and Google Calendar
- Side panel **Tasks** icon in Gmail; drag an email into Tasks to create a task linked to that message.
- Tasks with a date show in **Google Calendar** (enable *Tasks* under My calendars); mark complete from the calendar.
- Repeat tasks: set *Repeat* when adding a date.

## Apple
- **Reminders** syncs via iCloud; reminders with a date/time show in Calendar when the Reminders calendar is enabled (macOS Sonoma and iOS 17+).
- Siri: "remind me to call the bank tomorrow at 9".
- Mail: *Remind Me* on a message brings it back to the top of the inbox at a chosen time.

## A working method
- Capture everything into one list, not three apps.
- Add a due date only when there is a real deadline; otherwise use priority or *My Day*.
- Time-block: drag big tasks into a calendar slot so meetings don't eat the time.
- Weekly review: clear completed items, reschedule the rest.
