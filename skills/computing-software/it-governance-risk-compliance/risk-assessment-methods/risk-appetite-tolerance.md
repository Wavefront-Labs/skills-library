# Risk appetite, tolerance and capacity
**Goal:** thresholds that drive decisions.
- Appetite: the amount and type of risk the organisation will pursue (board level). Tolerance: acceptable deviation per objective, often numeric (e.g. annual loss above a stated amount escalates). Capacity: maximum it can bear.
- Map the risk matrix colours or loss bands to approval authority: who may accept a medium, high, critical.
- CSF 2.0 GV.RM and ISO 27001 6.1.2 acceptance criteria both require this.

## Self-check
Take a high risk and show who may accept it and where that is documented.
