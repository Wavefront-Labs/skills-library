# The 800-30 assessment process
**Goal:** follow a recognised method end to end.
- Prepare: purpose, scope, assumptions and constraints, information sources, risk model, analytic approach (threat-oriented, vulnerability-oriented or asset/impact-oriented).
- Conduct: identify threat sources and events; identify vulnerabilities and predisposing conditions; determine likelihood; determine impact; determine risk.
- Communicate and share results; maintain by monitoring.
- Risk is assessed at three tiers (organization, mission/business process, information system), per SP 800-39.

## Self-check
Produce the assessment plan page listing scope, assumptions and risk model.
