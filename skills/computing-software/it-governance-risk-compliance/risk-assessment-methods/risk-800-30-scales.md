# Qualitative and semi-quantitative scales
**Goal:** repeatable scoring.
- Qualitative: Very Low, Low, Moderate, High, Very High. Semi-quantitative maps to 0-100 or 0-10 bands.
- Likelihood has two parts: that threat events are initiated (adversarial) or occur (non-adversarial), and that they result in adverse impact; combine them.
- Appendix tables list threat sources, events, vulnerabilities, impact and risk combinations; adapt but document changes.

## Self-check
Score one scenario twice (qualitative and semi-quantitative) and explain any difference.
