# Treatment options and acceptance
**Goal:** documented, approved decisions.
- Mitigate: add or improve controls; transfer: insurance or contract (liability does not transfer fully; reputational risk stays); avoid: stop the activity; accept: formal, time-limited, with owner and approver.
- Cost-benefit: control cost versus reduction in expected loss (FAIR or ALE).
- Acceptance record: risk, residual score, rationale, approver, expiry date, review trigger.

## Gotchas
- Acceptance without expiry becomes silent permanent risk.

## Self-check
Show a risk acceptance and its expiry date.
