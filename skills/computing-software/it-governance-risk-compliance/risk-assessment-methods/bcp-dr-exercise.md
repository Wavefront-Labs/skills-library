# Continuity plans and exercises
**Goal:** plans proven by exercise.
- Plan content: invocation criteria, roles and alternates, contact tree, recovery sequence by dependency, workarounds, vendor contacts.
- Exercise types: walkthrough, tabletop, simulation, full failover; record results, time to recover versus RTO, and corrective actions.
- Annual minimum for critical services is common practice.

## Self-check
Show last exercise results with measured recovery time against RTO and the actions raised.
