# Vendor tiering and ongoing monitoring
**Goal:** lifecycle coverage.
- Tier by data sensitivity, access to systems, business criticality and substitutability; tier sets depth and frequency (e.g. annual for critical, longer for low).
- Monitor: report expiries, breach news, external security ratings (treat as signal, verify), financial health.
- Offboarding: revoke access, retrieve or certify deletion of data, update inventory.
