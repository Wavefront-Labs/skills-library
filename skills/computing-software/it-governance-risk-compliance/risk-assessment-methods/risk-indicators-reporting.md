# Key risk indicators and reporting
**Goal:** early-warning metrics.
- KRI properties: predictive, measurable, thresholds (green/amber/red), owner, source. Examples: percentage critical assets with overdue vulnerabilities, privileged accounts without MFA, mean time to detect, overdue third-party reviews.
- Report: top risks, movement since last period, risks outside appetite, treatment progress.

## Self-check
For each KRI show threshold, current value, and the action triggered on breach.
