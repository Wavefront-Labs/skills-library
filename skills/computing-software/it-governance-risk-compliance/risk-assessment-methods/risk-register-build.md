# Building and maintaining a risk register
**Goal:** a live register, not a spreadsheet museum.
- Columns: ID, scenario, asset/process, owner, inherent L/I/score, existing controls, residual score, treatment decision, actions, due date, status, last reviewed, linked controls and audit findings.
- Review high risks monthly, others quarterly; re-score after incidents and major changes.
- Report top risks and trend to the risk committee; keep closed risks for history.

## Self-check
Sort by residual score and verify every risk above appetite has an approved plan or acceptance.
