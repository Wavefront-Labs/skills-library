# TOGAF 10 Standard structure and fundamental content

Goal: know where to look in the TOGAF 10 Standard and what is core versus supporting.

- The Standard is modular. Fundamental content covers Introduction and Core Concepts, the ADM, ADM Techniques, Applying the ADM, Architecture Content, and Enterprise Architecture Capability and Governance.
- Series Guides add depth on topics such as digital, security, SOA and business architecture, and are updated independently.
- The ADM is the method; the Content Framework says what you produce; the Continuum and Repository say where assets live; the Capability Framework says how the team is run.
- Use the online edition, which is easier to track than the 9.2 single document.

Gotchas:
- TOGAF 9.2 phase names and artifact lists differ slightly from 10; check the edition before quoting.
- TOGAF is a framework to be tailored, not a mandatory process.

Verify: you can state the purpose of each fundamental part in one sentence and point to the matching ADM phase.
