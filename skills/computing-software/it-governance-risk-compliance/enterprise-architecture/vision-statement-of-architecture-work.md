# Develop the vision and Statement of Architecture Work

Goal: an approved, concise picture of the target and the plan to get there.

- Capture business goals, drivers and constraints that bound the work.
- Sketch baseline and target at a high level (diagram plus short narrative).
- Define value propositions and measurable KPIs per stakeholder group.
- Identify business transformation risks and mitigations.
- Write the Statement of Architecture Work: scope, approach, roles, schedule, acceptance criteria.
- Obtain sponsor approval before Phase B.

Gotchas:
- A vision that is only a diagram without outcomes will not be funded.
- Keep it short; if it needs 40 pages it is already Phase B.

Verify: sponsor sign-off on the Statement of Architecture Work is recorded.
