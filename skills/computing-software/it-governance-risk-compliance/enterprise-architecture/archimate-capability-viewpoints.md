# Strategy viewpoints: Capability Map, Resource Map, Value Stream and Outcome Realization

Goal: pick the strategy viewpoint to fit the concern.

- Strategy: overview of courses of action, capabilities, resources.
- Capability Map: nested capabilities, heat colouring for attributes.
- Outcome Realization: from outcomes via capabilities and projects.
- Resource Map: resources and their relation to capabilities.
- Value Stream: stages with the capabilities they need.

Gotchas:
- Ensure capability maps are mutually exclusive and collectively exhaustive at one level.

Verify: map levels are consistent and named with nouns.
