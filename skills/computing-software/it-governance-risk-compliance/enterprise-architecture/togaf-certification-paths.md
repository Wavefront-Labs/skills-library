# TOGAF certification Foundation and Practitioner

Goal: pass the TOGAF exams with correct study focus.

- Foundation (Part 1) tests knowledge of terminology, core concepts, ADM phases, ADM guidelines and techniques, content framework, continuum, governance and the capability framework; multiple choice.
- Practitioner (Part 2) tests applying the Standard to scenarios; scenario-based questions with an open reference to the Standard.
- Combined exam routes also exist; confirm current formats, durations and pass marks with The Open Group.
- Study order: ADM, then techniques, then content and governance; practise scenarios.

Gotchas:
- Memorise phase objectives and outputs for Foundation; practise reading the Standard quickly for Practitioner.

Verify: you score consistently above the pass mark on practice papers.
