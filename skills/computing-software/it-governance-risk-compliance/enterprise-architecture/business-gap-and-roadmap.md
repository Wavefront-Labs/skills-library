# Business gap analysis and candidate roadmap components

Goal: a prioritised list of what must change in the business.

- Run gap analysis (see the gap analysis topic): carried over, eliminated, new.
- Describe each gap as a candidate roadmap component with effort and benefit estimates.
- Resolve impacts on other architectures and on the wider landscape.
- Review formally with stakeholders and update the Architecture Requirements Specification.

Gotchas:
- Gaps expressed as technology items instead of business changes skew the roadmap.
- Impacts on adjacent enterprises or units are often overlooked.

Verify: gap register is complete, reviewed and linked to requirements.
