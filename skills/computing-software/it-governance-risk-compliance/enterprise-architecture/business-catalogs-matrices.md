# Business architecture catalogs, matrices and diagrams

Goal: produce only the artifacts that answer stakeholder concerns.

Typical set: Organization/Actor catalog, Driver/Goal/Objective catalog, Role catalog, Business Service/Function catalog, Location catalog, Process/Event/Control/Product catalog; Business Interaction matrix, Actor/Role matrix; Business Footprint, Business Service/Information, Functional Decomposition, Product Lifecycle, Goal/Objective/Service, Business Use-Case, Organization Decomposition, Process Flow, Event diagrams.

Gotchas:
- Catalogs are lists, matrices show relationships, diagrams show structure; do not mix purposes.
- Keep names consistent with the Architecture Repository so reuse works.

Verify: each artifact maps to a stakeholder concern from Phase A.
