# Scope the enterprise and confirm context

Goal: agree what enterprise and what mandate the EA effort has.

1. Identify the sponsor and the executive who will arbitrate disputes.
2. List organisational units, locations and partners in or out of scope.
3. Capture business drivers, strategy documents and regulatory pressures.
4. Review existing frameworks, methods and repositories already in use.
5. Record the mandate in the Request for Architecture Work.

Gotchas:
- Scoping to the whole group on day one rarely works; start with a bounded unit.
- Without a named sponsor, governance later has no teeth.

Verify: a one-page scope statement with sponsor sign-off exists.
