# Capability heat map and investment prioritisation

Goal: show where investment matters.

1. Use the capability map as the canvas.
2. Rate each capability for strategic importance and for current performance (use 1-5 or RAG).
3. Shade by gap = importance minus performance.
4. Choose the top gaps and link to strategy themes.
5. Validate with executives.

Gotchas:
- Ratings need evidence such as KPIs.
- Avoid rating at inconsistent levels.

Verify: the top priorities match the initiatives funded.
