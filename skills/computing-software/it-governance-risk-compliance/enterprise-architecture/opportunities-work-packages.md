# Identify work packages and implementation strategy

Goal: turn gaps from B, C, D into projects the business can fund.

1. Consolidate gap analysis across domains and remove duplicates.
2. Group related gaps into work packages and portfolio projects.
3. Decide build, buy or reuse for each SBB; check against the Solutions Continuum.
4. Choose strategy: greenfield, revolutionary, or evolutionary phased.
5. Identify dependencies and constraints between packages.

Gotchas:
- Grouping by technology rather than outcome delivers no usable change.
- Reconcile with in-flight projects before inventing new ones.

Verify: every gap is covered by a work package or explicitly accepted.
