# Business transformation readiness assessment

Goal: expose whether the organisation can absorb the change.

- Identify readiness factors (vision, desire and willingness, ability, capacity, funding, sponsorship, governance, skills).
- Score each factor, for example on a 1-5 scale, with evidence.
- Record actions to close the most important gaps and assign owners.
- Re-run at Phase E and F to confirm assumptions before committing the migration plan.

Gotchas:
- Scores without evidence are opinions; interview at least two levels.
- Do not hide low readiness; it should shape the roadmap sequencing.

Verify: a readiness table with scores, evidence and actions is attached to the Architecture Vision.
