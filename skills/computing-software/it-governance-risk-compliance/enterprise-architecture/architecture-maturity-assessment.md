# Architecture maturity assessment

Goal: know where the practice stands and what to improve next.

- Choose a model (the TOGAF architecture maturity model, a CMMI-style scale or a local one).
- Score dimensions such as process, governance, tools, skills, business engagement and repository.
- Interview stakeholders and sample projects for evidence.
- Set 12 month target levels and a handful of improvement actions.
- Re-assess annually.

Gotchas:
- Higher maturity is not always better; target what the business needs.

Verify: an improvement plan with owners exists and is tracked.
