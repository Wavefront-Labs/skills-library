# Reference models: TRM and III-RM

Goal: reuse generic taxonomies.

- TOGAF TRM: a taxonomy of platform services (application platform, communications infrastructure) with qualities; use it to check that all needed services are covered.
- III-RM: a specialisation focusing on boundaryless information flow, separating Business Applications, Infrastructure Applications and the application platform.
- Use as checklists and naming sources; map to your own standards.

Gotchas:
- The models are dated in parts; supplement with cloud-native services (confirm what the 10th edition retains).

Verify: each platform service in your target maps to a model category or an explicit extension.
