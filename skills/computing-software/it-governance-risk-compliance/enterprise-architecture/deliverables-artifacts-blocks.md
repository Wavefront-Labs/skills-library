# Deliverables, artifacts and building blocks

Goal: use the three content terms correctly.

- Deliverable: a work product that is contractually specified, reviewed and signed off (for example Architecture Definition Document).
- Artifact: a more granular architectural work product describing the architecture from a viewpoint: catalog (list), matrix (relationship grid), diagram (picture).
- Building block: a reusable component of capability, either ABB or SBB.

Gotchas:
- A diagram on its own is not a deliverable until reviewed and approved.
- Keep versions and approval status visible.

Verify: your deliverable list shows for each its artifacts, owner and approver.
