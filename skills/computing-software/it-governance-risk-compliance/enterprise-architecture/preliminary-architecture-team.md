# Establish the architecture organisation

Goal: the people and decision rights are in place before Phase A.

- Define roles: chief architect, domain architects, solution architects, repository custodian.
- Charter the Architecture Board with membership, quorum and decision scope.
- Draft initial principles (see the architecture principles topic).
- Set up a RACI for deliverables and for compliance review.
- Agree capacity: who is available part-time versus full-time.

Gotchas:
- A board made only of IT staff loses business legitimacy.
- Principles invented without business input are ignored.

Verify: charter, RACI and first principles are approved by the sponsor.
