# Assess and heat-map capabilities

Goal: use the map to decide investment.

- Define scales (1-5 maturity; importance high/medium/low).
- Gather ratings from business owners and evidence from KPIs.
- Layer attributes: maturity, importance, cost, risk, application count.
- Present as colour overlays; discuss outliers.
- Link capabilities to initiatives and applications.

Gotchas:
- Self-assessment bias; calibrate across owners.

Verify: the top five gaps align with executive priorities.
