# Develop baseline and target business architecture

Goal: a baseline and target description of strategy, organisation, processes and capabilities.

1. Select reference models, viewpoints and tools from the Preliminary tailoring.
2. Describe the baseline only as far as needed to support the gap analysis.
3. Describe the target with capability maps, value streams, process and organisation views.
4. Check target against principles and the business goals from Phase A.
5. Record decisions and assumptions.

Gotchas:
- Over-documenting the baseline is the classic time sink.
- Business architecture owners must be business people, not only architects.

Verify: each target element traces to a business goal or requirement.
