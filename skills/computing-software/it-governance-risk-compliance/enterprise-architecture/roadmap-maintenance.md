# Maintain and communicate the roadmap

Goal: roadmap remains trustworthy.

- Review quarterly with portfolio management.
- Track status (on track, at risk, late) and re-forecast.
- Version each baseline and keep a change log with reasons.
- Publish a view per audience (executive, delivery, operations).

Gotchas:
- Silent edits destroy trust; always log change.

Verify: stakeholders can see the last three versions and why they changed.
