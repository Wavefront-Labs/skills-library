# Architecture compliance review and levels

Goal: a consistent way to judge conformance to the architecture.

TOGAF 9.2 describes these levels: Irrelevant, Consistent, Compliant, Conformant, Fully Conformant, Non-conformant (confirm any renaming in the 10th edition). Use them as a vocabulary, not a score.

Process: project prepares a self-assessment; architect reviews against principles, standards and the ADD; outcome is approve, approve with conditions or reject; deviations go to the Architecture Board for dispensation.

Gotchas:
- Review early; late reviews become vetoes.
- Record dispensations with expiry so they are not permanent.

Verify: compliance checklist and decision log exist for each reviewed project.
