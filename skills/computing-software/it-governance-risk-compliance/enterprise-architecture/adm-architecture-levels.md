# Architecture landscape: strategic, segment and capability levels

Goal: right level of detail for the audience and horizon.

- Strategic: enterprise direction over a long horizon, high level.
- Segment: a business area or domain, medium horizon.
- Capability: one capability increment, delivered in projects.
- Partition the Architecture Landscape by these levels and by organisation boundaries.
- Use the same ADM at each level with different depth.

Gotchas:
- Segment architectures can contradict strategic direction; reconcile in governance.
- Avoid duplicating the same content at two levels.

Verify: each architecture in the repository is labelled by level and owner.
