# Architecture risk management technique

Goal: manage architecture and transformation risk.

- Risk categories: initial risk (before mitigation), residual risk (after).
- Classify effect: catastrophic, critical, marginal, negligible. Classify frequency: frequent, likely, possible, unlikely, rare.
- Combine into a risk level, then plan mitigation.
- Maintain a risk register with owner, trigger and status; review at each phase end.
- Record risks in the Architecture Vision and the roadmap.

Gotchas:
- Risks outside IT (change fatigue, regulation) matter as much.

Verify: top ten risks each have a mitigation owner and date.
