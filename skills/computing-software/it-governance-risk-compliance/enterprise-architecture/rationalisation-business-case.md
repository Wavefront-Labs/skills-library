# Consolidation, retirement and migration business cases

Goal: fund rationalisation with real numbers.

- Savings: licences, infrastructure, support, integration.
- Costs: migration, data conversion, training, parallel run, contract exit.
- Risks: data loss, user adoption, contractual obligations, regulatory retention.
- Decommission steps: freeze, migrate or archive data, redirect interfaces, remove access, terminate contracts, update CMDB.

Gotchas:
- Savings are often not realised unless contracts and infrastructure are actually removed.

Verify: benefits are tracked against the baseline after decommissioning.
