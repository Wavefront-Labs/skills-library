# Regulatory and standards compliance in architecture

Goal: compliance is designed in.

- List applicable regulations and standards with owners.
- Map each obligation to business processes, data entities, applications and technology controls.
- Add compliance requirements to the Architecture Requirements Specification.
- Define evidence sources and review cycles.
- Include in compliance reviews.

Gotchas:
- Cross-border data flows change the technology and hosting decisions (confirm legal advice).

Verify: each obligation has a mapped component and evidence location.
