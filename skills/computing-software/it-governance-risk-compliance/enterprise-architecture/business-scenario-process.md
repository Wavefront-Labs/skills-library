# Develop a business scenario

Goal: elicit requirements from a concrete story.

1. Identify, document and rank the business problem driving the scenario.
2. Document the business and technology environment.
3. Document objectives (measurable outcomes).
4. Identify human and computer actors and their roles.
5. Document the scenario steps (who does what, where).
6. Check for fitness for purpose and refine.
7. Derive requirements from the steps and objectives.

Gotchas:
- Scenarios should be specific enough to be testable.
- Include exceptional paths.

Verify: each requirement traces back to a scenario step or objective.
