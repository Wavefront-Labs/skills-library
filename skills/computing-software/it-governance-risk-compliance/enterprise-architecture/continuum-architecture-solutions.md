# Architecture Continuum and Solutions Continuum

Goal: place assets on the continuum to reuse general assets and specialise them.

- Architecture Continuum (abstract): Foundation, Common Systems, Industry, Organization-Specific architectures.
- Solutions Continuum (concrete): matching Foundation, Common Systems, Industry and Organization-Specific solutions.
- Architectures guide solutions; solutions feed back as proven patterns.
- In the ADM, start from the most general suitable asset and specialise.

Gotchas:
- A continuum is a classification model, not a physical folder structure.
- Do not treat vendor products as architectures.

Verify: each reference asset in your repository carries a continuum position.
