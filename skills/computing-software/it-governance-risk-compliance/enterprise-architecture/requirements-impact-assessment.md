# Requirements impact assessment and change handling

Goal: change is handled deliberately rather than by drift.

1. Log the new or changed requirement with its source.
2. Identify affected architecture elements through traceability.
3. Estimate rework cost and schedule impact.
4. Decide with the sponsor or board: accept now, defer, reject.
5. Update the Architecture Requirements Specification and notify stakeholders.

Gotchas:
- Silent scope creep appears as late rework in G.
- Keep a decision log with date and approver.

Verify: each approved change shows a link to updated artifacts.
