# Applying the ADM for security, SOA and digital contexts

Goal: add the right concerns to the ADM for specialised work.

- Security: add security stakeholders in A, threat and control views in B to D, security requirements in Requirements Management.
- SOA: add service catalogs, contract-first interfaces and governance of service lifecycle.
- Digital: add customer journeys, platform thinking, experimentation and data products.
- Check the relevant TOGAF Series Guide for the exact artifacts and steps (confirm current titles).

Gotchas:
- Do not create a parallel method; extend the existing one.

Verify: the tailored method document lists the extra steps and artifacts.
