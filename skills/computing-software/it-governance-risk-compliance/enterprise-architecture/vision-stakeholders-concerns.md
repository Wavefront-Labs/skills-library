# Identify stakeholders, concerns and business requirements

Goal: know who must be satisfied and what they care about.

1. List stakeholders (sponsor, business owners, delivery, operations, regulators, suppliers).
2. For each, record concerns in their own words and rate interest versus influence.
3. Convert concerns to requirements or questions the architecture must answer.
4. Pick the viewpoints that answer each concern.
5. Review the list with the sponsor before drafting the vision.

Gotchas:
- Concerns are not requirements; keep them traceable but separate.
- Missing operations or security stakeholders causes late rework.

Verify: every concern maps to a viewpoint and every viewpoint to a planned view.
