# Interoperability levels and determining requirements

Goal: define how much systems and organisations must interwork.

- Dimensions: technical (protocols, formats), information (shared meaning), operational (processes), organisational (governance, trust).
- Determine degree: from exchange of messages to full integrated operation.
- Identify partners and their standards.
- Capture requirements as part of Phase A and refine in B to D.

Gotchas:
- Semantics fail more often than transport.

Verify: each interface has stated formats, semantics owner and version policy.
