# Resources, competence, awareness, documented information (7)
**Goal:** satisfy clause 7 records.
- 7.2 competence: determine necessary competence, ensure it, act to acquire it, keep evidence (training records, certifications).
- 7.3 awareness: policy, personal contribution, implications of nonconformity.
- 7.4 communication: what, when, with whom, how (2022 adds who communicates).
- 7.5 documented information: creation, update and control (version, access, retention, disposal). Mandatory records include scope, policy, risk method, SoA, treatment plan, objectives, competence evidence, audit and review results.

## Gotchas
- Uncontrolled copies and missing version history on policies.

List the mandatory documented information and show the current approved version of each.
