# Establishing the ISMS and its processes (4.4)
**Goal:** show the ISMS as operating processes, not a binder.
- 4.4 requires the needed processes and their interactions to be determined (explicit in the 2022 text); map them: risk assessment, treatment, internal audit, management review, incident, change, supplier, training.
- Per process record owner, inputs, outputs, frequency and records; keep a master calendar (risk review, audit, management review).

## Gotchas
- Policies with no operating process behind them are the commonest nonconformity source.

## Self-check
For each process, produce its last executed record dated within the defined frequency.
