# Certification audit stages and cycle
**Goal:** know the certification cycle.
- Use an accredited certification body (accreditation to ISO/IEC 17021-1 with ISO/IEC 27006 competence). Stage 1 reviews documentation readiness; stage 2 audits implementation; certificate valid three years.
- Surveillance audits at least annually; recertification audit before expiry.
- Findings: major nonconformity blocks certification until corrected; minor needs a corrective action plan; observations are advisory.
- Before stage 2: complete one internal audit and one management review, finalise the SoA.
