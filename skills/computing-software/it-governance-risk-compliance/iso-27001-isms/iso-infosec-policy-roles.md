# Information security policy and roles (5.2-5.3)
**Goal:** a policy that meets 5.2 and roles that meet 5.3.
- 5.2 policy: appropriate to the purpose, includes objectives or a framework for setting them, commits to satisfying applicable requirements and to continual improvement; documented, communicated within the organisation, available to interested parties as appropriate.
- 5.3: assign and communicate responsibility and authority for ISMS conformance and for reporting performance to top management (ISMS manager, risk owners, control owners).
- Hierarchy: policy, topic-specific policies (Annex A 5.1), standards, procedures.

## Gotchas
- Policy approved but never communicated; keep distribution and acknowledgement records.

## Self-check
Check each topic-specific policy has an owner, approval date and review date.
