# Risk assessment process (6.1.2)
**Goal:** a documented method that yields comparable results.
- Set criteria first: risk acceptance criteria and criteria for performing assessments (likelihood and impact scales).
- The process must produce consistent, valid, comparable results; identify risks from loss of confidentiality, integrity and availability within scope; identify risk owners.
- Analyse (likelihood, consequences), then evaluate against acceptance criteria and prioritise.
- The 2022 text does not mandate an asset-threat-vulnerability method; ISO/IEC 27005 gives guidance (asset-based or event-based).
- Output: risk register with owner, score, treatment decision.

## Gotchas
- Different assessors scoring the same scenario differently signals a method gap; calibrate with worked examples.

## Self-check
Have two people score the same three risks independently and compare.
