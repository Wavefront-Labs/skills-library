# Monitoring, measurement, analysis (9.1)
**Goal:** metrics that show whether the ISMS works.
- Determine what to monitor and measure (processes and controls), methods giving valid results, when, who monitors, when results are analysed and by whom.
- Mix process KPIs and risk indicators: incident count and time to contain, patch latency, access review completion, training completion, audit finding closure age.

## Self-check
Pick one metric and reproduce its value from raw data.
