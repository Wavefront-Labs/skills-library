# Context and interested parties (4.1-4.2)
**Goal:** produce the clause 4.1/4.2 inputs auditors expect.
- 4.1: list internal/external issues relevant to the ISMS purpose (regulation, customers, tech estate, culture, threat landscape). Amendment 1:2024 added: decide whether climate change is a relevant issue (4.1) and whether interested parties have climate-related requirements (4.2).
- 4.2: table of interested party, their security requirement, source (law, contract, expectation), how addressed.
- Include legal, regulatory and contractual requirements; they feed the risk assessment and Annex A control 5.31.
- Review at least annually and at management review; keep as documented information.

## Gotchas
- A generic PESTLE dump with no link to security requirements fails to show relevance.
- Customer contract clauses are interested-party requirements; trace them to controls.

## Self-check
Pick three requirements from the table and show which risk, objective or control each drives.
