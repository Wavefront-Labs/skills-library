# A.7 physical controls (7.1-7.14)
**Goal:** the fourteen physical controls.
- 7.1 perimeters; 7.2 entry; 7.3 offices and facilities; 7.4 physical security monitoring (new); 7.5 environmental threats; 7.6 secure areas; 7.7 clear desk and screen; 7.8 equipment siting; 7.9 off-premises assets; 7.10 storage media; 7.11 utilities; 7.12 cabling; 7.13 equipment maintenance; 7.14 secure disposal or reuse.
- Evidence: access logs, CCTV retention policy, visitor logs, disposal certificates, UPS and generator tests.

## Self-check
Walk the site and verify two SoA controls against what is physically present.
