# A.5 incident, continuity, legal and compliance
**Goal:** cover resilience and compliance controls.
- 5.24 incident planning; 5.25 assess events; 5.26 respond; 5.27 learn; 5.28 evidence collection; 5.29 security during disruption; 5.30 ICT readiness for business continuity (new); 5.31 legal, statutory, regulatory, contractual requirements; 5.32 IP; 5.33 protection of records; 5.34 privacy and PII; 5.35 independent review; 5.36 compliance with policies; 5.37 documented operating procedures.
- Evidence: incident log with post-incident reviews, DR test results against RTO/RPO, legal register.

Show one incident from detection to lessons learned and one DR test result.
