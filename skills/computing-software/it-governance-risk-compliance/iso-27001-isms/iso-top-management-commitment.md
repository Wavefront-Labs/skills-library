# Leadership and commitment (5.1)
**Goal:** collect the proof that leadership drives the ISMS.
- 5.1 requires top management to ensure policy and objectives fit strategic direction, ISMS requirements are integrated into business processes, resources are available, importance is communicated, intended outcomes are achieved, people are directed and supported, and continual improvement is promoted.
- Evidence: signed policy, budget lines, management review minutes with decisions, steering committee attendance, objectives in business plans.

## Gotchas
- Auditors interview top management; they must describe the scope, policy and objectives in their own words.

## Self-check
Ask a director three questions (scope, top risk, last management review decision) and check answers match records.
