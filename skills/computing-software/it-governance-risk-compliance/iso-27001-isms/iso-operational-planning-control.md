# Operational planning, assessments and treatment (8)
**Goal:** prove the plan is actually executed.
- 8.1: implement processes to meet requirements and the 6.1 actions; keep documented information to show processes ran as planned; control planned changes and review unintended ones; control externally provided processes, products or services relevant to the ISMS.
- 8.2: perform risk assessments at planned intervals or on significant change; retain results.
- 8.3: implement the risk treatment plan; retain results.

## Gotchas
- Risk assessment performed once for certification and never repeated.

## Self-check
Show the last two risk assessment dates and the change that triggered any out-of-cycle run.
