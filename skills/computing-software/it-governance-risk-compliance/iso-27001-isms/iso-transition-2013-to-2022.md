# Transition from 27001:2013 to 2022
**Goal:** a gap assessment.
- 2013 Annex A had 114 controls in 14 domains; 2022 has 93 in four themes: organizational 37, people 8, physical 14, technological 34.
- 11 new controls: 5.7, 5.23, 5.30, 7.4, 8.9, 8.10, 8.11, 8.12, 8.16, 8.23, 8.28.
- ISO/IEC 27002:2022 contains mapping tables old to new; several 2013 controls were merged.
- The transition period for 2013 certificates ended 31 October 2025 (confirm with your certification body).

## Self-check
Produce the new-control gap list with owner and date for each of the 11.
