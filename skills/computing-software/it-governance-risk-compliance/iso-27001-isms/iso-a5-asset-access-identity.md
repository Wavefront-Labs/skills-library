# A.5 assets, classification, access, identity
**Goal:** the inventory-to-access chain.
- 5.9 inventory with owners; 5.10 acceptable use; 5.11 return of assets; 5.12 classification; 5.13 labelling; 5.14 information transfer; 5.15 access control; 5.16 identity management; 5.17 authentication information; 5.18 access rights.
- Evidence: inventory export, classification scheme, joiner-mover-leaver tickets, periodic access review sign-offs.

## Gotchas
- Inventory without owners; access reviews that skip privileged and service accounts.

## Self-check
Sample ten leavers and confirm access was removed within the stated time.
