# Risk treatment and Statement of Applicability (6.1.3)
**Goal:** an SoA that survives audit.
- 6.1.3 steps: choose treatment options; determine all controls necessary (from any source); compare with Annex A to check none necessary is omitted; produce the SoA; formulate the treatment plan; get risk owner approval of plan and residual risk.
- SoA lists every one of the 93 Annex A controls with applicable yes/no, justification for inclusion and exclusion, and implementation status.
- Link each applicable control to risks treated, owner and evidence location.
- Annex A is a reference set, not a closed checklist; add controls where needed.

## Gotchas
- Excluding a control without justification, or marking applicable but unimplemented with no dated plan, are classic findings.
- Residual risk acceptance must be by the risk owner, recorded.

## Self-check
Pick five SoA rows and trace each to a risk, a policy or procedure, and a recent evidence item.
