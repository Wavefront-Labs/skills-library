# Management review (9.3)
**Goal:** a management review that meets 9.3.
- Inputs: status of prior actions; changes in external and internal issues; changes in interested-party needs (2022 addition); performance feedback (nonconformities, monitoring, audit results, objectives); interested-party feedback; risk assessment and treatment status; improvement opportunities.
- Outputs: decisions on improvement opportunities, ISMS changes and resources; minuted.
- Hold at planned intervals, at least annually in practice.

## Self-check
Check minutes cover every required input and list decisions with owners and dates.
