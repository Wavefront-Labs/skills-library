# A.6 people controls (6.1-6.8)
**Goal:** the eight people controls.
- 6.1 screening; 6.2 terms and conditions; 6.3 awareness, education and training; 6.4 disciplinary process; 6.5 responsibilities after termination or change; 6.6 confidentiality agreements; 6.7 remote working; 6.8 event reporting.
- Evidence: screening records by role risk, signed terms, training completion, remote working policy and device controls, reporting channel use.

## Gotchas
- Screening must be proportionate and lawful in the jurisdiction.

## Self-check
Sample new joiners and show screening, signed terms and training completion dates.
