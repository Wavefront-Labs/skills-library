# A.5 policies, roles, segregation, threat intelligence
**Goal:** cover the governance-oriented organizational controls.
- 5.1 policies; 5.2 roles and responsibilities; 5.3 segregation of duties; 5.4 management responsibilities; 5.5 contact with authorities; 5.6 contact with special interest groups; 5.7 threat intelligence (new in 2022); 5.8 information security in project management.
- 5.7 evidence: sources subscribed (national CERT, vendor advisories), triage record, actions taken.
- 5.3: keep a conflicting-duty matrix and compensating controls where separation is not possible.
