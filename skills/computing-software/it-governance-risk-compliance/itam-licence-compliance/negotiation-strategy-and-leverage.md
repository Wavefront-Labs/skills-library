# Negotiation strategy and leverage

Goal: improve price and terms, not just discount.

Method:
- Preparation: ELP, usage, roadmap, alternative products, internal sponsor alignment.
- Know vendor fiscal year end and seller quotas; time commitments accordingly.
- Ask for ramp deals, price caps on renewals, flexible reduction rights, benefit clarity, audit clause limits.
- Use competitive quotes where realistic.
- Document all concessions in the contract, not email.

Gotchas:
- Discount percentage without a price hold hides future uplifts.
- Bundling hides the cost of unused products.

Verify: compare final terms to a pre-agreed target list.
