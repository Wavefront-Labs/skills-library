# Procurement contracts, leasing and buy-versus-lease

Goal: lowest total cost with manageable return risk.

Method:
- Compare TCO over the refresh cycle: purchase price, support, residual value, financing cost, admin.
- Lease: record contract end, return conditions (condition standard, missing parts charges), data wipe responsibility.
- DaaS: confirm who holds title, who performs sanitisation, and SLA for replacements.
- Negotiate volume tiers, advance replacement warranty and serial-number data feeds.

Gotchas:
- Lease returns with missing chargers or cosmetic damage incur penalties; inspect before return.
- Early termination fees apply when headcount shrinks.

Verify: lease register lists every device with end date and return deadline alert.
