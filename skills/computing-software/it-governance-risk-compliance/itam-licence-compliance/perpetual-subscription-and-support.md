# Perpetual, subscription and maintenance licences

Goal: understand what you keep and what you lose at term end.

- Perpetual: right to use the version forever; support/maintenance (annual, often 18-22% of licence price; confirm) gives updates and support and may confer extra rights (Microsoft Software Assurance benefits).
- Subscription/term: rights end at term; reducing seats mid-term is often not allowed.
- Usage-based: pay per consumption with reporting duties.
- Hybrid: subscription licences with perpetual rights after N years (rare).

Method: tag each entitlement with type and end date; model cost over 3-5 years for both options.

Gotchas:
- Dropping maintenance may forfeit re-instatement without back-pay.
- Cloud subscription may remove on-premises usage rights.

Verify: renewal calendar shows term ends.
