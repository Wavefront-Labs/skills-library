# Mergers, divestitures and licence transfer

Goal: avoid losing or breaching rights at corporate events.

Method:
- Review assignment and change-of-control clauses in top contracts before signing.
- Identify who is licensed party and who is an affiliate; check whether acquired entities get rights.
- Request vendor consent for transfers; some vendors charge transfer fees or re-price.
- After close, merge entitlements and installs; run ELP on the combined estate.
- For divestitures, split entitlements, and avoid carving out shared licences without consent.

Gotchas:
- Acquired companies' unlicensed software becomes your problem.
- Vendor may use event to push to a new agreement.

Verify: transfer approvals filed.
