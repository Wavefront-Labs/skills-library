# Selecting and auditing an ITAD vendor

Goal: transfer disposal risk to a vendor you can verify.

Method:
- Require recognised certifications (R2v3, e-Stewards, NAID AAA for data destruction; confirm current versions).
- Contract: audit rights, downstream vendor list, liability for data breach, insurance, serial-level reporting, SLAs for pickup and report turnaround.
- Visit the facility; watch intake, wipe and shred processes.
- Compare value recovery split and fees.

Gotchas:
- You stay liable as the data controller; subcontracting must be disclosed.
- Cheapest bid often skips serial-level reporting.

Verify: annual audit report with corrective actions closed.
