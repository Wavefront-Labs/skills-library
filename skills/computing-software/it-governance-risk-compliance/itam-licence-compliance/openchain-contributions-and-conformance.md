# OpenChain contributions, communication and conformance

Goal: sustain and prove conformance.

Requirements summary (confirm in the spec):
- Publicly visible way for external parties to contact you about open-source compliance.
- Process for responding to enquiries.
- Contribution policy and procedures if the organisation contributes.
- Keep records that demonstrate conformance; read the spec for the retention period, which this skill does not state.
- Conformance can be self-certified or obtained with an official OpenChain partner (independent assessment or third-party certification); OpenChain publishes a free ISO/IEC 5230 self-certification checklist.

Method:
- Publish a compliance contact (email alias plus page).
- Track enquiries to resolution.
- Schedule conformance review annually.

Gotchas:
- Mailbox unmonitored is a failure.
- Supply chain conformance can be requested by customers.

Verify: test the contact route with a dummy enquiry.
