# SAM reporting, renewals and true-up planning

Goal: right-size spend at each renewal.

Method:
- Build a renewal calendar 180 days ahead per vendor.
- Pre-renewal: update ELP, remove shelfware, forecast needs.
- Choose strategy: reduce, consolidate edition, move to subscription or enterprise agreement, or switch product.
- Use leverage: competitor options, end-of-quarter vendor targets, multi-year commitment.
- After renewal, update entitlements and keep the signed order form.

Gotchas:
- Auto-renew clauses may require 60-90 days notice to cancel.
- True-up pricing may differ from initial price; read the clause.

Verify: savings and risk avoided are quantified for each renewal.
