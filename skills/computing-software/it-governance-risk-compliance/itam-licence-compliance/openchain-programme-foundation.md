# OpenChain programme foundation

Goal: meet section 3.1 foundation requirements of ISO/IEC 5230.

Requirements (summarised, confirm the standard text):
- Documented open-source policy covering use, contribution and distribution, communicated internally.
- Defined roles and responsibilities, with assessed competence and records of training and awareness.
- Programme scope defined.
- Process to review and document licence obligations.

Method:
- Draft policy and a one-page developer summary.
- Run annual training with sign-off; keep attendance records.
- Appoint an OSPO/compliance owner and legal contact.
- Use the OpenChain self-certification checklist online.

Gotchas:
- Evidence matters; undocumented practice does not count.
- Policy must be accessible to all affected staff.

Verify: self-certification questions each answered with evidence.
