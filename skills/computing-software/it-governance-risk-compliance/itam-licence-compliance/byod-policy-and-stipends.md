# BYOD policy, stipends and ownership

Goal: reduce device cost without losing control of data.

Method:
- Choose models: COBO (corporate-owned), COPE (corporate-owned, personally enabled), BYOD, with a matrix of data allowed per model.
- Policy contents: supported OS minimum, encryption, passcode, jailbreak/root ban, lost-device reporting, selective wipe consent, legal hold handling.
- Stipend: set amounts and tax treatment (confirm locally).
- Exit: remove corporate profile and data, stop stipend, record in HR event.

Gotchas:
- Employee-owned devices complicate eDiscovery and works-council rules.
- Do not collect personal app inventory unless necessary.

Verify: legal and HR review documented.
