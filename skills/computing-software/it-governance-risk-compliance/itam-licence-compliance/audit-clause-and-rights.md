# Reading audit clauses and rights

Goal: know your obligations before any data leaves.

Check in the contract:
- Who may audit (vendor or third-party auditor) and how often.
- Notice period (typically 30 days; confirm) and duration.
- Scope: products, affiliates, locations, date range.
- Cost shifting: you pay if underlicensing exceeds a threshold (commonly 5%; confirm).
- Confidentiality and permitted use of findings.
- Remedies: back-maintenance, list price versus discounted price.

Method: put legal in the lead, route all vendor contact via one channel, and request the audit scope in writing.

Gotchas:
- Voluntary "self-assessments" can still be audits in effect.
- Data supplied is hard to retract.

Verify: written scope agreed before scripts run.
