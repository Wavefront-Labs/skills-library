# ISACA code of ethics and ITAF standards
**Goal:** professional conduct baseline.
- Code of ethics: support governance standards, perform duties with objectivity and due diligence, maintain privacy and confidentiality, maintain competency, disclose results, support professional education.
- ITAF structure: general standards (independence, competence, due professional care), performance standards (planning, evidence, supervision), reporting standards.
- Conflict of interest or lack of independence must be disclosed to the audit committee or management.
