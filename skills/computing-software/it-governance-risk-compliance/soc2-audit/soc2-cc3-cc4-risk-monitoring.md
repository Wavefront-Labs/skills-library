# CC3 risk assessment and CC4 monitoring
**Goal:** risk and monitoring evidence.
- CC3.1-3.4: specify objectives, identify and analyse risks, consider fraud risk, assess significant changes.
- CC4.1-4.2: ongoing and separate evaluations (internal audits, pen tests, control self-assessments); communicate deficiencies to responsible parties and management.
- Evidence: annual risk assessment, risk register, pen-test report and remediation tracking, control monitoring dashboard.

## Self-check
Show last year's risk assessment and the remediation record for a deficiency.
