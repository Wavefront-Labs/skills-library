# CC9 risk mitigation and vendor management
**Goal:** third-party and insurance evidence.
- CC9.1: identify, select and develop risk mitigation activities for business disruption (insurance, continuity).
- CC9.2: assess and manage vendor and business partner risks; contracts, reviews of vendor SOC reports, vendor inventory.
- Keep vendor reviews dated within the period and record how exceptions in vendor reports were evaluated.

## Self-check
Show the vendor inventory with criticality and the last assurance review for the top five.
