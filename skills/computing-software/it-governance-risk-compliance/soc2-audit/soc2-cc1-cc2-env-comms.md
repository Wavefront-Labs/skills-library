# CC1 control environment and CC2 communication
**Goal:** evidence the governance criteria.
- CC1.1-1.5: commitment to integrity and ethics, board independence and oversight, structure and authority, competence commitment, accountability.
- CC2.1-2.3: quality information, internal communication, external communication.
- Evidence: code of conduct acknowledgements, org chart, board or committee minutes, job descriptions, background checks, security policies communicated, customer-facing commitments.
- The criteria derive from the 17 COSO Internal Control framework principles.
