# Availability (A1) and Confidentiality (C1)
**Goal:** know when and how to include them.
- A1.1 capacity management, A1.2 environmental protections, backup and recovery infrastructure, A1.3 recovery plan testing.
- C1.1 identify and maintain confidential information, C1.2 dispose of it at the end of retention.
- Evidence: capacity dashboards, backup logs and restore tests, DR test report, data classification, retention and deletion records.
- Categories are chosen by the service organisation and customer expectations; Security (common criteria) is always included.
