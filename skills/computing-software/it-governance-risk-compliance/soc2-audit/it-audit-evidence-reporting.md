# Evidence, findings, reporting and follow-up
**Goal:** defensible findings.
- Evidence reliability: external over internal, system-generated over manual, direct observation over inquiry; corroborate inquiry.
- Finding: condition, criteria, cause, effect (risk), recommendation, management response with owner and date.
- Use sampling (statistical or judgmental) and document population and selection.
- Follow up until closure and re-test where risk is high.
