# Processing Integrity (PI1) and Privacy (P1-P8)
**Goal:** the less common categories.
- PI1.1-1.5: define processing specifications, inputs, processing, outputs complete and accurate, stored items protected.
- Privacy: P1 notice, P2 choice and consent, P3 collection, P4 use, retention and disposal, P5 access, P6 disclosure and notification, P7 quality, P8 monitoring and enforcement.
- Map to GDPR or other privacy law obligations; privacy criteria apply to personal information the service organisation handles.

## Self-check
Draw the personal data flow and attach each privacy criterion to a control.
