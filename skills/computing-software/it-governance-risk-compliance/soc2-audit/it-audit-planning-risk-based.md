# Risk-based audit planning
**Goal:** a risk-based plan.
- Audit universe, risk assessment, annual plan; per engagement: objectives, scope, criteria, resources, work programme.
- ISACA ITAF provides standards and guidelines; independence and objectivity required; also IIA standards for internal audit.
- CISA exam Domain 1 content: audit planning and execution, evidence, sampling, reporting, follow-up.
