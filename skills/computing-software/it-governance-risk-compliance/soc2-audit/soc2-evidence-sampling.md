# Evidence and sampling
**Goal:** answer a population request fast.
- Auditor requests a complete population (all changes, joiners, leavers, incidents) and selects samples. Frequency guides sample size: annual 1, quarterly 2, monthly 2 to 5, weekly 5 to 15, daily 20 to 40, multiple times a day 25 to 60 (typical ranges, firm-specific).
- Evidence quality: system-generated, dated, with the query or report parameters; screenshots with timestamp and user context.
- Keep a control owner for each control and an evidence calendar.

## Self-check
Produce the leaver population for the period and prove completeness against HR records.
