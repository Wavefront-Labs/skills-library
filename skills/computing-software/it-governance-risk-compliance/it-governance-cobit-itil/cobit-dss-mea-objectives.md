# DSS and MEA management objectives
**Goal:** operations and assurance objectives.
- DSS01 operations; DSS02 service requests and incidents; DSS03 problems; DSS04 continuity; DSS05 security services; DSS06 business process controls.
- MEA01 performance and conformance monitoring; MEA02 internal control system; MEA03 compliance with external requirements; MEA04 assurance.

## Self-check
Map your incident process to DSS02 practices and find gaps.
