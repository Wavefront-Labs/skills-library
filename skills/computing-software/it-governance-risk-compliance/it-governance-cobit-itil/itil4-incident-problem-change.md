# Incident, problem and change enablement practices
**Goal:** the three core practices.
- Incident: restore service fast; priority from impact and urgency; escalation (functional, hierarchical); major incident process.
- Problem: reduce incident recurrence; reactive and proactive; root cause, known error record, workaround.
- Change enablement: standard, normal, emergency changes; change authority proportional to risk; ITIL 4 rejects heavyweight CAB for low-risk changes.
- Link to ISO 27001 5.24-5.27 and 8.32.

## Self-check
Take one major incident and show the problem record, root cause and resulting change.
