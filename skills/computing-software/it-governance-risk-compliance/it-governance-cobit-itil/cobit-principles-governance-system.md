# COBIT governance system principles
**Goal:** the structure of COBIT.
- Six governance system principles: provide stakeholder value; holistic approach; dynamic governance system; governance distinct from management; tailored to enterprise needs; end-to-end governance system.
- Three governance framework principles: based on a conceptual model; open and flexible; aligned to major standards.
- Governance = evaluate, direct, monitor (EDM); management = plan, build, run, monitor.
- Components: processes, organisational structures, principles/policies/frameworks, information, culture/ethics/behaviour, people/skills/competencies, services/infrastructure/applications.
