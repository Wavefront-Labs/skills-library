# CISM domains and management scope
**Goal:** frame a security management programme.
- Domains (current outline): 1 Information Security Governance 17%; 2 Information Security Risk Management 20%; 3 Information Security Program 33%; 4 Incident Management 30%. Verify weights on isaca.org.
- The CISM focus is management: strategy, alignment with business, metrics, resources, reporting to board.
