# Service catalogue, SLA and relationship practices
**Goal:** service levels that mean something.
- Service level management: define targets from customer outcomes, measure, report, review; SLA, OLA (internal), UC (underpinning contract).
- Catalogue: service description, owner, request process, cost, SLA.
- Link availability and continuity targets back to BIA RTO and RPO.

## Self-check
Check one SLA metric is measurable from a system report.
