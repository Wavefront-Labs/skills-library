# Design factors and tailoring
**Goal:** a tailored target governance system.
- Design factors: enterprise strategy; enterprise goals; risk profile; I&T-related issues; threat landscape; compliance requirements; role of IT; sourcing model; IT implementation methods; technology adoption strategy; enterprise size.
- Goals cascade: stakeholder drivers to enterprise goals to alignment goals to governance and management objectives.
- Use the COBIT design guide toolkit to score factors and prioritise objectives, then adjust with expert judgment.

## Self-check
Pick the five highest-priority objectives from a factor assessment and justify each.
