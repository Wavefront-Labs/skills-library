# EDM governance objectives
**Goal:** board-level evaluate-direct-monitor practice.
- EDM01 ensured governance framework setting and maintenance; EDM02 ensured benefits delivery; EDM03 ensured risk optimisation; EDM04 ensured resource optimisation; EDM05 ensured stakeholder engagement.
- Practices per objective: evaluate, direct, monitor activities with inputs and outputs and RACI.
- Evidence: board papers, risk appetite approval, portfolio benefit tracking.
