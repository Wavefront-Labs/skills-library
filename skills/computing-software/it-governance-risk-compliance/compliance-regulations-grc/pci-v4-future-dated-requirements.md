# v4 future-dated requirements and v4.0.1
**Goal:** know which v4 requirements bind you now.
- v3.2.1 retired 31 March 2024. Requirements marked best practice until 31 March 2025 then became mandatory (e.g. targeted risk analysis, authenticated internal scans, payment page script controls 6.4.3 and 11.6.1, MFA for all CDE access, anti-phishing).
- v4.0.1 (2024) is a limited revision with clarifications and corrections.
- Confirm current status of any change on the PCI SSC site.
