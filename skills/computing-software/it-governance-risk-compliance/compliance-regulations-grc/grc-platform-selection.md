# Choosing a GRC platform
**Goal:** vendor-neutral selection.
- Categories: enterprise IRM suites (e.g. ServiceNow IRM, Archer, OneTrust), compliance-automation platforms (e.g. Vanta, Drata, Secureframe), spreadsheets, open-source options. Feature sets change, validate in a demo against your use cases.
- Criteria: supported frameworks and crosswalks, integrations (cloud, IdP, HR, ticketing, code), evidence collection, workflow and approvals, audit portal, API, data export and exit, pricing at your scale.
- Pilot with one framework and real integrations before commit.

## Self-check
Score two shortlisted tools against ten weighted requirements.
