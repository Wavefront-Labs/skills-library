# Access request and other rights (Art 12-22)
**Goal:** a working DSAR process.
- Rights: information (13-14), access (15), rectification (16), erasure (17), restriction (18), notification (19), portability (20), objection (21), automated decision-making (22).
- Respond without undue delay and within one month, extendable by two further months for complexity; free of charge generally; verify identity proportionately.
- Procedure: intake log, identify data across systems, review exemptions and third-party data, redact, respond, record.

## Self-check
Time a test request end to end against the one-month limit.
