# HIPAA Security Rule safeguards
**Goal:** a safeguards matrix.
- Administrative: security management process (risk analysis and risk management are required), workforce security, contingency plan, evaluation, BAAs. Physical: facility access, workstation and device controls. Technical: access control, audit controls, integrity, authentication, transmission security.
- Standards are required or addressable; addressable means implement or document an equivalent alternative.
- Breach Notification Rule: notify individuals without unreasonable delay within 60 days; HHS and media thresholds apply.
- Not legal advice; the HHS OCR audit protocol lists evidence requests.
