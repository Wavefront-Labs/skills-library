# SOX ITGC scope and NIS2 duties
**Goal:** know when these apply.
- SOX 302/404: management assesses internal control over financial reporting using COSO; IT scope = systems that feed financial statements; ITGCs: access, change, operations, with PCAOB AS 2201 audit.
- NIS2 (EU Directive 2022/2555): risk management measures (Art 21), incident reporting (early warning in 24 hours, notification within 72 hours, final report within one month under Art 23), management body accountability. Transposed into national law, so check member state rules.
