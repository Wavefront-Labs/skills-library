# Policy management lifecycle
**Goal:** policies that are current and attested.
- Hierarchy: policy (what), standard (rules), procedure (how), guideline (advice).
- Lifecycle: owner and approver, annual review, version history, employee acknowledgement tracking, exception register with expiry and compensating controls.
- Metrics: percentage of policies in date, acknowledgement rate, open exceptions.
