# Breach notification (Art 33-34)
**Goal:** a rehearsed breach clock.
- Controller notifies the authority without undue delay and, where feasible, within 72 hours of becoming aware, unless unlikely to result in risk. Notification can be phased.
- Content: nature, categories and numbers, DPO contact, likely consequences, measures taken.
- Individuals are told without undue delay when high risk. Processors notify controllers without undue delay.
- Keep a register of all breaches including those not reported.

## Self-check
Run a tabletop and time detection to decision to notification against 72 hours.
