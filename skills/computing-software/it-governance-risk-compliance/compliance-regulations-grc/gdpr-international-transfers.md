# International transfers (Art 44-49)
**Goal:** a transfer register with mechanisms.
- Mechanisms: adequacy decision (Art 45), appropriate safeguards (Art 46: SCCs, BCRs), derogations (Art 49) for limited cases.
- SCCs (2021 set) require a transfer impact assessment following Schrems II (C-311/18).
- Adequacy status changes (e.g. EU-US Data Privacy Framework for certified US organisations); confirm current status with counsel.
