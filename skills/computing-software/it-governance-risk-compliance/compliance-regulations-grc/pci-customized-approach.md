# Defined versus customized approach
**Goal:** flexibility with evidence.
- Defined approach: meet the requirement as written. Customized approach: meet the stated objective with a different control, documented with a controls matrix and targeted risk analysis and validated by a QSA; not available for SAQ merchants.
- Compensating controls remain an option (Appendix B) when a constraint prevents meeting a requirement exactly.

## Self-check
For a candidate control state the objective, the alternative control and the risk analysis.
