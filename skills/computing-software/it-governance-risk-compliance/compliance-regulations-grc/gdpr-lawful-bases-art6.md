# Lawful bases and special categories (Art 6, 9)
**Goal:** one valid basis per purpose.
- Six bases in Art 6(1). Legitimate interests need a documented three-part test (purpose, necessity, balancing) (LIA).
- Special category data (Art 9) needs an Art 6 basis plus an Art 9(2) condition; criminal data is Art 10.
- Changing the basis later is hard; record it in the ROPA and privacy notice.

## Self-check
Check every purpose in the ROPA has exactly one basis and, for special categories, an Art 9 condition.
