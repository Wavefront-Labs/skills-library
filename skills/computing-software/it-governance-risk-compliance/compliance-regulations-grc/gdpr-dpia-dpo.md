# DPIA (Art 35) and DPO (Art 37-39)
**Goal:** DPIA and DPO decisions with records.
- DPIA required for high-risk processing: systematic large-scale profiling, large-scale special categories, large-scale public monitoring; check supervisory authority lists. Consult the authority (Art 36) if residual high risk.
- DPIA content: systematic description and purposes, necessity and proportionality, risks to individuals, mitigating measures.
- DPO mandatory for public authorities, large-scale regular monitoring or special categories; independent, reports to highest management, cannot hold a conflicting role.
- Privacy by design and default are in Art 25.

## Self-check
Screen three projects with a DPIA threshold checklist and record the outcome.
