# Privacy notices and transparency (Art 12-14)
**Goal:** complete, concise notices.
- Art 13 when data collected from the person; Art 14 when from another source (within one month).
- Content: controller and DPO contacts, purposes and legal basis, legitimate interests if relied on, recipients, international transfers and safeguards, retention periods or criteria, rights, right to withdraw consent, complaint right, whether provision is mandatory, automated decisions.
- Layered notices and plain language.

## Self-check
Check the notice against the Art 13 list item by item.
