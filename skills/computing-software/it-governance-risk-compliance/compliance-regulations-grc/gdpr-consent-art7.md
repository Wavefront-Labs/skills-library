# Consent requirements (Art 7)
**Goal:** auditable consent.
- Opt-in by clear affirmative act; no pre-ticked boxes; unbundled from other terms; record who, when, what text, how.
- Withdrawal as easy as giving; do not make service conditional on unnecessary consent.
- Age of digital consent Art 8: 16 by default, member states may set 13 to 16.
- Cookie consent interacts with ePrivacy rules.
