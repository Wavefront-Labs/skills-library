# Article 5 principles
**Goal:** translate principles into controls.
- Seven principles in Art 5(1) and accountability in 5(2): the controller must demonstrate compliance.
- Controls: record of processing, retention schedule, minimisation review of forms, accuracy corrections, security measures (Art 32), privacy notices.
- Not legal advice: involve counsel/DPO for interpretation.

## Self-check
Pick a processing activity and show how each principle is met with a record.
