# Merchant levels, SAQs and ROC
**Goal:** right validation document.
- Merchant levels 1-4 by annual card transaction volume (set by card brands); level 1 normally needs a ROC by a QSA (or qualified internal assessor) and an AOC; others commonly use SAQs.
- SAQ A: fully outsourced e-commerce; SAQ A-EP: partial; SAQ D: all other. Eligibility criteria are strict, read the SAQ instructions.
- Quarterly external ASV scans where applicable. Brand and acquirer rules decide who must validate.

## Self-check
Confirm with your acquirer the level and form before you start.
