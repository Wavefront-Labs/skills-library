# Processor contracts (Art 28) and records (Art 30)
**Goal:** contracts and records in order.
- Art 28(3) contract must set subject matter, duration, nature and purpose, data types, and require processing on documented instructions, confidentiality, security measures, sub-processor authorisation and flow-down, assistance with rights and breaches, deletion or return, and audit information.
- Art 30 ROPA: purposes, categories of data and subjects, recipients, transfers, retention, security description. Processors keep their own records.
- Exemption for under 250 employees is narrow.

## Self-check
Take your ten largest processors and confirm a signed DPA with the Art 28(3) clauses.
