# Identify and Protect functions
**Goal:** map controls to ID and PR outcomes.
- ID: ID.AM asset management, ID.RA risk assessment, ID.IM improvement.
- PR: PR.AA identity management, authentication and access control; PR.AT awareness and training; PR.DS data security; PR.PS platform security; PR.IR technology infrastructure resilience.
- Subcategories (e.g. PR.AA-05) are outcomes, not controls: pick controls from 800-53 or ISO 27001 as implementation.
- CSF 2.0 categories were reorganised from 1.1; use the CSF 1.1 to 2.0 crosswalk from NIST when migrating.

## Self-check
Pick three subcategories and name the control and evidence that satisfies each.
