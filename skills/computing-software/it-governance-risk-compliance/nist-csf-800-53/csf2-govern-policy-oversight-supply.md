# GV.PO, GV.OV, GV.SC policy, oversight, supply chain
**Goal:** cover the remaining Govern categories.
- GV.PO policy: established, communicated, enforced, reviewed.
- GV.OV oversight: results of cybersecurity risk activities reviewed to inform and adjust strategy; performance measured.
- GV.SC supply chain risk management: programme established, suppliers identified and prioritised, requirements in contracts, suppliers monitored, included in incident planning and in offboarding.
- Link to SP 800-161 Rev 1 for supply chain detail.
