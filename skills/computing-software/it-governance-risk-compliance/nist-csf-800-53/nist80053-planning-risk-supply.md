# PL, PM, RA, SA, SR, CP, IR, PT families
**Goal:** programme-level families, including those new in Rev 5.
- Rev 5 has 20 families: AC AT AU CA CM CP IA IR MA MP PE PL PM PS PT RA SA SC SI SR. New in Rev 5: PT (PII processing and transparency) and SR (supply chain risk management).
- PM controls are organisation-wide, not baseline-assigned. RA-3 risk assessment, RA-5 vulnerability monitoring. SA-11 developer testing. SR-3 supply chain controls. CP-2 contingency plan, CP-9 backup. IR-4 incident handling.
