# GV.OC, GV.RM, GV.RR context, risk strategy, roles
**Goal:** turn Govern into documented decisions.
- CSF 2.0 (February 2024) added a sixth function, Govern (GV), alongside Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover.
- GV.OC organizational context: mission, stakeholder expectations, legal and contractual requirements, critical dependencies.
- GV.RM risk management strategy: risk appetite and tolerance statements, risk assessment scales, communication of strategy.
- GV.RR roles: leadership accountability, resourcing, HR practices that cover cybersecurity.
- Evidence: board-approved risk appetite, RACI, budget, cyber objectives.

## Gotchas
- Treating Govern as a policy library; the outcomes are statements of what is true (e.g. strategy is established and communicated), so show records.

## Self-check
For each category list one artefact that shows its outcome is achieved.
