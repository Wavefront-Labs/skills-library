# RMF Prepare and Categorize steps
**Goal:** a sound start to the authorization.
- RMF 7 steps: Prepare, Categorize, Select, Implement, Assess, Authorize, Monitor. Prepare has organization-level and system-level tasks.
- Prepare: assign roles (authorizing official, system owner, security officer), risk management strategy, common controls, system stakeholders.
- Categorize: describe the system, identify information types (SP 800-60), pick impact levels, document and get AO concurrence.

## Self-check
Check the categorization memo lists information types with impact per C, I, A.
