# RMF Assess and Authorize steps
**Goal:** an authorization package.
- Assess: select assessors (independence level by impact), plan, assess, produce SAR, remediate and reassess, prepare POA&M.
- Authorize: package = SSP, SAR, POA&M; risk determination and risk response; AO issues authorization to operate with terms and conditions and a duration, or denies.
- Rev 2 includes an authorization to use for reusing existing authorizations.

## Self-check
Check each open finding in the SAR has a POA&M entry with owner, milestone, date.
