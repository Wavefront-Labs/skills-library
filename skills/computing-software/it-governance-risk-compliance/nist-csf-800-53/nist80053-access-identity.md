# AC, IA, PS, AT, PE families
**Goal:** know the people and access families.
- AC: least privilege AC-6, account management AC-2, separation of duties AC-5, remote access AC-17, session lock AC-11.
- IA: IA-2 identification and authentication (organizational users) with enhancements for MFA, IA-5 authenticator management.
- PS: screening, termination; AT: role-based training AT-3; PE: physical access PE-3.
- Rev 5 control IDs are base control plus enhancements, e.g. AC-2(1).
