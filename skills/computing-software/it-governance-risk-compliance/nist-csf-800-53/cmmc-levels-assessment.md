# CMMC 2.0 levels and assessment
**Goal:** know which level a contract needs.
- Level 1: 15 FAR 52.204-21 practices (FCI), annual self-assessment. Level 2: 110 SP 800-171 practices (CUI), self or C3PAO third-party assessment depending on contract. Level 3: selected SP 800-172 requirements, government-led.
- Phase-in dates and exact rules depend on the final DoD rule; confirm current status on the DoD CIO CMMC site.
- Affirmation by a senior official is required annually.

## Self-check
Map your contracts' clauses to the required CMMC level.
