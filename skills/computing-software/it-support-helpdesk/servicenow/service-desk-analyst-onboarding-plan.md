# service-desk-analyst-onboarding-plan

## Before day 1
- Accounts and roles ready: ITSM (itil role), remote tool, AD/Entra helpdesk role,
  telephony, KB contributor (KCS candidate licence), MFA set up.
- Training ticket queue or sandbox instance access.
- Buddy assigned (experienced analyst, not the team lead).

## Weeks 1-2: observe and learn
- Tools walkthrough: ticket lifecycle, categorisation, priority matrix, SLA, escalation
  paths, VIP list.
- Shadow calls and chats (listen-only), then reverse-shadow (new analyst leads, buddy
  listens and intervenes).
- Read the top 30 KB articles by usage; those cover most contacts.
- Security basics: identity verification procedure, social engineering, data handling.

## Weeks 3-6: graduated ticket types
| Stage | Ticket types | Check |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Password resets, unlocks, how-to | Buddy reviews every ticket |
| 2 | Software installs, printers, mailbox access requests | Daily sample review |
| 3 | Outlook/Teams faults, VPN, device issues | Weekly quality audit |
| 4 | Phones live solo, on-call buddy | Normal QA |

## Competency matrix
List skills per tier (e.g. "reset MFA method", "rebuild Outlook profile", "triage a
phishing report") with sign-off by buddy or lead. Analysts see the matrix on day 1.

## Days 60-90
- Full queue, normal QA sampling, KCS candidate to contributor if article quality holds.
- Review: FCR, quality score, CSAT, knowledge contributions; agree development goals.

## Pitfalls
- Throwing new staff on phones in week 1 because the queue is busy; early bad calls
  damage confidence and quality for months.
- No security training before access to identity tools.

## Verify
Each new analyst has a signed-off competency matrix by day 90 and their quality scores
match team average.
