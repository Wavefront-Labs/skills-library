# handling-difficult-and-abusive-users

Goal: calm most angry users down and get the problem solved; protect
yourself when anger turns to abuse (A+ 220-1202 4.7: deal with difficult
customers — don't argue, don't be dismissive, clarify, don't disclose
experience on social media).

## Angry is not abusive
Most angry users are frustrated by impact (deadline, lost work, repeat
failure). Treat the impact, not the tone.

## De-escalation sequence
1. **Let them finish.** Interrupting escalates. Note facts while they talk.
2. **Acknowledge the impact specifically**: "You've lost the morning and the
   board pack is due at two — that's a real problem."
3. **Don't argue or defend** IT/colleagues; don't say "calm down" or "that's
   not possible".
4. **Clarify** with a focused question to move to problem-solving: "Can I
   check what you see right now on screen?"
5. **State what you will do and when** — specific and honest.
6. **Follow through** and update before they chase.
Lower your pace and volume slightly; use their name.

## Demanding users ("I want it now / I want your manager")
- Explain what's possible and the fastest route; offer a workaround now
  (loaner, web app) while the fix proceeds.
- Manager requests: offer it without resistance — "I'll ask my team lead
  to call you within 30 minutes" — and brief the lead first.

## Abuse (swearing at you, threats, discriminatory remarks)
Follow the organisation's policy, typically:
1. **Warn once** calmly: "I want to help, but I can't continue if the
   language continues."
2. If it continues: "I'm ending this call now; I'll note the ticket and my
   team lead will contact you." End the call.
3. Record facts in the ticket/incident log and inform your lead.
Threats of violence or self-harm → escalate immediately per policy
(security/HR/emergency services).

## After
Take a short break if needed; debrief with your lead. Don't vent about the
user on social media or in public channels.

## Verify
QA/CSAT for difficult interactions: problem resolved or clear next step,
policy followed for abuse, incident recorded.
