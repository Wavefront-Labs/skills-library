# floor-walking-and-hypercare-after-go-live

## Set up
- Period: typically 1-2 weeks after each wave, longer for core business systems.
- Rota of floor walkers by area and shift, in visible lanyards or T-shirts, plus a virtual
  channel (Teams chat) for remote staff.
- Brief walkers on the change, top expected questions, known issues, and how to escalate.
  Pair less experienced staff with project experts.

## On the floor
- Walk the area; ask "how is the new X going?" rather than waiting to be called.
- Fix small things on the spot; teach while fixing so users can do it next time.
- Capture every interaction quickly: a short form or tally sheet (user, area, issue,
  resolved/escalated). Raise proper tickets for anything not fixed in minutes.

## Daily rhythm
- Stand-up each morning with project, support and vendor leads: top issues by count,
  blockers, fixes being deployed, comms needed.
- Publish a known-issues list with workarounds; update the FAQ and KB daily.

## Exit criteria
Agree them up front: ticket volume for the change back near baseline, no open
high-priority defects, KB articles published, support team trained. Then hand over to
BAU with a written summary of issues and fixes.

## Gotchas
- Walkers fixing without recording hides the real defect volume from the project.
- Users save questions for walkers; announce when hypercare ends and where to go next.

## Verify
Daily issue log complete, exit criteria met and signed off by project and service desk
leads, and BAU KB covers the top issues found.
