# training-videos-champions-and-adoption

## Short how-to videos
- One task per video, 60-120 seconds: "Share a file from OneDrive", "Reset your
  password". Title = the task in user words.
- Script first (steps from the KB article), record at 1080p with a clean demo account
  and no personal data, zoom/highlight clicks, captions on (accessibility and silent
  viewing). Tools: Clipchamp, Stream, Camtasia, Loom.
- Host where users look: KB article (embed), portal, Teams channel, intranet. Update
  when the UI changes; date the video.

## Champions network
- Volunteers in each department (1 per 20-50 staff) who are enthusiastic users and give
  peer help, gather feedback and spread news.
- Support them: early access to changes, a private Teams channel with IT, monthly calls,
  recognition (manager acknowledgement, badges), simple materials to share.
- Use champions for pilot feedback and hypercare after rollouts; don't make them unpaid
  service desk staff.

## Adoption metrics
- Usage over activation: active users per product (Microsoft 365 usage reports, Adoption
  Score), feature-level usage (Teams meetings vs chat, OneDrive files synced vs local),
  tickets per user on the new tool, self-service portal use, training attendance and
  survey scores.
- Baseline before the rollout; measure at 30/60/90 days; segment by department to target
  help.
- Combine with qualitative feedback from champions and surveys.

## Phishing simulations
- Run via Defender for Office 365 Attack simulation training or a vendor platform:
  realistic templates, varied difficulty, regular cadence (monthly or quarterly).
- Teach, don't trap: immediate short training on click, praise for reporting via the
  report button, no public shaming; avoid cruel lures (bonuses, health scares) that damage
  trust.
- Measure report rate (more important than click rate), repeat clickers (targeted
  coaching), time to first report.
- Coordinate with the desk so analysts know a simulation is running (and don't escalate
  it as a real incident unless reported through the right channel).

## Verify
Videos are linked from the matching KB articles and current, every department has a
champion, adoption dashboards show trend vs baseline, and simulation report rates rise.
