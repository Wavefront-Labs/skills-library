# desk-side-visit-etiquette

Goal: users are glad you came — the fix happened, nothing was disturbed or
seen that shouldn't be (A+ 220-1202 4.7: professionalism — punctuality,
avoiding distractions, respecting customers' materials).

## Before
- Confirm time and location; say how long it'll take and what the user
  needs to do (save work, be present for sign-in).
- Running late: tell them before the appointment time, with a new ETA.
- Bring what you need (parts, cables, loaner, ESD strap) — repeated trips
  waste their time.

## Arriving
- Introduce yourself and the ticket; ask permission before sitting at or
  touching their computer.
- Ask them to sign in themselves and to close confidential documents
  or email first. Never ask for their password.
- Appropriate attire for the site; ID badge visible.

## While working
- **No distractions**: personal phone away, no personal calls, no social
  media; don't chat with others about unrelated things while the user
  waits.
- **Respect materials**: don't read papers on the desk, open documents,
  browse their files or look at their screen content beyond what's needed.
  Printer/fax output and confidential papers stay untouched.
- Explain what you're doing in plain terms, and roughly how long.
- Keep the area tidy; don't move personal items without asking (photos,
  mugs, ergonomic setups).
- Liquid near open equipment → ask to move it.

## Leaving
- Verify with the user the problem is fixed (their account, their task).
- Return the desk to how you found it; take packaging/old parts with you.
- Lock or sign out of any admin session; remove any temporary
  credentials.
- Tell them how to follow up; update the ticket promptly.

## Gotchas
- Executives' offices: extra discretion — calendars, board papers.
- If you see something concerning (prohibited content, safety hazard),
  follow the reporting procedure rather than commenting.

## Verify
Post-visit CSAT comments, no complaints about conduct, ticket updated the
same day.
