# FinOps newsletter and cost communications

Regular, short communication keeps cost visible between reviews and makes good work visible.

## Monthly format (readable in 3 minutes)
1. **Headline number**: total spend vs forecast and the unit cost trend in one line.
2. **Win of the month**: a team, what they changed, monthly savings, and the engineering effort.
   Names and numbers, approved by the team.
3. **What changed**: provider price changes, new instance generations, deprecations (e.g. extended
   support fees for old database engine versions), new commitment purchases.
4. **Policy and process notices**: new tagging values, schedule changes, deadlines (budget cycle).
5. **Tip**: one concrete technique (e.g. "set CloudWatch Logs retention", "S3 Intelligent-Tiering
   for unknown access patterns").
6. **Upcoming**: office hours topics, training dates.

## Channels
Email or intranet post plus a Slack/Teams summary; executive version as 3 bullets in the sponsor's
staff meeting notes.

## Steps
1. Fix a publishing date (e.g. 5th working day after month close, once data is final).
2. Collect wins continuously from closed optimisation tickets and champions.
3. Fact-check numbers against the cost warehouse; one wrong number costs more credibility than ten
   right ones earn.
4. Track opens/clicks, replies and dashboard visits from links.

## Gotchas
- Shaming teams by name for overspend; praise publicly, address overspend privately.
- Walls of charts; one chart maximum.

## Verify
- [ ] Published on schedule each month with verified numbers.
- [ ] Readership and dashboard click-through are tracked.
