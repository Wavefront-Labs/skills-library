# FinOps champions

The FinOps Framework mentions champions as a way to extend the reach of the FinOps team. The pages
give no prescriptive design for a champions network, so this skill covers only what they state.

## What the Framework says
- Education and Enablement, Walk: FinOps Champion programs support the development of a FinOps culture throughout the organisation.
- Practice Operations, Run: the FinOps team works at the highest and lowest levels of staff and employs FinOps champions and others to extend its reach.
- At Walk, FinOps assessments are completed with Business Units or Champions teams; at Run they are completed in Business Units by dedicated Champions on a regular cadence, with results shared with the central team (FinOps Assessment capability).
- FinOps practitioners identify champions within each persona and collaborate with them on common enablement requirements.
- A hub and spoke FinOps team model embeds members of a central team in Engineering teams.

## Steps
1. Identify champions within each persona and agree enablement needs with them by persona and team.
2. Give them training and certification routes (FinOps Foundation certifications, persona-based training, provider training) and a shared knowledge base for documentation, terminology and training materials.
3. Include FinOps awareness in onboarding and role descriptions with HR where appropriate.
4. Measure reach with the Framework KPIs: percentage of personas who have completed training, count of learning opportunities offered, participation in internal events, and consultation of the knowledge base.

## Verify
- [ ] Champions are named per persona or team and have access to enablement material.
- [ ] Training and participation KPIs are reported.
