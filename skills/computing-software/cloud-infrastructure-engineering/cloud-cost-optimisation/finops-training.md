# FinOps training plan

Education and Enablement builds a common understanding of FinOps concepts and terminology, which is
essential for a culture of accountability for costs.

## Steps
1. Define and document training or enablement needs by persona and by team; coordinate with the organisation's training and development programmes and with HR on job definitions, skills and role descriptions.
2. Provide a baseline: FinOps Certified Practitioner, Engineer or Professional certifications and persona-based training from the FinOps Foundation, Linux Foundation, technology providers or other external trainers. Add training on the providers, services and offerings your organisation actually uses, and on its priorities.
3. Add internal channels: all-hands, hackathon-style events, brown-bag sessions, FinOps Day events, challenges and speaking opportunities.
4. Include FinOps awareness in onboarding and job descriptions to cut introductory training later.
5. Keep an internal repository of terminology, glossaries, concepts and deliverables (tagging strategy, governance policies, optimisation playbooks).
6. By maturity: Crawl is team-driven and covers basics such as metadata and account organisation, cost allocation and ownership; Walk spans teams and adds forecasting, shared costs, budgets and environmental impact, with champion programs; Run makes training required for all personas and puts it in job requirements and onboarding.

## KPIs from the Framework
- Percentage of FinOps personas who completed training: certified count divided by target, times 100.
- Count of learning opportunities offered per period.
- Participation in internal events: attendees divided by invitees, times 100.
- Consultation ratio of the knowledge base or training material.

## Gotchas
- Mind different learning styles, disciplines and levels of responsibility.
