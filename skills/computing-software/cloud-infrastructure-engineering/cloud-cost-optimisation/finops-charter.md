# FinOps team charter

The charter documents how the FinOps team is positioned, staffed, funded and empowered to decide.
It draws on the Practice Operations capability, which covers building the team, managing the practice,
and collaboration and decision making.

## What to include
1. **Team model**: central team, virtual team (for example a committee of Finance and Engineering members), hub and spoke with members embedded in engineering, or another design. The name may differ (for example Technology Business Office) or sit inside Platform Engineering or a Cloud Center of Excellence.
2. **Leadership and reporting line**: a named FinOps lead. FinOps teams usually report to the executive responsible for technology selection and use; in digitally focused organisations that is often the CTO or CIO, and where financial control dominates it may be the CFO. Keep strong relationships with every persona leader.
3. **Staffing plan, resource list and budget**, including virtual support (cloud engineers, finance, reporting, training staff) instead of only full-time hires.
4. **Scope of work, objectives and timelines** for the first activities, using general FinOps goals and KPIs while complexity is low.
5. **Decision and accountability structure**: document who has decision authority and who is advisory, reuse existing governance mechanisms where possible, and connect the team structure to wider reporting lines. Include decision models for cross-functional or urgent calls such as prepaying reservations, managing budget overages and managing anomalies.
6. **Success measures**: a KPI for savings or cost avoidance by the FinOps team, organisational awareness and adoption, and use of reports and dashboards.
7. **Principles** the practice follows: teams collaborate; business value drives decisions; everyone owns their usage; data is accessible, timely and accurate; FinOps is enabled centrally; use the variable cost model.

## Steps
1. Plan for FinOps, socialise it across personas, prepare the organisation, then drive cultural adoption. Repeat in abbreviated form for new areas, reorganisations and acquisitions.
2. Review the team's needs, size, roles and placement regularly, with Leadership checking effectiveness.

## Verify
- [ ] Decision rights and advisory roles are documented.
- [ ] The charter names staffing, funding and a sponsor.
