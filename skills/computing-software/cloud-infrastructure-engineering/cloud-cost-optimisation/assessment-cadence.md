# Reassessment cadence

The FinOps Assessment capability includes continuous assessment: set a schedule for reassessment or
extra scope and teams, re-evaluate goals, and reassess as appropriate.

## Cadence by maturity (FinOps Framework)
- Crawl: an annual assessment for the core FinOps team in a relatively simple environment, with future annual goals defined from the result and clear KPIs. Documentation can be manual.
- Walk: semi-annual or quarterly assessments with the core team as technology use grows. Annual goals are tracked against assessment output and adjusted after each assessment. Use a consistent format so Target Groups and time periods can be compared. Appoint an assessor on the core team with a documented question bank.
- Run: quarterly assessments in the core team for very complex or distributed environments, more capabilities assessed (guided by KPIs), multiple trained assessors including in Business Units or Champions teams, a central repository of past assessments with standard questions per capability, and Business Unit results shared back to the central team.

## Steps
1. Choose the cadence for your maturity and put the dates in a schedule.
2. Assess a subset of capabilities with a small Target Group rather than the whole Framework; self-assess first.
3. Keep the format internally consistent so results are comparable over time.
4. After each assessment, re-evaluate goals and KPIs and adjust the roadmap.
5. Track program-level KPIs if useful: assessments completed per year, percentage of Business Units assessed, percentage of the Framework assessed, people trained as assessors.

## Gotchas
- The goal of a Run level everywhere is not the aim; attach KPIs to a goal state aligned to organisational objectives.

## Verify
- [ ] Reassessment dates are scheduled and the format is consistent between rounds.
- [ ] Goals are re-evaluated after every assessment.
