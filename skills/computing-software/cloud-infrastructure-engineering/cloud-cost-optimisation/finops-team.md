# Building the FinOps team

"FinOps team" is an inclusive term for the people who enable, perform and support the practice,
however they are organised or named.

## Steps
1. **Choose a model**: a single central team, a fully virtual team (for example a committee of Finance and Engineering members), hub and spoke (a central team with members embedded in Engineering), or the practice inside Platform Engineering or a Cloud Center of Excellence.
2. **Placement**: FinOps teams tend to report to the executive responsible for technology selection and use. That is often the CTO or CIO in digital transformation organisations and the CFO where financial control is the focus. Keep strong links with all persona leaders to limit silos.
3. **Leadership**: name a FinOps lead. The Framework suggests a mix of deep FinOps practitioner experience, FinOps Certified Professional certification, influence in the organisation and complex project management experience.
4. **Skills**: a mix of leadership, communication, analytical and technical skills; the FinOps Practitioner persona lists technical proficiency, analysis, cost management and optimisation, collaboration, continuous improvement, problem solving and change management.
5. **Sizing**: demand depends on organisational complexity and technology complexity, so teams tend to grow as technology use becomes more varied and more teams engage. At Crawl, use virtual support (cloud engineers, finance, reporting, training staff) rather than only full-time hires. Prepare a staffing plan, resource list and budget.
6. **Review** the team's needs, size, roles and placement regularly (continuously at Run), and make sure it has a regular funding source.

## Verify
- [ ] Team model, lead and reporting line are documented.
- [ ] Staffing, budget and a review cadence exist.
