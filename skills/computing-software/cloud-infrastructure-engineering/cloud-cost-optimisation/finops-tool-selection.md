# Selecting FinOps tools and services

The Framework capability covers FinOps tools (software that enables one or more capabilities),
automation solutions (scripting, workflows, APIs) and professional services (training,
consultancy, managed services). Every organisation uses a mix of native provider tooling,
third-party or open source tools, internal tools and automation, and services.

## Steps
1. **Inventory** the automation, tools and services already in use, then map them to Framework
   capabilities to find gaps against the outcomes that matter most.
2. **Set policy and governance criteria** for evaluating, selecting and using tools, including when
   professional services or automation are warranted.
3. **Define build, buy and automate use cases**, and compare native provider tooling,
   third-party commercial solutions and internal automation, documenting the rationale.
4. **Gather requirements from every persona** (the Framework wants Practitioner, Finance and
   Engineering at Walk, extended to other personas at Run). Do market research before selecting.
5. **Judge candidates** on functionality, integration complexity, cost, scalability and
   sustainability, and treat interoperability and open standards as key criteria.
6. **Use an ROI method** with quantifiable success criteria set up front, and have Finance quantify
   the fully burdened cost of ownership for each option.
7. **Plan the rollout**: configure, integrate and onboard personas, provide training with a
   dedicated enablement budget, and prioritise by organisational unit and scope. Do not
   underestimate the effort to onboard and understand the data and workflows a tool manages
   (access, usage, and which parts of the organisation adopt first).
8. **Contract terms** (Procurement): include data access, interoperability, open standards
   compliance and exit strategies to reduce lock-in.
9. **Re-evaluate** after implementation and periodically against requirements and emerging
   alternatives.

## Gotchas
- Over-reliance on professional services creates dependency and limits internal maturity; balance
  with education and enablement.
- Selecting without knowing existing investments, strategy or policies wastes effort on options
  that cannot be used.

## Measures of success
Savings or cost avoidance against total investment, idle cost avoided, adoption by personas, user
satisfaction (for example Net Promoter Score), share of manual activities automated and time to
value.
