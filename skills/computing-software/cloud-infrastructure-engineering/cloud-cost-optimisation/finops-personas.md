# FinOps personas

Personas are broad stakeholder groups that must collaborate, not individual people. A persona may
cover many roles, and in a very small organisation one person may hold several.

## Core personas and their Framework responsibilities
| Persona | Role in FinOps |
|---|---|
| FinOps Practitioner | Bridges business, engineering and finance; builds FinOps culture and evidence-based decisions. Skills include technical proficiency, analysis, cost management and optimisation, communication, change management and team leadership |
| Engineering | Designs, builds, manages and optimises infrastructure: architecture, platform management, deployment, resource optimisation, monitoring and alerting, security, automation, sustainability |
| Finance | Quantifies the value of technology spend: policy, budgeting and forecasting support, cost allocation analysis, reporting, compliance, invoice reconciliation |
| Product | Defines requirements and priorities that align FinOps with business objectives; stakeholder engagement and value delivery |
| Procurement | Vendor selection, contract and relationship management, negotiation and discounts, licence monitoring, compliance |
| Leadership | Sets business and technical objectives, prioritisation authority, accountability culture, owns revenue growth or other success criteria |

## Allied personas
ITAM, ITFM, Sustainability, ITSM/ITIL and Security work in adjacent disciplines. Their overlap with
FinOps is described in the Intersecting Disciplines capability.

## Steps
1. List which of your teams fill each persona; name the team, not just the role.
2. For each FinOps capability, record which personas act, using the capability pages' persona activity lists.
3. Where one person covers several personas, say so explicitly.

## Gotchas
- Personas are groups; do not read the list as a headcount or org chart.
