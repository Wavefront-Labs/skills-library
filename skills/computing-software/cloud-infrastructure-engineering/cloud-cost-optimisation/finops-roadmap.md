# FinOps improvement roadmap

## Steps
1. Start from assessment outcomes: low or zero lens scores, and KPIs that miss their goals.
2. Define a goal state aligned to organisational objectives and attach KPIs to it, then define a pathway from the current state.
3. Prioritise capabilities by business value. Per the Maturity Model, the aim is not Run in every capability, and effort on a capability that already meets its measure of success gives no benefit.
4. Look at which capabilities the next stage of the roadmap relies on. The assessment guide notes that strong Reporting and Analytics gives a base for dependent capabilities such as Anomaly Management.
5. Choose specific actions for the weak lenses (for example add metrics or automation to a capability that has none).
6. Work the Inform, Optimize and Operate phases iteratively, starting small and growing scope as experience builds.
7. Reassess on a schedule, re-evaluate goals and adjust the roadmap.

## Gotchas
- Adopt realistic short-term Target Scores; a lower target can be right for a new capability.
