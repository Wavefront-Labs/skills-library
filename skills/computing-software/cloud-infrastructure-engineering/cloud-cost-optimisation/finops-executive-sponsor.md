# Executive sponsorship for FinOps

## What the Framework says
- Strong leadership of the FinOps team ("FinOps leaders", sometimes FinOps Drivers) is needed to drive the broad changes complex IT demands.
- FinOps teams usually report to the executive responsible for making technology selection and use successful: often the CTO or CIO in digital transformation focused organisations, the CFO where financial control is the focus.
- Leadership (Practice Operations functional activities): input into the team's goals; support its actions across the organisation; regularly review its effectiveness and decide changes in staffing, scope, size or structure; provide funding and resources; take part in decision models such as prepaying reservations and budget overages.
- Executive sponsorship is a named input to the capability, alongside HR and organisational design for roles and hiring.
- Leadership persona (Personas page): sets business and technical objectives, holds prioritisation authority, drives accountability culture, and owns revenue growth or other success criteria.

## Steps
1. At Crawl, prepare an executive FinOps scope summary with clear details for each executive persona, and a staffing plan, resource list and proposed budget.
2. Ask for specific things: funding, a named lead, the team's position in the organisation, and the decision models for urgent cross-functional calls.
3. Report the KPIs that show team value: savings or cost avoidance generated by the FinOps team, awareness and adoption, and use of FinOps reports.
4. Have Leadership review the team's effectiveness on a regular basis and adjust staffing and scope.

## Verify
- [ ] A sponsoring executive and the reporting line are named.
- [ ] Funding and a review of team effectiveness are in place.
