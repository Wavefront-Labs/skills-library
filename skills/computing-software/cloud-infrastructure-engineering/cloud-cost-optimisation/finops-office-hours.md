# FinOps office hours and community of practice

Office hours give teams a low-friction place to ask cost questions; the community of practice
spreads what champions learn across teams.

## Office hours
- Weekly or fortnightly, 45-60 minutes, open video call plus a booking sheet for deep dives.
- Staff: one FinOps analyst plus a rotating platform/cloud engineer.
- Typical questions: "Why did our bill jump?", "Which commitment covers this?", "How do I estimate
  this design?", "How is shared cost allocated to us?"
- Triage: answer quick questions live; open a ticket for investigations; capture every answer in
  a searchable FAQ (wiki) with the date.

## Community of practice (monthly)
1. 10-minute showcase: a team presents a saving or architecture change with numbers.
2. 15-minute topic: new pricing (e.g. a provider pricing change), a policy, a tool feature.
3. Open discussion and asks to FinOps.
Rotate presenters across teams; record sessions and post a 5-line summary.

## Steps
1. Set the calendar series and announce in engineering channels and onboarding.
2. Seed the first sessions with prepared topics; attendance grows from showcases.
3. Track: attendance, questions asked, repeat questions (FAQ gaps), actions taken afterwards.
4. Prune or merge formats if attendance stays low for a quarter.

## Gotchas
- Office hours only FinOps attends; invite champions and announce topics in advance.
- Answers lost in chat; always write them into the FAQ.

## Verify
- [ ] FAQ updated after each session.
- [ ] Showcases come from different teams each month.
