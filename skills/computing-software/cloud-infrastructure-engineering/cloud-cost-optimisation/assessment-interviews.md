# FinOps assessment conversations and evidence

## Preparation
1. Fix the Target Scope (capabilities) and Target Group (part of the organisation). Assess a subset of capabilities with a small group rather than the whole Framework, and self-assess in the FinOps team first.
2. Identify the stakeholders and subject matter experts for each capability. Different capabilities need different personas and separate targeted discussions. Record who contributed which evidence.
3. Set lens weighting and Target Scores before you start.

## Running a session
1. Open with a short overview of the capabilities in scope so everyone shares the same understanding. Use it to educate.
2. Ask discovery questions first. Build and curate a library of discovery questions per capability and gather documented evidence behind the answers, stored with the assessment.
3. Score the five lenses (Knowledge, Process, Metrics, Adoption, Automation) per capability from the evidence, each on levels 0 to 4 in the guide. Be consistent and fair, bearing in mind realistic goals for that capability.
4. Treat disagreement between stakeholders as a finding: it points to a gap in shared understanding and an opportunity to align.
5. Group capabilities that share the same stakeholders into one conversation.

## Gotchas
- The assessment gives a high-level view and is not precise analytics.
- Metrics lens: it scores how metrics are defined and used, not how high the metric values are.

## Verify
- [ ] Each lens score has evidence recorded against it.
- [ ] The scope, group and stakeholders are recorded for later baselines.
