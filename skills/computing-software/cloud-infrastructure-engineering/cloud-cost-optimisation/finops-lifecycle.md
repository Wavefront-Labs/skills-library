# FinOps lifecycle: Inform, Optimize, Operate

FinOps works iteratively through three phases on the Framework capabilities. Practitioners cycle
through them rapidly; different team members can work on different phases or at different cadences.

## Inform (visibility and allocation)
Examine technology cost, usage and efficiency data. Data Ingestion, Allocation, Reporting and
Analytics, Forecasting and Unit Economics feed budgeting, forecast trends, KPIs, benchmarking and
metrics. Accurate allocation enables accurate reporting. Revisit Inform continuously to validate the impact of optimisation actions.

## Optimize (rates and usage)
Identify and document options to improve efficiency and value. Usage optimisation (fewer resources
for an acceptable outcome) needs engineering; rate optimisation (paying an appropriate amount) needs
procurement and leadership. Options can compete, so use a collaborative process and criteria based on organisational goals to prioritise, and keep a backlog for later cycles.

## Operate (continuous improvement and usage)
Implement the changes chosen in Optimize, even small incremental ones. This needs a culture of
accountability across engineering, finance and business, and may include maturing capabilities or
evolving FinOps operations. Loop back to Inform and Optimize continuously.

## Running the loop
1. Start small and act quickly on a regular cadence to avoid analysis paralysis.
2. Grow the size and scope of actions as experience builds.
3. Measure results, make incremental improvements and refine workflows to cut the time per cycle.

## Principles to check decisions against
Teams need to collaborate; business value drives technology decisions; everyone takes ownership for their usage; FinOps data should be accessible, timely and accurate; FinOps should be enabled centrally; take advantage of the variable cost model of the cloud.

## Verify
- [ ] The team can say which phase each in-scope capability is being worked in.
- [ ] Optimisation options sit in a prioritised backlog with agreed criteria.
