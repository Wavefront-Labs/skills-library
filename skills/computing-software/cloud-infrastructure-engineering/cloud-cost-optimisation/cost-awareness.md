# Cost awareness activities

Cost awareness is part of the FinOps Education and Enablement capability, whose goal the
Framework states as delivering FinOps knowledge and skills to all personas while continuously
reinforcing adoption. It supports the principle that everyone takes ownership for their
technology usage.

## Formats the Framework names
- Internal communications and learning: all-hands meetings, FinOps-centred internal gatherings,
  "hackathon" style events and informal brown-bag sessions.
- Regular FinOps Day events that gather engineering, finance, leadership and product personas to
  train on one capability, celebrate successes and reinforce goals.
- Initiatives that let people use what they learned: internal challenges, speaking at internal or
  external events, interactive team-building activities.
- A centralised knowledge base of FinOps documentation, terminology, training material and
  deliverables (tagging strategy, governance policies, optimisation playbooks).
- A forum where any persona can submit questions and feedback to the FinOps team.

## Steps
1. Identify the training and enablement needs of each persona and team before choosing a format.
2. Pair events with training that covers the basics of the providers and services your
   organisation uses, not just FinOps concepts.
3. Keep documentation and terminology in one accessible place so people can learn on their own.
4. Measure participation and reach (below) and ask managers whether they feel confident sharing
   cost data with their staff.

## Measures from the Framework
- Participation in internal FinOps events: attendees divided by invited, times 100. The page
  expects it to rise at first and stabilise once the target population is mobilised.
- Count of FinOps learning opportunities offered per period.
- Consultation ratio: teams or stakeholders who consulted the wiki or training material.
- Percentage of FinOps personas who completed training or certification.

## Verify
- [ ] Each activity has a stated audience and an enablement need it addresses.
- [ ] Participation and learning-opportunity counts are recorded per period.
