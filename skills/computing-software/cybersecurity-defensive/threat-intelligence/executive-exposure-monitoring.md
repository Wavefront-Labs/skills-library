# Executive exposure monitoring

Executives' personal information enables spear phishing, BEC, SIM swapping, doxxing and physical threats.

## Scope (with executive consent)
Named executives and board members, sometimes family; personal and corporate email addresses, phone
numbers, home addresses, social accounts, family members' public profiles.

## Monitoring
- **Data brokers and people-search sites**: home addresses, phone numbers; services (DeleteMe, Kanary,
  DRP vendors) or manual opt-out requests.
- **Breach and stealer data**: personal and corporate credentials (Have I Been Pwned domain monitoring,
  DRP vendor stealer-log coverage).
- **Impersonation**: fake social profiles, WhatsApp/Signal accounts using their photo, deepfake
  audio/video used for fraud.
- **Threat signals**: violent threats, doxxing posts, protest plans mentioning executives (with
  physical security team).
- **Oversharing**: travel posts, family details, real-time locations.

## Actions
- Removal requests to brokers (recurring; data reappears), platform takedown for impersonation.
- Credential resets and MFA hardening on personal accounts (offer support - personal accounts are
  often the weak link), carrier port-out/SIM-swap protection PINs.
- Awareness briefings tailored to the executive and their assistants; verification procedures for
  payment or sensitive requests (call-back to known numbers).
- Escalate credible physical threats to security/law enforcement immediately.

## Gotchas
- Privacy: monitoring personal life requires informed consent and clear boundaries.
- Executive assistants have access to everything; include them in protection.

## Verify
Quarterly exposure report per executive: items found, removed, outstanding; reduction in broker
listings over time.
