# Installation limits

- Maximum pulling tension for 4-pair UTP is commonly 110 N (25 lbf); exceed it and the pair geometry changes and tests fail.
- Do not overtighten zip ties; use hook-and-loop on bundles. Avoid sharp edges: use grommets and bushings.
- Separation from power: separate data and power cables by distances prescribed by the electrical code and TIA-569; pass at right angles where crossing is unavoidable.
- Support cable with J-hooks or baskets at intervals (commonly no more than 1.2-1.5 m); do not rest on ceiling tiles or grids.
- Leave service loops (slack) at both ends and at patch panels in line with the design.

## Verify
- [ ] No kinks or visible deformation, correct support spacing, and passing certification.
