# Labeling and records (TIA-606)

- Label both ends of every cable and each patch panel port with a unique identifier that maps to the record (room-panel-port style).
- Labels must be machine-readable-friendly, durable and wrap-around on cables; print them, not handwritten.
- Records: floor plans with outlet IDs, panel schedules, cross-connect lists, test reports. Keep them in a system where they are updated at every move-add-change.
- Colour conventions (cable jackets or patch cords by VLAN/function) help but are not a substitute for labels.
- Re-audit annually and after construction projects.

## Verify
- [ ] Random-sample outlets: label, record and switch port all agree.
