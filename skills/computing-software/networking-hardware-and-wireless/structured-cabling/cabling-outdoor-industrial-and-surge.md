# Outdoor and industrial runs

- Use outdoor/UV-rated, gel-filled or water-blocked cable; bury in conduit or use direct-burial rated cable. Drip loops at entry.
- Shielded cable with bonded ends needs a single clear grounding scheme; mixed grounds create ground loops. Fibre removes the problem between buildings.
- Surge protectors at both building entries for copper links between structures (lightning-induced voltages); follow local electrical code.
- Industrial: M12 (D- or X-coded) and sealed RJ45 for vibration and washdown; check the temperature range and IP rating.
- PoE over long outdoor runs: voltage drop and temperature; stay inside 100 m or use extenders designed for it.

## Verify
- [ ] Link is certified and the enclosure passes a visual IP-rating check (seals, glands) after install.
