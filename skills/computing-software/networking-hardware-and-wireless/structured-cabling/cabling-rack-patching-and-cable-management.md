# Rack practice

- Terminate horizontal cable to a patch panel, not to the switch; patch from panel to switch with short cords of the right length.
- Use colour by function (consistently documented) and label patch cords at both ends.
- Slack: service loops with strain relief; keep bend radius; no cable resting on equipment, and keep fibre and copper separated.
- Moves-adds-changes: update records at the same time; remove abandoned cables (they are also a code issue in plenum spaces).
- Photograph racks before and after changes.
