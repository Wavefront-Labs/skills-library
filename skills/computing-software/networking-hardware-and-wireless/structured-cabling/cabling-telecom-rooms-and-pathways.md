# Pathways and spaces (TIA-569)

- Plan telecom rooms for cooling, power, racks with clearances, and a ceiling-height/size per standard guidance. Do not share with water, plumbing or electrical panels.
- Fill: size trays/conduit for planned growth; commonly cited maximum fill is about 40 percent for conduit in new design, with growth buffer. Confirm figures with the standard and local code.
- Firestop every penetration with a listed system; document it.
- Grounding/bonding of racks and shielded cabling per TIA-607 and electrical code.
- Separate fibre and copper in trays with innerduct for fibre protection.

## Verify
- [ ] Room layout and pathways match the design drawing; firestopping inspected and signed off.
