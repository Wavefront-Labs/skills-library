# Manage app ownership and lifecycle
**Goal:** Avoid orphaned and unmanaged apps.

- Record owner and co-owner; review inactive assets; reassign when owners leave.
- Promote critical apps to managed solutions with support owners.
- Retire unused assets.

## Gotchas
- Apps owned by departed staff stop receiving connection updates.

## Self-check
Quarterly report of orphaned and unused assets is actioned.
