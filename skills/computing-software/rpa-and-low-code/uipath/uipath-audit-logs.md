# Review audit logs
**Goal:** Track configuration changes.

- Orchestrator audit logs record create/update/delete of assets, queues and processes with user and time.
- Export to SIEM.
- Retain per policy.

## Gotchas
- Audit retention may be limited by default; check settings.

## Self-check
Change to an asset appears in the audit log.
