# Alert on failures and measure
**Goal:** Detect failures early and report performance.

- Orchestrator alerts and the Insights module give job and queue metrics; integrate with email, Teams or ITSM.
- Alert on failed jobs, stalled queues and licence limits.
- Review weekly.

## Gotchas
- Alerts without an owner are ignored.

## Self-check
Forced failure triggers the alert path.
