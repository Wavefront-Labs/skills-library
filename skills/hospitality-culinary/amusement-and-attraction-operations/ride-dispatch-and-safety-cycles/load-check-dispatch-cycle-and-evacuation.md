# Load, check, dispatch — and evacuation

**Goal:** run every cycle the same safe way, at a steady dispatch interval, and know
exactly what to do when the ride stops. **This is a general method; each ride's
manufacturer manual and your park's Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) override it.**
Operators must be trained and signed off on the specific ride (ASTM F770 and local law
require operator training and records — *check your jurisdiction*).

## 1. Before opening (opening checks)
- Maintenance has signed the ride off for the day (daily inspection complete).
- Operator walk-through: platform, gates, restraints on each seat, signage, height checks,
  e-stops, communication (radio/intercom) tested.
- Required test cycles run empty (often 2+); listen and watch for anything unusual.
- Staffing meets the minimum in the SOP; every position knows its role.

## 2. The cycle
**Load:**
1. Air gates/exit gates open only on the operator's cue. Grouper assigns rows; check rider
   eligibility (height, posture, single-rider rules) at the gate.
2. Loose articles: phones, hats, glasses secured or in bins per ride rules.
3. Riders sit fully back, correct posture; restraints lowered.

**Restraint check (the critical step):**
4. **Physically check every restraint** — push/pull the lap bar or harness, check belt
   buckles, seat by seat, in a fixed pattern (e.g. left to right, front to back) so none is
   skipped. Look *and* touch; "it looks down" is not a check.
5. Anyone who doesn't fit the restraint safely (gap, over-the-limit, can't hold the position)
   is removed politely — never "make it fit".

**All clear and dispatch:**
6. Checkers clear the zone, step behind the line and give the **all-clear signal**
   (hand signal/button) to the operator.
7. Operator scans: gates closed, platform clear, no one in restricted areas, panel shows
   ready. Dispatch only with all conditions met — **never by pressure of the queue**.

**Unload:**
8. Vehicle stops in station; restraints release on operator command; guests exit
   to the unload side; check seats for lost property; exit gate closes.

## 3. Timing
- Parks set a **target dispatch interval** for each ride (e.g. 30–45 s on a coaster) and
  measure station time. Throughput comes from consistent rhythm: grouping ahead, loose-
  article management, clear spiel — not from rushed checks.
- Any repeated delay (a restraint that sticks, a tight seat) is reported, not worked around.

## 4. Stoppages
- An **e-stop** or system stop: stay calm, follow the panel's fault indication (see
  hospitality-culinary.amusement-and-attraction-operations.ride-control-systems.operator-console-block-zones-and-fault-recovery).
- Use the on-ride **spiel**: tell riders they're safe, to keep restraints closed, stay seated,
  and that help is coming. Repeat at intervals.
- Notify supervisor/maintenance per SOP. Do **not** reset and restart unless the SOP
  authorises that operator to do so for that fault.

## 5. Evacuation (principles)
- Only trained, authorised staff evacuate, using the ride's approved procedure (walkways,
  stairs, lifts, rope access, fire/rescue support for some rides).
- Ride must be locked out (lockout/tagout) before anyone enters the ride envelope.
- Evacuate in the planned order (often vehicles nearest hazards or most exposed first),
  one restraint released at a time under staff control.
- Guests with disabilities or children — follow the specific plan for them.
- After: guest recovery (water, shade, first aid, return/fast-pass compensation), and a
  written incident report.

## Gotchas
- Complacency on repetitive checks is the biggest risk; rotate positions per SOP (often every 30–60 min).
- Weather limits: lightning and wind thresholds close many rides — know them.
- Never let guests re-enter the ride area for dropped items; staff retrieve after closure/lockout.

## Verify
- [ ] Daily sign-off and test cycles done before first guests.
- [ ] Every restraint physically checked every cycle, in a fixed pattern.
- [ ] Dispatch only after all-clear and panel ready.
- [ ] Stoppage spiel delivered; restart only as authorised.
- [ ] Evacuation drills current; incident reports filed.
