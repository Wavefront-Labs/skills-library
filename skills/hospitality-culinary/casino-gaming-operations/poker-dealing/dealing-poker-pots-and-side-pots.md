# Dealing poker: pots, side pots and table control

**Goal:** run the hand so every player knows whose turn it is, how much it costs, and
who wins what — without a floor dispute. House rules and (for tournaments) the
event's rule set — many follow the Poker TDA rules — take precedence over this page.

## 1. Before the deal
- Button and blinds correct (dead button / moving button per house).
- Shuffle per procedure (often wash, riffle ×3, box, riffle, cut) with cards low and flat.
- Deal clockwise from the small blind; keep cards low so nobody sees a face.

## 2. Reading the action
- Announce each street: "Flop", and the pot size in limit or on request in no-limit.
- **Point at the player to act** if the table is slow; never act on a player's behalf.
- Watch for:
  - **String bet** — chips put out in multiple motions without declaring raise; it's a
    call by default in most rooms unless raise was verbally declared first.
  - **Single oversized chip** without a declaration = a call.
  - **Minimum raise** in no-limit: at least the size of the previous bet or raise on that
    street. Example: blinds 1/2, A raises to 6 (raise of 4); B's minimum re-raise is to 10.
  - **Verbal is binding** in most rooms: "raise" commits them to a legal raise.
- **Out-of-turn action**: stop it, per house rule it may be binding if action doesn't
  change. Call the floor when unsure.

## 3. Pot management
- Pull bets into the pot only when the street's action is complete.
- Keep the pot stacked by colour so it can be counted quickly.
- **Rake** (cash games): take per the posted schedule at the posted points (e.g.
  percentage up to a cap, only if a flop is dealt — "no flop, no drop" where used).
  Drop it immediately in the slot; never set it aside.

## 4. Side pots, step by step
When a player is all-in for less than others are betting:
1. Find the **smallest all-in** amount still in the hand.
2. Take that amount from **every** player who has that much or more in front → main pot.
3. The excess forms a side pot; repeat with the next smallest all-in.
Worked example: A all-in 50, B all-in 120, C calls 300 and D calls 300.
- Main pot: 50 × 4 = 200 (A, B, C, D eligible)
- Side pot 1: (120−50) × 3 = 210 (B, C, D)
- Side pot 2: (300−120) × 2 = 360 (C, D)
Total 770 = 50+120+300+300. ✔
At showdown, **award the last side pot first** (between the fewest players), then work
back to the main pot. Keep pots physically separate and announce each.

## 5. Showdown
- Last aggressor on the final street shows first (or per house rule).
- **Cards speak**: read every hand yourself; don't trust a player's announcement.
- A player must show all cards to win. Kill losing hands only when the winner is clear.
- Omaha: exactly **two from hand and three from the board** — the most common misread.
- Split pots: odd chip goes per house rule (often to first seat left of the button).

## 6. Tournament procedure
- Blinds/antes per the clock; announce level changes at the next hand.
- Chip race at colour-ups per procedure.
- Hand-for-hand near the bubble; "seat open" moves from the floor only.
- Never comment on play or hint at hands.

## 7. Table control
Calm, neutral, consistent. Enforce one-player-to-a-hand, no phones during a live hand
(per house), English-only/no-language rules if applicable. Call **"Floor!"** for any
dispute, irregularity or misdeal decision; don't argue rules yourself.

## Gotchas
- Pushing a pot before all hands are read.
- Building side pots from the wrong player (count the all-in's chips first).
- Exposing a card during the deal — follow misdeal rules exactly.
- Losing track of the button after a player busts.

## Verify
- [ ] Side-pot drill: totals always equal sum of all contributions.
- [ ] Minimum raise computed correctly in multi-raise sequences.
- [ ] Omaha boards read correctly in a timed drill.
- [ ] Floor called, not improvised, for every disputed ruling.
