# Dealing roulette and craps

**Goal:** run the wheel and the dice table accurately and at pace, with payouts you can
do in your head. Payouts below are the **common standard**; check your table's
signage and procedure manual, as houses and jurisdictions vary.

## Roulette

### Spin procedure
1. Call **"Place your bets."**
2. Spin the **wheel head** one way, then launch the **ball** in the opposite direction
   from the ball track, varying the force and the release point (house procedures
   often require this, and surveillance checks for dealer signature).
3. As the ball slows: **"No more bets"** (wave over the layout). Late bets are
   refused or called to the floor.
4. Ball drops: announce the number and colour ("17 black"), place the **dolly
   (marker)** on the winning number.
5. **Clear losing bets first** (outside, then inside), then **pay** — outside bets
   in place, then inside bets, with the winner's chips stacked beside the dolly.
6. Remove the dolly — betting reopens.

### Wheel chips and mucking
- Each player gets a **unique colour** of wheel chips with a value set at buy-in
  (marked by a "lammer"/button on the rack). Colours must never mix.
- **Mucking**: gathering losing chips and sorting by colour into stacks of 20 (or
  house standard) quickly and accurately. Build "cuts" of 5 for counting.
- Colour back to value chips when a player leaves.

### Payouts (single- and double-zero tables pay the same odds)
| Bet | Pays | | Bet | Pays |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Straight up (1 no.) | 35:1 | | Six line (6) | 5:1 |
| Split (2) | 17:1 | | Dozen / column | 2:1 |
| Street (3) | 11:1 | | Red/black, odd/even, high/low | 1:1 |
| Corner (4) | 8:1 | | | |
Memorise multiples of 35 and 17 (e.g. 7 × 35 = 245) and **picture bets**: a number
fully loaded (straight, splits, corners, streets) has a known total payout you learn as
a table. European single-zero tables may have *la partage/en prison* on even money bets.
*[Rule availability is house-specific.]*

### Wheel integrity
At open, check the wheel is level, the head spins freely, frets aren't loose, and log
results for surveillance analysis of biased wheels.

## Craps

### The crew
Typical full table: **stickman** (controls dice, calls the game, proposition bets),
**two base dealers** (each end — line, place, odds, field bets), **boxman** (supervises,
guards the bank). Smaller tables combine roles.

### Flow
1. **Come-out roll**: 7 or 11 wins pass line; 2, 3, 12 ("craps") loses pass line
   (don't pass usually pushes 12 — or 2, check the layout). Any other number becomes
   the **point**; place the puck "ON" that number.
2. Point roll: point repeated = pass wins; **7-out** = pass loses, dice pass to the next
   shooter.
3. Stick calls are standard and loud: "Seven, winner on the come-out", "Six, the hard
   way" / "easy six", "Seven out, line away".
Dice stay on the layout; a die off the table is checked per procedure before re-use.

### Payout maths
| Bet | Pays |
|---|---|
| Pass/come, don't pass/don't come | 1:1 |
| **Odds** behind pass on 4/10 · 5/9 · 6/8 | 2:1 · 3:2 · 6:5 (true odds) |
| **Place** 4/10 · 5/9 · 6/8 | 9:5 · 7:5 · 7:6 |
| Field (one roll) | 1:1; 2 and 12 often 2:1 / 3:1 (varies) |
| Hard 6/8 · hard 4/10 | 9:1 · 7:1 |
| Any seven · any craps · 11 (yo) · 2 or 12 | 4:1 · 7:1 · 15:1 · 30:1 (often "for 1" variants) |
Place 6/8 in multiples of $6 and 5/9 in $5s so the pay is clean. Check whether
the layout says "to 1" or "for 1" (for 1 includes the stake).

## Gotchas
- Paying before clearing — losing chips get swept into payouts.
- Calling the wrong number on a missed read; always read both dice.
- Mixing two players' wheel colours.
- Leaving the puck OFF/ON wrong — every subsequent decision goes wrong.

## Verify
- [ ] Picture-bet and 35×/17× drills done fast and error-free.
- [ ] Odds and place payouts correct for every point number.
- [ ] Spin procedure varies ball release; dolly always down before clearing.
- [ ] Clean-hands and mucking observed by a supervisor.
