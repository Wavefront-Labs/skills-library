# Jackpot hand-pays and slot attendant duties

**Goal:** pay the right person the right amount for a legitimate win, fix what you're
allowed to fix fast, and keep guests happy at the machines. Thresholds, forms and
signatures come from your property's internal controls and jurisdiction.

## 1. Jackpot hand-pay — step by step
A hand-pay happens when a win exceeds the machine's pay/printer limit or triggers a
tax-reporting lockup.
1. **Respond fast and congratulate.** The machine is locked; the guest is excited and
   watching.
2. **Verify the win on the machine**: the jackpot display, the winning combination on
   the reels/screen, credits bet, and the paytable. Check game history/recall if the
   procedure requires. Large jackpots: wait for the required supervisor or slot tech
   and, often, surveillance confirmation **before** anything is paid.
3. **Check for faults**: a tilt or malfunction voids pays per most machine glass
   ("Malfunction voids all pays and plays"); call slot tech if anything looks wrong.
4. **ID the guest** with valid government photo ID. Confirm age and that the guest is
   not on a self-exclusion or barred list (system check).
5. **Tax paperwork** where required. In the US, the IRS **W-2G** threshold for slots
   was long **$1,200**; it was raised (to $2,000, indexed, from 2026) by recent
   federal legislation. *[Verify the current threshold and your jurisdiction's own
   rules; outside the US, rules differ completely.]* Collect the taxpayer ID as
   required; note backup withholding rules.
6. **Paperwork and signatures**: jackpot slip with machine number, amount, time;
   witness signatures as thresholds require (attendant, supervisor, security, cage).
7. **Pay** in cash in view of camera (counting it out to the guest), or by cheque/
   ticket per policy. Get the guest's signature.
8. **Reset the machine** (key reset) and confirm it returns to normal play.

## 2. Common machine faults (attendant level)
| Fault | Usual first fix |
|---|---|
| Ticket printer out of paper / jammed | Replace stock, clear jam, test print |
| Bill validator jam / stacker full | Clear jam; stacker full → call for drop |
| Door open / door tilt | Close and secure; check the door was opened by authorised staff |
| Reel/screen tilt, RAM error, persistent error | **Call slot tech**; don't power-cycle unless allowed |
| Hopper empty (coin machines) | Hopper fill per procedure with paperwork |
Log every door opening and fault. Don't open machines beyond your access level; many
jurisdictions log each door access.

## 3. Disputes
"The machine took my money" / "I should have won": check game recall/history with the
guest, call a supervisor and slot tech if needed; surveillance can review. Never pay
on a claim without verification; never argue.

## 4. Floor layout awareness
- Know your section's machine numbers, denominations, banks and progressives.
- Report machines that are down, dirty, or have broken chairs and lights.
- High-traffic aisle machines, progressives and new titles drive play; feedback to
  slot operations helps placement decisions.

## 5. Guest service and responsible gambling
- Know where ATMs, cashier, restrooms and help lines are.
- Watch for signs of harm: very long sessions, distress, chasing losses, children left
  waiting, someone pleading for credit. Follow the property's responsible-gambling
  procedure — usually alert a supervisor or trained RG staff; don't diagnose.
- Never leave a guest's jackpot paperwork, ID or cash out of sight.

## Gotchas
- Paying before verification or supervisory sign-off.
- Missing a self-excluded player check.
- Resetting a machine before the jackpot is documented.
- Promising a payout on a malfunction.
