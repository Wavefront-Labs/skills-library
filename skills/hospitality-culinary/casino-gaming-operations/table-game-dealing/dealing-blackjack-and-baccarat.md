# Dealing blackjack and baccarat

**Goal:** deal accurate, fast, camera-readable games that follow your casino's written
procedure. **House procedure and local regulation always override this page** —
rules, payouts and hand signals vary by property and jurisdiction.

## 1. Principles that apply to every table game
- **Clean hands**: whenever you leave the table or move between chip rack and layout,
  show both palms front and back to the surveillance camera ("clearing hands").
- **Every action visible**: cards and chips stay above the table, over the layout.
  Nothing goes to pockets, apron or below the rail.
- **Announce** unusual events loudly for the floor supervisor: "Check play", "Change
  colour", "Money plays", "Cash change 500".
- **Never touch a player's chips after the result is known** except to pay or take.

## 2. Blackjack: the round
1. Players place bets inside the betting circle before the first card. Late bets
   are refused once the deal begins.
2. Deal from the shoe (or hand-held/pitch game per house) clockwise: one card to each
   player face up, one to the dealer, second round, dealer's second card per house
   (face down "hole card", or no-hole-card rules in some jurisdictions).
3. If the dealer shows an Ace, offer **insurance** (up to half the original bet, pays
   2:1). Peek for blackjack per house procedure.
4. Each player in turn: read **hand signals**, not just words — tap the felt to hit,
   wave flat to stand, a second matching bet beside the first for double or split.
   Repeat the decision aloud on busy tables.
5. Bust hands: take the bet and cards **immediately**.
6. Dealer plays to the house rule printed on the felt (e.g. "Dealer must stand on all
   17s" vs hits soft 17). No discretion.
7. Settle **right to left** (from your right, last position first), paying then
   clearing each spot.

## 3. Blackjack payouts
- Win: 1:1. Blackjack: usually **3:2** (a $10 bet gets $15); some tables pay **6:5**
  ($10 gets $12) — check the felt.
- Insurance: 2:1. Push (tie): bet stays, no action.
- Learn odd 3:2 multiples cold: $5→7.50, $15→22.50, $25→37.50, $35→52.50, $45→67.50.
  Pay in the fewest chips, cut and sized beside the bet so the camera can read it.

## 4. Baccarat (punto banco)
- Players bet **Player**, **Banker** or **Tie** (plus side bets per house). Neither
  side is "the player"; drawing is entirely by the fixed tableau — no decisions.
- Deal two cards each to Player and Banker; total is the last digit (10s and face
  cards = 0). 8 or 9 is a natural; otherwise apply the **third-card rules** printed
  in your procedure manual. Memorise them; don't improvise.
- Pay Player 1:1; Banker 1:1 **minus 5% commission** (tracked by commission markers
  and collected at shoe end) or the house's "no-commission" variant (e.g. Banker win on
  6 pays half). Tie typically pays 8:1; Player/Banker bets push on a tie.
- Midi/big-table baccarat may let players squeeze/turn cards per house rules.

## 5. Card handling and pitching
- Hold the deck in a mechanic's grip; pitch so cards slide flat to the player's spot
  without flipping. Practise until 90%+ land in the box.
- Burn cards and cut-card placement exactly as written.
- Spread and verify decks at open (all 52, faces then backs) for the camera.

## 6. Pace
Smooth beats fast. Typical full-table blackjack rates are often quoted around 50–70
hands per hour. *[Industry rule of thumb; varies widely.]* Lose pace on payouts, not
by rushing card reads. Errors cost more than seconds.

## Gotchas
- Paying before dealer completes their hand, or turning the hole card early.
- Settling out of order → missed or double payouts.
- Accepting a bet "past posted" (added after the result) — call the floor.
- Mixing chip denominations in a payout so the camera can't count it.
