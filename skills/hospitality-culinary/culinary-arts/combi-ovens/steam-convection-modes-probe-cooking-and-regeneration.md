# Combi ovens: modes, probe cooking and regeneration

**Goal:** use one oven to steam, roast and hold accurately by core temperature, and
regenerate hundreds of plated meals hot and uniform. Brands (Rational, Convotherm,
Unox, Eloma and others) name things differently; settings here are generic — check
your model's manual and built-in intelligent programs.

## 1. The three modes
| Mode | Temp range (typical) | Humidity | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Steam** | ~30–130 °C (above 100 = forced steam on some models) | 100 % | veg, rice, fish, dumplings, eggs, low-temp poaching |
| **Convection (hot air)** | ~30–300 °C | dry; vent open | roasting, browning, pastry, gratins |
| **Combination** | ~30–300 °C | adjustable (0–100 %) | roasts, braises, bread, reheating — moist inside, browned outside |

- **Humidity rules of thumb** (adjust to results): roasts 30–60 % first stage then
  drier to crisp; braises 70–100 %; bread a short burst of steam then dry; breaded/
  fried items 0 % with vent open.
- **Fan speed:** reduce for delicate items (meringue, soufflé, custards) so they don't
  skew or skin.

## 2. Core-probe cooking
1. Insert the probe through the **thickest part** to the centre, at an angle, not
   touching bone or the tray. Use the probe's multi-point sensing where available
   (it reads the coldest point).
2. Set target core, not time. The oven ends or switches stage when reached.
3. For a mixed tray, probe the **largest** piece.
4. **Delta-T cooking:** cabinet stays a fixed number of degrees (e.g. 30–50 °C) above
   the current core temperature, rising with it — gentler, less shrinkage on large joints;
   takes longer.
5. **Cook and hold / overnight:** low cabinet temp (e.g. 60–80 °C), probe to target
   (e.g. 58 °C for beef), then hold. Hold times must fit your HACCP plan.

## 3. Multi-stage programs
Example: pork belly
1. Combi 140 °C, 80 % humidity, to core 75 °C.
2. Combi 120 °C, 50 %, 2 h (collagen).
3. Convection 230 °C, 0 %, vent open, 10–15 min (crackling).
Save as a program with a name and photo; lock it so the result is consistent across
shifts.

## 4. Regeneration (banqueting)
- **Plate cold:** plate in advance onto cold plates, cover or stack on mobile plate racks
  with thermal covers, chill to ≤ 5 °C.
- **Regenerate:** combination mode, typically ~120–135 °C, moderate humidity, until a
  probed plate core is steaming hot all the way through and reaches the reheat temperature in your HACCP plan
  (gov.uk: reheat until steaming hot all the way through, and reheat only once).
- Plate the **dense items together** in the plate centre, sauce on just before or after
  regeneration to avoid skin; garnish after.
- Load the whole rack at once (preheated oven), close fast, serve fast.

## 5. Loading and capacity
- Gastronorm sizes: GN 1/1 = 530 × 325 mm; depths 20/40/65/100/150 mm. Shallower pans
  cook more evenly.
- Perforated pans for steaming veg; solid pans for liquids, placed on lower shelves.
- Mixed loads: same mode and temperature, different times — use the oven's per-shelf
  timers ("rack timing") where available.

## 6. Cleaning and water
- Run the built-in cleaning program daily with the manufacturer's tablets/liquids;
  **gloves and goggles** — the chemicals are caustic.
- Hard water scales the steam generator: use water treatment/softening as the
  manufacturer specifies and descale on schedule, or the warranty may not cover it.
- Clean the door seal and probe after each use.

## Gotchas
- Opening the door often dumps humidity; programs recover, but timings lengthen.
- Probe left in the oven during a clean cycle can be damaged on some models.
- Using convection for everything wastes the oven's main advantage.

## Verify
- [ ] Mode and humidity match the product type on the program sheet.
- [ ] Core probe placed in the largest piece; final core logged.
- [ ] Banquet regeneration: probed plate meets the reheat temperature before service.
- [ ] Daily clean run and logged; water treatment/descale on schedule.
