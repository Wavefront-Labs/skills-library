# Running the night audit and date rollover

**Goal:** close the hotel's business day so that every room, charge, payment and
report is correct before the date rolls — and keep guests and the building safe
while you're often the only person on duty.

Exact steps and button names come from your PMS (Opera, Mews, Cloudbeds,
Protel, etc.); most automate the posting itself. **Your job is the checking
before and after.** Order below is typical, not universal.

## 1. Before the audit (late evening)
1. **Arrivals still due:** check every outstanding arrival. Guaranteed late arrivals
   stay; non-guaranteed ones may be cancelled per policy at the cut-off time.
2. **Departures not checked out:** confirm with housekeeping/room status — a late
   check-out, a guest who left without settling, or a status error.
3. **Room status discrepancy report:** desk shows occupied but housekeeping says
   vacant (a "skipper" risk), or the reverse ("sleeper"). Resolve each one.
4. **Outlet charges posted:** restaurant, bar, spa, minibar — check every outlet has
   closed and transferred charges to rooms. Missing postings are the most common
   lost revenue.

## 2. Reconciliation
- **Cashier reports:** each shift's cash, card and other payments vs PMS totals.
  Count and record any variances with a reason, don't silently adjust.
- **Credit card batch:** card terminal or gateway totals vs PMS card payments.
- **High-balance report:** in-house guests whose balance exceeds their pre-auth or
  credit limit. Top up the authorisation or flag for the morning team to request
  payment.
- **Rate check:** each in-house room's rate vs its reservation (zero-rate or
  complimentary rooms must have manager authorisation codes).

## 3. No-shows
Process guaranteed no-shows per policy: charge the first night (or the cancellation
fee) to the guaranteeing card, note it, release the room. Double-check that it's a
true no-show and not a misspelled walk-in under another name.

## 4. Run the audit / roll the date
1. Back up or print key reports first if your PMS requires it (offline copies of
   in-house guests and arrivals help if the system goes down overnight).
2. Run the end-of-day: the PMS posts room and tax charges to each folio, closes the
   business date, and rolls to the next.
3. After rollover, check the **posted room and tax total** against expected (rooms
   occupied × rates) and investigate any exceptions the PMS lists.
4. Print or distribute the **manager's flash / daily report**: occupancy, ADR,
   RevPAR, revenue by department, arrivals and departures for the new day.

## 5. Solo overnight cover
- Keep the front door locked/controlled per policy after a set hour; verify
  identity before issuing a key or confirming a room number.
- Know: fire panel location and procedure, emergency contacts, first-aid kit,
  where the duty manager on call is.
- **Incident log:** record noise complaints, disturbances, medical calls, fire alarm
  activations, doctors called, damage — times, rooms, actions.
- Walk-ins at 3 am: valid ID and payment up front; you can refuse if you have
  concerns per house policy.

## Gotchas
- Rolling the date before outlets have posted — charges land on the wrong day.
- "Fixing" a cash variance with an adjustment rather than reporting it.
- Charging a no-show who actually checked in under another name.
- Issuing a duplicate key without verifying identity.
- Not having a downtime report printed when the PMS goes offline at 4 am.

## Verify
- [ ] All arrivals, departures and status discrepancies resolved before rollover.
- [ ] Every outlet closed and posted.
- [ ] Cash and card reconciled; variances recorded with reasons.
- [ ] High balances chased or flagged; rates checked.
- [ ] No-shows processed correctly.
- [ ] Date rolled; room and tax posting checked; flash report sent.
- [ ] Incident log complete for the night.

*Uncertain:* audit sequence and automation differ by PMS; follow your property's
SOP where it differs.
