# Room cleaning sequence, inspection and room status

**Goal:** every room is cleaned to the same standard in predictable time, released
to the front desk the moment it's inspected, and nothing — linen, amenities, room
status — is the reason a guest waits.

## 1. Morning allocation
- From the PMS housekeeping report, list departures, stayovers, arrivals with early
  check-in, VIPs, out-of-order rooms.
- **Allocate by credits, not room count:** e.g. departure = 1 credit, stayover = 0.5,
  suite = 1.5–2. A typical attendant shift is often quoted around 12–16 departure
  equivalents in limited-service hotels and fewer in luxury — **set your own from
  timed cleans**, since layouts and standards vary hugely.
- Prioritise early arrivals' due-out rooms and VIPs; group each attendant's rooms on
  one floor to cut walking.

## 2. The cleaning sequence (departure room)
Work **top to bottom, dry to wet, clean to dirty**, so you never dirty what you
just cleaned.
1. Knock, announce "Housekeeping", wait, knock again; respect DND. Prop the door
   with the cart (blocks the doorway, signals presence).
2. Open curtains/windows, lights on, check for guest items left behind and damage.
3. **Strip the bed** and remove dirty linen and towels; collect rubbish. Check
   mattress seams and headboard for **bed bugs** (rust-coloured spots, live insects)
   — if found, stop, close the room, report.
4. **Bathroom** first chemically: spray toilet, shower and basin so the product has
   contact time while you make the bed.
5. **Make the bed** with fresh linen (hospital corners or the property's standard).
6. Dust high to low: light fittings, picture tops, headboard, furniture, TV, remote,
   switches and handles (high-touch points).
7. Back to the bathroom: scrub, rinse, dry and polish; **toilet last** with its own
   colour-coded cloth (colour coding per house — commonly red for toilets).
8. Restock amenities, towels, coffee/tea, minibar per par.
9. Vacuum from the far corner back to the door.
10. Final look from the doorway, set lights/curtains to standard, close, update status.

**Stayovers:** make the bed (or change linen per the green/linen-reuse policy),
replace used towels, empty bins, tidy and wipe — don't move guests' belongings
beyond what's needed.

## 3. Inspection
An inspector or supervisor checks a sample (or every room in luxury properties)
against a written checklist: hair in the bathroom, dust on high surfaces, under-bed,
mirror smears, amenities, smell, working lights/TV/AC. Record defects and feed
back to the attendant the same day. Release the room to **Inspected / Vacant Clean**
only after it passes.

## 4. Room status — the link with the front desk
Status codes are typically: Occupied, Vacant Dirty, Vacant Clean, Inspected, Out of
Order (unsellable, maintenance), Out of Service (short-term, still in inventory),
DND, Sleep-out / Skipper discrepancies. Update in the PMS or app **immediately** —
a clean room still showing dirty is lost revenue and a waiting guest.
Run the **discrepancy check** (housekeeping vs front-desk status) at least daily.

## 5. Linen and chemicals
- Linen par: commonly cited as 3–5 sets per bed (one on the bed, one in laundry,
  one in store, plus spares). Count weekly; track losses and damage.
- Chemicals: correct dilution, never mix (bleach + acid or ammonia gives toxic gas),
  gloves and training per safety data sheets / COSHH-type rules.

## Gotchas
- Cleaning the bathroom last means no contact time and more scrubbing.
- Toilet cloth reused on the basin — the classic hygiene failure.
- Status not updated → desk sells a dirty room or holds a clean one.
- Lost property not logged with date, room, finder.
- Missing bed-bug checks until a guest finds them.

## Verify
- [ ] Rooms allocated by credits, priorities set for early arrivals and VIPs.
- [ ] Sequence followed: strip, pre-spray bathroom, bed, dust high-to-low, bathroom, vacuum out.
- [ ] Colour-coded cloths; toilet cloth never used elsewhere.
- [ ] Bed-bug check done on every strip.
- [ ] Room inspected and status updated in the PMS immediately.
- [ ] Discrepancy report run; linen counted against par.

*Uncertain:* credits per shift and linen pars are industry rules of thumb, not
standards — calibrate to your property.
