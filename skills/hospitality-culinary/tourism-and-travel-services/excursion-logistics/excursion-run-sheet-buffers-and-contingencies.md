# Excursion run sheet, buffers and contingencies

**Goal:** an excursion that runs on time because every booking is confirmed, every
transfer has slack, and every failure point already has a Plan B with a phone number.

## 1. The run sheet (one page per day)
A table, time-ordered, that anyone on the team could pick up and run:

| Time | Activity | Location (address/pin) | Supplier | Contact | Ref no. | Pax | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Include: pick-up/drop-off points with exact pins, coach registration and driver mobile,
ticket booking references and entry slots, meal times and dietary counts, toilet
stops, emergency numbers (local emergency, operator duty phone, nearest hospital),
and the group's medical/mobility notes. Print it *and* keep it offline on a phone.

## 2. Timing buffers
- **Transfers:** mapping-app time + 20–30 % in cities, more at rush hour. Add explicit time
  for loading (~10 min for a full coach plus luggage on transfer days).
- **Group movement:** a group moves at the pace of its slowest member. Walking 500 m takes a
  group of 30 well over twice as long as one person — budget ~2–3 min per 100 m plus
  regrouping.
- **Toilets:** a 40-person group needs **15–20 min** at a stop with few facilities.
- **Before timed entries:** arrive 20–30 min before your slot (security queues, ticket
  distribution). Late arrival often means the slot is forfeited.
- Shore excursions: plan to return to port **≥ 60 min before all-aboard**; ships don't wait
  for independent tours.

## 3. Tickets, permits and bookings
- Book timed-entry sites as soon as the window opens; many popular sites sell group slots
  out months ahead.
- Know each ticket's rules: named tickets (passport names must match), ID checks, child
  ages, change/refund deadlines.
- Permits: coach parking/drop-off permits in historic centres, filming or guided-group
  permits, national-park or protected-area permits, licensed-guide requirements.
  *Uncertain: highly local — check with the site or the DMC/ground handler.*
- **Reconfirm** every supplier **72 h and 24 h before**: time, pax count, pick-up point,
  dietary list, payment terms. Get the reconfirmation in writing (email/message).

## 4. Contingency matrix
For each line of the run sheet, ask "what if this fails?" and write the answer down.

| Failure | Plan B |
|---|---|
| Coach no-show / breakdown | Operator's backup number; local coach company list; taxis/rideshare for small groups |
| Site closed (strike, weather, event) | Pre-scouted alternative nearby; know the refund route |
| Rain / heat | Indoor alternative; water, shade breaks; ponchos |
| Running late | Which item gets cut first (agree in advance); call the next supplier early |
| Guest ill/injured | Nearest hospital/clinic, insurer assistance number, who stays with the guest |
| Lost guest | Meeting point + stated wait time, guest cards with your mobile and the hotel address |

## 5. On the day
- Start-of-day call with the driver: route, parking, timings.
- Call ahead whenever you're >10 min behind — restaurants and venues can usually adjust if
  warned.
- Keep a simple log: actual times, issues, supplier performance. It feeds the next costing
  and the supplier review.

## Gotchas
- One booking under the wrong date or pax count wrecks a day — reconfirm against the sheet, not memory.
- Public holidays change opening hours and traffic.
- Cash for tolls, tips and small-site fees in local currency.

## Verify
- [ ] Run sheet complete with contacts, refs and pins; available offline.
- [ ] Buffers added for transfers, group walking, toilets and timed entries.
- [ ] All suppliers reconfirmed at 72 h and 24 h, in writing.
- [ ] Contingency matrix covers transport, site closure, weather, lateness, illness, lost guest.
- [ ] Post-day log captured.
