# Running a pot still and making heads, hearts and tails cuts

**Goal:** collect the hearts — the clean, characterful middle of the run — at
consistent cut points, keep the rest for recycling, and do it without fire, a
pressure event or a scorched charge.

**Licensed operations only** (see mashing-grain-and-fermenting-a-distillers-wash).
Ethanol vapour is flammable; stills are pressure and fire hazards.

## 1. Two-run pot distillation (whisky/rum/brandy pattern)
1. **Stripping run:** the wash (~6–10% ABV) is distilled fast to collect
   **low wines** (often ~25–35% ABV), roughly a third of the charge volume. No cuts,
   or only foreshots discarded. Stop when the distillate falls to around 1–5% ABV
   (or when the still head temperature approaches ~100 °C).
2. **Spirit run:** low wines (plus any feints from previous runs) are redistilled
   slowly with cuts taken. Hybrid/plate stills can reach finished strength in one
   run — the cut logic is the same.

## 2. Heat and reflux control
- Charge to the manufacturer's maximum fill (leave headspace; foaming washes need more).
- Heat up steadily; **slow down as the first vapour approaches the condenser** so
  foreshots don't rush out.
- Run the spirit run gently — a steady stream rather than a gush. Faster runs smear
  heads into hearts.
- **Reflux** (dephlegmator water, plates, a tall neck, slower run) increases
  separation and lightness; less reflux keeps heavier, oilier character.
- Keep condenser water flowing before heating, and never run with a closed
  outlet. Know your still's pressure relief and never block it.

## 3. The fractions (in order)
| Fraction | Character | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Foreshots** | Solvent, nail-polish (acetone, ethyl acetate, some methanol) | Discard. Commonly a small fixed volume per charge |
| **Heads** | Fruity → harsh solvent, hot, glue-like | Collect separately as feints |
| **Hearts** | Clean, sweet, the spirit's character | Keep |
| **Tails** | Wet cardboard, wet dog, oily, cereal/porridge, falling ABV | Collect separately as feints |
Methanol is distributed across the run rather than all in foreshots; with grain and
molasses washes levels are low, with fruit washes higher — **hearts selection, not
just foreshots discard, is what keeps the spirit within legal methanol limits.**

## 4. Making cuts by nose and numbers
- Collect into small numbered jars (e.g. every 0.5–1 L at craft scale) through the
  spirit safe or parrot, recording ABV and temperature for each.
- Nose each jar diluted to ~20–30% ABV in a tulip glass (full strength anaesthetises
  the nose). Taste only within your house safety rules.
- **Heads→hearts:** when the solvent edge disappears and the spirit smells clean and
  sweet. **Hearts→tails:** when you first notice wet-cardboard/oily notes — and
  move earlier for a lighter spirit.
- Cross-check against ABV: typical hearts in a pot still might run from ~75–80%
  down to ~60–65% ABV — **depends strongly on still and spirit; use your own run
  records to build cut tables.**
- After a few runs, blend jars back next morning — aired jars smell different.

## 5. Feints and recycling
Heads and tails go into the next spirit run's charge (feints). Cut points shift
when the charge composition changes; monitor feints strength and volume to keep
runs consistent.

## 6. Records
Per run: charge volume and ABV, heat settings, time and ABV at each cut, volumes
of each fraction, hearts ABV and volume, sensory notes. Your cut table comes from
these records.

## Gotchas
- Running hot to save time — heads smear into hearts.
- Judging cuts at full strength — the nose saturates.
- Open flame/sparks near spirit receivers.
- Changing cut points without logging, so batches drift.

## Verify
- [ ] Licence in place; condenser flowing, relief valve clear, ignition sources away.
- [ ] Stripping run to low wines; spirit run at a gentle steady rate.
- [ ] Foreshots discarded; heads and tails collected separately as feints.
- [ ] Cuts made by diluted nose and cross-checked against ABV records.
- [ ] Hearts ABV and volume recorded; methanol within legal limits if tested.

*Uncertain:* volumes and ABV cut ranges are indicative; every still differs.
