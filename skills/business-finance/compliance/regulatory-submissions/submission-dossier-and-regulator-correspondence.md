# Submission dossier and regulator correspondence

**Goal:** get an application, notification or periodic filing to the regulator complete,
accurate and on time, with every statement traceable to evidence and signed off by the
right person — and handle the follow-up questions without creating new problems.

Applies across regimes (financial-services authorisation, change-in-control notices,
licence renewals, product or quality-system filings). *Each regulator prescribes its own
forms, portals and timelines; read the current guidance and form instructions before
starting — they change.*

## 1. Build a requirements matrix first
From the regulator's rules, guidance and form instructions, list every required item: one
row per question, document or data point. Columns: requirement and citation, owner, source
of evidence, status, reviewer, notes. Add items the regulator commonly asks for even if
not on the form (published guidance, feedback statements, prior questions on similar filings,
peer experience). This matrix is your completeness check and your project plan.

## 2. Gap analysis and plan
For each requirement, can the firm already evidence it? Gaps (no policy, no board minute,
a missing system capability) must be fixed, not described around. Work back from the filing
date — or the date the business needs the approval — allowing for the regulator's
assessment period, which may only start once the application is deemed complete.

## 3. Assemble the dossier
- **One document-controlled repository** with naming conventions and version numbers;
  no "final_v3_REALLY_FINAL".
- Write narratives in plain, specific language; answer the question asked; cross-reference
  supporting documents by exact name and page.
- **Consistency check** across documents: numbers (capital, headcount, forecasts), names of
  key individuals and their roles, group structure charts, dates. Regulators notice
  inconsistencies and treat them as a quality signal about the firm.
- Financial projections tie to the business plan; assumptions stated.
- Individuals' fitness/propriety forms: complete disclosure — omissions found later are far
  more damaging than the disclosed matter.

## 4. Review and sign-off
Technical review by the owner, independent quality review (compliance or a second reviewer),
legal review where statements carry liability, then **formal sign-off** by the accountable
senior person or board as the rules require. Keep the sign-off record. Many regimes make
knowingly or recklessly misleading submissions an offence.

## 5. File and log
Submit through the official channel; keep the submission receipt, the exact submitted
version (PDF/ZIP hash) and the date. Record the regulator's reference number and the
statutory clock if one applies.

## 6. Regulator correspondence
- Log every incoming question or request with deadline and owner.
- Answer precisely and only what's asked; check the answer is consistent with the dossier
  — or explicitly correct the dossier if it was wrong.
- Route all substantive communication through one coordinator; avoid informal calls that
  produce untracked commitments. File a note of every call or meeting.
- Ask for an extension *before* a deadline if needed, with a reason.
- Commitments made to the regulator (e.g. "we will hire a CRO before launch") go into an
  action tracker with owners — they become conditions you'll be checked against.

## 7. Recurring filings
Maintain a **regulatory calendar** of periodic returns and notifications with owners,
preparers, reviewers and internal deadlines several days before the regulator's. Reconcile
returns to the ledger or source system; keep the reconciliation as evidence.

## Gotchas
- Filing on time but incomplete: the clock may not start and credibility suffers.
- Copy-pasting from another firm's or an old application: wrong names, stale facts.
- Business-plan numbers changing late without updating every place they appear.
- Undisclosed material changes between filing and decision — most regimes require prompt
  notification.

## Verify
- [ ] Requirements matrix covers every form item and guidance expectation, each with an owner.
- [ ] Gaps fixed, not narrated; dossier under version control.
- [ ] Cross-document consistency check completed and signed off.
- [ ] Exact submitted version and receipt stored; reference and clock recorded.
- [ ] Correspondence and commitments logged with owners and deadlines.
