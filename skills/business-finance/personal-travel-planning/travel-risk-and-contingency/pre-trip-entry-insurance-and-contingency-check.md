# Pre-trip entry, insurance and contingency check

**Goal:** arrive without being turned away at a border, have cover for the expensive
failures, and know in advance what you'll do if a passport, wallet, flight or health
goes wrong. Entry rules change — always use the **destination government's official
site** and your own government's travel advisory as the source of truth.

## 1. Entry requirements (check 2–3 months out, re-check 1 week out)
For each country you'll **enter or transit**:
- **Passport validity:** many countries require validity beyond your stay (commonly 3
  or 6 months) and one or two blank pages. Airlines enforce this at check-in.
- **Visa or electronic authorisation:** visa-free countries increasingly need an online
  pre-authorisation (US ESTA, Canada eTA, UK ETA, Australia ETA, the EU's ETIAS — *check
  whether in operation for your dates*). Use only the official government site;
  look-alike agency sites charge large markups.
- **Transit:** some airports require a transit visa even airside — check the transit
  country, not just the destination.
- **Other conditions:** onward/return ticket, proof of funds, accommodation address,
  minors travelling with one parent (consent letter), customs limits on cash and
  medicines (some common medicines are controlled in certain countries — carry
  prescriptions in original packaging).
- Schengen-style **days-in-period limits** (90 in any 180) count across all member states.

## 2. Safety and health research
- Read your government's **advisory level** for the country and the specific regions
  (e.g. Smartraveller, UK FCDO, US State Department). Advisories also affect whether
  insurance pays.
- Note common local scams, transport safety, natural-hazard seasons (typhoon, monsoon,
  wildfire), and the local emergency number.
- **Health:** see a travel clinic 6–8 weeks ahead for vaccinations and malaria advice;
  pack a basic kit and enough regular medication plus a buffer.
- Register travel with your government's service where offered.

## 3. Choosing travel insurance
Buy soon after paying the first non-refundable cost — cancellation cover typically
starts from purchase. Compare on what matters:
1. **Medical and evacuation** limits — the catastrophic risk. Repatriation from remote
   areas can cost very large sums; prefer high or unlimited medical limits.
2. **Pre-existing conditions:** declare everything; undeclared conditions are a top
   reason claims are denied.
3. **Activities:** skiing, diving depth, motorbikes (often need a licence valid in the
   country and a helmet), trekking altitude — check exclusions.
4. **Cancellation/curtailment** reasons covered, and the amount vs your prepaid costs.
5. **Excess**, baggage sub-limits per item, and gadget cover.
6. Exclusions for travel against an advisory, alcohol, and "known events" (a storm
   already named when you bought).
Card-included insurance may exist but often has conditions (trip paid on the card,
activation, lower limits) — read the policy wording, not the marketing page.

## 4. Contingency plan (one page, shared)
- **Document backups:** photos of passport, visas, insurance policy and emergency
  number, cards' lost-card numbers — stored offline on phone and in a cloud account,
  plus a paper copy in separate luggage.
- **Money redundancy:** two cards on different networks, kept apart, plus emergency cash.
- **Lost passport:** nearest embassy/consulate address and hours; police report first;
  spare passport photos help.
- **Flight disruption:** airline app and phone number; know your rights for the
  region (see booking-logistics).
- **Medical:** insurer's 24/7 assistance number — call **before** non-emergency
  treatment where the policy requires pre-approval.
- **Contact plan:** itinerary with someone at home; check-in schedule; meeting point if
  a group gets separated.

## Verify
- [ ] Passport validity and blank pages meet the strictest country on the route.
- [ ] Every required visa/ETA approved, on official sites, for every entry and transit.
- [ ] Advisory read for each region visited; insurance doesn't exclude it.
- [ ] Insurance bought with all conditions declared and activities covered.
- [ ] Contingency page and document copies exist offline and with a contact at home.
