# Flight and accommodation booking

**Goal:** lock in the trip's components at a fair price, in an order that limits
losses if plans change, with connections and cancellation terms you actually
understand.

## 1. Booking order
1. **Scarce, date-critical items first:** timed-entry attractions, events, peak-season
   rooms in small towns.
2. **Flights** (usually the biggest fixed cost and least flexible).
3. **Accommodation** — on free-cancellation rates first, so you can firm up later.
4. **Ground transport** — some rail and coach fares are cheapest when sales open
   (often 1–6 months ahead *depending on operator*); city transport can wait.

## 2. Flights
- **Search on aggregators** (Google Flights, Skyscanner, Kayak and similar) with flexible
  dates and nearby airports; set price alerts. Then usually **book direct with the
  airline** — changes and refunds are far easier than through an online travel agent.
- **Compare like for like:** add checked bags, seat selection and carry-on rules.
  "Basic economy" or "light" fares may exclude cabin bags, changes and seat choice —
  cheap until it isn't.
- **Timing:** there's no magic day of the week; prices move with demand. For
  peak dates, earlier is generally safer; watch alerts and book when a fare is
  clearly below the recent range.
- **Connections:** keep everything on **one ticket** where possible — the airline then
  owes you a rebooking if the first leg is late. Self-transfers on separate tickets
  leave you on your own: allow several hours, and don't do it on the last flight of the day.
  On one ticket, the airport's minimum connection time is legal, but tight; add margin
  for terminal changes, immigration and checked bags on international-to-domestic.
- **Names exactly as on the passport.** Check passport validity rules at booking (see
  travel-risk-and-contingency).
- **Cooling-off:** US DOT rules give a 24-hour free cancellation (or hold) on many
  bookings made 7+ days out for flights to/from the US; *elsewhere, don't assume it*.

## 3. Accommodation
- **Location first, then price:** compute "cheap room + 40 minutes each way" honestly.
- Read the **cancellation policy tier**: free cancellation until a date/time (note the
  property's time zone), partially refundable, non-refundable. Book refundable early,
  then rebook if prices drop.
- **Recent reviews** for noise, cleanliness, air conditioning, Wi-Fi, lift access;
  photos from guests, not marketing.
- Holiday rentals: check cleaning and service fees in the **total**, check-in method,
  house rules, and keep communication and payment on-platform (off-platform payment
  requests are a common scam).
- Booking direct sometimes yields a better rate or perks; ask after finding the
  aggregator price.

## 4. Managing changes and cancellations
- Keep a **booking log**: item, confirmation number, cost, cancellation deadline,
  payment card. Put each free-cancellation deadline in your calendar.
- **Airline schedule change** of more than a few hours, or a cancellation, often entitles
  you to a free change or refund (and in the EU/UK, possibly compensation under EU261 /
  UK261 depending on cause and notice — *check current rules*). Accept a voucher only if
  you actually want one; ask for the refund to the original payment method.
- Re-check flight times ~a week before and day before departure.
- Disruption on the day: rebook via the airline app while queuing for the desk, and
  call simultaneously.

## Gotchas
- Some fare rules cancel your return if you miss the outbound leg (no-show rule).
- "Hidden-city" ticketing violates most carriers' terms; don't.
- Prices shown in a different currency may add FX fees — pay in the currency of your card if possible, or use a no-FX-fee card.
- Car hire: the excess (deductible) can be thousands; compare the counter's excess
  waiver with standalone excess insurance or card cover.

## Verify
- [ ] Flight fares compared including bags and seats; booked with the airline where practical.
- [ ] Every connection on one ticket, or self-transfers have generous gaps.
- [ ] Each booking logged with confirmation number and cancellation deadline.
- [ ] Passport names match tickets exactly.
- [ ] Re-confirmation reminders set before departure.
