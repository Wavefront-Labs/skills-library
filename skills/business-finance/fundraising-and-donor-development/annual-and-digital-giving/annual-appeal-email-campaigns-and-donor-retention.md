# Annual appeal, email campaigns and donor retention

**Goal:** raise reliable unrestricted income from many donors, and keep them giving
next year. Retention is the hidden lever: US sector data (Fundraising Effectiveness
Project) has for years shown overall donor retention around or below ~45% and
first-time donor retention around ~20%. Exact figures move each year; check the
latest report.

## 1. Segment before you write
Minimum segments: current donors (gave in last 12 months), **LYBUNT** (gave Last Year
But Unfortunately Not This year), **SYBUNT** (Some Year But not this year), first-time
donors, monthly donors (usually exclude from the cash appeal or give a different ask),
major-gift prospects (personal approach instead), non-donor contacts.
Ask amounts by segment: base the ask on each donor's **highest previous gift** (or most
recent), e.g. a ladder of last gift, ~1.25×, ~1.5×. Don't ask a $500 donor for $25.

## 2. The appeal letter / email
- One story about one person or place (consented), one clear problem, one specific
  thing the gift does ("$40 provides a week of meals"), one call to action, a deadline.
- Write to "you", not "we": the donor is the hero. Short paragraphs, bold or underline
  the ask, P.S. restating it (the P.S. is often read first in print).
- Email: subject line test (A/B on a slice of the list), one button above the fold,
  mobile-first. Year-end series: e.g. 3-5 emails across late November to 31 December,
  with the heaviest volume in the final days (a large share of online giving arrives in
  the last week of December in the US; timing differs elsewhere, e.g. UK tax year).
- Multichannel lifts results: mail + email + social + a phone call to top donors.

## 3. Donation page that converts
- Branded, matching the appeal's story; minimal fields; pre-set amounts matching the ask
  ladder; **monthly option visible** (often defaulted or highlighted); wallet payments
  (Apple Pay/Google Pay/PayPal); option to cover fees.
- UK: Gift Aid declaration checkbox (adds 25% on eligible gifts from UK taxpayers;
  follow HMRC wording rules). Other jurisdictions: tax-receipt info.
- Test the full flow on a phone, including the receipt email.

## 4. Thank fast, then report
- Automated receipt immediately; personal thank-you (letter, email, or call from a
  board member for first-time donors) within **48 hours** is a widely used standard.
- Within 90 days, without an ask: show what the gift did. The first impact
  communication without an ask is a common retention driver for first-time donors.

## 5. Monthly giving
Monthly donors typically retain far better and have higher lifetime value. Ask
one-off donors to convert after their second gift or in a dedicated campaign;
handle failed card payments with an automated retry and a friendly update-your-card email.

## 6. Measure every campaign
Track by segment and channel: response rate, average gift, total, cost per dollar
raised, new vs renewed donors. Annually: retention rate (by first-time vs repeat), LYBUNT
recapture, donor lifetime value. Keep a control group in tests to know what actually lifted.

## Gotchas
- Data hygiene: duplicates and deceased records in mailings damage trust; run
  deduplication and suppression (and national change-of-address/mortality screens where available).
- Consent rules: email marketing needs a lawful basis (e.g. UK/EU PECR/GDPR; US CAN-SPAM
  minimums). Honour unsubscribes everywhere, not just in the email tool.
- Over-mailing without thanks or impact drives lapse.

## Verify
- [ ] Segments defined; asks based on each donor's giving history.
- [ ] Appeal has one story, one specific ask, one deadline.
- [ ] Donation page tested on mobile end-to-end, monthly option visible.
- [ ] Thank-yous go out within 48 hours.
- [ ] Retention and LYBUNT recapture reported after each cycle.
