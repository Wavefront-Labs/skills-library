# Feasibility study, gift table and campaign phases

**Goal:** raise a large, time-bound sum (building, endowment, major programme) without
launching a public goal you can't hit. Campaigns usually run 2-5 years and depend on a
small number of very large gifts.

## 1. Readiness and the case
Before anything: a board that will give and ask, a clear project with costed budget,
staff capacity (a campaign roughly doubles major-gift workload), a donor database in
order, and a strong annual fund (campaigns must not cannibalise it; plan to keep it running).
Draft a **preliminary case for support**: the need, the project, the cost, the impact,
why now, why this organisation. Short (often 2-6 pages) plus a summary sheet.

## 2. Feasibility (planning) study
Usually run by an external consultant over ~2-4 months so interviewees speak candidly.
- Confidential interviews with ~30-60 top prospects, board members and community leaders.
- Test: reactions to the case, perceived organisation strength, the goal, potential
  leadership volunteers, and **each person's likely gift range**.
- Output: recommended goal (often lower than the board hoped), timeline, case changes,
  identified lead donors and chairs, readiness gaps.
The interview is also cultivation: prospects who are consulted tend to feel ownership.
Uncertain: consultant costs and whether a full study is needed vary with campaign size;
small campaigns sometimes run an internal "planning study" instead.

## 3. Gift range chart (gift table)
Build from the goal downward. A common shape (rule of thumb; adjust to your donor data):
- **Lead gift ≈ 10-20% of goal**.
- **Top ~10 gifts ≈ 50% or more**; top ~100 gifts often ≈ 80-90% of the total.
- Each gift level needs **~3-5 prospects per gift** you need at that level.
Example for $5M: 1 × $1M, 2 × $500k, 3 × $250k, 6 × $100k, 10 × $50k, 20 × $25k (≈ $4.35M),
then many smaller gifts. Map named prospects against each row; empty top rows mean the
goal is not yet feasible.

## 4. Phases
1. **Quiet / leadership phase**: solicit board (aim for 100% participation, at
   personally significant levels), then lead and major gifts, privately. Commonly
   **50-70%** of the goal is pledged before going public.
2. **Public phase**: launch event announces the goal with much of it secured; broader
   asks, community gifts, matching challenges, naming opportunities.
3. **Close and stewardship**: celebrate, report results, recognise donors, collect pledges.
Ask **top-down and inside-out**: largest prospects first, closest constituencies first,
because big early gifts set the scale for everyone else.

## 5. Volunteer leadership
Campaign chair(s) with credibility and capacity to give near the top of the chart, plus
a **campaign cabinet** of 6-15 volunteers who each give first, then make peer asks
alongside staff. Staff supply research, briefings, materials and follow-up; volunteers
open doors.

## 6. Pledges and money management
- Multi-year pledges (typically up to 3-5 years) documented in signed pledge forms
  stating amount, schedule, purpose, recognition, and what happens if the project changes.
- Track in the donor CRM: pledge balance, instalment schedule, reminders, write-offs.
- Cash flow: construction draws often precede pledge payments; finance may need bridge
  financing. Agree accounting treatment of pledges with your auditor (pledges are
  generally recognised as receivables under nonprofit accounting standards; check yours).
- Naming rights: written policy (minimum levels, duration, removal clause if the donor's
  reputation changes).

## Gotchas
- Announcing a goal before the quiet phase secures most of it.
- Underestimating the lead gift: without it, the chart collapses.
- Board members who won't give make every other ask harder.
- Cost overruns: decide early whether the campaign goal includes contingency.

## Verify
- [ ] Feasibility study done (or internal planning study) with a recommended goal.
- [ ] Gift chart built with named prospects at 3-5 per gift in the top rows.
- [ ] Board giving at 100% before the public phase.
- [ ] 50%+ pledged before the public launch.
- [ ] Signed pledge forms and instalment reminders in the CRM.
