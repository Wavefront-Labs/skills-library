# Proposal budget and institutional routing — the pre-award critical path

**Goal:** the investigator writes the science; you make sure the application is eligible,
the budget is allowable and correctly calculated, the institution has approved it, and it
reaches the sponsor before the deadline without validation errors.

Examples use US federal practice (Uniform Guidance, 2 CFR 200; NIH, NSF). Other funders
(UKRI, Horizon Europe, foundations) differ in cost models and rules — **always the specific
call's guidance first.**

## 1. Parse the funding opportunity (day 1)
Build a one-page checklist from the notice: eligibility (institution, PI, career stage),
limits on submissions per institution (internal competition needed?), budget cap and
whether it's direct or total costs, project period, required/forbidden sections and page
limits, cost-sharing requirements, letters and biosketch formats, special rules on F&A
(some foundations cap indirects at a low rate), and the submission system and deadline
time zone.

## 2. Build the budget
- **Personnel:** effort (person-months or %) × institutional base salary, with annual
  escalation per policy. Apply sponsor **salary caps** (NIH applies a statutory salary limitation that changes yearly; *check the current figure*).
  Add fringe benefits at the institution's negotiated rates for each employee category.
- **Equipment:** items above the capitalisation threshold (under 2 CFR 200.1, federal equipment is a unit cost at or above the lesser of the institution's capitalisation level or $10,000).
- Travel, materials and supplies, publication costs, consultants, participant support,
  other direct costs (core facility recharges, animal per diems).
- **Subawards:** collaborating institutions submit their own budget, justification, statement
  of work and letter of intent signed by their authorised official.
- **Modular budgets:** NIH modular budgets cover requests up to $250,000 in direct costs per year (excluding consortium F&A), in $25,000 modules; SBIR/STTR and foreign applications cannot use them. Check the funding notice.

## 3. Apply F&A (indirect costs) correctly
Use the institution's federally negotiated rate (on-campus/off-campus, research/instruction)
applied to the correct base — usually **MTDC** (modified total direct costs). MTDC excludes,
among others, equipment, capital expenditures, patient care, rental costs (full list in 2 CFR 200.1),
and each subaward's amount above the first $50,000 (2 CFR 200.1; *confirm which version of the Uniform Guidance applies to the award*). Getting the base wrong is
the most common budget error.

## 4. Budget justification
Explain each line in the sponsor's order: who, what role, effort, why that amount; equipment
quotes; travel purpose, trips and costs. Make it match the budget to the dollar and the
narrative to the person.

## 5. Internal routing and sign-off
Route through the institution's proposal system with: budget, justification, abstract,
compliance questions (human subjects, animals, biosafety, export control, conflict of
interest disclosures), cost-share commitments and departmental approvals. The **authorised
organisational representative (AOR)** signs on the institution's behalf. Most offices require
final budgets **several business days** before the deadline (e.g. 5) — enforce it; late
routing is where errors happen.

## 6. Submit early
Submit through the sponsor system (e.g. Grants.gov with eRA Commons for NIH; Research.gov
for NSF) at least a day or two early, then **check the assembled application image** and
validation messages. Errors flagged by the system can usually be corrected only before the
deadline.

## Gotchas
- Budget cap is "direct costs" but the team built it to total costs (or vice versa).
- Fringe or F&A rates from last year after a new rate agreement.
- Missing subaward documents arriving the day before deadline.
- Effort commitments across a PI's awards exceeding 100% if funded.
- Cost-share promised voluntarily in the narrative becomes binding if awarded — avoid unless required.
- Biosketch/other-support formats change; use the current templates.

## Verify
- [ ] Opportunity checklist complete; eligibility and limits confirmed.
- [ ] Budget lines reconcile with the justification; caps and current rates applied.
- [ ] F&A base computed with correct exclusions and subaward treatment.
- [ ] Internal routing approved with compliance questions answered; AOR sign-off obtained.
- [ ] Submitted early; assembled application viewed and validation errors cleared.
