# Tenant screening, inspections and repair coordination

**Goal:** good tenants in, homes kept safe and in repair, owners informed, and a paper
trail that holds up if anything goes to a deposit dispute, tribunal or court.
Landlord-tenant law is **highly jurisdiction-specific** (country, state/province, even
city). Everything legal below is a pointer to check locally, not advice.

## 1. Screening: written criteria, applied to everyone
- Publish criteria before advertising: income (set a documented affordability test; check local rules on what you may require),
  credit/referencing, landlord references, ID, and occupancy limits.
- Apply them **identically to every applicant** and record the decision basis.
  Discrimination laws (e.g. US Fair Housing Act protected classes (race, color, national origin, religion, sex, familial status, disability) plus state additions
  such as source of income; UK Equality Act) apply. Many places now restrict criminal
  history use, application fees, or blanket "no benefits" policies.
- Verify, don't just collect: call the previous landlord (find the number independently,
  not from the application), verify employment or use open-banking income checks.
- UK (England): **Right to Rent** immigration checks are a landlord/agent legal duty (done before the tenancy starts for tenants aged 18 and over; GOV.UK lists exemptions).
- Fraud signs: pressure to skip viewing, payslips with mismatched maths, refusal to
  verify income, offers to pay months upfront in cash.

## 2. Move-in: the inventory protects everyone
- Signed tenancy agreement; required notices and documents given and proof kept (e.g. in England: the documents listed in current GOV.UK landlord guidance, and deposit protection in a scheme within 30 days of receipt; assured periodic tenancies replaced assured shorthold tenancies on 1 May 2026; in the US, federal law requires lead-based paint disclosure and the EPA pamphlet for pre-1978 housing).
- **Inventory / check-in report**: room by room, condition and cleanliness, meter
  readings, keys issued, with **timestamped photos/video**. Tenant signs or comments
  within a set period. Without it, deposit deductions are very hard to justify.
- Deposits held per local rules (protection schemes, separate accounts, interest, caps).

## 3. Periodic inspections
- Frequency and notice follow the lease and local notice rules (give written notice). Respect quiet enjoyment; don't over-inspect.
- Check: smoke and carbon-monoxide alarms work, signs of leaks/damp/mould, unreported
  damage, occupancy matches the lease, safety hazards, garden/exterior.
- Report to the owner with photos and recommended actions; agree a timetable.

## 4. Repair coordination
- One intake channel (portal/app or email) so every request is logged with date and photos.
- **Triage**:
  - Emergency (same day / within hours): gas leak, no heat in winter, flooding, electrical
    danger, security breach, no water. Tell tenants how to reach you out of hours.
  - Urgent (24-72 h): no hot water, broken appliance the landlord supplies, toilet out
    (if only one).
  - Routine (within ~2 weeks or as agreed).
- Owner approval limit in the management agreement (e.g. agent may spend up to a set
  amount without asking; emergencies always). Get quotes above that.
- Use vetted contractors with insurance and required licences (e.g. Gas Safe in the UK,
  licensed electricians). Confirm completion with the tenant and photos before paying.
- Statutory repair duties and timelines vary; habitability/fitness rules mean some
  repairs aren't optional, and tenants may have rent-withholding or repair-and-deduct
  rights in some jurisdictions.

## 5. Rent and arrears (outline)
Automated reminders, contact on day one of arrears, payment plans in writing, and
follow the legal notice process exactly; defective notices are a leading cause of
failed possession claims. See rent-and-service-charge-administration.

## Gotchas
- Inconsistent screening is both unfair and a legal exposure.
- No check-in photos = deposit disputes usually lost.
- Entering without proper notice can breach the lease or law even for repairs.
- Annual safety certificates (gas, electrical where required) lapsing silently: diarise them.

## Verify
- [ ] Written screening criteria applied to every applicant, decisions logged.
- [ ] Required pre-tenancy documents served, with proof.
- [ ] Dated, photographed inventory signed or acknowledged by the tenant.
- [ ] Every repair request logged, triaged and closed with evidence.
- [ ] Compliance calendar holds all certificate renewals.
