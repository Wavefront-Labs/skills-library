# Performing wudu and salat — the five daily prayers

**Goal:** pray the obligatory salat correctly and on time: purified, facing the qibla,
with the right number of rak'ahs in the right sequence.

> Draft. Wording and minor details below follow the common Sunni pattern; the four
> Sunni schools (Hanafi, Maliki, Shafi'i, Hanbali) and Shia (Ja'fari) practice differ on
> points such as hand position, wiping the feet vs. washing, and combining prayers.
> Follow the practice of your own school or mosque where it differs.

## 1. Before you pray
- **Clean body, clothes and place.** A prayer mat is customary, not required.
- **Awrah covered:** for men at least navel to knee (shoulders covered is recommended);
  for women the whole body except face and hands (some schools also exempt the feet).
- **Time:** each prayer has a window. Use a local mosque timetable or an app
  (e.g. Muslim Pro, Athan, Pillars) and set the calculation method your community uses —
  the Fajr/Isha angles (e.g. MWL 18°, ISNA 15°) and the Asr method (Shafi'i/standard vs.
  Hanafi) shift times by up to ~45 minutes.
- **Qibla:** face the Kaaba in Mecca. Phone compass apps work; calibrate away from metal
  and cross-check with the mosque or a map line, as magnetic interference is common indoors.

## 2. Wudu (ablution), in order
1. Intention (niyyah) in the heart; say *Bismillah*.
2. Wash hands to the wrists ×3.
3. Rinse mouth ×3, then water into the nose and blow out ×3.
4. Wash the face, hairline to chin, ear to ear ×3.
5. Wash right arm to and including the elbow ×3, then left.
6. Wipe the head once with wet hands; wipe the ears.
7. Wash right foot to and including the ankle ×3, then left (Shia practice wipes the feet).

Wudu is broken by using the toilet, passing wind, deep sleep, or losing consciousness.
Major impurity (after intercourse, menstruation ending, post-natal bleeding ending) needs
**ghusl**, a full ritual bath. With no water or when water would harm you, **tayammum**
(dry ablution with clean earth/dust) substitutes.

## 3. Rak'ah counts (obligatory/fard)
| Prayer  | Time window (approx.)            | Fard rak'ahs | Recited aloud? |
|---------|----------------------------------|--------------|----------------|
| Fajr    | dawn to sunrise                  | 2            | yes            |
| Dhuhr   | after midday to Asr              | 4            | silent         |
| Asr     | afternoon to sunset              | 4            | silent         |
| Maghrib | just after sunset                | 3            | first two aloud |
| Isha    | nightfall to before dawn         | 4            | first two aloud |

Sunnah prayers (e.g. 2 before Fajr, Witr after Isha) are recommended additions.

## 4. One rak'ah, step by step
1. **Standing (qiyam):** intention; raise hands, say *Allahu akbar* (takbirat al-ihram).
2. Recite **al-Fatiha**, then (in the first two rak'ahs) another short surah.
3. **Ruku:** bow, back flat, hands on knees: *Subhana Rabbiyal-Azim* ×3.
4. Rise: *Sami'Allahu liman hamidah*, then *Rabbana wa lakal-hamd*.
5. **Sujud:** prostrate — forehead and nose, both palms, knees and toes touching the
   ground: *Subhana Rabbiyal-A'la* ×3. Sit briefly, then a second sujud.
After the 2nd rak'ah sit for the **tashahhud**; in the final sitting add the salawat on the
Prophet, then end with **taslim**: turn right, then left, *As-salamu alaykum wa rahmatullah*.

## 5. Jumu'ah
On Friday, Dhuhr is replaced (for men obliged to attend) by the congregational **jumu'ah**:
two sermons (khutbah) then 2 rak'ahs led by the imam. Arrive early, do ghusl, and stay
silent during the khutbah.

## Gotchas
- Praying after the window closes makes it a make-up (qada) prayer — pray it as soon as
  you remember rather than skipping it.
- Forgetting a rak'ah or a step: the **sujud al-sahw** (two prostrations of forgetfulness)
  repairs minor errors; missing a pillar (e.g. ruku) needs that rak'ah repeated.
- In congregation, follow the imam — never move ahead of him.
- Travellers may shorten 4-rak'ah prayers to 2 (qasr); rules on distance vary by school.
- Avoid praying exactly at sunrise, zenith and sunset (disliked times for voluntary prayer).

## Verify
- [ ] Wudu done in order and not broken since.
- [ ] Prayer started inside its time window, using your community's calculation method.
- [ ] Facing qibla, checked against a second source.
- [ ] Correct fard rak'ah count; tashahhud after the 2nd and final rak'ahs.
- [ ] Ended with taslim to both sides.
