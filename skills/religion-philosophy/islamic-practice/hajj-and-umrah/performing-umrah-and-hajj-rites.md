# Performing umrah and hajj

**Goal:** complete the pilgrimage rites validly and in order, as worship, without
being derailed by crowds, heat or paperwork.

> Draft. Sequence follows hajj *tamattu'* (umrah first, then hajj), the most common
> for pilgrims from abroad. Ifrad and qiran differ. Pillars vs. obligations vs. sunnah
> differ by school; travel with a group scholar and ask before improvising. Visa and
> permit rules (Saudi Nusuk platform, national quotas) change yearly — check the
> current official requirements.

## 1. Before you go
- **Hajj** is obligatory once for an adult Muslim who is able — physically, financially,
  and with a safe route. **Umrah** can be done any time of year.
- Hajj requires an official permit via your country's quota or licensed operator; going
  without one is illegal and risky. Women's travel rules and the meningitis (ACWY) vaccine
  requirement are set by Saudi authorities — check current policy.
- Learn the rites on paper and rehearse the duas before departure; you will be tired and
  crowded when you need them.

## 2. Ihram at the miqat
1. Ghusl, trim nails, (men) unscented body; then don ihram clothing: men two
   **unstitched white sheets** (izar + rida) and sandals exposing the ankle bone; women
   ordinary modest clothes, face and hands uncovered.
2. At or before the **miqat** boundary (on a plane, the crew announces it — change before
   boarding), make the intention and begin the **talbiyah**:
   *Labbayk Allahumma labbayk...*
3. Ihram restrictions: no perfume, cutting hair/nails, hunting, marital relations,
   (men) stitched clothes or head covering. Breaking one usually requires a penalty (fidyah).

## 3. Umrah
1. **Tawaf:** seven anticlockwise circuits of the Kaaba, starting and ending in line with
   the Black Stone (there is a marker; raise your right hand toward it each pass — do
   not fight to touch it). Men uncover the right shoulder (idtiba') and walk briskly in
   the first three circuits (raml). Wudu is required.
2. Two rak'ahs behind or near Maqam Ibrahim; drink Zamzam.
3. **Sa'i:** seven lengths between Safa and Marwa, starting at Safa, ending at Marwa
   (Safa→Marwa = 1). Men jog between the green lights.
4. **Halq or taqsir:** men shave the head (preferred) or trim; women cut a fingertip-length.
   Umrah complete; leave ihram.

## 4. Hajj days (8–13 Dhul-Hijjah)
- **8th:** re-enter ihram, go to **Mina**, pray there.
- **9th — Arafat:** the essential pillar. Be within Arafat's boundary between midday and
  sunset, in prayer and dua. Missing it means missing hajj.
- **Night of 9th:** **Muzdalifah** — pray Maghrib and Isha combined, sleep under the sky,
  collect small pebbles (chickpea-sized; ~49–70).
- **10th:** stone the large jamarah (Jamarat al-Aqabah) with 7 pebbles; animal sacrifice
  (hady, normally via an official voucher); halq/taqsir; then **tawaf al-ifadah** and sa'i
  in Mecca. Partial exit from ihram after stoning and hair-cutting.
- **11th–12th (13th optional):** stay in Mina; stone all three jamarat, 7 each, small →
  middle → large, after midday.
- **Leaving Mecca:** **tawaf al-wada'**, the farewell tawaf.

## Gotchas
- Heat and crowd crush are the real dangers: follow your group's assigned stoning times,
  carry water, an umbrella and a small bag; wear broken-in sandals.
- Losing count in tawaf/sa'i: use a counter ring or app; if unsure, build on the lower count.
- Menstruation prevents tawaf but not the other rites; group scholars advise on timing.
- Pebbles should be small; throwing shoes or big stones is not part of the rite.
- Keep your group card, tent number and phone charged — getting lost in Mina is common.

## Verify
- [ ] Ihram intention made before crossing the miqat.
- [ ] Tawaf 7 circuits with wudu; sa'i 7 lengths ending at Marwa.
- [ ] Present at Arafat between midday and sunset on the 9th.
- [ ] All stoning days completed in order; sacrifice voucher confirmed.
- [ ] Tawaf al-ifadah and farewell tawaf done.
