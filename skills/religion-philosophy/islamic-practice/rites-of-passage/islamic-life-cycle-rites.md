# Islamic life-cycle rites

**Goal:** carry out the main rites — birth, entering Islam, marriage and death —
correctly and without last-minute confusion, especially the janazah, which happens fast.

> Draft. Requirements (e.g. whether a bride needs a wali, number of takbirs, mourning
> periods) vary between schools and between Sunni and Shia practice. Civil law
> also applies: in many countries a nikah alone is not a legal marriage, and burial rules
> are set by the state. Your imam and funeral service are the authorities.

## 1. Birth
- Recite the **adhan** softly in the baby's right ear (and in some practice the iqamah in
  the left).
- **Tahnik:** rub a softened date on the baby's palate (optional, traditional).
- On the **7th day** (or 14th/21st if missed): name the child, shave the head and give the
  hair's weight in silver as charity, and perform the **aqiqah** — sacrifice of two sheep/
  goats for a boy, one for a girl (Sunni majority view), meat shared and given away.
  Arranging it through a halal butcher or a charity abroad is common.
- **Khitan** (male circumcision) is standard practice; timing varies, done by a medical
  practitioner.

## 2. Entering Islam (shahada)
- The person declares, understanding and intending it: *Ashhadu an la ilaha illallah, wa
  ashhadu anna Muhammadan rasulullah* ("I bear witness there is no god but God, and Muhammad
  is the messenger of God"). Shia practice often adds a third statement on Ali.
- Witnesses are customary (two Muslims), not strictly required; many mosques issue a
  certificate, which is needed later for hajj visas or a nikah.
- Follow with **ghusl** and a first lesson in wudu and prayer; pair the new Muslim with a
  mentor. Do not pressure a name change — it is optional.

## 3. Nikah (marriage)
Essentials of the contract:
1. **Consent** of both bride and groom, freely given.
2. **Wali:** the bride's guardian (normally father) — required in the majority of schools for
   a previously unmarried woman; not required by Hanafis for an adult woman.
3. **Two adult Muslim witnesses.**
4. **Mahr:** a gift from groom to bride, agreed and named in the contract — hers alone;
   it may be paid immediately or deferred.
5. **Offer and acceptance** (ijab and qabul) in one sitting, often after a short sermon.
Then the **walimah** (wedding feast) by the groom. Register the civil marriage too, before
or after per local law, and keep the signed nikah certificate.

## 4. Death and janazah
When death is near, prompt the dying gently with the shahada. After death:
1. Close the eyes, straighten limbs, cover the body, and inform the mosque/Muslim funeral
   service **immediately** — burial is done as soon as possible, ideally within 24 hours.
   Obtain the death certificate; tell the coroner/registrar if needed.
2. **Ghusl al-mayyit:** washing by same-sex family or trained volunteers (spouses may wash
   each other), odd number of washes, beginning with the right side and wudu limbs.
3. **Kafan:** shroud of plain white cloth — 3 sheets for a man, 5 pieces for a woman.
4. **Salat al-janazah:** standing prayer, no ruku or sujud, **4 takbirs** (Sunni; 5 in Shia
   practice): after the 1st al-Fatiha, after the 2nd salawat on the Prophet, after the 3rd dua
   for the deceased, after the 4th taslim.
5. **Burial:** in a Muslim section, without a coffin where permitted (otherwise a simple one),
   laid on the right side facing the **qibla**. Mourners each place three handfuls of earth.
Mourning: three days for the community; a widow observes **'iddah** of 4 months 10 days.
Condolence (ta'ziyah): visit, bring food, say *Inna lillahi wa inna ilayhi raji'un*.

## Gotchas
- Cremation and embalming are not practised; tell hospitals early to avoid a delayed release.
- Post-mortems may be required by law; ask about non-invasive options.
- A nikah without civil registration can leave a spouse with no legal rights.

## Verify
- [ ] Aqiqah done on/around the 7th day with name chosen.
- [ ] Shahada said with understanding; mentor and certificate arranged.
- [ ] Nikah had consent, wali (per school), two witnesses, stated mahr; civil registration done.
- [ ] Janazah: funeral service called at once, ghusl and kafan arranged, burial facing qibla.
