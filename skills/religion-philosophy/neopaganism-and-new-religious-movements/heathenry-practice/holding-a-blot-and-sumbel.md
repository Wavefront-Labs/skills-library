# Holding a blót and a sumbel

**Goal:** lead the two central communal rites of Heathenry — the **blót** (offering to
gods and other powers) and the **sumbel** (ritual round of toasts) — and maintain
right relationship with the land-wights, in a way that builds *frith* (peace and good
order) in the group.

> Draft. Heathenry reconstructs pre-Christian Germanic and Norse religion from lore
> (the Eddas, sagas, archaeology), so practice differs between groups — e.g. Iceland's
> Ásatrúarfélagið, The Troth, and many independent kindreds. Wording below is a common
> modern pattern, not a fixed liturgy. Many Heathen organisations explicitly reject
> racism and the misuse of Norse symbols by extremists; check a group's stated values
> before joining.

## 1. Roles and kit
- **Gothi/gythia** (priest/priestess) or blót leader; a horn-bearer for sumbel (often the
  host or lady of the hall).
- A **drinking horn** (food-safe lined, or use a cup), **mead** (or ale, cider, juice), a
  **bowl** (hlautbolli) and an evergreen sprig (**hlautteinn**) for sprinkling, a
  **hörgr** (stone altar) or a simple table, a fire or candle, a hammer if your group
  hallows with one.
- Offerings: mead, bread, food from your own table, crafted items. Outdoors, only
  biodegradable offerings safe for animals.

## 2. Blót, step by step
1. **Hallowing (making sacred):** walk the area with fire, or sign with a hammer at the
   four directions (*"Hammer, hallow and hold this holy stead"* in some groups). No circle
   is "cast" — the space is claimed and made holy.
2. **Statement of purpose:** which power is honoured and why — e.g. Thor at Yule, Freyr for
   harvest, the **dísir** at Winter Nights, the land-wights at any time.
3. **Invocation:** call the god(s) by name and heiti (bynames), speaking of their deeds.
   Stand upright, arms raised (the "elhaz" stance is one modern practice).
4. **Offering:** pour mead into the horn; the leader speaks over it and drinks (or not), the
   horn may pass; the rest is poured into the **blessing bowl**.
5. **Blessing (hlaut):** dip the evergreen sprig in the bowl and sprinkle participants and the
   hörgr — the gods' gift returned.
6. **Giving to the earth:** pour the bowl out onto the ground or onto the hörgr: *"From the gods
   to the earth, to us, from us to the gods..."* Leave food offerings.
7. **Close:** thanks; a feast often follows.

## 3. Sumbel
A formal, seated round of toasts in which words spoken over the horn are understood to
carry weight in the community's wyrd (fate).
- **Round 1:** toasts to the gods and goddesses.
- **Round 2:** to ancestors and honoured dead — kin and heroes.
- **Round 3:** open — **boasts** of deeds done, toasts to friends, and **oaths** (*heitstrengingar*).
The horn-bearer carries the horn to each person in turn, may offer a word of welcome, and
refills. Speak, drink (or touch the horn to the lips), pass. Silence and attention while others speak.
**Oaths are serious:** only swear what you will do; your group will hold you to it. Many
groups forbid oaths except in the third round with notice, and never under heavy drink.

## 4. Land-wights (landvættir) and daily practice
- Regular small offerings at a tree, rock or garden spot to the spirits of the land:
  bread, milk, porridge with butter (a traditional offering to house-wights at Yule).
- Household shrine to gods and ancestors; daily or weekly short offerings.

## 5. Holy tides
**Yule** (midwinter, often 12 nights), **Dísablót**, **Ostara** (spring), **Sigrblót**
(summer's beginning), **Midsummer**, **Winter Nights** (mid-October, start of winter,
honouring dísir and elves). Dates vary between groups.

## Gotchas
- Swearing rash oaths in sumbel.
- Treating sumbel as a drinking game — sips only; offer non-alcoholic options.
- Copying Wiccan circle-casting or quarter calls into a blót — most Heathens don't.
- Pouring sugary drinks or leaving plastic outdoors.

## Verify
- [ ] Purpose and the power honoured stated before invocation.
- [ ] Mead offered, hlaut sprinkled, the bowl given to the earth.
- [ ] Sumbel: three rounds, one speaker at a time, oaths only when meant.
- [ ] Non-alcoholic option provided; offerings biodegradable.
