# Casting and delineating a natal chart — from verified birth data to a coherent reading

**Goal:** produce a correctly calculated birth chart and a reading that synthesises
its themes in a fixed order, instead of reciting "Sun in X means..." cookbook lines.

## 1. Get the birth data right (most errors happen here)
You need three things: **date, clock time, and place (town plus country)**.
- **Source of the time:** ask where it came from. Practitioners grade it with the
  Rodden Rating: AA (birth certificate/record), A (memory of the person or family),
  B (biography), C (caution, unsourced), DD (conflicting). A "around lunchtime" time
  is not a birth time.
- **Time zone and daylight saving:** the software converts local clock time to UT
  using a historical atlas. Check the offset it chose. War time, double summer time
  (UK 1941–45, 1947), local mean time before standardisation, and regions that
  changed zones are the classic traps. If the client is unsure whether DST applied,
  cast both versions.
- **No birth time:** cast a sunrise or noon chart, say so on the chart, and do not
  read houses, Ascendant or the Moon's exact degree. Rectification is a separate,
  advanced job.

## 2. Calculate the chart (software settings)
Free: astro.com (Extended Chart Selection) or astro-seek.com. Desktop: Solar Fire
(Windows), Astrogold, Janus, Kepler. Set:
1. **Zodiac:** tropical for Western natal work (sidereal is the Vedic default — see
   religion-philosophy.astrology.vedic-astrology.reading-a-jyotish-chart-with-dashas).
2. **House system:** Placidus is the modern default; **whole-sign** is standard in
   Hellenistic and many traditional revival schools; Koch, Equal and Porphyry are
   also common. Pick one per reading and state it. Above roughly 66° latitude
   Placidus and Koch break down; use whole-sign or Equal there.
3. **Bodies:** the seven classical planets plus Uranus, Neptune, Pluto, the lunar
   nodes (mean or true — state which) and optionally Chiron. Minor bodies and many
   asteroids dilute a first reading.
4. **Aspects and orbs:** major Ptolemaic aspects (conjunction, sextile, square,
   trine, opposition). Orbs vary by school — a common modern setting is about 8°
   for conjunction/opposition/square/trine and 4–6° for sextile, wider (around 10°)
   when the Sun or Moon is involved. Traditional practice uses planetary moieties
   instead. Whatever you choose, apply it consistently.

## 3. Delineate in a fixed order
1. **Overview:** element and modality balance, hemisphere emphasis, chart shape,
   any stellium (three or more planets in one sign/house).
2. **The big three:** Sun, Moon, Ascendant — sign, house, closest aspects.
3. **Chart ruler:** the ruler of the Ascendant sign (traditional or modern rulership —
   state which); its sign, house, condition and aspects often colour the whole life.
4. **Planet by planet:** for each planet read *what* (planet) *how* (sign, including
   essential dignity: domicile, exaltation, detriment, fall) *where* (house) and *with
   whom* (aspects, tightest first). Angular planets (near ASC, MC, DSC, IC within
   about 5°) are emphasised.
5. **Houses:** occupied houses, then the condition of each house ruler for topics
   the client asked about.
6. **Synthesis:** find themes repeated by **at least two or three independent
   testimonies** (e.g. Saturn rising + Capricorn Moon + many earth placements). Lead
   with those; treat one-off factors as minor.

## 4. Present it
Open with the repeated themes, use concrete language, frame difficult placements as
challenges and growth areas, and invite the client to confirm or reject what you say.
Ethics: do not make medical, financial or legal predictions, and only read a third
party's chart with a clear, legitimate reason and ideally their consent.

## Gotchas
- An error of four minutes in birth time moves the Ascendant about one degree; a
  one-hour error can change the rising sign and every house cusp.
- "AM/PM" confusion and 24-hour clocks on foreign certificates.
- Software defaults differ (house system, true vs mean node, orbs), so two websites
  can disagree — compare settings before assuming an error.
- Interceptions and very unequal houses are artefacts of quadrant systems at higher
  latitudes, not special omens in every school.

## Verify
- [ ] Birth time source noted with a Rodden-style rating.
- [ ] Software's UT offset checked against the historical DST/zone for that place and date.
- [ ] Zodiac, house system, node type and orb policy stated on the chart.
- [ ] Reading leads with themes supported by at least two independent factors.
- [ ] No medical, financial or legal predictions; third-party chart has a reason/consent.
