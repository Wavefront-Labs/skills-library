# Electing a chart for an undertaking — choosing the best available start time

**Goal:** within the client's real-world constraints, find the moment whose chart
best supports the undertaking, and explain the trade-offs of the top two or three
candidates. A perfect election rarely exists; the job is the best one available.

## 1. Gather the brief
- **What is being started** and what "start" means: for a wedding, the vows or the
  pronouncement; for a business, the incorporation filing, contract signature or
  doors opening; for a product, the publish moment. Agree this first.
- **Place** (the location of the event) and the **window**: earliest and latest
  dates, days and hours that are actually possible (venue, office hours).
- **Natal charts** of the principals if available, with birth times. Using a third
  party's chart (e.g. a business partner) needs their consent.

## 2. Settings
Solar Fire (Electional search tool / Transit search), Astrogold, Morinus, or manual
checks on astro.com. Tropical zodiac; houses are usually **Regiomontanus** or
**whole-sign** in traditional elections (Placidus in many modern ones — state it).
Traditional rulerships, Part of Fortune shown. (Vedic *muhurta* uses different rules
— nakshatra, tithi, yoga — and is not covered here.)

## 3. Eliminate bad days (coarse filter)
Strike out:
1. Days with the **Moon void of course** at the start time (it will be on many
   afternoons/evenings; check the exact void times).
2. Days within about a week either side of an **eclipse**, and the dark Moon
   (roughly the day before to the day of the New Moon).
3. **Mercury retrograde**, including its stations, for contracts, purchases,
   communication or tech launches. Some astrologers also avoid Venus retrograde for
   weddings and Mars retrograde for competitive or military-style ventures.
4. Moon in the **Via Combusta** (about 15° Libra–15° Scorpio) or combust (within
   ~8.5° of the Sun), and Moon applying to a hard aspect of Mars or Saturn.
Many electional astrologers prefer a **waxing Moon** for things meant to grow.

## 4. Strengthen what matters (fine filter, within the surviving days)
For each candidate time (step the chart in 4-minute intervals; the Ascendant moves
about 1°):
1. **Ascendant and its ruler:** the ruler well dignified and well placed (angular or
   succedent, not in the 6th, 8th or 12th), not afflicted by Mars or Saturn.
2. **House of the matter:** 7th for marriage and partnerships, 10th for business
   reputation/launch, 2nd for money, 3rd for short journeys and communications. Its
   ruler strong and preferably applying to a benefic or to the Ascendant ruler.
3. **The Moon:** well placed, applying to a benefic (Venus, Jupiter) or to the
   ruler of the matter; not in her fall (Scorpio) or detriment (Capricorn).
4. **Benefics angular**, malefics cadent or at least not on the Ascendant/MC.
5. Optional refinements: planetary day and hour of a suitable planet (Venus for
   weddings, Jupiter or Sun for public launches); avoiding the outer planets on the
   angles; tying the chart to the principals' natal charts (e.g. Ascendant on a natal
   benefic, avoiding natal Saturn).

## 5. Rank and present
Score two or three candidates in a short table: Moon condition, Ascendant ruler,
house ruler, angular planets, problems. Give the client a primary and a backup time
with a tolerance (e.g. "begin between 14:20 and 14:40"). Explain that timing support
does not replace practical preparation, and do not frame an election as a guarantee
of financial, legal or medical outcomes.

## Gotchas
- Choosing the wrong "start" (booking date instead of ceremony) undermines the work.
- Time-zone/DST of the event location, not the astrologer's.
- Over-filtering leaves no dates; the rules conflict and you must trade them off.
- Schools disagree on the weight of retrogrades and planetary hours; say which you used.

## Verify
- [ ] The defined start moment and event location are written down and agreed.
- [ ] Void Moon, eclipses and relevant retrogrades checked for every candidate.
- [ ] Ascendant ruler, matter's house ruler and Moon assessed for each candidate.
- [ ] Primary and backup times given with a usable tolerance window.
- [ ] Trade-offs and house/rulership conventions stated to the client.
