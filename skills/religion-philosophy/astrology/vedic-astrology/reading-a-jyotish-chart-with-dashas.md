# Reading a jyotish chart with dashas — sidereal chart, nakshatras and Vimshottari timing

**Goal:** cast a correct sidereal (Vedic) birth chart, read its core promise from
the rashi and navamsa charts, and say which areas of life are active now using the
Vimshottari dasha sequence.

## 1. Birth data
Date, exact time and place, with the same time-zone/DST checks as Western work (see
religion-philosophy.astrology.natal-charting.casting-and-delineating-a-natal-chart).
Jyotish is very time-sensitive: divisional charts such as the navamsa (D9) can change
with a few minutes' error, and the dasha balance depends on the Moon's exact degree.

## 2. Software and settings
Jagannatha Hora (free, Windows), Parashara's Light, Kala, or astro-seek's Vedic
calculator. Set:
1. **Ayanamsa:** **Lahiri (Chitrapaksha)** is the Indian government standard and
   the usual default (about 24° in the 2020s). Raman and Krishnamurti (KP) are
   alternatives; some practitioners use True Chitrapaksha. State which.
2. **Houses:** whole-sign from the lagna (Ascendant sign) for the rashi chart. A
   *bhava chalit* (cusp-based) view is a secondary check some use.
3. **Nodes:** mean node is the common jyotish default (some prefer true).
4. **Chart style:** North Indian (fixed houses, diamond) or South Indian (fixed signs,
   grid) — the content is identical.
5. **Dasha:** Vimshottari, year length 365.25 days (some use 360; it shifts dates).

## 3. Read the rashi chart (D1)
1. **Lagna and lagna lord:** sign rising and the condition of its ruler (traditional
   rulers only; Rahu and Ketu are shadow planets, outer planets are not used).
2. **Graha strength:** exaltation/debilitation, own sign, friendly/enemy sign,
   combustion, retrogression, and placement in kendras (1, 4, 7, 10) and trikonas
   (1, 5, 9) as auspicious, dusthanas (6, 8, 12) as difficult.
3. **Graha drishti (aspects):** all planets aspect the 7th sign from themselves;
   Mars also the 4th and 8th, Jupiter the 5th and 9th, Saturn the 3rd and 10th
   (Rahu/Ketu special aspects are disputed). Aspects are by whole sign.
4. **Functional nature by lagna:** lords of trikonas are benefic for that chart,
   lords of 6/8/12 problematic, regardless of natural benefic/malefic status. A planet
   ruling both a kendra and a trikona is a *yogakaraka*.
5. **Yogas:** check the well-known combinations (Raja yogas, Dhana yogas, Gajakesari,
   Pancha Mahapurusha) but require the participating planets to be strong.
6. **Moon chart (Chandra lagna)** and **Navamsa (D9)**: confirm promises; a planet
   strong in D1 but debilitated in D9 delivers less. D9 is also read for marriage.

## 4. Nakshatras
Each of the 27 nakshatras spans 13°20' and has a ruling planet. The Moon's
nakshatra (*janma nakshatra*) describes temperament and fixes the dasha sequence;
the lagna's and key planets' nakshatras add nuance.

## 5. Time with Vimshottari dasha
1. The 120-year cycle runs Ketu 7, Venus 20, Sun 6, Moon 10, Mars 7, Rahu 18,
   Jupiter 16, Saturn 19, Mercury 17 years.
2. The first mahadasha belongs to the ruler of the Moon's nakshatra; the **balance**
   at birth is the unused fraction of that nakshatra. Software computes this.
3. Each mahadasha divides into **antardashas** (bhuktis) in the same order,
   starting with the mahadasha lord, proportionally sized.
4. Interpret the current mahadasha and antardasha lords by the houses they rule and
   occupy, their strength, and their relationship to each other (e.g. 6th/8th-lord
   periods for obstacles, 10th-lord periods for career).
5. Confirm with **gochara** (transits from the Moon and lagna), especially Saturn
   and Jupiter; transits give results only if the dasha promises them.

## 6. Remedies (upaya)
Traditional suggestions are mantra, charity, fasting on a planet's weekday, worship,
and gemstones. Practitioners disagree about gemstones; offer low-cost, devotional
remedies first, never pressure clients into expensive purchases, and do not make
medical, financial or legal predictions or frighten clients with doshas.

## Gotchas
- Using a Western tropical chart (positions differ by ~24°, usually a sign).
- Different ayanamsas or year lengths giving dasha dates months apart.
- Reading a yoga without checking whether its planets are strong.
- Treating Mangal dosha or Sade Sati as fixed doom; most texts list cancellations.

- [ ] Ayanamsa, node type, chart style and dasha year length recorded.
- [ ] Lagna lord, functional benefics/malefics and key yogas assessed in D1 and D9.
- [ ] Moon's nakshatra and dasha balance confirmed in the software output.
- [ ] Current mahadasha/antardasha interpreted by rulership and placement, checked with transits.
- [ ] Remedies offered are low-cost first; no frightening or medical/financial claims.
