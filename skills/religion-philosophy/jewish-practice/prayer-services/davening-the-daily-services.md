# Davening the daily services

**Goal:** pray the daily services (or follow them in synagogue) with confidence —
knowing where you are in the siddur, what needs a minyan, and how to lead.

> Draft. Structure is common to all rites, but the siddur and nusach (Ashkenaz,
> Sefard/Hasidic, Edot HaMizrach/Sephardi, Italian, Yemenite) differ in wording and
> order, and Orthodox, Conservative/Masorti, Reform and Reconstructionist practice
> differ on the minyan, egalitarian roles and the amount of Hebrew. Follow your
> community's siddur and rabbi.

## 1. The three services
| Service | When | Core |
|---------|------|------|
| **Shacharit** | morning, ideally by the first third of daylight (deadline for the Shema) | blessings, Pesukei d'Zimrah, Shema and its blessings, Amidah, (Torah reading Mon/Thu), Aleinu |
| **Mincha** | afternoon until sunset | Ashrei, Amidah, Aleinu |
| **Maariv/Arvit** | after nightfall | Shema and blessings, Amidah, Aleinu |
Use a zmanim app or your synagogue's luach for halachic times.

## 2. The two pillars
- **Shema:** *Shema Yisrael, Adonai Eloheinu, Adonai echad* (Deut. 6:4) then three
  paragraphs. Cover the eyes for the first line; say it with concentration on its meaning.
- **Amidah** (Shemoneh Esrei, "the standing prayer"): 19 blessings on weekdays (7 on
  Shabbat and festivals). Take three steps back and three forward before starting; stand
  feet together facing Jerusalem; bow at the four places marked (start/end of the first
  blessing and of *Modim*); say it quietly so only you hear. At the end take three steps back.
  In a minyan the leader repeats it aloud with **Kedushah**.

## 3. Minyan-only parts
Ten adults (ten men over bar mitzvah in Orthodox practice; ten adults in egalitarian
communities) are required for: Barchu, Kedushah, the leader's Amidah repetition, the
Torah reading, and Kaddish (including the Mourner's Kaddish). Praying alone, skip these and
say the rest.

## 4. Tallit and tefillin
- **Tallit** (fringed shawl): weekday and Shabbat mornings; say the blessing, wrap, then
  settle it on the shoulders. Check the tzitzit strings are intact.
- **Tefillin:** weekday mornings only (not Shabbat or festivals). Arm (shel yad) first, on
  the weaker arm's biceps facing the heart, blessing, seven wraps on the forearm; then the
  head (shel rosh) above the hairline centred; then the finger/hand wraps. Remove in
  reverse order. Have them checked by a sofer every few years.

## 5. The Torah service (Mon, Thu, Shabbat, festivals)
The scroll is taken from the ark and carried round; **aliyot** are given (3 on weekdays, 7
on Shabbat). If called up: touch the text with the tallit's corner, kiss it, say the
blessing before; the reader chants; say the blessing after. A **gabbai** runs the order —
ask them your Hebrew name (with parent's name) in advance.

## 6. Leading (shaliach tzibbur)
- Learn the nusach (melodies) for the specific service and season — weekday, Shabbat,
  festival and High Holiday chants all differ.
- Announce page numbers in mixed communities.
- Pace: start the next section when most of the minyan has finished; wait for ten to
  finish the Amidah before the repetition.

## Gotchas
- Missing the latest time for the morning Shema — check zmanim.
- Starting a minyan-only prayer without ten present.
- Wearing tefillin on Shabbat, or the arm tefillin on the wrong arm (lefties reverse).
- Talking during the Amidah or Kaddish responses.

## Verify
- [ ] You can find Shema and Amidah in your siddur without help.
- [ ] Today's services fell within their times.
- [ ] Amidah: steps, bows, quiet voice, facing Jerusalem.
- [ ] Minyan-only prayers said only with ten present.
- [ ] Tefillin on a weekday only, correct arm, head box centred above hairline.
