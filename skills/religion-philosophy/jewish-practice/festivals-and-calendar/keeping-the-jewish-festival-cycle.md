# Keeping the Jewish festival cycle

**Goal:** observe the festivals of the Jewish year correctly and on time, with the
practical set-up (sukkah, seder plate, menorah) done right.

> Draft. Practice varies by movement and custom (e.g. Diaspora communities keep an
> extra festival day — Yom Tov Sheni — which Reform and Israeli practice do not; the
> details of kitniyot at Passover differ). Ask your rabbi on specifics.

## 1. The calendar
Lunisolar: lunar months, with a leap month (Adar II) seven times in 19 years so Passover
stays in spring. Days run **sunset to nightfall**; every festival starts the evening before
its civil date. Get dates and candle times from a luach or a calendar such as Hebcal.

## 2. The festival cycle
**Rosh Hashanah (1–2 Tishrei)** — New Year. Candle lighting, kiddush, apple in honey and
symbolic foods (simanim), round challah. Hear the **shofar** (100 blasts in many
congregations: tekiah, shevarim, teruah, tekiah gedolah) on each day unless one is Shabbat.
**Tashlich** by flowing water on the first afternoon.

**Yom Kippur (10 Tishrei)** — 25-hour fast (no food, drink, washing for pleasure, leather
shoes, anointing, marital relations), from before sunset. Eat a full **seudah mafseket**
beforehand; ask forgiveness of people before the day. **Kol Nidre** in the evening;
services through to **Ne'ilah** and the final shofar. Exempt: children, the ill per
medical/rabbinic advice, women soon after childbirth.

**Sukkot (15–21 Tishrei)** — eat (and ideally sleep) in a **sukkah**:
- At least 2½–3 walls (3 is standard practice), sturdy enough not to sway in normal wind.
- Height at least ~80 cm (10 tefachim) and no more than ~9 m (20 amot).
- **S'chach** roof of cut, unprocessed plant material (bamboo mats certified for sukkah,
  branches, palm fronds), more shade than sun inside, but stars visible through it.
- Not under a tree or overhang.
Take the **four species** (lulav, etrog, 3 myrtle, 2 willow) each weekday morning: blessing,
then wave in six directions. Buy the etrog checked (pitam intact, no blemishes on the upper part).
**Shemini Atzeret / Simchat Torah** follow, with dancing with the Torah scrolls.

**Chanukah (25 Kislev, 8 nights)** — light the **menorah**: one candle the first night,
adding one each night, placed right-to-left, lit left-to-right (newest first) with the
shamash. Blessings before lighting; candles must burn ≥30 minutes after nightfall; on
Friday light *before* Shabbat candles with longer candles. Place in a window or doorway.

**Purim (14 Adar; 15 in walled cities)** — hear the **Megillah** (Book of Esther) read
evening and morning, every word; give **mishloach manot** (two ready-to-eat foods to at
least one person), **matanot la'evyonim** (gifts to at least two poor people), and hold a festive meal.

**Pesach (15–22 Nisan; 15–21 in Israel)** — remove chametz (see the kosher-kitchen skill,
`religion-philosophy.jewish-practice.kashrut-practice.setting-up-and-keeping-a-kosher-kitchen`).
**Seder**: the plate holds zeroa (shank bone), beitzah (egg), maror, charoset, karpas,
chazeret; three matzot. The 15 steps from Kadesh to Nirtzah — four cups, the four
questions, Maggid, matzah and maror, Korech, the meal, the afikoman, Hallel. Use a Haggadah
with the steps marked; eat the matzah portions within the stated times.

**Shavuot (6 Sivan)** — all-night study (tikkun leil Shavuot), the Ten Commandments read,
dairy foods. **Tisha B'Av (9 Av)** — fast and mourning for the Temples; reading of Lamentations.

## Gotchas
- Missing the start because the festival "is on Tuesday" — it began Monday night.
- Yom Tov cooking rules differ from Shabbat: cooking from an existing flame is permitted
  for that day; preparing for the next day needs an **eruv tavshilin** when a festival leads into Shabbat.
- Sukkah s'chach made of processed lumber or under a balcony.

## Verify
- [ ] Festival start/end times for this year are in your calendar (evening starts).
- [ ] Sukkah meets the wall, height, s'chach and open-sky rules.
- [ ] Four species checked and bought before Sukkot.
- [ ] Seder plate and Haggadah prepared; chametz search/burn scheduled.
- [ ] Menorah placed visibly with candles that last 30+ min after nightfall.
