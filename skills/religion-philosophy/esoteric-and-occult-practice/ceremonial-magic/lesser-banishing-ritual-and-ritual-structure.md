# The Lesser Banishing Ritual and the structure of a working

**Goal:** perform the Golden Dawn–derived Lesser Banishing Ritual of the Pentagram
(LBRP) correctly as a daily practice, and understand the common shape every larger
ceremonial working builds on.

The LBRP was published from Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn papers (Israel
Regardie's *The Golden Dawn*) and is practised in many lineages, including Thelemic
ones (Crowley's *Liber O* and the Star Ruby variant). Details — pronunciation,
directions, visualisation colours — vary by order. Where you have a lodge or teacher,
their version governs.

## 1. Setup
- A clear space where you can face east and turn in a circle. A dagger (air dagger)
  or your index finger to trace figures.
- Stand facing **east**.

## 2. The Qabalistic Cross
1. Touch the forehead; visualise brilliant light above the head. Vibrate **"Ateh"**
   (thou art).
2. Touch the breast (or groin in some versions), bringing light down. **"Malkuth"**
   (the kingdom).
3. Right shoulder: **"ve-Geburah"** (and the power).
4. Left shoulder: **"ve-Gedulah"** (and the glory).
5. Clasp hands at the chest: **"le-Olam, Amen"** (for ever, amen).
"Vibrating" means intoning a word on a full exhalation so it resonates in the chest
and seems to fill the space.

## 3. The pentagrams
Facing each quarter in turn, trace a **banishing earth pentagram**: start at the
lower left point and draw up to the top point, then continue the star, ending where
you began. Stab its centre and vibrate the divine name:
- **East:** "YHVH" (Yod-Heh-Vav-Heh).
- **South:** "Adonai".
- **West:** "Eheieh".
- **North:** "AGLA".
Between quarters, trace a line (often white light) round the circle so the four
pentagrams connect. Return to face east.

## 4. Evocation of the archangels
Arms out in a cross, say:
"Before me **Raphael**; behind me **Gabriel**; on my right hand **Michael**; on my
left hand **Auriel**. For about me flame the pentagrams, and in the column stands the
six-rayed star." Visualise each figure with its elemental attribution (air, water,
fire, earth).

## 5. Repeat the Qabalistic Cross
Closing the rite.

Total time: 5–10 minutes. Done daily, ideally morning and evening, for months before
attempting larger workings.

## The standard shape of a working
1. **Statement of intent** written beforehand, as one sentence.
2. **Purification and consecration** of self and space (water and incense).
3. **Banishing** (LBRP; often the hexagram ritual too).
4. **Opening** — declare the temple open; for invocations, the appropriate invoking
   pentagrams/hexagrams and names.
5. **The work** — invocation (calling a force into oneself), evocation (calling it to
   appear before oneself, traditionally into a triangle outside the circle), prayer,
   consecration of a talisman, etc.
6. **License to depart** — formally dismiss any forces called.
7. **Closing banishing.**
8. **Record** in the diary immediately.

## Tools (Golden Dawn attributions)
Wand (fire), cup (water), dagger (air), pentacle (earth). Many traditions make or
consecrate their own tools. Thelemic and other systems differ — follow your system.

## The magical diary
Date, time, moon phase, physical and mental state, ritual performed, results,
impressions; review weekly. Crowley and the Golden Dawn treat the diary as essential.

## Gotchas
- Learning from mixed sources and combining incompatible versions (e.g. different
  pentagram directions for the same element).
- Skipping the daily banishing to jump to evocation; most teachers warn against this.
- Mumbling instead of vibrating.
- Mental-health caution: if practice increases anxiety, intrusive experiences or
  sleep loss, pause and seek support; most teachers recommend a grounded daily life
  alongside practice.

## Verify
- [ ] You can perform the LBRP from memory facing the right quarters with the right names.
- [ ] Your pentagram direction is the banishing form in your system's version.
- [ ] Every working has a written intent, opening, license to depart and closing.
- [ ] A dated magical diary entry exists for each practice session.
- [ ] Your sources for each component are named and consistent.
