# Tree of Life pathworking and gematria

**Goal:** learn the structure of the Tree of Life, run a pathworking (guided
meditative journey along a path between two sephiroth), and use gematria as a
contemplative tool.

## Which Kabbalah?
- **Jewish Kabbalah** — a mystical tradition within Judaism (Sefer Yetzirah, the Zohar,
  Lurianic Kabbalah, Hasidic thought), traditionally studied within observant
  practice and Jewish law, often with a teacher.
- **Hermetic Qabalah** — the Western esoteric adaptation (Renaissance Christian
  Cabala, then the Golden Dawn, Crowley, Dion Fortune) that maps tarot, astrology
  and ritual onto the Tree.
This skill describes the Hermetic practice (pathworking is a Hermetic technique).
Be clear which tradition you are speaking from; don't present Hermetic correspondences
as Jewish teaching.

## 1. The Tree
Ten **sephiroth** in three pillars:
1 Kether (crown) · 2 Chokmah (wisdom) · 3 Binah (understanding) · [Daath, knowledge,
the non-numbered "hidden" sephirah] · 4 Chesed (mercy) · 5 Geburah (severity) ·
6 Tiphareth (beauty) · 7 Netzach (victory) · 8 Hod (splendour) · 9 Yesod
(foundation) · 10 Malkuth (kingdom).
Pillars: Severity (3, 5, 8), Mercy (2, 4, 7), Middle (1, 6, 9, 10).
**22 paths** join them, each assigned one Hebrew letter and (in the Golden Dawn system)
one Major Arcana trump. Paths are numbered 11–32 after the ten sephiroth. Example:
path 32, Malkuth–Yesod, letter Tav, trump The World/Universe.
Crowley's *777* is the standard Hermetic correspondence table (note Crowley swaps
two letter attributions — Tzaddi and Heh — relative to the Golden Dawn).

## 2. A pathworking session (30–40 min)
Beginners usually start with path 32 (Malkuth to Yesod).
1. Banish (e.g. the LBRP) and sit comfortably.
2. Visualise a temple of Malkuth: citrine, olive, russet and black floor; a doorway
   ahead bearing the letter Tav and the image of the World trump.
3. Pass through; walk the path, noticing the scenery, symbols and any beings. Keep
   imagery consistent with the path's correspondences (Saturn, indigo/black for Tav).
4. Arrive at the temple of Yesod (violet, the Moon). Spend time there.
5. **Return by the same path** to Malkuth; see the doorway close.
6. Return to body awareness: feel your feet, eat or drink something, banish.
7. Record everything in your diary.
Many groups use a pre-written script read aloud by one person.

## 3. Gematria
Each Hebrew letter has a number value: Aleph 1 … Yod 10, Kaph 20 … Qoph 100, Resh 200,
Shin 300, Tav 400 (final forms sometimes valued 500–900 in later systems).
1. Write the word in Hebrew letters.
2. Sum the values. E.g. אהבה *ahavah* (love): 1+5+2+5 = 13; אחד *echad* (one):
   1+8+4 = 13.
3. Words with equal value are contemplated together ("love" and "one").
Gematria is a contemplative and interpretive tool, not a proof; traditional
commentators used it alongside other readings. English "gematria" (A=1…) is a modern
invention and not part of either tradition.

## 4. The Middle Pillar exercise (Regardie)
Standing, visualise spheres of light on the middle pillar and vibrate a divine name at
each: above the head (Kether, *Eheieh*), throat (Daath, *YHVH Elohim*), heart
(Tiphareth, *YHVH Eloah ve-Daath*), genitals (Yesod, *Shaddai El Chai*), feet
(Malkuth, *Adonai ha-Aretz*). Then circulate the light. 10–15 minutes.

## Gotchas
- Returning from a pathworking by a different route or skipping the return.
- Mixing Golden Dawn and Crowley letter attributions.
- Gematria number-crunching without context, which can "prove" anything.
- Appropriating Jewish practice — be respectful and accurate about origins.
