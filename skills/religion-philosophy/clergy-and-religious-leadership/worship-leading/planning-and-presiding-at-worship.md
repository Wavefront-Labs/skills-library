# Planning and presiding at worship

**Goal:** a service where every participant knows their cue, the congregation can
follow without confusion, and the presider is free to pray rather than stage-manage.

The process is broadly shared across congregational traditions; the *content* (what
must be said, by whom, in what order) is set by your tradition's books and
authorities. Where they conflict with anything here, they win.

## 1. Start from the calendar (4–8 weeks out)
- Mark the season and feasts: Advent, Christmas, Lent, Holy Week, Easter, Pentecost;
  Ramadan nights, Eid; Rosh Hashanah, Yom Kippur, Sukkot, Pesach; or your
  community's cycle.
- Pull the fixed elements: readings (lectionary/parashah), proper prayers, required
  colours or vestments, seasonal changes (e.g. omitting the Gloria/Alleluia in Lent).
- Note special services that need extra rehearsal (processions, baptisms, a vigil).

## 2. Build the order of service (2 weeks out)
Make one master document, a **run-sheet**, with columns:

| # | Element | Who | Text/source | Music | Cue | Notes |
|---|---------|-----|-------------|-------|-----|-------|

Fill every row: gathering, readings, sermon, prayers, offering/collection,
sacramental or central rite, sending. Add timings; check the total against the slot.

Choose music with the musician, not for them: give them readings and the theme two
weeks ahead so hymns, psalms or nasheed fit the season and the congregation can sing them.

## 3. Brief the roles (1 week out)
Send each person their row(s) plus the full run-sheet:
- Readers: passage, pronunciation of names, where to stand, how to announce/close.
- Musicians/cantor: keys, introductions, how they'll know to start.
- Servers/acolytes/ushers: processions, collection, communion or distribution flow.
- AV/livestream: slide order, mic list, when cameras must not show (e.g. children, confessional moments).
Confirm each person replies. Unconfirmed = find a backup.

## 4. Prepare the space (day before / an hour before)
- Books marked with ribbons or sticky tabs in order of use.
- Elements prepared (bread, wine, water, candles, oil) per your tradition's rules.
- Mics tested at speaking volume; spare batteries out.
- Printed run-sheet at the presider's position, musician's stand and AV desk.
- Brief huddle 10–15 min before: walk the order, changes, a short prayer together.

## 5. Presiding
- Move and speak unhurriedly; the congregation takes its pace from you.
- Give clear invitations ("Please stand", "Let us pray") rather than gestures alone,
  especially for visitors.
- Leave real silence where the rite calls for it — count to ten; it will feel long.
- Watch for needs: someone unwell, a crying child, a reader who freezes. You are the
  host.

## 6. Recovering from mistakes
- Wrong reading started: let it finish unless it's seriously wrong; fix next time.
- Musician misses a cue: a calm "Let's sing hymn 123" restarts it.
- Skipped element: if it's essential to the rite's validity in your tradition, go
  back simply and without apology; otherwise carry on.
- Never correct a volunteer audibly in the service.

## 7. Debrief
Within two days: what went wrong, what to change, thank-yous to volunteers by name.
Keep the run-sheet as a template for the same feast next year.

## Gotchas
- Assuming the musician "knows the season".
- Livestream showing faces of people who didn't consent.
- Overloading the service with additions until it runs 20 minutes long.
- Seasonal changes forgotten (e.g. Lent omissions) because the template wasn't updated.

## Verify
- [ ] Run-sheet complete, timed, and in every role-holder's hands.
- [ ] Every role confirmed, with a backup for readers and music.
- [ ] Required texts/elements for the season are present per your tradition's books.
- [ ] Mics, books and elements checked before the doors open.
- [ ] Debrief notes saved with the template for next year.
