# Casting the geomantic shield chart

**Goal:** produce a correct 15-figure shield chart (plus Reconciler) from random
marks, check it arithmetically, and read the answer — the core of Western
(European/Renaissance) geomancy. Related systems such as Arabic *ʿilm al-raml* and
Malagasy *sikidy* share the binary figures but differ in procedure.

## 1. The sixteen figures
Each figure has four rows (head, neck, body, feet); each row is one dot (odd) or two
dots (even). Written top → bottom, 1 = one dot, 2 = two dots:

| Figure | Rows | Figure | Rows |
|---|---|---|---|
| Via | 1111 | Populus | 2222 |
| Conjunctio | 2112 | Carcer | 1221 |
| Fortuna Major | 2211 | Fortuna Minor | 1122 |
| Acquisitio | 2121 | Amissio | 1212 |
| Laetitia | 1222 | Tristitia | 2221 |
| Puella | 1211 | Puer | 1121 |
| Albus | 2212 | Rubeus | 2122 |
| Caput Draconis | 2111 | Cauda Draconis | 1112 |

## 2. Generate the Mothers
1. Focus on a single, clearly worded question.
2. Without counting, make 16 lines of dots or strokes (on paper, or traditionally in
   sand or earth with a stick).
3. Count each line: odd → one dot, even → two dots.
4. Lines 1–4 form Mother 1 (head to feet), 5–8 Mother 2, 9–12 Mother 3, 13–16 Mother 4.

## 3. Derive the rest
- **Daughters (transposition):** Daughter 1 = the heads of Mothers 1–4, in order;
  Daughter 2 = their necks; Daughter 3 = bodies; Daughter 4 = feet.
- **Addition rule:** combine two figures row by row: odd+odd = even, even+even = even,
  odd+even = odd. (Count the dots: 1+1=2 → two dots; 1+2=3 → one dot; 2+2=4 → two.)
- **Nieces:** N1 = M1+M2, N2 = M3+M4, N3 = D1+D2, N4 = D3+D4.
- **Right Witness** = N1+N2; **Left Witness** = N3+N4.
- **Judge** = Right Witness + Left Witness.
- **Reconciler** (optional 16th) = Judge + Mother 1.

## 4. Lay out the shield
Top row, **right to left:** M1, M2, M3, M4, D1, D2, D3, D4. Below them the four Nieces
(each under the pair that made it), then the two Witnesses, and the Judge at the
bottom point of the shield.

## 5. Check your arithmetic
The Judge must always have an **even total number of dots** — it can only be Via,
Populus, Conjunctio, Carcer, Fortuna Major, Fortuna Minor, Acquisitio or Amissio. Any
other Judge means an addition error; recompute.

## 6. Read the shield
1. **Judge** gives the overall answer (e.g. Fortuna Major, Acquisitio — favourable;
   Amissio, Carcer — loss or restriction; Via — change, movement; Populus — neutral,
   depends on context).
2. **Right Witness** = the querent's side or the past; **Left Witness** = the
   quesited/other side or the future. Two good Witnesses with a good Judge = strongly
   yes; mixed Witnesses qualify the answer.
3. **Reconciler** refines a doubtful Judge.

## 7. The house chart (for detail)
Place M1–M4 in houses 1–4, D1–D4 in houses 5–8, N1–N4 in houses 9–12 (astrological
houses: 1 querent, 2 money, 7 partner/opponent, 10 career, etc.). Identify the
house of the question, then look for **perfection** — the querent's figure (house 1)
connecting with the quesited's house by:
- **Occupation:** the same figure in both houses;
- **Conjunction:** the figure appears in a house adjacent to the other;
- **Mutation:** the two figures appear side by side elsewhere;
- **Translation:** a third figure links them.
Perfection says the matter comes about; its quality comes from the figures and Judge.
(Interpretation details vary across authors such as Agrippa, Fludd and modern
teachers — commit to one source's rules.)

## Gotchas
- Counting lines while making them — the randomness depends on not counting.
- Laying the Mothers left to right; tradition runs right to left.
- Casting repeatedly for the same question; many authors read the first chart only.
- Some traditions disallow a chart whose first house holds Rubeus or Cauda Draconis
  ("not radical") — check your source.

## Verify
- [ ] 16 lines → four Mothers recorded head-to-feet.
- [ ] Daughters built by transposition, not addition.
- [ ] Judge is one of the eight even figures.
- [ ] Shield laid out right to left with Witnesses and Judge placed correctly.
- [ ] Reading states Judge, Witnesses, and (if used) perfection mode in the house chart.
