# Reading lines, mounts and hand shape

**Goal:** read a hand systematically — overall shape first, details last — so the
reading is coherent and repeatable rather than a list of isolated line meanings.

Western palmistry (chiromancy) as taught today largely follows 19th–20th-century
writers (e.g. Cheiro, and the elemental hand types popularised later); Indian
*samudrika shastra* / hasta samudrika is a parallel tradition with different
emphases. Interpretations below are the common modern Western ones.

## 1. Which hand
The **dominant hand** is read as the present and developed self; the
**non-dominant** as inborn tendencies. Compare both; differences are a talking point.

## 2. Light and setup
Good daylight or a lamp from the side shows creases best. A magnifier helps for
fine lines. Ask permission before touching; let the person rest the hand on a cushion.

## 3. Hand shape (elemental system)
Measure palm (wrist crease to finger base) against finger length (middle finger):
- **Earth:** square palm, short fingers — practical, steady.
- **Air:** square palm, long fingers — intellectual, communicative.
- **Fire:** long palm, short fingers — energetic, impulsive.
- **Water:** long palm, long fingers — sensitive, imaginative.
Also note skin texture, flexibility (how far fingers bend back) and firmness.

## 4. Major lines (in this order)
1. **Heart line** — the upper horizontal line from under the little finger toward
   the index. Emotional life. Ending under the index = idealistic in love; under the
   middle finger = self-focused; straight and short = reserved.
2. **Head line** — below it, starting near the thumb side. Thinking style. Straight =
   logical; sloping toward the wrist = imaginative; joined to the life line at the
   start = cautious early life.
3. **Life line** — curves around the base of the thumb. Vitality and major changes —
   **not lifespan**. A short life line does not mean a short life.
4. **Fate line** — vertical up the centre toward the middle finger, if present. Sense
   of direction and career; absence is common and not negative.
5. Secondary: sun (Apollo) line, marriage/relationship lines on the edge below the
   little finger, girdle of Venus.
Breaks = changes; islands = periods of strain; forks at the end = divided interests.

## 5. Mounts (fleshy pads)
Under index: **Jupiter** (ambition); middle: **Saturn** (seriousness); ring:
**Apollo/Sun** (creativity); little: **Mercury** (communication). Base of thumb:
**Venus** (warmth, sensuality); opposite edge: **Moon/Luna** (imagination).
Read prominence: well-developed = the quality is strong; flat = less expressed.

## 6. Fingers and thumb
Long index relative to ring finger = confidence/leadership (in the tradition's
reading); strong, stiff thumb = willpower; flexible thumb = adaptability.

## 7. Synthesise and deliver
Combine: e.g. an Air hand + sloping head line + strong Moon mount = imaginative
thinker. Give the overall picture first, then two or three specific observations,
then invite the person's response.

## Ethics
- Never predict death, illness, accidents or number of children. If you notice skin
  or nail changes, suggest they see a doctor without diagnosing.
- Frame tendencies, not fixed fate — lines change over years.
- Consumer-protection laws in some places require readings to be presented as
  entertainment; know yours.

## Gotchas
- Reading the life line as lifespan (the most common harmful myth).
- Starting with a single line before the hand's overall shape.
- Forgetting to check which hand is dominant.
- Mistaking deep flexion creases at the wrist (rascettes) for major lines.

## Verify
- [ ] Dominant hand identified; both hands compared.
- [ ] Hand type classified with measured palm/finger proportion.
- [ ] Heart, head, life and fate lines read in order with their features.
- [ ] Mounts and thumb noted and combined into a synthesis.
- [ ] No health, death or fixed-fate predictions made.
