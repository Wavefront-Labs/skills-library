# Ruling requests, APAs and voluntary disclosures

**Goal:** replace tax uncertainty with a binding or near-binding position — before the
transaction (ruling, clearance, APA) or after a mistake (voluntary disclosure, penalty
relief) — at acceptable cost. US federal procedure (checked 2026-10-24) is the model, with UK analogues;
procedures are reissued regularly (the IRS publishes its ruling procedures and user fees in the first
revenue procedure of each year), so work from the current version. Not tax advice.

## 1. Choose the tool

| Need | Tool (US) | UK analogue | Typical timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| Certainty on a planned transaction | Private letter ruling (PLR) | HMRC clearance (check gov.uk) | Varies |
| Ongoing transfer-pricing certainty | Advance pricing agreement (unilateral/bilateral/multilateral) | HMRC APA (check gov.uk) | Varies; bilateral takes longer |
| Double taxation already arising | Mutual agreement procedure (MAP) | MAP | Varies |
| Past non-compliance | Voluntary disclosure / streamlined procedures | HMRC Digital Disclosure Service | Varies |
| Penalties already assessed | Abatement request | Reasonable excuse appeal | Weeks to months |

## 2. Private ruling request
1. Check the authority will rule: consult the current **no-rule** list and whether the
   issue is factual (usually not ruled on).
2. Request a **pre-submission conference** for novel or complex issues; it surfaces
   concerns early and shapes the request.
3. Draft: complete facts (every relevant document attached), the precise rulings
   requested, the law and analysis including contrary authority, required statements
   (penalties of perjury, pending audits/litigation, disclosure deletions) and the user fee.
4. The ruling binds only for the taxpayer and only on the facts stated. Any change in
   facts before closing voids its protection — conform the deal documents to the ruling.
5. Withdraw if the authority signals an adverse ruling; note that withdrawn issues may be
   referred to examination.

## 3. Advance pricing agreements
- Decide unilateral vs **bilateral**: bilateral avoids double tax but takes longer.
- Pre-filing meeting; then a full submission: functional analysis, industry analysis,
  proposed transfer-pricing method and range, critical assumptions, proposed term and any request to cover earlier years.
- Critical assumptions should be wide enough to survive normal business change.
- Annual compliance reports during the term; a breach of critical assumptions reopens it.

## 4. Voluntary disclosure
- Act before the authority starts an enquiry or receives information about the taxpayer;
  pre-clearance or eligibility checks are typically required.
- Distinguish **non-wilful** (streamlined or standard amended-return routes) from
  **wilful** non-compliance (formal voluntary-disclosure programmes, in the US the IRS Criminal Investigation Voluntary
  Disclosure Practice, with full cooperation).
- Prepare complete amended returns and pay or arrange payment of tax and interest.
- Never make a partial or inaccurate disclosure; it can be worse than none and may
  support criminal charges. Retain counsel for privilege where wilfulness is possible.

## 5. Penalty relief
- **Administrative waivers** (US first-time abatement: returns of the same type filed on time for the previous three years and no penalty, or any
  penalty removed, in that period) require little more than a request.
- **Reasonable cause:** serious illness, disaster, reliance on competent professional
  advice on full facts, inability to obtain records. Prove ordinary business care and
  timeline; attach evidence.
- Request in writing (or by phone for simple waivers) and appeal denials.

## Gotchas
- A ruling request educates the authority; if the answer is likely "no", the issue may
  be examined anyway.
- Ignoring the other country's APA programme timetable in bilateral cases.
- Voluntary disclosures that leave out a year or an account.
- Requesting reasonable-cause relief where the only reason is "the accountant forgot".

## Verify
- [ ] Current procedure (revenue procedure/HMRC guidance) cited and followed.
- [ ] No-rule and eligibility checks recorded.
- [ ] Facts in the ruling/APA match the executed documents and operations.
- [ ] Disclosure is complete across all years, accounts and taxes.
- [ ] Penalty-relief request supported by dated evidence.
