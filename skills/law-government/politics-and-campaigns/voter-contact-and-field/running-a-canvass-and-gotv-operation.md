# Running a canvass and GOTV operation

**Goal:** contact enough of the right voters, enough times, to identify supporters,
persuade the undecided and make sure every supporter actually votes. Models: US
precinct-based field programmes and UK doorstep canvassing and polling-day "knock-up".
Laws on canvassing hours, electioneering distance from polling places, automated
calls/texts (e.g. US TCPA rules) and data protection (e.g. UK GDPR for electoral
register use) differ — brief volunteers on the local rules.

## 1. Build the field plan from the vote goal
Start from the campaign's vote goal and target universes (see
law-government.politics-and-campaigns.campaign-management.writing-a-campaign-plan-and-win-number).
Work out the maths:
- Doors needed = target households x passes (often 2-3 for persuasion).
- Contact rate on doors is typically 20-35%; on phones lower. Plan attempts, not contacts.
- A volunteer canvasser covers roughly 15-25 doors an hour in dense areas (fewer in rural).
- Shifts needed = attempts / (doors per hour x hours per shift). Divide by weeks to set
  weekly volunteer-shift goals.

## 2. Recruit and keep volunteers
- Recruitment is continuous: ask every supporter you ID, every event attendee, every donor.
- Confirm every shift twice (48 hours and morning-of); flake rates of 30-50% for
  unconfirmed shifts are common.
- Train at each shift (15 minutes: script, app, safety); pair new canvassers with experienced
  ones; debrief and thank; ask for the next shift before they leave.
- Develop volunteer leaders who run their own staging locations — the only way to scale.

## 3. Scripts and voter ID
Door script outline: introduce (name, volunteer for X), ask the ID question, listen,
one persuasive point tailored to their concern, re-ask, record, leave literature.
Score each contact consistently, for example:
| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1 | strong supporter |
| 2 | leaning supporter |
| 3 | undecided |
| 4 | leaning opponent |
| 5 | strong opponent |
Also record: not home, moved, refused, wrong number, issue raised, volunteer interest,
needs a postal/absentee ballot. Accurate codes drive every later universe.

## 4. Data hygiene
- Enter results the same day (apps sync instantly; paper lists need data entry within 24 hours).
- Audit volunteers whose results look implausible (all 1s, impossible speed).
- Respect do-not-contact requests and remove deceased/moved records.

## 5. Early and postal vote chase
Where early or postal voting exists, a large share of votes is cast before election day.
Track who has requested and returned ballots (from official returned-ballot files where
available) and chase supporters who haven't returned theirs. Stop contacting people who
have already voted.

## 6. Election-day GOTV
- Staging locations staffed from opening, each with turf packets, a lead and a data runner.
- Contact supporters (1s and 2s) not yet recorded as voted: multiple passes through the day,
  prioritised by area, with reminders of polling location and hours.
- Rides to the polls and childcare offers where lawful and planned in advance.
- Polling-place observers or tellers only where permitted and trained in the rules.
- A hotline and lawyer on call for voter problems.

## 7. Track and adjust
Daily dashboard: shifts filled, attempts, contacts, IDs by code, persuasion results,
ballots returned. Move volunteers from over-covered to under-covered turf weekly.

## Gotchas
- Canvassing your opponents' strongholds because it's convenient.
- Inconsistent ID codes between volunteers, corrupting targeting.
- Electioneering inside restricted zones near polling places.
- Collecting or handling other people's ballots where that is illegal (ballot-collection
  rules vary widely by jurisdiction).

## Verify
- [ ] Attempt and shift goals derived from the vote goal and contact rates.
- [ ] Shift confirmation and volunteer training in place.
- [ ] Single ID scoring scheme used by everyone; data entered within 24 hours.
- [ ] Early/postal ballot chase lists updated from official files.
- [ ] Election-day staging, turf, legal hotline and local rules briefing ready.
