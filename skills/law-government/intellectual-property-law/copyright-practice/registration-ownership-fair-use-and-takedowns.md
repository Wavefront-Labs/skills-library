# Registration, ownership, fair use and takedowns

**Goal:** answer the four questions that recur in copyright counselling — is it
protected, who owns it, may we use it, and how do we get infringing copies taken down —
correctly for the jurisdiction. US rules are used as the worked frame because registration and fair use are distinctive
there. UK/EU/Commonwealth "fair dealing" works differently; check the local statute.

## 1. Is it protected?
- **Original** (independent creation with minimal creativity; EU: author's own
  intellectual creation) and **fixed** in a tangible medium (US requirement).
- Not protected: ideas, methods, systems, facts, short phrases and titles (generally),
  functional elements. Protection covers expression only.
- AI-generated material: the US Copyright Office issued registration guidance on 16 March
  2023 (88 Fed. Reg. 16,190) and published a report on the copyrightability of AI outputs
  on 29 January 2025. Read both and check current guidance and jurisdiction.
- Duration (US, 17 U.S.C. 302): life of the author plus 70 years; for anonymous,
  pseudonymous and work-made-for-hire works, 95 years from first publication or 120
  years from creation, whichever ends first.

## 2. Who owns it?
- Default: the author. Exceptions:
  - **Work made for hire** (US, 17 U.S.C. 101): a work prepared by an employee within
    the scope of employment, or a commissioned work in one of the nine listed categories
    where the parties expressly agree in a signed writing that it is a work made for
    hire. Add a fallback **assignment** to any contractor work-for-hire clause.
  - Other countries: ownership rules differ; check the local statute.
- Check formalities for assignments and licences in the governing law.
- **Joint authorship** and **moral rights** rules differ by country; check the local law.

## 3. Registration strategy (US)
- Registration (or preregistration) is generally required before suing for infringement
  of a US work (17 U.S.C. 411(a), with limited exceptions such as refusal of
  registration).
- Statutory damages and attorney's fees are unavailable for infringement of a published
  work that began after first publication and before registration unless registration
  is made within 3 months after first publication, and for infringement of an
  unpublished work that began before registration (17 U.S.C. 412).
- Register key works early; check the Copyright Office for group registration options and
  deposit rules.

## 4. Fair use analysis (US, four factors, weighed together)
1. **Purpose and character** — commercial? transformative? In *Andy Warhol Foundation
   v. Goldsmith* (decided 18 May 2023) the Court held this factor favoured the
   photographer where the licensing use shared substantially the same purpose as the
   original and was commercial.
2. **Nature of the work.**
3. **Amount and substantiality.**
4. **Market effect** on the actual or potential market for the original.
Write the analysis factor-by-factor with facts; give a risk grade, not a guarantee.
**Fair dealing** jurisdictions: first fit the use into a listed purpose (criticism/review,
quotation, news reporting, research/private study, parody/pastiche, etc.), then test
fairness.

## 5. Takedowns (US DMCA §512 model)
- **Notice** (17 U.S.C. 512(c)(3)): a written notice to the provider's designated agent
  with a signature of a person authorised to act for the owner, identification of the
  work, identification of the material and its location, contact details, a good-faith
  belief statement, and a statement of accuracy and, under penalty of perjury, of
  authority to act.
- Consider fair use before sending — knowingly misrepresenting infringement creates
  liability.
- **Counter-notice**: the user can respond; the provider restores the material not less
  than 10 nor more than 14 business days after receiving it unless the complainant
  notifies the agent that it has filed a court action (17 U.S.C. 512(g)(2)(C)).
- Other regimes (EU, UK and platform procedures) differ.

## Gotchas
- In the US, a proper copyright notice on published copies means no weight is given to
  an innocent infringement defence in mitigation of damages (17 U.S.C. 401(d)).
- Licence scope: media, territory, term and exclusivity must be explicit.
- Trademark, publicity rights and contract terms may restrict uses copyright allows.
