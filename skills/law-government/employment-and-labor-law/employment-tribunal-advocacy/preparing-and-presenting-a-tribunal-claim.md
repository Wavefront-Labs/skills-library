# Preparing and presenting a tribunal claim

**Goal:** win (or settle well) before a specialist employment forum where procedure is
lighter than court, but deadlines, pleadings and the list of issues still decide cases.
Examples here follow the Great Britain employment tribunal; equivalents exist in
labour boards and commissions elsewhere (e.g. Australia's Fair Work Commission,
Canadian labour relations boards).

## 1. Before the claim
- **Early conciliation**: an ACAS certificate is required before most ET claims.
  Limitation was 3 months less a day for most claims and is paused/extended by conciliation (the ACAS stop-the-clock rules are (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked)) — compute it exactly. Employment Rights Act 2025 s 152 raises the time limit for making tribunal claims from three months to six months; it is in force from 1 October 2026 (SI 2026/954 reg 3), but does not apply where the relevant date (generally the date of the act complained of) is before 1 October 2026 (reg 4(1)) — all confirmed on legislation.gov.uk. Check which limit applies to each claim.
- Settlement through ACAS produces a binding **COT3** agreement (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked); private settlements
  need a compliant settlement agreement.

## 2. Pleadings
- **ET1 (claim)**: identify each claim and its legal basis (e.g. unfair dismissal
  s.98 ERA; direct discrimination s.13 EqA (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked)), with key facts, dates, comparators and
  protected acts. Vague pleadings invite amendment fights and strike-out applications.
- **ET3 (response)**: due within 28 days of the date the claim form was sent by the Tribunal (Employment Tribunal Procedure Rules 2024, rule 17, confirmed); late response risks a
  default judgment. Plead the reason for dismissal, the process, and all defences
  (time bar, justification, reasonable steps).

## 3. Case management
- Preliminary hearing (case management): agree a **list of issues** — the single most
  important document; the hearing will be confined to it.
- Orders: disclosure of documents, bundle, witness statements, schedule of loss,
  dates. Comply exactly; non-compliance can lead to unless orders and strike-out.
- Applications: strike-out (no reasonable prospect), deposit orders (little
  reasonable prospect), amendment, specific disclosure, anonymity.
- Judicial mediation may be offered in discrimination cases.

## 4. Evidence
- **Bundle**: agreed, chronological, paginated, indexed; only relevant documents.
- **Witness statements**: in the witness's own words, chronological, numbered
  paragraphs, cross-referenced to bundle pages; they usually stand as evidence-in-chief.
- **Schedule of loss**: basic award, compensatory loss (past and future earnings,
  pension, benefits), injury to feelings (Vento bands, updated annually (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked)), mitigation
  of loss evidence, ACAS uplift, interest, grossing up. Respondent files a counter-schedule.

## 5. The hearing
- Order of evidence usually depends on who bears the burden (in unfair dismissal
  the employer typically goes first on the reason).
- Cross-examination on the list of issues; tribunals limit time, so prioritise.
- Unrepresented parties are common; the tribunal will assist them — don't treat that as bias.
- Closing submissions in writing for longer cases: issue by issue, findings sought,
  law with key authorities.
- Remedy may be decided at the same hearing or a separate remedy hearing.

## 6. Costs and appeals
Costs are the exception in ETs (unreasonable conduct, no reasonable prospects,
breach of orders); costs-warning letters set up applications. Appeal to the EAT on
points of law only, within 42 days of written reasons (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked).

## Gotchas
- A claim not in the ET1 or list of issues generally can't be argued at the hearing.
- Request written reasons promptly if you may appeal.
- Uncertain: rules were updated in 2024 and compensation caps change annually; check
  current rules and figures for your forum.

## Verify
- [ ] Limitation date computed including conciliation extension.
- [ ] Each claim pleaded with legal basis; ET3 lodged in 28 days.
- [ ] List of issues agreed and mirrored in witness statements.
- [ ] Bundle and statements cross-referenced; orders complied with.
- [ ] Schedule of loss evidenced, including mitigation.
