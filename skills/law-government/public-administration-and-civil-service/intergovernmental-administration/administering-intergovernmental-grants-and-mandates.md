# Administering intergovernmental grants and mandates

**Goal:** move funds and obligations between levels of government (national → state/
region → local) so the money is spent on what it was for, the lower level can actually
comply, and audits come back clean. Models: US federal grants under the Uniform Guidance
(2 CFR 200) and UK central-to-local grant practice (Managing Public Money, the grants
functional standard). Thresholds and rules are revised periodically (e.g. the US
single-audit threshold was raised in 2024 — check the current figure); always work from
the award's own terms.

## 1. Set up the award (pass-through role)
1. Read the source award: purpose, authorised activities, period of performance,
   match/cost-share, reporting, flow-down clauses (procurement, civil rights, data).
2. Determine the relationship: **subrecipient** (carries out part of the programme,
   subject to compliance) vs **contractor/vendor** (provides goods/services). It decides
   which rules flow down.
3. **Pre-award risk assessment** of each recipient: prior audit findings, financial
   systems, staff turnover, experience with similar awards. Higher risk → specific
   conditions (reimbursement-only, more frequent reporting, prior approvals).
4. Subaward or grant agreement must state: identifying award information, amount,
   period, scope and outcomes, all flow-down terms, reporting schedule, indirect-cost
   rate, closeout and clawback rights.

## 2. Payments and allowable costs
- Pay on reimbursement or tightly managed advances; minimise time between drawdown
  and spending.
- Test costs against the cost principles: necessary, reasonable, allocable, consistently
  treated, adequately documented, within the period of performance and not otherwise
  prohibited.
- Budget changes beyond set tolerances need prior approval; document every approval.

## 3. Monitoring
| Tool | Frequency (typical) | Looks for |
|---|---|---|
| Financial reports (e.g. SF-425-style) | Quarterly | Spend vs budget, match, programme income |
| Performance reports | Quarterly / semi-annual | Outputs and outcomes vs plan |
| Desk review | Per risk tier | Sample invoices, payroll, procurement files |
| Site visit | Annual for high risk | Physical verification, systems |
| Audit follow-up | After audit reports | Findings, corrective action plans |
Issue management decisions on audit findings within required deadlines; track
corrective action to closure.

## 4. Mandates and compliance reporting
- Maintain a **mandates register**: each requirement imposed on the lower level, its
  legal source, deadlines, reporting format and funding attached (if any).
- Before imposing a new mandate: estimate the cost to the lower level (in the US,
  UMRA requires cost estimates for significant unfunded federal mandates; many states have
  their own fiscal-note rules), consult representative bodies, and phase timelines.
- Consolidate reporting requests; ask for data once and share it across programmes.

## 5. Shared programmes and agreements
- Intergovernmental agreements/MOUs: roles, decision rights, funding formula, data
  sharing (legal basis, security), performance measures, dispute resolution and exit.
- A joint governance group with a named lead at each level; a shared risk register.

## 6. Closeout
Final financial and performance reports, reconciliation of advances, disposition of
equipment, recovery of unspent or disallowed funds, record retention for the required
period (often several years after closeout — check the award).

## Gotchas
- Misclassifying a subrecipient as a vendor — monitoring obligations are missed.
- Allowing spend after the period of performance ends.
- Mandates issued without funding or realistic timelines; compliance collapses quietly.
- Monitoring that checks paperwork but never outcomes.

## Verify
- [ ] Source award terms flowed down; relationship classification recorded.
- [ ] Risk assessment done; specific conditions match risk tier.
- [ ] Costs tested against cost principles; approvals documented.
- [ ] Monitoring schedule followed; audit findings resolved.
- [ ] Mandates register current with cost estimates; closeout complete.
