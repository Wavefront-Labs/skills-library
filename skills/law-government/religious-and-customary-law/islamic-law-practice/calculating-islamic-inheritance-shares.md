# Calculating Islamic inheritance shares

**Goal:** produce a correct, explainable distribution table for a Muslim estate under
classical Sunni rules (majority positions shown; Hanafi variants noted), and know when
state personal-status law or Shia (Ja'fari) rules change the answer. Many states
codify their own version — Egypt, Pakistan, Malaysia, the Gulf states and others
differ in detail — and in secular jurisdictions the distribution only binds if made by
a valid will or heirs' agreement. Have a qualified scholar or local counsel confirm any
real distribution.

## 1. Fix the net estate (in this order)
1. Funeral and burial costs (reasonable).
2. Debts, including unpaid dower (*mahr*) owed to a wife.
3. Bequests (*wasiyya*): max **one-third** of what remains, and (majority view) not to
   a legal heir unless the other heirs consent.
4. The balance is the inheritance (*tarika*) for heirs.

## 2. Identify who inherits
- Exclude the disqualified: an heir who unlawfully killed the deceased; difference of
  religion between Muslim and non-Muslim (majority view). Rules on apostasy, adoption
  (no inheritance through adoption) and children born outside marriage vary.
- Apply exclusion (*hajb*): nearer heirs block farther ones (a son blocks grandsons and
  siblings; father blocks siblings and grandfather; any child or son's child blocks
  uterine siblings).

## 3. Assign fixed shares (*ashab al-furud*)
| Heir | Share | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Husband | 1/2 · 1/4 | no child · with a child or son's child |
| Wife (all wives share) | 1/4 · 1/8 | no child · with a child |
| Daughter | 1/2 · 2/3 (shared) | alone · two or more; with a son she becomes residuary |
| Father | 1/6 (+ residue if no son) | there is a child |
| Mother | 1/3 · 1/6 | no child and fewer than two siblings · otherwise |
| Uterine brother/sister | 1/6 · 1/3 (shared equally) | one · two or more |
| Full sister | 1/2 · 2/3 | in absence of blocking heirs and a full brother |
| Grandmother | 1/6 | no mother |

## 4. Give the residue to residuaries (*'asaba*)
Order: son → son's son → father → grandfather → full brother → consanguine brother →
nephews → uncles. A son and daughter take the residue together at **2:1** male:female.

## 5. Adjust when the fractions don't add to 1
- **'Awl (over-subscription):** raise the common denominator to the sum of numerators;
  everyone abates proportionally.
- **Radd (surplus, no residuary):** return the surplus to fixed-share heirs pro rata —
  majority view excludes the spouse from radd.
- **'Umariyyatan:** spouse + father + mother only → mother takes one-third of what is
  left after the spouse's share, not one-third of the whole.

## Worked examples
**A. Wife, one son, one daughter.** Wife 1/8 = 3/24. Residue 21/24 split 2:1 →
son 14/24, daughter 7/24. Total 24/24.

**B. Husband, two full sisters, mother (no children).** Husband 1/2, sisters 2/3,
mother 1/6 (two siblings present) = 3/6 + 4/6 + 1/6 = 8/6 → 'awl to eighths:
husband 3/8, sisters 4/8 (2/8 each), mother 1/8.

Always present the result as a table: heir, basis, fraction, common-denominator
shares, monetary amount.

## 6. Variants and state law
- **Ja'fari (Twelver Shia):** heirs in three classes; no residuary preference for
  agnates; a daughter can exclude brothers; a wife's share in land is restricted in
  the classical view. Do not use the Sunni table.
- **Obligatory bequest (*wasiyya wajiba*) or representation** for orphaned grandchildren
  exists in several codes (e.g. Egypt; Pakistan's 1961 Ordinance) — check local law.
- In common-law countries, a will drafted to give Islamic shares must also meet
  local will formalities and may be subject to family-provision claims
  (see `law-government.religious-and-customary-law.state-religious-law-interface.recognition-of-religious-rulings-and-dual-compliant-drafting`).

## Gotchas
- Forgetting the mother drops to 1/6 when there are two or more siblings, even blocked ones.
- Treating the one-third bequest limit as applying before debts.
- Giving the spouse a radd share where the applicable code doesn't.
- Assets held jointly or with nominated beneficiaries may pass outside the estate.

## Verify
- [ ] Net estate computed after funeral, debts, dower and capped bequests.
- [ ] Disqualified and blocked heirs removed, with the reason recorded.
- [ ] Fractions sum to exactly 1 after 'awl or radd.
- [ ] School / state code identified and its variants applied.
- [ ] Result checked by a second calculation or qualified scholar.
