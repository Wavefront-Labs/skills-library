# Mortgage security and priority

**Goal:** the lender ends up with valid, perfected security, ranking where the deal says it
ranks, enforceable if the borrower defaults; the borrower's counsel ensures the terms are
workable.

Instruments differ: **legal charge** (England and Wales), **mortgage** or **deed of trust**
(US states differ, deed of trust with trustee's power of sale common in some), **hypothec**
(civil-law systems). Priority rules and enforcement procedures are local.

## 1. The document set
- **Facility / loan agreement**: amount, interest, covenants (LTV, DSCR/ICR), events of
  default, conditions precedent.
- **Note** (US): the debt instrument; separate from the security.
- **Security instrument:** mortgage/charge/deed of trust over the land, plus usually an
  assignment of rents and leases, security over bank accounts, fixtures filing, and
  guarantees.
- **Ancillary:** title insurance (lender's policy), valuation, insurance with lender named,
  legal opinions, board resolutions, environmental reports.

## 2. Conditions precedent
Don't release funds until each CP is satisfied and evidenced: title report clean or with
agreed exceptions; valuation addressed to lender; searches; insurance endorsements; signed
and witnessed security; corporate authority; KYC.

## 3. Perfection and registration
- Register/record the security at the land registry or recorder's office promptly. Unrecorded
  security may be good against the borrower but lose to later purchasers or creditors.
- Company borrowers may also need a filing at the company registry within a strict deadline
  (21 days beginning with the day after creation in England and Wales, Companies Act 2006 s.859A(4)), failing which the charge is void against a liquidator.
- Personal property and fixtures may need UCC or equivalent filings.

## 4. Priority
- Default rule is usually first registered/recorded. US recording acts vary: **race**,
  **notice** or **race-notice**.
- **Tacking** and **further advances** rules decide whether later advances under an existing
  charge keep its priority.
- **Subordination/priority agreements and intercreditor agreements** reorder priority by
  contract: who can enforce, standstill periods, payment waterfalls, cure rights, purchase
  options for junior lenders. Mezzanine lenders often hold security over shares in the
  property-owning entity rather than the land itself.
- Statutory super-priority claims (some taxes, some environmental or construction liens) can
  outrank you regardless.

## 5. Enforcement on default
Options depend on jurisdiction and documents: appoint a receiver (often over rents),
power of sale out of court, judicial foreclosure, taking possession. Consumer and
residential mortgages carry extensive protections (pre-action conduct, notices, mediation,
redemption rights, anti-deficiency rules). Get the notices right; enforcement defects are
expensive.

## Gotchas
- Missed company-registry filing deadline.
- Security granted by an entity that doesn't own the land (or only part of it).
- Leases granted after the mortgage without lender consent may or may not bind the lender;
  check.
- Intercreditor terms conflicting with the facility agreement.

## Verify
- [ ] All security documents executed correctly by the owning entity with authority.
- [ ] CPs evidenced before funding.
- [ ] Registrations/filings made and confirmed within deadlines.
- [ ] Priority confirmed by search results; intercreditor terms consistent.
- [ ] Enforcement route and required notices known in advance.
