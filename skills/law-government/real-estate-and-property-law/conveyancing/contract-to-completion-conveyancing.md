# Contract-to-completion conveyancing

**Goal:** move good title from seller to buyer, with the money, on the agreed day, and
get the buyer registered — with no surprises found after it is too late to walk away.
England & Wales (two-stage exchange then completion) is the main model; US closings
(escrow, title insurance, recording) and civil-law notarial systems are noted.
Searches, forms and tax rates are jurisdiction-specific — verify locally.

## 1. On instruction
- Identity, anti-money-laundering and source-of-funds checks on your client (and, for
  sellers, proof of ownership). Property fraud by impersonating sellers is common.
- Conflict check; engagement letter with fee and scope (leasehold, new-build, lender
  acting add work).
- Seller: obtain title documents, completed property information forms (E&W TA6/TA10,
  leasehold TA7), and any planning/building-control documents.

## 2. Pre-contract investigation (buyer)

| Check | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Official title copies and plan | Ownership, charges, restrictions, rights and covenants |
| Local authority search | Planning history, road adoption, enforcement notices |
| Drainage and water search | Connections and public sewers near the building |
| Environmental, mining, flood, other location-specific searches | Site risks |
| Physical survey (client's surveyor) | Condition; boundaries match plan |
| Replies to enquiries | Disputes, works, occupiers, guarantees |

Report to the client in writing, flagging every adverse item and its fix (indemnity
insurance, retention, price change, or walk away). US: the **title commitment** and survey
serve this role; object to title items within the contract's objection period.

## 3. Contract
- Parties, property description and plan, price, deposit (amount agreed between the parties),
  completion date, incorporated standard conditions and special conditions (vacant
  possession, fixtures and chattels, VAT, condition of property).
- Leasehold: landlord's consent, service charge information, ground rent.
- Buyer: mortgage offer received and checked against lender instructions; funds confirmed.

## 4. Exchange / signing
- E&W: exchange by telephone using the agreed Law Society formula; record time, who
  spoke, and the agreed completion date; deposit sent same day. Contract is then binding.
- US: contract binds on signing; contingencies (financing, inspection, title) run to
  stated dates — diary each.
- Civil-law systems: preliminary contract, then a notarial deed executed before a notary.

## 5. Pre-completion
- Draft and agree the **transfer deed** (E&W TR1 or equivalent); the buyer's priority
  search protects against intervening entries for a fixed window — complete inside it.
- Bankruptcy/insolvency searches against buyers who borrow.
- Completion statement: price, deposit, apportionments (rent, service charge, taxes),
  fees. Draw down mortgage funds in time for completion.

## 6. Completion and post-completion
1. Funds sent by bank transfer; seller's lawyer confirms receipt and releases keys and
   signed transfer; seller's mortgage redeemed with an undertaking to send the discharge.
2. File the land-transaction tax return and pay (E&W SDLT: return within a short statutory
   window, 14 days from the effective date per HMRC's SDLT manual SDLTM00070; check the separate rules in Scotland and Wales).
3. Apply to register the transfer and new charge before the priority period expires.
   US: record the deed and mortgage with the county recorder; issue the title policy.
4. Check the updated register; send copies to client and lender.

## Gotchas
- Last-minute changes to bank details by email — always verify by phone on a known number.
- Exchanging before the mortgage offer or survey is in.
- Missing the priority-search expiry, letting another entry take priority.
- Completion date falling before the seller's onward purchase can complete (chains).

## Verify
- [ ] ID/AML done for client; seller-impersonation risk considered.
- [ ] Every search and enquiry reply reported to the client in writing.
- [ ] Deposit, completion date and funds confirmed at exchange.
- [ ] Tax return filed and paid within the deadline.
- [ ] Registration complete; register checked and discharges confirmed.
