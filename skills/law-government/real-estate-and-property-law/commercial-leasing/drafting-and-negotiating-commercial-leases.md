# Drafting and negotiating commercial leases

**Goal:** produce a lease that records the commercial deal precisely and allocates the
long-term risks (cost, repair, exit, transfer) the way your client intends. England &
Wales and US practice are the models; statutory overlays (security of tenure, rent
controls on commercial premises in some places, registration of long leases) vary widely.

## 1. Start from agreed heads of terms
Check the heads/LOI cover: parties and guarantor, premises and measurement standard,
term and start date, rent and rent-free/fit-out allowances, review, service charge or
operating expenses, use, repair, alienation, breaks/options, security deposit, conditions
(consents, works). Flag anything missing before drafting — gaps become fights later.

## 2. Key clauses and the negotiating positions

| Clause | Landlord wants | Tenant wants |
|---|---|---|
| Rent review | Upward-only open-market review or fixed uplifts; wide assumptions | Upward/downward or capped index (CPI collar and cap); disregard tenant's improvements and goodwill |
| Outgoings | Full recovery (triple net / full service charge) | Caps, exclusions (capital improvements, structural defects, landlord's financing), audit rights |
| Repair | Full repairing and insuring (FRI) — tenant puts and keeps in repair | Obligation limited by a **schedule of condition**; no liability for inherent defects |
| Use | Narrow; landlord controls mix | Wide use clause (also helps rent review) |
| Alienation | Consent needed; conditions to assignment (guarantees, financial tests) | Consent "not to be unreasonably withheld"; intra-group transfers free |
| Breaks | Strict conditions (rent paid, vacant possession, no breach) | Only payment of rent as condition; clear notice mechanics |
| Insurance/damage | Tenant pays premium | Rent suspension; mutual termination if not reinstated within a set time |

## 3. Drafting points
- Define premises precisely (plan, inclusion/exclusion of structure, roof, windows,
  services) — this drives repair and service-charge liability.
- **Notices:** addresses, methods, deemed service; breaks and reviews often turn on them.
- **Alienation:** in E&W, a landlord can require an outgoing tenant to give an
  **authorised guarantee agreement** on assignment only if the lease permits it (check
  statutory framework for post-1995 leases).
- **Security of tenure (E&W):** business tenants may have a statutory right to renew;
  to exclude it, complete the contracting-out procedure (warning notice and declaration)
  **before** the tenant is bound. US leases rely on express renewal options.
- **Financing documents (US):** subordination, non-disturbance and attornment
  agreements (SNDA) and estoppel certificates — set response deadlines in the lease.
- Holdover: rate and status if tenant stays beyond expiry.
- Guarantee/deposit: amount, top-up, release conditions.

## 4. Process
1. Draft from a house precedent tuned to the deal; mark-ups exchanged with a running
   issues list (clause, issue, each side's position, status).
2. Escalate only commercial points to clients; settle legal points between lawyers.
3. Pre-completion: consents (superior landlord, lender), searches, plans, contracting-out
   steps, tax (SDLT/transfer tax on lease premium or rent), registration if required.
4. Completion: engrossments signed, deposit paid, rent start and rent-free periods diarised.

## 5. Lease management diary
Record: review dates and trigger notices, break dates and notice windows, expiry, renewal
option windows, insurance renewal, service charge reconciliation and audit deadlines.

## Gotchas
- Break clause conditions requiring "full compliance with covenants" — almost
  impossible to satisfy; tenant should resist.
- Rent-free periods that reappear in rent review assumptions.
- Plans and descriptions that make the tenant liable for the roof or structure unintentionally.
- Missing the contracting-out timing, giving an unintended renewal right.

## Verify
- [ ] Every heads-of-terms item reflected in the lease.
- [ ] Premises definition matches repair and service charge provisions.
- [ ] Review, break and renewal mechanics including notices tested against a timeline.
- [ ] Statutory formalities (contracting out, registration, tax) complete.
- [ ] Key dates diarised for the client.
