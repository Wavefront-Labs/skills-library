# Setting up MIG and stick for fillet welds in position

**Goal:** sound fillet welds on 3–10 mm mild steel with full root fusion and equal
legs, in any position a one-off fabrication puts you in.

## 1. Joint preparation
- Clean to bright metal 25 mm each side: mill scale, rust, paint and galvanising
  all cause porosity and lack of fusion.
- **T and lap fillets:** tight fit-up (gap ≤ 1 mm). Leg size ≈ thickness of the
  thinner plate is a common default for full-strength craft work.
- **Butt joints over ~5 mm:** bevel 30–35° each side (60–70° included), 1.5–2 mm
  root face, 1.5–3 mm root gap.
- Tack each end and every 50–100 mm; tacks small and fused into the final weld.

## 2. MIG (GMAW) set-up for steel
- **Wire:** ER70S-6, 0.8 mm for 1–5 mm work, 0.9–1.0 mm for thicker.
- **Gas:** 75/25 argon/CO₂ (C25) or 80/20, 10–15 L/min; pure CO₂ penetrates deeper but
  spatters more. Flux-cored (gasless, FCAW-S) outdoors in wind — reverse polarity
  to DCEN as the wire maker states.
- **Polarity:** DCEP for solid wire.
- **Settings:** set voltage for the thickness from the door chart, then tune wire
  speed by ear: a steady **"frying bacon"** crackle is right; popping/stubbing =
  too much wire or too little voltage; long hissing arc with globs = too little wire.
  Rough starting point for 6 mm fillet with 0.8 mm wire: ~19–20 V, wire 7–8 m/min
  (indicative only; check your machine's chart).
- **Stickout:** 10–15 mm contact-tip-to-work.
- **Angles:** work angle 45° into a T-joint; travel angle 10–15°, pushing for
  flatter, wider beads on thin material, dragging slightly for more penetration.

## 3. Stick (SMAW) set-up
- **E6013:** easy, soft arc, light fabrication and poor fit-up, modest penetration.
- **E7018 (low-hydrogen):** structural strength and crack resistance; keep rods dry —
  store in a rod oven (~120 °C holding; check the manufacturer's figures) once
  opened, or they cause porosity and hydrogen cracking.
- **E6011:** digs through rust and paint, deep penetration, AC capable.
- **Current:** roughly 30–40 A per mm of core diameter: 2.5 mm rod 70–100 A,
  3.2 mm 90–140 A. DCEP for 7018 on most DC machines.
- **Arc length** ≈ the core diameter; drag angle 10–20°.

## 4. Positions
- **Flat (1F) and horizontal (2F):** straight stringer beads; in 2F aim slightly
  more at the bottom plate to stop the top toe undercutting and the puddle sagging.
- **Vertical-up (3F):** turn amps down 10–15%. MIG or 7018: small side-to-side
  triangle or "Christmas tree" weave, pausing at the toes, moving quickly across the
  middle; torch/rod pointing slightly up (5–10°). Vertical-down only for thin sheet —
  it lacks fusion on thicker work.
- **Overhead (4F):** lowest amps that still fuse, short arc, fast small stringers;
  dress for spatter (cap, leathers, ear plugs).

## 5. Faults
- **Porosity:** gas flow, draughts, dirty steel, damp 7018.
- **Cold lap / lack of fusion:** too fast, too little voltage, or wrong angle.
- **Undercut:** too hot or too long an arc; pause at the toes.
- **Slag inclusions (stick):** chip and brush between passes; weave too wide.
- **Unequal legs:** wrong work angle or gravity in 2F.

## Safety
Helmet shade 10–13; flame-resistant cotton or leathers, no synthetics. Fume
extraction — mild steel fume is a recognised carcinogen in some jurisdictions
(e.g. UK HSE guidance). **Never weld galvanised or painted steel without removing the
coating and extracting fume.** Remove combustibles 10 m around; keep a fire watch for
30 minutes after hot work. Dry gloves only for stick — wet gloves or a damaged
electrode holder risk shock.

## Verify
- [ ] Joint clean; fit-up and bevel as planned; tacks fused.
- [ ] Machine settings tested on scrap of the same thickness and position.
- [ ] Legs equal and to size (fillet gauge); no undercut, porosity or cold lap.
- [ ] Break or fillet-break test on a scrap coupon shows root fusion.
- [ ] Fire watch done; area clear.
