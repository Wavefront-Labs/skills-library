# Building and trimming a balsa RC aircraft

**Goal:** an RC plane that is straight, light, correctly balanced and set up so
it flies predictably on its first flight. Most crashes on maiden flights trace back
to warps, a tail-heavy CG or reversed controls. Draft: common practice for electric
balsa kits; follow the kit plan's numbers.

## 1. Building board and plan
- A **flat, pinnable building board** (check with a straight edge). Pin the plan
  down under clear film (so glue doesn't stick).
- Sort and label wood; choose lighter balsa for tail parts (tail weight needs
  nose weight to balance — compounding).

## 2. Structure
- **Glues:** thin CA for wicking into tight joints; aliphatic (yellow) wood glue for
  stronger, slower joints; **epoxy** for high-stress joints (firewall, wing joiners,
  landing gear mounts).
- Build wings **pinned flat** (or on jig tabs/wash-out shims if the plan specifies
  washout) so they set without twist. Warps cause rolls and stalls.
- **Dihedral:** set with the plan's specified block under the tip when joining wing
  halves. Mismatched halves make the model roll.
- Tail surfaces square to the fuselage and to each other; check from behind.

## 3. Covering
- **Heat-shrink film** (Oracover/MonoKote-type) applied with a covering iron at the
  film's recommended temperature: tack edges, then shrink from the middle out with
  iron or heat gun. Wrinkles shrink out; overheating sags it.
- Shrinking can pull a wing into a warp — check straightness while covering;
  heat and twist gently to correct.
- Seal edges with overlap; seams facing backwards (airflow can't lift them).

## 4. Radio and power
- Servos mounted firmly; **linkages** without slop or binding: pushrods with
  clevises on control horns, servo arm at 90° to the pushrod at neutral.
- Electric: brushless motor, **ESC** sized for the current, LiPo battery; the
  battery's position is your main CG adjuster.
- Receiver antennas away from carbon and power wires.

## 5. Setup checks
- **Control direction:** right stick → right aileron goes **up**; up elevator →
  elevator goes up (trailing edge up); right rudder → trailing edge right. Reversed
  controls crash immediately. Check twice.
- **Throws:** measure deflection (mm or degrees) to the kit's figures; set low rates
  for the first flight, with some **expo** to soften centre stick.
- **Failsafe:** set so motor goes to idle/off on signal loss.
- **Range check** per the radio's manual.

## 6. Centre of gravity
- Balance at the plan's CG point (often a range around 25-33% of the wing chord
  from the leading edge for many trainers) with the battery installed.
- **Nose-heavy flies; tail-heavy crashes.** If in doubt, start at the forward end.
- Also check lateral balance (wingtip to wingtip).

## 7. Maiden flight
- Calm day, experienced pilot or instructor if new (buddy-box). Climb to height,
  trim for straight-and-level, check stall behaviour high up.
- Follow local regulations — many countries require registration, an operator ID or
  a test for model aircraft above a weight threshold, and limit altitude and
  proximity to people and airports. Check current rules where you fly.

## Verify
- [ ] Wing and tail sighted for warps; dihedral to plan.
- [ ] Controls move the correct direction; throws to spec; failsafe set.
- [ ] CG at the plan point with flight battery; laterally balanced.
- [ ] Range check passed; flying site and registration legal.
