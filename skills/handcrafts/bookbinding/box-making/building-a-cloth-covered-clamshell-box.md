# Building a cloth-covered clamshell box

**Goal:** a drop-spine clamshell that fits one book snugly without squeezing it,
with trays that nest without rubbing and a case that closes flat. The geometry
also applies to slipcases (one tray, open at one end) and portfolios.

## 1. Measure the book
- Measure **height, width (fore-edge to spine) and thickness** at the largest point.
  Measure with calipers or a measuring jig; books are rarely square, so take the max.
- Add clearance: about **1–2 mm** to height and width, and 1–2 mm to depth for the
  inner tray. Allow more for books with clasps or bosses.

## 2. Materials
- **Binder's board** (2–2.5 mm for most books; acid-free greyboard for
  conservation work); grain head to tail on all walls and case boards.
- **Book cloth** with a paper backing; PVA (pure, or mixed with methyl cellulose
  for more open time).
- Accurate cutting is everything: use a board shear or a mat cutter and a steel
  square; mark every piece with its role.

## 3. Inner tray
- **Base**: book height + clearance by book width + clearance.
- **Walls** (head, tail, fore-edge — no spine wall): height = book thickness +
  clearance. Head and tail walls run the full base length; the fore-edge wall sits
  between them, so its length = base height + 2 × board thickness (because the
  walls sit **on the outside of** the base edges, glued to them).
- Glue walls to the base edges with PVA, check square with a set square, weight and
  dry. Cover the tray in a single strip of cloth going around the outside walls and
  turning over the top edge and in; cover the base separately.

## 4. Outer tray
- Must just slide over the covered inner tray. Measure the **covered** inner tray
  (outside dimensions) and add ~1 mm. Walls = inner wall height + board thickness
  + ~1 mm (so it overlaps the inner tray's base when closed).
- Build and cover the same way.

## 5. Case
- **Case boards**: each 3 mm larger than its tray's base in height (squares of
  ~1.5 mm at head and tail) and flush to slightly proud at fore-edge.
- **Spine piece**: height as the boards, width = outer tray depth + 2 × board
  thickness.
- Lay out on glued cloth with gaps (**joints**) between boards and spine of about
  2 × board thickness, so the box hinges without strain. Turn in 15 mm. Line the
  inside with a single cloth or paper piece across the joints.
- Optionally add a spine label (blocked or printed) before assembly.

## 6. Assembly
1. Lay the case open, inside up. Glue the base of the **outer tray** and position it
   on one board, its back wall tight to the spine joint. Weight.
2. Glue the base of the **inner tray** and position on the other board,
   mirroring. Close the box with the book inside and check — then open, weight
   and dry overnight.

## Gotchas
- **Box won't close / trays rub**: outer tray too small after cloth thickness; always
  measure the covered inner tray before cutting the outer.
- **Warped case**: grain wrong or no lining on the inside to balance the cloth.
- **Book rattles**: too much clearance; pad with a board or an acid-free foam
  insert rather than rebuilding.
- **Lumpy corners**: trim cloth at the corners leaving a small tab to tuck; don't
  double over.
- Conservation boxes: use archival materials only; PVA must be pH-neutral.

## Verify
- [ ] Book drops in and lifts out without force, and doesn't shift when tilted.
- [ ] Inner tray slides into the outer tray without scraping the cloth.
- [ ] Case closes flat with no gap at the fore-edge and no spring open.
- [ ] All corners and walls square; cloth tight with no bubbles.
- [ ] Box sits flat on a table, no warp after 48 hours.
