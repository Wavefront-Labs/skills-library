# Pouring pillar and moulded candles

**Goal:** a free-standing candle with smooth sides, a flat base and a straight
centred wick, that burns without slumping. Pillar candles need harder wax than
containers and must shrink to release. Draft: general practice; check wax supplier
pour temperatures.

## 1. Wax
- **Pillar paraffin** (higher melt point) with ~10% **stearic acid / stearin** added is
  classic: harder, more opaque, better release.
- **Beeswax:** releases well from silicone, burns long; shrinks a lot.
- **Palm wax:** gives feathered/crystal surface patterns; sourcing concerns.
- Soy alone is too soft for most pillars; use a soy pillar blend.

## 2. Moulds
- **Metal (aluminium):** durable, cool quickly, glossy sides. Need a wicking hole and
  sealer.
- **Polycarbonate:** very glossy finish; crack if poured too hot or shocked.
- **Silicone:** flexible, for complex shapes and beeswax; slight matte finish; no
  release needed.
- Release spray (silicone release) helps metal and polycarbonate; clean moulds
  between pours — residue mars the surface.

## 3. Wick
- **Square braid** cotton wicks, sized for pillar diameter and wax (beeswax needs larger).
  Pre-wax (prime) the wick.
- Thread through the bottom hole, seal outside with **mould sealer** putty, and hold
  centred and taut at the top with a wick bar. The bottom of the mould = top of
  candle.

## 4. Pour
- Pour hotter than containers — many pillar waxes ~80-90 °C for metal moulds
  (cooler for polycarbonate; follow maker). Preheat metal moulds slightly for
  smoother sides; a cold mould gives "jump lines" and frosting.
- Tap the mould to release air bubbles. Some use a water bath to cool evenly and
  speed release.

## 5. Repour
- As wax cools it **shrinks** and forms a cavity around the wick. Poke holes around
  the wick as it sets (relieves trapped air and voids), then **repour** with wax a
  few degrees hotter, not over the original fill line (it seeps down the sides and
  shows).
- Possibly a second repour for large pillars.

## 6. Release and finish
- Fully cool (hours; large pillars overnight). Remove sealer, slide out (light tap,
  or a short spell in the fridge — not freezer, it can crack).
- Level the base on a warm metal plate or pan. Trim the wick.
- Options: **layered** pours at angles (tilt mould between pours, each layer set
  but warm), **overdipping** in coloured wax for colour coat, **carving** (dip in
  coloured layers then cut and curl through them, done warm and fast).

## Gotchas
- Pits and bubbles: pour too cool or mould too cold.
- Wick off-centre: wick bar slipped during set; check while cooling.
- Slumping or dripping in use: wax too soft for the diameter, or wick too big.
- Mottling (snowflake patterns) is from oil content in paraffin — either a fault or a style.

## Verify
- [ ] Pillar-grade wax chosen; wick primed and centred taut.
- [ ] Mould clean, sealed, and at sensible temperature.
- [ ] Relief holes and repour done; no hollow around the wick.
- [ ] Released cleanly; base level; burn test shows no slumping.
