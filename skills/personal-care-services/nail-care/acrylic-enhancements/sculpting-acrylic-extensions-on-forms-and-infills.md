# Sculpting acrylic extensions on forms, and infills

**Goal:** build a durable, thin-looking acrylic extension with the strength in the right
place (the apex over the stress point), no lifting at the cuticle, and keep it balanced
with infills — using safe, legal products with good ventilation.

## 1. Products and safety
- **Liquid (monomer)** — use **EMA** (ethyl methacrylate)-based products. **MMA** (methyl
  methacrylate) liquid is banned or restricted for nail use in many US states and elsewhere:
  it bonds too hard, damages natural nails, and smells strongly. Cheap salons using it is a
  known problem.
- **Powder (polymer)** — clear, pink, white, cover shades.
- Primer (acid-free or acid-based), dehydrator, brush (sable or synthetic acrylic brush,
  size 8–12), dappen dish with lid.
- **Ventilation:** local extraction at the table (source-capture), lidded dappen dish,
  dispose of used liquid by letting it harden with powder rather than pouring it away.
  Gloves don't fully protect against monomer — avoid skin contact with uncured product;
  **overexposure causes allergic contact dermatitis** in techs and clients.

## 2. Prep (where lifting starts or is prevented)
1. Sanitise hands; push back the cuticle; remove the dead cuticle tissue from the nail
   plate (any left under product = lifting).
2. Lightly remove shine with a 180-grit file — **don't thin the nail**.
3. Dust, then dehydrator, then primer **only on the natural nail**, sparingly. Keep it off
   skin.

## 3. Fitting the form
Slide the form under the free edge so it sits snugly with no gap and continues the natural
curve of the nail; check from the side (level, not angled down) and down the finger
(centred, straight). A bad form fit = crooked nail.

## 4. Mix ratio and placement
- Dip the brush in liquid, wipe one side, touch it to powder and draw a **bead**. Aim for
  a **medium-wet** bead (often described as about 1.5 parts liquid to 1 part powder): it
  holds shape, settles slowly, looks satin. Too wet → runny, weak, lifts, more allergy
  risk; too dry → grainy, porous, won't self-level.
- **Three-zone method:**
  1. **Zone 1 (free edge):** first bead on the form to make the extension length; shape
     the smile line (white tip if pink-and-white).
  2. **Zone 2 (stress area / apex):** second bead in the centre; this builds the **apex** —
     the highest point, roughly a third of the way back from the free edge where the nail
     leaves the finger.
  3. **Zone 3 (cuticle area):** a small, wetter bead, pressed thin so it tapers flush to
     the nail — never touching skin or the cuticle (leave a hair's gap).
- **Pinch** the C-curve with tweezers or fingers as it starts to set for a stronger arch
  (only if your system allows — many techs pinch lightly or not at all).

## 5. Filing and finishing
Remove the form once set (it should "click" when tapped). File with a medium (~150–180 grit)
file or e-file: shape sidewalls straight, the free edge thin, the cuticle area flush. Check
the **side profile**: a smooth curve rising to the apex and thinning towards the tip and
cuticle. Buff, cleanse, then top coat or gel top coat.

## 6. Infills (every 2–3 weeks)
1. Check for **lifting** — any lifted product must be filed away completely (don't seal
   over it; trapped moisture invites infection).
2. File down the old product, thin the regrowth line; reduce length and rebalance — the
   apex has moved forward with growth, so rebuild it back over the stress point.
3. Prep the new growth as for a fresh set; apply a zone-3 bead to the regrowth area and
   blend into old product.

## Gotchas
- Lifting near the cuticle: poor prep, product on skin, too-wet beads, oily nail plate.
- Breaking at the stress point: apex too thin or too far forward.
- Heat spikes are a gel issue mostly, but some fast-set acrylics also warm up.
- Don't use a drill on the natural nail without training (see electric-filing).
- Green spots under product = possible bacterial infection — remove, refer, no reapplication.

## Verify
- [ ] EMA-based liquid; extraction running; lidded dappen.
- [ ] Prep thorough: no cuticle tissue or shine left; primer only on nail.
- [ ] Form fit level and straight; product never touched skin.
- [ ] Side profile shows apex over the stress point; tip and cuticle area thin.
- [ ] Infills remove all lifted product and rebuild the apex.
