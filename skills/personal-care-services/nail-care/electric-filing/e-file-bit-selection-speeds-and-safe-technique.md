# E-file bit selection, speeds and safe technique

**Goal:** use an electric file to prep, shape, backfill and remove enhancements faster
and more precisely than by hand, without burning clients, cutting grooves ("rings of
fire") into natural nails, or damaging the cuticle area. E-files are powerful and
unforgiving — train on practice tips before clients, and check whether your regulator
restricts their use on natural nails.

## 1. The machine
- **Professional e-file**, not a craft or hardware rotary tool: smooth, low-vibration
  handpiece, variable speed up to ~25,000–35,000 RPM, forward/reverse switch.
- Handpieces hold standard 3/32-inch shank bits. Insert fully and lock; a loose bit wobbles
  and causes damage.
- Use **dust extraction** at the table — fine enhancement dust is a respiratory hazard.
  Wear a mask suitable for fine dust and eye protection.

## 2. Bits and what they're for
| Bit | Use | Notes |
|-----|-----|------|
| **Carbide** (fine to extra-coarse; barrel, cone, "UNC") | Removing/shaping acrylic and hard gel | Cuts rather than abrades; runs cool; *not* for natural nail |
| **Diamond** (fine grits) | Cuticle work, natural nail cleanup, detailing | Abrasive; gets hot faster |
| **Ceramic** | Product removal, shaping | Runs cooler than metal, smooth |
| **Sanding bands** on mandrel | Shaping, surface | **Single-use** (can't be disinfected) |
| **Safety bit** (rounded top) | Working near sidewalls/cuticle | Reduces risk of cutting skin |
Colour codes for grit (e.g. red fine, blue medium, green coarse) are **not standardised
across brands** — read the manufacturer's chart.

## 3. Speeds (starting ranges; follow the manufacturer)
- **Cuticle / natural nail prep:** low — roughly **5,000–10,000 RPM**.
- **Gel removal / surface refinement:** ~**10,000–15,000 RPM**.
- **Acrylic shaping and removal:** ~**15,000–25,000 RPM**.
Too slow and the bit grabs and jumps; too fast with pressure and it heats. Let the bit do
the work.

## 4. Handling technique
- Hold the handpiece like a pencil, rest your little finger on the client's hand for stability.
- **Bit flat to the surface**, light pressure, keep moving side to side across the nail.
  Holding still or tilting the bit's edge into the surface cuts **rings of fire** (grooves in
  the natural nail).
- **Direction:** forward (clockwise viewed from the back of the handpiece) for most
  right-handed work; switch to reverse to work the opposite side of the nail. Left-handed
  techs often reverse the convention. The goal is the bit rotating away from the cuticle
  and sidewalls.
- **Heat:** if the client feels heat, lift off immediately. Never work the same spot for long.
- Backfills/infills: use a carbide bit to thin regrowth line and cut a pocket for the new
  smile line.

## 5. Russian / combination manicure (dry, e-file cuticle work)
A dry manicure where the cuticle is lifted and refined with small bits:
1. Small flame or bullet diamond at low speed lifts the eponychium edge and removes dead
   cuticle from the plate.
2. A ball or needle bit cleans the sidewalls; stay at a shallow angle.
3. Some versions use nippers to trim lifted dead skin.
This is an advanced technique with genuine risk of cutting live skin; get hands-on
training; some regulators restrict it.

## 6. Bit hygiene
- After each client: brush off debris, clean with detergent (ultrasonic cleaner helps), then
  immerse in an appropriate disinfectant for the full contact time, or sterilise where
  required. Store dry and covered.
- Sanding bands, buffers: single-use, discard.

## Gotchas
- "Drill" damage to natural nails is the e-file's bad reputation — almost always technique,
  pressure or a coarse bit on natural nail.
- Hair, loose sleeves and jewellery can wind into a spinning bit; tie back and secure.
- A bit that's chattering or noisy is worn or loose — replace.
- Clients with thin nails or past damage: switch to hand filing near the plate.

## Verify
- [ ] Professional e-file with extraction running; bit fully seated and locked.
- [ ] Bit chosen by material (carbide for product, fine diamond for cuticle) and speed set to range.
- [ ] Bit flat and moving; no heat complaints; no grooves visible in the natural nail.
- [ ] Rotation direction switched appropriately for each side.
- [ ] Bits cleaned and disinfected; bands discarded.
