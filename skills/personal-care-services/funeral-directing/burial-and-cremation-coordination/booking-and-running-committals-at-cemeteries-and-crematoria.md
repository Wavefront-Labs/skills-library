# Booking and running committals at cemeteries and crematoria

**Goal:** the disposition happens on time, at the right place, with the correct grave or
chamber, a compliant coffin and all permissions in hand — and the family experiences a
calm committal.

## 1. Booking
- Provisionally hold the **service slot** as soon as the family has a preferred date,
  then confirm only once the paperwork route is clear (cremation forms or burial order
  — see documentation-and-compliance) and the officiant is available.
- Crematorium slots are fixed lengths (commonly 20–60 minutes including entry and exit).
  **Book a double slot** if the family expects long tributes or large numbers — overruns
  cascade onto the next family.
- Tell the cemetery/crematorium: coffin size, expected numbers, music system needs,
  webcast/recording requests, faith requirements (e.g. witnessing the charge, which some
  Hindu and Sikh families request), and any special access needs.

## 2. Burial specifics
- **Grave ownership:** for a reopened grave, the owner of the exclusive right of burial
  (grave deed) must authorise interment; if the owner has died, the right must usually be
  transferred first — this can take time, so start immediately.
- Confirm the grave has **depth for another interment**; the cemetery checks records.
- New graves: purchase of the right, plot location agreed with the family.
- Give the cemetery the coffin's external dimensions; oversize coffins need a wider grave
  and more notice.
- **Natural/woodland burial:** biodegradable coffin or shroud, usually no embalming, no
  standard memorials; check the site's rules.

## 3. Cremation specifics
- Coffin must meet the crematorium's/FBCA requirements: combustible materials, no PVC or
  heavy metal fittings, nameplate with name (and often age/date of death) matching the
  paperwork exactly.
- **Remove or declare implants:** pacemakers and some other battery devices must be
  removed before cremation (explosion risk) — confirm on the forms.
- Ask the family about personal items in the coffin; some (glass, batteries, large
  quantities of plastic) aren't allowed.
- Cremated remains: agree collection, scattering at the crematorium garden, interment in
  a grave or columbarium, or transfer to the family; issue a signed receipt.

## 4. Day-of committal
- Arrive with time to brief chapel staff and check music.
- At the graveside: check the grave is open, boarded, with lowering straps or device
  ready and grass mats/ timbers set; brief bearers on where to stand and the lowering
  procedure. Have spare straps and umbrellas.
- At the crematorium: confirm the committal moment (curtains close, catafalque lowers or
  coffin remains) as the family chose.
- After: confirm interment completed and record grave/section numbers in the file.

## Gotchas
- Name mismatches between nameplate, forms and booking stop cremations.
- Waterlogged or frozen ground can delay burials; have a contingency conversation.
- Faith communities may need same-day or next-day burial — make out-of-hours calls early.
- Ashes left uncollected become a long-term liability; follow up in writing.

## Verify
- [ ] Slot confirmed with adequate length; officiant and paperwork lined up.
- [ ] Grave authority/depth or crematorium coffin requirements confirmed.
- [ ] Implants addressed; nameplate matches forms.
- [ ] Bearers and site staff briefed; equipment checked.
- [ ] Remains/grave details recorded and family informed.
