# Extension method selection, application and removal

**Goal:** add length or volume that looks like the client's own hair and leaves their
own hair healthy when it comes out. The craft is mostly in choosing and placing;
the commonest damage is weight and tension on hair that can't carry it.

## 1. Consultation: can their hair carry it?
- **Density and strand strength** at the anchor zone. Fine, thin or post-partum
  shedding hair: lightweight methods only, or clip-ins, or none.
- **Scalp and hairline:** any thinning at temples, sore spots, or past traction
  alopecia = no attachment there. Ask about previous extension damage.
- **Lifestyle:** swimming, gym, heat styling, how often they'll come back for
  maintenance. A method they can't maintain becomes matting.
- **Allergies:** some tape and bond adhesives cause reactions — ask, and consider a
  test strip a day or two before (practice varies by brand).
- **Budget for the whole cycle** (install + move-ups + new hair), not just day one.

## 2. Methods at a glance
| Method | Attachment | Typical wear before move-up* | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clip-ins | Clips, daily | Removed nightly | No commitment; clips can pull fine hair. |
| Tape-ins | Adhesive panels sandwiching a thin slice | ~6–8 weeks | Flat, quick; no oils/conditioner on the tape. |
| Keratin bonds (fusion, K-tip) | Heated keratin bond per strand | ~3–4 months | Very natural movement; heat near the root; careful removal. |
| Micro-rings / I-tips / nano-rings | Small metal ring clamped on | ~6–10 weeks | No heat/glue; rings can slip or crimp hair if over-clamped. |
| Sew-in weft (braided base) | Wefts sewn onto cornrows or beaded rows | ~6–8 weeks | Braids must not be tight; scalp needs washing access. |
*Typical salon guidance; varies by brand, hair growth and maker training — not verified.

## 3. Placement and application
- Keep at least **~1–2 cm back from the front hairline and parting** and above the
  nape hairline so attachments are hidden and fragile edge hairs carry nothing.
- **Match the strand to the anchor**: each attachment should take a slice no bigger
  than the extension piece's footprint. Too little of the client's hair per bond =
  breakage; too much = bulky, visible bonds.
- Brickwork pattern (staggered rows) avoids visible lines.
- Attachments **a few mm off the scalp** — tight-to-scalp is painful and pulls.
- **Colour-match** under daylight to the client's mid-lengths and ends, not roots;
  blending two shades often looks more natural than one. Cut and texturise extensions
  *in* with the client's hair once installed.

## 4. Aftercare and move-ups
- Brush from ends up with a loop/soft brush, holding the bonds. Sleep with hair in a
  loose plait; dry roots fully (wet bonds and wefts can mat and smell).
- Move-ups reposition bonds as hair grows; **shed hair caught at the bond** combs out
  at removal — normal daily shedding (often quoted 50–100 hairs/day) accumulates.

## 5. Removal
- Use the method's remover (tape/bond solvent, ring pliers, unpicking thread); never
  pull or cut near the root. Work the solvent in, wait, slide off.
- Comb out accumulated shed hair and matting gently from below with a detangler.
- Assess the anchor hair before re-installing; give fragile hair a break.

## Wigs (brief)
Measure circumference, front-to-nape and ear-to-ear for cap size; braid or wrap own
hair flat; lace front trimmed close to the hairline and secured with tape/adhesive
the client has tested. Remove adhesive with the matching remover, never by peeling.

## Gotchas
- Daily tight ponytails over extensions = traction alopecia at the temples.
- Silicone-coated "remy" hair looks great at first and tangles once the coating washes off.
- Bond adhesives and wig glues may contain allergens — note what was used on the record.

## Verify
- [ ] Client hair density and hairline assessed; method matches what it can carry.
- [ ] No attachments within the hairline/parting zone; none tight to the scalp.
- [ ] Colour matched in daylight and blended by cutting in.
- [ ] Aftercare and move-up date given; removal leaves anchor hair intact.
