# Microdermabrasion, high-frequency, galvanic and LED operation

**Goal:** use the common facial machines for what they actually do, at settings that
don't injure, and recognise the clients they must not be used on. Machine settings and
timings vary widely by manufacturer — **the device manual and your training always
override the typical values below**, which are unverified general guidance.

## 0. Before any machine
- **Electrical contraindications:** pacemakers or other implanted electronics, metal
  plates/pins/braces in the treatment area (for galvanic and high-frequency), epilepsy
  (flashing LED and high-frequency), pregnancy (most makers exclude currents), loss of
  skin sensation, broken skin, active acne cysts for abrasion. Remove all jewellery
  from client and yourself.
- **Inspect the machine:** cables intact, electrodes uncracked, in-date electrical
  safety test (PAT or local equivalent). Never use a cracked glass electrode.
- Skin clean and dry unless the protocol says otherwise.

## 1. Microdermabrasion (diamond-tip or crystal)
- Mechanical exfoliation plus suction. Diamond tips come in grits: fine for sensitive
  and around eyes, coarser only for robust, thick skin.
- **Start with low suction** and increase only if the skin lifts too little; skin
  should rise gently into the tip, not be sucked hard.
- Hold skin taut ahead of the tip; **short straight strokes in one direction**, one or
  two passes per area. Don't stop moving on one spot (bruising, petechiae).
- **Avoid** the eyelids, lips, active acne, rosacea, telangiectasia, recent peels,
  isotretinoin users. Expect mild pinkness for an hour; anything streaked or abraded
  means too much suction/pressure or too many passes.
- Follow with soothing, no acids for several days; strict SPF.

## 2. High-frequency (Tesla / "violet ray")
- Rapid alternating current through a gas-filled glass electrode producing a mild
  warmth, ozone and a small antibacterial effect — used after extractions and on
  congested skin.
- **Neon (orange/pink glow)** usually for ageing/dehydrated skin; **argon
  (violet/blue)** for acne-prone skin — convention, brand-dependent.
- **Direct:** electrode on skin over a thin gauze or dry skin; place it on the skin
  *before* turning the intensity up, keep it moving, take it off only after turning
  down — lifting the electrode while live causes a spark (sometimes used deliberately
  over a pustule for 1–2 s; only if trained).
- **Indirect:** client holds the electrode while you massage; your fingers stay on the
  skin at all times. 5–8 minutes total is a common limit.
- No alcohol-based products before HF — sparks can ignite them.

## 3. Galvanic
- Direct current, two poles. **Desincrustation (cathode/negative, alkaline):**
  softens sebum before extractions. **Iontophoresis:** pushes a charged water-soluble
  product in — polarity must match the product's charge (labelled on product).
- Client holds or wears the indifferent electrode; the active electrode stays on wet
  skin. Increase current slowly to a tingle the client tolerates, reduce to zero before
  lifting off. Metallic taste = normal with fillings; strong burning = stop.
- Keep electrodes moving; galvanic burns can occur under a stationary electrode.

## 4. LED
- Panels or masks at specific wavelengths: **red (~630–660 nm)** marketed for
  calming/ageing, **blue (~415 nm)** for acne bacteria, **near-infrared (~830 nm)**
  deeper. Evidence ranges from moderate (blue for mild acne) to limited — keep claims
  modest.
- Eye protection, especially for blue light; avoid with photosensitising medications and
  light-triggered epilepsy. Typical sessions 10–20 minutes, at the device's distance.

## 5. Steamer
Fill with distilled water only; check the reservoir level before switching on; aim from
~30–40 cm, start before the client lies down so the first spit of hot water doesn't
hit the face. Ozone (if fitted) only per your local rules.

## Verify
- [ ] Contraindications screened for currents and light; jewellery off.
- [ ] Machine inspected and electrical test in date; electrode intact.
- [ ] Settings started low and raised only as skin response allowed.
- [ ] Skin pink at most, no abrasion, sparks or burns; treatment logged with settings.
