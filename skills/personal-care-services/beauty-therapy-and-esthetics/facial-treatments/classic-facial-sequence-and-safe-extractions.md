# Classic facial sequence and safe extractions

**Goal:** a facial where each step prepares the next, that leaves the skin calmer and
clearer — not red, broken or marked. Assume consultation and contraindication screening
are done (see personal-care-services.beauty-therapy-and-esthetics.skin-analysis-and-consultation.skin-typing-contraindications-and-treatment-planning).

## The sequence (about 60 minutes)
1. **Prep (2 min):** hair back with a headband, gloves on, eye makeup removed with a
   dedicated eye remover on cotton pads, wiping outward, not rubbing.
2. **First cleanse (3 min):** oil or balm cleanser to lift makeup and SPF; remove
   with warm damp mitts/sponges.
3. **Second cleanse (3 min):** cleanser matched to skin type (gel/foam for oily, cream
   for dry/sensitive). Now look again under the magnifier.
4. **Exfoliate (5–10 min):** enzyme for sensitive or reactive skin; mild AHA for
   dull/dry; BHA-based for congested/oily; physical scrubs only on robust skin, light
   pressure, never on active acne. One exfoliant per facial, not three.
5. **Steam (5–8 min):** vaporiser ~30–40 cm away, ozone off unless your unit and
   training say otherwise. **Skip or shorten** for rosacea, visible capillaries,
   sensitised skin; use warm towels instead.
6. **Extractions (≤10 min, see below).**
7. **Massage (10–15 min):** effleurage to start and finish, petrissage on cheeks and
   jaw, light tapotement; move upward and outward, lymphatic strokes toward the nodes
   at the ears and neck. Lighter or skip over active acne.
8. **Mask (10 min):** clay/kaolin for oily or congested areas, hydrating or soothing
   for dry/sensitised; "multi-masking" by zone is fine. Cover eyes with damp pads.
9. **Finish (3 min):** tone, serum, moisturiser, **broad-spectrum SPF 30+** for a
   daytime client. Give home-care advice for 24–48 hours (no other exfoliants, no
   makeup on extracted areas if possible).

## Extractions: the rules
- **Only open comedones (blackheads) and soft closed comedones** that are ready —
  softened by steam or a desincrustation solution. Leave inflamed papules and
  pustules that are deep, and **never touch cysts or nodules** (refer).
- Gloves; wrap index fingers in damp cotton or gauze. **Press down and in**, around
  the comedone, rolling toward it — not squeezing up with fingernails.
- **Two or three attempts per lesion, then stop.** If it doesn't come, it isn't ready.
- Comedone extractor loop: flat to the skin, gentle even pressure; slide, don't dig.
- **Milia and closed comedones needing a lancet:** only where your training,
  insurance and local regulation permit (some jurisdictions restrict skin-piercing in
  esthetics — unconfirmed for any particular one; check). Single-use sterile lancet,
  shallow horizontal nick, dispose in sharps.
- Finish with an antiseptic/soothing toner and high-frequency (if available) or a cool
  compress.
- **Darker Fitzpatrick types (IV–VI):** extraction trauma easily leaves PIH — be
  more conservative, fewer lesions, less pressure.

## Gotchas
- Hot steam + strong exfoliant + extractions + vigorous massage on sensitive skin =
  a red, angry client. Pick a lane.
- Product into the eyes: stop, flush with clean water/saline, record it.
- Hygiene: fresh linen, single-use or disinfected tools, no double-dipping into jars
  (use spatulas).
- Timings above are typical spa protocols, not a regulated standard.

## Verify
- [ ] Each step was chosen for this skin, not run from a fixed menu.
- [ ] Extractions limited to ready comedones, with no broken skin beyond pinpoint marks.
- [ ] Skin pink at most, settling within an hour; no swelling or welts.
- [ ] SPF applied; aftercare given and recorded with products used.
