# Multi-piece creature application and body paint

**Goal:** a complete non-human look built from several appliances, paint, lenses and
teeth — applied in a repeatable order within a call time, holding all day, and with the
performer kept safe and functional underneath. Builds on
personal-care-services.sfx-and-prosthetic-makeup.prosthetic-application.gluing-blending-painting-and-removing-appliances.

## 1. Design for the performer and the schedule
- **Break the design into pieces** that follow the face's movement zones: forehead/brow,
  nose, cheeks, upper lip, chin, ears, cowl (back of head/neck), hands. Seams fall in
  natural creases or under texture where they hide.
- Keep the **eyes and mouth areas mobile** so the performer can act and eat/drink;
  check the design with the director against what the scene needs (close-ups vs wides).
- Agree a **target application time** with the production (full creatures can take
  2–5 hours) and the **removal time** (often 45–60 min). Build these into turnaround
  rules — performer rest periods usually count from when they are out of the makeup.
- Consider lenses and teeth early: they need specialists (see below).

## 2. Application order (typical)
1. Skin prep, bald cap if hair is covered.
2. **Centre outward, then overlapping from front to back**: forehead/brow first, then
   nose, cheeks, upper lip and chin, then the cowl/ears overlapping the edges of the
   front pieces.
3. Glue each piece at the centre, check movement, then edges. **Overlap seams** by a few
   mm and blend with filler (bondo/silicone filler) and stipple texture across the join.
4. **Hands:** appliance gloves or individual finger pieces — the performer can't do
   much once these are on, so they go on last or after lunch.
5. Base colour unifying everything, detail paint, then hair work (punched or laid).
6. **Teeth and lenses last**, after paint fumes have cleared.

## 3. Colour and body paint
- **Airbrush** a unifying base across appliances and skin with alcohol-based or
  silicone-compatible paints; this is what makes separate pieces read as one surface.
  Low pressure (often ~10–20 psi for skin; check the paint) and thin layers.
- **Body paint** for exposed skin: water-activated paints for easy removal (sweat and
  touch will rub them) or alcohol-based for durability (harder to remove, more
  irritating). Seal high-contact areas.
- Paint in the creature's anatomy: shadows in recesses, highlights on new forms,
  "skin" patterns flowing over appliance and real skin alike.
- Check on the camera monitor under the actual lighting setup; creature colour shifts
  heavily under coloured light.

## 4. Lenses and teeth (specialist items)
- **Contact lenses, especially scleral (full-eye) lenses, must be prescribed and fitted
  by an eye-care professional** for the performer, with strict wear limits and an
  optician-approved hygiene routine. Never share lenses. Regulations vary by country
  (in some, cosmetic lenses are prescription-only).
- **FX dentures/teeth** are cast from the performer's dental impressions by a dental
  technician or FX dental maker; check speech and comfort in rehearsal.

## 5. Performer welfare
- Full-head appliances trap heat — schedule **cooling breaks**, provide water through a
  straw, fans or cooling vests, and monitor for signs of **heat stress** (dizziness,
  nausea, confusion = stop and cool down).
- Assign a person to watch and help: the performer may have reduced vision, hearing or
  dexterity. Agree a stop signal.
- Toilet breaks need planning with gloves and costume.

## Gotchas
- Seams aligned in a straight line read as seams; stagger them.
- Paint over un-sealed foam latex soaks in and blotches — seal first.
- Fatigue: the makeup team after a 4 am start; keep a written application checklist
  so steps don't get skipped on day 12.

## Verify
- [ ] Piece breakdown and application order written and timed in a test.
- [ ] Seams overlapped, filled and textured; invisible on the camera monitor.
- [ ] Lenses and teeth supplied and fitted by qualified professionals.
- [ ] Cooling, hydration and welfare checks scheduled through the day.
- [ ] Removal time protected in the call sheet; skin checked afterwards.
