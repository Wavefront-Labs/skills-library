# Body wraps and scrubs — exfoliation, envelopment and clean removal

**Goal:** deliver a full-body scrub or wrap that feels good, uses the right product for the
client's skin, keeps them warm and covered, and is removed cleanly — without overselling what
wraps can do.

Wraps don't "detox" or cause lasting weight loss; any inch loss is water and compression and
returns within hours. Describe them as skin-conditioning and relaxation treatments.

## 1. Consultation and screening
- Allergies: shellfish/iodine sensitivity before **seaweed/algae** products; nut oils; fragrance.
- Skin: sunburn, recent waxing, shaving or laser (wait **24-48 h** for scrubs), eczema flares,
  open cuts, rashes.
- Health: pregnancy (avoid heated wraps), heart conditions and blood pressure (heated
  envelopment raises body temperature), claustrophobia (tight cocooning), varicose veins
  (no vigorous scrubbing over them).
- **Patch test** new or active products (algae, essential oils, warming agents) on the inner arm.

## 2. Product selection
- **Salt scrubs:** coarser, more stimulating; sting on broken or freshly shaved skin. Good for
  rough, oily, robust skin.
- **Sugar scrubs:** finer, gentler, dissolve with water; better for sensitive or dry skin.
- Enzyme or fine bead polishes for very sensitive skin.
- **Muds and clays** (e.g. Dead Sea mud, kaolin, bentonite): absorbent, mineral-rich; applied warm.
- **Algae/seaweed:** marketed as mineral- and iodine-rich; screen for iodine and thyroid issues.
- **Cream or oil wraps:** for dry skin and relaxation.

## 3. Room setup
- Warm room (often 24-26 °C), table covered in layers from bottom: blanket, thermal (mylar)
  sheet or plastic, then towel/sheet you'll use for the scrub; prepare heated towels.
- **Wet room** with Vichy shower or hand shower is easiest; in a dry room, use hot towels to
  remove product.
- Disposable underwear and a towel for modesty; only the area being worked uncovered.

## 4. Scrub technique
1. Warm or dampen the skin slightly with warm towels or a little water.
2. Work one area at a time — back, back of legs, then turn: front of legs, arms, décolleté and
   abdomen (lighter pressure).
3. Circular and long upward strokes towards the heart; firm on feet, knees, elbows; light on the
   inner arms, chest and abdomen. Avoid the face, broken skin and varicose veins.
4. Remove with warm towels or shower, then moisturise.

## 5. Wrap (envelopment) technique
1. Optional scrub first to improve product contact.
2. Apply warm product quickly and evenly with a brush or hands, area by area, while the client
   is lying on the wrap layers.
3. **Envelop:** wrap the thermal sheet then blankets snugly, leaving the face and ideally arms
   free if claustrophobic. Pillow under the knees.
4. **Rest 15-25 minutes**, often with a scalp or face massage. **Do not leave the client alone**
   in a heated wrap; check comfort and temperature every few minutes, and give water.
5. **Unwrap** carefully, remove product (shower or hot towels), and finish with a moisturiser.

## Gotchas
- Overheating: dizziness, nausea, flushed face — unwrap promptly, cool, water.
- Slippery floors and tables after oils and muds; help clients up and down.
- Mud and algae stain towels; use dedicated dark linen.
- Salt in micro-cuts after shaving stings badly — screen properly.

## Verify
- [ ] Allergies (iodine/seaweed, nuts, fragrance) and recent hair removal checked.
- [ ] Product matched to skin type; new products patch-tested.
- [ ] Client covered except the area being worked; room and towels warm.
- [ ] Wrapped client checked regularly; never left alone; water given.
- [ ] Product fully removed, skin moisturised, client helped safely off the table.
- [ ] No detox or weight-loss claims made.
