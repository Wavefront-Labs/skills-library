# Commercial mower pre-start checks and blade care

**Goal:** a mower that starts the day safe, cuts cleanly (torn grass tips go brown and
invite disease), and an operator who goes home uninjured.

## 1. Daily pre-start (5 minutes, engine off, key out)
Walk round in the same order every day; tick a card.
1. **Fluids:** engine oil (on level ground, dipstick per manual), fuel, coolant on
   liquid-cooled engines, hydraulic oil level.
2. **Leaks:** look under the machine and along hoses. **Never feel for a hydraulic leak
   with your hand** — a pinhole jet can inject oil under the skin (a surgical emergency).
   Use cardboard.
3. **Tyres:** pressure per decal (uneven pressure on a wide deck = uneven cut and pulling
   on zero-turns). Check for cuts.
4. **Deck/cutting units:** blades secure, not bent or cracked; no wire or string wound
   round spindles; discharge chute or deflector in place (never run a side-discharge
   deck with the chute up).
5. **Safety systems:** seat switch, blade engagement (PTO) interlock, parking brake
   interlock. Test: with the blades engaged, rise off the seat — the engine or blades
   must stop. **A defeated interlock is a red-tag item; don't use the mower.**
6. **ROPS** (roll bar) up and locked, seat belt working where ROPS is fitted.
7. Air filter/pre-cleaner clean; radiator screen clear of clippings (overheating is
   the commonest cause of zero-turn engine failure in summer).
8. Lights, horn, beacon where fitted; fire extinguisher if required by the site.

## 2. Rotary blade sharpening and balancing
1. Disconnect the spark plug lead (or battery on EFI/diesel) before working under the deck.
2. Block the blade (wood block or blade holder) and remove the bolt — note the blade's
   orientation (the lifting "wing" goes up toward the deck).
3. Sharpen with a grinder or file following the original angle (often ~30°), only on the
   top/bevel side, keeping the edge slightly blunt (like a butter knife, not a razor —
   a razor edge chips immediately). Don't overheat (blue = temper lost).
4. Take equal metal off both ends.
5. **Balance** on a cone or nail balancer: the heavy end drops — grind the *back* of that
   end (not the cutting edge) until it sits level. An unbalanced blade destroys spindle
   bearings.
6. Replace blades when the wing is worn thin or cracked, or when sharpening has removed
   ~10–15 mm of width (check manual), or if bent — never straighten a bent blade.
7. Refit with a **torque wrench** to the specified torque (varies widely; check the manual).

## 3. Reel (cylinder) mower: reel-to-bedknife
1. Check sharpness with the **paper test**: a strip of thin paper (newsprint or a
   receipt) should cut cleanly along the whole width of the reel when you turn it by
   hand.
2. Adjust to **light contact**: the reel just touches the bedknife and cuts paper, but
   turns freely by hand. Heavy contact wears both and costs power and heat.
3. If adjustment can't get a clean cut: backlap with grinding compound (reversing the
   reel) or send for grinding.
4. Set **height of cut** with a bar gauge, across both ends of the roller.

## 4. Operating safely
- **Slopes:** follow the maker's maximum slope (often ~15° for ride-ons — check yours);
  zero-turns lose steering on wet slopes. Mow **across** with ride-ons where the manual
  says, avoid edges near drop-offs, water and retaining walls (keep ~1.5–2 m away; use a
  walk-behind or strimmer). Use an inclinometer app/gauge to check slope once.
- **Thrown objects:** walk the area for stones, bottles, toys first; stop blades when
  people come within the safety distance (often ~15–30 m for discharging decks).
- Disengage blades and stop the engine before clearing a blockage; never clear by hand.
- PPE: eye and hearing protection, safety boots; long trousers and visor for strimming.

## Gotchas
- Washing a hot engine or deck with cold water can crack components; let it cool.
- Mulching kits need sharper blades and slower ground speed than discharge.
- Slope limits, torque figures and blade wear limits are **machine-specific**; the
  numbers here are generic and unconfirmed for your mower.

## Verify
- [ ] Pre-start card ticked and signed before work.
- [ ] Seat/PTO interlock tested; ROPS up.
- [ ] Blades balanced and torqued; cut leaves clean (not shredded) tips.
- [ ] Reel cuts paper across the whole width with light contact.
- [ ] Slope and edge hazards on the site identified before mowing.
