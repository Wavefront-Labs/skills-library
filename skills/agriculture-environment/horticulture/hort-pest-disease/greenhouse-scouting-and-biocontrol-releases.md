# Greenhouse scouting and biocontrol releases

**Goal:** catch pests while populations are small and let beneficials keep them there,
using sprays only as a targeted, compatible back-up. Rates below are from one Extension
newsletter; always follow your supplier's release sheet and the pesticide label.

## 1. Scouting
- Begin regular scouting once the crop is growing. Check randomly chosen plants on a
  zig-zag route, ideally about 10% of the crop.
- Examine plants up close with a hand lens and record results in a notebook
  immediately.

## 2. Match agent to pest
| Pest | Agent and rate (UVM Extension) |
|---|---|
| Greenhouse whitefly | *Encarsia formosa*: introduce as a preventative or at low pest levels; about 1 wasp per 1 to 4 plants weekly for 4 to 6 weeks; stop for sweet potato whitefly once 70 to 80% of plants hold parasitised pupae. Works best when daytime temperatures exceed 72 F |
| Spider mites | *Phytoseiulus persimilis*: order enough for one mite per infested leaf; control reported at 10 predators per plant; establishes in about one week |
| Thrips | *Amblyseius cucumeris* / *A. barkeri*: preventative only (larval stages), 10 to 100 per square metre weekly or one per square foot monthly |
| Aphids | *Aphidoletes aphidimyza*: one cocoon per 10 square feet, repeat in two weeks |
| Mealybugs | *Cryptolaemus montrouzieri*: about 2 per square metre, or 2 to 5 per infested plant |

## 3. Sprays and compatibility
- Consider previous pesticide use and residues before introducing biocontrols.
- Use miticides carefully near *Phytoseiulus*: abamectin indirectly kills beneficial
  mites that feed on treated spider mites.

## 4. Hygiene
- Remove weeds, plant residues and old crop plants; remove heavily infested plants.
- Screen ventilation openings; hang hoses rather than leaving them on the floor.
- Keep vegetation mowed for at least 10 feet around the greenhouse.

## Verify
- [ ] Scouting data recorded on the same route each time.
- [ ] Pest identified before choosing an agent.
- [ ] Release dates, rates and areas logged.
- [ ] Spray history checked for residues before releasing.
