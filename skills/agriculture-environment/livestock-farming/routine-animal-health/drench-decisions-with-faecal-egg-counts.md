# Drench decisions with faecal egg counts

**Goal:** know whether a wormer is still working before resistance is total. Treatment
thresholds and regional programmes (WormBoss in Australia, SCOPS in the UK) are not
reproduced here: use your national programme and your vet.

## 1. Sampling
- Use fresh faeces; Farmers Weekly says less than an hour old, stored sealed in a cool
  place and examined within 48 hours.
- Teagasc: send samples to the lab as soon as possible, preferably the same day; if
  delayed, refrigerate and do not freeze or leave in direct sunlight.

## 2. Drench test (Teagasc lamb example)
1. Select a group of 15 lambs at random and record their tag numbers.
2. Collect up to 10 separate fresh dung samples from the group, keeping each separate.
3. Check that the dosing equipment is calibrated, then dose to the weight of the heaviest
   lamb in the group, following the manufacturer's instructions.
4. Collect the second sample 7 days after treatment for yellow wormers and 14 days
   after for white or clear wormers (Teagasc). Farmers Weekly gives 14 days for most
   wormers and seven for the yellow (levamisole) group.
5. Compare counts: a reduction above 95% means the treatment is effective (Teagasc); a
   product not killing at least 95% of worms means resistance problems (Farmers Weekly).
   Below 95%, contact your vet or adviser.

## 3. Quick post-drench check
Farmers Weekly describes an initial check on a pooled sample from 10 lambs that have
been wormed; the formal faecal egg count reduction test compares the wormer against an
untreated control.

## Safety and records
Record date, product, dose and batch, and observe the label withholding period for meat
and milk. Doses, withholding periods and off-label use (for example goats) are a matter
for the label and your vet.

## Verify
- [ ] Fresh samples handled as above and lab turnaround known.
- [ ] Equipment calibrated; dose set for the heaviest animal.
- [ ] Post-treatment sample timed to the wormer group (7 or 14 days).
- [ ] Reduction calculated against the 95% threshold.
