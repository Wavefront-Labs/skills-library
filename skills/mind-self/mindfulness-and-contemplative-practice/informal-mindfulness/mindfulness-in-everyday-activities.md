# Mindfulness in everyday activities

**Goal:** extend the attention you train on the cushion into ordinary life — eating,
chores, commuting, switching between tasks — using small, cued practices that fit a
busy day. Educational guidance; complements formal practice rather than replacing it.

## 1. One mindful task a day (start here)
Pick one routine activity of 2–10 minutes and do it with full attention every day for
a week:
- brushing teeth, showering, washing up, making coffee, the first few minutes of a walk.
During it: no phone, no podcast. Notice the sensory detail (temperature of water,
smell, sounds, textures). When you notice you're planning or replaying, come back to
the next sensation. Same loop as formal practice, just eyes open and hands busy.

## 2. Mindful eating
- **The raisin exercise** (from MBSR): take one raisin (or any small food) and spend
  3–5 minutes on it — look, feel, smell, place it in the mouth without chewing, chew
  slowly, notice the urge to swallow, swallow, notice aftertaste.
- **Everyday version:** the first three bites of each meal eaten slowly with attention,
  cutlery down between bites; no screens for at least that part of the meal.
- Notice hunger and fullness signals before, midway and after.

## 3. Cue-based micro-practices
Attach a 10–60 second practice to a cue that already happens many times a day:
| Cue | Practice |
|---|---|
| Phone unlock | One full breath before opening anything |
| Doorways | Feel your feet as you pass through |
| Kettle / microwave wait | Body check: shoulders, jaw, hands |
| Red traffic light (as passenger or stopped) | Three breaths, notice sounds |
| Before sending an important message | Pause, read once, notice mood |
Pick **one** cue at a time for a week; adding five at once usually means doing none.

## 4. STOP (a 30-second reset)
- **S**top what you're doing.
- **T**ake a breath — feel it.
- **O**bserve: body sensations, emotions, thoughts, surroundings.
- **P**roceed, choosing your next action rather than running on autopilot.

## 5. Three-minute breathing space (from MBCT)
1. **Awareness (≈1 min):** what's here — thoughts, feelings, body sensations?
2. **Gathering (≈1 min):** narrow attention to the breath in the belly.
3. **Expanding (≈1 min):** widen to the whole body, posture, face.
Use it at fixed times (e.g. three times a day) and when stressed.

## 6. Transition pauses
Between meetings, before entering the house, after closing the laptop: a 30-second
pause to let one activity end before the next starts. Reduces carrying stress from one
context into another.

## Gotchas
- "Doing everything mindfully" is unrealistic; small, cued, repeated beats ambitious.
- Mindfulness while driving means attention on the road, not eyes-closed exercises.
  Never do breathing spaces or eyes-closed practice while operating vehicles or machinery.
- Mindful eating is not a diet. If eating is entangled with disordered eating patterns,
  work with a clinician rather than self-applying eating exercises.
- Informal practice alone tends to fade without some formal practice to strengthen it.
- Uncertainty: cue examples are illustrative; the best cue is whatever already happens
  reliably in your day.

- [ ] You ate the first bites of at least one meal a day without screens.
