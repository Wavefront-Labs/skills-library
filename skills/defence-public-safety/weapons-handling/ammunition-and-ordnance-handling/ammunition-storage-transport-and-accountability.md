# Ammunition storage, transport and accountability

**Goal:** keep ammunition and ordnance from exploding, burning, degrading or going
missing, from receipt through storage, movement, issue and return. Most serious
ammunition accidents trace back to mixing incompatible items, overfilling a store, poor
condition control or lost accountability — not to the round itself. **National
regulations, your explosives licence and the item's technical data override this page.**

## 1. Classify before you store
Every item carries a UN Class 1 **hazard division (HD)** and **compatibility group (CG)**,
e.g. `1.4S`:
| HD | Hazard (summary) |
|---|---|
| 1.1 | Mass explosion |
| 1.2 | Projection (fragments), no mass explosion |
| 1.3 | Fire, minor blast/projection |
| 1.4 | Minor hazard, effects largely confined to the package |
| 1.5 / 1.6 | Very insensitive substances / articles |
Small-arms ammunition is usually 1.4S or 1.4C, but check the packaging — never assume.
**Compatibility groups** say what may share a store; a mixing table in your regulations
(based on IATG/AASTP) decides. Detonators and initiators are kept apart from main charges.

## 2. Quantity-distance (QD)
Each store is licensed for a maximum **net explosive quantity (NEQ)** per hazard division,
based on its distance from inhabited buildings, roads, other stores and the workplace.
- Never exceed the licensed NEQ "for a few days".
- When stores of different HDs mix, the aggregation rules in the regulations apply —
  don't add them up yourself.
- Changes to buildings, roads or occupancy near the site can invalidate a licence.

## 3. Store conditions and discipline
- Dry, ventilated, temperature-controlled where required; avoid direct sun on packages.
- Keep items **in original packaging**, stacked stable and off the floor, lot labels visible.
- **No sources of ignition:** no smoking, lighters, phones or non-approved electrics as
  per local rules; earthing and lightning protection maintained and tested.
- Fire-fighting equipment and **fire-division symbols** posted so responders know what's inside.
- Good housekeeping: no combustibles, empty packaging removed promptly.

## 4. Lot control and surveillance
- Record **lot/batch numbers** for everything received and issued.
- Issue oldest serviceable stock first (FIFO) unless directed otherwise.
- Act immediately on **suspension/restriction notices**: segregate and tag the lot; do
  not issue. Lot records are how you find every round of a bad lot.
- Report damaged, corroded, leaking or wet packages; segregate pending technical inspection.

## 5. Transport
- Follow dangerous-goods rules (ADR or national equivalent; military rules on-base):
  vehicle approval, placarding, documentation, trained drivers, load secured, fire
  extinguishers, no passengers beyond those allowed.
- Separate incompatible groups in transit as in storage.
- Never leave a loaded vehicle unattended outside a secure area.

## 6. Issue, return and accountability
- Issue against a signed demand to a named person, by quantity **and lot**.
- On return, reconcile: issued = fired + returned live + empty cases/links (where
  collected) + declared misfires. Investigate differences before closing the range.
- **Misfires and blinds:** never re-issue a round that failed to fire; segregate, label and
  hand over for disposal. Unexploded ordnance on a range or in the field is left in place,
  marked and reported to EOD — see
  `defence-public-safety.explosives-disposal.unexploded-ordnance-clearance.battle-area-clearance-and-demolition-site-management`.
- Periodic stock checks by someone independent of the store.

## Uncertainty
HD/CG mixing tables, QD distances and transport exemptions are jurisdiction-specific and
change; this page summarises IATG/AASTP concepts, not the binding tables.

## Verify
- [ ] Every item's HD and CG is known from its packaging and the mix is permitted.
- [ ] The store is within its licensed NEQ.
- [ ] Lot numbers are recorded; suspended lots are segregated and tagged.
- [ ] Transport meets dangerous-goods rules and the vehicle is never left unattended.
- [ ] Issue/return reconciles exactly, and misfires went to disposal, not back on issue.
