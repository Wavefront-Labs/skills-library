# Crew roles and range safety for crew-served weapons

**Goal:** run a machine-gun, mortar or anti-armour crew so that each member knows their
job, fire stays inside the authorised arcs and danger area, and stoppages and misfires are
handled by drill rather than improvisation. Weapon-specific load, stoppage and misfire
drills — and all wait times — come from the **issued handbook for that system**; this page
is the common frame around them.

## 1. Crew roles (names vary by army)
| Role | Typical duties |
|---|---|
| **Crew/detachment commander** | Receives the task, sets arcs, gives fire control orders, owns safety on the position, checks the crew before and after firing. |
| **Gunner / No. 1** | Lays and fires the weapon; first to call a stoppage or misfire. |
| **Assistant gunner / No. 2** | Feeds/loads, watches fall of shot, carries spare barrel or sight, performs barrel changes, takes over if No. 1 is hit. |
| **Ammunition bearers / No. 3+** | Prepare and supply ammunition, keep a running count, keep the ammunition protected and clear of backblast or heat. |

Cross-train every role. A crew that only works with its regular gunner fails the first time
someone is absent.

## 2. Controlling where rounds go
- **Arcs:** the commander gives a clearly identifiable **left and right of arc** on the
  ground and confirms each member can point to them. Mechanical limits (traverse stops,
  aiming-post marks) back up eyes.
- **Danger areas:** every range trace shows the **surface danger zone / weapon danger
  area** for that weapon and ammunition — including ricochet and overhead areas. The range
  safety officer confirms it is clear and controlled before "range open".
- **Overhead and flanking fire** by other troops needs its own published safety rules and
  authorisation; never improvise it.
- **Night and reduced visibility:** limits must be mechanical, not visual.

## 3. System-specific safety frames
- **Machine guns:** follow the published **cook-off** guidance — after sustained fire a
  chambered round can fire from barrel heat, so keep the muzzle in the arc and follow the
  handbook's barrel-change and rate-of-fire limits. Use heat-resistant gloves for barrel changes.
- **Mortars:** a dedicated **safety officer/NCO** checks bearings and elevations against
  the safe limits before every fire mission. The cardinal error is a **double load** —
  the loader must not drop a second bomb until the first has left; drill calls and
  hand signals exist to prevent it. Check **mask and overhead clearance** (branches,
  wires, cover) for every position.
- **Anti-armour / recoilless:** clear and physically check the **backblast area** behind
  the weapon and call "backblast area clear" before firing. Walls, vehicles and rooms close
  behind the firer reflect blast — use only firing positions the handbook permits.

## 4. Stoppages, misfires and hangfires
1. Gunner announces it loudly; crew stays in position, muzzle in the arc.
2. Apply **only the handbook immediate action** for that system.
3. If it is a misfire, wait the **published time** before opening or unloading — a
   hangfire can still fire. Do not shorten it under pressure.
4. Misfired rounds/bombs are set aside, marked and reported for disposal per orders;
   never re-use or repair them on the position (see
   `defence-public-safety.weapons-handling.ammunition-and-ordnance-handling.ammunition-storage-transport-and-accountability`).

## 5. Commander's checks
- **Before:** crew briefed on task, arcs, danger area, signals and casualty plan; weapon
  serviceable; ammunition lot correct and counted; hearing and eye protection on.
- **After:** weapon unloaded and **proved clear by a second person**; ammunition and
  empty cases counted against issue; any blind or misfire reported.

## Uncertainty
Role titles, misfire wait times and danger-area geometry are system- and nation-specific;
nothing here substitutes for the range standing orders and handbook.

## Verify
- [ ] Every crew member can point to left and right of arc and name the danger area limits.
- [ ] Each member can do every other member's job.
- [ ] Backblast/overhead clearance is physically checked, not assumed.
- [ ] Misfire wait times come from the handbook and are obeyed.
- [ ] Weapon proved clear and ammunition reconciled after firing.
