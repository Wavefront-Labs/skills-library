# Primary immunodeficiency: workup and immunoglobulin replacement

> General clinical/professional reference, not patient-specific advice. Laboratory reference ranges are age- and lab-specific; immunoglobulin access criteria vary by health system.

**Goal:** catch immune deficiency before organ damage (bronchiectasis, hearing loss)
accumulates, confirm it with a stepwise workup, and run replacement and prevention well.

## 1. When to suspect it
Pattern beats count. Think deficiency with:
- Recurrent **sinopulmonary bacterial infections** (antibody defect pattern), especially
  pneumonias needing IV antibiotics, or bronchiectasis without another cause.
- **Unusual organisms** or severity: PJP, invasive fungi, disseminated mycobacteria,
  recurrent Neisseria (complement), severe viral infection.
- Infants: failure to thrive, chronic diarrhoea, persistent thrush (think SCID — a
  medical emergency; newborn screening via TRECs exists in many regions).
- Autoimmunity/granulomas/lymphoproliferation **plus** infections (CVID, immune
  dysregulation syndromes). Family history of early deaths or known IEI.
- Always look for **secondary** causes first: drugs (rituximab, steroids,
  antiepileptics), protein loss (nephrotic syndrome, enteropathy), haematological
  malignancy (CLL, myeloma), HIV.

## 2. Tiered workup
1. **Screen:** FBC with differential (lymphopenia, neutropenia), IgG/IgA/IgM (+ IgE),
   HIV test, serum protein electrophoresis in adults.
2. **Antibody function:** specific antibodies to protein (tetanus) and polysaccharide
   (pneumococcal serotypes) antigens; if low, **immunise and re-measure at 4–8 weeks**.
   Interpretation criteria for pneumococcal responses are debated — use the lab's.
3. **Cellular:** lymphocyte subsets (CD3/4/8, CD19, NK), switched memory B cells.
4. **Complement/phagocyte:** CH50/AH50 for Neisseria or recurrent pyogenic infection;
   neutrophil oxidative burst (DHR) for catalase-positive abscesses (CGD).
5. Genetic panels: increasingly first-line in specialist centres; variants of
   uncertain significance are common.
Test before starting immunoglobulin — replacement invalidates antibody measurements.

## 3. Immunoglobulin replacement
- **Indication:** significant antibody deficiency with infections (CVID, XLA,
  specific defects with impaired vaccine response, selected secondary deficiency).
- **IVIG:** commonly 0.4–0.6 g/kg every 3–4 weeks. **SCIG:** weekly or fortnightly
  at home, smoother levels, fewer systemic reactions.
- Dose to **clinical outcome**, not a fixed trough: many target IgG trough
  > 7–8 g/L, higher with bronchiectasis. Track infection frequency.
- IVIG reactions: rate-related (headache, chills, flushing) — slow the rate, premedicate;
  first infusions and product switches carry higher risk. Rarer: thrombosis, aseptic
  meningitis, haemolysis, renal injury (sucrose-containing products).
- Check hydration, renal function, and product consistency; record lot numbers.

## 4. Prevention beyond immunoglobulin
- **Live vaccines** (MMR, varicella, BCG, oral polio, yellow fever, live influenza)
  are contraindicated in severe T-cell defects and usually in significant antibody
  defects; inactivated vaccines are safe (response may be poor). Vaccinate household.
- Prophylactic antibiotics (e.g. azithromycin, co-trimoxazole) where infections persist;
  PJP prophylaxis in T-cell defects.
- Airway clearance and surveillance CT/spirometry when bronchiectasis present.
- Irradiated, CMV-safe blood products in severe T-cell deficiency.

## Gotchas
- Low IgG on immunoglobulin replacement? Check whether you're measuring a trough.
- Selective IgA deficiency: mostly asymptomatic; anti-IgA antibodies and transfusion
  reactions are a rare but real concern.
- Repeated "chest infections" may be uncontrolled asthma — confirm with imaging/cultures.

## Verify
- [ ] Secondary causes excluded or documented.
- [ ] Antibody function tested (with post-vaccine titres) before replacement.
- [ ] Replacement dose, route, trough and infection log reviewed each visit.
- [ ] Live-vaccine status and household vaccination recorded.
- [ ] Organ surveillance (lung, liver, lymphoproliferation) planned.
