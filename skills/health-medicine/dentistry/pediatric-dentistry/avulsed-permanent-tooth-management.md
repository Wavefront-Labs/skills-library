# Avulsed permanent tooth management

**Goal:** maximise periodontal ligament (PDL) cell survival by getting the tooth back in its
socket as fast as possible, then splint, control infection and watch for resorption.
**Time and the storage medium decide the prognosis.**

## 1. First aid (advise by phone)
1. Confirm it is a **permanent** tooth. **Never replant a primary tooth** — it risks the
   developing successor.
2. Hold by the crown, not the root. If dirty, rinse briefly (seconds) in cold milk, saline or
   the patient's saliva — do not scrub.
3. **Replant immediately** at the scene if possible; bite on a handkerchief and come in.
4. If not possible, store in **milk**, **HBSS** (if available), saline or saliva (in the
   buccal vestibule only if the patient can be trusted not to swallow it). **Not water.**
5. Note the time of injury and how long the tooth was dry.

## 2. Assessment in the clinic
- Exclude head injury, other injuries, aspiration/ingestion of missing teeth (chest
  radiograph if a tooth is unaccounted for).
- Record **extraoral dry time** (the critical number: beyond **60 minutes** dry, PDL cells
  are considered non-viable) and storage medium.
- Radiographs of the area; check the socket for alveolar fracture.
- Assess **apex**: open (immature) or closed.

## 3. Treatment by scenario (IADT 2020)
**A. Replanted at the scene:** clean the area with water/saline/chlorhexidine, verify position
clinically and radiographically (do not extract), suture lacerations, splint.

**B. Stored in physiological medium, or dry < 60 min:** rinse the root surface with saline;
LA; irrigate the socket with saline and remove the clot; replant with gentle finger pressure;
verify position radiographically; splint.

**C. Dry > 60 min:** prognosis is poor (expect ankylosis/replacement resorption) but
**delayed replantation** is still generally recommended to preserve bone and space for later
options. Remove debris, replant, splint. In growing children, plan for later decoronation as
ankylosis develops.

**Splint:** passive, flexible (e.g. 0.3–0.4 mm stainless-steel wire or nylon line bonded with
composite) for **2 weeks**; longer (around 4 weeks) if there is an associated alveolar fracture.

## 4. Endodontics
- **Closed apex:** start root canal treatment within **~2 weeks** of replantation; calcium
  hydroxide intracanal dressing for up to ~1 month, or a corticosteroid/antibiotic paste
  placed immediately/soon after (avoid tetracycline-containing pastes on anterior teeth — staining).
- **Open apex** (replanted within 60 min): aim for **revascularisation**; do not start RCT
  unless signs of necrosis/infection appear (pain, sinus, discolouration with no response,
  apical radiolucency, inflammatory resorption) — then apexification or regenerative
  endodontics.

## 5. Adjuncts and advice
- Systemic antibiotics: IADT notes limited evidence; commonly given (e.g. amoxicillin or
  penicillin V; doxycycline only where age allows given tooth-staining risk) — verify
  against local formulary/guideline.
- **Tetanus** status: refer for booster if the tooth contacted soil or coverage is uncertain.
- Soft diet ~2 weeks; soft toothbrush; chlorhexidine 0.12–0.2% rinse; avoid contact sport.

## 6. Follow-up
Splint off at **2 weeks**; clinical and radiographic review at **4 weeks, 3 months,
6 months, 1 year, then yearly for at least 5 years**. Look for inflammatory resorption
(rapid — treat endodontically), replacement resorption/ankylosis (high metallic percussion
note, infraocclusion in growing patients).

## Gotchas
- Storage in water kills PDL cells by osmotic lysis — milk is far better.
- Rigid splints and long splinting increase ankylosis.
- Scraping or scrubbing the root removes the PDL you are trying to save.
- Infraocclusion of an ankylosed tooth in a growing child worsens with time — refer early.

## Verify
- [ ] Permanent tooth confirmed; primary teeth not replanted.
- [ ] Dry time, storage medium and apex stage documented.
- [ ] Replanted position confirmed radiographically; flexible splint placed.
- [ ] RCT timing set (closed apex) or revascularisation monitoring plan (open apex).
- [ ] Tetanus status addressed and full follow-up schedule booked.
