# Full-coverage crown preparation

**Goal:** remove just enough tooth, in the right places, to give the chosen material its
minimum thickness, a cleansable margin, and enough retention and resistance that the crown
stays on — while protecting the pulp and periodontium.

## 1. Before cutting
- Tooth is restorable: sound core, periodontally stable, endodontic status known; ≥1.5–2 mm
  **ferrule** of sound dentine achievable above the margin. No ferrule → consider crown
  lengthening, orthodontic extrusion or another plan.
- **Choose the material first** — it dictates reduction. Check occlusal space with the
  patient in intercuspation and excursions.
- Make a **silicone putty index** of the existing or waxed-up tooth to check reduction later
  and to fabricate the provisional.
- Shade before preparation (teeth dehydrate and lighten).

## 2. Reduction guide (typical textbook/manufacturer values — follow your material's IFU)
| Material | Occlusal | Axial | Margin |
|---|---|---|---|
| Full gold / cast metal | 1.0 (non-functional) – 1.5 mm (functional cusp) | ~0.5 mm | chamfer ~0.5 mm |
| Metal-ceramic (PFM) | 1.5–2.0 mm | 1.2–1.5 mm buccal; ~0.5 mm lingual metal collar | buccal shoulder ~1.0–1.5 mm; lingual chamfer |
| Lithium disilicate (full contour) | ~1.5 mm (more if layered) | ~1.0–1.5 mm | rounded shoulder/heavy chamfer ~1.0 mm |
| Monolithic zirconia | ~1.0 mm (some systems allow less) | ~0.5–1.0 mm | light chamfer ~0.5 mm |

## 3. Sequence
1. **Depth-orientation grooves** occlusally (following cusp inclines) and axially with a bur
   of known diameter, so reduction is measured, not guessed.
2. **Occlusal reduction** following anatomic planes; add a **functional cusp bevel**
   (buccal on lower posteriors, palatal on uppers).
3. **Axial reduction** with a tapered diamond held at the intended path of insertion.
   Target total occlusal convergence roughly **10–20°** (ideal ~6° is rarely achieved
   clinically; >20° loses retention quickly).
4. **Margin:** place supragingivally or equigingivally where aesthetics allow — easier to
   impress, clean and inspect. Where subgingival is needed, stay within ~0.5 mm of the
   gingival margin and respect the **supracrestal tissue attachment** (keep ~3 mm from
   margin to bone crest as a working rule).
5. **Resistance/retention:** minimum axial wall height ~3 mm on anterior/premolar teeth and
   ~4 mm on molars (Goodacre); add grooves or boxes on short or over-tapered preps.
6. **Finish:** round all line angles (essential for ceramics and digital milling); smooth,
   continuous, well-defined margin with no unsupported enamel lips.

## 4. Check, retract, record
- Check reduction with the putty index sectioned through the tooth, or with a reduction gauge;
  check the path of insertion with one eye from 30 cm (no undercuts).
- **Gingival retraction:** single or double cord (± haemostatic agent) for subgingival margins;
  margin must be visible and dry for PVS/polyether or digital impression.
- Fabricate a **provisional** (bis-acryl from the index): good margins, contacts and occlusion
  keep the gingiva healthy and the tooth from drifting. Temporary cement.

## Gotchas
- Under-reduction is the commonest cause of thick, opaque or fractured crowns — measure it.
- Over-tapering and short walls cause loss of retention; preps on molars lose retention fastest.
- Sharp line angles create stress concentrations in ceramics and cannot be milled accurately.
- Heat: use copious water spray and intermittent light pressure — pulpal damage is cumulative.
- Margins on restoration material rather than tooth risk leakage — extend onto sound tooth.
- Damage to the adjacent tooth: protect with a matrix band during proximal reduction.

## Verify
- [ ] Reduction confirmed against the index/gauge for the chosen material.
- [ ] Total convergence within ~10–20°, no undercuts along the path of insertion.
- [ ] Wall height/ferrule adequate or auxiliary retention added.
- [ ] Margin continuous, smooth, visible and recorded in the impression/scan.
- [ ] Provisional fits marginally, with contacts and occlusion checked.
