# Malocclusion assessment and classification

**Goal:** a complete, reproducible description of a malocclusion — skeletal, dental and soft
tissue — plus an objective need score, so the problem list (not the appliance) drives the plan.

## 1. Patient factors
Chief complaint in the patient's words, motivation, medical history, growth stage (age,
pubertal status, sibling/parent growth), habits (digit sucking), TMD symptoms, caries and
periodontal status — orthodontics only starts on a healthy, clean mouth.

## 2. Extraoral (three planes)
- **Anteroposterior:** skeletal Class I/II/III by profile and palpation of A and B points.
- **Vertical:** lower anterior face height relative to the middle third; Frankfort–mandibular
  plane angle (FMPA) by eye — average about 25–27°.
- **Transverse:** facial asymmetry, chin point and midline deviation.
- Lips: competent or incompetent, lower lip position relative to upper incisors; incisor show
  at rest and smiling; nasolabial angle.

## 3. Intraoral
- Teeth present, unerupted, missing (hypodontia), ectopic; palpate for canines from ~10 years.
- **Crowding/spacing** per arch: mild <4 mm, moderate 4–8 mm, severe >8 mm (space analysis).
- **Incisor classification (BSI):** Class I — lower incisal edges occlude on or below the
  upper cingulum plateau; Class II div 1 — lower edges behind the cingulum plateau, upper
  incisors proclined or average, overjet increased; Class II div 2 — upper centrals
  retroclined, overjet usually minimal, often deep bite; Class III — lower edges ahead of
  the cingulum plateau, reduced or reversed overjet.
- **Molar and canine relationships** (Angle): Class I — mesiobuccal cusp of upper first molar
  in the buccal groove of the lower first molar; record discrepancies in unit fractions.
- **Overjet** (normal ~2–4 mm) and **overbite** (normal ~1/3 coverage of lower incisors):
  increased/reduced, complete/incomplete, traumatic, anterior open bite.
- **Crossbites** anterior and posterior; check for **displacement** on closure from first
  contact — displacing crossbites have stronger indications.
- Centrelines relative to each other and to the face.

## 4. Records
Study models or intraoral scans, photographs, and radiographs as justified: panoramic for
development and pathology, lateral cephalogram when skeletal/incisor angulation matters.

**Cephalometric norms** (Eastman values for Caucasian samples; ethnic norms differ):
SNA 81 ± 3°, SNB 78 ± 3°, **ANB 3 ± 2°** (≈2–4° Class I; >4° Class II; <2° Class III),
MMPA 27 ± 5°, upper incisor to maxillary plane 109 ± 6°, lower incisor to mandibular
plane 93 ± 6° (verify against your reference set).

## 5. Index of Orthodontic Treatment Need (IOTN)
- **Dental Health Component (DHC)** grade 1–5 by the single worst feature (MOCDO order:
  Missing, Overjet, Crossbites, Displacement of contact points, Overbite). Examples:
  overjet >6 mm ≤9 mm → grade 4; overjet >9 mm → grade 5; reverse overjet >3.5 mm with
  masticatory/speech difficulty → 5; impeded eruption → 5; contact point displacement
  >4 mm → 4. Grades 4–5 = great need (verify thresholds with an IOTN ruler).
- **Aesthetic Component (AC)** 1–10 against the photograph scale; 8–10 = great need.
- Publicly funded thresholds differ by country (e.g. UK NHS uses IOTN DHC 4–5 or DHC 3 with
  AC ≥6).

## 6. Problem list → aims
List pathology, then problems in each plane, then patient concerns; derive aims, appliance
type, extraction/space decisions, anchorage and retention.

## Gotchas
- Classifying from molars alone misses the incisor problem the patient actually sees.
- Missing a functional displacement converts a skeletal reading into an error.
- Unerupted maxillary canines not palpable by ~10–11 years need radiographic investigation.
- Class II div 2 with a deep traumatic bite is a different problem from div 1 — note gingival trauma.
- Cephalometric norms are population averages; read the face, not just the numbers.

## Verify
- [ ] Skeletal pattern recorded in all three planes.
- [ ] Incisor, molar and canine classes, overjet (mm) and overbite documented.
- [ ] Crossbites checked for displacement; centrelines recorded.
- [ ] IOTN DHC and AC scored with the worst feature named.
- [ ] Problem list completed before any appliance is chosen.
