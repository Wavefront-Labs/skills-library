# Comprehensive exam and phased treatment plan

**Goal:** collect findings in a fixed order so nothing is missed, then sequence treatment
so disease is controlled before money is spent on definitive restorations.

## 1. Data collection, in order
1. **Chief complaint** in the patient's words, plus history of that complaint.
2. **Medical history and medications** — anticoagulants/antiplatelets, bisphosphonates or
   denosumab (MRONJ risk), prosthetic joints/valves (check current prophylaxis guidance),
   diabetes control, pregnancy, allergies (latex, local anaesthetic, penicillin), smoking.
   Record BP for adults before invasive care.
3. **Extraoral exam** — TMJ (clicks, deviation, max opening in mm), lymph nodes, muscles,
   facial asymmetry.
4. **Intraoral soft-tissue/oral cancer screen** — lips, buccal mucosa, lateral and ventral
   tongue, floor of mouth, palate, oropharynx. Any lesion persisting >2 weeks without an
   obvious cause → review/biopsy/referral pathway.
5. **Periodontal screen** — BPE/PSR by sextant (codes 0–4, `*` for furcation). Code 3–4
   or furcation → full 6-point charting: probing depth, recession, CAL, bleeding on
   probing, mobility (grade I–III), furcation (class I–III), plaque score.
6. **Hard-tissue charting** — existing restorations, missing teeth, caries using ICDAS
   codes 0–6 on clean, dried surfaces; note cracks, wear, defective margins.
7. **Occlusion** — Angle class, interferences, wear facets, parafunction.
8. **Radiographs** prescribed on need, not routine: bitewings for interproximal caries
   and crestal bone; periapicals for symptomatic teeth/endo/apical pathology; OPG for
   third molars or a broad survey. Interval follows caries risk (high-risk adults commonly
   6–12 months bitewings; low risk 24–36 months — confirm local selection criteria).
9. **Caries risk assessment** (e.g. CAMBRA-style: low/moderate/high/extreme) using
   new lesions in last 3 years, diet, fluoride, saliva/xerostomic drugs, plaque.

## 2. Diagnose before you plan
Write a **problem list** of diagnoses, not procedures: "Active caries 36 DO, ICDAS 5";
"Periodontitis Stage III Grade B, generalised"; "Symptomatic irreversible pulpitis 46".
Every planned procedure must map to a diagnosis on this list.

## 3. Phased sequence
1. **Systemic/medical phase** — medical consults, INR timing, glycaemic control.
2. **Urgent phase** — pain, swelling, infection, fractured teeth (extraction, pulpectomy,
   incision and drainage).
3. **Disease control phase** — OHI, scaling/root planing, remove active caries with
   interim restorations (GIC), extract hopeless teeth, endodontics, diet/fluoride
   programme, stabilise TMD.
4. **Re-evaluation** at ~6–8 weeks after non-surgical perio: re-chart, re-assess
   motivation and prognosis. Do not start crowns, bridges or implants before this.
5. **Definitive phase** — perio surgery, final restorations, fixed/removable prosthodontics,
   orthodontics, implants; sequence by tooth prognosis.
6. **Maintenance phase** — recall interval set by risk (3-month perio maintenance vs
   12–24 months for low-risk patients).

Present at least two options (including "no treatment" and its consequences) with costs
and prognosis; record the consent discussion.

## Gotchas
- ICDAS 1–2 (enamel-only, non-cavitated) lesions are for remineralisation and monitoring,
  not drilling; radiographs underestimate lesion depth.
- Probing a bleeding, inflamed site overestimates depth; re-chart after hygiene phase.
- A "hopeless" tooth left in to keep the patient happy can sabotage an adjacent implant
  or bridge — make the prognosis call explicit.
- Thresholds above (radiograph intervals, re-evaluation timing) vary by guideline body;
  verify against your national selection criteria.

## Verify
- [ ] Medical history updated and red-flag medications noted before any invasive step.
- [ ] Soft-tissue screen recorded, including tongue borders and floor of mouth.
- [ ] Periodontal status recorded (BPE/PSR, full chart if indicated) with stage/grade.
- [ ] Each procedure in the plan traces to a written diagnosis.
- [ ] Plan is phased with a re-evaluation gate before definitive work.
- [ ] Options, risks and costs discussed and consent documented.
