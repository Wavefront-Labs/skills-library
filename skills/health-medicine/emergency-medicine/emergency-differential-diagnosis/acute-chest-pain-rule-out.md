# Acute chest pain: the worst-first rule-out

**Goal:** for every ED chest pain, actively exclude the six killers — acute coronary
syndrome, pulmonary embolism, aortic dissection, tension pneumothorax, cardiac tamponade and
oesophageal rupture — before settling on a benign diagnosis, and document how each was excluded.

## 1. First 10 minutes
- ABCDE, monitoring, IV access, **12-lead ECG within 10 minutes** and read immediately.
- STEMI or STEMI-equivalent (posterior MI, new LBBB with ischaemic features/Sgarbossa
  criteria, de Winter T waves, occlusion-MI patterns) → activate the reperfusion pathway now.
- Unstable patient: bedside ultrasound for pericardial effusion, RV dilatation, lung
  sliding, aortic root dilatation/flap.

## 2. Targeted history and examination
Onset (sudden maximal at onset → dissection, PE, pneumothorax), character (tearing,
pleuritic, exertional pressure), radiation, associated syncope, dyspnoea, neuro deficit,
vomiting before pain (Boerhaave). Risk factors: vascular, VTE, connective tissue disease,
cocaine, recent instrumentation. Examine pulses and BP in both arms, murmurs (new AR),
JVP, chest expansion, calf swelling, subcutaneous emphysema.

## 3. Killer-by-killer approach
**ACS**
- Serial **high-sensitivity troponin** with an assay-specific accelerated protocol (e.g.,
  ESC 0/1-h or 0/2-h algorithms; single very-low troponin on arrival may rule out if pain
  onset >3 hours earlier). Cut-offs are assay-specific — use the local lab's validated values.
- **HEART score** (History, ECG, Age, Risk factors, Troponin; 0–10): 0–3 low risk and
  suitable for early discharge with follow-up when troponins are negative; 4–6 moderate;
  ≥7 high. Use as an adjunct, not a replacement, for the troponin pathway.

**Pulmonary embolism**
- Low gestalt pre-test probability → apply **PERC** (age <50, HR <100, SpO2 ≥95%, no
  haemoptysis, no oestrogen use, no prior VTE, no unilateral leg swelling, no recent
  surgery/trauma). All negative → no further testing.
- Otherwise **Wells score** (or YEARS): unlikely → D-dimer (age-adjusted cut-off age×10
  µg/L FEU in over-50s is widely used — check local assay); likely or positive D-dimer → CTPA.

**Aortic dissection**
- Use **ADD-RS** (high-risk conditions, pain features, exam features; 0–3). ADD-RS ≤1 with
  negative D-dimer has been used to make dissection unlikely (evidence still evolving);
  ADD-RS ≥2 → CT aortogram. A normal chest X-ray does not exclude it.
- Never give antithrombotics/thrombolysis until dissection has been considered when features fit.

**Tension pneumothorax** — clinical diagnosis in the unstable patient; decompress first,
image later. **Tamponade** — hypotension, raised JVP, ultrasound effusion with RV diastolic
collapse; pericardiocentesis. **Oesophageal rupture** — post-emesis or post-instrumentation
pain, surgical emphysema, pleural effusion; CT with oral contrast, NBM, antibiotics, surgical referral.

## 4. Then the rest
Pneumonia, pericarditis (diffuse concave ST elevation, PR depression), myocarditis,
pneumothorax (non-tension), biliary/pancreatic disease, musculoskeletal, anxiety —
diagnoses reached **after** the killers are addressed, not instead of them.

## Gotchas
- **Reproducible chest wall tenderness does not exclude ACS.**
- Women, older adults and diabetics more often present atypically (dyspnoea, fatigue, epigastric pain).
- A first troponin drawn too early after symptom onset can be falsely reassuring — respect the protocol timings.
- Response to GTN or antacid is not diagnostic.
- Raised troponin ≠ type 1 MI: consider PE, myocarditis, sepsis, renal failure, dissection involving coronaries.
- Search satisfaction: finding a pneumonia does not exclude a coexisting PE.

## Verify
- [ ] ECG within 10 minutes, reviewed by a decision-maker, repeated if pain recurs or changes.
- [ ] Troponin protocol completed with assay-specific timings and thresholds.
- [ ] PE, dissection, pneumothorax, tamponade and oesophageal rupture each considered and the reasoning documented.
- [ ] Risk score (HEART/Wells/PERC/ADD-RS) recorded where used.
- [ ] Discharge only with low-risk stratification, a follow-up plan and explicit return advice.
