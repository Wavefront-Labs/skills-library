# ECT and TMS: patient selection and course management

> General clinical/professional reference for trained neurostimulation teams, not
> patient-specific advice. Device settings follow the manufacturer's manual and the
> clinic's protocol; anaesthetic agents and doses are the anaesthetist's decision per
> local guidance. Legal requirements for consent and second opinions differ by
> jurisdiction.

**Goal:** offer neurostimulation to the people most likely to benefit, deliver each
treatment to an adequate, documented standard, and stop or adjust based on measured
response and side effects.

## 1. Indications (framework)
- **ECT**: severe or treatment-resistant depression, depression with psychotic
  features, high suicide risk or life-threatening poor intake, **catatonia**, severe
  mania not responding to drugs, some treatment-resistant schizophrenia (with
  clozapine). Fastest and most effective option for severe depression.
- **rTMS/iTBS**: treatment-resistant depression without the urgency or severity
  needing ECT; outpatient, no anaesthesia, minimal cognitive effects; generally less
  effective than ECT for severe illness. Some approvals for OCD and smoking cessation
  (device-specific).

## 2. Pre-ECT workup
- Psychiatric indication documented; medical review with **anaesthetic assessment**
  (cardiac, respiratory, airway, recent MI/stroke, raised intracranial pressure — the
  latter a high-risk situation requiring specialist review).
- Medication review: lithium (risk of prolonged confusion), benzodiazepines and
  anticonvulsants (raise threshold) — discuss with team; **per local protocol**.
- Baseline **cognitive measure** (e.g. MoCA, autobiographical memory) and symptom scale
  (MADRS/HAM-D).
- **Consent**: risks including memory effects, number of treatments, right to
  withdraw; where the patient lacks capacity, follow the local legal route (e.g. second
  opinion requirements).

## 3. Delivering ECT
- **Electrode placement**: right unilateral (RUL, ultrabrief pulse) for fewer cognitive
  effects; bitemporal or bifrontal when speed matters or RUL fails.
- **Seizure threshold titration** at the first session (empirical stepped dosing), then
  treat at a multiple of threshold — commonly ~**6× threshold for ultrabrief RUL** and
  ~**1.5–2.5× for bitemporal** (figures from guidance; follow clinic protocol).
  Age-based dosing is an alternative some clinics use.
- **Seizure monitoring**: EEG (and motor seizure in an isolated limb if cuffed);
  document duration, EEG quality (amplitude, post-ictal suppression), and
  haemodynamic response. Short/poor seizures → review anaesthetic, hyperventilation,
  and dose.
- Typically 2× weekly (UK) or 3× weekly (US) sessions; course length guided by
  response, not a fixed number.

## 4. Course review
- Rate symptoms weekly; reassess cognition regularly (e.g. after every few
  treatments). Worsening memory → consider switching to RUL, reducing frequency or
  stopping.
- Plan **relapse prevention** from the start: medication (e.g. nortriptyline +
  lithium has trial support) or continuation/maintenance ECT.

## 5. TMS practicalities
- **Motor threshold** determination (visible or EMG thumb twitch) each course and
  when coil/position changes; stimulus intensity commonly ~120% MT for high-frequency
  rTMS — device- and protocol-specific.
- Targeting: left **DLPFC** via scalp methods (e.g. Beam F3) or neuronavigation.
- Protocols: 10 Hz rTMS (~37 min) or **iTBS** (~3 min) with similar efficacy;
  accelerated protocols (e.g. SAINT, fMRI-guided, multiple sessions/day) — evidence
  developing.
- Screen: metallic implants near the head, cochlear implants, seizure history,
  medications lowering threshold. Hearing protection for patient and operator.
- Rare seizure risk; have a seizure management plan.

## Gotchas
- Missed titration → under- or overdosing for the whole course.
- Over-reliance on motor seizure duration; EEG quality matters more.
- Stopping ECT abruptly without continuation plan — high early relapse.

## Verify
- [ ] Indication, capacity and consent (or legal pathway) documented.
- [ ] Anaesthetic and medication review completed before first session.
- [ ] Seizure threshold determined; dose multiple recorded each session.
- [ ] EEG seizure adequacy and haemodynamics logged per treatment.
- [ ] Symptom and cognitive scales repeated through the course; relapse plan written.
- [ ] TMS motor threshold, target method and protocol recorded.
