# Labour epidural and caesarean anaesthesia

> General clinical/professional reference for trained obstetric anaesthesia providers, not patient-specific advice. Drug concentrations and pathways follow local labour-ward protocols.

**Goal:** effective labour analgesia that converts reliably to surgical anaesthesia, so
general anaesthesia (with its higher airway risk) is rarely needed.

## 1. Physiology that changes everything
- **Aortocaval compression** from ~20 weeks: supine hypotension → always left uterine
  displacement (wedge/tilt).
- Increased O2 consumption, reduced FRC → rapid desaturation on apnoea.
- Airway oedema (worse in pre-eclampsia and labour), larger breasts, full-stomach
  physiology, increased reflux → higher failed intubation and aspiration risk.
- Reduced LA and anaesthetic requirements; hypercoagulable state.

## 2. Labour epidural
- Check: consent (brief, during contractions is acceptable), platelets if pre-eclampsia
  or relevant history (thresholds often ~70–80 ×10⁹/L if stable), anticoagulant timing,
  IV access.
- Low-concentration LA + opioid (e.g. bupivacaine 0.0625–0.1% with fentanyl 2 µg/mL)
  via **programmed intermittent epidural bolus** ± PCEA — less motor block than older
  high-concentration techniques.
- CSE or dural puncture epidural for faster onset when needed.
- Hourly checks: block height bilateral, motor block (Bromage), BP, fetal heart.
- **Failing epidural** (unilateral, patchy, breakthrough): assess early, pull back
  catheter 1–2 cm, top-up, or **resite** — an epidural that doesn't work in labour
  won't work for a caesarean.

## 3. Caesarean section
- **Spinal** for elective/most urgent cases: hyperbaric bupivacaine 0.5% (~10–12.5 mg)
  plus intrathecal opioid (fentanyl, diamorphine or preservative-free morphine for
  postoperative analgesia).
- **Prophylactic phenylephrine infusion** started with the spinal, titrated to baseline
  BP (international consensus); bradycardia with hypotension → consider ephedrine or
  noradrenaline per local practice.
- Test block to **T4 bilaterally to cold** (and light touch to T5–T6 per some
  protocols) before incision; document modality.
- **Epidural top-up** for in-labour conversion: e.g. 15–20 mL lidocaine 2% with
  adrenaline ± bicarbonate ± fentanyl, given in the delivery room or theatre per urgency.
- Category 1 (immediate threat): choose the fastest *safe* technique; extend a working
  epidural or rapid spinal; GA when there's no time.

## 4. Obstetric general anaesthesia
- Antacid prophylaxis, head-up ramped position, preoxygenation with nasal high-flow.
- RSI with adequate propofol (awareness rates have historically been high) and
  rocuronium or suxamethonium; video laryngoscope first.
- **OAA/DAS algorithm:** limit attempts, SGA (second-generation) early; then decide —
  **wake or proceed** — based on maternal/fetal urgency, pre-agreed in the WHO checklist.
- CICO → front-of-neck access as non-obstetric.

## 5. Complications
- **High or total spinal:** ascending numbness, hand weakness, dyspnoea, hypotension,
  bradycardia → support airway/breathing, vasopressors, intubate if needed.
- LAST — see `health-medicine.anesthesiology.regional-anesthesia.neuraxial-and-ultrasound-guided-blocks-with-last-response`.
- **PDPH:** postural headache after accidental dural puncture; simple analgesia,
  hydration; **epidural blood patch** for persistent/severe cases. Exclude
  intracranial pathology with atypical features.
- Prolonged motor block after delivery → assess for haematoma/abscess urgently.

## Gotchas
- Forgetting uterine displacement in theatre or in the delivery room after the spinal.
- Testing the block with the wrong modality or only on one side.
- Not reviewing low-platelet trends in pre-eclampsia before catheter removal.

## Verify
- [ ] Platelets/anticoagulant timing checked before neuraxial.
- [ ] Epidural reviewed hourly; failing epidurals resited early.
- [ ] Phenylephrine infusion ready with spinal; uterine displacement in place.
- [ ] Block height and modality documented before incision.
- [ ] Wake-or-proceed decision discussed at the checklist for GA.
