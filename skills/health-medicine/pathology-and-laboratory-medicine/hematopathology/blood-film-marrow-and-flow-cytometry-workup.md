# Blood film, marrow and flow cytometry workup

*General professional reference. Classification systems (WHO 5th edition and ICC 2022 coexist), panel design and critical-result lists are institution-specific; follow local SOPs and current classification.*

**Goal:** get from an abnormal blood count to a precise diagnosis by integrating
morphology, immunophenotype, genetics and clinical context — and catch the few film
findings that need a phone call today.

## 1. When to look at a blood film
Analyser flags (blasts, atypical lymphocytes, NRBCs, platelet clumps), unexplained
cytopenias, new leucocytosis/lymphocytosis, suspected haemolysis, thrombocytopenia,
or a clinical request. Read the film systematically: **red cells** (size, shape, colour,
inclusions), **white cells** (differential, morphology, dysplasia), **platelets** (number,
size, clumps), and anything abnormal (parasites, organisms, circulating tumour cells).

## 2. Film findings that need urgent communication
| Finding | Concern |
|---|---|
| Blasts (new), especially with Auer rods or abnormal promyelocytes | Acute leukaemia; **APL** is an emergency (DIC risk) |
| Schistocytes + thrombocytopenia | **TTP / thrombotic microangiopathy** |
| Malaria or other parasites | Infection — quantify parasitaemia |
| Marked leucocytosis with blasts | Leucostasis risk |
| Spherocytes + anaemia (new) | Autoimmune haemolysis |
- Exclude **pseudothrombocytopenia** (EDTA-dependent platelet clumping) before reporting
  a low platelet count — check the feathered edge; recollect in citrate.

## 3. Bone marrow examination
- Indications: unexplained cytopenias, suspected leukaemia/MDS/myeloma, lymphoma staging
  (now often PET-guided), fever of unknown origin with cytopenias, storage diseases.
- Get the right samples: **aspirate** (morphology, flow, cytogenetics/FISH, molecular — first
  pull for morphology and flow, as later pulls are diluted with blood) and **trephine** (≥ 1.5-2 cm
  core recommended for adequacy per ICSH; architecture, cellularity, fibrosis, infiltrates).
- **Aspirate:** 500-cell differential where practical, blast %, dysplasia in each lineage
  (MDS usually needs ≥ 10% dysplastic cells in a lineage), iron stain (ring sideroblasts), plasma cells.
- **Trephine:** age-adjusted cellularity (a rough rule is ~100 minus age, ± 10-20%), distribution,
  reticulin/collagen fibrosis grade, immunohistochemistry (CD34, CD138, CD20/CD3 etc.).
- "Dry tap" often indicates fibrosis or packed marrow — the trephine (and touch imprints) carry the diagnosis.

## 4. Flow cytometry
- **Gating:** CD45 vs side scatter separates lymphocytes, monocytes, granulocytes and blasts (CD45-dim, low SSC).
- **Clonality:** B cells — **kappa:lambda light-chain restriction**; T cells — aberrant antigen
  loss (e.g. CD7, CD5) or TRBC1 restriction; plasma cells — cytoplasmic light chains with CD19/CD45 loss, CD56 gain.
- **Lineage assignment** of blasts with myeloid (MPO, CD13, CD33, CD117), B (CD19, CD22, CD79a), T (cCD3) markers.
- **Measurable residual disease (MRD)** needs validated high-sensitivity panels and enough events.
- Pitfalls: haemodilute samples, delayed samples (viability), small clones near the limit of detection, normal haematogones mimicking B-lymphoblasts.

## 5. Genetics and integration
Cytogenetics/FISH and molecular testing (e.g. *BCR::ABL1*, *PML::RARA*, *NPM1*, *FLT3*,
*TP53*, *JAK2/CALR/MPL*) define many entities in current classifications. Write an
**integrated report** combining morphology, flow, IHC and genetics with the diagnosis per
the named classification (WHO 5th or ICC — state which) and pending tests clearly listed.

## 6. Lymph node and extranodal lymphoma (outline)
Excisional or core biopsy (not FNA alone) for primary diagnosis; fresh tissue to flow;
architecture, IHC panels, clonality studies (IG/TCR rearrangement), EBV studies; classify per current system.

## Gotchas
- Calling APL late is lethal; suspect it from hypergranular promyelocytes, coagulopathy, and treat while confirming.
- Smudge cells are typical of CLL — not a sign of a poor film.
- A diluted aspirate underestimates blasts and plasma cells; compare with the trephine.

## Verify
- [ ] Analyser flags resolved by film review; pseudothrombocytopenia excluded.
- [ ] Urgent findings (blasts, schistocytes, parasites) phoned and documented.
- [ ] Marrow aspirate and adequate trephine obtained, with material for flow and genetics.
- [ ] Flow gating, clonality and lineage documented.
- [ ] Integrated report states the classification used and pending tests.
