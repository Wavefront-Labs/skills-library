# Bethesda reporting and FNA with rapid on-site evaluation

*General professional reference. Reporting categories, adequacy thresholds and associated malignancy risks are revised between editions; use the current edition your laboratory has adopted. Clinical management follows the relevant clinical guideline (e.g. ASCCP), not the cytology report alone.*

**Goal:** make a reliable diagnosis from cells: confirm the sample is adequate, place it
in a reproducible reporting category with a known risk of malignancy, and make sure
enough material is triaged for any ancillary test that might be needed.

## 1. Adequacy comes first
- Every report states adequacy. An inadequate sample must say so (and why: scant
  cellularity, obscuring blood/inflammation, air-drying, poor fixation) rather than being
  reported "negative".
- Cervical liquid-based cytology: minimum squamous cellularity thresholds apply
  (estimated cells per preparation per Bethesda); note transformation zone component.
- Thyroid FNA: commonly at least six groups of ≥ 10 well-preserved follicular cells,
  with exceptions (e.g. abundant colloid, atypia, inflammation).

## 2. Cervical cytology (Bethesda system)
| Category | Meaning |
|---|---|
| NILM | Negative for intraepithelial lesion or malignancy (organisms/reactive changes noted) |
| ASC-US | Atypical squamous cells of undetermined significance |
| ASC-H | Atypical squamous cells, cannot exclude HSIL |
| LSIL | Low-grade squamous intraepithelial lesion (HPV cytopathic effect) |
| HSIL | High-grade squamous intraepithelial lesion |
| SCC | Squamous cell carcinoma |
| AGC / AIS / adenocarcinoma | Glandular abnormalities |
- Many programmes now use **primary HPV testing** with cytology triage; management follows
  risk-based guidelines that combine current results with history.
- Keep the ASC-US rate and ASC-US:SIL ratio as lab QA metrics.

## 3. Thyroid FNA (Bethesda thyroid, I-VI)
I nondiagnostic, II benign, III atypia of undetermined significance, IV follicular
neoplasm, V suspicious for malignancy, VI malignant. Each category carries an estimated
risk of malignancy published in the current edition; molecular testing is often used for III/IV.
Similar tiered systems exist for salivary gland (Milan), urine (Paris) and serous effusions —
they aim to make reports reproducible and risk-linked.

## 4. FNA technique and ROSE
- Fine needle (commonly 22-27 gauge), multiple passes, quick smearing to avoid clotting.
- Make **air-dried** smears (Diff-Quik/Romanowsky — good for cytoplasm, ROSE) and
  **alcohol-fixed** smears (Papanicolaou — nuclear detail); rinse the needle into fixative
  for a cell block or liquid-based preparation.
- **Rapid on-site evaluation (ROSE):** a cytopathologist or cytotechnologist stains and
  reads a smear in the room to judge adequacy and give a preliminary impression.
  It reduces non-diagnostic rates and guides **triage**:
  - Suspected lymphoma → extra passes for **flow cytometry** (fresh in transport medium).
  - Suspected carcinoma needing biomarkers (lung) → dedicated passes for **cell block**
    and molecular testing.
  - Suspected infection → material for **microbiology** culture.
- Preliminary ROSE impressions are provisional; say so to the operator.

## 5. Fluids (effusions, washings, CSF, urine)
- Cell block from the sediment adds IHC capacity (e.g. distinguishing mesothelial
  cells from adenocarcinoma, primary site work-up).
- Reactive mesothelial cells can look alarming; use morphology plus IHC panels.
- CSF degrades fast — process promptly.

## 6. Cytology-histology correlation
Correlate cytology with subsequent biopsies/resections; investigate discordance
(sampling vs interpretive error) and use it for education and QA.

## Gotchas
- Air-drying an alcohol-fixation smear ruins nuclear detail for Pap stain.
- Blood clots in the needle trap diagnostic cells — express promptly.
- "Atypical" categories are not a dumping ground; overuse destroys their predictive value.
- A benign FNA does not cancel a suspicious clinical or imaging picture.

## Verify
- [ ] Adequacy stated explicitly with reason if inadequate.
- [ ] Category matches the current edition of the relevant reporting system.
- [ ] ROSE used where available; material triaged for flow, cell block, molecular or micro as indicated.
- [ ] Cell block made when ancillary testing may be needed.
- [ ] Cyto-histo correlation and atypia rates tracked for QA.
