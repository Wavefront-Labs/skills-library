# CAR-T: CRS and ICANS recognition and grading

> General clinical reference for teams in accredited cellular-therapy centres, not patient-specific advice. Tocilizumab, corticosteroid and other agent dosing and escalation thresholds must follow the centre's current protocol and product labelling; involve the cellular therapy consultant early.

**Goal:** detect cytokine release syndrome (CRS) and neurotoxicity (ICANS) early,
grade them consistently so that escalation is protocol-driven, and remember the
complications that appear weeks later.

## 1. Timing
- **CRS:** typically day 1–7 after infusion (earlier with CD28-costimulated products such as
  axi-cel; later with some BCMA products such as cilta-cel).
- **ICANS:** typically day 3–10, often after or with CRS; can occur without CRS.
- Patients stay near the centre for ~4 weeks (labelling/REMS-type requirements vary) with
  a carer, and carry an alert card.
- Similar toxicities, generally milder, occur with **bispecific T-cell engagers**.

## 2. CRS grading (ASTCT) — driven by fever, hypotension, hypoxia
- **Grade 1:** fever ≥ 38 °C, no hypotension or hypoxia.
- **Grade 2:** fever + hypotension **not requiring vasopressors** and/or hypoxia needing
  **low-flow** oxygen (≤ 6 L/min).
- **Grade 3:** hypotension needing **one vasopressor** (± vasopressin) and/or high-flow
  oxygen/face mask.
- **Grade 4:** **multiple vasopressors** and/or positive pressure (CPAP, BiPAP,
  ventilation).
Fever may be masked by antipyretics or steroids — grade by hypotension/hypoxia then.
Organ toxicities are graded separately (CTCAE).

## 3. ICANS grading — ICE score plus other domains
**ICE score (10 points):** orientation to year, month, city, hospital (4); naming 3
objects (3); following a simple command (1); writing a standard sentence (1); counting
back from 100 by 10 (1). Do it **at least twice daily** and whenever mental state
changes — handwriting deterioration is an early sign.
Grade = worst of: ICE score (7–9 = G1, 3–6 = G2, 0–2 = G3, 0 because unrousable = G4),
depressed consciousness, **seizure**, motor findings, **raised ICP/cerebral oedema**
(G3–4).

## 4. Management framework (protocol-specific detail varies)
- **Every fever = presumed neutropenic sepsis too**: cultures and empirical antibiotics.
- CRS G1: supportive; early **tocilizumab** considered in some protocols for persistent
  fever or high-risk products.
- CRS ≥ G2: **tocilizumab** (repeat doses limited per label); add **dexamethasone** if
  refractory or ≥ G3; ICU review at G2 with deterioration, ICU at G3–4.
- **ICANS: corticosteroids are the mainstay** (tocilizumab does not cross the blood–brain
  barrier and may worsen ICANS if there is no concurrent CRS). Seizure prophylaxis
  (levetiracetam) per centre policy; EEG, MRI/CT for ≥ G2; ICU for ≥ G3.
- Refractory cases: anakinra, siltuximab, high-dose methylprednisolone — specialist decision.
- Steroids probably don't impair CAR-T efficacy at short courses, but use the minimum needed.

## 5. Later complications (weeks–months)
- **IEC-associated HLH-like syndrome (IEC-HS):** rising ferritin, cytopenias, coagulopathy,
  liver injury, often after CRS resolves → anakinra/steroids.
- **Prolonged or biphasic cytopenias** (CAR-HEMATOTOX score predicts) — growth factor,
  transfusion, rarely stem-cell boost.
- **Infection:** bacterial early, viral/fungal later; antiviral (HSV/VZV) and PJP
  prophylaxis, IVIG for hypogammaglobulinaemia with infections; CMV/HBV monitoring.
- Delayed neurotoxicity (parkinsonism with BCMA products), secondary malignancy surveillance.

## Gotchas
- Patients and carers must know to call the centre — and avoid driving for ~8 weeks (label guidance).
- Grade on the numbers documented, not overall impression; under-grading delays escalation.
- Sepsis, CNS infection and disease progression mimic CRS/ICANS.

## Verify
- [ ] Vital signs and ICE score charted at the protocol frequency.
- [ ] CRS and ICANS graded separately with ASTCT criteria.
- [ ] Fever triggers cultures and antibiotics as well as CRS assessment.
- [ ] Tocilizumab and steroid availability confirmed on site before infusion.
- [ ] Late-effects plan: counts, immunoglobulins, prophylaxis, driving advice.
