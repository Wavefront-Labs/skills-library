# ILD pattern diagnosis and workup

> General clinical/professional reference, not a substitute for independent clinical judgement, current guidelines, or verified dosing references.

**Goal:** get from "crackles and a restrictive picture" to a confident multidisciplinary
diagnosis (IPF, HP, CTD-ILD, pneumoconiosis…) and a monitoring/treatment plan.

## 1. The history is half the diagnosis
- **Exposures (ask specifically, lifetime):** birds/feathers/down duvets, moulds
  (damp home, hot tubs, humidifiers), farming/hay, metalworking fluids, asbestos
  (shipyards, construction, plumbing, brake work), silica (quarrying, sandblasting,
  engineered-stone benchtops), coal, beryllium.
- **Drugs:** amiodarone, nitrofurantoin, methotrexate, bleomycin, checkpoint
  inhibitors, others (check a pneumotox database).
- **CTD symptoms:** Raynaud's, arthritis, rash, mechanic's hands, dysphagia, sicca,
  proximal weakness.
- **Smoking:** RB-ILD, DIP, PLCH, combined fibrosis-emphysema.
- **Family history** of pulmonary fibrosis (telomere disease).

## 2. Baseline tests
- **HRCT** (thin-section, supine inspiratory + expiratory, prone images to separate
  dependent atelectasis from early fibrosis).
- **Full PFTs:** FVC and DLCO are the monitoring numbers. Mixed obstruction + preserved
  volumes with low DLCO suggests combined fibrosis-emphysema.
- **6-minute walk test** with continuous oximetry (nadir SpO2, distance).
- **Serology:** ANA, RF, anti-CCP, ENA panel, CK, myositis panel (anti-synthetase:
  Jo-1, PL-7, PL-12…; MDA5), ANCA where relevant. Consider specific IgG to avian/mould
  antigens for HP (supportive only).

## 3. Reading the HRCT pattern (IPF guideline categories)
| Category | Key features |
|---|---|
| **UIP** | Subpleural, basal-predominant reticulation with honeycombing ± traction bronchiectasis |
| **Probable UIP** | Same distribution, traction bronchiectasis, **no** honeycombing |
| **Indeterminate** | Fibrosis with some features not typical of either |
| **Alternative diagnosis** | Upper/mid-zone, peribronchovascular, mosaic attenuation with 3-density sign (HP), extensive GGO, consolidation, cysts, nodules |

- **NSIP:** symmetric basal GGO/reticulation with **subpleural sparing**; think CTD.
- **Fibrotic HP:** mosaic attenuation and air-trapping on expiratory images, often
  mid/upper zone.
- **Asbestosis:** UIP-like fibrosis + pleural plaques; **silicosis:** upper-zone nodules,
  eggshell nodal calcification.

## 4. MDT diagnosis and tissue
Discuss at an ILD MDT (pulmonologist, radiologist, ± pathologist, ± rheumatologist).
In the right clinical context, UIP or probable UIP on HRCT can establish IPF without
biopsy. BAL lymphocytosis (often > 20–30%) supports HP. Transbronchial cryobiopsy or
surgical lung biopsy only when the result changes management and the patient can
tolerate it — surgical biopsy carries meaningful mortality in fibrotic ILD and acute
exacerbation risk.

## 5. Treatment framing (specialist-led)
- **IPF:** antifibrotics (nintedanib or pirfenidone) slow FVC decline. Avoid
  prednisolone + azathioprine + NAC combination (harmful in PANTHER).
- **Progressive pulmonary fibrosis** (non-IPF, ≥ 2 of: worsening symptoms, physiologic
  decline e.g. FVC fall ≥ 5% absolute or DLCO ≥ 10% in a year, radiologic progression):
  nintedanib is an option.
- **HP:** antigen avoidance first — the single most important intervention; then
  immunosuppression for inflammatory disease per specialist.
- **CTD-ILD:** immunosuppression with rheumatology (e.g. mycophenolate).
- **All:** oxygen for exertional desaturation, pulmonary rehab, vaccines, GORD and PH
  screening, early palliative symptom care, transplant referral early.
- **Occupational:** removal from exposure; notify/compensation pathways per
  jurisdiction; screen co-workers via occupational health.

## Gotchas
- Missing the exposure because only "occupation" was asked, not hobbies and home.
- Calling dependent atelectasis "early fibrosis" — check prone images.
- Starting steroids for presumed IPF.
- Antifibrotic GI side effects (nintedanib diarrhoea, pirfenidone nausea/photosensitivity)
  and LFT monitoring — check the product schedule.

## Verify
- [ ] Exposure, drug, CTD and family history documented.
- [ ] HRCT includes prone and expiratory images; pattern category stated.
- [ ] Serology panel sent; MDT discussion recorded.
- [ ] FVC, DLCO and 6MWT baselines set with a re-test interval (typically 3–6 months).
- [ ] Exposure removal and occupational notification addressed where relevant.
