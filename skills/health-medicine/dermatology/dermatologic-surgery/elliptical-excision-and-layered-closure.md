# Elliptical excision and layered closure

**Goal:** remove a skin lesion with the intended margin in one specimen and close it
as a fine linear scar lying in the relaxed skin tension lines, without dog-ears,
dehiscence or a spread scar.

## 1. Pre-operative check
- Indication and margin decided: diagnostic excision of a pigmented lesion ~2 mm;
  low-risk BCC ~4 mm; low-risk SCC ~4–6 mm (margins depend on tumour risk category and
  guideline — check local). High-risk facial tumours → consider Mohs referral.
- Anticoagulants/antiplatelets: usually **continued** for skin surgery; plan meticulous
  haemostasis rather than stopping them.
- Allergies (lidocaine, adhesive, chlorhexidine), pacemaker/ICD (prefer bipolar or
  battery-powered cautery), implants, keloid history, wound-healing risks (smoking,
  diabetes, steroids). Consent includes scar length (~3× lesion), infection, bleeding,
  incomplete excision.

## 2. Design
1. Mark the lesion edge, then the margin, with a surgical marker **before** anaesthetic
   distorts the skin.
2. Orient the long axis along **relaxed skin tension lines** (pinch test: the line
   along which the skin forms fine parallel wrinkles). Respect cosmetic subunit
   boundaries and free margins (eyelid, lip, alar rim).
3. Draw a fusiform ellipse with **length ≈ 3× width** and apical angles ≈ **30°** or less
   — shorter ellipses create standing cones.

## 3. Anaesthesia and excision
- Lidocaine 1% with adrenaline (epinephrine) 1:100,000–1:200,000; maximum ~7 mg/kg with
  adrenaline, ~4.5 mg/kg without (verify against local formulary/guideline). Buffering
  with sodium bicarbonate 1:10 and slow injection reduce pain. Wait ~7–10 min for
  vasoconstriction.
- Incise with a #15 blade perpendicular to the skin (bevel slightly outward in hair-bearing
  areas to avoid follicles), full thickness to the planned depth in one confident stroke.
- Remove the specimen at a uniform depth — **mid-fat** for most lesions, to fat/fascia for
  melanoma WLE. Mark orientation (suture at 12 o'clock) if margins matter.

## 4. Undermining and haemostasis
- Undermine in the correct plane: face — superficial subcutaneous fat (above SMAS and
  facial nerve branches); scalp — subgaleal; trunk/limbs — deep fat just above fascia.
- Undermine roughly the width of the defect on each side; more if tension remains.
- Haemostasis with pinpoint electrocoagulation; avoid charring the dermis.

## 5. Layered closure
1. **Deep (buried) layer** carries the tension: absorbable suture (e.g. 4-0/5-0
   polyglactin or poliglecaprone; 3-0 on the back) placed as **buried vertical
   mattress** or deep dermal sutures, knot buried. Aim for wound-edge **eversion**.
2. Use the **rule of halves** to avoid end-asymmetry.
3. **Superficial layer** only aligns edges: simple interrupted or running non-absorbable
   (5-0/6-0 on face, 4-0/5-0 elsewhere), or running subcuticular.
4. Standing cone at an apex → extend the ellipse, M-plasty to shorten, or a
   hockey-stick/Burow's triangle excision.

## 6. Aftercare
- Pressure dressing 24–48 h; petrolatum and keep clean.
- Typical suture removal: face 5–7 days, scalp 7–10, trunk and upper limb 10–14, lower
  leg ~14 days (varies with tension and buried support).
- Chase histology; document margin status and arrange re-excision if incomplete.

## Gotchas
- Back, shoulders and chest (high tension, keloid-prone) produce spread or hypertrophic
  scars — warn the patient, use strong buried sutures, consider longer support.
- Lower-leg wounds in older patients dehisce and heal slowly; minimise tension, consider
  graft or secondary intention.
- **Danger zones**: temporal branch of the facial nerve (temple, superficial over the
  zygomatic arch), marginal mandibular branch, spinal accessory nerve (posterior triangle).
- Inverted edges from superficial-only closure give a depressed scar.

## Verify
- [ ] Margin marked before anaesthetic; ellipse 3:1 along RSTLs.
- [ ] Specimen oriented and sent with clinical details.
- [ ] Undermined in the correct anatomical plane for the site.
- [ ] Buried layer bears tension; edges everted; no standing cones.
- [ ] Suture-removal date and histology follow-up arranged.
