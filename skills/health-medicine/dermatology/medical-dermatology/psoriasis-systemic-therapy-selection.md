# Selecting systemic therapy for plaque psoriasis

**Goal:** identify who needs systemic treatment, screen safely, and match the agent to
disease pattern and comorbidity — then measure response against a defined target.

## 1. Is systemic therapy indicated?
Typical triggers (exact thresholds differ between AAD, BAD/NICE and EuroGuiDerm — check local):
- **"Rule of tens"**: BSA >10%, PASI >10, or DLQI >10.
- Severe disease at **high-impact sites** (scalp, face, palms/soles, genitals, nails) even
  with low BSA.
- Failure of optimised topicals ± phototherapy, or rapid relapse after phototherapy.
- **Psoriatic arthritis** (screen every visit with PEST or similar), erythrodermic or
  generalised pustular psoriasis.

Record baseline **PASI, BSA, PGA and DLQI** — you cannot judge response without them.

## 2. Pre-treatment screen (all systemic agents)
- FBC, renal and liver function, lipids, HbA1c/glucose; pregnancy test and contraception plan.
- **Hepatitis B, C and HIV serology**; **latent TB** (IGRA ± chest X-ray) before biologics,
  JAK/TYK2 inhibitors and ideally methotrexate.
- Vaccinations up to date; no live vaccines on biologics.
- Comorbidity map: metabolic syndrome, NAFLD/alcohol, IBD, demyelination, heart failure,
  depression, malignancy history, pregnancy intention.

## 3. Conventional oral agents
| Agent | Key uses | Main cautions |
|---|---|---|
| **Methotrexate** (weekly, with folic acid; usual 7.5–25 mg once **weekly** — verify against local formulary/guideline) | First-line, cheap, helps PsA | Hepatotoxicity (alcohol, NAFLD, diabetes), myelosuppression, teratogenic; renal impairment; weekly-dosing errors are fatal |
| **Ciclosporin** (2.5–5 mg/kg/day — verify) | Rapid control, short courses (crisis, pregnancy when needed) | Nephrotoxicity, hypertension; limit duration (commonly ≤1–2 years) |
| **Acitretin** | Pustular/palmoplantar disease, HIV, skin-cancer-prone patients | Teratogenic for **3 years** after stopping; avoid in women of childbearing potential; hyperlipidaemia |
| **Apremilast** (PDE4) | Mild-moderate, nails/scalp, no lab monitoring | GI upset, weight loss, depression/suicidality |
| **Deucravacitinib** (TYK2) | Oral alternative for moderate-severe | Newer — check current label for infection/lab monitoring |

Fumarates (dimethyl fumarate) are first-line in some European guidelines.

## 4. Biologic class choice
Biologics are offered after failure/intolerance/contraindication of conventional agents
(payer criteria vary). Choose by comorbidity:
- **IL-23 (p19) inhibitors** (risankizumab, guselkumab, tildrakizumab): high, durable
  efficacy, infrequent dosing — strong default for skin-predominant disease.
- **IL-17 inhibitors** (secukinumab, ixekizumab, brodalumab, bimekizumab): fastest onset,
  good for PsA; **avoid in IBD**; candidiasis; brodalumab carries suicidality warnings.
- **TNF inhibitors** (adalimumab incl. biosimilars, etanercept, infliximab, certolizumab):
  preferred with **IBD** or uveitis; certolizumab where pregnancy is planned (minimal
  placental transfer); avoid in demyelination and moderate-severe heart failure.
- **IL-12/23** (ustekinumab): well-established, Crohn's-compatible.

## 5. Treat to target
- Assess primary response at ~12–16 weeks: commonly **PASI 75** (or absolute PASI ≤2–3)
  and DLQI 0–1 as targets; PASI 50–75 with DLQI ≤5 may justify continuation/optimisation.
- Primary failure → switch class; secondary failure → check adherence, weight, anti-drug
  antibodies, then switch.

## Gotchas
- Methotrexate prescribed "daily" by error kills — specify the day of the week, one strength
  of tablet, and patient education.
- Paradoxical psoriasis or eczema can occur on TNF/IL-17 agents.
- Systemic steroids can precipitate pustular/erythrodermic flares on withdrawal — avoid.
- Obesity reduces response to fixed-dose biologics; weight-based options exist.
- Check drug interactions: ciclosporin (CYP3A4), methotrexate (trimethoprim, NSAIDs).

## Verify
- [ ] Baseline PASI/BSA/PGA/DLQI and PsA screen documented.
- [ ] TB, hepatitis and HIV screening done before biologic/JAK/TYK2.
- [ ] Contraception/pregnancy plan recorded for teratogenic agents.
- [ ] Agent matched to comorbidities (IBD, PsA, heart failure, demyelination).
- [ ] Monitoring schedule and target-review date set.
