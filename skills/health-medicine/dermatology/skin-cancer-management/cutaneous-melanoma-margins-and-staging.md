# Primary cutaneous melanoma: staging, margins and sentinel node

**Goal:** turn a histology report of primary melanoma into the correct T stage, wide
local excision (WLE) margin, sentinel node decision and follow-up plan — and get the
case to the skin-cancer MDT.

## 1. Read the pathology report
Confirm it contains: **Breslow thickness** (to 0.1 mm), **ulceration**, mitotic rate,
subtype, Clark level (largely historical), microsatellites, lymphovascular/perineural
invasion, regression, peripheral and deep margin status. Request review if the biopsy
was a partial or shave that transected the base — the true thickness may be understated.

## 2. T stage (AJCC 8th edition)
| T | Breslow | a = no ulceration / b = ulceration |
|---|---|---|
| Tis | in situ | — |
| T1 | ≤1.0 mm | T1a: <0.8 mm, no ulceration; T1b: <0.8 mm with ulceration **or** 0.8–1.0 mm either way |
| T2 | >1.0–2.0 mm | a/b by ulceration |
| T3 | >2.0–4.0 mm | a/b by ulceration |
| T4 | >4.0 mm | a/b by ulceration |

Mitotic rate is no longer a T1 criterion in AJCC8 but is still prognostic — record it.
Microsatellites/satellites/in-transit metastases make it N1c or higher (stage III).

## 3. Wide local excision margins (clinical, measured from the scar/lesion edge)
| Thickness | Recommended margin |
|---|---|
| In situ | 0.5–1 cm (lentigo maligna often needs more — consider staged/Mohs-type margin control) |
| ≤1.0 mm | 1 cm |
| >1.0–2.0 mm | 1–2 cm |
| >2.0 mm | 2 cm |

(Exact ranges vary between NCCN, AAD, NICE and national guidelines — check local.)
Excise down to, but usually not including, deep fascia. On anatomically constrained
sites (face, digits, genitals) margins may be modified after MDT discussion; subungual and
digital melanomas may need amputation at an appropriate level.

## 4. Sentinel lymph node biopsy (SLNB)
- Done **at the same time as WLE**, before re-excision disrupts lymphatic drainage where
  possible.
- **T1a**: generally not offered (positivity <5%) unless other adverse features.
- **T1b (0.8–1.0 mm or ulcerated)**: discuss/consider (positivity roughly 5–10%).
- **T2–T4**: offer SLNB (positivity >10%), primarily for staging and adjuvant-therapy
  eligibility.
- After **MSLT-II** and DeCOG-SLT, a positive sentinel node is usually managed with
  **nodal ultrasound surveillance** rather than completion lymph-node dissection — no
  melanoma-specific survival benefit from routine completion dissection.

## 5. Systemic and molecular steps
- **BRAF V600** mutation testing for stage III/IV (and increasingly stage IIB/C) disease.
- **Adjuvant therapy** (anti-PD-1 immunotherapy, or BRAF/MEK inhibitors for BRAF-mutant
  stage III) is discussed for resected stage III and, in many systems, stage IIB/IIC.
  Eligibility criteria change frequently — follow current oncology guidance.
- Staging imaging (CT or PET-CT, brain MRI) for stage IIC and above; not routine for
  stage I–IIA.

## 6. Surveillance
- Teach monthly self-examination of skin and nodes to every patient.
- Typical schedule: stage IA ~every 6–12 months for 1–5 years then yearly or discharge;
  stage IB–IIA 3–6 monthly for 2–3 years; higher stages 3-monthly with imaging per
  protocol. (Highly variable between guidelines — check local.)
- Full skin exam each visit — about 5–8% develop a second primary melanoma.

## Gotchas
- A shave that transects the base understages; T stage is the **maximum** thickness
  across biopsy + WLE specimens, not a sum.
- Desmoplastic melanoma has high local recurrence; perineural spread may need wider
  margins or radiotherapy.
- Lentigo maligna's subclinical extension routinely exceeds 5 mm — clear histological
  margins matter more than a fixed number.
- Pregnancy, immunosuppression and patients on checkpoint inhibitors need MDT input.

## Verify
- [ ] Breslow, ulceration, mitoses and margins all recorded; AJCC8 T stage assigned.
- [ ] WLE margin chosen from thickness table and adjusted only via MDT.
- [ ] SLNB offered/discussed per T stage and done with WLE.
- [ ] BRAF testing and adjuvant discussion for stage III (and eligible stage II).
- [ ] Surveillance schedule and self-exam education documented.
