# Traumatic brain injury: surgical decision-making

> General neurosurgical reference, not patient-specific advice. Thresholds below come from guidelines largely built on observational data; decisions are individualised and made by the neurosurgical team. Osmotherapy and reversal-agent dosing must follow local protocols.

**Goal:** identify the mass lesions that need evacuation now, protect the brain from
secondary injury while the decision is made, and refer with the information the
neurosurgeon actually needs.

## 1. Prevent secondary injury first
- Avoid **hypoxia** (SpO2 < 90%) and **hypotension** (SBP < 90–110 mmHg depending on
  age; each episode worsens outcome).
- Intubate for GCS ≤ 8, loss of airway reflexes, agitation preventing care, or
  deteriorating consciousness; target normocapnia (PaCO2 ~4.5–5.0 kPa / 35–38 mmHg).
- Head up ~30°, neck neutral, ties not compressing jugular veins; normoglycaemia,
  normothermia, seizure control.
- **Reverse anticoagulants** urgently with bleeding (warfarin: PCC + vitamin K; DOACs:
  specific reversal agent or PCC per protocol).

## 2. Recognise herniation
Falling GCS (≥ 2 points motor), new unilateral dilated pupil, extensor posturing,
Cushing response (hypertension, bradycardia, irregular breathing) → temporising
**hyperosmolar therapy** (mannitol or hypertonic saline), brief hyperventilation as a
bridge only, and **immediate** neurosurgical discussion.

## 3. Mass lesions — guideline thresholds for evacuation
**Extradural (epidural) haematoma** — classically lentiform, arterial (middle meningeal),
lucid interval:
- Volume **> 30 mL** → evacuate regardless of GCS.
- Smaller (< 30 mL, < 15 mm thick, < 5 mm shift) in GCS > 8 without focal deficit →
  may be observed with serial CT in a neurosurgical centre.
- Comatose with anisocoria → surgery **as soon as possible**.

**Acute subdural haematoma** — crescentic, venous, often with underlying contusion:
- Thickness **> 10 mm** or midline shift **> 5 mm** → evacuate regardless of GCS.
- Smaller in GCS < 9 → surgery if GCS drops ≥ 2 points, pupils asymmetric/fixed, or ICP
  > 20 mmHg.
- Craniotomy with large bone flap; leave bone off (craniectomy) if brain is swollen.

**Chronic subdural haematoma** — older, anticoagulated, falls, subacute confusion or
headache:
- Symptomatic → **burr-hole drainage** with subdural drain (reduces recurrence).
- Middle meningeal artery embolisation emerging as adjunct to reduce recurrence
  (several 2024 trials positive) — availability varies.
- Steroids are **not** recommended (Dex-CSDH: worse outcomes).

**Posterior fossa lesions** → low threshold for surgery (small space, brainstem
compression, hydrocephalus).

## 4. ICP monitoring and tiered therapy
- Monitor ICP in severe TBI (GCS 3–8 after resuscitation) with abnormal CT, and in
  selected patients with normal CT (age > 40, posturing, hypotension).
- Treat ICP **> 22 mmHg** (BTF 4th ed.); target **CPP ~60–70 mmHg**.
- Tiers: sedation/analgesia, head up, CSF drainage (EVD) → osmotherapy, mild
  hyperventilation, neuromuscular block → **decompressive craniectomy** or barbiturate
  coma for refractory ICP.
- Craniectomy reduces mortality in refractory ICP (RESCUEicp) but increases survival
  with severe disability; early bifrontal craniectomy for moderate ICP rise did not help
  (DECRA). Discuss expected outcomes with family.

## 5. A good referral
Age, mechanism, time, GCS (eye/verbal/motor separately) and trend, pupils, focal signs,
anticoagulants/antiplatelets, other injuries, physiology, CT images transferred, and
what you've already done.

## Gotchas
- Talk-and-deteriorate patients (lucid interval) — don't be reassured by an early good GCS.
- Paediatric and elderly patients tolerate haematomas differently; atrophy hides mass effect.
- Hyperventilation lowers ICP by causing vasoconstriction — prolonged use causes ischaemia.

## Verify
- [ ] No hypoxia/hypotension episodes; airway secured when indicated.
- [ ] Anticoagulation reversed.
- [ ] Haematoma volume/thickness/shift measured against thresholds.
- [ ] Herniation signs treated as an emergency with neurosurgery engaged.
- [ ] ICP/CPP targets documented when monitored.
