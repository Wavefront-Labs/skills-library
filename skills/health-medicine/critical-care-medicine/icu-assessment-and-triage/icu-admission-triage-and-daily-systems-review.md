# ICU admission triage and the daily systems review

*General clinical/professional reference, not a substitute for independent clinical judgement, current guidelines, or verified dosing references.*

**Goal:** decide within minutes whether a deteriorating patient needs ICU (and which level of care), document why, and then review every ICU patient daily by organ system so that each day ends with explicit goals and a step-down decision.

## 1. Triage the referral (bedside, ~15 min)
1. **Resuscitate while you assess:** ABCDE first; triage is not a reason to delay oxygen, fluids or airway help.
2. **Quantify acuity:** NEWS2 ≥7 (or a single parameter scoring 3) is the usual emergency-response trigger; ≥5 is urgent review (verify against local escalation policy). Calculate a quick SOFA from available data.
3. **Ask the four triage questions:**
   | Question | What you need |
   |---|---|
   | Needs organ support the ward cannot give? | Invasive/non-invasive ventilation, vasopressors, RRT, intensive monitoring |
   | Is the process reversible? | Diagnosis or working diagnosis with a plausible treatment |
   | What was baseline? | Clinical Frailty Scale (CFS 1-9; ≥5 frail), functional status, major comorbidity |
   | What does the patient want? | Advance directive, prior wishes, surrogate view |
4. **Assign a level of care** (UK GPICS terms): Level 1 enhanced ward care, Level 2 single-organ support/HDU, Level 3 advanced respiratory support or ≥2 organs. US equivalents are ward, step-down/IMCU and ICU.
5. **Decide and document:** admit; admit with a time-limited trial (e.g. 48-72 h, with named endpoints); or ward-based ceiling of care with a treatment escalation plan. Discuss refusals with a senior and the referring team, and record the rationale.

## 2. Severity scoring (for audit and calibration, not individual prognosis)
- **SOFA:** 6 organs, 0-4 each (PaO2/FiO2, platelets, bilirubin, MAP/vasopressors, GCS, creatinine/urine output). Track daily; a rising score over 48 h signals failure of the current plan.
- **APACHE II / SAPS II / national models (e.g. ICNARC):** worst values in the first 24 h; use for case-mix-adjusted mortality, never to refuse an individual admission.

## 3. Daily systems-based review
Examine, then go system by system with the chart, labs, imaging and the nurse:
| System | Check |
|---|---|
| Neuro | GCS/RASS, CAM-ICU, pain score, sedation target |
| Respiratory | Mode, FiO2/PEEP, P/F ratio, plateau, SBT readiness |
| Cardiovascular | MAP, vasopressor dose trend, lactate, fluid balance (daily and cumulative) |
| Renal | Urine output (mL/kg/h), creatinine, K+, acid-base, RRT plan |
| GI / nutrition | Feeding route and calories, bowels, glycaemia (target 7.8-10 mmol/L / 140-180 mg/dL is common) |
| Haematology | Hb (transfusion threshold usually 70 g/L), platelets, VTE prophylaxis |
| Infection | Cultures, antimicrobial day count and stop date, lines: day count and still needed? |
| Lines/tubes/skin | Pressure areas, line sites, catheter |
| Family / goals | Last update, ceiling of care |

Use **FAST HUGS BID** as a closing checklist: Feeding, Analgesia, Sedation, Thromboprophylaxis, Head-up 30-45°, Ulcer prophylaxis (only if indicated), Glycaemic control, Spontaneous breathing trial, Bowels, Indwelling catheter removal, De-escalation of drugs.

End with **a problem list and 2-4 goals for the next 24 h** (e.g. "extubate if SBT passes", "wean noradrenaline off").

## 4. Step-down / discharge
Ready when: no vasopressors (or minimal per local policy) for ≥12-24 h, stable airway with modest oxygen needs, a plan the ward can deliver, and a ward bed with an outreach follow-up. Avoid night discharge (associated with higher readmission and mortality) and hand over a written summary with a ceiling of care.

## Gotchas
- Scores calibrate populations; a predicted mortality of 60% says nothing certain about this patient.
- Frailty and age are not the same thing; CFS is validated mainly in older adults (not for under-65s or stable single-disability patients).
- "Not for ICU" is not "not for treatment": write what *will* be done.
- Delayed admission of a deteriorating ward patient worsens outcome; when in doubt, review again in 30-60 min.
- Daily review drift: copy-forward notes hide day counts on antibiotics and lines.

## Verify
- [ ] Triage note states diagnosis, reversibility, baseline (CFS), patient wishes and decision.
- [ ] Level of care and any time-limited trial endpoints written.
- [ ] Daily SOFA trend recorded.
- [ ] Every system reviewed; FAST HUGS BID complete.
- [ ] 24-h goals and ceiling of care documented; family updated.
