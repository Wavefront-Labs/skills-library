# Analgesia-first sedation and the delirium bundle

*General clinical/professional reference, not a substitute for independent clinical judgement, current guidelines, or verified dosing references.*

**Goal:** keep each ventilated patient pain-free and calm but awake enough to follow commands, by treating pain first, sedating only to a written target, waking them daily and screening and preventing delirium every shift.

## 1. Assess pain first (every 2-4 h and before/after procedures)
- **Communicative:** numeric rating 0-10; act on ≥4.
- **Non-communicative:** **CPOT** (0-8; >2 indicates pain) or **BPS** (3-12; >5 indicates pain).
- Pre-empt procedural pain (turns, suction, drain removal) with a bolus or non-opioid adjunct.

## 2. Analgesia (analgosedation)
- Opioid infusion or bolus as the base: fentanyl (short context in short use) or morphine/hydromorphone (avoid morphine accumulation in renal failure). Use the lowest effective dose; check current dosing references.
- Multimodal adjuncts to spare opioid: regular paracetamol, low-dose ketamine, regional blocks, neuropathic agents where appropriate.
- Many patients need no hypnotic once pain is controlled.

## 3. Set and hold a sedation target
| RASS | Meaning |
|---|---|
| +4 to +1 | Combative to restless |
| 0 | Alert and calm |
| -1 / -2 | Drowsy, wakes to voice ≥10 s / <10 s |
| -3 | Movement to voice, no eye contact |
| -4 / -5 | Responds to touch only / unrousable |
- Default target **RASS -2 to 0** (or SAS 3-4). Deep sedation (RASS -4/-5) only for a stated reason: severe ARDS with dyssynchrony, neuromuscular blockade, raised ICP, status epilepticus, refractory hypoxaemia.
- Prefer **propofol or dexmedetomidine over benzodiazepines** (PADIS 2018). Dexmedetomidine helps when weaning or for agitation blocking extubation; watch bradycardia and hypotension. Propofol: monitor triglycerides and for propofol infusion syndrome with high doses over days (verify thresholds locally).
- Score RASS at least every 4 h and after every titration.

## 4. Daily spontaneous awakening trial (SAT), paired with the SBT
1. **Safety screen:** no active seizures, alcohol withdrawal, escalating sedation for agitation, NMB, raised ICP, or myocardial ischaemia.
2. Stop hypnotic (and opioid infusion if pain controlled) until the patient opens eyes to voice or for up to 4 h.
3. **Fail** if sustained agitation (RASS ≥+2), pain, RR >35, SpO2 <88%, arrhythmia or distress: restart at **half the previous dose** and titrate.
4. Pass: proceed straight to the SBT (ABC-trial pairing).

## 5. Delirium: screen, prevent, treat
- Screen **every shift (at least twice daily)** with **CAM-ICU** (if RASS ≥-3): (1) acute change or fluctuation, (2) inattention (squeeze on letter "A" in SAVEAHAART; >2 errors), plus (3) RASS ≠0 or (4) disorganised thinking. 1 + 2 + (3 or 4) = positive. ICDSC ≥4 is an alternative.
- **Hunt causes:** pain, hypoxia, sepsis, metabolic derangement, urinary retention, constipation, withdrawal, and deliriogenic drugs (benzodiazepines, anticholinergics).
- **Non-drug prevention:** glasses and hearing aids, orientation, family presence, day-night cycle, noise and light reduction at night, early mobilisation.
- **Drugs:** PADIS advises against routine antipsychotics for prevention or treatment (MIND-USA, AID-ICU showed no outcome benefit); short-term use for distressing agitation or psychotic symptoms endangering the patient is reasonable, stopped once resolved. Dexmedetomidine is suggested for agitated delirium preventing extubation.

## 6. The ABCDEF bundle
**A**ssess, prevent and manage pain; **B**oth SAT and SBT; **C**hoice of analgesia and sedation; **D**elirium assess, prevent, manage; **E**arly mobility and exercise; **F**amily engagement. Compliance correlates with lower mortality and delirium in cohort studies.

## Gotchas
- RASS -3 cannot be CAM-ICU assessed: record "unable to assess (UTA)", not negative.
- Hypoactive delirium (quiet, withdrawn) is the commonest and most missed form.
- Agitation is pain, hypoxia or a full bladder until proven otherwise; do not bolus sedation first.
- Abrupt stop after more than about 5-7 days of opioid or benzodiazepine risks withdrawal; taper.
- Physical restraints increase delirium and self-extubation is not reliably prevented by them.

## Verify
- [ ] Pain score (NRS/CPOT/BPS) recorded at least every 4 h.
- [ ] Written RASS target with reason if deeper than -2.
- [ ] SAT done (or contraindication stated) and paired to the SBT.
- [ ] CAM-ICU each shift; positive screens have a cause search.
- [ ] Mobility level and family contact documented daily.
