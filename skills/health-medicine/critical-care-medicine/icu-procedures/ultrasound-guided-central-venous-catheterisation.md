# Ultrasound-guided central venous catheterisation

*General clinical/professional reference, not a substitute for independent clinical judgement, current guidelines, or verified dosing references.*

**Goal:** a central line in the right vein, first pass where possible, with no arterial
injury, no pneumothorax, the tip at the cavoatrial junction, and every element of the
infection-prevention bundle done and documented.

## 1. Indication and site choice
- Indications: vasopressors or irritant infusions, no peripheral access, CVP/ScvO2
  monitoring, dialysis or plasmapheresis, transvenous pacing.
- Check first: platelets, INR and anticoagulants (thresholds vary; ultrasound-guided IJ
  or femoral is acceptable in many coagulopathic patients — verify local protocol), allergy
  to chlorhexidine, side of any existing pneumothorax or chest drain.

| Site | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| Internal jugular (right preferred) | Easy to image, compressible, straight to SVC | Carotid nearby; less comfortable for awake patients |
| Subclavian/axillary | Lowest infection and thrombosis rates | Pneumothorax risk; not compressible |
| Femoral | Fast in arrest/emergency, no pneumothorax | Higher infection/thrombosis risk; limits mobilisation |

## 2. Preparation
1. Consent (or document emergency/best-interests basis); procedural pause/checklist
   with a second person who can stop the procedure.
2. Pre-scan both sides: vein size and patency (full compressibility excludes thrombus),
   artery position, overlap (rotate the head ≤ 30° — more rotation increases overlap).
3. Position: Trendelenburg 10-15° for IJ/subclavian (raises vein diameter, reduces air
   embolism risk); bed at your waist height; machine in your line of sight.
4. Hand hygiene; **maximal barrier**: cap, mask, sterile gown and gloves, full-body drape.
   Skin prep with 2% chlorhexidine in alcohol, allow to dry (≥ 30 s).
5. Sterile probe cover and gel; flush all lumens; local anaesthetic (lidocaine 1% —
   check current references for maximum dose).

## 3. Cannulation (Seldinger, real-time guidance)
1. Short-axis (out-of-plane) view, vessel centred. Advance the needle while "walking"
   the tip: tilt the probe ahead and bring the needle back into view each time, so the
   echogenic tip is always seen. Long-axis (in-plane) shows the whole shaft and helps
   avoid back-wall puncture.
2. Aspirate dark, non-pulsatile blood. Colour alone is unreliable in shocked or hypoxic patients.
3. Pass the guidewire gently (≤ 15-20 cm for right IJ to avoid arrhythmias); it must never
   meet resistance. Keep a hand on the wire at all times.
4. **Confirm the wire is in the vein before dilating**: see it in long-axis within the
   vein (and in the IVC/RA on subcostal view if available), or manometry/transduced
   waveform via a short cannula. Dilating an artery is the injury that matters.
5. Skin nick, dilate only to the depth of the vessel, railroad the catheter, remove the wire
   and **say aloud "wire out"** (assistant confirms). Aspirate and flush every lumen.
6. Secure (sutureless device or suture), apply chlorhexidine-impregnated sterile dressing.

## 4. Confirm position
- Typical insertion depth to the cavoatrial junction: right IJ ~13-15 cm, left IJ ~16-18 cm,
  right subclavian ~15-16 cm (approximate; individualise).
- Ultrasound: lung sliding on both sides (excludes pneumothorax at the probe), rapid
  saline "bubble test" in RA within ~2 s; chest X-ray per local policy, tip at the
  carina level or ~1-2 carina-body heights below it.

## Gotchas
- Arterial puncture with a needle: withdraw, compress 5-10 min. Arterial dilation or
  catheter: **leave it in**, call vascular/interventional — do not pull it.
- Air embolism: occlude hubs, keep head-down, remove lines with the patient flat or head-down and
  during exhalation (or positive pressure).
- A thrombosed or tiny vein is a reason to change site, not to push harder.
- Lost wire: count wires out at the end; image if any doubt.
- Bundle elements are pass/fail: one skipped step (e.g. no full drape) breaks it.

## Verify
- [ ] Checklist completed with observer; all bundle elements documented.
- [ ] Venous position of the wire confirmed before dilation.
- [ ] Wire removed and confirmed; all lumens aspirate and flush.
- [ ] Pneumothorax excluded and tip position confirmed before vasopressor use where time allows.
- [ ] Line date, site and daily necessity review recorded.
