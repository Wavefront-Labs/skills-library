# Paracentesis and thoracentesis workflow

> General clinical/professional reference, not a substitute for independent clinical judgement, current guidelines, or verified dosing references.

**Goal:** get the right diagnostic sample (and safe therapeutic volume) out of the
abdomen or chest on the first pass, with ultrasound, a clean consent and a complication
plan. This is a reference for trained operators or supervised trainees — not a
substitute for hands-on credentialing.

## 1. Before the needle (both procedures)
- **Indication written down:** diagnostic (new effusion/ascites, suspected SBP or
  empyema) vs therapeutic (symptomatic tense ascites, dyspnoea from effusion).
- **Coagulation:** routine INR/platelet correction is generally *not* needed for
  paracentesis in cirrhosis (AASLD); for thoracentesis practice varies — many accept
  INR ≤ ~2 and platelets ≥ ~50×10⁹/L. Hold or time anticoagulants per local policy.
  Uncertain threshold — check your institution's procedure policy.
- **Consent:** bleeding, infection, organ puncture (bowel/liver/spleen), pneumothorax
  (thoracentesis), re-expansion pulmonary oedema, failed tap.
- **Time-out:** patient, side/site confirmed on ultrasound, allergies (lidocaine,
  chlorhexidine), anticoagulants.

## 2. Paracentesis
- **Site:** left lower quadrant ~2 finger-breadths medial and cephalad to the anterior
  superior iliac spine (thinner wall, away from caecum), or infra-umbilical midline.
  Avoid the inferior epigastric vessels (run along the lateral rectus border) — colour
  Doppler helps — and scars, collaterals, infected skin.
- **Ultrasound:** pocket ≥ ~2–3 cm free fluid without bowel; mark and scan again in
  the final position.
- **Z-track** insertion (displace skin ~2 cm before advancing) to reduce leak.
- **Diagnostic panel:** cell count with differential, albumin (with same-day serum
  albumin for **SAAG**), total protein, Gram stain and culture **inoculated into blood
  culture bottles at the bedside** (raises yield); cytology, amylase, TB tests as
  indicated.
  - SAAG ≥ 1.1 g/dL → portal hypertension. PMN ≥ 250 cells/mm³ → treat as SBP.
- **Large volume:** after > 5 L removed, give albumin ~6–8 g per litre removed (AASLD)
  to reduce post-paracentesis circulatory dysfunction.

## 3. Thoracentesis
- **Position:** seated, leaning forward on a table; or lateral decubitus if unable.
- **Site:** ultrasound-marked intercostal space with adequate fluid depth (often
  ≥ ~1 cm at site), above the diaphragm through the full respiratory cycle, usually
  mid-scapular or posterior axillary line. Insert **just over the upper border of the
  rib** to avoid the neurovascular bundle.
- **Diagnostic panel:** pH (in a heparinised blood-gas syringe, anaerobic, analyse
  promptly), protein and LDH with **paired serum** values for **Light's criteria**,
  glucose, cell count/differential, Gram stain and culture, cytology (send a large
  volume, e.g. 25–50 mL+), others as indicated.
  - Exudate if any: fluid/serum protein > 0.5; fluid/serum LDH > 0.6; fluid LDH > 2/3
    upper limit of normal serum LDH. Light's mislabels some diuresed transudates —
    check serum–fluid albumin gradient (> 1.2 g/dL favours transudate).
  - pH < 7.2 in a parapneumonic effusion → drainage needed.
- **Volume:** stop for chest pain, persistent cough, or pressure symptoms; many limit
  to ~1–1.5 L per session to reduce re-expansion oedema (pleural manometry allows more).

## 4. After
- Pressure dressing; watch for leak (paracentesis), haemodynamic change, chest pain or
  dyspnoea (thoracentesis).
- Routine post-thoracentesis chest X-ray is not required in asymptomatic patients
  after an uncomplicated ultrasound-guided tap; image if symptoms, multiple passes or
  air aspirated. Bedside ultrasound (lung sliding) is an alternative.
- Write a procedure note: indication, consent, time-out, site, technique, volume and
  appearance, samples sent, complications, follow-up.

## Gotchas
- Forgetting the paired serum sample makes Light's criteria/SAAG uninterpretable.
- Pleural pH through a blood-gas analyser only — never put frank pus in it.
- Re-mark if the patient moves; the fluid moves with them.
- Bloody fluid that clots in the tube suggests vessel puncture rather than haemorrhagic
  effusion — stop and reassess.

## Verify
- [ ] Indication and consent documented; time-out done.
- [ ] Ultrasound-marked site checked in the final position.
- [ ] Correct panel sent, with paired serum labs and bedside culture bottles.
- [ ] Volume within limits; albumin given after large-volume paracentesis.
- [ ] Post-procedure observations and note completed.
