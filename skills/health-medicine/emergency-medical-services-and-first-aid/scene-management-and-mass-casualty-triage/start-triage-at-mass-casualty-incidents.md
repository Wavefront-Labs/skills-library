# START triage at mass casualty incidents

**Goal:** when casualties outnumber rescuers, sort everyone quickly into priority groups
so the few resources go to those who will die without them and can be saved — doing the
greatest good for the greatest number, not the best for each individual.

## 1. First crew on scene: do not treat, command
- **Safety:** scene, self, survivors. Stage upwind/uphill for hazmat; consider secondary
  devices at deliberate incidents.
- **Declare** the major/mass casualty incident early — under-declaring is a far more
  common failure than over-declaring.
- Send a structured report — **METHANE** (UK/JESIP; ETHANE in some services):
  **M**ajor incident declared, **E**xact location, **T**ype, **H**azards,
  **A**ccess/egress, **N**umber and severity of casualties, **E**mergency services
  present and required.
- Establish **ICS** roles: incident/medical commander, triage officer, treatment and
  transport officers. Wear role tabards.

## 2. START primary triage (adults, about 30–60 s per patient)
1. **Walking?** "Everyone who can walk, go to [point]." → **GREEN / Minor (P3)**.
2. **Breathing?** If not: open airway once (reposition). Still not breathing →
   **BLACK / Dead or expectant**. Breathing after repositioning → **RED / Immediate**.
3. **Respiratory rate:** **>30/min → RED.** ≤30 → continue.
4. **Perfusion:** radial pulse absent **or capillary refill >2 s → RED** (control major
   bleeding). Present → continue.
5. **Mental status:** cannot follow simple commands → **RED.** Follows commands →
   **YELLOW / Delayed (P2)**.

Mnemonic: **RPM — 30, 2, can do.**

## 3. JumpSTART for children (roughly 1–8 years, or apparent child)
- Not breathing after airway opening: check pulse; if pulse present give **5 rescue
  breaths** — breathing resumes → RED, else BLACK.
- **RR <15 or >45 → RED.**
- No palpable peripheral pulse → RED.
- **AVPU:** inappropriate P (posturing/unresponsive) or U → RED.
- Infants who cannot walk are evaluated in full rather than auto-tagged green.

## 4. Life-saving interventions allowed during triage
Only quick ones: open the airway, **tourniquet** or direct pressure for massive bleeding,
place in recovery position, and in some systems auto-injector antidotes. Then move on.

## 5. Tag and flow
- Tag every casualty (colour card, tape or slap-wrap) and count by category.
- Casualties move to a **casualty clearing station** with treatment areas by priority.
- **Secondary triage (sort)** in the CCS uses physiology and anatomy — e.g. the
  **Triage Sort** using revised trauma score, or physician judgement — to set
  treatment and transport order.
- Transport officer distributes patients across hospitals, avoiding overloading the
  nearest one.

## 6. Re-triage
Triage is dynamic: re-triage every casualty on arrival at the CCS and whenever
condition changes, at transport, and at hospital.

## Alternatives
- **SALT** (Sort, Assess, Lifesaving interventions, Treatment/Transport): adds global
  sorting by walking/waving/still and an **Expectant (grey)** category.
- **NHS/UK Ten Second Triage**, **MIMMS triage sieve**: similar physiology logic with
  different thresholds (e.g. RR <10 or >30 in the sieve). Use the system your region
  trains on — mixing systems on one scene causes chaos.

## Gotchas
- **Treating the first casualty** you find instead of triaging — the commonest and most
  lethal error.
- **Over-triage** (too many reds) overwhelms the CCS and hospitals; under-triage kills
  quietly.
- **Walking wounded are not all minor** — some deteriorate (blast lung, crush, head
  injury); re-triage them.
- Capillary refill is unreliable in cold and dark; use radial pulse.
- Expectant/black decisions are emotionally hard; they are revisited as resources grow.

## Verify
- [ ] Scene safe, incident declared, METHANE/ETHANE sent within minutes.
- [ ] Command roles assigned and identified.
- [ ] Walking wounded directed away first.
- [ ] Each casualty triaged with RPM (or JumpSTART for children) in under a minute.
- [ ] Only quick life-saving interventions performed during the sieve.
- [ ] Counts by category reported up; re-triage happening at the CCS.
