# Manual defibrillation, synchronised cardioversion and transcutaneous pacing

**Goal:** deliver the right electrical therapy at the right energy with minimal
interruption to compressions and zero harm to the team — three modes on the same box
that are easy to confuse under stress.

## 1. Before you need it: the device check
- Daily/shift check per manufacturer: self-test passed, battery charged plus spare,
  pads in date and correct type (adult/paediatric), ECG electrodes, razor, pacing
  cables, printer paper, test shock into the test load if required.
- Know where your device's **sync**, **pacer**, **energy select**, **charge** and
  **shock** controls are — they differ between LIFEPAK, ZOLL, Corpuls and Philips.

## 2. Pad placement
- **Anterolateral:** right infraclavicular, left mid-axillary at V6 level (long axis
  vertical).
- **Anteroposterior:** left parasternal/apex and left infrascapular — useful for pacing,
  AF cardioversion and refractory VF in some guidance (e.g. DOSE-VF double sequential /
  vector change — check local policy).
- Avoid implanted devices (≥8 cm), patches, and breast tissue; dry the skin; shave hair.

## 3. Manual defibrillation (cardiac arrest)
1. Compressions continue while pads go on.
2. **Rhythm check** (pause ≤5 s). VF or pulseless VT = shockable.
3. Resume compressions; **charge during compressions**.
4. Announce "Charging — all clear except compressor, oxygen away."
5. Pause; confirm rhythm still shockable; "**Shocking — everyone clear**"; visual sweep;
   shock.
6. **Immediately resume compressions** for 2 minutes.
- **Energy:** biphasic first shock commonly **120–200 J** (follow manufacturer;
  if unknown, many algorithms use the maximum); escalating per device; monophasic 360 J
  (verify against local guideline). Peri-shock pause target: under 5–10 s total.
- If the rhythm changes to non-shockable while charged: **disarm** using the device's
  method (dump charge / change energy), do not shock.

## 4. Synchronised cardioversion (unstable tachycardia with a pulse)
- Indications: tachyarrhythmia with **adverse features** — shock, syncope, myocardial
  ischaemia, severe heart failure (ERC wording; AHA similar).
- Sedation/analgesia if conscious, per ALS protocol.
- Press **SYNC**; confirm sync markers appear on each **R wave** (choose a lead with a
  tall R and change lead if markers fall on T waves).
- Hold the shock button until discharge — there is a delay to the next R wave.
- **Typical starting energies (verify against device and local guideline):** narrow
  regular (SVT/flutter) ~**70–120 J** biphasic; AF ~**120–200 J** (ERC suggests
  maximum energy for AF); broad complex regular ~**100–150 J** then escalate.
- Many devices **default back out of sync** after each shock — re-press SYNC before
  repeating.
- Polymorphic VT / pulseless: do not sync; defibrillate.

## 5. Transcutaneous pacing (unstable bradycardia)
- Indicated for symptomatic bradycardia unresponsive to atropine (or where atropine is
  unlikely to work, e.g. high-grade AV block), per bradycardia algorithm.
- Attach 3-lead ECG (most devices require it for demand pacing) and pacing pads
  (anteroposterior preferred).
- Select **PACER**, demand mode; rate typically **60–80/min**.
- Increase current (mA) from the minimum until **electrical capture** (wide QRS with
  T wave after each spike), then add about 5–10 mA above threshold (check local).
- Confirm **mechanical capture** with a femoral pulse (carotid is confounded by muscle
  twitching) or SpO2 waveform/BP.
- Give analgesia/sedation — pacing is painful.

## Gotchas
- **Forgetting sync** — an unsynchronised shock on a T wave can induce VF.
- **Sync on in VF** — the device will not fire because there is no R wave; switch sync
  off.
- **Pacing spike artefact** mistaken for capture; confirm with a pulse.
- **Oxygen** pooling near pads, wet chests and touching the stretcher frame are
  burn/shock hazards.
- Prolonged hands-off pauses while the team waits for the charge.
- Pads past expiry or gel dried out → high impedance, failed shock.

## Verify
- [ ] Device passed its shift check; pads in date; spare battery present.
- [ ] Pads placed correctly on dry skin, clear of devices.
- [ ] Charging done during compressions; pre-shock pause under ~5 s; safety call given.
- [ ] Sync mode confirmed with markers on R waves for each cardioversion.
- [ ] Pacing: electrical and mechanical capture both confirmed; analgesia given.
- [ ] Energies and times documented.
