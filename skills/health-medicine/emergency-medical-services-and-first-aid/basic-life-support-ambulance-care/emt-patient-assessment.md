# EMT patient assessment (scene to transport decision)

**Goal:** find and fix life threats in the order they kill, then gather enough history
and examination to make a sound, timely transport decision — without the assessment
itself becoming the delay.

## 1. Scene size-up (before touching the patient)
- **Standard precautions** (gloves, eye protection; more for suspected airborne
  infection).
- **Scene safety:** traffic, violence, hazardous materials, unstable structures.
- **Mechanism of injury / nature of illness**, number of patients, resources needed
  (more crews, ALS, fire, police). Request early — cancelling is cheap.
- Consider spinal motion restriction from mechanism plus findings, per local criteria
  (e.g. NEXUS or Canadian C-spine rule where adopted).

## 2. Primary survey — XABCDE (life threats, treat as found)
- **X – eXsanguinating haemorrhage:** direct pressure, tourniquet for limb bleeding not
  controlled by pressure, wound packing for junctional wounds.
- **A – Airway:** talking = patent. Manual manoeuvres (jaw thrust if trauma), suction,
  **OPA** (sized angle of jaw to incisors; only if no gag) or **NPA** (avoid with
  suspected base-of-skull fracture per most protocols).
- **B – Breathing:** rate, effort, symmetry, SpO2. Oxygen titrated to target
  (commonly **94–98%**, **88–92%** in known CO2 retainers — check local). Bag-valve-mask
  ventilation if inadequate, about 1 breath every 6 s in apnoea with a pulse.
- **C – Circulation:** radial/carotid pulse, skin colour/temperature, capillary refill,
  obvious bleeding sources (chest, abdomen, pelvis, long bones). Splint pelvis if
  suspected.
- **D – Disability:** **AVPU** or **GCS**, pupils, blood glucose (hypoglycaemia is the
  great mimic).
- **E – Exposure/environment:** look everywhere that matters; prevent heat loss.
- Decide now: **time-critical?** Any uncorrected A/B/C problem or reduced GCS =
  minimal on-scene time, early pre-alert, consider ALS intercept.

## 3. Vital signs (baseline, then trend)
Pulse, respiratory rate, BP, SpO2, temperature, GCS, glucose, pain score. Early-warning
scores such as **NEWS2** are used in some systems. Repeat every 5 min if unstable,
15 min if stable — the trend matters more than any single set.

## 4. History
- **SAMPLE:** Signs/symptoms, Allergies, Medications, Past history, Last oral intake
  (and last menstrual period), Events leading up.
- **OPQRST** for pain: Onset, Provocation/palliation, Quality, Region/radiation,
  Severity, Time.
- Collateral history from bystanders, medication lists, alert jewellery.

## 5. Secondary survey
- **Trauma:** head-to-toe with **DCAP-BTLS** (Deformities, Contusions, Abrasions,
  Punctures, Burns, Tenderness, Lacerations, Swelling); log-roll for back if indicated.
- **Medical:** focused on the complaint (chest: lung sounds, oedema; neuro: FAST/BE-FAST
  stroke screen, limb power; abdomen: tenderness, guarding).

## 6. Transport decision
- **Destination:** nearest appropriate facility, using trauma triage criteria and
  pathway activations (stroke, STEMI, trauma centre) per local protocol.
- **Mode:** emergency lights/sirens only where time saves outcome; they carry crash
  risk.
- Reassess en route; document findings and times.

## Gotchas
- **Tunnel vision** on a dramatic injury while the airway obstructs.
- **Normal BP does not mean no shock** — young adults compensate until late; tachycardia
  and delayed cap refill come first. Beta-blocked patients may not become tachycardic.
- **Skipping glucose** in altered consciousness.
- **SpO2 is unreliable** with cold peripheries, CO poisoning, and poor waveform.
- **Over-staying on scene** for a complete secondary survey in a time-critical patient —
  do it en route.

## Verify
- [ ] Scene declared safe and resources requested before patient contact.
- [ ] Catastrophic bleeding and airway addressed before anything else.
- [ ] Time-critical decision made at the end of the primary survey.
- [ ] Two or more sets of vitals recorded, with glucose for any altered conscious level.
- [ ] SAMPLE and OPQRST completed; secondary survey done (on scene or en route).
- [ ] Destination matches pathway criteria; pre-alert given if time-critical.
