# Contraceptive counselling with the MEC

> General clinical/professional reference, not a substitute for independent clinical judgement, current guidelines, or verified dosing references.

**Goal:** help the person choose a method that fits their priorities and is
medically safe for them, and start it today wherever possible.

## 1. Patient-centred counselling
- Start with what matters to them: effectiveness, bleeding pattern, hormones or not,
  frequency of action, privacy, return to fertility, STI protection, cost/access.
- Avoid steering; respect autonomy and past experiences (including reproductive coercion).
- Present methods by **typical-use effectiveness** (approximate first-year pregnancy rates; the CDC/Trussell effectiveness table was not retrievable this session (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked)):

| Tier | Methods | Typical-use failure |
|---|---|---|
| Most effective (LARC) | Implant, LNG-IUD, copper IUD; sterilisation | <1% |
| Next | Injectable (DMPA) | ~4–6% |
| | Pill (combined or progestogen-only), patch, ring | ~7–9% |
| Less | Condoms, diaphragm, fertility awareness, withdrawal | ~13–22%+ |

## 2. Check medical eligibility (UKMEC / US MEC)
Categories: **1** no restriction; **2** benefits generally outweigh risks; **3** risks
usually outweigh benefits (use only with specialist judgement); **4** unacceptable risk.
Key **combined hormonal (oestrogen)** cautions (US MEC 2024 categories, checked in the MMWR text via Europe PMC):
- **Migraine with aura** (any age): CHC category 4.
- Age ≥35 and smoking ≥15 cigarettes/day: category 4 (<15/day: category 3).
- Systolic 140–159 or diastolic 90–99: category 3; ≥160/≥100 or vascular disease: category 4.
- Current or past VTE, known thrombogenic mutations.
- Postpartum not breastfeeding: <21 days category 4; 21–42 days with other VTE risk factors category 3. Breastfeeding <21 days: category 4. UKMEC 2016 differs (it is stricter for breastfeeding, up to 6 weeks (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked)).
- Current breast cancer; major surgery with prolonged immobilisation.
- Some liver disease, complicated valvular heart disease, SLE with antiphospholipid antibodies.
Progestogen-only methods and copper IUD have far fewer restrictions. **Enzyme-inducing
drugs** (some antiepileptics, rifampicin, St John's wort) reduce efficacy of pills,
patch, ring and implant — prefer IUD/IUS or DMPA.
Always measure **BP and BMI** before combined methods.

## 3. Exclude pregnancy reasonably
Reasonably certain not pregnant (US SPR 2024) if no symptoms or signs of pregnancy and any one of: ≤7 days
after start of normal menses; no intercourse since start of last normal menses; correct and
consistent use of a reliable method; ≤7 days after spontaneous or induced abortion;
within 4 weeks postpartum; fully or nearly fully breastfeeding (≥85% of feeds), amenorrhoeic,
<6 months postpartum. A negative urine test is only reliable ≥3 weeks after last
unprotected sex (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked).

## 4. Quick-start
If pregnancy can't be fully excluded but the risk is low, most hormonal methods can
be **started today** ("quick start") with additional precautions (US SPR 2024: abstain or use barrier methods for 7 days, or 2 days for a norethindrone/norgestrel POP; the amount depends on method and days since menses began) and a follow-up pregnancy test (timing per local guidance (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked)). Copper IUD is the exception — only
when pregnancy is excluded (or as emergency contraception).

## 5. Emergency contraception
- **Copper IUD**: within 5 days of the first act of unprotected sex, or later if no more than 5 days after estimated ovulation (US SPR 2024).
- **Ulipristal acetate 30 mg** one tablet within 120 h (5 days) (US ella label); interaction with progestogen taken just before or after reduces effect (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked); the label says to start or resume hormonal contraception no sooner than 5 days after.
- **Levonorgestrel 1.5 mg** as soon as possible within 72 h (3 days) (Plan B One-Step label); UPA appeared more effective than LNG at 3-5 days (US SPR). Dose adjustment with enzyme inducers or higher BMI in some guidance (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked).
Then offer ongoing contraception and STI testing.

## Gotchas
- Missed-pill rules differ by pill type — give written guidance for their brand.
- DMPA: reduced bone density with long use; delayed return of fertility (up to ~1 year (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked)).
- Unscheduled bleeding is the commonest reason for stopping — warn in advance.
- Condoms are the only method that protects against STIs.

Note: categories above are from CDC US MEC 2024 (MMWR Recomm Rep 2024;73(4), via Europe PMC PMC11315372); UKMEC not re-read. Check the current version as categories change.
