# First-trimester dating scan

> General clinical/professional reference, not a substitute for independent clinical judgement, current guidelines, or verified dosing references.

**Goal:** establish location, viability, number and gestational age accurately —
the CRL-based due date anchors every later decision (screening, growth, induction).

## 1. Machine setup and safety
- Obstetric preset; curvilinear transabdominal probe, transvaginal (TV) probe for
  early or unclear findings (with consent and chaperone offer).
- **ALARA**: keep the thermal index low and minimise dwell time (check your machine's
  safety guidance); **avoid pulsed/colour Doppler on the embryo** in early
  pregnancy unless clinically indicated (M-mode for heart rate instead).
- Zoom so the structure fills most of the screen; adjust depth, single focal zone at
  the region of interest, appropriate gain.

## 2. Location and viability
- Confirm an intrauterine gestational sac with yolk sac (± embryo). A
  **pseudosac** (central, no yolk sac, no double decidual sign) can accompany ectopic pregnancy.
- No intrauterine pregnancy with positive hCG = **pregnancy of unknown location** →
  serial hCG/clinical pathway; scan adnexa for ectopic.
- Embryo with **cardiac activity** documented by M-mode.

## 3. Suspected failed pregnancy
Use the conservative criteria and rescan intervals in your unit's early-pregnancy
protocol (NICE NG126 covers ectopic pregnancy and miscarriage). Do not diagnose failure
on a single borderline scan; get a second opinion or rescan before management.

## 4. Measure CRL correctly
NICE ties the 11+2 to 14+1 week scan to a CRL of 45.0 to 84.0 mm (combined screening range); use your unit's validated range for earlier dating.
- True **mid-sagittal** section of the whole embryo/fetus, horizontal on screen
  (ideally 90° to the beam).
- **Neutral position**: not hyperflexed (underestimates) or hyperextended (overestimates);
  fluid visible between chin and chest.
- Calipers at the outer edge of crown and rump, excluding the limbs and yolk sac.
- Take repeat measurements and use the best/mean per protocol with the unit's validated chart.
- Once CRL exceeds the valid range, date by the biometry your protocol specifies (usually head circumference).

## 5. When to re-date
Use the scan date over LMP when they differ by more than your protocol's threshold. **Once dated by a good early
scan, don't re-date later** — a later small measurement is a growth problem, not a dating error.
IVF pregnancies: date from embryo transfer.

## 6. Multiples — chorionicity now
NICE NG137 offers twin and triplet pregnancies a first-trimester scan for gestational age, chorionicity and amnionicity, ideally at the same scan:
- **Lambda (twin-peak) sign** → dichorionic.
- **T-sign** (thin membrane meeting placenta at right angles) → monochorionic diamniotic.
- No membrane → monoamniotic (high risk).
Label fetuses consistently (side/position) and store images. If views are poor
(retroverted uterus, high BMI) use a transvaginal scan; if chorionicity cannot be
determined, seek a senior second opinion, and manage as monochorionic until proven
otherwise. Monochorionic twins need ultrasound every 14 days from 16 weeks (NG137, TTTS
surveillance).

## 7. Report
Number and location, chorionicity/amnionicity, cardiac activity, CRL (mm) and
gestational age, EDD, any findings (adnexal masses, fibroids, subchorionic
haematoma), image quality limitations, and recommended follow-up.

## Gotchas
- An oblique section shortens CRL; a curled fetus under-measures — wait for a neutral position.
- Poor transabdominal views (retroverted uterus, high BMI) → switch to TV.
- Don't comment on sex in the first trimester; it's unreliable and not the scan's purpose.

Vendors differ in preset and thermal index display.

## Verify
- [ ] TI kept low; no Doppler on embryo without indication.
- [ ] Intrauterine location and cardiac activity documented.
- [ ] CRL in true mid-sagittal, neutral position, within valid range.
- [ ] Re-dating rule applied and EDD recorded.
- [ ] Chorionicity determined in multiples.
