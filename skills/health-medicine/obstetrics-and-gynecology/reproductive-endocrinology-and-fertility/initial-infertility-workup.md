# Initial infertility workup

> General clinical/professional reference, not a substitute for independent clinical judgement, current guidelines, or verified dosing references.

**Goal:** investigate both partners efficiently and in parallel, so the couple reaches
the right treatment (or reassurance) without months of serial tests.

## 1. When to start
- Infertility: no pregnancy after **12 months** of regular unprotected intercourse (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked).
- Start at **6 months** if the person with ovaries is **≥35**; immediately if ≥40 (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked) (NICE CG156 uses a different age cut-off for early referral; check it), or
  with known causes: amenorrhoea/oligomenorrhoea, known tubal or uterine disease,
  stage III–IV endometriosis, previous chemotherapy/radiotherapy, known male factor.
- Same-sex couples and single people seeking donor conception follow adapted pathways.

## 2. History (both partners)
- Duration, coital frequency and timing (every 2–3 days is sufficient; ovulation
  kits can cause stress), previous pregnancies.
- Menstrual pattern (regular 21–35 days suggests ovulation), dysmenorrhoea/
  dyspareunia (endometriosis), pelvic infection, surgery, galactorrhoea, hirsutism.
- Male: previous paternity, testicular surgery/undescended testes, mumps orchitis,
  testosterone/anabolic steroid use (suppresses spermatogenesis), heat, smoking.
- Both: BMI, smoking, alcohol, drugs, medications, occupational exposures.
- Coital frequency advice (every 2–3 days) and cycle-regularity cut-offs are (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked).

## 3. Core investigations
**Ovulation**
- **Mid-luteal progesterone** (~7 days before expected period, e.g. day 21 of a
  28-day cycle — adjust to cycle length). A level above ~30 nmol/L (~10 ng/mL)
  is commonly taken as confirming ovulation (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked); thresholds vary by lab.
- Irregular cycles: FSH, LH, oestradiol (early follicular), prolactin, TSH,
  testosterone/SHBG (PCOS), and assess for hypothalamic causes (low weight, exercise).

**Ovarian reserve** (predicts response to stimulation more than natural conception)
- **AMH** and/or **antral follicle count** on TVUS. Low AMH doesn't mean natural
  conception is impossible — counsel carefully.

**Semen analysis** (2–7 days abstinence, delivered within ~1 h at body temperature)
- WHO 2021 (6th edition) lower reference limits (5th centile): concentration 16 million/mL, total motility 42%, normal forms 4% (confirmed via PMC9527466, which quotes them); total number 39 million per ejaculate (same source); volume 1.4 mL and progressive motility 30% are (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked).
- Abnormal → repeat (ideally ~3 months later, or sooner if severe) and refer to
  andrology/urology for examination, hormones, genetics as indicated.

**Tubal patency**
- Low risk (no history of PID, ectopic, endometriosis, surgery): **HSG** or
  **HyCoSy** (ultrasound contrast).
- Higher risk: **laparoscopy with dye** (allows treatment of endometriosis/adhesions).
- Screen for chlamydia (or give prophylaxis) before uterine instrumentation.

**Also:** rubella immunity, cervical screening up to date, folic acid, TVUS for
uterine pathology (fibroids, polyps, Müllerian anomalies).

## 4. First-line pathways
- **Anovulation (PCOS):** lifestyle and weight optimisation where relevant;
  **letrozole** (commonly 2.5–7.5 mg days 3–7 (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked)) first-line ovulation induction, with
  monitoring; clomifene alternative; gonadotropins or ovarian drilling second-line.
- **Hypothalamic amenorrhoea:** restore energy balance; pulsatile GnRH or gonadotropins.
- **Hyperprolactinaemia:** dopamine agonist after imaging.
- **Unexplained / mild male factor:** expectant management or IUI per local
  guidance; IVF after a defined interval.
- **Tubal blockage, severe male factor (ICSI), reduced reserve with age, failed
  lower-level treatment:** IVF referral. Salpingectomy for hydrosalpinx before IVF.

## Gotchas
- Don't send the couple for tubal tests before a semen analysis — if severe male
  factor needs ICSI, tubal tests may be unnecessary.
- Age is the dominant prognostic factor; don't delay referral for someone in their late 30s.
- Ovulation induction without follicle monitoring risks multiple pregnancy and OHSS.
- Infertility is distressing; offer counselling and psychological support.

Uncertainty note: criteria from NICE CG156, ASRM and WHO 2021 semen manual; funding
and access criteria differ by system.
