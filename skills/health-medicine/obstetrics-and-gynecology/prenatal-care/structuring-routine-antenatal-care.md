# Structuring routine antenatal care

> General clinical/professional reference, not a substitute for independent clinical judgement, current guidelines, or verified dosing references.

**Goal:** give every pregnant patient a scheduled pathway that catches risk early,
offers screening at the right gestation, and moves anyone who becomes high-risk to the
right team.

## 1. Booking visit (ideally before 10 weeks (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked))
- **History:** obstetric (previous births, mode, complications — preeclampsia,
  preterm birth, PPH, stillbirth, GDM), medical (hypertension, diabetes, epilepsy,
  thyroid, VTE, cardiac, mental health), medications (switch teratogens; e.g.
  valproate, isotretinoin, warfarin, ACE inhibitors), family history, social (smoking,
  alcohol, substances, domestic abuse — ask when alone).
- **Measure:** BP, BMI, urine dipstick for protein.
- **Bloods:** FBC, blood group and antibodies, haemoglobinopathy screening (per
  local policy), infections (HIV, hepatitis B, syphilis; rubella/varicella immunity
  per local policy).
- **Supplements:** folic acid 400 mcg daily to 12 weeks (5 mg if higher risk — e.g.
  previous neural tube defect, diabetes, BMI ≥30, antiepileptics) (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked); vitamin D per
  local advice (NICE NG201 points to its vitamin D and maternal nutrition guidelines).
- **Risk triage:** midwife-led vs consultant-led care; document the plan.

## 2. Dating and screening by gestation (typical schedule)
| Gestation | Offer |
|---|---|
| 10–14 w (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked) | Dating ultrasound (CRL); combined screening (NT + hCG + PAPP-A) for trisomies; cfDNA/NIPT as first-line or contingent per system |
| 15–20 w | Quadruple test if booked late |
| 18–22 w | Anatomy (anomaly) scan |
| 24–28 w | GDM testing (OGTT between 24+0 and 28+0 weeks if at risk, NICE NG201 1.2.22, confirmed); universal testing per local policy |
| 28 w | FBC, blood group and antibodies, and anti-D prophylaxis if RhD-negative and not sensitised (NICE NG201 1.2.16, confirmed) |
| 16 w onwards | Each visit: BP, urine protein, fetal heart, movements, wellbeing; symphysis-fundal height from 24+0 w, no more often than every 2 weeks (NICE 1.2.30) |
Plus vaccines: NICE NG201 names flu, pertussis and RSV in pregnancy per national schedule; timing windows (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked).

## 3. Preeclampsia prevention
Offer **low-dose aspirin** (NICE NG133 1.1.2: 75–150 mg daily from 12 weeks until birth; some guidelines use up to 162 mg (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked)) if **one high-risk** factor (previous hypertensive pregnancy,
CKD, autoimmune disease, diabetes, chronic hypertension) or **two moderate** factors
(first pregnancy, age ≥40, interval >10 years, BMI ≥35, family history, multiple pregnancy).

## 4. Fundal height and growth
Measure symphysis-fundal height in cm from 24+0 weeks, plot on a chart (NICE NG201). A small-for-gestational-age measurement → offer a growth ultrasound (NICE 1.2.32); do not routinely scan after 28 weeks in uncomplicated singleton pregnancies (1.2.33). The 10th-centile cut-off and BMI >35 caveat are (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked).

## 5. Advice to give every time
When to call urgently: **reduced fetal movements** (don't wait until the next day),
bleeding, fluid leaking, abdominal pain, headache with visual disturbance or
epigastric pain or swelling (preeclampsia), itching of palms/soles (cholestasis),
and mental health concerns.

## Gotchas
- BP ≥140/90 on two readings after 20 w = gestational hypertension pathway; with
  proteinuria or organ involvement = preeclampsia — same day assessment.
- Late booking, missed appointments, and language barriers correlate with poor
  outcomes — provide interpreters and follow up non-attenders.
- Ask about mental health and domestic abuse at booking and later visits.

Uncertainty note: schedule and thresholds summarised from NICE NG201, ACOG and WHO
antenatal guidance; national screening programmes differ — follow local protocol.

## Verify
- [ ] Booking done with risk triage documented.
- [ ] Folic acid dose appropriate to risk.
- [ ] Screening offered at correct gestations with informed choice recorded.
- [ ] Aspirin criteria checked by 12 weeks.
- [ ] Each visit: BP, urine, growth, movements and red-flag advice.
