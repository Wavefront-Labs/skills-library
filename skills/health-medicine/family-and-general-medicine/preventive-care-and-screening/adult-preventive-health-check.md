# The adult preventive health check

**Goal:** in one structured visit, identify each person's evidence-based screening and
immunisation needs for their age, sex and risk, act on modifiable risk factors, and avoid
low-value tests. Programmes and intervals differ by country and are revised often —
**always confirm against the current national schedule** (USPSTF, UK NSC, national
immunisation schedule) before quoting numbers.

## 1. Build the risk profile (pre-visit questionnaire works well)
- Smoking (pack-years), alcohol (AUDIT-C), physical activity, diet, weight/BMI and waist.
- Family history: premature CVD (male <55, female <65), cancers (breast/ovarian, colorectal,
  prostate — age at diagnosis), diabetes, familial hypercholesterolaemia.
- Sexual health, pregnancy intention, mood screen (PHQ-2), falls (≥65).
- Prior screening results and dates, vaccination record.

## 2. Measurements
BP, BMI, pulse rhythm (AF opportunistically ≥65), lipids + HbA1c/glucose when indicated
by age/risk, urine ACR if hypertensive or diabetic.

## 3. Cardiovascular risk
- Calculate 10-year risk with the regional tool (QRISK3 UK, Pooled Cohort Equations US,
  SCORE2 Europe). Do not use them in established CVD, FH, or CKD where treatment is already indicated.
- Typical thresholds for offering a statin: QRISK3 ≥10% (NICE); PCE ≥7.5–10% with shared
  decision-making (ACC/AHA, USPSTF). Revisit with lifestyle first where appropriate.

## 4. Cancer and other screening (examples — verify current ages/intervals)
Schedules below are as understood in October 2026 and change often; the reader must confirm against the current USPSTF, UK NSC and NHS pages.

| Programme | Typical US (USPSTF) | Typical UK (NSC) |
|---|---|---|
| Cervical | 21–65: cytology q3y, or HPV-primary q5y from 30 | HPV-primary 25–64; England from 1 Jul 2025: HPV-negative 25–49 every 5 y (was 3), 50–64 every 5 y |
| Breast | Biennial mammography 40–74 | Mammography 50–71 q3y |
| Colorectal | 45–75 (FIT annual, colonoscopy q10y, others) | FIT 50–74 q2y (rolling age extension) |
| Lung | 50–80, ≥20 pack-years, current or quit <15y: annual LDCT | Targeted lung health check programme |
| AAA | Men 65–75 who ever smoked: one ultrasound | Men in year turning 65: one ultrasound |
| Prostate | 55–69: shared decision on PSA | No population programme; informed choice |
High-risk family history moves people to earlier/more intensive pathways — refer to genetics or
specialist protocols rather than the population schedule.

## 5. Immunisation catch-up
Check: influenza (annual), COVID-19 per current seasonal advice, Td/Tdap booster
status, pneumococcal (≥65 or risk groups), recombinant zoster (US ≥50; UK age cohorts),
HPV catch-up, hepatitis B for risk groups, MMR if no evidence of two doses, RSV in older
adults per current recommendations. Verify the schedule in force on the day.

## 6. Brief interventions (Very Brief Advice works)
- Smoking: **Ask–Advise–Act**: advise that stopping with support is most effective, refer/prescribe.
- Alcohol: feed back AUDIT-C score against low-risk limits.
- Activity: ~150 min/week moderate activity + strength twice weekly.

## Gotchas
- **Overdiagnosis** is a real harm (PSA, thyroid ultrasound, whole-body scans). Explain
  benefits *and* harms in absolute numbers; offer, don't impose.
- Do not order unvalidated "executive" panels (CA-125, tumour markers, routine ECG in
  asymptomatic low-risk adults) — USPSTF recommends against several.
- Screening stops: past upper age limits, benefit usually falls below harm; decide with
  life expectancy, not just age.
- Trans and non-binary patients: screen by organs present, not recorded sex.
- Screening tests are not for symptomatic people — symptoms go down the diagnostic pathway.

## Verify
- [ ] Screening status for each eligible programme recorded with next due date.
- [ ] CV risk score calculated and discussed, with decision documented.
- [ ] Vaccination record reconciled and catch-ups offered/booked.
- [ ] At least one brief intervention delivered where a risk factor was found.
- [ ] Schedule checked against the current national source, not memory.
