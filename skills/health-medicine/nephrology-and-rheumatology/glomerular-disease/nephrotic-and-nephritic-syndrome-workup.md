# Nephrotic and nephritic syndrome workup

> General clinical/professional reference, not a substitute for independent clinical judgement, current guidelines, or verified dosing references.

**Goal:** turn "protein and/or blood in the urine" into a syndrome, a short
differential and a biopsy decision — fast enough to rescue kidneys in rapidly
progressive glomerulonephritis (RPGN).

## 1. Look at the urine yourself
- Dipstick then **fresh urine microscopy**: dysmorphic red cells (acanthocytes) and
  **red cell casts** = glomerular bleeding. Fatty casts/oval fat bodies ("Maltese cross")
  = heavy proteinuria.
- Quantify: **uPCR** (or 24-h urine). Nephrotic-range ≥ 3.5 g/day (uPCR ≥ ~3 g/g,
  ≥ 300 mg/mmol). Check uACR for lower ranges.

## 2. Classify the syndrome
| | Nephrotic | Nephritic |
|---|---|---|
| Proteinuria | ≥ 3.5 g/day | Variable, usually < 3.5 |
| Haematuria | Minimal | Prominent, casts |
| Other | Hypoalbuminaemia, oedema, hyperlipidaemia, thrombosis risk | Hypertension, oedema, rising creatinine, oliguria |
| Classic causes | Minimal change, FSGS, membranous, diabetic, amyloid | IgA, post-infectious, lupus, ANCA vasculitis, anti-GBM, MPGN/C3G |
Many diseases overlap (lupus, MPGN, IgA can be both).

**RPGN** (creatinine rising over days–weeks with active sediment) is an emergency:
same-day nephrology, urgent serology and biopsy; don't wait for biopsy to start
treatment if anti-GBM or ANCA vasculitis is likely with organ threat (e.g. pulmonary
haemorrhage).

## 3. Structured blood panel
- Creatinine, albumin, lipids, FBC, glucose/HbA1c, LFTs, clotting.
- **Complement C3 and C4:** low in lupus (both), post-infectious GN and C3
  glomerulopathy (C3), cryoglobulinaemia (C4), MPGN. Normal in IgA, ANCA, anti-GBM,
  membranous, MCD/FSGS.
- **ANCA** (MPO/PR3 by ELISA), **anti-GBM**, **ANA/anti-dsDNA**.
- **Anti-PLA2R** (± THSD7A) for membranous nephropathy.
- **Serum free light chains + serum protein electrophoresis/immunofixation** (amyloid,
  light chain deposition, monoclonal gammopathy of renal significance), especially if
  > ~50 years.
- Hepatitis B, hepatitis C, HIV; ASO/anti-DNase B if post-streptococcal suspected;
  cryoglobulins (warm sample).
- Renal ultrasound (size, two kidneys, no obstruction) before biopsy.

## 4. Biopsy decision
Usually indicated for nephrotic syndrome in adults, nephritic syndrome, unexplained
AKI with active sediment, and to guide immunosuppression. Exceptions/considerations:
- **High-titre anti-PLA2R** with typical nephrotic picture and normal eGFR may allow
  diagnosis of membranous without biopsy (KDIGO 2021).
- Children with typical nephrotic syndrome → steroid trial before biopsy (likely MCD).
- Long-standing diabetes with retinopathy and gradual albuminuria → diabetic kidney
  disease without biopsy unless atypical (active sediment, rapid decline, no retinopathy).
- Pre-biopsy: BP control, clotting/platelets, stop antiplatelets/anticoagulants per
  protocol, consent for bleeding risk.
- Biopsy needs light microscopy, immunofluorescence and electron microscopy.

## 5. Supportive care for everyone (while awaiting diagnosis)
- BP control; **ACE-I/ARB** for proteinuria (if not hypotensive/AKI); SGLT2i for
  persistent proteinuria in many CKD-type GN (e.g. IgA) per KDIGO updates.
- Oedema: loop diuretics (high doses often needed; albumin binding), salt restriction.
- **Anticoagulation** consideration in nephrotic syndrome with very low albumin
  (e.g. membranous with albumin < ~2.0–2.5 g/dL) balanced against bleeding risk.
- Statin for hyperlipidaemia; vaccinations and *Pneumocystis* prophylaxis when starting
  immunosuppression; bone protection with steroids.

## Gotchas
- Dipstick detects albumin only — light-chain proteinuria may be missed; use uPCR.
- ANCA can be negative in some pauci-immune GN; anti-GBM and ANCA can coexist (check both).
- Infection-related GN (e.g. endocarditis) can mimic vasculitis — blood cultures before
  immunosuppression.
- Nephrotic patients are prone to infection, AKI from over-diuresis and VTE.

## Verify
- [ ] Urine microscopy done; proteinuria quantified.
- [ ] Syndrome classified; RPGN excluded or escalated same day.
- [ ] Complement, ANCA, anti-GBM, ANA/dsDNA, PLA2R, light chains, viral serology sent.
- [ ] Biopsy decision documented with exceptions reasoned.
- [ ] Supportive therapy (BP, RAS blockade, diuretics, VTE assessment) started.
