# Four examinations to pattern differentiation

*General clinical/professional reference, not a substitute for independent clinical judgement, current guidelines, or verified dosing references.*

**Goal:** collect the four examinations systematically, reduce them to one or two named patterns (bian zheng) with stated supporting signs, and derive a treatment principle — while making sure the patient has had, or is sent for, appropriate biomedical diagnosis.

## 1. Biomedical safety first
TCM pattern diagnosis is not a disease diagnosis and cannot exclude serious pathology. Before or alongside it, screen for red flags (unexplained weight loss, haemoptysis, rectal bleeding, chest pain, new neurological signs, persistent fever, lumps) and refer. Record any existing medical diagnosis and medications.

## 2. The four examinations (si zhen)
| Examination | What to record |
|---|---|
| **Looking** | Shen (spirit/vitality), complexion colour, body build, movement; **tongue**: body colour (pale, red, purple), shape (swollen, thin, tooth-marked, cracked), coating (thin/thick, white/yellow, absent/peeled), moisture, sublingual veins |
| **Listening/smelling** | Voice strength, breathing, cough quality, odours |
| **Asking** (ten questions) | Chills/fever, sweating, head and body, stool and urine, appetite and taste, thirst and drink, sleep, chest/abdomen, ears/eyes, menstruation/gynaecology; plus emotions and lifestyle |
| **Palpating** | **Pulse** at cun/guan/chi on both wrists, three depths; rate (count beats in 60 s), strength, depth, width, rhythm, quality (e.g. wiry, slippery, choppy, thready); abdomen and channels |

Tongue: examine in daylight, before coloured food/drink, tongue relaxed for under 15 s. Photograph with consent for later comparison.

## 3. Organise: eight principles
Answer four pairs: **interior/exterior**, **cold/heat**, **deficiency/excess**, **yin/yang**. Example: pale swollen tooth-marked tongue, weak pulse, loose stools, fatigue → interior, cold, deficiency.

## 4. Refine: substance and organ
- Qi, blood, fluids: deficiency, stagnation, stasis, dampness/phlegm.
- Zang-fu: locate in the organ system whose functional signs dominate (e.g. Spleen qi deficiency for digestion, fatigue, loose stools).
- Add pathogenic factors (wind, cold, damp, heat, dryness) for acute or exterior presentations.

## 5. Write the pattern statement
Format: **Primary pattern** (root) + **secondary pattern** (branch) + supporting signs for each + conflicting signs noted.
Example: "Spleen qi deficiency with damp accumulation (root); Liver qi stagnation (branch) — supporting: ...; conflicting: slightly rapid pulse."

## 6. Treatment principle
Derive directly from the pattern: tonify Spleen qi, resolve damp, smooth Liver qi. Decide root vs branch priority (acute branch first if severe). The principle then drives point selection (`health-medicine.complementary-and-traditional-medicine.acupuncture.safe-acupuncture-treatment-session`) or formula choice (`health-medicine.complementary-and-traditional-medicine.chinese-herbal-medicine.classical-formula-selection-and-modification`).

## 7. Reassess
Re-examine tongue and pulse each visit; patterns change. Track a symptom measure (e.g. MYMOP) so progress is not judged only by tongue/pulse.

## Gotchas
- Inter-rater reliability of tongue and pulse diagnosis in studies is modest — different practitioners often reach different patterns. Anchor conclusions in several concordant signs, not one.
- Medications distort signs: steroids, antibiotics, beta-blockers (pulse rate), smoking and coffee (coating).
- Do not translate patterns into biomedical claims ("your Kidney is weak" is not kidney disease) — explain the terminology to avoid alarm.
- ICD-11 Chapter 26 codes TM patterns for documentation only; they sit alongside, not instead of, a disease code.

## Verify
- [ ] Red flags screened; biomedical diagnosis and medications recorded.
- [ ] All four examinations documented, including tongue description and bilateral pulse.
- [ ] Eight-principles summary stated.
- [ ] Pattern statement lists supporting and conflicting signs.
- [ ] Treatment principle follows logically and a symptom outcome measure is set.
