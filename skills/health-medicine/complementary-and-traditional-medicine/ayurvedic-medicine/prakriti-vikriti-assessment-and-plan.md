# Prakriti–vikriti assessment and treatment plan

*General clinical/professional reference, not a substitute for independent clinical judgement, current guidelines, or verified dosing references.*

**Goal:** distinguish the patient's lifelong constitution (prakriti) from the current dosha imbalance (vikriti), state agni and ama, and give a dietary, routine and herbal plan that is safe with their medicines and free of contaminated products.

## 1. Safety intake
Medical diagnoses, medications, pregnancy, kidney/liver disease, eating-disorder history. Red flags (weight loss, bleeding, chest pain, neurological signs, persistent fever) → medical referral before Ayurvedic treatment.

## 2. Eightfold examination (ashtavidha pariksha)
Pulse (nadi), urine (mutra), stool (mala), tongue (jihva), voice (shabda), skin/touch (sparsha), eyes (drik), general appearance (akriti). Add questioning on appetite, digestion, sleep, energy, emotions and seasonal pattern.

## 3. Prakriti vs vikriti
| Dosha | Prakriti traits (stable since youth) | Vikriti signs (current) |
|---|---|---|
| Vata (air/space) | Thin frame, variable appetite, quick, cold hands | Dry skin, constipation, gas, anxiety, insomnia, joint cracking |
| Pitta (fire/water) | Medium build, strong appetite, warm, focused | Heartburn, loose stools, rashes, irritability, heat intolerance |
| Kapha (water/earth) | Solid build, steady, slow digestion | Heaviness, congestion, weight gain, lethargy, oversleeping |

Ask about traits across life for prakriti; recent change for vikriti. Write both as ratios (e.g. prakriti V2 P3 K1; vikriti V↑). Treat the vikriti; prakriti guides long-term lifestyle. Structured questionnaires exist but reliability varies — state the method used.

## 4. Agni and ama
Assess digestive fire (regular, variable, sharp, slow) and ama (thick tongue coating, heaviness, poor appetite). Correcting agni and clearing ama usually precedes tonification.

## 5. Plan
- **Diet (ahara):** foods with opposing qualities to the aggravated dosha — warm, moist, grounding for vata; cooling, less spicy for pitta; light, warm, less sweet/oily for kapha. Keep it nutritionally adequate; avoid restrictive diets in eating-disorder history, diabetes or frailty without medical input.
- **Routine (dinacharya):** regular meal and sleep times, seasonal adjustment, appropriate exercise and yoga/pranayama.
- **Herbs:** prefer single herbs or classical churnas from tested suppliers (e.g. triphala, ashwagandha). Check interactions: ashwagandha with thyroid medication, sedatives, immunosuppressants, and reports of liver injury; guggul with anticoagulants and thyroid drugs; turmeric high-dose extracts with anticoagulants and reports of hepatotoxicity.

## 6. Contamination — non-negotiable
Rasashastra (herbo-mineral) preparations may deliberately contain lead, mercury or arsenic; Saper et al. found about 1 in 5 internet-sold products contained detectable heavy metals. Use only products with a certificate of analysis for heavy metals; do not prescribe bhasmas unless your jurisdiction permits and you can document testing. If a patient on Ayurvedic products has abdominal pain, anaemia, neuropathy or fatigue, suggest a blood lead level via their doctor.

## 7. Panchakarma
Cleansing therapies (vamana emesis, virechana purgation, basti enema, nasya, raktamokshana) carry real risks: dehydration, electrolyte disturbance, aspiration, perforation. Not for frail, pregnant, cardiac, renal or eating-disorder patients; require medical clearance and supervised setting. Gentle preparatory oleation (snehana) and steam (swedana) are lower risk but watch heat in cardiovascular disease.

## Gotchas
- Evidence from rigorous trials is limited for most Ayurvedic interventions; consent must say so.
- Ghee-based oleation can raise lipids and glucose.
- Patients may bring products bought abroad or online; ask to see them.
- Do not advise stopping prescribed medication.

## Verify
- [ ] Red flags and medications recorded; referral where needed.
- [ ] Eightfold exam documented; prakriti and vikriti stated separately.
- [ ] Diet plan nutritionally adequate and suited to medical conditions.
- [ ] Every product has a heavy-metal certificate; interactions checked.
- [ ] Panchakarma only with clearance; review date set.
