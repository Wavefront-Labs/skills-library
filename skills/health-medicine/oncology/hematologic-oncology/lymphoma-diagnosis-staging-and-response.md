# Lymphoma diagnosis, staging and response

**Goal:** reach a precise lymphoma subtype on adequate tissue, stage and risk-stratify it
with standard tools, and judge response so treatment can be escalated or stopped.

## 1. Get the right tissue
- **Excisional lymph-node biopsy or generous image-guided core biopsies.** FNA alone is
  inadequate for primary diagnosis because architecture matters.
- Choose the most accessible **representative** node; PET-CT before biopsy can direct it
  to the most avid site if transformation is suspected.
- Send fresh tissue where possible for flow cytometry, cytogenetics/FISH (for example
  MYC, BCL2, BCL6 rearrangements) and molecular tests.
- Classify with the current **WHO 5th edition or ICC 2022**; they differ in places, so
  state which is used. Review by a haematopathology specialist is standard.

## 2. Stage with Lugano (modified Ann Arbor)
| Stage | Involvement |
|---|---|
| I | One node or group of adjacent nodes, or single extranodal lesion without nodal disease |
| II | Two or more nodal groups on the same side of the diaphragm (II bulky has disease-specific handling) |
| III | Nodes on both sides of the diaphragm, or nodes above diaphragm with spleen involvement |
| IV | Additional non-contiguous extralymphatic involvement |

- **PET-CT** is the staging standard for FDG-avid histologies (Hodgkin, DLBCL, most
  follicular); CT for non-avid types (for example small lymphocytic lymphoma).
- **B symptoms** (fevers, drenching night sweats, weight loss over 10% in 6 months)
  are recorded; under Lugano the A/B suffix is retained mainly for Hodgkin lymphoma.
- **Bulk** definitions vary by histology (often around 10 cm for DLBCL); check the
  disease-specific threshold.
- Bone marrow biopsy is often omitted when PET-CT is done in Hodgkin and DLBCL, but
  still used in other subtypes.
- Baseline bloods: FBC, LDH, renal/liver function, urate, HIV, hepatitis B and C
  serology (rituximab can reactivate hepatitis B).

## 3. Risk-stratify
- **IPI** for DLBCL (age over 60, LDH above normal, ECOG 2 or more, stage III–IV, more
  than one extranodal site); R-IPI and NCCN-IPI are refinements.
- **FLIPI / FLIPI-2** for follicular lymphoma.
- Hodgkin: early favourable/unfavourable and advanced-stage groupings (EORTC/GHSG
  criteria); IPS for advanced disease.
- Consider CNS-relapse risk (CNS-IPI) in DLBCL.

## 4. Treat according to subtype (outline only)
- DLBCL: rituximab-based chemoimmunotherapy (R-CHOP or newer variants such as
  pola-R-CHP); radiotherapy selectively.
- Hodgkin: ABVD-based or escalated BEACOPP-type approaches, PET-adapted; newer
  regimens incorporate brentuximab or checkpoint inhibitors.
- Indolent lymphoma: watch-and-wait if low tumour burden (GELF-type criteria), else
  rituximab-based therapy.
- Relapsed/refractory aggressive disease: salvage chemotherapy and autologous transplant,
  bispecific antibodies, or **CAR-T cell therapy** in specialist centres.
Regimens and doses: per current guideline and local protocol. Assess **tumour lysis**
risk before the first cycle.

## 5. Assess response with Deauville
PET response uses the **Deauville five-point scale** relative to mediastinal blood pool
and liver:
1 no uptake; 2 at or below mediastinum; 3 above mediastinum but at or below liver;
4 moderately above liver; 5 markedly above liver and/or new lesions.
- Scores 1–3 generally indicate complete metabolic response at end of treatment
  (interpretation of 3 depends on context and trial).
- 4–5 with reduced uptake = partial metabolic response; unchanged = no response;
  increased or new = progressive metabolic disease.

## 6. CAR-T specifics
Grade **cytokine release syndrome** and **ICANS** with the ASTCT consensus scales;
manage with tocilizumab and/or corticosteroids per centre protocol. Monitor for
prolonged cytopenias, hypogammaglobulinaemia and infection.

## Gotchas
- Steroids before biopsy can melt the lymphoma and ruin the diagnosis.
- Reactive nodes, sarcoidosis and infection are PET-avid; correlate.
- Always screen hepatitis B before anti-CD20 therapy.
- Transformation of indolent lymphoma presents as rapid growth, rising LDH or a single
  intensely avid node: rebiopsy.

## Verify
- [ ] Diagnosis on adequate tissue reviewed by haematopathology; classification named.
- [ ] Lugano stage, bulk and extranodal sites documented with PET-CT where appropriate.
- [ ] Prognostic index calculated; hepatitis B/C and HIV screened.
- [ ] Tumour lysis risk assessed before treatment.
- [ ] Interim/end-of-treatment PET scored with Deauville and acted on.
