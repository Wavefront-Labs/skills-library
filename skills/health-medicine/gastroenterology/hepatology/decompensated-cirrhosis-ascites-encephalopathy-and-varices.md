# Decompensated cirrhosis: ascites, encephalopathy and varices

> General clinical reference, not patient-specific advice. Drug doses (diuretics, beta-blockers, terlipressin, antibiotics) must be checked against current guidelines and the local formulary.

**Goal:** recognise each decompensating event early, treat it by the established
bundle, look for the trigger, and refer for transplant assessment before it is too late.

## 0. Every admission: find the precipitant
Infection (check ascitic fluid even without abdominal signs), GI bleeding, alcohol,
drugs (sedatives, NSAIDs, nephrotoxins), constipation, electrolyte disturbance,
dehydration/over-diuresis, new portal vein thrombosis or **HCC**.

## 1. Ascites
- **Diagnostic paracentesis** in every admitted patient with ascites and any new
  ascites: cell count with **neutrophils**, albumin (for SAAG), total protein, culture
  in blood-culture bottles at the bedside.
- **SAAG ≥ 11 g/L (1.1 g/dL)** = portal hypertension.
- **SBP** = ascitic neutrophils **> 250 cells/mm³** → empirical antibiotics per local
  policy **plus IV albumin** (reduces renal failure and mortality).
- Uncomplicated: sodium restriction (moderate, not severe), spironolactone ± furosemide;
  stop diuretics for AKI, severe hyponatraemia or encephalopathy.
- **Large-volume paracentesis** for tense/refractory ascites, with **albumin replacement**
  (typically ~8 g per litre removed when > 5 L — verify locally).
- Coagulopathy is **not** a contraindication to paracentesis; routine FFP/platelets are
  not needed.
- Refractory ascites → consider TIPS and transplant referral.

## 2. Hepatic encephalopathy
- Clinical diagnosis (West Haven grade); ammonia level is not required and a normal value
  helps exclude it more than a high one confirms it.
- Exclude other causes: hypoglycaemia, sepsis, intracranial bleed, drugs, alcohol withdrawal.
- **Lactulose** titrated to 2–3 soft stools/day; add **rifaximin** for recurrence.
- Grade 3–4 → airway protection, HDU/ICU.

## 3. Varices
- **Primary prophylaxis:** non-selective beta-blocker (Baveno VII favours **carvedilol**)
  for clinically significant portal hypertension; band ligation if intolerant.
- **Acute variceal bleeding** bundle:
  - Restrictive transfusion (target Hb ~70–80 g/L) — over-transfusion raises portal pressure.
  - **Vasoactive drug** (terlipressin, octreotide or somatostatin) started on suspicion.
  - **Antibiotic prophylaxis** (e.g. ceftriaxone) — reduces rebleeding and death.
  - Endoscopy within 12 h with **band ligation**.
  - **Pre-emptive TIPS** within 72 h in high-risk patients (Child-Pugh C 10–13, or
    B > 7 with active bleeding at endoscopy) where available.
  - Uncontrolled bleeding: balloon tamponade or oesophageal stent as a bridge to TIPS.
- Secondary prophylaxis: beta-blocker + repeat banding to eradication.

## 4. Kidney injury
- **HRS-AKI** is a diagnosis of exclusion: stop diuretics/nephrotoxins, give albumin
  volume expansion, exclude shock, parenchymal disease and obstruction.
- Treat with vasoconstrictor (terlipressin, or noradrenaline in ICU) plus albumin; watch
  for terlipressin respiratory toxicity (fluid overload, hypoxia).

## 5. Prognosis and referral
- Score with **Child-Pugh** and **MELD/MELD-Na** (or MELD 3.0).
- Refer for **transplant assessment** at first major decompensation or MELD ≥ ~15
  (local criteria vary); alcohol-related disease needs a documented abstinence and
  support plan — timing rules differ between centres.
- Recognise **acute-on-chronic liver failure (ACLF)**: organ failures + high short-term
  mortality → ICU and transplant centre discussion.
- **HCC surveillance:** ultrasound ± AFP every 6 months.

## Gotchas
- A "normal" creatinine overstates kidney function in sarcopenic cirrhotics.
- Albumin infusions for low serum albumin alone are not indicated.
- Proton-pump inhibitors are over-prescribed in cirrhosis and linked to SBP/HE — review.
- Avoid NSAIDs, aminoglycosides and benzodiazepines.

## Verify
- [ ] Precipitant actively sought, including diagnostic tap.
- [ ] SBP treated with antibiotics + albumin.
- [ ] Variceal bleed bundle: vasoactive, antibiotics, restrictive Hb, endoscopy ≤ 12 h, TIPS considered.
- [ ] Diuretics/nephrotoxins stopped in AKI; HRS criteria applied.
- [ ] MELD calculated and transplant referral decision documented.
