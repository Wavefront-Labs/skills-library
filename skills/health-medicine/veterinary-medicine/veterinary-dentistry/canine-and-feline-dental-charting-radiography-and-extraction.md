# Canine and feline dental charting, radiography and extraction

> General clinical/professional reference, not a substitute for independent clinical judgement, current guidelines, or verified dosing references.

**Goal:** find all the oral disease (most of it is below the gum line), chart it in a
standard language, and remove teeth completely without fracturing roots or jaws.

## 1. Conscious exam and planning
- Awake exam gives only a partial picture; estimates of treatment and cost should say
  so. Full assessment requires **COHAT** (comprehensive oral health assessment and
  treatment) under general anaesthesia with a cuffed endotracheal tube and
  throat pack.
- Anaesthesia-free "dentals" are not acceptable — no subgingival cleaning, no
  radiographs, airway unprotected (AAHA/WSAVA).

## 2. Charting (modified Triadan)
- Quadrants: 1 right maxilla, 2 left maxilla, 3 left mandible, 4 right mandible; teeth
  numbered from midline (01 = first incisor; **04 = canine; 08 = fourth premolar
  (108 = right upper carnassial); 09 = first molar (309 = left lower carnassial)**). Cats lack
  some premolars/molars — numbering still follows the "rule of 4 and 9" (canines 04,
  first molars 09).
- Record for every tooth: missing, fractured (enamel/complicated with pulp exposure),
  mobility (M0–M3), furcation exposure (F1–F3), **periodontal probing depth** at
  several points, gingival recession, resorption, discolouration.
- Normal probing depth: dog < 3 mm, cat < 0.5–1 mm.

## 3. Periodontal stage (AVDC)
PD0 normal; PD1 gingivitis only; PD2 < 25% attachment loss; PD3 25–50%; PD4 > 50%.
Decide by probing **and** radiographs.

## 4. Full-mouth intraoral radiographs
- Every patient (studies find clinically important findings in a large share of
  teeth that looked normal). Digital sensor or phosphor plate.
- Parallel technique for mandibular premolars/molars; **bisecting-angle** technique
  elsewhere (beam perpendicular to the line bisecting the tooth and sensor angle) to
  avoid elongation/foreshortening.
- Look for: bone loss (horizontal/vertical), periapical lucency, retained roots,
  **tooth resorption** (type 1 vs type 2 in cats), unerupted teeth (dentigerous cysts),
  fractures.

## 5. Local anaesthesia
Regional nerve blocks reduce inhalant requirement and post-op pain: **infraorbital**,
**caudal maxillary**, **middle mental**, **inferior alveolar** (avoid tongue
desensitisation by careful technique — self-trauma risk especially bilateral). Aspirate
before injecting; calculate total local-anaesthetic dose per kg (cats are sensitive).

## 6. Extraction technique (multirooted and canines)
1. Radiograph first.
2. **Mucoperiosteal flap** (envelope or with releasing incisions at line angles).
3. Remove buccal alveolar bone (conservatively) with a bur under irrigation.
4. **Section multirooted teeth** into single-rooted pieces (start at furcation).
5. Elevate each root with **steady rotational force held 10–15 s**, no levering against
   neighbouring teeth; luxate to break the periodontal ligament.
6. Remove root; **post-extraction radiograph** to confirm complete removal.
7. Smooth alveolar bone (alveoloplasty), debride, close **tension-free** with
   absorbable suture (e.g. 4-0/5-0 monofilament).
- Mandibular canine and first molar in small dogs: jaw fracture risk — careful
  technique, radiograph the bone height first.
- **Feline tooth resorption:** type 1 → full extraction; **type 2 (roots replaced by
  bone)** → crown amputation with intentional root retention may be acceptable if no
  periodontitis, endodontic disease or stomatitis — radiographs decide.

## 7. Aftercare
Analgesia (multimodal, NSAID where safe), soft food 1–2 weeks, recheck, home care
plan (daily toothbrushing is the gold standard; VOHC-accepted products).

## Gotchas
- Charting from the conscious exam only.
- Skipping radiographs → retained roots, missed resorption.
- Levering roots against adjacent teeth → fractures.
- Crown amputation on a type 1 resorption.

## Verify
- [ ] Full chart with Triadan numbers, probing depths, mobility, furcation.
- [ ] Full-mouth radiographs taken and reviewed before extraction.
- [ ] Nerve blocks and local-anaesthetic total dose documented.
- [ ] Post-extraction radiographs confirm complete removal.
- [ ] Home-care plan and recheck date given.
