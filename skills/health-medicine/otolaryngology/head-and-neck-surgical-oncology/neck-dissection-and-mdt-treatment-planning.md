# Neck dissection and MDT treatment planning

> General clinical/professional reference, not a substitute for independent clinical judgement, current guidelines (e.g. NCCN, national MDT guidance) or local protocols. Not patient-specific advice.

**Goal:** stage the patient completely before anyone decides on treatment, give the MDT
a resection-and-reconstruction plan it can compare with (chemo)radiotherapy, and do a
neck dissection that removes the right levels while sparing what doesn't need removing.

## 1. Work-up before MDT
- **Tissue diagnosis** of the primary (biopsy, under GA panendoscopy if needed), and
  **p16/HPV** status for every oropharyngeal tumour. HPV-positive disease uses a
  separate AJCC 8 staging system and usually has a better prognosis.
- **Imaging:** MRI for oral cavity/oropharynx soft tissue and perineural spread, CT for
  larynx/bone, CT chest (or PET-CT for advanced stage or unknown primary).
- **Dental assessment** before radiotherapy (extract teeth that can't be saved, to cut
  osteoradionecrosis risk). Add nutrition, speech and swallow baseline, and smoking/alcohol
  support.
- Fitness: performance status, comorbidity, frailty, and patient priorities (voice,
  swallowing, appearance).

## 2. What the MDT is deciding
- Primary surgery ± adjuvant (C)RT **vs** primary (C)RT with surgery for salvage.
- Rough patterns (they vary by centre and evidence changes, so treat this as orientation):
  oral cavity → surgery first; early larynx → transoral laser or RT; advanced larynx →
  organ preservation CRT vs total laryngectomy (cartilage destruction/poor function often
  favours laryngectomy); oropharynx → TORS or CRT, with de-escalation trials ongoing for HPV+.
- Record the decision, the reasoning, and the planned surveillance in the MDT outcome.

## 3. Neck dissection: pick by levels
| Type | Removes | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| Selective (SND) | Chosen levels, e.g. I–III (supraomohyoid) for oral cavity, II–IV for larynx/oropharynx | Elective cN0 neck, limited N+ |
| Modified radical (MRND) | I–V, keeps ≥1 of SCM, IJV, accessory nerve | Bulky N+ |
| Radical | I–V + SCM, IJV, CN XI | Rare: nerve/vein invaded |

- **Elective neck treatment** for cN0 when occult metastasis risk is high (commonly quoted
  threshold ~15–20%). For oral tongue, **depth of invasion > ~3–4 mm** pushes towards an
  elective neck. **Sentinel node biopsy** is a validated alternative for early oral cancer
  in experienced centres.
- Level IIb and V add morbidity (shoulder). Include them only when the indication supports it.
- After primary CRT, a **PET-CT at ~12 weeks** decides whether to dissect the neck
  (PET-NECK approach). Don't plan routine planned neck dissection by default.

## 4. Margins and pathology that change adjuvant therapy
- Aim for clear margins (often defined as ≥5 mm on histology; definitions vary). Frozen
  sections help but are taken from the **specimen** side where possible.
- **Positive margins or extranodal extension (ENE)** → adjuvant chemoradiotherapy is
  generally indicated. Others (pT3–4, multiple nodes, perineural/lymphovascular invasion)
  → adjuvant RT discussed.
- Orient and ink the specimen. Label the neck levels separately for the pathologist.

## 5. Reconstruction planning
- Small defects: primary closure or local flap. Larger oral/pharyngeal defects: **radial
  forearm** (thin, pliable) or **ALT** free flap. Mandible: **fibula** (check leg
  vasculature with CTA). Plan vessels in the neck before you take them in the dissection.

## Complications to watch
- **Chyle leak** (left level IV, milky drain output that rises with feeding): low-fat/MCT
  diet, pressure, octreotide in some protocols, re-exploration if high volume.
- Accessory nerve injury → shoulder drop and pain; offer physio.
- Carotid blowout risk in irradiated, fistulous necks. Flap failure: monitor
  closely for the first 24–72 h.
- Hypocalcaemia/hypothyroidism after laryngectomy or central neck work.

## Gotchas
- Treating an "unknown primary" before tonsillectomy/tongue-base mucosectomy and PET-CT.
- Starting surgery without the reconstruction team booked.
- Delays between surgery and adjuvant RT; aim to start RT within ~6 weeks of surgery.

## Verify
- [ ] Histology, p16 (oropharynx), full staging and dental review done before MDT.
- [ ] MDT outcome lists treatment intent, modality and reasoning.
- [ ] Neck dissection levels match primary site and nodal risk; levels labelled for pathology.
- [ ] Margin and ENE status reviewed and adjuvant decision re-taken at post-op MDT.
- [ ] Surveillance plan (PET-CT timing, clinic schedule) recorded.
