# LDL cholesterol lowering strategy

**Goal:** match the depth of LDL-C lowering to absolute cardiovascular risk, get there
fast with well-tolerated combinations, and catch genetic causes (FH, high Lp(a)) that
standard risk calculators miss.

## 1. Measure properly and exclude secondary causes
- Full lipid profile (non-fasting is acceptable for most); LDL-C by Friedewald is
  unreliable when TG >400 mg/dL (4.5 mmol/L (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked)) or LDL-C very low — use direct/Martin-Hopkins or non-HDL-C/apoB.
- Secondary causes: hypothyroidism (check TSH), nephrotic syndrome, cholestasis, poorly
  controlled diabetes, alcohol, drugs (glucocorticoids, retinoids, antiretrovirals, ciclosporin).
- Measure **Lp(a) at least once** in adulthood; ≥50 mg/dL is a risk modifier in the ESC/EAS 2025 focused update (secondary source; (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked)); nmol/L equivalents are not directly convertible.

## 2. Stratify risk and set the target (ESC/EAS)
| Risk category | Examples | LDL-C goal |
|---|---|---|
| Very high | Established ASCVD, diabetes with organ damage, severe CKD (eGFR <30), FH with ASCVD (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked) | **<1.4 mmol/L (55 mg/dL)** and ≥50% reduction |
| High | Markedly raised single factor (e.g. LDL-C >4.9 mmol/L), FH without ASCVD, moderate CKD (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked) | <1.8 mmol/L (70 mg/dL) and ≥50% reduction |
| Moderate | Younger diabetes without risk factors, moderate calculated risk | <2.6 mmol/L (100 mg/dL) |
| Low | Low calculated risk | <3.0 mmol/L (116 mg/dL) |
Goals confirmed in the ESC/EAS 2019 guideline text (Eur Heart J 2020;41:111) and unchanged in the 2025 focused update per ESC; the "<1.0 mmol/L (<40 mg/dL)" goal may be considered after a second vascular event within 2 years on maximal statin.
Use SCORE2/SCORE2-OP (Europe), PCE (US) or QRISK3 (UK) for primary prevention. US
guidance uses intensity and thresholds (e.g. LDL-C ≥70 mg/dL to add non-statins) rather than
identical targets; NICE uses a % reduction and non-HDL-C goal — follow the local framework.

## 3. Stepwise therapy (verify against local formulary/guideline)
1. **Lifestyle** in all (diet, weight, activity, smoking cessation).
2. **Statin** — high intensity for very high/high risk: atorvastatin 40–80 mg or
   rosuvastatin 20–40 mg (~≥50% LDL-C reduction). Moderate intensity ~30–49% (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked).
3. **Ezetimibe 10 mg** if not at goal (~20–25% further reduction (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked)). Upfront statin + ezetimibe
   is reasonable when baseline LDL-C is far from goal (e.g. after ACS).
4. **PCSK9 monoclonal antibody** (evolocumab 140 mg every 2 weeks or 420 mg monthly (US label);
   alirocumab US label 75 mg every 2 weeks or 300 mg every 4 weeks, adjustable to 150 mg every 2 weeks) ~50–60% further (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked); or **inclisiran** (siRNA) 284 mg SC
   at 0, 3 months, then every 6 months (US label, adults and ages 12+). Access criteria often restrict use.
5. **Bempedoic acid 180 mg** (US label: with ezetimibe 10 mg as one daily tablet, Nexlizet; lipids 8–12 weeks after starting) for statin-intolerant patients (CLEAR Outcomes showed CV benefit (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked)).
- Recheck lipids **4–12 weeks** after each change (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked), then every 6–12 months; adjust until at goal.

## 4. Familial hypercholesterolaemia (FH)
- Suspect when LDL-C ≥4.9 mmol/L (190 mg/dL) in adults (lower in children), tendon xanthomas,
  premature ASCVD in patient or first-degree relative.
- Score with **Dutch Lipid Clinic Network** (>8 definite, 6–8 probable, 3–5 possible (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked)) or
  **Simon Broome**; offer genetic testing and **cascade screening** of relatives.
- Treat to high/very-high-risk targets; most need combination therapy. Homozygous FH needs a specialist centre.

## Gotchas
- **Statin intolerance** is often nocebo or reversible: stop, rechallenge with a different statin,
  lower dose or alternate-day rosuvastatin/atorvastatin. Check TSH (hypothyroidism
  worsens myopathy) and CK if symptomatic; stop if CK >10× ULN or rhabdomyolysis (ESC text describes rhabdomyolysis as CK ≥10× ULN, often ≥40×; the 10× stopping rule itself (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked)).
- Drug interactions raise statin levels: CYP3A4 inhibitors (clarithromycin, azoles, protease
  inhibitors), ciclosporin, gemfibrozil; simvastatin is most affected.
- Statins are contraindicated in pregnancy/breastfeeding — counsel on contraception.
- Severe hypertriglyceridaemia (≥10 mmol/L / ~880 mg/dL (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked)) is a pancreatitis emergency, not an
  LDL problem: strict fat restriction, fibrate, treat secondary causes.
- Normal LDL-C does not exclude risk from raised Lp(a) or apoB (insulin resistance).

## Verify
- [ ] Secondary causes excluded; Lp(a) measured once.
- [ ] Risk category and LDL-C goal (or % reduction) documented using the local framework.
- [ ] Statin intensity matches risk; adherence checked before adding drugs.
- [ ] Follow-up lipid test booked 4–12 weeks after each change.
- [ ] FH considered and cascade screening offered when criteria met.
