# Adrenal incidentaloma workup

**Goal:** answer two questions for every adrenal mass found on imaging done for another
reason — **is it malignant?** and **is it hormonally active?** — then stop follow-up of
the large majority that are benign, non-functioning adenomas.

## 1. Imaging phenotype (is it malignant?)
- **Unenhanced CT attenuation ≤10 HU** and homogeneous = lipid-rich adenoma (benign).
  ESE/ENSAT 2023: such masses need **no further imaging, independent of size**.
- If >10 HU ("indeterminate"), options:
  - Contrast washout CT: absolute washout ≥60% or relative ≥40% supports adenoma (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked)
    (specificity is debated; less relied on in recent guidance).
  - Chemical-shift MRI (signal loss on opposed-phase = intracellular lipid).
  - Repeat non-contrast CT or MRI after 6–12 months (ESE/ENSAT 2023); resection is suggested if the maximum diameter grows by more than 20% and by at least 5 mm.
  - FDG-PET/CT in selected cases; or surgery if large/heterogeneous.
- Features worrying for adrenocortical carcinoma (ACC) or metastasis: size >4 cm,
  heterogeneity, irregular margins, necrosis, calcification, invasion, rapid growth,
  known extra-adrenal cancer.
- **Bilateral masses**: also consider congenital adrenal hyperplasia, metastases,
  haemorrhage, infiltrative disease and adrenal insufficiency.

## 2. Hormonal evaluation (is it functioning?)
1. **Cortisol excess — all patients:** 1 mg overnight dexamethasone suppression test (DST)
   (dexamethasone at 23:00, serum cortisol ~08:00–09:00).
   - ≤50 nmol/L (1.8 µg/dL): autonomous cortisol secretion excluded.
   - >50 nmol/L without overt Cushing features: **mild autonomous cortisol secretion (MACS)**;
     higher values increase certainty (the 138 nmol/L / 5 µg/dL cut-off is (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked)).
   - Confirm/characterise with ACTH (suppressed = ACTH-independent), DHEAS (low supports),
     repeat DST or late-night salivary cortisol / 24-h urinary free cortisol if overt Cushing suspected.
   - Screen MACS patients for hypertension, diabetes, dyslipidaemia, osteoporosis/vertebral fracture.
2. **Phaeochromocytoma:** plasma free metanephrines (or 24-h urine fractionated metanephrines)
   for indeterminate masses (>10 HU). ESE/ENSAT 2023 recommends testing in lesions with features not typical for a benign adenoma, so it can be omitted for a homogeneous unenhanced HU ≤10 lesion.
   Values >2–3× ULN are strongly suggestive (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked); mild rises need repeat after removing interfering drugs.
3. **Primary aldosteronism:** aldosterone/renin ratio if hypertension or hypokalaemia
   (correct potassium first; interpret with drug effects — check local ratio cut-off and units).
4. **Sex hormones/steroid precursors** (androstenedione, testosterone, 17-OH progesterone,
   urine steroid metabolomics where available) when ACC suspected or virilisation present.

## 3. Decide
- Benign imaging + non-functioning: **discharge**, no further imaging or hormonal testing (unless new symptoms).
- MACS: individualise; adrenalectomy considered when cortisol-related comorbidities are
  worsening and the mass is unilateral; otherwise treat comorbidities and review annually.
- Overt Cushing, phaeochromocytoma, unilateral aldosterone excess (after adrenal vein sampling,
  usually), or suspected ACC: refer for adrenal surgery in an experienced centre (MDT).
- Phaeochromocytoma: **alpha-blockade before surgery** (e.g. phenoxybenzamine or doxazosin)
  — verify against local formulary/guideline.

## Gotchas
- **Never biopsy** an adrenal mass before excluding phaeochromocytoma (hypertensive crisis);
  biopsy is reserved for suspected metastasis when it changes management.
- False-positive DST: oral oestrogens (raise CBG), CYP3A4 inducers (carbamazepine, phenytoin,
  rifampicin), poor absorption, non-adherence. Measuring dexamethasone level helps if available.
- After unilateral adrenalectomy for cortisol secretion (overt or MACS), the contralateral
  gland may be suppressed — give perioperative glucocorticoid cover and test before stopping.
- Metanephrine false positives: tricyclics, SNRIs, levodopa, sympathomimetics, sampling seated.
- Incidental adrenal mass in a young patient (<40 y) is less likely to be an adenoma — lower threshold to investigate.

## Verify
- [ ] Unenhanced HU, size, homogeneity and laterality recorded.
- [ ] 1 mg DST done in all; metanephrines done unless HU ≤10 per guideline.
- [ ] Aldosterone/renin done if hypertension or hypokalaemia.
- [ ] Interfering drugs and conditions reviewed before interpreting tests.
- [ ] Clear outcome: discharge, surveillance plan, or MDT/surgical referral.
