# Thyroid nodule evaluation

**Goal:** find the few clinically significant cancers among very common, mostly benign
nodules, while avoiding needless biopsy and surgery for low-risk disease.

## 1. History, examination, TSH
- Red flags: childhood head/neck irradiation, family history of thyroid cancer or MEN2,
  rapid growth, hoarseness, fixed hard nodule, cervical lymphadenopathy.
- **Measure TSH first.**
  - **TSH low/suppressed** → radionuclide scan (Tc-99m or I-123). A **hyperfunctioning
    ("hot") nodule** is rarely malignant → usually no FNA; manage as toxic adenoma.
  - TSH normal/high → proceed to ultrasound-guided assessment.
- Routine serum calcitonin is debated (more used in Europe); thyroglobulin is not useful for diagnosis.

## 2. Ultrasound and risk scoring (ACR TI-RADS)
Score five categories: composition, echogenicity, shape (taller-than-wide = 3), margin
(extrathyroidal extension = 3), echogenic foci (punctate = 3). Sum the points:

| Level | Points | FNA if ≥ | Follow-up US if ≥ |
|---|---|---|---|
| TR1 benign | 0 | no FNA | none |
| TR2 not suspicious | 2 | no FNA | none |
| TR3 mildly suspicious | 3 | 2.5 cm | 1.5 cm |
| TR4 moderately suspicious | 4–6 | 1.5 cm | 1.0 cm |
| TR5 highly suspicious | ≥7 | 1.0 cm | 0.5 cm |

- ATA 2015 sonographic patterns (high suspicion: FNA ≥1 cm; intermediate ≥1 cm; low ≥1.5 cm;
  very low ≥2 cm or observe; pure cyst no FNA) and EU-TIRADS are acceptable alternatives —
  state which system was used. Thresholds differ between systems.
- Always scan the cervical lymph nodes; a suspicious node is biopsied (with washout
  thyroglobulin) regardless of the nodule's size.
- Subcentimetre high-suspicion nodules: active surveillance is often reasonable in selected adults.

## 3. FNA cytology — Bethesda system (3rd ed.)
Approximate risk of malignancy (ROM) varies by series; the figures below are from memory of the Bethesda 3rd edition (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked), so check the current edition.

| Category | Approx. ROM | Usual next step |
|---|---|---|
| I Non-diagnostic | ~13% | Repeat US-guided FNA (often after ≥3 months unless suspicious) |
| II Benign | ~4% | Surveillance US; interval by sonographic pattern |
| III AUS | ~22% | Repeat FNA or molecular testing; or surveillance/lobectomy |
| IV Follicular neoplasm | ~30% | Molecular testing or diagnostic lobectomy |
| V Suspicious for malignancy | ~74% | Surgery (extent per risk) |
| VI Malignant | ~97% | Surgery, or active surveillance for selected low-risk papillary microcarcinoma |

- **Molecular testing** (e.g. ThyroSeq, Afirma GSC) for Bethesda III/IV can avoid diagnostic
  surgery when the result is benign; availability and performance vary by platform and region.

## 4. Follow-up of benign or non-biopsied nodules
- Repeat US at ~12–24 months for high suspicion pattern even with benign cytology (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked)
  (consider repeat FNA); longer intervals (or none) for low-suspicion benign nodules.
- **Significant growth**: ATA 2015 Recommendation 10 cites growth of more than 20% in two dimensions on US surveillance; the 2 mm minimum and the 50% volume criterion are (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked). Re-evaluate/re-biopsy.

## Gotchas
- Skipping TSH leads to biopsy of hot nodules, whose cytology can look falsely
  follicular-neoplastic.
- Do not biopsy every nodule in a multinodular goitre — score each and biopsy the
  highest-risk ones that meet size criteria.
- FDG-PET-avid incidental nodules carry higher malignancy risk; evaluate with US and FNA if ≥1 cm.
- Purely cystic nodules are benign; spongiform appearance is low risk. Echogenic foci with
  comet-tail artefact are benign (colloid), not microcalcifications.
- Lobectomy is often adequate for low-risk differentiated cancer 1–4 cm; total
  thyroidectomy decisions are multidisciplinary.

## Verify
- [ ] TSH checked; scintigraphy done if TSH suppressed.
- [ ] Each nodule has a documented risk category, the system used and its size.
- [ ] FNA decision matches the size threshold for that category; nodes assessed.
- [ ] Bethesda category reported and the matching next step offered (incl. molecular testing).
- [ ] Surveillance interval and growth criteria documented.
