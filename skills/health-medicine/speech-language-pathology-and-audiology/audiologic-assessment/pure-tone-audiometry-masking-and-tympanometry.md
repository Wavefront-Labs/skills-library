# Pure-tone audiometry, masking and tympanometry

> General clinical/professional reference, not a substitute for independent clinical judgement, current guidelines, or verified dosing references.

**Goal:** obtain valid ear-specific thresholds, separate conductive from sensorineural
loss, and recognise results that need urgent referral.

## 1. Set-up
- Daily subjective check of the audiometer (listening check, transducer cables); annual
  calibration in date. Ambient noise in the booth within standard limits.
- **Otoscopy first:** wax occluding > ~50%, discharge, perforation, collapsing canals
  (use insert earphones if canals collapse under supra-aural headphones).
- Case history: onset, laterality, tinnitus, vertigo, noise exposure, ototoxic drugs,
  family history.

## 2. Air conduction thresholds (modified Hughson–Westlake)
- Familiarise at 1 kHz, then **down 10 dB after each response, up 5 dB after each
  miss**; threshold = lowest level with responses on ≥ 2 of 3 (or 50%) ascending runs.
- Order: 1 kHz, 2, 4, 8 kHz, re-test 1 kHz (± 5 dB agreement), then 500, 250 Hz.
  Test intra-octaves (3, 6 kHz) when adjacent octaves differ by ≥ 20 dB.
- Better ear first.

## 3. Bone conduction
Mastoid placement (not touching the pinna). 500 Hz–4 kHz. Unmasked bone conduction
represents the **better cochlea** — not necessarily the ear you placed it on.

## 4. Masking — when
- **Air conduction:** mask the non-test ear when AC threshold of the test ear minus BC
  of the non-test ear ≥ **interaural attenuation** (~40 dB supra-aural; ~55 dB inserts
  — conservative values used in practice).
- **Bone conduction:** mask whenever there's an **air-bone gap ≥ 10–15 dB** in the test
  ear (interaural attenuation for bone ≈ 0 dB).
- **Plateau method:** narrowband noise in non-test ear, start at minimum effective
  level, raise masking in 10 dB steps; true threshold is where it stays stable over
  ~3 masking steps. Watch for **over-masking** (masking crossing to test ear) and the
  **masking dilemma** in bilateral large conductive loss (inserts help).

## 5. Reading the audiogram
- Degree (e.g. WHO 2021 grades or ASHA): ≤ 20 dB HL normal; mild, moderate,
  moderately severe, severe, profound.
- **Air-bone gap > 10 dB** → conductive component. Both AC and BC down together →
  sensorineural.
- Patterns: noise notch at 4 kHz (3–6), presbycusis sloping high-frequency, low-
  frequency SNHL (Ménière's), Carhart notch at 2 kHz on BC (otosclerosis).

## 6. Tympanometry and reflexes (226 Hz adult; 1 kHz in infants < 6 months)
- **Type A** normal; **As** shallow (stiff — otosclerosis); **Ad** deep (ossicular
  discontinuity, flaccid TM); **B** flat — check ear-canal volume: normal volume → fluid,
  large volume → perforation/grommet patent, small volume → wax/probe against wall;
  **C** negative pressure (Eustachian tube dysfunction).
- Acoustic reflexes absent with conductive loss in the probe ear, or with retrocochlear
  pathology (VIII/VII) — **reflex decay** suggests retrocochlear.

## 7. Speech audiometry
Speech recognition threshold should agree with the pure-tone average (± 10 dB) — if not,
suspect non-organic loss or test error. Word recognition poor out of proportion to
thresholds → retrocochlear concern.

## Referral flags
- **Sudden SNHL** (≥ 30 dB over 3 contiguous frequencies within 72 h): same-day ENT —
  steroid treatment is time-sensitive.
- **Asymmetric SNHL** (e.g. ≥ 15–20 dB difference at 2+ adjacent frequencies, local
  criteria vary) or unilateral tinnitus → MRI IAMs for vestibular schwannoma.
- Conductive loss with discharge, cholesteatoma signs, or children with persistent
  middle-ear effusion and hearing loss.

## Gotchas
- Collapsed canals faking a high-frequency conductive loss.
- Unmasked thresholds in asymmetric loss → "shadow curve" from the better ear.
- Interpreting a flat tympanogram without the canal volume.
- Missing non-organic loss by not cross-checking SRT vs PTA.

## Verify
- [ ] Otoscopy and calibration checks done.
- [ ] Masking applied where rules require; plateau documented.
- [ ] Tympanogram type with canal volume and reflexes reported.
- [ ] SRT/PTA agreement checked.
- [ ] Referral criteria reviewed and actioned.
