# FPS esports — crosshair placement, aim and utility executes

**Goal:** win more gunfights by needing to aim less, and win more rounds by
arriving at a bombsite as five players with coordinated utility instead of
one at a time. Written for tactical round-based shooters (Counter-Strike 2,
Valorant, Rainbow Six-style); the aim sections transfer to arena and
battle-royale shooters.

## 1. Set up once, then stop touching it
- **Sensitivity:** measure it as **cm per 360°** (distance the mouse travels
  for one full turn), not the in-game number. Most tactical-FPS pros sit
  roughly in the 30–60 cm/360 range; arena/tracking games trend faster.
  In CS, `eDPI = DPI × in-game sens` (pros commonly ~600–1200); Valorant's
  sens scale differs (CS sens ÷ ~3.18 is the usual conversion — verify
  with a converter for your exact titles).
- **Raw input on, mouse acceleration off**, Windows "Enhance pointer
  precision" off, polling rate ≥1000 Hz.
- **Crosshair:** small, static, high-contrast colour (cyan/green/yellow on
  most maps). Dynamic/spread crosshairs hide where the first bullet goes.
- Lock settings for at least 2–4 weeks. Constant sens changes are the most
  common reason aim "never improves".

## 2. Crosshair placement beats flicks
- Keep the crosshair at **head height** wherever an enemy could appear,
  and **pre-aimed at the edge** of the angle you're about to clear — the
  enemy should walk *into* your crosshair.
- Clear angles **one at a time** (slicing the pie); never expose yourself
  to two angles at once if cover allows.
- Gotcha: looking at the floor while walking, and placing the crosshair in
  the middle of a doorway instead of at the corner a player would peek from.

## 3. Movement and shooting
- **Counter-strafe:** tap the opposite movement key to stop dead before
  firing; in CS/Valorant moving accuracy is terrible. Shoot only when stopped.
- **Tap/burst at range, spray close.** Learn the first ~10 bullets of the
  main rifle's spray pattern in a practice map before learning all 30.
- **Peeks:** wide swing to take a fight you expect; jiggle/shoulder peek to
  bait info or a shot from an AWP/Operator without committing.
- **Trading:** stay close enough to a teammate that if they die you can kill
  their killer within ~1–2 seconds. Untraded deaths lose rounds.

## 4. A 20-minute aim routine (before ranked)
1. 5 min deathmatch or bot "flick" scenario — first-bullet headshots only.
2. 5 min tracking scenario (Aim Lab / KovaaK's) at your game sens.
3. 5 min spray control against a wall then bots.
4. 5 min prefire map of the map you'll play, walking common angles.
Aim trainers help mechanics; they do not teach placement or decision-making.
Spend more total time on game sense than on scenarios.

## 5. Utility and executes
- Learn **2–3 lineups per site** per map first: one smoke that blocks the
  main defensive sightline, one flash that blinds the closest defender, one
  molotov/incendiary to clear a common corner. Practise in a private server
  with grenade trajectories shown.
- **Execute order:** smokes land → flashes pop as entry players swing →
  molly clears a corner → entry goes first, trader second, others follow
  1–2 s behind. Utility thrown 5 seconds before anyone enters is wasted.
- **Economy (CS):** full-buy, force or save as a *team*; one player buying
  rifles while four eco wastes both rounds. Track the enemy's money too.
- **Comms:** call location + number + health ("two B site, one tagged 40"),
  then go quiet. Dead players give one short info call and stop.

## Gotchas
- Blaming sens/hardware for bad placement.
- Throwing all utility at round start "to see what happens".
- Solo-queue hero plays instead of trading — rank rises faster from playing
  *with* the team than from aim.
- Patches shift spray patterns, agent kits and map callouts; lineups
  break after map updates. Recheck after every major patch. **(Values
  above are typical community ranges, not verified against current patch.)**

## Verify
- [ ] cm/360 known and unchanged for 2+ weeks; raw input on, accel off.
- [ ] In a replay, your crosshair is at head height on >80% of angles you clear.
- [ ] You stop before shooting (no running-shots in kill-cam).
- [ ] You can throw 2 working lineups per site on your main map from memory.
- [ ] Deaths in your demo are mostly traded by a teammate.
