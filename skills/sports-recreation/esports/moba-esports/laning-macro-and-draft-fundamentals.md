# MOBA esports — laning, macro and draft fundamentals

**Goal:** turn mechanical play into won games by controlling minion waves,
moving to objectives at the right time and drafting a team that has a plan.
Examples use League of Legends (LoL) terms with Dota 2 equivalents in
brackets; the principles are shared.

## 1. Laning: gold first, kills second
- **Last-hitting** is the base economy. Benchmark (LoL): ~7–8 CS/min is solid
  amateur, 9–10+ is pro-level. Drill in practice tool: 10 minutes, no items,
  target 80+ CS. (Dota: deny your own creeps too; denies cut enemy XP.)
- **Wave states:**
  - *Freeze* — let the wave sit just outside your tower; the enemy must
    overextend to farm. Do it when ahead or when your jungler is near.
  - *Slow push* — kill only caster minions so a big wave builds; crash it
    into the enemy tower before recalling or roaming.
  - *Fast push / crash* — clear quickly when you need to back, take plates or
    move to an objective.
- **Recall timing:** back right after crashing a wave so you lose no farm,
  and when you have gold for a meaningful item component.
- Trade when the enemy goes for a last-hit or uses a key cooldown; don't
  trade into a bigger minion wave.

## 2. Map awareness and vision
- Look at the minimap every 3–5 seconds — set a habit trigger (every last
  hit, every ability cast).
- Track the enemy jungler's start and likely path; when they show on one
  side, the other side is safe to push.
- Ward the approach routes to the next objective ~60–90 s before it spawns;
  clear enemy wards as a group, not alone.

## 3. Macro: what to do after laning
- **Objective trades:** if the enemy takes dragon top-side, take a tower or
  herald on the other side rather than flipping a losing fight.
- **Tempo:** push the waves in the lanes nearest the objective *before* it
  spawns, so enemies must choose between farm and the fight.
- **Side-laning:** one strong duelist pressures a side lane while four hold
  mid; collapse when the enemy commits to them.
- **Baron/Roshan:** only start it with vision control and the enemy's key
  players dead or visibly far away.
- **Death timers** grow late game; a single pick at 30+ minutes can end it.

## 4. Draft basics
- Have a **win condition**: early skirmish, scaling teamfight, split push,
  poke/siege or pick. Every pick should fit one plan.
- Balance damage type (physical/magic), engage vs disengage, and frontline.
- **Bans** remove the opponent's comfort picks or the current meta threats.
- **Power spikes:** know when each champion/hero comes online (level 6,
  first/second item) and fight around your spikes, avoid theirs.
- Gotcha: five "best in meta" picks with no engage or no frontline lose to
  a coherent mid-tier comp.

## 5. Team comms
Short and actionable: "Drake in 60, shove bot and reset", "Jungle seen top",
"Flash down mid". Avoid blame — tilted teams make worse macro decisions.

## Gotchas
- Chasing kills past a lost wave — the gold is in the minions.
- Recalling with a wave pushing into your own tower (you lose it all).
- Fighting at an objective that is not up yet.
- Patches change numbers, items and jungle timers frequently. **CS
  benchmarks and spawn timings are approximate; check the current patch.**

## Verify
- [ ] Practice-tool CS at 10 min ≥80 (LoL) without items.
- [ ] You can name the wave state you want before each recall.
- [ ] Vision placed around objectives a minute before spawn.
- [ ] Your team's draft has one stated win condition.
- [ ] Replay review shows minimap glances every few seconds in lane.
