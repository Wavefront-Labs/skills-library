# BMX: gate starts, pumping and manuals

**Goal:** get out of the gate fast, generate speed without pedalling through rhythm
sections, and own the manual and bunny hop — the building blocks of racing and freestyle.

## 1. Gate starts (racing)
- **Setup:** front wheel against the gate, cranks with the lead foot forward (around
  1–2 o'clock), the other foot on the ground or on the pedal (balanced on the gate —
  practise both).
- **Weight:** hips back and up over the rear wheel, arms bent, ready to drive forward.
- **Cadence:** UCI-type gates use a voice sequence and random-timed lights/tones
  ("Riders ready, watch the gate…" then beeps) — learn the specific cadence your track
  uses; reacting to the lights/tones beats reacting to the gate dropping.
- On the drop, push the bike forward and down the ramp with the arms while driving the
  lead pedal, stay out of the saddle, pedal hard to the first straight.
- Common faults: pulling back on the bars (front lifts), early start (gate hit),
  late reaction.

## 2. Pumping — speed without pedalling
On rollers, doubles and pump tracks:
- **Unweight** as you go up the face of a roller (arms and legs flex, letting the bike
  rise into you).
- **Push down** on the back side (extend arms and legs, driving the bike down the slope).
Timing makes speed; push too early (on the up-face) and you lose speed. Learn on a pump
track: aim to complete laps without pedalling.

## 3. The manual
Riding on the rear wheel without pedalling:
1. Roll at moderate speed, pedals level, standing.
2. **Shift hips back** (straighten arms) rather than pulling up with the arms — the bike
   comes up as your weight moves behind the rear axle.
3. Balance fore-aft with the hips and knees; cover the rear brake as the safety
   (tap it if you go too far back).
4. Practise on flat ground, then over small rollers (manualling rhythm sections is a
   key racing skill).

## 4. Bunny hop
1. Compress, then lift the front (manual motion, hips back).
2. As the front rises, push the bars forward and **scoop/lift the rear** with the feet
   (pushing the pedals backward and up) and tucking knees.
3. Level the bike in the air, land both wheels together, absorb.
Learn the front lift and rear lift separately first.

## 5. Jumps
- Learn on small tabletops before gaps (doubles).
- Approach at a consistent speed; neutral position; as the lip pushes the bike up,
  stay centred and let the bike come up into you; don't yank the bars.
- Look at the landing; match the bike's angle to the landing slope.
- Over-jumping and casing are both speed issues — adjust approach speed in small steps.
- In freestyle parks, learn drop-ins and quarter-pipe airs with coaching and pads.

## 6. Equipment
- **Race BMX:** 20" wheels (also "cruiser" 24" class), gear ratios typically ~2.4–2.8
  to 1 depending on rider and track (unconfirmed range — tune with coach), crank
  length to rider height, clipless or flat pedals.
- **Freestyle:** stronger frames, pegs, gyro or brakeless setups.
- Safety: full-face helmet for racing (mandatory under UCI/national rules), long
  sleeves/trousers, gloves; knee/elbow pads for park riding.

## 7. Track etiquette
Follow the track's practice direction, start in organised motos, don't stop on the
landing of a jump, check the landing is clear before jumping.

## Common faults
Pulling the bars to manual (loops out), stiff arms on rollers, pedalling through
rhythm sections instead of pumping, staring at the gate rather than reacting to the cadence.
