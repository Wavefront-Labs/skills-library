# Cycling training: FTP testing, power zones and interval design

**Goal:** measure your threshold reliably, train in the right zones for your goal, and
load progressively without digging a fatigue hole.

## 1. Kit
A **power meter** (pedal, crank, hub) or a **smart trainer** in ERG mode; a head unit or
app (Zwift, TrainerRoad, intervals.icu, Garmin/Wahoo). Calibrate (zero offset) the power
meter before tests. Indoor and outdoor numbers can differ by a few percent — test
where you'll train.

## 2. FTP testing
**FTP** (functional threshold power) ≈ the highest power you can sustain for roughly an hour.
- **20-minute test:** warm-up with a few short hard efforts; optional 5-min all-out
  "blowout" (in some protocols); recover; **20 minutes at the best even pace you can
  hold**; FTP ≈ 95% of the 20-min average.
- **Ramp test:** power steps up each minute until failure; FTP ≈ ~75% of the best
  1-minute power (as used by several platforms). Easy to do but overestimates FTP for
  riders with a strong anaerobic capacity and underestimates for diesel types.
- Retest every 4–8 weeks or when workouts feel consistently too easy/hard.
- Pacing the 20-min test: start slightly conservatively and finish strong; a fade
  invalidates it.

## 3. Zones (Coggan-style, % of FTP)
| Zone | % FTP | Use |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Active recovery | < 55% | Recovery spins |
| 2 Endurance | 56–75% | Base, long rides |
| 3 Tempo | 76–90% | Muscular endurance |
| (Sweet spot) | ~88–94% | Time-efficient threshold building |
| 4 Threshold | 91–105% | Raise FTP |
| 5 VO2max | 106–120% | Aerobic ceiling |
| 6 Anaerobic | 121–150% | Short hard efforts |
| 7 Neuromuscular | max | Sprints |

## 4. Interval design
Pick intervals by goal, progress by adding time-in-zone, then intensity:
- **Sweet spot:** 3 × 12–15 min at ~90% FTP, 5 min rest → build toward 2 × 30 min.
- **Threshold:** 4 × 8 min or 3 × 10 min at 95–105%, rests ~half the work time;
  progress to 2 × 20 min.
- **VO2max:** 5 × 3–5 min at 106–120% with equal rest; or 30/30s (30 s hard / 30 s easy)
  × 10–20. Hard — 1–2 sessions per week max.
- **Anaerobic/sprint:** 6–8 × 30 s all-out with full recovery for crit/track riders.
- **Endurance:** long zone 2 rides remain the aerobic foundation.
Intensity distribution: many amateurs do best with mostly easy riding plus 2 hard
sessions per week ("polarised" or "pyramidal" approaches). Evidence comparing them is mixed.

## 5. Load management
- **TSS** (Training Stress Score) per ride: 100 ≈ one hour at FTP.
- **CTL** ("fitness", ~6-week rolling load), **ATL** ("fatigue", ~7-day), **TSB**
  ("form" = CTL − ATL). A sustainable CTL ramp is often ~3–7 per week; much more
  invites illness or burnout.
- Build 3 weeks / recover 1 week (reduced volume ~40–50%) is a common pattern.
- Arrive at target events with TSB positive (fresh) after a taper.
- Metrics are models — sleep, HRV trends, mood and motivation are equally important.

## 6. Indoor training tips
Big fan (overheating suppresses power), towel, plenty of fluid, ERG mode for intervals
(but practise free-ride pacing too), raise the front wheel to level the bike.

## Common faults
Testing tired; ramp FTP used without sanity check; every ride "moderately hard" (zone 3
junk miles); too many VO2 sessions; ignoring recovery weeks.

## Verify
- [ ] Power meter zeroed; FTP test completed with even pacing.
- [ ] Zones set in your app from the tested FTP.
- [ ] Weekly plan has ≤ 2 hard sessions and a long easy ride.
- [ ] CTL ramp rate and recovery weeks scheduled.
- [ ] Workouts reviewed: target power hit within ~3–5% for each interval.
