# Racquetball — kill shots, ceiling balls and centre-court control

**Goal:** serve to force a weak return, end rallies with low shots into the
front wall, use the ceiling to reset when you're out of position, and hold
centre court. Four-wall racquetball is fast; most rallies are short.

## 1. Serving
- Serve from the **service zone**; the ball must hit the front wall first
  and land beyond the **short line**. It may touch one side wall, but not
  both side walls, the ceiling, or the back wall on the fly (all faults —
  rules vary slightly by organisation; check USA Racquetball or IRF rules).
- **Drive serve:** low and hard into a back corner. The standard attacking
  serve.
- **Z-serve:** front wall near a corner, then the side wall, crossing the
  court and coming off the opposite side wall at an awkward angle.
- **Lob serve:** high and soft, dying in the back corner — useful as a
  second serve or against power players.
- **Screen serve:** if the ball passes so close to the server that the
  receiver can't see it, it's a screen (replay or fault depending on rules).
- Most rules give **two serves**; some formats allow only one.

## 2. Swing basics
- Side-on stance, racket back early, step into the ball, contact low (knee
  height or below) beside the front foot, wrist snap. Similar in spirit to a
  sidearm throw.
- **Low contact = low shot.** The single biggest improvement for most
  players is letting the ball drop lower before hitting it.

## 3. Shots that end rallies
- **Kill shot:** hits the front wall so low it bounces twice before the
  opponent can reach it. Highest reward, highest risk.
- **Pinch:** hits the side wall first then low on the front wall — dies in
  the front corner. Effective when the opponent is deep.
- **Splat:** side wall close to you, hit hard so the ball "splats" across the
  front of the court.
- **Down-the-line pass:** straight along a side wall, past an opponent who's
  out of position.
- **Crosscourt pass:** wide enough to get past the opponent before it
  reaches the back wall.
- Pick the shot by where the opponent is: deep → pinch/kill; up front →
  pass.

## 4. Ceiling ball
A defensive shot that hits the **ceiling first**, then the front wall, then
bounces high into the back court, pinning the opponent deep. Use it when
you're out of position. Rallies of ceiling balls continue until someone
gets a short ceiling ball to attack.

## 5. Centre-court control
- The best place to be is just behind the **receiving line**, roughly
  centre.
- After every shot, move back to centre court; make the opponent go around
  you (but you must give them a fair shot at the ball).
- Watch the opponent hit (from the corner of your eye, with eyewear on)
  to read the shot early.

## 6. Hinders and safety
- **Hinder:** unintentional interference — replay.
- **Avoidable hinder:** you failed to move out of the way or blocked a shot
  — point/side-out to the opponent.
- **Eyewear** designed for racquet sports is mandatory in sanctioned play;
  the ball is fast and the court is enclosed.
- A **wrist tether** on the racket is required in most rules.
- Hold up rather than swing when the opponent is in the way.

## 7. American handball
Similar court and rules (four-wall, one-wall, three-wall) but the ball is
struck with the gloved hand. The same principles apply: low shots win,
ceiling shots reset, centre court is home. Both hands must be developed —
the off hand is targeted relentlessly.

## Gotchas
- Hitting the ball at waist height (sends it up and back for a set-up).
- Leaving centre court to watch your own shot.
- Playing without eyewear — never.

## Verify
- [ ] Drive serve into both back corners 6 of 10.
- [ ] 10 consecutive ceiling balls landing deep.
- [ ] Kill/pinch attempts from a set-up in drills: 5 of 10 bounce twice before the short line.
- [ ] Eyewear and wrist tether on every session.
