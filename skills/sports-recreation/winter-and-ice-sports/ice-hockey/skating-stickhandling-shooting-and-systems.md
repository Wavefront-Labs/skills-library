# Ice hockey — skating, stickhandling, shooting and systems

**Goal:** be a useful player at rec/club level: skate on edges, handle and
shoot the puck with your head up, know the offside/icing rules, and play your
role inside the team's breakout and forecheck.

## 1. Skating is the game
- **Stride:** knees bent, push out to the side and slightly back, full
  extension, return the foot under the body. Toe flick at the end.
- **Edges:** inside and outside edge control on one foot (C-cuts, figure-8s).
  Weak outside edges show up as slow tight turns.
- **Stops:** two-foot hockey stop (both blades skid sideways), then one-foot
  stops each side. **Backward skating** with C-cuts; backward crossovers for
  defence.
- **Crossovers and tight turns:** lean into the circle, inside knee bent, stick
  on the ice. Skates sharpened regularly (hollow depth is a preference: deeper =
  more bite, shallower = more glide).

## 2. Stick fit
- **Length:** standing in skates, the top roughly at the chin to nose (shorter
  for handling, longer for defenders' reach). Cutting makes it stiffer.
- **Flex:** a common starting rule is around half body weight in pounds;
  too stiff and you can't load it on a shot.
- **Lie:** the blade should sit flat on the ice in your skating stance —
  check the tape wear on the blade heel vs toe.
- **Handedness:** a *left shot* has the left hand lower on the stick (blade on
  the left side). Many right-handed people shoot left; try both.

## 3. Stickhandling and passing
- Soft hands, top hand doing most of the work, puck in the middle of the blade.
  **Head up:** feel the puck, look at the play. Drill: stickhandle while reading
  numbers held up by a partner.
- Passing: sweep through the puck, follow through at the target, receive with
  a soft "give" of the blade. Pass to the tape or to space in front of a skating
  team-mate, not behind them.

## 4. Shots
| Shot | Use | Key point |
|---|---|---|
| Wrist shot | accurate, quick | puck from heel to toe, weight transfer, follow through at target |
| Snapshot | quick release | short pull back, load the stick into the ice |
| Slapshot | power from the point | strike the ice just behind the puck to flex the stick |
| Backhand | in tight | lift with a roll of the wrists |
| One-timer | off a pass | timing; the puck meets a loaded stick |
Shooting low far-side produces rebounds; high glove/blocker beats goalies cleanly.

## 5. Rules you must know
- **Offside:** an attacker may not cross the opponent's blue line before the
  puck. Both skates fully over the line = offside. (Rec leagues may use tag-up.)
- **Icing:** shooting the puck from your half past the opponent's goal line
  untouched; faceoff comes back to your end and (in many leagues) your line
  can't change. Not called when you're shorthanded.
- Common penalties: tripping, hooking, slashing, high-sticking, interference,
  too many men. Many rec leagues are no-checking — know your league.

## 6. Systems (team play)
- **Breakout:** defender retrieves, looks up, options: up the boards to the
  winger, the centre swinging low, or D-to-D. Wingers on the boards at the
  hash marks, centre supports low.
- **Forecheck:** **2-1-2** (two forwards pressure, one high, two D) is
  aggressive; **1-2-2** (one pressures, two wait in the neutral zone) is
  conservative and transitions into the **neutral-zone trap**.
- **Defensive zone:** man-to-man or zone (box +1); centre covers low, wingers
  the points, D in front of the net.
- **Special teams:** power play (umbrella, 1-3-1), penalty kill (box, diamond).

## 7. Goaltending (basics)
Ready stance, **butterfly** to cover the bottom, angle play (stand on the line
from puck to goal centre), post integration such as RVH (reverse VH), rebound
control into corners.

## Verify
- [ ] Two-foot and one-foot stops on both sides at speed.
- [ ] Stick length, flex and lie checked against your stance.
- [ ] You can explain offside and icing (including the shorthanded exception).
- [ ] You know your team's breakout and forecheck roles for your position.
- [ ] Shots hit corners in practice, head up while handling.
