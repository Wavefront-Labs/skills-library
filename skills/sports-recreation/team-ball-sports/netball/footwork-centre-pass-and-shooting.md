# Netball — footwork, centre pass and shooting

**Goal:** catch and land legally, get free for the centre pass, and shoot a
consistent technique under pressure. Seven a side, no dribbling, no
running with the ball.

## 1. Footwork rules (the most-called fouls)
- **Landing foot:** if you land on one foot, that foot becomes the landing
  foot. You may step with the other foot and pivot on the landing foot, but
  once you lift the landing foot you must release the ball before it touches
  down again.
- **Two-foot landing:** you choose which foot becomes the pivot.
- **Three seconds:** you must pass or shoot within three seconds of
  catching.
- **Replayed ball:** you can't catch, drop and re-catch your own pass unless
  it touched another player or the post.

## 2. Contact and obstruction
- **Obstruction:** a defender must be at least **0.9 m (3 feet)** from the
  landing foot of the player with the ball while defending them, measured
  from the landing foot. Arms may be raised only from that distance.
- **Contact:** no contact that interferes with play (pushing, tripping,
  knocking the ball from hands). Penalty: penalty pass or shot, with the
  offender standing out of play beside the thrower.

## 3. Positions and zones
The court is split into thirds. Each position's bib shows where it may go:
- **GS** (goal shooter): attacking third and goal circle.
- **GA** (goal attack): centre and attacking thirds, including the circle.
- **WA**: centre and attacking thirds, **not** the circle.
- **C** (centre): everywhere except the two goal circles.
- **WD**: centre and defending thirds, not the circle.
- **GD**: centre and defending thirds, including the circle.
- **GK**: defending third and circle.
Entering a zone you're not allowed in is **offside**. Only GS and GA may
score.

## 4. Centre pass set-ups
Centre passes alternate between teams after each goal.
- Before the whistle, only the two centres are in the centre third; all
  other players wait in their goal thirds.
- WA, GA and often WD/GD **lead** into the centre third on the whistle.
- **Set plays:** e.g. WA drives hard to the side of the centre third while
  GA holds then cuts back for a "front" ball. Use **dodges** (fake one way,
  explode the other) and a strong final lead with hands up.
- Defenders try to front their attacker (body between attacker and ball)
  and force them wide.

## 5. Shooting technique
- Feet roughly shoulder-width, facing the post, knees soft.
- Ball held on the fingertips of the shooting hand, above and slightly in
  front of the head; non-shooting hand balances on the side.
- Eyes on the **back of the ring**; bend the knees, then extend legs, elbow
  and wrist together to send the ball high with backspin.
- **Follow-through:** wrist flick, fingers toward the ring.
- **Super Shot:** some competitions (e.g. Suncorp Super Netball, Fast5)
  score two points for shots from an outer zone in certain periods. Check
  your competition — standard netball is one point everywhere in the circle.

## 6. Defending
- Watch the attacker's hips, not the eyes.
- Stay 0.9 m away once they have the ball; intercept passes in flight.
- **Rebounds:** GK and GD box out their shooter and take the ball at the
  top of their jump.

## Gotchas
- Shuffling the landing foot when pivoting (the most common footwork call).
- Defending too close and conceding obstruction penalties.
- Centre-pass leads that all go to the same space.

## Verify
- [ ] 10 land-and-pivot reps with no footwork errors.
- [ ] Defenders hold 0.9 m on a coach's demonstration.
- [ ] Each player can name their legal zones from their bib.
- [ ] Shooters hit 7 of 10 from a close-range spot with the same routine.
