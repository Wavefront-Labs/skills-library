# Futsal — sole control, rotations and pivot play

**Goal:** keep the ball in tight space with the sole, rotate as a five so
defenders can't man-mark comfortably, and use the pivot to break pressure.
Futsal uses a smaller, low-bounce ball (size 4), a hard court, five a side
including the goalkeeper, and unlimited rolling substitutions.

## 1. Rules that change how you play (FIFA Futsal Laws)
- **Four seconds:** kick-ins, free kicks, corners and goal clearances must
  be taken within 4 s once ready. The goalkeeper may control the ball in
  their own half for only 4 s.
- **Kick-in** (not a throw-in) from the touchline, ball stationary on the
  line.
- **Accumulated fouls:** from the **sixth** team foul in each half, every
  direct free kick becomes a **second-penalty kick** (10 m) or a direct
  free kick from the spot of the foul if closer, with no wall.
- **No offside.**
- **Goalkeeper return:** after the keeper releases the ball, they can't
  receive it back in their own half from a teammate until an opponent
  touches it (check current Laws; this was revised).
- Running clock stops when the ball is out of play (40 minutes of play).

## 2. Sole control
- **Sole stop:** receive under the sole of the foot to kill the ball
  instantly; the low-bounce ball makes this reliable.
- **Sole roll / drag:** move the ball sideways or backwards with the sole
  while the body shields it.
- **Pull-push:** sole drags back, inside of the same foot pushes forward
  past a lunging defender.
- **Shielding:** side-on, arm out for balance (not to hold), ball on the far
  foot.
- **Toe-poke (punteo):** fast shot with no backswing — ideal in tight spaces
  and catches keepers before they set.

## 3. Positions
- **Goleiro** (goalkeeper): also a build-up player.
- **Fixo:** the deepest outfield player, organises defence and build-up.
- **Alas** (wingers): wide, run the rotations.
- **Pivô** (pivot): the target player near the opponents' goal, back to
  goal.

## 4. Rotation systems
- **3-1:** fixo, two alas, one pivot. The pivot holds up the ball; the
  three others rotate behind (e.g. the ala passes to the fixo and runs
  diagonally forward, the fixo shifts across, the other ala drops into the
  fixo position). Good for beating a press with a strong pivot.
- **4-0:** no fixed pivot — four players rotate in a box, making
  diagonal runs into the space behind a defender and refilling the
  positions they vacate. Needs mobile players who all receive well.
- **Principle:** pass and move — the passer runs into a new channel;
  standing still after a pass kills the rotation.

## 5. Pivot play
- **Hold up:** receive with back to goal on the sole, shield, lay off to an
  oncoming player.
- **Turn:** if the defender is too tight or too far off, roll them.
- **Second post:** arrive at the far post for wide passes across the box.

## 6. Pressing and the power play
- **Pressing lines:** full-court, half-court or a deeper block. Press
  together with a trigger (a back-pass to the keeper, a bad touch).
- **Fly goalkeeper / power play:** replace the keeper with an outfield
  player (or let the keeper come out) to make 5v4 in attack. Common when
  chasing a game. Risky: a turnover means an empty goal.
- **Defending the power play:** a diamond or box shape that blocks central
  lanes and forces long shots.

## Gotchas
- Using the instep for control on a hard court — the ball runs away.
- Watching after passing instead of moving.
- Ignoring the team foul count: the sixth foul onward is a free shot.
