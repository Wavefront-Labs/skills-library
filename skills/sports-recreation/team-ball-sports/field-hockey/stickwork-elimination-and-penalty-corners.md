# Field hockey — stickwork, elimination and penalty corners

**Goal:** control the ball on both sides of the stick, beat a defender, and
run a penalty corner routine that actually scores. Only the flat (left) face
of the stick can play the ball; the rounded back is illegal ("backstick").

## 1. Grip and carrying position
- **Left hand** at the top of the stick, the "V" of thumb and forefinger
  down the back edge; it does the rotating.
- **Right hand** loosely about a third of the way down; it supports and
  steers.
- Low body, knees bent, ball out in front and to the right of the right foot
  so you can see the ball and the pitch at the same time.

## 2. Dribbling and receiving
- **Indian dribble:** move the ball side to side by rotating the stick with
  the left hand, alternating open (forehand) and reverse (stick turned over)
  sides. Keep the ball within a stick's length of you.
- **Receiving forehand:** stick angled slightly forward over the ball so it
  doesn't pop up; cushion on contact.
- **Reverse receiving:** stick turned over, low, ball taken in front of the
  left foot — practise it as much as forehand.
- Scan before receiving, as in any invasion game.

## 3. Passing and shooting
- **Push pass:** no backswing, stick in contact with the ball, weight from
  back to front foot. Accurate and quick to release.
- **Slap hit:** hands apart, long sweeping swing low to the ground; more pace
  than a push, popular for long passes and deflections.
- **Hit:** hands together at the top; most power. **Raised balls** are
  dangerous play if aimed at an opponent or lifted into a crowd — umpires
  judge danger; a raised hit at goal from outside the circle isn't allowed
  in most rules. Check current FIH rules for aerials and self-pass.

## 4. Eliminating a defender
- **Drag:** shift the ball quickly from one side to the other just outside
  the defender's reach, then accelerate past.
- **3D skills:** lift the ball over the defender's stick (a small flick
  over the stick or a jink over a tackle). Legal when not dangerous.
- **Timing:** attack the defender's stick side, make them commit their
  stick, then go away from it. Elimination fails most often when you go too
  early from too far away.
- **Self-pass:** from a free hit, play it yourself (current FIH rules allow
  it; the 5 m rule applies near the circle).

## 5. Penalty corners (PCs)
Awarded mostly for defensive fouls in the circle.
- **Injector** pushes the ball from the backline mark to the top of the
  circle. Must be fast and flat.
- **Stopper/trapper** stops it dead on the circle edge.
- The ball must leave the circle before a shot can be taken; a hit at goal
  must not cross the goal line above the backboard height (460 mm) on the
  first shot — **drag flicks** and other "flicks/pushes" can go higher if not
  dangerous. Rules here change; confirm with the current FIH rulebook.
- **Drag flick:** ball dragged along the stick from the back foot with a low
  body, then slung toward goal. Requires a lot of practice and strength;
  learn under a coach (lower-back strain is common).
- **Defending PCs:** goalkeeper plus four defenders; a first rusher at the
  ball, and others covering post and deflections. Rushers wear protection
  (face masks permitted for defending PCs).

## 6. Pressing shapes
Full press (high), half-court press and a deep defensive press. Press as a
unit with clear triggers — a reverse-stick receive or a pass to the
sideline — and force play one way.

## Gotchas
- Using the back of the stick (backstick foul).
- Foot faults: ball deliberately played with the foot (accidental contact
  without advantage isn't penalised).
- Practising only on the forehand.

## Verify
- [ ] Indian dribble at walking pace without losing the ball for 20 m.
- [ ] Clean reverse receive 8 of 10.
- [ ] PC team can run inject, trap and shot in under 2 seconds.
- [ ] Everyone knows the raised-ball and 5 m rules at your level.
