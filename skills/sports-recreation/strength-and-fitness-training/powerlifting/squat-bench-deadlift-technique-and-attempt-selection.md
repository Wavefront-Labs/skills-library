# Powerlifting — squat, bench, deadlift technique and attempt selection

**Goal:** perform the three lifts to competition standard and build a total
on the day: three attempts each at squat, bench press and deadlift; best of
each counts. Federations (IPF and affiliates, USAPL, others) differ in rules —
read your federation's rulebook.

## 1. Bracing (all three lifts)
- Big breath into the belly and sides (360°), brace hard against it (Valsalva),
  hold through the hard part of the rep. Belt gives the abdomen something to
  push against — tighten it to allow a full breath.
- Re-brace between reps. Lifters with high blood pressure or cardiac issues
  should talk to a doctor about breath-holding under load.

## 2. Squat
- **Bar position:** high-bar (on the traps) or low-bar (on the rear delts) — low
  bar typically lets more hip involvement and more weight.
- Stance about shoulder width or wider, toes out ~15–30°; knees track over toes.
- **Depth rule:** the top of the hip at the hip joint must go **below the top of
  the knee** (hip crease below parallel). Most red lights for new lifters are depth.
- **Commands (IPF style):** "Squat" to start after walking out still, "Rack" at
  the end. Don't move before commands.

## 3. Bench press
- **Setup:** shoulder blades pulled back and down, chest up (an arch is legal
  within rules), feet flat (or as the rules require), glutes on the bench.
- **Grip:** max 81 cm between index fingers in IPF; wrists stacked over elbows.
- Lower to the lower chest/sternum, **pause motionless** on the chest until the
  "**Press**" command, then press; wait for "**Rack**".
- **Leg drive:** push through the floor without lifting the hips.
- Common faults: heaving (bar sinks after pause), uneven lockout, buttocks off the bench.

## 4. Deadlift
- **Conventional** (feet hip width, hands outside the legs) or **sumo** (wide
  stance, hands inside) — both legal in most federations; choose by body
  proportions and what you're strongest in.
- Setup: bar over mid-foot, shins to the bar, hinge and grip, pull the slack out
  of the bar ("wedge"), chest up, lats engaged, then push the floor away.
- Lockout: stand tall with knees and hips locked, shoulders back — no
  **hitching** (resting the bar on the thighs in jerky stages) and no downward
  movement. "Down" command, then lower under control (don't drop).

## 5. Raw/classic vs equipped
Raw/classic allows belt, knee sleeves, wrist wraps (federation-specific);
equipped adds supportive suits and shirts that add significant weight. Use only
federation-approved kit (IPF approved lists).

## 6. Meet day
- **Weigh-in** (often 2 h or 24 h before, federation-dependent) — make your class
  without a dangerous cut.
- Know rack heights (squat and bench safeties/rack positions) and submit them.
- Warm-ups timed to your flight; bring food, chalk, kit, membership card.
- Three referees: white light = good, red = no lift; 2 of 3 whites passes.

## 7. Attempt selection
- **Opener:** a weight you could triple on a bad day — commonly around
  **90 % of your expected best** (or your training heavy single at RPE ~7–8).
  Its job is to get you in the meet.
- **Second:** around **95–97 %** — a strong but likely lift, often close to a
  previous best.
- **Third:** a PR attempt, or a strategic choice (e.g. to secure placing or a total).
- Attempt changes have time limits after each lift — know them.
- Bomb out (three misses) on a lift = no total. A conservative squat opener
  prevents most bomb-outs.

## 8. Scoring
Placings by weight class; best lifter across classes by formula (IPF **GL points**;
DOTS and Wilks used by other federations).

## Verify
- [ ] Squats filmed side-on show hip crease below top of the knee.
- [ ] Bench reps have a motionless pause and wait for commands in training.
- [ ] Deadlift lockout without hitching.
- [ ] Equipment is on the federation's approved list.
- [ ] Openers ~90 %, second ~95–97 %, third planned.
