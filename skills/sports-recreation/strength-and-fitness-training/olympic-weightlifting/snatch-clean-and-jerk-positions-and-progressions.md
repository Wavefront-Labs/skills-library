# Olympic weightlifting — snatch, clean and jerk positions and progressions

**Goal:** lift a bar from the floor to overhead in one movement (snatch) or two
(clean, then jerk), with positions that hold up as weights rise. Competition
total = best snatch + best clean and jerk, three attempts each.

## 1. Kit and safety
- **Bumper plates** and a platform so the bar can be dropped safely from overhead.
  Learn to **bail**: snatch — push the bar forward and step back; jerk — push
  away and step back or drop with control.
- **Weightlifting shoes** (raised, solid heel) help depth and stability.
- **Hook grip:** thumb wrapped around the bar and trapped under the index and
  middle fingers — uncomfortable at first, essential for heavy pulls.

## 2. Grip widths
- **Snatch:** wide — commonly so the bar sits in the hip crease when standing
  with straight arms (measure: elbow-to-elbow with arms out, or knuckle-to-
  knuckle methods). 
- **Clean:** about a thumb's length outside the shoulders.

## 3. The start position and first pull
- Feet roughly hip width, bar over the balls of the feet / mid-foot, shins near
  the bar, shoulders slightly in front of the bar, back flat and tight, arms
  straight, lats pulling the bar into the body.
- **First pull** (floor to knees): legs push, back angle stays constant, bar
  moves slightly back toward the lifter. Don't rush it — speed comes later.

## 4. The second pull and extension
- As the bar passes the knees, the knees move forward under the bar (**scoop/
  double knee bend**), bar slides up the thighs to the **power position** (hips
  for snatch, upper thigh for clean).
- **Extension:** violent leg and hip drive — "jump" — with the bar close.
  Arms stay long until the legs finish; pulling early with the arms is the
  commonest fault.

## 5. The turnover and catch
- After extension, **pull yourself under** the bar fast; feet move from pulling
  width to squat width (stamp, not jump wide).
- **Snatch catch:** bar locked out overhead, armpits forward, in a full overhead
  squat. **Clean catch:** elbows fast and high in the front-rack, full front squat.
- Meet the bar — don't let it crash on you; a soft catch in the clean costs the jerk.

## 6. The jerk
- **Dip:** short, straight-down dip with the torso vertical, weight mid-foot.
- **Drive:** explosive leg drive; the bar travels up as you drop under.
- **Split jerk:** front foot steps forward, back foot back on the ball, bar
  locked over the ears/back of the neck; recover front foot back first, then back foot.
- **Power jerk/push jerk:** catch in a partial squat, no split.

## 7. Progressions (top-down teaching)
Snatch: overhead squat → snatch balance → hang power snatch from hip → hang
snatch from knee → snatch from floor.
Clean: front squat → hang power clean from hip → hang clean from knee → clean.
Jerk: push press → power jerk → split jerk footwork drills → split jerk.
Accessories: snatch/clean pulls, front and back squats, overhead squats, RDLs,
pressing, core.

## 8. Competition (outline)
Declare attempts; a clock limits time to lift after being called; three referees;
lifts fail for press-outs (arms bending and re-extending overhead), touching
the platform with anything but feet, or not standing still at the finish.
Weight increases follow federation rules (minimum 1 kg increments).
Best lifter by **Sinclair** coefficient in many meets.

## Verify
- [ ] Hook grip used on all pulls.
- [ ] Bar path stays close; arms straight until full extension.
- [ ] You can hold a full overhead squat with an empty bar.
- [ ] Front-rack elbows high in the clean catch.
- [ ] Split jerk lands with the bar over the back of the neck, recovers front foot first.
