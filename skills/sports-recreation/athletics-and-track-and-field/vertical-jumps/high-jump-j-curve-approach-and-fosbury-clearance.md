# High jump J-curve approach and Fosbury clearance

**Goal:** turn horizontal speed into vertical lift using a curved approach, then
rotate over the bar back-first with hips high.

> Draft: standard Fosbury-flop coaching. Measurements are examples; individual
> approaches vary with speed and leg length. Pole vault is a separate skill set (pole
> carry, plant, swing and rockback) not covered here.

## Which foot, which side
The **take-off foot is the one furthest from the bar**. A left-foot take-off approaches
from the right side of the bar (as you face it) and curves left; a right-foot
take-off mirrors that. Test with a few scissors jumps to find the natural foot.

## Measure the J
A common starting layout (adjust to you):
1. Stand at the **take-off point**: about an arm's length out from the near standard
   and 60–90 cm in front of the bar's plane (i.e., not under the bar).
2. Walk ~4–5 big steps along the bar's line away from the standard — then turn 90° and
   walk ~8–10 big steps straight back from the bar. Mark the **start**.
3. Typical: **10 strides** — 5 straight, 5 on the curve (or 4+4 / 6+4). The straight
   part builds speed; the curve creates lean.

## Run the curve
- Accelerate on the straight, upright and tall.
- On the curve, **lean the whole body away from the bar** (into the centre of the curve)
  — lean from the ankles, not by dropping a shoulder.
- Keep speed through the curve; don't slow down to "set up".

## Plant and take-off
- **Last step quicker**; take-off foot plants pointing roughly toward the far standard
  (about 20–30° to the bar), ahead of the body, heel first rolling to the toe.
- Body still leaning away from the bar at plant — the lean converts to rotation.
- **Drive** the free knee up and across, and drive both arms (double-arm) or the inside
  arm (single-arm) upward. Hips rise.
- **Don't jump into the bar**: take-off direction is up; the curve carries you across.

## Clearance
1. As the back rises over the bar, **head back, look over the shoulder**, hips up into
   an arch.
2. When the hips clear, **tuck the chin** to the chest — this lifts the legs.
3. **Kick the legs** up and land on the upper back/shoulders on the mat.

## Competition rules
- Three consecutive failures (at any heights) eliminate you. You may **pass** a
  height; misses carry forward.
- Ties: fewest misses at the tied height, then fewest total misses; for first place
  in some competitions a **jump-off**.
- Jump within the time limit once called (varies by number of athletes remaining).
- Knocked bar = failure; touching it is fine if it stays up.

## Gotchas
- **Drifting into the bar** (take-off too close or running straight at it) — move the
  take-off point out.
- **Cutting the curve** (running straight then turning hard at the end) loses lean.
- **Early arch / late chin tuck**: hips or heels clip the bar. Video and cue timing.
- Landing on the neck — always use a full-size, certified landing mat.

## Verify
- [ ] Approach measured and marked; start and curve start repeatable.
- [ ] Take-off point consistently ~arm's length out, under 1 m in front of bar.
- [ ] Video shows lean on the curve and upward take-off.
- [ ] Clearance shows head-back arch then chin tuck at hip clearance.
