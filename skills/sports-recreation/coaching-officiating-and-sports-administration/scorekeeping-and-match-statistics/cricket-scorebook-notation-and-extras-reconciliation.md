# Cricket scorebook notation and extras reconciliation

**Goal:** record every ball so that the batting total, the bowling figures and the
extras all agree — the scorebook is the official record of the match.

> Draft: based on the Laws of Cricket (MCC) and standard scorebook conventions.
> Symbol conventions vary a little between books and associations; competition
> regulations (e.g. extra runs per wide/no-ball in some formats) override the Laws.
> Many leagues now use apps (Play-Cricket Scorer, NV Play, CricHeroes etc.) — the
> logic below is identical.

## Common notation (bowling grid, one box per over)
- **·** (dot) — no run. **1, 2, 3, 4, 6** — runs off the bat.
- **+** (or similar) — **wide**; runs taken off a wide are added as dots/marks in the
  arms of the cross.
- **○** — **no-ball**; runs scored off the bat written inside the circle.
- **△** — **byes** (number inside); **▽** — **leg-byes**.
- **W** — wicket credited to the bowler (bowled, caught, lbw, stumped, hit wicket).
  Run outs are not credited to the bowler: many scorers mark "R" or the run count
  plus a note.
- A **maiden** (no runs conceded by the bowler in an over) is marked by joining the
  dots into an M.

## Who is charged with what
| Event | Team extras | Bowler | Batter's runs | Balls faced | Legal ball? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Runs off bat | — | yes | yes | yes | yes |
| Wide (+ runs run) | 1 + runs | yes, all | no | no | no — re-bowled |
| No-ball, hit for runs | 1 | 1 + bat runs | bat runs | yes | no — re-bowled |
| No-ball, byes run | 1 + byes | 1 only | no | yes | no |
| Byes / leg-byes | runs | no | no | yes | yes |
| Penalty runs | 5 per incident | no | no | — | — |

(One-run wide/no-ball penalties are the Laws' default; some competitions award two.)

## Batting card
For each batter: runs ball by ball (cumulative), balls faced, 4s, 6s, how out, bowler,
fielder. **Fall of wickets**: team score at each wicket and the batter out.

## Balancing the book (do it every over at least every few overs)
1. **Batting**: sum of all batters' runs + total extras = team total.
2. **Bowling**: sum of runs conceded by all bowlers + byes + leg-byes + penalty runs
   = team total. (Wides and no-balls are already inside bowlers' figures.)
3. **Wickets**: bowlers' wickets + run outs (+ retired/obstructions etc.) = wickets down.
4. **Balls**: each over has 6 legal deliveries in the bowling grid.
5. Compare with the **scoreboard** and, where there are two scorers, with each other
   at intervals; resolve differences immediately while memories are fresh.

## Gotchas
- **Overthrows**: credited to the batter if off the bat, otherwise to byes/leg-byes.
- **Boundary off a no-ball**: 1 no-ball + 4 (or 6) to the batter = 5 to the bowler.
- **Short runs** and **runs disallowed** on umpire's signal — watch the umpires and
  acknowledge every signal.
- Bowlers changing ends mid-over (injury): record the over parts to each bowler.

## Verify
- [ ] Batting total + extras = team total.
- [ ] Bowling analysis + byes + leg-byes + penalties = team total.
- [ ] Wickets accounted for: bowler, run out, other.
- [ ] Every over has six legal balls; scoreboard matches the book at each interval.
