# Skateboarding — ollie progression and skatepark basics

**Goal:** go from first push to a reliable rolling ollie, first grind and
first drop-in, without picking up bad habits or injuries, and fit in at a
skatepark.

## 1. Set-up and safety
- **Board:** a complete from a skate shop (not a toy-store board). Deck
  width roughly to shoe size/preference (~8.0–8.5" common for adults);
  softer wheels (~78–90A) for rough streets, harder (~99A+) for parks.
- **Helmet** always; wrist guards for beginners (wrist fractures are among
  the most common skate injuries); knee/elbow pads for transition.
- Flat, smooth ground away from traffic to start.

## 2. Stance and pushing
- **Regular** = left foot forward, **goofy** = right forward. Test: which
  foot you'd naturally step forward with if nudged from behind.
- Front foot over the front bolts, pointing roughly forward while
  pushing; push with the back foot, then place it on the tail and turn
  both feet sideways.
- Avoid **mongo** (pushing with the front foot) — it makes many tricks harder later.
- **Stopping:** foot brake (drag the back foot's sole flat), or step off
  at low speed. Tail-stops wear the tail and come later.

## 3. Falling
- Fall to the **side and roll**, onto forearms/shoulder and hips; don't
  catch yourself with straight arms.
- On ramps, **knee-slide** on pads.
- Practise falling deliberately on grass or with pads.

## 4. The ollie, step by step
1. **Stationary on grass/carpet** first (board won't roll away).
2. Back foot: ball of foot on the tail's centre. Front foot: just behind the front bolts, angled slightly.
3. Crouch, then **snap the tail** down hard while jumping.
4. As the tail hits, **drag the front foot's outside edge** up the grip
   tape toward the nose — this levels the board.
5. **Lift the back knee** so the tail can rise.
6. Land with feet over the bolts, knees bent.
7. Move to slow rolling ollies, then ollie over a line, then a small stick.
Common faults: no knee lift (board stays low), front foot sliding too
early (board doesn't pop), leaning back (board shoots out).

## 5. Next tricks
- **Kickturn** (pivot on back wheels) and **manual** (balance on back wheels)
  — build board control.
- **Pop shove-it** (tail scoop, board spins 180) — usually easier than the kickflip.
- **Kickflip:** ollie motion, front foot flicks off the heel-side of the nose.
- **50-50 grind** on a low ledge/rail with wax, once ollies clear the height.

## 6. Transition and dropping in
- Start pumping and kickturning on a small quarter or mini-ramp.
- **Drop-in:** tail on the coping, back foot holding it; front foot on the
  front bolts; **commit and lean forward** over the front truck. Leaning
  back is the classic slam. Do it first with a friend holding your hand.

## 7. Skatepark etiquette
- **Take turns**; watch the flow, make eye contact, go when there's a gap.
  Cutting someone's line is **snaking**.
- Don't sit or stand on landings or in the middle of runs.
- Beginners: early hours are quieter. Clear the area fast after a fall.
- Respect younger kids and scooter riders — the park is shared.

## Gotchas
- Learning in socks/runners with thick soles — flat vulcanised skate shoes
  give board feel and wear well.
- Trying kickflips before a consistent ollie.
- Riding wet ground (bearings and grip suffer, slipping is likely).
- **Wheel durometer and deck sizes are typical ranges; preference varies.**

## Verify
- [ ] Can push, carve and foot-brake comfortably.
- [ ] Rolling ollie over a line 8/10 attempts.
- [ ] Has fallen and rolled out on purpose.
- [ ] Dropped in on a small ramp with weight forward.
- [ ] Takes turns without snaking at a busy session.
