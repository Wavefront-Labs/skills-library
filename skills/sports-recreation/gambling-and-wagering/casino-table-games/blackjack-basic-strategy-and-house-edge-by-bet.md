# Casino table games — blackjack basic strategy and house edge by bet

**Goal:** if you play, play the bets that cost least. Every casino game has
a house edge; strategy minimises it but (outside legal advantage play)
can't remove it. **Set a session budget before you sit down.**

## 1. Blackjack basic strategy (multi-deck, dealer stands soft 17, double after split allowed)
Read the dealer's upcard, then:

**Hard totals**
- 17+: stand.
- 13–16: stand vs 2–6; hit vs 7–A.
- 12: stand vs 4–6; hit otherwise.
- 11: double vs 2–10; hit vs A (double vs A if dealer hits soft 17).
- 10: double vs 2–9.
- 9: double vs 3–6; else hit.
- 8 or less: hit.

**Soft totals (hand with an ace counted as 11)**
- A,9: stand. A,8: stand (double vs 6 under H17 rules).
- A,7: double vs 3–6 (H17: 2–6); stand vs 2, 7, 8; hit vs 9, 10, A.
- A,6: double vs 3–6, else hit.
- A,4 / A,5: double vs 4–6, else hit.
- A,2 / A,3: double vs 5–6, else hit.
(If doubling isn't allowed, hit — except soft 18 stands.)

**Pairs**
- Always split A,A and 8,8. Never split 10s or 5s (play 5,5 as hard 10).
- 9,9: split vs 2–6, 8, 9; stand vs 7, 10, A.
- 7,7: split vs 2–7. 6,6: split vs 2–6.
- 4,4: split vs 5–6. 2,2 / 3,3: split vs 2–7.

**Surrender** (if offered, late surrender): 16 vs 9, 10, A; 15 vs 10.
**Insurance:** don't take it (negative EV without counting).

Small rule differences shift a few edge cases; strategy cards for your
exact rules are allowed at most tables — verify with the casino.

## 2. Blackjack rules that matter
- **3:2 blackjack payout vs 6:5:** 6:5 adds roughly **1.4%** to the house
  edge — the single most important rule. Avoid 6:5 tables.
- Dealer hits soft 17 (H17): ~+0.2%. No double after split: ~+0.15%.
- With good rules and perfect basic strategy the edge is around **0.5%**.
  Errors raise it quickly.

## 3. Card counting (brief)
- **Hi-Lo:** 2–6 = +1, 7–9 = 0, 10–A = −1. Running count ÷ decks remaining
  = true count; bet more at high true counts and apply index deviations.
- Legal in many places but casinos may ban counters, shuffle or limit
  bets. Continuous shuffling machines defeat it. Edge is small and variance large.

## 4. House edge by bet (approximate)
| Game / bet | House edge |
|---|---|
| Baccarat banker (5% commission) | ~1.06% |
| Baccarat player | ~1.24% |
| Baccarat tie (8:1) | ~14.4% |
| Roulette single-zero (European) | 2.70% (≈1.35% on even bets with la partage / en prison) |
| Roulette double-zero (American) | 5.26% (0-00-1-2-3 bet 7.89%) |
| Craps pass / come | ~1.41% |
| Craps don't pass / don't come | ~1.36% |
| Craps free **odds** behind pass | 0% |
| Craps place 6 or 8 | ~1.52% |
| Craps any seven | ~16.7% |
In craps, the best play is a pass line bet with maximum allowed odds
behind it; avoid centre "proposition" bets.

## 5. Session discipline
- Fixed budget, fixed time; leave when either runs out.
- **Betting systems (Martingale etc.) don't change the edge** — they
  trade many small wins for occasional large losses and hit table limits.
- Comps are worth less than the expected loss that earns them.
- Alcohol and fatigue cause strategy errors.

## Gotchas
- 6:5 blackjack marketed as "blackjack pays".
- Splitting 10s, taking insurance on a "good hand".
- **Edge figures are standard published approximations; rules vary by casino.**

## Responsible gambling
Use pre-commitment limits and self-exclusion programmes if gambling
causes harm; contact local gambling support services.
