# Matchday and tailgating — tickets, tailgates and away days

**Goal:** get in with a genuine ticket, enjoy the build-up, stay safe in
the crowd and get home — whether it's a home game, a car-park tailgate or
an away day in another city.

## 1. Tickets
- **Buy from the club/venue or its official resale platform first.**
  Many clubs run a member/season-ticket exchange; official resale is
  usually price-capped and the ticket is re-issued to your name.
- Unofficial resale: in some countries reselling certain tickets without
  authorisation is illegal (e.g. UK football tickets — I believe this is
  still an offence under the 1994 Act, verify locally), and transfer
  restrictions can get the ticket cancelled at the gate.
- **Scam signs:** payment by bank transfer or gift card, "screenshots" of
  mobile tickets (most are rotating barcodes that a screenshot won't scan),
  sellers on social media with no history. Pay with a card or a platform
  that has buyer protection.
- Away tickets are often allocated by loyalty points or membership — know
  your club's scheme and the sale dates.
- Download mobile tickets to the wallet app before leaving; stadium phone
  signal is often poor.

## 2. Getting in
- Check the venue's **bag policy** — many US stadiums allow only clear bags
  of a set size; many European grounds forbid bags above A4/A3 or have a
  bag drop. Also banned items: flares, glass, umbrellas, large flags with
  poles (varies).
- Arrive early: security queues peak 30-45 minutes before kick-off.
- Agree a meeting point with your group in case phones die or there's no
  signal.

## 3. Hosting a tailgate
- **Check the lot rules:** opening time, whether open flames/grills are
  allowed, alcohol rules, space-per-vehicle, no-saving-spots policies.
- **Kit:** pop-up canopy (weight the legs — wind turns them into kites),
  folding table and chairs, grill + spare fuel, cooler(s), rubbish bags,
  hand sanitiser, first-aid kit, phone power bank.
- **Food safety:** separate cooler for raw meat; keep cold food at or below
  about 4 °C / 40 °F; cook to safe internal temperatures (thermometer, not
  colour); don't leave food out more than about 2 hours (1 hour in heat).
- Two coolers: drinks get opened all day, food stays cold.
- Let the grill cool and dispose of coals in designated bins — never under
  a car or in a rubbish bag.
- Pack up by kick-off unless the lot allows in-game tailgating; leave the
  space clean.

## 4. Inside the ground
- Learn the chants and songs before you go — club fan sites and videos;
  join in after you've heard them once.
- Respect the section's norms: standing sections stand; family sections
  don't swear; away fans keep their colours in the away end.
- **Crowd safety:** know the nearest exit; in a crush, keep arms up in front
  of your chest and move diagonally away from pressure; don't climb
  barriers.

## 5. Away days
- Book transport that returns after a late finish or extra time;
  check for engineering works and kick-off changes for TV.
- Follow police/club travel advice for high-risk fixtures (sometimes
  official coaches only or designated pubs).
- Keep ID — some away allocations check it.

## Gotchas
- Kick-off times change for broadcasting weeks before the game; check again
  before booking non-refundable travel.
- A screenshotted ticket from a stranger is the most common scam.
- Heat and alcohol: drink water alternately; a long tailgate in sun is a
  classic way to miss the game.

## Verify
- [ ] Ticket bought from an official or buyer-protected source and stored in the wallet app.
- [ ] Bag complies with the venue policy.
- [ ] Tailgate allowed by the lot rules; raw meat separated; coals disposed of safely.
- [ ] Group has a meeting point and a confirmed route home.
