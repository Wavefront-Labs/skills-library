# Motocross and off-road — attack position, ruts, jumps and enduro obstacles

**Goal:** ride standing and loose so the bike can move under you, corner
in ruts without fighting the bike, and take jumps and obstacles in a
controlled way. Learn at a beginner track or trail with a coach; most
injuries happen when riders go beyond their skill.

## 1. Attack (standing) position
- Balls of the feet on the pegs, knees gripping the bike (not splayed),
  bent knees and hips like an athletic squat.
- **Elbows up and out**, head over the handlebar crossbar area, eyes far
  ahead.
- Light grip — the bike moves under you and the arms and legs absorb it.
- Fingers covering the clutch and front brake (one or two fingers each).
- Stand on rough ground and straights; sit to corner.

## 2. Cornering
- **Brake** standing and in a straight line before the corner, rear and
  front together, weight back slightly.
- **Sit forward** on the seat — practically on the tank — as you enter.
  Weight on the front wheel gives steering grip.
- **Inside leg out** forward (foot near the front axle, off the ground)
  for balance; outside foot weighted on the peg.
- **Ruts:** look ahead through the rut, not down at it. Keep the bike
  leaned to match the rut walls, steady throttle; the rut does the steering.
- **Berms:** carry speed into the banked turn and drive out on the throttle.
- **Flat turns:** more weight on the outside peg, smooth throttle.

## 3. Jumps
- **Approach** in the attack position with consistent speed (the right
  speed comes from building up gradually, never from guessing).
- **Up the face:** steady or slightly increasing throttle; don't chop the
  throttle on the lip (the rear kicks up and the bike nose-dives).
- **In the air:** knees grip, small adjustments — a tap of the rear brake
  drops the nose; a blip of throttle lifts it.
- **Landing:** match the downslope (front slightly first or both wheels
  together), absorb with legs and arms, back on the throttle.
- **Scrub** (laying the bike over to stay low) is an advanced skill for
  later.
- **Whoops:** a series of rollers — either skim the tops at speed with the
  weight back, or jump/roll through each one slowly.

## 4. Gate starts (motocross)
Front wheel against the gate, weight forward over the bars, in second gear
(for many bikes; some use first), revs held at a practised level, eyes on
the gate. Release the clutch as the gate drops. The first corner (the
**holeshot**) is a scramble — choose a line with an exit.

## 5. Enduro and trail obstacles
- **Logs:** approach square, compress the forks just before the log
  (preload), blip the throttle to lift the front over, shift weight forward
  as the rear hits so the bike doesn't stall or flip. Bigger logs need a
  clutch-dump "double blip" — practise small ones first.
- **Rocks:** stand, look past them, steady momentum, let the bike move.
- **Hill climbs:** pick the line from the bottom, momentum before the
  climb, weight forward, keep the rear wheel driving (spin = stall). If it
  fails: front brake on, get off on the uphill side, roll back down with the
  clutch in, turning the bike sideways carefully.
- **Clutch control** — feathering the clutch is the core enduro skill.

## 6. Protective gear (non-negotiable)
Certified off-road helmet, goggles, boots made for motocross, knee braces
or guards, chest/roost protector, gloves, long trousers and jersey. Neck
braces are common. Ride with others in remote areas, carry water and tools.

## Gotchas
- Sitting down and stiff-armed through rough ground.
- Looking at the obstacle (target fixation) instead of the exit.
- Hitting jumps at unknown speed or casing the landing.

## Verify
- [ ] 10 minutes standing on rough terrain without fatigue in the arms.
- [ ] Cornering in a rut with the inside foot forward and eyes up.
- [ ] Clean table-top jumps landing on the downslope at a beginner track.
- [ ] Full gear check before every ride.
