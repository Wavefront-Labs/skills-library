# Boxing: stance, jab-cross and defensive fundamentals

**Goal:** a balanced stance, a jab and cross that land with weight and return to guard,
footwork that never crosses the feet, and basic defence — the base for every
combination. Learn under a coach; this is a practice guide, not a substitute.

## 1. Stance (orthodox; mirror for southpaw)
- Feet roughly shoulder width, lead foot forward, rear foot back and out so a line from
  lead toe to rear heel is roughly straight; toes angled ~45°.
- Weight about 50/50, on the balls of the feet, rear heel slightly raised.
- Knees soft, hips angled, chin tucked, eyes up through the brows.
- **Guard:** rear hand by the cheek/chin, lead hand at eye height a fist or so in front,
  elbows down protecting the body.
Test: a partner pushes gently from front, back and sides — you shouldn't stumble.

## 2. Jab
- Lead hand straight from guard, rotate the fist to palm-down at the end, shoulder rises
  to cover the chin.
- Small step with the lead foot on a power jab; rear foot follows the same distance.
- **Snap back** along the same line to the guard — the return is as important as the punch.
- Rear hand stays home on the chin.

## 3. Cross (straight right for orthodox)
- Drive off the rear foot: pivot the ball of the foot, heel turning out ("squash the bug"),
  hips and shoulders rotate through, arm extends straight, fist turns over.
- Weight transfers forward but the head stays over the lead knee, not beyond it.
- Lead hand returns to guard the face during the cross.
- Return to stance immediately.

## 4. Footwork rules
- **Step-drag:** move the foot nearest the direction first, the other follows — forward
  = lead foot first; back = rear foot first; left (orthodox) = lead foot; right = rear foot.
- Never cross the feet or bring them together.
- **Pivot** on the lead foot to change angle.
- Drill: ladder/line drills, then shadow boxing combining steps and punches.

## 5. Defence
- **Parry:** small deflection of an incoming jab with the rear palm — don't reach.
- **Slip:** small rotation and knee bend so the head moves just outside the punch line
  (slip the jab to the outside, cross to the inside). Eyes stay on the opponent.
- **Roll (bob and weave):** bend the knees to dip under a hook in a U shape — move with
  the legs, not bending at the waist.
- **Block / shell:** gloves to temples, elbows tight — absorbs hooks and body shots.
- Always **return fire**: slip-and-counter is the point of defence.

## 6. Training structure (typical session)
Skipping/warm-up → shadow boxing 3 × 3 min → pad work with a coach → heavy bag
3–6 rounds → defensive drills with a partner → conditioning → stretch. Round timers at
3 min on / 1 min rest mirror competition.

## 7. Equipment and safety
- **Hand wraps** every time (protect the metacarpals and wrist); gloves around
  14–16 oz for sparring (heavier for heavier boxers; follow gym rules), mouthguard,
  headguard for amateur-style sparring.
- Sparring only under supervision and at controlled intensity; head trauma is
  cumulative. Stop for any concussion symptoms and follow return-to-play guidance.

## Common faults
- Dropping the rear hand when jabbing; looping the cross; leaning forward over the lead
  knee; flaring elbows; looking at the floor when slipping; feet too narrow.

- [ ] Jab and cross return to guard with the non-punching hand protecting the chin (check on video).
- [ ] Footwork drills done without crossing feet.
- [ ] Can slip a slow partner jab and counter.
- [ ] Wraps, mouthguard and supervised sparring are non-negotiable.
