# Wrestling: single and double leg takedowns and the sprawl

**Goal:** shoot a leg attack that scores from a sound stance, finish it, and stop the same
attacks with a sprawl. Drill with a coach and a cooperative partner first.

## 1. Rules context (check the current rulebook)
- **Freestyle** (UWW): leg attacks allowed; takedowns score; exposure ("danger") scores;
  par terre turns like the gut wrench.
- **Greco-Roman:** no attacks below the waist and no using the legs to attack — this
  skill's leg attacks don't apply; the sprawl/hips-down defence partly does.
- **Folkstyle** (US scholastic/collegiate): leg attacks allowed, riding time and escapes
  matter, back exposure scored as near fall.
Point values and passivity rules change; verify for your competition.

## 2. Stance and motion
- Feet slightly wider than shoulders, staggered or square; knees bent, hips down,
  back straight, head up, elbows in, hands active in front.
- Move in **short steps without crossing feet**; circle; keep your level.
- **Hand fighting:** control wrists and ties (collar tie, underhook) to create angles
  and pull the opponent's weight onto a leg.

## 3. Level change and penetration step
The shot fails if you bend at the waist. Sequence:
1. **Level change:** drop the hips by bending the knees, keep the back upright and head up.
2. **Penetration step:** lead foot steps deep between the opponent's feet; drive off
   the rear foot; lead knee touches down lightly (or not at all on some styles).
3. Head position: for a double leg the head goes to the side, ear against ribs, not down
   the middle (neck injury risk and easy front headlock for the opponent).

## 4. Double leg finish
Arms wrap behind both knees. Options:
- **Drive across:** bring the rear leg up (step up), drive through at an angle, turn the
  corner so they land on their side/back.
- **Lift and return** (when they sprawl partially): stand up, lift, return to the mat
  under control — safely, no slamming (illegal and dangerous).
Keep driving with the legs; don't stop and pull with the arms.

## 5. Single leg finish
Capture one leg (head inside or outside depending on setup), bring the leg up between
your legs or to your hip ("high crotch" or "shelf"), keep your head up and pressure
into them. Common finishes: run the pipe (step back and turn, driving them down),
trip the far leg, or switch to a double. Don't let the head drop below their hips.

## 6. The sprawl
When they shoot:
1. **Hips back and down** hard, legs shoot backward and wide, on the balls of the feet.
2. **Hands/forearms** block the head and shoulders (crossface or downblock).
3. Hips heavy onto their head/shoulders.
4. **Circle** (spin behind) to score your own takedown.
Drill reaction time: partner shoots on a random signal, you sprawl and go behind.

## 7. Drilling plan
- Stance-and-motion and shot drills solo (penetration steps across the mat).
- Partner drilling: setup → shot → finish, 10–20 reps per side, then live
  "go-behind" situations from fixed positions.
- Conditioning matters: wrestling is anaerobic-heavy; 2–3 minute periods.

## Safety
- Never slam; keep control when lifting.
- Neck: avoid driving with the head down; strengthen the neck progressively.
- Skin infections (ringworm, impetigo, herpes gladiatorum) spread on mats — shower
  after, clean mats daily, no training with an active lesion.

## Common faults
Bending at the waist, shooting from too far away, stopping the feet on contact, head
down on the single, sprawling with hips high.
