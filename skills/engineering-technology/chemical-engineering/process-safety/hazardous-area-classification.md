# Hazardous area classification

**Goal:** Classify hazardous areas for flammable gases and vapours (zones or divisions) and select suitable equipment; defer to the local code and a competent person.

## Steps
1. Identify the sources of release (grade: continuous, primary, secondary; grade definitions are in the standard (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked)) and the ventilation effectiveness, to assign zone 0, 1 or 2 (IEC and ATEX system) or Class I Divisions (North American system).
2. Use the relevant standards (IEC 60079-10-1:2020, Explosive atmospheres - Part 10-1: Classification of areas - Explosive gas atmospheres, per the IEC webstore; NFPA 497 and API RP 505 in North America (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked); confirm the current editions and the locally adopted version).
3. Draw the classification on the plan and elevation, with the extent determined by the release rate, the gas density and the ventilation.
4. Select equipment with the protection method, gas group and temperature class suited to the zone and the substance.
5. Record the basis in the area classification document and review on change; a competent person carries out and signs it. Professional practice guidance only; the governing regulators, standards and a qualified engineer decide.

## Gotchas
- Zone and division systems are different; do not mix them.
- Dusts are classified separately.

## Verify
- [ ] Source of release schedule is complete.
- [ ] Equipment certificates match the zone and gas group.
