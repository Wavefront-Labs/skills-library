# Static electricity and bonding

**Goal:** Control static electricity in flammable service: bonding and earthing, charge relaxation, flow velocity limits and non-conductive materials.

## Steps
1. Identify the charge generation: flow of low-conductivity liquids in pipe, filling with splash, powder transfer, and the use of non-conductive materials.
2. Bond and earth all conductive equipment (hoses, vessels, tanks, funnels) with a continuous path and check the resistance in line with the standard practices (for example NFPA 77, Recommended Practice on Static Electricity, or IEC 60079-32-1; confirm the edition (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked)).
3. Limit the velocity in pipelines for low-conductivity liquids and use dip pipes or bottom filling to avoid splash.
4. Avoid insulated conductors and loose isolated metal parts; handle non-conductive containers with care.
5. Include inert gas blanketing for flammable atmospheres and train the operators. Professional practice guidance only; the governing regulators, standards and a qualified engineer decide.

## Gotchas
- A good earth does not remove the charge on insulating materials.
- Velocity limits depend on the conductivity.

## Verify
- [ ] Continuity of bonding is inspected.
- [ ] Filling procedure avoids splash.
