# Pre-startup safety review

**Goal:** confirm that a new or modified facility is ready to introduce hazardous material safely. OSHA 29 CFR 1910.119(i) requires a PSSR for new facilities and for modified facilities where the modification changes process safety information; other jurisdictions and company standards have comparable requirements. Confirm the current regulation and your procedures.

## What the review confirms
Paragraph (i)(2) requires confirmation, before hazardous chemicals are introduced, that: construction and equipment are in accordance with design specifications; safety, operating, maintenance and emergency procedures are in place and adequate; for new facilities a PHA has been performed and recommendations resolved or implemented before startup (modified facilities meet the MOC paragraph (l)); and operator training is complete. The list below expands these into typical practice and is not all regulatory text.
1. **Construction and equipment** as designed: walk-down against P&IDs, equipment installed per specification, pressure tests and inspections done, certificates and as-built drawings.
2. **Safety, operating, maintenance and emergency procedures** are in place and adequate.
3. **PHA** has been done for new facilities and its recommendations resolved or tracked for completion before start-up (or with approved interim controls).
4. **MOC** requirements satisfied for modified facilities.
5. **Training** completed for operators and maintenance staff, including new procedures.
6. **Instrumentation and protective systems**: loop checks, trips tested, relief devices installed with correct set points and lock-open valves in position, alarms functional, SIF functional tests recorded.
7. **Utilities, fire protection, gas detection and emergency systems** ready.
8. **Mechanical integrity** baseline inspection done and the programme set up.
9. **Housekeeping and temporary items** removed (blinds, jumpers, temporary supports, construction debris).

## Process
1. Prepare the checklist tailored to the project; assign discipline owners.
2. Walk down with a multidisciplinary team including operations.
3. Categorise findings: must be completed before start-up, or after start-up with an approved plan and date.
4. Sign-off by authorised management; record in the project file.
5. Start up stepwise: leak test with inert fluid, then introduction of hazardous material with defined holds.

## Gotchas
- Schedule pressure leads to "start-up with open items"; open critical items must block start-up.
- Verifying documents is not the same as verifying the plant; do the walk-down.

## Verify
- [ ] Signed PSSR report with the list of completed and deferred items and authorisations.
- [ ] Walk-down record with photos or checklists.
