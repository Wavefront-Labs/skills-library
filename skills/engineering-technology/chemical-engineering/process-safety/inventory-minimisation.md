# Inventory minimisation

**Goal:** Reduce hazardous inventory by design: smaller vessels, continuous in place of batch, just-in-time storage, and compact equipment.

## Steps
1. Map where the largest inventories of hazardous material are (storage, reactors, surge drums, columns).
2. Cut the vessel holdup by a suitable design: smaller surge times consistent with the control, continuous process, internals with low liquid holdup, thin-film evaporators.
3. Reduce storage by changing the delivery frequency and keeping only the day tanks near the plant.
4. Consider intensified equipment (compact heat exchangers, microreactors) where applicable.
5. Re-evaluate the consequence and the regulatory threshold effect of the lower inventory.

## Gotchas
- A smaller buffer reduces the operational flexibility.
- Confirm the threshold quantities in the applicable regulation.

## Verify
- [ ] Inventory before and after is quantified.
- [ ] Operability review accepts the reduced buffers.
