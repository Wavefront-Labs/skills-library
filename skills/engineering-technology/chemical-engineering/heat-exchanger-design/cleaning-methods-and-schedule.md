# Cleaning methods and schedule

**Goal:** Choose cleaning methods (chemical, hydroblasting, mechanical, online) and an economic cleaning schedule for an exchanger.

## Steps
1. Select the method from the deposit type and the access: chemical cleaning for scale, high-pressure water jetting for hard deposits, mechanical tube cleaners, and online systems for cooling water.
2. Check the material compatibility of chemicals and the waste handling.
3. Set the cleaning interval by minimising the total cost (lost performance cost growing with time plus the cost of each cleaning).
4. Plan access, isolation and entry according to the site safety procedures.
5. Test after cleaning (pressure drop and U) to verify the recovery.

## Gotchas
- Hydroblasting needs trained crews and exclusion zones.
- Chemicals can damage tubes if not controlled.

## Verify
- [ ] U and pressure drop return to near the clean values.
- [ ] Waste disposal route is defined.
