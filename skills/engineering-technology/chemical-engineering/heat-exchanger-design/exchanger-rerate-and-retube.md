# Exchanger rerate and retube

**Goal:** Re-rate an existing exchanger for new duties and decide whether to clean, retube, add shells or replace.

## Steps
1. Gather the datasheet, drawings and the new process data, and calibrate a model to the plant data first.
2. Rate for the new duty with the actual fouling and compare the required area to the available area (over-surface).
3. Options in rising cost: clean and operate differently, add baffles or turbulators, retube with a different material or pitch, add a shell in series or parallel, or replace the unit.
4. Check the pressure drop, the vibration and the mechanical limits of the shell and the nozzles.
5. Estimate the cost and the shutdown needs, and choose by economic comparison.

## Gotchas
- Retubing to a closer pitch can raise vibration risk and pressure drop.
- Check the remaining life of the shell before an investment.

## Verify
- [ ] Calibrated model matches the plant data.
- [ ] New duty is met with margin.
