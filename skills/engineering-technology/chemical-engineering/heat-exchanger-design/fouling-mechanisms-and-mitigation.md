# Fouling mechanisms and mitigation

**Goal:** Identify fouling mechanisms (particulate, crystallisation, corrosion, biological, chemical reaction) and choose mitigation.

## Steps
1. Classify the fouling: particulate, crystallisation or scaling, corrosion, biofouling, chemical reaction (coking, polymerisation).
2. Control with velocity (higher velocity reduces deposition), lower wall temperature, filtration, water treatment and antifoulant chemicals.
3. Use design measures: tube-side location of the fouling fluid, straight tubes for mechanical cleaning, and spare capacity for cleaning.
4. Choose design fouling factors from TEMA tables or company practice, and recognise they are allowances and not predictions.
5. Monitor after startup to learn the real rate.

## Gotchas
- Overspecifying the fouling resistance leads to low velocity and more fouling.
- Chemical programmes need monitoring.

## Verify
- [ ] Mechanism is identified from deposit analysis.
- [ ] Mitigation has a measurable indicator.
