# Spiral and pche selection

**Goal:** Decide when to select spiral or printed-circuit heat exchangers: duty, pressure, fouling and cost compared with other types.

## Steps
1. Spiral exchangers suit viscous, fouling or slurry streams with moderate pressure; they offer a single flow path and self-cleaning tendency.
2. Printed circuit exchangers (PCHE) suit high pressure and temperature clean fluids and compact duties, as in gas processing, LNG and hydrogen; fine channels need clean fluids and filtering.
3. Compare using the pressure and temperature limits, the fouling propensity, the cleaning method and the capital cost with the vendors.
4. Provide the vendor with full process data, cases and allowable pressure drops.
5. Check the inspection and repair options; the compact types are hard to repair.

## Gotchas
- Compact types have limited cleaning access; wrong fouling assumption is costly.
- Vendor data decide the limits.
