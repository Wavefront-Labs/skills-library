# Tube failure modes and leak detection

**Goal:** Recognise common tube failure modes (corrosion, erosion, fatigue, fretting, freeze) and find leaks in an exchanger.

## Steps
1. Identify the mode from the location and appearance: pitting and under-deposit corrosion, impingement erosion at the inlet, fretting at the baffles from vibration, fatigue at the tubesheet, freeze damage.
2. Detect a leak by process signs (contamination of the cooling water, loss of product, pressure change) and by analysis of the cooler water.
3. Test by hydrotest of the shell side, by pressure decay, or by helium or air tests, followed by plugging the leaking tubes.
4. Inspect with eddy current or ultrasonic internal rotary inspection and keep the data to trend wall loss.
5. Fix the root cause rather than only plugging; keep a plug limit (a common practice is to retube beyond a given fraction; use company criteria).

## Gotchas
- Leak testing of a high-pressure side into a low-pressure side needs a safe procedure and relief.
- Isolation and cleaning before entry follows site safety rules.

## Verify
- [ ] Root cause is recorded.
- [ ] Plug count is tracked against the limit.
