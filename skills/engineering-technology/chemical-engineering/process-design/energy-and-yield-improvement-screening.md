# Energy and yield improvement screening

**Goal:** Screen energy and yield improvements: loss accounting, quick wins, pinch targets and ranking by payback.

## Steps
1. Build a loss ledger: where energy and material leave the process (flue gas, cooling water, purge, off-spec, side products).
2. Rank opportunities by size of loss; identify operating changes with no capital first.
3. Use a pinch or simulation to estimate recoverable heat and the cost to recover it.
4. Estimate yield gains from selectivity, recycle and purge improvements.
5. Rank by simple payback and net present value; include risk and interaction between measures.

## Gotchas
- Savings from different measures are not always additive.
- Verify with measurements after implementation.

## Verify
- [ ] Ledger totals match the plant balance.
- [ ] Each measure has a stated saving and cost basis.
