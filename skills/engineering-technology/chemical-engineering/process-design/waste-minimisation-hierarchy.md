# Waste minimisation hierarchy

**Goal:** Apply the waste hierarchy in process design: prevent at source, reduce, reuse, recycle, recover energy, treat and then dispose.

## Steps
1. Map each waste stream and its source on the flowsheet (purges, spent solvents, off-spec, wastewater).
2. Prevent first: change the chemistry or conditions, improve selectivity or conversion, and reduce purges.
3. Reuse and recycle within the process (solvent recovery, catalyst recovery), then recover energy and only then treat and dispose.
4. Cost each option including disposal and the regulatory burden.
5. Check that recycling does not build up impurities and harm quality.

## Gotchas
- Legal waste classes and duties differ by jurisdiction; confirm local rules.
- Recycles need a purge for impurity control.

## Verify
- [ ] Each waste stream has a hierarchy decision recorded.
- [ ] The cost basis includes disposal fees.
