# Life cycle assessment for processes

**Goal:** Carry out a process LCA following the ISO 14040 and 14044 framework: goal and scope, inventory, impact assessment and interpretation.

## Steps
1. Goal and scope: define the functional unit (for example 1 kg of product) and system boundary (cradle-to-gate or cradle-to-grave).
2. Inventory: gather inputs, outputs and emissions from the mass and energy balances and from databases for upstream materials.
3. Impact assessment: choose methods and categories (for example climate change) and record the method version.
4. Interpretation: check completeness and sensitivity, uncertainty and allocation choices.
5. Report assumptions and data sources; third-party review is needed for comparative claims to the public.

## Gotchas
- Allocation and boundary choices can change the answer; test them.
- Confirm the current edition of ISO 14040 and 14044 before citing.

## Verify
- [ ] Functional unit is the same across alternatives.
- [ ] Sensitivity to the main assumptions is shown.
