# Ultrafiltration fouling and cleaning

**Goal:** Control ultrafiltration fouling: flux decline mechanisms, critical flux, backwash and chemical cleaning cycles.

## Steps
1. Identify the foulant (particles, organics, biofilm, scale) from feed analysis and flux decline pattern.
2. Operate below the critical flux and use crossflow or air scour to limit the deposition.
3. Backwash periodically; set the cycle from the transmembrane pressure rise.
4. Chemical cleaning: choose chemicals by foulant (alkaline with oxidant for organics, acid for scale) within the membrane tolerance given by the vendor.
5. Track the permeability and the cleaning recovery; fix pretreatment if irreversible fouling grows.

## Gotchas
- Chemical tolerance of the membrane (pH, chlorine exposure) is a vendor limit.
- Cleaning wastes need treatment.

## Verify
- [ ] Permeability recovers to near baseline after cleaning.
- [ ] Cleaning frequency is within the target.
