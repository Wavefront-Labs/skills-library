# Membrane and microreactors

**Goal:** Know when membrane reactors and microreactors help: equilibrium shift, selective feed, heat control and numbering-up.

## Steps
1. Membrane reactors remove a product (for example hydrogen) or distribute a reactant, shifting equilibrium or improving selectivity; the benefit depends on permeance and selectivity.
2. Check membrane stability at the reaction temperature and compatibility with the gases.
3. Microreactors give very high surface-to-volume ratios, so fast heat and mass transfer; good for highly exothermic or hazardous fast reactions with small inventory.
4. Scale by numbering-up parallel channels; design the flow distribution; watch fouling and blockage by solids.
5. Compare the economic case against a conventional reactor.

## Gotchas
- Membrane cost and lifetime often decide feasibility.
- Solids are a problem in microchannels.

## Verify
- [ ] Conversion gain is estimated against the conventional case.
- [ ] Distribution across parallel channels is checked.
