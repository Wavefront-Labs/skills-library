# Polymerisation reactor considerations

**Goal:** List the design issues for polymerisation reactors: viscosity rise, heat removal, molecular weight control and reactor type choice.

## Steps
1. Choose the process (bulk, solution, suspension, emulsion, gas phase) from the heat removal need and the product form.
2. Viscosity can rise by orders of magnitude with conversion; this limits mixing and heat transfer and requires special agitators or loop reactors.
3. Control molecular weight with temperature, initiator or catalyst, chain transfer agent and monomer concentration.
4. Chain growth is exothermic; allow for the heat of polymerisation and the autoacceleration (gel effect) that can speed the rate.
5. Plan safe operation: inhibitor addition, emergency cooling and relief for runaway.

## Gotchas
- Runaway polymerisation is a major hazard; involve process safety specialists.
- Data for the real recipe are needed; textbook rules are indicative.

## Verify
- [ ] Heat removal exceeds the peak heat release.
- [ ] Product specification range is achievable at the design conditions.
