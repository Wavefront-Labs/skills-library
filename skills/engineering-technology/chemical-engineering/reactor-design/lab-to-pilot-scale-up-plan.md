# Lab to pilot scale up plan

**Goal:** Plan a lab-to-pilot scale-up: what to measure, what to hold constant, scale factor and the data to collect for the plant design.

## Steps
1. Define what the pilot must prove: kinetics, selectivity, catalyst life, mass transfer, separation behaviour, fouling and materials.
2. Choose the scale factor with the cost and the risk in mind; use modelling to identify unknown parameters.
3. Hold constant the dimensionless groups or intensive properties that control the phenomenon of concern.
4. Instrument for the balances (mass, energy, composition) and for the dynamics and run long enough for deactivation and impurity build-up.
5. Document the results as design data with uncertainty and plan the step to the demonstration scale if needed.

## Gotchas
- A pilot that is not representative (wall effects, wrong materials) gives misleading data.
- Include startup and upset testing.

## Verify
- [ ] Mass balance closes in the pilot.
- [ ] Design data are recorded with the uncertainty.
