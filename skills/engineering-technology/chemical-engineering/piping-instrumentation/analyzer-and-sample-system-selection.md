# Analyzer and sample system selection

**Goal:** Select process analyzers and design the sample system: fast loop, conditioning, and a representative low-lag sample.

## Steps
1. Define the measured property, accuracy, response time and the maintenance level the plant can support; consider whether a lab sample suffices.
2. Choose the technology (gas chromatograph, near infrared, pH, conductivity, oxygen, moisture) for the component and the matrix.
3. Design the sample system: tap location, probe, fast loop to reduce lag, conditioning (filter, cooler, pressure reduction, vaporiser) and return or safe disposal of the sample.
4. Place analyzers in suitable shelters and review the hazardous area classification and the toxic gas release risk.
5. Plan the calibration and validation checks.

## Gotchas
- Most analyzer problems are in the sample system.
- Sample lag can exceed the response of the analyzer.

## Verify
- [ ] Total lag time meets the control requirement.
- [ ] Calibration gas and validation routine are defined.
