# Pid review checklist

**Goal:** Review a P&ID with a checklist: equipment, isolation, drains and vents, instruments, safeguards, relief, utilities and operability.

## Steps
1. Check each equipment item for isolation valves, bypasses, drains, vents, sample points, and spares.
2. Verify instruments: measurement ranges, control valve failure positions, locations, and independence of safety instrumented functions.
3. Check relief devices and discharge routes, and the consistency with the relief study.
4. Verify the line classes, spec breaks, insulation, tracing and the pressure rating across the flange.
5. Walk the start-up, shutdown and abnormal cases, and mark up the changes with revision clouds; record actions.

## Gotchas
- Reviews done only on the normal case miss start-up problems.
- Cross-check the P&ID against the PFD and the line list.

## Verify
- [ ] Review actions are logged and closed.
- [ ] P&ID revision is issued after the changes.
