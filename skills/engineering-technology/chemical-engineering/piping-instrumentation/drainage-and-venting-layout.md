# Drainage and venting layout

**Goal:** Lay out drains and vents for process piping: high-point vents, low-point drains, slope, closed drain systems and safe discharge.

## Steps
1. Place vents at high points and drains at low points of every pipe section that can be isolated for hydrotest or maintenance.
2. Slope lines to avoid pockets; use eccentric reducers (flat on top for pump suction) and avoid liquid traps in gas lines.
3. Use closed drain systems for hazardous liquids and route vents to a safe location or to a flare system according to the hazard.
4. Size drain and vent connections per the piping class standard, with a double isolation where the fluid is hazardous.
5. Consider the access for operators, the elevation and the freezing risk.

## Gotchas
- Open drains of toxic or flammable liquids are not acceptable under most company standards.
- Pocket in gas lines gives liquid slugs.

## Verify
- [ ] All isolated sections have drain and vent.
- [ ] Discharge routes are safe.
