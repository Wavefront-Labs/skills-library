# Child restraint systems and ISOFIX

## 1. Regulatory frame (confirm current)
The UN regulation and ISO 13216 statements below are unconfirmed (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked).
- **UN R44** (older, weight-group based) and **UN R129 (i-Size)** (height-based, includes side impact and rearward-facing to a higher age) govern child restraint approval in UN-regulation markets. **UN R14** covers belt anchorages including ISOFIX points and **UN R16** seat belts.
- **US:** FMVSS 213 (child restraint systems; the eCFR text now notes it applies unless the seat or vehicle is certified to the 213b version) and FMVSS 225 (child restraint anchorage systems, "LATCH"); titles checked in 49 CFR Part 571. Canada has parallel rules.
- Consumer ratings (Euro NCAP, NHTSA, IIHS) also assess child-seat installation and dummy readings in vehicle tests.
Check which regulations are mandatory in each target market and which edition and amendment series applies.

## 2. Vehicle-side hardware
- **ISOFIX lower anchorages:** two rigid bars at the seat cushion/back junction on outboard rear seats (and others), specified in ISO 13216 and local rules for position, strength and accessibility. Load paths into the body must carry the child seat crash load; design the cross-beam or seat-back structure accordingly.
- **Top tether anchorage** (US) or **support leg floor contact / top tether** (UN i-Size options): anchor on the parcel shelf, seat back or floor; strength tested by regulation.
- **Seat-belt geometry:** belt routes, buckle position and stalk length must allow tight installation; belt buckles that interfere with the child seat base cause misuse.
- **Seat shape:** cushion slope and back angle for stable contact; clear space for rearward-facing seats behind a front seat.
- **Child lock** and window controls for rear doors.

## 3. Airbag interaction
Front passenger airbag must be deactivated or suppressed when a rearward-facing child seat is installed (manual cut-off switch with indicator, or occupant classification system); labels warn of the hazard. Side and curtain airbag deployment envelopes must not injure a child seated close to the door.

## 4. Verification
Fit lists with child-seat manufacturers (installation compatibility list), anchorage strength tests, dummy tests with child dummies (Q-series, Hybrid III child dummies per the protocol), usability checks (installation time, misuse). Provide clear owner-manual instructions.
