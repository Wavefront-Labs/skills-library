# Rear impact and fuel system protection

1. Regulations: FMVSS 301 limits fuel leakage after frontal, side and rear impacts (rear test with a moving deformable barrier at 80 +/- 1 km/h, 70 percent overlap; frontal fixed barrier up to 48 km/h; side MDB 53 +/- 1 km/h; each followed by a static rollover; 49 CFR 571.301 S6), and UN R34 covers fire risks including rear impact (gated: unconfirmed, unece.org returned 403). FMVSS 301 spillage limits in barrier crashes: at most 28 g from impact until motion stops, 142 g in the first 5 minutes after, then 28 g per 1-minute interval for the next 25 minutes. Confirm the current texts.
2. Layout: place the fuel tank ahead of the rear axle in the protected zone, within the wheelbase and under the rear seat (design practice, gated: unconfirmed), away from crush zones, with shielding and protected fittings; filler neck with breakaway features.
3. Structure: rear longitudinals and crash zones in steel/aluminium to absorb energy; cross-members to protect the tank; avoid sharp edges near the tank.
4. Seats: seat-back strength to limit rotation and occupant ramping up; head restraint geometry for whiplash (consumer tests include dynamic seat tests).
5. Components: fuel pump flange and lines with shut-off valves (rollover valve), fuel line routing and clips with burst tolerance, electrical isolation of the fuel pump on crash signal.
6. EV: rear-mounted components such as motors and the charging port; battery location mostly under the floor; keep HV lines away from crush zones; see HV post-crash safety.
7. Test: rear-barrier tests, plus component tests (fuel tank impact, static rollover).

**Gotchas:** welded tank brackets failing; filler hose pulling out.

## Verify
- [ ] No fuel leakage above the limit after the specified test sequence.
- [ ] Tank intrusion clearance measured in test.
