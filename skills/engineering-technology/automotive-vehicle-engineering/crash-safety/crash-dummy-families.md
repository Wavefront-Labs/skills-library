Safety-critical, regulated work. This is professional practice guidance: confirm the current regulation, edition and market, and defer sign-off to qualified safety and homologation engineers.

# Crash dummy families

Dummy assignments by regulation are unconfirmed (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked) except that FMVSS 214 (49 CFR 571.214) references the ES-2re side dummy (checked in eCFR).

## 1. Why many dummies
Each dummy is designed for a loading direction, body size and set of injury measures. The protocol names the dummy; do not substitute without approval.

## 2. Frontal
- **Hybrid III** 50th percentile male (long-standing, used in FMVSS 208 style tests), 5th percentile female, and child versions (3, 6 and 10-year-old).
- **THOR-50M**: a newer frontal dummy with more biofidelic spine, pelvis, thorax and abdomen and more instrumentation; adopted in some current consumer tests (check the active protocol).
- **Hybrid II** is older and appears in some historical standards.

## 3. Side impact
- **ES-2 / ES-2re** (EuroSID-2 family) for regulatory side tests (UN R95 and FMVSS 214 variants use specific side dummies; check).
- **SID-IIs** (5th female) in US and IIHS procedures.
- **WorldSID** 50th male, a more biofidelic side dummy used in some modern protocols.

## 4. Rear impact (whiplash)
**BioRID II**, a seated dummy with a detailed articulated spine for rear-end neck loads (consumer seat tests).

## 5. Child dummies
Q-series (Q0 to Q10) for European child restraint tests and Euro NCAP; Hybrid III children and CRABI infants in US procedures.

## 6. Pedestrian impactors
Headforms (child, adult), and legforms such as flexible pedestrian legform impactor (flexPLI) in UN R127 and GTR 9 style tests, upper legform for bonnet leading edge.

## 7. Instrumentation
Typical measures: head acceleration (HIC, 3 ms clip), neck forces and moments, chest deflection and acceleration, femur and tibia loads, pelvic acceleration, abdomen force. Calibration (certification) tests must be run before each series; record dummy temperature and humidity conditions. Data processing channel filter classes follow the standard SAE J211 style (CFC filters); confirm in the protocol.

## 8. Practical points
Dummies are calibrated, expensive and wear; keep certification records. Positioning (H-point, seat position, seat back angle, belt routing) strongly affects results and must follow the protocol. Biofidelity limits mean dummy numbers are not direct injury probabilities.

## Verify
- [ ] Protocol-specified dummy and instrumentation used.
- [ ] Certification tests recent and recorded.
- [ ] Positioning and belt routing documented with photographs.
- [ ] Filtering and injury formulae follow the protocol edition.
