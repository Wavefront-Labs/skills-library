# Greenhouse site selection and orientation

**Goal:** a site that gives light, shelter and services at low cost.

## Checklist
1. **Light**: no tall obstructions to the south (in the northern hemisphere) within a distance of several times their height; consider winter sun angle.
2. **Orientation**: at high latitudes, gutter-connected ranges are often oriented with ridges running north-south for even light distribution across the day; single-span houses for winter production at high latitude are often oriented east-west to maximise winter light. Choose by local light and crop; verify with a light simulation if necessary.
3. **Wind**: windbreaks (trees or fences) protect from prevailing winds; allow space (often 10-15 times the windbreak height leeward is the zone of effective protection, but keep trees and buildings from shading the greenhouse); leave gaps for ventilation.
4. **Drainage and soil**: level or gently sloping, well-drained land outside flood plains; check soil bearing.
5. **Water supply**: quantity and quality (EC, alkalinity, iron); rainwater harvesting from the roof (area times rainfall).
6. **Energy**: natural gas line, three-phase power, or biomass supply; heat sources like industrial waste heat.
7. **Access and logistics**: truck access, packing area, labour, expansion room.
8. **Regulations**: zoning, building permits, water use and discharge rules.
