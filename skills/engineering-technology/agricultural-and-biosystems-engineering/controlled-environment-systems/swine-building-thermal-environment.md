# Swine building thermal environment

This is professional practice guidance; animal welfare and building codes of the jurisdiction apply.

## Temperature needs by stage (indicative)
Needs fall with age and size: newborn piglets need a warm creep area at roughly 30-35 C; weaned pigs about 26-30 C initially; growing pigs about 18-24 C; finishing pigs and sows roughly 15-20 C (breed, feed intake, floor type and group size shift these). The lower critical temperature rises with thin, wet or draughty conditions. Check the nutrition and housing guide for the specific system.

## Design elements
1. **Zone heating** for farrowing and nursery: heat lamps, heated mats or covered creeps so that the room can be cooler for the sow while piglets stay warm.
2. **Minimum ventilation** to control moisture, ammonia, CO2 and dust; sets the winter airflow per pig from the heat and moisture balance; avoid draughts at pig level.
3. **Pit ventilation** under slatted floors: pull odour and gases through the pit; do not allow reverse flow.
4. **Cooling**: sprinklers, drippers, cooling pads or tunnel ventilation for sows and finishers in hot weather.
5. **Floor type** (solid, slatted, bedded) affects heat loss and thermal comfort.

## Safety
Manure pits release hydrogen sulphide, ammonia, methane and CO2; during agitation and pumping, concentrations of H2S can be immediately lethal. Ventilate rooms, remove animals when agitating, use gas detectors and never enter pits without confined-space procedures.
