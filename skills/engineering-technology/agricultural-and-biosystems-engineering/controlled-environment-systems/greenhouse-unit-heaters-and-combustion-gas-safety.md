# Greenhouse unit heaters and combustion gas safety

Gas and oil heating appliances and their venting are regulated by fuel gas and building codes; installation must be by qualified, licensed people. This is practice guidance only.

## Types
- **Hanging unit heaters** (gas-fired, oil-fired) with a fan and heat exchanger; **direct-fired** heaters (not recommended in plant houses because exhaust enters the space).
- **Horizontal air flow (HAF) fans** and **polyethylene convection tubes** distribute the warm air through the crop area.

## Plant and people hazards
- **Ethylene** from incomplete combustion damages plants (flower drop, leaf curl) even at very low concentrations; **sulphur dioxide** and nitrogen oxides burn foliage.
- **Carbon monoxide** is a hazard for workers; adequate combustion air and venting prevent it.
- **CO2** from heaters can enrich the air in a controlled system, but only with clean combustion and proper control.

## Installation practice
1. Use **sealed-combustion (direct-vent)** or properly vented heaters with exhaust discharged outside; check clearances to combustible covers.
2. Provide **combustion air** from outdoors through a dedicated opening or duct sized by the manufacturer; the lack of it is a common cause of incomplete combustion.
3. Install CO sensors and alarms.
4. Maintain burners (cleaning, tuning, annual inspection) and check flue integrity.
5. Fuel storage and gas piping per code.
6. Keep distribution tubes and fans from blowing directly onto crops.

- [ ] Combustion analysis (CO, CO2, O2) on commissioning and annually; ethylene symptoms absent on indicator plants such as tomato seedlings.
