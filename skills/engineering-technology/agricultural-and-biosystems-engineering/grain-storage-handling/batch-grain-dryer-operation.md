# Batch grain dryer operation

**Goal:** consistent final moisture per batch with low damage and no fire.

## Operation
1. **Load** uniformly to the fill level specified; do not overfill (plenum blockage).
2. **Set air temperature** per the maker's table for grain type, initial moisture and end use (feed, milling, seed). Seed lots need low temperature.
3. **Time**: estimated from the moisture points to remove and the maker's drying rate; monitor with a moisture meter on samples taken from the unloading stream or a probe.
4. **Cooling**: run the fan with the burner off until the grain is near ambient before unloading to avoid sweating in storage (or move to a cooling bin using dryeration).
5. **Unload** and clean the dryer to remove fines and cracked grain between batches.
6. Record initial and final moisture, temperature, fuel use and time.

## Safety
Dryer fires often start from fines, residues and burner issues; clean screens, check flame safety controls and high-limit thermostats, keep the area around the burner clear, and have extinguishing and emergency procedures.

## Gotchas
- Mixing wet and dry grain in a batch creates a spread of final moisture.
- Over-drying shrinks weight and reduces income.

## Verify
- [ ] Batch average final moisture within target; the range among samples is within about 1 point.
