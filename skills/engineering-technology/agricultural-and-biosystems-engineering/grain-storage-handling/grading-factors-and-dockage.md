# Grading factors and dockage

**Goal:** know what a grade is made of, so handling and cleaning decisions protect the value.

## Standards
Grain is graded under official standards by the national inspection authority: the US official grain standards (Federal Grain Inspection Service, USDA), the Canadian Grain Commission grading guides, Australian and European standards for commodities and trade (including Codex and contract standards). Confirm the standard and edition named in the sales contract.

## Common factors
In the US official standards (7 CFR part 810) the wheat grade table (810.2204) uses minimum test weight per bushel, damaged kernels (with a heat-damaged part), foreign material, shrunken and broken kernels, contrasting classes, stones and other count limits; the corn table (810.404) uses minimum test weight, heat-damaged and total damaged kernels, and broken corn and foreign material; the soybean table (810.1604) uses damaged kernels, foreign material and splits, with no test weight limit. Moisture is not a factor in those tables.

Other factors seen across systems:
- Test weight (bulk density per volume), damaged kernels (heat, insect, mould, sprout, frost, disease), broken kernels and foreign material, shrunken and broken kernels, other grains, objectionable odours, and for some crops specific factors (such as falling number for wheat sprouting, protein, vomitoxin tests where required).
- Moisture is usually reported and subject to price adjustment or discount rather than being a grade factor; wet grain is discounted or refused.
- Dockage is defined for US wheat (810.2202) as all matter other than wheat removable by an approved device, plus small shrivelled pieces of wheat that cannot be recovered by rescreening; in some markets for wheat, barley and other crops it is material removable by cleaning and is deducted by weight before grading. In others, foreign material is a grade factor, and the grain is simply graded as received. The treatment differs by crop and country.

## Practice
- Clean grain on the farm before sale if the discount for foreign material or dockage exceeds the cost (`air-screen-cleaner-selection`).
- Keep the grain dry and cool so that heat damage and mould do not appear in the grade (`mould-growth-and-mycotoxin-risk-limits`).
- Take your own sample before delivery and test moisture (`grain-moisture-measurement-and-meter-checks`) to avoid disputes.
