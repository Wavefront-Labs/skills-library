# Hermetic storage design and monitoring

**Goal:** control insects and moulds in dry grain without chemicals by sealing the grain in a gas-tight space.

## Principle
Grain, insects and microbes respire inside a sealed enclosure. Oxygen falls and carbon dioxide rises until insects are suppressed or killed and mould growth is limited. No insecticide is required, which suits organic and smallholder storage. Moist grain is not safe: sealed storage does not stop anaerobic mould and yeast activity or stop spoilage in wet grain, so the starting moisture must be below the safe level for the grain and the period.

## Forms
- Hermetic bags and multi-layer liners (for example the Purdue Improved Crop Storage triple-layer bags and similar products (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked)), drums and metal silos with gasketed lids, for smallholders.
- Silo bags (long polyethylene tubes filled by a bagging machine) for large volumes in the field.
- Sealed concrete or steel bins and bunkers with gas-tight roof, doors and aeration ports; often also used for storing under controlled atmosphere (`controlled-atmosphere-storage-design-and-control`).

## Design and setup
1. Check grain moisture and temperature before sealing (`grain-moisture-measurement-and-meter-checks`); the grain must be clean and cool.
2. Fill the container as full as possible to reduce headspace and air.
3. For rigid structures, pressure-test the structure and measure the pressure half-life (time for pressure to fall from a set test pressure to half) and compare with the specified value (maker or project specific (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked)); seal leaks with sealant and gaskets. Provide a pressure relief for the diurnal pressure changes so walls and roofs do not crack.
4. Protect bags and silo bags from rodents, birds and punctures with fencing and inspection; mend holes quickly with tape.

## Monitoring
Check O2 and CO2 in the headspace if sensors exist (`co2-monitoring-for-early-spoilage-detection`); check temperature and moisture at set intervals; when opening a sealed container, treat it as a confined space with low oxygen (`grain-bin-engulfment-prevention-and-entry-procedure`).
