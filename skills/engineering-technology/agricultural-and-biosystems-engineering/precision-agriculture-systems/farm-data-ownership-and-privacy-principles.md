# Data ownership and privacy principles

**This is practice guidance, not legal advice.** Contracts and laws differ by country; use a lawyer for a binding agreement.

**Goal:** know what you are agreeing to when a farm data platform, equipment maker, advisor or buyer wants your data, and negotiate sensible terms.

## Frameworks to compare against
- The American Farm Bureau Federation's Privacy and Security Principles for Farm Data (2014; date (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked)) cover ownership, collection and use notice, transparency, consent, portability, data disclosure, and security, and underpin a certification scheme (Ag Data Transparent).
- The EU Code of conduct on agricultural data sharing by contractual agreement (2018; date (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked)) sets contractual principles for data in the farm sector; the General Data Protection Regulation covers personal data in the EU.
- National rules on privacy, competition and consumer law may also apply; confirm them.

## What to check in an agreement
1. Ownership and control: does the farmer keep the rights to raw data and to data derived from it? Does the company claim a licence beyond what is needed to deliver the service?
2. Purpose limits: use only to provide the service, or also for research, product development, sale of aggregated and anonymised data? Are those sales opt-in?
3. Sharing with third parties: who and under what terms; sale of the company (what happens to the data); access by governments and law-enforcement.
4. Portability and exit: can you export the data in open formats (`shapefile-isoxml-and-geotiff-handling`) and delete it on request, and how quickly?
5. Security: encryption, access control, breach notification.
6. Liability, changes to terms and notice.
7. Landlord and contractor rights: who may see data from rented land or from custom work.

## Practice
Keep a register of platforms and data types; store a local master copy of key data (boundaries, yield, applications); use separate accounts for contractors; read renewal and update notices.
