# Filtration and pressing equipment

**Goal:** a filter or press that separates the solids at the required rate and dryness.

## Cake filtration (Darcy)
dt/dV = (mu x alpha x c / (A^2 x dP)) x V + mu x Rm / (A x dP), where V is filtrate volume, A the filter area, dP the pressure drop, c the cake solids per unit filtrate volume, alpha the specific cake resistance and Rm the medium resistance. At constant pressure, plot t/V against V: the slope gives alpha (cake resistance) and the intercept the medium resistance. Run a small laboratory leaf or Buchner test on the real slurry and scale on area, accounting for cake compressibility (alpha rises with pressure for compressible cakes).

## Equipment
- Plate and frame or recessed-plate presses: batch, high solids retention, wine and juice clarification, edible oil bleaching; with filter cloths or paper sheets.
- Rotary vacuum filters: continuous, sugar and starch; precoat filters with filter aids.
- Belt presses and screw presses: dewatering and juice or oil extraction (fruit, oilseed, pomace); capacity is fixed by screw geometry and speed; moisture of cake is set by pressure and residence time.
- Filter aids (diatomaceous earth, perlite, cellulose) keep cakes permeable; use food-grade, approved material and handle dust properly (diatomaceous earth can contain crystalline silica, so use respiratory protection and local exhaust).
- Cake washing recovers product; air blow dries the cake.

## Operation
Pressure rise during a cycle indicates cake build-up; terminate at the maximum pressure or minimum flow; clean, change cloths and sanitise per the plan. Pressurised presses are hazardous: use interlocked guards and release pressure before opening.
