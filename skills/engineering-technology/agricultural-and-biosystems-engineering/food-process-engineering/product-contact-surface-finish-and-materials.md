# Product-contact surface finish and materials

**Goal:** surfaces that can be cleaned to a hygienic standard and resist corrosion.

## Materials
- Austenitic stainless steels: 304 (1.4301) general use; 316L (1.4404) for chloride-containing or acidic products and aggressive cleaning; duplex grades for severe duty.
- Non-metals: food-grade elastomers (EPDM, silicone, FKM) and plastics approved for food contact under the relevant regulation (for example FDA 21 CFR Part 177 or EU framework regulation 1935/2004). Check compatibility with fat, CIP chemicals and temperature.
- Avoid copper, brass, galvanised and painted surfaces in contact with food or in the splash zone.

## Surface finish
- Product-contact surfaces commonly specified at a roughness Ra of 0.8 micrometres or smoother (the 3-A and EHEDG guidance for many applications (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked)); electropolishing improves cleanability and corrosion resistance for critical or sticky products.
- Mill finishes (2B) are acceptable for many uses; mechanically polished grits should be uniform and in the direction of flow.

## Welds and joints
1. Continuous, full-penetration welds, ground and polished smooth to the same finish; orbital TIG welding for tubing with inert gas back-purging to avoid heat tint and oxidation.
2. No crevices, threads or overlapping joints in the product zone; use sanitary clamps with correct gaskets, flush fits.
3. Internal corners with radius (EHEDG typically asks for radii of about 3 mm or more (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked)).

## Verify
- [ ] Ra measured with a profilometer on a sample of surfaces and welds.
- [ ] Passivation and inspection records for new stainless equipment.
