# Process validation and challenge studies

**Goal:** objective evidence that the process achieves the required pathogen reduction.

## Targets
Regulations and guidance set performance standards for some foods (for example the US juice HACCP rule requires a 5-log reduction of the pertinent pathogen, 21 CFR 120.24; low-acid canned foods use process authority-established lethality). Others require the company to define a performance target from hazard analysis.

## Validation routes
1. **Scientific literature and regulatory guidance**: published thermal or non-thermal process data for the same food matrix, pH and aw.
2. **Mathematical modelling** using D and z values and verified heat transfer (see D/z skill).
3. **Challenge study**: inoculate product with the target pathogen cocktail (in an accredited lab with biosafety controls) and process under worst-case conditions, count survivors.
4. **Surrogate organisms** (non-pathogenic strains with equal or greater resistance) allow in-plant trials; the surrogate must be validated against the target for that process.
5. **In-plant temperature mapping** to prove the cold spot reaches the required conditions.

## Documentation
Protocol, worst-case rationale, results with statistics, conclusions and revalidation triggers (formulation, equipment, line speed change) retained as part of the food-safety plan.

## Gotchas
- Validation is distinct from verification (routine checks).
- Use worst-case variables (highest solids, largest piece, lowest temperature setpoint).

## Verify
- [ ] Documented log reduction meets the target with margin and tracks the monitored critical limits.
