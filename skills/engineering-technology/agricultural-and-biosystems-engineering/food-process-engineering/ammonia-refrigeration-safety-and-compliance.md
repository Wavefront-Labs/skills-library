# Ammonia refrigeration safety and compliance

This is professional practice guidance, not a substitute for the standards, regulators or a competent engineer. Ammonia is toxic and flammable at higher concentrations; design, operation and modification of ammonia systems must be done by qualified people.

## Governing frameworks (check the current edition and your jurisdiction)
- **ASHRAE 15** (safety standard for refrigeration systems) and **ASHRAE 34** (refrigerant classification).
- **IIAR standards** (International Institute of Ammonia Refrigeration): the IIAR 2 series for design, plus ones for installation, testing and maintenance, and emergency systems.
- **US OSHA Process Safety Management (29 CFR 1910.119)** and **EPA Risk Management Program (40 CFR Part 68)** apply when the ammonia inventory meets their thresholds (the threshold quantity for anhydrous ammonia is 10,000 lb in both, per 29 CFR 1910.119 Appendix A and 40 CFR 68.130; aqueous ammonia at 20 percent or greater is 20,000 lb under Part 68, and PSM lists solutions above 44 percent separately). Other countries have equivalent pressure-equipment and major-hazard rules.

## Engineering controls
1. **Machinery room**: ventilation (normal and emergency), ammonia detectors with alarms at defined levels, emergency stop outside, eyewash and shower, no open flames.
2. **Pressure protection**: relief valves piped to a safe discharge, replacement on schedule, high-pressure cutouts.
3. **Minimise charge**: low-charge packaged systems or secondary-loop systems (glycol or CO2) reduce risk.
4. **Piping**: materials suitable for ammonia (carbon steel, stainless; no copper or brass), labelled, supports for thermal expansion.
5. **Hazard analysis**: PHA, mechanical integrity inspection, management of change and documented operating procedures where PSM applies.
6. **Emergency planning**: coordination with the fire service and training in response.

## Verify
- [ ] Detector calibration and relief valve replacement records current.
- [ ] Operators are trained and competent, with procedures reviewed on a schedule.
