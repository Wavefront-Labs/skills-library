# Mobile irrigation lab audit procedure

**Goal:** a field-based evaluation that gives the grower a prioritised list of savings and fixes.

## Steps
1. Interview: crop, soil, schedule, water source, energy bills, last maintenance, problems.
2. Inspect: layout, nozzle package (`center-pivot-design-and-nozzle-package`), pressure regulators, leaks, filters, damaged emitters.
3. Measure flow (flow meter, or an ultrasonic meter if there is none) and operating pressure at several points; compare with design.
4. Pumping plant test: measure flow, discharge and suction pressures or pumping water level, and power input (kWh meter, or fuel flow) to give overall pumping plant efficiency (`pumping-plant-efficiency-and-energy-audit`).
5. Uniformity: catch-can test for sprinklers (`catch-can-uniformity-test`) or emitter flow sampling for drip; surface irrigation advance-recession (`advance-recession-field-evaluation`).
6. Soil moisture check: probe or sensors to compare with the schedule.
7. Report: findings, estimated water and energy savings, priority fixes with approximate costs; leave the data with the grower.

## Notes
Programs and agencies that provide these audits vary by region; where one exists it often follows a published protocol. Use calibrated instruments and record the calibrations.
