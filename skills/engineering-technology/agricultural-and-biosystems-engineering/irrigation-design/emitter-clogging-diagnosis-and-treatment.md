# Emitter clogging diagnosis and treatment

**Goal:** find the cause (physical, chemical or biological) and apply the right control.

## Diagnose
1. **Field**: measure flow from a sample of emitters, calculate EU; map low-flow zones (head end, tail end, particular sections).
2. **Inspect** clogged emitters and filter screens under a microscope or by acid and bleach tests: carbonate dissolves in acid; organic slime dissolves in chlorine.
3. **Water analysis** with hazard ratings (Nakayama and Bucks guidance (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked)) for: pH (below 7 minor, above 8 severe), dissolved solids (below 500 mg/L minor, above 2000 severe), manganese (below 0.1 mg/L minor, above 1.5 severe), iron (below 0.2 minor, above 1.5 severe), hydrogen sulphide (below 0.2 minor, above 2 severe), and bacterial population (below 10,000 per mL minor, above 50,000 severe). Use as screening, not hard limits.

## Treat
- **Physical**: improve filtration, flush laterals regularly (flow velocity of about 0.5 m/s or more in laterals (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked), per maker).
- **Carbonate**: acidify to bring pH down (typically to around 5.5-6.5 for continuous injection or to pH 2 for a shock (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked)) per the maker's and extension guidelines; follow acid safety rules.
- **Iron and manganese**: aeration and settling before filters, or chlorination to oxidise and then filter.
- **Biological**: chlorination with a residual at the system end (often 1-2 mg/L free chlorine continuous, higher for shock (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked)); check maker limits on tubing and emitters.
- Injected acid and chlorine are hazardous; involve a qualified irrigation or water-treatment professional and follow labels and local regulation.
- Always verify chemical compatibility and follow labels, discharge rules and operator safety.

## Gotchas
- Chlorine is ineffective at high pH; acidify first.
- Mix injection locations to avoid precipitation.

## Verify
- [ ] Emitter flow recovers to within about 10 percent of new after treatment (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked).
