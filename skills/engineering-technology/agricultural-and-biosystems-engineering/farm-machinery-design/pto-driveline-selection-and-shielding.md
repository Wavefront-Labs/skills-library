# PTO driveline selection and shielding

**Goal:** a driveline that transmits the power at working angles, is the right length and is fully guarded.

## Selection
- Match tractor PTO speed (540 or 1000 rpm; check the stub and splines) and power to the driveline rating, with a service factor from the maker.
- Use constant-velocity (wide-angle) joints where turning angles are large.
- Match overload protection to the implement (`slip-clutch-and-shear-bolt-protection`).

## Fit
- Length: at the shortest position the halves must not bottom; at the longest they must keep the maker's minimum overlap. Measure on the tractor with the implement in extreme positions and cut as the maker instructs.
- Joint angles: keep small and equal at both ends; large angles raise vibration and wear.
- Align the yokes so the shaft runs in phase.

## Guarding
Cover shaft, joints, tractor master shield and implement input guard. Guards must turn freely on bearings with restraining chains attached. Never run a PTO without its master shield. ISO driveline standards and national machinery rules apply; confirm for your market.

## Safety
Stop the engine, remove the key and wait for the PTO to stop. Loose clothing and hair cause fatal entanglement.

## Verify
- [ ] Overlap and clearance measured at extremes.
- [ ] Guards rotate freely, chains attached.
- Unconfirmed: overlap rule per brand; use the manual.
