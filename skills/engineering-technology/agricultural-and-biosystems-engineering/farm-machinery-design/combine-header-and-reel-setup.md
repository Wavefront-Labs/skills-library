# Combine header and reel setup

**Goal:** gather and feed the crop to the feeder house with minimal loss.

## Header types
- **Auger platform**: general use for cereals; flex versions follow ground contour (important for soybeans).
- **Draper header**: wider, smoother crop flow, lower loss in many crops; belt tension and wear are service items.
- **Maize header**: snapping rolls and deck plates strip ears; plate spacing controls butt shatter and stalk residue.
- **Pickup header**: for windrowed crops.

## Setting the reel
- Reel speed: typically 10-25 percent faster than ground speed (reel index of about 1.1 to 1.25); too high shatters grain, too low causes crop to pile on the cutterbar.
- Reel position: tines at or just ahead of the knife for standing crops; set further forward and lower for lodged crops.
- Tine pitch: more aggressive for lodged crop.

## Cutterbar
Keep knife sections sharp and guards aligned; check knife speed and clearance; set cutting height to minimise soil intake and pods lost in soybeans.

## Gotchas
- Header height control and sensors for contour following need calibration.
- Trailer-transported headers must be stowed correctly; observe road regulations.
- Crop dryness and variety shatter risk affect speed.

## Verify
- [ ] Header loss counts at the cutterbar and ground behind the machine under limits.
- [ ] Even flow into feeder house without bunching.
