# Vacuum seed metering setup

**Goal:** one seed per hole at working speed.

## Principle
A vacuum draws seed against holes in a rotating disc; a singulator removes extra seeds, a release point drops the seed into the tube or conveyor. Alternatives: finger-pickup and plate metering for different seed shapes.

## Setup
1. Choose the disc (hole size and count) for the crop and seed size grade; discs are marked by the manufacturer for seed type and size. Seed size grades differ per lot.
2. Set vacuum by manufacturer guidance for the seed type and size; start from the manual's range and adjust on a test stand or with the monitor. Too high: multiples and seed damage; too low: skips.
3. Adjust singulator (tooth or finger type) so doubles are removed without causing skips.
4. Check seed-tube or conveyor and sensor cleanliness; lubricate seeds with talc or graphite only if the manufacturer permits it; some seed treatments need dust-control additives.
5. Verify speed: metering performance degrades as disc peripheral speed rises; test at planned working speed.

## Gotchas
- Seed lots differ; recheck when changing hybrids or treatments.
- Worn discs, seals and housing leaks reduce vacuum at the disc.
- Check the vacuum at the meter, not only at the fan or gauge on the tractor.

## Verify
- [ ] Test-stand or field count shows acceptable singulation (see seed-spacing skill).
- [ ] Vacuum steady across rows, hoses free of leaks.
