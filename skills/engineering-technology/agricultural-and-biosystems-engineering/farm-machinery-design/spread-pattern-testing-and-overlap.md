# Spread pattern testing and overlap

**Goal:** a track spacing and settings that give an acceptable coefficient of variation (CV) of applied product in the field.

## Principle
A spinner-disc spreader makes a triangular or humped pattern; adjacent passes overlap so the sum levels out. Pattern depends on product (density, size, flow), spinner speed, vane angle, drop point and ground speed.

## Test
1. Lay collection trays across the full spread width at regular spacing, perpendicular to travel. Published fertilizer-spreader test protocols (ASABE and EN standards; check the current edition) fix tray size, spacing and run length; use the one your regulator or customer requires.
2. Run through at working speed; weigh each tray.
3. Compute the single-pass pattern, overlay shifted copies at candidate track spacings and compute the CV of the sum.
4. Choose the spacing with the lowest CV in the practical range. Many agronomic references accept about 15 percent or less for fertilizer; confirm for your crop and jurisdiction.

## Adjust
- Skewed pattern: worn vanes, uneven feed point.
- Heavy centre or edge: move the drop point on the disc.
- Wind and segregation of blended products change the real pattern; test one product at a time.

## Verify
- [ ] CV computed for each candidate spacing; chosen spacing and settings recorded.
- [ ] Repeat when product or lot changes.
