# Spreader calibration and application rate

**Goal:** applied rate within tolerance of the target (kg/ha or lb/ac).

## Arithmetic
Rate = product mass delivered / area covered. Area per unit time = ground speed x effective swath width (not spread width). Mass per unit time comes from flow measured at the gate or conveyor.

## Procedures
1. Stationary test: collect product for a known time at the gate setting with the conveyor running at calibration speed; weigh; combine with measured speed and swath.
2. Area test: weigh a full hopper, spread a known area, weigh the remainder.
3. Adjust gate or controller and repeat until inside tolerance (often within 5 percent for fertilizer, unless a regulation says otherwise).

## Notes
- Product density varies by lot and moisture; recalibrate when the product changes.
- Check the speed sensor against a measured 100 m.
- Controller systems need the product density and calibration factor entered; verify by weighing.
- Nutrient-limit and record-keeping rules apply in many places; confirm locally.

## Verify
- [ ] Calibration weights recorded with date, lot and settings.
- [ ] Field area-method check matches controller within tolerance.
