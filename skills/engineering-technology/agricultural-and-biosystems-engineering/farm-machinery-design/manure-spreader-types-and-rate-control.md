# Manure spreader types and rate control

**Goal:** apply manure at a known rate and uniformity, based on measured nutrient content and field need.

## Types
- Solid: box spreaders with apron and horizontal or vertical beaters, or slinger types. Rate is set by apron speed, gate opening and ground speed.
- Liquid: tankers with splash plates, bands (trailing hose or shoe) or injectors. Rate is set by pump flow, valve and speed, or by a flow-meter controller.
- Odour, ammonia loss and runoff are lower with banding and injection than broadcast; these are often regulated or incentivised.

## Calibration
- Solid: weigh a load, spread a known area, compute t/ha; calibrate per manure type.
- Liquid: from tank volume and a measured area (m3/ha), or from a calibrated flow meter checked by tank level drop.

## Nutrients
Sample representative composites from storage and have a laboratory analyse total N, ammonium N, P and K; book values are a fallback only. Rate must follow the nutrient management plan and local rules on nitrogen, phosphorus, seasons, setbacks and weather.

## Verify
- [ ] Rate measured at least once per season per manure type.
- [ ] Lab results recorded with date and source.
- Unconfirmed: local spreading-season limits; confirm with the regulator.
