# Liquid injection and toolbar design

**Goal:** uniform rate and depth with low odour and loss, within tractor draft and toolbar strength.

## Design points
- Openers: sweeps and knives mix soil but draw high draft; coulters and discs suit residue and grassland with lower draft but shallower placement.
- Spacing follows opener spread and crop; wider spacing means more flow per outlet.
- Distribution: a macerator-distributor with equal-length hoses and similar bends keeps outlets even.
- Fibre and straw plug small orifices; check the maker's dry-matter limit.

## Draft and structure
Use `implement-draft-prediction-asabe` for a first draft estimate. Toolbars take impact loads on rocks and in transport; design with a safety factor and use shear or hydraulic break-back.

## Operation
- Catch-test each outlet at working pressure before the season.
- Match ground speed to flow to hold m3/ha; avoid over-application at headlands.
- Record rate, date and field for regulators.

## Verify
- [ ] Per-outlet flow within a tolerance agreed with the manufacturer.
- [ ] Depth confirmed by digging trenches.
- Unconfirmed: maximum dry matter and depth per machine; follow the manual.
