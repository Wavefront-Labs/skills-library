# Combine separation and cleaning shoe adjustment

**Goal:** separate grain from straw and chaff, deliver clean grain, keep return flow low.

## Separation
Straw walkers (conventional) or rotor/axial mechanisms separate remaining grain from straw. Overload shows as grain in the straw at the rear; reduce ground speed or feed rate, adjust rotor vanes or walker speed per the manual.

## Cleaning shoe
1. Grain from the grain pan falls to the chaffer (upper sieve), then the lower sieve; air from the fan lifts chaff.
2. **Fan speed**: increase if the sample is dirty; reduce if grain is blown out of the back of the shoe (bright grain in the chaff).
3. **Chaffer opening**: open if tailings return is high but the sample is clean; close if the sample is dirty.
4. **Lower sieve**: set smaller than the grain size with the manual's crop setting.
5. Observe the pattern across the width: slope operation requires hillside or levelling shoe equipment.

## Troubleshooting
- Dirty grain tank sample: increase fan or close sieves.
- High tailings volume: usually under-threshing or overloaded shoe.
- Losses over the shoe: reduce ground speed, open sieves, adjust fan.

## Gotchas
- Setting depends on crop moisture, trash, and slope; check at each crop change.
- Lodged crops overload the machine at normal speed.

## Verify
- [ ] Sample sieved and weighed shows clean-grain percentage meeting the buyer's standard.
- [ ] Catch pan checks over the shoe and straw show acceptable losses.
