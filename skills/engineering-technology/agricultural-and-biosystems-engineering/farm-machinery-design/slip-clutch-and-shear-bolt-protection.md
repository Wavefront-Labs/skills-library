# Slip clutches and shear-bolt protection

**Goal:** protect gearboxes and shafts from overload without nuisance trips.

## Devices
- Shear bolt: breaks at a defined load. Use only the specified grade and size; a stronger bolt defeats the protection.
- Friction (slip) clutch: spring-loaded discs; trip torque set by spring length. After storage the discs rust and stick, so loosen, run to free them, then reset.
- Ratchet or cam clutch: disengages on overload and re-engages as load falls; common on heavy balers and mowers.
- A mid-driveline torque limiter can protect against shock.

## Setting
- Set trip torque at about the rated driveline torque with the margin the implement maker specifies. Too low means constant slip; too high breaks gearboxes.
- Do not run a friction clutch slipping for long; it overheats and burns the discs.

## Safety
Parts can coast after an overload; stop the PTO and engine before clearing.

## Verify
- [ ] Clutches freed, run-in and reset after storage per the manual.
- [ ] Spare shear bolts of the right grade carried.
- Unconfirmed: trip torque for your model; use the manual.
