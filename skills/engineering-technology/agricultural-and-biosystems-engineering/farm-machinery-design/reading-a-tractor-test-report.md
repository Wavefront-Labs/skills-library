# Reading a tractor test report

**Goal:** fair comparisons of power, fuel and hydraulic performance from an official test.

## Sources
The University of Nebraska Tractor Test Laboratory reports on tractors sold in the US; OECD standard codes govern tests in other countries, and national labs publish their own. Test conditions such as ambient temperature and fuel are stated.

## Contents
- PTO power at rated speed and at varying loads; maximum and rated power; torque and torque rise.
- Fuel consumption at set loads, from which you compute specific fuel consumption.
- Drawbar performance (pull, slip, speed, fuel), often on a concrete track, sometimes on soil.
- Hydraulic power, flow and lift capacity.
- Test notes: ballast, tyres, repairs and adjustments.

## Using it
- Compare PTO power at rated speed, specific fuel use at part load, and the drawbar data for your work.
- Concrete drawbar results overstate pull on soil; pair with `drawbar-power-and-tractive-efficiency` and `wheel-slip-measurement-and-traction-curve`.
- Check the tested configuration (tyres, ballast) matches what you will buy.
