# CoCrMo alloys

**Goal:** choose and specify CoCrMo for bearing and structural implants with the right condition and verification.

## Forms
- **Cast CoCrMo (ASTM F75):** used for femoral heads and components, knee femoral components, and dental frameworks; as-cast microstructure has coarse carbides and segregation; solution annealing, hot isostatic pressing and investment-casting controls improve it.
- **Wrought CoCrMo (ASTM F1537):** forged or rolled alloy with finer grains and higher strength; alloy conditions (annealed, warm worked) are in the standard; used for stems and stronger components.
- **Carbon content:** high-carbon alloys have hard carbides giving wear resistance; low-carbon alloys are more ductile. For metal-on-metal bearings, carbon content and carbide distribution influence wear (see metal-on-metal-lessons).

## Properties
- High strength and hardness, excellent wear and corrosion resistance in the passive state; modulus about 210 to 240 GPa (high, which increases stress shielding in stems).
- Fretting corrosion at modular tapers and mixed-metal junctions is a known problem (see taper-corrosion).
- Contains cobalt, chromium and some nickel traces; ion release and sensitisation are considerations (see nickel-release).

## Process notes
- Surface finish and polish for bearing surfaces; passivation; control of porosity in castings by non-destructive testing.

## Verify
- [ ] Heat and lot certificates against the standard.
- [ ] Microstructure and hardness verified for the finished part.
- [ ] Corrosion, wear and fatigue tests relevant to the application.
