# Drug-eluting coatings

**Goal:** deliver a controlled dose of drug locally for a defined time without coating failure or harmful residues.

## Elements
- **Drug:** antiproliferative agents such as sirolimus analogues or paclitaxel for vascular devices; dose per device and per surface area sets exposure.
- **Carrier:** durable polymer, bioresorbable polymer or polymer-free surface (reservoirs, micro-porous metal); release is controlled by diffusion, polymer degradation or both.
- **Drug-coated balloons:** a coating (often with an excipient) transfers drug to the vessel wall in a short inflation time.

## Characterisation
1. **Drug content** per device and uniformity across devices and along the device (HPLC), with impurities and degradation products.
2. **In vitro elution:** release profile in physiological medium at 37 C in a flow-through or other apparatus; discriminating test with correlation to in vivo pharmacokinetics.
3. **Coating integrity:** SEM and particulate tests after crimping, deployment, bending and over-expansion; tracking and handling effects on coat loss for balloons.
4. **Residual solvents and sterilisation effects** (EO and radiation can degrade the drug).
5. **Pharmacokinetics and tissue response** in animals; local and systemic safety (toxicology of the drug and polymer).
6. **Clinical outcomes:** a late-mortality signal for paclitaxel-coated peripheral devices was raised in 2019 FDA letters, and FDA later updated its position that current data no longer support a probable late mortality risk (fda.gov letter to health care providers); follow the current regulator statements.

## Regulatory
- Drug-device combination products involve both device and drug quality requirements, with a lead centre in the US and notified body plus medicines authority consultation in the EU; plan early with regulatory affairs and confirm requirements.

## Verify
- [ ] Content uniformity and elution profile within specification with validated analytical methods.
- [ ] Coating integrity and particulates after simulated use.
- [ ] Stability data over shelf life for the drug and coating.

> Practice guidance, not regulatory or clinical advice. Confirm current standard editions and rules for your jurisdiction, and defer to qualified regulatory, clinical and engineering judgement.
