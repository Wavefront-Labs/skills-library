# Hydrophilic lubricious coatings

**Goal:** a low-friction, durable coating that stays on the device and does not shed harmful particles.

## Chemistries
- Polyvinylpyrrolidone (PVP), polyacrylic acid, polyethylene oxide and hyaluronan based hydrogels, applied as a thin layer that becomes very slippery when wet.
- **Attachment:** physical entanglement in a base coat, or covalent grafting using UV or thermal cure; a tie layer improves adhesion to substrates such as nylon, polyurethane, Pebax or PTFE.

## Process
- Dip coating with controlled withdrawal speed and concentration, cure conditions, and control of thickness and coverage (including distal tips); process parameters validated as a special process.
- Cleaning and activation of the substrate before coating.

## Testing
1. **Friction:** coefficient of friction in a wet, tortuous or clamp-pull fixture before and after repeated cycles to show durability.
2. **Dry-out and hydration time** (activation) as users will handle the device.
3. **Particulate generation:** quantify particles released in simulated use in a tortuous model by light obscuration or microscopy; compare against regulator expectations and the application; coating embolisation has led to field actions for some devices.
4. **Adhesion and integrity:** inspect coating after bending and over-the-wire passes.
5. **Biocompatibility** including extractables and haemocompatibility for blood contact (see hemocompatibility-testing).
6. **Sterilisation and shelf-life effects** (EO or radiation) on coating performance.

## Verify
- [ ] Friction and durability specs met after sterilisation and ageing.
- [ ] Particulate data in simulated use meet the risk-based limits.
- [ ] Coating coverage and thickness verified at critical regions.
