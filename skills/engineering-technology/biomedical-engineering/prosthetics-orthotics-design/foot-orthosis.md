# Foot orthoses

**Goal:** control foot position and redistribute plantar pressure in a shoe. Prescription and fitting belong to clinicians (podiatrists, orthotists).

## Types
- **Accommodative:** soft materials to cushion and off-load (diabetic foot, ulcer prevention); total contact with relief cutouts.
- **Functional (semi-rigid) orthoses:** shells in polypropylene, carbon or composite to control motion; arch support and heel cup.
- **UCBL and deeper heel-cup designs** for hindfoot control.
- **Posting and wedges:** rearfoot or forefoot posts (varus or valgus) to change loading.

## Design steps
1. Capture the foot shape (plaster, foam impression or 3D scan) in the prescribed position (neutral or weight-bearing).
2. Modify the positive model or the digital shape: arch fill, heel cup depth, relief areas.
3. Fabricate: vacuum-form, CNC mill or 3D print; add top cover and posts.
4. Fit in the shoe; check volume (enough depth), heel slip and comfort.
5. Offload pressure assessments with in-shoe sensors for high-risk feet.

## Verify
- [ ] Orthosis fits the footwear and does not cause rubbing.
- [ ] Plantar pressure or clinical measures meet the goal.
- [ ] Review after break-in and at agreed intervals.
