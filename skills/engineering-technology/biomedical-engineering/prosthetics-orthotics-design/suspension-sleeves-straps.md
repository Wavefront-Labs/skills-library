# Sleeves and straps for suspension

**Goal:** hold the prosthesis on the limb with the least pistoning and the least skin or circulation compromise.

## Options
- **Suspension sleeve (neoprene, silicone, fabric):** rolls over the socket brim and limb; adds sealing and suspension, can also back up a primary method. Heat, hygiene and skin irritation are issues.
- **Supracondylar or suprapatellar cuff:** strap around the femoral condyles above the knee, typical for patellar tendon bearing (PTB) and some transtibial designs.
- **Thigh corset and side joints:** older design, adds load transfer; heavy.
- **Silesian belt:** a belt around the pelvis attached to a transfemoral prosthesis to limit rotation and add suspension.
- **Pelvic band and hip joint:** more control, bulk.

## Design factors
- Sealing sleeve with a one-way valve provides partial vacuum benefits.
- Strap width spreads pressure; avoid narrow straps or over-tight sleeves that restrict circulation.
- Cleaning and replacement schedule; allergy check (latex, neoprene).

## Verify
- [ ] Pistoning measured or observed in gait is acceptable.
- [ ] No skin breakdown or circulation signs after wear.
- [ ] User can don and doff the system independently.
