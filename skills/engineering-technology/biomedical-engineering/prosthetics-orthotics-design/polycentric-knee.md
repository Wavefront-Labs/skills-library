# Polycentric prosthetic knees

**Goal:** choose and set a four-bar-linkage knee so that stance stability, swing clearance and cosmesis fit the user.

Prescription and alignment are the prosthetist's responsibility; this explains the mechanics.

## Mechanism (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked)
A polycentric knee has four or more pivots (usually a four-bar linkage). The **instantaneous centre of rotation (ICR)** moves as the knee flexes, tracing a path; it is the intersection of the lines through the linkage's bars.
- **Stance stability:** at full extension the ICR is posterior to and above the weight line in many designs, so the knee is stable under load (a ground reaction line passing anterior to the ICR extends the knee); this allows stability with more normal hip extensor demand than a single-axis knee.
- **Swing shortening:** as the knee flexes, the shank moves up and back, shortening the leg to clear the toe.
- **Cosmesis:** with long residual limbs (knee disarticulation) a compact four-bar linkage keeps the thigh length near the sound side because it sits below the socket, and the knee centre is distal.

## Selection
- Knee disarticulation, bilateral amputation and long femoral residual limbs.
- Users needing a stable knee at heel strike and quick stability, including those with weak hip extensors.
- Often paired with hydraulic or pneumatic swing control and with a locking or stance-control option.

## Alignment effects
- Moving the knee centre posterior relative to the weight line increases stability and makes initiation of knee flexion harder (a heavier push-off is needed); moving it anterior makes the knee more flexible but less stable.
- Set the extension stop and the swing resistance after the static alignment.

## Limits
Polycentric knees add mass and parts to wear; the ICR path varies with design; stairs and kneeling are possible with some designs (the flexion range is large).

## Verify
- [ ] Stability test: user can bear weight with the knee extended and lightly flexed.
- [ ] Toe clearance in swing and ability to sit.
- [ ] Check wear and maintenance intervals in manufacturer instructions.
