# ESR foot selection

**Goal:** match foot stiffness to user weight and activity so the keel deflects in stance and returns energy at push-off without causing instability.

Selection is a clinical prosthetist decision using the manufacturer's current weight and activity tables; this explains engineering principles.

## Principles
- ESR feet use a flexible carbon-fibre (or composite) keel. It deflects in late stance and returns part of the stored energy; real returned energy is modest compared with the biological ankle, but gait smoothness and roll-over improve.
- **Stiffness category:** a number or letter chosen from a manufacturer chart by user body weight (and by activity). Stiffness effects below are clinical teaching (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked). Too soft: keel collapses early, and the user feels dropped at toe-off. Too stiff: the user cannot load the toe, so the sound-side step shortens and the roll-over feels like walking uphill.
- **Heel stiffness:** a stiffer heel raises the knee flexion moment at heel strike (can make a transfemoral knee feel unstable), and a softer heel slows foot-flat and creates a hyperextension tendency.
- **Activity classification:** US payers often use functional levels (K-levels); other systems differ (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked). ESR feet are typically for levels with variable cadence and above, but coverage and criteria vary by payer and country.
- **Other features:** heel height adjustment, multi-axis motion, water resistance, cosmetic cover.

## Procedure
1. Gather weight with typical load (carrying), activity, residual limb strength and prior foot experience.
2. Pick a stiffness category from the chart; confirm weight limit.
3. Fit and align (see bench-alignment-setup), then walk; review heel loading and toe roll-over.
4. Adjust category up or down if the user feels instability or collapse.
5. Check the heel height setting matches the shoe.

## Verify
- [ ] Weight and activity within the manufacturer's range.
- [ ] Smooth roll-over; no early drop-off or late toe-off.
- [ ] Shoe heel height matched.
