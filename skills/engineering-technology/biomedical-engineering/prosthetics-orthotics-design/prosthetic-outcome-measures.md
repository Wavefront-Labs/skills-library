# Prosthetic outcome measures

**Goal:** choose measures that are valid for the population and tie component choice and progress to evidence.

## Common measures
- **Functional level (K-levels, K0 to K4):** a US Medicare coverage classification of potential ambulation: Level 0 no ability or potential to ambulate or transfer safely, through Level 4 prosthetic ambulation exceeding basic skills with high impact, stress or energy levels (CMS LCD L33787 Lower Limb Prostheses); confirm current payer policy.
- **Amputee Mobility Predictor (AMPnoPRO and AMPPRO):** (Gailey et al., Arch Phys Med Rehabil 2002;83:613-627) performance test of mobility without and with prosthesis; scores relate to K-level estimates.
- **PLUS-M (Prosthetic Limb Users Survey of Mobility):** self-report, computer adaptive or short form.
- **Performance tests:** timed up and go, 2-minute or 6-minute walk test, 10-metre walk, L-test, four square step test, single-limb balance.
- **Self-report:** satisfaction and pain (for example Trinity Amputation and Prosthesis Experience Scales, Gallagher and MacLachlan, Rehabil Psychol 2000;45:130-154), fall history and activity monitors.

## Practice
1. Use the same measure and conditions at each visit; record device and assistive aids.
2. Report change against the minimal detectable change for the instrument and population.
3. Combine performance and self-report; neither alone describes function.
4. Component justification: link documented function and goals, not only a classification.

## Verify
- [ ] Measure suits the K-level and setting; administered as in the manual.
- [ ] Change interpreted using published minimal detectable or important change.
- [ ] Data stored with date, device configuration and assistive aids.
