# Terminal devices

**Goal:** match the device at the end of an upper-limb prosthesis to the user's tasks, control method and tolerance for weight and cost.

## Types
- **Hooks (split hook):** durable, light, good visibility of grasped objects; voluntary opening (VO) hooks are held closed by springs or bands (grip force from the elastic) and voluntary closing (VC) allow adjustable grip force.
- **Hands:** passive cosmetic, body-powered and myoelectric hands; lateral (key) grip and tripod patterns; multi-articulating hands offer additional grips.
- **Specialised devices:** work tools and sports adapters.
- **Wrist units:** quick-disconnect for interchange, rotation, flexion; passive or powered.

## Selection factors
- Tasks and environment (wet, dirty, impact), cosmesis priority, weight at the end of the limb, control method (cable excursion or myoelectric sites; see myoelectric-site-selection and pattern-recognition-control), battery and maintenance, cost.
- Grip force and speed trade off; heavy hands strain suspension.

## Verify
- [ ] Grasp tasks that matter to the user demonstrated in trial.
- [ ] Weight and balance acceptable with socket and suspension.
- [ ] Training and maintenance plan provided.
