# RULA and REBA assessment

**Goal:** screen postures for musculoskeletal risk and prioritise redesign, not diagnose individuals.

## When to use
- **RULA:** upper-limb loading in seated, repetitive tasks (benches, screen work). Grand score runs 1 to 7 (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked) with action levels from acceptable to investigate and change immediately.
- **REBA:** whole-body postures including handling loads and coupling, for varied tasks (healthcare handling, manual work). Final score 1 to 15 (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked) with risk levels from negligible to very high.

## Workflow
1. Choose the task cycle and the posture(s) that look worst or are held longest; use video or photographs and record the side (left, right).
2. Score body region groups using the published sheets: for REBA trunk, neck, legs, load, upper arm, lower arm, wrist, then coupling and activity; for RULA upper arm, lower arm, wrist, wrist twist, then neck, trunk, legs, with muscle use and force.
3. Combine via the published tables (use the official tables, not memory) to the final score and action level.
4. Redesign: adjust height, reach, tool, load or work method; re-score.

## Limits
- Snapshot of a posture, no exposure duration or cumulative load, low sensitivity to individual differences; inter-rater reliability needs training.
- Complement with force and duration methods (for example NIOSH lifting equation, see niosh-lifting-equation) and posture standards (ISO 11226, Ergonomics - Evaluation of static working postures; confirm edition).

## Verify
- [ ] Posture selection rationale and rater training recorded.
- [ ] Score tables from the original method used.
- [ ] Redesign re-scored and compared.
