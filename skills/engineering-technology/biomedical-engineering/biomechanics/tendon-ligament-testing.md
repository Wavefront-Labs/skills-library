# Tendon and ligament testing

**Goal:** get valid force-elongation (structural) and stress-strain (material) data for soft tissue with the usual testing pitfalls controlled.

## Method
1. **Specimen:** keep hydrated and at controlled temperature; handle frozen specimens consistently; record donor and site.
2. **Cross-section area:** measure under a defined preload with a laser micrometre, shadow, or casting method; calipers deform the tissue. Area is the dominant source of stress error.
3. **Gripping:** slip and stress concentration at grips ruin tests; use cryo-clamps, sandpaper-lined or serrated clamps, or leave bone ends for bone-ligament-bone (structural) tests. Failure at the grip is not a valid failure.
4. **Preload and preconditioning:** small preload to remove slack then several load cycles to reach a repeatable response.
5. **Strain:** measure on the tissue with optical markers or digital image correlation; grip-to-grip displacement overestimates strain.
6. **Loading:** quasi-static ramps (defined strain rate), stress relaxation, creep and cyclic; report rate because tendon is viscoelastic.
7. **Outputs:** the toe region (crimp uncrimping), linear modulus, yield or failure load and strain, energy to failure; report both structural and material properties.

## Verify
- [ ] Area method, preload and rate reported.
- [ ] Failure location (midsubstance, avulsion, grip) recorded.
- [ ] Strain measured locally.
