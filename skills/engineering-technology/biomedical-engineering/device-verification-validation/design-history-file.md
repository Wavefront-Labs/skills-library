# Design history file and technical file

**Goal:** a complete, indexed, retrievable record that proves the device was designed under controls and supports regulatory submissions.

## Contents
- Plans, user needs, design inputs, outputs (specifications, drawings, software), risk management file, verification and validation protocols and reports, design review minutes, design changes, design transfer records, usability file, clinical evaluation, labelling, and links to biocompatibility, sterilisation and packaging validation.
- **Index with cross references** (pointers to the location of records managed elsewhere) rather than a copy of everything.

## Relation to other files
- The **design and development file** (ISO 13485 term) is the internal record per product or family. FDA's QMSR, effective 2 February 2026 (FDA QMSR FAQ page), revised 21 CFR Part 820; 820.10(a) requires a quality management system complying with ISO 13485 (incorporated by reference), and 820.10 points to ISO 13485 clause 7.3 for design and development. FDA states the QMSR does not use the term design history file (reported from FDA QMSR material; the FAQ page itself does not repeat it) (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked).
- The **device master record** (production definition) becomes the medical device file under the harmonised US rule (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked) (confirm current text).
- **EU technical documentation** (MDR Annexes II and III) is a structured set describing the device, information supplied, design and manufacturing, general safety and performance requirements, benefit-risk and risk management, and verification and validation; compile from the design file in the required structure (see technical-documentation-structure).

## Practice
1. Maintain the index from project start; link records in the document system.
2. Ensure traceability: requirement to test to result.
3. Close open items before design transfer and submissions.
4. Keep legacy products' files under change control with updates after post-market changes.

## Verify
- [ ] Index lists all required records with revision status.
- [ ] Random trace samples from need to validation succeed.
- [ ] Records are retrievable for audit and for the retention period required.
