# Technical documentation structure

**Goal:** an organised file that regulators and notified bodies can review without searching.

## EU MDR (Annex II technical documentation, Annex III post-market surveillance documentation)
- Device description and specification, including variants and accessories, and reference to previous generations.
- Information to be supplied by the manufacturer (labels, instructions for use, languages).
- Design and manufacturing information (processes, sites, suppliers).
- General safety and performance requirements and the evidence applied (see gspr-checklist).
- Benefit-risk analysis and risk management.
- Product verification and validation: preclinical (bench, biocompatibility, software, electrical, sterilisation, packaging, usability), and clinical evaluation, with the post-market clinical follow-up plan.
- Post-market surveillance plan and reports under Annex III.

## Other structures
- **STED (summary technical documentation):** a harmonised structure from the former GHTF and IMDRF used by several jurisdictions.
- **FDA eSTAR** is the electronic template for 510(k) and De Novo submissions, with its own sections and attachments; confirm the current version and what is required for your pathway.
- Other markets (for example Japan, Canada, China, Australia, Brazil) have their own formats and locally required elements.

## Practice
1. Build one master technical file with a table of contents mapped to each market's structure, so documents are reused; ensure the content is consistent (device descriptions, indications).
2. Use document control, revisions and consistent naming.
3. Maintain a traceability matrix from general requirements to evidence.
4. Keep summaries concise and refer to detailed reports.
