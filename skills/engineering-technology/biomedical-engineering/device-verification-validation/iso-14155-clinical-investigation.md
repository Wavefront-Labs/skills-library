# ISO 14155 clinical investigations

Edition: ISO 14155:2026 (March 2026) replaced ISO 14155:2020 according to the iso.org catalogue; clause content of either edition (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked). 21 CFR Part 812 title and 812.2 abbreviated-requirements categories confirmed on eCFR. EU MDR chapter and annex numbers: confirm in the Regulation text.

**Goal:** conduct a clinical investigation that protects subjects and produces credible data. Regulatory and ethical decisions rest with qualified clinical, regulatory and ethics personnel.

## Key elements
- **Clinical investigation plan (CIP):** objectives, design, endpoints, sample size justification, inclusion and exclusion criteria, procedures, statistics plan, risk analysis and benefit-risk, and amendments control.
- **Investigator's brochure:** device description, preclinical data, risk information.
- **Ethics committee approval and informed consent:** documented before any subject enrolment.
- **Regulatory submission:** in the US an investigational device exemption (or abbreviated or exempt categories) under 21 CFR 812; in the EU a clinical investigation application under MDR; other markets differ.
- **Roles:** sponsor responsibilities, investigators, monitors, independent data monitoring committee where needed; delegation logs and training.
- **Monitoring:** risk-based monitoring plan, source data verification, deviations management.
- **Adverse events:** definitions (adverse event, serious adverse event, device deficiency, unanticipated adverse device effect) and reporting timelines to ethics committees and regulators.
- **Data management:** electronic data capture validation, database lock, statistical analysis per plan.
- **Close-out:** final report, archiving, publication and registration of the study.

## Verify
- [ ] CIP, brochure and consent approved before enrolment.
- [ ] Monitoring and safety reporting procedures in place.
- [ ] Final report consistent with the CIP and statistics plan.

> Practice guidance, not regulatory or clinical advice. Confirm current standard editions and rules for your jurisdiction, and defer to qualified regulatory, clinical and engineering judgement.
