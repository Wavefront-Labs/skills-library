# Production and post-production risk information

**Goal:** treat the risk file as a living document, updated by real-world data.

## Method
1. **Plan collection:** the risk management plan states what information is gathered and how: complaints, service records, non-conformances, CAPA, vigilance and field safety actions, literature, registries, similar devices, user feedback, social media where relevant, and new standards.
2. **Review:** assess whether the information shows a new hazard, a hazardous situation not previously identified, a changed probability of harm or severity, or that the state of the art has changed.
3. **Reassess risks:** update the risk analysis, evaluate the benefit-risk balance, and decide on risk control actions (design change, labelling, field action).
4. **Feed into** CAPA (see capa-process), design change control (see design-change-control), complaint handling (see complaint-handling) and, in the EU, post-market surveillance reports and periodic safety update reports under MDR (confirm requirements by class and market).
5. **Record:** the risk management report and file are updated with each review; the review of the file for continuing acceptability is part of management review.

## Practice
- Define triggers such as complaint rates by failure mode, with thresholds.
- Use consistent failure mode coding across complaints, service and the risk file to allow trend analysis.
- Report serious incidents to regulators within the timelines of each market (confirm; they are short).

## Verify
- [ ] Surveillance plan lists data sources and review frequency.
- [ ] Risk file revisions trace to specific post-market inputs.
- [ ] Overall residual risk and benefit-risk conclusions remain justified.

> Practice guidance, not regulatory or clinical advice. Confirm current standard editions and rules for your jurisdiction, and defer to qualified regulatory, clinical and engineering judgement.
