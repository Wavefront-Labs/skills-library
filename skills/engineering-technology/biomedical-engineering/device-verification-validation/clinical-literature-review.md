# Clinical literature review

**Goal:** collect and appraise published evidence on the device and the state of the art, reproducibly.

## Method
1. **Protocol before searching:** questions (safety, performance, clinical benefit, state of the art, equivalent devices), inclusion and exclusion criteria, databases, and appraisal method.
2. **Search strategy:** several databases (for example PubMed and Embase and trial registries), keywords including device names, generic terms and clinical conditions, date ranges; document exact strings and dates so it can be repeated; supplement with registries, field safety notices and regulator databases.
3. **Screening:** two-stage (title and abstract, full text), preferably two reviewers, with a record of exclusions and reasons (flow diagram per PRISMA).
4. **Appraisal:** assess study design, quality, relevance to your device and population, and weight data accordingly; a predefined scoring scheme for methodological quality and relevance is common in clinical evaluation reports.
5. **Data extraction:** outcomes, adverse events and follow-up in structured tables; analyse by device or generic group.
6. **Synthesis:** compare results with state-of-the-art benchmarks, derive acceptance criteria and identify gaps needing clinical data.
7. **Update** per the clinical evaluation plan (frequency by risk class and post-market data).

## Verify
- [ ] Protocol and search strings archived and reproducible.
- [ ] Inclusion flow and appraisal records complete.
- [ ] Conclusions state the strength of evidence and gaps.
