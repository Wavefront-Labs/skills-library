# Post-market clinical follow-up (PMCF) plan

**Goal:** proactively collect clinical data after market entry to confirm safety and performance, identify emerging risks and update the clinical evaluation.

EU MDR guidance changes; confirm current text and notified body expectations. Not all devices need PMCF studies, but the plan must justify any decision not to carry them out.

## Content of the plan (MDCG 2020-7 structure (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked))
1. **Specific objectives** linked to the clinical evaluation gaps: confirm safety and performance throughout the expected lifetime, identify side effects and contraindications, detect emerging risks, ensure continued acceptability of benefit-risk, check for systematic misuse or off-label use, and for equivalence-based claims, confirm the underlying data.
2. **Methods and procedures:**
   - General: user feedback, complaints, literature surveillance, registry data, surveys.
   - Specific: device registries (existing or sponsored), post-market clinical studies (prospective or retrospective), patient or clinician surveys, evaluation of retrieved devices.
3. **Endpoints** (clinical outcomes, device performance parameters, adverse event rates) with predefined acceptance criteria drawn from the state of the art.
4. **Study design and sample size** with statistical rationale where a study is planned; duration and follow-up times.
5. **Rationale for the choice** of methods and for the justification of not conducting a PMCF study if applicable.
6. **Reference to the CER and risk management file**, and to the post-market surveillance plan.
7. **Timeline, responsibilities and data management** (including data protection and ethics approval where studies are needed).

## Outputs
- The **PMCF evaluation report** (MDCG 2020-8): results, conclusions and impact on the clinical evaluation report, risk management and labelling. Updated regularly with the clinical evaluation, and for higher-class devices feeds the periodic safety update report (Article 86: PSUR updated at least annually for class IIb and III, and when necessary and at least every two years for class IIa; checked on EUR-Lex; MDCG 2022-21 is the PSUR guidance. MDCG 2020-13 is the notified body clinical evaluation assessment report template, not PSUR guidance).
- Corrective actions or label changes if results demand.

## Gotchas
- Plans that list generic activities without endpoints or acceptance criteria.
- Registry data without data access agreements.
- Not updating the CER with PMCF results.

## Verify
- [ ] Each clinical evaluation gap maps to a PMCF activity.
- [ ] Endpoints and acceptance criteria are prespecified.
- [ ] PMCF evaluation reports are issued on schedule and reflected in the CER.
