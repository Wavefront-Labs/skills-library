# Guidewire design

**Goal:** a wire that steers one-to-one, tracks tortuosity, supports device delivery and has an atraumatic, visible tip.

## Construction
- **Core:** stainless steel or nitinol; taper profile sets stiffness transition. A shaping ribbon or flattened tip allows tip shaping.
- **Coil or polymer jacket** over the distal core adds radiopacity (platinum or tungsten coil), lubricity and tip atraumaticity; jackets smooth the profile.
- **Coating:** PTFE (hydrophobic) or hydrophilic polymer (see lubricious-coatings); trade friction against tactile feedback.
- Common outer diameters are expressed in inches (0.014, 0.018, 0.035) matched to the delivery catheter lumen.

## Performance measures
- Tip load (force to buckle or deflect), stiffness profile along length, torque response (input versus output rotation), tensile strength and tip-to-core joint strength, kink resistance, coating integrity and particulates, radiopacity.

## Verify
- [ ] Tip and joint tensile and fatigue tests meet the requirement with reliability stated.
- [ ] Torque response tested in a tortuous fixture.
- [ ] Coating durability and particulates evaluated.
- [ ] Biocompatibility for the contact duration and tissue type.
