# Wearable skin-contact design

**Goal:** a device that stays attached and works for the wear period without irritating or injuring skin.

## Design factors
- **Contact category and duration** (ISO 10993 part scopes (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked)): intact skin contact; long wear raises the biological evaluation scope (cytotoxicity, sensitisation, irritation; see biocompatibility-evaluation-iso-10993).
- **Adhesives:** acrylic, silicone and hydrocolloid; trade adhesion against removal trauma (medical adhesive related skin injury); breathable backings reduce maceration.
- **Moisture and sweat:** moisture vapour transmission, venting and electrode gel behaviour.
- **Pressure and shear:** straps, edges and rigid corners cause pressure injury; radius edges and spread loads.
- **Temperature:** applied-part surface temperature limits for prolonged contact are specified in IEC 60601-1 (commonly cited as about 43 C for long contact (gated: unconfirmed, source is paywalled or blocked); confirm the table and edition).
- **Allergens:** latex, nickel, acrylates; declare materials.
- **Placement variability:** body sites, skin tone and hair, motion; test across a diverse group.

## Verify
- [ ] Biological evaluation matches contact type and duration.
- [ ] Wear test at expected duration with skin assessment.
- [ ] Surface temperature measured at worst-case operation.
- [ ] Removal and re-application behaviour documented.
