# Screening and scoring candidate needs

**Goal:** turn a long list of observed needs into a ranked short list using criteria fixed before scoring.

## Method
1. Write each need as a need statement (problem, population, desired outcome), not a solution.
2. Choose criteria and weights up front. Typical: size and severity of the disease burden, gap in current solutions, who pays and reimbursement likelihood, regulatory pathway and evidence burden, technical feasibility, IP freedom to operate, fit with team skills, time and capital to reach market.
3. Score each need 1-5 per criterion with the evidence beside the score; flag scores that are guesses.
4. Use a weighted decision matrix to rank; run a sensitivity check by changing weights. A need that wins only under one weighting is fragile.
5. Reserve at least one criterion for a clinical-champion reality check.

## Traps
- Scoring after falling in love with a solution (post-hoc weights).
- Market size from top-down percentages without a reimbursed price point.
- Treating regulatory class as fixed; confirm the likely class and pathway with regulatory staff for each jurisdiction.

## Verify
- [ ] Criteria and weights recorded before scoring.
- [ ] Each score has a cited basis; guesses are marked.
- [ ] Top needs survive a weighting sensitivity test.

> Practice guidance, not regulatory or clinical advice. Confirm current standard editions and rules for your jurisdiction, and defer to qualified regulatory, clinical and engineering judgement.
