# Compaction specification and control

## Laboratory
- Proctor test: compact soil at varying water contents; plot dry density against water content for maximum dry density (MDD) and optimum moisture content (OMC). Standard (ASTM D698) vs Modified (ASTM D1557, about 4.5 times the energy, higher MDD and lower OMC). The specification must name which.
- Zero-air-voids line: gd = Gs gw / (1 + w Gs). A compaction curve must plot below it: a plausibility check.

## Specification
Relative compaction RC = field dry density / MDD. Typical figures are 95% standard for structural fill and 90% for general embankment; project specs govern. Add a moisture window around OMC (for example -2% to +2%). Clay compacted wet of optimum is softer and less permeable; dry of optimum is stiffer and more brittle. Clean sands and gravels may use relative density or method specifications (roller passes).

## Construction
Loose lifts of about 150 to 300 mm matched to roller and soil; a test strip with the actual plant sets the passes; proofroll the subgrade; do not compact over a pumping subgrade.

## Field control
Sand cone (D1556) or nuclear gauge (D6938; needs licensed operators and radiation safety). Frequency per specification (a common figure is one test per lift per set area or volume). Compare each test with the right curve (one-point Proctor for varied soils).

## Verify
Failing areas are reworked and retested; each report states MDD/OMC, soil description, lift and location.
