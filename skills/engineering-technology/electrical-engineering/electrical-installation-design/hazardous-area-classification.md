# Hazardous area electrical design

1. **Classification:** by a qualified specialist per IEC 60079-10-1 (gas) or 10-2 (dust). Zones 0, 1, 2 (gas) and 20, 21, 22 (dust) by likelihood of an explosive atmosphere; NEC Articles 500 to 506 use Class/Division or Zone systems.
2. **Protection concepts (IEC 60079):** Ex d (flameproof enclosure), Ex e (increased safety), Ex i (intrinsic safety, with safety barriers and entity parameters), Ex p (pressurisation), Ex n / Ex ec (non-sparking), Ex m (encapsulation), Ex o (oil), Ex q (sand).
3. **Marking:** group (I mines, II gas, III dust), gas subgroup (IIA, IIB, IIC), temperature class (T1 to T6) with maximum surface temperature, Equipment Protection Level (Ga, Gb, Gc). Match the equipment to zone, gas group and the autoignition temperature.
4. **Installation:** certified cable glands, sealing, earthing and bonding, and avoidance of unsuitable ingress; EU ATEX 2014/34/EU equipment directive and 1999/92/EC workplace directive; follow the national scheme (IECEx).
5. **Inspection:** initial and periodic per IEC 60079-17 by trained personnel; repairs per IEC 60079-19.
6. **Documentation:** area classification drawings, equipment register and dossier.

**Verify:** the verification dossier must exist before commissioning; this skill is not a substitute for the classification study.
