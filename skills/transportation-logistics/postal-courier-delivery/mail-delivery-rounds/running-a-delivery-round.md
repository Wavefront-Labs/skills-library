# Running a mail delivery round

**Goal:** deliver every item on the round to the right address, in one efficient
pass, with signed-for items accounted for and nobody bitten, hurt or late.

Terms vary by country (a *round* / *walk* / *route*; *frame* / *case*; *signed-for* /
*recorded* / *certified*). Procedures for accountable mail are set by each operator;
the sequence below is a generic pattern. *Draft: verify scanner steps against your
operator's handheld.*

## 1. Prep in the office
1. **Sequence (case) the mail** in delivery order on the frame: each slot is one
   delivery point in the order you'll walk it. Learn odd/even street sides and where
   the round doubles back.
2. **Apply redirections and holds** before bundling: check the redirect list against
   the frame and pull those items. Common error: a new redirect added today, missed
   because the list was checked yesterday.
3. **Bundle by loop**: if you park-and-loop, bundle each loop separately with an
   elastic or tray divider, in order, so you never sort at the doorstep.
4. **Accountable items** (signed-for, tracked, customs-charge, registered): scan them
   out to yourself, keep them separate (inner pocket or locked pouch), and list them.
   You personally own them until they're delivered or back in the office.
5. **Packets that won't fit letterboxes:** note which addresses have safe places,
   neighbours or parcel boxes on record; plan card-and-return for the rest.

## 2. On the round
- **Order of work:** deliver in sequence; never skip ahead to "do the big one later" —
  that's how items end up in the wrong box.
- **Check every item at the door** against the house number *and* street name; same
  number on two parallel streets is a classic misdelivery.
- **Letterbox technique:** push items fully through; use a letterbox brush tool or
  finger guard on stiff boxes rather than your fingers (some boxes have sharp springs and
  dogs behind them).
- **Signed-for:** scan the item, capture the signature/name, and deliver only to someone
  at the address. If no one is home, leave a card with the item's reference and return
  the item — don't leave it in a letterbox or safe place unless your operator's rules
  allow it for that service.
- **Vehicle loops:** handbrake on, engine off, keys with you, doors locked every stop —
  unlocked vans are the main source of lost accountable mail.

## 3. Dog and hazard awareness
- Rattle the gate and **pause before entering** any garden; if a dog is loose, don't
  enter — card it and report it.
- Keep the satchel or trolley between you and a dog; never turn your back and run.
- Record dog addresses and hazards (ice-prone steps, broken gates, aggressive
  occupants) on the round's hazard notes so relief staff know.
- Weather: grip-soled boots or ice cleats in frost; hi-vis on roads; hydrate in heat.
- **Lifting:** keep satchel weight within your operator's limit (often ~11–16 kg —
  *check local policy*); use a trolley or relay box rather than overloading.

## 4. Back in the office
- Return accountable items not delivered, scan them back in, and reconcile against
  your list — every item scanned out is either delivered or returned.
- Note new address issues: vacant properties, "not known at address", new builds.

## Gotchas
- Relief staff on an unfamiliar round: walk the frame order in your head first and mark
  oddities (back-door deliveries, letterboxes at gate posts, flats with a communal box).
- Never "save" undeliverable mail to try tomorrow without logging it — it disappears.
- Same surname at neighbouring houses is not permission to swap items.

## Verify
- [ ] Frame sequenced in walking order and redirections pulled before leaving.
- [ ] Every accountable item scanned out is now scanned as delivered or returned.
- [ ] No item was posted without matching number *and* street.
- [ ] Hazard and dog notes for the round updated.
- [ ] Vehicle locked at every unattended stop.
