# Working dive planning and in-water problems (commercial air diving)

**Goal:** get the job done inside the planned depth and time, with every likely problem
already rehearsed, so an in-water failure triggers a drill rather than a panic.

> Draft: general commercial surface-supplied air/nitrox practice. Depth limits differ
> by regime (e.g. IMCA surface-supplied air commonly to 50 m; US commercial/US Navy
> surface-supplied air tables to ~190 fsw). The company manual, the supervisor and
> local regulations govern every dive.

## 1. The diver's part of the plan
- **Depth and bottom time** from the agreed table, including how bottom time is counted
  (usually leaving surface → leaving bottom).
- **Task breakdown**: steps, tools, and what "done" looks like — e.g. "clean 4 anodes,
  photograph each, measure CP reading with probe". Unclear tasks waste bottom time.
- **Hazards**: currents and tide windows, **differential pressure (delta-P)** at intakes,
  outfalls, pipes and sluices, vessel thrusters/props, overhead environments, lifting
  operations, contaminated water, marine life.
- **Gas**: primary, secondary and bailout confirmed by the panel operator; for nitrox,
  the analysed O₂% and the corresponding table/MOD.
- **Comms and signals**: agreed words and line-pull backup.

## 2. Descending and working
- Descend on the **downline/shot line** or stage at the planned rate; report depth
  and condition ("on the bottom, 18 metres, vis nil, current light").
- Clear ears early; stop and ascend slightly if you can't equalise.
- Stay oriented with the umbilical — know which way it runs back to the surface; avoid
  wrapping it around structures (go out and back the same way).
- **Tools**: lower them on a line, not with the diver. Tether hand tools. Hydraulic
  tools: supply off (isolated topside) while connecting/disconnecting, and announce
  "tool live" before operating.
- Lifts: rig, then **leave the lift zone** and confirm with topside before any load moves.

## 3. Zero-visibility technique
- Hands are your eyes: work **hand over hand** along the downline, jackstay or structure.
- Before moving, describe what you feel so topside can visualise it.
- Keep a fixed reference hand on the job while the other works.
- Count turns, measure in hand spans, and mark found locations (tape, clamps).

## 4. In-water problems (drills)
| Problem | Diver action | Topside |
|---|---|---|
| **Loss of surface gas** | Open bailout, report "on bailout", stop work, head to the downline/stage | Switch to secondary, standby diver ready, recover diver |
| **Free-flow / breathing hard** | Adjust demand/free-flow, report | Check supply pressure vs depth |
| **Loss of comms** | Line-pull signals; follow last plan/abort | Line-pull check, prepare recovery |
| **Fouled umbilical** | Stop, don't pull — trace it back by hand, report | Don't haul; send standby if needed |
| **Entrapment/delta-P suction** | Don't fight it; stay calm, report | Isolate source (pump/valve) |
| **Injury or symptoms** | Report; stop work | Abort, recover, supervisor decides on chamber |
**Abort criteria** are agreed before the dive: loss of primary gas, loss of comms,
unplanned hazard, any diver symptom, or the supervisor's call.

## 5. Ascent
- Leave bottom at the planned time — not "just one more bolt".
- Ascend at the table's rate; make stops exactly as instructed; on surface-
  decompression procedures, move quickly and as briefed.
- After the dive: report findings, condition of equipment, and any symptoms. Stay near
  the chamber for the period your manual requires.

## Gotchas
- Delta-P hazards are invisible and can kill instantly — never approach an intake that
  topside hasn't confirmed isolated.
- Bottom time vanishes on searching; good topside directions and marked locations save it.
- Cold, heavy work and CO₂ build-up raise decompression stress — mention it.

## Verify
- [ ] Task, depth, time, hazards and abort criteria were briefed before dressing in.
- [ ] Delta-P sources and thrusters were confirmed isolated.
- [ ] You can do a bailout switch blind and report "on bailout".
- [ ] Tools were lowered on lines and hydraulics announced before use.
- [ ] Left bottom on time and ascended per the table.
