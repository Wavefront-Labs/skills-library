# Gas panel and umbilical pre-dive checks (surface-supplied)

**Goal:** before the diver leaves the surface, every gas path, the backup, the
umbilical and the comms have been proven — and the panel operator knows the numbers to
hold for the planned depth.

> Draft: general commercial surface-supplied air practice (IMCA/ADCI/US Navy style).
> **This is not training or a procedure.** The company's operations manual, the
> equipment manufacturer's manuals (helmet/mask, panel) and your regulator's rules are
> authoritative; figures below are illustrations to be checked against them.

## 1. Gas supply line-up
1. **Primary supply** (LP compressor with volume tank, or HP bank) and **secondary
   supply** (independent HP bank), each able to supply the diver alone.
2. **Supply pressure required** = ambient pressure at max depth + the **helmet
   manufacturer's minimum over-bottom pressure**. Example only: 30 msw ≈ 3 bar gauge
   (≈ 44 psi), plus a manufacturer over-bottom of, say, ~8–10 bar
   (≈ 120–150 psi) — check your helmet's table; requirements change with depth and model.
3. Panel: primary on, secondary isolated but pressurised and ready; regulator set to
   the required supply pressure; gauges read and logged.
4. Check **non-return valves** and that each diver's supply is independent at the panel.

## 2. Pneumofathometer (pneumo)
- A small open hose in the umbilical; panel gauge reads depth (often directly in fsw/msw).
- Test before the dive: blow through, close, check the gauge zeroes at the surface.
- In water: briefly flow gas until bubbles stop, then read the stable value — that's
  the diver's depth.

## 3. Umbilical
- Components: gas hose, pneumo, comms cable, strength member (and hot-water hose if
  used), taped or married together at regular intervals.
- Inspect the full length: cuts, kinks, crushed sections, end fittings, strain relief.
- **Flake it** in figure-eights on deck so it pays out without tangles; mark lengths
  if required by your procedures.
- Connect to the helmet/mask with the strength member taking the load, not the gas
  fitting.

## 4. Helmet/band mask and bailout
- Helmet: demand regulator breathes cleanly, free-flow (steady-flow) valve works,
  exhaust valve seals, neck dam/clamp fits, comms (earphones/mic) clear.
- **Bailout cylinder** (diver's emergency gas): full (log pressure), valve opened per
  procedure, first stage connected to the helmet's emergency valve and its interstage
  pressure set as the helmet manual requires.
- Diver performs a **bailout switch check** at the surface: close surface supply valve on
  helmet sideblock / open emergency → breathes off bailout → back to surface supply.

## 5. Comms
- Two-wire or four-wire comms box; check diver-to-panel and panel-to-diver at the
  surface and again on reaching the water.
- Record the dive on the comms recorder if fitted.
- Agree **line-pull signals** as backup. A common US Navy-style set (verify with your
  manual): from tender, **1 pull** "are you OK?"; from diver, **1** "I'm OK", **2** "give
  me slack", **3** "take up slack", **4** "haul me up", **2-2-2** "I'm fouled and need
  assistance", **3-3-3** "fouled but can clear myself", **4-4-4** "haul me up
  immediately".

## 6. Tending in the water
- **One tender per diver**, umbilical in hand at all times.
- Keep slack minimal: you should feel the diver's movements; excess slack fouls and
  delays emergency recovery.
- Watch bubbles on the surface — they show where the diver is.

## Gotchas
- Supply pressure set for 10 m and diver goes to 30 m: the helmet still breathes, but
  harder, and margins disappear. Re-set when depth changes.
- Umbilical through a moon pool, over a sharp edge, or near thrusters: protect, route
  and isolate hazards (see dive supervision skill
  `transportation-logistics.diving-life-support-craft.dive-supervision.dive-operations-planning-and-standby-readiness`).
- A bailout not opened (or empty) only shows when it's needed.

## Verify
- [ ] Primary and secondary supply independent, pressurised and logged.
- [ ] Supply pressure ≥ ambient at max depth + manufacturer over-bottom.
- [ ] Pneumo, comms and line-pull signals tested before splash.
- [ ] Bailout full, on and tested by a surface switch check.
- [ ] Umbilical inspected end to end and flaked clear.
