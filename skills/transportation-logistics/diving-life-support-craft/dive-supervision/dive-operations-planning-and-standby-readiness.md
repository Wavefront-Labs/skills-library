# Dive operations planning and standby readiness (dive supervision)

**Goal:** a dive that starts only when hazards are controlled and the team is ready,
and an emergency that runs on a plan everyone already heard.

> Draft: general practice drawn from IMCA/ADCI/HSE-style frameworks. The appointed
> supervisor holds legal responsibility for the dive; the diving project plan, company
> manual and applicable regulations (e.g. UK Diving at Work Regulations, US OSHA
> commercial diving, local equivalents) define the real requirements.

## 1. Planning (before mobilisation and before each shift)
1. **Scope**: tasks, location, depths, expected durations, tools and lifts.
2. **Diving method**: surface-supplied air/nitrox, wet bell, sat — justified by depth,
   duration and hazards.
3. **Risk assessment**: currents/tides, visibility, delta-P sources, vessel movement
   and thrusters, cranes, contaminated water, marine life, overhead environments, weather.
4. **Team**: size and roles set by regulation/company rule (for surface-supplied air
   a common minimum is supervisor, diver, standby diver and tenders — often around
   five people; check your regime). Fitness, certificates and medicals in date.
5. **Equipment**: maintenance records, certification of lifting gear, chamber
   availability (on site or within the time your rules require, depending on depth/deco).
6. **Emergency and rescue plan**: casualty recovery to deck, first aid, oxygen, chamber,
   medevac route and contacts (hyperbaric doctor, coastguard/helicopter).

## 2. Isolations and permits
- **Permit to work** linking the dive to vessel/installation operations.
- Isolate and **lock out/tag out**: intakes, pumps, sluices, thrusters and propellers near
  the diver, and any equipment the diver will work on. Get confirmation in writing/radio.
- On **DP vessels**: confirm DP status, umbilical lengths limited so the diver can't reach
  thrusters, and agree DP alert procedures (green/yellow/red) with the bridge.
- Cranes: no lifts over the dive site without supervisor's permission.

## 3. Toolbox briefing (every dive)
Cover: task steps, depth and bottom time, decompression plan, gas line-up, hazards and
isolations in place, comms and signals, abort criteria, each person's role, and the
**emergency drill** (who does what if the diver goes unconscious or on bailout).
Invite questions; confirm understanding, not just attendance.

## 4. Standby diver readiness
- Dressed in, equipment checked, **able to enter the water immediately** (your manual may
  set a time), at the entry point.
- Umbilical long enough to reach the working diver (often the working umbilical length
  plus margin), on a separate gas supply, with comms tested.
- Standby is not a spare hand for deck work during the dive.
- Rehearse the rescue: standby descends the diver's umbilical, assesses, switches the
  diver to bailout/secondary, recovers to the surface/stage.

## 5. Running the dive
- Log: leave surface, reach bottom, task progress, leave bottom, stops, surface, times
  and depths (pneumo), gas used.
- Listen to the diver's breathing on comms — it's the earliest sign of trouble.
- Watch everything else: weather, vessel movement, crane activity, other contractors.
- **Stop the dive** for any change outside the plan.

## 6. Emergency command
1. Clear instruction: "Diver emergency — standby diver in the water."
2. Assign roles: tender, panel, chamber operator, comms/first aider, radio to bridge
   and shore.
3. Gas: switch diver to secondary; confirm bailout.
4. Recover casualty, first aid with oxygen, decide on recompression with the diving
   doctor.
5. Record times and actions; preserve equipment for investigation.

## Gotchas
- An isolation "confirmed verbally" by someone who doesn't control the valve isn't one.
- Standby diver doing other tasks → minutes lost when they're needed.
- Weather windows close faster than tasks finish; plan the abort time.

## Verify
- [ ] Risk assessment, team and equipment checks done before the dive.
- [ ] All delta-P sources and thrusters isolated with confirmation recorded.
- [ ] Toolbox talk covered tasks, hazards, abort criteria and emergency roles.
- [ ] Standby diver dressed, checked, at the entry point and unoccupied.
- [ ] Dive log complete with times, depths and gas.
