# Writing measurable postsecondary goals and transition services

**Goal:** a transition plan that starts from what the *student* wants for adult life,
backs it with assessment data, and lines up school services and adult agencies so the
handoff at graduation isn't a cliff.

*US IDEA: transition content is required in the IEP in effect when the student turns 16
(earlier in many states, often 14). Federal compliance is checked against "Indicator 13".
Check your state's age and form.*

## 1. Age-appropriate transition assessment
Use several sources, updated yearly:
- **Interviews** with student and family (person-centred planning tools like MAPS/PATH
  help with students who communicate less verbally).
- **Interest and preference inventories** (career interest surveys).
- **Aptitude/skills:** academic data, work-sample or situational assessments, job-site
  observations.
- **Adaptive/independent-living** checklists (money, cooking, transport, self-care).
- **Self-determination** scales (e.g. AIR, Arc's Self-Determination Scale).
Summarise results in the present levels: strengths, preferences, interests, needs.

## 2. Measurable postsecondary goals (MPSGs)
Required areas: **education/training** and **employment**; **independent living**
"where appropriate". Formula:
> **After high school, [student] will [specific, observable outcome].**

- ✔ "After high school, Maya will enrol in the 2-year veterinary technician program at
  [community college]." *(Use a generic description in shared documents.)*
- ✔ "After graduation, Leo will work part-time in a grocery store with job-coach support."
- ✘ "Maya will consider college." / "Leo hopes to work." — not measurable outcomes.
Goals happen **after** exit; they aren't annual IEP goals. Each must be supported by the
assessment data.

## 3. Transition services and course of study
- **Course of study:** the multi-year sequence of classes that leads to the goal
  (diploma track, CTE pathway, required science courses for vet tech).
- **Transition services** — a coordinated set of activities: instruction, related
  services, community experiences, employment and adult-living objectives, daily-living
  skills and functional vocational evaluation if appropriate. Name the activity, who's
  responsible, and the timeline (e.g. "job shadow at an animal clinic, CTE coordinator,
  spring term").
- **Annual IEP goals** that build the skills needed for the MPSGs (e.g. self-advocacy:
  "Maya will explain her accommodations to a new teacher…").

## 4. Agency linkage
- Invite (with **consent** from parent or adult student) agencies likely to provide or
  pay for services: **state vocational rehabilitation (VR)**, developmental-disability
  services, mental-health services, community colleges' disability offices.
- VR must offer **Pre-Employment Transition Services** (job exploration counselling,
  work-based learning, workplace readiness, postsecondary counselling, self-advocacy
  instruction) to eligible students — refer early, often age 14–16. *Ages vary by state.*
- Note waitlists (e.g. Medicaid waiver services) — apply years ahead.

## 5. Age of majority and exit
- At least **one year before** the state age of majority, inform the student and parents
  that IDEA rights transfer to the student. Discuss decision-making options (supported
  decision-making, powers of attorney, guardianship as the most restrictive) — refer to
  legal resources rather than advise.
- At exit with a regular diploma or ageing out, provide a **Summary of Performance**:
  academic/functional performance and recommendations for meeting postsecondary goals.

## Gotchas
- **The student isn't at the meeting** — they must be invited when transition is
  discussed; prepare them to lead part of it.
- **Goals copied from last year** with no updated assessment.
- **Services that don't connect** to the goals (vet-tech goal with no science course).
- Colleges don't run IEPs — prepare students to self-disclose and request accommodations
  from disability services.

## Verify
- [ ] Transition assessments from multiple sources are summarised and current.
- [ ] Postsecondary goals for education/training and employment (and living, if
      appropriate) are measurable outcomes after exit.
- [ ] Course of study and transition services link to each goal, with owners and dates.
- [ ] Student was invited; agencies were invited with consent.
- [ ] Age-of-majority notice and Summary of Performance are scheduled.
