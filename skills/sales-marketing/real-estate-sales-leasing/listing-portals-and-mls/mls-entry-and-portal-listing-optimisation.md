# MLS entry and portal listing optimisation

**Goal:** get the listing in front of every relevant buyer with data they can
trust, copy and photos that earn the click and the inspection, and replies to
enquiries fast enough to keep the buyer. Platforms differ by country: **MLS**
with syndication to Zillow, Realtor.com, Redfin (US/Canada); **Rightmove,
Zoopla, OnTheMarket** (UK); **realestate.com.au, Domain** (Australia);
**Trade Me** (NZ). **Status draft:** field names and rules below are generic —
follow your MLS's data rules and the portal's listing guidelines.

## 1. MLS / feed data entry
Accuracy is the priority — buyers filter on fields, and errors hide the listing or
create liability.
- Property type, beds, baths (full/half), parking, land size, internal area
  (state source: plans, measurement, records), year built.
- Price or price guide per local rules; status (active, under offer/pending, sold).
- Features checklist complete (pool, air-con, garage, accessibility) — filters
  rely on these.
- Geocode check: pin on the right lot on the map.
- Showing/inspection instructions and times.
- Remarks: public remarks (buyer-facing, no agent-only info) vs private/agent remarks.
- In the US, check your MLS's rules on when a listing must be entered after public
  marketing begins (NAR's Clear Cooperation Policy and later variations) — **uncertain:**
  rules have changed recently and differ by MLS.
Proofread against the seller-signed listing data sheet before publishing.

## 2. Photos and media
- Professional photos, landscape orientation, bright, straight verticals.
- **Photo order**: hero shot first (best exterior or best interior — whichever is
  strongest), then follow a walk-through: living, kitchen, dining, main bedroom,
  bathrooms, other bedrooms, outdoor, view, street/area. Lead with what makes the
  home special.
- Floor plan — portals report it as one of the most viewed items; include dimensions.
- Video or 3D tour for higher-value or remote-buyer listings.
- Don't digitally remove permanent defects or add features that aren't there;
  virtual staging must be labelled where required.

## 3. Listing copy
- **Headline**: the one reason to look (e.g. "North-facing family home on a quiet
  cul-de-sac, walk to station"). Specific beats superlatives.
- First two lines carry the most weight on mobile — lead with the lifestyle benefit
  and key facts.
- Body: flow through the home logically, then location and amenities (with distances
  or walk times), then practical details (inspection times, price guide).
- Bullet the facts: beds, baths, parking, land, key features.
- Compliance: no discriminatory language about who should live there ("perfect
  for young couples", "no kids") — fair-housing/anti-discrimination rules; no
  misleading claims about size, zoning or potential.

## 4. Portal upgrades and timing
- Premium placements (featured, top spot, boosts) — use for launch week when
  interest peaks; measure views and enquiries per dollar.
- Launch timing: many markets see highest portal traffic on weekday evenings and
  weekends; publish ahead of first open home.
- Refresh photos/headline if views are strong but enquiries are low (content
  problem) — or review price if views are low (search filters problem).

## 5. Enquiry triage
- Reply within minutes during business hours; auto-acknowledge after hours with
  inspection times and a contract/disclosure pack link.
- Qualify: inspected yet? finance ready? selling first? timeline?
- Log every enquiry into the CRM (see pipeline-stages-and-drip-campaigns) and tag source.

## Gotchas
- Wrong bedroom/area count discovered at contract stage = misrepresentation risk.
- Duplicate listings across offices or agents confuse portals and buyers.
- Forgetting to update status (sold/under offer) generates complaints and wasted calls.

## Verify
- [ ] MLS/feed fields complete, accurate and proofread against signed data sheet.
- [ ] Hero photo first, logical sequence, floor plan included.
- [ ] Headline specific; copy compliant with fair-housing and accuracy rules.
- [ ] Portal performance (views, enquiries) reviewed after week one.
- [ ] Enquiries answered fast and logged with source.
