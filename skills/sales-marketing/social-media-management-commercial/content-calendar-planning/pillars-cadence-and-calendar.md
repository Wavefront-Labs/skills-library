# Pillars, cadence and calendar

**Goal:** a brand account that posts consistently, on-brand and on-purpose — with every post
traceable to a business goal — without the team scrambling daily.

## 1. Define 3–5 content pillars
A pillar = a recurring theme that serves an audience need *and* a business goal.
Example for a coffee roaster:
| Pillar | Audience need | Business goal | Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brew education | make better coffee | authority, saves | 35% |
| Behind the roastery | trust, story | brand affinity | 20% |
| Customers & community | belonging | UGC, reach | 20% |
| Product & offers | what to buy | sales | 25% |
Rule of thumb: keep direct-sell content a minority (often ≤20–30%); test your own mix.

## 2. Set a cadence you can sustain
Consistency beats volume. A starting point for a small team (adjust from analytics):
- Instagram: 3–5 feed posts/Reels a week + stories most days.
- TikTok: 3–5 videos a week.
- LinkedIn (B2B): 2–4 posts a week.
- Facebook: 3–5 posts a week, if the audience is there.
Platform guidance changes often; treat these as a hypothesis to test, not rules.
Calculate capacity: hours per asset × posts per week. If it exceeds team hours, cut a
platform rather than lower quality everywhere.

## 3. Build the calendar
Use a spreadsheet, Notion/Airtable board, or your scheduling tool (see
sales-marketing.social-media-management-commercial.social-management-platforms.scheduling-inbox-and-listening).
Columns: `date | platform | pillar | format | hook/caption draft | asset link | CTA | status | owner | campaign tag`.
1. **Lock fixed dates first:** launches, sales, holidays and industry events for the quarter.
2. **Campaign bursts:** around each, plan a teaser → launch → proof → last chance sequence.
3. **Fill always-on slots** by pillar mix percentages.
4. **Leave ~10–20% of slots open** for reactive and trend content.
5. Plan 2–4 weeks ahead in detail, the quarter in outline. Batch-produce weekly.

## 4. Trend-jacking decision rule (decide in minutes)
Jump on a trend only if all are yes:
- Does it fit our voice and one of our pillars?
- Can we publish within **24–72 hours** (trends decay fast)?
- Is the audio/meme safe to use commercially? (Business accounts often have restricted
  music libraries — use the platform's commercial sound library.)
- Is there zero risk of seeming to exploit a tragedy or sensitive topic?
Any no → skip. Have a named approver who can say yes the same day.

## 5. Review monthly
Per pillar: reach, engagement rate, saves/shares, link clicks, sales attributed. Shift the
pillar mix toward what performs; kill formats that don't after a fair test (e.g. 8–10 posts).

## Gotchas
- Calendars built in isolation from sales/product miss launches — get the commercial
  calendar first.
- Scheduling everything removes the ability to react; check scheduled posts before a
  crisis or major news event and pause if needed.
- Cross-posting the same asset to every platform underperforms; adapt format and caption.
- Cadence numbers above are general practice, not platform-published rules.

## Verify
- [ ] 3–5 pillars each linked to an audience need and a business goal.
- [ ] Cadence fits team capacity in hours.
- [ ] Calendar has fixed dates, campaign sequences and 10–20% reactive slots.
- [ ] Trend checklist and same-day approver defined.
- [ ] Monthly pillar performance review shifts the mix.
