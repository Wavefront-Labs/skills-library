# Shoppable catalogs and live shopping

**Goal:** a viewer goes from content to purchase in as few taps as possible, and you can
see which content made the sale.

**Caveat:** social-commerce features, eligibility, fees and regional availability change
frequently (TikTok Shop and Meta's checkout options have both changed scope in recent years).
Check current seller-centre documentation before planning.

## 1. Get the catalog right (everything depends on it)
- Connect your store platform's sales channel (e.g. Shopify's Facebook & Instagram and TikTok
  channels) or upload a **product feed** (CSV/XML or scheduled URL) to Meta Commerce Manager
  or TikTok Shop Seller Center.
- Each product needs: unique ID matching your store SKU, title (clear, no all-caps or promo
  text), description, price, availability, high-quality images on plain background,
  brand, GTIN where available, variants (size/colour) grouped.
- Sync inventory automatically; selling out-of-stock items in-app causes cancellations,
  and platforms may penalise seller scores.
- Expect a **review/approval** step for the shop and products; prohibited and restricted
  categories differ by platform.

## 2. Shoppable posts
- Tag products in posts, Reels and stories (Instagram product tags; TikTok product links /
  showcase). Tag the exact variant shown.
- Use content that already performs as organic content — demo, before/after, styling —
  then tag. Hard-sell catalogue shots underperform.
- Pin best-sellers in the shop tab/showcase; build collections that mirror your website's.

## 3. TikTok Shop operations (summary)
- Seller Center: products, orders, fulfilment SLAs (ship within the stated window),
  returns, customer messages, and performance scores — check daily.
- **Affiliate programme:** set an open commission rate or targeted plans; send samples
  to creators who match the product; their videos and lives carry your product link.
- Promotions: platform-funded and seller-funded discounts; check the margin after fees,
  commission and discount stack.

## 4. Running a live shopping session (60 minutes)
**Before:** pick 5–10 products; pin a run of show (product order, talking points, offer,
time per product ~5–8 min); test lighting, mic, stable internet, stock levels; announce
the time 3–7 days ahead and again 1 hour before.
**During:**
- Host + a producer/moderator (pins products, answers comments, handles issues).
- For each product: hook → demo it → answer live questions by name → state the offer and
  stock left → pin it → call to action ("tap the bag, it's item 3").
- Recap the offers every ~10 minutes for people joining late.
**After:** replays, clip highlights into short videos, reply to unanswered questions.

## 5. Measure
Per post/live/creator: views, product clicks, add-to-carts, orders, GMV, conversion rate
(orders ÷ product clicks), return rate. Compare net margin after platform fees and
affiliate commission against your web store.

## Gotchas
- Fees: platform referral fees + payment fees + affiliate commission + discount can erase
  margin — model it per SKU.
- Claims in lives are advertising: no unsubstantiated health/performance claims.
- Fulfilment SLAs are strict; late shipping hurts shop scores and visibility.
- Platform names and menus above may be outdated; verify in-app.

## Verify
- [ ] Catalog synced with live inventory; products approved; IDs match store SKUs.
- [ ] Top content has product tags on the exact variant shown.
- [ ] Unit economics per SKU include fees, commission and discounts.
- [ ] Live session has run of show, moderator, stock checked and offers recapped.
- [ ] Conversion and net margin tracked per post, live and creator.
