# Group quotes, holds, deposits and name lists

**Goal:** win the group, lock space at a group rate, and get every traveller named, paid
and documented before each supplier deadline — without the agency being liable for empty seats.

Group policies differ by supplier (what counts as a "group" — often around 10 rooms/cabins
or passengers — deposit levels, release dates). The structure below is common; the
numbers in each contract are what count.

## 1. Qualify the group
- Organiser (group leader) and decision process: who signs, who collects money?
- Size estimate *and* confidence: "Firm 20, possibly 30?"
- Dates, flexibility, destination, budget per person, room/cabin mix (twins, singles —
  singles often cost more), special needs.
- How travellers pay: organiser pays in one sum vs each traveller pays you directly.
- Why they travel (wedding, club, school, incentive, reunion) — shapes what you sell.

## 2. Request group quotes
Send each supplier one clear request: dates, numbers (rooms/cabins/seats by type),
board basis, special requests, and ask for: group rate, deposit schedule, **free-of-charge
(FOC/"tour conductor") places** (e.g. one free per N paying — varies), amenities,
**attrition** allowance, **release/cut-off dates**, name-change rules, cancellation scale.
Compare offers on total cost per traveller including taxes and fees, and on terms — the
cheapest rate with harsh attrition can cost more.

## 3. Read the contract's key terms
| Term | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Allotment / block | Space held for the group | Record exactly how many of each type |
| Attrition | How much you can reduce without penalty (e.g. down to 80%) | Size the block to the *firm* number, not the hope |
| Release / cut-off | Date unsold space returns to the supplier | Calendar it, chase names before |
| Deposit schedule | Amounts and dates | Collect from travellers *before* each supplier date |
| Name-list deadline | When names must be final (airlines often strict) | Calendar it |
| Cancellation scale | Penalties rising toward departure | Reflect it in your traveller T&Cs |

## 4. Price and sell to travellers
- Build a per-person price including your margin/fees; decide how FOC places are used
  (organiser's free place, or spread across the group to lower the price).
- Group-leader incentives: a free or discounted place, an upgrade, a cash credit —
  disclose arrangements appropriately where rules or the group expect it.
- Give the leader a one-page flyer and a booking link/form with deadlines.

## 5. Deposits and payments
- **Collect from travellers 1–2 weeks before each supplier deadline** so late payers
  don't put you in breach.
- Ledger per traveller: deposit due/paid, balance due/paid, insurance status.
- Release unpaid places by the release date rather than absorb penalties — agree this
  rule with the organiser at the start.

## 6. Name and rooming lists
- Spreadsheet: surname/first name **exactly as in passport**, gender (if supplier needs),
  date of birth, room/cabin assignment and sharer, dietary/medical notes, contact, payment status.
- Send to suppliers by the name-list deadline; check name changes rules (airlines may
  charge or forbid).
- Final version sent to every supplier and the leader; one master file, version-dated.

## Gotchas
- Blocking optimistic numbers → attrition penalties.
- Paying the supplier deposit before collecting from travellers — the agency's cash at risk.
- Name typos on group air tickets; corrections can be expensive or impossible.
- Leader collecting money informally and disappearing — prefer direct payment to the agency.

## Verify
- [ ] Group qualified with firm vs possible numbers and payment method agreed.
- [ ] Supplier contract terms (attrition, release, deposits, name deadline) in the calendar.
- [ ] Traveller deadlines set before supplier deadlines.
- [ ] Payment ledger and name/rooming list current, names matching passports.
- [ ] Final lists confirmed by every supplier in writing.
