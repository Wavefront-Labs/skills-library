# Call reviews, deal coaching and skill gaps

**Goal:** each rep improves one specific skill at a time, measured in their numbers —
not a manager who just takes over deals.

## 1. Diagnose with the funnel, then confirm on calls
Pull each rep's conversion by stage vs team median (last 1–2 quarters):
| Weak metric | Likely skill gap |
|---|---|
| Low meetings from outreach | prospecting / messaging |
| Low meeting → qualified | discovery, qualification |
| Low qualified → proposal | building value, multi-threading |
| Low win rate late stage | negotiation, closing, mapping decision process |
| Long cycles, many slips | next-step discipline, champion building |
| Low deal size | scoping, value-based pricing |
Pick **one** gap per rep per month.

## 2. Call reviews (weekly, 30 min)
- Use recordings (Gong, Chorus, Zoom, Teams; get consent per local law and company
  policy) or a ride-along.
- Agree a **scorecard** for the skill: e.g. for discovery — asked about current process,
  quantified pain, identified decision-maker, set next step with a date.
- Rep reviews the call first and self-scores. Then listen together to 2–3 key moments.
- Ask before you tell: "What were you trying to achieve there?" "What else could you have
  asked?" Then give one specific suggestion and role-play it for 5 minutes.
- Useful signals: rep talk ratio (many teams aim under ~50–60% in discovery — verify with
  your own winning calls), number of open questions, whether a dated next step was agreed.

## 3. Deal coaching (in 1:1s)
Coach the rep, don't run the deal. For one important opportunity:
1. "What does the customer say the problem costs them?"
2. "Who else is affected and have we met them?"
3. "What happens if they do nothing?"
4. "What's the next step, and why would the buyer want it?"
5. "What would you do differently if this was your only deal?"
End with the rep owning 1–2 actions with dates. Join a call only when the rep asks or the
deal truly needs executive presence — and debrief afterwards.

## 4. Structure the 1:1
A simple shape (based on the GROW coaching model): **Goal** for this session → **Reality**
(numbers, recent calls) → **Options** → **Will** (committed actions). Keep a shared doc
with actions and review last week's first.

## 5. Performance conversations
When coaching hasn't moved the numbers over a reasonable period:
- Be specific and documented: the metric, the expected level, the gap, the support given.
- A **performance improvement plan** sets measurable targets, a timeframe (often 30–90
  days), weekly check-ins and support. It must be achievable, or it's a disguised exit.
- Involve HR early; employment law varies by country and state.

## Gotchas
- Coaching everything at once = coaching nothing. One skill, one scorecard.
- Top performers need coaching too — or they leave.
- Activity metrics (calls/emails) are easy to game; use them as input, not as the goal.
- Recording consent rules differ (one-party vs all-party consent jurisdictions); check.

## Verify
- [ ] Each rep has one named skill focus tied to a funnel metric.
- [ ] Weekly call review done with a written scorecard and a role-play.
- [ ] Deal coaching ends with rep-owned actions and dates.
- [ ] 1:1 notes track actions week to week.
- [ ] The targeted metric is re-checked after 4–8 weeks.
