# Online and hybrid auction management

**Goal:** sell through online bidding platforms without losing the competitive tension
of a room, and without the operational errors (missed bids, wrong winners, unpaid
lots) that online sales make easy.

Platform features, fees and menu names change; the steps below are the common pattern.
Check each platform's current seller documentation before your first sale on it.

## 1. Pick the format
| Format | How it works | Fits |
|---|---|---|
| **Timed online** | Each lot closes at a set time; highest bid wins | General goods, high lot counts, low staffing |
| **Live online (simulcast/hybrid)** | Auctioneer calls the sale; internet bids arrive alongside room/phone | Higher-value sales, established rooms |
| **Marketplace auction (eBay)** | Single-item listings, platform-run | Individual items, wide consumer reach |
Aggregator platforms (e.g. LiveAuctioneers, Invaluable, the-saleroom) list your
catalogue to their bidder base and charge fees, often including an additional internet
buyer's premium; your own white-label software (e.g. HiBid-type systems) costs less
per lot but brings fewer bidders.

## 2. Build the listing
- Import the catalogue (CSV/XML template from the platform): lot number, title,
  description, condition, estimate, starting bid, reserve flag, dimensions, images.
- 6–12 good photos per valuable lot; first image is the thumbnail that sells the click.
- Set **starting bids** low enough to attract early bids (activity attracts bidders) but
  protect with the confidential reserve.
- Publish terms: buyer's premium (room vs online), taxes, payment methods and
  deadlines, shipping/collection, and condition-report contact.
- Go live 2–3 weeks before a sale so watchers and absentee bids accumulate.

## 3. Timed-auction settings
- **Soft close / extended bidding:** any bid in the last few minutes extends that lot
  (e.g. by 2–5 minutes) — prevents sniping and lets bidding run its course.
- **Staggered closing:** lots close in sequence (e.g. one lot every 30–60 seconds), so
  bidders who lose one lot can move to the next.
- **Proxy / max bids:** the system bids for the buyer up to their maximum in increments;
  make sure the increment table matches your room's.

## 4. Live online (simulcast) operations
- Staff roles: **auctioneer**, **internet clerk** (accepts/relays online bids on the
  platform console), **sale clerk** (records hammer and paddle), **phone bank** staff.
- Auctioneer announces internet bids ("online at 500") and gives online bidders a fair
  moment before the hammer — platform latency can be several seconds.
- Video/audio stream: wired internet, a backup connection (mobile hotspot) and a test
  stream the day before.

## 5. Absentee and phone bids
- **Absentee (commission) bids:** record in the book with time received; execute them
  as competitively as possible (only up to what's needed to beat other bids). Tied
  absentee bids: earliest received wins, by common practice.
- **Phone bids:** register in advance with the lot list; staff call 2–3 lots before
  theirs; minimum lot value for phone service keeps it manageable.

## 6. After the hammer
Reconcile platform results with the clerk's sheet, issue invoices same day, chase
unpaid lots per terms (reminder, then relist and charge the defaulter per conditions),
and leave honest feedback/ratings where the platform uses them.

## Gotchas
- Hard-close timed sales reward snipers and often depress prices — use soft close.
- Online premium on top of room premium surprises buyers; display it clearly.
- Platform approval of new bidders (card checks, limits) protects you from non-payers;
  don't approve everyone instantly on high-value sales.
- Clock/time zone mismatches on closing times produce disputes — state the time zone.

## Verify
- [ ] Format chosen and platform fees/premiums modelled per lot.
- [ ] Catalogue imported with photos, estimates, starting bids and published terms.
- [ ] Soft close and staggered closing configured for timed sales.
- [ ] Live sale had internet clerk, backup connection and test stream.
- [ ] Platform results reconciled to clerk sheet; invoices sent same day.
