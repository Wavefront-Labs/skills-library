# Campaign budgeting, pacing and post-campaign reporting

**Goal:** spend exactly what was approved, on what was planned, know weekly whether
you're on track, and finish with a report that changes the next campaign.

## 1. Build the budget
Set up one sheet (Excel/Google Sheets) with these blocks:
- **Working media** (money that buys exposure), by channel → vendor → flight.
- **Non-working** costs: creative production, talent/usage rights, agency fees,
  ad-serving and verification fees (often charged per thousand), research.
- **Contingency** 5–10% held centrally.
- Totals checked against the approved figure; currency and tax treatment stated.
Track the working-to-non-working ratio. Ratios vary widely; if non-working creeps past
what the business expected, flag it before money is committed.

## 2. Pace weekly
Columns per line: planned spend to date, actual (invoiced or platform-reported) spend
to date, pacing % = actual ÷ planned, delivery (impressions/GRPs) to date, and the
KPI to date.
- **Digital:** pull spend daily/weekly from platform reports (DSP, Google Ads, Meta).
  Rule of thumb: act if pacing drifts outside ±10% of plan — under-pacing risks unspent
  budget, over-pacing risks the flight running dry early.
- **Offline:** use the booked schedule plus post-buy delivery; invoices arrive late,
  so pace on *commitments*, not cash.
- Re-forecast to end of flight every week: projected total = spend to date +
  committed + expected remaining.
- Move money with a written change log (date, from, to, reason, approver).

## 3. Reconcile
At flight end, match three sources line by line: **booked** (IO/schedule), **delivered**
(post-buy, ad-server/platform data), **billed** (invoices). Chase discrepancies:
under-delivery → makegoods or credit; billing above delivered → dispute.

## 4. Post-campaign report (PCA / wrap report)
Keep it to 5–8 slides or 2 pages:
1. **Objective and KPIs** as set in the brief (no moving goalposts).
2. **Spend:** budget vs actual, working/non-working split.
3. **Delivery:** reach, frequency, impressions, GRPs vs plan.
4. **Outcomes:** the KPI results — sales or leads, CPA/ROAS, brand-lift or tracking
   shifts — with the comparison base (prior period, control region, benchmark).
5. **What worked / didn't**, by channel, creative and timing, with numbers.
6. **Recommendations:** 3–5 specific changes for next time ("shift 15% from display
   to CTV; retire execution B after week 3").
State confidence honestly: last-click platform numbers overstate their own channel;
note when results are correlation, not tested causation.

## Gotchas
- Platform-reported spend can include or exclude fees and tax inconsistently; agree
  one definition and stick to it.
- Agency commission on gross vs net media changes the totals — confirm which the
  budget is quoted in.
- Late invoices make month-end actuals look under-spent; use accrued commitments.
- A report that only lists metrics without decisions is filed and forgotten.

## Verify
- [ ] Budget sheet total equals approved budget, contingency shown.
- [ ] Weekly pacing with re-forecast exists for every line; drift >10% has an action.
- [ ] Change log covers every reallocation.
- [ ] Booked vs delivered vs billed reconciled; discrepancies claimed.
- [ ] Wrap report compares results to the original KPIs and ends with specific recommendations.
