# Add-to-box, member shop and tier upgrades

**Goal:** raise revenue per subscriber by selling more of what the curation already made
them love — without making the box feel like an advert.

## 1. Add-to-box offers (before the box ships)
- Window: after the reveal or customisation deadline opens, before packing (e.g. a 5–10 day
  window). Add-ons ship in the same box → no extra postage, high margin.
- Offer 3–8 items: full-size versions of this month's samples, complementary items, past
  favourites, a gift add-on in gift season.
- Price at a **member price** (visible saving vs RRP) — still check margin after packing.
- Check weight: add-ons can push a box into a higher postage band; set rules per variant.
- Email + in-account banner + SMS (where consented) at window open and 24 h before close.

## 2. Member shop (after the box lands)
- The natural moment is **after unboxing**: "Loved the hand cream? Get the full size at
  20% off." Link each item card in the box (QR code) to its shop page.
- Stock what's rated highly: use per-item ratings from the box survey to choose shop lines
  and quantities. Many items in boxes are samples — negotiate full-size supply in advance with
  brand partners (often they'll offer wholesale or affiliate terms).
- Members-only access (login required) reinforces membership value.
- Ship shop orders free over a threshold or with the next box (cheaper).

## 3. Flash sales
- Short windows (24–72 h) on past-box surplus, overstock and bundles; announce to members
  first, then public.
- Use them to clear leftovers from limited drops and seasonal boxes.
- Don't run them so often that members wait for sales instead of subscribing at full price.

## 4. Tier upgrades
- Define tiers clearly: e.g. Standard / Deluxe (more items or full-size hero) / Annual prepaid
  (discount for commitment).
- **Trigger** upgrade offers on signals: rated the last 2 boxes 5/5, bought from the member
  shop, reached a tenure milestone, or chose premium add-ons repeatedly.
- Offer: "Upgrade to Deluxe from your next box — first month at the Standard price."
- **Annual prepay** improves cash flow and retention; make the saving clear and the refund
  policy compliant with local consumer law.
- Make downgrades just as easy (it's also a retention tool — see
  sales-marketing.subscription-box-curation.member-retention-offers.churn-signals-pause-flows-and-win-backs).

## 5. Measure
- Add-on attach rate (% of boxes with ≥1 add-on), add-on revenue per box.
- Member shop conversion from the in-box QR codes; repeat purchase rate.
- Upgrade rate and the churn rate of upgraded vs non-upgraded members (an upgrade that
  increases churn isn't a win).
- **Revenue per subscriber per month** and lifetime value by cohort.

## Gotchas
- Too many offers per month erode the "curated gift" feeling; cap marketing touches.
- Add-ons must be in stock at pack time — oversold add-ons delay the whole box.
- Brand partners may restrict discounting of their full-size products; check agreements.
- Tax and pricing display rules apply to member pricing and "was" prices — keep reference
  prices genuine. Suggested windows and discounts above are illustrative.

## Verify
- [ ] Add-to-box window set before pack date; weight and margin checked per add-on.
- [ ] Member shop stocks top-rated box items with full-size supply agreed.
- [ ] In-box QR codes link to the right shop items.
- [ ] Upgrade offers triggered by rating, purchase or tenure signals; downgrades easy.
- [ ] Attach rate, revenue per subscriber, and churn of upgraded members tracked monthly.
