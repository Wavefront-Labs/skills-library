# Partner recruitment, deal registration and co-selling

**Goal:** a small number of partners who actively lead with your product, protected from
channel conflict, trained enough to sell and deploy it, and measured on pipeline they source.

Margins, discount levels and MDF figures vary widely by industry; treat any numbers as
illustrations, not benchmarks.

## 1. Define the ideal partner profile
- **Type**: reseller/VAR (resells, often handles billing), distributor (logistics, credit,
  reaches many resellers), MSP (runs it as a managed service), systems integrator
  (implementation services), ISV/tech partner (integration, co-sell).
- **Fit**: they already sell to your target buyer; your product grows *their* revenue
  (services, attach, recurring margin); they have sales capacity and technical staff.
- **Conflict check**: do they also push a direct competitor? That's common; know which
  one they lead with.

## 2. Recruit
- Shortlist from customers' existing vendors ("who installed your current system?"),
  partners of adjacent tech vendors, and inbound applications.
- Pitch the partner business case, not the product: margin per deal, services
  attached, deal protection, leads you'll share, time to first deal.
- Start with a **joint business plan**: target accounts, 90-day goals (e.g. trained staff,
  first registered deal), named contacts on both sides.

## 3. Program structure
| Element | Typical design |
|---|---|
| Tiers | e.g. Registered → Silver → Gold, by revenue, certified staff, customer satisfaction |
| Benefits | higher discount/margin, MDF, leads, deal-reg protection, NFR licences |
| Requirements | certifications, annual plan, minimum pipeline, reporting |
Keep it simple at first; a complex tier matrix with 5 partners is overhead.

## 4. Deal registration (the conflict preventer)
- Partner submits the opportunity in the PRM/partner portal (or CRM partner module):
  customer, contact, product, value, expected close, what the partner did to find it.
- **Rules written in advance**: what qualifies (new opportunity, not already in your
  CRM), approval SLA (e.g. 48 h), protection period (e.g. 90 days, renewable with activity),
  first-to-register wins, extra margin for registered deals.
- Your direct reps must check registrations before engaging an account; comp plans
  should pay direct reps on partner deals in their territory (or at least not punish them)
  — otherwise they'll compete with partners.
- Disputes go to one named channel manager; decisions logged.

## 5. Enablement
- Role-based paths: sales (positioning, discovery questions, pricing), presales (demo,
  POC), delivery (implementation, support).
- Assets: battle cards, demo environment/NFR licence, proposal templates, reference
  stories, pricing calculator.
- Certification gates for tiers; ride-alongs on the first few deals.

## 6. Co-selling and co-marketing
- Joint account mapping: compare target lists (share only what agreements and privacy
  rules allow) to find overlap.
- On co-sold deals agree roles up front: who leads the relationship, who demos, who
  prices, who delivers.
- MDF: fund campaigns with a plan and measured outcome (leads, meetings, pipeline);
  pay on proof of performance.

## 7. Measure and review (partner QBR)
Track per partner: registered deals, partner-sourced vs influenced pipeline, win rate,
revenue, certified people, customer satisfaction. Quarterly review against the joint plan;
move inactive partners down a tier rather than carrying them indefinitely.

## Gotchas
- Signing many partners and enabling none — focus on a few until each closes deals.
- Direct team "stealing" registered deals; trust in the program collapses quickly.
- Price lists leaking end-customer discounts that undercut partners.
- Unclear who supports the customer post-sale.

## Verify
- [ ] Written ideal partner profile and joint business plan per active partner.
- [ ] Deal-registration rules published with approval SLA and protection period.
- [ ] Direct-sales comp plan doesn't penalise partner deals.
- [ ] Each partner has certified staff and access to demo/NFR.
- [ ] Partner-sourced pipeline tracked and reviewed quarterly.
