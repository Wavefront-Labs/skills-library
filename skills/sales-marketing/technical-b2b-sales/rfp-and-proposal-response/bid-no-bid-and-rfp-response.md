# Bid/no-bid and RFP response

**Goal:** bid only where you can win, then make every evaluator's scoring job easy.
A compliant, clear response beats a clever one that's hard to score.

## 1. Bid/no-bid (decide within 48 h of receipt)
Score 1–5, weight, total; agree a threshold (e.g. bid if ≥ 70%):
| Factor | Weight | Question |
|---|---|---|
| Relationship | high | Did we know about this before it was published? Have we met the evaluators? |
| Fit | high | Can we meet every mandatory requirement without exceptions? |
| Competitive position | med | Is it written around a competitor's product (wired RFP)? |
| Value | med | Is the deal worth the effort (often dozens to hundreds of person-hours)? |
| Capacity | med | Do we have SMEs available before the deadline? |
| Risk | med | Contract terms, liabilities, penalties acceptable? |
Unseen RFPs with a requirement list that mirrors a rival's spec sheet usually lose —
"no-bid" is a valid result. Record the decision and reason.

## 2. Kick-off (day 1–2 after "bid")
- Read the whole document twice; extract every requirement and instruction.
- **Calendar** backwards from the deadline: final submission (aim 24 h early), final
  review, pricing sign-off, drafts due, clarification deadline.
- Assign owners per section; one bid manager owns the whole.
- Submit **clarification questions** before the cut-off — ambiguous scope, pricing format,
  page limits. Answers usually go to all bidders; phrase questions so they don't hand
  competitors your strategy.

## 3. Compliance matrix (the backbone)
Spreadsheet: `Req ID · RFP section/page · requirement text · mandatory? · comply (Y/Partial/N) ·
response section · owner · status · evidence`.
Every "shall/must" becomes a row. Check it at every review; a missed mandatory can
disqualify the whole bid.

## 4. Win themes
2–4 themes tied to the buyer's stated goals and evaluation criteria, each with proof:
"Live in 90 days — 12 similar migrations delivered on schedule (case study, ref)."
Repeat them in the executive summary and in each relevant answer.

## 5. Writing answers evaluators can score
- **Mirror their numbering and headings exactly.** Evaluators score with a sheet keyed
  to their structure.
- Answer first ("Yes, fully compliant."), then how, then proof.
- Use their vocabulary, not your product's.
- Respect page/word limits and formatting rules — over-limit content may be ignored.
- A content library (Loopio, Responsive/RFPIO, or a well-tagged shared folder) speeds
  re-use; always tailor re-used answers — evaluators spot pasted text.

## 6. Pricing
Follow the price schedule format exactly. Price to the evaluation model: if price is 30%
of the score with a formula against the lowest bid, model your score at a few price points.
List assumptions and exclusions clearly. Get commercial and legal sign-off on terms and
any exceptions to the contract.

## 7. Reviews and submission
- **Pink team** (outline/storyboard), **red team** (full draft, scored by reviewers using
  the buyer's criteria), **gold** (final sign-off). Colour-team names vary by company.
- Upload early to the portal; portals fail near deadlines. Keep the submission receipt.

## 8. Panel presentation and debrief
- Shortlist presentation: team that will actually deliver, answer their scoring criteria
  in order, rehearse Q&A with hostile questions, stick to time.
- **Always request a debrief**, win or lose: scores by section, strengths, weaknesses.
  Feed findings into the content library.

## Gotchas
- Public-sector procurement often forbids contacting evaluators during the process —
  use only the official channel. Rules differ by jurisdiction; read the instructions.
- Uploading the wrong file version; name files exactly as instructed.
- Writing "we are the leading..." instead of answering the requirement.

## Verify
- [ ] Bid/no-bid scored and recorded with reasons.
- [ ] Every mandatory requirement appears in the compliance matrix as Y with a response section.
- [ ] Response numbering mirrors the RFP exactly; page limits met.
- [ ] Pricing modelled against the evaluation formula and signed off.
- [ ] Submitted at least 24 h early with receipt saved; debrief requested.
