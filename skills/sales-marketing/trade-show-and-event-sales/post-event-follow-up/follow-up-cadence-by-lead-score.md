# Follow-up cadence by lead score

**Goal:** contact every lead while they still remember you, at a depth matching their
score, so hot leads become meetings within days and warm leads stay warm until they're ready.

Input: the scored, noted lead export from the show (see
sales-marketing.trade-show-and-event-sales.lead-capture-and-scoring.badge-scan-notes-and-floor-scoring).

## 1. Prepare follow-up before the show
Write templates and build the sequences/nurture tracks *before* you travel. After the
show everyone is tired and behind — prepared follow-up actually goes out.

## 2. Route within 24 hours of each show day
| Score | Owner | First touch |
|---|---|---|
| Hot | Account executive / field rep for the territory | Personal email + call within 24–48 h |
| Warm | SDR/BDR or inside sales | Personal-template email within 2–3 days |
| Cold | Marketing | Thank-you email + nurture (only with marketing consent) |
| Existing customer / open opp | Account owner | Personal note, no sequence |
Assign in the CRM with the event campaign tag, so pipeline from the show can be measured.

## 3. First email — reference the conversation
Every hot/warm first email uses the note from the floor:
```
Subject: Our chat at <Show> about <their problem>

Hi <Name>,
Good to meet you at <Show> on <day>. You mentioned <NEED from note> and that
<NOW / context>. <One line on how we help with exactly that.>
As promised, here's <the thing you offered>. Would <specific day/time> work for
the <30-min demo / call> we talked about?
<Your name>   P.S. <PERSONAL hook>
```
Never send "Thanks for stopping by our booth!" with no specifics to hot leads — it reads
as a mass email and gets ignored.

## 4. Cadences by tier (adjust days to your cycle)
**Hot (2 weeks):** D1 email + call · D3 call · D5 email with proof (case study matching
their industry) · D8 call + LinkedIn · D12 email "close the loop". Meeting booked →
exit to opportunity process.
**Warm (3–4 weeks):** D2 email · D6 email with useful content · D10 call · D17 email · D24
final email; if no response, move to nurture rather than closing.
**Cold / nurture:** monthly or bi-weekly content emails; re-score on engagement (e.g. visits
to pricing page, replies) and route to sales when signals pass a threshold.

## 5. Field hand-off rules
- Rep receives: contact, company, score, full note, what was promised, and any booked time.
- Rep accepts or rejects within an SLA (e.g. 24 h); rejected leads get a reason and go back
  to SDR/nurture — not into a black hole.
- If the meeting was booked at the show, the rep confirms it the next working day.

## 6. Keep leads warm until the cycle starts
For "next year" leads: set a dated task at the timeline they gave, plus light nurture.
Invite them to webinars, local events or the next show's meeting room.

## 7. Track
By score tier: % contacted within SLA, reply rate, meetings booked, opportunities,
pipeline value, closed revenue over the following months. These feed the go-again decision
(sales-marketing.trade-show-and-event-sales.event-roi-and-selection.cost-per-lead-and-go-again-decision).

## Gotchas
- Leads imported a week late — response rates drop sharply with every day of delay.
- Hot leads dropped into a generic marketing blast.
- No consent captured, so cold leads can't legally be nurtured in some jurisdictions.
- Reps cherry-picking; check every hot lead had a logged first touch.

## Verify
- [ ] Templates and sequences built before the show.
- [ ] Every lead routed to an owner within 24 h with the campaign tag.
- [ ] Hot/warm first emails reference the floor conversation.
- [ ] Rep accept/reject SLA met; rejected leads re-routed.
- [ ] Conversion by tier reported 30, 90 and 180 days after the show.
