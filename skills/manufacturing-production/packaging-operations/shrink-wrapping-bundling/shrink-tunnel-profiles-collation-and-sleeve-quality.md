# Shrink tunnel profiles, collation and sleeve quality

**Goal:** multipacks and shrink sleeves that are tight, square, legible and hold together
through handling — at line speed and without wasting film or burning it.

## 1. The process
1. **Collation:** product is grouped into the pack pattern (e.g. 2×3 cans) by lane
   dividers, pushers or a flighted conveyor.
2. **Wrapping:** film is wrapped round the group — a **sleeve wrapper** leaves open
   ends (bullseye pack); an **L-sealer** or side sealer fully encloses it.
3. **Shrinking:** a tunnel of hot air (or steam for printed shrink sleeves on bottles)
   heats film above its shrink temperature so it contracts round the product.

## 2. Film
- **Polyethylene (LDPE/LLDPE blends):** the usual bundling film for beverage and can
  multipacks; thicker gauges for heavy bundles.
- **Polyolefin (POF) and PVC:** thinner, clearer, for retail-look full wraps; PVC is
  being phased out in many markets.
- **Shrink ratio** differs in machine direction (MD) and transverse direction (TD):
  orient the film so the greater shrink goes where you need it. Check the data sheet.
- **Pre-printed film** needs registration (eye-mark) so print lands on the right face,
  and distortion allowance in the artwork for the shrink.

## 3. Collation — fix this first
Poor collation can't be fixed in the tunnel. Check:
- Product enters with a gap between packs and in the correct count every time.
- Lane guides set to the product diameter (not tight enough to jam, not loose enough to
  let product twist).
- Pushers/flight bars square and synchronised with the film cut.

## 4. Tunnel settings
The three levers are **temperature, air velocity (fan speed, dampers, nozzle
direction) and dwell time (conveyor speed)**. Heat delivered to the film is what
matters, so they trade off against each other.
- Start from the film supplier's and machine maker's recipe for that film and pack.
- Change **one** variable at a time and run several packs; the tunnel takes minutes
  to restabilise after a temperature change.
- Direct air to where shrink is needed: bottom air for the underside, side air for
  bullseye ends.
- Hold a constant gap between packs; varying gaps mean varying heat per pack.
- Cooling after the tunnel (fans) sets the film so the pack stays tight.

## 5. Defect diagnosis
| Defect | Typical cause | Fix to try |
|---|---|---|
| Loose film / wrinkles | Too little heat; film too wide; low shrink film | More temp or dwell; narrower film |
| **Dog ears** (pointed corners) | Too much film at corners; uneven heat | Reduce film length/width, adjust air at corners |
| Burn-through / holes | Too much heat; film too thin; product stopped in tunnel | Less temp; check conveyor didn't stall |
| Bullseye too large / small | Film length or heat on ends | Adjust film cut length or end air |
| Seal opens | Seal temp/time; contaminated sealing wire | Clean wire, raise seal setting |
| Distorted print | Uneven heat, wrong film orientation | Even air, rotate film MD/TD |
| Crushed or tipped bottles | Too much shrink force on light containers | Less heat, different film |

## 6. Stops and safety
- When the line stops, product in the tunnel overheats: most tunnels lift the hood,
  run the conveyor out or drop the heat automatically — make sure that works and don't
  disable it.
- Tunnel surfaces and fresh packs are hot; use gloves and wait for it to cool before
  clearing jams inside, under isolation.
- Sealing wires/knives: guard and lockout before cleaning.

## 7. Pack presentation check
Square, tight, print facing correctly, legible barcode through the film (shrink can
distort it — scan it), consistent bullseye, holds together when lifted by the film or
handle.

## Verify
- [ ] Film type and orientation match the recipe.
- [ ] Collation correct and consistent before the wrapper.
- [ ] One-variable-at-a-time tunnel adjustment logged.
- [ ] Packs tight, no burns, barcodes scan, lift test passed.

*Uncertain: tunnel temperatures vary widely by film and machine, so none are given here
— use the supplier recipe as the start point.*
