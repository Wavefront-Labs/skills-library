# Foresight methodology — CLA, backcasting, wheels, horizons, and choosing

**Goal:** pick the method that fits the question, run it well, and combine methods so
evidence, creativity, expertise and participation are all covered.

## 1. Method selection
Clarify three things first:
- **Purpose:** explore what *could* happen (exploratory), judge what is *likely*
  (predictive), or design how to reach what is *wanted* (normative).
- **Evidence base:** quantitative data available? or mainly perspectives and narratives?
- **Participants:** experts only, stakeholders, the public?

| Purpose / situation | Methods |
|---|---|
| Find early change | Horizon scanning (social-sciences.futures-studies.horizon-scanning.scanning-protocol-signal-coding-and-clustering) |
| Quantify likely paths | Trend analysis, Delphi, elicitation (see sibling skills) |
| Explore alternative futures | Scenarios (social-sciences.futures-studies.scenario-planning.scenario-construction-consistency-and-validation) |
| Unpack assumptions/worldviews | **Causal layered analysis** |
| Trace consequences of one change | **Futures wheel** |
| Plan transition from present to a desired future | **Backcasting**, **Three Horizons** |
| Test a policy/strategy | **Wind tunnelling** across scenarios |
Popper's **foresight diamond** classifies methods by creativity, expertise, interaction and
evidence; a good project usually covers at least three corners.

## 2. Causal layered analysis (CLA)
Analyse an issue on four layers, then reconstruct:
1. **Litany** — the headline view, data and news ("traffic congestion is worsening").
2. **Systemic causes** — social, economic, political, technical drivers (land-use policy,
   car subsidies).
3. **Worldview/discourse** — the paradigms that make those systems seem natural (mobility
   = freedom = private car).
4. **Myth/metaphor** — the deep, emotional story ("the open road").
Then **reconstruct**: propose a new metaphor ("the city as a living room") and work back up
— what worldview, systems and litany would follow. Changes at the litany level alone tend
not to last. Use in workshops: one column per stakeholder group shows how different
worldviews frame the same issue.

## 3. Backcasting
1. Define a **desirable future state** at a horizon with explicit criteria (e.g. "net-zero
   regional transport by 2050, with access for all within 15 minutes").
2. Describe that end state in detail.
3. Work **backwards**: what must be true at 2045, 2040…? Identify milestones, enabling
   changes, barriers.
4. Assemble a pathway and near-term actions; check feasibility and robustness against
   exploratory scenarios.
Use when the trend-based future is undesirable and a step change is needed (sustainability
transitions). Keep multiple pathways rather than a single plan.

## 4. Futures wheel
Write a change at the centre; first-order consequences in a ring; second- and third-order
consequences outward. Mark positive/negative and link interactions. Good for surfacing
unintended effects quickly; weak on probability.

## 5. Three Horizons
H1 = the dominant system declining in fit; H3 = emerging alternatives (pockets of the future
in the present); H2 = transitional innovations that may support H1 or enable H3. Participants
map the present onto the three curves, then discuss which H2 moves to back.

## 6. Wind tunnelling
Test each policy option against every scenario: does it still work, need adapting, or fail?
Robust options perform acceptably across all; contingent options get trigger indicators.

## 7. Quality in foresight research
- Transparency: document inputs, participants, judgements and how outputs were made.
- Pluralism: include dissenting and marginalised perspectives deliberately.
- Avoid prediction framing for exploratory outputs.
- Evaluate impact: did users change decisions, assumptions or monitoring?

## Gotchas
- CLA that stops at deconstruction without reconstructing an alternative.
- Backcasting from a vague vision gives vague milestones.
- Using every method in a short project — each needs time and the right participants.

## Verify
- [ ] Purpose, evidence base and participants stated; method choice justified.
- [ ] Methods cover several foresight-diamond corners.
- [ ] CLA includes all four layers and a reconstruction.
- [ ] Backcast has explicit end-state criteria, milestones and alternative pathways.
- [ ] Policies wind-tunnelled against scenarios; documentation complete.
